Our story

A small woodworking and handyman shop in Crescent Springs, Kentucky.

A

Built on the basics

MoJoe Services started with a simple idea: do good work, show up when you say you will, and treat every project like it is going in your own home. We are based at 566 Park St in Crescent Springs and serve the surrounding Northern Kentucky neighborhoods.

B

Craft first

Woodworking is a patient trade. We measure twice, cut once, and finish like someone will look closely, because they will. That same care goes into every handyman repair, from a patched wall to a re-hung door.

C

Straight talk

If a repair is not worth doing, we will tell you. If a project needs a different specialist, we will say so. Honest advice has earned us more repeat calls than any advertisement.
