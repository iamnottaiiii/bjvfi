Services

General contracting, building, and excavation services for Boylston and Worcester County.

General Contracting

Clark & Mott Construction acts as your single point of responsibility for the project. That means managing schedules, coordinating subcontractors and trades, keeping the site safe and clean, and making sure the finished work matches the plan and the building code. Whether it is a renovation, an addition, or a new build, you get one contractor accountable for the whole job from first shovel to final walkthrough.

Commercial & Industrial Building

Commercial and industrial construction for businesses across central Massachusetts, from ground up buildings to tenant build outs and expansions. Business listings describe Clark & Mott Construction as a building contractor for commercial and industrial work, serving clients who need the job done on schedule and on budget.

Excavation & Site Work

Every solid structure starts with solid ground. Excavation and site work includes site clearing, grading, trenching, and preparing the footprint so construction starts right. Getting the earthwork done properly protects everything built on top of it.

Home Building

New home construction and major residential builds, managed end to end. From foundation to framing to finish work, the goal is a home built to Massachusetts code, built for New England weather, and built to be lived in for decades.

Real Estate Development

Business directories also list Clark & Mott Construction in connection with real estate development and property work. For development projects, a contractor who understands both the dirt and the building is an asset from the earliest planning stages.
