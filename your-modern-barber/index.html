Our story

A Harlem chair
with history

The shop at 102 W 116th Street has served Harlem under the name Shabazz Your Modern Barber Salon, a neighborhood name regulars still use with pride.

The shop

Your Modern Barber,
Harlem's own

On West 116th Street, between the brownstones and the bodegas, sits a barbershop that regulars know by heart. Listed in neighborhood directories as Shabazz Your Modern Barber Salon, it is the kind of shop where the barbers know your name, your fade, and your stories.

Reviews say the same thing: the guys in the shop are great, the conversation never stops, and you walk out with a fresh cut and a little extra swag. That is the whole business model, and it works.

  • A longtime W 116th Street neighborhood shop
  • Known for fresh cuts and great conversation
