New fence installation

Full tear out of the old fence and professional installation of the new one, with posts set in concrete and everything leveled and squared.

  • Wood privacy fences: cedar or redwood, 6 ft standard, custom heights where allowed
  • Vinyl privacy fences: low maintenance panels with reinforced rails
  • Chain link: galvanized or vinyl coated, with or without privacy slats
  • Ornamental iron: powder coated panels and custom fabrication options
  • Permits and lines: we check local requirements and property lines before we dig

Sample project note: a typical 6 ft cedar privacy run is often priced per linear foot after measuring. sample Your written estimate gives the exact price for your yard.

Fence repair

You rarely need to replace the whole fence. We repair the weak parts and make the rest stand straight again.

  • Post replacement: rotted or leaning posts reset in concrete
  • Board and picket replacement: broken or warped boards swapped to match
  • Sagging gate fixes: rehang, new hinges, anti sag hardware
  • Storm damage: wind blown sections rebuilt to match the original
  • Staining and sealing: refresh weathered wood fences

Gates

A gate that drags or will not latch is a daily annoyance. We build and hang gates that work right from day one.

  • Walk gates: 3 to 4 ft gates with self closing hinges and latches
  • Driveway gates: single or double swing, wood or iron
  • Side yard gates: secure access with lockable hardware
  • Hardware upgrades: hinges, latches, and locks replaced or upgraded

Commercial fencing

Secure perimeters for lots, storage yards, and small business properties across the Valley.

  • Chain link enclosures: secure runs for equipment and inventory
  • Security upgrades: taller heights, barbed or razor options where permitted
  • Dumpster enclosures: code compliant enclosures with gates
  • Scheduled maintenance: keep commercial fencing sound year round
