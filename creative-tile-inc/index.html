Featured

The full tile project, handled end to end

Creative Tile Inc. is both designer and installer. That matters, because the best tile work starts before the first tile is cut: choosing the right material, planning the layout, and thinking through grout lines, edges, and transitions.

Then comes the installation itself: level surfaces, tight joints, clean caulk lines. The result is tile work that looks intentional from every angle, whether it is a kitchen backsplash or a full bathroom.
