The shop

Auburn's chair

A neighborhood barbershop on Central Street where the cuts are sharp, the shop is spotless and the regulars are on a first-name basis with their barber.

What the shop is known for

Clean shop.
Serious craft.

Reviewers keep coming back to the same themes: a shop that is always extremely clean, a cool vibe, and barbers who take on any style challenge. The walls carry original artwork done by Adam himself.

Families drive in from surrounding towns. One reviewer moved to the area and now brings their son back every time. Another has trusted Jay with their cut for nearly five years without a single disappointment.
