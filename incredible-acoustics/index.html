What the name means

Most drywall contractors hang board and move on. An acoustical contractor thinks about what the room sounds like when the work is done: less echo in the conference room, less bleed between offices, a home theater that actually feels like one.

The work sits at the intersection of two trades. Drywall craft makes the surfaces flawless, and acoustic treatment makes the space perform. Incredible Acoustics lists itself in both worlds.
