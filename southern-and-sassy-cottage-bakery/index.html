Our Story

A little cottage bakery with big Southern heart.

🏠

Cottage Baked in Dyer

Southern & Sassy is a home cottage bakery on Henry Street in Dyer, Indiana. Everything is baked in small batches to order, the old fashioned way, with real homemade taste in every bite.

💖

Southern at Heart

The name says it all. Warm, playful, and full of charm, like a Sunday supper at grandma's house. We bake treats that taste like somebody loves you.

🎁

Made for Sharing

From Christmas packages to party platters, our sweets are made for gatherings. Folks order boxes by the dozen for family, coworkers, and celebrations of every kind.

Taste the Homemade Difference

One bite of a strawberry shortcake truffle or a warm pumpkin cinnamon roll and you will know exactly what the fuss is about.
