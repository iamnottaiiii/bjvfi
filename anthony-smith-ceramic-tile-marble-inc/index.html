About

Anthony Smith, tile craftsman

Anthony Smith Ceramic Tile & Marble Inc. has been operating out of Cincinnati, Ohio since 2000. It is owner-operated: Anthony runs each job himself and brings in subcontractors only for extra labor on larger work.

The business holds a BBB A+ rating with a file opened in 2015, and public directory listings credit 26 years of experience in the trade. Pricing is determined by the job, and no travel charges apply.
