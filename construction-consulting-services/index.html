How we help

A second set of expert eyes on every phase

Most construction problems start long before the first shovel hits the ground. We get involved early, so you build with a clear plan instead of costly surprises.

01

Plan before you build

We review your goals, your lot and your drawings, then map a realistic path from idea to permit. Scope, schedule and sequencing, worked out on paper first, where changes are cheap.

02

Control the budget

Line-item budgets, allowance tracking and change-order review keep spending visible. You approve every dollar before it is committed.

03

Navigate permits

Plan checks, correction lists and city submittals are where projects stall. We coordinate the paperwork so approvals keep moving.

04

Oversee the work

Site walks, milestone inspections and contractor coordination while the build is underway, so quality stays high and the schedule stays honest.

Services

Consulting for every stage of the build

Pick a tab to see what each service covers. Service descriptions below are starter wording the owner can adjust.

Tab 01Pre-construction planning

Before you spend on drawings or contractors, we define what the project actually is: what is feasible on your lot, what it will likely cost, and the smartest order to do things in.

  • Site and lot feasibility review for builds, additions and ADUs
  • Scope definition: what is included, what is optional, what can wait
  • Realistic scheduling from first drawing to move-in day
  • Plan and drawing review before they go to the city

Tab 02Budgeting and cost control

Most builds go over budget because nobody tracked the small decisions. We build a working budget and watch it with you, line by line.

  • Detailed construction budgets with allowances for finishes
  • Change-order review: what is fair, what is padding, what to push back on
  • Payment schedule tied to completed milestones, not calendar dates
  • Value engineering: where to save without hurting quality or resale

Tab 03Permits and plan checks

The permit counter is where good projects lose months. We keep your submittal clean, your correction responses fast, and your approvals moving.

  • Permit strategy: which permits, which order, which agency
  • Plan check correction responses coordinated with your designer
  • Inspection scheduling and punch list follow-through

Tab 04Contractor selection and bids

Choosing the wrong contractor is the most expensive mistake in construction. We help you compare bids fairly and sign a contract that protects you.

  • Bid leveling: comparing contractor proposals line by line
  • License, insurance and reference verification
  • Contract review: scope, payment terms, timelines and warranties

Tab 05Project oversight

Once construction starts, we stay involved: walking the site, checking milestones and keeping communication between you and your contractor clear.

  • Scheduled jobsite walks with written observation reports
  • Milestone sign-offs before progress payments are released
  • Schedule tracking and delay documentation
  • Final walkthrough and punch list management
A note on pricing: consulting fees depend on project size and scope. Call (213) 805-7804 to talk through your project and get a clear, written quote before any work begins.
About

Consulting for people
who want to build once

Construction Consulting Services is a construction consulting firm based in Northridge, California, serving homeowners and small builders across the San Fernando Valley.

Who we are

An owner's advocate, not a contractor's

Contractors answer to their own bottom line. We answer to yours. As an independent consulting firm, we work for the owner: reviewing plans with fresh eyes, questioning costs that do not add up, and making sure the project you approved is the project that gets built.

Whether you are building a new home, adding square footage, or planning an ADU, we bring structure to a process that too often runs on guesswork.

Why it matters

Construction rewards preparation

The cheapest time to fix a problem is on paper. Every hour spent planning, budgeting and vetting up front saves weeks of rework and thousands of dollars later.

How we work

Clear, documented, honest

Written roadmaps. Line-item budgets. Site reports you can actually read. If something is not in your best interest, we say so, plainly.
