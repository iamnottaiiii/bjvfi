Our story

Carmelo's chair.

Delux Hairstylist has been cutting hair on Kennedy Boulevard in North Bergen for decades. The man behind the chair is Carmelo, a classic Italian barber with what one reviewer called "close to half a century of service" and a beautiful soul.

He still charges twenty dollars a haircut, which reviewers call very fair and reasonable. The shop plays Sinatra. The conversation flows. Customers have followed him from his previous location because some things are worth the trip.

It is cash only, family friendly, and there is metered street parking out front. Come as you are.
