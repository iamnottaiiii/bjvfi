Refinish instead of replace

Nearly two decades at the workbench

Crow Wood Finishing began serving the community in 2007, according to business directory records, and its Angi profile lists 19 years of experience in carpentry, woodworking, cabinet making, restoration, refacing, and refinishing. The company works across kitchens, bathrooms, doors, furniture, bookcases, and entertainment centers, with paint and stain work done on site, in house, or at an offsite woodshop.

Directory listings place the shop at 1541 Old State Rte 74 in Batavia, Ohio, and note service across Mason, Ohio and surrounding areas. The company offers free project estimates and a warranty on its work, per its Angi profile.
