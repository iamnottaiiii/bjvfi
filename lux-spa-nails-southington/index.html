Our story

A quiet retreat on Queen Street

Lux Spa & Nails is a nail salon and day spa at 360 Queen St in Southington, Connecticut. Guests describe it as a tranquil retreat: a clean, comfortable space where the team takes its time with every manicure, pedicure, facial and massage.

What sets the salon apart, according to reviewers, is precision. Detailed filing, careful cuticle work, and polish applied with a steady hand have earned the team loyal regulars, some of whom have visited for eight years or more.

Whether it is your first-ever manicure or your monthly standing appointment, the welcome is the same: walk in, settle in, and let the team handle the details.
