Why homeowners stay

A crew you recognize, a lawn you do not have to think about.

Cherry Valley Landscaping has tended Montgomery County lawns for over three decades. Every person on the crew brings years of landscaping experience, and the work is never handed off to subcontractors. That means the people who show up in spring are the same people you see in fall.

"We do not ever have to worry about our lawn." That is the whole pitch: dependable, week after week.

Weekly and seasonal plans cover mowing, edging, trimming, and cleanup, while one-time services handle the heavy lifts: aeration and seeding, mulch beds, leaf removal, and gutter cleaning. Plant designs and installations turn tired beds into something you actually enjoy looking at.

Emergency service is available when a storm leaves branches across the yard. Every visit ends the way it should: beds edged, clippings blown off the walk, and the gate latched behind us.

Our story

Three decades on the same streets.

Cherry Valley Landscaping started in Rockville in the late 1980s with a truck, a mower, and a simple standard: do the job right the first time. Thirty-six years later, the standard has not changed, and neither has the address: 4805 Cherry Valley Dr, where the company is still based.

We do not hire subcontractors. The crew on your lawn works for us, and has for years.

Every employee brings five or more years of landscaping experience. That depth shows up in the details: seed matched to shade, beds edged in a clean line, and plantings chosen for Maryland clay, not for a catalog photo.

Homeowners in Rockville, Olney, and Gaithersburg have kept us on their lawns for years at a time. Many of our longest relationships started with a single cleanup and turned into seasonal care that just keeps going.
