Our Story

Keepers of a Vanishing Craft

Queen City Services began with a simple realization, told in the owner's own words:

"QCS began with the realization that the skills and knowledge associated with pre 1900 masonry construction were being lost. We manufacture lime based mortar for historic projects as well as stockpile a variety of historic clay bricks. We specialize in invisible tuck pointing and repairs."

Why We Do It

Cincinnati is the Queen City, and its crown is its brickwork: the rowhouses, churches, and warehouses built before 1900. Modern materials and methods can damage those old walls. We keep the old ways alive so the city's heritage stands for another century.

Asked what they love most about the job, the answer was simple: "The never ending variety of work that we do."

And the best reaction ever gotten from a customer? "We fully warranty all work so all our clients are satisfied."
