Our story

Rooted in Fort Erie

Old Erie Acres grows from George Rd: a gardener's eye, honest work, and a love for what the Niagara region can grow.

Grown, not installed

We think in seasons, not weekends. Plantings are chosen to settle in, spread, and look better in year three than year one.

Yards people live in

Pretty matters, but so does practical. Paths you actually walk, beds you can reach, vegetables you will eat.

Right plant, right time

Pruning and planting follow the plant's calendar. That is how you get flowers, fruit, and hedges that thrive.
