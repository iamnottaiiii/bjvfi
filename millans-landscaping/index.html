About

A Glendale crew for Glendale yards

Millan's Landscaping is a landscaping business based at 329 W Cerritos Ave in Glendale, California. The crew works across Glendale and the surrounding neighborhoods, handling the everyday yard work that keeps properties looking cared for: mowing, trimming, irrigation, tree work, and cleanups.

What matters here

Landscaping is a trust business. Someone is coming onto your property, around your plants and your pipes. Millan's approach is straightforward: show up, do the work that was agreed on, and leave the yard cleaner than it was found.

The service list tells the story. This is a working crew, not a design studio: lawns, sprinklers, palms, trees, drainage, sod. The unglamorous jobs that keep a yard alive.

Only public facts are stated here. No founding story or history is published for this business, so none is claimed. The best way to learn about the crew is to call and talk through your project.
