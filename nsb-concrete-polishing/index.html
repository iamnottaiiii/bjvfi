Why polished concrete

One floor. Zero waxing. Decades of shine.

Polished concrete is ground flat, hardened with densifier and refined to your chosen gloss. The result resists stains, needs only dust mopping, and never peels like coatings do.

  • ✓Garages, warehouses, retail floors, showrooms and homes
  • ✓Matte, satin or high-gloss finishes, with optional stain color
  • ✓Low maintenance: no wax, no strip, no reseal schedule
  • ✓Dust-proofed and hardened against tire marks and foot traffic

Badly worn slab? NSB re-services and resurfaces concrete, including micro-topping overlays, so old floors get a fresh canvas before the polish goes on. Countertops get the same treatment.

Finish options

Choose your gloss

Matte / Level 1

Flat, natural look with light refinement. Great for shops and warehouses.

Satin / Level 2

Soft sheen that hides traffic well. The go-to for garages and retail.

Semi-Gloss / Level 3

Noticeable reflection and depth. Showrooms and offices love it.

High-Gloss / Level 4

Mirror finish with crisp reflections. Maximum wow for homes and lobbies.

About

11 years of floors that shine

NSB Concrete Polishing has spent over a decade turning dull gray slabs into finished floors across North Hollywood and the greater Los Angeles area.

The crew specializes in one trade and does it well: mechanical concrete polishing, plus the resurfacing and countertop work that goes with it. No jack-of-all-trades shortcuts, no coatings that peel in a year. Just ground, hardened, polished concrete built to take daily abuse.

Homeowners get garage and interior floors that clean with a dust mop. Businesses get retail, warehouse and showroom floors that hold their gloss under forklifts and foot traffic.
