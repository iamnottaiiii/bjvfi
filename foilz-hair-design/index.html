Our story

A Bay Ridge salon
with deep roots

Foilz Hair Design has been part of the Bay Ridge neighborhood for decades, growing from a small 3rd Ave studio into a color destination.

How we got here

Relaxed, intimate,
and all about the craft

Foilz began with a simple goal: a relaxed and intimate atmosphere where creativity could flourish. After several years at a first location on 72nd Street and 3rd Avenue, the salon moved to its current home at 9318 3rd Ave in 1994, a warmer, more elegant space built around soft colors and real wooden furniture.

From day one, Foilz has held a high standard: in-house training, professional seminars, and quality products. The team believes in continuing education to stay at the cutting edge, which is why the salon is so widely respected as color specialists.

And Foilz has always given back to Bay Ridge, donating services to local schools and fundraisers for neighborhood organizations.

The gold standard for hair care in Bay Ridge, kept up one appointment at a time.
