Who we are

Based in Lake Balboa, serving the Valley

Aquaman Pool & Spa Service-Contractor works out of Lake Balboa and cares for pools and spas across Van Nuys and the surrounding San Fernando Valley neighborhoods. We are a small crew, which means the people who answer your call are the people who show up at your gate.

What makes us the best, in our view, is customer service and a clean record. Our quality pool care has kept clients using us for many years. We understand that transparency is vital, and going above and beyond is what we do every day.
