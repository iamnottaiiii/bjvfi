The Punch List

Every service below comes straight from the business listing. If it is masonry, it is on the list. One thing they do not take on: roofing.

  • Chimney Repairs

    Leaks, spalling brick, damaged flues and worn mortar joints repaired to keep your chimney safe and draft-ready.

  • Chimney Caps

    Caps fitted to keep rain, animals and debris out of the flue, extending the life of the whole chimney.

  • Teardown and Relay

    Chimneys too far gone for patching are taken down and rebuilt course by course.

  • Tuckpointing

    Old, crumbling mortar raked out and replaced so brickwork looks sharp and stays watertight.

  • Brick Restoration

    Historic and damaged brickwork restored with care to match the original look. Listed as a specialty.

  • Retaining Walls

    Sturdy block and stone retaining walls built to hold grade and dress up the landscape.

  • Fireplaces

    Masonry fireplaces built or repaired, indoors and out.

  • Block Work

    Concrete block walls and foundations, laid plumb and true.

  • Cultured Stone

    Cultured stone veneer installed for the look of natural stone without the weight or cost. Sample
