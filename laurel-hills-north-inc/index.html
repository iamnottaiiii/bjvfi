Communities

Laurel Hills North,
Enola, Pennsylvania

A well established residential community of condominiums and townhomes in East Pennsboro Township, developed in phases from 1989 onward.

A neighborhood that has stood the test of time

Laurel Hills North grew in phases from the late 1980s through the 2000s, which is why you will find a mix of attached home styles: cozy two bedroom condos and larger three and four bedroom townhomes, many with garages, decks, and private courtyards.

  • Attached homes: condos, townhomes, and lofts
  • Two to four bedrooms, two to four baths
  • Courtyard and garage parking throughout
  • Minutes to Harrisburg and the West Shore

Homeowner resources

Laurel Hills North is governed by its condominium association, with recorded declarations and community rules on file with Cumberland County. If you own or rent in the community, your association documents are the first place to look for answers about dues, parking, pets, and exterior changes.

For questions about the development itself, including historical plats and plans, contact our office at (717) 732-8950. For day to day association matters such as dues and maintenance, please reach your association manager directly.

Partnering on what comes next

We are a local developer, not a national builder, which means decisions are made in Enola by people who know the township, the county process, and the trade base. If you are a landowner, municipality, or investor with a residential project in Central PA, we would like to hear about it.

Start with a conversation: (717) 732-8950, Monday through Friday.

Our story

One community,
done right

The Laurel Hills North story begins with a declaration recorded in Cumberland County in 1989, submitting the land to the Pennsylvania Uniform Condominium Act and creating Laurel Hills North Condominium 1. Phase by phase, the neighborhood grew into the community you see today.

Laurel Hills North Inc carries that work forward: the developer's office at 1540 Yorkshire Pl, right inside the community it built. We believe a developer should be reachable, accountable, and still around long after the last unit sells.

  • Pennsylvania corporation, based in Enola
  • Office inside the community we developed
  • Recorded declarations on file with Cumberland County
