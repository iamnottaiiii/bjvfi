What public records say about Tanda Drywall, Llc. Short, factual, no tall tales.

Tanda Drywall, LLC was organized in Kentucky on August 10, 2011, and remains active and in good standing with the Kentucky Secretary of State. It is registered as a construction special trade contractor, which is the formal way of saying: drywall is the trade.

The company is based in Union, in Boone County, Kentucky, and serves the Northern Kentucky area. State filings list Amanda Wilson as the organizer and a member, and Todd Wilson as a member and owner.

That is the public record. The rest is the work itself: hanging, taping, finishing, and repairing drywall for homes across the county, one smooth wall at a time.
