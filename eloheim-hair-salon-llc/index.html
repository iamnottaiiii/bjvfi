Our story

Where faith meets beauty

The name says it all. Eloheim was founded on the belief that looking your best and feeling your best go hand in hand, and that a salon should be a place of warmth, dignity, and genuine care.

On Allentown Road in Suitland, the team welcomes everyone: women, men, and children, every hair type and every texture. Stylists take time to listen, consult honestly, and deliver results that fit your life, not just the trend of the moment.

Guests notice the difference the moment they walk in: a clean, professional space, a calm and friendly atmosphere, and stylists who are truly knowledgeable about their craft.
