Services

General dentistry, done right

The core services most smiles need, most of the time. Call (410) 243-5512 with questions about any of them.

Exams and cleanings

Regular checkups, professional cleanings, and x-rays when needed. The foundation everything else is built on, and the best value in dentistry: prevention costs less than repair.

Fillings and crowns

Cavities repaired with tooth-colored fillings, and damaged teeth rebuilt with crowns that look and feel natural. We match shade and shape to your own teeth.

Bridges and dentures

Missing teeth replaced with bridges or full and partial dentures, fitted for comfort so you can eat, speak, and smile without thinking about them.

Root canal treatment

Deep decay or infection treated from inside the tooth, relieving pain and saving teeth that would otherwise need to come out.

Extractions

When a tooth truly cannot be saved, it is removed as gently as possible, with your options for replacement laid out clearly.

Emergency care

Dental emergencies get priority. Call (410) 243-5512 early in the day and describe the problem; we will get you seen as soon as we can.

About the practice

Dentistry with a light touch

Brill Warren a DDS is a general dentistry practice at 3902 Greenway in Baltimore, MD 21218. The philosophy is simple: explain everything, recommend only what is needed, and treat people the way you would want your own family treated.
