About us

A new salon, a decade of experience

Our story

Everything is new here, except our hands

JR Nails is a new salon with everything fresh, and the team behind the chairs brings more than 10 years of nail work experience to every appointment.

That experience shows in the details: French tips with hand-painted florals, dip powder sets that clients wear proudly for weeks, and a calm, unhurried approach even when you walk in last minute.

We are patient with every client and serious about quality. Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, you will be greeted warmly and looked after well.

Patience first

We take our time with every set. No rushing, no cutting corners, just careful work.

Clean and comfortable

A fresh space and hygienic practices, so you can relax while we work.

Fair pricing

Clients call us well priced for the quality. Great nails should not break the bank.
