The Contractor Behind the Signal

A full service general contractor specializing in telecommunications, working out of Spring Grove Avenue in Cincinnati.

When networks go up, somebody has to build what they stand on. Integrated Construction Services, Inc., known on the street as ICS, is a full service general contractor specializing in telecommunications work, based at 3844 Spring Grove Ave in Cincinnati, Ohio.

The company describes itself plainly: full service general contracting with a telecommunications specialty. That means the structural, electrical, and site work that keeps wireless and wireline networks running, handled by one accountable contractor.

ICS keeps weekday hours, Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and publishes its company site at icsperforms.com. If you are planning a telecom build or a commercial project in the Cincinnati area, the crew is a phone call away. The listed number carries a 519 area code, so confirm it when you call.
