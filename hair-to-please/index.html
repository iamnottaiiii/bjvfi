Our Story

A Syracuse salon built around one simple idea: you should leave looking and feeling your best.

A full-service salon for Syracuse.

Hair to Please is a hair salon at 104 Arterial Rd offering all hair services for women, men and children: haircuts, color, balayage, highlights, keratin treatments, perms, extensions, weaves, wig installs, locs and braiding.

Beyond hair, the salon offers eyelash extensions, lash lifts and tints, airbrush makeup and facial waxing, so clients can complete their whole look in one appointment.

The salon specializes in wedding, prom and special occasion makeup and hair. From trial runs to the wedding day, bridal parties to prom nights, the team creates looks that photograph beautifully and last all night.
