Why it matters

Three problems.

Most homeowners never see inside their vents, ducts, or chimney. That is exactly where trouble hides.

01

Fire risk in the laundry room

Lint is extremely flammable. When the vent line clogs, heat builds up and airflow drops. A professional dryer vent cleaning clears the full run from dryer to exterior hood, cutting fire risk and drying time.

02

Dust cycling through every room

Your HVAC system recirculates the same air all day. Dust, pet dander, and debris in the ductwork end up on your shelves and in your lungs. Clean ducts mean cleaner air and a system that does not work as hard.

03

A chimney that is not ready for winter

Creosote buildup, cracked liners, and damaged caps turn a cozy fireplace into a hazard. J&Y inspects, cleans, and repairs chimneys so you can burn with confidence when the cold hits.

Our story

The crew Stoneham calls first

J&Y started with a simple idea: do the jobs other companies rush through, and do them right.

From our base on Spencer Street in Stoneham, we serve homeowners and businesses across the Greater Boston area with dryer vent cleaning, air duct cleaning, and chimney repair. Our technicians take vent piping apart to get real access instead of waving a brush at the opening. We explain the process before we start, protect your home while we work, and double check the airflow when we finish.

Customers tell us the same things: we showed up prepared, the price was what we quoted, and the results were visible. Some jobs are small, a clogged laundry vent. Some are bigger, like the air balancing and smoke testing we did for a new sandwich shop to help them track down ventilation problems. Either way, you get clear communication from the first call to the final walkthrough.
