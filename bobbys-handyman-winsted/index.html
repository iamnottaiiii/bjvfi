Our story

Local, family owned, and proud of the work

Bobby's Handyman / Property Management Services is based at 26B Greenwoods Ave in Winsted, Connecticut, and serves homeowners and landlords across the area.

According to the business listing, the team is local and 100% family owned, with more than 40 years in the business. The philosophy is simple: when we work at someone's house, we treat it as our house, and we do not stop until the job is exactly how the customer wanted it.

That means showing up on time, protecting your floors and furniture, and cleaning up at the end of the day so the only sign we were there is the finished work.
