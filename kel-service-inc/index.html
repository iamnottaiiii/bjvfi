Services

What the shop handles

Pick a category to see the work. Every job starts with a diagnosis and an honest estimate. Call (862) 291-2677 for pricing.

Check engine diagnostics

Computer scan plus hands on testing to find the real cause behind warning lights, not just the code.

Engine repair

Belts, hoses, gaskets, sensors and cooling system work for engines that overheat, leak or lose power.

Tune ups

Spark plugs, filters and adjustments that smooth out rough idles and sluggish acceleration.

Exhaust work

Mufflers, pipes and catalytic converter checks when the car gets loud or smells wrong.

Brake pads & rotors

Full brake inspection, pad and rotor replacement, and brake fluid service for confident stops.

Shocks & struts

Replacement of worn shocks and struts that cause bouncing, nose dives or uneven tire wear.

Wheel alignment

Alignment checks and corrections when the car pulls to one side or the steering wheel sits crooked.

Steering repair

Tie rods, ball joints and power steering fixes for loose, stiff or noisy steering.

Oil changes

Conventional and synthetic oil changes with filter, plus a quick look over the whole car.

Fluid services

Coolant, transmission fluid, brake fluid and power steering fluid checks and top offs.

Filters & wipers

Air filters, cabin filters and wiper blades, the small stuff that keeps driving pleasant.

Scheduled maintenance

Factory interval services to keep warranties valid and small issues from turning big.

Battery testing & replacement

Free battery tests, replacements and terminal cleaning for no starts and slow cranks.

Alternators & starters

Charging system diagnosis and replacement when the car dies overnight or clicks instead of turning over.

Tire sales & service

New tires, rotations, balancing and flat repairs matched to your car and your driving.

Lights & wiring

Headlights, taillights, fuses and wiring repairs that keep you visible and legal on the road.

Good to know: this list covers our most common work. If your car needs something not listed here, call (862) 291-2677 and ask. If we can fix it, we will tell you straight. If we cannot, we will tell you that too.
