Our story

A Monrovia contractor built on follow through

Alliance Construction Team works out of Monrovia, California, serving homeowners and businesses across the San Gabriel Valley. The company was built around a simple idea: one crew, one point of contact, and work that holds up.

That means answering the phone, showing up when we say we will, and treating the schedule like a promise. Residential remodels, commercial build outs, and everything in between get the same treatment: a clear plan, a written price, and steady communication until the punch list is done.

We are easy to reach and easier to work with. When you call Alliance Construction Team, you talk to the people who will actually build your project.
