Our story

A Bakery With a Kitchen, or a Kitchen With a Bakery?

Honestly, it is both, and that is the whole point.

Barbara's Specialty Bakery and Restaurant Home Cooking grew out of one simple idea: the same hands that bake your birthday cake should be able to cook you dinner, too. On Broadway in Merrillville, the ovens turn out custom wedding cakes, banana pudding cake, and fresh cupcakes while the stovetop simmers collard greens and layers chicken lasagna.

It is a small, family-feeling shop where Ms. Barbara knows her regulars and newcomers get treated like regulars by the second visit. Folks drive in from Valparaiso, Porter County, and even Cleveland, Ohio for a taste of down-home Southern cooking they cannot find closer to home.

What keeps people coming back is care. Diabetics find real sugar-free options. Vegans find a menu that goes far beyond a side salad, with jackfruit dishes and plant-based comfort classics. And everyone finds the banana pudding, the dessert people write home about.

This is food for the soul, baked and cooked with love, served with the kind of customer service you remember.
