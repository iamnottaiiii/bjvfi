The Atelier Journal

How a restoration unfolds, in five chapters

01

Intake and Assessment

Your vehicle is received, photographed, and inspected panel by panel. A written condition report records everything we find before any work begins.

02

Disassembly and Cataloging

Each component is removed, labeled, and cataloged. Original parts are set aside for refurbishment; missing pieces are noted for sourcing.

03

Metalwork and Fabrication

Rust is cut away, panels are straightened or fabricated, and the body shell is brought back to true before a single coat of paint is mixed.

04

Finish and Assembly

Paint, brightwork, glass, and trim are fitted in sequence. Mechanical systems are rebuilt and tuned alongside the body.

05

Detailing and Delivery

A final concours level detail, a road test, and a walkthrough of the completed journal with you before the car leaves the atelier.
