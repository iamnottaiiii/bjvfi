What Is on RecordPublic sources only

Louis Auto Repair is an auto repair shop at 505 Elberon Ave, Cincinnati, OH 45205, reachable at (616) 821-4009. Public listings describe it as an independent shop known for honest, reasonably priced work: tire repairs, general maintenance, and fixes such as mirror replacements and radiator issues, with reviewers noting the shop avoids unnecessary upselling.

Reviewers also mention the shop helping travelers and college students in a pinch, staying late when a car needed to be safe, and doing careful work on older vehicles.
