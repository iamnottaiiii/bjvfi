Our story

A Linden contractor with a designer's eye

Endless Services, LLC is a general contracting company based at 426 Morristown Rd in Linden, NJ, led by contractor Demetri Georgallis. The company takes on kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and whole-home updates across Union County.

Clients describe a crew that is professional, courteous, and highly skilled, with work that is meticulous and often finished ahead of schedule. One reviewer tells how Demetri stepped in when a kitchen designer and cabinet provider sent the wrong measurements and damaged materials, straightened everything out, and kept the project on track. That level of care is the company standard, not the exception.

The approach is simple: plan carefully, communicate constantly, and leave the work area clean and organized every day until the final walkthrough.
