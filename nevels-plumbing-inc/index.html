Our story

A family trade, carried forward

Nevels Plumbing Inc is a family-run plumbing shop based at 9219 Camp Ernst Road in Union, Kentucky. The trade runs in the family: longtime customers report calling on Josh Nevel today, and his father before him, across many years of service.

It is a small, local operation, the kind where you talk to the people who will actually do the work. Reviewers describe the crew as dependable, congenial, and experienced, and several say they have hired the company again and recommended it to others.

The shop is listed with decades of experience in residential plumbing, from water heaters and fixture installs to leak repair and drain work, serving homes across Union, Boone County, and Northern Kentucky.
