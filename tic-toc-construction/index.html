Our process

How your project runs

No surprises, no vanishing contractors. Here is exactly what happens after you call.

01

Free walkthrough

We come out, measure everything and talk through what you want. You describe the goal; we flag anything structural or permit-related.

02

Written bid

You get an itemized bid with a start date and timeline, in writing. No vague allowances, no open-ended hourly billing.

03

Permits and prep

We pull permits where required and order materials before demo day, so the job does not stall waiting on deliveries.

04

Construction

One crew on your job, daily cleanup, and a check-in at every milestone so you always know where things stand.

05

Inspections

We schedule and pass all required inspections. You never have to chase the city yourself.

06

Final walkthrough

We walk the finished work together and fix any punch-list items. The job is done when you sign off.
