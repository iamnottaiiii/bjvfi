Do it yourself, minus the hassle

Bring your baskets and use our washers and dryers. Big loads are welcome, including comforters and blankets that will not fit in a home machine.

  • Large-capacity washers for bulky loads
  • Fast, hot dryers that actually dry
  • Clean, well-lit shop in Bay Ridge
Machine pricing is posted in store. Call (718) 238-3828 for current rates.

Drop it off, pick up fresh

Our wash and fold service is what regulars rave about: your laundry washed, dried, and neatly folded, ready to put away. Regulars call it one of the best values in the area.

  • Washed, dried, and folded with care
  • Pickup and delivery available
  • Great for busy weeks and big families
Priced by the pound. Call (718) 238-3828 for the current per-pound rate and pickup scheduling.

Delicates and the good stuff

For the pieces that need more than a machine cycle. Regulars bring their dry cleaning and tailoring here too, and leave satisfied.

  • Dry cleaning for suits, dresses, and coats
  • Comforters, blankets, and bulky bedding
  • Tailoring and alterations help
Pricing varies by garment. Call (718) 238-3828 for a quote before you drop off.
