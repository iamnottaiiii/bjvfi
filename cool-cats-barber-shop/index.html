Our Story

The shop on Transit Road

Cool Cats Barber shop sits at 6045 Transit Rd in Depew, New York, serving the neighborhood with the kind of barbering that never goes out of style. It is a shop built on the simple idea that a great haircut changes how you carry yourself.

Walk ins are welcome, regulars are remembered, and every client gets the same careful attention in the chair. From a first back to school cut to a weekly touch up, the goal is the same: leave sharper than you arrived.

"Leave sharper than you arrived."
