Small roof problems become big ones fast

A missing shingle, a cracked tile, flashing that lifted in the last windstorm: water finds every one of them. The good news is that most leaks are cheap to fix when they are caught early.

  • Water stains on ceilings or walls
  • Missing, cracked or curling shingles and tiles
  • Granules piling up in the gutters
  • A roof older than 20 years that has never been inspected
Get a free inspection

When repair is not enough, we replace it right

Some roofs are past patching. When that is the case, we will tell you straight, walk you through material options, and install a roof rated for Valley sun, Santa Ana winds and winter rain.

Every replacement starts with a full tear-off inspection of the decking, proper underlayment, and flashing details done to manufacturer spec. No shortcuts you cannot see.

Pro Craft Roofing Services is a local roofing contractor based in Granada Hills, built on a simple idea: inspect honestly, quote straight, install clean.

We look first, sell second

Too many roofing quotes start with a price and end with pressure. We do it the other way around: we get on the roof, document what we find with photos, and explain your options in plain language.

Sometimes the answer is a $200 flashing repair. Sometimes the roof is done and patching it would be throwing money away. Either way, you hear the truth from the person who was actually up there.

  • Free, photo-documented inspections
  • Repair-first mindset, replace only when it makes sense
  • Clean job sites, magnet-swept when we leave

Your neighbors' roofs are our reputation

We work where we live. Most of our jobs come from Granada Hills, Northridge, Mission Hills, San Fernando and the surrounding Valley, often from one neighbor telling the next.

That is the whole business model: do the job right, leave the place cleaner than we found it, and let the street do the talking.
