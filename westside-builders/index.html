About

Woodland Hills roots,
Valley wide reach.

Westside Builders is a construction company based at 20907 Naranja Road in Woodland Hills, California, building across the San Fernando Valley and the Westside.

How every job runs

1. Walkthrough. We visit, measure, and listen before we quote anything.

2. Written estimate. Line items, allowances, and a schedule, all on paper.

3. Permits and build. We pull permits, manage inspections, and keep you updated.

4. Final walkthrough. Punch list finished before the final check is written.

What we will not do

No verbal change orders. No disappearing for two weeks mid project. No surprise line items on the final invoice.

Construction has a trust problem. We think the fix is boring on purpose: answer the phone, write everything down, and finish what we started.
