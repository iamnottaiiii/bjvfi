Our story

A Cincinnati shop with a simple rule

Lambert Heating & Air Conditioning is based on Fairway Drive in Union Township, Cincinnati. We are a working HVAC contractor, not a franchise with a script. The rule here is the one customers keep writing about in reviews: treat people fairly, and the business takes care of itself.

  1. A

    Honest first

    One customer wrote that our tech tried the cheaper repair first to save them the cost of a new blower fan, and when the problem came back, credited the first visit toward the replacement. That is not a policy memo. That is just how the shop runs.

  2. B

    Show up and diagnose

    Customers mention fast diagnosis, ordered parts without drama, and jobs that went great start to finish. We aim for the kind of visit where you learn exactly what is wrong before anyone picks up a wrench.

  3. C

    Homes and light commercial

    From single-family homes to small commercial spaces around Cincinnati, we install, repair, and maintain central air, heating, boilers, and the ductwork and ventilation around them.
