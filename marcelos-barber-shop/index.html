About

The story of Marcelo's Barber Shop

Marcelo's Barber Shop is a long-standing Downey barbershop on Imperial Highway. Barbers Marcelo, Mario and Pancho are known for classic cuts, fades and fast, friendly service.

Located at 9059 Imperial Hwy., Downey, CA 90242, the shop serves Downey and the surrounding neighborhoods. Walk-ins and regulars are both welcome, and the team is happy to talk through the cut before the clippers come out.
