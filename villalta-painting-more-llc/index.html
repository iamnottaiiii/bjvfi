Interior Painting

Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, whole homes. We move and cover furniture, mask everything, and leave crisp edges.

  • Walls, ceilings, trim, baseboards and doors
  • Accent walls and two-tone rooms
  • Low-odor paints for lived-in homes
Sample price: a standard bedroom often runs a few hundred dollars. Call for your free written estimate.
02

Exterior Painting

Florida weather punishes exteriors. We pressure wash, scrape, prime and finish with coatings made for sun and rain.

  • Stucco, siding, brick and wood exteriors
  • Pressure washing and surface prep included
  • Mildew-resistant exterior coatings
Sample price: full home exteriors vary widely by size. Free on-site estimates across Orlando.
03

Cabinet Painting & Refinishing

A new kitchen without the remodel price. We spray or brush cabinets to a smooth, factory-like finish.

  • Kitchen and bathroom cabinets
  • Degrease, sand, prime and topcoat system
  • Durable finishes that stand up to daily use
Sample price: cabinet projects are quoted per door and drawer. Ask for a free count and quote.
04

Drywall Repair & Texture

Dents, holes and cracked seams fixed before paint goes on, so the finish looks seamless.

  • Patching holes, cracks and nail pops
  • Texture matching for a seamless blend
  • Water stain sealing and priming
Sample price: small repairs are often under a hundred dollars when bundled with painting.

Commercial Painting

Offices, rentals, storefronts and common areas. We work around your schedule to keep downtime low.

  • Evening and weekend scheduling available
  • Consistent color across multi-unit properties
Sample price: commercial work is quoted per project. Call to schedule a walkthrough.

Color Consulting

Staring at fifty swatches? We help you pick colors that work with Florida light and your furniture.

  • Whole-home color schemes that flow room to room
  • Exterior palettes that suit the neighborhood

Our story

A local crew,
an honest brush

Villalta Painting & More, LLC was formed in Orlando in 2023 with a straightforward idea: do the prep work other painters skip, and the finish speaks for itself.

We are a small, family-run painting company based near Laguna Beach Circle in southeast Orlando. Because we are local, we know what Florida homes go through: blazing sun, afternoon downpours, humidity that finds every weak spot in old paint. We choose coatings and prep routines that hold up here.

The "more" in our name is a promise too. Drywall dings, tired cabinets, a color you cannot decide on: if it stands between you and a room you love, we handle it.

  • Active Florida business registration since 2023
  • Based in Orlando, serving the greater metro area
  • Free written estimates, no high-pressure sales
