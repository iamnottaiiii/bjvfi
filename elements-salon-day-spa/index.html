Our story

A hidden gem in Depew

Tucked away on Forestream Drive, Elements has spent over a decade becoming the place locals recommend to friends.

Photo placeholder ❦

Walk in and you are greeted right away. Every employee has a smile. The waiting room is beautiful, and through the windows you see trees. That is how guests describe their first moments at Elements Salon and Day Spa, and it sets the tone for everything that follows.

The philosophy is simple: a customized experience. Hair treatments, relaxing facials, and massage therapy are shaped around you, not a menu board. It is the kind of personal care that turns a first visit into a standing appointment.

Directory listings credit the salon with 11 years in business, and more than a hundred reviewers have given it a 4.7 star average. Come in and feel the difference for yourself.
