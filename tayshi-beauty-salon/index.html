What we do

Hair services,
done with care

From everyday trims to full transformations, every service starts with a real conversation about your hair.

Precision haircuts

Short cuts, long layers, bobs, and trims. Angela works with all hair types, and clients especially praise her work with curly and thick hair.

Color services

Single-process color, root touch-ups, and highlights. Bring a photo of what you love and talk it through first.

Blowouts and styling

Smooth blowouts, event styling, and finishes that hold. A quick way to feel polished for anything on the calendar.

Threading

Clean, precise threading for brows and the face. Gentle technique, quick visits, easy on the budget.

Kids and family

Patient, friendly cuts for younger clients too. A neighborhood salon means the whole family is welcome.
