About

A local crew for local homes

Fairway Home Products Inc is a small general contractor on Crescent Road in Fort Erie, focused on the parts of a house that face the weather head on.

Our story

Big enough to do the job, small enough to care

Fairway Home Products started as a local outfit serving Fort Erie homeowners with one specialty: the exterior envelope. Windows, steel doors, soffit, fascia and eavestroughs.

We keep the crew small and the service personal. You will not get a sales rep reading from a script. You get the contractor who measures your windows and the crew that installs them.

Fort Erie homes face real weather, lake-effect snow, spring thaws, summer storms. We install products and details that are chosen for this climate, fitted properly and finished cleanly.

  • Based at 611 Crescent Rd, Fort Erie, serving Niagara
  • Free written quotes on every project
  • Residential focus: your home, done right
