Our Story

A Wakefield practice with range.

Dr. Dean J. Darr, DMD, leads Darr Dental LLC at 9 Richardson Avenue in Wakefield, Massachusetts. He practices as the sole member and dentist of the office, which means the person who greets you and the person who treats you are one and the same.

What sets the practice apart is its breadth. Public listings credit Darr Dental LLC with general, pediatric, prosthodontic, and endodontic care, a combination that lets families handle routine visits, children's dentistry, root canals, and complex restorations under one roof. The practice holds Massachusetts dental license 17451 and has served the Wakefield area for years.
