A parlor, not a production line

Walk into Got Ink Tattoos and you are greeted by name, not by a number. This is a woman-owned and operated shop where the artists take their time, talk through your idea, and make sure first-timers feel at ease. One reviewer walked in with only a vague idea and walked out thrilled. Another calls it the place her whole family has trusted for years.

The shop sits on High Street in historic Burlington, and the atmosphere matches the neighborhood: warm, personal, and unhurried. Whether it is your first tattoo or your fiftieth, you will find the level of artistry and experience here without rival, as the Main Street Burlington directory puts it.

Services

Everything is custom. Nothing leaves the shop until it is right.

i.

Custom Tattoo Design

Your idea, drawn from scratch and fitted to your body. From delicate fine line to bold full color, the artists design around you, not from a book.

ii.

First-Timer Sessions

Patient consultations and a calm, welcoming shop make this a favorite first-tattoo destination. Walk in with a vague idea and walk out with a plan.

iii.

Walk-In Tattoos

Spontaneous ink for spontaneous days. Call ahead at (609) 386-2440 to check artist availability.

iv.

Flash Events

The shop draws crowds for special flash days, including Friday the 13th events with a text notification system so you never lose your place in line.

v.

Consultations and Aftercare

Talk through placement, sizing, and budget before you commit, and leave with clear healing guidance for every piece.
