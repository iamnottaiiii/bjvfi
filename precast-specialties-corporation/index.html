About us

Concrete is our craft

Precast Specialties Corporation is a precast concrete contractor based in Holden, Massachusetts, focused on quality products, straight answers and dependable lead times.

01

A yard built for precast

Precast work is part manufacturing, part craft. Every piece we cast is formed, finished and cured with care, because once it is set in concrete, it has to be right the first time.

We keep common stock items on hand for contractors on tight schedules, and we take on custom casting for projects that need something that does not come off a shelf.

02

How we work

  • You send plans. Drawings, sketches or just a description.
  • We quote it. Clear pricing and realistic lead times.
  • We cast it. Formed, finished and cured to spec.
  • You pick up or we deliver. Ready when your crew is.
03

Why contractors call us

Because precast either fits or it does not, and we would rather tell you straight than sell you the wrong piece. Honest lead times, consistent quality and a phone that gets answered.
