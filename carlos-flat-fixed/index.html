About

The shop

A neighborhood garage, run the right way.

Carlos’ Flat Fixed is a tire shop in Midtown Manhattan at 503 W 45th St, a few blocks from the Theater District.

The focus is narrow on purpose: flats and tires. When a shop does one thing all day, it gets fast at it, and drivers with a hissing tire do not want to wait.

Call (212) 765-1280 from the curb and describe what you see. If the tire can be saved, it gets repaired. If it cannot, you will hear that too, with options.
