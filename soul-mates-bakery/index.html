Our story

The name says it all

A neighborhood bakery on Porter Street in Gary, Indiana.

Soul Mates bakery is a local bakery in Gary, Indiana, serving the neighborhood from 450 Porter St. The name captures the philosophy: baking that pairs people with the treats they love, one cake, pie, and cookie at a time.

Like many beloved neighborhood bakeries, the day starts early and the case fills with familiar favorites. Celebration cakes, pies, and sweet classics are the heart of the menu, with custom orders welcome for the moments that matter.

Every bite is made with soul.
