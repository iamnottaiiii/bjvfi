Services

Commercial aquatic construction, end to end

Service descriptions below follow the company’s own published scope: new construction and renovation of commercial aquatic facilities of every type. For a bid on your project, email bids@calcommpools.com or call (909) 394-1280.

Ground-up construction of commercial aquatic facilities: recreational swimming venues, competitive aquatic facilities, water play parks, resort pools, and fountains. Work is completed to project plans using generally accepted construction methods and practices, per the company’s bid documents.

  • Municipal and community aquatic centers
  • School and university competition pools
  • Resort and hospitality pools

Renovation of aging commercial pools: surge pit and pump pit work, plumbing penetration repairs, valve replacement, and tile target restoration, as shown in recent public project scopes.

  • Structural and plumbing repairs
  • Tile, plaster, and finish renewal
  • Equipment and valve upgrades

About

Who we are

California Commercial Pools, Inc. is a Glendora, California company that specializes in all types of commercial aquatic facilities. In the company’s own words, it has become “the leader in aquatic facility construction on the west coast,” completing 30 to 40 projects a year.

The company describes its approach as organized to adapt to client needs: design professionals, capable estimators, experienced project managers, and seasoned job superintendents, with relationships to recognized aquatic design consultants.
