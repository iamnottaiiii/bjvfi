About us

Full service roofing, based in Northborough

Pitch Perfect Roofers is a full service roofing installer located at 34 Mohawk Dr in Northborough, Massachusetts. We work across the surrounding Central Mass towns on homes and buildings of every shape and slope.

Our range is our strength: all major brands of asphalt shingles for everyday homes, TPO and EPDM roll roofing for flat and low slope sections, and specialty work in slate, standing seam metal, and composite shingles when the project calls for it.

We keep it simple. Show up on time, price the job fairly, install it cleanly, and leave the property the way we found it. That is the whole business plan.
