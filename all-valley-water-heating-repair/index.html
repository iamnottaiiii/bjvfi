Water heater repair

No hot water, lukewarm water, pilot lights that will not stay lit, rumbling sediment, or leaks at valves and fittings. Most repairs are diagnosed in one visit and fixed the same day.

  • Thermostat and heating element replacement
  • Gas valve, pilot, and thermocouple service
  • Pressure relief valve and drain valve replacement
  • Sediment flush and performance check

New water heater installation

When repair no longer makes sense, we install a right-sized tank with proper venting, earthquake strapping, drain pan, and permits. Most swaps finish the same day.

  • Gas and electric tank models, 30 to 75 gallon
  • Code upgrades: strapping, pan, expansion tank
  • Old unit hauled away and recycled
  • Permit paperwork handled for you
