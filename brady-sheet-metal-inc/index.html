Our Story

Brady Sheet Metal Inc is a family-owned and operated sheet metal contractor in Burbank, California. The company holds California CSLB license 288668 and has served contractors, property owners, and builders across Los Angeles with design, fabrication, and installation of sheet metal work.

Our range runs from general functional flashing and waterproofing to finished architectural metal. The through line is the same: measure twice, cut once, and stand behind the fit. Customers say it plainly, they come back because the work is excellent and the staff is friendly.

We are a working shop, not a showroom. Call during shop hours and you will talk to people who know sheet metal.
