Our story

A small shop with strong opinions about wood.

Aged Timber is a flooring contractor in Sylmar, California, built around one idea: new floors should not look like they came out of a box. The shop's public profile is modest, but the work speaks through the reviews. Clients describe a knowledgeable crew and a beautiful custom product their own clients loved.

The focus is custom finishing and texturing on wood flooring and all the wood parts of a room: moldings, stair treads, handrails, and wainscoting. If you want floors with depth, grain you can feel, and a finish that will age gracefully, you are in the right shop.
