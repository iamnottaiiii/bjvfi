About the salon

A village salon
with a personal touch

A Look for All Seasons is a hair salon on Fayette Street in Manlius, known for skilled stylists and a genuinely joyful chair.

Our way

Stylists you know
by name

In a village salon, the relationship matters as much as the result. Clients mention stylists like Linda and Wendy by name, praising careful layering and shaping, especially for thick hair, and the sheer pleasure of the visit itself.

That is the standard here: technical skill plus a warm welcome. Whether you need a seasonal refresh, a bold new color or just a trim that behaves, you will be listened to first and styled second.

And with doors open until 9 PM Monday through Saturday, great hair fits around your schedule, not the other way around.
