What we do

Every service, done right.

Dry cleaning is only the start. Here is the full menu of garment care at 864 Lexington Street.

Professional dry cleaning

Suits, coats, dresses, silks, and woolens are cleaned with the right solvents and finished by hand, never rushed through a machine line. Bring a stain; point it out at the counter and we will pre-treat it on the spot.

Special occasion and wedding gowns

Longtime customers trust us with wedding gowns and delicate special occasion dresses. These pieces get individual attention, gentle cleaning, and museum style pressing so the details stay perfect.

Alterations and tailoring

Hems, waist adjustments, tapers, and fit fixes handled in house. A good alteration makes an old favorite feel new again, and it costs far less than replacing the garment.

Wash and fold laundry

Drop off the everyday load and pick it up washed, dried, and folded. A simple way to get hours back every week, priced by the pound.

Leather cleaning

Jackets, handbags, and suede pieces are cleaned and conditioned to restore softness and color without drying the hide.

Same day service

In a rush for an event or a trip? Ask about same day turnaround at drop off. Availability depends on the day, so call ahead: (781) 894-6292.

Ready when you are

Drop by the plaza on Lexington Street by Lake Avenue, or call ahead and we will have your order tagged and ready.

Our story

A neighborhood cleaner with a loyal following.

Wal-Lex Cleaners Inc has served Waltham from its spot in the shopping plaza on Lexington Street by Lake Avenue for years. Regulars come back because the staff knows them by name, and because delicate pieces, from silk blouses to wedding gowns, come home looking their best.
