What we do

One crew for paint and drywall.

Most jobs need both. Instead of juggling two contractors, our team handles the drywall repair, the prep, and the finish coats in one schedule.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and cabinets. We protect floors and furniture, patch and sand before we paint, and cut clean lines so the finish looks factory made.

Exterior painting

Siding, stucco, brick, and trim across South Jersey homes and commercial buildings. Proper scraping, priming, and caulking so the coating survives real weather.

Drywall and repairs

Hang, tape, finish, and texture, plus patching for dings, cracks, and water damage. Walls leave our hands smooth and ready for paint.

Commercial projects

Offices, retail, and institutional spaces. A crew of about six painters means we can staff larger jobs and work around your business hours.

How it works

From first call to final walkthrough.

1 · Free estimate

Call (856) 256-1760 and tell us about the project. We visit, measure, and give you a written quote with no pressure.

2 · Prep and protect

Furniture moved and covered, floors masked, surfaces scraped, patched, sanded, and primed. Prep is where a good paint job is made.

3 · Paint and finish

Two full coats as standard, premium paints, clean cut lines, and daily cleanup so your space stays livable while we work.

4 · Walkthrough

We walk every room with you before we call it done. Anything you spot, we fix on the spot.

Our story

A working crew, not a sales team.

Diamond Trade Group, LLC started in Sewell, New Jersey, as a straightforward idea: show up on time, prep every surface like it matters, and leave each job cleaner than we found it. Based at 6 Enterprise Ct, our crew of about six painters and drywall finishers takes on residential and commercial work across Gloucester County and the surrounding South Jersey area.

We are set up as a limited liability company because we take the business side as seriously as the brushwork: written estimates, clear scheduling, and one point of contact from the first walkthrough to the final inspection. Alongside painting, our team handles drywall installation and repair, so a single project does not need two contractors and two schedules.

We would rather spend an extra hour on prep than an extra day fixing a shortcut.

How we work, every job

Our commercial background means we are comfortable with larger institutional and office projects, but most weeks we are in family homes: bedrooms, kitchens, exteriors, and cabinets. Whatever the size, the standard is the same, which is why most of our new work comes from repeat customers and referrals.
