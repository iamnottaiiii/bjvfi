About

A neighborhood nail spa on Nottingham Road

Holly Nails Spa has served Syracuse since about 2017, building a loyal following on steady service and a calm, carefully maintained space.

Steady and Consistent

Guests describe technicians who check in during the service and welcome special requests about length, color, and shape.
