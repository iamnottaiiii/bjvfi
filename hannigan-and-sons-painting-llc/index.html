Who we are

HANNIGAN AND SONS PAINTING LLC is a painting company based in Magnolia, New Jersey, at 309 Atlantic Ave SE, Magnolia, NJ 08049.

The name says it all: a family business where the work carries the family name. When your name is on the side of the truck, every job is personal, and every customer is a neighbor.

Public listings for this business are light, so the best way to learn about the work is the old-fashioned way: call (609) 922-1732 and talk to the painters themselves.

How we work

1. Walkthrough. We look at the job in person and give you a straight estimate.

2. Prep. Floors covered, surfaces fixed, everything masked. Prep is the job.

3. Paint. Even coats, clean lines, quality paint applied with care.

4. Walkoff. You inspect the finished work before we pack up.
