A Neighborhood Institution

Barnes Barber Shop is a family-run, old-school barbershop on Woodburn Ave in Cincinnati. Customers describe it as a second-generation shop with a warm, family atmosphere, where the barbers know your name and your usual cut.

Step inside and you will find buzzing clippers, good conversation, and walls of barbering memorabilia. Reviewers say it is a place with no cursing, just friendly talk, where women feel comfortable and regulars have been coming since they were kids, some for more than fifty years.

Barbers customers mention by name include Tommie Barnes, Tommy Jr., Dave, and Art. It is the kind of shop where people do not just get a cut, they join a family.
