The craft

Real masonry, by people who live it

Masonry is slow, heavy, honest work, and it shows. A well-built brick wall or a cleanly repointed chimney outlasts almost everything around it. That is the standard G & G Langenbrunner holds on every job, from a single brick column to a full siding replacement.

Public permit records show the crew's range: retaining walls torn down and rebuilt, brick columns restored after vehicle damage, outdoor terraces and walkways repaired, chimneys taken down and made right.

A family trade

Passed down, not bought out

Guy Langenbrunner built G & G Langenbrunner Masonry into a successful Cincinnati business and led it for decades. When he retired in 2014, he handed the work to his son Greg, with the next generation, daughter Katlin and sons Gerad and Grant, working alongside (per the Elder High School alumni page).

That matters on a job site. Family crews do not cut corners, because the name on the truck is their own.

Services

Every kind of brick, stone, and block work

From tuckpointing to full siding replacement. Typical work drawn from public permit and directory records.

01

Brick & Stone Tuckpointing

Repair

Crumbling mortar joints cut out and repointed, with new mortar matched to the old so repairs blend in. Reviewers single this out as a specialty.

02

Fireplace Repair & Builds

Repair · New

Brick and stone fireplace repair, plus full fireplace builds. One customer had Greg repair exposed brick walls, then hired the crew to build a brick fireplace as well.

03

Brick & Stone Siding

Install · Replace

Brick or stone siding installed new or completely replaced, giving a home a durable, classic face that lasts for decades.

04

Retaining Walls

Rebuild

Failing retaining walls torn down to the footer and rebuilt properly, with a concrete footer poured for a solid base.

05

Chimneys

Repair · Rebuild

Chimney tear-downs and rebuilds, plus repointing and restoration to keep the stack safe and solid.

06

Terraces & Walkways

Repair

Outdoor terraces and walkways repaired and restored, no interior work needed, just solid outdoor masonry.

07

Brick Columns & Wall Restoration

Restoration

Brick columns and wall sections rebuilt to match, including restoration after impact damage.

Good to know: this list reflects the company's public listings and permit history. Every project is different, so call (513) 662-2414 to talk through yours.
