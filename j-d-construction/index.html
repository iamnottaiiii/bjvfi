About

Your Local Canyon Country Contractor

J D Construction is a general contracting business based on Sierra Highway in Canyon Country, California, serving homeowners across the Santa Clarita Valley. It is the kind of shop built on word of mouth: find them once, and they become your guys for every project after.

Public reviews describe exactly the experience homeowners hope for. One reviewer found them through an online ad, met the crew on a hunch for a quote, and came away impressed by how easy it was to talk through design ideas and hear honest advice on what would work best. Always on time, easy to communicate with, fast responses, great quality and time efficiency. That review ended with a line that says everything: this will be my new guys from now on.

No project is too small to get the same care. From a single room refresh to a full remodel, you deal with people who answer, show up and finish.
