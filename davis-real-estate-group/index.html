Our story

Build where you belong.

A development company is only as good as the neighborhoods it leaves behind.

We started Davis Real Estate group with a simple belief: Northern Kentucky deserves developments planned by people who live here. National builders see lots on a spreadsheet. We see the school bus route, the Friday night lights and the neighbor who will live next door for thirty years.

That local view shapes every decision, from how wide the streets are to where the sun hits the front porch. It is also why our office door is open six days a week at 755 Jimae Ave. Come talk about your plans in person.
