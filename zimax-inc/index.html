About Zimax Inc.

What public records and customer reviews say about this shop.

Zimax Inc. is a small, family owned and operated handyman company based at 1193 OH-131 in Day Heights, Ohio. Public directory records list 1994 as the year the business was founded, and its own profile describes a team with decades of collective experience focused on craftsmanship over volume.

Customer reviews on public directories repeatedly mention one specialty: older homes. Reviewers describe complex jobs on old houses handled with experience and care, and several say they have used the company for years across many projects.

The company's own public profile states its goal is not to be the biggest in the industry but the best, treating a home as more than an investment, and that it charges for estimates. If you are only shopping for the cheapest price, its profile says it may not be a good match.

  • Family owned and operated, per its own public profile
  • Reviewers note a strong knowledge base on older homes
  • Repeat customers across multiple years, per public reviews
  • Warranties offered, per public directory records (call to confirm)
