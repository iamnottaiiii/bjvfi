What we do

Services

Everyday car care, done with neighborly honesty.

If something's off with your car, bring it by. We'll look it over and talk you through your options before any work begins.

Diagnostics

Check engine lights, weird sounds, mystery smells. We track down the cause and explain it clearly.

Oil Changes

Fast, tidy oil changes plus a friendly once-over of your fluids, belts, and filters.

Brake Service

Pads, rotors, and brake inspections to keep every stop smooth and safe.

Tires and Rotations

Tire rotations, balancing, and replacements. We check tread and pressure too.

Batteries and Electrical

Dead battery? Dim lights? We test charging systems and get you powered back up.

Heating and Cooling

Coolant leaks, radiators, and A/C troubles. Stay comfortable in every season.

General Repairs

Suspension, steering, belts, hoses, and more. If it keeps your car rolling, we can help.

Pre-Purchase Inspections

Thinking of buying a used car? Bring it by first for a straight answer on its condition.
