About

The company behind Country Club Acres

Rooted in Worcester

Worcester Country Club Acres, LLC was formed in 2004 to develop the Country Club Acres condominium community in Worcester, Massachusetts, an age-restricted community of townhome-style residences.

That development background shapes how the company builds: planned communities and homes alike come down to solid framing, honest materials, and details that age well.
