Our Story

A Sun Valley contractor built on showing up and doing the work right.

Rooted in the Valley

Huerta's Construction Door operates as Huerta's Construction and Improvements, Inc., a California corporation with its principal office at 8801 Haddon Ave in Sun Valley. This is not a crew that drives in from two counties away. The shop is here, the trucks are here, and the work is here, across the San Fernando Valley.


The name says it plainly: construction, with doors as a calling card. A door is the first thing you touch coming home and the last thing you see leaving. Getting it hung plumb, sealed tight and finished clean is the standard applied to every project, big or small.

How we work

1. Walkthrough. We come out, look at the project and listen to what you want.

2. Written estimate. Scope, materials and price in writing before anything starts.

3. The work. Clean site, steady progress, punch list finished before we call it done.

One point of contact

Estimates, scheduling and the crew all run through one number. No phone tag between a salesperson, a scheduler and a foreman. Call (818) 606-6475 and you talk to the people doing the work.

The standard

Show up when we say. Protect the floors. Clean up daily. Finish the punch list. It is not complicated, and it is why neighbors call back for the next project.
