What the practice offers

Chiropractic spinal adjustments

Hands-on manipulative treatment to restore joint motion and relieve pain across spinal regions, tailored to each area of injury.

Corrective exercises

Targeted exercises that reinforce adjustments, build stability, and support long-term spinal health.

Soft tissue manipulation

Therapeutic work on muscles and connective tissue to ease spasms, tension, and soreness.

Physiotherapy approaches

Nutritional counseling

Guidance on nutrition and supplements as part of a whole-body approach to feeling better.

Lifestyle counseling

Practical advice on daily habits, posture, and movement that protect the progress you make in the office.

About

Meet Dr. Theodora C. Caraway

A Doctor of Chiropractic in independent practice in Sewell, New Jersey, caring for patients since 2003.

PORTRAIT PLACEHOLDER · ORIGINAL ARTWORK

Thorough, unhurried, and on your side

Dr. Caraway graduated from Logan College of Chiropractic in 2003 and has practiced as an independent chiropractor for more than 23 years. She is a sole proprietor, licensed in New Jersey, and accepts Medicare assignment.

Her patients describe a doctor who listens to every word, reviews imaging before laying out a plan, and treats each injured area individually rather than rushing through a routine.

New patients are currently being accepted at her Sewell office on Hurffville - Cross Keys Road.
