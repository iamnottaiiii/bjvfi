Our story

Richmond Highway's trusted cleaner

On Richmond Highway in Alexandria, Lee's Cleaners has built its name the old fashioned way: one garment at a time. It is the kind of shop where the staff knows what careful work looks like and treats every drop off accordingly.

Customers describe a business that gets the details right. A silk blouse with a tough stain comes back pristine. Pants turned into shorts get a hem so clean it looks original. An urgent request gets met, not shrugged off. That consistency is why reviewers call it a favorite for both cleaning and alterations and rate it 4.6 out of 5.

Polite, accommodating, reliable: those are the words customers use for the people behind the counter. With reasonable pricing and early weekday hours, it is built around the routines of the neighbors it serves.
