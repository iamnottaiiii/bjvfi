Our story

Art of Hair has been part of the Syracuse salon scene since 2007, and today the team welcomes clients at 527 Charles Ave in Geddes Plaza. Owner Ashley leads a crew of stylists, including Scott, Erin, Christine, Karen, and Barb, who treat regulars like family.

The move to the new first floor location brought easier parking and more visibility, but the formula never changed: listen first, cut second, and make sure every client leaves feeling pampered. Business directories trace the salon back to 2007, and longtime clients say they have been coming for as long as the doors have been open.
