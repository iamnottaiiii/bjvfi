Our story

On Metropolitan Ave

A family-run Gulf service station in Queens, pairing a fuel stop with a licensed repair shop for half a century.

Since 1975

Two names, one corner,
fifty years

Graffeo & Ingra Services Station opened its doors in 1975, and it has been part of the Metropolitan Avenue streetscape ever since. Public business records list it as a small, family-run Gulf station, with ownership in the Graffeo family name.

Over the decades the business grew from a fuel stop into a full service station: New York State licensed as a repair shop and as a public inspection station, so drivers could handle fuel, maintenance and their yearly inspection in a single visit.

It is the kind of shop the neighborhood knows by sight. Generations of Queens drivers have pulled in for gas, for an oil change, or for that inspection sticker, and found the same straightforward service waiting.
