Our Story

A Northridge Tile Shop Built on Craft

Placeholder
Tile is permanent. Do it right.

Franks Tile Services Inc is a tile contracting company based at 17382 Napa St in Northridge, CA. Tile is a trade where the details show forever, so this shop was built around one idea: measure twice, cut once, and never rush the grout.

From kitchen backsplashes to full shower builds, the team handles both residential and commercial work across Northridge and the greater San Fernando Valley. Every job starts with a clear plan, honest pricing, and a straight answer about timeline.

Directory listings describe Franks Tile Services Inc as a tile installation company known for quality craftsmanship, attention to detail, and reliable, efficient installations for homes and businesses.
