The short story

A working shop, not a waiting room

Antoine's Auto Services sits at 53 Sherman Ave in Newark's South Ward, an independent shop where the work speaks for itself. Public listings describe it as a full-service auto station with computerized diagnostic abilities, handling everything from brake repair and tune-ups to transmissions, electrical work, shocks, and wheel balancing.

The same listings note road service and a used-car dealership on the premises, so the shop serves drivers whether they need a repair, a diagnosis, or their next used car.

What little public feedback exists keeps landing on the same point: this guy knows his stuff, at a great price.
