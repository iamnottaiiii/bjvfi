The clinic method

How a visit works

A simple, transparent process from check-in to follow-up, modeled on a medical visit.

1

Check-in

Tell us the symptoms: noises, warning lights, or anything that feels off. We listen and take notes.

2

Diagnosis

Your vehicle gets a careful inspection to find the real cause, not just the obvious symptom.

3

Treatment plan

We explain what is wrong and what the repair involves before any work begins.

4

Follow-up

After the repair, we confirm the issue is resolved and note anything to watch for next time.
