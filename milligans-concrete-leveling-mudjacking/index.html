How mudjacking works

Four steps to level ground.

1

Assessment

We look at the settled slab and find what caused it to sink.

2

Small holes

Small ports are drilled through the concrete, far smaller than a tear-out.

3

Lifting

Material is pumped underneath, raising the slab back to grade.

4

Sealed and done

Holes are sealed and the surface is ready to use. Call to confirm timing for your job.
