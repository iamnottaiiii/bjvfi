Our story

A village studio
with a personal touch

LJ Co Salon is a boutique hair salon in Suite 214A at 1 Grove St, in the heart of Pittsford village.

The studio

Some salons are built for volume. LJ Co Salon is built for you. Set in a boutique suite on Grove Street, it keeps the experience personal by design: limited availability, one-on-one attention, and a stylist who listens before picking up the shears.

The posts from the studio tell the story best: seasonal blowout specials, brightening color refreshes, complimentary consultations, and notes of gratitude to guests around the holidays. It is a small business with a warm heart, right in the middle of the village.

The approach

Consult first, always. Complimentary consultations mean you never commit to a color or cut blind. You talk it through, see the plan, and book only when it feels right.

Honest and unhurried. With a boutique schedule, there is no rushing you out of the chair. Your appointment time is yours.

Village convenient. At 1 Grove St, you can pair your appointment with the shops and cafes of Pittsford village. Hair day becomes a whole lovely outing.
