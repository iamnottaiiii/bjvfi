Lab 01 / Assessment

Every slab tells a story

Cracks, spalling, settlement, and worn coatings each point to a different fix. Work starts with a look at the surface, the base, and the drainage around it, so the repair matches the real cause.

Lab 02 / Restoration

Repair before replace, when it counts

Sound concrete and masonry can often be restored instead of torn out. Patching, resurfacing, tuckpointing, and coating extend the life of driveways, walks, patios, and brickwork.

Lab 03 / Replacement

New pours done to spec

When concrete is past saving, rip out and replace puts a fresh slab in its place. New installs for driveways, sidewalks, and patios are also part of the work list.

Lab 04 / Coatings

Epoxy over a prepped surface

Concrete epoxy coatings go on properly prepared floors for a hard, chemical resistant, easy to clean finish. Prep is the lab step that decides how long the coating lasts.

Test Protocol: How a Job Runs

Protocol P-1
01

Survey and measure

Walk the site, measure the area, and identify what is failing and why. You get a plain explanation of the options.

02

Prep and repair

Cleaning, grinding, crack repair, or tear out comes first. Coatings and pours only go over a sound, prepared surface.

03

Restore or coat

Patching, resurfacing, masonry repair, fresh concrete, or the epoxy coating system goes down per the agreed scope.

04

Cure and inspect

Cure times are respected, then the finished work is walked through with you before the job is called done.
