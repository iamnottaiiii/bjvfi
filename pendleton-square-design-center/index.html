The Pendleton setting

Pendleton is one of Cincinnati's 52 neighborhoods, set in the city's urban basin near Over-the-Rhine. The studio sits near St. Paul Church and the Hard Rock Casino Cincinnati, in a district known for historic architecture.

Our Story

Built for older buildings

Historic neighborhoods reward contractors who can read an old building: plaster walls, uneven floors, and details worth keeping. A design center, not just a job site office, reflects that sensibility.

Whether the project is a full remodel or a single room refresh, the goal is the same: work that respects the building and fits the way you live.

Our Story

A design center on Pendleton Street

Pendleton Square Design Center brings together three disciplines that usually live in separate companies: general contracting, architectural design, and home decor. The idea is simple. When the people who draw the plans are in the same studio as the people who build them, fewer things get lost between the sketch and the site.

The studio operates from 1118 Pendleton Street in Cincinnati's Pendleton neighborhood, part of the city's historic urban basin near Over-the-Rhine. The area's 19th-century building stock is exactly the kind of architecture a design-minded contractor is built to serve.

The business keeps weekday hours, Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and can be reached at (513) 621-7619.
