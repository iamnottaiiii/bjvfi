Our Story

Personal cleaning, built on relationships.

Mo's Marvelous Cleaning Company is a personal and professional cleaning service based in Union City, Georgia, serving the Atlanta surrounding areas. The focus is simple: housekeeping, carpet cleaning, and move-in and move-out cleaning delivered with a personal touch.

The goal is to build personal relationships and keep long term clients by showing up on time, communicating clearly, and treating every home with respect. Care.com lists the business as in business since 2017, and the Better Business Bureau shows a record opened in 2021 for this Union City LLC.

Services cover residential homes and small offices: bathrooms, kitchens, general room cleaning, laundry, floor cleaning, oven and refrigerator detail, and more. New clients get 10% off their first cleaning.
