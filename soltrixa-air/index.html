The case for clean vents

Lint is quiet. The risks are not.

Most homeowners never see inside their dryer vent. Here is what builds up in there, and why it matters.

01

Fire safety comes first

Lint is extremely flammable. When it coats the inside of a dryer vent, heat from the dryer has nowhere safe to go. A professional cleaning removes that fuel before it becomes a problem.

02

Shorter drying times

A choked vent traps moist air inside the drum, so clothes take two or three cycles to dry. Clear the vent and the dryer breathes again, often cutting drying time noticeably.

03

Lower energy bills

A dryer fighting a blocked vent runs longer and hotter on every load. Restoring full airflow means the appliance does the same work with less energy.

04

A longer-lasting dryer

Heat and strain wear out heating elements, thermostats, and motors early. Keeping the vent clear is one of the simplest ways to protect the machine you already own.

If your dryer feels hotter than it used to, or a load needs a second cycle, the vent is the first place to look.Soltrixa Air vent cleaning Service · Staten Island, NY
