About the Shop

Brothers Truck Repair is a truck repair shop on Tramway Drive in Cincinnati, Ohio, serving truck drivers, owner operators, and small carriers.

What the shop does

The shop describes itself in public listings this way:

"General repair, computer diagnostic, water pump, alternator, oil change, tires, shocks and brakes."

Public categories also list the shop as a tire dealer and an oil, lube, and filter service.
