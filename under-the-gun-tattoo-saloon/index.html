From the shop ledger

A saloon, not a clinic

Punk, metal and rock on the speakers. A crew that are close friends, not just coworkers. Twenty plus years of the same promise: clean, safe, friendly, and excellent work.

How a visit goes

Walk in or call ahead. No appointment is needed for a consultation, and consultations are always free. Come look at portfolios, talk through your idea, and find the right artist for it.

Book your session. If you need to cancel, call 48 hours ahead or you forfeit your deposit. That is the one hard rule, and it keeps the books fair for everyone.

Mind the house rules. 18 to get tattooed, no exceptions without a parent. And do not bother coming in intoxicated, you will be shown the front door.

Chris Gamm, proprietor · since 2002
