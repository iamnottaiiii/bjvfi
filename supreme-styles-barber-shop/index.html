SUPREME IS NOT A NAME. IT IS A STANDARD.

Supreme Styles Barber Shop sits at 1569 Chase Ave in Cincinnati, Ohio, and it runs like a neighborhood institution. Public reviews describe skilled, experienced barbers, a welcoming atmosphere, and cuts so consistent that clients keep coming back, some for decades.

Reviewers name barbers including G, Mark, Martin, and LaRon Muhammad, praised for taking their time, nailing the details, and treating every head in the chair like it matters. That is the whole manifesto: no rushing, no guesswork, just sharp work and real conversation.

The shop holds a 4.7 star rating across 90+ public reviews, with regulars calling out the laid back energy, the clean and organized space, and an atmosphere where you can be yourself.
