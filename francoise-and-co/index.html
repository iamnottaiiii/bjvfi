The studio

Philosophy

Simple, minimalistic, sanctuary

The studio's philosophy is that a well-designed room should lower your shoulders the moment you walk in. Francoise Levin and Kami Engel edit ruthlessly, layer natural textures, and let light do the talking, so every space feels calm, personal, and unmistakably yours.
