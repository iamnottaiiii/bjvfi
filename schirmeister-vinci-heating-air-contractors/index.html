Our Story

Let Our Family Help Your Family

Family Owned and Operated

John Schirmeister IV and Joe Vinci have been best friends since grade school. When John Schirmeister III stepped down in 1994, John and Joe formed a partnership and have been working together ever since, providing quality HVAC service and products to an ever growing family of satisfied customers.

Joe Vinci runs the installation department, customizing heating and air conditioning systems, air duct systems, air purifying systems, and ventilation, while also overseeing the crews to ensure the quality equipment and craftsmanship that have become synonymous with Schirmeister-Vinci. John Schirmeister manages the service department, overseeing daily air conditioning and heating service and repairs.

Quality service and products are synonymous with the name Schirmeister-Vinci. We not only stand behind our work but our customers as well. All of our residential high efficiency custom installations come with our own 3 year warranty, as long as the system is cleaned and serviced twice a year by Schirmeister-Vinci.

We proudly serve all of Los Angeles County, including the Antelope Valley, East and West Valleys, San Fernando, Santa Clarita, and Valencia. We are fully licensed and insured, CA License No. 726884.
