Our story

A Main Street fixture, run by Kathy and Peter

E-Z Way Cleaners has been part of Woburn life at 227 Main Street for a very long time, and for good reason. Owners Kathy and Peter work the counter themselves, greeting regulars by name and treating every garment like it matters, because to them it does.

Many families have been loyal customers for more than 25 years. They started coming for the dry cleaning, they stayed for the honesty: fair prices, work done right, and alterations finished when promised. Customers describe the shop as clean, organized, and run with genuine care.

Word of mouth built this business. One reviewer put it simply: they know all of their customers well, because customers keep coming back, just like they have for over 20 years.

Since

Long before fast fashion

E-Z Way has cleaned clothes for generations of Woburn families, from work suits to wedding gowns to the comforter on the guest bed.
