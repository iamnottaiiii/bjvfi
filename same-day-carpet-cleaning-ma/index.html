Deep Carpet Cleaning

Our signature hot water extraction clean reaches deep into carpet fibers to lift embedded dirt, dust, pet dander, and allergens, leaving carpets fresh and revitalized.

  • Hot steam extraction for a true deep clean
  • Pre-treatment of high-traffic lanes
  • Pile lifting so flattened carpet looks new again
  • Furniture moved with care on request

Stain Removal

Food spots, drink spills, paint spots, and mystery stains get targeted treatment. Our technicians work the tough ones by hand until they lift.

  • Targeted treatment for set-in stains
  • Paint, food, and beverage spot removal
  • High-traffic area blending
  • Honest assessment before we start

Pet Stain and Odor Treatment

Pet accidents need more than surface cleaning. We use enzyme cleaners that break down organic stains and neutralize odors at the source.

  • Enzyme treatment for urine and organic stains
  • Pet-safe, biodegradable cleaning solutions
  • Odor neutralization, not just masking
  • Follow-up drying help if areas stay damp

Fabric and Stain Protector

Add a protective barrier after cleaning to help your carpet resist future spills and spots, especially valuable in homes with kids and pets.

  • Applied after deep cleaning
  • Helps spills bead up instead of soaking in
  • Extends the life of your clean
  • Great for homes with pets and children
