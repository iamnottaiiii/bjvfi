Our Story

38 Years on the Same Brooklyn Block

William Wenzel Roofing started on April 2, 1988, and never left. Owner William S. Wenzel Jr. runs a family shop out of 1661 86th Street, the kind of place where the person who quotes your roof is the person who climbs it.

We have roofed row houses, brownstones, and commercial buildings through every kind of New York weather. That history is why our BBB file, opened in 2009, carries an A+ rating, and why 28 Google reviewers give us 4.8 out of 5.

Big enough to handle a full replacement, small enough to remember your name. That is the whole business plan.
