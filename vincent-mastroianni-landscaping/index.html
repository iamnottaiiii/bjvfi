Landscaping is maintenance, drainage, and design in one.

Most yard problems are connected. A lawn that will not grow is often a drainage problem. A planting bed that floods is often a grading problem. We look at the whole property, not just the symptom, and fix what is actually wrong.

That means lawn maintenance on a real schedule, french drains and dry wells where water collects, and plantings chosen for shade, sun, and soil instead of guesswork. One company, one phone number, and a yard that holds up through spring storms and summer heat.

Start with a call. Tell us what is bothering you about your yard and we will come take a look. Estimates are straightforward and the work is done by people who do this every day.

About

A local crew for local yards.

Vincent Mastroianni Landscaping is based in Auburndale, in the heart of Newton, Massachusetts. We are a working landscaping company, not a call center: the people you talk to are the people who show up with the mowers, the shovels, and the drainage pipe.

Our work covers the full life of a yard. Weekly lawn maintenance keeps things looking sharp. Drainage work, french drains and dry wells, solves the water problems that ruin lawns and threaten foundations. Plantings, organic feeding programs, and water features finish the picture.

The short version: if it is outside your house and it is not working, call us. We will come look, tell you straight what it needs, and do the work right.
