Preventive care

Regular preventive visits are the cheapest, easiest way to keep your teeth for life. The practice welcomes routine patients of all ages.

  • Dental exams and checkups
  • Professional cleanings
  • Digital X-rays
  • Gum health evaluations
  • Home care guidance
Call to book a checkup

Restorative care

When a tooth is damaged, decayed, or missing, restorative treatment rebuilds strength and function so you can eat and smile comfortably.

  • Tooth colored fillings
  • Dental crowns
  • Bridges
  • Full and partial dentures
  • Extractions
Call about restorative care

Cosmetic care

Small improvements can make a big difference in how you feel about your smile. Ask what is realistic for your teeth during a visit.

  • Professional whitening
  • Tooth colored bonding
  • Smile evaluations
Ask about your smile

Tooth pain and urgent care

Toothaches, broken teeth, and lost fillings should not wait. Call the office, describe what happened, and you will be told the fastest path to relief.

  • Toothache evaluation
  • Broken or chipped teeth
  • Lost fillings or crowns
  • Root canal treatment when needed
