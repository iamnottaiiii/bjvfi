Our story

A Campbell County dirt company, registered in Kentucky since 2003 and still run by its founder.

Rooted in California, KY

Allender Enterprises LLC was registered with the Kentucky Secretary of State on July 24, 2003, and has operated out of 7774 Truesdell Road in California, Campbell County ever since. Owner and president Robert W. Allender built the company as a heavy construction contractor: the kind that moves earth, sets septic tanks, and clears the lots the rest of the trades build on.

BBB records carry an A+ rating for the company, listed under Excavating Contractors, Septic Tank Contractors, and Land Clearing, with a service area covering Boone, Bracken, Campbell, and Kenton counties.
