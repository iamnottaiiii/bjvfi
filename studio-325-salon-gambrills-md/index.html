Appointment based

Each stylist schedules individually, so every visit is focused and unhurried. Walk ins are welcome to stop in and book a future appointment.

A salon built on craft

Studio 325 opened in May 2016 with a simple idea: give talented stylists a beautiful, welcoming room and let them be their own boss. The result is a salon with real energy, where years of experience show in every finished look.

Guests call it a wonderful salon where every stylist operates as their own business, and you schedule by contacting them individually.

Why the model works for you

You choose your stylist, not a random chair. Build a relationship with one artist who learns your hair over time, or try different specialists for cut, color and texture. Either way, you get focused one on one time in a calm, upscale setting.
