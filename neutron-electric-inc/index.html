Our story

The local electrician, done right

Neutron Electric Inc is an electrical contractor based in West Hills, California, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley. We are licensed by the state of California, and bonded and insured, because electrical work is not a place to cut corners.

Our focus is residential electrical: the services, upgrades and repairs that keep a home safe and comfortable. As homes have changed, we have changed with them, adding EV charging station installation and backup generator installation to the classic work of breakers, lighting and inspections.

Licensed, bonded and insured. Friendly, courteous and very experienced.

Ask our clients and a theme comes back again and again: technicians who are friendly, courteous and very experienced. That is the whole business plan. Show up, do it right, treat people well.
