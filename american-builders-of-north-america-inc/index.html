Our story

An American builder, rooted in San Fernando

American Builders of North America, Inc. is a California construction corporation with its roots in the San Fernando Valley.

American Builders of North America, Inc. filed as a California corporation in May 2020, with construction as its stated line of business. The company is led by Andrew A Aguilar, who serves as chief executive officer, chief financial officer, and secretary.

From its San Fernando base, the company takes on residential and commercial construction across the greater Los Angeles area: new builds, additions, remodels, and the kind of finish work that makes a project feel complete.

If you can dream it, we can build it, and we will stand behind it when we do.

The American Builders approach

Every project runs on the same principles: straight talk about scope and schedule, careful crews who respect your property, and work done right the first time. That is how a builder earns a name worth putting on the door.
