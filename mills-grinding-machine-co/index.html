About Mills Grinding & Machine Co

A machine shop in Delhi Township

Mills Grinding & Machine Co operates at 1000 Devils Backbone Rd in Delhi Township, on the west side of Cincinnati. It is listed publicly as a machine shop with automotive repair work, keeping weekday hours of 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

This is the kind of shop engine builders and home mechanics rely on: the place with the grinders, the hones, and the measuring tools that turn worn iron back into usable parts. Bring your heads, your block, or your crank, and talk through the job in person or by phone.
