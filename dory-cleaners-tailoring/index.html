Our story

42 years of
Swampscott trust

A family-run cleaner that grew up with the town, one pressed shirt at a time.

Dory Cleaners & Tailoring has served Swampscott and Marblehead for 42 years, long enough to become the kind of place customers recommend by name to newcomers. In November 2025 the shop moved from its long-time Humphrey Street home to 124 Burrill Street, three minutes away, with full hours restored.

The owners take genuine pride in the work. Reviewers describe them as proactive: if a garment might not survive cleaning, they call first and explain the risk. That honesty is why one customer calls them their go-to cleaner of more than a decade.

The shop also earned the Reader's Choice Award for dry cleaners, and uses a non-toxic, eco-friendly cleaning system that is easier on clothes, skin, and the planet.
