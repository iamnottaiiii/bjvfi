How we work

Design first, then build it right

Every project starts with a walk of the property and an honest conversation about budget, drainage, sun, and how you actually use the space. No cookie-cutter beds, no surprises mid-project.

Our crews do the hands-on work themselves: prepping and grading soil, laying sod, building retaining walls and fences, and shaping planting beds that hold up through Pennsylvania seasons.

The company

Local people, real dirt
under the nails

Classic Landscape serves homeowners in New Cumberland, PA and the surrounding area. The team is known for long, hardworking days on site, the kind of crew that treats your yard like a job site worth doing right.

Former crew members describe the day-to-day work in public reviews: laying sod, building fences and retaining walls, prepping dirt for planting, and learning to run the equipment it takes to move a project along.

That hands-on background shapes how we design. Plans are drawn with an installer's eye: grades that drain, walls that hold, beds you can actually maintain.

Straightforward by habit

We would rather talk you out of work you do not need than sell you work you will regret. If a simpler fix solves the problem, we will say so.
