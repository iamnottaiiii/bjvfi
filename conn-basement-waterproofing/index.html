The problem

Cincinnati basements take a beating

Clay soil, heavy spring rains, and humid summers push water toward your foundation. Cracks, seepage, and musty air are not just annoying. Left alone, they get worse and more expensive.

A dry basement protects your foundation, your air quality, and everything you store downstairs.

That is where we come in. Conn Basement Waterproofing focuses on one thing: keeping water out of your basement, so the rest of your house stays sound.

Why it matters

Small leaks become big bills

Water finds the path of least resistance. A hairline foundation crack today can mean a flooded basement after the next big storm. Waterproofing now is almost always cheaper than repairing water damage later.

Common signs you should call us: water stains on basement walls, efflorescence (that white powdery residue), puddles after rain, a musty smell that will not go away, or a sump pump that runs constantly.
