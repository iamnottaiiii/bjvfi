Our story

A real hardware store,
on Smith Place

No maze of aisles, no self-checkout. Just the parts, the tools, and people who know where they are.

Why Rockaway Hardware

The counter that knows your project

Rockaway Hardware serves Far Rockaway from 1-25 Smith Pl: a full-line hardware store and licensed locksmith counter in one stop. Contractors swing through for lumber-yard stock and electrical and plumbing supplies; neighbors come in for a key cut, a deadbolt, or the one weird fastener the big box stopped carrying.

For the trade

Lumber, electrical and plumbing stock in one stop, with a counter that speaks contractor. Get in, get out, get back to the job.

For the home

Keys cut while you wait, locks rekeyed, paint matched, and plain-English advice when the project is bigger than the toolbox.

For the weekend fix

Open six days a week. When the faucet leaks on Saturday morning, you do not have to wait for Monday.
