About

Four decades, one family, zero shortcuts.

Sparkle Clean Maid and Lawn Service, LLC is a family owned business serving Bethel, Felicity, and the surrounding Clermont County area. The facts below come from its BBB business profile and Angi listing.

1985

The business starts in the Bethel area, offering house cleaning to local families. (Per BBB: business started 8/12/1985.)

2004

Incorporated as Sparkle Clean Maid and Lawn Service, LLC. (Per BBB: 8/25/2004.)

2008

Becomes BBB accredited. The accreditation has been held continuously since 6/18/2008, per the BBB profile.

Today
