Our story

Four decades on Hanover Street

Waterfront Laundry has been part of the North End for 42 years, a rare constant on one of Boston's busiest streets. Generations of neighbors, students, and visitors have walked through the door at 418 Hanover St with a bag of laundry and walked out lighter.

The formula never changed: next day wash and fold, honest dry cleaning, and self service machines that work, with prices and times stated plainly before a single item goes in.

Some customers have been coming for several years and find it simply convenient. Others discovered it on a family holiday and call the staff member who helped them a lifesaver. Either way, the laundry comes back quickly, promptly, and beautifully.
