What we do

One shop for the gate and everything behind it

Most gate companies either build the gate or wire the opener. Anderson's Electric Gates does both, so your rolling, swinging or overhead gate is designed, fabricated, automated and wired by one local crew.

Gate Fabrication

Custom rolling, swinging and overhead gates in wrought iron and wood, built for your driveway and your style.

Gate Automation

Openers, remotes, keypads and sensors installed and tuned so your gate moves smoothly every time.

Entry Control

Telephone entry, keypads, card readers and intercoms that decide who gets in and keep a record of it.

Electrical Wiring

Full electrical wiring for gate systems and more, handled by the same team that builds your gate.

Rolling Gates

Swinging Gates

Overhead Gates

Wood Gates

Entry Control Systems

Electrical Wiring
