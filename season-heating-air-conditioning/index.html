Our story

Season Heating & Air Conditioning is a Pasadena-based heating and air conditioning contractor serving homes and businesses across the San Gabriel Valley from 630 N Rosemead Blvd.

Local, and proud of it

We work where we live. Pasadena summers are long and the winter nights can surprise you, so we built Season around one idea: a local team that shows up, explains things clearly, and fixes it right the first time.

How we work

Every visit starts with listening. We diagnose before we quote, show you what we found, and give you options at different price points. No pressure, no mystery fees, just straight answers about your system.

Honest diagnosis

We find the real problem and tell you about it in plain English before any work starts.

Clean job sites

Shoe covers, drop cloths, and a full cleanup. Your home looks the same as when we arrived, only more comfortable.

One team, both seasons

Heating and cooling under one roof, so the same crew that tunes your AC in spring services your furnace in fall.

Serving Pasadena and the San Gabriel Valley

Pasadena, San Marino, Arcadia, Alhambra, South Pasadena, San Gabriel, Temple City, and nearby neighborhoods.
