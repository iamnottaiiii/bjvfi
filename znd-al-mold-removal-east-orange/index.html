Why it matters

Mold does not stay in one corner

Left alone, mold spreads through drywall, basements, and attics, and can affect the air your family breathes. A proper remediation removes the growth and addresses the source of moisture behind it.

  • Musty odors that will not fadeOften the first sign of hidden growth behind walls or under floors.
  • Visible spots on walls or ceilingsDiscoloration in bathrooms, basements, or around windows.
  • After leaks or floodingWater damage that was never fully dried out.

How a remediation visit works

1. We inspect the affected areas and find how far the growth reaches.
2. We contain the area so spores do not spread during removal.
3. We remove the mold and clean the surfaces.
4. We address the moisture source and repair damaged areas.
