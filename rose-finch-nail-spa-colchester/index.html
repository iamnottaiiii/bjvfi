Our story

A neighborhood salon built on care.

Rose finch nail spa sits on S Main St in Colchester, and it has earned its reputation the old-fashioned way: one careful appointment at a time.

The kind of place clients call their favorite.

Walk in and the first thing you notice is the calm: a clean, relaxing space where techs take their time. Reviewers call out the friendly, patient staff and the attention to detail, from a perfectly shaped nail to a design that matches the picture you brought in.

Hygiene is part of the promise here. One-time-use disposable pedicure liners and individual pedicure sets mean every client gets a fresh, safe start.

Flexible scheduling and walk-ins keep things easy, and reasonable pricing means great nails do not have to be a splurge.
