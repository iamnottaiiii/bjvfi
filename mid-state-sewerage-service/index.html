Why call us

The tank you forget about until it reminds you

A septic system does its job quietly, which is why it is easy to ignore until the day it does not. Slow drains, gurgling pipes, soggy spots in the yard, or a smell that was not there last week are all the system asking for attention.

We have been answering that call in Millbury and the surrounding towns since 1948. Pumping on schedule, honest Title V inspections, and installations done right the first time. That is the whole business model, and three generations later it still works.

Services

Everything below ground is our department

From routine pumping to full system installation, this is the complete list of what the Mid State Sewerage Service crew handles. If it involves a tank, a line, or a hole in the ground, start here.

01

Septic pumping and maintenance

The bread and butter, on schedule.

Routine septic tank pumping and cleaning for homes and businesses. Regular pumping is the cheapest insurance a septic system has, and we will put you on a schedule so you never have to think about it.

02

Title V inspections

Selling or buying? Start here.

Massachusetts Title V septic inspections and certifications for real estate transactions. We inspect, document, and certify, and we will explain the findings in plain language.

03

New septic system installation

Designed, dug, and done right.

Full installation of new septic systems, including excavation with our own backhoes and equipment. One crew from the first dig to the final grade.

04

Grease traps

For restaurants and food businesses.

Grease trap installation and cleaning, plus grease trap and waste pit service. Keep the health inspector happy and the drains flowing.

05

Commercial and industrial pumping

Bigger tanks, same reliability.

Commercial wastewater pumping, holding tank installation and pump out, and septage and sludge removal and hauling for industrial sites.

06

Sewer and water line work

Hookups, repairs, and replacements.

Installation and repair of city sewer and water lines, sewer hookups, and sewer line and sewer system service for properties connecting to municipal systems.

07

Catch basins and storm drains

The stuff under the parking lot.

Storm drain and catch basin cleaning, with a specialty in catch basin service. Also drainage systems, unclogging and cleaning, and back-up prevention.

08

Site work and excavation

Our own iron, our own operators.

Backhoe, bulldozer, and dump truck service, digging, and general site work. When a septic job needs earth moved, we move it ourselves.

The story

Founded in 1948, still family run

Mid State Sewerage Service began in 1948, founded by the father of current owners Peter and Thomas Stratford. What started as a local septic outfit in Millbury grew, truck by truck and tank by tank, into a company serving homes and businesses across Central Massachusetts.

Seventy-five-plus years in, the formula has not changed: show up, do the job right, charge a fair price, and answer the phone. It is a family owned business, and the name on the truck is the family name.

Their own tagline says it best: "We're #1 in the #2 Business." Corny? Sure. Wrong? Ask around Millbury.
