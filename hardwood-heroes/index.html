How it works

Sand. Stain. Seal. Done right.

Refinishing is a craft, not a shortcut. Every Hardwood Heroes project follows the same careful sequence so your floors look beautiful and stay that way.

  • 1
    Sand to bare wood.
    Old finish, scratches, and wear are removed down to clean wood.
  • 2
    Repair and prep.
    Gouges filled, boards fixed, gaps addressed, edges detailed.
  • 3
    Stain your color.
    From natural to deep walnut, matched to samples you approve.
  • 4
    Seal the finish.
    Durable topcoats applied and cured for a floor built to last.

What we do

Floor services, start to finish

Pick a service to see what is included. Every job starts with a conversation and a clear quote.

Full sanding and refinishing

The complete reset for worn hardwood. We sand down to bare wood, removing old finish, scratches, and discoloration, then build the floor back up with fresh finish.

  • Whole-room and whole-home refinishing
  • Original floors revealed under old carpet
  • Dust-conscious sanding with careful cleanup
  • Durable topcoats in your choice of sheen

Stain and color matching

Love the wood, want a new tone. We apply rich, even stain color and can match new work to existing floors so additions blend in.

  • Sample boards for you to approve first
  • Natural, warm, and deep tones
  • Color matching between rooms and levels
  • Modern staining and finishing techniques

Floor repairs

Deep gouges, pet damage, water spots, and loose boards. Customers tell us repaired areas come back looking brand new.

  • Gouge, scratch, and dent repair
  • Board replacement and re-weaving
  • Gap and squeak attention
  • Spot refinishing where a full sand is not needed

Stairs and landings

Staircases take the hardest beating in the house. We refinish treads, risers, and landings to match your main floors for one continuous look.

  • Tread and riser refinishing
  • Landings blended to room floors
  • Non-slip friendly finish options

Recoat and maintenance

Floors that are dull but not damaged may only need a screen and recoat: a fresh topcoat without a full sanding.

  • Screen-and-recoat refreshes
  • Honest advice on refinish vs. recoat
  • Care guidance to protect your finish

Our story

Heroes for hardwood, right here in Newport.

Hardwood Heroes is a Newport, Kentucky refinishing crew with one specialty: bringing wood floors back to life. No kitchen remodels, no bathroom tile, just floors, done with care.

The name says it all. Under old carpet, beneath years of wear, there is usually a beautiful hardwood floor waiting to be rescued. That rescue is what we do, and customers tell us the before-and-after difference is hard to believe.

What sets the work apart is not complicated: show up on time, do what you said you would do, price it fairly, and clean up before you leave. Reviewers keep coming back to those same points, and that is exactly how we want it.
