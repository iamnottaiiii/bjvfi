About The Shop

JEB GROUP TRUCK & TRAILER REPAIR is a truck repair shop at 3935 Kraus Ln in Fairfield, Ohio. The shop describes itself this way on its public listings: "We have a full range of services for semi trucks and trailers. Diagnostics, DOT inspection!"

Drivers who have been through the yard rate it 4.6 out of 5 across 75 Google reviews, with reviewers calling the work professional and noting the mechanics know what needs to be done.

Fairfield sits right in the freight corridor between Cincinnati and Dayton, and this yard is an easy stop for rigs running I-275 and the surrounding routes. SAMPLE
