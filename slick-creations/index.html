The Studio

Designed around how you actually live

Slick Creations takes on the decisions that stall a room: which wall color works with the floors you already have, how furniture should sit so the space flows, and which pieces are worth keeping. The result is a home that feels pulled together without feeling overworked.

Working from Jonesboro, the studio serves homeowners across Clayton County and the south Atlanta area who want a designer in the room with them, not a plan dropped in a box.

Good design is not decoration. It is the difference between a room you use and a room you love.

How a project runs. It starts with a walkthrough of your space and an honest conversation about budget and priorities. You get a written plan with color, layout, and sourcing recommendations. Then Slick Creations helps you execute, from ordering pieces to styling the final layer of art and accessories.

One room or the whole house. Smaller projects get the same attention as full-home work. Many clients start with the room that bothers them most, often the living room or primary bedroom, and come back for the rest once they see the difference.

Before you call

Have a few things ready and your first call will go further: which room you want to start with, photos of the space as it looks today, and a rough budget range you are comfortable with. Even a ballpark helps scope the plan.

Bringing samples of fabrics, paint swatches, or inspiration photos you already love? Bring them along. The consultation moves faster when we start from what you know you like.
