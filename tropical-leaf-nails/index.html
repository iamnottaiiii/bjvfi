Our story

Your neighborhood tropical getaway

Take a mini vacation, one manicure at a time.

Tropical Leaf brings a little island calm to Bath Avenue in Brooklyn's Bath Beach neighborhood. The name says it all: step inside and trade the rush of the avenue for warm color, green palms, and an hour that is entirely yours.

Guests describe a bright, spotless salon where the staff are easy to talk to, appointments run on time, and the detail work speaks for itself. Many mention the relaxing hand massage that finishes a manicure, and the smooth booking that makes fitting in a visit simple.

Whether you want a clean classic polish, long-lasting dip powder, sculpted acrylics, or art that starts conversations, the team takes the time to get the shape, length, and color right.
