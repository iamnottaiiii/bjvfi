About

A Severna Park staple for hair, skin and nails

John Michael Salon LLC sits on Baltimore Annapolis Boulevard in Severna Park, serving the surrounding Anne Arundel County community. The salon built its name the slow way: one appointment at a time, with stylists who remember your preferences and greet you like a regular.

Specializing in Redken, Esthederm and O.P.I. products.

What the salon is known for

Reviewers highlight friendly stylists who stay current on styles, thorough consultations, and results that hold up between visits. Many guests mention coming back to the same stylist for years.

The product promise

The salon works with three professional lines: Redken for hair, Esthederm for skin, and O.P.I. for nails, so every service starts with salon grade formulas.
