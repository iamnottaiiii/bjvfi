Chapter One Why the sparrow flies here

A shop built on word of mouth

Based on about 190 Google reviews, Dead Sparrow Tattoo holds a near perfect rating. The themes repeat: clean, friendly, patient artists, and ink that heals beautifully.

Spotless & Sterile

Clients call the space spotless again and again. Machines autoclave sterilized, single use needles, hospital grade habits in a street shop.

First Tattoo Friendly

Nervous about your first piece? Reviewers say the artists talk you through placement, sizing and aftercare until you feel completely at ease.

Custom, Never Cookie Cutter

Bring a rough idea and leave with original art. The crew is known for detailed custom work, cover ups and pieces with real personal meaning.

Chapter Two Tattoos & piercings under one roof

What the shop does best

Two crafts, one standard: careful, clean, and completely yours.

Custom tattoo artistry

From small first pieces to full custom sleeves, the artists draw for you, not off a wall. Cover ups are a particular strength: Ryan earns special praise for turning old ink into arm pieces clients love. Rachel Mumie is known for realism, linework and illustrative work, and William for bold original masculine designs.

Professional body piercing

C.J. Wolfinger handles piercings and earns glowing reviews for septum and ear work, painless technique and thorough aftercare coaching. For piercing availability and pricing, send a message to C.J. Wolfinger, per the shop.

Walk in curious, walk out marked, in the best way.

Chapter Three Your first visit

How booking works

On site services run by appointment, so every client gets unrushed, dedicated time.

1 · Reach out

Call (856) 783-5008 or stop by the Pike. Bring references, sketches, or just a rough idea.

2 · Consult

Talk placement, sizing and style with your artist. They will be honest about what will age well on skin.

3 · Get inked

Settle in. Clients must be 18, or 16 with a parent present, per the shop policy.

The artists

The hands behind the ink

A small crew with a big reputation across Clementon and South Jersey. Reviewers name names, and they name them happily.

Mike Oliver

Owner · Founder

Bought the shop he started at and never looked back. Reviewers call him patient, friendly, and a great first tattoo artist. His note to neighboring shops on the Pike, wishing them all success, says everything about the man.

Kelsey

Tattoo Artist

A client favorite many times over. Sweet, personable and down to earth, Kelsey is praised for nailing exactly what clients describe, even band emblems and tricky placements, and for putting nervous first timers completely at ease.

Makayla

Tattoo Artist

Detailed work and a personable chair side manner. Clients return to Makayla piece after piece and praise the easy, welcoming vibe between the artists.

Ryan

Tattoo Artist · Cover Ups

The cover up specialist. Clients bring Ryan tired old arm pieces and leave with work they show off. Nice, personable, professional, and an expert at second chances.

C.J. Wolfinger

Piercing Specialist

The piercing pro. Fantastic septum and ear work, a great personality, and aftercare coaching that goes above and beyond. Message C.J. directly for piercing availability and pricing.

Rachel Mumie

Tattoo Artist

Known for realism, fine linework and illustrative styles. A wonderful first tattoo artist who makes the whole process comfortable from stencil to aftercare.

Artist lineup can change, and specialties evolve. Call (856) 783-5008 to ask who is the right hand for your idea.
