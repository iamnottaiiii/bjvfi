Our story

Dog Tales Grooming Salon has served East Rochester for years, with some clients coming for two decades. Shawn carries on the shop's reputation for kind, gentle, thorough grooming: the crew comes out to welcome frightened first-timers, rescue pups get time and patience, and dogs jump into the groomers' arms at pickup. It is the salon where your dog's name is remembered and their tail does the talking.
