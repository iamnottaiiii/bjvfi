About Uni-Ref Inc

A Cincinnati manufacturer of heat

Uni-Ref Inc has manufactured industrial electric heaters and refractory materials from its Cincinnati, Ohio facility since 1978. Industry directories list the company as a small manufacturer and exporter, with an estimated team of under ten people and ISO 9002:1994 certification on record.

The company sits at 11301 Jefferson Street in the 45241 corridor, serving industrial buyers who need reliable process heating equipment and high-temperature materials.

Because Uni-Ref is a manufacturer rather than a residential service contractor, the best way to work with them is to call with your part number or process requirements and get a direct quote.
