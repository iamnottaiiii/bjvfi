How a commission moves

From site walk to planting plan

A typical path through the studio. Every project is quoted individually, so call to confirm the process for yours.

Sample process outline
1

Walk the site

Sun, soil, drainage, and how you live outdoors get recorded on the first visit.

2

Sketch the concept

Loose hand studies explore two or three directions for layout and character.

3

Draft the plan

The chosen direction is refined into a dimensioned, buildable drawing set.

4

Guide the build

Layout questions get answered in the field so the drawing becomes the garden.

The drawing set

The studio works as a landscape architecture practice, not a mow-and-blow crew. Services below are sample scopes drawn from the kind of work a landscape architect performs. Call (440) 829-2045 to confirm what is offered for your project.

A-201, Residential concept plans Sample scope

Whole-property layout studies for homes in Strongsville and the surrounding Cleveland suburbs. Beds, lawns, walks, and gathering spaces composed as one drawing.

  • Site analysis sketches
  • Two to three concept directions, call to confirm
  • Refined master plan sheet

A-301, Planting design Sample scope

Layered planting plans with quantities and spacing a contractor can price and plant from, tuned to Ohio hardiness, sun exposure, and soil.

  • Plant palette studies
  • Dimensioned planting plans, call to confirm
  • Seasonal interest sequencing

A-401, Hardscape layout studies Sample scope

Plan-view studies for patios, walks, steps, and low walls, coordinated with grading and planting so materials and plants share one logic.

  • Patio and walk layout studies
  • Material direction boards, call to confirm
  • Coordination with planting plans

A-501, Garden renovation studies Sample scope

For tired or overgrown landscapes: a measured look at what stays, what goes, and what the next twenty years could look like.

  • Existing conditions documentation
  • Phased improvement plans, call to confirm
  • Plant rescue and relocation notes
