Our story

The little cleaner on East Drive

Vet Cleaners sits at 5311 East Dr in Halethorpe, a few blocks from the Arbutus line, serving neighbors who would rather hand their clothes to someone they know than to a faceless chain.

Known to many as Arbutus Vet Dry Cleaners, the shop has become a fixture for the blocks around East Drive: the place where the counter staff know your name, remember how you like your shirts starched, and notice the loose button before you do.

We keep it simple. Careful inspection at drop-off. The right process for each fabric. Honest turnaround times. Clothes back in your hands looking their best.

That is the whole business model, and it has worked for years.

How we handle your garments

Every piece is inspected at the counter, tagged, and sorted by fabric and care needs. Stains are pre-treated, buttons and trims protected, and each item finished with a careful press before it goes on the rack for pick-up.

What we believe

Care is the whole job

  • Look before we clean. Every garment inspected at drop-off, with stains and special care noted together.
  • Right process, right fabric. Silks, wools, and synthetics each get the method they need.
  • Honest turnaround. A pick-up day you can plan around, and a call if anything changes.
