Chapter III: The Lore of the Wizard

Auto Wizard is an auto repair shop keeping Covington's chariots running, set upon W 35th Street. What follows is sample lore written for this page; the true history of the shop has not been publicly verified.

Sample

Long ago, or perhaps not so long, a mechanic of uncommon patience took up his wrench beneath the sign of the Wizard. Travelers from Covington and the surrounding towns bring him their ailing cars, and he listens to their engines the way a scholar reads an ancient text, patiently, until the trouble reveals itself.

His creed is simple: honest work, plain speech about what thy car needs, and no spell cast without thy leave. Whether thy brakes sing their warning song or thy engine coughs upon cold mornings, the Wizard's garage door stands open to thee.
