About

Forty-five years of
golden touches

Scruples the Golden Touch sits at 5300 Reisterstown Rd in Baltimore, a full-service beauty salon known for professional, friendly, clean service.

What sets it apart is longevity: reviewers report coming here for over 45 years, referring relatives, friends and co-workers through the decades. The stylist specializes in hair care, not just styling it.

From the first visit to the fortieth year, the promise is the same: excellent service and great hair care.
