Our story

Four decades of
doing it right.

Ark All Corporation was founded by Edward Day and Mary Jane, inspired by their upbringing and the years they spent working in their family business. Having seen the American dream up close, they started a company built on the values that shaped them: top notch materials, meticulous attention to detail, honest timelines, and customer satisfaction above all.

Over four decades they have assembled a dependable, courteous team that treats every home like their own. Today Ark All Corporation handles siding, decks, painting, stucco and Texcote, carpentry, and rain gutters for homes and businesses across Studio City and the greater Los Angeles area.

The promise has never changed: quality work, fair prices, and a crew you are glad to have at your door.
