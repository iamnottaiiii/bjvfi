Our story · 03

Four decades
of paint and patience

Artemis Decorative Painting is a Baltimore decorative painting studio with over 40 years of professional work in a wide variety of styles, finishes, and murals.

The studio

Not a painting crew.
A painting studio.

Most painters change a wall's color. Artemis changes what a wall is: a mural, a marble, a plastered sky, a commissioned piece made only for you.

Our public profile describes over 40 years as a professional decorative painter, full service with murals, Venetian plaster, backdrops, commissioned paintings, watercolors, and faux finishes, for both residential and commercial clients.

  • Hand-applied finishes, never spray-and-go
  • Residential and commercial projects
  • Consultation on finish, color, and light
