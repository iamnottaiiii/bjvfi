Our story

A cozy studio
with high standards

XPO Nails & Spa, also known around town as Xpo Nail Studio, is a small nail salon on Chili Avenue in Rochester.

Who we are

Small team,
big reputation

XPO is not a big-box nail factory. It is a cozy, welcoming studio where a small team of techs does one thing exceptionally well: nails.

Clients drive in from across the area because of the consistency. Walk-ins with tired, grown-out nails walk out with a flawless set. Pedicure regulars call it the best they have ever had, thanks to the relaxing foot massage and the unhurried pace.

The studio was remodeled into a fresh, modern space, and clients notice the cleanliness: sanitized after every customer, spotless from chair to polish wall.

Cozy, welcoming and straight to the point, with attention to detail on every single nail.
