Why restoration

Mortar fails long before brick does.

Most masonry problems start in the joints: crumbling mortar, spalled faces, and hairline cracks that let Ohio freeze and thaw cycles push water deeper every year. Restoration is not demolition. It is matching the original material, joint by joint, so the wall looks right and stays dry.

  • Mortar matched to your brick, not just any gray mix
  • Work focused on repair, not selling you a rebuild you do not need
  • Clean joints, straight lines, and a tidy site when we leave

The Handcraft standard

Every project we take on is held to the same bar: it has to be symmetrical, beautifully lined, and built to handle another few decades of Cincinnati weather. If a repair will not hold up, we tell you before we start, not after.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

From the first look to the final wash-down, you always know what is happening and what it costs.

01

Look

We inspect the masonry up close, find where water is getting in, and tell you what actually needs repair.

02

Match

Brick, stone, and mortar are matched to your building so repairs blend in instead of standing out.

03

Repair

Old mortar is raked out and joints are repointed, bricks replaced, and steps rebuilt by hand.

04

Protect

We seal where sealing helps, walk the site with you, and leave everything broom-clean.
