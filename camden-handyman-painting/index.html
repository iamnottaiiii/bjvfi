Our story

Camden's friendly fix-it crew

Camden Handyman & Painting started with a simple idea: Camden homeowners deserve a crew that answers the phone, shows up on time, and charges fair prices. From our shop at 1840 Broadway, we serve homes and small businesses across the city and nearby neighborhoods.

Our team pairs experienced handymen with skilled painters, so most jobs get finished in a single visit. Customers mention our patience, our professionalism, and the way a fresh coat of paint can make a living room feel cozy again. That is the standard we hold on every job.

Whether it is a pocket door that never slid right, screens that need rebuilding, or a whole house that needs color, we would love to help.
