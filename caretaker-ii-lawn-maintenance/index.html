Our story

The name is the job description.

Caretaker II Lawn Maintenance has looked after Hebron, Kentucky properties since 2007. Nineteen years of mowing routes, mulch beds, fall cleanups, and winter plow runs, all from the same local crew.

The business is led by owner Jamie Stidham, and it runs on a simple idea: a caretaker treats a place like it is their own. That means showing up on the day you said you would, keeping your word when the weather turns, and leaving every gate latched and every walk blown clean.

Folks around Hebron say the crew acts fast and keeps its word. That is the reputation the route is built on, one property at a time.

Homeowners get a steady weekly visit they never have to think about. Businesses get grounds care in the green months and dependable snow removal and salting when winter hits, so their members and customers stay safe.

Mowing, landscaping, mulch, shrub planting, aeration, overseeding, bobcat work, concrete, gutter cleaning, leaf removal, snow removal. If it keeps a property looking cared for, it is on the route.
