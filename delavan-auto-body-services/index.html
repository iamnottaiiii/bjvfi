About

Meet the shop

Delavan Auto Body Services is a neighborhood body shop on Delavan Place in Belleville, NJ, run by people who have been doing this work for decades. The operation is small on purpose: a tight crew, a hands-on owner, and a focus on the kind of fit-and-finish work that big chains rush past.

Drivers come back because the estimates are honest and the paint matches. Whether it is a parking-lot scrape or a full collision rebuild, the shop treats your car like it is the only one in the bay.
