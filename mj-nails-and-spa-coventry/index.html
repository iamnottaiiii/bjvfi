Our Story

Coventry's quiet little luxury

MJ Nails and Spa is a neighborhood nail salon on Main Street known for spotless stations, unhurried service, and techs who remember your name.

Walk in and the first thing you notice is how clean and bright everything feels. The second is how welcome you feel. The team at MJ Nails and Spa has built its name the slow way: one careful manicure, one relaxing pedicure, one happy guest at a time.

Reviewers describe techs who are meticulous and sweet, who take extra time with tricky feet, who add a complimentary massage while your polish dries, and who make a simple appointment feel like a small retreat from the week. That is the whole idea: a place where Coventry comes to slow down and leave polished.

  • Sanitized tools and fresh linens for every guest
  • Massage pedicure chairs and a calm, bright interior
  • Techs experienced in gel, dip, acrylic and nail art
  • Friendly, unhurried service for all ages

Clean first, always

Cleanliness is the most-mentioned theme across guest reviews. Stations are wiped down, tools are sanitized, and the salon stays bright and fresh all day.

The little extras

Guests mention complimentary massages after treatments and the team's habit of going above and beyond. It is why so many reviews end with "I will be back."

Good for groups

Planning a day out with friends or family? Reviewers mention visiting with moms and granddaughters. Call ahead and the salon will do its best to seat you together.
