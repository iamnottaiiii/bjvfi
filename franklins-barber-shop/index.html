A Sharper Upper West Side, One Chair at a Time

By The House Staff · Dateline: 490 Amsterdam Avenue

Walk through the door at 490 Amsterdam Avenue and the first thing you notice is the hum: clippers, conversation, and the easy rhythm of a shop that knows exactly what it is doing. Franklin's Barber Shop has held down its corner of the Upper West Side with the kind of steady, no nonsense craft that turns first time visitors into regulars.

Public review listings give the shop a 4.3 star rating across 127 reviews, and the word that comes up again and again in writeups of the shop is precision: razor cuts and fade cuts done with care, at prices the neighborhood can live with. Reviewers describe a friendly room where baseball plays on the TV and the conversation moves as fast as the clippers.

Walk ins are welcome, the phone is answered, and the chairs are ready. For the full rundown of what the shop offers, see the Service Menu on page two. To read what the neighborhood has to say, turn to Letters to the Editor.

Feature Story

The Corner Shop That Cuts It Straight

Reported from public records and neighborhood listings · Upper West Side, Manhattan

Every neighborhood has a barbershop that functions as something more than a barbershop: a bulletin board, a debate hall, a place where the regulars have assigned seats and the newcomers are welcomed like old friends. On Amsterdam Avenue, that shop is Franklin's.

Here is what the public record tells us. Franklin's Barber Shop operates at 490 Amsterdam Ave, New York, NY 10024, reachable at (212) 595-3009. City inspection records show the shop passed Department of Consumer Affairs inspections with no violations issued, most recently in April 2019. Public review listings rate the shop 4.3 stars across 127 reviews, with writeups praising razor cuts and fade cuts at reasonable prices and a friendly room where baseball plays on the TV.

What the record cannot tell you is the feel of the place: the hum of clippers, the smell of aftershave, the easy back and forth between barber and chair. For that, you will have to take a seat yourself. Walk ins are welcome, and the phone is answered seven days a week.
