About

Three decades of getting it built

Kane Contracting is a full-service construction company based in Clinton, Massachusetts.

The company

Licensed, insured, and local since 1994

Kane Contracting describes itself as a full-service construction company with over 30 years of general contracting knowledge and work, serving all of Worcester County from 125 Allen St in Clinton.

The company holds Massachusetts license CS 059835 and HIC registration 127048, is fully insured, and has been serving the community since 1994. It is a BBB accredited business with an A+ rating.

From frame-to-finish additions to a single storm door, the approach is the same: show up, do it right, stand behind it.
