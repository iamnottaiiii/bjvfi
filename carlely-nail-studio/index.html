Our story

A quieter kind of nail appointment

Carlely Nail Studio opened on Grovedale Court in Franconia with a simple idea: a nail appointment should feel unhurried. No rushing, no assembly line, just careful work in a calm space.

Every guest gets sanitized tools, honest advice about what their nails need, and polish applied like it matters. Whether you come in for a quick polish change or a full spa pedicure, you leave with nails that look deliberate.

Unhurried appointments. Immaculate detail.
  • Sanitized tools for every guest
  • Unhurried, one guest at a time focus
  • Quality polishes and gentle products
  • Easy parking on Grovedale Court
