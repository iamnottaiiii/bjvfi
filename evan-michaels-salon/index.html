Our story

A salon built on craft and care

Evan Michaels Salon was founded on a simple goal: provide the highest quality customer service in a sophisticated and unique environment. Owner Kasey Castetter leads a team that treats education as a way of life, constantly training in the newest techniques and trends so every client gets the best the industry has to offer.

Recognition

An award-winning team

Evan Michaels is a multi-year award-winning Top 100 L'anza Salon in the USA and Canada, and the area's only L'anza Healing Center, with a full L'anza retail center in the salon. The team pairs that pedigree with a warm, unpretentious welcome.

Clients drive in from hours away, some from as far as Binghamton, for appointments with Kasey and the team. When your hair is the thing people compliment first, you understand why.

"Loyal customers drove from hours away that day."
The experience

More than a haircut

From the moment you walk in, the team takes time to listen. Consultations are thorough, suggestions are honest, and nobody rushes you out of the chair. Add in beauty events and partnerships with local businesses, and you get a salon that is as much a part of the community as it is a place to get your hair done.
