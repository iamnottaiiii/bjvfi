Since 1982

Barnard Landscaping was founded in 1982 and is led by owner Robert Barnard. The company serves Lexington, Burlington, Winchester, Woburn, Arlington, Billerica, Bedford and the surrounding Metro North and West communities.

It is a full-service outdoor company: landscaping, lawn care, masonry, excavation, chimneys, tree work and snow removal, with free estimates and warranty on completed work.

Our story

Four decades of Lexington yards

Long before most local landscaping companies existed, Barnard was already mowing, planting and building stone walls in Lexington. That history shows up in the details: crews who know the town's soil, its trees and its leaf seasons, and stonework built to outlast trends.

The company has grown into a full outdoor-services business, adding masonry, excavation, chimney work and snow removal so customers can rely on one trusted name for the whole property. Reviewers give it 4.5 stars on Angi across 16 reviews and it holds a 4.06 average across 22 online ratings tracked by Porch.

Barnard is a licensed company offering free estimates and warranty coverage. It also serves the community publicly: owner Bob Barnard has spoken to the press about the realities of landscaping work in Lexington, including the town's equipment rules that now shape how every local landscaper operates.
