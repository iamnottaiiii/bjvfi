Our Story

Over 35 Years Behind the Chair

A traditional barber shop with experienced, skilled barbers.

Royal Barber Shop has been serving the Kenwood and greater Cincinnati community for over 35 years, in Kenwood Square at 7324 Kenwood Rd. It is an authentic, old-time barber shop: antique barber supplies, a classic barber pole, and the tools of a craft that the barbers here never abandoned.

The shop still uses straight razors to shave outlines, and offers straight razor face shaves for anyone who wants the full traditional experience. Reviewers describe the look and feel as reminiscent of a classic military barber shop, with barbers who know classic and military styles inside and out.

"Remember, in our shop you are not a stranger, just a friend we have not met yet." Shop motto

Customers describe a comfortable, welcoming atmosphere and fair prices. Some have been coming for decades: one reviewer notes forty-six years with the same barber. Newcomers are treated like friends from the first visit.
