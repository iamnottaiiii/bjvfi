Why finish carpentry matters

Rough framing holds the house up. Finish work makes it home.

The trim, moldings, cabinets and doors are the parts of your home you touch and see every single day. We sweat the joints, the reveals and the lines so everything reads clean and lasts.

  • Crisp casings, baseboards and crown molding cut tight and coped right.
  • Cabinet design and installation that fits your space, not the other way around.
  • A small, focused crew: the same hands from first measure to final touch-up.

Services

Finish carpentry services in Worcester, MA

Cabinet design and installation

Kitchen and bath cabinets, vanities and built-ins: planned around your space, installed level, plumb and solid.

Trim and molding

Interior doors and hardware
