About

Meet Sharon

🎨

The Cake Lady of Crete

Sharon Komes has been designing custom cakes from her Benton Street shop since 1994. Neighbors call her Crete's cake lady, and families across the surrounding towns trust her with their most important celebrations.

✦

Craft You Can Taste

Reviewers praise cakes that meet or exceed expectations, from a beloved lemon cake with raspberry filling to showpiece wedding tiers. Sharon is known for being friendly, responsive, and easy to work with.

🎉

Part of the Community

Sharon's hosts a Holiday and Bridal Open House during Crete Country Christmas, and her cakes have graced bridal showers, weddings, and town celebrations for more than three decades.
