Our Story

A coatings crew that preps like it matters

Aspire Coatings is an Orlando floor coating company serving homes and businesses across Central Florida, built on one belief: the prep is the floor.

How we work

Most coating failures are prep failures

01

On-site evaluation

We look at your slab, ask how you use the space, and test for moisture rising through the concrete. In Florida, this step is non-negotiable.

02

Grind and repair

Diamond grinding opens the surface for mechanical bonding. Cracks and joints get repaired with honest expectations about slab movement.

03

Prime, build, topcoat

Moisture-mitigating primer, base coat, your decorative layer (flake or metallic), then a protective topcoat. Each layer cures before the next goes on.

What guides us

Three promises on every job

A

Honest scope

If your slab needs repair before it is ready for coating, we tell you. If a cheaper system fits your space, we say so.

B

Florida-ready systems

UV-stable topcoats, moisture mitigation and hot-tire-proof bonding, spec'd for Central Florida, not copied from a northern catalog.

C

Clean handoff

We leave the space ready to use and tell you exactly when you can drive, walk and wash on it.
