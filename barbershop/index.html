An honest shop
on Pacific Coast Hwy

Barbershop is a no-frills neighborhood barbershop in Torrance, built on the belief that a great haircut should not require a downtown appointment or a downtown price.

The shop

Classic barbering, South Bay style

Tucked into 3902 Pacific Coast Hwy in Torrance, the shop keeps things simple: skilled barbers, sharp tools, hot towels, and a chair that is actually comfortable. It is the kind of place where regulars are known by name and new faces get the same treatment from the first visit.

Whether you wear a classic side part, a tight fade, or a full beard, the cut is tailored to your hair and your life, not a one-size template.

The promise

Walk out sharper

Every cut leaves with a hot lather neckline shave and a styled finish, so you walk out photo-ready.

01

Listen

What do you want, what is your hair like, how much work do you want to do at home? Straight answers both ways.

02

Cut and finish

Precision cutting, blended fades, razor-sharp lines, then the hot towel and the style. You check the mirror before you pay, every time.
