Our story

A general contractor that shops the trades for you

Allied Building Contractors is a Cincinnati general contractor working out of Beechmont Ave. The company's model is simple: instead of you juggling a carpenter, a roofer, an electrician, and a plumber, Allied lines up trade specialists, verifies their insurance and workers compensation, and coordinates the whole sequence.

You deal with one contact person no matter how many specialists a project needs. The trades Allied brings in work their trade full time, which keeps the work current with materials and local building codes, and keeps the schedule moving without crews tripping over each other.

The focus is residential: remodeling, restorations, room additions, kitchens and baths, roofing, flooring, and framing, with select light commercial work. Estimates are free, and the company describes itself as fully insured and bondable.
