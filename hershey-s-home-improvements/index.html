Services

What we build and fix

Our trade areas are masonry and carpentry for homes, additions, and concrete curbs and sidewalks. Pick a tab to see what each one covers.

Trade area

Masonry

Brick, block, and stone work for homes: repairs, rebuilds, and new masonry built to last through Pennsylvania winters.

From a crumbling chimney shoulder to a new block wall, masonry is unforgiving work, and it shows when it is done right.

Trade area

Carpentry

Rough framing to finish trim: the woodwork that holds your home together and the details you see every day.

Straight, square, and tight. Good carpentry is invisible, and that is the point.

Trade area

Home additions

Extra bedroom, bigger kitchen, or a whole new wing: additions planned and built to look like they were always there.

An addition touches everything, foundation, framing, roof, finishes. Having one contractor across the trades keeps it coherent.

Trade area

Concrete curbs and sidewalks

New and replacement concrete: curbs, sidewalks, and flatwork poured level, finished clean, and built to handle freeze and thaw.

Good concrete is all preparation. We do the groundwork so the surface holds up for years.
