The salon

A calm space for the whole package.

"From a basic trim to colouring and a perm to the whole package, LALA HAIR is the perfect place to go."

Ellis and Vivian established LALA HAIR in December 2018. Ellis brings more than twenty years of styling experience from Los Angeles, known for reviving damaged ends and delivering precise color and cuts, even for thin or fine hair. Vivian is the salon's expert in makeup and skincare.

Our story

Experience you can feel in the chair.

"Ellis, a long-time stylist, is known for reviving damaged ends and delivering precise color and cuts."

Ellis brings more than twenty years of hair styling experience from Los Angeles to Maryland. Vivian rounds out the team as the salon's expert in makeup and skincare, so clients can get the whole package in one visit.
