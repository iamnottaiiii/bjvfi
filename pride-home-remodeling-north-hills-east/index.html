Why Pride

One crew. One plan. No surprises.

Remodeling is stressful enough without guessing games. Every Pride project runs on a written scope, a clear schedule, and a single point of contact from demo day to the final walkthrough.

Whole home thinking

As a licensed-scope general contractor, Pride coordinates every trade under one roof, so your kitchen, bath, or addition moves as a single project, not five separate ones.

Written scope first

You approve a detailed, line by line scope before work begins. If something changes mid project, it is priced and approved in writing before anyone picks up a tool.

Clean, livable job sites

Dust control, daily tidy up, and protection for the rooms you are still living in. Most clients are surprised how normal life feels during the build.

A remodel should feel like an upgrade, not an ordeal. That is the bar for every job we take on.

Our Story

A contractor is only as good as its last job.

Pride Home Remodeling North Hills East is a general contracting business based on Lanark Street in Van Nuys, serving North Hills East, Van Nuys, and the surrounding San Fernando Valley neighborhoods.

The name sets the standard: take pride in the work, or do not take the job. That means straight lines, honest timelines, and treating a client's home like it matters, because for the weeks we are in it, it is our workplace and their life at the same time.

Most of our projects come from one room leading to another. A kitchen done right earns the bathroom. A bathroom done right earns the addition. We plan to earn the next room on every job.

Measure twice, communicate constantly, and leave every room cleaner than you found it.
