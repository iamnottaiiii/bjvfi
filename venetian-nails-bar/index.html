Our story

A little Venetian calm
on Richmond Ave

Venetian Nails Bar is a full-service nail bar in Staten Island, built around one simple idea: great nails should never feel rushed.

Why we opened

Slow down, sit back,
leave polished

We named the salon after Venice for a reason. Not the gondolas, the pace. The Venetian way is unhurried: linger over the details, take pride in the craft, and never rush something beautiful.

That is how we approach every set. Careful prep, precise shaping, clean lines. Whether you want a sheer nude for the office or hand-painted art for a wedding, you get the same unhurried attention.

Our station setup keeps things calm and comfortable, and our sanitation routine is non-negotiable: sanitized tools and fresh supplies for every single client, every single time.

Great nails are never an accident. They are prep, patience and practice.

Prep first

A beautiful set starts before the color. We take cuticle care and shaping seriously, because that is what makes polish last.

Clean always

Hospital-grade sanitation on every implement, fresh files and buffers per client. Ask us about our process anytime.

Color for everyone

From barely-there sheers to full chrome drama, our color wall has a shade for every mood, season and occasion.
