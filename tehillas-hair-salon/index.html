Our story

A 49th Street favorite

Tehilla's Hair Salon sits at 1207 49th Street in Brooklyn, in the heart of a neighborhood where word of mouth is everything. The salon has earned a perfect 5.0 rating across 10 Google reviews, built on consistency: guests say Tehilla always makes time for them and does exactly what they ask for.

The team is known especially for wig work, cutting, fitting and styling wigs so they look natural and feel comfortable, and for occasion styling that holds through weddings and long evenings.

"The best ladies in town."

That is how one guest put it, and the sentiment repeats across reviews: an amazing team, fantastic wedding hair, and styling done with genuine care.
