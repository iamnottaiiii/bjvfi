About

46 years of good hair in Nedrow

C & C Hairstylist has been part of the S Salina St corridor for 46 years, according to Yellow Pages. The reviews tell the rest: a spotless shop, a wonderful owner in Cindy, and stylists like Janeen and Lisa that clients ask for by name.

"Cindy is wonderful. Her place is spotless. My hair has never looked better."

That sentiment shows up again and again across 16 reviews and a 4.8 star average. People describe great people, great haircuts, a neat and tidy shop, and an easy location at 6506 S Salina St.
