How we work

Four steps to concrete that holds up

Most concrete problems start below the surface. We do the unglamorous prep work so your slab does not crack, settle or heave.

01

Site prep

We grade, compact and lay a proper gravel base. The groundwork decides the next thirty years.

02

Form and reinforce

Clean forms, correct slope for drainage, and rebar or wire mesh where the load demands it.

03

Pour and finish

The right mix for the job, screeded flat and finished smooth, broomed or stamped to your taste.

04

Cure and seal

Proper curing time and a quality sealer protect the surface against freeze, thaw and salt.
