Our story

Rooted in North Hills.

One Stop Landscaping Inc. is a landscape design company based on Index St in North Hills, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley. The idea behind the name is simple: homeowners should not need three different contractors to get a yard they are proud of.

So the company was built to cover the whole job, from the first sketch to the plants in the ground to the regular visits that keep it all thriving. One team, one phone number, one standard of care.

Whether it is a full backyard redesign, a water smart front yard, or a standing maintenance visit, the work is planned around Valley conditions: hot summers, clay soil, and plants that love both.

How we work

Straightforward, start to finish.
