About

A Broadview Heights pool crew, through and through

Mikey's Pools LLC is an inground swimming pool contractor based in Broadview Heights, Ohio, serving homeowners across Northeast Ohio.

The business is led by Mike Smolen, who started out digging holes for above ground pools with a shovel and worked his way up to running full crews installing and servicing pools across the region.

Today the company is a full service inground pool shop: custom liner replacement, seasonal openings and closings, repairs, weekly maintenance, and a full line of pool chemicals and products.

  • Certified Pool Operator (CPO) on staff, recognized in all 50 states.
  • Approved on HomeAdvisor and Angi with a 4.8 rating across 31 verified reviews.
  • Inground only. A focused specialty, not a side offering.
