What this business is

Gillen Carpentry / Handyman Services is a carpenter and handyman business based at 44 Greendale Ave in Worcester's Greendale neighborhood. The work covers everyday home projects: building and repairing, installing and refinishing, inside and out.

Public business listings describe the trade mix as carpentry, handyman services, cabinets, window installation and service, siding, deck repair, painting, and kitchen remodeling. Public reviews mention closet builds, floor replacement, porch repairs, floor refinishing, and painting, with customers noting fair pricing, on-time work, and clean worksites.
