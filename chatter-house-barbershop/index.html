Our story

Three decades behind the chair.

Chatter House Barber Shop serves the great city of Buffalo, New York with second to none service and style, dedicated to over 30 years of experience.

The name says it all: this is a shop where conversation flows as freely as the clippers move. Regulars, first timers, and everyone in between get the same treatment, a careful cut and a chair that feels like the neighborhood living room.
