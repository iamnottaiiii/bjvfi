Regalos, picked with heart

"Regalos" means gifts, and that is the whole idea here. Whether it is a first veladora or a statue for a new home, we will help you choose something that says the right thing.

  • CandlesGlass veladoras, pillars and votives for the altar.
  • Statues and imagesFigures and framed prints of the beloved devotions.
