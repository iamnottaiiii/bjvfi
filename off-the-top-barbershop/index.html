Above It All: Our Story

What public sources say about Off the Top Barbershop.

The Shop

Off the Top Barbershop sits at 345 W Pike St in South Lebanon, Ohio. Public listings describe a friendly neighborhood barbershop known for haircuts, beard trims, and eyebrow shaping, with reviewers frequently praising the staff's patience with kids and a clean, welcoming atmosphere.

Per its Google business listing, the shop is owned and operated by Rob Upton, a local resident of Morrow, who graduated from the Ohio State College of Barber Styling in 2000.
