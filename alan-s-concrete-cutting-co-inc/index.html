What we do

Services

Pick a specialty below. Every job starts with a look at the slab and a straight answer about the best way to cut it.

Concrete sawing and cutting

Diamond-blade sawing through reinforced slabs, walls, and pavement. Straight cuts at controlled depths for repairs, openings, and removals.

  • Flat sawing for driveways, patios, floors, and parking lots
  • Wall sawing for door, window, and vent openings
  • Trench cuts for plumbing, electrical, and drainage
  • Wet cutting to keep dust and slurry under control

Concrete removal and breakout

When a slab is past saving, we break it out and get it off your property, leaving a clean base ready for the next pour.

  • Driveway, patio, and walkway removal
  • Interior slab removal for remodels and additions
  • Haul-off of broken concrete and debris
  • Base prep guidance for your replacement pour

Control joints and crack control

Concrete cracks. Sawn control joints decide where. Proper joint spacing and depth protect new pours and extend the life of existing slabs.

  • Sawn control joints in new driveways, patios, and floors
  • Joint layout planning before the pour
  • Re-cutting and deepening joints in existing slabs

Curb and pavement work

Sawing and removal for curbs, gutters, and asphalt or concrete pavement around commercial sites and subdivisions.

  • Curb cuts and curb removal
  • Pavement patching prep and straight-line cuts
  • Parking lot repair cuts for overlays and patches
