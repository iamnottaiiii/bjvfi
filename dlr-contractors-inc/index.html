Our story

A Valley contractor, plain and simple

DLR Contractors, Inc. works out of 14950 San Jose St in Mission Hills, serving the San Fernando Valley and surrounding Los Angeles neighborhoods. We are builders first: the value we offer is showing up, doing the work right, and standing behind it.

How we bid

Written scope, no fog

Every project starts with a clear written scope of work. If it is not in the scope, we talk about it before it becomes a surprise on the invoice.

How we build

Clean sites, straight lines

A job site is someone's home or business. We keep it orderly, protect what is not being worked on, and finish the details most people would skip.

How we finish

Walkthrough, then handshake

Nothing is done until you walk it with us and sign off. That final walkthrough is part of every job, not an extra.
