Our story

A mobile crew from Lynn, MA

Bee-Clean Mobile Pressure is a mobile pressure washing business based in Lynn, Massachusetts. The concept is simple: instead of you renting a machine and spending a weekend on a ladder, the washing equipment comes to your property.

Public listings describe the business as a professional pressure washing service for residential and commercial properties, using high powered equipment to remove dirt, grime, and mold and restore surfaces to their original condition.

Lynn sits on the North Shore just north of Boston, with plenty of homes and businesses whose exteriors take a beating from New England weather. That is the job Bee-Clean was built for: showing up, washing it down, and leaving the place looking like itself again.
