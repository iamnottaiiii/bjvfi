Our story

A real shop,
on a quiet street

Auto Butler Enterprises & Wolk Enterprises Collision Center operates from a dedicated 2,460 sq ft facility on Putnam Blvd in Essington, built for auto repair and collision work.

Built for body work,
run by people.

The shop sits on a safe, quiet street in Essington's light-industrial corridor, minutes from Philadelphia International Airport. Inside, energy-efficient LED lighting covers a fully air-conditioned shop floor, heated in winter by a 150,000 BTU natural gas system and powered by robust three-phase electrical for professional equipment.

What matters most is simpler: when your car is damaged, you want one person to call, one honest estimate, and one shop that treats the repair like it is their own car.

  • Dedicated collision facility, not a corner of a lot
  • Climate-controlled shop, year round
  • Appointments recommended, walk-ins welcome
