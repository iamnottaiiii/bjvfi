Our Story

A Pasadena Roofer With a Storm-Shield Standard

BluePathPrimeRoofing is a roofing contractor serving Pasadena, California from 48 Marion Ave. The work is straightforward: show up on time, diagnose honestly, and leave every roof tighter than we found it.

Southern California roofs take a beating, from Santa Ana winds to sudden winter downpours. Our crews focus on the details that keep water out: proper flashing, sealed penetrations, and drainage that actually drains.

When storms roll through the San Gabriel Valley, we answer. Emergency tarping and rapid repair keep small damage from becoming a big claim.
