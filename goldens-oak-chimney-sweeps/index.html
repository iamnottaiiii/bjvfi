Why it matters

A dirty chimney is a quiet hazard

Creosote builds up every time you burn wood, and it only takes a spark to turn that buildup into a chimney fire. Most homeowners never look inside their flue, which is exactly why the Chimney Safety Institute recommends an annual sweep and inspection. We do both in a single visit, and we show you photos of everything we find.

From the triple deckers of the Hill to the colonials of Westville, we have seen what New Haven winters do to masonry. Cold snaps, freeze thaw cycles and heavy rain wear down crowns and mortar year after year. A small repair now beats a rebuild later.

We treat every fireplace like it heats our own home. Clean, careful and honest about what your chimney actually needs.

Call (475) 224-2204 and tell us what your fireplace has been doing. Smoking back into the room? Strange smell? We will tell you straight what it takes to fix it.

Our story

A New Haven crew with an old fashioned work ethic

Goldens OAK Chimney Sweeps. started with a simple observation: plenty of companies would sell a New Haven homeowner a new chimney, but few would take the time to sweep the one they had properly. So we built our name on the unglamorous work done well, thorough sweeps, honest inspections and repairs sized to the actual problem.

Our base on Whalley Avenue puts us minutes from every neighborhood in the city, from Fair Haven to the Annex to West Hills. We answer our own phone, show up in the window we promise, and leave the living room cleaner than we found it.

If your chimney does not need work, we will tell you. If it does, we will show you exactly why.

Every visit ends with photos of your flue and a plain English rundown of what we found. No scare tactics, no upsells for things that can wait. That is how a local crew earns repeat business, one honest sweep at a time.
