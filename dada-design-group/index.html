What we do

Design that starts with how you live.

Every project begins with a conversation about daily life: how you cook, where you gather, what you need a room to do for you. From there we build a plan around light, proportion, and materials that will age well.

Our scope runs from single room refreshes to full home and workplace transformations, with furnishing, color, and styling handled in house.

Our story

A Montrose studio built on restraint.

DADA Design Group works from a studio on Honolulu Avenue in the heart of Montrose, serving homeowners and businesses across La Crescenta, Glendale, Pasadena, and the greater Foothills.

We believe the best rooms are edited, not filled. Our work favors natural materials, honest craftsmanship, and palettes drawn from the California landscape, so a space feels calm on day one and still right years later.

"We design for real mornings, real dinners, real workdays. Beauty follows function that is done well."
