OUR STORY

A Sun Valley builder, plain and simple.

ISMC Builders, Inc. is a California corporation based at 10950 Tuxford St in Sun Valley. We are a working construction company, not a sales office: the people who answer the phone are the people who show up on site.

Our record speaks in permits. Public building permit data shows ISMC active on permitted projects across recent years, earning a BuildZoom score of 106, which places the company above 93% of contractors in California by record.
