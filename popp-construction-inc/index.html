Our story

A name built since 1996

"Since 1996, we've built our name, our business and our reputation honoring this commitment." That commitment is the company's Seal of Satisfaction: quality workmanship and materials, professional integrity, exceptional service, and maximum value.

Popp Construction Inc was formed in Kentucky in 2004 and is based at 778 Smith Hiteman Road in Alexandria. Kentucky Secretary of State records list Samuel J. Popp as president and Tammy L. Popp as secretary. It is a family-led company serving Northern Kentucky homeowners.
