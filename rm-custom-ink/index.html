How a session works

Appointment only, so your artist's full attention is on you from the first sketch to the final wipe-down.

  1. Call to book

    Reach the shop at (267) 421-8507. Describe your idea, where you want it, and roughly how large. You will get an honest quote and a time that is all yours.

  2. Design together

    Your artist sketches the piece around your idea, then refines it with you until it feels exactly right.

  3. Get inked

    Sterile setup, careful linework, and a calm room. First-timers are welcome and walk out as regulars.

  4. Heal it right

    You leave with clear aftercare instructions so the piece heals clean and stays sharp for years.
