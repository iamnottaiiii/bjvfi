The salon

Where the gossip is all about great hair

Hair Gossip Salon is a beauty salon at 7871 Heritage Dr in Annandale, Virginia, easy to reach for anyone in the Washington, D.C. metro area. Inside you will find a relaxed, welcoming space where walk-ins are the norm and stylists take the time to get your cut right.

The reputation here was built the old-fashioned way: one happy head of hair at a time. Guests tell friends about the stylist who matched their photo exactly, the fade that finally looked right, and the cut-with-wash that cost less than they expected.
