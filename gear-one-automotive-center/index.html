About the shop

Meet Gear One

Gear One Automotive Center sits on Governor Printz Boulevard in Essington, serving Delaware County drivers, local businesses, and fleets. The shop is run by co-owners Sam and John D., and customers know them by name.

The reputation is built on second opinions that save people money. Reviewers describe Sam test-driving a car, finding the real problem, and sending it home fixed in under an hour. Fleet customers say the team has kept their vehicles running for years.

Veterans get a discount, walk-ins get a fair shake, and everybody gets the straight story about their car.

  • Longtime local shop
    Serving Essington and Tinicum Township drivers for decades.
  • New ownership, same trust
    Customers stayed loyal through the ownership change because the honest service never changed.
  • Today
    4.8 stars across 48 Birdeye reviews, with regular praise for fair prices, quick work, and the friendliest counter in town.
