Our story

Thirteen years learning the trade. Then a business of his own.

Marathon Insulation is a family-owned business founded in 2022 in Arleta, California. Owner Jonathan spent 13 years working for a reputable insulation company before deciding to start his own crew, bringing the knowledge, the hands-on experience and the customer-first habits with him.

In his own words on Angi: he has all the knowledge and hands-on experience to answer any of your insulation questions. That promise shows up in the work: eco-friendly materials, real safety measures on every job, and crews that treat your home like their own.

Common questions

Good to know before you call.

How much does insulation cost?
Every home is different, so Marathon gives free, in-writing estimates after a look at your attic and walls. Call (818) 679-5893 to schedule one.
How do I know if my insulation is failing?
Hot rooms in summer, cold floors in winter, and energy bills that keep climbing are classic signs. An energy checkup finds the leaks fast.
Do you work with older homes?
Yes. Retro-fitting older Valley homes is a core service, and the crew works to minimize disruption to walls and ceilings.
Do you speak Spanish?
Yes, Spanish-speaking customers are welcome. Hablamos español.
