Our story

A Burbank builder since 2002

ACRO Constructors, also listed as Acro Development Inc., has been planning and building across the Los Angeles area for more than two decades.

The approach

Driven by results,
built on planning

ACRO approaches each project thoroughly, strategically planning every phase of construction before the first shovel hits dirt. Whether the job starts as a conceptual sketch for an office or a full remodeling of a medical facility, the team manages it proactively.

Clients describe the same qualities again and again: responsiveness, adaptability to changing needs, and communication clear enough that decision makers always know their options.

The company is led by owner Javier Primera and operates from Burbank, serving commercial and residential clients across the region.
