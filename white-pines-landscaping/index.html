Our story

A local crew with deep roots

White Pines Landscaping is a Shrewsbury-based landscaping company serving homeowners across the area. We handle the full life of a yard: design and installation, weekly lawn care, seasonal cleanups, and the hardscape touches that tie it all together.

Being local matters. We know New England soil, New England weather, and which plants actually thrive here. And because we are right around the corner on Crescent St, we are easy to reach when you need us.

Our approach is straightforward: show up on time, do careful work, and price it fairly. Call for a free estimate and see the difference a dedicated crew makes.
