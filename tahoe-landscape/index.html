Our story

A local crew that treats your yard like our own.

Tahoe Landscape is a landscaping company based on Foothill Boulevard in Sylmar, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley. Our work covers the full life of a yard: design and planting when you want a fresh start, and steady maintenance to keep it looking sharp.

Valley yards live through hot, dry summers and sudden winter rain, so we plan every project around water wise choices: the right plants in the right spots, efficient drip irrigation, and soil prep that helps roots hold on. The result is a yard that looks good without demanding constant attention.

We keep things simple: show up on time, price the job honestly, clean up before we leave. That is how a 5.0 star rating on Google is earned, one yard at a time.
