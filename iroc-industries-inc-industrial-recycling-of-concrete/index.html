From Rubble to Resource

Demolition debris does not have to end at the landfill. Our Oceanside facility accepts concrete, brick, block, and asphalt, then processes it into crushed aggregate ready for reuse on Long Island projects.

Why recycle concrete?

  • ✓Diverts heavy debris from landfills and transfer stations
  • ✓Local material, shorter hauls, lower project costs
  • ✓Recycled aggregate works for subbase, fill, and drainage
  • ✓One permitted facility, full regulatory compliance

Built for contractors

IROC Industries is set up to serve the working contractor: a straightforward intake process, a permitted yard in Oceanside, and a team that knows job site material. Bring us your concrete debris and leave with, or pick up, recycled aggregate for the next phase of work.

Our Story

IR

IROC Industries Inc.

Industrial Recycling of Concrete is a NYSDEC registered construction and demolition debris handling and recovery facility (Permit No. 30W44R, Region 1) at 55 New Street in Oceanside, NY. The facility is operated by Mike Loguidice, Sr., and has held its state registration since 2008, serving contractors across Long Island and the New York metro area.

The idea is simple and it still works: concrete is too valuable to bury. Every load we process is one less load in a landfill and one more local source of aggregate for the next road, foundation, or drainage job.

Step 01

Receive

Contractors bring concrete and masonry debris to our Oceanside yard.

Step 02

Sort

Material is inspected and sorted so only clean debris moves forward.

Step 03

Crush

Debris is crushed and screened into graded recycled aggregate.

Step 04

Reuse

Aggregate goes back to local sites as subbase, fill, and drainage stone.

Permitted and accountable

We operate under NYSDEC registration as a construction and demolition debris handling and recovery facility, with annual reporting to the state. That paperwork is your assurance that your debris is handled by the book.
