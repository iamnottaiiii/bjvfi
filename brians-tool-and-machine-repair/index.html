Services

How a repair ticket moves

Every job that comes over the counter follows the same four steps. No mystery, no jargon, just the ticket.

01Intake

Drop off and ticket

Bring the tool or machine to the shop. What is wrong, when it started, and what it was doing when it quit all go on the ticket.

Bring the tool, the charger, and any parts you already have.
02Diagnosis

Diagnose and estimate

The unit gets opened up and tested. You get a plain explanation of the fault and an estimate before any work starts.

Estimates are per job. Call to confirm current pricing.
03Repair

Repair and rebuild

Worn parts are replaced or rebuilt, machines are reassembled, and everything is torqued, greased, and set to spec.

Rebuild where possible, replace where it counts.
04Pickup

Test and pickup

The repair is bench tested before it goes back to you, so what leaves the shop is proven working.

Test it at pickup. Ask questions while it is in your hands.
