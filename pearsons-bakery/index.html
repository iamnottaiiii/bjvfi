Our Story

Decades on 16th Street

Pearson's Bakery has been a fixture at 1 W 16th St in Chicago Heights for decades, listed at 45 years in business by one directory. Generations have walked through its doors for birthday cakes, Sunday pies, and weekday-morning donuts.

What keeps people coming back is simple: fresh daily baking, friendly staff, cakes decorated to order, and prices that respect the neighborhood.

And as the sign in the shop reminds every visitor: stressed spelled backwards is desserts.
