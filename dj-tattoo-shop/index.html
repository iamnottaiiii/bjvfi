How it works

From idea to healed

1. Call or stop by. Tell us what you want: a name, a design you found, or just a rough idea. We will talk size, placement and price.

2. We draw it up. Your artist prepares the design and shows it to you before anything is permanent. Changes are part of the process.

3. You get tattooed. Clean setup, single-use needles, and a steady hand. Most small pieces take under an hour.

4. You heal it right. Every tattoo leaves with simple aftercare instructions. Follow them and your ink will thank you for years.

Bring a photo ID, eat beforehand, and wear something comfortable. We will handle the rest.
