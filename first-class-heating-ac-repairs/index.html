Why call

Signs your system needs attention

Any of these sound familiar? A quick diagnostic visit usually finds the cause fast.

  • 1Warm air from the vents when the thermostat is set to cool
  • 2Uneven temperatures from room to room
  • 3New noises, rattling, banging or grinding from the unit
  • 4Short cycling, the system turns on and off constantly
  • 5Rising energy bills with no change in how you use the system
  • 6A system that will not start at all, the clearest signal to call

What we do

Heating and cooling services

Three service lines cover the full life of a home comfort system, from the first repair to the eventual replacement. Pick a tab to explore each one.

Cooling

Air conditioning repair and installation

Glendale summers ask a lot of an AC. Cooling service starts with a real diagnosis: refrigerant levels, electrical components, airflow and the thermostat, so the fix matches the fault.

  • ✓AC troubleshooting for systems that blow warm, leak or refuse to start
  • ✓Component repair, capacitors, contactors, motors and controls
  • ✓Full system installation when repair no longer makes sense
"Cooling season waits for no one." Book a tune-up before the first heat wave
Heating

Furnace and heating repair and installation

When the temperature drops, a dead furnace is an emergency. Heating service covers gas furnaces and heating systems of all kinds, repaired with safety first and replaced only when it is the honest answer.

  • ✓Heating repair for no-heat calls, strange smells and ignition trouble
  • ✓Safety checks on burners, heat exchangers and ventilation
  • ✓Heating installation sized to the home, not the sales quota
"Test the heat before you need the heat." A fall checkup beats a winter emergency

First Class Heating and AC Repairs is an HVAC contractor based at 1851 S Victory Blvd in Glendale, California. The name is the promise: first-class work on the systems that keep a home livable.
