Our story

A parlor worthy of its name

The Kings Parlor Barbershop brings old-school barbershop craft to Broadway in Fountain Hill. The idea is simple: treat every client like royalty, take the time each cut deserves, and never let anyone leave the chair less than sharp.

I

Craft first

Every cut starts with a consultation and ends only when the details are right. No assembly line, ever.

II

Everyone welcome

From a first haircut to a weekly regular, clients of all ages get the same royal treatment.

III

Community rooted

A neighborhood shop on Broadway, proud to serve Fountain Hill, Bethlehem and the Lehigh Valley.
