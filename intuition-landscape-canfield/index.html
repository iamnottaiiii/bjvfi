Why groundwork matters

Every solid project starts below the surface

Drainage, grading and excavation are the parts of a build you never see again, which is exactly why they have to be done properly. Intuition Landscape Inc. focuses on the earthwork side of the job: moving dirt, shaping grade, cutting trenches and preparing sites so the rest of your project can move forward with confidence.

01

Site grading and shaping

Resloping yards, swales and building pads so water flows away from structures instead of toward them. Proper grade is the cheapest insurance a property can have.

02

Foundation and footing digs

Excavation for new foundations, additions and outbuildings, dug to spec and ready for the forms crew when they arrive.

03

Trenching and utilities

Clean trenches for water lines, electrical conduit and drainage tile, backfilled and compacted so the ground stays put.

04

Land clearing and driveways

Brush and tree clearing, stump removal, and gravel driveway builds and repairs for rural properties around Canfield.

What we do

Excavation services

Every project starts with a site visit and a straight conversation about scope and cost. Call (905) 708-3443 to talk through what you need.

Site grading and resloping

We reshape the ground around your home or building so surface water drains away from foundations. Includes swale cutting, topsoil stripping and respread, and final grade checks.

  • Yard resloping for drainage
  • Building pad preparation
  • Swales and ditches
Sample pricing: most residential grading jobs fall in the low thousands depending on size. Call for a firm quote.

Foundation and footing excavation

Digs for new homes, additions, garages and shops. We work from your plans, dig to the specified depth and elevation, and leave a clean, safe excavation ready for the next trade.

  • Full foundation digs
  • Footing and pier excavation
  • Backfill and compaction
Sample pricing: quoted per project from plans. Call for a firm quote.

Trenching for utilities

Narrow, clean trenches for water service, hydro conduit, gas lines and septic laterals, with careful backfill and compaction to prevent settling.

  • Water and sewer service trenches
  • Electrical and communications conduit
  • Septic system excavation support
Sample pricing: typically quoted per linear foot. Call for a firm quote.

Drainage and weeping tile

French drains, weeping tile around foundations, and outlet grading to move water away from where it causes trouble. A wet basement often starts with a grading problem we can fix from outside.

  • French drains
  • Foundation weeping tile
  • Culvert and ditch work
Sample pricing: drainage projects vary widely by length and depth. Call for a firm quote.

Land clearing

Brush, small trees and stumps cleared for new builds, paddocks, trails or expanded yards. Material can be piled, chipped or hauled depending on your preference and local rules.

  • Brush and tree clearing
  • Stump removal
  • Trail and paddock clearing
Sample pricing: depends on acreage and density. Call for a firm quote.

Gravel driveways

New gravel lane construction and resurfacing of tired driveways, with proper crown and drainage so they hold up through freeze and thaw.

  • New driveway construction
  • Resurfacing and regrading
  • Culvert installation
Sample pricing: based on length, width and material. Call for a firm quote.
