About

A family shop
on Hastings St

Mendon Barber Shop is a family barbershop at 14 Hastings St in Mendon, Massachusetts, specializing in men's and children's haircuts. It is the kind of small, personal shop where the barbers know your name and your usual cut.

With just two chairs, the shop stays quick and personal. Reviewers say wait times are usually very short, and that the barbers truly listen, delivering on haircuts, trims, and full shaves. One regular of three years calls it the best barber shop in the area, with a perfect cut every time.

Customers also praise barber Lynne as personable and friendly, and the shop's straightforward way of doing business: cash only, with an ATM conveniently next door.
