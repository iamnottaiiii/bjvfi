One contractor for the whole project

Remodels stall when trades do not talk to each other. We run the job as one team: scope, schedule, permits coordination and finish work, under a single point of contact.

02 / How we work
Process

From first call to final walkthrough

  • Site visit: we walk the property and listen first
  • Written scope: line-item estimate, no vague allowances
  • Schedule: start date and milestones in writing
  • Build: managed crew, tidy site, weekly updates
  • Walkthrough: punch list closed before final payment

No surprises, just progress

Construction has a trust problem, and we know it. That is why the scope is written down before work starts, the schedule is shared, and changes are priced before they happen, never after.

Our San Fernando shop shares its address with our sister stone fabrication operation, so countertops and custom stonework can stay in-house on the same schedule.

Our story

Filed in California. Rooted in San Fernando.

JNA BUILD & DEVELOPMENT INC. was filed as a California corporation on March 11, 2021, and keeps an active standing with the California Secretary of State. We operate from our shop at 660 Arroyo Street in San Fernando.

The JNA name covers two sides of the same trade: construction and development here, and marble and granite fabrication through our sister operation at the same address and phone number. That pairing means stonework does not have to leave the job or the schedule.

We are a working contractor, not a sales office. When you call, you talk to the people who will be on your site.
