Our Story

About Villages On Heritage Green

The story below comes from published community records for the Villages on Heritage Green in Cincinnati, Ohio.

Community Timeline

  • Around 1987: The Villages on Heritage Green residential community was developed by Great Traditions Land and Development Company, and construction on the first home was completed.
  • 1991: The community was recognized as a premium real estate development by the Home Builder's Association of Greater Cincinnati when it hosted Homearama in the Estates of Heritage Green.

Five Phases

The Villages on Heritage Green is made up of five phases of construction, each with its own name and lifestyle.

Single Family Sections

  • The Estates of Heritage Green
  • The Forest
  • The Glen
  • Heritage Village

182 homes total across these sections.

Managed Maintenance Villages

  • Grand Master's Village, 30 homes
  • Old Carriage Village, 143 homes

Amenities include lawn mowing, fertilization, landscape maintenance, and snow removal. Annual gutter cleaning is listed for Old Carriage Village.

Named Home Designs

Community records describe home designs offered and built by Mercurio Homes, named for Cincinnati area places: The Amberly, The Clifton, The Clifton D, The Glendale, The Hyde Park, The Indian Hill, The Kenwood, The Madeira, The Mariemont, The Montgomery, The Mount Lookout, and The Wyoming.
