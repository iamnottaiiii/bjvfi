About

A neighborhood salon
that takes its time

S & F Miracle Beauty Salon sits on Atlantic Ave in Cypress Hills, Brooklyn, where regulars come for the craft and stay for the warmth.

The salon

Focused attention, every appointment

This is a salon that runs on appointments, not crowds. That means your stylist's full attention is on your hair: the cut you actually asked for, curls styled with technique instead of heavy product, color that suits you.

The team is known for Dominican hair expertise, blowouts, roller sets, relaxers and silk presses, alongside a full menu of color, keratin smoothing, weaves, braids and perms.

Reviewers often mention stylist Sofia by name, praising her patience and precision, and they describe the whole team as kind, professional and welcoming.

Good to know

Appointments, no long waits

The salon operates by appointment so there are no long wait times. Same-day appointments are often available, including for Brazilian blowouts. Walk-ins are welcome when the schedule allows. Closed on Sundays.
