Our story

Thomas Laurence Salon describes itself as making Rochester beautiful for over 23 years from its home in the heart of the city. The room runs on loyalty: reviewers report stylists like Arlie cutting their hair for twenty years, Philip earning "no one else is allowed to touch my hair" devotion, and Kasha becoming the answer for hard-to-cut Asian hair. Affordable, unpretentious, and consistently kind, it is the neighborhood salon people recommend to anyone.
