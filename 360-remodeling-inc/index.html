Our Story

Built around the whole house, starting outside

360 Remodeling, Inc. set out with a simple objective: provide full-service remodeling and construction, with a specialty in the home exterior. From the Burbank office on West Magnolia Boulevard, the company serves Los Angeles, Orange, Ventura, Riverside, and San Bernardino counties.

The name says it all. Instead of hiring a separate roofer, window crew, painter, and kitchen contractor, homeowners get one team that plans the project end to end: in-home consultation, scheduled installation, on-time completion, and a final walk-through and inspection.
