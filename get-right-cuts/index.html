About

A slanted manifesto for sharp hair

Get Right Cuts sits at 7365 Brookcrest Dr in the Roselawn Center, Cincinnati, Ohio. This is a working neighborhood shop, not a salon with attitude. The mission is simple: everybody who sits in the chair leaves with the cut they asked for.

Public listings describe Get Right Cuts as a women owned barber shop and hair salon serving men, women, and kids, with walk ins welcome. Directory listings show a 4.6 star rating across dozens of reviews, and long evening hours six days a week so working people can actually get a cut after work.
