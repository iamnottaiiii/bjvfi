Our story

A handyman built around one promise

Handyman Same Day started with a simple observation: the hardest part of getting something fixed is not the repair, it is the wait. Appointments booked weeks out, no-shows, and jobs too small for big contractors to care about.

So we built the business around speed and reliability. Based at 725 River Rd in Edgewater, NJ, we serve homeowners across the Hudson riverfront and nearby Bergen county towns with same day and next day visits whenever the schedule allows.

Customers rate us 4.7 stars across 28 reviews, and the theme that keeps coming up is simple: we show up, we fix it, and we leave the place clean.

No invented slogans about decades of history - just a local crew that treats small jobs with the same care as big ones.
