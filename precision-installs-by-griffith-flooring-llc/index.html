Our Story

A small local crew with high standards

Precision Installs by Griffith Flooring, LLC is a Kentucky limited liability company based in Campbell County, in active good standing since 2023. We are a small team, which means the people who quote your job are the people who do your job.

Precision is the point

It is in our name because it is our standard. Straight lines, tight seams, level surfaces, and transitions you barely notice. Reviewers call out our attention to detail again and again.

Your home, respected

We show up on time, protect your space, and communicate clearly from the first visit to the final walkthrough. When a concern comes up, we address it, not dodge it.

Done in one visit chain

Furniture moved, old flooring removed, new floors installed, everything cleaned. You come home to finished floors, not a project.
