Our story

Edgemere's everyday laundry

Micky's Laundromat is a neighborhood self-service laundromat on N Snyder Ave, serving Edgemere and the Sparrows Point area with an inexpensive, no-frills way to do the wash.

About us

A straightforward place
to get it done

About 50 washers and dryers, machines that accept cards and mobile payments, and hours that stretch from 5 AM to midnight. That is the whole idea: a clean, safe-feeling, well-equipped space where laundry day goes quickly.

Neighborhood friendly

Regulars describe friendly staff, a safe feel, and a tidy room of machines. It is the kind of place where people from Edgemere and Sparrows Point get to know the regulars.

Inexpensive by design

Listed at an inexpensive price level, with reasonable machine prices and free folding space. Laundry day should not break the budget.

Modern payment

No hunting for quarters. Credit cards and mobile app payments work right at the machines, so you can start loads the moment you walk in.
