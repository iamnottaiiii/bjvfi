Services

Pool services, start to finish

Choose a tab to see what each service covers. Final scope and pricing are confirmed on site, so every quote fits your pool.

New Pool Construction

A custom gunite pool designed around your yard, your style, and how your family swims.

  • 3D design consultation and layout planning
  • Permitting handled with the city and county
  • Excavation, steel, plumbing, and shotcrete shell
  • Tile, coping, decking, and plaster finishes
  • Spas, Baja shelves, waterfalls, and lighting options
Call for a Construction Quote Pricing is quoted per project after an on site visit. No ballpark numbers without seeing your yard.

Remodels and Resurfacing

Give a tired pool a second life without starting from scratch.

  • Replastering in standard and premium finishes
  • Waterline tile and coping replacement
  • Deck resurfacing and cool deck coatings
  • Shape changes, depth adjustments, and added features
  • Full equipment pad modernization
Call for a Remodel Quote Remodel pricing depends on pool size and finish choices. Call to schedule a look.

Equipment and Repairs

Fast diagnosis and honest repair for the gear that keeps your water moving.

  • Variable speed pump installation and replacement
  • Heater repair and energy efficient upgrades
  • Salt systems, filters, and automation controls
  • Leak detection and plumbing repair
  • LED lighting and electrical troubleshooting
Call for Repair Service Service call fees and repair quotes are confirmed by phone before we roll a truck.

Weekly Pool Service

Consistent, thorough care so your pool is always swim ready.

  • Skimming, brushing, and vacuuming
  • Chemical testing and balancing
  • Basket emptying and equipment checks
  • Filter cleaning on a set schedule
  • Photo reports after every visit on request
Call for Service Plans Monthly plans vary by pool size and equipment. Call for current plan pricing.
