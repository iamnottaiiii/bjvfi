Services

The full rundown.

Every service starts with an inspection and ends with a walkthrough. Pricing is quoted up front before work begins. Call (475) 382-0593 for an estimate.

01

Air Duct Cleaning

Dust, pet dander, and years of buildup collect inside your ductwork and recirculate every time the system runs. Our cleaning clears supply and return ducts, registers, and grilles so your HVAC moves cleaner air with less strain.

  • Full supply and return run cleaning
  • Register and grille detailing
  • System airflow check after service
Most popular
02

Dryer Vent Cleaning

A clogged dryer vent means longer dry times, higher energy bills, and a serious fire hazard. We clean the entire vent path from dryer to exterior cap, including long and hard-to-reach runs.

  • Full-length vent run cleaning
  • Exterior cap and hood inspection
  • Airflow test when finished
Safety pick
03

Chimney Inspection

Our chimney inspector services check the flue, liner, damper, and masonry for creosote, blockages, and damage. Catching problems early keeps your fireplace or stove safe all season.

  • Flue and liner inspection
  • Creosote and blockage check
  • Written findings and recommendations
Seasonal
04

Chimney Sweeping

Regular sweeping removes creosote and soot buildup from regular fireplace use. We protect your hearth and floors, sweep the flue top to bottom, and leave the room clean.

  • Top-to-bottom flue sweeping
  • Firebox and damper cleaning
  • Debris fully removed from your home
Seasonal
05

Fireplace Inspection

Gas and wood fireplaces both need a yearly look. We check burners, glass, vents, and clearances so your fireplace is ready before the first cold night.

  • Gas and wood unit checks
  • Vent and clearance review
  • Season readiness report
