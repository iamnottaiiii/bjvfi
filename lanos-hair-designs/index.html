Our story

A neighborhood salon, through and through

Lanos Hair Designs opened its doors on New Dorp Lane to do one thing well: take care of the people in its neighborhood. No rush, no pressure, just solid hair work and honest conversation.

Regulars come for the consistency. New clients stay for the welcome. Whether it is a quick trim between errands or a full color transformation, the chair at Lanos is a place to relax and leave looking like the best version of yourself.

We serve women, men and kids, and we love being part of the New Dorp community, one great haircut at a time.
