Our story

The neighbor you call when something breaks

JR Home Repair is a Union, Kentucky handyman service built around a simple idea: small jobs deserve the same care as big ones.

Why a handyman, not a contractor

Big contractors book big projects, and the small stuff slips through the cracks. JR exists for everything in between: the repair that takes an hour, the install that has been sitting in a box, the odd job that keeps sliding to next weekend.

Straight talk, fair price

You describe the job, JR gives you a straight quote, and the price you approve is the price you pay. No hourly meter anxiety, no upsells, no mystery line items.

Tidy by default

Drop cloths down before work starts, tools wiped and packed after, and a walkthrough before JR leaves. Your home should look better when the job is done, not just the part that got fixed.
