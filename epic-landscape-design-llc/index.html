A Cincinnati landscape contractor founded on a simple principle: landscaping is an investment that enriches the beauty of your home and the value of your property.

Why Homeowners Choose EPIC

EPIC Landscape and Design was built on professional service and exceptional quality. All services and products are guaranteed, and the team brings over 25 years of design experience to every project. The result is loyal customers who come back project after project.
