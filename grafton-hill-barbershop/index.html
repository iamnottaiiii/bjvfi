Why Grafton Hill

A Shop Built on Second Chances and Sharp Lines

Opened by Worcester native Chase Joinville in October 2015, Grafton Hill Barbershop gave new life to a Hamilton Street storefront that had served the neighborhood as a barbershop and salon for more than 25 years.

Today it is known as a kid-friendly, family-oriented shop where barbers take the time to listen, and where every client leaves looking and feeling their best.

Our Story

A Second Chance on Hamilton Street

Chase Joinville grew up in the Grafton Hill neighborhood and had been cutting hair since he was 18. Opening a shop in the area he grew up in had always been his dream.

In October 2015 that dream became Grafton Hill Barbershop at 120 Hamilton St, giving a second chance both to its new owner and to a storefront that had served the neighborhood as a barbershop and salon for more than 25 years.

His goal from day one: be a positive role model, give back to the community, and make things right by paying it forward, one haircut at a time.

Giving Back

Rooted in Worcester

Every year since opening, the shop has proudly sponsored youth baseball teams at Jack Barry Little League, supporting families and the next generation of the neighborhood.

Clients describe the shop as warm, welcoming, clean, and well-kept, with a family-friendly atmosphere that makes first-time visitors feel right at home.

Walk in for a trim, stay for the conversation. That is the Grafton Hill way.
