About us

A Brooklyn contractor that covers the whole job

Elite Construction USA Inc. is a licensed general contractor based on Schenectady Avenue in Brooklyn. We handle all exterior work, brick and roofing, plus complete interior remodels and renovations. Because licensed plumbers and electricians are part of the operation, plumbing and electrical work stays in house instead of being passed off to strangers.

Past clients point to driveways and sidewalks done great, whole-home renovations finished nicely, and smooth, well-finished work they recommend to others. That is the standard we hold on every job.
