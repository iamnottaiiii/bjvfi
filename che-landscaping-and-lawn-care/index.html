From the yard, for the yard

Notes from a working landscaper in Duarte.

A lawn in Duarte has to earn its keep. Between the foothill heat and the Santa Ana winds, grass here wants steady water, sharp mower blades, and edges that hold their line. That is the kind of routine care Che Landscaping & Lawn Care was built around: the weekly rhythm that keeps a yard looking cared for instead of caught up with.

Most of the work is simple and honest. Mow it even. Edge it clean. Haul away what falls. The difference is in showing up on schedule and treating a front yard like it is the first thing neighbors see, because it is.
