Your pool's surface is the whole pool

Stained, rough, or peeling plaster doesn't just look tired. It makes the water harder to balance, harder to keep clean, and rougher on feet and swimsuits.

As a dedicated pool plastering contractor, Ray's focuses on the part of the pool that touches the water, so your pool feels new again and stays that way.
