About Angel Nail & Spa

Angel Nail & Spa is a family owned nail salon on Ebenezer Road in Nottingham, Maryland. The salon offers manicures, pedicures, acrylic and gel nails, nail art, waxing and threading. Guests describe a clean facility, reasonable prices and technicians who remember their clients, with refreshing drinks offered during longer visits.
