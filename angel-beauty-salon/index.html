Our story

Personal service,
one client at a time

Angel Beauty Salon is the kind of neighborhood shop where the stylist takes her time: cutting, checking, and checking again until the style is right. Reviewers describe meticulous work, friendly conversation, and hair tips worth coming back for.

Some clients have been in the chair for years, and they bring their families with them. The shop is small and cozy, wheelchair accessible, and always welcoming to walk-ins.

  • Walk-ins welcome, no appointment needed
  • Wheelchair accessible, credit cards accepted
  • Street parking and free wi-fi
