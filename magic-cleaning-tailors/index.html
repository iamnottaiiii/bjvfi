What we do

Cleaning & tailoring services.

Everything below is handled in house at our Reading shop. Pricing depends on the garment, call for a quote before you drop off.

Dry Cleaning

Careful cleaning for suits, dresses, coats, and everyday garments. Finished and pressed so clothes come back ready to wear.

Alterations & Tailoring

Hemming, taking in, letting out, and fit adjustments. One customer had a bridesmaid dress hemmed on short notice and it turned out perfectly.

Sewing & Repairs

Seam fixes, re-stitching, and small garment repairs. Simple repairs have been finished while customers waited.

Have something unusual, like a delicate fabric or a rush job? Call (781) 944-3207 and ask first. Pricing depends on the garment.

Our story

A neighborhood cleaner with a tailor inside.

Magic Cleaning & Tailors has served Reading from Haven Street since 2009, combining a full dry cleaning service with an in-house tailor and seamstress.

The idea is simple: the same shop that cleans your suit should be able to make it fit. Customers come back because the work is careful, the prices are reasonable, and the service is personal.
