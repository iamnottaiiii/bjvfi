Our story

FILE: SHOP-01

CLIMAPEX Refrigeration & Air Conditioning is an HVAC contractor based on North Lake Avenue in Pasadena, serving homes and businesses across the San Gabriel Valley.

The name says what the shop does: refrigeration and air conditioning, the two halves of keeping people and product cool. Residential AC, heating, and commercial refrigeration all run through the same ticket book.
