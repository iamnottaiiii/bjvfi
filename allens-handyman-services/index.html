About us

A local handyman for Steelton

Allen’s Handyman Services is a handyman business based at 229 Locust St in Steelton, PA, serving homeowners and landlords across the Harrisburg area.

The to-do list grows whether you look at it or not. That dripping faucet, the cracked drywall, the door that never quite latches. We handle the repair and improvement jobs that are too small for a general contractor but too important to ignore.
