Our story

A Glendale shop
with water on the mind

Aqua World Design is a swimming pool contractor based in Glendale, California, serving homeowners across the greater Los Angeles area.

Who we are

Design first,
details always

Every pool we touch starts the same way: with a careful look at the yard, the light, and how the family plans to use the water. A lap swimmer, a toddler splash zone, and a quiet evening float all ask for different pools.

From there we handle the unglamorous parts that make a pool last: sound structure, honest plumbing, and finishes chosen for the long run, not just the first summer.

And we stay around after the build. A pool is a living system, and we would rather be the team that knows yours than the one you call in a panic.

We design every pool like we will be the ones maintaining it. Because usually, we are.
