Our story

A Nostrand Avenue salon built on Dominican technique.

Mary Salon Dominican Style is a Dominican hair salon at 2030a Nostrand Ave in Brooklyn, serving clients with all hair types, including 4-type hair.

The salon is known for its expertise in Dominican hair techniques: careful relaxers, thorough shampoo and conditioning, and color work including light blonde and bleach results that keep clients returning.

Guests describe attentive staff who address concerns promptly, and regulars say building a relationship with the stylists leads to the best experience.

Whether it is a weekly blowout or a full color transformation, the focus stays the same: healthy hair first, beautiful finish always.
