Our story

A Pacoima roofer built on straight talk.

ARIAS ROOFING CORP works out of 12627 Van Nuys Blvd in Pacoima, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles. The business earned its name one roof at a time: show up on time, do the work right, and leave the place cleaner than you found it.

Reviewers describe a professional, dedicated team, many noting the crew by name, and praise the honest pricing and transparent communication. When other companies suggest full replacements, Arias Roofing is known for pausing, assessing thoroughly, and recommending only what the roof actually needs.

The crew serves a bilingual community, and the business proudly works in both English and Spanish.
