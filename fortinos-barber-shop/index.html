Our story

Since 1989, one street, one craft.

Fortinos Barber Shop opened its doors on South Pacific Avenue in 1989. Thirty seven years later it is still here, still cutting, still the kind of shop where the barbers know your name and your usual.

The shop

A neighborhood institution.

This is a working barbershop, not a salon pretending to be one. Walk-ins are the house rule, the wait is honest, and the work is careful. Barbers like Issac, Flavia, and Will have built loyal followings here by taking pride in every head they touch.

The promise

You leave looking like you.

Reviewers say it best: clean tapers, sharp lines, and a beard cut done right. No rush, no upsell, just a barber who treats your haircut like his reputation depends on it. Because here, it does.
