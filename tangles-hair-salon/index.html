About Donita Green

Donita Bethea-Green is the stylist and owner of Tangles Hair Salon, a sole-proprietor Baltimore salon in business since 2016. Reviewers call her an amazing stylist and haircare specialist, noting her attention to detail, her calm and professional manner, and how she treats every client like her only client.
