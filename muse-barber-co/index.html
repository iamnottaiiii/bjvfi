The Muse approach

Muse Barber Co is a modern grooming shop at 640 Park Ave in Worcester, MA. The shop keeps things personal: one chair, one client at a time, and a barber who listens before the clippers come out.

Reviewers highlight the skilled and meticulous work, the collaborative approach to choosing a style, and a welcoming space with comfortable seating and easy free parking.

Appointments can be booked through the Muse Barber Co app or by phone. Walk ins are welcome when the chair is open, but calling ahead is the surest way in.
