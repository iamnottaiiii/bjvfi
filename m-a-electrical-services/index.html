About

The shop behind the switch

M&A Electrical Services, LLC is a Burlington, Kentucky electrical contractor serving homes and businesses across Northern Kentucky. The company keeps a low profile and a full schedule: most of its work comes from repeat calls and word of mouth.

Public records show a history of commercial electrical work, including meter and service equipment installs supporting telecom sites, alongside everyday residential service. That mix is the point: whether it is a dead outlet in a farmhouse or a new meter on a commercial building, the standard is the same, neat terminations, labeled panels, and no loose ends.

There is no storefront to visit. Call and you reach the people who will actually do the work.
