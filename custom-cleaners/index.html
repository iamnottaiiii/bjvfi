Our Story

More Than a Dry Cleaner

We set out to be the shop Union City can bring anything to. Dry cleaning and laundry are the heart of what we do, but our work extends to on-site alterations, shoe repair and cleaning, embroidery, monograms, logos, and screen printing. We clean and care for wedding gowns, carpets and rugs, and delicate materials like leather, suede, and fur.

When you need a garment fixed, finished, or freshened, bring it to our counter on Jonesboro Road and we will take care of it.
