Our story

A Jacksonville crew
that does what it says

B Construction LLC is a construction company based in South Jacksonville, FL, serving homes across the Southside and surrounding neighborhoods.

How we work

Straight talk,
steady hands

We started B Construction LLC on a simple idea: most people dread hiring a contractor, and they should not have to. So we run every job the way we would want it run in our own home.

That means answering the phone. Showing up when we say we will. Putting the scope and the price in writing. And leaving the place clean at the end of every day, because you still live there while we work.

We focus on residential work, the rooms and repairs that matter most to a household. No job is too small to do right.

What you can expect

  1. Answered calls. You reach a real person who knows your project.
  2. Written estimates. Scope and price, on paper, before work starts.
  3. Kept schedules. Start and finish dates you can plan around.
  4. Clean sites. Daily cleanup, final walkthrough, no loose ends.
