The Story So Far

An Old-Fashioned Shop on the West Side

Del Fair Barber Shop sits at 931 Devils Backbone Rd in Cincinnati, and public reviews paint a consistent picture: a classic, old-fashioned barbershop with a loyal clientele, some of whom reviewers say have been coming since they were kids. Directories list it as a long-established neighborhood shop.

The Vibe

Reviewers describe a laid-back, friendly room full of conversation, where regulars and first-timers alike feel welcome.

The Craft

Conventional haircuts done by experienced barbers. Reviewers call it a shop for classic cuts, not fashion trends, and praise the steady hands.

The Crowd

Dads, kids, and grandfathers. Reviewers highlight kind treatment of elderly customers and patience with little ones.

We could not verify a founding year, owner history, or staff roster from public sources, so we keep the story to what reviewers actually report. Want the full history? Ask the barbers in the chair: that is where the real stories live.

About 169 reviews

Aggregated from public listings (Birdeye and Google). We do not invent reviewer names or quotes: below is a fair summary of the real sentiment.
