About

A local specialist in dental handpieces

Our story

The Dentists Choice has served dental offices in the Pittsburgh area since 1996. What started as a focused handpiece and parts business grew into a trusted B2B supplier for practices that want their tools repaired right the first time.

We are a small, hands-on team. The person who answers the phone knows the shop, and the shop knows your handpieces.

Why offices choose us

Downtime is the enemy of a dental schedule. Our work is built around quick turnaround, quality components, and honest guidance: repair when it makes sense, replace when it does not.

We serve dental practices across Wexford, the North Hills, and the greater Pittsburgh area.
