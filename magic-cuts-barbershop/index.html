Our story

The magic is in the details

Magic Cuts Barbershop earned its name the honest way: one great haircut at a time on Bloomfield Avenue in Verona. Owner Mustafa has become a local favorite, with customers calling him one of the most skilled and consistent barbers in the area.

The shop runs on a simple promise. Take the time to get the cut right, keep the price fair, and treat every client like a regular. Barber Kevin has his own following for careful, unhurried fades, and the whole crew is known for quick, quality service that never feels rushed.

Step in once, and the barbers will remember your cut the next time. That is how regulars are made.

From classic looks to modern styles, beard work to brow cleanups, Magic Cuts is the kind of shop Verona relies on: skilled hands, fair prices, and a chair that is always ready.
