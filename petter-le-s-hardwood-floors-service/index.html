What we do

Hardwood services, priced in plain terms.

Every job is different, so every price is quoted free after we see the floor. Here is how the work typically breaks down.

Hardwood floor installation

New solid or engineered hardwood, from subfloor prep to the final walkthrough. We help you pick the wood, stain and finish that fit the room and your budget.

Free estimate

Sanding and refinishing

Full sanding down to bare wood, then stain and protective finish coats. The classic way to make an old floor look brand new without replacing it.

Free estimate

Floor repair and board replacement

Water damage, pet stains, gouges and squeaky boards. We patch, replace and blend boards so the repair disappears into the rest of the floor.

Free estimate

Staining and color refresh

Change the tone of your floors, from natural light oak to rich walnut. Includes a test board so you approve the exact color before we commit.

Free estimate

Recoating and maintenance finish

For floors that are in good shape but look tired, a fresh topcoat adds shine and protection without the cost of a full refinish.

Free estimate
Final pricing depends on square footage, wood type, and the condition of the existing floor. Call (617) 470-2388 and we will schedule a free in-person estimate. Service areas include Revere and surrounding Boston-area communities.
How a job runs

Simple from first call to final coat.

1

Call and describe the floor

Tell us the rooms, the rough size, and what is wrong or what you want. We will set up a time to come see it.

2

Free in-person estimate

We measure, inspect the wood, and talk through options like refinish versus replace. You get a clear written price before anything starts.

3

The work, kept tidy

We protect your furniture and keep dust to a minimum while we sand, stain and finish. Most refinishes take two to four days.

4

Walkthrough and care tips

We walk the floor with you, and leave you with simple care steps so the finish stays beautiful for years.

Our story

A Revere shop built around one craft.

Hardwood is all we do

Petter Le's Hardwood Floors Service is a flooring contractor based at 230 Malden St in Revere, Massachusetts. We focus on hardwood floors: installing them, refinishing them, and repairing them. That single focus means every job gets the full benefit of tools and techniques tuned for wood, not a little bit of everything.

Homeowners call us when their floors look tired and they want an honest read. Sometimes the answer is a full refinish. Sometimes it is a repair in one room. Either way, you get a straight answer and a price that stays put.
