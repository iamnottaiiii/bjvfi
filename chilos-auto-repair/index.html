Finding a mechanic you trust is hard, and Chilos Auto Repair was built to be the exception. The shop at 285 Mt Pleasant Ave keeps things simple: listen first, diagnose carefully, and explain the fix in words anyone can follow.

Most visits start with something small, an oil change, brakes that squeal, a light on the dash. Those small jobs get the same attention as the big ones, because a car that is maintained on time rarely turns into a car that breaks down.

Good to know: the shop is open Monday through Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM, and closed Sundays. Calling ahead gets you the fastest slot.

Drivers keep coming back for the same reason: the price you approve is the price you pay, and the car comes back road tested and ready. That is the whole business model.

How a visit works

From drop-off to pickup in five straightforward steps.

  1. Tell us what is wrong

    Call or stop by 285 Mt Pleasant Ave and describe the noise, light, or leak. The more detail, the faster the diagnosis.

  2. We inspect and quote

    The car goes on the lift, we find the cause, and you get a clear price before any work begins.

  3. You approve the work

    Nothing happens without your okay. If we find something extra, you hear about it first.

  4. We fix and road test

    Repairs are completed and the car is road tested before we hand back the keys.

  5. You drive away informed

    We explain what was done and what to watch for next, in plain language.
