Rooted in West Genesee Street since 2004

Rejuvenations Day Spa began when its owners transformed a graceful older home at 1735 West Genesee Street into a haven of calm. More than two decades later, it remains deliberately intimate: a place where you are known by name and every service is one-on-one.

As a natural Aveda day spa, the team works with botanical products and rituals, blending skincare expertise with the unhurried pace of a true retreat. Guests come for facials, massage, body treatments, and hand and foot care, and return for the personal attention they cannot find at larger spas.
