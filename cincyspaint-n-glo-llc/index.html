About

A local Cincinnati exterior crew

Cincyspaint-N-GLO LLC is a landscaping business based at 2420 Berthbrook Dr in Cincinnati, Ohio. The name says it plainly: paint, and glow, alongside classic landscape work, for homeowners who want the whole exterior handled by one local team.

Why one crew for paint and glow

Most homeowners juggle a landscaper, a painter, and an electrician for three jobs that all touch the same yard. This business was built around the idea that one local crew can cover the full exterior: the beds and lawn, the paint that frames them, and the lighting that shows it all off at night.
