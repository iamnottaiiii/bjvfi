About

A neighborhood salon
with loyal clients

Unique Beauty Salon sits at 5221 Reisterstown Rd in Baltimore, serving the neighborhood with personal, unhurried hair care. Reviewers describe a salon where clients are well satisfied each visit.

The reviews tell the story: a stylist kept for over 20 years. Careful, patient braiding for a granddaughter. A salon people recommend to everybody. This is a place built on relationships, not walk-in volume.

Whether you need a fresh cut, a new color, or protective braiding done with patience, you will be listened to first and styled second.
