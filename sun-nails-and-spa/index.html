Our Story

A sunny little salon on Abbott Rd

Sun Nails and Spa has been serving Buffalo's south side for years, building a loyal base of regulars who keep coming back for the prices, the short waits, and the friendly faces.

Longtime clients describe the team as friendly, attentive, and talented. Many reviewers say they found their go-to nail tech here and would never leave with a complaint.

Whether it is your first acrylic set, a relaxing pedicure, or a full glam lash appointment, the goal is simple: you leave happier than you arrived.
