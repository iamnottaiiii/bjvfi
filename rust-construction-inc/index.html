Our Story

A Working Contractor on Decoursey Pike

Rust Construction Inc is based in Ryland Heights, Kentucky, a small city in Kenton County just south of Covington. We are a working general contractor, not a sales office, and we take on the jobs other companies walk away from.

Every job starts the same way: we come out, look at the work, and talk straight about what it takes. No pressure, no inflated bids, no disappearing after the deposit.

We handle the trades that matter most in a home remodel: kitchens and baths, concrete, and the general contracting that ties a whole project together.
