Our story

A small business with
a simple promise

Precise service, fair prices, and a job done right. That is the whole business model.

Clean Sweep of Hartford started with a simple observation: most people do not need a big franchise to clear out a garage. They need someone reliable who shows up, works carefully, and charges a fair price.

So that is what we built. A small, detail oriented crew based on Broadview Terrace in Hartford, serving homeowners, renters, landlords, and small businesses across the city. We keep overhead low and standards high, which is how we keep prices flexible enough to fit whatever items you need handled.

Every job gets walked through with you first, priced before we start, and cleaned up after we finish. No surprises, no fine print. Just a clean sweep.
