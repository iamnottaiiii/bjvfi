Our story

A Pasadena crew that sweats the prep

SolidWave Painting Co. is a painting company based at 1375 Tropical Ave in Pasadena, California, serving homes and businesses across the San Gabriel Valley. The name says it all: steady, reliable work, wave after wave of clean finishes.

Ask any painter where jobs go wrong and they will tell you the same thing: the prep. We built our process around that truth. Surfaces get cleaned, repaired, caulked, and primed before a single finish coat goes on, because a paint job is only as good as what is underneath it.

When the job is done, we do a final walkthrough with you, touch up anything that is not perfect, and leave the space clean. No mystery charges, no leftover mess, no chasing us for callbacks.

What we believe

The rules on every job

Prep is the job

Anyone can roll paint. We spend the time on cleaning, patching, sanding, and priming, because that is what makes a finish last.

Your home stays yours

Floors covered, furniture protected, walkways kept clear. You should be able to live your life while we work.

Clear numbers, in writing

Every estimate spells out the scope, the materials, and the price before work begins. No surprises mid-project.

We finish like guests

Final walkthrough, touch-ups on the spot, and a full cleanup. We do not leave until you would invite us back.
