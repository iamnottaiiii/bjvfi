The cover story

A neighborhood salon with a specialty guests swear by

On New Utrecht Avenue, Magic Mirror's Beauty Salon has become a go-to for one thing above all: brows. Guests keep returning for careful shaping, threading and waxing, and for brow and lash tinting that reviewers call excellent.

It is an honest, unpretentious place: kind people, fair prices, and work that speaks for itself. Reviewers mention electrolysis, makeup and bridal makeup too, all delivered with the same straightforward care.

What reviewers keep saying about Magic Mirror's, in one line. Here is the longer version.

A beauty salon the neighborhood trusts

Magic Mirror's Beauty Salon sits on New Utrecht Avenue in Brooklyn's Borough Park area, and it has earned its reputation the old-fashioned way: one set of brows at a time.

Guests describe the staff as nice and kind, the prices as fair, and the brow and lash work as genuinely excellent. It is the kind of place where regulars return for the same service for years, because the results are consistent.

From everyday brow shaping to bridal makeup, the salon keeps things straightforward: come in, get great work done, leave feeling like the best version of yourself.
