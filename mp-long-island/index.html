Services

Pest control and termite treatment

Core services for Long Island homes and businesses. Call for a full list of covered pests and a free phone estimate.

Ongoing protection against the most common household pests: ants, cockroaches, spiders, silverfish, and pantry pests. Inspections identify entry points so treatment stops the cycle, not just the current sightings.
Termites can damage a home silently for years. We inspect for mud tubes, frass, and wood damage, then apply targeted termite treatment and monitoring to protect the structure.
Mice and rats in walls, attics, or basements. We remove active rodents, seal entry points, and set a plan that keeps them out as weather turns cold.
Bed bugs need a thorough, room-by-room approach. We confirm the infestation, treat affected areas, and schedule follow-ups to make sure they are gone.
Wasps, hornets, and yellow jackets around eaves, decks, and yards. Safe nest removal so your outdoor space is usable again.
Restaurants, offices, and retail spaces in Hempstead and beyond. Discreet service, documentation for inspections, and scheduled visits that fit your hours.
