Repair and leak fixes

Most leaks do not need a new roof. They need someone who will climb up, find the true entry point, and prove it before quoting a fix.

  • Leak detection with hose testing and photo documentation
  • Flashing repair around chimneys, skylights and walls
  • Valley and cricket rebuilds where water collects
  • Storm and wind damage repair
  • Tile, shingle and flat roof patching

If a full replacement is not needed, we will tell you that. Honest diagnoses are one of our most mentioned review themes.

Our story

A Pasadena crew with roofing in its blood.

Stay Dry Roofing Company describes itself as a third-generation roofing outfit with more than 25 years of experience, based right here on East Washington Boulevard in Pasadena.

That local footing shapes how the work gets done. Customers deal directly with the people doing the work, not a call center. Estimates are explained on the roof, in plain language, with photos of what was found.

No upsell to a full replacement you do not need. Just the fix, done right.
