Our Story

A Dominican salon with a neighborhood heart, on Sand Lane in Staten Island. Here is what Coco's is about.

Rooted in Dominican hair culture

Dominican salons built their name on one promise: hair that looks healthy, moves beautifully, and lasts. Coco's brings that tradition to Staten Island, with the wash, set, and blow-dry rituals perfected over generations.

It starts at the shampoo bowl, with a real wash and scalp massage, not a rush job. Then the set: rollers or round brush, tension and heat applied with care, finished with oils and serums for that lit-from-within shine.

Photo placeholder

A spa, not just a salon

The "Beauty Spa" in our name is a promise. Hair, skin, and makeup in a single visit, so you can walk in for a blowout and walk out fully ready for your event. Guests mention the clean space, the fresh equipment, and a team that treats regulars like family.

"Come for the blowout. Stay for everything else."
