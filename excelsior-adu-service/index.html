About Excelsior ADU Service

DUARTE, CA

A local builder focused on one thing: ADUs

Excelsior ADU Service is a construction company based in Duarte, California, specializing in accessory dwelling units. Rather than splitting attention across every kind of remodel, the work centers on the full ADU process: checking what your lot allows, drawing permit-ready plans, getting approvals, and building the unit.

California law now requires cities to allow ADUs on most single-family lots, which has made backyard cottages, garage conversions, and in-law suites one of the most practical ways for homeowners to add space, house family, or earn rental income. Excelsior ADU Service exists to guide Duarte and San Gabriel Valley homeowners through that process from first sketch to final walkthrough.
