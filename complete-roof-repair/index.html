Our Story

Complete Roof Repair started with a simple observation: most roof problems are small when they are found, and expensive when they are not. So we built a roofing company around finding them early and fixing them right.

From our office on Foothill Boulevard in La Cañada Flintridge, we serve homeowners across the foothills who want a roofer who shows up, tells the truth about the roof, and leaves the property cleaner than we found it.

We do not push replacements you do not need. If a repair will honestly give your roof five more good years, we will tell you that, and fix it for the price of a repair.
