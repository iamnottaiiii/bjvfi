Chapter One

How your visit goes

Four steps between you and a fresh look.

Step 1

Walk in or call ahead

Drop by the shop on Bay 16th Street or ring (347) 873-0415 to plan your visit. Walk-ins are welcome.

Step 2

Talk it through

Bring a photo or just an idea. Your stylist or barber listens first, then recommends the cut, color or fade that fits your hair and your face.

Step 3

The cut, color or shave

Precision work in the chair: haircuts, fades, beard detailing, color and styling, all finished with care.

Step 4

Finish and style

A clean finish, styling tips to take home, and a look that turns heads on the walk back down Bay 16th.
