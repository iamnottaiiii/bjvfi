The Shop Behind the Wrench

Bryson's Auto Repair and Performance is a full service auto and light truck repair facility at 400 W Pike St in Morrow, Ohio. The shop services, repairs, and modifies antique, classic, muscle, and performance cars alongside everyday drivers.

The team brings thirty years of experience on Toyota and Lexus vehicles, factory trained, plus more than thirty five years working on antique and classic autos. Fast, friendly service with competitive pricing is the house standard.
