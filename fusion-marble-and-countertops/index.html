Stone we work with

From classic marble to hardworking granite, every slab is measured, cut, and finished to fit your space exactly. Ask us about the full range of natural and engineered stone options during your free estimate.

Marble

Timeless veining and a cool, luxurious surface, a favorite for kitchens and baths.

Granite

Exceptionally hard and heat friendly, with bold natural patterns in every slab.

Quartz

Consistent color, low maintenance, and a clean modern look that lasts.

Quartzite

Natural stone with marble like movement and granite like toughness.

Porcelain

Slim, sleek, and stain resistant, a sharp choice for contemporary spaces.

Our story

The countertop experts

Fusion Marble and Countertops is a North Hills stone shop built around one idea: countertops done right, from the first measurement to the final polish.

We specialize in kitchen and bathroom remodels, along with commercial and residential fabrication design. After 17 years of quality experience, we have seen what makes stonework last, and we bring that judgment to every job, large or small.

When you call, you talk to people who cut stone every day. We will help you pick the right material for your budget and your life, then fabricate and install it with care.
