Our story

Drawn from the details up

5-99 Engineering is a civil engineering company based at 1240 Sagemont Pl in Altadena, California. The firm was started on a conviction that small and mid size projects deserve the same engineering rigor as the big ones: careful calculations, clean drawings, and honest answers about what a site can support.

The name reflects the mindset. Engineering is not about the ninety percent that is straightforward; it is about the five to ninety nine percent of edge cases, constraints, and agency comments that decide whether a project gets built. That is where our attention goes.

Today we serve property owners, developers, and builders who need civil design they can submit with confidence. When the plan checker asks questions, we answer them. When the site throws a surprise, we redesign around it. Call (310) 991-4831 to talk through your project.
