Our story

A contractor that shows up

Griffin Services LLC is a Cincinnati concrete contractor that built its name on water problems nobody else fixed. Based on Sutton Road on the East Side, we work across Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana on both residential and commercial jobs.

Our reputation comes from a simple pattern customers keep describing: we drive out to look, we send a clear written estimate fast, and we finish in the time frame we promised. One reviewer called us straight shooters after we fixed a warehouse flooding problem a previous contractor created. Another said years of drainage trouble ended with a foundation seal and French drain delivered exactly as quoted.

We specialize in the overlap most contractors avoid: concrete work and storm drainage together. That means the slab, the slope, and the drain are designed as one system, not three separate guesses.
