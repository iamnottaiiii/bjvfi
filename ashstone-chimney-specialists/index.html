Fireplace safety basics

Simple habits that keep your home safer through burning season.

1

Sweep once a year. Annual cleaning is the standard recommendation for wood-burning fireplaces.

2

Burn seasoned wood only. Dry, seasoned hardwood makes less creosote than green wood.

3

Keep the damper open. Open it fully before lighting, and leave it open until the fire is fully out.

4

Use a screen. A mesh screen or glass doors keep sparks and embers inside the firebox.

5

Clear the area. Keep furniture, rugs, and decor at least three feet from the hearth.

6

Check detectors. Working smoke and carbon monoxide detectors belong on every level of the home.
