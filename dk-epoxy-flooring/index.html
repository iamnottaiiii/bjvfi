01 · WHY EPOXY

One pour. Years of good looks.

02 · PROCESS

Prep is the whole job.

Most epoxy failures trace back to bad prep. Here is how a proper install goes, start to finish.

01

Grind and profile

The concrete is diamond-ground so the coating can bite in, not just sit on top.

02

Repair cracks

Cracks, pits, and spalls are filled and smoothed before a drop of epoxy goes down.

03

Build the system

Primer, color coat, flake or metallic layer, then a clear protective topcoat.

04

Cure and walk

Fast-cure topcoats mean most garages are back in service within a day or two.

HOW WE WORK

Coatings are only as good as the prep

Anyone can roll color onto concrete. The difference is in the hours nobody sees: grinding the slab, chasing cracks, testing for moisture, and building the system coat by coat. DK treats prep as the job and the gloss as the reward.

  • Diamond-grind surface prep on every job
  • Crack and pit repair before coating
  • Clear topcoats for UV and chemical resistance
