About

Your neighborhood electricians.

Miller Brothers Electrical Contractor is based at 340 W Baltimore Ave in Media, Pennsylvania, serving homes and small businesses across Delaware County. We are the kind of electrical contractor that answers the phone, shows up when promised, and leaves the work area cleaner than we found it.

Our work covers the full range of residential and commercial electrical needs: panel upgrades, lighting design and installation, EV chargers, troubleshooting, rewiring, and code compliance. Every job gets a written price before work starts and a walkthrough when it is done.

Media's housing stock runs from 19th-century twins to new construction, and we know how to work with all of it: old wiring brought safely up to code, modern panels in tight basements, and lighting that suits the character of the house.
