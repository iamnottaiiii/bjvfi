Services

Electrical services for Staten Island homes

Services below are based on our listing as a licensed electrician plus typical residential work. Confirm details and pricing on your free estimate call.

  1. Wiring and circuit repair

    Diagnose dead outlets, tripping breakers, flickering lights, and faulty wiring runs.

  2. Lighting installation

    Recessed lighting, chandeliers, sconces, dimmers, and exterior security lighting.

  3. Ceiling fan installation

    New fans and replacements with code-compliant support and balanced, quiet operation.

  4. Panel and service upgrades

    100 to 200 amp upgrades and new dedicated circuits for appliances and EV chargers. Availability varies. Call to confirm.

  5. Home safety inspections

    Whole-home electrical checkups that flag aging panels, ungrounded outlets, and overloaded circuits.

  6. Remodel electrical rough-in

    Wiring for kitchen, bath, and basement remodels, coordinated with your contractor.
