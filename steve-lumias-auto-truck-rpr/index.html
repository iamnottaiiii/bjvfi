Our story

A family shop, run by the family

Steve Lumia's Auto and Truck Repair is the kind of shop Western New York used to be full of: the owner's name is on the sign, the owner is in the shop, and your car gets treated like it matters.

1980s

The shop opens on Transit Road

Steve Lumia starts serving East Amherst drivers from 8433 Transit Rd. Listings show the business serving the community since 1984, and it incorporated as Steve Lumia's Auto and Truck Repair, Inc. in 1987.

Reputation

Honesty becomes the brand

Word spreads the old fashioned way. Steve test drives repaired cars himself and tells customers plainly what needs fixing now and what can wait. Families start coming back for decades.

Today

The next crew carries it on

Recent reviews praise Nick and the current crew for the same fairness and integrity: quality work, reasonable pricing, and jobs finished on time.

How the shop works

You describe it. Bring the car in or call ahead and tell them what you are hearing, feeling, or seeing.

They diagnose it honestly. Real inspection, real answers. If a repair can wait, they will tell you so.

You approve it. Fair pricing quoted up front. No work happens without your say so.

You drive it home. Quality work, finished on time, and the shop stands behind it.

Good to know

Licensed shop serving East Amherst, Clarence, Amherst, and the surrounding towns. Cars, SUVs, and trucks welcome. Appointments recommended, especially for inspections.
