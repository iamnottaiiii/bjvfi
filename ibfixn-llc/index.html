Our story

A straightforward shop on Richmond Highway.

IBFIXN, LLC is an auto repair shop serving the Fort Belvoir and Richmond Highway community in Virginia. It is the kind of shop the corridor needs: skilled technicians, honest assessments, and work that holds up.
