About

A Richwood stone yard

Stone Center of Richwood Inc. has operated on Dixie Highway (US-25) in Walton, Kentucky since 2007, nearly two decades as a natural stone supplier. Public business records list Mr. Pete Petroze in management. It is a working yard, not a boutique: small crew, big inventory, and prices customers call fair.
