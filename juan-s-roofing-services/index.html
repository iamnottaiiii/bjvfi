Our story

Sun Valley Born, Roof by Roof

Sample photo

Juan's Roofing Services started the way most good trades do: one roofer, one truck, and a reputation earned one neighbor at a time. Based at 7653 Sancola Ave in Sun Valley, we have spent our years on the roofs of the San Fernando Valley, learning exactly how the California sun, Santa Ana winds, and winter rains wear a roof down.

We are a small, local crew, and we like it that way. When you call (818) 861-4122, you talk to the people who will actually be on your roof. We answer our own phone, show up when we say we will, and treat your home like it is our own.
