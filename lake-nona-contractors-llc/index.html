About

A Lake Nona contractor,
rooted in Orlando

Lake Nona Contractors Llc has been an active Florida company since 2011, serving homeowners and businesses across Lake Nona and greater Orlando.

Our story

Local roots,
professional standards

We started in 2011, when Lake Nona was still mostly pasture and promise. Since then we have remodeled kitchens, built additions and fit out commercial spaces across the medical city corridor and greater Orlando.

We stay small on purpose. Fewer jobs at once means the owner is on your site, your calls get answered, and the work gets done right the first time.

2011

Founded in Orlando

Lake Nona Contractors Llc is established as a Florida limited liability company, working residential remodels in Central Florida.

Growth

Kitchens, baths, additions

The company grows into full general contracting: permitted additions, whole-home renovations and managed trade crews.
