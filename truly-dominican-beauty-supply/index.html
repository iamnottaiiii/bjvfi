Our story

Canarsie's Dominican
hair home

Truly Dominican Beauty Supply is a Dominican hair salon and beauty supply on Avenue L, run by Juana and her team with warmth, honesty and care.

The salon

An owner clients trust with their hair

Clients describe Juana as amazing, caring, warm and easy to talk to. She takes her time, does not over cut, and makes suggestions without pushing.

That trust shows in the reviews: clients whose hair grew back stronger, who call the team the best in Brooklyn, and who praise fair prices and a humble, relaxing environment.

The salon doubles as a beauty supply, so the products that keep your hair looking great between visits are right there on the shelf.
