On the job in JessupLicensed crew, clean sites

Luvawe Home Improvement LLC is a general contracting company based in Jessup, Maryland. We serve homeowners across Howard, Anne Arundel, and surrounding counties with remodeling and renovation work built to last.

We hold an active Home Improvement license from the Maryland Home Improvement Commission (License #157106). That license means we meet the state's requirements for experience, insurance, and consumer protection, including coverage under Maryland's Home Improvement Guaranty Fund.

Being local and licensed is not just paperwork to us. It means we answer our own phone, we show up when we say we will, and we stand behind the work after the final payment clears.

How We Work

1. Walkthrough

We visit your home, listen to what you want, measure the space, and talk through what is realistic for your budget.

2. Written Quote

You get a clear, itemized estimate in writing. No vague allowances, no surprise add-ons halfway through.

3. The Build

We keep the site clean, protect the rest of your home, and communicate daily so you always know what is happening.

4. Final Walkthrough

We walk every detail with you before the job is done. If something is not right, we fix it.
