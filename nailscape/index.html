Our story

NAILSCAPE opened in 2016 with a simple mission: high quality nail care and customer service. The Monroe Avenue salon has grown on word of mouth ever since. Reviewers describe a quiet spot with friendly staff, technicians who stay late to finish a set perfectly, and pedicures that beat the expensive places. The room does not smell heavily of chemicals, the colors number in the hundreds, and the team never rushes you out of the chair.
