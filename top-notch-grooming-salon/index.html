What we do

Top Notch Grooming Salon in Reisterstown has served local pet owners since 2007. Owner Cathy trained at the Baltimore School of Dog Grooming and continues her education through seminars and classes, bringing hospital and kennel experience to every appointment.

Why book with us

The salon works one pet at a time for a quieter, low-stress experience, and uses gentle, skin-safe products. Mobile grooming to your driveway is also available.
