Who we are

Small team, serious license

SCHI Solutions Inc was licensed as a California General Building contractor in December 2021 and has been pulling permits and delivering projects ever since, 18 building permits since 2022.

The portfolio runs from whole-home renovations to specialized room updates, with an emphasis on efficient use of space, practical material selections, and modern interior finishes that hold up.

We work with homeowners planning their forever home and property managers who need reliable, documented, code-compliant work across their units.
