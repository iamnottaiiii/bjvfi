Our story

A Bay Parkway neighborhood staple

J&R Hair Salon has served Bensonhurst from 6918 Bay Pkwy for years, building a loyal base of regulars. One reviewer writes they have been coming for about 10 years and call it their forever favorite place for cut, color and styling.

The formula is simple: listen to what the client wants, deliver it with care, and charge a fair price. Appointments are efficient, often under an hour, and the team is known for friendly, knowledgeable service.
