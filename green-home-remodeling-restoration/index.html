Why green remodeling

A home that works with the people in it.

Remodeling is about more than new finishes. When we rebuild a kitchen or restore a room after water damage, we choose low-VOC paints, efficient fixtures and durable materials that make the home healthier and easier to live in for years to come.

Every project starts with a walkthrough and an honest conversation about what your home needs.

We serve Sherman Oaks and nearby Valley neighborhoods with a focus on two things: quality construction and responsible materials. Whether it is a single bathroom or a full home restoration, you get a clear plan, regular communication and a crew that treats your home with care.

Our story

Building better, one home at a time.

Green Home Remodeling and Restoration grew out of a belief that remodeling should leave a home healthier than it found it. Too many projects cut corners on materials and indoor air quality, so we made responsible building the whole point of the business.

Good construction and good conscience do not have to be separate things.

Based in Sherman Oaks, we work across the San Fernando Valley on kitchens, bathrooms, whole-home remodels and restoration after water, fire or mold damage. Every project gets the same care: a clear plan, quality materials, and communication you can count on from the first walkthrough to the final walkthrough.
