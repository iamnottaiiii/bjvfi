Our story

A Whalley Ave institution: decades of careful cleaning in New Haven's Beaver Hills neighborhood.

Since the neighborhood remembers

Trusted since before most closets were built

Directory records put Sylvan Cleaners at 79 years in business on Whalley Ave. Customers say they have been coming since 1987 and 1988, and that the shop only got better when Jane took over.

That is the whole story, really: a neighborhood dry cleaner that learned every regular's name, treated fine clothing like its own, and never gave anyone a reason to go elsewhere.
