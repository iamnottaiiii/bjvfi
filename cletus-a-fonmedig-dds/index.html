About the doctor

Cletus A. Fonmedig, DDS

Dr. Fonmedig is a general dentist in Rosedale, Maryland (NPI 1518129998), licensed in Maryland and active in the NPI registry since June 2008. His practice, Cletus A. Fonmedig, D.D.S., LLC, is located at 1709 Chesaco Ave, Rosedale, MD 21237.

According to the practice's Birdeye profile, Dr. Fonmedig graduated in 1997 with a Dental Surgery degree from the University of Benin in Nigeria, served residencies in Nigeria and at the Catholic Mission General Hospital in Cameroon, spent five years in private practice in Botswana, completed post-graduate study in clinical dentistry at the University of Western Cape College of Dentistry in Cape Town, South Africa, and received his D.D.S. from New York University College of Dentistry in 2008. From 2008 to 2010 he served as Clinical Director for the Aspen Dental Group in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. He has authored seven published papers on clinical dentistry and is a member of the American Dental Association and the Maryland State Dental Association.
