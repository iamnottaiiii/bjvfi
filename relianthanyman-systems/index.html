01 · What we fix

One visit, a whole list crossed off

Save up your to-dos and knock them out together. Below are sample services; describe your list on the call and we will tell you straight whether it is our kind of job.

Minor home repairs

Loose handles, squeaky hinges, sticky doors, cracked trim. The annoying little things, fixed properly.

Drywall patching

Holes, dents, and cracked seams patched, sanded, and textured to blend with the wall around them.

Painting touch-ups
