Our story

A Medford contractor built around the trades that matter

Wall's Construction & Home Services LLC. is based on Fulton Spring Rd in Medford, doing the wall and home work that keeps older New England houses solid.

Why walls

The part of the house you touch every day

Walls are the background of your whole life at home, and when they are cracked, wavy, or water-stained, the whole room feels off. We started Wall's around that simple idea: get the walls right, and everything else gets easier.

Plastering is a real trade, and Medford's housing stock is full of real plaster. We repair it, re-skim it, and match it, instead of tearing out what still has decades of life in it.

Why home services

One contractor for the whole job

A plaster repair often uncovers a bathroom that needs re-tiling, or a kitchen wall that is coming down anyway. Instead of handing you a list of other numbers to call, we handle the surrounding work too: drywall, kitchens, baths, and small concrete jobs.

Fewer contractors, fewer scheduling headaches, one person accountable for the finished room.

Look first, quote clear

We look at the actual job before pricing it, and the price we give is the scope we do.

Respect the house

Floors covered, dust controlled, and the space left clean enough to live in the same day.

Local and reachable

Based in Medford, not three towns and a voicemail away. Call (781) 866-9092 and talk to us directly.
