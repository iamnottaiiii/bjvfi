Our story

A local crew that shows its work.

WeatherLock Roofing Services was started by roofers who were tired of vague estimates and disappearing contractors. The idea was simple: do the inspection on camera, write the quote in plain language, and stand behind every square foot.

  • Every estimate includes photos of your actual roof, not stock images.
  • Repair first when repair is honest. We will tell you when a patch is enough.
  • Daily cleanup and landscaping protection on every job, no exceptions.
  • Final walkthrough on the roof with you, before the final invoice.
