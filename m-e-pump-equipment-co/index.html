About M & E Pump & Equipment Co

Verified facts from public listings and company profiles. No history claims beyond what is documented.

M & E Pump & Equipment Co is a commercial and industrial equipment company based at 6064 Kells Ln in Milford, Ohio. Public company profiles describe a business dedicated to the sales, service, and application of fluid handling equipment, including a full service repair facility and a broad catalog of pumps, heat exchangers, motors, and related equipment.

The company is listed as a stocking distributor of Griswold pumps, with pumps and parts ready for immediate shipment. Business directories report roughly 35 years in business and weekday operating hours of 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
