About

A Dominican salon
with Brooklyn heart

Dominican fantasy Beauty Salon sits on Dumont Ave in East New York, carrying on a beauty tradition that has defined Dominican salons for generations.

The tradition

The Dominican blowout, a ritual of its own

Ask anyone who grew up with Dominican salons: the ritual is sacred. A thorough wash, careful rollers, time under the dryer, and then the blowout, section by section, until the hair moves like water.

It is equal parts technique and patience, and it is what this salon does every day. Clients come in with curls, coils, new growth or damage, and leave with hair that swings.

Beyond the chair, the salon doubles as a beauty stop for the whole routine: brow threading, hair removal and makeup, so you can walk out fully finished for whatever the night holds.
