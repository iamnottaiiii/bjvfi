Our Story

A Neighborhood Salon with Heart

Bella Capelli Salone sits on Main Street in East Aurora, a village known for its arts, its architecture, and its warmth. Our salon was built to match that spirit: a friendly, inviting space where stylists take the time to know you and your hair.

Guests often become regulars for years. They come for precision cuts and luminous color, and they stay for the conversation, the care, and the confidence of walking out looking their best.

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, you will be greeted like a friend and leave feeling renewed.
