Ways to buy

Three doors into a fixer

Foreclosures

Bank owned and auction properties. These move fast and often sell as is, so knowing repair costs before you bid is everything. We help you read the listing, estimate the work, and decide your number.

Short sales

Homes selling for less than the mortgage balance, with lender approval. Slower process, real discounts. We keep the paperwork moving and the expectations honest.

Cosmetic fixers

Dated but solid homes where paint, flooring, and fixtures do the heavy lifting. The lowest risk way to buy sweat equity in the Valley.

How we help

From listing to paint brush

Buying a home that needs work is a different game than buying turnkey. Here is what we do for fixer and foreclosure buyers.

Find the homes

We track foreclosure, short sale, and fixer listings across Granada Hills, Northridge, Porter Ranch, and the wider San Fernando Valley, and alert you when something matches your budget and your tolerance for work.

Size up the work

Before you offer, we walk the property and separate cosmetic work from the expensive kind: roof, foundation, plumbing, electrical. You get an honest read on what the renovation really costs.

Win the deal

Distressed listings attract investors with cash. We help you write a competitive offer, line up financing that works for fixers, and move on the timelines these sellers demand.

Our story

A classified ad with a real office behind it

"Fixers. Foreclosures, Need Work, Bring your Paint Brush and Save 1000's! C-21" started as a listing title with a simple message: homes that need work are homes you can afford. The listing is tied to the CENTURY 21 office at 11011 Balboa Blvd in Granada Hills, a full service brokerage branch serving the San Fernando Valley.

That office, CENTURY 21 Peak, has served Valley buyers and sellers for about 25 years across its branches. The fixer and foreclosure desk carries the same idea the headline always had: skip the bidding wars on perfect homes, and build your equity with a paint brush instead.

If you are handy, patient, or working with a good contractor, the homes everyone else scrolls past might be exactly the ones worth a second look.
