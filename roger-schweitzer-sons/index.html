Our story

A third-generation boiler company

Founded in 1976 by Roger Schweitzer

Roger Schweitzer started the company in 1976 with a simple specialty: commercial and industrial boiler systems. His family carried it forward as a third-generation business serving Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky.

The company earned a reputation as a high-quality, reliable partner to area facilities, known for the installation, maintenance, and replacement of commercial and industrial boilers.

In 2019, Roger Schweitzer and Sons joined the Reedy Industries family, becoming part of Monroe Mechanical, its mechanical services presence in southwestern Ohio and Northern Kentucky.
