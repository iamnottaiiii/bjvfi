Services

Heating and cooling, covered.

Residential HVAC service and installation across Cincinnati. Call for exact pricing on your job, and call to confirm availability of any service listed.

01

AC Installation

Load calculation, right-sized equipment, new line sets and pads. Typical high-efficiency systems rated up to 18 SEER2. SAMPLE

02

AC Repair

Capacitors, contactors, refrigerant leaks, blower motors, and electrical faults diagnosed on the first visit whenever possible.

03

Furnace Installation

Gas furnace replacement with proper venting and combustion-air setup. High-efficiency models up to 96% AFUE. SAMPLE

04

Furnace Repair

No-heat troubleshooting: ignitors, flame sensors, control boards, and gas valves. Common parts stocked on the truck.

05

Maintenance Plans

Twice-yearly tune-ups: spring cooling check and fall heating check, filter changes, and priority scheduling.

06

Indoor Air Quality

Media filters, UV lights, and whole-home purification add-ons for cleaner air through every vent.
