Who we are

Two offices, one standard

Cody Development keeps two Florence locations: 225 Main St, Suite 3, and 7870 Foundation Dr, Suite 4. Being local means the crew is never far, and the reputation is always on the line.

Their own trade profile puts it plainly: 22 years of experience in the remodeling industry, and a promise to handle everything from the dirt to the doorknob. That means concrete and carpentry under the same roof as tile and trim, so a kitchen, a patio, and a punch list can all land with one call.

They work across Northern Kentucky, including Alexandria, Burlington, Butler, Covington, Erlanger, Florence, Hebron, Independence, Newport, Union, Walton, and Warsaw.
