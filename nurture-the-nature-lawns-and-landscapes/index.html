Why nurture the nature

Not just another mow and go

A different quality of lawn care: small, family-run, and built around what is best for your soil, your family, and the local environment.

Rooted in how nature actually works

Healthy lawns start underground. We build programs around soil health first: testing and amending pH, aerating compacted ground, overseeding thin spots, and feeding with organic or hybrid nutrition. The result is grass that stands up to drought, weeds, and Pennsylvania winters on its own strength.

◆ Trained in sustainable land care through Phipps Conservatory programs, and a PA certified pesticide applicator for both synthetic and organic treatments.

Family-run, personal attention

A small business on purpose. The owner is directly involved on site, so every visit gets the same careful eye.

Year-round care

Spring wake-ups, summer health, fall cleanups, and winter storm work. One call covers the whole calendar.

Through the seasons

What your lawn needs, when it needs it

Spring

Cleanups, dethatching, aeration and overseeding, first fertilization, and mulch to wake the beds up right.

Summer

Regular mowing, organic or hybrid fertilization, hedge and shrub trimming, and landscape lighting to enjoy the evenings.

Fall

Leaf removal and fall cleanups, soil amendments, final mow, and prep that sets up a strong spring.

Winter

Storm-downed tree and branch cut-up and removal, plus planning for next season's beds and plantings.

Our approach

Small business,
big principles

We started Nurture the Nature to offer a different kind of lawn care: ethical, modern, and centered on what is sustainable for your yard and your family.

The philosophy

Feed the soil,
the lawn follows

Most lawn companies sell you a spray schedule. We start with the ground under the grass: what your soil is made of, what it is missing, and what it needs to support deep roots. Organic and hybrid programs replace harsh chemical dependence with nutrition the soil can actually use.

As a small family business, we give each client personal attention. The person planning your program is involved on site, so nothing gets lost between the office and the mower.

We are based in the Springdale area and serve surrounding communities in the Allegheny Valley.

Credentials and care

  • Training in sustainable land care through Phipps Conservatory programs
  • PA certified pesticide applicator, for both synthetic and organic treatments
  • Fully licensed and insured, with $2M in coverage (per company profile)
  • Partnered with organic land care programs on sustainable practice
