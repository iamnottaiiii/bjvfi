About the shop

A body shop
that speaks plainly

middle village body works inc. is an independent auto body shop on Metropolitan Avenue in Middle Village, Queens, known for fair estimates and insurance help.

The short version

George, Joe, and
a straight answer

Customers know the shop by its people: George, who walks you through the estimate line by line, and Joe, who helps get the job scheduled and the car back on time.

It is the kind of place where the owner gives a fair price on a bumper estimate in person, where an adjuster visit gets scheduled for you, and where drivers say the car came out looking exactly the way it should.

The shop is registered with New York State as a vehicle safety repair facility, and sits right on Metropolitan Avenue, one of the busiest body-shop corridors in Queens.

  • Free, itemized written estimates before any work
  • Direct help with insurance claims and adjuster visits
  • Cars returned when promised, work explained plainly
  • NYS-registered repair facility on Metropolitan Ave
