Our story

A Long Beach crew with a simple rule

ANS Contracting is a general contractor based at 515 Long Beach Blvd in Long Beach, New York, led by Anthony and his team. The rule is simple: treat every home like it is your own, and go above and beyond on every job.

That approach shows up in the reviews. Homeowners talk about dated bathrooms reborn as modern, hotel quality spaces. They talk about floors that look amazing. They talk about a crew they were glad to have in the house, job after job.

ANS stays deliberately hands on. One crew, one point of contact, and work the team is proud to put its name on. When clients say they recommend ANS to everyone they know, that is the whole business plan working.
