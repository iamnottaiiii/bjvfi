Our Place on 77th St

Our Place Hair salon is a barber shop at 311 77th St in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn, serving the neighborhood with men's haircuts, beard trims and head shaves, plus haircuts and coloring for women.

Reviewers praise the friendly, professional staff and the fair prices, and one reviewer notes Danny has been cutting hair for 28 years. Families keep coming back for years, calling it a good haircut every time.
