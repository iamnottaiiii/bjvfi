The Teitelbaum method

Clean it in summer.
Trust it in winter.

A proper boiler cleaning happens in the spring or summer off season. The boiler is shut down and left to go cold. Then every area of the boiler and combustion chamber is cleaned and vacuumed, the tube assembly is scrubbed with wire brushes, soot is vacuumed out of the boiler, and the breaching, the part of the chimney that connects to the boiler, is cleaned too.

That is the work that gets a building through January without a surprise. Our president Michael Costelloe has explained this seasonal approach in industry coverage of New York building maintenance.

Who we are

Boilers are
what we do

Robert L. Teitelbaum Inc. works on the boilers that heat New York: oil-burning and gas-burning models, steam systems, and commercial heating plants. Our shop is at 177-25 Rockaway Blvd in Jamaica, Queens.

Our president, Michael Costelloe, has spoken in industry coverage about how boilers should be maintained: two kinds of attention, with a major cleaning in the spring or summer off season, and the discipline to shut the boiler down, let it go cold, and clean every part of it properly.

That philosophy shapes every job we take. A boiler is the beating heart of a building in the cold months, and it deserves more than a quick look.

How a proper cleaning goes

1. The boiler is shut down before we arrive, so it goes cold.

2. Workers clean and vacuum all areas of the boiler and combustion chamber.

3. The tube assembly is scrubbed with wire brushes and soot is vacuumed out.

4. The breaching, where the boiler meets the chimney, is cleaned as well.
