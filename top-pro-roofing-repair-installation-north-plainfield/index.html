When to call a roofer

If you notice any of these, get a professional set of eyes on your roof soon.

Water where it should not be

Ceiling stains, attic dampness, or drips after rain usually point to a breach in the roof system.

Shingles looking rough

Curling, cracking, or missing shingles and granules in the gutters are signs the surface is wearing out.

After a big storm

High winds and hail can loosen flashing and lift shingles. A post-storm inspection catches hidden damage early.

Roof is 20 plus years old

Most asphalt shingle roofs reach end of life around two decades. Plan ahead instead of reacting to a failure.
