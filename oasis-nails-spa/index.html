About

A calm corner
of Camillus

OASIS NAILS & SPA is the neighborhood nail salon on Kasson Rd where clean, careful work is the whole point.

Our promise

Meticulous, every time

Clients describe nail artists who are detail-oriented and thorough, who take the time to make sure the work is high quality. Disposable pedicure liners, timed massages for consistency, and a shop that stays spotless.

Clean by habit

A spotless shop and careful hygiene, noticed and mentioned in review after review.

Friendly by nature

Accommodating staff who make first-timers comfortable and regulars feel at home.

Fair by practice

Reasonable prices with discounts and loyalty perks for repeat clients.

Welcoming to all
