ABOUT THE SHOP

JYC Customs Junkyard Customs sits at 9242 West Rd in Cleves, Ohio, where it works as both a motorcycle repair shop and a salvage yard. It is the kind of place built around the lift and the parts pile instead of a showroom floor.

The idea is simple and old school: fix what can be fixed, and when a part costs too much new, pull a good one from a donor bike. That parts sourcing angle is what sets a junkyard customs shop apart from a standard repair bay. Riders keep coming back because the crew is straight with them, knowledgeable, and not chasing a quick buck, at least according to the folks who have left reviews.

The shop is also listed for motorcycles and ATV sales and service, so two wheels or four, if it has a motor and it is acting up, it belongs on their lift.
