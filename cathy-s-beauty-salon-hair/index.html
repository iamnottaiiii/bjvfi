About

Enfield's friendly neighborhood salon

Cathy's Beauty Salon Hair sits at 442 Enfield St in Enfield, Connecticut, where it has served the local community for many years. It is the kind of place where clients are greeted by name and appointments never feel rushed.

The salon focuses on what it does best: careful manicures, relaxing pedicures, classic haircuts, and waxing, all delivered in a clean, professional, and welcoming environment.

Reviewers consistently mention the excellent service, superb quality, and the warm, accommodating way every guest is treated. That reputation is why so many first time visitors become regulars.
