Our Story

Meet the crew behind the current.

Shore Electrical Services is a Millersville based electrical contractor run by owner Mike Denton and a small team of five.

Educate. Protect. Serve.

That is the mission in Mike's own words. Every job starts with education: we explain what is wrong, what it will take to fix it safely, and what it will cost before any work begins. Protection means safe electrical practices on every single job, no shortcuts. And service means we treat you like family, from the first call to the final walkthrough.

Local remodeling companies have used Shore Electrical across multiple projects for over a year, citing fair prices and crews that go above and beyond. Homeowners call us for everything from a single pantry outlet to full panel upgrades, and they call back because we show up, explain everything, and leave the place clean.
