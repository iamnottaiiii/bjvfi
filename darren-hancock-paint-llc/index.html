What we do

Painting services, done properly

A great paint job is 80 percent preparation. Here is how we approach each service, and what is included. Call for hours and scheduling: (856) 262-4670.

Interior Painting

Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, hallways and whole homes. We protect floors and furniture, fill nail holes and cracks, sand smooth, prime bare spots and apply even coats for a crisp, lasting finish.

  • Walls, ceilings, trim, doors and baseboards
  • Low odor, family friendly paint options
  • Careful masking and daily cleanup
  • Free color help on every project
Get an estimate

Exterior Painting

Vinyl and wood siding, trim, shutters, doors, porches and decks. We pressure wash first, scrape and sand failing paint, caulk gaps, spot prime and finish with coatings rated for New Jersey weather.

  • Full prep: wash, scrape, sand, caulk, prime
  • Weather resistant exterior coatings
  • Siding, trim, shutters, doors and porches
  • Landscaping protected during the job
Get an estimate

Cabinet Painting and Refinishing

A factory smooth cabinet finish without the replacement price. Doors and drawer fronts come off, get degreased, sanded and sprayed or brushed in hard wearing enamel for a smooth, washable surface.

  • Degrease, sand, prime and enamel finish
  • New hardware options on request
  • Kitchens, baths and built ins
Get an estimate

Drywall Repair and Prep

Dents, holes, cracks and old water stains fixed right before paint. We patch, tape, texture to match and prime so repairs disappear into the wall.

  • Hole and crack patching
  • Texture matching
  • Stain sealing and priming
  • Included with every interior job
Get an estimate
