About

A Cleves crew that knows the ground

Welsh Excavation Co., Inc. is a general contractor based at 5780 OH-128 in Cleves, Ohio, specializing in excavation, underground utilities, and sitework. That is the description the company puts on itself, and the public record backs it up: bid lists show the company competing on municipal water distribution and sewer work across southwest Ohio.

The company is listed in The Blue Book trade directory for subcontractors, a listing it has held since 2006, covering site development, demolition, underground utilities, excavating contracting, and land clearing across the Cincinnati, Columbus, and Dayton area of southern Ohio.

A public directory review puts it plainly: customers call Welsh Excavation the most reliable contractor they have worked with, noting the crew understands more than the excavation side of development and knows what the finished product should look like.
