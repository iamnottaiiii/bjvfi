Our story

A Buffalo Rd original

Elegant Hair Fashion has been part of the Rochester beauty scene for years, building its name one head of hair at a time.

03 · The studio

Unfussy, skilled,
and genuinely warm

Elegant Hair Fashion is the kind of salon where regulars are remembered and newcomers are put at ease. The focus is simple: hair styling and coloring done well, in a comfortable studio where you can relax and be honest about what you want.

Stylists here are known for taking time with consultations, so the cut or color you leave with matches the picture in your head. It is an approach that has earned steady 4.4 star feedback and clients who return by name, asking for their favorite stylists.

04 · The promise

Your hair,
taken seriously

No rushed chairs, no one-size-fits-all formulas. Whether it is a routine trim, a full color transformation, or an updo for a big day, the team works with your hair type, your lifestyle, and your budget.

Located at 1458 Buffalo Rd in Rochester, the salon is easy to find and easy to reach by phone. Call ahead to talk through what you have in mind, then come in and let the stylists do what they do best.
