Triago Handyman is a Kissimmee-based home repair service built on a simple idea: show up on time, do careful work, and treat every home like your own.

Our way of working

No mystery,
no mess left behind

A good handyman visit should feel easy. You describe what is wrong, we give you a clear estimate, and the work gets done the way it was promised.

We bundle small jobs into single visits so you are not paying for five separate trips. We protect your floors, vacuum up the dust, and haul away the debris before we call it done.

Whether it is one nagging repair or a whole page of them, the standard is the same: careful work, honest pricing, and a home you are happy to walk back into.
