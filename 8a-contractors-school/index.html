Law and Business Exam Prep

Every CSLB applicant takes the 125 question Law and Business exam. This program walks through the topics it tests in plain language.

  • Contract law, project management, and business practices
  • Safety, labor, and workers compensation basics
  • Sample questions in the multiple choice exam format

Trade Exam Prep

The second exam covers your specific trade or certification area, typically 80 to 125 questions. Study by classification.

  • General Building (B) exam preparation
  • Specialty trade classifications, from plumbing to painting
  • Trade math, estimating, and code references

Our story

Built for working tradespeople.

8A CONTRACTORS SCHOOL is a vocational school in Canoga Park, California, focused on one goal: helping experienced tradespeople become licensed California contractors.

Most of our students already know their trade. What they need is a clear path through the CSLB exams, study materials that respect their time, and instructors who speak the language of the jobsite.

Classroom learning, jobsite mindset.
