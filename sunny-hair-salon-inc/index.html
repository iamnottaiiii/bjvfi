Who we are

Small salon,
serious skill

Tucked into 20th Ave in Brooklyn, Sunny Hair Salon Inc has built its name the slow way: one head of hair at a time. Reviewers reach for the same words again and again, warm, clean, and first-class skill.

The salon is listed as both a hair salon and beauty salon, serving a diverse neighborhood clientele with texture work like perms and keratin treatments alongside everyday styling.

Walk-ins are welcome, but weekends fill up. A quick call ahead is the smart move, and we accept credit cards, debit cards and mobile payments.

Clean and comfortable

Reviewers specifically praise the tidy, welcoming space. It is a place you relax in, not just pass through.

Texture specialists

Perms, keratin smoothing and curly hair care are the services directories list first. That is our home turf.
