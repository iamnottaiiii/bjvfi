Nuestra historia

A family, an oven,
and Kennedy Ave

Santa Maria Bakery 1 is a family-run panadería in the heart of Hammond, built on recipes, early mornings, and neighbors who became regulars.

Desde el corazón

Baked like family,
because it is family

Long before sunrise, the ovens are already warm. Trays of conchas, churros and pan dulce move from the bakers' hands to the cooling racks to the display case, every single day.

The team behind the counter is family, and it shows: in the care taken with each tray, in the patience at the register on a busy Saturday, and in the way regulars are greeted by name.

We bake the calendar of our culture, too: Rosca de Reyes in January, sweet breads for Valentine's, and pan de muerto for Día de los Muertos, when families gather and remember. These aren't just products to us; they're the traditions we grew up with.

Panadería Santa Maria se esfuerza día con día para ofrecerle el mejor producto.

The Santa Maria family

Tradition in every tray

From chorreadas with piloncillo to café de olla the way abuela made it, the flavors of home, baked daily.

Neighbors first

When a summer storm knocked out our power, neighbors rallied around us. We handed out free bread while we got back on our feet. That's the kind of street this is.

A growing family

What started on Kennedy Ave has grown. You'll also find our sister shop, Bakery #2, over on Hohman Ave. Same family, same oven-warm promise.

Come taste the story.

We're at 7024 Kennedy Ave, Hammond, open early, every day. Bring the family; leave with a warm bag.
