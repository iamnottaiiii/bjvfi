Services

Painting services
for Philadelphia homes

House painting, interior and exterior. Every job starts with a conversation and a free estimate, so you know the plan and the price up front.

01

Interior Painting

Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, hallways, full interiors. We cover floors and furniture, patch and prime where needed, and leave clean, even coats on every wall and ceiling.

  • Walls and ceilings
  • Trim and baseboards
  • Doors
  • Whole-home repaints
02

Exterior Painting

Siding, trim, shutters, porches, and front doors. We scrape, sand, and prime failing paint before the top coat goes on, because the prep is what makes an exterior finish last.

  • Siding and trim
  • Shutters and doors
  • Porches
  • Weather-ready finishes
03

Color Matching

Bring a swatch, a pillow, or a photo. Customers say Rico can match just about any color sample, keeping touch-ups and repaints consistent with the rest of your home.

  • Swatch matching
  • Touch-up blending
  • Whole-room repaints
04

Rental and Flip Refreshes

Quick, clean repaints between tenants or before a listing goes live. One crew, one schedule, and a unit that photographs well and rents fast.

  • Turnover repaints
  • Neutral color palettes
  • Fast scheduling
