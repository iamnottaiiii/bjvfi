Our Story

A Working Shop on Lankershim

West Coast Custom Sheet Metal is a sheet metal contractor based at 8125 Lankershim Blvd in North Hollywood, California. We are a corporation with a shop team of about a dozen fabricators, and our work centers on one thing: custom metal parts made to measure.

Our daily work covers HVAC ductwork, commercial kitchen exhaust components, flashing and architectural trim, and one-off fabrication for contractors, property managers, and industrial customers across Los Angeles County. We build to drawings, to field measurements, or to a sample part you bring through the door.

Because everything is fabricated in house, we can turn around odd sizes, tight deadlines, and retrofit pieces that off-the-shelf suppliers cannot match. No catalogs, no middlemen, just a shop that knows sheet metal.

Walk-ins are welcome during shop hours. Call ahead at (818) 252-7500 and we will have someone ready to look at your job.
