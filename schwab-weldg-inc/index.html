About the shop

A small shop on Harrison Ave

Schwab Weldg Inc sits at 7046 Harrison Ave on the west side of Cincinnati, Ohio, in the 45247 zip. Directory records date the business to the 1980s, and it has stayed small: a shop of about three people doing railings, sheet metal work, iron work, and general welding repairs.

No big sales floor, no call center. When you call (513) 353-4262 you talk to the shop. Bring a broken part, a measurement, or a sketch, and we will tell you straight what it needs.
