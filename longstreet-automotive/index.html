How a job moves

01 Inspect

Every casting and crank that comes through the door gets measured first. No cutting starts until the numbers say so.

02 Machine

Boring, honing, surfacing, and grinding bring worn parts back inside factory tolerance or to the oversize your build calls for.

03 Verify

Finished work is checked again against spec before it leaves. If it does not measure right, it does not ship.

04 Deliver

Pick up your machined components or arrange delivery with the shop. Call ahead so the job is ready when you are.

Why machine shops matter

An engine is a stack of tolerances. When cylinders wear, heads warp, or cranks score, the fix is not a new part off the shelf, it is a machinist bringing the old part back to spec. That is the trade this shop is listed for.

If you are rebuilding a daily driver, a tow rig, or a weekend project, the machine work is the foundation the whole build stands on. Bring your specs, your parts, and your questions. The shop can tell you what is salvageable and what is scrap.
