Our story

A family shop,
Springfield proud since 1990

For over three decades, Mr. and Mrs. Lee have kept Springfield looking sharp, one garment at a time.

Since 1990

More than a cleaner,
a community staple

The Drycleaners opened its doors in 1990, and it has been owned and managed by Mr. and Mrs. Lee ever since. In an industry of chains and drop-off kiosks, this shop stayed personal.

Customers describe the owners as kind, responsive, and deeply attentive. Many say walking in feels like family, and reviews consistently praise the attention to detail, from a perfectly set hem to a gown rescued before a wedding.

The shop also takes its footprint seriously: eco-friendly cleaning methods and a recycling program keep the process gentler on fabrics, on skin, and on the planet.

Customers call it a neighborhood gem, and say the commute is worth it.
