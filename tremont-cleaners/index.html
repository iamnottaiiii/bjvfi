Our story

A family owned dry cleaner serving Peabody since 1983.

Tremont Cleaners has been a family owned business since 1983, known for expert tailoring and excellent customer service. Generations of Peabody customers have trusted us with wedding dresses, work suits, and everyday favorites.

We believe great garment care is personal: every item inspected, every stain treated with care, every alteration fitted right.
