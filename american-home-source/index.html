Who we are

Our Story

A Glendora builder since 2009

American Home Source, Inc. is a residential construction company based at 223 S Glendora Ave in Glendora, California. The company is led by Daryl W. Overlock and holds a California General Building license.

The focus has always been homes: custom builds, remodels and renovations for single-family properties. Clients describe working with Daryl as a turn-key experience, with help on everything from materials to financing.

Permit records show the company’s work on Glendora homes, including residential bathroom remodels filed with the city building department. Business directory listings note single-family housing construction as the company’s industry since 2009.
