Our Services

Every Roof, Every Repair

Residential roofing across Sun Valley and the San Fernando Valley. Every project starts with a free on-site estimate. Call (818) 764-1020 for pricing.

Full Roof Replacement

Complete tear-off and reroof. We replace rotten wood underlayment, lay triple layers of 40 lb felt on slopes, and finish with the shingle, tile, or rolled roofing you choose.

Leak Detection and Repair

Leaks next to chimneys, around vents, and over garages. We trace water to its real entry point and repair it properly the first time.

Spanish Tile Roofing

Tile reset and replacement on peaked sections, paired with sealed rolled roofing on the flats. A classic Valley look built to last.

Flat and Rolled Roofing

Layered 90 lb granule paper over felt for flat sections, garages, and additions. Sealed to the weather, ready for heavy rain.

Shingle Roofing

Top-of-the-line architectural shingles in light, heat-reflecting colors. One customer reports their shingle roof never moved in 50 mph Santa Ana winds.

Roof Inspections

Honest condition reports before you buy, sell, or repair. We tell you what the roof needs and what it does not.

Our Story

Roofing the Valley Since 1945

United Roofing Company INC. has worked out of Sun Valley, California for generations. Our contractor license number, 137650, is listed as the oldest active roofing license number in the greater Los Angeles area, and we treat that history as a promise: do the job right, or do not do it at all.

We are a working roofer, not a sales operation. The people who give you the estimate are the people who know roofs. Crews are punctual, the proposal is detailed, and when something unexpected turns up under the old roofing, like rotten wood underlayment, we show you, explain it, and fix it with your approval.

That approach is why one homeowner trusted us with three separate projects, why a customer wrote a one-year update saying their new roof held perfect through 50 mph Santa Ana winds, pea-sized hail, and a 120-degree August, and why neighbors and even realtors have knocked on doors to ask who did the roof.

How We Work

The process reviewers describe, step by step.

1. Free detailed estimate

We inspect the roof, photograph problem areas, and write a clear proposal. No pressure, no vague numbers.

2. On-time start

Crews arrive when promised. Materials are staged neatly, and tile is stacked and old roofing cleared before the rebuild begins.

3. Clean finish

Rotten wood gets replaced with your approval, layers go down to spec, and the site is cleaned every evening. You get a roof that looks fantastic on day one and holds up for years.
