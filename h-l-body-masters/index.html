Services

What we do

H L Body Masters in East Orange restores damaged vehicles to factory-fresh condition. Collision repair, paint, and refinishing are handled in-house with careful prep work, because a great finish starts long before the paint goes on.

Our craft

How we work

01

Prep is everything

A flawless finish is decided in the prep bay. Panels are sanded, primed, and sealed before a drop of color goes on.

02

Color, matched

Computerized mixing matches your factory color, then blending extends the repair invisibly into surrounding panels.

03

Built for NJ roads

Repairs are finished to stand up to real driving: car washes, sun, salt, and all.
