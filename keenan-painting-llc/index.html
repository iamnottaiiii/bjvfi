Who we are

Keenan Painting LLC is a painting company based on Ginger Drive in Lumberton Township, New Jersey, serving the 08048 area and surrounding Burlington County communities.

The owner has spent 20 years in the painting industry, and the company operates as a small professional crew known for clean job sites and fast, careful turnarounds. Directory profiles note the business as background-checked, and customers highlight fair estimates and clear communication about paint choices.

If you want a painter who shows up, explains the work, and leaves your home cleaner than expected, that is the bar we aim to clear on every job.

  • Free written estimates with clear pricing
  • Guidance on paint type, sheen and color
  • Careful prep: patching, sanding and masking
  • Furniture, floors and fixtures protected
  • Clean, fast work from a small professional crew
  • Final walkthrough before the job is done
