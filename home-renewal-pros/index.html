Our Story

A Contractor That Respects Your Space

Home Renewal Pros is a Panorama City contractor built around a simple idea: remodeling should improve your life, not interrupt it. The team plans each job so occupied homes stay comfortable and commercial sites stay open while the work happens.

Projects span both commercial and residential work, from single-room remodels to multi-phase new construction. Homeowners mention the same things again and again: professional communication, knowledgeable estimates, and honest, budget friendly pricing.

One recent customer hired Home Renewal Pros after a detailed estimate from Arman, then went on to remodel the bathroom, paint the whole house, and add solar, calling the crew great to work with and very professional.

That is the standard every project gets: clear plans, clean sites, and a final walkthrough you will actually enjoy.
