Our story

A small Burbank crew with one standard

West side hvac inc is a three person, background checked HVAC team based at 7524 N Hollywood Way in Burbank. We have been installing and replacing heating and cooling systems across Burbank and nearby neighborhoods for about four years.

We keep the operation small on purpose. Fewer jobs at a time means the person who quotes your system is the person who installs it, and nothing gets rushed.

Our reputation so far is simple: quality work, on time, at a reliable price. That is the whole business plan.
