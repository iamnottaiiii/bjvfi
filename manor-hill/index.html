About this community

New construction,
without the compromise

Manor Hill is a community of new Drees Homes in Independence, Kentucky, one of Northern Kentucky's fastest-growing family towns. The plans range from right-sized ranches to generous two-stories, and every home can be personalized with structural options and designer finishes.

Community listings highlight everyday advantages: no HOA, an A-minus rated elementary school nearby, and a location that keeps Florence's shopping and dining close while the evenings stay quiet.
