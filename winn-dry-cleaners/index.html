Our story

A neighborhood cleaner since 1997.

Kind service, meticulous care

Winn Dry Cleaners has been part of the Davis Square neighborhood for nearly three decades. Customers know the shop for its kindness, efficient turnaround and meticulous care, with top notch quality on both dry cleaning and alterations. The lovely and affable team is just as attentive to details as their customers are, and prices stay very affordable and fair.

Located right at 22 College Ave, literally in Davis Square, it is a convenient stop for commuters, Tufts students and neighbors alike. The shop also offers a 10% discount for new customers and college students.
