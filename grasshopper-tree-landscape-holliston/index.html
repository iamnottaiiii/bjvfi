Services

Three ways we help your property

Pick a focus area below. Call (508) 429-2482 to talk through what your property needs.

Tree Care

Healthy trees add value and shade to a property. Neglected ones become a risk. We evaluate your trees and do the pruning and removals that keep them sound.

  • Pruning for structure, clearance, and health
  • Removal of dead or hazardous trees
  • Storm cleanup and broken limb removal
  • Advice on planting the right tree in the right spot
Sample

Landscape Design

A good design starts with how you actually use the yard: where the kids play, where you grill, where the sun hits in the evening. We shape beds, plantings, and layout around that.

  • Planting beds and borders planned for the site
  • Foundation and entry plantings
  • Privacy screening with trees and shrubs
  • Drainage and grading considerations
Sample

Maintenance

Designs only stay good with follow through. Seasonal visits keep beds clean, plants shaped, and small problems from turning into expensive ones.

  • Seasonal cleanups, spring and fall
  • Mulching and bed edging
  • Shrub and hedge trimming
  • Ongoing care plans for the property
