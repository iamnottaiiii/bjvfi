Our story

A local shop built on fair dealing

Local Plumbing Specialists started with a simple belief: a plumber should be judged by the quality of the work and the honesty of the price, not by the size of the ad budget.

With over 14 years of experience in the trade, our team has seen every kind of plumbing problem Glendale homes and businesses can throw at us, from 100 year old galvanized lines to brand new tankless installs. That experience is what lets us diagnose accurately, fix it right, and tell you straight when a repair can wait.

We uphold a standard of integrity bound by fairness, honesty, and personal responsibility. Our customers interests come first, and their concerns are the basis of our business.
