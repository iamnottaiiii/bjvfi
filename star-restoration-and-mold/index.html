What to do right now

If you have active water coming in: stop the source if you safely can (a shutoff valve, a bucket under the drip), move valuables out of the way, and call us. Do not touch standing water near electrical outlets.

Then let us take it from there.

Process

How a restoration job goes

A typical job, step by step. Timelines depend on the extent of the damage.

01

You call and describe the damage

Tell us what happened and where. We talk through what to do right now and what comes next.

02

We assess the affected area

An on-site look at the water or mold: how far it spread, what is wet, and what it will take.

03

Water out, area contained

Extraction of standing water, and containment of mold areas so spores do not spread.

04

Dry and remediate

Drying equipment runs, affected materials are removed or treated, and surfaces are cleaned.

05

Verify and hand back

Moisture checks confirm the area is dry, and you get guidance on preventing it from happening again.

Good to know: this is a typical sequence, not a promise of timing or outcome. Insurance questions? Ask on the call: (424) 353-4640.
