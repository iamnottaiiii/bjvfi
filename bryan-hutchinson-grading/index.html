02Our story

A Topanga grading company with a public record

Bryan Hutchinson Grading, also listed as Bryan Hutchinson Grading and Excavation, works out of Topanga Canyon, California. It is the kind of company canyon neighbors call when the ground itself is the project: steep, rocky, and unforgiving of shortcuts.

Public records show the company operating as Hutchinson Grading and Excavation, Inc., registered for trucking work, and trusted with grading services on public conservation land.

Public record: 2011

The Mountains Recreation and Conservation Authority authorized a sole-source contract with Hutchinson Grading and Excavation, Inc. for grading services in Browns Canyon, not to exceed $65,000 (Resolution No. 11-32, March 2, 2011).

Public record: 2018

A federal roadside inspection of a company straight truck at Calabasas Landfill recorded zero violations across driver and vehicle checks.
