Services

What we do, done well.

Every project starts with a free on-site estimate. If it involves stone, brick, pavers, or the yard around them, call us.

Interlocking Paver Installation

Patios, walkways, steps, and driveways in interlocking pavers, with proper excavation, base, and edge restraint.

Brick & Stone Patios

Walls & Edging

Landscape Construction

Walks & Steps

Our work

A local crew with construction in its name.

Nolasco Landscaping and Construction is based in Marblehead and works across the North Shore. The name says it plainly: we do both the landscape and the construction, so patios, walls, and plantings come from one accountable crew.
