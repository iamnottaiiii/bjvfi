01 · WHY CALL

Roof work, handled by a local contractor

From a small leak to a full replacement, it starts with one call. Here is what to expect when you reach out.

STEP 1

Describe the problem

Missing shingles, a stain on the ceiling, an aging roof. Tell us what you are seeing and we will ask the right follow-up questions.

STEP 2

Talk through options

Repair or replace, and what each path involves. We explain the trade-offs in plain language so you can decide with confidence.

STEP 3

Plan the work

Once you choose a direction, we schedule the job and keep you posted on timing, materials, and what to expect on work day.

STEP 4

Done and checked

After the work is finished, we walk through what was done so you know your roof is ready for the next storm.
