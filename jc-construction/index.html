Our Story
Who we are

A local contractor you can actually reach

JC Construction is a general contracting business based in Arleta, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley. We are the crew homeowners call when they want straight answers, honest pricing, and work that holds up.

Built on word of mouth

Most of our work comes from neighbors telling neighbors. That only happens one way: by doing what we said we would do, when we said we would do it, for the price we agreed on.

We keep our operation lean on purpose. You talk to the people doing the work, not a call center. Questions get answered the same day, and if something is not right, we come back and make it right.

  • Local to Arleta and the San Fernando Valley
