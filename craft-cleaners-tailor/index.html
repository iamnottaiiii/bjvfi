Our Story

A Lexington mainstay for decades

Craft Cleaners & tailor has served Lexington from Depot Square for decades, pairing old-fashioned garment care with modern eco-friendly cleaning.

Decades of experience

A family-run shop with roots in Lexington going back generations. We know our neighbors by name, and we treat every garment like it matters, because to you it does.

Eco-friendly by choice

Our cleaning process is designed to be gentle on delicate fabrics and kinder to the environment, while still removing the stains and odors other shops leave behind.

Tailoring on site

True custom tailoring and alterations, done by an experienced tailor. From a simple hem to a full refit, your clothes come back fitting the way they should.
