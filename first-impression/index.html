Our story

A neighborhood favorite

First Impression is a beauty salon at 1871 Albany Avenue in Brooklyn, New York. In New York State appearance enhancement records, the salon is listed under the name Browne Simpson Barbara.

Guests describe it as the place they go when they want to stop thinking about what to do with their hair. They say they have come for many years, always leave satisfied, and get compliments afterward.

The salon works by appointment, keeping visits personal and unhurried. Call ahead and the team will take it from there.
