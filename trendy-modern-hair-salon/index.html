No velvet ropes, no sky-high prices, no rushed chairs. Trendy Modern Hair Salon built its name the old-fashioned way: by doing good hair at prices the neighborhood can actually afford.

Open every single day from 9 to 7, walk-ins welcome. That consistency is why so many Bay Ridge regulars have been coming for years.
