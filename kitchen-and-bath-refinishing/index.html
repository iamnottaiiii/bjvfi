Why refinish

Replace the look, keep the tub

Tearing out a bathtub or tile surround means demolition, plumbing, and weeks of dust. Refinishing bonds a durable new coating to your existing surfaces, so the bathroom looks brand new without the tear out.

  • Most tubs and tile refinished in one day
  • A fraction of full replacement cost
  • No demolition, no plumbing changes
  • Smooth, glossy, easy to clean finish
Get a Free Quote

How it works

We clean and etch the surface, repair chips and cracks, then apply a professional grade coating that cures to a hard, glossy finish.

1 day
