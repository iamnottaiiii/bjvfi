1Services

Everything your attic needs, one contractor.

Tap a service to see what is included. Every scope is confirmed with a free on-site assessment.

Blown-in insulation

Loose-fill fiberglass or cellulose blown evenly across the attic floor to the target depth. Ideal for topping up thin insulation or bringing an older attic up to modern R-values without opening walls.

Batt insulation

Pre-cut fiberglass batts fitted between joists and studs. Best for accessible attics, garage ceilings, and wall cavities during remodels.

Insulation removal and attic cleaning

Old, flattened, water damaged, or rodent contaminated insulation is extracted with sealed equipment, then the deck is cleaned before new material goes in.

Air sealing

Sealing around recessed lights, plumbing and electrical penetrations, top plates, and attic hatches. The highest value upgrade most attics are missing.

Rodent proofing

Entry points sealed with metal mesh and rodent grade sealants so new insulation stays clean and your attic stays protected.
