Our story

Twenty years at the saw

Digital stone LLC is a countertop contractor based on West 144th Street in New York. Our team has spent more than two decades in the stone business, turning raw slabs into finished kitchens and baths.

We keep fabrication in house because the fit is everything. A countertop is only as good as its template, its cut, and its install, and we control all three.

Customers on Thumbtack rate us 4.9 out of 5, most often for work quality, professionalism, and responsiveness.
