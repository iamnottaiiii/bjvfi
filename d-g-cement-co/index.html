About D and G

Family owned since 1967.

D and G Cement Co is a family-owned concrete contractor in Southgate, Michigan, pouring residential and commercial concrete since 1967. They are licensed and insured and do driveways, floors, patios, porches, decorative concrete, brick work and more.

From the first footing to the final broom finish, every pour gets the same old-school attention: proper base, proper mix, proper cure. Call (734) 284-5060 to talk about your project.
