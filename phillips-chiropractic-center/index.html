Services

What chiropractic care addresses

A chiropractor treats problems of the nerves, bones, muscles, ligaments, and tendons. Typical care at a chiropractic center includes:

01

Chiropractic adjustments

Spinal manipulation and adjustment, the foundation of chiropractic treatment for alignment and pain relief.

02

Back pain management

Evaluation and adjustment-based care for low back and mid back pain.

03

Neck pain and stiffness

Targeted cervical care to ease stiffness and improve range of motion.

04

New patient exams

First-visit assessment to understand your history, symptoms, and goals before treatment begins.Sample service

When a referral is the right call
