Why vents and ducts get dirty

  • Every dryer load pushes lint past the trap and into the vent line.
  • Long or bendy vent runs trap lint faster than short straight ones.
  • Ductwork collects dust, pet hair, and renovation debris over the years.
  • Clogged vents make dryers run hotter and longer, raising energy bills.
  • Dirty ducts keep pushing settled dust back into living spaces.
