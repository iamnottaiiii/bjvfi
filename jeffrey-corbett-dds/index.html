Our story

Meet Dr. Jeffrey Corbett.

A North Shore dentist known for looking beyond the tooth, and treating the person.

The dentist

Training that goes deeper

Dr. Corbett graduated from the Columbia School of Dental and Oral Surgery in 1985 and completed a two year General Practice residency at the University of Connecticut before establishing his practice in Lynnfield, Massachusetts. He has provided high quality dental care to the Boston area and North Shore for decades.

What sets the practice apart is Dr. Corbett's belief in a health centered approach. He has completed extensive research and study in physiologic dentistry, craniofacial growth and development, myofunctional therapy, and TMJ and sleep apnea care, so treatment plans consider your bite, airway, and long term wellness, not just today's filling.

NS

North Shore Smiles

The practice at 1 Post Office Square combines innovative technology with a whole body strategy for healthy living, serving patients of all ages in a comfortable, personal setting.

For kids

Early guidance matters

Many bite and airway problems can be recognized earlier than most parents expect. Early preventive steps in childhood can guide healthy jaw development and may avoid lengthy orthodontic treatment in the teen years.

Community

Rooted on the North Shore

Dr. Corbett lives with his family on the North Shore and stays active in his community and in professional dental associations. Patients describe a practice that feels personal: professional, courteous, knowledgeable, and caring, visit after visit.
