About us

A Valley contractor with a simple promise

Gold Construction Service North Hollywood is a general contractor based at 8041 Webb Ave in North Hollywood, California.

The company was founded on the belief that good construction is mostly good management: clear scopes, honest timelines, skilled tradespeople, and a jobsite left clean at the end of every day. Homeowners across the San Fernando Valley rely on that approach for projects large and small.

Whether it is a full kitchen remodel, a new room addition, or the long list of repairs you have been putting off, the crew treats your home like it is their own reputation on the line, because it is.
