Our story

Rooted in Montgomery Village

Green Ground Landscaping LLC is a local landscaping company built on a simple idea: show up, do the work right, and treat every yard like it matters.

From a truck, a trailer, and a route through Gaithersburg, Green Ground grew the way good local businesses do: one satisfied neighbor at a time.

There is no shortcut to a great-looking yard. It takes the right cut at the right height, beds that get edged before the mulch goes down, and a crew that notices the details most people walk past. That is the work Green Ground Landscaping LLC was built to do.

Based on Brassie Way in Montgomery Village, the company serves Gaithersburg and the surrounding Montgomery County neighborhoods. Being local is the whole point: short drives mean the crew can keep schedules tight, respond quickly after a storm, and actually know how Maryland's clay soil, humid summers, and leaf-heavy falls treat a lawn.

"A yard is never finished. It is kept. We are the keepers."

The Green Ground crew

Residential or commercial, one-time cleanup or year-round care, the approach stays the same: walk the property first, quote honestly, then do the work like it is our own block. Because it is.
