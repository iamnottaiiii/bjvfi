Custom tattooing

One of a kind designs drawn for the person wearing them. Bring an idea and work it through together from sketch to skin.

An inclusive studio

Now and Forever is a collective built as a safe space for women, nonbinary, and trans artists and the clients they tattoo.

Walk-ins and appointments

The studio welcomes both walk-in clients and booked sessions, and regularly hosts guest artists passing through Philadelphia.

A studio with a story

Now and Forever Tattoo was founded in 2020 by artist Kristel Oreto as an artist collective where lady, trans, queer, and nonbinary tattooers could work and be seen. The studio was featured in a WHYY mini documentary on women and nonbinary artists reshaping the tattoo industry.

Hope tattoos from 2043 Hope St, a short stroll from the collective's Fishtown storefront, in the heart of the 19122 neighborhood.

What you can get

Styles and services

Every piece is custom. The list below reflects the services the Now and Forever collective offers at its Fishtown shop.

Custom tattooing

Fine line, illustrative, botanical, and ornamental work drawn around your idea. Styles vary by artist, so bring references and ask what each hand does best.

Small pieces and flash

Delicate designs, script, and small symbolic work suited to a first tattoo or a quick meaningful addition.

Cover-ups

The collective's artists take on challenging cover-up projects, reworking old ink into pieces you want to show off.

Body piercing

The studio's booking page also lists body, ear, and nose piercing alongside tattooing.

Tattoo removal

Tattoo removal is listed among the studio's services for clients looking to clear old work before a new piece.

Guest artist sessions

Traveling artists regularly guest at the shop, so keep an eye out for limited booking windows with visiting hands.

Service list is based on the studio's public Fresha booking page for Now and Forever Tattoo. Call (445) 264-1077 to confirm what is available for your appointment.
