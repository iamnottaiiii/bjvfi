Our story

The neighborhood salon on Bath Ave.

Tanya's Beauty Center has served Bath Beach for years as a beauty salon, hair salon and beauty supply store in one. Reviewers describe a warm, family style team: friendly, helpful and affordable, with clients who travel out of their way to return.

Golden hands, kind prices

That is how longtime clients describe the team, in English and in Russian. Many reviewers mention the shop's Russian speaking staff and welcoming atmosphere.

Senior friendly

One reviewer highlights a Wednesday senior citizen discount day with very low haircut prices. Ask about current specials when you call.

Good for families

Reviews note the salon is good for kids and walk-ins are welcome.
