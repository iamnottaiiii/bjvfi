Our story

A Sylmar roofing crew with old-school standards.

Granite Rise Roofing Contractor is based at 12950 San Fernando Rd in Sylmar, in the northeast corner of the San Fernando Valley. We started with a simple observation: too many roofing jobs go wrong because of rushed inspections and vague pricing.

So we built the business the other way around. We inspect the whole roof, explain what we find in plain language, and put the price in writing before we touch a shingle. No pressure, no mystery line items.

When the job is done, the site is clean, the gutters are clear, and we walk the roof with you so you can see exactly what changed.

How we work

The standards behind every job.

Straightforward quotes

Written scope and price before work starts. If something changes mid-job, you hear about it first.

Tidy job sites

Tarps, magnetic nail sweeps, and a full cleanup every day. Your yard should look better than we found it.

Show up on time

Scheduled start times we keep. If weather forces a change, we call you before you have to call us.
