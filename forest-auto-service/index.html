Our story

The neighborhood shop on Marlboro Pike

Forest Auto Service is the kind of shop where the parking lot stays full and the same customers keep coming back year after year. Drivers from District Heights and across Prince George's County bring their cars here because the work is honest and the prices are fair.

It is a working shop, not a showroom. You will find a friendly face at the front desk, a tech who explains things in plain language, and a crew that treats your car with respect. Regulars drop their vehicles off in the morning and pick them up ready to go, often the same day.

Whether it is a simple oil change or a repair that another shop could not figure out, the goal is the same: fix it right, charge fairly, and earn your trust for the next visit.
