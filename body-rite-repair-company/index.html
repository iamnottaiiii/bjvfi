About Body Rite Repair Company

Body Rite Repair Company is an auto body shop in Secaucus specializing in collision repair and refinishing. From minor dents to major body work, the team restores your vehicle's look and helps you through the repair process step by step.

  • Collision Repair Body panels repaired or replaced after accidents, aligned to factory specs.
  • Dent Removal Dents and dings smoothed out without disturbing the surrounding finish where possible.
  • Paint Matching Computerized color matching so repairs blend into the original finish.
  • Bumper Repair Cracked and scuffed bumpers repaired or replaced for a clean look.
  • Frame Straightening Structural measuring and straightening to bring the body back into line.
  • Detailing Interior and exterior detailing that leaves your car looking its best.
