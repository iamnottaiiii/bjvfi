Our Story

Three decades of roots
in Glenshaw

Walter Michael Landscaping Inc is a family-run company on Breezewood Drive, doing the work ourselves since the 1990s.

Family crew · no subcontractors
Since the 1990s

The Walter family,
on the job

Walter Michael Landscaping started as a local landscaping outfit in Glenshaw and grew the old-fashioned way: one satisfied neighbor telling the next. Today the company is run by owner Michael Walter with family behind him, including Linda Walter handling the office side of things.

We keep it small on purpose. Five employees, no subcontractors, and the same crew from the estimate to the final walkthrough. When you call, you talk to people who know your property because they have worked on it with their own hands.

  • Registered Pennsylvania corporation, active and local
  • A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau
  • Insured: $1,000,000 general liability policy on file
  • Free estimates and emergency service, always
