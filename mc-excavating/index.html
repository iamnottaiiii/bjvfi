Services

Excavation services in Azusa and the San Gabriel Valley

Tap a service for details. For pricing, timelines, and anything not listed here, call (626) 969-1190.

Rough grading to shape the lot and finish grading to hit final elevations. A level, compacted pad is the foundation everything else stands on.

Open trenches for water, sewer, gas, and conduit, plus backfill and compaction so the ground settles right the first time.

Removal of old structures, slabs, and unwanted vegetation, with debris hauled off so the site is clean and ready to build.

Footings and foundation digs cut to plan, so concrete crews can pour without surprises on grade day.

Import and export of soil, rock, and aggregate. We move material in and out efficiently to keep the site work flowing.
