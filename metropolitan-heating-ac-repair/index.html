Our story

Altadena's neighborhood HVAC crew

Metropolitan Heating & AC Repair is an air conditioning contractor based on North Lincoln Avenue in Altadena.

We started with a simple frustration: too many HVAC visits ended with a vague diagnosis and a pushy sales pitch for equipment the homeowner did not need. So we built the company we wanted to call, one that treats repair as a craft and the customer as a neighbor.

Altadena is a demanding place for heating and cooling. Summer heat pushes past 95 degrees, Santa Ana winds coat outdoor units in dust, and wildfire smoke seasons punish filters and coils. Winter nights in the foothills get cold enough to expose every weakness in a furnace. We size, service, and repair systems for these exact conditions.

Our approach has not changed since day one: show up on time, find the real problem, explain it plainly, fix it right, and leave the place cleaner than we found it.
