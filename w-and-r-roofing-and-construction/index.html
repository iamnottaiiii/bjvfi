Roof Repair

Most roof problems start small: a lifted shingle, cracked flashing, a slow drip that only shows up in heavy rain. Repairs target the failure point instead of replacing the whole system.

  • Leak detection and tracing to the source
  • Shingle and tile replacement
  • Flashing repair around chimneys, vents, and valleys
  • Storm and wind damage patch-ups
Call About a Repair

Re-Roofing

When repairs stop making sense, a full replacement resets the clock. Ask about tear-off versus overlay, underlayment, and materials that handle Glendale heat.

  • Full tear-off and replacement
  • Underlayment and decking evaluation
  • Material options for sun and heat exposure
  • Cleanup and debris haul-off
Ask About Re-Roofing

Roof Inspections

Buying, selling, or just wondering how many years are left. An inspection gives you a clear read on condition, trouble spots, and what to budget for.

  • Overall condition assessment
  • Pre-purchase and pre-listing checkups
  • Storm damage documentation
  • Maintenance recommendations
Schedule an Inspection

Gutters & More

Roofs work as a system. Gutters, downspouts, and ventilation all affect how long a roof lasts and how dry the house stays.

  • Gutter cleaning, repair, and installation
  • Downspout and drainage fixes
  • Vent and skylight flashing
  • Ask about other exterior construction work
