Our story

A family business built on showing up.

Montanast Handyman is a small business owned by a married couple, based on W Montana St in north central Pasadena. It started in 2019 with a simple idea: treat every home like it is your own, finish on time, and charge a fair price.

Jose handles the tools and the troubleshooting. Brenda keeps the schedule, the quotes, and the follow-up. Customers know both of them by name, and that personal accountability is the whole business model.
