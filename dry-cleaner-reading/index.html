A neighborhood cleaner that treats your clothes like its own.

Dry Cleaner is a family-owned shop in Reading, listed as serving the community since 1994. The work is done on-site, so your garments never travel to a far-away plant.

  • On-site dry cleaning, done in the store
  • Repairs and alterations for all fabrics, including leather and suede
  • Wedding gowns, comforters, and boots handled with care
  • Old hangers accepted for recycling
