What we do

Services

Dental care for the whole family, from first checkups to everyday restorative treatment.

Please note: the services below reflect typical general and pediatric dentistry care. Call (703) 914-0020 to confirm that a specific treatment is offered. Pricing varies by treatment; call the office for an estimate.

Exams and cleanings

Routine checkups and professional cleanings that keep teeth and gums healthy and catch small issues early.

Preventive education

Hands-on guidance on brushing, flossing, and everyday habits, a hallmark of this practice according to patient reviews.

Oral health screenings

Thorough evaluations of teeth, gums, and bite during regular visits.

Tooth-colored fillings

Natural-looking repairs for cavities and minor tooth damage.

Crowns

Protective restorations for teeth that are cracked, weakened, or extensively filled.

Root canal treatment

Care for infected or damaged tooth pulp, focused on relieving pain and saving the natural tooth.

Extractions

Gentle removal of teeth that cannot be saved, with clear aftercare instructions.

First dental visits

Patient, pressure-free introductions to the dentist for young children.

Pediatric checkups

Ongoing preventive care for kids and teens, with coaching on home care the whole family can follow.

Family scheduling

Book the whole family together. Call (703) 914-0020 to arrange back-to-back appointments.

Call and ask. The team is happy to explain options in plain language before you book.
