Services

Tile work, start to finish

From tear-out to final grout seal, we handle the whole tile job. Typical services below. Every project is quoted individually, so call for a free estimate.

01

Tile Floor Installation

Ceramic, porcelain and natural stone floors. We prep the subfloor right, lay out for clean sight lines, and set tile flat and true.

02

Kitchen Backsplashes

The fastest way to change a kitchen. Subway tile, mosaics and large formats, finished with crisp edges at counters and cabinets.

03

Bathroom & Shower Tile

Shower walls, tub surrounds, bathroom floors. Waterproofing and slope details done the way they should be, because leaks are forever.

04

Tile Repair

Cracked or loose tiles replaced to match, caulking and transitions redone, problem spots fixed before they spread.

05

Regrouting & Grout Refresh

Stained, cracked or missing grout ground out and replaced. A regrout can make old tile look new again.

06

Fireplace & Accent Walls

Fireplace surrounds and feature walls in tile or stone, for a focal point that anchors the room.

Our story

Setting tile in Cedarhurst
since 1986

Lanztile Ltd is a New York domestic corporation registered in December 1986, and it has been setting tile on Long Island ever since.

The long view

Forty years of the same trade

Most contractors come and go. Lanztile Ltd has been at 342 Roselle Ave in Cedarhurst since 1986, doing one thing: tile. That kind of staying power is its own reference.

We are specialists, not generalists. When tile is all you do, the details become the whole job: the layout lines up, the cuts are tight, the grout is straight, and the waterproofing is done right the first time.

1986

Lanztile Ltd is incorporated in New York State, based in Cedarhurst.

Decades on Roselle Ave

Steady tile work across the Five Towns and Nassau County, mostly by word of mouth.

Today

The same trade, the same standards: floors, walls, backsplashes and bathrooms, set to last.

Forty years in one trade is not an accident. It is a reputation, earned one floor at a time.
