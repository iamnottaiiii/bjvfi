Our story

Barberito’s Barbershop is a family owned shop in Worcester’s Millbury Street neighborhood, cutting hair for kids and adults since 2011. The formula has never changed: friendly barbers, attention to detail, and time spent on every client.

Reviewers describe a welcoming environment, barbers who listen, and consistently great haircuts that keep people coming back and recommending the shop to others.
