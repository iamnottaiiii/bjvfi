Our story

Three decades of Florida roofs

Pro Roofing Management Inc. has been working on Orlando roofs since 1993, through every hurricane season Florida has thrown at us.

Roofing is half construction.
The other half is trust.

Anyone can nail shingles. What homeowners remember is who showed up after the storm, who told the truth about what the roof needed, and who stayed until the insurance paperwork was sorted.

That is why we built the company around the hard part of the job: storm damage, catastrophe restoration, and insurance claims. A leak is stressful enough without a contractor who disappears and an adjuster who needs photos you never took.

Since 1993, our approach has stayed the same: inspect honestly, document everything, fix what needs fixing, and treat your home like it is our own.
