About us

A family crew with deep roots

Tello's Tree Service is a family-owned tree company based in Cleves, Ohio, serving the greater Cincinnati area. The owner got into the tree business working for family and decided to start doing their own thing, and the company has been climbing ever since.

With over 20 years of experience in the trade, the crew specializes in tree removal, pruning, trimming, and elevating, plus landscaping, shrub care, and mulching. The team describes itself as prompt and professional, and customers regularly praise the cleanup: brush dragged, big wood cut, and the yard left clean.

The crew is bilingual, so English and Spanish speaking customers get the same clear communication. Estimates are free, and emergency services are offered when storms strike.

The company holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau (not BBB accredited) and is listed across major directories with a 5.0 Google rating.
