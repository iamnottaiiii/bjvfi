Our story

Two sisters,
one neighborhood salon

Mimi & Mina Hair Salon has been serving Annandale for over a decade, building a loyal following one great haircut at a time.

How we got here

A salon built on listening

Ask regulars why they keep coming back and the answer is the same: the stylists really listen. Whether it is a first-time client unsure what they want or a regular of ten years, every appointment starts with understanding the person in the chair.

The salon has earned a reputation for expertise with Asian hair, from precision cuts to digital and Japanese straight perms. Men's cuts are a quiet specialty too, with clients calling it a hidden gem for a sharp, fairly priced cut.

  • Serving Annandale for over a decade
  • Known for perm and straight-perm expertise
  • Fast, efficient appointments that respect your day
