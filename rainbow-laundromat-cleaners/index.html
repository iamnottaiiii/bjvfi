How wash and fold works

Three steps and you are done.

1

Drop off

Bring your laundry to the counter at 1540 Dixwell Ave any time between 7 AM and 10 PM. We will weigh it and note any special instructions.

2

We wash, dry and fold

Your clothes go through our commercial machines with quality detergent, then get dried and folded with care. Most orders are ready the same day.

3

Pick up fresh

Come back to clean, fresh laundry, bagged and ready to put away. Pay at pickup, and tell us how we did.

A note on folding

A few reviewers mentioned wash and fold orders arriving loosely packed rather than neatly folded. If crisp folding matters for your order, say so at drop off and we will take extra care.

Our story

A Hamden laundry with a simple standard: spotless.

Rainbow Laundromat & Cleaners sits at 1540 Dixwell Avenue in Hamden, serving neighbors who want their laundry done right without driving across town or overpaying. The idea behind the name is simple: laundry day should feel a little brighter.

What regulars notice first is the cleanliness. Each machine gets cleaned every night, the floors stay spotless through the day, and the whole store is set up to feel comfortable while you wait. It is the kind of place people recommend to friends without being asked.

Under new management in recent years, the store has kept what worked and sharpened the details: faster wash and fold turnaround, attentive counter service and prices that reviewers consistently call lower than other laundromats in the area.

What we believe

Clean machines, honest prices, friendly faces.

Laundry is a chore nobody loves, so we make it as easy as possible: a store you are happy to walk into, machines you can trust, and a team that treats your clothes like their own. That is the whole business plan.
