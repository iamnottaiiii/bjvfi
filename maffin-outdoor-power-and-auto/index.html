About the shop

Maffin Outdoor Power and Auto in Van Meter repairs outdoor power equipment, cars, and trucks. On the outdoor side, the shop services ATVs, motorcycles, snowmobiles, outboard motors, tillers, trimmers, chainsaws, snow blowers, and push and riding mowers.

On the automotive side, the shop handles cars and trucks, domestic and imports, with general maintenance, diagnostic services, oil changes, brakes, tire changes and repair, and transmission removal and installation. Estimates are free, and reviewers give the shop a perfect 5.0 rating for knowledgeable, honest work.
