Our story

A Cincinnati earthwork crew with deep roots

J & L Equipment works the ground around Cincinnati from its yard on 8 Mile Rd. The company is listed in the Blue Book as an excavating contractor, and has held that listing since 2000, a run that speaks to staying power in a trade where reputation is everything.

Alongside excavation, the company deals in the materials every job needs: sand, gravel, and topsoil. Running equipment and materials under one roof means the crew that digs your site can also finish it, without waiting on a second contractor.

Most work stays close to home: residential digs, grading, land clearing, and material deliveries across Cincinnati's east side and Clermont County. If your project involves moving dirt, chances are it is exactly what this crew does every day.

  • Based on 8 Mile Rd. Cincinnati, Ohio, serving the east side and surrounding communities.
