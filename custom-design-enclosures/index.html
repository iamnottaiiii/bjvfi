Services

What the shop fabricates.

Pick a category to see typical scope. Every enclosure is custom, so the final word is always a conversation.

Custom Sheet Metal Enclosures

Enclosures and cabinets built to your exact dimensions and application, in steel, aluminum, or stainless.

  • Electrical and equipment enclosures
  • Control boxes and instrument housings
  • One-off builds and short production runs
  • Sized from your drawings or measured on site

Precision Cutting, Bending, and Forming

The core metalwork: flat stock in, formed parts out, held to the tolerances your assembly needs.

  • Shearing and precision cutting
  • Press brake bending and forming
  • Welding and assembly of formed parts
  • Prototype through small-batch quantities

Cutouts, Louvers, and Hardware

The details that make an enclosure install-ready instead of almost-ready.

  • Precision cutouts for connectors, displays, and cable entry
  • Ventilation louvers and perforated panels
  • Hinges, latches, and mounting hardware
  • Powder coat and protective finishes

Repair and Modification

Existing enclosures and sheet metal parts reworked instead of replaced.

  • Modification of existing enclosures for new equipment
  • Panel replacement and structural repair
  • Retrofit cutouts and added ventilation
  • Refinishing of worn or damaged units

Who We Are

Custom work is the whole business model.

Custom Design Enclosures is a sheet metal contracting shop at 3032 Dolores St in Glassell Park, Los Angeles. The name says it plainly: enclosures designed around your equipment, your dimensions, and your install, not pulled from a catalog.

Public business listings credit the shop with 27 years in business, the kind of tenure that comes from doing precise work for people who come back with the next drawing.

When you call, you talk to the shop that will cut your metal. Bring dimensions, drawings, or a rough sketch and get a straight answer on what is possible, what it costs, and when it is done.
