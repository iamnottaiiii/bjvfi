Our story

A Harrisburg crew that grew up on the job

Swope Home Improvements began in 2011 doing handyman work around Harrisburg: the handrails, disposals, and ceiling fans that keep a house running. Homeowners kept asking for more, so the work grew into full kitchen and bathroom remodels, finished basements, room additions, garages, and decks. The rule never changed: show up, do it right, and treat the house like it is your own.
