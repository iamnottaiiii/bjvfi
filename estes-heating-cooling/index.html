Our story

Started in 1999 with a simple rebellion.

1999 · ELSMERE, KY

The founding idea

Steve had spent years in the heating and cooling trade and gone through trade school. He kept seeing the same thing: jobs oversold, corners cut, customers talked into work they did not need. He wanted to change that. So he and Sonya started Estes Heating, Cooling and Electric with three rules of their own: only fix what needs to be fixed, always tell the truth, and do the job to the best of their ability with no shortcuts.

THE SHOP TODAY

Still small, still local

From 4117 Dixie Highway in Elsmere, Estes serves homes and businesses across Northern Kentucky: Erlanger, Florence, Covington, Independence, and beyond. The work covers furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, air duct cleaning, and related electrical, with permit records showing both residential and commercial jobs over the years.
