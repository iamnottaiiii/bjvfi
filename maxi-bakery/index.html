Our story

The corner Commercial Ave counts on

Maxi Bakery grew the old-fashioned way: one regular at a time, one warm tray at a time.

Found on accident. Loved on purpose.

Ask around South Chicago and you will hear the same story: somebody wandered in by chance, tried the tacos and the sweets, and told the whole family. That is how a bakery becomes a landmark.

Reviewers describe a welcoming, family-feeling spot where the staff is professional, the service is spectacular, and the food is fresh. Some come three times a week. Nobody is in a hurry to leave.

It is a bakery and a kitchen because the neighborhood needed both: warm bread in the morning, a real lunch at noon, and a team that treats community events like family business.

8712 S Commercial Ave · Chicago

Busy at peak hours. Worth the wait.

Regulars will tell you straight: at lunch rush, there can be a line. They will also tell you it is worth it, because everything that comes out of this shop is good.

The bakery side keeps the ovens going from 5 AM, so the case is full when the doors open. The kitchen side keeps the grill hot through the afternoon. Between the two, there is something for every craving and every budget.

And when the neighborhood celebrates, Maxi shows up: catering trays for events, seasonal specials, and a team that knows its regulars by name.
