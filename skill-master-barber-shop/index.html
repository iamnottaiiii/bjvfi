Skill Master Barber Shop sits at 4927 Paddock Rd in Cincinnati’s Bond Hill neighborhood. It is a barber shop offering barbering, hair and styling, beard trims, head shaves, and hot towel shaves, according to its listed service menu.

The shop is open Tuesday through Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM, and closed Sunday and Monday. Walk in or call (513) 242-7545 ahead of your visit.
