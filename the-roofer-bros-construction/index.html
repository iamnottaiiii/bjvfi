Our story

Roofers first, builders by demand

The Roofer Bros Construction is a California licensed contractor (Lic. #1107595) based at 9410 Topanga Canyon Blvd in Chatsworth. What started as a roofing outfit grew the way good trades grow: customers kept asking the same crew to fix the stucco, hang the gutters, and remodel the kitchen once the roof was done.

Today the team covers roofing, exterior work, and full remodeling across Los Angeles, including Beverly Hills, San Fernando, Chatsworth, Glendale, Pasadena, Burbank, and the beach communities, with work extending across Los Angeles County.

The review record tells the story better than we can: a 5.0 rating across 251 Google reviews, 4.9 across 305 Birdeye reviews, and a 5.0 across 110 Thumbtack reviews with 47 hires.

Common threads in customer feedback: a dedicated project manager who communicates clearly, crews that arrive on time and clean up daily, fair pricing, and workmanship that holds up on architecturally challenging roofs.
