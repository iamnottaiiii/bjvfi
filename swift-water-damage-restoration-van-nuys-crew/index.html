Who we are

Our Story

A Van Nuys water damage crew built around one idea: when water is in your home, minutes matter.

A local crew for the worst day of your year

Swift Water Damage Restoration Van Nuys Crew is a water damage restoration service based at 6607 Valjean Ave in Van Nuys, California. We serve homes and businesses across Van Nuys and the surrounding San Fernando Valley.

Water damage is disorienting. One minute everything is fine, the next there is a burst pipe, a failed water heater, or storm water pushing under the door. We built this crew to be the calm, fast, capable team that shows up in that moment and takes the problem off your shoulders.

Our process is the same on every job: stop the source, extract the water, dry the structure completely, remediate anything the water left behind, and rebuild what was damaged. And we document everything along the way, because a smooth insurance claim is part of getting your life back.

The call

You reach a real person who dispatches the crew and tells you exactly what to do while we are en route.

Mitigation

Extraction equipment goes in immediately. The goal is to stop secondary damage before it starts.

Drying

Air movers, dehumidifiers, and daily moisture readings until every affected area tests dry.

Rebuild

Drywall, flooring, paint, and finishes restored. One crew, start to finish.
