Our story

One baker, one planet of flavor

Cake Planet by Daria LLC started with a simple idea: a bakery where every dessert feels like a small celebration. Daria bakes custom flavor cupcakes, cakes, cake jars and cake rolls at 1920 E Columbus Dr in East Chicago, and her regulars will tell you she treats every order like it is the most important one of the week.

The shop describes itself as the place for "all of your dessert cravings," and the 4.9 star rating across dozens of reviews backs that up. Guests keep coming back for the friendly service, the creative custom designs and flavors like turtle cupcakes and taffy grapes you will not find just anywhere.

Planning a wedding, a birthday or a just-because treat? Bring your theme and your craving, and Daria will bake a little planet of sweetness around it.
