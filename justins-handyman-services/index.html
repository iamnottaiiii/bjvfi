How it works

Simple from first call to finished job.

1. Call and describe the job. Walk through your list by phone and get a sense of timing and cost.

2. Get the work done. Justin arrives with his own tools and materials and works through the list.

3. Walk through the results. Everything is checked with you before he packs up, and the space is left clean.

Small jobs are welcome: the list does not need to be big to be worth doing right. If a project is outside what a handyman should take on, you will hear that honestly up front.
