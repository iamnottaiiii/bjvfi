Replacement windows

Old windows cost you comfort and energy dollars. We replace worn units with modern, efficient windows sized to your openings.

  • Double-hung, slider, casement, and picture windows
  • Full-frame and pocket replacement options
  • New flashing, insulation, and interior trim
  • Fogged-glass and failed-seal replacements
Call (513) 843-4663 for an estimate

What to expect

We measure every opening, review styles and glass options with you, and schedule the install. Most homes are completed with minimal disruption, and we haul away the old windows.

Vinyl siding installation

A full siding replacement transforms curb appeal and gives your home a fresh, weather-tight exterior that is easy to maintain.

  • Full-house vinyl siding replacement
  • House wrap and weather barrier detailing
  • Soffit, fascia, and trim coordination
  • Color and profile selection guidance
Call (513) 843-4663 for an estimate

What to expect

We strip failing siding, inspect the sheathing underneath, and install the new system with careful laps, corners, and J-channel work so water sheds the way it should.

Siding and trim repair

Not every problem needs a full replacement. We fix storm damage, loose panels, and worn trim to keep the exterior sealed.

  • Storm and wind damage repair
  • Loose or buckling panel replacement
  • Soffit, fascia, and corner-board repair
  • Caulking and seal touch-ups
Call (513) 843-4663 for an estimate

What to expect

We find the source of the problem, not just the symptom, match the existing materials as closely as possible, and leave the repair weather-tight.

Window service and repair

Sticky sashes, broken hardware, and drafty frames can often be serviced without replacing the whole window.

  • Sash, balance, and hardware repair
  • Weatherstripping replacement
  • Glass and screen service
  • Draft diagnosis and sealing
Call (513) 843-4663 for an estimate

What to expect

We inspect the window, explain whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense, and give you a straight answer before any work begins.

Our story

A Batavia exterior contractor

Focused on the outside of your home

Advanced Window & Siding is based on Amelia-Olive Branch Road in Batavia, Ohio, serving homeowners across Clermont County. We are a contractor with a narrow focus: windows and siding.
