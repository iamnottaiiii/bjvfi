Services

Brick, block and stone walls

Privacy walls, fence walls, retaining walls, planter walls and freestanding garden walls. Laid plumb, reinforced properly, and built to handle hillside soil and seismic movement.

Driveways, patios and pool decks

Concrete flatwork, pavers, stamped and colored concrete, pool coping and new pool decks. Proper base preparation so the surface stays flat for decades, not seasons.

Repairs, tuck pointing and restoration

Sidewalk replacement, driveway approaches and curb work done to city spec, so permits and inspections go smoothly.

Our story

A Tujunga craftsman since 1989

Dale Naber started Dale Naber Masonry on January 1, 1989, and has been laying brick, block and stone in the same community ever since.

The way we work

Show up early, work steady, clean up after

Thirty-seven years in one trade teaches you what matters. Dale makes worthwhile suggestions at the estimate stage, not after the work has started. He and his crew arrive when they say they will, work a full day without interruption, and leave the site tidy each evening.

One customer put it plainly after a reconstruction project: the whole thing was completely satisfactory, so they hired Dale for a second project, which went equally well. That is the reputation this business runs on.

The promise

Warrantied work, honest advice

Masonry is permanent. Once a wall is up or a driveway is poured, you live with it for decades. We treat every project that way: honest assessments, proper reinforcement and drainage, and warranties that stand behind the workmanship.

No emergency services, no rush jobs. Just careful masonry, scheduled properly and finished right.
