About Platinum.

A Northridge-based HVAC contractor serving homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley.

Platinum Northridge works out of Business Center Drive in Northridge, in the heart of the Valley. That means short drive times, local knowledge of the housing stock, and a tech who knows what Valley heat does to an aging condenser.

Our approach is the boring kind that works: show up on time, diagnose before quoting, explain the options in plain language, and stand behind the work.

Whether it is a rental with a dead AC in July or a homeowner planning a full system upgrade, you get the same straight answers.
