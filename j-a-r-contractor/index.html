How we got here

Still here because
the work holds up

J.A.R. Contractor started in the San Fernando Valley in 1998, and has been building and remodeling homes here ever since. We have watched neighborhoods change, codes change and trends come and go. What has not changed is the formula: show up on time, do what you wrote down, and leave the house cleaner than you found it.

Most of our work comes the old fashioned way. A neighbor sees a finished kitchen, asks who did it, and calls the number. That only happens when the work speaks for itself, so we build every job like the next one depends on it.
