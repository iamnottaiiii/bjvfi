Our story

A builder's mindset, a neighbor's accountability

PSG Construction Co. is a contractor rooted in Canoga Park, California, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley. The company was built on a simple belief: construction goes better when the contractor treats your home like their own job site reputation depends on it, because it does.

Most of the Valley's housing stock went up in the postwar decades, and remodeling it well takes more than a hammer. It takes planning, honest pricing, and respect for the people living through the project. That is the standard every PSG Construction Co. job is held to, whether it is a single bathroom or a full home renovation.

When you hire local, you get a contractor who answers the phone, shows up, and is still in the neighborhood when the job is done.

  • Local to Canoga Park. Based at 7033 Topanga Canyon Blvd, serving the surrounding Valley communities.
  • Clear scopes. Written plans before work starts, so expectations match reality.
  • One point of contact. You always know who is running your job and how to reach them.
  • Clean job sites. Daily cleanup and respect for your home and neighbors.
  • Straight talk. If a detail will cost more or take longer, you hear it up front.
