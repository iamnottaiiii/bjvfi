Our story

Forty years of building, one family name on every home.

Roger Barrick started building homes the way his family always worked: show up, work hard, and build something you would be proud to put your name on. Four decades later, that is still the entire business plan.

The White Tail Homes neighborhood is the clearest proof. Roger started building there in 2003, one home at a time. Today the neighborhood features hundreds of gorgeous, quality built, affordable homes, and it stands as the family's signature in Marysville.

People who grew up around the Barrick family say the same thing: these are among the hardest working people you will ever meet. That reputation was earned on job sites across the Susquehanna Valley, one honest build after another.

"Work ethic and dedication you can see in the finished home."
