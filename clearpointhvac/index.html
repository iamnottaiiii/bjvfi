Services

Paving, repair,
sealcoat & striping

Every project starts with a site look and a written quote. Pricing varies by size, condition, and access, so call for exact numbers.

01

Driveway Paving

New asphalt driveways from the ground up. We grade for proper drainage, compact the base, and lay a smooth, even mat with clean edges. Residential only or shared access, sized to your home.

→
02

Parking Lot Paving

Commercial lots engineered for traffic and weight. We plan stall layout, drainage flow, and curb transitions so the lot drains right and holds up under daily use.

→
03

Asphalt Overlays

When the base is still sound, a fresh overlay adds years of life at a fraction of full replacement cost. We mill edges at transitions so the new surface ties in cleanly.

→
04

Patching & Crack Repair

Potholes, alligator cracking, and failing spots get cut out to solid material and patched properly, not just skimmed over. Cracks are routed and sealed to stop water intrusion.

→
05

Sealcoating

A protective coat shields asphalt from sun, oil, and water. Sealcoating restores that deep black finish and, done on schedule, can double the life of a driveway or lot.

→
06

Line Striping & Markings

Crisp parking stalls, ADA markings, arrows, and curbs painted to spec. Fresh striping makes a lot safer and instantly looks more professional.

Our story

Built from the
ground down

A Burbank paving crew
with one standard

ClearPointHVAC is a paving contractor based at 1357 N Catalina St in Burbank, serving homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley. Our name is on every ton of asphalt we lay, so every job has to be one we would drive over with pride.

Pavement fails from the bottom up: soft base, trapped water, skipped prep. That is why we put the most effort where nobody sees it, in the grading and compaction that decide whether a driveway lasts five years or twenty.

When you call, you talk to someone who has stood on the roller, not a call center. We show up when we say we will, we clean up when we leave, and we put the quote in writing before we start.
