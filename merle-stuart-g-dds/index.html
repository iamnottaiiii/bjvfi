Our story

A Lynn practice with
decades of smiles

Merle Stuart G DDS has served Lynn families since 1975, a small neighborhood practice built on trust, patience, and gentle care.

Small practice,
big on kindness

Big dental chains have their place, but there is something special about a small practice where the team knows your childs name. That has been our way since 1975.

As a pediatric-focused practice, everything here is built around young patients: unhurried appointments, plain-language explanations, and an approach that treats a nervous first-timer with the same care as a confident teen.

Today the practice continues that tradition at 225 Boston St in Lynn, welcoming new families and caring for the next generation of smiles.
