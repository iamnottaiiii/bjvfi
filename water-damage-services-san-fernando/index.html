Why speed wins

Industry guidance is blunt: the faster drying starts, the less you tear out. Here is what the clock does to an untreated leak.

First hours

Water wicks into drywall, baseboards and carpet padding. Staining starts.

First two days

Humidity spikes, odors set in, and mold can begin colonizing damp materials.

First week

Swelling, warping and microbial growth turn a dry-out into a tear-out.

The bill

Fast professional drying often saves materials that delayed action would condemn.

What to do before we arrive

Three things you can do in the first ten minutes that genuinely help.

Stop the source

Shut the main water valve if a pipe burst. Move valuables and electronics to dry ground.

Kill the power

If water is near outlets or appliances, switch off the breaker for the wet area. Do not wade into standing water near electricity.

Call us

We will talk you through the rest on the phone while the crew rolls: (818) 867-8507.

Skip the fans

Household fans on wet drywall can spread contamination and hide moisture. Let the meters decide what gets dried.
