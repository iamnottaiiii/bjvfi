Why duct cleaning

Your ducts collect more than air

Dust, pet dander, pollen, and everyday debris settle inside ductwork over time. Every time the system runs, it can push that buildup back into your rooms. A professional cleaning pulls it out at the source.

a.

Fresher indoor air

Removing dust and debris from ducts means the air your system circulates starts clean instead of recycled.

b.

Less dust everywhere

Clean ducts spread less dust onto furniture, shelves, and floors, so the house stays cleaner between dustings.

c.

Better airflow

Clear passages let your heating and cooling system breathe easier, helping rooms feel more even.

Services

Everything we clean, numbered

  1. 01

    Air duct cleaning

    Our core service. We clean the full run of your supply and return ductwork, removing the dust, dander, and debris that builds up over years of use.

    →
  2. 02

    Vent and register cleaning

    The grilles and registers in each room catch the most visible grime. We clean them so the air entering your rooms starts fresh.

    →
  3. 03

    Dryer vent cleaning

    Lint packed into a dryer vent slows drying and strains the machine. We clear the vent path so air and moisture can escape freely.

    →
  4. 04

    System inspection

    Before we clean, we look. An inspection of accessible ductwork spots disconnections, damage, or heavy contamination so you know exactly what you are dealing with.

    →
  5. 05

About

A duct cleaning company on York Blvd

Basic Clean Duct Exp. is based at 5142 York Blvd in Highland Park, Los Angeles, California. We specialize in one thing: cleaning the air ducts, vents, and related pathways that carry heated and cooled air through your home.

It is focused work with a simple payoff. Dust and debris that sit inside ductwork get recirculated every time the system runs. Removing that buildup means cleaner air from every vent, less dust settling around the house, and a system that does not have to fight through clogged passages.
