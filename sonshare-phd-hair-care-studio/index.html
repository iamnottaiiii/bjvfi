Our story

A Far Rockaway staple since 1998

Sonshare PHD Hair Care Studio has served the Beach 20th Street neighborhood since 1998. Directory records list the studio's start in 1998, and long time clients say they have been coming since that first year.

Walk in and you will notice it right away: a clean, quiet space and a warm welcome. The team is known for being conscientious and knowledgeable, the kind of stylists clients recommend to everyone they know.

Clean and calm

Clients consistently mention the spotless, quiet salon. Your appointment should feel like a reset.

Knowledgeable stylists

Welcoming service

An enthusiastic welcome from the moment you walk through the door.
