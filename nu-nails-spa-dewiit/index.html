our promise

Customers first,
always

NU Nails & Spa Dewiit is a locally owned salon where the staff believes customer satisfaction is the number one priority. The team is well trained and friendly, and they mean it: guests keep coming back for the care, not just the color.

Both men and women are welcome, the salon is wheelchair accessible, and free Wi-Fi keeps you connected while you unwind.
