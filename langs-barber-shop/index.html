The shop

Old school chair. New school precision.

Lang's has been cutting hair in Buffalo since Jim first opened his clippers, and today his family carries on the tradition on Hertel Avenue near Colvin. The shop is spotless, the magazines are current, and the haircut is exactly what you asked for.

Our story

It started with Jim. It continues with his family.

Long before the shop landed on Hertel Avenue, Jim was cutting hair on Delaware near Delevan. Customers followed him to Delavan near Main, and then they followed his family to the current shop on Hertel near Colvin.

One reviewer has been coming for over fifty years, through every one of those moves. His grandsons come now too. That is the kind of shop Lang's is: the cut stays consistent because the family behind the chairs does.

"I have been going there for 50 years and Jim always did a great job and his daughters are just as good."

Today the shop is run by Jim's family, the chairs are always full but the wait is never long, and the reading material on the table is always current. Walk in any Tuesday through Saturday and you will understand why nobody leaves.
