Nuestra Historia

A little bakery with a big heart

Blanquita's Bakery sits at 10611 S Ewing Ave on Chicago's East Side, a neighborhood panaderia where the day starts at 5 in the morning and the sweet bread is still warm when the first customers walk in.

Customers describe a cute little Mexican bakery with lovely owners, great customer service, and a spotless shop. The hours are a point of pride: open from 5 AM to 8 PM every single day, so you can stop by before work or after.

The prices are easy on the wallet, the tres leches is the talk of the block, and the welcome is always warm. That is the whole recipe.
