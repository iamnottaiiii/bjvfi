Our story

Rebuilding machines
since 1958

Studwell Engineering inc has been a Sun Valley machine and tools repairing company for generations. We built our name on scraping and alignment services, the unglamorous, exacting work that determines whether a machine tool holds tolerance year after year.

Today our technicians service mills, lathes, grinders, presses, and more, both in our shop and in the field. We rebuild, retrofit, install, relocate, and align with lasers, and as an authorized Acu-Rite dealer we keep older iron competitive with modern digital readouts.
