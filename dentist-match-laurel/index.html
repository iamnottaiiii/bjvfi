Emergency help

When to call right away

01

Severe or spreading tooth pain

Pain that keeps you up at night, throbs constantly, or comes with swelling in the face or jaw needs prompt attention.

02

Knocked-out, cracked, or broken tooth

Time matters most here. Call immediately and we will help you find care that can see you quickly.

03

Lost filling, crown, or bridge

Exposed teeth are vulnerable. We help you get seen before a small problem becomes a big one.

04

Bleeding or injury to the mouth

If you have uncontrolled bleeding or a mouth injury from an accident, call now. For life-threatening emergencies, call 911 first.

Not sure it is an emergency? Call anyway. A quick conversation beats a long night of pain.
