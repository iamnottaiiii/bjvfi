Our Story

A neighborhood salon built on value.

Trendy Angel Hair Salon opened on Bay Ridge Avenue with a simple idea: dependable hair services, done well, at prices regular people can afford.

The space is clean, bright, and organized around an efficient queue system that separates quick cuts from full services, so a stop-in haircut stays quick and a full appointment stays relaxed. Reviewers consistently mention courteous, attentive stylists who take time to understand what each client wants.

From everyday trims to color, keratin treatments, and styling for special occasions, the salon covers the full routine. Fair pricing and friendly service have made it a regular stop for Bay Ridge locals.
