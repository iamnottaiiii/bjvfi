Our story

On Division St since 1988.

Head-On Collision, Inc. was incorporated in 1988 at 199 Division St in North Tonawanda, and it has been fixing wrecked cars from the same address ever since. That kind of staying power does not happen by accident: it comes from fair estimates, thorough work, and customers who send their neighbors.

The shop is led by Jim, a name that comes up again and again in customer reviews. Drivers describe him as thorough and detailed, the kind of tech who fixes the scratch you pointed out and the one you missed.

It is an independent, local body shop in the truest sense: no corporate playbook, no upsell script. Just a crew that takes a banged-up car and hands back one that looks like the accident never happened.
