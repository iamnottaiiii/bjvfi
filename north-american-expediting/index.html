Our Story

In business since 1991

North American Expediting has operated out of Presto, Pennsylvania since 1991. That is more than three decades moving freight in and around the Pittsburgh region.

Business listings categorize the company under transportation consulting, and the phone still rings at the same St. Mellion Drive address it has long called home: (412) 279-5585.

Longevity in this business is earned one on-time delivery at a time. That is the whole story, and it is enough.
