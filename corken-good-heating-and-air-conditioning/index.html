Our Story

Corken Good Heating & Air Conditioning is part of the Corken Steel Products Company family, a family-owned business with deep roots in Covington, Kentucky.

1955: It Starts in Covington

Four Corken brothers, a cousin, and a friend open Corken Steel in Covington, KY, supplying sheet metal products to the roofing industry.

The Move Into HVAC

The founders realize many roofing customers are skilled mechanics who need winter work, so they start stocking furnace fittings and an equipment line, entering the HVACR market.

Growing Across the Tri-State

The business grows into a multi-location HVACR distributor serving Kentucky, western Ohio, and eastern Indiana, with fabrication shops, technical staff, and deep inventory.

Today: Corken Good

Corken Good Heating & Air Conditioning serves Covington from 200 W Rivercenter Blvd, carrying forward the family philosophy: the answer is yes, now what is the question?
