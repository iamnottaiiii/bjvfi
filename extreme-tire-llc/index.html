Our Story

About Extreme Tire Llc

Extreme Tire Llc is an independent tire shop on Woolper Avenue in Cincinnati's Northside neighborhood. It is the kind of shop where the work speaks plainly: tires mounted, balanced, rotated, and repaired for everyday drivers and work trucks alike.

Reviewers mention reasonable prices and speedy service, and one driver credits the shop owner with helping push a broken down car out of rush hour traffic. That is the reputation the shop is building, one tire at a time.

Walk in with a flat, a worn set, or a truck you want set up for rougher roads, and the crew will get you sorted. Sample
