About Cavazos Barbershop


Cavazos Barbershop is a neighborhood barbershop in East Los Angeles built around sharp cuts and honest service. Walk in for a classic cut, a skin fade, or a hot towel shave, and leave looking sharp.


Every appointment starts with a consultation: what you like, what you do not, and how much upkeep you want. The result is a cut that looks sharp on day one and grows out clean.


Find us at 5924 Whittier Blvd, East Los Angeles, CA 90022, or call (323) 269-6659 to book your chair.
