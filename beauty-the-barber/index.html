Our Story

beauty meets the barber

Beauty and the Barber brings two traditions together under one roof at 6477 Fruit St in Huntsville, Logan County, Ohio. One side of the craft polishes and styles; the other sharpens and defines.

Whether you are after a refined haircut, a careful styling session, or classic barber detailing, you will find unhurried, personal service and an eye for the finish.
