Services

Everything your home needs to shine.

One team for cleaning and small maintenance, so your whole to do list gets shorter in a single visit.

Regular House Cleaning

Weekly, biweekly, or monthly visits covering kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, and living areas. Dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and surfaces, every time.

Deep Cleaning

A detailed top to bottom clean: baseboards, grout lines, inside cabinets and appliances, ceiling fans, and window sills. Perfect for seasonal resets.

Move In and Move Out Cleaning

Empty-home cleaning that leaves every room ready for keys to change hands. Cabinets, closets, floors, and fixtures detailed throughout.

Apartment Cleaning

Focused cleans for apartments and condos, sized to your space. Great for busy schedules and rental turnovers.
