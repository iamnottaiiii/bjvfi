Company brief

About Enduracrete

Enduracrete is the concrete services division of MARBLELIFE of Cincinnati, based at 106 Pike Street in Bromley, Kentucky. The team specializes in transforming concrete surfaces through repair, coating, polishing, and protection.

MARBLELIFE describes itself as a science company serving homes and businesses, part of a network of 55 franchises across the continental United States. The company manufactures its own products and works in surface services, a combination it says is unique in the industry.

The local Bromley operation carries a BBB rating of A and serves the greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky area.
