About

The story of Headliners Barbershop

Headliners Barbershop on South Inglewood Avenue keeps things simple: a clean shop, friendly staff and solid fades. It is a popular stop for parents bringing kids in for a trim.

Located at 1148 S Inglewood Ave, Inglewood, CA 90301, the shop serves Inglewood and the surrounding neighborhoods. Walk-ins and regulars are both welcome, and the team is happy to talk through the cut before the clippers come out.
