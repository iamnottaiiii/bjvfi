Chapter one · The craft

Old school standards, modern technique

Every head that sits in the chair at D' REYES BARBESHOP gets the same treatment: a careful consult, a deliberate cut, and a finish checked in the mirror from every angle. No rushed clipper passes, no one-look-fits-all.

Fades are blended to disappear. Lineups are drawn razor straight. Beards are sculpted to frame the face. It is the kind of barbering that gets noticed across the room and holds up weeks later.

The story

A neighborhood shop on N 10th

D' REYES BARBESHOP holds down its corner of Allentown at 501 N 10th St, serving the streets around it with barbering built on repeat business. The formula is simple: take every cut seriously, keep the prices fair, and treat every client like the regular they are about to become.

The shop's chairs see fades, designs, beard sculpts, and first haircuts alike. Walk-ins are welcome, regulars get remembered, and the clippers stay sharp. When you need to look your best for the week ahead, this is the stop.
