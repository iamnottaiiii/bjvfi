About

A small Carteret fuel company that treats customers like neighbors.

Who we are

Small crew, direct line.

C & C Oil & Burner Co operates out of 44 Clauss St in Carteret, NJ, as a small fuel oil distributor and heating contractor. With a crew of just a handful of people, the person you talk to on the phone is close to the person who shows up at your door.

That scale is the point. No call centers, no corporate script. When reviewers say the team goes out of their way to help, that is what a small shop looks like at its best.

What we believe

Show up, do the job, be straight.

Heating oil is not glamorous work. It is winter work: deliveries on schedule, burners fixed before the house gets cold, honest answers about what something will cost.

Most of our reviews describe exactly that: friendly, professional service and deliveries that arrive as promised. We aim to make every visit one of those.
