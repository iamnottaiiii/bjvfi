What we do

One trade, done right

Pendleton Roofing focuses on roofs: the inspections that catch problems early, the repairs that stop leaks, and the full replacements that protect your home or business for decades.

01

Roof Replacement

Full tear-offs and re-roofs with quality shingles and underlayment, installed to handle lake-effect snow and wind.

02

Roof Repair

Leaks, missing shingles, flashing failures, and storm damage. Fast, targeted repairs that stop small problems from becoming big ones.

03

Roof Inspections

A thorough look at shingles, flashing, gutters and ventilation, with a straight report on what your roof actually needs.

04

Storm Response

Buffalo weather is rough on roofs. When wind, ice or falling branches do damage, we respond and secure your home.

05

Commercial Roofing

06

Gutters & Flashing

Gutter work, drip edge and flashing details that keep water moving away from your roof and foundation.

Western New York puts roofs through freeze-thaw cycles, heavy snow loads and driving rain. A roof that is inspected, maintained and repaired on time costs far less than the water damage it prevents.
