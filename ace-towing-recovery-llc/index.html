About ACE Towing & Recovery

ACE Towing & Recovery LLC is a family owned and operated towing and auto repair shop in Cincinnati, Ohio, located at 3212 Dickinson Ave.

We are family owned and operated. Newly opening. We offer towing and auto repair. We also buy all junked or unwanted vehicles, we offer $60 to $500 depending on the year, make and model, the condition, if it has a title or not, and if it runs and drives.

The quote above is the business's own description from its public listing. As a newly opened shop, we are building our name one tow and one repair at a time across Cincinnati.

Need a tow, a repair, or cash for a junk vehicle? Call (513) 596-2034 during shop hours.
