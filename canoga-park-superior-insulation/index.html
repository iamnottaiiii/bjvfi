Our story

A Valley contractor that knows Valley heat.

Canoga Park Superior Insulation is an insulation contractor based at 7939 Canoga Ave in Canoga Park, California. We work the neighborhoods we live in: Canoga Park, Winnetka, Woodland Hills, Reseda, and the surrounding San Fernando Valley.

Many Valley homes were built between the 1950s and 1980s, and their insulation has settled, compressed, or been tunneled through by rodents. We have seen the same pattern hundreds of times: an upstairs that runs hot, an AC that never shuts off, and bills that climb even when the thermostat never moves. Our work starts with diagnosing that pattern and ends with an attic that performs the way current energy standards intend.

A

Diagnose first

We look at insulation depth, air leaks, and ventilation before recommending anything, so you only pay for work you need.

B

Seal, then insulate

Insulation slows heat flow; air sealing stops air movement. The combination is what actually lowers bills.

C

Built for Title 24

Installations follow California energy standards, with documentation you can keep for permits or resale.
