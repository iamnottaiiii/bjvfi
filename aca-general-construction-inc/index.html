Our story

A contractor you can actually reach.

ACA General Construction, Inc. was built on a simple idea: show up, do the work right, and answer the phone. Based in Agua Dulce at 35303 Mills Ave, we are a California licensed general contractor (CSLB License 1057000, licensed August 2019) serving homeowners across the San Fernando and Santa Clarita valleys.

We stay intentionally hands on. The person who walks your job is involved in your job, which is why so many of our customers call us back for a second and third project.

We carry workers compensation insurance and a $25,000 contractor surety bond, so your project and your property are protected from day one.
