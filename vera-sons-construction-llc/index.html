Finish levels, explained

Every wall finished to its level

Drywall finishing runs from Level 1 to Level 5. The right level depends on your lighting, paint, and texture. Tell us about your project and we will recommend the finish that fits.

1

Tape Set

All joints and angles have tape set in joint compound. Standard for areas hidden from view, like attics and service corridors.

2

One Coat

A thin coat of compound over the tape. The base for tile and other heavy coverings in garages and storage areas.

3

Two Coats

An extra coat over tape and fasteners, ready for heavy texture or commercial-grade wall coverings.

4

Smooth Standard

The classic smooth finish for flat paint and light texture in most homes and offices.

5

Showcase Smooth

A thin skim coat over the whole surface. The highest quality finish for glossy paint and critical lighting.

Drywall services

The core work of a drywall contractor, from bare studs to a paint-ready wall. Services below are typical of the trade. Please call to confirm what is offered for your project.

Drywall Installation

Measuring, cutting, and hanging drywall sheets on walls and ceilings, with openings cut cleanly for doors, outlets, and fixtures.

Taping and Finishing

Tape, mud, and sanding carried through the finish levels, from a basic Level 1 to a glass-smooth Level 5.

Repairs and Patching

Holes, cracks, water damage, and nail pops cut out and blended back so the repair disappears into the wall.

Texture Application

Orange peel, knockdown, skip trowel, and other textures matched to your existing walls or applied fresh.

Ceilings

Ceiling drywall hung and finished smooth, including repairs to sagging or damaged ceiling sections.

Paint-Ready Prep

Final sanding and touch-ups so the surface takes primer and paint evenly, with no flashing or ridges.
