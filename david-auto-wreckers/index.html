About

The shop behind the name

David Auto Wreckers at 1368 E Bay Ave. in the Bronx has served local drivers since 2000 as a licensed vehicle dismantler (NY DMV #7079320).

The yard stocks used parts for all years, makes and models, buys junk cars for cash, runs a tire shop with new and used tires, and partners with an on-site body shop for collision repair.
