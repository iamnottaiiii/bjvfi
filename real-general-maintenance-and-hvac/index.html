Who we are

The maintenance-first
HVAC shop

REAL GENERAL MAINTENANCE AND HVAC is based on Valerio St in Van Nuys and works across the San Fernando Valley. Our name says what we believe: most HVAC headaches are maintenance problems first, and repair problems second.

That shapes how we work. We would rather tune up your system twice a year than meet you on the hottest Saturday of the summer with a dead compressor. And when something does break, we diagnose it straight and fix what is actually wrong.

We are a working contractor, not a call center. When you dial (818) 844-7405, you reach people who do the work.
