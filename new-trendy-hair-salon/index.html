Our story

A neighborhood chair on 71st Street.

New Trendy Hair Salon is a local hair salon in the heart of Bensonhurst, Brooklyn, serving neighbors from Bay Ridge, Dyker Heights and Bath Beach.

The salon is a New York State licensed appearance enhancement business, which means it meets the state's standards for cosmetology services. The focus is simple: listen to what you want, deliver a clean result, and keep the visit comfortable.

Whether it is a quick trim before work or a full color change for the season, you are welcome to walk in or call ahead.
