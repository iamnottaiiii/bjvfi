Our Story

International Auto Care was first established in 1997 in the DMV area. With over 25 years of experience, it is one of the highest rated auto shops in the area.

The shop takes pride in offering customers the highest quality service, using the best and most up to date diagnostic machinery to diagnose your automotive concerns on your initial visit.

Owner Masood and the team work on all car makes and models. Customers describe the crew as friendly, honest, reliable, and reasonable, with many returning for years and recommending the shop to friends and family.
