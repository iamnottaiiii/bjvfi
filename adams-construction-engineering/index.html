Our story

An engineer with a tool belt.

Adam's Construction and Engineering was built on a simple operating principle: one project at a time. Adam is an experienced engineer and a hands-on builder, and he is on site every day while your project is underway.

General engineering plus building contractor: the planning rigor of engineering with the accountability of a builder who shows up daily.

That combination shapes every job, from a single bathroom to a custom home. Projects are scoped carefully, scheduled honestly, and worked continuously until they are complete. No bouncing between sites, no weeks of silence, no downtime from start to finish.

Based at 725 N Catalina St in Burbank, serving homeowners across Burbank and the surrounding area.
