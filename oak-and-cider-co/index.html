Our shop

About Oak and Cider Co.

Oak and Cider Co. is a woodworking business based in Jefferson, Massachusetts. The shop focuses on custom furniture and carpentry: pieces measured, cut, and finished by hand for the homes they will live in.

Because every commission is different, we keep the process simple: you describe what you need, we talk through wood, dimensions, and finish, and then we build it.

How we work

From idea to finished piece

1. Talk. Call or message with your idea, measurements, and any inspiration photos.
2. Plan. We agree on wood, dimensions, finish, and timeline before any sawdust flies.
3. Build. Your piece is built in our Jefferson shop and finished to last.
