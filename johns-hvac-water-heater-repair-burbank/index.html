Our Story

we fix it right.

John's HVAC & Water Heater Repair Burbank is a Burbank-based heating contractor with a simple focus: water heaters, furnaces, and air conditioning for local homes, done properly.

What we believe

Three rules on every job

Honest diagnosis

We find the actual fault and tell you what it is, in plain language. If a repair is not worth it, we say so.

Repair before replace

When a fix will safely extend the life of your equipment, that is what we recommend. Replacement only when it earns it.

Respect for your home

Clean work area, shoe covers where it matters, and a system tested with you before we leave.

What to expect

From first call to fixed

  • A real person answers. Tell us the symptoms: no hot water, cold house, strange noise. We will ask a few questions and set a visit.
  • You approve before we touch a wrench. We explain the fault, the fix, and the cost. Nothing happens without your okay.
  • Fixed and tested. We complete the repair, test the system with you, and make sure it is doing its job.
  • Local and reachable. Based at 1611 N San Fernando Blvd Ste G in Burbank. If something is not right after the visit, call us back.
