About

A South Philly painter with a simple standard

CP Painting LLC is a painting contractor based at 1107 Daly Street in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, serving homeowners across the city.

The work follows one rule: do the unglamorous part properly. That means scraping what is loose, filling what is cracked, and priming what is bare, before the finish coat ever comes out of the can.

Every job ends with a walkthrough. If an edge is not clean or a spot was missed, it gets fixed before we pack up.
