Our Services

Select a flooring type to learn more. Installation for residential and commercial spaces in Van Nuys and the San Fernando Valley.

Carpet Installation

Wall-to-wall carpet installed over fresh pad, with seams placed to disappear and edges finished clean against every wall and threshold.

  • Bedrooms, living rooms, and stairs
  • Carpet removal and haul-away
  • Pad replacement and subfloor prep

Hardwood Flooring

Solid and engineered hardwood, nailed or glued down, sanded and finished for a surface that ages beautifully instead of wearing out.

  • Solid and engineered hardwood installation
  • Refinishing of existing wood floors
  • Transitions and baseboard detailing

Luxury Vinyl Plank and Tile

Waterproof, scratch-resistant vinyl that handles kids, pets, and rental turnover while looking like real wood or stone.

  • LVP and LVT click-lock and glue-down
  • Kitchens, baths, and high-traffic areas
  • Underlayment and moisture barriers

Tile Flooring

Ceramic and porcelain tile set level and grouted tight, for entries, kitchens, bathrooms, and anywhere water shows up.

  • Ceramic and porcelain tile installation
  • Demolition of old tile and thinset
  • Grout and caulk finishing

Floor Repairs

Targeted fixes instead of full replacements: stretched carpet, popped planks, cracked tile, and squeaky subfloors.

  • Carpet re-stretching and patching
  • Plank and tile replacement
  • Subfloor and squeak repair
