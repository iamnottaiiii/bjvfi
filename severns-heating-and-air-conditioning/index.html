Preventive Maintenance

The cheapest repair is the one you never need. Seasonal tune-ups catch small problems early, keep energy bills down, and extend the life of your system by years.

  • Seasonal AC tune-ups
  • Seasonal heating tune-ups
  • Filter replacement programs
  • Electrical and refrigerant inspections
  • System performance reports
  • Priority scheduling for plan members

Indoor Air Quality

Comfort is more than temperature. Cleaner air means fewer allergens, less dust, and a healthier home, especially during the months the system runs constantly.

  • Whole-home air filtration
  • UV germicidal lights
  • Humidity control and dehumidifiers
  • Duct inspection and sealing
  • Ventilation improvements

Our Story

A local HVAC crew on Ritchie Highway

Severn's Heating and Air Conditioning is an air conditioning contractor based at 546 Ritchie Highway in Severna Park, Maryland. We are the kind of shop where the person who answers the phone knows the streets you live on, because we drive them every day.

Anne Arundel County homes ask a lot of their heating and cooling systems: humid summers, freezing snaps, and older houses with ductwork that has seen things. We show up, diagnose honestly, and fix it right the first time. That is the whole business model.

Open when you need us

Seven days a week, 7:00 AM to 8:30 PM. Heating and cooling problems do not keep office hours, so neither do we.

Honest diagnosis

If a twenty dollar part fixes it, that is what we will tell you. We would rather earn your trust than sell you a system.

Rooted in Severna Park

We serve Severna Park and the surrounding Anne Arundel County communities. Local work, local reputation, local accountability.
