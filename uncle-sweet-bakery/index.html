A Neighborhood Bakery

Like family, but with better bread.

Uncle Sweet & Bakery is a Middle Eastern bakery and sweet shop on Harlem Avenue in Worth, Illinois. The shop describes itself simply: a place for popular sweets, shrak bread, and taboon bread, the everyday foods of home.

Walk in and you will find breads coming off the stone and trays of sweets cooling in the case. It is the kind of bakery neighbors stop into on the way home, and the kind they drive across town for when a celebration needs a proper dessert tray.

Come taste what the neighborhood already knows: fresh bread, honest sweets, and a warm welcome.
