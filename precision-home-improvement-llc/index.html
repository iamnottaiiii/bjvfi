Our story

Small crew, long memory

Precision Home Improvement LLC began serving Burlington in 2012, and the model has not changed since: a one to four person crew, no subcontractors, and the owner on every job.

That structure is a deliberate choice. When the same hands quote the work and do the work, nothing gets lost between the estimate and the execution. Customers notice, and the business has run on that reputation across Boone County for over a decade.

The terms are plain too. Estimates are free. The bill is not due until the job is complete in most cases. Every project carries a 5-year warranty. It is an old-fashioned way of doing business, and it still works.
