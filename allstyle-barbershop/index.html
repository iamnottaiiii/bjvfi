Our story

A Verona fixture for two decades

Allstyle Barbershop has served Bloomfield Avenue for 20 years, building its name one chair at a time. Owner Sammy leads a crew that includes longtime barbers such as Joe, and Master Barber Sharon, with over 20 years of experience behind her shears.

What keeps customers coming back is simple: the shop listens. Walk in with a picture or just a description, and the barbers take their time to get it right. No rushed chairs, no surprises at the register, just fair prices and careful detail work.

Families are welcome. Sons get their first cuts here, neighbors park in the rear lot on Pine Street, and nobody needs an appointment to sit down. That is the Allstyle way: a professional, welcoming shop that treats every head of hair like it matters.
