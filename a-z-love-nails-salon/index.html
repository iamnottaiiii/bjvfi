About

A salon built on regulars

A&Z Love Nails Salon sits on Avenue U in Gravesend, Brooklyn, and runs on the oldest model in the book: do good nails, charge fairly, and people come back.

The room

Clean first, everything else second

The word that comes up most in reviews is clean. A tidy, professional room where the tools, the stations and the service all feel cared for. "Amazing pedicure, this place is so clean," as one reviewer put it.

The people

Techs with regulars of their own

Most clients have their nail tech. Names like Anna and Angel come up in reviews. The team is described as expert, nice and patient, and honest about matching you to the right tech for nail art.

The rhythm

Monthly, like clockwork

"I come here once a month to get gel nails." That is the typical cadence: steady clients, steady chairs, a salon that earns its keep one set at a time.

The deal

Fair prices, no drama

Third-party listings describe it as professional and affordable. Extensions and gel services land in the moderate range, and walk-ins are welcome most days.
