Our story

A Hebron fence crew, plain and simple

American Fence LLC. works out of 2291 Petersburg Road in Hebron, Kentucky, right in the middle of the Boone County neighborhoods we serve. We are a fence contractor, full stop: no side gigs, no bait-and-switch, just fences done right.

The Better Business Bureau gives us its A+ rating, and we build with US-made materials that meet industry standards. We talk you through vinyl, chain link and wood honestly, helping you pick the material and color that fits your property and your budget, not our sales quota.

We serve residential and commercial customers across Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky. If your current fence just needs some love, our repair, power washing, staining and painting services bring it back to like-new. And if you are the do-it-yourself type, we will sell you the materials and point you in the right direction.
