Garys Junk Removal Services is a family-owned junk company based in Waterbury, Connecticut, built on fair prices, fast response, and doing the job right.

Who we are

Garys Junk Removal Services has spent about six years hauling for neighbors across Connecticut. We are a small, family-run crew: the person who answers the phone is the person who shows up with the truck.

Customers describe us the same way again and again: responsive, professional, punctual, and very reasonable on pricing.

Fair, upfront pricing. Charged per pickup truckload, quoted free before we start.

Speed. Same-day service on many jobs, and we are usually the first to respond.

Respect for your property. We load carefully, sweep up, and leave the space clean.

Honest work. Cash and Zelle accepted, no hidden fees, no games.
