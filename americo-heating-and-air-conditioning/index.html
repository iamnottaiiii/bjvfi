Maintenance plans

Most breakdowns give warning signs first. Regular maintenance catches them early, keeps efficiency up, and extends the life of your equipment.

Seasonal checkupsHeating check in the fall, cooling check in the spring, timed to the weather.
Filter replacementClean filters protect your system and your lungs. We handle the schedule.
Coil and blower cleaningRemoving buildup that forces your system to work harder than it should.
Performance testingMeasuring temperatures, pressures, and airflow to confirm everything is in spec.

Ductwork inspectionChecking ducts for leaks, disconnections, and blockages that waste energy.

Airflow balancingEvening out hot and cold spots from room to room.
Humidity guidanceAdvice on managing dry winter air and muggy summer air indoors.

About us

A heating and cooling company rooted in Highland Park

Americo Heating and Air Conditioning is based at 5921 York Blvd in the Highland Park neighborhood of Los Angeles, California. We focus on one thing: keeping the indoor temperature right, whatever the weather is doing outside.

Los Angeles asks a lot of its HVAC systems. Long hot stretches push air conditioners to their limits, and the occasional cold snap reminds everyone that heaters need attention too. We service both sides of that equation, so our customers deal with one company all year.

How we work

  • Listen first. You describe the symptom, and we ask the right questions before we touch a wrench.
  • Explain clearly. We show you what we found and lay out your options in plain language.
  • Fix it right. We do the work you approve, test the system, and make sure it is running properly before we leave.
