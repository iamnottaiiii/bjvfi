03 · Our Story

A local shop for two stubborn problems

Based on S Church St in Mount Laurel Township, we work on the two things that quietly wear a house down: the chimney and hidden moisture.

A chimney is easy to ignore until it isn't. So is mold. We exist for the moment a homeowner decides to stop ignoring both.

A&M Restoring-Chimney's & Mold Removal Services In Mount Laurel NJ is based at 1045 S Church St in Mount Laurel Township, NJ. The pairing is deliberate: leaky chimney crowns, cracked flue liners, and failing flashing are some of the most common ways water gets into a house, and where water goes, mold follows. One visit can look at the whole chain, from the top of the stack to the damp corner of the basement.
