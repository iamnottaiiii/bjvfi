About the yard

Roots

The HP2X operation in Cleves

HP2X runs its construction and equipment operations from the Harrison Pike yard in Cleves, OH, serving commercial, industrial, and infrastructure work across Hamilton County and the Cincinnati area.

The Fiori discovery

Searching for larger-volume site dumpers

When moving material the old way took too much time, HP2X went looking for larger-volume site dumpers and discovered the Fiori product line. VP and co-owner Harvey Pelley knew immediately the Fiori D40 was right for tight jobsites.

Doubling down

One dumper becomes a fleet

The first D40 improved operations so much that HP2X bought a second within two weeks. Pelley noted the company held the largest Fiori fleet in North America at the time, and the reversible dumpers helped finish a job two weeks ahead of schedule.

Concrete, self-loaded

DB460 mixer plus mobile silo

HP2X then invested in a Fiori DB460 self-loading concrete batch vehicle and a mobile horizontal cement silo, raising efficiency on bigger pours.

"We work in Ohio. Winters can be harsh here, especially driving around in an open vehicle at 20 degrees."

Harvey Pelley, HP2X Vice President and Co-owner, quoted in Construction Equipment Guide
