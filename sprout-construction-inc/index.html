Our story

Sprout Construction was founded in 1986 by Springer Goyne and Mike Stout as a part time business building decks, performing handyman services, and finishing basements.

The company grew from those small jobs into building additions, decks, kitchens, and whole house renovations. Along the way it became a qualified contractor for Arundel Community Development Services, helping remodel homes for Anne Arundel County residents, and served as a preferred installer for a major lumber retailer from the late 1990s through 2008, installing doors and windows throughout the Baltimore-Washington area.

Today Sprout Construction focuses on its own customers requests for remodeling, repairs, and additions, plus ACDS projects, and is subcontracted for warranty service to several window and door manufacturers.
