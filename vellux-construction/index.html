Our story

A local construction company built on craft, honesty, and showing up.

Rooted in La Cañada Flintridge

Vellux Construction is based at 4546 Rockland Pl in La Cañada Flintridge, California. We serve homeowners and businesses across the foothill communities with remodeling, additions, and new construction.

We are a small, hands-on team. That means the person who walks your project on day one is the person answering your calls on day thirty. No handoffs, no vanishing acts.

Every job starts with a free walkthrough and a written estimate, and ends with a walkthrough you sign off on. That is the whole business model.
