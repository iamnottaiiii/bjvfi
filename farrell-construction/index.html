Our story

Honesty, integrity, and a strict timeline

Farrell Construction is a locally owned and operated company based at 1 E Park Terrace #3 in Worcester, MA. The company values honesty and integrity, and treats your home as if it were its own.

With over 25 years of experience in residential construction and 10 years in large-scale commercial construction, Farrell handles everything from kitchen and bath remodels to additions and commercial construction management. A licensed contractor since 2006, the team brings the confidence of decades on the job to every estimate.

The goal on every project is the same: competitive pricing, close attention to detail, a fair quote that is honored, and a finished job you are satisfied with, so the relationship lasts longer than the build.
