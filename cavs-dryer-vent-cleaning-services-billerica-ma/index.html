What we do

A dryer that works the way it should

Over time, lint sneaks past the trap and builds up inside the vent. Airflow drops, drying cycles stretch out, and energy bills climb. A professional cleaning restores the full airflow the dryer was built for.

  • Full-length vent cleaning, dryer to outside cap
  • Vent inspections that find clogs, kinks and bird nests
  • Repair and replacement for crushed or leaky vent runs
All services

What a visit looks like

  1. Airflow check. We measure how the vent is breathing before we start.
  2. Full cleanout. Rotating brushes and vacuum clear the entire vent run.
  3. Cap and connection check. Outside cap, elbows and dryer connection all get inspected.
  4. Final test. Airflow checked again, work area left clean.

How often should a vent be cleaned?

Most households benefit from a professional cleaning about once a year. Homes with pets, large families, or older flexible vent runs may need it more often. If clothes suddenly take two cycles to dry, do not wait for the calendar, call.

Not sure? A quick airflow check will tell you.
