Our story

Rooted in Winchester

Country Landscape & Design Inc is a local landscaping company based on Middlesex Street in Winchester, Massachusetts. We serve homeowners across Winchester and the nearby towns with design, planting, and year-round grounds care.

Our approach is simple: walk the property, listen to what the homeowner wants, and do the work the way we would on our own yard. Clear estimates up front, tidy crews, and plantings chosen to thrive in New England conditions.
