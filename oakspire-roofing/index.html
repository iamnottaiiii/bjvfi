Services

Everything your roof needs

From a single leak to a complete replacement, one call covers it. Every job starts with a free inspection and a written estimate.

Full roof replacement

Complete tear-off, new underlayment, flashing, and shingles. We help you pick materials that fit your home, your budget, and the foothill climate.

Repairs and leak fixes

Missing or damaged shingles, lifted flashing, cracked vents, and active leaks. Most repairs are completed in a single visit.

Inspections and reports

Detailed roof inspections with photos, ideal before buying or selling a home or after a wind or rain storm.

Maintenance plans

Seasonal checkups, debris clearing, sealant touch-ups, and gutter-adjacent care that add years to your roof.
