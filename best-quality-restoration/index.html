Our services

Restoration Services in Pasadena

We handle private residential homes and professional commercial locations, big or small. From corner cafes to multi-level corporate offices, we are ready to restore.

Fast extraction stops damage from spreading. We respond to:

  • Tub, toilet, and sink pipe breaks
  • Flooding and storm water intrusion
  • Fire sprinkler system discharges
  • Leaking washers and water heaters
  • Dishwasher malfunction sprays
  • Slab leaks and foundation or roof leaks

Sewage backups are a health hazard and must be handled immediately and safely. Do not wait or try to handle it alone. We remove contaminated water, sanitize the area, and dispose of unsalvageable materials.

Our service techs bring the right equipment to dehumidify and sanitize. Thorough drying and antimicrobial treatment keep mold from taking hold after a water event.

Water emergencies cause extraordinary delays in workspace areas. We handle professional locations of any size and work to get your business back up quickly with minimal downtime.

We offer direct billing to your insurance company and have helped customers handle the claims coverage process since 2008. We photograph, document, and itemize all the details so you do not have to chase paperwork.
