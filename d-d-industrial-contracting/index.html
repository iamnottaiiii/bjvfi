Our story

Three decades of setting steel in Kentucky.

D & D Industrial Contracting, Inc. was formed in Kentucky in 1994 and set up its headquarters on Mullen Drive in Walton around 1995, where it still operates today. From that shop, a crew of more than 30 has grown the company into a regional industrial contractor with roughly $10 million in annual work, per business records.

Leadership comes from president Shawn P. Murray and the Childress family, and the company has kept its focus where it started: conveyors, millwright work, and material handling for plants that cannot afford downtime. Much of the work runs through Kentucky's distilled spirits industry, and the company has grown its own equipment fleet, from 2 forklifts to 20, to keep jobs moving without waiting on rentals.
