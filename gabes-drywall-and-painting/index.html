Services

Drywall and painting, under one roof

Tap a service to see what is included. Every job starts with a free estimate.

New drywall for remodels, additions, basements, and garages. Hung true, taped and finished to a level that is ready for paint.

  • New construction and remodels
  • Basement and garage finishing
  • Moisture-resistant board where it counts
  • Clean, paint-ready surfaces
Sample pricing: most single-room installs start in the low hundreds. Final price depends on size, board type, and finish level. Free estimates always.

From doorknob holes to full panel replacements, we patch it so the repair disappears into the wall.

  • Holes, dents, and cracks
  • Water damage cut-outs and replacement
  • Corner bead and seam repairs
  • Nail pops and settling cracks
Sample pricing: small patches often start under $150. Larger repairs are quoted on site, free of charge.

Match what you have or start fresh. We blend repairs into existing texture and retexture whole rooms and ceilings.

  • Orange peel and knockdown
  • Smooth wall and ceiling finishes
  • Popcorn texture removal
  • Ceiling repairs and retexturing
Sample pricing: popcorn removal is typically priced per square foot. Call for a sample range for your ceiling size.

Crisp lines, even coverage, and a finish that holds up. Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and cabinets.

  • Single rooms and whole homes
  • Ceilings, trim, and doors
  • Cabinet refinishing
  • Color consultations by phone or in person
Sample pricing: single rooms often start around $300 to $500 depending on size and prep. Free written estimates.

Curb appeal that stands up to Kentucky weather, with proper prep before a single drop of paint goes on.

  • Siding, brick, and stucco
  • Power washing and surface prep
  • Caulking and minor wood repair
  • Weather-resistant coatings
Sample pricing: exteriors vary widely by size and condition. A free on-site estimate is the only honest quote.

Our story

A local crew for Covington homes

Gabe's Drywall & Painting is a Covington, Kentucky contractor built around a simple idea: one crew that can take a room from bare studs to a finished, painted space without a parade of subcontractors.

We work across Northern Kentucky on the jobs other crews rush: the patch that has to disappear, the texture that has to match, the trim line that has to be crisp. Drywall and painting are partner trades, and doing both under one roof means the finish work gets the attention it deserves.
