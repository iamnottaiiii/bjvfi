Why chimneys need a pro

Creosote buildup is the leading cause of chimney fires. A yearly sweep and inspection is the simplest, cheapest protection your fireplace can get.

  • Creosote and soot removal
  • Firebox and damper service

Chimney Sweeping

The core of chimney safety: removing flammable creosote and soot so your fireplace drafts properly and burns efficiently.

  • Full flue and liner brushing
  • Smoke chamber and smoke shelf cleaning
  • Firebox, damper, and ash removal
  • Blockage and nesting debris clearing
Sample pricing: most single flue sweeps fall in the $150 to $250 range depending on condition and access. Final quote always given before work begins.

Chimney Inspections

A trained eye over every part of the system, from the firebox to the crown, with photos and a plain language report.

  • Annual safety inspections
  • Real estate and pre purchase inspections
  • Video scan of flue interior
  • Written report with photo documentation
Sample pricing: standalone inspections typically $99 to $175. Inspections are often discounted when bundled with a sweep.

Chimney Repair and Masonry

Connecticut freeze and thaw cycles are hard on brick and mortar. Repairs stop small damage from becoming a rebuild.

  • Tuckpointing and mortar repointing
  • Crown repair and rebuilding
  • Waterproofing treatments
  • Flashing repair and leak diagnosis
Sample pricing: minor repointing often starts around $300; crowns and rebuilds are quoted per project after inspection.

Chimney Caps and Liners

Caps keep rain, animals, and sparks where they belong. Liners protect the masonry and keep flue gases moving safely out.

  • Stainless steel chimney cap installation
  • Top sealing damper upgrades
  • Stainless liner relining
  • Gas and wood appliance venting
Sample pricing: quality caps installed typically $250 to $450; liner projects are quoted after a flue video scan.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Lint packed dryer vents are a fire hazard and make dryers work twice as hard. A full line cleaning restores airflow in under an hour.

  • Full vent line rotary cleaning
  • Exterior termination inspection
  • Airflow testing after service
  • Bird guard installation available
Sample pricing: most dryer vent cleanings run $120 to $200 depending on vent length and roof access.
