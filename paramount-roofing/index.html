When the roof talks, listen

Stains on the ceiling, shingles in the yard after Santa Ana winds, a drip that only shows up in a storm. Small signs now save big repairs later.

A roof rarely fails all at once. It wears, loosens, and lets water in a little at a time, and by the time you see the damage inside, the fix is bigger than it needed to be. A quick inspection catches the small stuff: lifted shingles, tired flashing, clogged valleys.
