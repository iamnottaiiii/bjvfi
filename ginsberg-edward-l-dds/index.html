Why pediatric dentistry matters.

Children are not just small adults. Pediatric dentists have extra training in caring for growing teeth, guiding dental development, and helping anxious young patients feel at ease.

Kid-friendly visits

Pediatric offices are arranged with children in mind, using approaches that help kids feel comfortable during exams.

Growth guidance

Monitoring how teeth and jaws develop, and spotting bite or alignment issues early.

Prevention first

Cleanings, fluoride, and sealants that protect young teeth before problems start.

Gentle treatment

Fillings and other treatments delivered with patience for young patients.

Parent partnership

Guidance for parents on brushing, diet, and habits that shape lifelong oral health.

Special needs care

Pediatric dentists are trained to care for children with special health care needs.

Pediatric dental services.

General descriptions of pediatric dental care. Call (410) 788-4555 to confirm which services this office currently offers.

  1. 1

    Exams and cleanings for kids

    Regular checkups sized for children, with cleanings that keep young teeth healthy and visits that build good habits.

  2. 2

    Fluoride and sealants

    Preventive treatments that strengthen enamel and shield molars from decay during the cavity-prone years.

  3. 3

    Tooth-colored fillings

    Repairing cavities in baby and permanent teeth with materials that blend in naturally.

  4. 4

    Growth and bite monitoring

    Tracking jaw and tooth development to catch orthodontic concerns at the right time.

  5. 5

    Emergency care for injuries

    Prompt attention for knocked-out, chipped, or painful teeth after playground and sports accidents.

Service descriptions are general information about pediatric dentistry, not promises of specific treatments at this office. Fees are not published online.
