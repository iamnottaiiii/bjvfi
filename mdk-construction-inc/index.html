About us

A local crew that stands behind its work

MDK Construction Inc is a general contractor in Littlerock, California, building and improving homes across the Antelope Valley.

Contracting the way it should be

Ask around the Antelope Valley about contractors and you will hear the same complaints: no-shows, vague pricing, jobs that drag on for months. MDK was built to be the opposite of that.

We are a local company, based on E Ave S in Littlerock. Our reputation is earned one driveway, one kitchen, one addition at a time, and in a town this size, reputation is everything.

Every project gets a clear scope in writing, a realistic schedule, and a crew that treats your property with respect. If something is not right, we make it right. That is the whole business model.

  • Written scope and schedule before work begins
  • Daily cleanup and protection of your home
  • One point of contact from start to finish
  • Final walkthrough on every job, no exceptions

Show up on time, do what you said you would do, and leave it cleaner than you found it.
