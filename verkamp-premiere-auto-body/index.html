About

A Craftsmanship Atelier

Est. Newport KY
Photo coming soon

Verkamp Premiere Auto Body is a small, family run body shop at 602 Dayton St in Newport, Kentucky. Public records list John Verkamp as the owner, and reviewers describe an honest, hard working family that gives fair prices and stands by its word.

Rather than a high volume production line, the shop works like an atelier: each vehicle is assessed individually, repaired carefully, and finished with attention to how it looks rolling out the door. Customers mention being driven to and from work while their car is in the shop, and repairs finished in days rather than weeks.

If you want straight talk about what your car needs and what it does not, this is the shop to call. SAMPLE
