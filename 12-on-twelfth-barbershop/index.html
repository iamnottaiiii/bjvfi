About

A Neighborhood Shop With Campus Energy

12 On Twelfth Barbershop sits at 12 E 12th Ave in Columbus, right in the campus district. It is a mostly walk-in shop known for a lively, friendly atmosphere, talkative barbers, and cuts that keep students, families, and longtime regulars coming back.

The crew handles everything from everyday trims to detailed fades and beard work, with a reputation for being great with kids and first timers.
