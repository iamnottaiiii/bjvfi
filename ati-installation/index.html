What to expect on install day

A little prep makes the visit fast and smooth.

  • Clear a path from the door to the install spot
  • Know where your water shutoff is
  • An adult (18+) must be home
  • Keep pets in another room during the visit
  • Measure doorways if the unit is large
  • Ask for any extra charges in writing first
Good to know: the installer will test the appliance before leaving. Run it once yourself while they are there, so any issue gets caught on the spot.
About

The crew behind the install

ATI Installation is an appliance installation contractor based in Sylmar, serving homes across the San Fernando Valley.

Installers, not salespeople

ATI Installation specializes in one thing: getting major home appliances delivered, hooked up and running. The company works out of 12457 Gladstone Ave h in Sylmar and installs appliances for homeowners across the valley.

ATI also handles installs through major retailer installation programs in the area, which is how many customers first meet the crew: the store sells the appliance, and ATI shows up to install it.

Local directories list the company as serving the San Fernando Valley for over two decades. Hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM.

How a typical install works

  1. You buy the appliance. From a retailer or on your own, pick the unit that fits your space.
  2. We schedule the visit. Call (818) 837-3430 to line up a day and time.
  3. We install and connect. Hookup, leveling, water and gas lines, venting, and a full test run.
  4. We haul the old one away. Old unit out, packaging cleared, room left clean.
