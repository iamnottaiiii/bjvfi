Our story

A Hamersville contractor,
plain and simple

Innovations Renovations is a general contracting company based on Oak Corner Road in Hamersville, Ohio, serving homeowners in the surrounding area.

Every town has houses with good bones and tired rooms. Kitchens from another decade. Bathrooms held together by caulk and hope. That is where we come in.

Innovations Renovations takes on the renovation and remodeling work that turns those rooms around: kitchens, baths, additions, and the finish work that ties a house together. One contractor, one plan, one crew you get to know by name.

We keep it straightforward. We look at the project in person, we tell you what it will take, and we treat your home like it is our own while we are in it. Drop cloths down, dust kept down, and a walkthrough at the end where nothing is called finished until you agree it is.

If you are in Hamersville or nearby and thinking about a project, the next step is easy: call (513) 734-7005 and describe what you have in mind.
