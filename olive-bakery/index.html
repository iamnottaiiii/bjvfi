Our Story

A neighborhood bakery with old-world roots.

Roots

Recipes from Home

Olive Bakery brings Middle Eastern baking traditions to Orland Park: saj griddles, zaatar, and dough made fresh every single day.

Craft

Baked, Not Rushed

Thin saj breads, hand-folded pies and crisp falafel are made to order, so everything lands on your table at its best.

Welcome

A Table for Everyone

Guests praise the kind service and reasonable prices. Whether it is your first manoushe or your fiftieth, you are family here.

Peace

Why "Olive"?

The olive tree stands for peace, patience and deep roots, everything we try to put into our baking.

Like Good Olive Oil

Simple ingredients treated with care become something unforgettable. That is the whole philosophy behind our oven.
