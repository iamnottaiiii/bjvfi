Good to know

Signs you need an electrician now

Electrical problems get worse, not better. If you see any of these, call before it becomes an emergency.

A

Warm outlets or burning smells

An outlet that feels hot, a faint burning odor near a switch or panel, or scorch marks are warning signs of a failing connection. Cut power to the circuit and call.

B

Breakers tripping again and again

One trip is a fluke. Repeated trips mean an overloaded circuit, a short, or a ground fault that needs tracing.
