About

A Slatington concrete crew you can reach

T J’s Concrete is a concrete contractor based on Cutoff Road in Slatington, PA, serving residential and commercial customers in the surrounding area. Concrete is unforgiving work: once it sets, it is set. So we treat the unglamorous parts, base prep, forming, and curing, as the whole job.

How we approach a pour

1. Look first. We see the site in person: grade, drainage, access, and what the old concrete is telling us.

2. Prep honestly. Excavation and a compacted stone base, formed straight and to depth, before a single yard is ordered.

3. Pour and finish. Placed, screeded, and finished in one steady pass, with control joints cut where the slab needs to move.

4. Cure and clean. Proper curing for strength, then a clean site when we leave.
