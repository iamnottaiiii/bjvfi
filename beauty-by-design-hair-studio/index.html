About Beauty By Design Hair Studio

Beauty By Design Hair Studio is located at 645 Chestnut St in Union's Five Points area. Since 2003, owner and stylist Jessica Baez and her team have served men, women and children with haircuts, color, highlights, blowouts, keratin smoothing, Japanese straightening, bodywaves, precision cuts and facial waxing.

Clients describe a warm, homey atmosphere with great prices and walk-ins always welcome. Hours: Tue 10 AM to 2 PM, Wed 10 AM to 6 PM, Thu and Fri 10 AM to 7:30 PM, Sat 9:30 AM to 5 PM, closed Sun and Mon. Ample on-site parking. Call (908) 624-9669.
