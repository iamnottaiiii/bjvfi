Our Story

From Tassels to the Atelier

Photo coming soon

Shanahan Wildermuth Interiors was founded by Anne Shanahan and Tenesa Wildermuth. The studio began life as Tassels, a luxurious home furnishings boutique, and evolved into a full service interior design agency with a new brand identity built for high end clients and five star service.

The duo's signature is vintage glam: colorful furniture with great lines, chinoiserie, mirrors, wicker and bamboo, and vintage barware, all layered with different eras and far off finds into fresh, whimsical, collected rooms. A splash of black for drama. A surprise in every room.
