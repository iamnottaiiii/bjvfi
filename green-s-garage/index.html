About Green's Garage

Green's Garage is a local, family owned and operated towing and automobile repair business serving the Hampstead area since 1977. For generations of drivers, one name has meant the same thing: show up fast, fix it right, and charge fair.

The shop offers emergency towing and recovery alongside full automotive service, from body work to air conditioning. Green's Garage is ASE Certified, an AAA towing service provider, and MD State Police approved.

Customers say the shop handles cars as if they were the shop's own, from daily drivers to classics.
