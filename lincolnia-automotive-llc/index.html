Our story

A family-owned shop Alexandria trusts

Lincolnia Automotive LLC is a family-owned auto repair shop on Lincolnia Road in Alexandria, Virginia. It was built on a simple idea: treat drivers the way you would want your own family treated.

That idea shows up in the reviews. Customers write about glowing referrals from neighbors, about coming in for an inspection and getting same-day help, about being talked out of unnecessary work. Drivers say the staff takes time with them, explains repairs in plain language, and charges prices that are fair and upfront.

Some customers have been coming here for years, through oil changes, inspections and bigger repairs alike. Many say the same thing: they finally found a mechanic they trust, and they are not going anywhere else.

Family owned and operated.
A neighborhood shop where regulars are known by name and new customers get the same honest treatment.
