Our Story

A family company with a craftsman's standards.

Reded Construction is a family-owned Carpentry and Painting company based in Clark, New Jersey. We built our name one careful project at a time, delivering quality services to homeowners and businesses across the state.

Our team handles everything from structural carpentry and demolition to the fine finish work and painting that people see every day. We show up on time, protect your home, and leave every jobsite clean.

  • Registered New Jersey Home Improvement contractor
  • Residential, commercial, and institutional experience
  • Family-run, with personal attention on every job

How we work

  1. Walk the project. We look at the space, listen to what you want, and flag anything we see.
  2. Do it right. Careful prep, quality materials, and craftsmanship we would sign our name to.
  3. Leave it clean. Furniture protected, dust handled, and a final walkthrough before we call it done.
