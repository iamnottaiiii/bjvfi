Our story

Small shop, deep roots

Vaivoda Plumbing & Heating LLC was founded in Oxford in 2001. More than two decades later, it is still the same kind of business it started as: a local shop where the person who answers the phone is connected to the person doing the work.

Doing both plumbing and HVAC under one name was a deliberate choice. Homeowners get one number to call for a leaking pipe in July and a cold furnace in January, and a crew that already knows the house.

The reputation was earned the slow way: showing up on time, explaining the fix in plain language, and charging fairly. That is why the reviews read the way they do.
