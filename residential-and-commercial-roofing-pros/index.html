Condition reports for buyers, sellers, and homeowners who want to know where their roof stands.

Flat and low slope systems

TPO and modified bitumen membranes installed for long term ponding water resistance and energy performance.

Roof coatings

Reflective coating systems that extend the life of an aging membrane and cut heat gain on the building.

Our story

A roofing crew with a simple rule

Residential And Commercial Roofing Pros is a roofing contractor based on E Altadena Dr in Altadena, California. The business grew out of a straightforward observation: homeowners and property managers were getting the same rushed treatment from roofers who treated every job like a commodity.

So the company built itself around the opposite approach. Take the time to look at the whole roof. Explain what is actually wrong. Do the work the way you would want it done on your own building. Then stand behind it.

That is the whole philosophy. No pressure tactics, no mystery pricing, no disappearing after the check clears. When a storm rolls through the foothills or a tenant calls about a drip, clients know the number to call: (626) 707-1798.
