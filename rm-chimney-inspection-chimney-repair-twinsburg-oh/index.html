LOG ENTRY 02: WARNING SIGNS

Signs Your Chimney Wants Attention

General guidance for homeowners. If any of these sound familiar, call and ask for an inspection.

White Staining

Efflorescence on the masonry can mean moisture is getting in.

Cracked Mortar

Crumbling joints let water and heat reach places they should not.

Poor Draft

Smoke spilling into the room often points to a flue or damper problem.

Water Inside

Dampness near the firebox suggests cap, crown, or flashing trouble.

General information only. A professional inspection is the way to know what your chimney needs.

FLUE DIAGRAM

Anatomy of Your Chimney

Diagram for illustration. When we log an inspection, we note the condition of each part below, from cap to hearth.

  • AChimney cap: keeps out rain, animals, and debris
  • BCrown: the sloped top seal that sheds water
  • CFlue liner: the inner passage that carries smoke up
  • DSmoke chamber: funnels smoke into the flue
  • EDamper: opens and closes the throat
  • FFirebox and hearth: where the fire burns
