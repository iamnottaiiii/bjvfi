About Us

The Ken Helmlinger Company has served Columbus since 1975, designing and installing residential landscapes with quality plants and trusted workmanship.

The company focuses on outdoor spaces designed around each client's lifestyle, from plantings and patios to water features and lighting. Their work has earned recognition from the Ohio Nursery and Landscape Association.
