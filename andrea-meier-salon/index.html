About

A neighborhood salon with loyal chairs

Since day onePlaceholder art

Your premier beauty salon in Fayetteville

Andrea Meier Salon provides complete beauty treatments and hair care services for all your needs. With an experienced and skilled staff, the salon's goal is simple: bring out the better you.

The salon sits right on East Genesee Street in Fayetteville, an easy stop for the village and the surrounding Syracuse area. Clients praise the clean, relaxing atmosphere and the professional, friendly team, and many have trusted the same stylists for years.

Years of client loyalty
