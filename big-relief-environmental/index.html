How a restoration call works

The standard process for getting a water-damaged property dry and clean again.

01

Call and assess

Describe what happened and where the water is. The crew schedules an on-site inspection of the affected areas.

02

Extract the water

Standing water is removed with extraction equipment, from basements to soaked carpets and flooring.

03

Dry the structure

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry walls, floors, and framing, with moisture checks to confirm hidden areas are dry.

04

Clean and treat

Affected surfaces are cleaned and treated to discourage mold and odor after water exposure.

05

Document everything

The damage and the work are documented step by step, which helps when filing an insurance claim.

Common water damage calls

The situations a restoration service in central Pennsylvania sees most.

A

Burst and frozen pipes

Winter pipe failures soak walls and ceilings fast. Extraction and drying limit how much has to be torn out.

B

Basement flooding

Heavy rain and sump pump failures leave standing water. Pump-out, extraction, and structural drying follow.

C

Appliance leaks

Washing machines, water heaters, and supply lines leak quietly for weeks. Moisture mapping finds what eyes miss.
