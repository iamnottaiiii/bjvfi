Our story

Rooted in Buffalo, building for its future.

Terzo Development, LLC is a real estate development firm headquartered at 505 Ellicott Street in downtown Buffalo, New York. We are a small, hands-on team, which means the people you meet are the people doing the work.

Buffalo is a city of second acts: warehouses become lofts, vacant lots become gathering places, and old corridors find new energy. We started Terzo to be part of that pattern, project by project, block by block.

How we work

  • Local first. We develop in the market we know: Buffalo and Western New York.
  • Patient capital. We underwrite for the long term, not the quick flip.
  • Straight talk. If a site does not pencil out, we will tell you before you spend a dollar.
