About

Hi, I'm Lucy.
Clean is my love language.

Lucy's Cleaning Service is a women-owned, Latinx-owned house cleaning business based at 61 Johnny Rd in Revere, MA, serving Revere, Malden, Everett, Chelsea, and the greater Boston area.

The way we work is simple: before we start, we ask what you would like done. Then we clean with a meticulous, detail-obsessed eye, the kind reviewers call "very detail oriented" and "meticulous when cleaning."

People find us online, try one visit, and stay for years. "Wonderful people that get the job done fast. Great communication. No need to hire anyone else," one customer wrote. That is the review we work for, every single visit.
