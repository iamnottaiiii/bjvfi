About

Specialists in architectural metal

Modernized Architectural Sheet Metal is a sheet metal contractor based in Leicester, Massachusetts, focused on custom fabrication and installation for roofs, walls and trim.

Measured to fit

Every panel, flashing and trim piece is formed for the building it goes on. No forcing stock parts into place.

Clean details

Straight seams, tight corners and color matched accessories. The details are the job.

Systems designed for New England wind, ice and freeze thaw, installed to shed water for decades.
