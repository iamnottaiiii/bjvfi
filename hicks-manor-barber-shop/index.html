The Manor Chronicle

A Fairfield House Since 1965

Hicks Manor Barber Shop has stood on Magie Avenue for generations, keeping the old-school craft alive in the Greater Fairfield and Cincinnati area.

History below comes from public listings and the shop's own published description. Details not independently verified.
  • 1965

    The shop is established at the corner of Holiday and Magie Avenues in Fairfield, per public listings.

  • 1968

    The manor moves to its long-time home at 1133 Magie Avenue, where it remains today.

  • The Founders

    Founders Clarence and his brother Elvin Strunk fill the shop with southern style, old-school Tennessee hospitality. Haircuts were only $2.00 in those days.

  • Today

    The barbershop keeps its down-home, relaxing, Mayberry type of feel. The barbers take pride in their work and want every customer to leave satisfied. They will even make house calls for some special customers who can no longer make it into the shop.
