When to call sooner

  • Persistent toothache or sensitivity to heat and cold
  • Swollen gums or a bump on the gumline
  • A broken tooth, crown, or filling
  • Bleeding gums that do not improve with brushing

Cracked, worn, or heavily filled teeth protected with custom crowns that restore strength and shape.
