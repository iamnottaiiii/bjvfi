Our Story

A Woburn team that treats your home like their own

Small, background checked, and proud of it. We clean the way we would want our own homes cleaned.

How we started

Elem’s cleaning services began in Woburn with a simple belief: a cleaning service should leave your home noticeably, wonderfully different. Not just tidy, but transformed: the smell when you walk in, the order in every room, the shine in the kitchen and baths.

Eight years later, that is still the standard. Elem leads a small, background checked team that shows up ready to work hard and sweat the details other cleaners skip: under the furniture, inside the fixtures, along every baseboard.

Our customers are busy families, professionals, and property managers across Woburn and nearby towns. Many started with a single deep clean and never looked back.

What we believe

No corners cut

Every room gets the full treatment, every time. Thorough is not an upgrade, it is the baseline.

Honest value

Fair prices for genuinely great work. We would rather earn your repeat business than oversell a visit.

Respect for your home

Your space, your belongings, your privacy. We handle all three with care.

The finishing touch

Folded blankets, arranged pillows, rooms in perfect order. The details are what make it feel like magic.
