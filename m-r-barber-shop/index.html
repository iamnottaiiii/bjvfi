The Shop

Keep It Simple,
Cut It Right

M & R Barber Shop has held its corner of W 8th St in San Pedro as a working neighborhood barbershop: the kind of place where the regulars are on a first-name basis and the conversation is part of the service.

It is a traditional operation. No booking apps, no velvet ropes. You walk in, you sit down, you get a proper cut. Reviewers who love the shop really love it: one longtime customer calls the man behind the chair a legend with the clippers, a barber you can trust to cut and style.

The Craft

Earned One
Head At A Time

The work is classic barbering: clean fades, sharp lines, beard trims, hot towel shaves. The kind of cutting that keeps a chair full without advertising.

The shop keeps long hours, open seven days, and walk-ins are welcome. When it gets busy, a quick call to (310) 548-9886 is the smart move. Street parking is right out front on 8th Street.
