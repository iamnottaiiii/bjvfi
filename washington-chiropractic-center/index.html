What chiropractic care is

Chiropractic is built on a simple idea: the relationship between the body's structure, especially the spine, and the nervous system affects overall health.

A chiropractor evaluates your history, examines the spine, and uses hands-on adjustments and supportive therapies to improve alignment and movement.
