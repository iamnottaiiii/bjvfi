Real stucco is a system of coats, each one doing a job. We build (and repair) them right.
How we work

Three coats. No shortcuts.

A proper stucco wall is built in layers. Here is the craft behind every job we take on.

01

Scratch coat

The bonding foundation. We key the base coat into the lath so everything above it has something real to hold onto.

02

Brown coat

The straightening layer. This is where we true up the wall, build thickness and set up a flat, even surface.

03

Finish coat

The face of the wall. Texture, color and weather resistance, matched to your home and troweled to last.

Why it matters

Your stucco is your weather shield

In the San Gabriel Valley, stucco takes the sun, the Santa Anas and the occasional hard rain. Small cracks become big repairs when water gets behind the wall. We fix the problem at the layer where it started, and we match the finish so the repair disappears.

✓

Match the wall

Patching is only half the job. We match texture and color so repaired sections blend into the original wall.

✓

Fix the cause

Cracks tell a story: movement, moisture, age. We look for the cause before we trowel, so the crack does not come back.

✓

One trade, done right

We are stucco contractors, not generalists. Plaster work is all we do, and that focus shows in the finish.
