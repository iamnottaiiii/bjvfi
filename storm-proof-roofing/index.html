Our story

Local roofers,
storm-season ready

Storm Proof Roofing is a Covington roofing contractor built around one idea: when Kentucky weather does its worst, your roof should do its best.

Born from storm season

Every year, hail and high winds sweep through Northern Kentucky and leave a trail of bruised shingles and lifted flashing. Too many homeowners only discover the damage when the ceiling stains appear, months later and far more expensive.

We started Storm Proof Roofing to be the crew that shows up fast, tells the truth about what a roof needs, and builds it to take the next storm, not just the last one.

Your roof is the only thing between your family and the sky. We take that personally.
