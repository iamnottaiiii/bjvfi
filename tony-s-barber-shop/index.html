About Tony's Barber Shop

Tony's Barber Shop on West 7th Street is a longtime neighborhood stop known for its family vibe, great work with kids, and reasonable prices. The shop carries on a local legacy with the same welcoming spirit.

Every visit follows the same simple rhythm: a real consultation about what you want, careful work in the chair, and a finish you can feel good about. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
