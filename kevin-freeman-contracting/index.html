About Kevin Freeman Contracting

A general contractor based at 4648 Belleview Ave in the Blue Ash area of Cincinnati, Ohio.

Reliability, great communication, integrity, and quality work.

That is how the company describes itself on its HomeAdvisor profile, and the public reviews back it up. Clients say the crew is easy to talk to, knowledgeable, polite, and reasonable on price, and that the finished work is detailed and top notch.
