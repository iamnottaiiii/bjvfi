Our story

Kisetsu: a season,
a standard

A luxury beauty boutique in the heart of Midwood, built around the idea that great nail work is a craft, not a rush.

The philosophy

Calm room,
careful hands

Step inside Kisetsu Nail Labo and the first thing you notice is the atmosphere: warm, quiet, and unhurried. Clients consistently describe a salon that feels professional and expensive in the best way, a small retreat from the Avenue M bustle.

The second thing you notice is the work. The team is known for precision techniques like the Russian manicure, honest advice about what your nails can handle, and nail art that regulars call some of the best in the city.

Every manicure and pedicure comes with complimentary skin care and massage, because the experience matters as much as the result.

心

100% guest satisfaction

That is the standard the salon holds itself to: friendly, knowledgeable staff who go above and beyond, and technicians who take real pride in their craft, so every client leaves with perfectly polished nails.
