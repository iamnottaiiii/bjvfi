The shop

One name on the door.

Nick Yannuzzi has had a passion for working on cars and trucks since he was old enough to pick up a screwdriver. That is not a slogan on the wall, it is the reason the shop exists: an owner who learned the trade young and still treats every vehicle like his reputation depends on it, because it does.

Yannuzzi Auto Repair opened its doors in 2017 at 54 Broadway in Florham Park. It is a small, owner-operated shop, the kind where the person who greets you is the person who fixes your car. No service writers upselling work you do not need, no corporate script, just straight talk about what your car needs and what it does not.

From routine oil changes to general repairs on cars and trucks, and roadside assistance when your day goes sideways, this is the shop for drivers who want one mechanic they can trust.

Our story

A lifelong passion,
now a Florham Park shop

Nick Yannuzzi has had a passion for working on cars and trucks since he was old enough to pick up a screwdriver. In 2017 he turned that passion into Yannuzzi Auto Repair at 54 Broadway in Florham Park, New Jersey, an independent, owner-operated shop built on doing the job right.

Nick is known as sociable and well educated in the automotive field. Customers notice it in the workmanship, and in the appearance of the shop itself: pride of ownership is not a phrase here, it is visible the moment you walk in.

Being small is the point. There is no front office relay, no upsell quota, no wondering who actually touched your car. You talk to Nick, Nick fixes your car, and Nick stands behind it.

Why drivers choose us

An independent shop means your money goes to the work, not the overhead. Fair estimates, clear explanations, and a mechanic who remembers you and your car the next time you roll in.
