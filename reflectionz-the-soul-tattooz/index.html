i.The Studio

A calmer room
for permanent art

Walk into Reflectionz and the first thing you notice is the quiet. No crowds, no rush, no pressure. The studio works by appointment only, so every session gets the room to itself: focused artists, honest guidance, and the time your piece actually deserves.

That calm is the point. Tattooing is intimate work, and the artists here treat it that way, from the first sketch to the final wipe-down. Clients describe the atmosphere as peaceful and relaxing, the kind of place where a long session feels shorter than it is.
