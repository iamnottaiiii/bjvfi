N&T is a Lexington, Massachusetts contractor focused on the hidden systems: dryer vents, air ducts, and chimneys. The work is out of sight, so we make the results impossible to miss.

Why this work matters

Small neglect,
big consequences

A dryer working twice as hard doubles your energy use and your fire risk at the same time. Dust-choked ducts make allergies worse and furnaces die young. A cracked chimney crown lets water in all winter and turns into a four figure repair by spring.

None of it is dramatic until it is. That is why we do this work: to catch the quiet problems while they are still cheap.
