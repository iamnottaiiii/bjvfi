The craft

Wood floors reward people who do them properly.

A hardwood floor is one of the few things in a home that gets better with age, but only if it is installed and finished right. We have spent decades working with real wood: reading the grain, acclimating the boards, and finishing floors that stand up to daily life.

A floor done right outlives the trends around it.

Refinishing

Your old floors may just need a second chance.

Worn finish, dull traffic lanes, surface scratches: most of that lives in the top layer, not the wood. Professional sanding and refinishing brings tired floors back without the cost of replacement.

Our story

Three decades of hardwood in Alexandria.

Hehman's Hardwood Inc has been part of Alexandria, Kentucky for around 30 years, operating from 9717 Licking Pike. We are a flooring contractor and materials shop: we install and refinish hardwood floors, and we sell the hardwood and lumber that goes into them.
