Our story

35 years on Pittsburgh Street

American Hardware of Cheswick has been an independent, family-run hardware store for more than three and a half decades. We have watched the valley grow, weathered every storm with it, and kept our promise simple: honest goods at honest prices.

Customers keep coming back for the same reason reviewers mention again and again: the person behind the counter knows the work. Mower checkups every spring, the right fitting on the first try, and advice that treats your wallet like our own.
