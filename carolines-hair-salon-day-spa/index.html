Our story

A village salon
with a spa soul

Caroline's brings together two things Pittsford loves: a friendly neighborhood hair salon and a true day spa, in one welcoming space on State Street.

People first

Every appointment starts with listening. Your stylist or therapist learns what you want, what you have tried, and what your week looks like before a single snip or stroke.

Real expertise

From precision cutting to advanced color and esthetics, our team trains continuously so the village gets big-city skill without the drive.

A place to exhale

Soft light, calm music, warm towels. Whether you are here for a quick trim or a full spa day, you will feel the day slow down the moment you arrive.

Come as you are. Leave glowing.

That is the whole philosophy at Caroline's. No judgment, no rush, no one-size-fits-all. Just skilled hands, honest advice, and a chair that is always warm for you.
