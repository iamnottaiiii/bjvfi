Our Story

A Bay Street neighborhood salon

Long Nail is a nail salon on Bay Street in Staten Island's Stapleton area, serving neighbors with manicures, pedicures, facial treatments, and lash extensions seven days a week.

The business is licensed in New York as Bay Street Long Nail Inc. Customers describe a comfortable, welcoming shop with friendly staff, efficient service, and minimal wait times even on busy weekends.

Walk in for a quick polish change or settle in for a full set: the team is known for skilled, careful nail work at fair prices.
