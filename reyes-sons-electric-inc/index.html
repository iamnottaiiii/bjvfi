About us

A family name on every job

Reyes & Sons Electric, Inc. has been wiring Southern California since 1994, from a Sylmar office to job sites across the region.

We see electricity differently

Founded in 1994 and led by the Reyes family, we are a full-service electrical company offering quality services for residential, commercial and industrial customers.

Our promise is simple: quality workmanship through the knowledge and experience of our employees, and through the commitment of our leadership team. We look closely at the details of every job before we commit, and our reputation depends on following through on what we promised.

We have been listed in the Blue Book of Building and Construction since 2000, we are BBB accredited with an A+ rating, and we have pulled more than 48 building permits since 1999.

It is not just about turning on lights. It is the heartbeat that keeps businesses alive, sparks new ideas, and brings light to our neighborhoods.
