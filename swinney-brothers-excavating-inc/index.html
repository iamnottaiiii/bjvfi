Swinney Brothers Excavating Inc has been a leader in the excavating industry since 1972: site development, underground utilities, and earthwork for commercial and residential projects across Indiana.

I. Salt of the Earth

Since 1972, Swinney Brothers Excavating has worked on nearly every project type with general contractors throughout the entire state of Indiana. A small family-owned and operated business with the capacity and expertise to manage commercial projects of most all sizes, the company has thrived on long-term relationships since day one.

Specialties include excavating, site development, and underground utilities, with full capabilities for design-build, water, storm, sanitary, and underground storm retention systems.
