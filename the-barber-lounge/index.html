Services

Every service includes a consultation so you leave with exactly the cut you pictured.

At The Barber Lounge, the menu is built around what a barbershop should do best. Precision cutting, careful beard work, and the kind of hot towel shave that slows the day down.

  • Men's Haircut. Consultation, precision cut, wash and style.
  • Skin Fade. Seamless fade blended down to the skin.
  • Beard Trim. Shape and detail with hot lather edging.
  • Hot Towel Shave. Traditional straight razor shave with hot towels.
  • Kids Cut. Patient, friendly cuts for young gentlemen.
  • Lineup / Edge Up. Crisp lines, sharp temples, clean neckline.
  • Head Shave. Smooth razor shave with soothing finish.
  • Gray Blending. Subtle color service that softens gray.

Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments keep the wait short. Call (562) 321-4107 to book your chair.

About The Barber Lounge


The Barber Lounge serves Downey with precision haircuts, beard work, and classic shaves. Bring a photo or just an idea, take a seat, and let the barbers handle the rest.


Every appointment starts with a consultation: what you like, what you do not, and how much upkeep you want. The result is a cut that looks sharp on day one and grows out clean.


Find us at 9312 Firestone Blvd, Downey, CA 90241, or call (562) 321-4107 to book your chair.
