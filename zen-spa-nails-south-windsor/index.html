About

A calm chair on Ellington Road

Zen Spa Nails South Windsor is a nail salon at 1678 Ellington Rd #3 in South Windsor, Connecticut. The salon offers manicures, pedicures, gel and acrylic nails, eyelash extensions, waxing, and skin care in a quiet, unhurried setting.

Clients often mention the peaceful atmosphere and the technicians by name, with some regulars returning for a decade or more. Walk-ins are welcome when space allows, and appointments are just a phone call away.
