Our story

Brooklyn's neighborhood nail spot

Annise Nail Salon Inc has made its home at 1702 Flatbush Avenue, serving the Brooklyn community with steady, skilled nail care. It is the kind of salon where technicians remember your preferences and regulars send their friends.

The salon is LGBTQ+ friendly and welcomes everyone, with a comfortable space, gender-neutral restroom, and easy payment by credit card, debit card, or mobile pay.

Skilled technicians, honest value, and nails you will want to show off.
