How a project flows

From first call to final walkthrough

Every build starts with a conversation. Here is the typical path a Green City Ecostruction project follows.

  1. 01

    Walkthrough and consult

    We visit your property, listen to your goals, and talk through green options that fit your budget. Free estimates, per public business listing.

  2. 02

    Green design and materials plan

    Sustainable design methods with environmentally responsible materials: EnergyStar windows and insulation, high efficiency HVAC, FSC-certified lumber, natural coatings, and more.

  3. 03

    Permits and preparation

    As a City of Cincinnati licensed contractor (Contractor # CBECOD1725), we handle the paperwork and site prep so the build starts clean.

  4. 04

    Build with sustainable practices

    Courteous craftspeople carry out the work, from green roofs and pervious concrete to solar, geothermal, and healthy interior finishes.

  5. 05

    Final walkthrough and warranty

    We walk the finished project together and back our work. Warranty is offered, per public business listing.

Typical project flow shown for illustration. Your project may differ. Call (513) 236-4180 to confirm details.
