Our story

A studio with standards

Level 7 Hair Studio is a beauty salon and hair salon at 9540 Marlboro Pike in Upper Marlboro, Maryland, known for healthy-hair philosophy and unrushed appointments.

The studio

Relaxed while being styled, excited when you leave

That is how the studio describes its own promise, and reviewers agree. Guests say they arrive to a clean, stylish, comfortable shop and leave feeling excited about their new look, with the knowledge to keep their hair healthy between visits.

The team specializes in haircuts, hair color, weaves, hair extensions and a full range of nail services, from manicures and pedicures to gel and acrylic nails. Reviewers especially praise the studio's knowledge of natural hair and its friendly, professional, no-rush approach.

By appointment

The studio works by appointment only, so your time in the chair is fully yours. Call to set up your visit.

Healthy hair, taught well

Along with beautiful hairstyles, the team promotes hair care education, so you know how to care for your hair at home.

Wheelchair accessible

The studio welcomes every guest, with accessible facilities, free wi-fi and credit cards accepted.
