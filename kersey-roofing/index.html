Signs Your Roof Needs Attention

If you notice any of these, it is worth a call before a small issue becomes a big one.

Inside the House

Water stains on ceilings or walls, peeling paint near the roofline, or daylight visible in the attic all point to roof trouble.

On the Roof

Missing, cracked, or curling shingles, and granules piling up in gutters, mean the surface is wearing out.

After a Storm

High winds and hail can lift shingles and break seals. A post-storm look catches damage early.

Age

Most shingle roofs last around two decades. If yours is getting up there, an inspection tells you where you stand.
