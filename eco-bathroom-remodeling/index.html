Why eco

Remodels that respect water and energy

A bathroom is the hardest working room in the house, and the thirstiest. We plan every remodel around fixtures and materials that cut water use without cutting comfort.

  • Water saving fixtures. Low flow toilets, showerheads, and faucets that keep pressure while using less.
  • Smarter materials. Durable tile, recycled content options, and low VOC finishes.
  • Built to last. Quality installation that avoids the waste of doing the job twice.

About

A bathroom remodeler with a green streak

Eco Bathroom Remodeling is a bathroom remodeling company based in Los Angeles, serving homeowners who want beautiful bathrooms that use less water and energy.

We focus on one room and do it well: honest estimates, clear scopes, durable materials, and fixtures chosen to cut waste without cutting comfort. No pressure sales, just a straightforward plan and a clean build.
