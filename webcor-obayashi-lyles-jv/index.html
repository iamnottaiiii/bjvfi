Heavy civil construction for Los Angeles water infrastructure

Webcor Obayashi Lyles JV is a Los Angeles based heavy civil construction joint venture bringing together Webcor, Obayashi, and Lyles. Public City of Los Angeles contracting records list the joint venture as the contractor for the Silver Lake Reservoir Complex Storage Replacement Project, also known as the Headworks Reservoir Project, for the Los Angeles Department of Water and Power.

01 / ABOUT THE VENTURE

Three builders, one project team

The joint venture combines the civil construction experience of Webcor, Obayashi, and Lyles into a single contracting entity. The venture holds an active California contractors license (CSLB 1037944, issued April 2018) and is recorded in City of Los Angeles documents as the design and construction contractor for major water storage infrastructure at the Silver Lake Reservoir complex.

02 / WHAT THE RECORDS SHOW

Work described in public contracting records

City of Los Angeles Board of Water and Power Commissioners records describe the joint venture agreement (No. 47071-2) as covering construction of the Headworks Reservoir, including the flow control station, associated piping, and phased site development and restoration at the Silver Lake Reservoir complex.

S.01

Reservoir and storage structures

Construction of buried and covered water storage facilities within the Silver Lake Reservoir complex, per the LADWP storage replacement program.

PUBLIC RECORD
S.02

Flow control station

Headworks flow control facilities and associated structures, described in city contracting records as a principal phase of the project.

PUBLIC RECORD
S.03

Piping and site utilities

Associated piping and utility connections serving the reservoir complex and flow control facilities.

PUBLIC RECORD
