Chiropractic care for the neuromusculoskeletal system

A chiropractor like Dr. Sheffer treats problems of the nerves, bones, muscles, ligaments, and tendons, using spinal adjustments and manipulation plus other clinical interventions to manage pain and support overall health.

01

Spinal adjustments and manipulation

Hands-on chiropractic manipulative treatment of the spinal regions, the core of Dr. Sheffer's practice for more than 30 years.

02

Back pain care

Evaluation and adjustment-based management of low back and mid back pain, one of the most common reasons patients seek chiropractic care.

Neck pain and stiffness

Targeted cervical care aimed at easing stiffness and improving range of motion in the neck and shoulders.
