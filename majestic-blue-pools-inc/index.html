About

A pool company
from Pacoima

Majestic Blue Pools INC is a swimming pool contractor based on Garber St in Pacoima, working across the San Fernando Valley.

Built around the water

In the Valley, a pool is not a luxury extra: it is where summers happen. We treat every pool like the centerpiece of the backyard, whether we are building one from dirt or keeping a twenty-year-old pool running clean.

Our work covers the full arc: new construction for families starting fresh, remodels and resurfacing for pools showing their age, equipment repair when the system quits, and weekly service routes that keep the chemistry right without you thinking about it.

Call us and you talk to the people who will actually show up at your gate. Straight answers, clear quotes, and water you can see the bottom of.
