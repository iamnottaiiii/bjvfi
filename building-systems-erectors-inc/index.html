OUR STORY

Steel is a family business

Decades of erection experience, rooted in Erlanger, Kentucky.

The Huser family has been raising steel in Northern Kentucky for generations. Building Systems Erectors, Inc. operates from 3140 Crescent Avenue in Erlanger as a small, family-led construction company, with George A. Huser, Jr. serving as president and CEO.

Kentucky Secretary of State records show the company as a domestic corporation formed January 18, 2002, classified as a special trade contractor. Its Yellow Pages listing credits 63 years in business, and its public business listing cites five decades of experience in pre-engineered metal buildings, metal roofing, equipment rigging, and structural steel erection.

With over 20 million square feet of buildings erected, recognition from multiple PEMB manufacturers for sales and quality erection, and AISC Advanced certification, BSE has become a leading erection contractor for general contractors, design-builders, and construction managers.
