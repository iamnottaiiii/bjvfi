About the company

Joe's Construction is a small excavating outfit out of Rising Sun, Indiana, run by owner Joseph Getz. Public business records date the company to 1996, which puts about three decades of dirt work behind the name.

Small by design. A one-man-scale operation means the person who quotes your job is the person who does your job.

Local ground. Based on State Road 250 in Rising Sun, working the river-town and farm country around southeastern Indiana.

Reviewed by customers. A 4.4 out of 5 rating across 16 Birdeye reviews, with the recurring theme that Joe does excellent work and earns recommendations.
