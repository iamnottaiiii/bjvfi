About IPI Construction

Nearly two decades of building in the San Fernando Valley, one project at a time.

IPI Construction is a Panorama City based general contractor serving Los Angeles and the surrounding Valley neighborhoods. The company takes on residential and light commercial construction projects, from kitchen and bathroom remodels to home additions and structural improvements.

Our process covers pre-construction consultation, planning, and execution, and every project is completed to local building standards. We hold an active California contracting license (830782, General Building plus Painting and Decorating classifications) and have pulled more than 15 building permits since 2008.

We are a full-service, licensed and bonded company. Our reputation is built on integrity, pride, and trust: we work closely with owners, designers, and municipal officials so the project moves without surprises.
