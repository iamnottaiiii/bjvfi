About

Two Decades of Basements

The Basement Guys operated in Ohio for 24 years as a basement-only contractor: waterproofing, foundation solutions, crawl space repair, and basement finishing.

In April 2024, the company was acquired by Groundworks, the national foundation and water management company, and its services were brought together with Ohio Basement Systems and Ohio Basement Authority under the Ohio Basement Authority name.

The company was a multi-time recipient of the Angi Super Service Award and was accredited by the Better Business Bureau, per the acquisition announcement.
