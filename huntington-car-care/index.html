Our story

The neighborhood shop by the Metro

Honest work, walking distance from Huntington Station

Huntington Car Care has served the Huntington and Mount Vernon corridor from 2600 Huntington Ave for years, building its reputation one fair invoice at a time. Longtime customers mention the crew by name in reviews and describe the same experience over and over: reliable work, no nickel and diming, and problems diagnosed correctly the first time.
