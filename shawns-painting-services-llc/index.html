What we paint

Services, plainly listed

Every job starts with a walkthrough and a written estimate. We cover interior and exterior residential painting across Pilgrim Gardens and nearby Delaware County neighborhoods. If a surface takes paint, we can almost certainly handle it. Call to confirm your project.

i.

Interior painting

Rooms, hallways, ceilings, closets, and accent walls. Full masking and furniture protection included.

ii.

Exterior painting

Siding, trim, fascia, shutters, and porches. Scrape, prime, caulk, then two finish coats.

iii.

Trim and doors

Crisp enamel work on baseboards, crown molding, window casings, and interior and exterior doors.

iv.

Drywall repair

Patching, spackling, and sanding before paint so nail pops and dings disappear for good.

v.

Cabinet refinishing

Kitchen and bath cabinets sprayed or brushed to a smooth, durable factory style finish.

vi.

Color consultation

Help choosing colors and sheens that suit your light, your furniture, and your home.
