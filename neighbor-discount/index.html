Our story

A neighborhood shop with neighborhood roots.

The Burgh's corner stop.

Neighbor Discount sits on 5th Ave in Lansingburgh, the North Troy neighborhood locals call the Burgh. It is the kind of place people pass every day, and the kind of place that becomes part of the routine: fuel on the way to work, a repair you can actually afford, a quick stop at the store on the walk home.

Reviewers describe owners who are incredibly friendly, staff that make everyone feel welcome, and prices that stay fair. Some customers have been coming for close to a decade. That does not happen by accident. It happens when a shop treats people right, job after job.

Whether it is your car, your tank, or your pantry, we aim to be the stop you trust.

What we stand for.

Fair prices

Regulars say our gas often runs lower than the area and our store prices stay decent for a corner shop. Fair is the whole business model.
