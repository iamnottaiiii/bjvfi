What a handyman visit covers

Typical services

Everyday wear and tear, handled in one trip:

  • Drywall patching, cracks, and nail pops
  • Door adjustments, sticking doors, new hardware
  • Cabinet hinges, drawer slides, and handles
  • Grout touch-ups and re-caulking tubs and sinks
  • Screen repair and window latch fixes
Typical services

Small painting jobs that freshen a room fast:

  • Interior touch-ups and single-room repaints
  • Baseboards, trim, and door painting
  • Drywall patch prime and paint
  • Exterior touch-ups, shutters, and railings
Typical services

The flat-pack and fixture jobs nobody wants to start:

  • TV mounting and shelf installation
  • Light fixture and ceiling fan swaps
  • Curtain rods, blinds, and mirrors
  • Furniture assembly, desks, and storage units
  • Grab bars and safety hardware
Typical services

Small fixes, done safely. Bigger work gets referred to a licensed specialist:

  • Faucet and showerhead replacement
  • Toilet flappers, fill valves, and seats
  • Garbage disposal resets and swaps
  • Outlet and switch cover replacement
  • Smoke and CO detector installation
Typical services

Made for landlords, property managers, and busy households:

  • Move-in and move-out touch-up lists
  • Rental turnover repairs between tenants
  • Seasonal maintenance checklists
  • Weather stripping and draft sealing
Good to know: California limits handyman projects that need a contractor license. If your job is too big for a handyman visit, we will say so and point you toward the right specialist.
