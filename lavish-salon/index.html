Great hair is a long story.

Lavish Salon is a full service hair and nail salon on S Main St in North Syracuse. Clients have trusted Krista, Kaitlyn, Erika, Toni and Kelly for 8, 10, 15 years and counting. Color, cuts, keratin, extensions, and nails, all in one welcoming chair.

Some salons are a transaction. Lavish Salon is a relationship. The review boards tell the same story again and again: clients who walked in a decade ago, or a decade and a half ago, and never found a reason to leave.

Krista Owens, the owner and master stylist, has cut some clients' hair for 15 years. Toni counts her regulars in eight year friendships. Kaitlyn and Erika have their own loyal books. There is no style they cannot create, and no schedule they will not try to work with.

The colorists people stay with

Hair coloring is Lavish's signature strength. Balayage, dimensional color, gray coverage, and rich all over tones, applied by stylists whose clients trust them enough to stay a decade or more. Toni's color clients count their loyalty in eight year stretches.

Smoothing, straightening, and curls done right

Competitive prices, careful detail, and stylists who make sure you are 100 percent satisfied before you leave the chair.

Cuts and styling

Precision cuts for every head of hair. Women's haircuts tailored to face shape and lifestyle, curly hair cuts that respect the curl, and blow dries with staying power.

  • Curly hair cuts
  • Blow dry and styling

Smoothing and straightening

Frizz, handled. Keratin treatments and permanent hair straightening for hair that fights back. Smooth, manageable, and built to last through Syracuse humidity.
  • Keratin treatment
  • Permanent hair straightening
Sample: smoothing services priced by consultation
Why clients stay: These are longer appointments with real transformation. Book a consultation first so your stylist can recommend the right approach for your hair type.

Extensions, braids, and wigs
