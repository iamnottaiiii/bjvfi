CHAPTER 01 / SERVICES

What we build.

From raw hillside lots to finished luxury residences, Jensen Construction delivers a comprehensive turn-key service. Expand each offering below.

Ground-up construction of high-end custom homes. The team specializes in difficult builds, including hillside properties, and coordinates architects, designers, and a specialized network of luxury suppliers from the first sketch to a livable, exceptional home.

One accountable partner from design through move-in. Following best building practices and active collaboration with your architect and designer, Jensen Construction delivers a finished home without the handoffs and finger-pointing of fragmented contracting.

Steep lots, complex grading, and tight access are where this builder earned its name. Some of the most challenging custom homes in the world have come out of this small studio, concentrated in Beverly Hills, Bel Air, Pacific Palisades, West Hollywood, Venice, La Canada, and Malibu.

Already own the property? The team breathes life into existing homes with full-scale renovations and rebuilds, applying the same design/build integrity as new construction.
