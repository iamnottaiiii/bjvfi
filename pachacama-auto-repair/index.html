Our story

Pachacama Auto Repair is a neighborhood auto shop in Springfield, Virginia, built on a simple idea: treat every car like it belongs to a neighbor, because it does.

Drivers find us, come back, and send their friends. Our reviews are written in English and Spanish, and they say the same thing in both languages: good prices, good service, and people who treat you well.

We keep things personal. You talk to the people who will work on your car, you hear what is wrong in plain language, and you pay a fair price for honest work. That is the whole philosophy, and it is not changing.

How we work

  • Listen first. You describe the problem in your own words.
  • Diagnose honestly. We find the cause, not just the symptom.
  • Price fairly. A straight quote before any work begins.
  • Stand by it. Work done efficiently and punctually.
