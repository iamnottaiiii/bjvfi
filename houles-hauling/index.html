Our story

A Manchester hauler with a simple promise

Houle's Hauling started with a truck, a strong back, and a simple idea: hauling junk away should be easy for the customer. No confusing pricing, no waiting around all day, no mess left behind. Just honest work at a fair, upfront price.

Based on Park Street in Manchester, we are a local waste management crew serving our neighbors across Hartford County. We take pride in doing the job right: showing up when we say we will, treating your home with respect, and disposing of everything responsibly. Donate what can be donated, recycle what can be recycled, and handle the rest properly.

Our rule is simple.

You should never have to lift a finger. Point at the junk, we handle everything else.
