A gate that sticks, grinds or refuses to open is usually a motor, sensor, hinge or wiring problem. We diagnose the fault and repair or replace the failed part so the gate runs smoothly again.

  • Safety sensors and photo eyes
  • Hinges, rollers, tracks and hardware
  • Wiring and electrical connections
  • Keypads, remotes and access controls
