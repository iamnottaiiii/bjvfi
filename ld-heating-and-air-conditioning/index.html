About

A heating and cooling contractor for Northampton County.

LD Heating and Air Conditioning is based in Northampton, PA and serves homes across the Lehigh Valley.

Straightforward HVAC work.

Heating and cooling systems are simple in one way: they either keep the house comfortable or they do not. Our job is to get them back to comfortable and keep them there.

That means diagnosing before replacing, explaining the options in plain language, and sizing any new equipment to the house instead of the invoice. No upsell pressure, no mystery line items.

We work on the equipment common in Lehigh Valley homes: gas and oil furnaces, boilers, central air conditioners, and heat pumps.
