The studio

Design, sourcing, and supply under one roof on Harrisburg's riverfront corridor.

A design practice rooted in the hospitality world, brought home to Central Pennsylvania.

Hersha Interiors & Supply pairs interior design with a furniture and supply showroom, so the pieces we specify for your project are pieces you can see, touch, and take home. From a single room refresh to a full property furnishing, our designers work with you from first sketch to final placement.

Our background is in hospitality design and purchasing, which means we plan for durability and guest comfort as well as beauty. That discipline shows up in every project, whether it is a family living room or a multi-room renovation.
