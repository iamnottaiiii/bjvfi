Our story

A family partnership, on paper and on site

Stan Wagner Construction is not a franchise and not a lead broker. It is a small Kentucky corporation run by two partners who share a last name.

The facts, from public filings

Stan Wagner Construction, Inc. was formed in Kentucky on September 10, 2003 (Secretary of State company 0567833). The principal office is at 6958 Mystic Rose Lane in Melbourne, Campbell County. The listed partners are Stanley Anthony Wagner and Andrew James Wagner, a father-and-son team. The state classifies the business as a small company in building construction, general contractors and operative builders.
