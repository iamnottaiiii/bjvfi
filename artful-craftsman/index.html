The shop

Metal fabrication with a craftsman's eye

Artful Craftsman is a metal fabrication shop in Sun Valley, California, working in iron and steel for residential and commercial projects. Every gate, railing, and fence is measured, cut, welded, and finished in-house.

From a single security door to a full perimeter fence, you deal directly with the people building your project.

About

A working fabrication shop, not a showroom

Artful Craftsman operates out of Sun Valley, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley's industrial corridor. The shop handles design, cutting, welding, and finishing under one roof, which keeps lead times short and quality consistent.

Listings describe the business as a metal fabricator and iron works shop also serving carpentry and custom cabinet needs, so if your project mixes steel and wood, it can be handled in one place.
