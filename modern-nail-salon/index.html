Services

Nail care, start to finish

A complete menu of nail and waxing services. Pricing and availability are confirmed when you call or stop by the salon.

01

Manicures

Classic and gel manicures with careful cuticle work, shaping and polish. A tidy, long lasting finish for everyday wear.

02

Pedicures

Relaxing foot care with soak, exfoliation, massage and polish. Clients mention the unhurried pace and thorough detail.

03

Dip Powder

Durable dip powder sets in a wide range of colors, applied with a light hand for a natural feel and weeks of wear.

04

Gel Manicures

Chip resistant gel color cured to a glossy finish. A favorite for clients who want polish that keeps up with them.

05

Nail Art and Design

Hand painted designs and airbrush accents for special occasions, or just because. Bring an idea and we will make it happen.

06

Waxing

Eyebrow shaping and facial waxing services to finish your look in one visit.
