About us

Like visiting family, with better nails

Sister Nails is a neighborhood nail salon on Bath Avenue, known for warm service, meticulous work, and a genuinely welcoming room.

Our story

A salon where everybody belongs

Sister Nails has built its name the old-fashioned way: one careful manicure at a time. Clients describe an open, spacious salon with a mellow and relaxed atmosphere, and a team that treats every design request like it matters.

The salon is known for being inclusive and welcoming to all, with wheelchair accessible parking and seating, and thoughtful touches like gender-neutral restrooms. It is the kind of place regulars recommend to their sisters, hence the name.

From hard gel manicures that protect and strengthen natural nails to pedicures praised for their attention to detail, the work here earns its reputation every day.
