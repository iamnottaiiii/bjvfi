Our Story

A little shop with a long memory

Welcome to Shea's

Tucked along Montgomery Road in Cincinnati, Barber Shop Shea's is the kind of place that feels like walking back in time, in the best way. The decor is vintage, the pace is unhurried, and the owner Shea has been cutting hair in this shop for decades. Regulars say it best: good, cheap, and quick, with a friendly hello every time.

This is a one chair shop. That means no waiting room full of strangers and no rushed appointments. One customer at a time gets Shea's full attention, so it is smart to call ahead at (513) 731-4118 and let her know you are coming.

Over 28 years behind the chair Sample is the kind of claim we take seriously, so we badge it honestly: public reviews speak warmly of Shea's long tenure, and we invite you to stop in and hear the story straight from the source.
