About

A west side crew that treats service work with pride

Rueve Landscape Company is a family owned landscape contractor based in Bridgetown, serving Cincinnati's west side and beyond since 2004.

Our story

Rueve Landscape Company started in 2004 and has grown into a trusted name on Cincinnati's west side. Owner Philip Rueve leads a team known for treating every yard as if it were their own.

We believe service work should not come with a stigma. We want to be a company known as a great opportunity to earn a living, grow, and serve a purpose in something greater than each individual. A great company can usher in change in our community, creating a healthy place for both our employees and our clients.

That is why customers stay with us for a decade or more, and why our crews are described as honest, hardworking, and respectful.
