01, The community

01

A neighborhood of twin homes, set in Allen Township.

Stone Ridge is a residential community of twin and townhome residences gathered around Graystone Circle in Northampton, Pennsylvania. The homes are known for open first floors with hardwood floors, gas fireplaces in the living room, kitchens with granite counters and stainless appliances, and two-car garages.

Move-in ready twin homes with open floor plans, minutes from the Nor-Bath Trail and Allen Township Dog Park.

The community sits in Allen Township in the Lehigh Valley, close to shopping, restaurants, major routes, and the airport. Homes here typically offer three bedrooms, two and a half baths, and over two thousand square feet of living space, with fenced yards and rear decks.

Residences at Stone Ridge

Twin and townhome residences around Graystone Circle, each with its own character. Descriptions below reflect the typical features of homes in this community.

Sample layout

Style one

The Twin

Semi-detached twin homes with Colonial architecture, stone and vinyl exteriors, and open-concept first floors.

The Twin is the signature Stone Ridge residence: three bedrooms and two and a half baths across two levels, with vaulted or tray ceilings, walk-in closets, and a gas fireplace in the living room. The main level carries hardwood floors from the foyer through the living and dining areas.

  • Open-concept living room, dining area, and kitchen
  • Kitchen with granite counters, tile backsplash, and stainless appliances
  • Primary suite with walk-in closet and dual vanities
  • Two-car attached garage with inside access

Style two

The Townhome

Two-story townhomes with finished lower levels, lofts, and first-floor laundry for everyday ease.

The Townhome adds flexible space: a second-floor loft that works as a home office, playroom, or quiet retreat, plus finished basements in many residences for media rooms, wet bars, or guest space. First-floor laundry and powder rooms keep daily life simple.

Style three

The Corner Residence

End-unit residences with extra windows, side yards, and panoramic views of the community.

Corner residences enjoy the most light: additional windows across two sides, larger side yards, and views across the neighborhood. Inside, the same open floor plan, gas fireplace, and granite-appointed kitchen define the Stone Ridge standard.

  • Vaulted ceilings and abundant natural light

The community

Northampton, Pennsylvania

Stone Ridge sits in Allen Township, just outside Northampton in the heart of the Lehigh Valley. The community of roughly one hundred twin homes gathers around Graystone Circle, a quiet loop of residences with landscaped front yards and fenced rear yards.

Life nearby

Residents are minutes from shopping centers, restaurants, and entertainment, with easy access to major routes and the airport. Outdoor time is close too: the Nor-Bath Trail and Allen Township Dog Park are nearby, and the Lehigh Valley's parks and trails are a short drive away.

Quiet streets, friendly neighbors, and everything the Lehigh Valley offers, minutes from your door.

A community that holds its value

Stone Ridge homes are consistently described as highly sought-after in the Northampton market, with well-kept exteriors, stone and vinyl siding, and mature landscaping along the circle.
