Why hardwood, why oak.

Oak has been the workhorse of American flooring for generations: hard, stable, and easy to refinish again and again. French oak brings a wider plank, a calmer grain, and a finish that hides everyday wear.

Services

Below are the kinds of work flooring contractors commonly take on. Call (626) 969-7983 to confirm exactly what French Oak Flooring offers for your project.

Hardwood floor installation

New floors, room by room or whole home. Ask about:

  • Solid and engineered oak plank installation
  • Subfloor preparation and leveling
  • Transitions, thresholds, and trim
  • Stain and finish options

Sanding and refinishing

Existing hardwood can often be brought back instead of replaced. Ask about:

  • Sanding down worn or scratched surfaces
  • Re-staining to a new color
  • Protective finish coats
  • Dust control during the job

Floor repairs

Targeted fixes for damaged boards and problem spots. Ask about:

  • Replacing damaged or cupped boards
  • Squeak and movement fixes
  • Water damage assessment
  • Spot refinishing to blend repairs

Project consultation

Not sure whether to refinish or replace? Start with a conversation. Ask about:

  • On-site or photo-based estimates
  • Material and finish guidance
  • Timeline and preparation advice
  • Care and maintenance tips
