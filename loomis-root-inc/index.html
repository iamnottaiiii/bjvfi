Our story

A family shop built around one trade.

Loomis-Root, Inc. is run by two brothers, Jim and Marvin, who have spent their working lives in and around vehicle lifts. It is the kind of shop where the owner answers the phone, does the install, and remembers your bay layout the next time you call.

The business serves repair shops, dealerships, and service stations across the Buffalo area and Western New York, selling and installing professional lifts and keeping them running with service and certified inspections. Industry listings name Loomis-Root among distributors with certified lift inspectors on staff, a credential that matters when a shop's insurance or compliance is on the line.

Customers keep coming back to the same point: the brothers know lifts. One reviewer put it plainly, saying the two bring nearly seven decades of knowledge between them. Another praised a two-post lift installed the previous year as "great," with customer service to match.
