SECTION 01

Why owners bring in a third-party inspector

ENTRY 01

Unbiased eyes on the job

APIS works for the specification, not the applicator. Independent verification keeps surface prep, mixing, and application honest at every hold point.

ENTRY 02

Calibrated instruments

Thickness gauges, adhesion testers, and environmental meters, checked against standards, so every reading in the report can be trusted and repeated.

ENTRY 03

A paper trail that holds up

Daily reports with photos, readings, and non-conformances give you documentation for warranties, payouts, and disputes, long after the crew leaves.

SECTION 02

How an inspection engagement runs

A typical sequence from first call to final sign-off. Scope and hold points are set with you before work starts.

Review the documents

Coating specifications, product data sheets, and the contractor's procedures are reviewed before mobilization.

Monitor preparation

Surface cleanliness, anchor profile, and ambient conditions are verified at each prep hold point.

Measure application

Wet and dry film thickness, adhesion, and continuity are tested with calibrated instruments.

Report and sign off

Daily photo reports, punch lists, and a final summary document the job for your records.

INSPECTION PROTOCOLS

Services

Independent verification of protective coating work, from spec review to final report. Each protocol below references the industry standard it typically follows.

SSPC-PA 2
QA OVERSIGHT

Coating inspection and QA oversight

Hold-point inspections during surface preparation and coating application, with daily reports, photos, and non-conformance tracking for owners and engineers.

Typical
ASTM D7091
SSPC-PA 2

Dry film thickness (DFT) measurement

Calibrated gauge readings taken across the coated surface and reported statistically, so you know the film meets the specified range, not just a few lucky spots.

Typical
SSPC-SP
ISO 8501

Surface preparation verification

Checks of surface cleanliness, anchor profile depth, and soluble salt levels before a single gallon of coating goes on.

Typical
ASTM D4541

Adhesion testing

Pull-off adhesion tests that quantify how well the coating bonds to the substrate, a key predictor of service life.

Typical
ASTM D5162

Holiday and pinhole detection

Low and high voltage discontinuity testing that finds pinholes and holidays invisible to the naked eye, critical for immersion and chemical service.

Typical
ENV
LOGGING

Environmental condition monitoring

Ambient temperature, surface temperature, dew point, and relative humidity logged before and during coating to confirm conditions stay within the product's window.

Typical

ANALYSIS

Coating failure analysis

Documentation and assessment of blistering, peeling, corrosion breakthrough, and other failures, with findings you can hand to a contractor or manufacturer.

Typical

Review of coating specifications, product data, and contractor quality plans before work starts, catching conflicts while they are still cheap to fix.
