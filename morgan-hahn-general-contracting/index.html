About us

A small builder with deep roots

Morgan Hahn General Contracting is a small, well-established residential builder based at 115 Millers Gap Rd in Enola, Pennsylvania. Directory listings describe the company as operating since 1980, specializing in single-family housing construction, with additions and remodels among its services.

How we work

Building is a relationship business. We keep communication direct: you know the schedule, you know the cost, and you know who to call. No layers, no surprises.

Craft over volume

We are a small company by design. Fewer projects at a time means the person quoting your job is the person overseeing it, and details do not slip through cracks.

Local knowledge

Based in Enola in the Harrisburg-Carlisle area, we know local codes, local suppliers, and the housing stock here. That matters when your addition has to match a 1960s ranch or your remodel has to work with older framing.
