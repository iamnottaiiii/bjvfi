Our Story

A neighborhood shop with a reputation that travels

Buffalo Barberz of Western NY sits at 448 Amherst St in Buffalo, a licensed New York barbershop that has become one of the West Side’s most reviewed chairs. Around 80 Google reviews average 4.5 stars, and the words that keep coming up are professional, talented, and laid back.

“Meli cuts my nephew’s hair, she is great. She spends a very ample amount of time with him and always makes sure to make him feel special.” Paraphrased from a Google review

The shop’s own description keeps it simple: wave cuts, blow outs, tapers, edge ups and cutz. In other words, the modern barbershop canon, executed with fundamentals-first craft. Regulars say the barbers will tell you what your hair actually needs, rescue mistakes other shops made, and take their time with kids who need patience in the chair.

Find the shop on Instagram at @buffalobarberz and on Facebook as Buffalo Barberz Of WNY for the most current looks coming out of the chair.
