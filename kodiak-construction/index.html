Our story

A local crew with a bear sized work ethic

Kodiak Construction is based at 48 Harvard Ave in Lynbrook and serves homeowners across Nassau County. We describe ourselves simply: your local source for quality construction and remodeling.

We are general contractors and construction managers who specialize in the projects that transform daily life: kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, and garages. Our clients tell us what sets us apart is not complicated. We show up, we stay on the job, we do skilled work, and we talk straight about money.
