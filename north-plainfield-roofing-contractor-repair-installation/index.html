How we work

A simple, predictable process from first call to final walkthrough.

1. Call or visit

Tell us what you are seeing: stains, missing shingles, storm damage. We schedule a look, usually within a day or two.

2. Honest assessment

We show you photos of what we found and lay out repair versus replacement with real costs for each.

3. Clean, fast work

Most replacements finish in one to three days. We protect your yard and haul away every scrap.

4. Final check

We walk the property with you, review the work, and make sure you are satisfied before we call it done.

About us

Local to North Plainfield

North Plainfield Roofing Contractor Repair and Installation is based at 503 Somerset St in North Plainfield, NJ. We work across Somerset County and nearby towns, and we know the housing stock here: the older shingle roofs, the flat-roofed additions, the storm patterns that hit this corridor every year. That local knowledge goes into every estimate.

We are a repair-first shop. Plenty of roofers push replacement because it pays more. We will show you photos of your roof and tell you honestly whether a repair buys you five more good years or whether replacement is the smarter money.
