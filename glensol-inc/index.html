Our story

Built for the solar decade in Southern California

Glensol inc grew up alongside the rooftop solar boom in Los Angeles County. From our Glendale base, we have completed more than a thousand solar installations for homeowners and businesses who wanted lower bills without the runaround.

What sets us apart is simple: we are licensed for both general building and electrical work, and we carry an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau. That means the crew that designs your system is the crew that wires it, permitted and inspected, with one company accountable from first site visit to final monitoring check.

Ten plus years in, our mission has not changed: make going solar easy, honest, and built to last.
