MS Salon opened on Richmond Rd to be the kind of shop Staten Island deserves: honest work, fair chairs, and cuts worth coming back for.

The shop

Built for the neighborhood

Situated on Richmond Rd, MS Salon keeps barbering simple: clippers, shears and attention to detail. No gimmicks, no upsell games, just the craft done right. Every visit starts with a conversation about what you want and ends with a cut you will want to show off.
