The warning signs

A clogged vent tells on itself. Listen.

Lint builds up silently, load after load. Most homeowners never see it until the dryer stops working or worse. If any of these sound familiar, your vent is overdue.

What we clean
  • ⏱Clothes take forever to dryTwo cycles for one load means hot air has nowhere to go.
  • 🔥The dryer or laundry room feels hotTrapped heat is the top sign of a restricted vent.
  • 👃A burning or musty smellLint is combustible. A smell means it is time, not later.
  • 🧺Excess lint, even after cleaning the trapThe trap only catches a fraction. The rest coats the vent.

Our story

The name says it all

DryerIdentiFIRE: we identify fire hazards before they start. A locally owned company in Seymour, CT, built around one mission: safer, more efficient dryers.

Why we exist

Most people never think about the vent.

They clean the lint trap and assume the job is done. But the trap catches only a fraction of the lint. The rest coats the inside of the vent, the inside of the dryer, and builds up year after year.

That is where DryerIdentiFIRE LLC comes in. We specialize in dryer vent cleaning and inside-of-dryer cleaning, which extends the life of the dryer, keeps it running efficiently, dries clothes faster, and reduces the risk of dryer fires.

And we do not stop at lint. We test for leaks on flexible gas fuel lines and valves with a gas leak detector. We check for cracked or worn power cords on gas and electric dryers. We clean refrigerator coils and AC or heat pump condenser coils. And on every visit, we ask our customers about their smoke detectors.
