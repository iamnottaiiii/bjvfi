Our Story

Earned one chair at a time

Hair Place is a single location, New York State licensed barbershop in Riverside, Buffalo. Directory records list the shop under the state appearance enhancement program, and public listings have carried its name on Tonawanda Street for years.

What keeps the chairs full is simpler than any listing: steady hands, honest prices, and cuts people notice. With a 4.9 star average across 127 Google reviews, it ranks among the best reviewed barbershops in the city.

No gimmicks, no rush. Sit down, say what you want, and leave sharper than you came in.
