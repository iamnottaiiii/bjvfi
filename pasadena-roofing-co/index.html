Warning signs

Signs your roof may need attention

Roofing problems often begin with smaller warning signs. Addressing concerns early may help prevent more extensive damage to the property.

  • Roof leaks or interior moisture
  • Missing, cracked, or deteriorated materials
  • Flashing problems around edges and penetrations
  • Storm damage from wind, rain, or debris
  • Standing water on flat or low-slope roofs
  • An aging roof needing frequent repairs

Repair or replacement?

The best solution depends on the age of the roof, the severity of the damage, and whether the problem is isolated or widespread. Localized damage may be repairable, while extensive deterioration may make replacement worth considering.

Roofing questions from Pasadena property owners

How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

Whether a roof should be repaired or replaced depends on its age, overall condition, how widespread the damage is, and whether the problem is isolated or recurring.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. Pasadena Roofing Co handles commercial roofing projects involving business properties, low-slope roofing systems, and larger commercial buildings.

What are common signs of roof damage?

Common signs include roof leaks, deteriorated roofing materials, damaged flashing, interior moisture, drainage concerns, and recurring problems in previously repaired areas.

What roofing materials are available?

Roofing options may include shingles, tile, metal roofing, flat roofing, and other systems depending on the building and the project.
