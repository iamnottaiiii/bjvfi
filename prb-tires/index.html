What we do

Tires and tire service, done in one visit

Everything a tire shop should do, without the upsell. Call (443) 945-1298 to check stock for your size before you drive over.

New Tires

New tires in common passenger and light truck sizes, mounted and balanced while you wait. Call with your size and we will tell you what is in stock and the out the door price.

Used Tires

Quality inspected used tires for drivers who need safe tread on a budget. A smart option for a commuter car, a spare, or a vehicle you are keeping on the road a little longer.

Mounting and Balancing

Proper mounting, computer balancing and torque checked lug nuts. Reduces vibration, extends tire life and keeps your ride smooth at highway speed.

Flat Repair

Plugs and patches for repairable punctures, done fast. Turnpike travelers regularly report being back on the road in under five minutes.

Tire Rotation Checks

Have us look at wear patterns and tread depth when you stop in. Even wear means longer tire life and better grip in rain.

Road Trip Help

Caught a flat passing through Penns Grove? Call first and we will get you in the same day so your trip keeps moving.
