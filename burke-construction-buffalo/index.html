Our story

A Buffalo Builder, Plain and Simple

Burke Construction LLC is a general contracting company based at 266 Oak Street in downtown Buffalo, NY. We serve homeowners and businesses across Buffalo and Western New York.

Our approach is straightforward: one contractor, one crew, one point of accountability. We plan carefully, communicate clearly, and treat your home or business like it is our own while we are in it.

Whether it is a kitchen that needs new life, a basement waiting to become living space, or a commercial build-out on a deadline, we bring the same standard to every job: solid work, clean sites, and honest dealing from estimate to walkthrough.
