Our craft

Gonzalez's Iron Works is a small, family-name iron shop in North Hollywood. Directory records list it under architectural metalwork, established in 2002, with a small crew working out of the Sherman Way shop.

"Measure twice, cut once, weld it like it has to outlast the house."

That is the ethic behind every panel and gate that leaves the shop. Public records show a business that has stayed small on purpose: a couple of skilled hands, one shop, and work that carries the family name.

How a project runs

  • You call and describe the fence, gate, or railing you need.
  • The shop measures the opening and talks through design options.
  • Your piece is fabricated, finished, and welded in the shop.
  • It is delivered, set, and aligned on site.
