Our story

An ADU company with one focus

D-Best ADU Company is a construction company based on Foothill Boulevard in La Crescenta, focused on one thing: accessory dwelling units done right. While general contractors juggle kitchens, bathrooms, and additions, we spend every week on ADUs, which means we know the zoning quirks, the plan checkers, and the details that make a small space live large.

We handle the whole project under one roof: feasibility, design, permits, and construction. One team, one point of contact, and a build process designed to avoid the surprises that stall so many ADU projects.

If you have been thinking about a backyard cottage, a garage conversion, or a home for a parent, start with a conversation. We will tell you honestly what your lot can hold and what it will take.

Why homeowners choose a specialist

ADU projects live or die on the details: setback math, utility trenching, shear walls, and energy compliance. A team that builds ADUs every week spots problems on paper instead of discovering them mid-build, which saves months and real money.

What to expect from us

Straight answers about what your lot allows. A written scope and budget before work begins. Photo updates while we build. And a final walkthrough where we hand over keys, manuals, and warranty info, not a punch list of excuses.
