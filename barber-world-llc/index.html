About Barber World LLC

A neighborhood barber shop with a following

Barber World LLC sits at 8172 Mall Rd in Florence, Kentucky, inside the Mall Road Center shopping center. Reviewers trace the shop back to an earlier location on Dixie Highway in Erlanger, and loyal customers followed it to Florence.

The shop keeps a relaxed, family friendly feel: televisions and video games for entertainment, multiple barbers so most hair types are covered, and walk-ins welcome alongside scheduled appointments.

Honest cuts, honest prices, no passport required.

Details here come from public review sites and directories. Where we could not verify something, we say so rather than guess.

Fade cuts are the headline

Multiple five star reviewers call Barber World the best place in Northern Kentucky for a fade cut. One long time customer says they go to no one else and followed the shop from its old Erlanger location. Another drives 45 minutes each way every two weeks and says the barber far exceeds expectations.

Source: public reviews via Google, Birdeye, and review directories.

Great with kids and teens

Parents praise the shop for first barber experiences and for precision cuts their teens are proud to show off at school. Reviewers describe the barbers as professional, on time, and concerned with image.

Source: public reviews via Google, Birdeye, and review directories.

Shaves and value

A reviewer calls the shave great and the service even better, saying the price is far cheaper than anywhere else they checked for a decent shave, and calling the barber their forever barber.

Source: public reviews via Google and review directories.

Not every trip goes smoothly

In the interest of honesty: one reviewer says they scheduled two appointments a week out and the barber was not there, and they could not be worked in. Another says the shop was rude and messed up their son's hair, preferring to go back to a chain instead. A few low ratings exist alongside the many five star ones.

Source: public reviews via Google and review directories.
