Our Story

D & R Auto Repair LLC is a locally owned auto repair shop at 8026 Old Alexandria Ferry Rd in Clinton, Maryland. We opened our doors to give Southern Maryland drivers a repair shop that treats them like neighbors, because that is what they are.

Our approach is simple: diagnose carefully, explain clearly, and repair correctly. Whether it is routine maintenance or an unexpected breakdown, you will get an honest assessment and work that stands up.

Why Clinton drivers choose us

We keep our scheduling straightforward, our estimates clear, and our communication open from drop-off to pickup. When your car is ready, we walk you through what was done and what to watch for next.
