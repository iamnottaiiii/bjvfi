A shop that treats you like a regular, even on day one

There is a reason the same families keep coming back to 691 Grafton Street. Justin's is the old school kind of barbershop: unhurried chairs, honest conversation, and barbers who remember how you like it.

About the Shop

Justin's Barber Shop opened its doors in 1997 on Grafton Street, and it has been a Grafton Hill fixture ever since. Nearly three decades in, the formula has not changed: skilled barbers, fair prices, and a chair that feels like yours.

The shop keeps things personal. Reviewers name barbers they trust by name, including Justin, Craig, Chad, and Andrew, and describe building real relationships with the whole crew over the years.

It is also one of the more patient shops in the city for kids. One reviewer wrote that their very fidgety son felt immediately comfortable here and became a willing, regular customer, calling it a big win for their family.

Walk-ins are welcome, prices stay friendly, and the vibe stays easy. That is the whole business plan, and it has worked since 1997.
