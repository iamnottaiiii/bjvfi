03 / About

About Fetsko

The company

A New Cumberland Builder

Fetsko Construction Inc is a construction company based in New Cumberland, Pennsylvania, serving the Harrisburg-Carlisle area. We work primarily on residential and multifamily construction, renovations, and additions.

We run a straightforward operation: clear scopes, honest schedules, and workmanship we would put our own name on. When you call, you talk to the people who will actually be on your job site.

How We Work

  • Written scope before work begins
  • One point of contact per project
  • Licensed trades coordinated in-house
  • Job site kept clean and safe
  • Final walkthrough on every job
