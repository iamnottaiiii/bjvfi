Our Story

A Florida company,
on the river

Brophy Contracting Inc. has been a Florida corporation since 2000, working out of Ruskin on the south shore of Tampa Bay.

Since 2000

Family-led.
Florida-built.

Brophy Contracting Inc. was incorporated under Florida law on April 24, 2000, and remains an active corporation in good standing. The company is led by president Drew A. Brophy, keeping it a family-run operation rather than a branch of some national chain.

The shop sits at 1539 River Dr SW in Ruskin, minutes from the Little Manatee River and the water it feeds. That matters here: building on the south shore means building for humidity, summer storms, and salt air. We spec materials and methods that hold up to Florida weather, not just Florida budgets.

As a general contractor, we take the whole job off your plate: permits, scheduling, the trades, the finishes, and the final walkthrough. You get one number to call and one crew accountable for the result.
