About the Shop

A Note from the Shop

Dear neighbor,

Preferred Cutz opened its doors on West Galbraith Road in 2007 with a simple idea: give every client a careful, unhurried cut at a fair price. Nearly two decades later, that is still the whole philosophy.

This is a neighborhood barbershop in the truest sense. Men, women, and children are welcome in the chair. Regulars stop in for their standing appointment, first timers walk in off the street, and everyone leaves a little sharper than they arrived.

There is no rush here and no pretense. Just clean tools, a steady hand, and the kind of conversation you can only find in a barbershop.

With thanks,
The team at Preferred Cutz
