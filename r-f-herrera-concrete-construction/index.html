Our story

One family. One trade. Since 1978.

R F Herrera Concrete Construction was founded in 1978 and has stayed a family owned operation ever since. Owner Richard Herrera runs a small crew, one to four people, which means the person you talk to is the person on your job site.

The company holds an active California contractor license in both Masonry and General Building, license number 356559, and carries a contractor bond. Nearly five decades in, the reputation is built the old way: show up, do it right, and make the customer glad they called.
