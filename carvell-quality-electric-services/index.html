About

A Highspire shop with old-school standards

Carvell Quality Electric Services is a small electrical contracting business based on Market Street in Highspire, Pennsylvania. We serve homeowners and small businesses across Highspire, Steelton, Middletown, and the greater Harrisburg area of Dauphin County.

The way we work

One job at a time, with a clean truck and a full stock of parts. We diagnose before we quote, explain what we found in plain words, and stand behind the work.

What we believe

Electricity is not the place to cut corners. Tight connections, proper boxes, correct breaker sizes, and wiring you would be proud to show an inspector.

Who we serve

Homeowners with older houses that need patient rewiring, landlords turning units, and small businesses that cannot afford downtime.

If it involves wire, ask us first

Emergency sparking or burning smell? Call now. Everything else, call during business hours or reach out through the contact page.
