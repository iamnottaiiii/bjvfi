What we do

Services, end to end.

Tap any service to see what is included. Every job starts with a conversation about what you need and a clear quote before work begins.

Panel replacements and upgrades for homes and businesses outgrowing their current electrical capacity. If breakers trip often or your panel is decades old, an upgrade brings safer, more reliable power distribution to the whole building.

Call to discuss your panel and get a clear quote for the work.

Interior and exterior lighting installation, including recessed lighting, fixtures, landscape lighting, and security lighting. Good lighting changes how a space looks, feels, and functions after dark.

Ask about energy efficient options when you call.

New wiring for remodels and additions, plus rewiring for older buildings with outdated or damaged wiring. Proper wiring is the foundation everything else in your electrical system depends on.

Installation and replacement of outlets, light switches, dimmers, and GFCI protection where it is needed. Small upgrades that make daily life noticeably more convenient.

Safe ceiling fan installation with proper support boxes and wiring, including rooms where no fan was wired before. A fan installed correctly runs quietly and safely for years.

Dedicated circuits and home EV charger installation so you can charge overnight with confidence. The right circuit sizing keeps charging fast and your panel protected.

Diagnosis and repair of electrical problems: breakers that trip, outlets that stop working, flickering lights, and power loss in part of a building. The goal is always to find the real cause, not just the symptom.

Whole property electrical inspections that check panels, wiring, outlets, and grounding for safety issues. Useful before buying a property, after storm damage, or as periodic peace of mind for older buildings.
