Services

Repairs done right,
priced in writing

Every job starts with an on-roof look and a written quote. No obligation, no pressure for work you do not need.

Roof leak repair

We trace active leaks to their true entry point, around chimneys, valleys, skylights, sidewalls and vents, then seal and re-flash so they stay fixed.

Shingle replacement

Wind-lifted, cracked, curled or missing shingles replaced to match your existing roof as closely as materials allow.

Flashing and vent repair

Step flashing, counter flashing, pipe boots and attic vents are where most leaks begin. We rebuild those details the right way.

Storm and wind damage

Fast assessments after wind, hail and heavy snow. Emergency patching to keep water out, plus photos and notes for your insurance claim.

Roof inspections

Buying, selling, or just worried about that stain? A full walk of the roof with photos and a plain-language report on what we find.

Roof tune-ups

Sealant touch-ups, nail pops reset, small flashing fixes, the maintenance that keeps a small problem from becoming a replacement.
