About us

WC Renovation Corp is an interior construction contractor based in Woodmere, NY, serving the Five Towns and greater Nassau County.

Our story

Renovations are won
in the details

WC Renovation Corp started with a simple observation: most interior renovations fail in the handoffs, between the demo crew and the tile setter, between the plumber and the painter. So we built a company that keeps the whole interior under one roof.

As a contractor, home improvement and carpentry outfit, we handle kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, drywall, trim and full interior build-outs. One point of contact, one schedule, one standard of finish.

Based at 1 Club Dr in Woodmere, we work across the Five Towns: Cedarhurst, Hewlett, Inwood, Lawrence and Woodmere, plus nearby Nassau County neighborhoods. Evening and Saturday hours mean you can actually reach us after work.

How we work

Straightforward from day one

Real communication

You get updates as the work progresses, and a phone number that actually gets answered.

Finish-first mindset

Caulk lines, grout, paint edges, hardware alignment. The last 5 percent gets the first 5 percent of our attention.

Respect for your home

Shoe covers, dust barriers and daily tidying. We treat an occupied home like it is occupied.
