Our story

From Southside Marketplace to McHenry Row

The name on the door changed. The promise behind the counter did not.

Same team, new home

A familiar cleaner in a new location

McHenry Row Cleaners began as Southside Marketplace Cleaners, serving Baltimore with dry cleaning, laundry and alterations. When the shop moved to its new home at 1614 Whetstone Way in the McHenry Row shopping center, the name changed, but the team, the craft and the neighborhood-first attitude came along for the ride.

Today the shop sits in the heart of Locust Point, an easy stop on the way to or from the Fort McHenry area. Regulars followed the move, and new customers keep finding their way in, drawn by word of mouth and a 4.5 star rating built one pressed shirt at a time.

What has not changed: a friendly staff, careful work, fair prices, and a willingness to help when you need it fast. That is the whole story, and it is a good one.
