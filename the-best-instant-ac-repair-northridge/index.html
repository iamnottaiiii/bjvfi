Good to know

Small signs your AC
needs attention

Catching these early usually means a smaller repair bill.

Weak or warm airflow

Often a refrigerant issue, a dirty coil or a failing compressor. The longer it runs weak, the harder it works.

Short cycling

Turning on and off in quick bursts points to thermostat, electrical or airflow problems that wear the system down.

Water where it should not be

Puddles around the indoor unit usually mean a clogged drain line. Left alone, it can damage ceilings and walls.

Rising energy bills

A system working harder than it should costs you every month. A tune-up often brings efficiency back.
