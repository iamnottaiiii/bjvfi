Our services

Heating and cooling, end to end.

Every service starts with a real diagnosis and an upfront price. No surprises.

01

Air conditioning repair

Systems blowing warm air, short cycling, leaking water, or making unusual noise. We find the fault, from capacitors and contactors to refrigerant issues, and repair it with quality parts. Call for a service visit.

02

Heating repair and service

Furnaces and heat pumps that will not start, blow cold air, or cycle constantly. We service gas and electric heating equipment to keep your home safe and warm.

03

System installation and replacement

When repair no longer makes sense, we design a replacement sized for your home with proper load calculations, install it to manufacturer spec, and verify performance before we leave.

04

Preventive maintenance

Seasonal tune-ups: coil cleaning, refrigerant checks, electrical inspection, thermostat calibration and airflow measurement. The best way to avoid a breakdown on the hottest day of the year.

05

Indoor air quality

Upgraded filtration, duct inspection and airflow balancing to reduce dust, allergens and hot or cold spots around the house.

06

Thermostats and controls

Smart thermostat installation and setup, zoning advice, and control wiring for better comfort and lower bills.

Our story

Pasadena's local comfort team.

Heating and cooling is all we do

Thermal Air Conditioning Inc is a heating and air conditioning contractor on East Colorado Boulevard in Pasadena, California. We focus on one trade so we can do it well: keeping homes and businesses comfortable through every season.

Our work covers the full life of your system. Repairs when something breaks, maintenance to keep it running, and full replacement when the old unit has given all it has. Every visit starts with listening to what you are experiencing, then a real diagnosis before any work is quoted.

We serve Pasadena and the surrounding San Gabriel Valley. If your home is too hot, too cold, or just not right, give us a call and we will take it from there.
