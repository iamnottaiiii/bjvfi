Roofing Services

Everything your roof needs

Premier Restoration Corporation is a full service roofing contractor in Jamaica, Queens. Every project starts with a free inspection and an honest written estimate.

Roof Replacement Most requested

When repairs stop making sense, we replace the whole system: tear-off, decking check, underlayment, shingles, flashing, and ventilation, finished with a full cleanup.

  • Architectural asphalt shingle systems
  • Full tear-off with deck inspection
  • Ridge vents and proper attic ventilation
Free written estimate, call (718) 712-3260

Roof Repair

Most leaks are small problems caught late. We find the source, fix it properly, and check the surrounding area so the same leak does not come back next storm.

  • Leak detection and repair
  • Missing or damaged shingle replacement
  • Flashing, chimney, and vent repairs
  • Storm damage response

Flat and Low-Slope Roofing

Flat roofs need the right system and the right drainage. We install and service flat roofing for rowhouses, extensions, garages, and commercial buildings.

  • Flat roof installation and replacement
  • Seam and ponding water correction
  • Routine flat roof maintenance

Gutters and Waterproofing

A roof only works if water has somewhere to go. We install gutters and apply waterproofing that protects foundations, basements, and facades.

  • Seamless gutter installation
  • Gutter repair and cleaning guidance
  • Roof and foundation waterproofing

Inspections and Maintenance

Twice-a-year checkups catch cracked shingles, worn flashing, and clogged drainage before they become interior damage.

  • Free visual inspections with photos
  • Pre-storm and post-storm assessments
  • Maintenance plans for landlords and managers
Inspections are free, call (718) 712-3260
Our Story

Queens roofers, through and through

Premier Restoration Corporation is a roofing contractor based at 155-25 Bayview Ave in Jamaica, New York. We started with a simple belief: a roof is the most important part of a home, and the people who work on it should treat it that way.

New York roofs take a beating: summer heat, winter freeze, nor'easters, and everything in between. We build and repair roofs specifically for this climate, using materials and methods proven on Queens homes and commercial buildings.

What sets us apart is straightforwardness. We show up when we say we will, we explain what we find in plain language, and we clean up like we were never there. No pressure, no mystery line items, just honest roofing work.

How we work

  1. Free inspection. We look at the whole roof and document what we find.
  2. Honest estimate. Written pricing, plain language, no pressure.
  3. Clean install. One crew, full protection of your property, daily cleanup.
  4. Final walkthrough. We review the finished work with you before we leave.

Serving Jamaica and the five boroughs

From single-family homes in Queens to commercial buildings across New York City, our crews are local and ready to roll.
