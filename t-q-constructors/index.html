Capabilities

What we build

Specialized fabrication and installation of process systems. Every project, from customized solutions to meticulously built process skids, meets the highest quality and performance standards.

Fabrication

We offer specialized fabrication of process systems in our Dayton, Kentucky and Miamiville, Ohio facilities. As an ASME BPE Committee member, we excel in fabricating components, systems and skids to the highest standards.

High-purity welds, exact tolerances and full documentation come standard on every build.

Process Systems

We specialize in fabricating and installing high-quality process systems and skids for the food, beverage and pharmaceutical industries, building tailored systems that meet rigorous quality and efficiency standards.

From single skids to full plant systems, our builds enhance productivity while ensuring compliance with strict industry regulations.
