Our Story

A Stockbridge handyman built on word of mouth, one fixed fence at a time.

The guy next door, with the toolbox

Thatguy handy service started the way most good handymen start: fixing things for neighbors. A gate here, a leaky faucet there, and soon the phone would not stop ringing.

Based at 325 Estates Ave in Stockbridge, we serve Henry County homeowners who would rather call one reliable person than juggle three contractors. The name says it all: need a guy? That guy is us.

We keep it simple on purpose. Show up when we say, charge what we quoted, and clean up like we were never there.
