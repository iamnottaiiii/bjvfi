About

Solid as granite

Granite Roofing Tech is a roofing contractor based in San Fernando, California, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley. The name is the mission: roofs built with the permanence of stone and the precision of modern roofing tech.

We keep the operation lean on purpose. Fewer layers between you and the crew on your roof means better communication, cleaner job sites, and quotes that reflect the work, not the overhead.
