Our story

漂亮人生: a beautiful life, one haircut at a time

Maeda Beauty Salon Inc opened its doors on Avenue U in 2019, and in just a few years it built one of the most loyal clienteles in the neighborhood.

The beginning

A salon that listens

From day one, the formula was simple: treat every client like a regular. Stylists take time to understand what you want, what your hair does naturally, and what fits your life, then deliver it with care.

Word spread the way it always does in Brooklyn: one happy client at a time. Reviewers say they found their go-to spot for haircuts, highlighting and styling here, and many kept returning even after moving away.

The signature

The wash everyone talks about

If there is one thing Maeda is known for, it is the wash: a relaxing scalp massage during the shampoo, followed by a hot scalp mask that leaves hair silky and the client completely at ease.

Guests describe the service as attentive and patient, the space as clean and comfortable, and the whole visit as fast without ever feeling rushed.

The promise

Beautiful hair, honest prices

Quality service, great prices, great staff: that is the review that sums up Maeda. No appointment needed, minimal wait, and a finish that looks like you spent far more.

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, the welcome is the same. Come see why the chairs stay full.
