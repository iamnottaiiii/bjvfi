About

Bavarianpowerhp

Bavarianpowerhp is a Bloomfield machine shop built around BMW, Mercedes and Porsche engines. From cylinder head resurfacing to full performance builds, every job leaves the shop measured, balanced and documented.

Bavarianpowerhp started with one belief: a German engine deserves a machinist, not a guess. The shop pairs old-school measuring tools with modern CNC discipline.

Every job is quoted before metal is cut, and every finished part ships with its measurements. Call ahead so the crew can schedule your block or head.
