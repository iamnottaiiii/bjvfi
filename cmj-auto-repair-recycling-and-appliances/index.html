How the loop works

Every car and appliance that comes through our doors gets the same honest question: can this be fixed, reused, or recycled? Here is what that means for you.

Step 1

Repair

Skilled hands diagnose the real problem first. Fixing what you already own is almost always cheaper than replacing it, and it keeps a working machine in service for years.

Step 2

Reuse

Working parts and refurbished appliances get a second life. Reused components cost less and perform just fine when they are picked and tested by someone who knows them.

Step 3

Recycle

When something is truly done, its metals and materials are recycled responsibly instead of dumped. Nothing that can be recovered goes into the ground.

Our story: waste not, want not

CMJ auto repair/recycling and appliances is a local Cincinnati repair service built around a simple idea: almost everything is worth a second look before it becomes trash.

The shop philosophy

Too many repairable cars and appliances get tossed because replacing feels easier than fixing. We push back on that. Repair comes first, reuse comes second, and recycling catches everything that is left.

  • Repair first. Honest diagnostics and fair fixes for cars and household appliances.
  • Reuse always. Working parts and refurbished machines go back to neighbors, not the dump.
  • Recycle responsibly. Scrap metal and dead units are recycled so raw materials live on.
