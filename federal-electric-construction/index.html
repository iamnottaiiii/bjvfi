Services

Electrical construction and service

Choose a tab. If your project is not listed, call and ask.

New construction wiring

Full electrical scope for new builds: service entrance, distribution, device rough-in, and trim-out through final inspection.

  • Service entrance + panels: meter setups, main and sub-panels
  • Rough-in: branch circuits, device boxes, low-voltage pathways
  • Trim-out: devices, fixtures, final connections
  • Inspection support: code compliance from plan to sign-off

Commercial fit-outs

Electrical build-outs and upgrades for offices, retail, and light industrial spaces.

  • Fit-out wiring: power and lighting for new tenant spaces
  • Lighting upgrades: LED retrofits and controls
  • Dedicated circuits: equipment and machinery feeds
  • Service changes: capacity upgrades for growing loads

Residential service

Repairs, upgrades, and installations for homes around Stratford.

  • Troubleshooting: fault-finding for outages and faults
  • Panel upgrades: service changes and sub-panels
  • Lighting + devices: fixtures, fans, dimmers, outlets
  • Safety: GFCI/AFCI protection and grounding checks
