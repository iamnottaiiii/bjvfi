One family name, two houses of style

Jay Gee's Barbershop & Beauty Salon sits at 1123 Sycamore St in the heart of Cincinnati, built on a simple idea: great grooming should not make anyone choose between a barbershop and a salon. So both live here, side by side, under one roof.

The barber's side

Charcoal walls, steel chairs, and the steady rhythm of clippers. This is where tradition holds court: crisp lines, honest conversation, and finishes that last.

The beauty side

Rose tones and a calmer tempo. This is where detail takes over: color, texture, and finishing work done with patience and care.

Walk-ins are welcome when chairs are open, and regulars know the fastest way to a chair is a quick call to (513) 975-7691.
