When to come see us

Do not drive on these

Tires talk. If yours are saying any of this, swing by 6729 Yocum St before a small problem becomes a roadside one.

  • Tread is gone

    Bald spots, the wear bars showing, or failing the penny test.

  • Losing air

    Topping off the same tire every few days means a slow leak worth fixing.

  • Shakes and pulls

    Vibration at speed or drifting to one side often points at tires or balance.
