De Nuestra Vitrina

A panadería is a morning ritual. Here is what ours looks like.

Every panadería has its own rhythm: the trays coming out warm, the smell of sugar and yeast on the air, the regulars who know exactly which shelf holds their favorite. At Panadería Maya on Western Avenue, the case is meant to be full and the welcome warm.

Pan Dulce

Conchas with their scored sugar shells, flaky orejas, buttery cuernos, crumbly polvorones, and fruit empanadas. The sweet breads that turn an ordinary morning into something worth sitting down for.

Pan Salado

Crusty bolillos and soft teleras, baked for tortas, sandwiches, and the family table. Simple bread, done right, every day.

Nuestra Historia

A neighborhood panadería on Western Avenue

Panadería Maya INC is Blue Island's neighborhood panadería, located on Western Avenue. The name Maya is a nod to the rich baking heritage of Mexico and Central America, where pan dulce is more than food. It is family, celebration, and home.

Like any true panadería, our case is meant to be full and our welcome warm. Whether you are picking up conchas for the morning, bolillos for dinner, or a cake for a special day, we are glad you stopped by.

Hablamos español. Pregunta por la selección del día. Call ahead at (872) 224-8870 and we will tell you what just came out of the oven.
