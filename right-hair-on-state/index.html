Our story

A State Street staple, built one loyal client at a time.

Right Hair On State describes itself with over 40 years in the hair industry, and it shows. Clients say they have been getting their hair done here for years, calling it their go to salon in Syracuse for cuts and highlights that earn compliments.

Reviewers praise the friendly atmosphere and affordable prices, and many name Michelle, the stylist clients have trusted and referred since 2007. The common thread in every review is simple: personalized care, a comfortable visit, and hair done right.

Walk in with an idea, or walk in with no idea at all. Either way you leave looking your best.
