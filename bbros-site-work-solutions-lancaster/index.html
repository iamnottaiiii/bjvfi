About

Builders who start with the dirt

BBros.Site Work Solutions is a Lancaster, New York home builder built around its own site work operation. The idea is simple: the crew that shapes the lot and the crew that builds the house work as one team.

Why site work matters

A home is only as good as the ground it sits on. Proper grading, drainage and excavation up front prevent the foundation problems that cost owners later.

Straightforward and local

Based on Fairfield Ave in Lancaster, serving Erie County. You talk to the people doing the work, not a sales office.

Coordinated schedules

Site prep and construction planned together means fewer delays and no finger pointing between subcontractors.
