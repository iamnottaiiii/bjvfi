What we treat

Full-service pest defense
for your home

From one-time knockdowns to year-round protection plans, every service starts with a free inspection and a plan built for your home.

01

Ant Control

Odorous house ants, carpenter ants, pavement ants. We trace trails back to the colony and eliminate it at the source instead of chasing foragers around your kitchen.

InteriorExteriorColony elimination

Southern Maryland homes see heavy ant pressure in spring and summer. Our barrier treatments stop new colonies from moving in, and baiting programs finish the ones already inside your walls.

02

Roach Control

German, American and brown-banded cockroaches. Thorough crack-and-crevice treatment in kitchens and baths, plus sanitation guidance so they have nowhere to breed.

KitchensFollow-up visits

Roaches multiply fast and trigger allergies. We do not do one-and-done sprays; we schedule follow-ups until monitoring shows the population is gone.

03

Rodent Control

Mice and rats. Entry-point exclusion, trapping, and exterior bait stations that intercept rodents before they reach your walls.

ExclusionTrappingAttics and crawlspaces

A mouse needs only a dime-sized gap. We seal the gaps, remove the current population, and keep watch so the next ones never get in.

04

Spider and Wasp Control

Web-building spiders, ground nests and aerial wasp nests. Safe removal of active nests and de-webbing around eaves, porches and play areas.

Nest removalDe-webbing

We remove the nest first, then treat the harborage so new queens do not rebuild in the same spot next season.

05

Occasional Invaders

Centipedes, millipedes, silverfish, earwigs, crickets. The damp-basement crowd, handled with moisture-source advice and perimeter defense.

BasementsPerimeter defense

These pests signal moisture and entry gaps. We address the conditions that invite them in, not just the bugs you see.

06

Year-Round Protection Plan

Scheduled barrier treatments through every season. Each visit re-inspects, re-treats the perimeter, and catches new activity early.

RecurringAll seasons

The most reliable way to stay pest-free. Pests change with the seasons; your protection should too. Call for plan details and pricing.
