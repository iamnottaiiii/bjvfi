Our Story

Born in Pittsburgh, built on showing up

Stackr Moving LLC started with a simple observation: most moving stress comes from movers who do not communicate. Late arrivals, vague pricing, and crews that rush through your home. We decided to run the opposite kind of company.

Based in Pittsburgh, PA, we serve Etna, Shaler, Millvale, and the greater metro area with small, well trained crews. Every move gets a confirmed arrival window, a clear hourly rate, and a crew lead you can actually reach by phone.

We are open seven days a week, 8 AM to 9 PM, because moves do not always happen on a weekday schedule. Call us and you will talk to someone who can book your move on the spot.
