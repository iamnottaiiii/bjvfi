Installation done right, plank by plank

A great floor starts long before the first board goes down. We measure moisture, level the subfloor, and acclimate every plank to your home, so the finished floor stays flat and quiet for years.

Choose from solid hardwood, engineered wood, laminate, and luxury vinyl plank, installed with clean transitions and tight seams.

Sanding and refinishing

When the wood is sound but the finish is tired, refinishing restores color and protection without tearing out a single board.

  • Full sanding to bare wood
  • Stain color consultation with real samples
  • Matte, satin, and semi-gloss finishes
  • Dust-aware sanding equipment
  • Screen-and-recoat refreshes for lightly worn floors

Pricing: call for a free in-home estimate.

Floor repairs

Targeted fixes that blend into your existing floor, so one damaged board does not mean a whole new room.

  • Board replacement and lacing in new wood
  • Squeak diagnosis and subfloor fastening
  • Water-damage assessment and repair
  • Scratch and dent touch-ups
  • Transition and threshold fixes
