Lawn Care

Regular mowing that keeps grass healthy and the whole property looking sharp.

  • Weekly and biweekly mowing with trimming and edging
  • Overseeding to thicken thin spots
  • Fertilization scheduling guidance for New England lawns
  • Leaf and debris removal with every visit

Spring and Fall Cleanups

The heavy lifting that resets your yard for the season ahead.

  • Leaf removal from lawns, beds, and corners
  • Branch and stick pickup after winter storms
  • Bed clearing and cut-back of perennials
  • Gutter-line and curb cleanup so the property reads clean

Spring slots fill fast in Burlington. Booking a spring cleanup early keeps your yard first in line once the snow melts.

  • Mulch delivery and spreading in natural dark brown or black
  • Bed edging and weeding for crisp, defined lines
  • Shrub and perennial planting suited to Massachusetts soil

Pruning and Shaping

Careful cuts that keep shrubs healthy and hedges looking intentional.

  • Hedge trimming and shaping
  • Shrub pruning timed for each species
  • Small tree limbing for clearance and light
