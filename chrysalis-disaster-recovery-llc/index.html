How we work

From damage to done,
in four steps

Disaster recovery is stressful enough. Our process is simple, and we walk you through every step.

1Emergency Response

Water pouring in or a tree through the roof, call us first. We stabilize the situation: water extraction, board-up, tarping, whatever stops the damage from getting worse.

  • Emergency water extraction
  • Board-up and roof tarping
  • Damage containment

2Assessment & Insurance

We document everything and work with your insurance adjuster, so the claim reflects the real scope of the damage.

3Cleanup & Mitigation

Dry-out, smoke and soot removal, mold remediation. We get the property clean and safe before rebuilding starts.

4Rebuild & Restore

Drywall, flooring, roofing, paint: the full construction rebuild, handled by one company, finished to like-new condition.

Water Damage Restoration

Burst pipes, appliance leaks, flooding, roof leaks: we extract the water, dry the structure, and stop mold before it starts.

  • Emergency water extraction
  • Structural drying and dehumidification
  • Damaged material removal
  • Moisture monitoring to verified dry

Fire & Smoke Damage

After the firefighters leave, the real work starts. We clear soot and smoke odor, then rebuild what the fire took.

  • Emergency board-up and site security
  • Soot and smoke residue removal
  • Odor neutralization
  • Full structural rebuild

Storm Damage Repair

Long Island storms do not mess around. We secure the building fast, then repair roofs, siding, windows, and interiors.

  • Emergency roof tarping
  • Tree and debris impact repair
  • Roof, siding, and window repair
  • Interior water intrusion repair

Mold Remediation

Mold is a health issue and a property issue. We find the source, contain it, remove it, and fix the moisture that caused it.

  • Mold inspection and testing coordination
  • Containment and safe removal
  • Moisture source correction
  • Post-remediation rebuild

Rebuild & General Repair

One company from demo to final paint. Drywall, flooring, kitchens, bathrooms, roofing: finished to like-new condition.

  • Drywall, paint, and trim
  • Flooring replacement
  • Kitchen and bath rebuilds
  • Insurance claim documentation

Our story

Built for the worst days,
here for the rebuild

Chrysalis Disaster Recovery LLC is a Cedarhurst construction company focused on one thing: getting homes and businesses back after water, fire, and storm damage.

Why we exist

A bad day should not become a bad year

A flooded basement or a fire-damaged kitchen can stall a family's life for months when the recovery is handled piecemeal: one company for demo, another for drywall, another for paint, and you in the middle chasing all of them.

We do it differently. Chrysalis handles the full arc: emergency response, mitigation, insurance documentation, and the complete construction rebuild, under one roof, with one point of contact.

The name says it: a chrysalis is where transformation happens. Our job is to make sure what comes out the other side is stronger than what went in.

Disaster is the event. Recovery is the craft. We are in the recovery business.

The Chrysalis approach

Damage compounds by the hour. We move quickly to stop it, then work the full recovery.

One accountable team

From first call to final walkthrough, one company owns the whole job.

Insurance ready

Thorough documentation and direct adjuster coordination, so claims go smoothly.
