Our Story

Nottingham's neighborhood
nail boutique

Unique Boutique Nails and Spa sits on Belair Road in Nottingham, the kind of salon people drive to from across Baltimore because they trust the results.

Reviewers talk about consistency: great customer service, everyone friendly, and a shop that stays beautifully clean and current with the latest colors and styles.

Whether it is a same-day gel mani, a relaxing spa pedicure, or dip nails that last, the goal is simple: you leave happy, and your nails get the compliments.
