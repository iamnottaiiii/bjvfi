Our story

Why we fix cars where they sit.

A broken car is stressful enough without the tow truck, the shuttle ride and the days of waiting for a call back. Mobile Mechanic Services was built around a simpler idea: the mechanic comes to you, at your home or your office, with the parts and the know-how to get the job done right there.

From Oxon Hill, we serve drivers across the area with everything from quick brake jobs to full engine replacements. Our customers describe a crew that works fast, explains the work plainly, charges fair prices and keeps its word. When a family was stranded five hours from home, the team worked through the weekend to get their truck rolling again.

Honesty is the part people mention most. One customer came in convinced they needed a starter; the diagnosis showed a transmission problem instead, and the mechanic said so and pointed them to the right shop rather than selling the wrong job. That is how you earn sixty-plus five-star style reviews and customers for life.

  • Full repairs at your home, office or roadside
  • Clear, fair prices quoted before work begins
  • Honest diagnosis, even when it costs us the job
  • Weekend availability when you are stranded
