About Us

The Shop Mt Healthy Turns To

Cincinnati Collision Center aims to be the auto custom and collision repair facility drivers turn to for all their automotive needs. For the many years the shop has been in business, the team has worked to deliver the best quality service possible and earn customers who come back again and again.

Whether you drive an import, a domestic, or a high performance exotic car, the shop handles window tinting, collision repairs, custom services and automotive designs, with one goal: keep you and your vehicle safe and on the road.

The shop offers a lifetime warranty on most services, free estimates, and welcomes all insurance claims.
