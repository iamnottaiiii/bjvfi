Jim Lambert Body & Paint is a family operation in Monfort Heights, Cincinnati, doing auto body repair and painting plus towing for the west side.

What the neighborhood knows us for

Public reviews describe a shop that shows up fast after an accident, tows the car, and works with drivers through the stressful parts: insurance paperwork, getting belongings out of the vehicle, and keeping owners updated while repairs take longer than expected.

Customers call out fair pricing and repair work that comes back in great condition. A few reviewers single out the crew by name for going out of their way in tough situations.
