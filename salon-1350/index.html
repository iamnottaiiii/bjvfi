The story

A neighborhood salon with a loyal chair

Salon 1350 opened its doors in Chittenango in 2017 and has grown by word of mouth ever since. Guests describe a warm, positive room where stylists are detailed, personable, and genuinely invested in the result.

Whether you need a quick trim, a color fix, or a completely new look, the team invites you to call for an appointment today.
