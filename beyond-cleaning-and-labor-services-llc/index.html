About us

Beyond Clean Is a Promise, Not Just a Name

Beyond Cleaning and Labor Services LLC is a cleaning service based in Mt Healthy, Ohio, serving homes and businesses across the greater Cincinnati area. We started with a simple idea: give people a dependable team that treats every job like a checklist, and checks off every line.

Cleaning is our craft, and labor support is our bonus. Whether you need a recurring home cleaning, a one time deep clean before company arrives, janitorial care for your office, or extra hands for a move, one call puts the plan in motion.

We show up on time, communicate clearly, and finish with a walkthrough so you can see the results for yourself. That is what going beyond means to us.
