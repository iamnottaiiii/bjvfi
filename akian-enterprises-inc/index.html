Our Story

Three decades of Valley building

Akian Enterprises Inc has been working out of Northridge since 1990, doing business as Uptown Builders Co. That is more than thirty years of kitchens, baths, additions and retrofits across the San Fernando Valley.

A family-run contractor

We are a local, family-run firm, not a call center. When you call (818) 701-6540 you reach the people who will actually be on your job site. Our name is on the work, and our reviewers hold us to it.

Green building, standard

We build with sustainable materials and energy-smart practices as the default, because a remodel should lower your bills, not raise them.

Design to done

Remodels, seismic retrofits and tenant improvements all run through one team. We plan the work, pull the permits, swing the hammers and walk the finished job with you.

Rooted in Northridge

Our office is at 18611 Eddy St in Northridge. We know Valley homes, Valley soil and Valley inspectors, and that local knowledge keeps projects moving.
