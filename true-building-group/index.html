Services

What a general contractor takes off your plate

As a general contractor in Woodland Hills, True Building Group can be your single point of contact for projects like the ones below. Every job is scoped individually, so call (424) 292-8207 to talk through yours.

Project types

Kitchen remodeling

Kitchens are the heart of the home and the most permit-heavy room to remodel. A general contractor sequences layout changes, cabinetry, countertops, plumbing, and electrical so the room comes back together on schedule.

  • Layout changes and wall removal
  • Cabinetry and countertop installation
  • Plumbing and electrical rough-in
  • Flooring, tile, and finish work

Bathroom remodeling

Small rooms, tight tolerances. Bathroom remodels need precise waterproofing, ventilation, and fixture placement. Managed start to finish so every trade lands in the right order.

  • Shower and tub conversions
  • Tile, vanities, and fixtures
  • Waterproofing and ventilation
  • Accessibility upgrades

Room additions

Adding square footage is structural work: foundations, framing, roofing, and tying new systems into old. A general contractor handles the drawings, permits, and inspections that additions require in Los Angeles.

  • Bedroom and family room additions
  • Second-story additions
  • Foundation and framing work
  • Roof tie-ins and exterior finish

Whole-home renovation

When several rooms need attention at once, phasing matters. A whole-home renovation planned by one contractor avoids rework, keeps you livable where possible, and compresses the total timeline.

  • Multi-room sequencing and phasing
  • Systems upgrades: electrical, plumbing, HVAC
  • Flooring and finishes throughout
  • Exterior and curb appeal updates

New construction

Building from the ground up means coordinating architects, engineers, the city, and every trade. A general contractor runs that entire chain from foundation to keys.

  • Ground-up residential builds
  • Coordination with architects and engineers
  • Site work and foundation
  • Full build-out to final inspection

ADUs

Accessory dwelling units are one of the smartest ways to add space and value on a Valley lot. ADUs have their own permit path in Los Angeles, and a general contractor who knows it saves months of back and forth.

  • Detached and attached ADUs
  • Garage conversions
  • Permit drawings and city submittal
  • Full utility hookups and finishes

Scopes above describe typical general-contractor project types. Your project gets its own written scope and plan. Call (424) 292-8207 to start with a walkthrough.
