Our Story

A clinic for cars, run like a good neighbor.

Rich's Auto Clinic sits on Waugaman Street in Aliquippa, a small independent shop where the person who diagnoses your car is the person who fixes it. The shop's name says it all: bring your car in sick, and it leaves healthy.

Customers drive in from 30 minutes away because they trust what they find here. Honest assessments, fair prices, and work they stand behind. Drivers praise Rich's knowledge of vintage cars, his fair dealing on big engine and transmission jobs, and a staff that keeps drop-off and pick-up flexible around real life.
