Our story

Systems, not shortcuts

RapidPro Handyman Systems started with a simple frustration: handyman visits that showed up late, priced by the hour with a shrug, and left a mess behind. We built the business we wanted to hire.

The "Systems" in our name is the whole point. Every job follows the same playbook: confirm the scope before we start, protect your floors and furniture, do the work to a standard we would accept in our own homes, and walk the finished job with you before we call it done.

We serve La Cañada Flintridge from 1921 Parkdale Pl, and we specialize in the jobs that fall between the cracks: too small for a general contractor, too annoying to keep putting off. Drywall, doors, mounting, assembly, caulk, trim. One visit, tidy work, fair price.
