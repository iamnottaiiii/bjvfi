What we do

Remodeling, done like it matters

Every project starts with a walkthrough and an honest conversation. We look at the space, listen to how you live in it, and map out a plan that fits the house and the budget. No pressure, no jargon, just a clear picture of what your remodel will take.

From there we handle the work start to finish: framing and drywall, tile and flooring, cabinetry and trim, paint and punch list. One crew you know by name, one point of contact, and a job site kept clean while we are in your home.

Precise work, kept promises, and a crew you are glad to have in your home.

Homeowners call us back for the next room, and the next one after that. That kind of repeat business only happens when the work is precise, the schedule is kept, and the people in your home are professional and personable.

Our story

A name we try to live up to

Meraki is a Greek word. It means to do something with soul, creativity, and love, to put a piece of yourself into your work. We chose it because that is the only way we know how to build.

Meraki Contractors is based in Walton, Kentucky, and serves homeowners across Northern Kentucky and the Cincinnati area. We focus on residential remodeling, the rooms people live in every day: kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and the outdoor spaces that make a house feel like home. We also take on small commercial remodeling work.

We chose the name Meraki because it is the only way we know how to build.

Our project history runs from kitchen and bath remodels to custom tile work, cabinetry, framing, drywall, painting, and trim, plus decks and outbuildings. Public records show 11 building permits pulled since 2020, and the BBB lists about seven years in business.

The goal has never changed: collaborate with the homeowner at every step, from design and material selection to the final walkthrough, and leave behind work we are proud to sign.
