Why restore

Replacement is the last resort

Tearing out marble is dusty, slow, and expensive. Most dull or damaged stone can be restored in place, usually in a day or two, for a fraction of replacement cost.

Less mess

No demolition. We work in place with dust-controlled equipment and protect the surrounding rooms.

Less cost

Restoration typically costs far less than new stone plus demolition and reinstallation.

Keep the character

Original marble has veining you cannot buy twice. We keep it and make it shine again.

Services

Everything marble needs

From a dull entryway floor to an etched kitchen island, each service is tailored to your stone.

01

Marble floor restoration

Full mechanical restoration for marble floors: diamond grinding to level wear and lippage, honing to refine, and polishing to your chosen sheen. Finished with a professional sealer.

  • Diamond grinding
  • Honing
  • Polishing
  • Sealing
02

Countertop honing and polishing

Kitchen and bath counters brought back from dull, scratched, and etched to smooth and luminous. Edges and backsplashes detailed by hand.

  • Etch removal
  • Scratch removal
  • Satin or gloss finish
  • Edge detailing
03

Shower and tub surround care

Soap scum, hard water marks, and dull stone in wet areas cleaned, honed, and sealed for a fresh, even look that is easier to maintain.

  • Deep cleaning
  • Hard water removal
  • Honing
  • Wet-area sealing
04

Stain and etch repair

Targeted repair for the marks marble collects: rings from glasses, lemon and wine etches, rust, and oil spots. Treated and blended into the surrounding stone.

  • Spot treatment
  • Poultice stain removal
  • Etch blending
  • Color evening
05

Grout cleaning and recoloring

Marble installations are only as good as their grout lines. We deep clean grout and can recolor it for a uniform, like-new grid.

  • Deep grout cleaning
  • Grout recoloring
  • Caulk replacement
06

Maintenance plans

Keep the shine with scheduled maintenance: periodic cleaning, touch-up honing, and resealing on a schedule that fits how you use the space.

  • Scheduled cleaning
  • Resealing
  • Touch-up honing
  • Care guidance

Stone first

Honest assessment

Care tip

Marble hates acid and loves sealer

Wipe spills fast, skip vinegar-based cleaners, and reseal on schedule. Do that and a professional restoration can last for years.
