Our Story

A neighborhood shop with a simple promise

Fix it right, inspect it honestly, and treat every driver like a neighbor.

Andrews Auto Repair Shop & State inspections & uber/ lyft inspection sits on Auth Road in Camp Springs, minutes from Joint Base Andrews, serving commuters, families, and rideshare drivers across Prince George's County.

The shop grew around a practical idea: drivers were bouncing between a mechanic for repairs, an inspection station for the state check, and somewhere else for their Uber or Lyft vehicle inspection. Doing all three under one roof saves drivers time, and it means the same techs who fix your car are the ones signing off on its safety.

"They didn't try to upsell me on unnecessary services. Hard to find trustworthy mechanics, but I felt very comfortable here."

What customers notice most is the honesty. Reviews describe techs who diagnose quickly, explain what actually needs doing, and skip the upsell. One driver came in expecting a major repair and left with a simple, affordable fix. Another praised the crew for fitting them in last minute and getting them back on the road the same day.

That approach extends to inspections. Whether it is the Maryland state inspection or a rideshare vehicle check, the process is careful and unhurried, because the signature on that form means something.

If you drive for Uber or Lyft, this shop understands your schedule. Keep the car compliant, keep it safe, and keep earning.
