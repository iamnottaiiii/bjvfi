Example services

What a general contractor does

Every project is different. Below are typical examples of general contracting work. Call to talk through what your project needs.

Kitchen Remodels

Layout changes, cabinets, counters, and finishes planned around how you actually cook and gather.

Bathroom Renovations

Showers, tubs, tile, and fixtures, rebuilt with proper waterproofing behind the pretty.

Room Additions

Extra bedrooms, offices, or living space tied into your existing home so it looks original.

Office Buildouts

Turning empty square footage into offices, break rooms, and customer-facing space.

Tenant Improvements

Retail and commercial interiors updated to fit your business and your lease.

Facility Repairs

Keeping commercial buildings sound, safe, and presentable with scheduled repair work.

Drywall and Paint

Patching, texture matching, and fresh paint that makes rooms feel new again.

Flooring

Laminate, tile, and plank flooring installed level, tight, and ready for daily life.

Small Projects

Doors, trim, fences, and the punch-list items every property collects over time.

Heads up: these are example services only, not a confirmed list. The fastest way to know if Tito's construction is the right fit is a quick call about your specific project.
