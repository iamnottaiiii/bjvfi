About

Rising in Burlington, Kentucky

A Northern Kentucky body shop built on the belief that no car is beyond saving.

Our Story

ReInCARnation Auto Body Repair and Paint is a full service auto body and paint shop at 1589 Production Dr in Burlington, KY. In the shop's own words:

"Reincarnation Auto Body and Paint has extensive experience in the automotive painting industry and collision repair. We love what we do and put the extra effort into each and every job. We work closely with all insurance companies and take great pride in helping our clients with their insurance claims."

That philosophy is also what customers repeat in public reviews: honest advice, fair prices, and work done right. One reviewer wrote that the owner told them a repair could be handled with a simple at-home solution instead of charging for the work, saving them money rather than filling the shop's own pockets.
