ACA / AAAP Concrete is a concrete contractor based at 3509 N Front St in Harrisburg, PA, serving homeowners and businesses across the area. How we work

No shortcuts underground

  • Excavation to the right depth for the load the slab will carry
  • Compacted stone base and proper forming before any concrete arrives
  • Reinforcement and joint layout planned, not improvised
  • Curing guidance after the pour so the concrete reaches full strength
  • Job site left clean, with debris hauled away
