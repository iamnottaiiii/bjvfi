Our Story

A Stainless Shop in North Hollywood

Fivestar Stainless / 3D Stainless Mfg is a stainless steel manufacturer operating from 7350 Atoll Ave #8 in North Hollywood, California. Our trade is custom stainless fabrication: parts, assemblies, and equipment built to order for food service, commercial, and industrial customers.

Stainless work demands a different discipline than general sheet metal. Welds must stay clean and corrosion resistant, finishes must be consistent, and tolerances must hold across a run. That is the work we do every day, from single replacement brackets to short production runs.

Send a drawing, a sketch with dimensions, or the worn part you need replaced. Call (818) 643-1866 and talk directly with the shop about your job.
