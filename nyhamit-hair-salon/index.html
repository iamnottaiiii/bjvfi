the short version

Skilled hands.
Fair prices.

Walk into NYHAMIT HAIR SALON on Kings Highway and you get a clean, welcoming shop where the focus is on doing great hair at a fair price.

Hamit and the team have built a loyal following the old-fashioned way: careful, professional service and cuts clients love. Reviewers keep saying the same thing: they come in, Hamit does an amazing job, and they leave feeling confident.

From kids’ first haircuts to keratin treatments and extensions, the salon handles the whole family’s hair in one stop.

Very clean, welcoming and fairly priced. Highly recommend.
