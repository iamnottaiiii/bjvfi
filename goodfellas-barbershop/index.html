Our story

A neighborhood shop
with regulars for life

Goodfellas Barbershop is a family friendly establishment on Pleasant St, built around one simple idea: mens haircut styles done right.

The shop

Led by the owner,
backed by the team

Owner Johnathan Rosado leads the chairs, and customers keep mentioning the same names: Angel and Johnathan, praised for patience, personality, and cuts that hold up weeks later.

It is the kind of shop where parents bring their kids with confidence, where walk-ins are genuinely welcome, and where the conversation is as good as the cut.

Specialties are right there in the chair: skin fades, tapers, beard line-ups and shape-ups, hot towel razor shaves, and classic cuts that never go out of style.

A family friendly establishment. We specialize in mens haircut styles.

The Goodfellas team

Detail obsessed

Reviewers call out the precision: crisp lines, clean blends, no rushed finishes.

Good company

Great conversation comes standard. First-timers say they felt at home right away.

For the whole family

From a first careful kids cut to a senior cut, every age gets the same attention.

Easy to book

Online booking on Booksy or walk right in. Your chair, your call.
