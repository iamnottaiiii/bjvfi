What we do

Masonry work, from the ground up

Common projects we take on. If it is brick, block or stone, call and ask.

01

Brick and block work

New walls, rebuilds and veneer in brick and concrete block. Clean lines, solid mortar joints, work that holds.

02

Stone and veneer

Natural and manufactured stone for walls, facades and accents that give a home real curb appeal.

03

Repointing and repair

Crumbling mortar, cracked joints, spalled faces. We cut out the bad and lay in the good.

04

Walkways, patios and steps

Brick, block and stone flatwork that stays level and drains right, season after season.

05

Retaining walls

Walls that hold back the hill and look good doing it. Structural first, handsome second.

06

Chimneys

Rebuilds, repointing and cap work. A chimney is only as good as its mortar, we keep both sound.
