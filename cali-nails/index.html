Services

The full menu, unhurried.

Service descriptions below are based on the salon's public business listings. Call (410) 882-5916 for current pricing and availability.

01

Manicures

From a tidy classic manicure to long-wearing gel, technicians shape, care for and finish your nails with steady attention. Clients on review sites single out the quality of the gel work in particular.

02

Pedicures

Spa pedicures in comfortable chairs, with careful attention to shaping and polish. Reviewers mention clean pedicure chairs and bowls as a mark of the salon's standards.

03

Massage therapy

Beyond the nail table, Cali Nails offers massage therapy, making it easy to pair a service with your manicure or pedicure for a longer reset.

04

Facial treatments

Facial services round out the spa menu, so skin gets the same care as nails during your visit.

05

Spa packages

Specialized packages for individuals, couples and occasions such as weddings. The salon's larger space accommodates group bookings comfortably.
