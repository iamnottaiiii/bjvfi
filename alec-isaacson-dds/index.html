Services

The notes below describe general dentistry in general terms. Call the office to ask which treatments are offered here.

General dentists commonly provide routine exams and cleanings that help catch small problems before they grow. Ask the office about scheduling a checkup.

When teeth are damaged by decay or injury, general dentists commonly provide fillings, crowns and other restorative treatments. Call to ask what this office provides.

Healthy gums support healthy teeth. General dentists evaluate gum health during exams and advise on home care. Ask the office for guidance at your visit.

Many patients ask about whitening, veneers and other ways to improve the look of their smile. Call the office to ask what cosmetic options are available.

Toothaches, chipped teeth and lost fillings happen. If you have an urgent dental problem, call (410) 823-0044 promptly to ask about same-day options.
