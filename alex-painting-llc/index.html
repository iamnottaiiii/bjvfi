Our story

Quality is always in style

Alex Painting LLC is a Wilmington, Delaware painting crew built on a simple idea: show up, do neat work, and stand behind it.

From 701 Woodsedge Rd, the team serves homes and businesses across New Castle County. The work goes beyond brushes and rollers. Drywall replaced where wood panels used to be, ceilings re-tiled, waterproof luxury vinyl plank flooring installed, decks brought back to life with bold new color choices, and a Pilates studio and a courthouse among the commercial projects finished.

Every project gets the same treatment: free estimates, careful prep, clean job sites, and before and after photos that speak for themselves. That is why the motto stuck: the painters you need, at the service of the community.
