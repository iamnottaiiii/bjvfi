Our story

Bodywork is a craft. We treat it like one.

American Autobody and Paint has worked on Tonnelle Ave in North Bergen for years, repairing collision damage and refinishing cars to a clean, even shine. We are a working body shop, not a showroom: the focus is on straight panels, matched paint, and cars returned on time.

Every job starts with a walkaround and a written estimate. We photograph the damage, price the repair clearly, and keep you updated while the car is with us. Insurance work is welcome, and we handle the claim paperwork with you.

From bumper scuffs to full collision repair, the goal is always the same: a car that looks right, drives right, and is safe for your family.

Straight panels, matched paint, on time.

Call (201) 974-8830 or stop by 5401 Tonnelle Ave, Monday to Friday 8 AM to 5 PM or Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM, for a free estimate.
