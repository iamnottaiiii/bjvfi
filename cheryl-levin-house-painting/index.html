Services

The craft, spread by spread

Every project starts with a conversation about your home, your light, and your life. Here is what the studio does best.

01
Core craft

Interior House Painting

Full-service interior painting done the careful way: thorough patching and fixing of walls, crisp lines at trim and ceilings, and finishes chosen for how they live in your light.

  • Walls, ceilings, trim and doors
  • Patching, caulking and surface repair
  • Color consultation included
02
Artisanal

Faux and Decorative Painting

Where the studio began in 1994. Layered glazes, textured plasters and hand-worked finishes that give a wall depth you cannot get from a single coat.

  • Glazing, ragging and color washing
  • Textured and plaster effects
  • Accent walls with real depth
03
For little ones

Kids' Wall Murals

Hand-painted murals that turn a child's room into a storybook: skies, forests, castles and creatures, designed together with you and your kids.

  • Custom designs, painted by hand
  • Child-safe paints
  • Nursery to teen-room themes
04
Fine detail

Gold Leafing

Genuine gold leaf applied to moldings, mantels, ceiling medallions and furniture. A small amount of gilding can change the whole feeling of a room.

  • Architectural elements and trim
  • Furniture and frame accents
  • Repair of existing gilding
05
The foundation

Wall Patching and Repair

From nail holes to cracked plaster, every surface is patched, sanded and primed before a drop of finish paint goes on. Great paint starts with great prep.

  • Drywall patching and skim coating
  • Crack and nail-pop repair
  • Priming for a lasting finish

Since 1994

Three decades
of beautiful walls

Cheryl Levin House Painting started in 1994 as a decorative painting company and has grown into a full-service painting studio. That origin still shapes everything: even the most straightforward repaint gets the eye of someone trained in faux finishes, murals and gilding.

The studio works both ways, conventional painting and artisanal painting, and offers a personal, consultative way of doing business. You talk through color, light and lifestyle first, then the brushes come out.

The specialty, in one line: turning your house into a home.

Personal

Artisanal
