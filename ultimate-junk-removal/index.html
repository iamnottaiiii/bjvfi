Our story

A family crew that treats your home like its own

Rooted in the Foothills

Ultimate Junk Removal is a family run hauling business based at 600 Foothill Blvd in La Cañada Flintridge. We started with one truck and a simple idea: junk removal should be fast, fair, and honest.

Today we serve homes, rentals, offices, and job sites across the Foothills and the San Gabriel Valley, from La Crescenta and Altadena to Pasadena and Glendale. Many of our jobs come from neighbors who called us once and never called anyone else.

When you call (626) 780-4860, you talk to the people who will actually show up at your door.

Donate and recycle first

A junk pile is rarely all junk. We sort every load: usable furniture and goods go to local donation partners, metal and electronics go to recyclers, and only what is left goes to proper disposal.

It takes a little more effort on our end, but it keeps good items in use and keeps the Foothills clean. That is how we want to do business.

Open every day, 5:00 AM to 10:30 PM, because junk does not keep office hours.
