About Mashur & Sons Contracting

Mashur & Sons Contracting is a family-run excavating contractor based at 1106 Park Ave in Plainfield, NJ. As our name says, this is a family operation, and we treat every job site like it carries our name, because it does.

We focus on one thing and do it well: moving earth. That focus means the right equipment on site, experienced operators, and honest communication from the first phone call to the final grade.

Homeowners and contractors across Union County and the surrounding area call us when the groundwork has to be right.
