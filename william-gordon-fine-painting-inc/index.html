Services

What fine painting
actually means

Four disciplines, each given its due. Every project begins with a walkthrough and a clear, written understanding of the work.

No. I

Fine interior painting

Whole homes, single rooms, and everything between. Surfaces are cleaned, patched, sanded, and primed before a brush ever touches the finish coat, because the coats you see are only as good as the work beneath them.

Customers have trusted Bill with entire interiors before move-in day, the highest compliment a painter can get: a blank house, a deadline, and total confidence in the result.

No. II

Drywall and plaster repair

Cracks, dents, water stains, and the aftermath of other trades. Damaged drywall is cut out, re-hung, taped, and skimmed until it disappears into the surrounding wall. One customer with major drywall issues called the outcome terrific.

Paint over bad drywall is just expensive wallpaper. We fix the wall first.

No. III

Trim, crown molding, and millwork

Baseboards, casings, crown, and built-ins, repaired where needed and finished with the crisp lines that make a room feel custom. Customers specifically note crown molding repaired as part of the job.

This is slow work, and it is the work that separates a fine painter from a fast one.

No. IV

Custom finishes

For clients who want more than a flat coat: layered color, specialty finishes, and the kind of custom work customers describe as amazing. Bring the idea, and Bill will tell you honestly what it takes.

About

The painter
behind the name

William Gordon Fine Painting Inc. is the work of Bill Gordon, a Narberth, Pennsylvania painting contractor based at 220 Lantwyn Ln. The "fine" in the name is a promise customers say he keeps: beautiful work, an honest manner, and a clean job site.
