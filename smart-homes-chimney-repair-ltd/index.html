Why it matters

Creosote does not wait. Neither should you.

Burning wood leaves creosote on your flue walls. Left alone, it builds up into one of the leading causes of chimney fires. An annual sweep is the simplest way to protect your home.

Smart Homes & Chimney Repair Ltd treats every visit like it is our own hearth: drop cloths down, soot contained, and a clear report on what we found.

Good to know

Signs your chimney needs attention.

  • Smoke rolling back into the room
  • A strong odor from the fireplace, especially in summer
  • White staining (efflorescence) on the outside bricks
  • Cracked or crumbling mortar joints
  • A damper that sticks or will not seal
  • It has been more than a year since the last sweep

Services

Sweep, inspect, repair, repeat.

Complete chimney care for wood, gas, and pellet systems. If it vents through your roof, we can service it.

Chimney Sweeping

Thorough cleaning of the flue, smoke chamber, smoke shelf, firebox, and damper. We protect your floors and furniture, seal the fireplace opening, and leave the room as clean as we found it.

Chimney Inspections

Level 1 annual inspections for continued use, and Level 2 camera inspections when buying or selling a home, after a chimney fire, or when changing systems. You get a written report of findings.

Masonry Repair

Tuckpointing and repointing, brick replacement, crown and wash repair, and partial or full rebuilds for stacks that are leaning or badly deteriorated.

Caps, Crowns & Liners

Chimney caps that keep rain and animals out, crown sealing, and stainless steel liner installs for damaged flues or new appliance hookups.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Lint-clogged dryer vents are a fire hazard and an energy drain. We clear the full run so your dryer runs safer and faster.

Waterproofing

Vapor-permeable masonry sealers that stop water intrusion without trapping moisture, extending the life of your chimney for years.
