Services

Full service structural engineering.

HJH Engineering offers a variety of design expertise for new construction and existing buildings across Southern California. The work below reflects the firm's stated practice areas.

01

Custom residential

Structural engineering for one-of-a-kind houses: hillside cantilevers, open plans, ADUs, and additions.

02

Commercial

Ground-up and adaptive commercial structures: office, retail, restaurant, and light industrial.

03

Tenant improvement

Reworking existing structural shells to host new programs without disturbing the things you cannot move.

04

Mixed-use

Retail-over-residential buildings where the structural grid has to serve two different programs at once.

05

Multi-family

Apartment, condo, and small-lot subdivision structures, from wood-frame podium to fully engineered CMU.

06

Seismic retrofit

Strengthening existing buildings: unreinforced masonry, non-ductile concrete, and older wood frame.

07

Soft-story retrofit

Complete retrofit service under the Los Angeles Mandatory Soft-Story Program, from screening through final sign-off.

08

Seismic evaluation

ASCE 41-based evaluations of existing buildings, Tier 1 through Tier 3, with a clear deficiency list.

09

Feasibility studies

Fast, numbers-backed studies that confirm whether a structural idea pencils before design begins.

10

Due diligence studies

Pre-acquisition structural reviews that surface what a property will actually take to develop.

Not sure which service your project needs? Call (818) 519-8572 and describe the property. We will point you to the right starting point.

Our story

Led by Jack Hadjian, M.S., P.E.

Jack founded HJH Engineering after completing his Master's in Structural Engineering at the University of Southern California. He has since immersed himself in the design of new structures and the remodeling of existing ones across Southern California.

The firm's approach is straightforward: work closely with clients during the early stages of construction, partner with the architect from schematic design so the structural language supports the architectural intent, and stay with the project from feasibility through construction administration.

Based at 23006 Erwin St in Woodland Hills, HJH Engineering serves architects, developers, and general contractors throughout the Los Angeles area.

01

Architectural partnership

SoCal seismic expertise
