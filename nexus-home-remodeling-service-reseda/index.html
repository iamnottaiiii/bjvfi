Our story

A Reseda contractor that
treats your home like its own.

Nexus Home Remodeling Service Reseda is a construction company based on Rhea Ave in Reseda, California. We got into this trade because we like the moment a homeowner walks into a finished room and finally sees what they pictured.

Why we do it

Remodeling is personal.

A kitchen is where the family lands at the end of the day. A bathroom is the first room you see in the morning. When those rooms do not work, the whole house feels off. We take that seriously.

Our approach is simple: listen first, plan in writing, communicate during the build, and stand behind the finished work. No disappearing acts, no surprise line items, no cutting corners where you cannot see them.

How we work

Small crew, tight standards.

We keep our crews small on purpose. The person who plans your project is around during your project. Job sites get cleaned daily because you still live there while we work.

When a job needs licensed specialists, plumbing, electrical, structural, we bring in trade partners we trust and coordinate them ourselves, so you still have one phone number to call.

Written scopes

What is included, what it costs, and how long it takes, in writing before we start.

Daily communication

You always know what happened today and what is next, no chasing us for updates.

Clean job sites

Dust control, end-of-day cleanup, and respect for the parts of your home we are not remodeling.
