What to ask about when you call

Every treatment plan is personal. Use the tabs below to gather questions for your call or visit, then confirm availability and details directly with the office.

First visits

  • Ask what to bring to a first appointment, such as ID and insurance information.
  • Ask whether the office is welcoming new patients and how soon a visit can be scheduled.
  • Ask what a typical first exam includes at this office.

Ongoing care

  • Ask about scheduling routine exams and cleanings.
  • Ask how the office handles reminders and follow up visits.
  • Ask about X-rays and preventive screenings offered on site.

Repairs and restoration

  • Ask which restorative options the office provides, such as fillings or crowns.
  • Ask how treatment options and costs are explained before work begins.
  • Ask about payment options and insurance the office accepts.

Urgent needs
