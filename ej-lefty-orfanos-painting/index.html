Services

Painting, washing,
and light carpentry

One crew, one call. Every job starts with a free estimate and ends with a walkthrough.

Interior Painting

Rooms, hallways, ceilings, trim, and doors. We patch, sand, caulk, and prime before the first coat goes on, and mask and drape everything in between.

Exterior Painting

Siding, shutters, fascia, and trim. Scraped to sound wood, primed, and finished with coatings that stand up to coastal New England weather.

Pressure Washing

The right start for any exterior job, and a great standalone refresh. Siding, decks, fences, and walkways washed clean of mildew and grime.

Light Carpentry

Rotted trim, damaged siding, and tired molding repaired or replaced before paint. No need to call a second contractor.

Color Consultation

Stuck between three shades of white? We will bring swatches, test on your walls, and help you pick with confidence.

Commercial Painting

Offices, storefronts, and rental properties across the North Shore. Scheduled around your business hours to keep you open.

How it works

to final walkthrough

Free estimate

Prep and protect

Scraping, patching, caulking, priming, masking, and draping. The unglamorous part we take seriously.

Paint

Quality coatings, applied the right way, with a tidy site at the end of each day.

Walkthrough

We go over every surface with you before we call it done. If it is not right, we make it right.

Pricing: every job is quoted individually after a free, on-site estimate. Final prices are set per job and confirmed in writing before work begins. Sample prices are not listed because no two homes are the same.

Our story

Sixty years of
fresh starts

EJ “Lefty” Orfanos Painting has been a residential and commercial painting contractor since 1966. In that time, the crew has serviced hundreds of homes and businesses across the North Shore and north of Boston.

What keeps customers calling back is not a slogan. It is the basics, done the same way on every job: show up when promised, protect the home like it is your own, prep like the paint has to last, and leave the place cleaner than you found it.

Today the company handles interior and exterior painting, pressure washing, and light carpentry, all under one roof, with free estimates and full insurance on every project.
