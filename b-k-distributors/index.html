Who we are

A distributor that installs

B & K Distributors, Inc. is a full-service distributor, supplier, and installer of commercial and residential hardware. Working throughout the Mid-Atlantic, our areas of operation span Maryland, Virginia, West Virginia, Washington DC, Pennsylvania, and Delaware.

For 33 years, we have remained a leader in the industry for both residential and commercial projects throughout the region. From the personal attention demanded by a single family homeowner to the coordination of an expansive, multi-unit residential complex, we take pride in our commitment to quality, service, and customer satisfaction.

Our team of about 39 people covers the warehouse, the office, and the road. That scale means we can stage a whole apartment building's worth of hardware and still answer the phone when a contractor needs one closer by Friday.

  1. 33 years in business

    Three decades serving the new home and commercial building trade across MD, VA, WV, DC, PA, and DE.

  2. Supply and install under one roof

    Distribution plus field installation, so schedules, keying, and hardware arrive as one coordinated package.

  3. Jessup, Maryland home base

    Warehouse and office at 8260 Patuxent Range Rd, positioned for the Baltimore-Washington corridor.

How we work

Exact takeoffs. We build hardware schedules from your plans so bidding is clean and ordering is right the first time.

Coordinated delivery. Keying schedules, finishes, and lead times are tracked per opening, not per box.

Installed by our crews. The same company that quoted the hardware puts it in, which keeps accountability in one place.
