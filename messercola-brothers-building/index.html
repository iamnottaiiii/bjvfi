Our story

A family name in New Jersey building

Messercola Brothers Building is a family building company rooted in Somerset County, NJ. Public property records show Messercola Brothers Building Co Inc owning and developing land in Westfield as far back as 2016 and 2017, evidence of a builder with deep local history rather than a passing operation.

The company is known for handling the full arc of construction work, from overseeing large scale projects to managing detailed renovation jobs. Residential, commercial, and industrial: the common thread is a builder who stands behind the work.
