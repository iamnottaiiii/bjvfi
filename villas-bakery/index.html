Our Story

A Burbank panaderia, since 2004

Business directories list Villas Bakery as serving Burbank since 2004. Two decades later, the routine has not changed: early mornings, scratch dough, and a case full of pan dulce.

The Beginning

2004, on 79th Street

Villas Bakery opened its doors in Burbank as a traditional Mexican panaderia, bringing scratch-baked pan dulce to the southwest suburbs. The founding year comes from public business records.

The Craft

Baked from scratch, daily

Every concha, oreja, and bolillo is made on-site from scratch. No shortcuts, just flour, patience, and ovens that start before sunrise.

The Neighborhood

Burbank mornings

Regulars stop in for coffee and a warm tray before work. Kids press their noses to the case. Quinceanera cakes go out on weekends. That is the rhythm of 79th Street.

Cake lovers

"The chocolate cake and Oreo cheesecake are the reason we keep coming back." Celebration cakes get the most passionate praise, especially chocolate and cheesecake varieties.

Pan dulce fans
