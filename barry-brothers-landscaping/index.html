Our story

Built yard by yard in Central Mass

Barry Brothers Landscaping has over 11 years of experience in the landscaping and hardscaping industry. What started as brothers with trucks and tools has grown into a full-service outdoor company serving Boylston, Shrewsbury and the surrounding towns.

We handle the jobs homeowners dread: the tree leaning toward the house, the hill that will not stay put, the patio that was never quite right. Tree removal, tree pruning, landscape design, fencing, hardscaping and more, from the same crew, start to finish.

We accept cash, personal checks, and major credit cards (American Express, Discover, Mastercard, Visa), and every job starts with an honest on-site conversation about what it will take.
