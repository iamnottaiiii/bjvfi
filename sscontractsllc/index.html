Services

Sample descriptions, call to confirm scope and pricing

Panel Upgrades & Breaker Work
Main panels, subpanels, breaker replacement
Older panels and tired breakers are a common cause of nuisance trips. A panel evaluation looks at capacity, condition, and code concerns, then lays out upgrade options. No prices are quoted here, call the shop for an estimate.
Troubleshooting & Repairs
Dead outlets, flickering lights, mystery trips
Electrical faults get traced to the source: bad connections, failing devices, or overloaded circuits. Repairs are made so the circuit runs the way it should. If it cannot be fixed safely in one visit, you get a straight answer about next steps.
Lighting Installation
Fixtures, recessed lights, outdoor lighting
Interior fixtures, ceiling fans with proper support boxes, recessed cans, and exterior security lighting. Placement and switching are planned with you before any holes are cut.
Wiring & Rewiring
New circuits, remodel wiring, additions
New branch circuits for kitchens, workshops, and additions, plus rewiring where old conductors have seen better days. Work is sized to the load it will carry, not just the outlet count.
Outlets, Switches & Dimmers
GFCI, USB outlets, smart switches
GFCI protection where water is nearby, tamper resistant receptacles, USB combo outlets, and dimmers matched to the bulbs they control. Small jobs, done neatly.
Safety Inspections
Whole home electrical checkups
A walkthrough of the panel, devices, and visible wiring to flag hazards and aging components before they fail. You get a plain language report of what was found, ranked by urgency.
