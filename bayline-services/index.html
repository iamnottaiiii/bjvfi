Waterproofing Services

From a single crack to a full basement system, Bayline services handles the waterproofing work Pasadena homes need. Call for hours and scheduling.

Basement Waterproofing

Interior drainage channels and wall systems that capture seepage and route it to your sump before it reaches your floor.

Foundation Crack Sealing

Injection and exterior sealing that close wall and floor cracks, the most common entry points for groundwater.

Sump Pump Installation

Primary pumps plus battery backups that keep working through storms and outages, when you need them most.

Yard Drainage and Grading

French drains, downspout extensions, and grading that move water away from your foundation instead of toward it.

Crawl Space Encapsulation

Vapor barriers and sealed crawl spaces that cut moisture, musty odors, and mold risk under your home.

Waterproofing Inspections

Thorough basement and foundation inspections that pinpoint active leaks and the conditions causing them.
