The short version

Your ducts are the lungs of your house.

Every time the heat or AC kicks on, air moves through your ductwork and into every room. When that ductwork is lined with dust and debris, it all ends up back in the air you breathe. A professional duct cleaning pulls that buildup out at the source.

Shepherd’s Duct Clean Inc is a Jonesboro cleaning company focused on air ducts and dryer vents. One specialty, done carefully, home after home.

“If you can’t remember the last time your ducts were cleaned, it’s probably time.”

Dryer vents clogged with lint are a real fire risk and make dryers work twice as hard. A vent cleaning is quick, and the difference shows up on the first load.
