Our Story

JDI AUTO CARE is an independent auto repair shop on Burnt Mill Road in Cherry Hill, New Jersey. We are the kind of shop drivers recommend to their neighbors: honest, fast and fair.

Our philosophy is simple, and it is how we describe ourselves: we love cars and we treat our customers' cars as if they were our own. That means written estimates before work begins, repairs prioritized to your budget, and technicians who take the time to explain what your car needs and why.

"It's rare to feel blessed after a mechanic visit, but I surely felt blessed after my experience."
Based on Google reviews of JDI Auto Care. See reviews on Google for the full comments.

We service Cherry Hill and the surrounding South Jersey communities, from daily drivers to work trucks like the 2019 Ram 1500s we keep running cool and reliable. We report qualifying services to CARFAX, offer financing options, and our team speaks Spanish.
