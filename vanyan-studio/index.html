The studio

Design for the way people actually heal, work, and live

Vanyan Studio works at the intersection of architecture and interior experience. Published profiles of the studio describe a practice devoted to beautiful, functional environments across healthcare, corporate, and residential sectors, with a portfolio that includes medical office buildings, cafeterias, and wellness centers within the Kaiser Permanente network.

The studio collaborates with architects and photographers on complex institutional work, bringing a designer's eye for light, material, and human scale to projects where most firms see only program and code.

Studio

A Shadow Hills practice with an institutional portfolio

Vanyan Studio is based at 10078 McBroom St in Shadow Hills, Los Angeles, a quiet hillside neighborhood that suits a practice built on close attention. From this studio, the firm takes on work across Southern California, with published project experience concentrated in healthcare design.

Profiles of the studio highlight work within the Kaiser Permanente network, including medical office buildings, cafeterias, and wellness centers, and note collaboration with SLAM architects and architectural photographer Tom Bonner. The throughline is environments that perform technically while feeling humane.

Residential and corporate commissions round out the practice, each approached with the same discipline: light first, material honestly, and the user's experience as the measure of success.
