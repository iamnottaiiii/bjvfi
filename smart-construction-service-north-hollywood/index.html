Who we are

Our Story

A North Hollywood general contractor built on a simple idea: smart planning makes for strong buildings and happy clients.

Placeholder

A local crew with a planning first mindset

Smart Construction Service North Hollywood is a general contracting company based at 12719 Sherman Way in North Hollywood, California. We serve homeowners and businesses across the San Fernando Valley.

The name says what we believe: the smartest thing a contractor can do is plan well. Most remodeling headaches come from poor sequencing, vague scopes, and trades tripping over each other. We run every job from a written plan, with materials ordered ahead and inspections scheduled before the crew is standing around waiting.

That planning first approach lets us take on full kitchen and bath remodels, room additions, ADUs, and new construction with the same steady process, whether the project is a single bathroom or a ground up home.

Written scopes

Every job starts with a documented scope and a clear number, so expectations match the invoice.

Sequenced trades

Plumbing before tile, electrical before drywall. The right order protects quality and the schedule.

Clean job sites

We protect your floors and furniture, contain dust, and leave the site tidy at the end of each day.

One point of contact

You talk to the person running your job, not a call center. Questions get answered the same day.
