Our Story

An Allston original on the Ave

Commonwealth Laundry sits at 1283 Commonwealth Ave, in the middle of one of Boston's busiest student and renter neighborhoods. Here is what this corner of Allston is all about.

The neighborhood

Allston is Boston's student capital, packed with Boston University students, young professionals, and longtime residents. Most live in walk-ups and brownstones with no in-unit machines, which is exactly why a reliable laundromat matters here.

The location

Our address on Commonwealth Avenue puts us steps from the Allston Street stop on the Green Line B branch, and a short walk from Harvard Avenue's shops and restaurants. Laundry day pairs well with errands.

The routine

Open afternoons into the evening most days, the shop fits the rhythms of the neighborhood: class schedules, work shifts, and weekend reset routines. Drop in, load up, and be back on the Ave in no time.

The promise

Clean machines, a straightforward process, and a counter that treats every bag like it matters. That is the whole philosophy, and it has kept Allston coming back.
