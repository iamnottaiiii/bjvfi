Why a sweep matters

Creosote builds up every time you burn. A yearly sweep keeps the flue clear, the draft strong, and your family safe from chimney fires and carbon monoxide.

Fire prevention

Creosote is the leading cause of chimney fires. Regular sweeping removes the buildup before it becomes a hazard.

Better draft

A clear flue draws better, so fires light easier, burn hotter, and send smoke up and out instead of into your room.

Early warning

Every sweep includes a visual check of the flue, cap, and crown, so small cracks get caught before they turn costly.
