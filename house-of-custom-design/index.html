About

The story

Since 2013, House of Custom Design has done exactly what the name promises: hair that is designed, not just done. Every appointment starts with a real consultation about what you want, what you will maintain, and what suits you.

The studio team of hairstylists and cosmetologists works one-on-one, with product recommendations tailored to your hair, not a sales pitch.

Tucked on Brown Ave, it is a calm alternative to the big-box salon: personal service, comfortable pacing, and results that grow out gracefully.
