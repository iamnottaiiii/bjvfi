Our story

Canarsie's beauty corner
on Glenwood Rd

Juanita's II is a neighborhood beauty salon in Canarsie, Brooklyn, built around personal service and stylists clients ask for by name.

The salon

Professional, personal, unhurried

What clients mention most is the way appointments feel here: professional from start to finish, with stylists who value your time and listen before they style.

As a beauty salon with hair and personal care under one roof, it is the kind of place where a trim can turn into a full refresh, all without leaving Glenwood Rd.

The neighborhood

Rooted in Canarsie

Canarsie is a neighborhood of regulars, and Juanita's II fits right in: easy to get to on Glenwood Rd, open weekdays 9 to 6, and close enough to become part of your routine.

Whether you are stopping in after work or booking a Saturday-ready look on a Friday, the door is open.
