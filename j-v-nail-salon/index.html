Our Story

For years, J V Nail Salon has been a familiar name on Highbridge Street in Fayetteville. It is the kind of salon where the techs learn your name, remember your favorite color, and fit you in even when your schedule is tight.

The formula is simple: a spotless salon, skilled technicians, honest prices, and genuine warmth. Reviewers call out the friendly team by name, the easy scheduling, and nails that look great for weeks after the visit.

Whether it is your weekly manicure, a pedicure before vacation, or nails for a wedding, you will leave feeling pampered.

Why clients stay

Reviewers describe the salon as clean and well maintained, with easy scheduling and no long waits.
