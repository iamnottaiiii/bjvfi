Our Story

A Sylmar contractor built around the homeowner

MyHome Renovation Experts is a general contractor based on Sunburst Street in Sylmar, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley. We focus on the projects that change daily life most: kitchens, bathrooms, and full-home refreshes.

Our approach is simple. Show up on time, explain the work in plain language, keep the site clean, and treat your budget like our own money. Most of our work comes from referrals, and we plan to keep it that way.

When you call, you talk to the people who will actually manage your project. No call centers, no hand-offs into the void.
