How your fence gets built

Four steps, no surprises. This is the process we follow on every job.

01

Measure

We walk the property line with you, check grade and gates, and talk through what each material will do.

02

Quote

A straight written estimate with the style, height, and footage spelled out. No pressure, no fine print.

03

Set posts

Posts go deep and plumb, braced and backfilled so the fence stays straight season after season.

04

Build out

Rails, pickets, or panels go up square, gates are hung true, and the yard is left clean.

What we believe

A fence is only as good as its posts. Anyone can nail pickets to a rail, but the part that keeps a fence straight through Ohio winters is buried in the ground where nobody looks.

That is why we start every job the same way: on the property line, tape in hand, talking through grade, gates, and drainage before a single post hole is dug.

All Around Fence Co serves Cincinnati, OH 45245 and the surrounding east-side neighborhoods with residential fencing built the slow, right way.
