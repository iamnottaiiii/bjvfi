About Bravo

Bravo Equipment & Construction Co. is a Pasadena, Maryland company working across general contracting, construction management, and real estate development. The company is a member of the Maryland Motor Truck Association, reflecting its equipment and site-work roots. Led by Joseph Olup, the team brings a contractor's practicality to every project: clear scopes, honest schedules, and work that holds up.
