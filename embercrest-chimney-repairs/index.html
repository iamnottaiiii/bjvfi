The Embercrest Way

Sample The story below reflects our approach to the craft. Specific company history details are not published, so what follows is philosophy, not biography.

Why We Sweep

A chimney sweep's job is older than the telephone, and the core of it has not changed: climb, look, clean, repair, and leave the house safer than you found it. We hold to that old standard while using modern brushes, cameras, and sealants where they genuinely help.

Every visit starts with listening. How the fireplace burns, whether smoke ever spills into the room, how old the liner might be. The chimney tells its story if you ask the right questions, and we ask them before we quote a price.

We are based in Independence, Kentucky, and most of our work is within a comfortable drive of home. That keeps our schedule honest and our word good, because we will see you at the grocery store on Saturday.

What We Believe

  • Say what you see. If the flue is fine, we say so and sweep only what needs sweeping.
  • Protect the house. Drop cloths, sealed hearths, and a full cleanup are part of every job, not an upgrade.
  • Explain the work. You should understand what we did and why before we pack the brushes.
  • Price before, not after. The number you agree to is the number you pay. Call to confirm current rates.
  • Leave it better. Small caulking touch-ups and minor fixes get handled on the spot when we can.

Meet Us at the Hearth

The best way to know a sweep is to watch one work. Give us a call and we will earn the rest of the story in person.
