About John Rothenberger Jr & Son

John Rothenberger Jr & Son is a family-run general contracting business based on Ridgewood Ave in Glen Ridge, New Jersey. For years, the focus has been one thing: careful, honest home renovation work.

From kitchen and bath updates to repairs and carpentry, the approach is the same on every job. Listen first, plan well, and treat every home like it matters, because it does.
