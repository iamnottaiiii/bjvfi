03, About

A painter with an interiors mindset

"The paint is the easy part. The care is the craft."

Barnes Interiors is a painting contractor based at 16 Oaklandvale Ave in Saugus, Massachusetts, serving homeowners across Saugus, Lynn, Revere, Malden and the nearby North Shore towns.

The business takes its name seriously: painting is treated as interior work, not just coverage. That means attention to sheen, color undertones and how a finish looks in your actual light, plus the unglamorous fundamentals of prep, priming and protection.

What a project looks like

  1. Walkthrough. Measurements, surface review and color talk at your home.
  2. Written quote. Scope, paint, prep and timeline in writing.
  3. Prep and paint. Protected rooms, primed surfaces, two-coat finishes.
  4. Walkthrough. You approve the finished work before final payment.
