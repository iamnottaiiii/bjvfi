Our story

A family oven in Worth

Al Medina Bakery is a family owned and operated Middle Eastern bakery and grocery on Harlem Avenue.

Photo soon The family behind the counter Owner: swap in a team or storefront photo here.

Walk in and the first thing you notice is the smell: warm bread, thyme, toasted sesame. The second is the welcome. Reviewers describe the owners as friendly and kind, quick with a recommendation, and known for generous gestures, like falafel on the house for first-time guests.

Every pie is rolled and baked to order, the pita comes out of the oven through the day, and the grocery shelves carry the staples of a Middle Eastern pantry. It is the kind of neighborhood spot where regulars are greeted by name and newcomers leave as regulars.

Come hungry, leave with a warm bag of pita, and do not be surprised if someone insists you try something new.
