What we do

Design, development, and construction, under one roof

For over three decades, Greenleaf Farms Development has been building fine custom homes and whole subdivisions in Central Massachusetts. We handle the planning, the design, and the construction ourselves, so nothing gets lost between the drawing board and the job site.

We keep pace with the latest building trends and materials, and we work only with artisans we trust. The result: homes built on some of the most desirable land between I-495 and Worcester, in towns like Shrewsbury, Grafton, Boylston, Northborough, and Stow.

Our work has been featured in HOME: The Magazine of Remodeling and Decorating and Boston Design Magazine.

Beyond homes, we have also constructed several commercial properties. Whether it is your first custom home or your forever one, it is our desire to make your home fulfill your dreams and more.

About

A family builder,
rooted in Central Mass

The Tomaiolo family of Greenleaf Farms Development has been building custom homes in Central Massachusetts for over 35 years. What began as a family's craft has grown into one of the region's most experienced home-building names.

Our attention to detail, planning, design, and construction are second to none. Over the years we have gathered a network of the highest quality artisans, producing superior workmanship on every project, from the foundation to the finish trim.

It is our desire to make your home fulfill your dreams and more.

That philosophy shows up in the land we choose, some of the most desirable parcels between I-495 and Worcester, and in the neighborhoods we have shaped: more than 20 residential communities and over 300 homes across Shrewsbury, Grafton, Boylston, Northborough, and Stow.

We stay current by participating in national building conferences, keeping abreast of the latest trends and materials, and our work has been featured in HOME: The Magazine of Remodeling and Decorating as well as Boston Design Magazine.
