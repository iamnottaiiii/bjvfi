What the crew can do for your roof

Pick a topic below. Every project starts with a conversation, so call (443) 305-5328 to talk through yours.

Roof Replacement

When patching stops making sense, a full replacement resets the clock on your home. The crew removes the worn roof down to the deck, checks the structure, and installs a new system built for Maryland weather. Ask about material options, timelines, and what the finished job includes.

Roof Repair

Leaks rarely fix themselves. Missing shingles, lifted flashing, cracked boots around vents, and small punctures can all be repaired quickly when caught early. Describe the symptom on the phone and the crew will tell you what to expect.

Storm Damage

High wind and hail leave marks that are easy to miss from the ground. After a storm, a trained eye can spot lifted shingles, bruised mats, and compromised flashing before the next rain finds them.

Roof Inspection

Buying, selling, or just due for a checkup? An inspection documents the current condition of your roof so you can plan with facts instead of guesses. Useful before listing a home or after a rough weather season.

Maintenance

Small upkeep stretches a roof's life: clearing debris, resealing penetrations, and keeping gutters and flashing in shape. Ask what a maintenance visit covers for your roof type.
