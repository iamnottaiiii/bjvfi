Our story

A shed by name, a salon by craft

The name says it all: Hair Shed is unpretentious on purpose. It is the kind of salon where the coffee is hot, the conversation is easy, and the cuts are anything but basic.

Stylists here build long-term relationships with their clients. Regulars mention stylist Michelle by name in reviews, and first-timers often become regulars after one visit. The formula is simple: listen first, cut second, and never rush the chair.

Whether you need a quick trim, a full color transformation, or a style for a big day, you will leave the Shed feeling like the best version of yourself.
