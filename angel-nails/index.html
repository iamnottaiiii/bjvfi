Our Story

A neighborhood salon built on loyal clients

Tucked into the Crossroads Centre on Southwestern Boulevard, Angel Nails has become a regular stop for Orchard Park clients who want great nails without fuss or high prices. Reviewers describe the owner as kind and hardworking, and many clients say they have been coming back for years.

The approach is simple: get you in quickly, treat your nails with care, and send you out with color that holds up for weeks. Clients praise the huge polish selection, the affordable prices, and the minimal wait, even on busy weekend afternoons.

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, you will be greeted warmly and seated right away. Walk in, pick a color, and leave with nails worth complimenting.
