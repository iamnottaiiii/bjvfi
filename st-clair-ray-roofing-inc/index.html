Our story

Since 1954: one family,
one trade, one promise

St Clair Ray Roofing Inc is listed as a DBA of Ray St. Clair Roofing Inc, a family-owned company founded by Raymond A. St. Clair.

1930s

It started on the roof

Raymond A. St. Clair learned roofing alongside his father Walter and his two brothers beginning in the 1930s. In 1954, Ray struck out on his own with one truck and a crew of three, and the family tradition of commitment to customers and quality workmanship was set.

Ray's son joined full time in 1970, took over daily operations in 1980, and the third generation has since taken the controls. The company celebrated its 70th anniversary in 2024.

Why it matters

Longevity is the credential

Over half of the company's customer base is repeat or referral business, according to a Cincinnati Magazine feature. When you call, a live person answers. When the crew leaves, the yard is clean. That is how a one-truck company from 1954 becomes a 100-person team.
