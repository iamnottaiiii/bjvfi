Our story

A local electrical crew with a simple standard

Prime Spark Electricians is a Van Nuys based electrical team serving homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley. We started with a simple idea: show up on time, explain the work in plain language, and leave every panel, outlet, and fixture safer than we found it.

How we work

Clean, careful, and code safe.

Electrical work is invisible when it is done right. That is the whole job: sizing circuits correctly, torquing connections, labeling panels, and testing everything before we pack up.

  • Written estimates before work starts, so you approve the price first.
  • Shoe covers, drop cloths, and a full cleanup when the job is done.
  • Permits pulled where required and work installed to current code.
Values
