03Our story

A Livingston Road institution

Hunters Mill Cleaners has served Fort Washington from 9513 Livingston Rd as the area's go to dry cleaner, one pressed garment at a time.

Every neighborhood has one shop it trusts with the good clothes. On this stretch of Livingston Road, that shop is Hunters Mill Cleaners. It is the kind of place first time visitors mention by name in reviews: clean, well organized, and staffed by people who are kind and professional even when you only stop in to ask a question.

That reputation shows up in the numbers. Across 23 public reviews the shop holds a 4.2 rating, and among 18 Washington Consumers' Checkbook raters, 83% rated the shop superior overall. Reviewers call out the friendly, helpful staff, the affordable prices and alteration work so precise it disappears into the fabric.

"She did a great job on the repair, you can not even see it. This place works for me."
