Our story

Small studio,
personal touch

Allure Nail Spa sits at 20 Finn Road in Henrietta, a short drive from RIT and the Jefferson Road corridor. It was built around a simple idea: a nail appointment should feel like time for you, not a slot on a conveyor belt.

That is why the studio keeps it intimate. Appointments are private and unhurried, the conversation is easy, and the work is done with a steady hand and an eye for detail, from careful cuticle work to designs tailored to your nails.

Guests come for everyday upkeep, and for the big moments too: one reviewer shared how the studio stayed open two hours past closing so she would be ready for a special event that same day, then helped her pick a design that suited her nails best.

What to expect

Call ahead to book. Arrive, settle in, and talk through the look you want. Your artist takes it from there: shape, care, color, finish. Leave with nails that still look beautiful a week later.
