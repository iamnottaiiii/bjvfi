Why gutters matter as much as shingles

A roof is only as good as the water it moves away

Most roofing companies treat gutters as an afterthought. For Hellman, gutters are half the name on the truck: the system that carries every storm off your roof and away from your foundation.

On the west side of Cincinnati, plenty of homes still carry their original box gutters, built right into the roofline decades ago. They are beautiful, historic, and unforgiving: when a box gutter fails, water goes straight into the fascia and the walls. Hellman Roofing and Gutters works on both worlds, repairing and coating old box gutters and installing modern K-style systems where they make sense.

Roofers who do gutters properly are rare. We put it in the company name so you know where we stand.

Customers in public reviews describe quick responses, professional repairs, and crews that explain the problem and the remedy in plain detail, then leave the yard looking like they were never there. Several reviewers say they have recommended Hellman to family and friends, and that they keep coming back for other properties.

Our story

A Delhi Township roofer with gutters in the name

Hellman Roofing and Gutters is a Cincinnati roofing company run by David Hellman and his crew, working out of 4298 Glenhaven Rd in Delhi Township. The business covers the two systems that protect a house from above: the roof that sheds the weather, and the gutters that carry it away.

The specialty that sets the company apart is box gutters: the built-in troughs on older Cincinnati homes that most modern crews will not touch.

Public reviews paint a consistent picture of the crew: they show up, they explain what is wrong and how they will fix it, they do clean work, and they leave the property tidy. Reviewers mention fair pricing, quick responses to repair requests, and even small extras like trimming tree limbs that had grown over the roof from a neighbor's yard.

The company is not a large franchise. It is a local outfit whose customers say they recommend it to family and friends and bring it back for their other properties. That kind of repeat business is earned one clean job site at a time.
