About Modern Barbershop

Modern Barbershop on West 3rd Street pairs classic technique with a contemporary eye. The shop keeps a busy chair and a loyal following for clean, current styles.

Every visit follows the same simple rhythm: a real consultation about what you want, careful work in the chair, and a finish you can feel good about. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
