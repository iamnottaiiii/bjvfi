Chimney sweeping and cleaning

A full sweep clears creosote, soot, and debris from the flue and firebox so your fireplace drafts properly and burns cleaner.

  • Flue and smoke chamber brushing
  • Firebox and ash cleanup
  • Draft and blockage check

Chimney inspections

A careful look at the whole system, from the cap down to the firebox, so small problems get found before they become expensive ones.

  • Flue liner condition check
  • Crown, cap, and flashing review
  • Masonry and mortar spot check
  • Plain-language report of what we found

Chimney repairs

  • Crown and masonry repairs
  • Flue and liner work

  • Smoky fireplace troubleshooting

Caps and dampers

  • Chimney cap installation and replacement
