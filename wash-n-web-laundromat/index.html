Our story

Malden's laundry with Wi-Fi

Why the name

Wash n Web was built around a simple idea: laundry does not have to be dead time. With free Wi-Fi, comfortable space, and plenty of machines, customers browse, work, or stream while their clothes spin.

Located at 190 Commercial St in Malden, the laundromat serves busy professionals, families, and anyone who wants clean clothes without the wait.

What customers value

Plenty of dryers. Ample machines even at the busiest times.

Reliable drop-off. Years of wash and fold customers who keep coming back.

Friendly staff. Helpful, and quick to fix anything that is not right.

Open late. 6:30 AM to 11 PM daily, with parking on site.
