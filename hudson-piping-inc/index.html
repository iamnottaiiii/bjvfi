Capabilities

Mechanical services,
tab by tab

Hudson Piping Inc is a specialty contractor in heating, ventilating, and air conditioning, with piping and testing and balancing under the same roof.

Commercial HVAC

Complete heating, ventilating, and air conditioning scopes for commercial and institutional buildings: equipment setting, ductwork, and controls coordination, installed per plans, specs, and addenda.

  • Heat pumps, make-up air units, and exhaust fans
  • Air distribution devices and duct systems
  • Additions and renovations of occupied facilities

Mechanical piping

Three decades of pipe work behind every joint. Process and mechanical piping for commercial and institutional projects, from new construction to retrofits.

  • Hydronic and refrigerant piping systems
  • Water-source heat pump coil connections
  • Coordination with other trades on congested sites

Testing, adjusting & balancing

Air and water balancing to prove the system performs as designed: electronic submittals, documented procedures, and final balance reports for the engineer and owner.

  • Air-side balancing of fans, units, and air devices
  • Water-side balancing of coils and circuits
  • Electronic balance reports and closeout documentation

School & institutional

Public-sector work with public-sector paperwork done right. Recent project records show Hudson Piping on Kentucky school district facilities: support operations centers and technical center additions.

  • K-12 and district facility HVAC scopes
  • Subcontractor and prime-contractor roles
  • Spec submittals, addenda tracking, and commissioning support
