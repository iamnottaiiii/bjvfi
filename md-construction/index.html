Our story

A Valley general contractor

MD Construction is a general contractor based at 7025 Jamieson Ave in Reseda, California. We take on the projects other contractors complicate: the kitchen that needs rethinking, the addition the family has outgrown into, the rental that needs to be brought up to standard.

Plain talk, written scopes

Construction has a reputation for vague promises. We work against it with written scopes, clear pricing and honest timelines. If something will take longer or cost more, you hear it from us first.

Permits and inspections, handled

We pull the permits, meet the inspectors and build to code, so your project adds value instead of headaches when it is time to sell or refinance.

Service area

Rooted in Reseda, working across the Valley

We take projects throughout the San Fernando Valley, including Van Nuys, Northridge, Tarzana, Woodland Hills, Canoga Park, Encino, Sherman Oaks and North Hollywood. Tell us where the project is and we will tell you if we can get there.
