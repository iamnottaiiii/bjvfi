Our story

A Chester Pike shop built on word of mouth

The short version

Authority Auto Repair opened its bays on South Chester Pike in Glenolden to do auto repair the way it should be done: diagnose carefully, quote fairly, and fix it right the first time.

There is no corporate script here. The people who answer the phone are the people who work on your car, and customers say that shows. Reviewers mention being treated with respect, getting clear explanations, and leaving with exactly the repair they approved, nothing extra.

That reputation is why so many reviews read like referrals to a friend. Long-time customers keep coming back year after year, and new drivers find the shop because a neighbor told them where to go.
