Our story

A Duke Street staple for beautiful nails

Tucked into the Shoppes of Foxchase, Nails Hurricane Too has been part of the Alexandria nail scene for years, serving neighbors one careful manicure at a time.

The salon

Relaxed, friendly, and easy to reach

Guests describe a welcoming room where the staff take their time: careful shaping, attentive cuticle work, and little touches like tea while you wait. Under new management, the atmosphere has kept improving, with thoughtful service regulars notice.

It is the kind of place where people find their tech and stick with them, calling ahead to book the same chair, year after year.

The salon
The experience

Pampering without the premium price tag

One thing comes up again and again in reviews: value. Guests say the work rivals pricier salons nearby, at prices that make regular visits easy. Gel manicures that last, pedicures that feel like a treat, and acrylics shaped with care.
