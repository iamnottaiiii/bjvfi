Our story

A working contractor, not a showroom.

N.A. General Construction is based at 11040 De Foe Ave in Pacoima, in the middle of the neighborhoods it serves. It is a builder's business: the kind where the estimate comes from someone who has swung a hammer, and the schedule is built around real crew capacity, not wishful thinking.

As a general contractor, the company takes responsibility for the whole project. That means coordinating trades, keeping the site moving, and being the single person you call when something needs an answer.

Homeowners and property managers across the San Fernando Valley call when they want one accountable name on the job instead of a stack of subcontractor business cards.
