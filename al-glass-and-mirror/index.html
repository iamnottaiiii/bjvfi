Our Story

A neighborhood glass shop, built on careful work

Al Glass and Mirror serves Pasadena, Altadena, and the surrounding San Gabriel Valley from its shop at 1844 New York Dr.

Local, independent, and hands on

The shop operates as AL Glass Mirror and More LLC, a California-registered company, and works directly with homeowners, renters, and local businesses. Reviewers know the team by name and describe on-site measurements, communicated arrival times, and tidy finished installs.

Service is offered in English and Spanish, and the shop handles both residential projects, like a single bathroom mirror or a foggy window, and commercial work such as storefront glass.

  • Registered California LLC: AL Glass Mirror and More LLC
  • Shop address: 1844 New York Dr, Pasadena, CA 91104
  • English and Spanish spoken
  • Residential and commercial projects

How a typical job goes

Most projects follow the same careful path: you call and describe the job, the team measures the opening on site, the glass or mirror is cut to those exact dimensions, and then it is installed, sealed, and cleaned up.

Because every opening in an older Pasadena or Altadena home is a little different, on-site measurement is the step that keeps the finished piece fitting the first time.
