Why it matters

A clogged vent is a quiet hazard

Lint buildup is one of the leading causes of dryer fires. Most vents have never been properly cleaned. Here is what a clear vent does for your home.

Warning signs your vent needs cleaning

  • Clothes need two cycles to dry
  • The laundry room feels hot and humid
  • A burning smell when the dryer runs
  • The outside vent flap barely opens
  • Lint visible around the dryer or cap
  • It has been more than a year since a cleaning

About us

Focused on one job: safe vents

Arvon Dryer Vent Cleaning in New Haven, CT is a dryer vent specialist serving the New Haven area.

Why a vent only business

Dryer vent cleaning is one of those jobs that gets skipped until something goes wrong. Clothes take forever to dry, the energy bill creeps up, and lint keeps packing into the vent line, which is a genuine fire risk.

We started Arvon to make that job easy to get done. No upsells into duct cleaning or appliance repair, just a focused vent service: inspect, clean, verify airflow, and leave the laundry area tidy.

Based at 145 Hemlock Rd in New Haven, we work with homeowners, renters, landlords, and property managers across the area. If your dryer has been working harder than it should, give us a call.
