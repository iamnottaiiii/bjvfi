Our story

The roof is the building's first promise.

We keep it.

Commercial roofs are all we think about.

San Dimas Roofing & Commercial Roof Repair is a roofing contractor based on Bircher St in Granada Hills, focused on the roofs other crews avoid: big flat commercial decks, aging warehouses, and buildings where a leak means lost business, not just a bucket in the hallway.

Our approach is simple: inspect honestly, document everything, repair what can be repaired, and tell you straight when replacement is the smarter money.
