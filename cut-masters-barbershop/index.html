Our story

A neighborhood shop,
earned one cut at a time

Cut Masters Barbershop sits on Liberty Heights Ave in Baltimore, a working barbershop built on repeat customers and word of mouth.

The shop

Regulars made
this place

What the public reviews tell us is the real story: customers who have been coming to this shop for years, one reviewer for twenty, and who call the atmosphere great and the service professional.

Reviewers mention a barber named Pat by name, and describe a chair where everyone is made to feel welcome. That kind of loyalty is not bought; it is earned, one haircut at a time.

The shop offers the classic barbershop menu: haircuts, beard trims, head shaves, hot towel shaves, and shampoo and conditioning, the work a proper shop does, done with attention to detail.
