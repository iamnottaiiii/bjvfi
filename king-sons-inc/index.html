On the record

27 years, one family name

King & Sons, Inc. was incorporated in Kentucky on November 8, 1999. State filings name Charles R. King as president and Donna S. King as secretary: a genuine family company, not a brand.

Permit records place its work in Villa Hills, Ft. Mitchell, Park Hills, Independence, Union, Burlington, and Erlanger. New homes, additions, commercial demolition, interior alterations.

Our story / since 1999

A King family business,
literally

Big kids playing in the dirt, since before it was a tagline.

King & Sons, Inc. started in 1999 in Villa Hills, Kentucky, and the name on the state filings has not changed since: Charles R. King, president; Donna S. King, secretary. Nearly three decades later the company is still active, still small, and still working the same Northern Kentucky neighborhoods.

The permit record reads like a tour of the river cities: new homes in Ft. Mitchell and Union, additions on Lake Shore Drive, a garage rebuilt off the yard in Park Hills, interior work in Independence, commercial demolition for the Villa Hills Civic Club. This is a builder that says yes to the odd jobs too.

"We love what we do and strive for perfection on every job."

That is the company's own description of itself, and it fits the ledger. Customer interaction and communication, in their words, "at its best." If you want a builder who picks up the phone and walks the site with you, that is the pitch, straight from the source.
