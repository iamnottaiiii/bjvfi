Our story

A homey salon in the heart of Syracuse

Felicia Hair Designs is a neighborhood beauty salon at 1751 W Genesee St in Syracuse, NY. Clients describe it as warm, welcoming, and fun, the kind of place where you are greeted by name.

Reviewers praise the personal touch of the team, with several calling out stylist Michael for cuts and color, and noting the relaxed, comfortable atmosphere.

Whether you need a trim, a full color refresh, or styling for a special occasion, the team takes the time to understand your hair and your style.
