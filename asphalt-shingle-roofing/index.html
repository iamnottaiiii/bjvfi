Asphalt shingle installation

For new construction and additions, we install complete asphalt shingle roof systems: decking inspection, underlayment, shingles, flashing, and ridge ventilation, finished with a full cleanup.

Asphalt shingles remain the most popular residential roofing choice for good reason: dependable protection, a wide range of looks, and a cost that makes sense for most homes.

Call (717) 200-2551 Get in touch
  • Decking inspection before anything is laid
  • Ice and water shield plus synthetic underlayment
  • Architectural asphalt shingles in your chosen color
  • New flashing at chimneys, walls, and valleys
  • Ridge vent and cap for balanced ventilation
  • Full magnetic nail sweep and site cleanup

Full roof replacement

When a roof is past patching, replacement is the honest answer. We tear off the old layers, check the decking underneath, and build the roof back up properly, most homes finished in one to three days depending on size and complexity.

You get a written scope before we start: what comes off, what goes on, and what it costs.

Call (717) 200-2551 Get in touch
  • Complete tear-off of existing shingles
  • Decking repair where boards are soft or damaged
  • Fresh underlayment and ice and water shield
  • New architectural shingles, ridge to eave
  • Replaced pipe boots, flashing, and drip edge
  • Daily cleanup and final walkthrough with you

Roof repair

Not every problem needs a new roof. Missing shingles, small leaks, lifted flashing, and worn valleys can often be fixed with a targeted repair that buys your roof years of extra life.

We find the actual source of the problem, not just the stain on the ceiling, and tell you straight whether a repair will hold or replacement makes more sense.

Call (717) 200-2551 Get in touch
  • Leak tracing to the true entry point
  • Replacement of missing, cracked, or curled shingles
  • Flashing repair and resealing at penetrations
  • Valley and ridge cap fixes
  • Pipe boot and vent replacement
  • Honest read on repair versus replacement

Storm damage response

Central Pennsylvania wind and hail can strip shingles, crack them, or loosen whole sections. After a storm, we inspect the roof, document what we find with photos, and get damaged areas sealed against the next rain.

If you are filing an insurance claim, clear documentation of the damage helps the process move faster.

  • Post-storm roof inspection with photos
  • Emergency sealing of exposed areas
  • Documentation to support insurance claims
  • Repair of wind-lifted and hail-damaged shingles
  • Gutter and downspout damage check
  • Straightforward next steps, in writing

Why shingles

Why we bet on asphalt shingles.

For most homes in our area, asphalt shingles hit the sweet spot: reliable in snow, rain, and summer heat, available in styles that suit just about any house, and replaceable in sections when damage is local.

Proven in local weather

Architectural shingles stand up to the freeze-thaw cycles and summer storms Pennsylvania throws at them.

Repairable by design

Individual damaged shingles can be replaced without touching the rest of the roof, which keeps repair costs down.

A look for every home

Dimensional shingles come in a wide range of colors and profiles, from classic to high-contrast.
