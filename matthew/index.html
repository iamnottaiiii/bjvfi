The shop

Profile

Old-school barbering, personal service

Matthew is the kind of barber shop Allentown regulars describe as small, intimate and cozy, in the best way. The chairs fill with repeat clients: dads who have been coming for years, kids getting their first real cuts, neighbors who call and get seen the same day.

The approach is personal. Barbers take the time to understand what you want, keep the conversation easy, and send you out the door with a cut that holds up until your next visit. Reviewers single out the welcoming atmosphere and the consistent quality, visit after visit.

No gimmicks, no rushed chairs. Just a Union Street shop that treats a haircut like it matters, because to the person in the chair, it does.
