Our story

Valerie's chair, San Pedro's barber shop

Shellback Barber is owned and run by Valerie, a barber San Pedro locals have trusted for the better part of a decade. She built the shop around a simple idea: one client at a time, every cut given full attention, and nobody rushed out of the chair.

As a women-owned shop in a trade that does not see many women behind the chair, Shellback has earned a loyal following of regulars who value consistency. Many guests have been coming for years, and they say they would not trust their hair to anyone else.

What the name means

Once a shellback, always a shellback

In Navy tradition, a shellback is a sailor who has crossed the equator. San Pedro is a port town to its bones, so the name fits the neighborhood: working harbor, salty air, and people who take pride in doing things the right way.

That pride shows in the details. The shop is small and kept immaculate. Tools are sanitized, the floor is swept, and the chair is yours alone for the whole appointment.

By appointment only

Your time is the point

There is no waiting bench here because you never need one. Every appointment is booked one at a time, so walk in at your slot and the clippers start warm. Call (424) 477-2125 to grab a time.
