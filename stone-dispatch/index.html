Good to know

Ordering tips from the desk

Know your quantities

Have your square footage, depth, or tonnage estimates handy when you call. It speeds up scheduling and avoids a second trip.

Book ahead for pours

Concrete and large aggregate orders do best with advance notice, especially during the busy building season.

Check site access

Make sure trucks can reach your delivery point. Tell your dispatcher about gates, soft ground, or tight turns.

Who we are

Stone Dispatch is a concrete contractor and building materials dispatch operation based in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. From our desk on Paxton Street, we coordinate deliveries of stone, aggregate, sand, and concrete materials to job sites across the area.

Our work is straightforward: take your materials order, schedule the trucks, and make sure every load arrives when your crew is ready for it. Contractors rely on us to keep projects supplied; homeowners rely on us for driveway stone, fill, and materials delivered without the runaround.
