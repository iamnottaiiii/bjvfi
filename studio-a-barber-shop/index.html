The shop

A Main Street shop with a steady hand

Tucked into downtown Marlborough at 130 Main St, Studio A is the kind of barber shop where the coffee is self-serve, the parking is easy, and nobody rushes your cut. The team, including Agnaldo, Ricardo and Pedro, has been giving excellent haircuts for years.

Walk-ins find a friendly chair, and first-timers who feel unsure about barber shops tend to leave as regulars. Reviewers keep coming back to the same words: kind, professional, and precise.

Services

The chair menu

Every service starts with a consult: what you want, what your hair does, and how much upkeep you want at home.

01
Most requested

Classic Haircuts

Full-service mens cuts, scissor over comb to clipper work, finished with a clean neckline and styling. The team caters to all hair types and takes the time to get the shape right.

02
House specialty

Skin Fades

The cut the shop is known for. Tight, blended fades from low to high, with crisp lines and detail work that holds its shape as it grows out.

03
Family favorite

Kids Cuts

Patient, friendly cuts for young clients. The barbers are known for putting nervous first-timers at ease, and parents appreciate a shop that treats kids like regulars.

04
The experience

Walk In, Settle In

Help yourself to the self-serve coffee station while you wait. No appointment needed for most visits, and the chair is always welcoming, even if barber shops usually intimidate you.
