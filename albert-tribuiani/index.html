About Albert

An artist and decor specialist in South Philadelphia.

Albert Tribuiani is an artist and decor specialist based at 1526 W Moyamensing Ave in Philadelphia, PA. According to his business profile, he started creating custom rooms and decor for customers right out of trade school and has been at it for over five decades.

In the early 1960s, he was one of the only artists creating six foot long light up murals for customers across the United States. In 1968 he made a lamp for his newborn daughter. A children's furniture store owner from 5th Avenue in New York City saw it and asked Albert to create a line for his chain of children's and teen furniture stores. From that point on, Albert created children's decor and murals centered on the materials and ideas his clients brought him.

Over the years he has created art for furniture stores including Bellini's, USA Baby, Louis of London, Colistics, Carol's Furniture, Goldfingers, Small World, and M and B New York, among others. Each order is customized to the needs of the client, each piece is cut out by hand and painted by Albert, and he signs every item. Look closely and you will see a "J" before his signature, in remembrance of his sister Jacquelyn, who he describes as an inspiration.

Albert currently lives in Philadelphia with his wife and, per his profile, still creates one of a kind pieces for clients around the world.
