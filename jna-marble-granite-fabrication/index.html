Our story

A fabrication shop, not a showroom

JNA Marble & Granite Fabrication is a marble contractor based at 660 Arroyo St in San Fernando, California. We are a working fabrication shop: slabs come in, templates are cut, edges are polished, and finished stone goes out to kitchens, baths, and businesses across the Valley.

Because fabrication and installation happen under one roof, nothing gets lost between the salesperson and the installer. The measurements, the seams, and the schedule stay in one set of hands.

SHOP

What that means for you

Direct pricing without showroom markup, seams planned by the people who cut them, and a crew that treats your home like a job site worth keeping clean.
