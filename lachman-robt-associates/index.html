Safety first

Old wiring is a quiet risk

Many foothill homes still run on wiring that predates modern loads. Warm outlets, buzzing panels, or breakers that trip often are warning signs worth a look.

We offer safety inspections and honest assessments: what is urgent, what can wait, and what it will take to fix.
