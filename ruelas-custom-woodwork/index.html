Our craft

A neighborhood woodshop with a simple standard: do it right.

Ruelas Custom Woodwork is a carpentry shop on Heintz Street in Baldwin Park, California. We are a small, hands-on operation: the person who measures your project is the person who builds it.

Our listed trade is trim carpentry, the detail work that most people notice without realizing it: the straight lines where wall meets floor, the crown that ties a room together, the shelf that fits its alcove to the sixteenth of an inch.

We keep our hours simple, Monday through Friday, 9 to 6, and our promises simpler: show up, do careful work, charge fairly.

How we work

Three steps, no surprises

1. Talk

Call or stop by. Describe the project, send a photo, or invite us to measure. We will tell you honestly if it is in our wheelhouse.

2. Build

We cut, join, sand, and finish in the shop or on site, keeping the workspace clean and the timeline clear.

3. Stand behind it

Walk the finished work with us. If a joint is not right, we make it right. That is the whole policy.
