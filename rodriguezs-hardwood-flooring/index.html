Our story

A flooring shop on Victory Boulevard

Rodriguez's Hardwood Flooring is a flooring contractor located at 13540 Victory Blvd, Suite 204, in Van Nuys, California, serving homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley.

The shop focuses on one trade and does it well: hardwood. Installation, sanding and refinishing, repairs, and the moldings and transitions that make a floor look finished. The team also installs laminate and luxury vinyl plank for spaces that need the wood look with extra durability.

Walk in to browse species and finishes, or call for an in home estimate. Every project starts with a measurement, an honest quote, and a clear schedule.
