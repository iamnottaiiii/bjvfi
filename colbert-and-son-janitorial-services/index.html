About us

A family name
on every job

Colbert and Son Janitorial Services is a family-run cleaning company based in downtown Baltimore.

Our story

Small crew,
high standards

Colbert and Son started with a simple idea: treat every office, storefront and home like it carries the family name, because it does. From the downtown base at 110 N Calvert St, the team serves Baltimore with office cleaning, window washing and residential cleaning.

The company is managed day to day with a business-first mindset: clear quotes, reliable scheduling, and a simple promise that every customer ends up beyond satisfied with each service they receive.

Whether it is a daily office route, a storefront that needs its glass gleaming, or a home that needs a fresh start, the approach is the same: show up, work hard, and do not leave until it is right.

Led with care

The business is managed by Ni'Sheerer Colbert, who brings a business and project management background to every job: organized scheduling, clear communication, and customers who stay because the work stays excellent.

Goes above and beyond to ensure that every customer is beyond satisfied with each service they receive.

Why family-run matters

When the name on the invoice is your own, every job is personal. No call centers, no rotating strangers, just a team that knows your space and your standards, visit after visit.
