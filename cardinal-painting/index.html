Our story

The Cardinal Painting standard

Cardinal Painting grew up on Northridge job sites, learning that great paint jobs are won or lost in the prep work. We built our name one careful project at a time, treating every home like it belongs to family.

Today we are a California licensed contractor (CSLB license 1011808), carrying a $15,000 contractor bond and the classifications to handle painting, decorating, and related carpentry repairs the right way.

From our shop at 19224 Bryant St #1, we serve Northridge, Granada Hills, Porter Ranch, Chatsworth, and the greater San Fernando Valley.
