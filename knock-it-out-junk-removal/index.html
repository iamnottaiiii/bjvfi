Who we are

A local crew for the jobs nobody wants to do twice.

Knock It Out - Junk Removal is a Vernon, Connecticut debris removal service. The work is simple to describe and hard to do: show up, lift the heavy things, load the truck, and leave the place cleaner than you found it.

The business runs alongside Knock It Out Property Services, the home repair and maintenance side of the operation. That matters for customers, because a crew that also handles property work knows how to move through a house or a job site without tearing it up: doors, floors, and landscaping get the same care as the haul itself.

Hours are straightforward: open every day from 8 AM to 6 PM, serving Vernon and the surrounding Tolland County area. One phone number, one crew, one promise: you point at it, and we knock it out.
