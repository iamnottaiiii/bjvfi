Our Story

A Family Name on Every Job

C Frank & Sons Plumbing & Heating is a family run shop based on Midway Ave in Enola, PA. The name says it all. This is a family business where the people who answer the phone are the people who stand behind the work.

We grew up around wrenches, torches, and thermostats, and we built the company on a simple idea: treat every home like it belongs to family. That is why neighbors call us a jack of all trades. Heating, cooling, plumbing, and the electrical work that ties it together, we handle the full picture so you only need one number.

As an Armstrong Air dealer, we install equipment we trust in our own homes: oil and gas furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, package units, and air handlers built for Pennsylvania weather.
