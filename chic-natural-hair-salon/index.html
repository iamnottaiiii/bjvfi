Our story

A neighborhood braid studio,
built on skill and warmth

Chic Natural Hair Salon LLC was registered in New York in October 2019 and has been braiding East New York ever since.

From the chair

Where great hair
meets great energy

Ask reviewers what keeps them coming back and the answer is consistent: the braids come out exactly how they pictured them, the vibe is warm, and the service is quick and reliable.

Stylist Chika leads the chair with a steady hand for knotless and boho styles, backed by a team clients describe as patient, skilled and genuinely kind. Movies play, conversation flows, and hours pass fast.

From small knotless to butt-length bohemian braids, from men braids to natural hair care, every appointment is built around one goal: you leave happy with your hair.
