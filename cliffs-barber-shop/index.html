About the Shop

A Hamilton Barbershop on Fourteenth Street

Cliff's Barber Shop is a barber shop at 727 Fourteenth Street in Hamilton, Ohio. It serves the neighborhood with traditional barbering: haircuts, trims, and shaves done in a working shop, not a showroom.

Expect a straightforward visit. Take a seat, talk through what you want, and get a cut built on skill and repetition. That is the whole pitch, and it has worked for a long time.
