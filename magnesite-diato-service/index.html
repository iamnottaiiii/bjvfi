Fiber Deck restoration system

Our signature deck restoration rebuilds the integrity of an existing walking deck and upgrades its waterproofing in one system. High-quality acrylic resins, premium fiberglass reinforcement, and a handsome knockdown troweled finish.

  • Restores concrete, polymer-modified concrete, presealed magnesite, and elastomeric decks
  • Knockdown texture with UV-protection topcoat sealer
  • Enhances the look of the building while it seals it

Magnesite flooring, the original system

A special formulation of magnesium oxychloride cement with inert fillers and aggregates. High quality, Class A fire-retardant flooring for interior and exterior walking decks and stair surfaces.

  • Hard, strong, and long lasting with flexibility that controls cracking
  • Sloped to promote drainage; applied in textures, patterns, and finishes
  • Unaffected by grease, oil, and solvents; resists bacteria and fungi in kitchens

OUR STORY

Satisfying clients since 1974

Five decades of magnesite flooring, waterproof decks, and specialty coatings across Southern California.

A specialty trade, done right

Magnesite Diato Service has spent 50 years on one thing: surfaces that live outdoors and take punishment. Walking decks, balconies, stairs, and commercial floors that must stay waterproof, stay textured, and stay safe underfoot.

The company is headquartered in Mecca, California, with crews serving the greater Southern California region. Its own materials describe the work simply: "Satisfying Clients Since 1974!"

Licenses on record with the California Contractors State License Board cover synthetic products, painting and decorating, and flooring and floor covering, per BuildZoom. The company carries a surety bond and workers compensation coverage, and holds a triple-A rating with the Better Business Bureau per its LeadIQ profile.
