Services

Everything a paint job needs, under one roof

Every project starts with a free estimate at your home. Call (443) 858-1158 and tell us what you are picturing.

Interior painting

Full rooms or single walls, ceilings included. Two coats, patched and sanded first, with furniture protected throughout.

Exterior painting

Power washing, scraping, priming, and finish coats on siding, brick, stucco, trim, and porches.

Cabinets and trim

Kitchen and bath cabinets degreased, sanded, and sprayed smooth. Doors, baseboards, and crown molding too.

Color consultation

Large swatches in your own lighting, honest advice on undertones, and help narrowing it down to one confident choice.

Drywall repair and prep

Dents, cracks, and nail pops repaired and textured to blend, so the paint goes onto walls that look new.

Move-in and rental refresh

Fast, clean repaints between tenants or before move-in day, scheduled around your timeline.
