Valley roofs take a beating

Chatsworth summers bake shingles to 140 degrees, and winter storms find every weak flashing joint. Here is what we watch for on local roofs:

  • Curling or missing shingles from UV and heat
  • Cracked flashing around vents and chimneys
  • Clogged gutters that back water under the edge
  • Attic heat that shortens shingle life
