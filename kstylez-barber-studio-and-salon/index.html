03 / The studio

Built like a studio, run like family

KStylez barber studio and salon opened its doors in June 2021 at 6100 Vine St in Cincinnati, Ohio. From day one the brief was simple: a barber shop and salon where craft comes first and everybody gets the same care.

The team keeps up with continued education, uses quality products, and keeps the room clean and welcoming. Clients come for fades and stay for the conversation, the music, and barbers who actually listen to what you want.

Men's cuts, women's cuts, kids' cuts, shaves, and beard work. Multiple services, one location, zero rushed chairs.
