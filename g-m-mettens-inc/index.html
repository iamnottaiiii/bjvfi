About

Serving Northern Kentucky since 1979

G M Mettens Inc is a family-run landscape supply company based at 6129 Taylor Mill Road in Covington, Kentucky, in the Latonia area.

For decades, the yard has supplied contractors, landscapers, and homeowners with bulk mulch, decorative rock, sand, topsoil, gravel, and stone. Customers consistently describe it as a one stop shop: fair prices, knowledgeable staff, and advice that covers the project beyond the material sale.

Reviewers mention the crew by name and praise the punctual, accommodating service. Whether you are resurfacing a driveway, refreshing beds, or building a patio, this is the yard where the project starts.
