About

A landscaping company that does it all

BOTANICS Landscaping is a Cincinnati landscape company based at 4612 Kellogg Ave. The team describes its work simply: landscape design and build, paver walks and patios, landscape lighting, retaining walls, water features and ponds, clean-ups, mulching, leaf removal, turf care, horticultural consulting, seasonal displays, and storm water management.
