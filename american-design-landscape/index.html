Our story

Designers who build, builders who design.

American Design & Landscape grew out of a simple frustration: beautiful plans handed to crews that never read them. So we do both under one roof at 8377 Paso Robles Ave in Northridge.

The person who draws your yard is the person accountable for how it gets built. That keeps designs honest, budgets real, and the finished yard matching the sketch you approved.

We work across Northridge and the surrounding Valley, mostly on residential projects where the yard has to work as hard as it looks.

One team.
From sketch to soil.
What we believe
  • Plan before planting. A drawn design prevents the expensive do-overs.
  • Build for the Valley. Heat, clay soil, and water rules shape every plant choice.
  • Finish clean. The last day looks like the first: swept, raked, and walked with you.

We would rather talk you out of a bad idea than bill you for one. If a design will not thrive on your lot, we will say so and draw one that will.
