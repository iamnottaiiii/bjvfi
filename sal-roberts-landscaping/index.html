Landscaping Services

Edging

Arbor Construction

Garden arbors and similar small structures that add shape and shade to your landscape.

Artificial Grass Installation

Neighbors serving neighbors.

Sal & Robert's Landscaping is based on Dudley Street in Arlington, Massachusetts, and works on properties throughout the area. From the name to the neighborhood, this is a small, local operation: the people you talk to are the people who show up.

Our listed specialties include landscaping services and edging, arbor construction, artificial grass installation, brick and stone driveway installation and repair, and brush removal. If your project touches any of those, we would like to hear about it.
