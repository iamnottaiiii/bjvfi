Our story

A new chapter, written in polish

The Nail Story sits at 63 E Cedar St, Unit 3 in Newington, CT. Guests say the salon is under new management, and the current team gets it: fast, meticulous, and genuinely kind.

Reviewers who tried the salon after the change describe night and day: appointments that run on time, designs that nail the trends, and a team that takes pride in every set.
