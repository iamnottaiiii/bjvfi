What we do

Masonry that holds up, year after year

Every job starts with a look at the structure underneath the surface, not just the surface itself. That is how a repair lasts decades instead of seasons.

Leaking crowns, spalling brick and crumbling mortar joints are the calls we answer most. We rebuild, repoint and reflash chimneys so they vent safely and shed water.

Our story

A masonry crew, not a sales team

Action Masonry Contractors is a masonry contractor based at 162 2nd Ave in Elizabethport, New Jersey. We work across Elizabeth, Linden, Rahway and the surrounding Union County towns, and our phone is answered by people who do the work, not a call center.

The trade is simple to describe and hard to do well: bed the unit level, tool the joint clean, and make sure water has a way out. That is the standard on every chimney, wall and walkway we touch.

If you are comparing bids, ask each contractor how they handle flashing, drainage and mortar matching. The answers tell you most of what you need to know.
