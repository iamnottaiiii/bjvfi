Our story

Furguson's Haircare opened its doors in 1977 and has been an Ontario fixture ever since. Owner David Furguson keeps the shop clean, the prices reasonable, and the customers happy. Dave is the stylist reviewers call truly the best they have ever had. Mrs. Leaty keeps beards sharp enough to bring clients back monthly. Nearly five decades of great hair on Ridge Road.
