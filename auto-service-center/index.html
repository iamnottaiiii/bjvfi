Our story · The counter

A Richmond Highway fixture

Family-run, community-minded, and built on the belief that an honest explanation is part of every repair.

How the shop works

The best repair shops are the ones you find once and never leave. Auto Service Center aims to be that shop for Alexandria: a place where the person at the counter knows your car, knows your name, and tells you the truth about both.

The team services domestic and foreign vehicles, from commuter sedans to work trucks, with a focus on getting the diagnosis right the first time. Oil changes, brakes, inspections, and the full range of mechanical work all happen under one roof on Richmond Highway.

"Done right the first time, explained in plain language."

Fair, transparent pricing is the house rule: a detailed explanation of the work, a clear breakdown of the costs, and no surprises at pickup. That is how a neighborhood shop earns customers who come back year after year.
