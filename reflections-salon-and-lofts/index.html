Our story

About Reflections

Reflections Salon and Lofts is a hair and nail salon at 913 OH-28 in Milford, Ohio, serving the Eastgate and greater Cincinnati area.

The salon

A modern loft-style salon where stylists run their own suites and guests enjoy a comfortable, friendly atmosphere. The team handles nearly any hair need, from everyday cuts and blowouts to dimensional color like balayage and highlights, smoothing keratin treatments, perms, extensions, and braiding. Two nail technicians round out the menu with most nail services and pedicures.
