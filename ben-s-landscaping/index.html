Services

Landscaping services for homes and properties in Worcester, MA.

Lawn Care & Maintenance

Routine mowing with crisp edging along walks, drives, and beds. Regular care keeps turf dense, which crowds out weeds and keeps the lawn looking its best all season.

  • Mowing and edging
  • Seasonal feeding
  • Overseeding thin areas

Landscape Design

A plan for your yard that fits the space, the sunlight, and how you actually use it. Design work covers bed layouts, plant selection, and simple changes that make a big visual difference.

  • Bed layout and plant selection
  • Foundation plantings
  • Curb appeal refreshes

Planting & Mulch

Fresh plantings paired with clean mulch beds and defined edges. Mulch holds moisture, suppresses weeds, and gives beds that finished look.

  • Flower and shrub planting
  • Mulch installation and refresh
  • Bed edging and reshaping

Seasonal Cleanups

Spring cleanups clear winter debris and wake the yard up. Fall cleanups handle leaves and cut backs so beds and lawn go into winter clean and healthy.

  • Spring debris removal
  • Fall leaf cleanup
  • Perennial cut backs

Hedge & Shrub Trimming

Shaping and thinning for hedges, shrubs, and small ornamentals, timed to the plant so it stays healthy and looks intentional.

  • Hedge shaping
  • Shrub thinning and renewal
  • Overgrowth cut backs
