The story in brief

E&A Hair Salon is the kind of place Laurel locals recommend to each other: a full service salon where the staff speaks your language, literally. English and Spanish are both at home here, and the team covers everything from precision fades to rich color to detailed nail work.

Reviewers mention talented barbers, careful color, and stylists who take the time to get every detail right. It is an easy, affordable stop for the whole family.
