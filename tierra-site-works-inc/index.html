What we do

Full-service site work for residential and commercial projects

From complete structure demolition to rough grading and site dressing, Tierra Site Works brings the manpower and equipment to prepare your site for what comes next.

Tierra Site Works, Inc. is based at 7263 Woodley Ave in Van Nuys and serves the greater Los Angeles area. Our crews perform complete demolition of single-family properties, including house and garage removal, foundation and slab removal, and clearing of concrete, brick flatwork, trees, and landscaping within the property lines.

Every job is finished with the site dressed to a uniform condition, ready for the next phase of construction. Typical services include demolition, excavation and fill, grading, shoring and underpinning, and concrete work.

Our story

A licensed site-work contractor in the Valley

Tierra Site Works, Inc. is a California licensed contractor (License #1065513) based in Van Nuys, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley. The company focuses on the heavy first phase of construction: demolition, earthwork, and getting a site ready for builders.

Our public bid proposals show a straightforward way of doing business: a clear scope of work, stated exclusions, and defined terms, so property owners and developers know exactly what is included before a machine ever rolls on site.

Licensed, bonded, and insured, with a written scope on every job.

The company carries workers compensation insurance and a contractor surety bond, and serves residential and commercial clients across the Los Angeles area. For bid inquiries, call (818) 616-4204.
