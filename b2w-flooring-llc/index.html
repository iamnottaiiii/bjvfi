The showroom

See it, touch it, walk on it before you commit

Choosing a floor from a tiny sample chip is a gamble. At the B2W showroom on Utley Drive, you can compare full boards of hardwood, luxury vinyl plank, laminate, tile, and carpet side by side, and get honest guidance on what fits your home, your budget, and your lifestyle.

The right floor changes how a room feels. We help you find that floor, then install it properly.

As a flooring contractor, B2W handles the full job: measuring, material selection, removal of old flooring, subfloor prep, installation, and finishing touches. Commercial clients and builders can also source material through the wholesale supply side of the business.

About B2W Flooring, LLC

A Camp Hill flooring contractor with a showroom, not just a warehouse

B2W Flooring, LLC operates from 25 Utley Drive in Camp Hill, Pennsylvania, combining a flooring showroom with full contracting services. Homeowners shop materials in person; contractors and commercial clients source through the wholesale supply side.

One team sells it, measures it, and installs it.

Because B2W is a contractor first, the advice in the showroom comes from installation experience: which products hold up to dogs, which subfloors need prep, and which finishes suit a busy kitchen versus a quiet bedroom.
