Our story

Mediterranean Decorators Painting Services is a painting company based at 293 Shetlands Ln in Glen Burnie, Maryland, led by Paul Zotos.

The company is known for its Mediterranean touch: vibrant colors, warm finishes, and craftsmanship that transforms both homes and businesses. Years of hands on experience go into every job, from the first coat of primer to the final finishing touches.

What clients mention most is the care: punctual arrivals, tidy work areas, thoughtful color suggestions, and a stress free experience from beginning to end.
