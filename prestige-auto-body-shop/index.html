Who we are

Prestige auto & body shop is an auto body shop in Springfield Township, Cincinnati, Ohio, at 2222 Sevenhills Dr.

Our work centers on three things: collision & body repair, complete auto repair, and auto sales. One shop, one phone number, everything your car needs in one stop.

We are open six days a week, including Saturdays until 4 PM, so getting your car in does not have to mean taking a day off work.
