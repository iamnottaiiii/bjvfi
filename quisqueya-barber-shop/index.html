About Quisqueya

A barbershop rooted in Caribbean culture, right on Dixie Highway in Fairfield.

Quisqueya is the Taíno name for the island shared by the Dominican Republic and Haiti, and it is a name Dominicans use with pride for their homeland. Quisqueya Barber Shop brings that heritage to Fairfield, Ohio, offering barbershop services in a warm, community minded setting.

The shop sits at 6625 Dixie Hwy Unit T, in a busy commercial corridor of Fairfield, easy to reach from Hamilton, Springdale, and the greater Cincinnati area.

What we can verify from public listings: the shop is listed as a barber shop at this address, with a phone of (513) 330-6707. Public listings describe it as cash only with a wheelchair accessible entrance and parking.
