About

A Gibsonia dental tradition, carried forward.

Wirtz Dental is led by Dr. Tod A. Wirtz, DMD, a licensed Pennsylvania dentist (license DS024333L) practicing general dentistry at 5548 William Flinn Hwy in Gibsonia.

1956: A Pittsburgh dental education

Dr. Paul Henry Wirtz graduated from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, then served as a dentist in the United States Navy at Camp Lejeune.

Four decades in Gibsonia

After his service, Dr. Paul Wirtz returned to the Pittsburgh area and started a private dental practice in Gibsonia, where he cared for patients for 40 years.

Today: Dr. Tod A. Wirtz, DMD

Dr. Tod Wirtz continues the family practice in Gibsonia, known to patients for thorough explanations, a gentle approach, and a staff that goes above and beyond.
