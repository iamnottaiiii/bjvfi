The Winter Readiness Guide

A Harrisburg winter is the final exam for your heat

Dauphin County winters are not a suggestion, they are a test. January nights drop well below freezing, and a heating system that limped through November will not hide in January. The houses that stay warm are the ones whose owners listened early.

Cold spots that never quite warm up, a furnace that cycles on and off too often, burners that rumble or delay, and fuel bills that outpace the thermostat setting are all reasons to call before the cold settles in.

C E Miceli Co provides heating system service for Harrisburg, PA homes: furnace repair, new system installation, and the seasonal tune-ups that keep everything running through the cold months. Call (717) 564-5560 to schedule.

October is when you meet your furnace. January is when it meets you.

A fall service visit checks burners, heat exchangers, controls, and airflow while the weather is still forgiving. Small fixes now cost less than emergency calls later.
