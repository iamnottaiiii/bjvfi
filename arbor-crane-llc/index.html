Our Story

A Connecticut Crane Company With Staying Power

Arbor Crane llc is a crane service based in Windsor Locks, Connecticut. Directory listings put the company at 19 years in business, and federal carrier records list it as an active Connecticut carrier.

The name says it plainly: this is a crane company rooted in tree work. Over the years that work has grown to cover the full range of residential and commercial lifts, from storm-damaged trees to rooftop units and structural steel.

Small crews. Direct communication. Picks planned before the boom goes up. That is the whole business model.
