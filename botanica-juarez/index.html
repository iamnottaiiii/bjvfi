La Tienda

Everything for the mesa and the home

Botanica Juarez keeps a complete little shop: candles in every color, herbs weighed to order, and the oils and soaps people come back for again and again.

Candles

Veladoras, votives and long burn candles for novenas and intentions.

Herbs & roots

Romero, ruda, salvia, ramos and the classic yerbas for baths and sweeps.

Oils & perfumes

Aceites espirituales, esencias, aguas and colognes by the bottle.
