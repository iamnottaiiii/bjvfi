Our story

A family shop built on trust

Tom & Son Auto Repair Service is a family-run repair shop on Amosland Road in Holmes, Delaware County. The name says it all: a family team doing careful, capable work for their neighbors.

Expert mechanics

Tom and Brian are experienced, knowledgeable mechanics who take the time to explain what is going on with your car. Customers call them trustworthy, reliable and genuinely helpful, and many have brought their families here for years.

The next generation

The shop is a family affair. A young nephew works alongside the team and customers describe him as a dedicated, hardworking kid learning the trade. It is the kind of shop where people know each other by name.
