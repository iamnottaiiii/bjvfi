About

A working yard in Paxton

City Line Landscape Supply is a landscaping supplies business. Our Paxton location is a material and storage yard supporting our landscaping businesses in Worcester and Rutland.

What we do

Supplies, not services

We are a landscaping supplies business, not a landscaping business. The yard exists to sell materials and stage them for the crews that do the work, ours and yours.

Our operation here is a continuation of Pleasant View Nursery's prior operations on the property. We do not sell nursery stock. What we do is keep the material side of the trade running: stock, storage, and a yard that is easy to load from.

The Paxton yard

Our site sits on about 8 acres on Pleasant St, with roughly 3 acres in active use. The garage handles equipment storage, and the yard holds pallets and dry storage for materials.

The Town of Paxton has been part of the story too: the property has been cleaned up and put back to work, and the local building inspector has backed the cleanup effort.

No fluff, just material

You will not find a garden boutique here. You will find piles, pallets, and people who know what a yard of loam weighs. If you need landscaping materials in the Paxton area, that is exactly what this yard is for.
