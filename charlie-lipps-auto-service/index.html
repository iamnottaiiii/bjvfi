Station History

A Delhi Pike Landmark

Charlie Lipps Auto Service has been providing automotive repair, mechanical, and maintenance services for the Delhi, Price Hill, and Western Hills areas since 1957. What started as a classic mid century service station grew into a trusted full service garage for generations of Cincinnati drivers.

The formula never changed: fair prices, straight talk, and work done right the first time. This is an independently owned and operated shop, the kind of place where the person who answers the phone is the person who fixes your car.

  • 1957

    The station opens its doors on Delhi Pike, serving the growing west side of Cincinnati.

  • Through the decades

    The shop keeps pace with every era of the automobile, from carburetors to computer diagnostics, while keeping its old school service.

  • Today

    Charlie Lipps Auto Service remains an independent garage at 4240 Delhi Rd, Cincinnati, OH 45238, open weekdays 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
