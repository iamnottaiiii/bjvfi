How it works

Simple from first call to final walkthrough

1. Call and describe it. Tell us what you want built, fixed, or changed. Photos help.

2. We look and price it. We see the site, measure twice, and give you a clear number.

3. We build it. Scheduled work, tidy site, and a walkthrough at the end so you can check everything.

Why Crosscut

The careful way to build

Construction is a trust business. You are letting someone tear into your home or your land, so you deserve plain dealing: a clear scope, a clear price, and work that holds up.

That is the standard we hold ourselves to on every job, big or small. If something is not right, we make it right.

Services

Construction, covered

As a general construction company and contractor, our work spans new builds, additions, remodeling, and repairs. Every project is scoped individually, call to talk through yours.

01

New construction

Ground-up builds planned and executed with care, from layout to final finishes. Bring your plans or your ideas and we will work from there.

02

Additions

More room where you need it: bedrooms, living space, garages. Built to tie into your existing home cleanly.

03

Remodeling

Kitchens, baths, basements, and whole-room updates that make the space work the way you live.

04

Structural and exterior work

Framing, roofing-adjacent repairs, siding, decks, and the structural details that keep a building sound.

05

Repairs and upkeep
