Notre Histoire

Paris Trained, Valpo at Heart

the American dream, baked daily

Bao is a 100% French trained pastry chef. She graduated from the prestigious Paris Auguste Escoffier School with her professional pastry degree, then honed her craft in patisseries and bakeries across Paris.

In early 2018, Bao and her husband made a bold choice: they gave up their comfortable Paris life and moved to Valparaiso, Indiana. They were enchanted by the community, the quality of life, and the beauty of the dunes country around Lake Michigan.

"A nice taste of Paris in every bite."

For their first year, Bao and her husband sold authentic French pastries at the farmers markets in Valparaiso and Chesterton. Customers kept asking for more, and by popular demand, the family opened their boutique storefront at 12 Jefferson Street in downtown Valparaiso.

Today, BAO's PASTRY is family owned and operated by Bao and Hà, baking artisanal French pastries, traditional desserts, and savories fresh every single day.

The adventure continues at 607 E Lincolnway, the bakery's second downtown location, home to the new kitchen and a future Parisian-style high-tea room.

Everything is handcrafted, from delicate macarons and towering opera cakes to custom celebration cakes designed one-on-one with each guest. Bao also teaches pastry workshops, sharing the techniques she learned in Paris with Northwest Indiana.

Come taste what Valpo fell in love with at the farmers market, and stay for a little piece of Paris.
