Our Story

A family shop on E 116th Street, built on craft and kindness.

Guerrerense Barber Shop sits in the heart of East Harlem at 217 E 116th St, and it has the feel of a place that has been part of the block for a long time. The name nods to Guerrero, Mexico, and the shop carries that warmth into everything it does.

This is a unisex barber shop in the fullest sense: barbers and stylists cut and style hair for men, women, and children, from sharp fades and beard work to women's cuts, blowouts, and color. Reviewers call the team detail oriented, friendly, and hardworking, people who take their time to get it right.

The shop's own philosophy says it best: being a stylist is not just cutting hair, it is knowing how to bring out each client's beauty. Walk in, and you will feel like family.

Being a stylist is not just cutting hair, it is knowing how to bring out the beauty in my clients. Here you will feel like family.
