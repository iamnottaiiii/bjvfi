Our Story

A Sherman Oaks contractor built on craft and care

Fine Builders Designs is a full service general contractor based in Sherman Oaks, California, serving Los Angeles and Ventura County. We specialize in kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, room additions and general remodeling, from backyard projects and roofing to patios, painting and pavers.

Our approach is personal and creative: we design with you, build with quality craftsmanship, and work to keep your project on schedule and within budget. Throughout each stage of work, we are dedicated to keeping your home as clean and comfortable as possible.
