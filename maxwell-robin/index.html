Services

Dental services

General dentistry covers the diagnosis, treatment, and ongoing care of your teeth and gums. Below are the kinds of services a general dental practice provides.

01

Exams and cleanings

Routine checkups and professional cleanings that catch small issues before they grow.

02

Fillings

Tooth-colored restorations that repair decay and blend with your natural smile.

03

Crowns and bridges

Durable restorations that rebuild damaged teeth and replace missing ones.

04

Extractions

Gentle removal of teeth that cannot be saved, with clear aftercare guidance.

05

Dentures

Full and partial dentures designed for comfort, fit, and a natural look.

06

Gum care

Screening and treatment plans to keep gums healthy and inflammation in check.
