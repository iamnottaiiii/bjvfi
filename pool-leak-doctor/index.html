What we do

Leak detection is all we do, and we do it well

A focused specialty beats a generalist guessing game. Here is how we track down your water loss.

Leak Detection

Full-system investigation of your pool and spa: structure, fittings, and underground plumbing, until the source is found.

Structural Leak Repair

Cracks, skimmers, lights, tile lines and returns: located by dive inspection and dye testing, then sealed properly.

Plumbing Pressure Testing

We pressure-test underground lines to find the breaks you cannot see, no digging up the whole yard to find them.

Dye Testing

A careful dye test shows exactly where water is escaping, so repairs target the leak, not the whole pool.

Equipment Area Checks

Pump, filter, heater and valves: we inspect the equipment pad for the slow drips that add up to real water loss.

Water Audits

Evaporation or leak? We walk you through the bucket test and measure real loss, so you know what you are dealing with.

Why a leak specialist, not a pool company

Most pool companies do a little of everything: cleanings, chemicals, repairs. Leak detection is a different discipline. It takes patience, methodical testing, and the willingness to prove the answer before billing for it.

Pool Leak Doctor focuses on that one hard problem. When your water level keeps dropping and nobody can tell you why, that is exactly the call we want.

We work directly with homeowners across Fleming Island, Orange Park, Green Cove Springs and the surrounding Clay County area. You talk to the person who will actually be in your pool.
