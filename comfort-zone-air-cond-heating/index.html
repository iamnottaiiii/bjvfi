Our story

A local shop for a local climate

Comfort Zone AIR Cond & Heating is a locally owned and operated HVAC contractor based on Farley Avenue in Scotch Plains, New Jersey. We do one thing: keep homes comfortable through every swing of a New Jersey year, from August humidity to January cold snaps.

Because we are a neighborhood business rather than a national chain, you talk directly to the people doing the work. That means honest diagnostics, straight answers about repair versus replacement, and a crew that treats your home like it belongs to a neighbor. Because it does.

We hold an active New Jersey home improvement contractor license, verified through the state license board records listed on BuildZoom, where the company carries a score of 90. We show up when we say we will, we clean up after ourselves, and we stand behind the work.
