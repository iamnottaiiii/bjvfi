Our story

Skill, warmth and braids that last

Fatima's Hair Braiding Shop has become a go to name for braiding on Syracuse's North Side. Clients describe Fatima as accommodating and professional, with a shop that feels welcoming from the moment you sit in the chair.

The team is known for working efficiently, many styles are finished in just a few hours, and for braids that reviewers say lasted up to two months. It is a family run feel, clean and tidy, where regulars keep coming back.
