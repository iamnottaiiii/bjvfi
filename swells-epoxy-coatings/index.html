Why epoxy

A new floor without pouring a new floor

Replacing concrete is loud, dusty, and expensive. A professionally installed epoxy system bonds to the slab you already have and seals it against the things that wear concrete down: moisture, salt, oil, and daily traffic.

  • Resists hot tire pickup, chemicals, and abrasion
  • Brightens dark garages and basements with a reflective finish
  • Seals cracks and pores so the slab stops shedding dust
  • Simple upkeep: sweep and damp mop, no waxing needed

How it works

From cracked slab to coated floor in four steps

01

Free estimate

Call or message with your floor size and photos. You get an honest quote and a straight recommendation on the right system.

02

Prep and repair

Concrete is diamond ground for adhesion, cracks are filled, and moisture is checked so the coating bonds for the long haul.

03

Coat and flake

Epoxy base coat goes down, decorative chips are broadcast to full coverage, and the floor cures overnight.

04

Seal and walk

A clear topcoat locks everything in. Most floors are ready for light use in 24 hours and full use in about three days.

Our story

Industrial roots, residential focus

Swells Epoxy Coatings grew out of years of industrial, commercial, and educational coatings work, plus deep experience in cement finishing. That background matters, because most failed epoxy jobs fail at the concrete, not the coating.

Today the focus is on homeowners: decorative coating systems that give a garage or basement the look of a showroom floor with the toughness of an industrial slab. Damaged concrete is repaired and refinished instead of torn out, which keeps the project cleaner, faster, and more affordable.

The pitch is simple and it is on the shop wall: fix what you have, coat it right, and enjoy a damage resistant decorative floor for decades.
