What we do

One trade, done well

Southside Drywall focuses on drywall and nothing else. That means installation that is level and plumb, tape and mud work that disappears under paint, and repairs that blend into the wall around them.

  • New drywall installation for rooms, additions, and remodels
  • Repairs for holes, cracks, dents, and nail pops
  • Taping, mudding, sanding, and texture matching
  • Water damage drywall replacement
