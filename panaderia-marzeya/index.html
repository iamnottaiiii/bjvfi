Why Marzeya

A South Chicago staple,
one tray at a time

Longtime customers call it the best bread in the area. Here is what keeps them coming back, some for more than a decade.

Try this
01

Pan de nata

The house specialty regulars name first: a rich, tender sweet bread made with cream. Ask for it warm if you time it right.

02

Baked before sunrise

Doors open at 4 AM, seven days a week, so the first trays of conchas and pan dulce are still warm when early risers arrive.

03

Cakes for every event

Birthday cakes are a customer favorite, and the bakery takes custom orders for all kinds of celebrations. Call ahead to plan yours.

04

Cream-filled bismarks

Pillowy bismarks with a generous cream filling, plus donuts and danishes for the morning crowd. One reviewer drives in twice a week just for these and a cinnamon coffee.

05

Small conchitas, big fans

Bite-sized conchas with the classic crunchy sugar shell. Customers call them out by name and recommend them to newcomers.

06

Good pan, fair prices

Reviewers keep using the same words: fresh, delicious, friendly staff, great prices. An easy neighborhood habit.

Nuestra historia

A family bakery on
Commercial Ave

Panaderia Marzeya is a family-run Mexican bakery in the heart of South Chicago, up before the sun so the neighborhood never has to wait for fresh bread.

01

Ten years and counting

Longtime customers say they have been coming for over a decade, calling the bakery one of the best things South Chicago has to offer. That kind of loyalty is earned one tray at a time.

02

Family behind the counter

Customers describe it as a family business through and through: friendly faces, familiar service, and the kind of place where regulars are known.

03

New chapter, same oven

A 2021 reviewer noted new owners with, in their words, a great touch in all their baked goods. The neighborhood noticed, and approved.

Come taste the tradition.

8908 S Commercial Ave, Chicago. Open daily 4 AM - 7:30 PM. Ask for the pan de nata.
