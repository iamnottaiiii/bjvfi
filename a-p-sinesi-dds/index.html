Meet the dentist

Dr. Akis P. Sinesi, DDS

Dr. Sinesi has practiced general dentistry in Brooklyn for 43 years, caring for generations of families at his Marine Park office. His care philosophy is simple: patient comfort, health and satisfaction is our number one priority.

A graduate of St. Johns University with a BS in Biology earned cum laude, he went on to the Georgetown University School of Dentistry, graduating with top honors. His practice has been listed among topDentists since 2012.

Patients know the office as a personal, comfortable place, where modern compassionate dentistry is delivered by a dentist who takes the time to listen.
