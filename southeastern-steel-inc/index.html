About

A Georgia steel contractor

Southeastern Steel Inc is a structural steel and precast concrete contractor operating out of Jonesboro, Georgia. The company is registered in Georgia and works commercial and industrial projects across the metro Atlanta area.

Our work shows up in the bones of buildings: the frames that carry the load, the lintels that span the openings, and the connections that tie it all together. We price from plans, document changes clearly, and show up when the schedule says we will.

For bid requests, send drawings and specs to our direct line and we will turn around a number.
