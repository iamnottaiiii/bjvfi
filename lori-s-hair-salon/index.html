About Lori's

A neighborhood salon built on listening, in Solvay since 2016.

Lori's Hair Salon opened its doors on Montrose Avenue in 2016, and it has grown the old-fashioned way: one happy guest telling the next.

Before Solvay, Lori styled hair in Baldwinsville, and longtime clients followed her here because she understands their hair, including tricky thinning hair that needs a careful, honest approach. Guests describe a stylist who listens first, suggests what will genuinely work with their features, and never rushes the chair.

The salon keeps things simple: cuts, color, highlights, perms, and styling at prices neighbors can afford, in a relaxed setting where you are treated like a regular from the first visit.
