Our Story

A classic barber shop, run the classic way.

Meet Tommy

Tommy's Barber Shop has been serving Fairview since 2003, and Tommy himself brings decades of experience to the chair. He is a master barber in the traditional sense: skilled with the scissors, honest about what will suit you, and happy to talk while he works.

The shop welcomes clients of all ages, from kids getting their first haircut to seniors keeping a signature style they have worn for years. Some clients have been coming for more than 30 years. That kind of loyalty is earned one good cut at a time.

At Tommy's, a great haircut is more than a service, it is an experience. Come relax in a comfortable chair, enjoy the conversation, and leave looking your best.
