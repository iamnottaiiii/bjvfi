Why the fossil theme (inference)

Cincinnati sits on world-famous Ordovician fossil beds, limestone and shale laid down about 450 million years ago. Road cuts and building excavations around the city regularly expose them. This site's design borrows that local geology as a motif. It is a design choice, not a claim about the company's history.
