About The Shop

An independent repair shop in Milford, Ohio, built on honest work and long term customer relationships.

The Owner

Mike Salatin owns and runs Mikes Automotive Solutions. Customers know him as the kind of mechanic who will not sell you a repair you do not need.

In his own words, posted publicly in response to a customer review: "We will never sell you something or tell you that you need something repaired, if you do not. We do not just work on cars. We build long lasting relationships with all of our customers."

Source: Owner response on the shop's public CARFAX profile

The Shop

Mikes Automotive Solutions is a full service, independent auto repair shop located at 1083 OH-131, Milford, OH 45150.

The shop services Chevrolet, Dodge, Ford, Honda and Toyota vehicles, plus many other makes, covering everything from routine oil changes to engine, transmission and electrical repair.

Reviewers describe the shop as trustworthy, fair and personal, the opposite of a cold dealership experience.
