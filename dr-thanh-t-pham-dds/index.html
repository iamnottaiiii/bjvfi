Our story

A neighborhood dental practice built on trust, one patient at a time.

Thanh T. Pham, D.D.S., P.C.

Dr. Thanh T. Pham, DDS practices general dentistry at 6926 Little River Turnpike in Annandale, Virginia, caring for patients of all ages and their everyday dental needs.

What sets the practice apart is not a slogan. It is the pattern you see across 28 five-star patient reviews: emergencies welcomed, treatment explained plainly, and families who have trusted Dr. Pham with their smiles for 15, 20, even 30 years.

The office keeps things personal. Patients know the team by name, appointments run on time, and nobody is judged for how long it has been since their last cleaning. You are simply welcomed and put back on track.
