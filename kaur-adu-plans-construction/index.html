What we build

ADU types for Valley lots

Every property is different. We match the ADU type to your lot, your budget, and your goals, then handle the drawings and the build.

Garage conversions

Turn unused garage space into living space

Full conversions with kitchens, baths, insulation, and separate entrances. Often the fastest path to a permitted ADU on a standard lot.

Detached ADUs

A private backyard home

New standalone units with their own footprint, designed for privacy from the main house.

Attached ADUs

Connected, not cramped

Additions and attached units that share a wall but live like their own home.

Plans and permits

Drawings that survive plan check

Site plans, floor plans, elevations, and structural coordination prepared for Los Angeles permitting, plus corrections handled until approval.

How we work

01

Services

ADU plans drawn for your lot

We measure your property and design a unit that fits the lot, the code, and your goals. Layouts balance livability with cost, so you get a bedroom that fits a real bed, a kitchen you can cook in, and storage that works.

  • Site survey and as built measurements
  • Floor plans, elevations, and sections
  • Kitchen and bath layouts with fixture selections
  • Structural coordination with licensed engineers
  • Title 24 energy compliance documentation
Deliverable

A permit ready plan set

You receive a complete drawing package prepared for Los Angeles plan check, with revisions handled through approval.

Permitting handled start to finish

ADU approvals involve zoning review, building plan check, and sometimes planning or public works clearances. We prepare the submittal, track it, and answer corrections so you are not chasing paperwork.

  • Zoning and feasibility research up front
  • Permit application preparation and submittal
  • Plan check corrections and resubmittals
  • Coordination of required clearances
  • Inspection scheduling during construction
Why it matters

Corrections are normal

Most ADU submittals get correction comments. Having the designer and builder on the same team means faster, cleaner responses.

Garage to home conversions

Converting an existing garage is often the most economical ADU. We handle the full transformation: insulation, plumbing, electrical, windows, and finishes, plus a separate entrance that gives the unit its own identity.

  • Structural evaluation of the existing garage
  • Full insulation, drywall, and flooring
  • Kitchen and bathroom rough in and finish
  • New windows, doors, and separate entry
  • Heating, cooling, and water heater setup
Good fit when

You have a sound garage

If the slab and walls are in decent shape, a conversion can move faster than new construction. We verify that during the site visit.

New detached backyard ADUs

A standalone unit gives the most privacy for tenants or family. We design the footprint around your yard, manage the foundation and framing, and finish the interior to a clean, durable standard.

  • Site layout for privacy and light
  • Foundation, framing, and roofing
  • Full plumbing, electrical, and HVAC
  • Kitchen, bath, and finish carpentry
  • Final inspections and certificate of occupancy
Design judgment

Placed for daily life

Window placement, entry angles, and fencing are planned so the ADU feels like a cottage, not an afterthought crowding the main house.

One accountable project manager

From the first drawing to the final inspection, you have a single point of contact. Schedules, trades, inspections, and budget tracking stay in one place, and you get straight updates without chasing anyone.

  • Single point of contact throughout
  • Trade scheduling and site supervision
  • Inspection coordination at each stage
  • Budget and change order transparency
  • Final walkthrough and punch list closeout
Our standard

Clean, safe job sites

Construction is planned to protect your existing property and keep access to the main house open throughout the build.

Our story

A North Hollywood builder focused on one thing: ADUs

Who we are

Plans and construction under one roof

Kaur ADU Plans & Construction is a general contractor based at 7007 Lankershim Blvd in North Hollywood, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley. Our name says what we do: we draw the ADU plans and we build what we draw.

That design build pairing matters on ADU projects. When the people holding the pencil also hold the schedule, details get resolved on paper instead of on your dime in the field.

How we work

Honest feasibility first

Not every lot pencils out, and we say so early. A site visit and zoning check come before any design fee, so you know what is possible before you spend on drawings.

What we believe

An ADU should feel like a home, not a compromise

Small does not have to mean cramped. We trade a few square feet of living room for a real bedroom wall, a usable closet, and a kitchen where two people can stand without bumping shoulders. That is the difference between a unit that rents for a season and one that holds a tenant, or a parent, for years.
