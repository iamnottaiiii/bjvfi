Our story

A Rutland original

Lee's Barber Shop has been serving the Rutland area for years from its spot at 249 Main Street. It is the classic American barbershop: a striped pole, comfortable chairs, and barbers who treat cutting hair as a craft.

The shop's reputation was built the old fashioned way, one great haircut at a time. Regulars describe the barbers as professional, skilled, and genuinely friendly, the kind of people who remember your usual and ask how the family is doing.

The Lee's way

  • Listen first, cut second
  • Classic technique, modern styles
  • No rushed chairs, no upsells
  • A shop the whole town trusts

Small town, big loyalty

It is not uncommon to see neighbors chatting and catching up while they wait their turn. The atmosphere is warm, the decor is classic, and the whole experience feels like a step back to when the barbershop was the heart of Main Street.
