Our story

About The Purple Peach Nail Weave & Lash bar

The Purple Peach Nail Weave & Lash bar is a nail, weave and lash bar on North Charles Street in Baltimore. The philosophy is simple: top-notch nail care, the hottest trends, and a visit that feels like a treat.

Walk-ins are welcome and appointments are suggested, so fitting in a fresh set is easy. Call (443) 743-6673 to book your chair.
