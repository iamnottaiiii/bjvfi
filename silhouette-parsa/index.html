Our story

A quieter kind of salon

silhouette parsa was built on a simple idea: beauty services feel better in a space that feels calm.

i.

The space

Modern, spotless, and warm. Reviewers describe relaxing music, a cozy atmosphere, and positive energy the moment you walk in. Every station is kept immaculate, and guests notice.

ii.

The people

Stylists are praised by name in review after review: Aksana for meticulous, long-lasting manicures and pedicures, Omid for precision hair work. Professional, detail-oriented, and genuinely warm.

iii.

The philosophy

One place for everything. Hair, nails, makeup, and spa services under one roof means you can plan a full beauty day without running across town. Quality products, honest advice, no rush.

iv.

The neighborhood

On Richmond Road in Staten Island, silhouette parsa has become a local favorite for guests who want an upscale experience without the downtown trek.
