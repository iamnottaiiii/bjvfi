The dentist

Dr. Yves Conte, DDS

Dr. Conte is a general dentist licensed in New York, practicing in Jamaica, Queens since 1983. That is more than four decades of cleanings, crowns, root canals and dentures, and four decades of patients who keep coming back.

His practice follows a traditional model: the dentist you call is the dentist you see. Appointments are unhurried, explanations are clear, and treatment plans are built around what you actually need.

He is enrolled in Medicare and speaks English, French, Spanish and Creole, reflecting the community he serves.
