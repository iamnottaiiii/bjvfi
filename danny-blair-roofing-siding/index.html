About Danny Blair Roofing & Siding

A Cincinnati-area roofer and sider

Danny Blair Roofing & Siding operates from 517 Maple St in the Elmwood Place area of Cincinnati, Ohio. Directory profiles list the business as both a roofing contractor and a siding contractor serving Hamilton County, including Cincinnati and nearby towns such as Cleves, Harrison, Miamitown, North Bend, Addyston, and Hooven.

Known for straight talk

The oldest public reviews go back to 2000. In a 2011 Angi review of a siding project, the reviewer wrote that Danny talked them out of replacing structural columns that were still sound, then built custom decorative caps on request. The reviewer said the crew worked steadily all week and the house ended up nearly maintenance free.
