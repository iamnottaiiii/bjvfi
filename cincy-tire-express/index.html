The shop at 10265 Spartan Drive Suite Q

Cincy Tire Express is an owner-operated tire shop at 10265 Spartan Drive Suite Q in Cincinnati, Ohio. It is a one-man operation: John sells the tires, schedules the appointments, and works on the vehicles himself.

The shop focuses on tire sales, installation, and repair, with a reputation for specialty work: commercial and fleet tires, industrial and heavy equipment tires, and performance and collector car tires.

What keeps customers coming back, by their own accounts, is the combination of fair pricing, honest advice, and speed. Reviewers describe John as knowledgeable, efficient, and unwilling to sell you something you do not need. Many say he actively warns them away from inferior products sold by larger tire chains.

The tradeoff for that personal service: the shop runs by appointment only, with no walk-ins. John is a one-person crew, so every job on the schedule is planned work.
