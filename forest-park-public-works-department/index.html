Four divisions, one route

How the department is organized

The Public Works Department is organized into four divisions that together maintain the city's streets, parks, buildings, and equipment.

Street Maintenance

Road repairs, resurfacing, signs, and signals.

Park Maintenance

Fields, courts, landscaping, and tree care.

Building Maintenance

Upkeep of city-owned facilities.

Equipment Maintenance

Fleet service for city vehicles.

2026 project route

Street resurfacing and curb replacement

The city will revitalize roads and curbs in 2026 through its annual resurfacing program, combining city funds and grant funding. Resurfacing priority is based on Pavement Condition Ratings, with the streets in greatest need first. Curb replacements align with the resurfacing work.

Snow emergency reminders

  • Crews salt and plow continuously in winter storms, starting with primary routes, then residential streets.
  • Move parked vehicles off streets during snowfall so plows can pass.
  • When a snow emergency is declared, vehicles must be removed from streets with "No Parking During Snow Emergency" signs.
  • Clear sidewalks within 72 hours after snowfall stops, and do not shovel snow into the streets.
Work order board

Services

Every service below is listed on the City of Forest Park's official Public Works page. This is a municipal department serving residents, not a private contractor, so work is performed on public infrastructure according to city schedules.

Work orderWO-01

Street repairs and pothole patching

Patching potholes and repairing city streets to keep roadways safe and smooth.

Work orderWO-02

Sidewalk and curb maintenance

Maintaining public sidewalks and curbs. Homeowners are responsible for driveways and private sidewalks, and a right-of-way permit is required for work on driveway aprons or public sidewalks.

Work orderWO-03

Storm sewer upkeep and catch basin cleaning

Keeping storm sewers flowing and catch basins clear to reduce flooding.

Work orderWO-04

Street sweeping and road painting

Sweeping streets and painting roadways, parking lots, and speed zones.

Work orderWO-05

Snow and ice control

Salting and plowing to keep roads accessible during winter storms, starting with primary routes.

Work orderWO-06

Traffic signal and sign maintenance

Maintaining traffic signals and street signs across the city.

Work orderWO-07

Tree planting and landscaping

Planting trees and maintaining landscaping on public property.

Work orderWO-08

Sports fields and courts upkeep

Maintaining sports fields and courts in city parks.

Work orderWO-09

Fleet maintenance for city vehicles

Servicing and repairing the city's vehicle fleet.

Work orderWO-10

Right-of-way cleanup and grass cutting

Clearing rights-of-way and cutting grass on public land.

Work orderWO-11

Chipper service, brush and limb disposal

Curbside chipping for residential homeowners, available April to October, once per month. Branches must be under 8 inches in diameter, placed curbside by 7:00 AM with cut ends facing the street, left loose (no wire or rope). Old firewood, lumber, split rail fencing, leaves, roots, and stumps are not accepted. Crews are allotted 10 minutes per stop.

Residential only, schedule varies by area
Work orderWO-12

Building maintenance

Upkeep and repair of city-owned buildings and facilities.

  • For a pothole or damaged sign, note the nearest intersection or address.

  • For driveway apron or public sidewalk work, ask about the right-of-way permit.

About the department

The Forest Park Public Works Department is a municipal department of the City of Forest Park, Ohio, committed to maintaining and improving public infrastructure and delivering safe, efficient city services to residents.

Our mission

Per the city's official site: "The City of Forest Park Public Works Department is committed to maintaining and improving public infrastructure, ensuring safe and efficient city services for residents." The department's closing line: "Forest Park Public Works is committed to maintaining a safe, clean, and thriving community."

It is organized into four divisions: Street Maintenance, Park Maintenance, Building Maintenance, and Equipment Maintenance.
