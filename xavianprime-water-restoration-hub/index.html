Response protocol

The typical restoration process

Every job is different, but professional water damage restoration generally follows these steps.

STEP 1

Inspection

The source is found and moisture is mapped with meters.

STEP 2

Water extraction

Pumps and vacuums remove standing water fast.

STEP 3

Drying

Air movers and dehumidifiers pull moisture from materials.

STEP 4

Cleaning

Affected areas are cleaned, sanitized, and deodorized.

STEP 5

Repairs

Damaged drywall, flooring, and finishes are restored.
