What we do

Painting services for Baltimore homes

From a single accent wall to a whole-house refresh, every project starts with a free, no-pressure estimate and ends with a walkthrough you approve.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, and doors. We protect your floors and furniture, patch and sand, then roll on smooth, even coats.

Exterior painting

Siding, brick, stucco, and porches painted with weather-ready coatings made for Maryland summers and winters.

Cabinets and trim

Kitchen cabinets, baseboards, crown molding, and built-ins refinished for a factory-smooth, durable finish.

Our story

A Baltimore painting company built on doing it right

Chrystal Painting LLC is a painting company based on Castlemill Circle in Baltimore, Maryland. We take on the projects other painters rush: careful prep, sharp cut lines, and finishes that hold up to real life.

Prep first

Anyone can roll paint. We earn the result in the hours of patching, sanding, and masking that come before it.

Clean lines

Where wall meets ceiling and trim meets wall, the line should be razor straight. We hold ourselves to it.

Respect your home

Shoe covers, drop cloths, and daily cleanup. We treat your space like it is our own.
