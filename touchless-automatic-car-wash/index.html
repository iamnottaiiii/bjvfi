About the wash

Why touchless

Brushes trap grit and drag it across paint. A touchless automatic wash uses pressurized water, detergents and air, so nothing abrasive ever touches your car.

  • No brushes on your paint
  • Fast, drive through format
  • Open to all cars and SUVs
