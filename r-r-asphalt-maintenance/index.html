Our story

Twenty years of blacktop

R and R Asphalt Maintenance has been working pavement in the Cincinnati area since 2006. Based on Forest Road in Anderson Township, it is a sole proprietorship, which means the person you talk to is the person on the job.

The philosophy is simple and unfashionable: maintain asphalt instead of replacing it. A driveway sealed on schedule and crack-filled early can outlast one that gets ignored until it fails, at a fraction of the cost. That is the pitch, and it has held up for two decades.
