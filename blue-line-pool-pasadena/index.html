Straightforward pool care

Keeping a pool safe and swimmable takes regular attention. Here is what a visit typically covers.

  • ✓Chemical testing and balancing. Chlorine and pH checked and adjusted for safe, comfortable water.
  • ✓Skimming, brushing, and vacuuming. Debris removed from the surface, walls, and floor.
  • ✓Equipment walkthrough. Pump, filter, heater, and skimmer checked for visible issues.
  • ✓Water level and leak checks. Levels topped off and visible leaks noted.

Pool services in Pasadena

The work below describes typical pool contractor services. Call to confirm availability, scheduling, and pricing for your pool.

Routine Pool Cleaning

Regular visits to skim, brush, and vacuum, with chemical testing and adjustment so the water stays clear and safe.

Call for scheduling and pricing.

Equipment Diagnosis

Inspection of pumps, filters, heaters, and chlorinators to find the cause of poor circulation or weak heating.

Chemical Balancing

Water testing and careful chemical adjustment for chlorine, pH, and alkalinity, with safe chemical handling.

Green Pool Recovery

Step-by-step cleanup for pools that have turned green, including treatment planning and follow-up checks.
