Our story

A Granada Hills exterior company built on integrity, craftsmanship, and showing up like a neighbor.

Since 1979

Forty-seven years, one standard.

Mr Exteriors Inc was founded by Michael Rosen in 1979 and has served Southern California ever since. The approach has never changed: hands-on work, honest pricing, and personal service from a team that treats your home like their own.

Homeowners know us for the details: stucco patched so the texture matches, crews who are pleasant and professional, and an owner who checks in on the work personally.
