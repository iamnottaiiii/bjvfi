Our story

A Valley tile shop since 1976.

Lashmet Tile Co was established in 1976 in North Hills, California, and has been a ceramic tile contractor ever since. Owner Richard Lashmet runs a small two-person operation, the kind of shop where the person quoting your job is the person setting your tile.

Fifty years in one trade teaches you what shortcuts cost. This shop does the prep, the waterproofing, and the layout planning that makes tile last decades instead of years.

Homeowners across the San Fernando Valley have trusted the Lashmet name for bathrooms, kitchens, and floors. The company holds a 5.0 overall rating on Angi.
