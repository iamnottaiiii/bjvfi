Our story

A small local crew with deep Valley roots

Rivera Gardening Service has been caring for yards in Panorama City since 2016. We are a small, family-run crew, the kind where the person who answers your call is the person who shows up to do the work.

Our promise is simple: reliable and affordable landscaping. No upsells you did not ask for, no mystery charges, just honest work on a schedule you can count on.

From weekly lawn routes to one-time hillside cleanups, we treat every property like it is our own front yard.
