Exterior stone veneer

Full or partial facades, wainscoting, entry columns, and garden walls. Veneer is set over a prepared substrate with proper flashing and drainage so the wall stays dry behind the beauty.

  • Front facades and accents
  • Wainscot and foundation wraps
  • Columns, piers, and planters

Interior stone

Fireplace surrounds, accent walls, and entry features. Interior veneer is lighter work with no weather exposure, which means more stone styles to choose from and a faster install.

  • Fireplace surrounds
  • Accent and media walls
  • Entryways and niches

Repair and refresh

Re-setting loose stones, repointing mortar joints, sealing, and matching discontinued stone. A repair visit starts with an honest assessment of whether a fix or a redo makes sense.

  • Loose and fallen stone reset
  • Mortar joint repointing
  • Sealing and touch-ups

Our story

A San Dimas masonry crew.

California Stone Veneer Inc is a masonry contractor based in San Dimas, California, focused on one craft: stone veneer, installed to last.

Who we are

Stone is all we do.

Plenty of contractors do a little of everything. We keep our focus narrow on purpose: stone veneer and the masonry that supports it. That focus shows up in straight courses, tight joints, and walls that look as good in ten years as they do on day one.

We work with homeowners and builders across San Dimas and the surrounding San Gabriel Valley communities.
