Fast response when water strikes

Water damage gets worse by the hour. Our process moves from emergency extraction to full drying so your property can get back to normal quickly.

Step 01

Inspection and Assessment

We find the water source, map the affected areas, and check for hidden moisture behind walls and floors.

Step 02

Water Extraction

Powerful pumps and vacuums remove standing water from floors, carpets, and furnishings.

Step 03

Drying and Dehumidification

Air movers and commercial dehumidifiers pull residual moisture from hard to reach places.

Step 04

Cleaning and Sanitizing

Thorough cleaning removes contaminants and odors, especially after sewage or floodwater exposure.

Step 05

Mold Prevention

Fast drying stops mold before it starts. Affected materials are treated or removed as needed.

Step 06

Restoration and Repairs

Drywall, flooring, and finishes are repaired or replaced to bring your property back to pre-loss condition.
