Our story

Fairport's quiet
little gem

Luna Nail Salon has been pampering Fairport from Turk Hill Rd since 2011, one meticulous manicure at a time.

A studio built on detail

Luna Nail Salon is an appointment-only nail studio on Turk Hill Rd in Fairport, led by owner and nail tech Meredith. Since opening its doors, the salon has built a loyal following the slow way: careful work, honest service, and nails that clients love to show off.

Reviewers describe the experience as meticulous and creative, with real attention to cleanliness. It is the kind of place where a first-time manicure turns into a standing appointment.

Calm, personal, unhurried

Because every visit is by appointment, there is no crowded waiting room and no rushed chairs. Your time is protected, the atmosphere stays relaxed, and the focus stays exactly where it belongs: on your nails.

From everyday manicures to wedding-day sets, from waxing to lashes, Luna is a one-stop refresh in the heart of Fairport.
