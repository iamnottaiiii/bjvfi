Our story

A local electrician, not a call center

DAK Electric is based on OH-125 in Amelia, Ohio, and serves homeowners and small businesses across Clermont County and the surrounding area.

Why local matters

When the power is out or a breaker keeps tripping, you don't want a dispatcher three states away. You want someone who can actually get to your street. We're based here, we work here, and our reputation is built one neighbor at a time.

How we work

Show up on time. Diagnose honestly. Quote before we start. Clean up when we finish. It's not complicated, but it is rare.

Safety first, always

Electrical work done wrong can burn a house down. We wire to code, pull permits where required, and never cut corners on grounding, bonding, or protection.

Residential and light commercial

Homes, rentals, small offices, and shops. From a single dead outlet to a full service upgrade, if it runs on electricity, we can wire it, fix it, or make it safer.
