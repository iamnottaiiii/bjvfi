Services

Ground work, done right

Excavation is the whole job: opening the ground, shaping it, and closing it back up so whatever comes next sits right. Pick a project type to see what is usually involved.

01Excavation

Digging is the core of the trade, and it is never just digging. Soil type, depth, access, and what is already buried all change the plan.

  • Foundation and basement excavation
  • Footings and additions
  • Septic system excavation
  • Stump and debris removal
Get a free estimate

02Site preparation

Before a single board goes up, the site has to be cleared, leveled, and ready. Good prep is what keeps a project on schedule.

  • Land clearing and lot preparation
  • Rough and finish grading
  • Fill, compaction, and leveling
  • Building pad preparation
Get a free estimate

03Trenching and drainage

Water goes where the ground tells it to. Trenching and drainage work makes sure that is away from your foundation, not toward it.

  • Utility and water line trenching
  • French drains and curtain drains
  • Culverts and swales
  • Erosion problem fixes
Get a free estimate

04Driveways and grading

A driveway that holds up starts six inches down. Proper base, proper pitch, proper compaction, then the surface goes on.

  • New driveway excavation and base
  • Driveway regrading and repair
  • Parking and turnaround areas
  • Yard regrading and leveling

About

Three decades of moving dirt

Paul Morris still answers the phone

Morris Excavating Co. was started on July 13, 1992, and has been owner-operated ever since. The company is based in the Leicester and Rochdale area, working residential and commercial sites across Central Massachusetts.

The business holds an A+ rating on its BBB business profile and an active Massachusetts Construction Supervisor license. No accreditation badges bought, no franchise playbook: just a local excavating contractor with 34 years of ground under its boots.
