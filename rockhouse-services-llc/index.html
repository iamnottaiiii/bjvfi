Our story

19 years of
fresh coats

Rockhouse Services LLC has been painting and remodeling in Burlington, Kentucky since 2007, growing one careful job at a time.

Since 2007

Local, steady,
and still here

Rockhouse Services LLC is owned by Jay Woods and based on Dartmouth Drive in Burlington, Kentucky. The business started in 2007 and has spent 19 years doing what it says on the truck: full interior and exterior painting, plus bath, kitchen and basement remodeling for homes and businesses.

The reputation was built the slow way. Customers describe a crew that shows up on time, is easy to have in the house, sweats the details, and finishes the job exactly as promised. That is why so many reviewers say the same thing: they would call Rockhouse again.

From a two-story entry repaint to a full exterior, the approach is the same: a free estimate, careful prep, and a finish you are proud to show the neighbors.
