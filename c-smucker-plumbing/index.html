About the shop

A family shop, not a franchise.

C Smucker Plumbing is a small family plumbing business in Milford, Ohio, run as a father-and-son shop with a four-person crew. Public directory listings report about 37 years in the trade. Every job is done in-house. There are no subcontractors, so the people you meet on the first visit are the people who finish the work.

The shop works out of 779 US-50 in Milford and serves the Milford area. Emergency services are offered, and reviewers frequently mention fair pricing and quick response when jobs get urgent.

  • The family model

    Father-and-son ownership with a small, steady crew. Work is never handed off to strangers.

  • Licensed across the river

    Licensed in Ohio and Kentucky, bonded in four Ohio counties plus the City of Cincinnati, with a state backflow license.

  • Rated by the people we serve

    A 4.9-star public rating across review directories, built one job at a time.
