The studio

A private room,
not a production line

Addiction Ink is a one artist studio inside the Great Valley shops on Lancaster Ave. No walk in chaos, no waiting room crowd. Just you, Don, and the design.

Designed, not picked off a wall

Clients say Don's depth of knowledge shows most in the design phase. He configures, lays out, and refines each piece with you before a single line goes in, so the tattoo fits your body and your story.

Whether it is your first tattoo or your fortieth, the process stays the same: a real conversation, a custom drawing, and zero pressure.

Clean, calm, and comfortable

Review after review mentions the same things: a very clean, relaxed, private setting, and an artist who is easy to talk to for hours in the chair. One client has logged more than 22 hours across four tattoos and would trust no one else.

Families come back together. First timers leave planning their second piece. That is the whole business model.

In his own words

All about the art

Don describes himself simply: an award winning published tattoo artist who does most styles, with a focus on realism and new school neotraditional. Clients describe him with more words: talented, knowledgeable, easy to talk to, and completely trustworthy with permanent decisions.

The studio has been running since 2017 out of a private shop space on Lancaster Ave in Malvern. One artist, one chair, and a reputation built review by review.

  • Award winning and published

    Recognized work in the tattoo press, with a portfolio spanning realism, color, and black and grey.

  • Design first philosophy

    Clients consistently praise the design phase: layout, configuration, and honest guidance before the needle.

  • First timer friendly

    A relaxed private setting and a patient artist make this a common choice for first tattoos, and families keep returning.
