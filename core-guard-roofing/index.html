FAQ

Straight answers

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?+
That is exactly what the free inspection is for. If the damage is localized and the roof has life left, we will say so and quote the repair. If the system is at the end of its life, we will show you why and price the replacement. We do not sell replacements where repairs will do.
How fast can you get out for a leak?+
Active leaks get priority. Call (582) 256-9972 and tell us water is coming in, we will get you on the schedule as fast as we can, typically within a few days during the work week.
What areas do you serve?+
We are based in Pacoima at 13216 Goleta St and serve Pacoima plus the surrounding San Fernando Valley. Call to confirm we cover your neighborhood.
Is the estimate really free?+
Yes. We come out, inspect the roof, take photos, and give you a written price. No charge, no obligation, no pressure.
