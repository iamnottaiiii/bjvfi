About Marvin Fong & Associates

Marvin Fong & Associates Inc. is a design firm based at 1111 1/2 Highland Oaks Dr #2 in Arcadia, California 91006. According to the firm's published site, it is dedicated to producing quality design solutions shaped around each client's needs and preferences, specializing in new custom single-family residences as well as remodel and addition projects.

Marvin Fong was born and raised in Southern California and earned a Bachelor of Architecture degree from the University of Southern California. Before opening his own practice in 2003, he was Senior Project Designer at Lim Chang Rohling and Associates in Pasadena, designing single-family homes for the high-end development market and custom clients.

The firm states two guiding responsibilities: serving each client's needs and desires for their home project, and making positive contributions to the built environment through architectural projects, education, and community involvement.
