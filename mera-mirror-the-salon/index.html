Our story

About Mera Mirror The Salon

Owner Damera Baker was looking for a home for her growing full-service hair salon when she found the 3100 block of Erdman Avenue in Belair-Edison. She purchased the building and made it her own, completely renovating the interior.

Owning the space changed everything. She added a second floor for custom services, hired more stylists, and invested in the block itself, painting the exterior and adding a mural of the salon logo with support from Belair-Edison Neighborhoods Inc.

Since the move, the salon has seen more walk-ins and steady growth, and Damera has become a visible part of the Main Street community she now calls home.
