About Kenwood Auto Sales

A small, independent used car lot on Old State Route 74 in Batavia, Ohio.

Kenwood Auto Sales is an independent used car dealer serving Batavia and the surrounding Clermont County area. Directory records show the business was established in 2010 and operates from a single lot at 984 Old State Rte 74.

The lot keeps things simple: cash deals for straightforward buyers and in-house 1-year auto loans for buyers who need financing handled on site. The Better Business Bureau lists Kenwood Auto Sales, LLC with an A+ rating. The business is not BBB accredited, which is common for small independent lots and does not affect the rating itself.
