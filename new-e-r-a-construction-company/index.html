Our story

New E.R.A Construction Company is a construction company based at 12091 Hoyt St in Lake View Terrace, California, serving the northeast San Fernando Valley and nearby communities.

The name says what we stand for: a new era of how construction gets done. Fewer surprises, clearer numbers, and a crew that treats your home like it is their own project.

Whether it is a single bathroom or a full addition, the process is the same: walk the job together, put everything in writing, keep the site clean, and finish the punch list before we call it done.

How a project runs

  1. Walkthrough. We visit, listen, measure, and photograph.
  2. Written scope. Line-item estimate with materials and timeline.
  3. Build. Scheduled crews, protected home, daily cleanup.
  4. Walkthrough. Final review and punch list, signed off by you.
