SINCE 1993

Our Story

L & C Electric Inc has been listed in the construction industry Blue Book as an electrical contractor since 1993, working out of Hanover, Maryland.

We are a commercial and industrial electrical contracting company based at 7521 Duckens St in Hanover, MD. Our team of roughly two dozen electricians and staff serves clients across Anne Arundel County and the Washington DC area.

Our project experience spans the CSI divisions electricians live in every day: low voltage and medium voltage distribution, wiring device schedules, grounding and bonding for integrated automation, process control wiring, and overhead power systems.

That breadth is why general contractors and facility managers keep calling. We understand the drawings, the schedule pressure, and the inspection process, because we have been through it for more than three decades.
