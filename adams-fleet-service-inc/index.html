Our Story

Adams Fleet Service, Inc is a trusted, family-owned auto repair shop on Brandywine Road. For years we have served neighbors across Brandywine and the surrounding area with the same promise: outstanding service, trustworthy work, and fair pricing.

What sets us apart is attention to detail. Our highly skilled team uses modern technology and equipment to diagnose and repair problems, and takes pride in getting every repair done right the first time.

Customers tell us they appreciate the straight-to-the-point repairs and the family feel of the shop. Some bring in project trucks they are excited to get back on the road. Others come for a tune-up and leave saying the truck runs phenomenal. Either way, they leave as part of the family.

We understand your time is valuable, so we work efficiently to get you back on the road as quickly as possible. Book ahead when you can, the schedule fills up.
