About the store

Baltimore City Painting Star is a building materials store located at 4609 White Ave in Baltimore, Maryland, serving the surrounding neighborhoods with paint, supplies, and everyday materials.

The focus is simple: keep the shelves stocked with what local painters and homeowners actually use, and make it easy to get in, get what you need, and get back to the job.

Questions about stock or a project? Call the store directly.
