Our story

A roofing contractor your neighbors can reach

Superior Roofer Granada Hills is based at 11130 Balboa Blvd in Granada Hills, CA. We are a local roofing contractor focused on one thing: keeping valley homes dry, safe and looking sharp from the street.

Roofing is trust work. You cannot see most of what we do from the ground, so we earn it the old way: show up on time, photograph what we find, explain your options in plain words, and stand behind the finished job.

Whether it is a single leak or a full roof, call and talk to a roofer, not a call center.
