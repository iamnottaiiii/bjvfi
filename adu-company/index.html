Our story

A Valley contractor with one focus

ADU Company is a general contractor based in Van Nuys, California, focused on accessory dwelling units for San Fernando Valley homeowners.

Why ADUs, and only ADUs

California changed the rules and backyards changed with them. We chose to specialize: instead of juggling every kind of remodel, we run ADU projects all day, every day. That focus means we know the local plan check process, the common lot pitfalls in the Valley, and how to keep a small footprint project on schedule.

Our goal on every job is simple: a legal, well built unit that adds real value to your property, delivered with clear communication from day one.
