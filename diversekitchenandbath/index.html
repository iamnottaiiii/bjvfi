Our story

A contractor with a specialty

Diverse Kitchen and Bath is a general contractor based in Park Hills, Kentucky, with a focus on the two hardest-working rooms in the house: kitchens and bathrooms.

The name says it plainly. Rather than spreading across every trade, the work centers on remodels where design, plumbing, tile, cabinetry, and finish carpentry all have to come together cleanly. That focus means fewer surprises and a finished room that looks intentional down to the grout lines.

Projects run on clear communication: you know the plan, the schedule, and who to call. If you are comparing contractors in Northern Kentucky, ask about recent kitchen and bath work first. That is the portfolio that matters.
