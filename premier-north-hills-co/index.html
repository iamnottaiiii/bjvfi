Why speed matters

Water does not wait. Neither should you.

A small leak behind a wall can soak framing, flooring and insulation in a single day. The faster extraction starts, the less there is to tear out and rebuild.

That is the whole business model of a restoration company: show up quickly, dry thoroughly, and document everything so insurance claims go smoothly.
