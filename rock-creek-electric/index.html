Our Story

A local shop with a commercial-grade work ethic.

Rock Creek Electric is based at 514 Ash Street in Elsmere, Kentucky, and has been listed in the Blue Book of building and construction since 2018. The shop serves Boone County and the wider Cincinnati, Columbus, and Dayton region of Southern Ohio.

Customers know Tim by name, and they mention the follow-through: written cost analyses, rebate guidance, and savings that beat the estimate.

It is a small-company experience with big-job capability, from a dead outlet in a spare bedroom to a full LED retrofit across a commercial building. Every job gets the same treatment: show up on time, diagnose honestly, fix it to code, and stand behind the work.
