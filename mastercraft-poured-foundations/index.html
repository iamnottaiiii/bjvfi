About Us

The Foundation Crew of Milford

Mastercraft Poured Foundations is a concrete contractor based at 1335 US-50 in Milford, Ohio, serving residential and commercial projects across Clermont County and the greater Cincinnati area.

Built on Solid Work

We specialize in one thing and do it well: poured concrete foundations. From footings to full basement walls, our crew handles the structural base your home or building stands on.

Mastercraft Poured Foundations is registered as an active trade name with the Ohio Secretary of State. Our work covers new residential construction, additions, and commercial foundation pours, plus foundation repair and inspection services.

Every pour is scheduled around your builder's timeline, and every wall leaves the site straight, solid, and ready for the next trade.
