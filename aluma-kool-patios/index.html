Our Story

Kool shade, honest work

Aluma Kool Patios is a construction company based at 9569 Claire Ave in Northridge, California, specializing in aluminum patio covers and outdoor living projects across the San Fernando Valley.

The name says what we believe: a patio should be the coolest spot in the house, even in August. Aluminum gets you there without the maintenance treadmill of wood. No painting every few years, no termite worries, no warped boards. Just solid shade that looks sharp season after season.

We keep the process simple. You call, we come look at your patio, you get a clear quote, and we build it clean. As a local Northridge company, our reputation is built one backyard at a time, and we plan to keep it that way.
