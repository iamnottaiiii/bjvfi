Our Story

A neighborhood shop on Delsea Drive with a simple operating principle: treat people straight and fix cars right.

Beidemans Auto Service has served Westville and the surrounding Gloucester County towns for years as an independent, family-style repair shop. Head mechanic Steve leads the bays alongside Mr. B and Bud, a small team that knows its regulars by name and by vehicle.

The reputation that shows up again and again in customer reviews is honesty: upfront explanations, no beating around the bush, fair prices, and work that holds up. Customers have trusted the shop for over twenty years, and new drivers who break down nearby get treated like family too.
