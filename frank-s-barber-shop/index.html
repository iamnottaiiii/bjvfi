The shop

One owner, one standard

Frank is the owner, and he is also your barber. That is the whole business model: the person who answers for the shop is the person in the chair with you.

Reviewers describe a very experienced barber who cuts all types of hair, a clean shop that is easy to get in and out of, and an atmosphere folks call respectable and friendly, the kind of place where women and children feel comfortable too.

The signature move is simple: he takes his time. Customers say Frank does a great job because he does not rush, and he aims to please. Pair that with good conversation and an honest price, and you get a shop people drive past three other barbershops to reach.
