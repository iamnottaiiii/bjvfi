Our Story

Built on Integrity, Honesty, and Loyalty

Leo's Construction Inc was founded in 1984 with a simple goal: serve the immediate community of neighbors. That goal has not changed. The company does not chase volume. It is not quantity but quality, and over the decades that focus built a lasting relationship of trust with customers across Brooklyn.

The owner holds a personal Special Riggers license issued by the NYC Department of Buildings, allowing the company to rig scaffolding on buildings of any number of stories. Every job is fully licensed and insured in New York State, and the company is registered with the Department of Consumer Affairs. Leo's has also been affiliated with HomeAdvisor since 2008, where customers rate the work 4.7 out of 5.
