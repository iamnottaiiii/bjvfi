About Potomac City Gutter Star

A local building materials store in Potomac, Maryland, focused on gutter products and the parts that go with them.

Gutters are one of the hardest working parts of a house, and one of the easiest to overlook. Potomac City Gutter Star exists for the people who do not overlook them: the contractors installing new runs, the homeowners tackling a repair, and anyone trying to keep water moving away from the foundation.

Instead of a little bit of everything, the focus here is narrow: gutter materials, guards, downspouts, and the fittings that make a system work. That focus means the stock on the shelf is the stock your project actually calls for.

Stop by the store in Potomac, or call ahead at (240) 819-1505 and we will help you put together a materials list before you make the trip.
