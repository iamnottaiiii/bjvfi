Our story

A family name on the door

Bud McCloskey & Sons is a family-run auto repair shop on Lombard Avenue in Holmes, Delaware County. The name on the sign is the person who greets you at the counter.

Small shop, long memory

This is the kind of shop where the owner knows your car and your name. Customers describe Bud as an awesome guy who always finds time to help, even when the bays are full.

The formula has not changed: diagnose it properly, price it fairly, and stand behind the work. Simple maintenance like oil changes gets the same care as bigger repair jobs and warranty work.

Drivers keep coming back because the experience is consistent. Honest, down to earth, reasonable. Those three words show up in review after review, and they are the reason the shop earns referrals across Holmes, Ridley Park, and the surrounding towns.
