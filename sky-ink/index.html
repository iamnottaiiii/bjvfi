Three quick steps, the way most first timers do it.

Talk through the idea, the size, and the placement. Bring any reference images you like.

Services and styles

The style wall

Styles the shop works in. Not sure which fits your idea? Call and ask, that is what the consult is for.

Black and grey

Soft shading, smooth gradients, and portraits with depth. A shop staple for larger pieces.

Fine line

Delicate, precise linework for minimalist designs, script, and small detailed pieces.

Traditional

Bold lines, solid color, classic flash motifs that age beautifully.

Blackwork

Heavy black coverage, geometric patterns, and ornamental designs with serious presence.

Script and lettering

Names, quotes, and dates in clean hand lettering, placed exactly where you want it.

Cover-ups and reworks

Faded or regretted tattoos reimagined. Bring the old piece in and talk options.
