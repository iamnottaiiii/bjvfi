Our story

Two decades in the stone business

Pesce Marble & Granite has served Greater Cincinnati since 2004. Owner Joe Pesce brings more than 25 years of experience in the natural stone industry to every template, every cut and every install.

We're a working fabrication shop, not a middleman. The people who help you pick your slab are the people who cut it and set it.

Our stone countertops can be found in households and businesses all across the tristate area, from kitchen vanities to full commercial projects.
