Our story

A breath of fresh air for New Haven homes.

FreshAir started with a simple idea: a cleaning service that treats your home like its own. Based on Huntington St in New Haven, we are a local crew, not a call center, and every home gets the same careful attention we would give our own front rooms.

We built our reputation on consistency. The same team shows up, the same checklist gets worked, and the same walkthrough closes out every visit. No surprises, no shortcuts, just a home that feels brand new when you walk through the door.

From weekly upkeep to once a year deep cleans, FreshAir is here to give New Haven back its weekends.
