Our story

A Milford shop built chair by chair

Local directories trace The Central Barbershop's start to 2014, when it opened on Central Street as a two-chair shop. Word got around, and it grew into the four-chair shop Milford knows today.

Classic by design

Old-school service with modern skill: scissor work, fades, and hot towel shaves in one shop.

Family friendly

Parents bring their kids, kids grow into regulars. Reviewers call it a perfect family-friendly shop.

Good company

80s and 90s rock on the speakers, a Bob Ross painting on the TV, and barbers who remember your name.
