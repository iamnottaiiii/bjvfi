How a shoot goes

From excavation to a shell
ready for tile and plaster

Every gunite job follows the same disciplined sequence. Here is the shape of it.

1

Excavation

The pool is dug to design depth and dimensions, with clean lines for forming.

2

Steel

Rebar is tied to spec, forming the structural cage the gunite bonds to.

3

Plumbing

Lines are set and pressure-tested before any concrete is shot.

4

Gunite

Concrete is pneumatically shot into place, sculpted, and finished true.

5

Cure

The shell cures to full strength, ready for tile, coping, and plaster.

Since 1994

A specialist, not a generalist

Gunite is a trade of its own. The nozzle work, the mix, the timing, the finishing: it takes years on the hose to get it right. Precision Gunite has spent more than three decades on exactly that, shooting pool shells and shotcrete across the San Fernando Valley and the greater Los Angeles area.

We work with homeowners planning a first pool, pool builders who need a reliable gunite crew, and commercial clients with structural shotcrete scopes. One trade, done with care, backed by a current California contractor license, workers compensation coverage, and a contractor surety bond.

Our yard and office are at 562 Glenoaks Blvd in San Fernando, where the trucks roll out to job sites across the region.
