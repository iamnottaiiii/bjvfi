Our Story

A small family business that treats you like a neighbor

S & R Construction Inc started in Sunland with a simple idea: be the contractor you would recommend to your own family. No runaround, no waiting around, no surprise line items.

Today the crew of about ten handles roofing, windows, HVAC, plumbing and remodeling across Sunland-Tujunga and the San Fernando Valley. Customers keep coming back for the same reasons: the crew shows up when promised, explains the options in plain language, and leaves the place cleaner than they found it.

Hire us once and you join the extended family, which is exactly how the business describes itself on its Thumbtack profile.
