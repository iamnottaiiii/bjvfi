About

Glendale's comfort crew

Armin Air Systems Inc is a general contractor focused on heating and air conditioning, serving homes and businesses across Glendale, California.

Our story

Comfort is a system, not a guess

Anyone can swap a part. We start with the whole picture: how your building holds heat, how air moves through it, and what your system was designed to do. That is how you get rooms that actually feel the same, bills that make sense, and equipment that lasts.

Based at 1545 N Verdugo Rd in Glendale, we work where we live. Our customers are neighbors, local businesses and property managers who need a contractor that shows up, explains the options in plain language, and stands behind the work.

Whether it is a same-day AC repair in August or a planned furnace replacement in October, the approach is the same: diagnose honestly, price clearly, and leave the place cleaner than we found it.

Honest diagnostics

Local and accountable
