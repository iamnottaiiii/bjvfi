Our story

Dr. Malcolm Morris and Artistic Dentistry

A Wexford dental practice built on the idea that great dentistry is equal parts science and art.

Training and background

Dr. Morris earned a chemistry degree from Washington and Jefferson College, graduated from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, and completed the Perio-Prosthetic program at the University of Pennsylvania. He went on to teach prosthodontics and occlusion at the University of Pittsburgh for nine years.

The bite, studied deeply

Occlusion, the art and science of bite pattern, is described as the most difficult part of any dental career. Dr. Morris studied it independently for twenty years, and that expertise shapes the practice's restorative work.

A practice since 2000

Artistic Dentistry opened its doors in Wexford in 2000. Patients in public reviews describe Dr. Morris's calm and friendly personality, and say he puts nervous patients at ease from the first office visit.
