Why the chimney matters

A fireplace is the heart of a home in winter, but only when the chimney above it is clear and sound. Creosote, nesting debris, and cracked flues turn a cozy evening into a hazard.

Regular sweeping and inspection keep the draft strong and the masonry sound. Marvels Fireplace and Chimney Service serves the Columbus area with careful, honest chimney work. Call to confirm services and pricing.
