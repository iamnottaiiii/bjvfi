The Shop

Remodeled,
recommitted

Loose ENDZ sits at 14595 S Western Ave in Gardena, and it does not look like a shop that is coasting. The room was recently remodeled, the stations are kept spotless, and the whole place carries a professional, good-energy atmosphere that reviewers notice the moment they walk in.

The team is small, about two to five barbers, and that is the point. This is a shop built on repeat customers: men who found their barber and keep coming back, parents who bring their kids, neighbors who recommend it by name. The cuts are the advertisement. It is usually busy, which regulars will tell you is the best review a barbershop can get.

The Craft

Serious about
the work

Fades, classic cuts, beard sculpting, hot towel shaves: the shop covers the full barbershop range, and reviewers consistently praise the skill behind the chair. One Booksy reviewer says his barber Tyrone gives him his best haircut, the kind of loyalty you cannot buy.

Prices stay reasonable, walk-ins are welcome when chairs are open, and the shop is known as a good place for kids. If you want the full experience without the wait, call (310) 323-7123 and lock in your appointment.
