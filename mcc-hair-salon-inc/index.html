Our Story

A working salon
for a working neighborhood

MCC Hair Salon Inc. sits on 11th Avenue in Brooklyn, the kind of salon where the chairs turn over fast, the prices stay low and the regulars keep coming back.

No frills. Just hair.

This is not a luxury spa, and it does not pretend to be one. It is a practical, high-volume neighborhood salon: walk in, get a good cut or color at a fair price, and get on with your day.

Online reviewers return to the same themes again and again: quick service, low prices and stylists who know their regulars. One reviewer on LocalityBiz credits stylist Winnie with "incredible haircuts" for the whole family.

"Quick, inexpensive and friendly, my stylist does incredible haircuts."

What to expect

Walk-ins are welcome. Appointments can be made by phone. If you are coming in for color or a chemical service, say exactly what you want, show a photo and confirm the price before the stylist begins. Clear communication makes for a better visit everywhere, and this salon is no exception.
