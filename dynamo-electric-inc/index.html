Our story

An electrician Granada Hills trusts with the panel open

Dynamo Electric Inc. has served the San Fernando Valley since 2004 as a licensed electrical contractor. The company handles both sides of the trade: careful residential service work for homeowners, and larger commercial projects including custom homes, multifamily buildings, and tenant improvements.

The reputation was built the slow way. Reviewers describe a lead electrician who answers questions plainly, keeps customers updated on arrival times, finds and fixes the real problem fast, and charges a fair price. That is why panel upgrades, EV charger installs, and landscape lighting are the jobs people call back for, and the jobs they recommend to neighbors.

Dynamo Electric Inc. holds California Electrical License 976255, active and in good standing, and is insured and bonded for residential and commercial work.
