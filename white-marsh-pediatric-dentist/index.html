Services

Pediatric dental services

Dental care designed for growing smiles. Call (443) 725-4185 with questions.

1

Checkups and cleanings

Gentle exams and cleanings sized for small mouths, with fluoride treatments to strengthen enamel.

2

Cavity care

Tooth-colored fillings and, when needed, crowns designed for baby teeth.

3

Sealants

Protective coatings on molars that help block cavities in hard-to-brush grooves.

4

Growth checkups

Monitoring how teeth and jaws develop, with early guidance if alignment needs attention.

5

Sports mouthguards

Custom-fitted guards that protect young athletes' teeth during play.

6

Emergency visits

Tooth injuries, pain, and swelling in kids get fast attention. Call (443) 725-4185.
