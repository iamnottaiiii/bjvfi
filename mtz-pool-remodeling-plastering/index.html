How We Work

Our Process

A typical remodel follows these steps. Timelines vary by pool size and scope.

1. Look & Listen

We visit, inspect the plaster, tile, and structure, and hear what you want from the pool.

2. Clear Plan

You get a written scope: what comes off, what goes on, and what it costs. No surprises.

3. Demo & Prep

Old plaster and tile come out. We fix what we find underneath, like exposed rebar, and prep the surface.

4. Tile & Coping

New waterline tile, trim, and coping go in first, set straight and sealed.

5. Fresh Plaster

The new finish is applied and troweled smooth, then we guide you through the curing start-up.

6. Fill & Enjoy

We walk the finished pool with you, answer questions, and hand it over ready to swim.

Example process. Your project plan is confirmed in writing before work begins.
