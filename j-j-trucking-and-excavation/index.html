Our story

A Burlington family,
a working fleet

J&J Trucking & Excavation started in 2016 with a simple idea: do the dirt work right, charge a fair price, and show up when you say you will.

Since 2016

Built truck
by truck

J & J Trucking & Excavating, LLC is run by Jay and Julie Kirby from their Burlington, Kentucky office on Regal Ridge Drive. What started as a small family operation has grown into a 14-truck fleet with 12 drivers, registered as an interstate motor carrier.

That growth came the honest way: excavation and hauling jobs done on schedule, at a decent price, with a crew willing to work around the customer's timeline. Most of our work comes from repeat callers and word of mouth across Boone County and Northern Kentucky.

We are contractors, not middlemen. Our drivers run our trucks, and the people you talk to on the phone are the people responsible for your job.
