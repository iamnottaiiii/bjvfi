Our story

The team drivers ask for by name.

Read the reviews for Gateway and two names keep showing up: Tom and Debbie. Tom runs the bodywork, Debbie keeps the front of the house friendly and the process simple. It is the kind of small shop where the person who fixed your car remembers your car.

Gateway Auto Sales is licensed as a body repair facility at 279 Young Street in Tonawanda, with the owner of record listed as Thomas A. Barnes in public state records. Local listings note inexpensive pricing, and customers back that up: quality paint and body work without the dealership markup.
