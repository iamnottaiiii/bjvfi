Our Story

Rooted in Sun Valley

Valley Paving Stone Inc. was founded and is led by Gilberto Rios, a proud local owner who believes your home’s exterior should be as inviting and customized as the interior.

With over 20 years of dedicated experience, we have mastered the art of transforming ordinary backyards and driveways into extraordinary, functional, beautiful landscapes. We are more than contractors. We are creators of outdoor living spaces where families build memories.
