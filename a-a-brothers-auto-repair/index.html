Our story

A&A Brothers Auto Repair is a family-run auto repair shop on Terminal Rd in Lorton, Virginia.

Who we are

A family shop,
not a chain

When you bring your car to A&A Brothers, you talk to the people who will actually work on it. No service advisors reading from a script, no upsell quotas, just mechanics who take pride in a job done right.

We are located on Terminal Rd in Lorton, convenient for drivers across Fairfax County and the surrounding Northern Virginia area. Whether it is a daily driver, a work truck or the family SUV, it gets the same careful attention.

Our philosophy is simple: explain the problem honestly, fix what needs fixing, and charge a fair price for it. That is how you earn a customer for life, one repair at a time.

What we stand for

Honest work, fair prices

We would rather talk you out of a repair you do not need than sell you one you do. That approach has kept our bays busy and our customers coming back, and we would not have it any other way.

Family run

The brothers are in the shop, on the lift, and behind the counter. You always know who is responsible for your car.

All makes welcome

Cars, trucks and SUVs, domestic and import. If it has wheels and an engine, we are happy to take a look.

Lorton local

Right on Terminal Rd, easy to reach from Lorton, Newington, Springfield and Woodbridge.
