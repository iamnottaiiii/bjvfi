Prep is the paint job.

Anyone can roll color onto a wall. The difference between paint that lasts two years and paint that lasts ten is everything that happens before the first coat.

A careful painter washes, scrapes, sands, caulks, and primes, because the finish is only as good as what is underneath it.

Before you hire any painter, make sure the quote spells out the prep work, the number of coats, the paint and primer to be used, and how long the job will take.
