Our story

A builder that answers to its name

American Builder Construction Company is a general contracting firm based at 6308 Woodman Ave in Van Nuys, serving Los Angeles County. Public listings describe the company's approach in plain terms: old-fashioned integrity and skill, applied to today's high standards in the construction business.

How the company works

  • The owner works directly with each client, from first call to final walkthrough.
  • Management stays involved in all project planning and administration.
  • The team is organized to avoid red tape, with decision makers hands-on at the job site.
  • Standardized procedures keep projects efficient and delivery on schedule.
  • Every client is treated as unique, with solutions tailored to their needs.

What the company is known for

  • Remodeling and new construction of residential buildings.
  • Commercial building construction and renovations.
  • Medium to high-end kitchen and bathroom remodeling.
  • Room additions and structural improvements.
  • Free estimates and warranty on completed work, per public listings.
One point of contact

No phone trees, no runaround. The owner guarantees complete satisfaction on every project, and picks up the phone when you call.
