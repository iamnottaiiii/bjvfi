About StoneCrest

A Buffalo roofing supply counter serving contractors, handymen and homeowners.

Supply for the Way Roofers Work

StoneCrest Contractors is a roofing supply store at 499 Hickory St in Buffalo, NY. We keep the materials local roofers reach for every day: shingles, metal panels, underlayment, gutters, flashing, fasteners and tools.

Buffalo roofs take a beating. Lake-effect snow loads, ice dams and spring freeze-thaw cycles mean the materials matter as much as the labor. Our counter stocks products suited for Western New York conditions, and we will tell you straight what works and what does not.

Contractors buy by the pallet. Homeowners buy by the bundle. Both get the same straight answers at our counter.
