About the shop

A Bay St fixture,
trusted for decades

The shop

Happy Cleaners has served Staten Island from 465 Bay St for decades. It is the kind of neighborhood dry cleaner where customers stay for twenty years and recommend it by name.

The work

Every garment is cleaned, pressed, and finished individually. No rushing, no cutting corners, just the careful work a good suit or a favorite dress deserves.

The people

Regulars describe friendly, no-problems service and alterations work they trust. Bring something in once and you will understand why they keep coming back.

In the same spot for over thirty years. That kind of staying power is earned, one garment at a time.
