The Full Prep method

Four steps, in this order, every time

Skipping steps is how paint jobs fail early. We do not skip steps.

1

Inspect

We walk the space with you and note every crack, stain, and soft spot before quoting.

2

Prep and repair

Patching, sanding, caulking, and masking. The longest phase, and the most important one.

3

Prime

The right primer for the surface, so the topcoat bonds and the color stays true.

4

Paint and walkthrough

Clean coats, careful cleanup, then a final walkthrough with you before we leave.
