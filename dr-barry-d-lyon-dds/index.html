Kids dental care

Choose your child's age group to see what a pediatric dental visit focuses on. Call the office to ask which services are currently offered.

First visits, made friendly

Early visits are about comfort and habit building: a gentle look at new teeth, guidance on brushing and diet, and answers to parents' questions about thumb sucking, bottles, and teething. The goal is a child who is happy to come back.

Growing smiles, protected

School-age visits focus on keeping every new permanent tooth healthy: regular cleanings, cavity checks, fluoride, and coaching on brushing and flossing as kids take over their own routines. Sports and sealants can be discussed with the office.

Confidence for the teen years

Teen visits keep an eye on alignment, wisdom teeth development, and the habits that affect a maturing smile, from sports drinks to orthodontic referrals when needed. Ask the office what is offered.

Meet the dentist

Dr. Barry D. Lyon, DDS

Dr. Barry D. Lyon received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland and has dedicated his career to pediatric dentistry. He practices at Pediatric Dentistry And Orthodontics Of Maryland in Glen Burnie.

He serves as a Clinical Associate Professor of Pediatric Dentistry at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, and is a past president of the Maryland Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the Maryland Society of Dentistry for Children.
