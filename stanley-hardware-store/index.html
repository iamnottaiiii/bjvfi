Our story

A corner of Bleecker Street where Ridgewood comes to fix things.

Stanley Hardware Store is an independent, neighborhood-run hardware store at 17-11 Bleecker St in Ridgewood, Queens. Walk in and you get what the big-box stores cannot give you: somebody who knows the stock, knows the building stock of this neighborhood, and will talk through the repair with you.

Reviewers keep mentioning the same things: friendly and helpful owners, fair prices, and a small shop that somehow has exactly what the project needs, from an electrical fix to a home renovation. That is the whole business model. Keep the shelves deep, the advice free, and the door open six days a week.

Ridgewood is a neighborhood of prewar buildings, garden apartments and fixer-uppers, and this store exists to keep them running: plumbing parts for old pipes, fasteners by the box, paint for the move-out touch-up, and tools for everything in between.
