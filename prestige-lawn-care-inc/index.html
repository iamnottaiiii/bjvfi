Our Story

A Cincinnati Crew with
a Reputation Earned Yard by Yard

Prestige Lawn Care Inc. is based on South Timberlane Drive in Cincinnati and has served local homes and businesses for years. Reviews of the company stretch back over a decade, and the pattern is consistent: customers keep coming back because the work gets done right, on time, without being asked twice.

The typical visit runs like clockwork. A small crew arrives with a truck and trailer, mows with large professional machines where the ground is open and push mowers where it is tight, then edges every sidewalk, driveway, and fence line with a trimmer before blowing the clippings clean. The owner bills at the end of the month, simple and predictable.

Today the company handles mowing and maintenance, shrub trimming and removal, seasonal work like spring mulching and fall leaf removal, snow plowing in winter, and recurring care for commercial properties.
