About us

Veteran owned, client loved

Cosmopolitan Salon and Spa is a veteran owned full service salon offering Syracuse world class hair and nail services at an affordable rate.

One roof, everything

Beauty salon, barber shop, nail salon, beauty supply, and spa all located under one roof. Get your hair, nails and brows done in a single visit.

A team that pitches in

Clients keep mentioning the teamwork: the owners are super nice and everyone helps take care of each customer. Walk in as a guest, leave as a regular.

Calm on purpose

To keep a relaxing and peaceful environment for everyone, we ask that clients not bring young children while getting services. Thank you for your cooperation.

Easy to book

Online booking with live prices, or walk right in. Appointments are recommended on weekends, but the team is famous for squeezing people in.
