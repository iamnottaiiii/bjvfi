The problem

Your pull-down stairs are an open window in your ceiling

Warm air rises, and in most homes it rises straight through the gaps around the attic stairs. National energy publications have long listed the attic entrance as one of the worst air leaks in a house, right up there with drafty windows and doors.

Draft Cap's answer is the Atticap: an insulated cover that sits over your attic stairs or hatch from above, sealing the opening and keeping conditioned air where it belongs. It is a simple fix with an outsized effect on comfort and heating bills.

"One of the least expensive ways to cut a major source of home energy loss starts at the attic entrance."

Our story

A Lincoln company built around one good idea

Draft Cap is based at 279 Cambridge Turnpike in Lincoln, Massachusetts, and its name comes from its product: a cap for drafts. The company manufactures the Atticap, an insulated cover for attic stairs and hatches, and works as an insulation contractor for the homes around it.

The Atticap has been recommended in national energy publications as a fix for attic air leaks, listed alongside the best-known attic entrance products in the country. The idea is simple: the hole in your ceiling deserves the same insulation as the rest of it.

"Seal the attic entrance first. It is the cheapest square footage of insulation you will ever add."

As an insulation contractor, Draft Cap pairs the product with the trade work that surrounds it: air sealing, blown-in insulation, and upgrades that turn a drafty attic into a sealed, comfortable part of the house.
