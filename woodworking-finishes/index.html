What we do

Wood surfaces that look new again

From tired kitchen cabinets to a scratched family table, we strip, stain, and seal wood so it earns its place in your home again.

Cabinet refinishing

We strip tired finishes, sand to a smooth base, and spray a hard, furniture-grade enamel or stain that stands up to daily kitchen life.

Staining and clear coats

Rich custom stains plus lacquer, polyurethane, or oil finishes that protect doors, trim, built-ins, and tabletops for years.

Furniture refinishing

Heirloom tables, chairs, and sideboards get careful stripping, repair, and a finish that honors the wood instead of hiding it.

How it works

From worn wood to finished, in four steps

A simple process that keeps you in the loop from the first call to the final walkthrough.

1. Free estimate

We visit, look at the wood, talk through finish options, and give you a written price on the spot.

2. Color samples

We brush real stain samples on your actual wood so you see the true color in your light before we commit.

3. Prep and finish

Careful sanding, grain filling where needed, then stain and multiple topcoats with proper cure time.

4. Walkthrough

We inspect every surface together and clean up fully. You get simple care tips to keep the finish beautiful.

About

A finish-first shop in Stoneham

Woodworking finishes is a Stoneham, MA finishing service built on a simple belief: the wood in your home deserves the same care as the day it was installed. We focus on the final surface, the part you see and touch every day, and we take the time to get it right.

Craft over speed

We schedule fewer jobs at a time so each piece gets full attention: proper dry times, level topcoats, and no shortcuts.

Materials we trust

Professional-grade stains, conversion varnish, lacquer, and marine finishes matched to each project, never one-size-fits-all.

Straight answers

If a piece is better replaced than refinished, we'll tell you. We'd rather earn your trust than sell you a job you don't need.
