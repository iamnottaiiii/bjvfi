Our story

Built for the way you live

Ace Mobile Mechanic LLC launched in 2026 with one belief: most car repairs do not need a waiting room. Based at 829 Mason St in Schenectady, we bring tools, diagnostics, and parts straight to your driveway.

The traditional shop model wastes your time: towing, shuttles, half days off work. A mobile mechanic flips that. You keep your schedule, and the work happens where your car already sits.

Every job starts with a confirmed quote and a plain-language explanation. No surprise line items, no pressure. If something is not worth fixing, you will hear it from us first.
