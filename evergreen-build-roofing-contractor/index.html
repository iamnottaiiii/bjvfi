What we do

Roofing services, start to finish

From a single leak to a full replacement, plus the materials to back it up. Select a service to learn more, then call for a quote.

Roof Repair

Small problems become big ones fast when water is involved. We track down the source of leaks and damage and repair it properly, matching materials to your existing roof.

  • Leak detection and leak repair
  • Missing, cracked, or curling shingles and tiles
  • Flashing, vent, and skylight sealing
  • Storm and wind damage repair
Call about a repair

Roof Replacement

When repairs no longer make sense, a full replacement gives you a fresh start. We remove the old roof down to the deck, repair what is underneath, and install a complete new system.

  • Full tear-off and re-roofing
  • Asphalt shingle, tile, and flat roof systems
  • Underlayment and deck repair
  • Ventilation and flashing upgrades
Call for an estimate

Roof Inspections

Buying a home, filing an insurance claim, or just want peace of mind? An inspection gives you a clear picture of your roof's condition, in plain language, with photos.

  • Pre-purchase and real estate inspections
  • Storm damage assessments
  • Maintenance checkups to extend roof life
Schedule an inspection

Materials & Supply

  • Underlayment, flashing, and vents
  • Sealants, coatings, and repair products
  • Advice on picking the right product for the job

Our story

Rooted in Glendora,
raised on good roofs

Evergreen Build Roofing Contractor is a local roofing contractor and supply store on N Glendora Ave, built around a simple idea: do the job right and stand behind it.

Who we are

A contractor you can call,
a counter you can visit

Evergreen Build combines two things Glendora homeowners and roofers need: a contractor who shows up and does careful work, and a supply store stocked with real roofing materials.

Because we both sell and install roofing materials, we know exactly what goes on your roof, how it performs in Southern California weather, and how to fix it when something goes wrong.

Our approach is straightforward: honest inspections, clear options, clean job sites, and communication from the first call to the final walkthrough.

How we work

No pressure,
no mystery

Here is what to expect when you work with us:

  • Inspect first. We look at the whole roof, not just the obvious spot, and show you what we find.
  • Explain options. Repair versus replace, material choices, and what each costs, in plain terms.
  • Do clean work. Protected landscaping, tidy tear-offs, and a final walkthrough with you.

A good roof is quiet. You never think about it, and that is the point.
