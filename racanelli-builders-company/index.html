Good to know

Before you call

Have a rough scope ready. Even a sentence helps: "replace the driveway," "remodel the kitchen," "build a block wall."

Photos help. A few phone photos of the area let the builder come prepared.

Ask about permits. Structural work, additions and most remodels in Glendale need permits. A good contractor handles the paperwork.
