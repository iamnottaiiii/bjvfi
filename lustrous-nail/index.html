Our story

A small salon with
a steady hand

Lustrous Nail opened on Avenue U in Gravesend with a simple idea: slow down and do nails properly. The space is freshly renovated and kept spotless, with massaging pedicure chairs and a calm, unhurried feel that regulars notice the moment they walk in.

Guests describe the salon as appointment-only by design, so each visit gets real time and attention rather than a rushed turnaround. Technicians are praised for being detail oriented, checking the shape, length and color with you before anything is finished, and for helping indecisive clients find the perfect shade from a wide color wall.

It is the kind of place where people become regulars: guests write about leaving with exactly what they asked for, nails that last for weeks, and a team they trust with their hands and feet month after month.
