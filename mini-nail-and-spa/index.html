our story

A neighborhood salon
built on small kindnesses

Mini Nail & Spa sits on 18th Ave in Bensonhurst, the kind of block where shop owners know their regulars. That is the whole idea behind this salon: a place where getting your nails done feels easy, personal and unhurried.

a

Patience first

Techs take their time with shaping and detail, especially for guests who know exactly how they like their nails. Short natural nails get the same care as long extensions.

b

Clean you can see

Reviewers mention the tidy, comfortable space again and again. Tools, stations and pedicure chairs are kept spotless, visit after visit.

c

Prices that respect you

This is a salon for the neighborhood, not a luxury markup. Fair pricing on gel, acrylics and spa pedicures, with no surprises at the register.
