About the salon

Queen Street's neighborhood nail salon

Queen Nails is a locally loved nail salon at 853 Queen St in Southington, Connecticut. Guests describe a warm, calming space where the team takes genuine care: no rushing, honest advice on designs, and nails finished exactly the way you pictured them.

Chi, one of the salon's most praised technicians, is named in review after review for her patience, speed, and eye for detail. Walk-ins are welcome, parking is free in the lot, and credit cards are accepted.

  • Spotless stations and sanitized tools
  • Walk-ins welcome · free lot parking
