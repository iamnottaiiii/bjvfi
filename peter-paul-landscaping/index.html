What we do

Landscaping services
for Boston homes

Straightforward yard work, done carefully. If it grows, spreads, piles up, or needs a clean edge, it is probably on this list.

  1. Lawn mowing and upkeep

    Routine mowing, string trimming around fences and foundations, and a full cleanup pass so the lawn looks finished, not just cut.

  2. Garden beds and planting

    Bed cleanup and reshaping, planting flowers, shrubs, and perennials suited to New England, plus weeding to keep beds looking intentional.

  3. Shrub and hedge trimming

    Shaping overgrown shrubs and hedges back into clean lines, and cutting back perennials so beds stay open and healthy.

  4. Seasonal cleanups

    Spring cleanups that clear winter debris and wake the yard up, and fall cleanups that handle leaves, branches, and putting beds to bed.

  5. Mulch and edging

    Fresh mulch to hold moisture and keep weeds down, with crisp edges along walks, driveways, and beds for a sharp finished look.

  6. General yard care

    The odds and ends that keep a property tidy: debris removal, small brush clearing, and one-off help when the yard gets ahead of you.

Good to know: the services above are typical landscaping services. Call (617) 242-5406 to confirm availability for your project and to talk through pricing for your yard.

How we work

Careful work beats fast work

Landscaping is detail work. A lawn is not really mowed until the edges are trimmed and the clippings are gone. A bed is not really mulched until the edge is clean and the depth is even.

That is the standard we hold every visit to, whether it is a weekly mow or a full spring cleanup. The details are the difference between a yard that looks "done" and a yard that looks cared for.

  • Every visit ends with a full cleanup pass
  • Straight talk about what your yard actually needs
  • Respect for your property, your neighbors, and your time
