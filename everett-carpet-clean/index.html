About us

A family shop on Main St, Everett

Everett Carpet Clean is a family-owned carpet and rug cleaning business at 340 Main St in Everett, MA. We are the kind of shop where the people behind the counter know rugs the way a tailor knows suits.

Our reviewers say it best: "great people, great service," and one customer told us they only trust us to clean their oriental rugs. That trust is earned one rug at a time, with careful hand washing, honest pricing, and a turnaround of just 3 to 4 days on area rugs.

Bring your rugs to the shop and save 10% when you drop off and pick up yourself. Or call us and we will come to you, anywhere in Everett and the surrounding towns.
