About

J.H. Construction and Renovation is a general contractor on Main St in Landisburg, PA, serving Perry County and the surrounding communities.

The short version:
we build things right.

J.H. Construction and Renovation is a small, local general contractor based at 208 Main St in Landisburg. We handle renovations, additions, and general construction work for homes and businesses.

We are the kind of company where the person who answers the phone is involved in the work. That keeps communication simple and accountability clear: one contractor, one plan, one finished job you are proud of.

Landisburg sits in the heart of Perry County, and we are glad to be part of it. When neighbors recommend us, it is the best advertising there is, and we work to earn it on every job.

Do it once, do it right, and leave the place better than you found it.

How we work, every job

Straight communication

Clean, careful job sites

Your home is your home, even mid-project. We treat it that way.

Local roots

Based in Landisburg, working across Perry County and nearby towns.

What to expect
working with us

1. A conversation. You call, we talk. Tell us what you want, what is bothering you about the space, and what you are hoping for.

2. A plan. We look at the project, talk through options, and lay out scope and sequence in plain language.

3. The work. We show up, do the job carefully, keep the site tidy, and keep you in the loop.

4. The walk-through. We go over the finished work together and make sure you are satisfied before we call it done.
