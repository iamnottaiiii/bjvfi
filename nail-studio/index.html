Our story

Victor’s mall nail stop

A fixture at Eastview

Nail Studio has served the Victor area from inside Eastview Mall for years, welcoming everyone from mall shoppers squeezing in a polish change to loyal regulars who have trusted the same technicians for over a decade.

The formula is straightforward: a huge color library, careful technique and strict cleanliness, with fresh tools for every guest and disposable pedicure liners as standard.

What guests value most

Longtime customers praise the meticulous, consistent work, especially from veteran technicians, and say their manicures last longer here than at other salons they have tried. The spa pedicures, with organic sugar scrub and a cooling ice mask, get special mention as a real treat.

Walk in during a shopping trip or book ahead for the weekend. Either way, the color wall is waiting.
