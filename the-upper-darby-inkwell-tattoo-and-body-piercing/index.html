Practical notes

Before you visit

The studio keeps things simple and honest. A few practical notes reviewers pass along to first timers:

  • Walk-ins are welcome during shop hours, appointments recommended for larger pieces.
  • The shop is cash only, with an ATM on site. A Wawa sits across the street.
  • Bring a valid photo ID. Eat beforehand and bring your reference images.
  • The piercer is generally available Wednesday through Saturday.
  • Questions by email: udinkwell@gmail.com.

The artists

Reviewers call them out by name, so we will too. A family crew with specialties across ink and steel.

The script hand

Trinity

Hand drawn names and dates with beautiful, precise linework. Kind, patient, and praised for seeing clients right away. A favorite for memorial and script pieces.

The piercer

Stacy

The calm voice of the piercing room. Thorough explanations, great follow up, and a gentle hand that talks first timers through every step. Generally in Wednesday through Saturday.

The back piece man

Large scale custom work, including back pieces outlined in a single relaxed sitting. Met at local tattoo conventions and worth the drive to the shop.
