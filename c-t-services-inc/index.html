What Gets Checked Sample

A typical inspection visit covers the systems below. Exact checklist items vary by vehicle, so call ahead to confirm what your inspection will include.

Brakes and Tires

Pads, rotors, lines, tread depth, and tire condition are measured and recorded.

Lights and Signals

Headlights, brake lights, turn signals, and hazard flashers are tested one by one.

Steering and Suspension

Play in the wheel, shocks, struts, and linkage are checked for wear.

Fluids and Leaks

Oil, coolant, brake fluid, and transmission fluid levels are reviewed for leaks.
