Family owned

Built by family,
for community

Paradise Desserts is owned and run by husband and wife Michael and Nneka Muhammad, together with their daughter Tasnim. What began with one bean pie recipe, taught by Nneka 25 years ago, has grown into a South Side dessert destination.

Their mission is simple: serve the community wholesome, made-from-scratch desserts, and keep growing, together.

Our story

One bean pie,
a whole paradise

Twenty-five years ago, Nneka Muhammad taught her husband Michael to make a bean pie. That recipe became a family business, and that business became a South Side landmark.

The beginning

It started with a recipe

"My wife actually taught me how to make the bean pie 25 years ago," says co-owner Michael Muhammad. That bean pie, offered in flavors from classic to apple, cheese, caramel, blueberry cream, and strawberry banana, became the signature of Paradise Desserts.

From there the family expanded into cakes, cookies, and butternut squash pie, always made from scratch, always made with love.

"Taste of Heaven on Earth"
Community

Doing for self,
serving the South Side

In October 2023, Paradise Desserts held its grand opening as the on-site bakery at Brewer Coffee and Custard in Auburn Gresham, bringing their desserts together with Brewer's coffee and frozen custard. People traveled from all around the city for the celebration.

Today the family serves the community from their own shop on Halsted Street in Roseland. As daughter Tasnim Muhammad puts it: "Our goal is really to be of service in our community. We just want to keep growing and do more and more for our community."

The family shares their journey on Instagram at @paradisedessertsllc.
