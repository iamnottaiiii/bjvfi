Our story

The man behind the wrench

Hi, I am Steve.

Electric City Motors Inc is an American owned and operated small business on State Street in Schenectady. I am a Ford dealer trained technician, and I have been working on cars for over 20 years.

This is a one man operation on purpose. When you drop off your car, you talk to me. When I find the problem, I explain it to you. When the work is done, it is done by my hands. There is no service writer translating, no commission on parts, and no mystery on the bill.

1 Owner, technician, and the person who answers the phone

Honest work, fair prices

My customers say it better than I can: easy drop off, efficient service, and they have never felt ripped off here. Women drivers especially tell me they trust this shop, and that trust is the thing I am proudest of.
