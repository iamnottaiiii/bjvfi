Services

Every surface, handled with care.

From a single accent wall to a full home refresh, Fine Painters covers interior and exterior work for Philadelphia homes. Call (215) 906-3299 for a free estimate on any project below.

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, closets, and hallways. Includes minor drywall patching, sanding, caulking, and two finish coats with premium low odor paint. Furniture is moved and covered, floors are protected, and we clean up daily.

Popular for: bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, bathrooms, hallways.

Exterior Painting

Siding, brick, stucco, trim, shutters, and porch ceilings. We pressure wash, scrape, prime bare wood, and apply weather resistant coatings built for Philly summers and winters.

Cabinet Refinishing

A budget friendly alternative to replacement. Cabinets are degreased, sanded, primed, and sprayed or brushed to a smooth, durable finish.

Trim, Doors, and Molding

Baseboards, crown molding, window and door casings painted with crisp lines for a sharp, finished look.

Drywall Patching and Repair

Nail pops, cracks, holes, and water stains repaired and textured to blend with the surrounding wall.

Color Consultation

Stuck between three shades of white? We help you narrow choices, test swatches in your actual light, and pick finishes (flat, eggshell, satin, semi gloss) that fit each room. Every estimate starts with a free, no obligation visit. Call (215) 906-3299 today.

Our story

A neighborhood painter with a simple promise.

Fine Painters is a painting contractor based in West Philadelphia, at 437 S 49th St. We work in homes across the city, bringing the same care to a single bedroom that we bring to a full exterior.

Our philosophy is straightforward: show up on time, protect your home like it is our own, prep every surface properly, and leave the space cleaner than we found it. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and word of mouth in the neighborhood.

When you call (215) 906-3299, you talk to the people who will actually do the work. We would be glad to earn your trust, one wall at a time.
