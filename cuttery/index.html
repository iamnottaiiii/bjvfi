Our story

Four decades on South Main

Cuttery has been part of North Syracuse for 40 years. That kind of staying power does not happen by accident. It happens one great haircut at a time, one loyal client at a time, until a salon becomes a landmark.

"Experienced stylists, unmatched professionalism, and a place that feels like home."

Owner Joanne Stanzione and the team have built the salon around a simple idea: do excellent work, treat people warmly, and they will keep coming back. Clients ask for their stylists by name, and many have been in the chair for years.

Today the salon offers hair, skincare, and makeup services in one welcoming space, and the next generation of North Syracuse is already discovering why their parents never went anywhere else.

The team

Stylists clients ask for by name

Joanne, Stacie, Kim, and the rest of the crew bring decades of combined experience behind the chair. New to the salon? Tell us what you are looking for and we will match you with the right stylist.
