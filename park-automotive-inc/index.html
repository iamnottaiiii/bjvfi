Our Story

The shop Round Lake recommends to friends

Est. 2005

Rooted in Round Lake

Park Automotive Inc has been repairing cars and trucks on Ushers Rd for two decades. Owner Brian Hicks and the crew built the shop on a simple promise: diagnose accurately, explain clearly, and only fix what is necessary.

That promise shows up in the reviews again and again. Customers describe a clean, organized shop, fair pricing, and a team that treats every car like it belongs to family.

Our standard

How every job goes

1. Listen. We hear your concern and ask the right questions.

2. Diagnose. We find the real cause before recommending work.

3. Explain. You get clear options and honest pricing.

4. Repair. Quality parts, careful work, road-tested results.
