Our story

Forty years of green thumbs in New Cumberland.

Gordon's Landscaping LLC is a family run landscaping business based at 733 Fishing Creek Rd in New Cumberland, Pennsylvania. Owner Dwayne Gordon and his crew have been caring for local lawns and landscapes since 1986, earning an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau along the way.

It's a simple formula that has worked for four decades: show up, do the work right, and charge a fair price. Most of the crew's work comes from repeat customers and neighbors who recommend them down the street.

The Gordon's way

Show up. Do it right. Charge fair.

Reviewers describe Dwayne and his crew as efficient and very reasonable. That's the reputation the business has built one yard at a time since 1986, and it's the standard every job is held to.
