Our story

Built on the block we serve.

Taj Mahal Contracting Corp works out of 719 Coney Island Ave in Brooklyn, in the same neighborhood as many of its clients. The company is listed as a minority business enterprise certified firm and describes its mission plainly: providing construction, consultation, and management for real estate firms, developers, and owners.

Past projects range from small framing jobs to six figure masonry contracts covering parapet work, grinding and pointing, and fire scraping. Reviewers most often mention home renovations and masonry work done by courteous crews who kept them in the loop.
