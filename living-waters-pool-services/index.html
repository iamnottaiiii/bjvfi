The weekly ritual

A pool is only fun when the water is right.

Leaves, dust, sunscreen, Santa Ana winds. An Arcadia pool collects all of it, and chemistry drifts a little more every day it is ignored. That is why weekly care matters more than any single deep clean.

Our technicians show up on schedule, test and balance the water, clear the baskets and skimmers, brush the walls, and check the equipment. You just get to swim.

What weekly service includes

Every visit covers

  • Water testing and chemical balancing
  • Skimming, brushing, and vacuuming as needed
  • Skimmer and pump basket emptying
  • Equipment check: pump, filter, heater, timer
  • A quick report on anything that needs attention

Who we are

Arcadia pools are our whole world.

Living Waters Pool Services is a swimming pool contractor based in Arcadia, California, with over 30 years of experience caring for residential and commercial pools throughout the area.

We started with a simple idea: a pool should be the easiest part of your week, not a chore. So we built our service around consistency. Same day, same thorough checklist, same clear water, week after week.
