Services

How a RentFlow project moves

Select a phase to see what happens at each stage. Phase descriptions are illustrative. sample call to confirm

01 - Site Sourcing

We look for parcels where location, zoning, and demand line up. Target areas, off market outreach, and broker relationships feed the pipeline.

  • Market and submarket screening
  • Zoning and access review
  • Initial pricing conversations

02 - Feasibility and Underwriting

Before anything is drawn, the numbers have to work. We pressure test rents, costs, and timelines against conservative assumptions.

  • Pro forma modeling
  • Environmental and title diligence
  • Go or no go decision gate

03 - Design and Approvals

Architectural concepts become permit ready drawings while entitlements move through local review.

  • Schematic and design development
  • Entitlement and permit coordination
  • Community and stakeholder input

04 - Construction

Managed construction with clear milestones, budget tracking, and regular progress reporting.

  • Contractor selection and bidding
  • Schedule and budget management
  • Quality inspections at each milestone

05 - Delivery and Lease Up

The building turns over ready to occupy, with leasing or sale execution handled to the finish line.

  • Final inspections and punch list
  • Leasing or disposition strategy
  • Transition to long term management
