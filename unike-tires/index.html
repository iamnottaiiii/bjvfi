About

01

Unike Tires is a locally run tire shop at 5614 Ridge Ave in Cincinnati, serving the Pleasant Ridge corridor and drivers across the east side of the city.

The shop describes itself as Cincinnati's destination for both new and used tires, with an emphasis on value: used tires starting at $50, fittings for rims up to 30 inches, and service from technicians who do this all day, every day.

Because tire trouble rarely happens on a schedule, the shop also advertises 24 hour towing and roadside assistance, seven days a week.
