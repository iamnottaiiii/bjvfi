Our story

A small team doing honest home work

A Team Home Improvement has been serving the greater Connecticut area from its Brooklyn location since 2003. Owner Lee Williams leads a small crew of one to four people, the kind of size where the person you talk to is the person who shows up.

Local roots

Based at 95 South Main Street in Brooklyn, CT. We work where we live, and our reputation travels by word of mouth.

Small by design

No layers, no call centers. You call, you talk to the team, and the team does the work.

Two trades, one call

Listed for handyman work and carpet cleaning, so one visit can handle repairs and freshen floors.

Why neighbors keep our number

Most of our work comes from repeat customers and referrals. Show up on time, charge what you said, clean up after yourself. It is not complicated, and we do it every visit.
