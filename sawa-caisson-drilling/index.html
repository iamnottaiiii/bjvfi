Why SAWA

Foundations are forever. Drill them right.

A caisson is only as good as the hole it fills. We drill to the bearing stratum your engineer specifies, keep the shaft clean and plumb, and pour it full.

PLUMB

Shafts drilled true to line and grade, verified before the cage goes in.

CLEAN

Bottoms cleaned of loose material so concrete bears on solid ground, not muck.

FULL

Continuous concrete placement with proper slump, no voids, no shortcuts.

Services

Drilling services, spec by spec

Representative scope below. Final diameters, depths, and methods are set by your engineer and soils report. Call to confirm what your site needs.

01

Foundation caisson drilling

Straight-shaft drilled piers for new homes, additions, and commercial structures. Drilled to the bearing stratum, cased where soils cave, poured full with reinforced concrete.

 TYPICAL
18-36 IN DIA
02

Auger-cast piles

Continuous-flight auger piles for sites where vibration and noise must stay low. Grout or concrete pumped through the auger as it withdraws.

 LOW VIBRATION
03

Retaining and shoring piers

Soldier-pile and lagging support for cuts, basement excavations, and hillside lots common around Pasadena.

 HILLSIDE READY
04

Limited-access drilling

Compact rigs for backyards, side yards, and slopes where full-size equipment cannot reach. Tight gates and steep grades are our normal.

 TIGHT SITES
05

Test borings and layout support

Pilot holes and verification drilling ahead of foundation work, plus layout support so your piers land where the plans say.

About

The crew under your foundation

SAWA Caisson Drilling is a foundation drilling contractor based at 3030 E Colorado Blvd in Pasadena, California, serving homeowners, builders, and engineers across the San Gabriel Valley.

SPECIALIST

Drilling is all we do. No framing, no concrete flatwork on the side. One trade, done properly.

ENGINEER-FIRST

We drill to your soils report and foundation plan, and we flag it when field conditions differ from the paper.

SITE-READY

Full-size rotary rigs and compact machines, so hillside lots and tight backyards are both in range.

Field standards

What good drilling looks like

A

Dry, stable shafts

We manage groundwater and caving soils with casing and proper technique instead of hoping for the best.

B

Clean bottoms

Loose material is removed before concrete goes in, so every pier bears on competent ground.

C

Documented depths

Each shaft is logged. Your engineer gets records that match the field, not estimates from memory.
