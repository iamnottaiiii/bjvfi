Our Story

A welcoming shop that feels like family

Bousso African Hair Braiding is a beauty salon on Montgomery Avenue in Staten Island, specializing in traditional African hair braiding techniques.

Customers describe a warm, family-like atmosphere with fast, courteous service. The stylists are known for knowledgeable advice, including helping parents choose the right hair products for children with allergies, and for braids that come out neat, comfortable, and beautiful.

Many clients say they leave satisfied and eager to return, making it a go-to spot for braids, locs, and protective styles in the neighborhood.
