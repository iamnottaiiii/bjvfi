Our promise

The walk-through is where the clean begins

We start every job the same way: we walk through your space, listen to what bothers you, and agree on exactly what a great result looks like. No surprises, no rushed corners, no checklist you never asked for.

Being a family business means every visit carries our name. From that first walk-through to project completion, the same care goes into a studio apartment and a full commercial contract. We bring the equipment, the supplies, and the patience to do it right.

Carpets get real steam and shampoo, tile floors get stripped and re-waxed the proper way, and upholstery gets cleaned without harsh shortcuts. If a stain can be saved, we will tell you honestly, and if it cannot, we will tell you that too.

Our story

A family name on every clean since 2003

Andrade Cleaning started as a family business with a simple idea: put the customer first, every single time, and the rest takes care of itself. More than twenty years later, that is still how we work.

We are not a franchise and we are not a call center. When you book with us, you get people who treat your home like their own and your business like it matters, because to us it does. From the initial walk-through to project completion, we listen closely and then we deliver.

That reputation for outstanding customer service is what keeps our schedule full: neighbors who call us back year after year, offices that trust us with their keys, and new customers who heard about us from the people down the street.

We carry general liability insurance and workers comp, we accept projects of all sizes, and we serve homes and businesses across Massachusetts from our base in Saugus.
