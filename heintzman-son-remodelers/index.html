About

A Family Name on the Work

Heintzman & Son Remodelers is a general contracting business based at 410 Pike St in Reading, Ohio, serving homeowners across the greater Cincinnati area.

Public building-permit records tie Heintzman remodeling work to bathroom remodels, finished basements, garage additions, and residential alterations in Cincinnati, Milford, West Chester, and Liberty Township, with permitted project valuations ranging from a few thousand dollars to $60,000.

How we work

  • Measure first: every project starts with field-verified dimensions, not guesses.
  • Permitted work: jobs are pulled and inspected through the local building department.
  • Direct line: one call reaches the shop during business hours.
