The work

One trade, done right

A chimney sweep removes creosote, soot, and blockages from the flue so smoke drafts properly and heat goes where it belongs. It is straightforward work that protects your home.

Why sweep

Fire safety starts in the flue

Fire safety agencies recommend having chimneys inspected every year and swept as needed. A neglected flue collects creosote, and creosote is fuel for chimney fires.

  • ✓Lower fire risk. Removing creosote takes away the fuel a chimney fire needs.
  • ✓Better draft. A clear flue pulls smoke up and out instead of into your living room.
  • ✓Early warning. A sweep spots cracked liners, missing caps, and masonry wear before they turn costly.
  • ✓Cleaner burn. A well-drafting fireplace burns hotter and wastes less wood.
