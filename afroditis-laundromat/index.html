Our Story

A neighborhood laundromat on Saratoga Street, serving East Boston with care since 1988.

Since 1988

A Saratoga Street staple

Afroditis Laundromat has been part of the Day Square neighborhood for decades. Business records list its start in 1988, and it remains a small, owner-run shop where the staff know the regulars and the regulars know the staff.

Reviewers call out team members by name, Sofia, Lydia, Dalia, Maria, and describe their work as an art: careful, attentive, and finished well. That is the reputation the laundromat has earned one load at a time.

Come for the machines, stay for the people. Open every day, 7 AM to 9 PM, right on the corner where Eastie does its laundry.
