New Traffic Signal Construction

Our Story

A family trade on Maryland roads since 1983.

Scott A Duncan Inc started in 1983 as a Maryland traffic signal contractor, and it has stayed in that lane ever since. Based at 7529 Old Coaling Road in Harmans, the company builds and maintains the signalized intersections drivers pass through every day.

Our main line of business has always been traffic control systems and devices. Agencies across the state rely on specialists who know the specs cold: foundation depths, conduit schedules, phasing diagrams, and the inspection routines that prove the work.

Prequalified and proven

Scott A Duncan Inc appears on Baltimore County prequalified contractor lists and has performed work for the Maryland State Highway Administration, including signal installations on state routes. Public work demands documentation, safety records, and crews that show up ready. That is the business we built.

What keeps us busy

New intersections where development meets the state highway system. Aging signals brought up to modern LED and pedestrian standards. Trail crossings and school zones made safer with beacons and flashers.

The crew standard

A signal that works right is invisible to drivers, and that is the point. Every head aligned, every wire labeled, every foundation poured to spec, and the intersection turned on without drama. Four decades of that discipline is why the phone keeps ringing.
