About Creative Hair Factory


A local shop for classic and modern cuts in Los Angeles. Creative Hair Factory focuses on the details: clean lines, even fades, and a chair you will not want to leave.


Every appointment starts with a consultation: what you like, what you do not, and how much upkeep you want. The result is a cut that looks sharp on day one and grows out clean.


Find us at 1705 W El Segundo Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90047, or call (323) 777-9911 to book your chair.
