Our story

A Brooklyn demolition company built on finished jobs

D B Demolition Inc is a demolition contractor based at 1875 Ralph Ave in Brooklyn, NY. We take on the jobs other crews avoid: full building teardowns, tight-lot demolitions, and selective strip-outs where precision matters as much as power.

Our recent project history includes general construction and site-work permits filed in New York City, covering sidewalk and driveway replacement, concrete walkways, curbs, guardrails, and pavers. That range, from heavy demolition to finished concrete, means one crew can take your site from standing structure to clean, build-ready lot.

Clients describe working with our crew as easy and pleasant, and recommend us without hesitation. We plan to keep it that way: clear quotes, kept schedules, and a site left cleaner than we found it.
