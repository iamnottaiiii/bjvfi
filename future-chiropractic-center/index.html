Services

What the practice treats, drawn from its published practice description. Tap a tab to explore.

Headache care

A stated specialty of this practice. Chiropractic care aims at the neck and spinal sources that feed recurring headaches, working to reduce both frequency and intensity over time.

  • Evaluation of headache patterns and triggers
  • Neck-focused adjustments
  • Ongoing care plans built around your response

Neck pain care

Stiff, aching, or sharp neck pain gets direct attention here. Treatment targets restricted joints and the surrounding muscle tension that keeps pain coming back.

  • Gentle cervical adjustments
  • Care for stiffness from posture, strain, or injury
  • Guidance for between-visit relief

Upper and lower back pain

From the shoulders down to the lower back, spinal adjustments work to restore motion and ease the ache of everyday strain, overuse, and injury.

  • Thoracic and lumbar adjustments
  • Care for chronic and acute back pain
  • Plans shaped by 30+ years of clinical experience

Extremity conditions

Beyond the spine, the practice treats extremity complaints with the same hands-on approach.

  • Carpal tunnel syndrome
  • Foot and ankle conditions
  • Shoulder injuries

Dr. Miranda is a 1982 graduate of Logan College of Chiropractic in St. Louis, Missouri, and brings more than 30 years of chiropractic experience to his patients in Sewell.
