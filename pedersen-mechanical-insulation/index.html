About Pedersen

Pedersen Mechanical Insulation has served commercial and industrial customers since 1963. As a second-generation, family-run company, we bring decades of hands-on experience to every mechanical insulation project.

Our crews are union trained through Local 50, and we specialize in the insulation systems that commercial buildings and industrial plants depend on: fiberglass, metal and PVC finishes, high-temperature work, and ammonia refrigeration.
