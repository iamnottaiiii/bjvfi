Our story

Twenty years of
measuring twice.

McCullough Contractors LLC is a Tampa-area general contractor built around one conviction: the finish work is the job.

Plenty of contractors can frame a wall. Fewer can trim it so cleanly that you stop noticing the trim and just feel the room. That second skill is where McCullough Contractors LLC started, and it is still the standard every job is held to.

The company was founded in Florida in 2006 and has operated as an active Florida limited liability company ever since, serving Tampa and the surrounding Hillsborough County communities. What began with finish carpentry, the crown molding, baseboards, and millwork that make a house feel finished, grew into full general contracting: kitchens, baths, additions, and repairs, all run with a finish carpenter's discipline.

Ask the crew what matters and you will hear the same three things: show up when you say you will, keep the site clean enough to live in while the work happens, and never ask a homeowner to accept a joint you would not sign your name to. It is a simple code, and it is why the business keeps growing on referrals.

2006

Founded in Florida

McCullough Contractors LLC files as a Florida limited liability company and starts taking on finish carpentry work around Tampa.

2007

Roots in Lutz

The company's principal office settles in the Lutz area, north of Tampa, where it still keeps its base.

2015

Reinstated and rolling

The company reinstates its filing and expands into full general contracting, kitchens, baths, and additions.

Today

Tampa Bay's finish-first contractor

An active Florida LLC with filings current, serving homeowners across Tampa and Hillsborough County, one tight joint at a time.
