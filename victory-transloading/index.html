What we do

The handoff point your supply chain is missing

Transloading is the transfer of freight from one mode of transport to another: container to trailer, railcar to truck, or inbound delivery broken down into outbound loads. Victory Transloading handles that handoff cleanly, with freight checked, sorted, and reloaded on schedule.

Services

Freight services, tab by tab

Select a service below for details. Every job is quoted on the freight itself, so call (410) 312-0194 for pricing on your load.

Container Unloading

Inbound containers and trailers are unloaded, counted, and checked against your paperwork. Damaged or short freight is flagged before it ever reaches your customer.

  • Floor-loaded and palletized container devanning
  • Piece counts and condition checks on arrival
  • Photos available on request for damage claims

Sample rate: container unloading is typically quoted per container. Call for current pricing.

Cross-Docking

Freight moves straight from inbound to outbound with minimal dwell time. Inbound loads are broken down and rebuilt into outbound shipments bound for final delivery.

  • Same-day inbound to outbound turns
  • Sort by destination, SKU, or customer
  • Scheduled dock appointments

Sample rate: cross-dock handling is typically quoted per pallet. Call for current pricing.

Palletizing and Rework

Loose freight gets palletized, stretch-wrapped, and labeled to your spec. We also handle rework: relabeling, repackaging, and rebuilding pallets that arrived wrong.

  • Palletizing and stretch wrap to spec
  • Relabeling and barcode application
  • Rebuilding damaged or unstable pallets

Sample rate: palletizing is typically quoted per pallet. Call for current pricing.

Short-Term Holding

When your outbound truck is not ready, freight can wait with us instead of on a chassis. Short-term staging keeps your freight secure until pickup.

  • Staged freight by load or appointment
  • Secured yard and dock area
  • Flexible pickup scheduling

Sample rate: short-term holding is typically quoted per pallet per day. Call for current pricing.
