About

A Tujunga builder with a creative streak

New Art Construction is a construction company based at 6708 Foothill Blvd in Tujunga, California. The name says it all: this is a builder that treats construction as a craft, bringing unique and artistic elements to every project.

The team works across residential renovations and commercial construction, with a focus on creativity and quality craftsmanship. Clients describe working with skilled professionals who take the time to understand the vision and then build it well.

Based in the Sunland/Tujunga community, New Art Construction serves local homeowners and businesses looking for a builder who cares about the details.
