About

A Worcester contractor, plain and simple

Leicester Estates Corporation is a building contractor headquartered at 43 Monroe Avenue in Worcester, Massachusetts. The company is led by its president, Salah Asfoura, and serves property owners across Worcester and the surrounding towns.

The focus is straightforward construction work: new builds, additions, renovations and repairs, managed with clear communication and finished to a proper standard. No layers of middlemen, no disappearing acts mid-project.

As a corporation rather than a one-truck outfit, Leicester Estates is set up to take responsibility for a project as a whole: planning, coordination and delivery under one name, with the owner of the property always knowing who is accountable.

If you are planning a build, an addition or a serious renovation in the Worcester area, the next step is a conversation. Call (508) 754-5415 and describe the project.
