Our story

A Northridge shop built on holding the line

Setpoint A/C & Heating is a Northridge-based air conditioning and heating contractor serving the San Fernando Valley. Our home base is on Shirley Avenue, and our work covers homes and businesses across the Valley: cooling that stands up to triple-digit summers, heating that is ready when the desert cold rolls in, and refrigeration that keeps commercial kitchens and stores running.

The name says it all. A setpoint is the target temperature a system is built to hold. We bring that same discipline to every service call: diagnose honestly, fix it right, and leave your system holding steady long after we pack up.

  • Local Northridge contractor, not a national call center
  • Residential homes and commercial HVAC plus refrigeration
  • Straight talk on repair vs. replace, every time
