Our Story

About Webb's

Webb's Painting And Contracting is a Philadelphia painting and contracting business working out of 4730 Mercer St. The focus is simple: show up on time, prep the surface properly, and leave clean, even coats behind.

Most paint jobs are won or lost before the brush comes out. Scraping, patching, sanding, and priming are the unglamorous steps that decide whether a finish lasts two years or ten, and that is where this crew spends its time.

Homeowners get a walkthrough before the first coat and another after the last one, so what was promised matches what was painted. If you are comparing bids in Philadelphia, we are glad to put a clear, line-by-line number next to the others.
