Our story

Three decades of
careful cleaning

A neighborhood dry cleaner in Salem, built on green technology, honest work, and garments done right.

A Salem fixture, 34 years strong

Royal Cleaners has served the North Shore for 34 years from its home in Crosby's Plaza at 125 Canal St. That kind of staying power comes from doing the small things right, every garment, every time.

Reviewers describe a team praised for attention to detail, professionalism, and kindness, the kind of place that saves a favorite outfit another cleaner called unsalvageable.

Voted the North Shore's best, three years running

Royal Cleaners was named Best Dry Cleaner in the North Shore for three consecutive years (2019, 2020, and 2021) in the WickedLocal Readers' Choice Awards.

It is recognition we are proud of, and a standard we work to live up to with every order.
