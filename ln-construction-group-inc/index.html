Our Story

A Long Island Builder Since 1956

According to its business profile, L&N Construction Group Inc was established in 1956, giving the company decades of experience building and improving homes across Long Island. The firm operates out of Lynbrook, NY, in Nassau County.

The company describes itself as a one stop shop for all phases of home improvement and general construction. That philosophy shapes how projects run: instead of hiring a separate contractor for every trade, clients work with one team that plans, coordinates, and stands behind the full job.

From custom home builds to kitchen and bath renovations, the focus stays the same: practical craftsmanship, clear communication, and homes that serve families well for years.
