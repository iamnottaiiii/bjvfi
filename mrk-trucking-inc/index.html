About

MRK Trucking Inc

MRK Trucking Inc is a trucking company based at 5603 Grand Ave in Coraopolis, Pennsylvania. We move freight for the businesses that keep the Ohio Valley working: warehouses, shops, contractors, and anyone with a load that needs to get where it is going. Our approach is simple on purpose. Answer the phone, quote the job honestly, show up on time, and treat every load like it matters, because it does.

01

Freight first

Hauling is the whole business. We focus on moving cargo well, not on being everything to everyone.

02

Straight talk

If a load is not a fit, we say so. If it is, you get a clear price and a real pickup window.

03

Local knowledge

Grand Ave puts us close to the river corridors and the airport, with fast access to I-79 and Route 60.
