About Leo's

A small shop with a simple way of doing business.

Leo's Auto is a neighborhood auto repair shop at 131a Barron Dr in Cincinnati, Ohio, serving Wyoming and the surrounding communities.

We keep things simple: look at the car, explain what is wrong in plain language, fix it for a fair price, and get you back on the road. No upsells you do not need, no jargon to hide behind.
