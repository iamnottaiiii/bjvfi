About American Home Energy Alliance

A West Chester Township contractor with one specialty: helping homeowners take control of rising energy costs. The facts below come from public business listings.

According to its public contractor profile, American Home Energy Alliance was founded to give homeowners a practical way to fight rising utility bills, combining hands-on experience with customer service focused on efficiency.

The company's catalyst was the Copperflect Insulation System, a reflective energy-saving product introduced to the tri-state area by founder James Watson. The idea began with a simple request: a senior citizen on a fixed income asked for help lowering her monthly electric bill. That one job grew into the company's core offering.

Public reviews describe a professionally run family business whose people put customer satisfaction first, from the first phone call through installation.
