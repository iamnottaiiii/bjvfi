About The Shop

A family owned independent tire shop on Pippin Road in North College Hill, Cincinnati. The facts below are from the shop's public BBB profile and listings.

Affordable and dependable

The shop describes itself in its own words as new and used tire sales, service and repair that is "always affordable and definitely dependable."

Motor City Tires LLC has been in business since 2009, run by co-owners Tiffany D. Spencer and Gregory D. Spencer, serving drivers in North College Hill and the wider Cincinnati area.

The shop sells used tires, custom rim sets and trailer tires, and posts its latest arrivals on its public listing pages, like the 18 inch MSW Custom rims by O.Z. and 20 inch Massiv sets listed in 2025 and 2026.
