About Duarte Auto Wrecking

A family-run style wrecking yard on Alpha Street in Irwindale, serving the San Gabriel Valley since 2005.

What we do

We specialize in used auto parts for almost any make and model. Engines, transmissions, electrical parts, body panels and interior parts are pulled from our own yard, checked, and sold with a warranty. If we don't have the part you need, we will help you find it.

Why buyers trust the yard

Engines and transmissions carry a 90-day warranty, electrical parts 15 to 30 days, and local delivery is free. Reviewers on Birdeye rate the business 4.8 out of 5 across 16 reviews, and buyers regularly mention fair prices and parts that were tested before payment.
