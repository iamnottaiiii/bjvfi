Creosote builds up. We take it down.

Every fire you burn leaves creosote and soot inside your flue. Left alone, that buildup becomes the leading cause of chimney fires in New England homes.

When to call

Three signs your chimney is overdue

Smoke backing up

Smoke spilling into the room instead of rising up the flue is a classic sign of blockage or heavy creosote buildup.

It has been over a year

Fire safety guidance calls for an annual inspection and sweeping. If you cannot remember the last one, it is time.

Visible damage

Crumbling mortar, a cracked crown, or a missing cap lets water and animals in. Small fixes now prevent big repairs later.

What we do

Chimney services, done right

From routine sweeping to full repairs, every job gets the same careful attention. Call for a quote on your specific project.

Chimney Sweeping

Complete removal of creosote, soot, and debris from the flue and firebox. Cleaner burn, safer home, better draft.

Chimney Inspections

A full top to flue look at your chimney system. We flag liner cracks, masonry wear, and blockages before they become hazards.

Chimney Repairs

Mortar repointing, crown repair, and flue fixes that keep water out and the structure sound for years to come.

Chimney Cap Installation

A quality cap keeps rain, snow, and animals out of your flue while letting smoke escape freely. Fitted to your flue.

Masonry Repointing

Worn mortar joints let water in and weaken the chimney. Fresh repointing restores strength and the original look.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Lint packed into dryer vents is a real fire risk. We clear the full vent run so your dryer runs safer and faster.
