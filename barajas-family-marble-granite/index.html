Materials

Six stones, endless kitchens

Granite

Natural stone cut from the earth, so no two slabs match. Heat resistant, hard wearing, and full of character. A classic for busy kitchens.

  • Kitchen counters and islands
  • Outdoor BBQ islands and bars
  • Fireplace surrounds

Marble

The stone of cathedrals and classic kitchens. Soft, luminous veining that makes bathrooms and vanities feel like a spa.

  • Bathroom vanities and tub decks
  • Full-height backsplashes
  • Fireplace facades

Quartz

Engineered for real life: non-porous, stain resistant, and consistent in color. The lowest-maintenance way to get a high-end look.

  • Family kitchens and rentals
  • Laundry and mudroom counters
  • Commercial break rooms

Quartzite

Natural stone with marble's looks and granite's toughness. Harder than granite, with soft, flowing movement. A designer favorite.

  • Statement kitchen islands
  • Waterfall edges
  • Book-matched feature walls

Porcelain

Slim, modern, and nearly indestructible. UV stable for outdoor kitchens and available in oversized slabs with minimal seams.

  • Outdoor kitchens
  • Slim-profile modern counters
  • Full slab backsplashes

Onyx

Translucent and dramatic. Backlit onyx turns a bar top or vanity into the centerpiece of the room.

  • Backlit bar tops
  • Feature vanities
  • Accent walls

Since 1985
A family trade

Stone work you learn with your hands

We started in 1985 with a small shop, a few saws, and a simple rule: treat every kitchen like it is your own. Forty years later the shop is bigger and the saws are better, but the rule never changed.

Our team walks you through the whole process: picking the slab, templating your space, choosing an edge profile, and installing with seams you have to hunt for. One family, one accountable crew, start to finish.

  • Free in-person estimates, no pressure
  • In-house templating, fabrication and install
  • Residential kitchens, baths, fireplaces and outdoor spaces
How we work
The process

From first call to final polish

1. Free estimate. We visit, measure, and talk through stone options and timing, in person and in plain language.

2. Slab selection. You pick the exact slab, not a sample chip. We help you read the veining so you love the whole piece.

3. Fabrication and install. We template, cut, polish and set your stone, then walk the finished work with you before we call it done.
