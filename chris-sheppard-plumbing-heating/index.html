The short version

Built on quality work
and integrity

Chris Sheppard Plumbing, LLC has served Seymour and the surrounding Connecticut towns for 20 years, handling every phase of residential plumbing: service and repair, installation, and full plumbing for additions and remodels.

The reputation was earned the slow way. Customers describe a plumber who finds the creative, cost-effective fix instead of pushing the expensive replacement, who comes back the next day with the right part when a job turns stubborn, and who leaves the work area clean.

The business philosophy is straightforward: the very best in service, installation, and repair, with attention to detail on every project, quality work, and integrity in every transaction. The goal is long-term relationships with customers, not one-time tickets.
