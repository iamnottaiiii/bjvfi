Our story

A family business since 1989. Bracken Electric Inc is a family owned and operated electrical contracting company based in West Hills, California. For more than three decades we have served homeowners and builders across Los Angeles and Ventura counties.

Our mission is simple: provide the best service possible with high quality craftsmanship that is second to none. We are proud of what we do, and it shows. We receive compliments on our work from inspectors, contractors and clients on a regular basis, and references are available upon request.

We do not advertise. Our business has grown over the years through referrals and repeat business, which is exactly how we like it: every job has to earn the next one.

As electrical and lighting systems have become more complex, we have stayed ahead of them. We specialize in Lutron HomeWorks whole-home lighting control, designing, installing and programming systems that are reliable, user friendly and beautiful in any home, on any budget.
