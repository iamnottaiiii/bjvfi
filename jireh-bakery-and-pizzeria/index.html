Our Story

A name with meaning, and a shop with two hearts.

On 147th Street in Posen, JIREH does something few shops attempt: it runs a full bakery and a full pizzeria out of the same kitchen. The day starts at 4 AM with pan dulce and fresh bread, and it rolls straight into pizza nights for families who want dinner without cooking.

It is a practical kind of place. Prices are easy on the wallet, the dining area is small and unpretentious, and the food does the talking. Guests single out two things above all: the pan dulce, described as excellent, and the stuffed pizza, called very good at a reasonable price.

  • ✓Bakery from 4 AM: pan dulce, croissants, cookies, and custom cakes.
  • ✓Pizza all day: stuffed and classic pies from the same ovens.
  • ✓Neighborhood prices: family meals that stay affordable.

What does "Jireh" mean?

Jireh comes from a Hebrew phrase meaning "will provide." For a shop that feeds its neighbors sweet bread at dawn and hot pizza at dusk, it is a fitting name: provision, twice a day, every day.

Whether you come for the conchas or the stuffed crust, you leave with something warm.
