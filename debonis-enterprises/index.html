Masonry is all we do, so we do it right

Water, freeze-thaw cycles and settling soil are hard on Pittsburgh brick and block. Debonis Enterprises repairs and rebuilds masonry the careful way: proper demolition, matched materials, and mortar mixed for the structure it sits on.

About

A working mason, not a sales office

Debonis Enterprises is a masonry contractor based on Crider Lane in Ross Township, serving Pittsburgh and the surrounding North Hills. The business is built around hands-on trade work: one point of contact from first call to final walkthrough.

Pittsburgh homes, many of them brick and many of them older, take a beating from western Pennsylvania winters. The freeze-thaw cycle opens hairline cracks into real structural problems if they are ignored. The fix is straightforward but unforgiving: take out what is failing, match the materials that remain, and build it back with proper drainage and the right mortar for the job.

That is the approach on every Debonis Enterprises project, whether it is a chimney crown, a retaining wall holding back a slope, or a full stone facade.
