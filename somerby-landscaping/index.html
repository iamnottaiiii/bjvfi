Our story

A Marblehead Landscaping Company

Local by Design

Somerby Landscaping is based at 17 Blueberry Road in Marblehead, Massachusetts. As a landscaping specialist, the company offers edging, grass seeding, and more for homes across Marblehead and the North Shore.

The focus is practical, dependable yard work: lawn care, cleanups, brush removal, plantings, and stone and driveway projects, done by people who know the local soil, seasons, and neighborhoods.

Part of the Community

Somerby Landscaping supports Marblehead youth programs as a banner sponsor of the Marblehead Boosters. When you hire local, the money stays local, and this is a company that gives back to the town it serves.

Hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Call (781) 631-0122 to talk through your project.
