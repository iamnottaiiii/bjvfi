About

One electrician, every detail.

Max Power Electric is a North Hills electrical contractor built around a simple idea: the electrician who quotes your job is the one who does the work, explains it plainly and leaves the place cleaner than he found it.

Reviewers mention the same things again and again: careful explanations with visual diagrams, honest quotes with options at different price points, fast clean work, and details other contractors skip, like asking where you want the switch before drilling a single hole.
