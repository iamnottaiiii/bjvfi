About

The story of Central Barber Shop

Central Barber Shop has served the South Central neighborhood from its Central Avenue location. It is a straightforward shop for classic cuts, fades and beard work.

Located at 4060 S Central Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90011, the shop serves Los Angeles and the surrounding neighborhoods. Walk-ins and regulars are both welcome, and the team is happy to talk through the cut before the clippers come out.
