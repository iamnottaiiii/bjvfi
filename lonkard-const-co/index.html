Since 1968

Three generations
in the dirt

Lonkard Const Co started as a family operation in 1968 and never left. Today the third generation runs the same kind of honest, hard infrastructure work it was founded on.

1968

Pete & Marlene Lonkard start the company

A family-owned construction business is founded in Northern Kentucky, built on practical field knowledge and a reputation for dependable work.

The middle years

Underground becomes the specialty

Watermain, storm and sanitary sewer, culverts, and site utility work become the company's core, serving private developers as well as city, county, and state projects.

Now a third-generation contractor, Lonkard delivers water, sewer, stormwater, and site utility work with safety-driven operations and clear project communication.
