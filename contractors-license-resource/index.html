How it works

From jobsite to licensed contractor, step by step

The path is well defined. The school walks you through each part so nothing stalls your license.

A

Confirm you qualify

California requires four years of journey-level experience in your trade within the last ten. Staff help you document it correctly.

B

Prepare for both exams

Every license needs the Law and Business exam plus a trade-specific exam. Prep covers both, with practice tests.

C

File and test

Guidance on the CSLB application, fingerprinting, and scheduling so your filing is accepted the first time.

D

Launch the business

Bonding, insurance, bidding basics, and marketing guidance to turn the license into paying work.

About

A trade school built for working people

Contractors License Resource serves the San Fernando Valley from Sylmar, helping electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, and other tradespeople turn years on the tools into a California contractor license.

The school's whole model is practical: exam preparation for the Law and Business and trade exams, hands-on help with the licensing application, and coaching on the business side of contracting, from bonding and insurance to bidding work and marketing your new company.

Because most students study around full-time jobs, programs are designed to fit working schedules. Call to ask about current class times and formats.
