ABOUT THE SHOP

Plush Cut Barber Shop sits at 867 Central Ave in Hamilton, Ohio. Public review listings describe it as a real old school type barber shop: clean, welcoming, and known for top notch cuts with excellent customer service.

Reviewers mention walking in with no issues, and praise the shop for fades, line ups, razor cuts, and beard trims. Public listings show a moderate price level and ratings from 4.5 to 4.8 stars.
