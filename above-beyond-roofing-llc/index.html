What we do

Roofing services for Central Florida homes

Pick a service below to see what is included. Every job starts with a free estimate and ends with a full cleanup.

Full roof replacement

When a roof is past patching, we tear off the old system and install a new one built for Florida heat, wind, and rain. Typical residential replacements finish in one to three days.

  • Tear off, deck inspection, and replacement of damaged wood
  • Architectural shingle systems rated for high wind zones
  • Underlayment, flashing, ridge vent, and drip edge included
  • Permit handling and final inspection coordination
  • Full property cleanup with magnetic nail sweep
  • Manufacturer and workmanship warranty documentation
Pricing: sample pricing only. A typical Central Florida shingle replacement can run from the high four figures into five figures depending on size, pitch, and material. Call for a free written estimate on your home.

Targeted roof repairs

Not every problem needs a new roof. We find the actual leak source, fix it properly, and tell you honestly how much life your roof has left.

  • Leak detection and source tracing
  • Missing, cracked, or lifted shingle replacement
  • Pipe boot, vent, and skylight resealing
  • Valley and flashing repair
  • Fascia and soffit touch ups tied to roof work
  • Photo documentation of before and after
Pricing: sample pricing only. Minor repairs often land in the low hundreds to low thousands. Call and describe the issue for a realistic ballpark before we visit.

Roof inspections and reports

Buying a home, renewing insurance, or just want to know where your roof stands? We provide thorough inspections with photos and plain language findings.

  • Full exterior walk and attic check where accessible
  • Photo report with each issue marked and explained
  • Remaining life estimate in honest terms
  • Insurance and real estate documentation support
  • Priority scheduling before hurricane season
Pricing: sample pricing only. Standalone inspections typically run in the low hundreds. Inspection fees are often credited toward repair or replacement work you book with us.

Storm and wind damage response

After a storm, fast action keeps a damaged roof from becoming water damage inside your home. We respond quickly across Kissimmee and nearby areas.

  • Emergency tarping to stop active leaks
  • Wind uplifted shingle and ridge cap repair
  • Fallen branch and debris damage assessment
  • Insurance claim documentation and photos
  • Full restoration back to pre storm condition
Pricing: sample pricing only. Emergency tarping is usually a few hundred dollars; storm repairs vary widely. Call (407) 247-8042 right away after a storm for the fastest response.

Gutters, flashing, and details

The small parts of a roof system cause a surprising share of leaks. We handle the details that keep water moving off your roof and away from your foundation.

  • Seamless gutter installation and repair
  • Gutter cleaning and downspout rerouting
  • Chimney, wall, and valley flashing replacement
  • Drip edge and ridge vent installation
  • Skylight flashing and reseal service
Pricing: sample pricing only. Gutter and flashing work is usually quoted per linear foot or per detail. Ask during your free estimate and we will price it on the spot.

Our story

A local roofing company with a simple promise

Above & Beyond Roofing LLC. was formed in Florida in 2023 and is based in Kissimmee, led by Rodolfo Velez Jr. The name is the business plan: do the job, then go a step beyond it.

What above and beyond means here

It means answering the phone. It means showing up when we say we will. It means photographing what we find on your roof so you see what we see, and explaining repair versus replace in plain language instead of sales talk.

It means protecting your landscaping before we start, cleaning up every day, and running a magnet over your yard so nobody finds a nail with a bare foot. And it means standing behind the work after we leave.

We are based at 3661 Late Morning Cir in Kissimmee, and most of our work is right here in Osceola County. Our reputation lives on this street, which is exactly how we want it.

Do the job right, clean up like you were never there, and treat the homeowner like a neighbor. Because they are.
