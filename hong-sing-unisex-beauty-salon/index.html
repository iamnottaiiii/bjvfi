On Henry Street, a salon that feels like home

Tucked into Chinatown at 44 Henry St, Hong Sing Unisex Beauty Salon has built its name the old-fashioned way: one good haircut at a time. The room is intimate and supportive, the kind of place where regulars are greeted by name and newcomers are put at ease.
