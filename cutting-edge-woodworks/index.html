About

A Working Woodshop in Harrison, Ohio

Cutting Edge WoodWorks is a custom woodworking and cabinet manufacturing shop located at 11029 State Route 128 in Harrison, Ohio. We design and build custom cabinets, millwork, mouldings, shelving, doors, and fine wood pieces for residential and commercial clients.

The company has been operating since 1991, and works as a small, hands-on shop where projects are custom designed and built to suit each job, from hand-shaped mouldings to full cabinet installations.

Business listings identify the shop as a small team serving the greater Cincinnati area from its Harrison workshop. Day-to-day leadership is listed as Dave Heinrich, Vice President. Per public listings

Why custom matters

Stock cabinets and trim are made for an average room. Your room is not average. Custom work means exact fits, matched grain, and joinery that stays tight for decades.

Built for the long haul

We build like we have to live with the piece, because our name is on it. Solid joinery, careful sanding, and finishes applied in our own shop.
