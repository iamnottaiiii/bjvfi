Our Story

A neighborhood spa on Hylan Blvd

Basin Nail Spa opened its doors on Hylan Blvd to give Staten Island a calmer place to get nails done. The idea was simple: a clean, welcoming shop where the techs take their time and the chairs feel like a break, not a chore.

Every appointment starts with sanitized tools and a fresh setup. Cuticles are cared for, shapes are evened, and color goes on smooth. Whether you want a natural nude for the office or chrome that catches the light, the goal is the same: nails you love when you walk out the door.

Walk-ins are welcome whenever there is an open chair, and regulars know a quick call to (718) 447-2200 is the fastest way to lock in a favorite time.

Sanitized. Steady. Friendly.
