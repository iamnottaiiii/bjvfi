About Us

A Family Name Since 1939

Gallenstein Brothers, Inc was established in 1939 by Harry Gallenstein, primarily engaged in residential home building. The company gradually diversified into land development, commercial construction, and mining. Today, Gallenstein Brothers, Inc. is still family owned, operated by a staff with a combined total of over 100 years of construction and property management experience.

For over a half century, Gallenstein Brothers have been providing complete construction services to companies large and small, foreign and domestic, built on a foundation of trust. Members of the Gallenstein Companies have participated in fine developments including Brookstone Homes, O'Bannon Meadows, Settler's Walk, Mariemont Landing, Delaware Crossing, The Reserves at Ivy Hills, the Crestview Hills Professional Center, Indian Lake at Northview Farm, Norgal Industrial Park, the Blue Ash Industrial Park, Mariemont Promenade, and Meadow Park Plaza.

Read the full company record on the official company website: gallensteincompanies.com.
