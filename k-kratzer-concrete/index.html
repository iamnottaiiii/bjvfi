SEC. 01

Why a good pour starts before the truck arrives

Concrete fails at the base, not at the surface. K.Kratzer Concrete spends the real effort where you never see it: excavation, compaction, stone, and pitch, so the slab you walk on stays level year after year.

Driveways and patios in the Lehigh Valley take a beating. Freeze and thaw cycles, road salt, and heavy spring rain will find every weak spot in a slab. That is why prep work matters more than the finish. Proper sub-base and drainage keep water from undermining the pour.

Every project starts with a site visit and a straight answer about what your ground needs. You get a written scope, a clear timeline, and one crew that sees the job through from forms to final cure.

“Do the base right and the concrete almost pours itself.”

About K.Kratzer Concrete

K.Kratzer Concrete is a concrete contractor based in Bath, Pennsylvania, serving homeowners across the Lehigh Valley. The work is straightforward: prepare the ground properly, pour to spec, finish clean, and leave the site tidy.

Concrete is unforgiving. You get one chance to place it right, and the difference between a slab that lasts and one that cracks in two winters is almost always in the prep. That is where this crew puts its time.

Good concrete is 80 percent preparation and 20 percent pour.

SEC. 03B

How a project runs

1. Site visit. We walk the area, check drainage and access, and talk through options and finishes.

2. Written scope. You get a clear description of the work, the timeline, and the price before anything is scheduled.

3. Prep and pour. Excavation, stone base, forming, then the pour and finish in one coordinated day.

4. Cure and cleanup. Control joints cut, site cleaned, and curing guidance so the slab reaches full strength.
