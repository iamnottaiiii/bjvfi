Our story

Built on Blucher Ave

Perez Disposal Inc runs out of 12000 Blucher Ave in Granada Hills, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley. The company started as a waste hauler and grew into demolition because customers kept asking the same question: can the crew that tears it down also take it away?

Today the operation runs a 7 truck fleet that is registered with the FMCSA and permitted to haul waste in the City of Los Angeles. Demolition and disposal under one roof means tighter schedules, cleaner handoffs, and one phone number from estimate to final load.

Concrete, wood, metal, and green waste are separated for recycling whenever the job allows, keeping usable material out of landfills and disposal costs down for the customer.
