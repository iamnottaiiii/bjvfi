Services

Electrical services for home and business

Panels and power

The panel is the heart of your electrical system. If yours is old, maxed out or uses breakers that are no longer made, it may be time for an upgrade.

  • Electrical panel upgrades (100A to 200A)
  • Panel replacement and repair
  • Circuit breaker replacement
  • Sub panel installation
  • Whole house surge protection
  • Service entrance and meter work
Older homes in the Five Towns were often built with panels that cannot keep up with modern loads. An upgrade adds capacity and safety in one visit.

Lighting

Good lighting changes how a room feels and how safe a property is after dark. We install, repair and update interior and exterior lighting.

  • Recessed and can lighting
  • Fixture installation and replacement
  • Ceiling fan installation
  • Landscape and pathway lighting
  • Security and motion lighting
  • Dimmer and smart switch setup
Switching to LED fixtures and dimmers can cut lighting energy use noticeably while giving you better control of every room.

Wiring and devices

From adding an outlet where you actually need one to wiring a renovation, we handle the circuits behind the walls.

  • Outlet and switch installation
  • GFCI and AFCI protection
  • Rewiring and circuit additions
  • Kitchen and bath wiring
  • Dedicated appliance circuits
  • EV charger circuit installation
Renovating a kitchen or bath? New circuits for appliances and code required GFCI protection are jobs for a licensed electrician.

Safety and repair

Flickering lights, warm outlets and breakers that trip are your system asking for help. We find the cause and fix it properly.

  • Electrical troubleshooting and repair
  • Home electrical safety inspections
  • Code violation corrections
  • Smoke and CO detector wiring
  • Storm and surge damage repair
  • Pre purchase electrical checkups
If you smell burning near an outlet or panel, or see scorch marks, turn the circuit off and call right away. Do not wait on that one.
