Our Story

A North Hollywood original.

DARROW Heating & Air Conditioning has served the community since 1948, growing from a local shop into a full heating, air conditioning, and mechanical contracting company. Trade directories list us under California contractor license #114301, and we have been listed in the Blue Book of Building and Construction since 1998.

Our technicians are trained on changing technology and take pride in quality equipment, clean work, and options that fit each customer’s budget and energy goals. That customer-service focus is what keeps clients on annual maintenance plans for years, and sometimes decades.

We serve North Hollywood and surrounding communities across the San Fernando Valley and portions of Los Angeles, San Gabriel, and Ventura counties. Free estimates are available, and senior discounts are offered on request.
