Why it matters

The hidden job
your home depends on

Dryer vents and air ducts do their work out of sight. When they clog, your home pays the price.

!

Lint is a fire hazard

Thousands of home fires start in the laundry room each year, and lint-clogged dryer vents are a leading cause. A packed vent traps heat, forces your dryer to work harder, and can ignite.

Regular professional cleaning is one of the simplest safety steps a homeowner can take.

$

Clogged vents cost money

A restricted vent means longer drying cycles and higher energy bills. It also wears out your dryer faster. Clean vents let the dryer do its job in one cycle, the way it was designed to.

~

Dusty ducts, stale air

Dust, pet dander, and debris collect inside ductwork over the years. Every time your heating or cooling runs, it pushes that buildup back into the rooms you live in.
