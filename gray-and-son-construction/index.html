Our story

Three decades of
Cincinnati builds

A family-run construction company on Cincinnati's west side, still answering its own phone after 34 years.

Gray & Son Construction has been working in Cincinnati for 34 years, and that kind of longevity in this business comes from one thing: doing what you said you would do.

From our shop on South Road, we take on residential and commercial work across the west side and greater Cincinnati: renovations, remodeling, additions, and new construction. Homeowners and property managers call us because one call gets the whole job handled, with quality craftsmanship and attention to detail at every step.

We are a working contractor, not a sales operation. When you call, you talk to the people who will actually build your project. That is how we have stayed in business since the early nineties, and it is how we plan to stay.
