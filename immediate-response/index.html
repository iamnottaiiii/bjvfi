Our story

Built for the worst day, ready every day

Who we are

Immediate Response is a water damage restoration service based at 15235 S Brand Blvd in Mission Hills, CA. We exist for one reason: when water invades a home or business, fast professional action saves the property.

A burst pipe, a failed water heater, a storm that found its way in. We have seen how quickly a small leak becomes a big problem, and we built our work around speed, thorough drying, and clear communication.

How we work

We answer, we show up, and we get the water out. Then we dry the structure completely, because moisture left behind becomes mold, odor, and rot. We document everything with photos and moisture readings, so you have what your insurance company needs.

No drama, no upsell. Just the job done right, and a property handed back dry.
