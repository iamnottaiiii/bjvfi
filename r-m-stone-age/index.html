Why masonry

The most durable material is also the most beautiful one.

Masonry does not warp, rot, or fade. A well laid stone wall or brick facade adds real value to a property and asks for very little in return. We work with brick, block, and natural and manufactured stone, and we match existing work so repairs disappear into the original.

  • Brick and block walls, planters, and pillars
  • Stone veneer for facades, fireplaces, and entries
  • Retaining walls engineered for Valley soil
  • Tuckpointing, repointing, and crack repair

Who we are

Our story

A masonry shop in the heart of Panorama City.

R M Stone Age is a masonry contractor based at 8550 Willis Ave in Panorama City, California. We serve homeowners and property managers across the San Fernando Valley who want stone and brick work done right the first time.

Masonry is a slow trade done well. We take the time to match stone and mortar, set true lines, and finish clean, so the work looks like it has always belonged there.
