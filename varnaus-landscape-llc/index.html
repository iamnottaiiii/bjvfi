About

Built on sweat equity

Varnau's landscape, LLC started in March 2012, when Tanner Varnau turned yard work into a real trade. The company incorporated as a limited liability company in 2016 and has been listed as a commercial landscape contractor with the BBB of Cincinnati ever since.

The business works out of the Cincinnati area, with records tying it to both Union Township (Summerside Rd) and West Chester (Crossbridge Dr). Commercial properties are the core trade, but residential customers get the same show-up-on-time standard.
