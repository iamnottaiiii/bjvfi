Our story

Paint Finesse, West Chester Township

Paint Finesse describes itself as a family owned business with over 20 years of experience in automotive body work. It operates from 9051 Innovation Pl in West Chester Township, Ohio, serving drivers across the northern Cincinnati area.

Customers know the crew by name. Public reviews repeatedly mention Tony and his team, including back-to-back jobs on a Mercedes AMG GT-S and a Mercedes ML-350 that the owner says came out perfect. A commercial customer who uses the shop regularly for touch-ups, powder coated wheels, and vinyl wraps says the shop always stands behind its work.

The shop keeps weekday hours, Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and is closed weekends, per public listings.
