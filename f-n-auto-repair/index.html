About F & N Auto Repair

F & N Auto Repair keeps daily drivers on the road in the City of Orange. The shop handles routine maintenance and everyday repairs with straight answers and fair treatment, so you can get back behind the wheel with confidence.

  • Brake Service Pads, rotors, and calipers checked and replaced to keep your stopping power strong.
  • Oil Changes Conventional and synthetic oil changes with filter replacement on your schedule.
  • Engine Diagnostics Computer diagnostics that find the real cause of warning lights and odd noises.
  • Tune Ups Spark plugs, filters, and fluids serviced to keep your engine running clean.
  • Suspension & Steering Shocks, struts, and steering parts inspected for a smooth, safe ride.
  • Pre-Purchase Inspections A thorough once-over before you buy a used car, so there are no surprises.
