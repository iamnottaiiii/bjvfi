Our story

One painter. Four and a half decades.

Painting By Howard has been serving all of Southern New Jersey for over 30 years, with the owner Howard Kyser giving every project his personal attention. That is the whole business model, and it has not changed.

Directory listings credit the company with 45 to 46 years in business, which puts Howard among the longest-running independent painters in Gloucester County. Longevity like that does not come from advertising. It comes from showing up, doing the prep nobody sees, and leaving rooms cleaner than you found them.

Customers who have hired the company for larger work, including bathroom remodels, hardwood floors and backsplashes, describe decent work at reasonable prices in directory reviews. That reputation, fair prices and steady hands, is what keeps the phone ringing.

There is no franchise, no call center, and no rotating crew. When you call (856) 848-7786, you talk to the painter.
