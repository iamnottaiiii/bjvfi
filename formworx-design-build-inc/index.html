About

Twenty-five years of
Pasadena homes

Formworx Design + Build, Inc. is a Pasadena-based architecture firm and general contractor, working across design, permitting and construction for more than two and a half decades.

Designers who build, builders who design

Most firms do one or the other. Formworx was built on the belief that the best homes come from a single team that carries a project from the first sketch through the last inspection. That is what design-build means here: no handoffs, no finger-pointing, no surprises between the drawing and the framing.


Based at 547 S Marengo Ave in Pasadena, the firm has pulled 58 building permits since 2000, a record of steady, permitted, inspected work across kitchens, baths, additions and whole-home transformations. It holds active California General Building licenses 889341 and 1025360, per BuildZoom records.


The work is residential at its core: helping Pasadena families stay in the homes and neighborhoods they love by making those homes work better.
