Every project, four phases, one accountable team
How we work

Five phases. Zero surprises.

Our process for commercial and residential projects, from first call to final walkthrough.

Phase 01Consult & Estimate

We walk the site, listen to what you need, and put real numbers on paper. No vague allowances.

Phase 02Design & Permits

Plans, pricing, and permits handled in-house and coordinated with local authorities.

Phase 03Preconstruction

Scheduling, subcontractor coordination, and materials procurement, locked before day one.

Phase 04Construction

Managed jobsites with a single point of contact and regular progress updates.

Phase 05Closeout & Handover

Punch list, final inspections, and documentation. We hand over keys, not loose ends.
