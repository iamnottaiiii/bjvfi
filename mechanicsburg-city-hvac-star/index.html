Heating

Furnaces, heat pumps, and steady winter warmth

When the temperature drops in Cumberland County, your heating system is the most important machine in the house. We repair furnaces and heating systems, and keep them running safely all winter.

  • Furnace repair and diagnostics
  • Heat pump service
  • Thermostat and control troubleshooting

If your heat is out, call (717) 551-6737 and describe what the system is doing. We will take it from there.

Cooling

Air conditioning that stands up to summer

Pennsylvania summers are humid, and a struggling AC shows it fast. We service central air systems, find airflow and refrigerant issues, and get cool air moving again.

  • Central AC repair
  • Refrigerant and airflow diagnostics
  • Coil cleaning and tune-ups

Warm air, weak airflow, or strange noises are all reasons to call before the next heat wave.

Installation

New systems, sized and installed right

When a system is past its prime, replacement beats another repair. We help you choose equipment that fits your home and budget, then install it cleanly.

  • Furnace and AC replacement
  • Heat pump installation
  • Proper sizing and duct evaluation
About pricing: every installation is quoted on site after we see your home and your current system. Call (717) 551-6737 for a straightforward, no-pressure quote.
Maintenance

A yearly visit keeps your system efficient, catches small problems early, and extends equipment life. Heating check in the fall, cooling check in the spring.

  • Fall heating inspection
  • Spring cooling tune-up
  • Filter, coil, and safety checks

Ask about scheduling a regular maintenance visit when you call.
