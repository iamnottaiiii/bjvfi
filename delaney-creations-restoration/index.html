About

Stewards of Cincinnati's Older Homes

Delaney Creations Restoration is a Cincinnati restoration company based at 3212 Rinda Ln. Our listed trade is damage restoration service, with home improvement and construction work alongside it.

Our public listing describes the company as BBB A+ rated and IICRC certified, and those standards shape every job: assess honestly, preserve first, restore faithfully, finish beautifully.

Cincinnati's housing stock is full of houses with good bones and long stories: plaster walls, old-growth trim, porches that have seen a century of evenings. We believe those houses deserve craftsmen who see the story, not just the damage. Sample

We do not just repair houses. We return them to the families who love them, with their character intact.
