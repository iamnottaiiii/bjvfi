Our story

A South Salina neighborhood salon

Michele Unisex Hair Salon sits at 6136 S Salina St in Syracuse, a welcoming chair for men, women, and kids across the neighborhood. It is the kind of small salon where the stylist learns your name, your hair, and exactly how you like it.

Clients talk about the personal connection: honest advice before the cut, patience in the chair, and prices that respect a working budget. No rush, no upsell, just good hair.
