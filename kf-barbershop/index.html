About the shop

A neighborhood barbershop doing what barbershops do best: great cuts, honest prices, and a chair that feels like yours.

📍

On Linden Street

KF barbershop sits at 1102 Linden St in Allentown, an easy stop for the neighborhood and anyone passing through the 18102 area.

🕐

Open Six Days

Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed Sundays. Ten full hours a day to fit a cut around your schedule.

👋

Walk-Ins Welcome

No appointment needed. Walk in, take a seat, and leave sharper than you came. Calling ahead at (484) 820-0209 is always fine too.

✂

Classic and Modern

From timeless scissor cuts and hot towel shaves to the latest fades and designs, the shop covers the full range of modern barbering.

🧑

For the Whole Family

Grown men, young gentlemen, and everyone in between. Kids cuts are handled with patience and care.
