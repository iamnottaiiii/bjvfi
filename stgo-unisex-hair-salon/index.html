The shop

A barber shop built for the whole neighborhood

STGO sits on 14th Avenue in Dyker Heights, Brooklyn, and works as both a classic barber shop and a unisex hair salon. Walk in for a clean fade or a fresh cut, or book time for styling and color.

Open seven days a week with evening hours on weekdays, the shop fits around work and school schedules.
