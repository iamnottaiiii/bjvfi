Services

From soaked to settled

Select a tab to see what each stage covers. Call to confirm your situation fits, and we will tell you what happens next.

Water extraction

The first and most urgent step: getting standing water out before it migrates into walls, cabinets, and subfloors.

  • Standing water removal with truck mounted and portable extractors
  • Carpet and pad water removal
  • Source identification and damage assessment
  • Emergency board up referrals when needed

Structural drying

Moisture you cannot see does the most damage. We dry wall cavities, flooring, and framing with measured, monitored equipment.

  • Commercial air movers and dehumidifiers
  • Moisture mapping and daily readings
  • Targeted drying of walls, ceilings, and subfloors
  • Humidity control to discourage mold growth

Mold remediation

Where water sat too long, mold follows. We contain the area, remove affected material, and clean what remains.

  • Containment of affected areas
  • Removal of mold damaged materials
  • HEPA filtration and surface cleaning
  • Post remediation moisture verification

Cleanup and sanitizing

Water carries contaminants, especially from sewage or storm backups. We clean, sanitize, and deodorize the space.

  • Antimicrobial treatment of affected surfaces
  • Odor removal and deodorization
  • Content cleaning coordination
  • Safe disposal of unsalvageable materials

Repairs and rebuild

Once everything is dry and clean, we put the space back together: walls, floors, paint, and fixtures.

  • Drywall replacement and finishing
  • Flooring repair and replacement
  • Paint and trim restoration
  • Insurance documentation support

While you wait for help

  1. Shut off the water source if you can do so safely.
  2. Move valuables, papers, and electronics to dry ground.
  3. Avoid standing water near outlets or appliances.
  4. Do not run the HVAC if ducts may be wet.
  5. Take photos for your records before cleanup begins.
