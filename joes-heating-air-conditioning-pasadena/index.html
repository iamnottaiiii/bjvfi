Our story

A local shop with a simple standard: do it right.

Joe's Heating & Air Conditioning is a Pasadena HVAC contractor serving homes from our shop on East Washington Boulevard. We are the kind of shop that answers the phone, shows up on time, and treats your home like our own.

We believe most HVAC problems come down to honest work: careful diagnosis, quality parts, and clean installation. No pressure tactics, no mystery fees, no cutting corners to win on price.
