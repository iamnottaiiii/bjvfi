What we do

Services

Full-service auto care for the cars and trucks our neighbors drive every day. Pick a category below, then call to talk through your specific car.

Maintenance that keeps you rolling

The routine care that prevents big bills later. If you are not sure what your car is due for, call and we will look it up together.

  • Oil changes and fluid checks
  • Filters, belts, and hoses
  • Tune-ups and spark plugs
  • Scheduled factory maintenance

Brakes and tires, done right

Stopping and steering are the two things you never gamble with. If your pedal feels soft or your tires are wearing unevenly, come in.

  • Brake pads, rotors, and calipers
  • Tire mounting and balancing
  • Wheel alignments
  • Suspension and steering checks

Engine work and real diagnostics

Check-engine light on? Rough idle? We diagnose before we replace, so you pay for the fix, not for guessing.

  • Check-engine light diagnostics
  • Engine repair
  • Exhaust system repair
  • Pre-purchase inspections

Comfort and electrical systems

From a weak battery to an AC that quit in July, the systems that make driving livable are our everyday work.

  • Battery testing and replacement
  • Heating and air conditioning
  • Starters and alternators
  • Lighting and wiring
