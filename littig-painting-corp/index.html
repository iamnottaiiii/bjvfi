Services

Interior, exterior, and the prep in between

Typical services below. Every project starts with a free estimate and a clear quote.

Interior Painting

Whole-home repaints, single rooms, hallways, stairwells and ceilings. Clean lines around every trim, window and door. Furniture moved and floors protected, always.

Exterior Painting

House exteriors, siding, trim, shutters and front doors. Thorough scraping, sanding and priming with paints built for Massachusetts weather.

Drywall & Prep

Patching, crack repair, caulking and surface prep. The finish is only as good as what is underneath, and we do not skip this step.

Color Consulting

Not sure which shade? We help you pick colors that work with your light, your trim and your home's style, before a single can opens.

Residential & Commercial

Homes, apartments, offices and storefronts. A professional crew that shows up on time and keeps the site tidy.

Final Walkthrough

Every job ends with a walkthrough and touch-up pass. If something is not right, we fix it on the spot.
