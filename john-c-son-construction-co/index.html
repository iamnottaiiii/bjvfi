Prep Is the Job

Concrete fails from the bottom up. We compact the base, set forms true, and pour to proper thickness so your slab stays put for decades.

  • Compacted gravel base
  • True forms and proper slope
  • Control joints cut to plan
  • Cured and sealed correctly

WHY IT MATTERS

Michigan freeze thaw cycles punish shortcuts. Proper base prep and jointing are what keep concrete from heaving and cracking.
