About

Built around one idea: your home can grow with you

Expand Your Home California is a construction company based at 815 Fair Oaks Avenue in South Pasadena, California. The company works as a general contractor, planning and building residential additions that give homeowners more living space without a move.

South Pasadena is full of well built older homes that were designed for a different era. Growing families, home offices, and multigenerational living all ask more of a floor plan than it was drawn to give. An addition answers that need while preserving the character and location that made you choose the home in the first place.

Our approach is straightforward: visit the property, define the scope in writing, handle the plans and permits, and build the work on a schedule you can plan around. You always know who is on your job and what happens next.
