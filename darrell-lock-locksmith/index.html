Our Story

A local locksmith shop built on showing up fast and treating people right.

A North Versailles original

Darrell Lock & Locksmith started with a simple idea: the Pittsburgh area deserves a locksmith who answers the phone, shows up on time, and charges a fair price. Based at 100 Walmart Dr in North Versailles Township, we serve homeowners, drivers, and small businesses across Allegheny County.

From a lost car key in a grocery store parking lot to a full rekey after moving into a new home, we handle the jobs the big call centers overcharge for, with the personal touch only a local shop can give.

How we work

Every job starts with a clear quote. We arrive with professional tools, open locks without damage whenever possible, and stand behind the work we do. If we say we will be there, we will be there.

What we believe

Fair pricing, honest advice, and fast response. We would rather earn a customer for life than squeeze an extra dollar out of one visit.
