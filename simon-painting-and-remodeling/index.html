Services

The work, spelled out

Painting and remodeling under one roof. Every project starts with a free estimate and a clear scope in writing.

  • Interior Painting

    Full rooms, single accent walls, ceilings, trim, crown molding, and doors. We patch, sand, prime, and paint, and we protect your floors and furniture while we work.

  • Exterior Painting

    House siding, fascia, shutters, and front doors. Proper scraping, priming, and caulking so the finish stands up to NJ weather.

  • Cabinet Painting & Refinishing

    A cost friendly kitchen refresh: sprayed or brushed finishes that look factory new without the cost of new cabinets.

  • Kitchen Remodeling

    From cosmetic refreshes to full renovations: cabinets, countertops, backsplash, lighting, and flooring.

  • Bathroom Remodeling

    Vanities, tile, fixtures, and finishes. Small powder rooms to full primary baths.

  • Home Renovations & Repairs

    Drywall repair, trim and molding work, flooring touch ups, and general home improvements that keep a house sharp.

Simon Painting And Remodeling started with a brush and grew into full home remodeling. Based on Vanderbilt Avenue in Woodbury Heights, the company serves homeowners across Gloucester County who want one accountable contractor for painting and renovation work.

The approach is straightforward: show up on time, prep like it matters, communicate in plain language, and leave the house cleaner than the day started. Estimates are free, and the scope is agreed in writing before work begins.
