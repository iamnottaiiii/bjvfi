About Weaver Barns

A sturdy name in Amish built outdoor structures across the Midwest.

Weaver Barns builds sheds, barns, cabins, garages, pavilions and custom outdoor structures, serving customers across Ohio, Indiana, Michigan, Pennsylvania and Kentucky. The Fairfield lot on Dixie Highway is where you can walk the models, talk through options and start your order.

The approach is simple: quality materials, careful craftsmanship, and personal, attentive customer service that is hard to find at larger outdoor living retailers. Before a building is created, the team works through your color choices, roofing type, siding and any custom options you want included.

  • Amish built sheds, barns, cabins, garages and pavilions
  • Dealer locations across Ohio, Michigan, Pennsylvania and Indiana
  • Custom colors, roofing, siding and options on every order
  • Personal service from design through delivery
