Our story

One man, one standard

Devon's Carpet Services started with a simple frustration: carpet cleaning that rushes the job, hides the real price, and leaves you wondering what you paid for. Devon decided to do the opposite.

Five years and 222 jobs later, the approach has not changed. Devon does every job himself, from the first phone call to the final walk through. He takes the time competitors skip: extra extraction passes, hand detailed edges, honest talk about which stains will come out and which will not.

Customers describe the same things again and again: early arrivals, tidy work in wet weather, sofas that look new, and carpet that feels new underfoot. That reputation earned Top Pro honors two years running and a 4.9 rating across 146 verified reviews.

Based at 4738 Aldgate Green in Arbutus, Devon serves Arbutus, Halethorpe, and surrounding Baltimore County neighborhoods. Payment is simple: cash, credit card, Venmo, or Zelle.
