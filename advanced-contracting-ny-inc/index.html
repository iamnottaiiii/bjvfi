Our story

A Brooklyn contractor / built on steady work

Advanced Contracting NY Inc. is a contracting company based at 780 E 2nd St, Suite 2D, in Brooklyn, NY. Per its business listing, the company has served the community since 1971, focusing on interior renovations and exterior restoration.

What we do best

Most of our contracts over the years have been roofing, sidewalk replacements, painting and masonry projects. Our crew is equipped to take on a wide range of renovation and restoration work, from carpentry and plastering to bricklaying and concrete.
