Why it matters

A clean chimney is a safe chimney

Creosote buildup, blockages and cracked flues turn a cozy fireplace into a hazard. Regular sweeping keeps the fire where it belongs.

01

Creosote does not wait

Every fire leaves flammable creosote on the flue walls. A professional sweep removes it before it becomes a chimney fire.

We look at the whole system

A visit is not just a brush up the flue. We check the firebox, damper, smoke chamber and cap, and we tell you plainly what we find.

If something needs attention, you hear about it on the spot, with options, not pressure.

What we do →

Burn season comes every year

Connecticut winters ask a lot of a chimney. An annual sweep and inspection before the cold sets in keeps drafts strong, smoke out and heat in.

It is the cheapest insurance a fireplace can get.

Ready when the first frost hits

Fall fills up fast. Call early to get your sweep on the calendar before burn season.

How we work

Old trade,
straight dealing

Chimney sweeping is one of the oldest trades there is, and the good version of it has not changed: climb up, look close, brush clean, tell the truth about what you find.

That is the whole business model. No upsell scripts, no mystery line items. You get a clean flue, a clear report and a fair price quoted before work starts.
