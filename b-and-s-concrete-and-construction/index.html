Our story

A Northern Kentucky concrete crew

Who we are

Built on strength, poured with pride

B & S Concrete & Construction is a concrete contractor based in Covington, Kentucky, serving homeowners and businesses across Northern Kentucky. We focus on one trade and do it well: concrete that is prepped right, poured right, and finished to last.

Good concrete work is mostly what happens before the truck arrives: the grade, the base, the forms, the reinforcement. That is where we earn our keep, so your driveway, patio, or foundation holds up for decades, not seasons.

How we work

Straight talk, clean sites

We show up when we say we will, give you a straight price before we start, and leave the site clean when we are done. No disappearing acts, no surprise line items.

Residential or commercial, small repair or full pour, every job gets the same care: proper prep, quality materials, and a finish we are proud to put our name on.
