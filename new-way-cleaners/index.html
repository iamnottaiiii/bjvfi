About us

A neighborhood cleaner with old-school standards

New Way Cleaners serves Lexington and the surrounding towns from our shop on Massachusetts Ave.

Our promise

Your clothes treated like our own

Anyone can run a machine. The difference is in the details: checking every button before pressing, catching the faint stain nobody else noticed, and making sure your favorite suit comes back looking like the day you bought it.

We are a small, local shop, which means you talk to the people actually handling your garments. No call centers, no conveyor belt, just careful work and a fair price.

  • Every garment inspected before it goes out
  • Stain spotting included with dry cleaning
  • Easy drop-off on Massachusetts Ave
