Our story

Upscale Nails and Spa brings a little luxury to Penfield Road: massage chairs for every pedicure, a calm and clean space, and owner Kay, whose gel and SNS manicures clients say last for weeks. It is not fancy, reviewers say, but it is relaxing, friendly, and efficient. Weddings and birthday parties are welcome, walk-ins are too, and the parking is free.
