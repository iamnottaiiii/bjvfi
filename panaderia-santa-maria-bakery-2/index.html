Three reasons to stop by

One corner, three sweet traditions

Panaderia Santa Maria Bakery #2 grew from the family shop on Kennedy Ave into a Hohman Ave destination: a panadería, a café, and a paletería side by side.

Panadería

Pan dulce, fresh daily

Trays of sweet bread baked through the morning, plus savory favorites like the famous jalapeño and cream cheese bread. Grab a bag to go or linger over the case.

Café

A real coffee bar

Horchata lattes, Nutella lattes, mocha lattes and frappés made to order. Reviewers call the coffee great and the pastries amazing. The perfect morning pairing.

Paletería

Homemade paletas next door

The family paletería at 3807 Hohman Ave serves homemade paletas and ice cream. Cool down after the bakery run, it is all one sweet stop.

Nuestra historia

A family bakery, grown with Hammond

Panaderia Santa Maria started as a family dream: bake the pan dulce of home, fresh every morning, at prices neighbors can afford. The original shop on Kennedy Ave built a loyal following one concha at a time.

Bakery #2 on Hohman Ave carries that same family spirit with room to grow: a full coffee bar, more room for custom cake orders, and a paletería next door serving homemade paletas and ice cream. Reviewers keep saying the same things: everything tastes fresh and homemade, the coffee is great, and it feels like supporting family, because it is.

Open early every day of the week, this is the kind of place where the bakers know the regulars and the trays never sit long. Come taste why Hammond keeps coming back.
