About Kenoyer Construction and Painting

Kenoyer Construction and Painting has been adding value to homes and businesses since 1992, serving customers all over central Iowa from Van Meter.

Owner Everett Kenoyer Jr. leads a crew known for professional service at reasonable prices, always with a free exact quote. Services cover commercial and residential work, interior and exterior.
