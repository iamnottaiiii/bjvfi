Why Empire

SEC. 01 / THE COMPANY
Concrete First

A concrete contractor that does the dirt work too

Most jobs need more than a pour. Empire handles the excavation, grading, and pipe work that has to happen before the concrete truck shows up, so you are not juggling three different crews. Driveways, slabs, pads, and footings go in on ground that was prepped by the same team finishing the surface.

Own Equipment

Bobcat, backhoe, and the tools to move earth

Empire runs its own bobcat and backhoe, plus bush hogging equipment for overgrown lots and acreage. That means grading, trenching, clearing, and site prep happen on your schedule, not a rental yard's. One owner-operator outfit, accountable from first cut to final grade.

Year Round

Summer pours, winter snow removal

When the ground is workable, the crew is pouring and grading. When winter hits Northern Kentucky, the same equipment keeps driveways and lots clear with snow removal service. It is a working relationship that does not end when the season changes.

Our story

Built in Morning View

A small Kentucky construction company, run by its owner

Empire Construction LLC was formed in Kentucky in 2016 and is owned and operated by Brandon Williams out of Morning View in Kenton County. It is registered as a small business in the construction special trades, and the work reflects that: hands-on, equipment-driven, and local.

There is no sales team and no call center. When you call (859) 393-7005, you talk to the people who will show up with the machines. The company's public listing puts it plainly: concrete, excavation, bush hogging, bobcat, backhoe, pipe work, snow removal, and anything else construction related.

That range is the point. A driveway job might need clearing, grading, pipe for drainage, and then the pour. A farm lane might need bush hogging in summer and snow removal in winter. Empire keeps the equipment and the crew to handle the whole sequence, and every job starts with a free estimate.
