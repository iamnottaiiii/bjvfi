Truster Excavating Co is based at 4237 Grissom Dr in Batavia, Ohio, and is listed as owner-operated by Gary Truster in a public business directory. It is a small company by design: lean, local and on the job, not in an office.

Federal carrier records back that up. Truster Excavating is registered with the FMCSA as a motor carrier (USDOT 1219504), with its most recent filing in April 2026 and over 33,000 miles logged in 2025. That is a company that is actively working, not sitting still.
