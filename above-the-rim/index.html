An old-school garage with new-school heart

Above The Rim is a neighborhood auto repair shop and tire dealer on Madison Road in Cincinnati, run the way garages used to be run.

Above The Rim New and Used Tires, LLC has been serving Cincinnati drivers since 2010. This is a blue-collar shop in the best sense: experienced mechanics, straight answers, and a willingness to work with customers who are on a tight budget.

Customers keep coming back for the honesty. Reviewers describe a crew that educates you about your vehicle, recommends the cheapest fix that actually works, and goes above and beyond even when the answer is "this one is not worth fixing."

Tires are the shop's bread and butter, with both new and used options, plus general auto repair for the daily driver. No corporate scripts, no upsell pressure, just people who like keeping cars on the road.
