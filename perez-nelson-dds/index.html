About

Meet Dr. Nelson Perez

A Brooklyn general dentist patients describe as honest, funny, and wonderfully gentle.

The dentist

Dr. Nelson Perez, DDS is a general practice dentist at 8820 20th Ave in Brooklyn, NY. He is licensed in New York State (license 040389) and has been listed in the national provider registry since 2006.

As a general dentist, he is the primary dental care provider for patients of all ages, responsible for the diagnosis, treatment, management, and overall coordination of services related to oral health.

Patients consistently mention his great sense of humor, his honest approach to treatment, and how comfortable and relaxed the office makes them feel.
