About Winegard Energy

Winegard Energy is a general contractor based in Duarte, California, working in the insulation, heating and air conditioning, and drywall trades.

From the shop at 1859 Business Center Drive, the company serves homeowners and businesses across Duarte and the surrounding San Gabriel Valley. The focus is practical: work that improves how a building performs, delivered on a clear schedule.

Because every building is different, Winegard Energy quotes each project individually after a site walkthrough. Call to discuss your timeline and scope.
