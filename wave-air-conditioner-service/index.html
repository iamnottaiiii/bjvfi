About

A Valley contractor
built on cool air

Wave Air Conditioner Service is an HVAC contractor based on Van Nuys Blvd in Sherman Oaks, serving homes across the San Fernando Valley.

Our approach

Show up fast. Diagnose straight.
Fix it right.

Valley summers punish weak AC systems. We started Wave Air Conditioner Service with a simple idea: a local contractor who answers the phone, shows up when promised, and treats every home like it is the only job on the schedule.

We service all major makes and models, from older central air units to modern high efficiency systems and ductless minisplits. Repairs get done right the first time. Replacements get sized correctly, installed cleanly, and tested before we hand you the thermostat.

Local and reachable

Our shop is at 5632 Van Nuys Blvd #3 in Sherman Oaks. You are calling a neighbor, not a call center.

Honest recommendations

If a repair makes sense, we repair. If replacement saves you money long term, we say so and show the math.

Respect for your home

Drop cloths, shoe covers, and a full cleanup. Your home looks the same when we leave, only cooler.

Built for Valley heat

We know what 100 degree weeks do to undersized systems and tired compressors, and we plan for it.
