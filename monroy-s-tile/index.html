How it works

From estimate to grout, one crew you can reach.

Every project starts with a free estimate. Standard-size showers and kitchens typically take about a week to complete, and you will get an estimated timeline when your project is scheduled.

One practical note from experience: shower pans need to be hot mopped by a third-party company before tile installation begins. We are happy to provide a list of such companies.

    Our story

    A tile contractor built on repeat customers.

    Monroy's tile is a Los Angeles area tiling service with more than two decades in the trade. The work spans porcelain and ceramic floors, natural stone, deco and mosaic tile, and custom countertops in granite, quartz, and marble.

    What keeps customers calling back is straightforward: crews that show up when scheduled, clear pricing without surprise change orders, and craftsmanship people notice. Reviewers have used Monroy's on many occasions across commercial and residential settings and single out the mosaic tile setting as exceptional.
