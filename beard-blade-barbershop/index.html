Our story

A TRADITIONAL
BARBER HAVEN

Beard & Blade Barbershop opened its doors on Harris Hill Road in Buffalo with a simple idea: bring back the classic barbershop. Master barber Dave built the shop around precision work and genuine hospitality, and the neighborhood noticed fast.

Reviewers call it a 10 out of 10 spot for a quality, affordable haircut. They praise the always-clean shop, the down to earth and relaxed atmosphere, and an owner who is super friendly. Parents especially recommend Dave for kids cuts, including scissors-only cuts for children who hate the buzzer.

Walk-ins are the house rule. As the shop puts it: who needs to plan when your hair is in good hands.
