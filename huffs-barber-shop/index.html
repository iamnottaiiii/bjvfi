About the Shop

A Burlington Landmark Since 2017

Huff's Barber Shop describes itself simply: a classic barber shop in Burlington, Ky. Public business records list the shop's founding year as 2017, and nearly a decade later it still runs like the old-school shops it takes after: vintage chairs, traditional cuts, and a price level reviewers consistently call inexpensive.

The crew gets named in review after review: Jerome, Ben, and Dave are praised for friendly, great work, especially with kids. Customers say the barbers remember their names, ask about their day, and treat regulars like family.

The shop is known as a local business that supports other local businesses and the community. Step in once, reviewers say, and you will not need to find another barber shop again.

History details above come from public listings and customer reviews. For the full story, ask the barbers in the chair.
