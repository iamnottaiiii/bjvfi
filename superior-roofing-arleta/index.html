Spotted any of these?

Common signs your roof is asking for attention. If one sounds familiar, give us a call.

Water stains

Brown spots on ceilings or walls usually mean water is getting past the roof somewhere above.

Missing pieces

Shingles or tiles on the ground after wind are a clear sign the roof needs a visit.

Curling or cracking

Sun-baked shingles curl and crack with age, opening the door for leaks.

Granules in gutters

Piles of gritty granules mean the shingles are wearing thin.

Sagging spots

A dip in the roofline can signal trapped moisture or structural trouble. Do not wait on this one.

Rising energy bills

A failing roof lets Valley heat straight in, and your AC pays for it.
