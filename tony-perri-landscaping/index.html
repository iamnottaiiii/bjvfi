About Us

A West Newton family business since 1985

Tony Perri Landscaping Inc is a family owned, full service landscaping business run from Cherry Street in West Newton. We are fully insured and serve both residential and commercial properties.

What started as lawn care grew into complete landscape service: gardens designed and planted, stone and hardscape work, tree pruning and removal, irrigation, and snowplowing with sanding through the winter months. Our crew of twelve keeps the same properties looking sharp all year, so customers deal with one familiar team instead of a different contractor every season.
