About BuildFort

Built on craftsmanship.

BuildFort is a Sherman Oaks based tile and handyman company serving Los Angeles, Santa Monica, the San Fernando Valley, and nearby cities. Our craftsmen specialize in tile of all sizes, bathroom and shower work, and the full range of home repairs.

Who we are

Led by Alex. Backed by a crew that cares.

Alex L. leads the BuildFort team, and customers mention him by name again and again: knowledgeable, professional, clean, and fair on price. Natalie and the rest of the crew bring the same standard to every job, from a full bathroom remodel to a single curtain rod.

The team includes experienced plumbers, electricians, painters, and carpenters, so most projects never need a second contractor.
