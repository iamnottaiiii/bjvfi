Services

Heating, cooling, and the property care around them. Pick a category to see what is included.

  • Central air conditioning repair: no-cool calls, weak airflow, frozen coils, and electrical faults diagnosed and fixed.
  • Heating repair: furnaces and heat pumps that will not start or will not keep up.
  • Thermostat and control repair: wiring, sensors, and low-voltage controls.
  • Same-visit fixes: most common parts and repairs handled in one trip.
  • Seasonal tune-ups: full system check before summer heat or winter cold.
  • Coil and filter service: cleaning and replacement that keep efficiency up and bills down.
  • Refrigerant and electrical checks: levels, connections, capacitors, and safety controls.
  • Preventive plans: ask about keeping your system on a regular care schedule.
  • System replacement: right-sized air conditioners, furnaces, and heat pumps.
  • Air handler work: indoor unit service and replacement.
  • Thermostat upgrades: modern, easy-to-use controls set up for you.
  • Startup testing: every install is tested and walked through before we leave.
  • Property maintenance: ongoing care for rental properties and homes.
  • Small repairs: the handyman-level fixes that keep a property in shape.
  • Recommendations you can trust: honest advice on what to fix now and what can wait.
Service fees: $95 for local customers (within about 45 minutes), $125 for non-local calls, waived when you hire us for the repair. Payment by cash, card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Venmo, or Zelle.
