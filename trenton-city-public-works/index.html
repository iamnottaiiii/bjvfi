Keeping Trenton Running, Every Day

The Department of Public Works maintains the streets, water systems, and public spaces of Trenton, Michigan, and runs the services residents rely on week after week.

Explore City Services Contact the Department

Resident Services at a Glance

The core programs run out of the Public Works building on West Road.

Street Maintenance

Report bad road surfaces and street light outages so crews can respond quickly.

Water Services

Water leak reporting, hydrant meter permits, cross-connection control, and the annual water quality report.

Recycling Programs

Electronics recycling and household hazardous waste collections for Trenton residents.

Leaf Collection

Seasonal curbside leaf collection schedule published each fall by the department.

Transfer Station

1801 Van Horn Road, open Tuesday, Friday, and Saturday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM.

Special Pickup Line

Schedule large-item pickups by calling the message line at (734) 675-5496.

After-hours emergencies: call the Police Department at (734) 676-3737. Final water readings: (734) 675-6510. Fax: (734) 675-8688.

Department Services

Programs maintained by the Trenton Department of Public Works.

Water System

Water leak reports, hydrant meter use permits, and backflow preventer tester listings for irrigation systems.

Street Lights

Report street light outages directly to the department for repair scheduling.

Road Surfaces

Report bad road surfaces and potholes anywhere in the city.

Hazardous Waste

Household hazardous waste collection events keep chemicals out of the watershed.

Electronics Recycling

Drop-off recycling for electronics and e-waste at designated collections.

Watershed Info

Watershed information and the cross-connection control program protect local waterways.

Pay Service Fees

Service fees can be paid through the city; contact the department for current options.

Bloomdale Cemetery

The department maintains Bloomdale Cemetery for the city.

Hours of operation: Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM. The Transfer Station at 1801 Van Horn is open Tuesday, Friday, and Saturday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM.
