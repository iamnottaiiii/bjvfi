Why Manlius Nails&Spa

A little luxury, right in the village

Manicures, pedicures, enhancements and spa treatments in a calm, clean salon on Seneca Street. Friendly techs, hundreds of colors, and an eye for detail.

Everything nails, plus the spa extras

From a quick polish change to a full spa pedicure with hot stone massage, from classic acrylics to hand-painted nail art. Add an eyebrow wax or a tension-relieving massage and make it a real escape. Walk-ins are welcome and groups and bridal parties can book ahead.

Gel & dip specialists

Long-wearing gel manicures, dip powder sets in every shade, and acrylic and gel extensions built to your length and shape.

Spa pedicures done right

The classic and lavender spa pedicures are local favorites: thorough, unhurried, and finished with a hot towel and massage.

Nail art

Hand-painted designs, ombre, French tips and seasonal looks. Bring a photo and the techs will make it happen.

Waxing

Eyebrows shaped to frame your face, plus lip, chin and facial waxing. A longtime favorite of regulars.

Massage

Tension-relieving massages, including hot stone options with the spa pedicure. Leave lighter than you arrived.

Our promise

Clean tools, honest work, zero rush

Reviewers keep saying the same things: the pedicures are thorough, the gel lasts, the staff is kind, and the salon is spotless. People drive in from miles away and keep coming back.
