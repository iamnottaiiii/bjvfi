About the practice

A personal practice on Little River Turnpike.

HENRY PARK, DDS is the general dentistry practice of Dr. Henry Insuk Park, located in the Heritage Center business area at 7611 Little River Turnpike in Annandale, Virginia. The practice focuses on everyday dental care for patients of all ages.

Dr. Park holds an active Virginia dental license and is enrolled in the Medicare program. His NPI record lists general practice dentistry as his specialty, with licensing also on record in Maryland and New York. He is recognized in Northern Virginia Magazine's dentist listings.

Care here is by appointment only. The office accepts Delta Dental PPO and Delta Dental Premier insurance plans, and payment by Visa or Mastercard.
