Our story

A local crew that treats your yard like its own

Atlantic Lawn & Landscape, Inc. is a lawn care service based at 450 State Rd 13 N in St Johns, Florida. We work in the neighborhoods around State Road 13 and across the St Johns area, doing the kind of steady, detail minded yard work that only comes from doing it every week.

Our approach is simple. Show up when we say we will. Edge every line. Trim what needs trimming. Blow off the walks and the driveway before we leave. A yard is the first thing people see when they pull up to a home or a business, and we take that personally.

Whether you need a weekly mowing route, a one time cleanup after a storm, or a full refresh of tired beds and patchy turf, one call puts the whole job in our hands.
