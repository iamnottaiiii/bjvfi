Our story

A repair shop on wheels

Castle Mobile Auto is a mobile auto repair service based in Somerdale, New Jersey. Instead of asking you to rearrange your day around a repair shop, we bring the shop to you.

Our technicians arrive with the tools, parts and diagnostic equipment needed for the most common repairs, from brake jobs and batteries to oil changes and starters, and complete the work in your driveway, at your office or wherever your car is parked.

  • Service at your home or workplace across Somerdale and South Jersey
  • Evening and weekend hours, open seven days a week
  • Upfront pricing confirmed before any work begins
  • Most repairs finished in a single visit
