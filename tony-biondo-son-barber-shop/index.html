Ledger entry no. 3 • The family

The Family Ledger

Father, son, and the chairs between them

Tony Biondo & Son Barber Shop is a family-owned barbershop at 5133 Pleasant Ave in Fairfield, Ohio. Public review listings describe vintage barbering tools and photographs on the walls, a relaxed and welcoming room, and regulars who are greeted by name and treated like family.

Entry I • The founder's chair

Tony's chair

Reviewers name Tony as the founder behind the shop's name and reputation. "Tony is a great barber, and he has really reasonable prices," one public review reads, advising visitors to call ahead during the week.

Entry II • The son's chair

Angelo's chair

Angelo Biondo is the son in the shop's name, and the most-named barber in public reviews: "Angelo is the best barber in town bar none," "Angelo Biondo never fails to do a fantastic job," and "My son asks to go see Angelo." Reviewers praise his consistency, his patience with kids, and his flat tops.

Entry III • The next chairs

Josh and the regulars

Public reviews also name Josh behind a chair: "Both Josh and Angelo are great barbers, highly recommend." Generation after generation, Fairfield families keep coming back. One reviewer reports going for "40 plus years"; another says her family has come "since the late 70's"; a third dates visits to "probably since 1979."

Dates above are per public reviews, not shop records

What the room feels like

Buzzing clippers, friendly chatter, vintage decor. Reviewers describe "great barbers, great conversation," a clean and comfortable shop, and a no-frills visit where the cut does the talking.

How regulars describe it

"Like a big Italian familia." "A true man's place to get your haircut." "The only place I go for a cut." Loyalty here is measured in decades, not visits.

Straight from the book: the honest word

Not every entry is glowing, and the ledger keeps those too. Several reviewers mention long waits at busy times: "you may find a long wait at times," and "sure you may have to wait a little extra depending on when you go in, but more than worth it." One public 1-star account describes a bad Saturday morning wait and says he would not return. The pattern across 67 reviews is clear: this is a popular, old-school shop, so call ahead and expect a wait when it is busy.
