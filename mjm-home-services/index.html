Our story

A local crew with a squeegee and a standard.

MJM Home Services started with a simple observation: Ansonia has beautiful older homes with big windows, and most of them had not been properly cleaned in years. We set out to be the crew that treats every pane like it is the front of our own house.

We do not rush the glass. Rushing is how streaks happen.

Based on W Road in Ansonia, we work the Valley and the surrounding towns, showing up when we say we will and leaving homes brighter than we found them. Window cleaning is our core, and gutters, pressure washing, and screens grew naturally from customers asking, while you are here, can you also do this?

That is the whole business model: do the job right, answer the phone, and come back when people call again. Most of our work comes from neighbors telling neighbors. We plan to keep it that way.

The MJM standard

Every job follows the same checklist: protect the floors, clean the whole window system (not just the glass), detail the edges, and walk the finished work with the customer. If a step gets skipped, the job is not done.

Local, not a franchise

No call center, no rotating strangers. You talk to the people who will actually be on the ladder. We know Ansonia streets, the old window styles, and how the Valley weather treats glass.

Straightforward pricing

Free on site quotes with a firm price before work begins. The sample prices on our services page are real starting points, and the final number is agreed up front, in writing if you want it.

What we believe

Three rules on every ladder.

1. Protect the home first

Drop cloths, shoe covers, and careful ladder placement. Your landscaping and floors matter more than our speed.

2. Clean the whole window

Glass, frames, sills, tracks, screens. A sparkling pane in a dirty frame is a job half done, and we do not do halves.

3. Inspect together

The final walkthrough is with you, not without you. If you see something we missed, we fix it before we leave.
