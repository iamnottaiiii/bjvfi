Attic Insulation

In the San Fernando Valley, the attic is usually the biggest source of heat gain. New or upgraded attic insulation slows that transfer, easing the load on your air conditioner and keeping rooms more even in temperature.

  • Evaluation of your current attic insulation level
  • Upgrade options matched to your attic type
  • Clean, professional installation with minimal disruption
Call for a quote

Blown-In Insulation

Blown-in insulation is installed with specialized equipment that fills cavities completely, covering gaps and irregular spaces that batts can miss. It is a common choice for attic top-offs and hard-to-reach areas.

  • Even coverage across the full attic floor
  • Reaches around pipes, wires, and fixtures
  • Fast installation, often completed in a day
Call for a quote

Insulation Removal

Old, compressed, or contaminated insulation underperforms. Removal clears the way for a fresh install that performs the way it should, and gives a clean view of the attic for air sealing first.

  • Safe removal of old attic insulation
  • Clean-up so the space is ready for new material
  • Pairs well with air sealing before reinstall
Call for a quote

Air Sealing

Insulation slows heat flow, but air leaks move heat directly. Sealing gaps around recessed lights, plumbing penetrations, duct chases, and attic hatches makes every inch of insulation work harder.

  • Sealing of common attic air-leak points
  • Best performed before adding new insulation
  • Helps with drafts, dust, and uneven room temperatures
Call for a quote
