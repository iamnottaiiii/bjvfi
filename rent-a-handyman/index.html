About

A local hand for local homes

Rent-A-Handyman is a handyman service based at 10 Dogwood St in Laurys Station, Pennsylvania. The work is simple by design: show up on time, do the job right, and leave the home cleaner than it was found.

Big contractors pass on the small stuff. That is exactly where a handyman earns their keep. The dripping faucet, the door that will not latch, the room that has needed paint for two years. These jobs matter because you live with them every day.

How every job is handled

  • Clear scope agreed before work begins
  • Drop cloths and shoe covers in your home
  • Walkthrough with you before wrapping up
  • Debris and old materials hauled away
