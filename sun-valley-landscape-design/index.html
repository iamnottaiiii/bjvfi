Services

What the crew builds

Services below reflect the work customers describe in public reviews: design, planting, stonework, beds, and irrigation, installed by a crew that takes pride in the finish.

Landscape design

Every project starts with a plan built around what you asked for, plus ideas you might not have considered. Customers describe a patient design process with revisions until the plan feels right.

  • On site consultation and measurements
  • Plant and material palettes for heat and low water use
  • Revisions until you are satisfied with the plan
  • Front yards, backyards, and curb appeal makeovers

Native planting & raised beds

Low maintenance layouts with plants that handle San Fernando Valley heat. Raised beds add structure, color, and easy growing space where the ground is tough.

  • Native and drought tolerant plant selection
  • Raised planting beds built to order
  • Shade trees for cooler, calmer yards
  • Mulch and soil prep for healthy roots

Walkways & stone

Curved stone walkways and hard edges that give the planting room to breathe. Customers mention walkways added exactly as planned, finished with a swept clean site.

  • Curved stone walkways and stepping paths
  • Edging, borders, and bed frames
  • Decorative rock and gravel areas
  • Clean, tidy finish on every install

Drip irrigation

Efficient drip lines installed with the landscape, so new plantings get exactly the water they need and nothing more.

  • Drip system design and installation
  • Zoned watering for beds, trees, and lawn areas
  • Timers and adjustments after install
  • Water wise layouts that cut daily upkeep

How we work

Listen first, then build

What comes through in public feedback is a simple way of working: the designer takes time to understand what you want, sketches a plan that includes ideas you had not thought of, and revises it with you until it is right.

Then the install crew shows up and treats the yard like their own. Customers describe hardworking installers who take pride in their work, finish on schedule, and leave the site swept clean of soil and clippings.

It is the kind of work neighbors notice. One reviewer said their new yard drew so many compliments they were proud to show it off.

Built for the valley

Landscapes that fit California

Sun Valley summers are hot and dry, and water matters. That is why the work leans on native plants, drip irrigation, and layouts that look full and colorful without demanding daily attention.

Whether it is a front yard that needs curb appeal or a backyard that needs shade, the goal is the same: a yard that works for your life, not a second job.
