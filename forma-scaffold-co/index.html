Services

The full scaffold package

Services below are taken from the company's own published business description. Select a tab for details, then call (747) 208-0055 for pricing on your job.

Scaffold rental

Rent the access equipment your job needs without buying and storing it. FORMA's rental offering covers frame scaffolding and related access gear for construction, painting, and maintenance work.

  • Sized to your site and your schedule
  • Delivery and pickup coordinated with your crew
  • Payment by Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, or cash

Scaffold erection and dismantle

FORMA's crews erect your scaffold system and dismantle it when the work wraps. One contractor handles the whole cycle, so setup and teardown stay on the project timeline.

  • Professional erection on your schedule
  • Safe dismantle and site clear when done
  • Available around the clock, 24/7

Scaffold engineering and design

For permitted work and complex access, FORMA provides scaffold engineering and design services: planned systems that meet the job's structural and safety requirements.

  • Engineered access plans for complex sites
  • Design support for permitted scaffold work
  • One point of contact from plan to teardown
