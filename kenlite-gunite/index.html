Why gunite

The strongest way to build a pool

Gunite is dry-mix concrete sprayed at high velocity over steel reinforcement. The result is a dense, monolithic shell that can take any shape, curves, benches, Baja shelves, raised spas, and hold it for decades.

Structural strength

A properly shot gunite shell is one continuous structure with no cold joints, giving it the compressive strength concrete pools are known for.

Any shape you draw

Because it is sprayed, not poured into forms, gunite follows freeform designs: lazy rivers, grottos, beach entries, and vanishing edges.

Built to outlast

With correct steel placement, curing, and a quality interior finish, a gunite shell routinely serves 30 years or more before major work.

How we work

From layout to plaster in five steps

Site layout

We stake the pool shape, check setbacks and utilities, and confirm access for equipment.

Excavation

The dig is cut to spec with proper slopes, then steel reinforcement is tied to engineering.

Gunite shoot

Our crew sprays the shell in one continuous application for a monolithic structure.

Cure and finish

The shell cures, then tile, coping, and your choice of interior finish complete the pool.
