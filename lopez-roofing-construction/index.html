Full roof replacement

Complete tear off and replacement with architectural shingles, built to current Florida building code for wind uplift and water intrusion.

  • Full tear off to the deck
  • Decking repair where needed
  • Synthetic underlayment and ice and water shield
  • Architectural shingles, ridge vent, new flashing
  • Permit handling and final inspection
02

Roof repairs

Leak tracing and targeted repairs that solve the actual problem, from lifted shingles to failed flashing around chimneys and walls.

  • Leak detection and diagnosis
  • Shingle replacement and resealing
  • Flashing, valley, and vent repair
  • Pipe boot and skylight curb fixes
03

Metal roofing

Standing seam and metal panel systems that handle Florida sun, salt air, and hurricane season while cutting cooling costs.

  • Standing seam metal panels
  • Exposed fastener systems for outbuildings
  • High wind rated attachment
  • Energy reflective finishes

Storm season

Storm and wind damage

Fast response after storms: emergency tarping, photo documentation for insurance, then repair or full replacement.

  • Emergency tarping and dry in
  • Damage documentation for claims
  • Insurance paperwork guidance
  • Full restoration and replacement
05

Roof inspections and maintenance

A documented checkup that catches small problems before they become ceiling stains, with photos and a plain English report.

  • 21 point visual inspection
  • Photo report with findings
  • Gutter and drainage check
  • Maintenance plan and priorities

Gutters, fascia, and soffit

The edges of the roof do the quiet work of moving water away. We repair and replace the trim that protects your eaves.
