About the Shop

A Small Shop on Skidmore Street

Stanley Automotive is an auto repair shop at 101 Skidmore Street in Cleves, Ohio. We keep our operation simple: you tell us what your car is doing, we look it over, and we tell you straight what it needs and what it can wait on. Call to confirm

Small towns run on word of mouth, and a repair shop is no different. We would rather send you home with a five-minute fix and no bill than sell you work your car does not need. That is how neighbors earn neighbors.

"No work done without your okay. No surprises on the bill. That is the whole business plan."

We work on everyday cars and light trucks: brakes, oil service, tires, batteries, diagnostics, cooling systems, and the maintenance that keeps a vehicle dependable year after year. If a job is outside what we do well, we will tell you that too, and point you somewhere that fits. Call to confirm
