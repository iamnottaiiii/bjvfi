What to ask before you book

With no review history to lean on, these questions protect you with any mobile mechanic:

1. What is the diagnostic fee, and is it applied to the repair?

2. Do you warranty parts and labor, and for how long?

3. Will I see the old parts that were replaced?

4. What is the total price before you start the work?

5. Can you text or email the quote in writing?
