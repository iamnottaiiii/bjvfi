The shop

Family hands, honest wood

Mauro And Sons Cabinetry, Inc. operates from our workshop at 420 7th Ave NE in Ruskin. We are a general contracting team with cabinetry at the center of everything we do.

Our approach is simple: measure carefully, build solidly, and install like it is going in our own home. No shortcuts in the joinery, no surprises in the invoice.

When you call (941) 726-8831, you talk directly to the people who will build your project.

How we work

From first call to final walk-through

  • Measure and plan. We visit, measure, and talk through layout, materials, and budget.
  • Build in the shop. Your cabinets are built to your exact dimensions and finish.
  • Install and inspect. We install, adjust, and walk the job with you before we call it done.
