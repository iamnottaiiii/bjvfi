Services

Flooring services, picked by tab

Every service starts with a free estimate. Call (818) 927-7764.

Hardwood installation

Solid and engineered hardwood, nailed or glued down over a properly prepped subfloor. We handle acclimation, layout, and clean cuts around every doorway and vent.

  • Oak, maple, and popular engineered lines
  • Stairs, landings, and hallway runs
  • Baseboards and shoe molding finished to match

Laminate flooring

Budget friendly floors that look like wood and handle daily life. Floating click lock install with proper underlayment for quiet, comfortable steps.

  • Scratch resistant surfaces for pets and kids
  • Fast installs, often room by room
  • Matching transitions at every doorway

Vinyl plank (LVP)

Waterproof luxury vinyl plank is the go to for kitchens, bathrooms, and rentals. Warm underfoot, quiet, and easy to keep clean.

  • 100 percent waterproof cores
  • Realistic wood grain textures
  • Ideal for kitchens, baths, and laundry rooms

Refinish and repair

Keep the wood you have. We sand out scratches and wear, fix damaged boards, and apply a fresh stain and protective finish.

  • Sanding and screening for worn floors
  • Stain color changes and natural finishes
  • Board replacement and squeak repairs

Tile flooring

Porcelain and ceramic tile for entries, kitchens, and bathrooms, set level with tight, even grout lines.

  • Large format and wood look tile
  • Entryways, kitchens, and bathrooms
  • Grout color matched to the tile

Our story

A flooring crew that treats your home like its own

Gonzalez custom flooring inc is a flooring contractor based in North Hollywood, California, serving homes across the San Fernando Valley. We are installers first: the people who measure your rooms are the people who show up to do the work.

Our approach is simple. We look at the subfloor before we promise anything, because a great floor starts underneath. We lay out boards so seams fall where they should, keep lines straight across long rooms, and finish edges and transitions like they matter, because they do.

Most of our work comes from referrals and repeat customers. That only happens when the floors look right and the experience feels easy, so that is what we aim for on every job.
