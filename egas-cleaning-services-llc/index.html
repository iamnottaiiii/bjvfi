Our story

A Rahway crew that does the hard jobs

EGAS CLEANING SERVICES LLC started with a simple idea: a cleaning company that says yes to the work others skip. House cleaning and office janitorial pay the bills, but the crew built its name on the heavier jobs like construction cleanup, floor waxing, and power washing across Rahway and Union County.

Today the team serves homeowners, landlords, and small businesses from 299 Wilson Ave in Rahway, NJ. Landlords call for move in and move out turns. Contractors call for final cleanups. Families call because the floors, the carpets, and the exteriors all look right again.
