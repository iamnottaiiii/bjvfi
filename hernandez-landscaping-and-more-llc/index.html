Our story

A local crew that treats your yard like our own

Hernandez Landscaping And More LLC is a locally run landscaping company based on Estona Drive in Silver Spring, Maryland. We serve homeowners across Silver Spring and the surrounding neighborhoods with full-service lawn care, cleanups, planting, and snow removal.

We started with a simple idea: do honest work, show up on time, and treat every property with respect. That is how a one-time cleanup turns into a customer who calls us back every season.

Whether it is a weekly mow, a spring refresh, or a full yard rescue, you get the same care: a clear quote up front, a crew that tidies up before leaving, and a yard you are proud to come home to.
