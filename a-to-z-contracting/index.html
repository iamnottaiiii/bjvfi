Our Story

A local company that does what it says.

Rooted in Pasadena

A to Z Contracting is based at 685 203rd St in Pasadena, Maryland, and serves homeowners across Anne Arundel County and the surrounding area. Being local means showing up, answering the phone, and standing behind the work.

18 years in the trade

With 18 years of experience reported on Angi, the company brings long practice to home remodeling: basements, bathrooms, kitchens, repairs, and additions. That is the full span behind the name, A to Z.
