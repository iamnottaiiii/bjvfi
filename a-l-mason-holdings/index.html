How a pour goes

The standard sequence for residential concrete work.

STEP 01

Grade and form

Excavate, compact the base, and set forms to the right elevation and slope so water runs off, not in.

STEP 02

Pour and screed

Place the mix, screed it level, and cut control joints where the slab needs room to move.

STEP 03

Finish and cure

Broom or decorative finish, then proper curing so the surface hardens dense and resists freeze-thaw.
