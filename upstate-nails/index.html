Our story

Upstate Nails opened its doors on Chili Avenue and has been building a loyal clientele ever since, some for over ten years. A BBB-accredited business with an A+ rating, run by owner My-Le Nguyen, the salon is known for beautiful, clean work, pleasant staff and results that are consistently impressive. From simple French tips to sculpted sets and eyebrow waxing, guests leave feeling taken care of.
