Our story

Plainfield's working contractor

Authentic General Contracting is based at 1328 E 2nd St in Plainfield, New Jersey, and works across Union County. We are a fully licensed and insured general contractor, which means one accountable team for your project instead of a rotating cast of subcontractors you never met.

What customers mention most is not the big stuff. It is the jobsite care: carpets covered, floors protected with paper, and crews that communicate clearly from the first visit to the final walkthrough.

We keep regular hours, Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, so you can always reach a real person about your project.
