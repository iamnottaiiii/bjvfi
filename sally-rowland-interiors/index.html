Our story

A Florence studio built on one belief: the best rooms start with the people who live in them.

Sally Rowland Interiors is an interior design studio on Dogwood Lane in Florence, Kentucky, serving homeowners across Northern Kentucky and the Cincinnati area. Local directories credit the studio with decades of design experience, and its work centers on livable, layered rooms rather than showroom looks.

Projects range from single-room refreshes to full furnishing plans and finish selections for kitchen and bath remodels. Every engagement starts with a conversation, not a catalog: how you use the room, what you already love, and where you want to land.
