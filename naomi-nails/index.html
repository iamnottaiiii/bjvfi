how we bloom

Twenty years of trust,
one manicure at a time

Some of our clients have been coming to Naomi for their manicures for twenty years. That kind of loyalty is not an accident. It grows from careful work, honest prices and a team that genuinely likes people.

The salon is kept spotless, the pedicure chairs are made for lingering, and the polish wall holds a shade for every mood and every occasion, from everyday neutrals to garden-party brights.

Whether it is your weekly standing appointment or your very first visit, you will be greeted warmly, listened to carefully, and sent out the door feeling a little more polished than when you walked in.
