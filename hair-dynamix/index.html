Our story

A Gravesend staple since 2008

Hair Dynamix opened its doors on Kings Highway in 2008 as a small, single location neighborhood salon. More than fifteen years later, it is still here, still independent, and still built on the same idea: great hair without the fuss.

The salon serves the Gravesend community with a small team of stylists who take time to listen. Regulars describe being treated like family, and many have been in the same chair for years.

No rushed appointments, no upsell pressure. Just careful cuts, honest color advice, and reasonable prices.
