Our Story

A professional image for handyman work

Home Medic of N Ky set out to bring a professional image to handyman services in Northern Kentucky, and 26 years later that is still the mission.

We are in this business full time, not part time. That means when you call, you reach people whose whole workday is devoted to home repairs, not a side gig squeezed between other jobs. Quality work, delivered on time, at a fair price: that is the whole business model.

Our niche is smaller jobs, not major remodeling or construction projects. The drywall patch, the tile repair, the cabinet that will not close, the outdoor light that went dark: the fixes that make a house feel like home again. One call, one visit, done right.
