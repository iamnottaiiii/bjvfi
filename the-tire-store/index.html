Our story

Three decades on Boston Post Road

The Tire Store has been selling and servicing tires in East Lyme since 1994. That is more than thirty years of flats fixed, winters swapped, and drivers sent back onto Boston Post Road with confidence.

The shop describes its crew as carrying over 70 years of combined experience, and it shows. Reviewers talk about patient explanations, wide tire selections with honest pros and cons, and prices that stay reasonable without cutting corners on the work.

It is a shop built on repeat business. Customers say they would not buy tires anywhere else, and out-of-town drivers who limped in with a problem left as fans. One reviewer even notes a military discount, so ask when you call.

"Customer satisfaction A+. I will be recommending this place to anyone."
