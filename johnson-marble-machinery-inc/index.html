Chapter 02 / Our story

Since 1980, one idea: make stone easier to work.

Johnson Marble Machinery has always believed the way to grow the stone industry and help fabricators is to make it easier and faster to process natural stone. That belief has guided every machine to leave the North Hollywood campus.

Established in 1980 and incorporated in California, the company designs, builds, and sells stone industry machinery from 7325 Varna Ave in North Hollywood. The cutting and finishing of stone is one variable a shop can control, and our tools exist to give fabricators more consistency, more productivity, and more profit.

"If you are not successful, we are not successful."FROM JOHNSON MARBLE MACHINERY COMPANY MATERIALS

Today more than 700 JMM machines are installed in North America, running in some of the highest-production shops in the United States. Employing the latest technology, built with simplicity in mind: that is the philosophy carried over more than four decades in business.
