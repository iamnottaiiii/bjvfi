Services

What we fix, coat, and finish.

Residential concrete work across Buffalo and Western New York. If your slab is cracked, pitted, or just worn out, start here.

01

Concrete Repair

Cracks, pits, and spalled areas are not just ugly, they let water in and get worse every winter. We cut out the damage, stabilize the slab, and repair it so the fix lasts.

  • Crack repair and stabilization
  • Pitted and spalled surface repair
  • Fixing damage left by previous contractors
02

Concrete Resurfacing

When the slab is sound but the surface is worn, resurfacing gives you a like-new finish without the cost and mess of a full tear-out. A popular choice for driveways, patios, and walkways.

  • Driveway and walkway resurfacing
  • Patio resurfacing and refresh
  • Power washing before and after
03

Epoxy Floor Coatings

Our signature finish. After repair and resurfacing, a professional epoxy coating turns a tired garage or basement floor into a tough, stain-resistant surface that is easy to clean and looks sharp.

  • Garage floor epoxy coatings
  • Basement floor coatings
  • Repair plus coating in one project
04

Patios & Outdoor Concrete

Outdoor slabs take a beating from Buffalo winters. We resurface worn patios, then power wash and reseal so the new surface stands up to the weather.

  • Patio resurfacing and resealing
  • Power washing and surface prep
  • Return visits to refresh older work
