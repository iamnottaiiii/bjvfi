About

A downtown shop with next level standards

VIP the Next Level Barbershop sits at 1021 Hamilton St in Allentown, PA, serving men of all ages with professional cuts and grooming.

The shop is known for its attention to detail and friendly atmosphere: traditional and contemporary styles, careful finish work, and an experience clients describe as comfortable from the moment they walk in.

Walk-ins are welcome, the doors stay open late six days a week, and the barbers take pride in every head that leaves the chair.
