How a pool project flows

The standard sequence for a new pool or a remodel. Confirm exact steps and timeline with the office.

1

Site visit

Yard measurements, access check, and a written quote for the work.

2

Design and permits

Shape, depth, tile, and equipment choices, then permits with the city.

3

Build or remodel

Excavation, steel, plumbing, plaster or new finish, then equipment set.

4

Fill and handoff

Water chemistry balanced, equipment walkthrough, care schedule set.
