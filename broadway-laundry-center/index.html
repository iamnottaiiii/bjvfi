About

A South Boston laundry that does it all

Photo soon

Broadway Laundry Center sits at 142 W Broadway in the heart of South Boston, a neighborhood where triple-deckers, young families, and busy professionals all share the same problem: laundry piles up faster than the week allows.

That is why the shop runs as a full-service laundry, not just a row of machines. Walk in and do it yourself, drop a bag at the counter for wash and fold, or get on the pickup and delivery route and never carry a basket again.

  • Self-serve washers and dryers for do-it-yourself days
  • Wash and fold drop off with per-pound pricing
  • Pickup and delivery on a local South Boston route
  • Open six days a week, 8 AM to 6 PM
