Our Story

A Family Trade in Reseda

PALACIOS HARDWOOD FLOORS is a hardwood flooring company based at 8146 Yarmouth Ave in Reseda, CA, led by Ricardo Palacios Jr. The company holds an active California Flooring and Floor Covering contractor license and ranks among the top licensed contractors in the state.

The shop describes itself as expert in sanding and refinishing existing hardwood floors, staining, repairs, mouldings, and installation of pre-finish solid, engineered, and laminate materials, plus sports floors with game lines, logos, and lettering, and yearly maintenance.

For years the team has served Reseda and the greater San Fernando Valley with one standard: a floor should look better the day they finish than the day it was first installed.
