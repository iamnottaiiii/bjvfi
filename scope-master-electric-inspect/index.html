Why get your electrical inspected

Aging panels, overloaded circuits, and worn wiring are some of the most common hazards in older buildings. An inspection documents what is working, what is failing, and what should be prioritized.

🔍

Safety first

An inspection looks for fire and shock hazards before they turn into emergencies, from overheated breakers to faulty grounding.

🧾

Clear documentation

Get a straightforward rundown of issues found, ranked by urgency, so you can plan repairs with confidence.

🏠

Buyer and owner ready

Buying, selling, or renovating? A documented electrical inspection helps everyone involved make informed decisions.

Services

Electrical inspections and repairs for homes and businesses in Irwindale and the San Gabriel Valley.

Full-scope visual and functional inspection of panels, breakers, outlets, switches, grounding, and visible wiring, with issues documented and ranked by urgency.

Assessment of breaker panels, load capacity, and subpanel condition, plus upgrades and replacements when a panel is outdated or unsafe.

Dead outlets, flickering or dimming lights, breakers that will not stay on, and intermittent faults traced to the source and repaired.

Ceiling fixtures, recessed lighting, dimmer switches, and outdoor security lighting installed or replaced.

New outlet and switch installs, GFCI and AFCI protection, dedicated circuits for appliances, and code-minded wiring corrections.

Level 2 home charging circuits planned and installed, with load calculations to confirm your panel can handle the added demand.
