About

Four decades of Cincinnati comfort.

Miami Heating & AC is an air conditioning contractor at 3992 Kenkel Ave in Cincinnati, Ohio. The company has been in business since 1980 and is led by president Thomas H. Lawless.

Self-employed and owner-operated, the business services all brands of heating and cooling equipment for residential and light commercial customers. It is insured and bonded and holds state and city licenses.
