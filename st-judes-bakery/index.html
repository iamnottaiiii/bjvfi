Our Story

A family bakery, a neighborhood tradition.

Panaderia tradicional

Traditional Recipes, Honest Baking

St Jude's Bakery is a family-run panaderia in the heart of Midlothian. Our promise is simple: the highest quality pan dulce, cakes, and conchas, made with traditional recipes and served with genuine warmth.

Every morning starts early, with ovens warming before sunrise and the first trays of conchas coming out as the neighborhood wakes up. We bake the breads we grew up with: soft, sweet, and made to be shared around a kitchen table with coffee.

Over the years our tres leches cakes have become the centerpiece of countless birthdays and celebrations across the south suburbs, and our little shop on 147th Street has become a daily stop for families who know good pan dulce when they taste it.

Clean cases, friendly faces, reasonable prices, and bread that tastes like home. That is St Jude's Bakery, and we are glad you found us.
