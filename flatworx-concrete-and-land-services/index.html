land people.

Flatworx Concrete and Land Services is a Jacksonville, Florida company doing both halves of the job: the ground work and the concrete work.

Who we are

A local crew that does it all

Flatworx Concrete and Land Services is registered as a Florida limited liability company and operates out of Jacksonville at 1404 Sherbrooke Ct. We serve homeowners and businesses across Northeast Florida, including St. Johns County.

The idea is simple: most concrete problems start under the concrete. Bad base, bad drainage, bad compaction. Because our crew also does land work, grading, clearing and site prep, we fix the ground first and then pour on top of it.

That means fewer callbacks, fewer cracks and driveways that still look right years later. Call (904) 383-2876 and talk to the people who will actually do the work.

A slab is only as good as the ground under it. So we take care of both.
