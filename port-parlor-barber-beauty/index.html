Our story

A port-town shop with salon polish

Port Parlor opened its doors in 2018 on W Channel Street, right in the heart of San Pedro. The idea was simple: keep the easy energy of a neighborhood barbershop, but add the range of a real salon, so a dad, a teenager, and a bride-to-be can all get what they need in one visit.

That mix is what guests talk about most. Alongside classic barbering, the shop offers salon services including color and makeup, which makes it a practical stop before events and special occasions.

  • Established 2018 in San Pedro
  • Barbers including Rob, Ruben, Drew, and Robert
  • Known for clean fades and attention to detail
  • Family friendly, from young children to adults
  • Good vibes, good music, cold drinks on the house
