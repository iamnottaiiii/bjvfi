Chapter one · The work

Comfort is a system, not a box

Heating and cooling equipment only works as well as the service behind it. Here is how Neal Heating & Air conditioning Northridge keeps Valley homes comfortable through every season.

Cooling

Air conditioning service and installation

When the Valley hits triple digits, your AC is the hardest working machine in the house. Neal Heating & Air conditioning Northridge diagnoses and repairs air conditioning systems, and installs new equipment when repair no longer makes sense.

Heating

Furnaces and heating repair

Desert nights get cold. Furnace tune-ups, repairs, and replacements keep heat reliable when you need it, without surprise breakdowns in January.

Care

Maintenance that pays for itself

Coils cleaned, filters checked, refrigerant levels verified. Routine maintenance catches small problems early and keeps energy bills from creeping up.

Call before the season turns. A tune-up in spring costs far less than an emergency repair in August.

A Northridge HVAC contractor

Where

Based on Reseda Blvd

Neal Heating & Air conditioning Northridge operates from 9330 Reseda Blvd in Northridge, California, serving homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley. The San Fernando Valley is one of the hottest residential markets in the country, which makes reliable heating and cooling less of a luxury and more of a necessity.

What

Heating and cooling, end to end

As an air conditioning contractor, the work covers the full comfort system: cooling equipment for the long hot season, heating for the cold desert nights, and the maintenance in between that keeps everything running efficiently.
