Our story

A Maryland corporation built on specialty craft

Specialty Interior Construction, Inc. is an incorporated Maryland company based at 8268 Preston Court in Jessup. We are a specialty contractor working across commercial, healthcare, and institutional interiors throughout the region.

Our discipline list reads like a specification section because that is how we work: metals, plaster and gypsum board, flooring, painting and coatings, countertops, ceilings, wall finishes, finish carpentry, and tile, each executed to the standard the drawings demand.
