Our Story

A local crew with a simple standard.

Yalla Construction is a construction company based in La Crescenta-Montrose, California, at 2629 Foothill Blvd. The name says it all: yalla means let us go, and that is how the crew works.

What sets the company apart is not a slogan. It is the pattern in every review: the team arrives on time, works the job as contracted, and finishes when promised. Clients describe the crew as professional, trustworthy, and honest, and one reviewer singled out Mike for customer service that aimed at nothing less than a completely pleased customer.

The work ranges from full bathroom renovations and hardwood flooring to painting, drywall repair, and complete restoration after water leaks. Residential clients, landlords, and property managers alike keep the number handy.

If you have a remodel, a repair, or a property that needs attention, call and describe the job. You will get a straight answer.
