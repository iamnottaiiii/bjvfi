Fishtown Painting is a painting company based at 1107 Marlborough St in Philadelphia's Fishtown neighborhood.

Who we are

Neighbors painting
for neighbors

We work out of Marlborough Street, right in the heart of Fishtown, painting the rowhouses, storefronts, and apartments that make this neighborhood what it is.

Old brick needs a different touch than new drywall, and we know the difference. Prep comes first, the paint goes on clean, and we walk the job with you before we call it done.
