The short story

A shop where trailers get fixed

J&T Road Services LLC sits at 103 Sherman Ave in Newark's South Ward, a few minutes from the New Jersey Turnpike, Newark Liberty International Airport and the Port of New York and New Jersey. The business is listed as a trailer repair shop, the kind of place fleets and owner-operators call when a trailer needs body work, running-gear repairs, lights or a pre-trip once-over.

In the freight business, a trailer sitting is money leaking. The job of a shop like this is simple: diagnose it, quote it straight, fix it, and get the rig back on its route.
