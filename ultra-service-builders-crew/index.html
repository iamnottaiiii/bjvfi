Our Story

A crew, not a call center

"Most remodel stress comes from handoffs. We keep the handoffs to zero."

Ultra Service Builders Crew works out of Van Nuys, in the middle of the Valley neighborhoods we serve. We are the crew that shows up: the same faces from demo day to the final walkthrough, because the person who tears it out should be accountable for how it goes back together.

Homeowners hire us when they are tired of chasing subcontractors. Our model is simple on purpose. One crew handles framing, drywall, tile, paint, and finish work in house, and we coordinate the licensed trades for electrical and plumbing so you never play project manager.

Every job starts with a walkthrough at your home, not a phone estimate. We measure, we listen to how you actually live in the space, and we put the scope in writing before work begins. If something is not in the scope, we talk about it before we touch it.

We keep sites clean, we keep neighbors in mind, and we keep our word on the schedule. That is the whole business plan.
