About

A Duarte mason with decades behind the trowel

Reds Masonry (listed as Steven E Crawford dba Red's Masonry) is a masonry contractor based at 1944 Baylor St in Duarte, California.

Directory listings describe a highly skilled crew specializing in custom brick, stone, and tile work, with more than 25 years of experience, a California state contractor license, and bonding and insurance in place.

Customers know the business for its attention to detail: excellent ledger stone installations, fast email responses, next-day estimates, and pricing reviewers call very reasonable.

If your project needs brick, block, stone, or pavers done right the first time, this is the crew to call.
