Our story

A Brooklyn chair built on trust

Hair By Yossi Full Service Salon serves the Gravesend neighborhood from 717 Avenue U in Brooklyn. The salon built its name the old fashioned way: one head of hair at a time, with cuts and color clients keep coming back for.

As a full service salon, the chair covers the whole menu. Precision cutting, dimensional color, balayage, keratin smoothing and extensions all happen under one roof, so you never have to split your hair routine across town.

Families are welcome here. Reviewers call out the salon as a good spot for kids, and the team keeps the chair calm and friendly for younger clients and first timers alike.
