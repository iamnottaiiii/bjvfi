Our story

A small salon with a big regular crowd.

Mandy's Beauty Nails is a self owned neighborhood salon on 86th St in Bath Beach, Brooklyn. Clients describe a clean, calm space where the team takes its time and treats people kindly.

Q

What makes Mandy's different?

Clients say the technicians are extremely gentle, the salon is clean, and the work is detailed. One reviewer noted great service with no pressure around tipping.

Q

Is it good for nail art?

Yes. Reviews say the team can recreate any design clients show them, often better than the reference photos, and the results last.

Q

Who works here?

A small team that clients describe as hardworking, sweet and professional. Regulars call it their number one favorite nail salon in Brooklyn.
