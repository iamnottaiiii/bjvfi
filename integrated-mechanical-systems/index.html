01 Who we are

Integrated Mechanical Systems is a full-service commercial heating, ventilating, and air conditioning contractor based in the San Gabriel Valley. Our field staff of over 75 qualified technicians is supported by an engineering and design department, a sheet metal fabrication shop, and service and preventive maintenance divisions that emphasize quality and safety.

We are dedicated to the satisfaction of our contractors and clients, employing the most cost-effective design approach combined with high quality workmanship.

02 Our philosophy

Our company philosophy is to pursue select projects that showcase our talents, and to develop long-term relationships with the general contractor, developer, and owner or tenant, built upon quality, integrity, and communication.

We are signatory to the Local #105 Sheet Metal Workers Union and the Local #250 Air Conditioning & Refrigeration Worker's Union.
