Finish samples, Levels 1 to 5

Drywall finishes are graded Level 1 through Level 5. Below is a plain-language guide to what each level means. Finish samples shown are Sample illustrations, call to confirm which level fits your project.

L1Sample

Level 1

Tape set in compound at joints. Used where the surface stays hidden, like attics and service areas.

L2Sample

Level 2

One coat of compound over tape. Common behind tile or in garages where appearance is secondary.

L3Sample

Level 3

Two coats over tape plus one over fasteners. Ready for heavy texture or thick wallpaper.

L4Sample

Level 4

The standard smooth-wall finish for most homes. Ready for flat or eggshell paint.

L5Sample

Level 5

Skim coat over the whole surface. The flattest finish, for glossy paint and critical lighting.

Straight answers

How do I get a quote?

Call (440) 327-8627 and describe the rooms and the work. Photos of the space help. Exact pricing is given after seeing the job, never guessed here.

What finish level do I need?

Most living spaces get a Level 4 finish, which is the standard smooth wall under flat or eggshell paint. Ask about Level 5 if you have large windows, glossy paint, or critical side lighting.

Do you handle repairs or just new installs?

Both. Patching holes, fixing cracks, and repairing damaged sections are everyday work. Call to confirm the specific repair you need.
