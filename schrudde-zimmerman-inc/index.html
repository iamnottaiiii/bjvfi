SEC. 03

Sixty years on the bid list

Schrudde and Zimmerman Inc was established in 1965 as a general contractor in Northern Kentucky. It is a veteran-owned Subchapter S corporation, led today by president Mark N. Zimmerman, working from 1671 Park Road in Covington.

The company has spent six decades doing the unglamorous, exacting work of commercial and institutional construction: building alterations, interior renovations and ground-up projects for colleges, schools, hospitals and government agencies across Kentucky and Southern Ohio.

Public records tell the story. Listed in the Blue Book of Building and Construction since 1998. A member of ACI, the Allied Construction Industries. Registered for federal work with a CAGE code. And in February 2026, selected by the Kenton County Board of Education as the best evaluated bidder for the Ryland Heights Elementary renovation, a $10.6 million project.
