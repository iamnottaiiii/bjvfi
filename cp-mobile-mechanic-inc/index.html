About CP Mobile Mechanic

A Sun Valley mobile repair company built on a simple idea: the mechanic should come to the car, not the other way around.

Who we are

CP MOBILE MECHANIC, INC is a California corporation, active and based in Sun Valley since 2012. Instead of a shop you drive to, we run a shop on wheels that drives to you.

That matters most when it matters most: a truck that dies on the 5, a car that will not start in a grocery store lot, a work vehicle that has to be running by Monday morning.

Public feedback points to the same themes again and again: quick arrivals, real know-how with trucks, and straight, courteous service.

Why mobile works

No tow bill. If the car will not move, the repair still comes to it.

No waiting room. You stay home, stay at work, or stay with your family while we work.

Straight pricing. Free quotes by phone before we roll, so there are no surprises.

Truck know-how. Work trucks and diesels are our comfort zone, not an afterthought.
