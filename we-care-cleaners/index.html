Our story

A Main Street
institution since 1991

Over 30 years of pressing, mending and delivering for Stoneham families, one garment at a time.

Since 1991

The cleaners that
treats clothes like family

We Care Cleaners has served Stoneham from 319 Main Street since 1991. In that time, the shop has built a reputation for getting the hard jobs done: stains other shops give up on, alterations that fit just right, and garments returned without a mark.

Customers describe service that is fast and accurate, quality that matches shops in pricier towns, and prices that stay honest. Many have been coming for years, and some drive in from well beyond Stoneham.

What sets us apart

Skill you can see
in every seam

The owner knows fabrics, and it shows. Reviewers praise the team's ability to get out stains, mend holes, remove gum and beautify clothes, all with no damage to fabric or leather.

Friendly counter service, free Wi-Fi while you wait, private lot parking, and delivery to your front door round out the experience.
