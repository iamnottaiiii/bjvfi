About us

A Jacksonville contractor built on management discipline

Jsl construction llc is an active Florida limited liability company based in Jacksonville, led by owner Jeffrey Lott Jr.

Our story

Management first, always

Jsl construction llc was built around a simple observation: the difference between a smooth build and a painful one is rarely the tools, it is the coordination. So we made project management and coordination our specialty, not an afterthought.

We serve both commercial and residential clients across the Jacksonville area, running active projects through Procore since June 2023. That means real schedules, real budgets, and a single accountable point of contact from the first site walk to the final walkthrough.

As an active Florida LLC with its principal place of business in Jacksonville, we are local, reachable, and invested in the work we put our name on.

  • Active Florida LLC, principal place of business in Jacksonville
  • Project management and coordination as a core specialty
  • Commercial and residential market experience
  • Projects managed through Procore since 2023
