    How it works

    From dusty swatch to showroom fresh

    A simple four step visit, tuned to your fabric.

    Inspect

    We identify the fiber, check dye stability and map stains before a drop of solution touches the fabric.

    Pre-treat

    Spots and high traffic zones get a targeted pre-treatment matched to the soil type.

    Deep clean

    Thorough cleaning of the full piece: body, cushions, arms, backs and crevices.

    Finish

    Rinse, groom the pile, deodorize on request, and walk you through drying and aftercare.
