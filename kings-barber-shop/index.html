About

The story of King's Barber Shop

King's Barber Shop on Paramount Boulevard in Downey is a neighborhood shop for fades and beard lineups. Barbers Sara and Joseph are familiar faces to the regulars.

Located at 12043 Paramount Blvd, Downey, CA 90242, the shop serves Downey and the surrounding neighborhoods. Walk-ins and regulars are both welcome, and the team is happy to talk through the cut before the clippers come out.
