02 / Why it matters

Your ducts are the lungs of your home

Every time your furnace or AC runs, air travels through your ducts. If they are coated in dust, pet hair, and debris, that is what your family is breathing. A professional cleaning removes the buildup at the source.

Cleaner indoor air

Removing trapped dust and allergens helps every breath feel easier, especially for allergy and asthma sufferers.

Our services

Better system efficiency

Clear ducts let your HVAC system move air freely, which can lower strain on the equipment and on your energy bills.

Our services

A safer dryer vent

Lint-packed dryer vents are a leading fire risk at home. A thorough vent cleaning protects your family and your dryer.

Full ductwork cleaning

We clean the supply ducts, return ducts, registers, and grilles throughout your home using professional agitation and vacuum equipment.

  • Inspection first

    We walk the house with you and check vent condition, so the estimate matches the actual work.

  • Every vent and return

    Each supply register is removed, cleaned, and replaced. Returns get the same treatment.

  • Main trunk lines

    The big runs that feed your whole house get scrubbed clean of years of settled dust.

Dryer vent line cleaning

Lint builds up inside the vent run from your dryer to the outside wall or roof. We clear the full length of the line and check the exterior cap.

  • Full-length clearing

    We brush and vacuum the entire vent run, including bends where lint loves to pack in.

  • Exterior cap check

    We make sure the outside vent opens freely and is not blocked by nests, lint, or damage.

  • Faster dry times

    A clear vent lets your dryer do its job in one cycle, which saves energy and wear on clothes.

Photo soon

If your dryer takes two cycles to finish a load, a packed vent is the most common cause.
