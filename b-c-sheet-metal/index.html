The trade

Sheet metal contractors cut, bend, and join thin metal into ductwork, flashing, gutters, and custom parts. Common materials include galvanized steel, aluminum, copper, and stainless steel.

It is precision work. A duct that does not seal wastes energy, and flashing that does not fit lets water in. Good shops measure carefully and fabricate to the job, not the other way around.
