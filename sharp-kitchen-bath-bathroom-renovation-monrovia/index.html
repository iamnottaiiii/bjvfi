Sharp Kitchen Bath Bathroom Renovation Monrovia is a kitchen and bath remodeling shop on E Lemon Ave, serving Monrovia and the San Gabriel Valley.

The workshop way

We treat every job like it has our name on it

Because it does. We are a local remodeling shop, not a call center with subcontractors. When you call, you talk to the people who will be in your home.

Kitchens and bathrooms are the two rooms where craftsmanship shows, and where shortcuts cost the most. A crooked tile line stares at you every morning. A missed waterproofing step shows up as a ceiling stain a year later. We built our reputation on getting those details right the first time.

  • Local crew, local accountability
  • Written estimates before any demo
  • Job sites left clean, every day

Anyone can demo a bathroom in a day. The craft is in what you cannot see: the waterproofing, the slope, the seal.

Finishes that last
