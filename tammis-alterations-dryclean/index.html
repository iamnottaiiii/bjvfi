Our story

Tammi's Alterations & Dryclean

A neighborhood tailor shop in the heart of Jonesboro, Georgia.

Sewn with care on Main Street

Tammi's Alterations & Dryclean serves Jonesboro and the surrounding Clayton County area from our shop at 309 N Main St. We combine old fashioned tailoring skill with convenient dry cleaning service, so customers can get a perfect fit and a fresh press in one trip.

Whether it is a wedding dress that needs to fit perfectly for the big day, a suit that needs a sharper line, or everyday clothes that deserve careful cleaning, we treat every garment like it matters.

Women owned
