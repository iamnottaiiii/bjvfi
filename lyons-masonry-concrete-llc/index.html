About

A local crew
that shows up.

Lyons Masonry & Concrete LLC is a concrete contractor based on Baltimore Pike in Springfield, PA.

Our story

Concrete is simple.
Doing it right is not.

Anybody can pour concrete. Doing it so it stays level, drains properly and survives twenty Pennsylvania winters takes prep work most people never see: the base, the forms, the mix, the finish, the cure.

Lyons Masonry & Concrete LLC was built on that unglamorous part of the job. We are a local Springfield crew serving Delaware County homeowners and businesses with driveways, patios, foundations, masonry and repairs. We measure twice, quote straight and clean up after ourselves.

When you call, you talk to the people doing the work. When we give you a price, it is the price. That is the whole business model.
