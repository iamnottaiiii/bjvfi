What we do

Full service, one roof

D&A Autoglass is a Newark auto glass shop built around speed and honesty. Cracked windshields, smashed side windows, and broken back glass get replaced fast, often the same day you call.

  • Windshield replacement
  • Windshield chip and crack repair
  • Side and back glass replacement
  • Semi truck windshields
  • Mobile glass service
  • ADAS recalibration
  • Glass vacuuming and cleanup
  • Insurance claim help

The team also runs a mobile service, so the work can come to you anywhere in the area. Semi truck windshields are a specialty, and every job ends with the broken glass vacuumed out of your car.
