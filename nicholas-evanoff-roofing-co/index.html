Our Story

A Harrisburg roofing name since 1951

Nicholas Evanoff Roofing Co has been part of the Harrisburg roofing trade for more than seven decades. What began as a local roofer serving Dauphin and Cumberland counties grew into a full-service company handling shingles, rubber, metal roofing and custom sheet metal fabrication.

Through it all, the formula never changed: show up, tell the truth about the roof, and do the work like it is your own house. That is why the Evanoff name kept getting passed from one neighbor to the next, and why directory reviewers still call the company dependable with great pricing.

Today the company serves homes and businesses across the Harrisburg area from 2311 N Front St, offering free project estimates, emergency services, and the kind of craftsmanship that only comes from decades on the roof.
