Our story

The Roth Ave standard

Neighborhood first

PROCUTS BARBERSHOP sits at 1301 Roth Ave in Allentown, serving the east side community one chair at a time. No appointments needed for most services, just walk in and take a seat.

Customers describe the shop as super clean with great people and great music, and one reviewer put it simply: "One of the best barbers around. I have been coming here for cuts ever since I moved to Pennsylvania."

What we stand for

  • 01 Consistency: the same sharp cut every visit, from every barber.
  • 02 Cleanliness: sanitized tools and a fresh station for every client.
  • 03 Respect for your time: efficient service without rushing the details.
  • 04 Fair pricing: quality cuts at prices the neighborhood can afford.

For the whole family

From a first haircut to a grandfather's weekly trim, the shop welcomes all ages. Kids cuts are handled with patience, and seniors get a standing discount.
