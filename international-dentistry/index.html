Services

General dental services

The everyday care your smile needs. Call (410) 633-4649 with questions.

1

Exams and cleanings

Routine checkups, professional cleanings, and x-rays when needed. Prevention is the cheapest dentistry there is.

2

Fillings

Tooth-colored composite fillings that repair cavities while blending with your natural teeth.

3

Crowns and bridges

Damaged teeth rebuilt and missing teeth replaced with sturdy, natural-looking restorations.

4

Dentures

Full and partial dentures fitted for comfort, so you can eat and speak with confidence.

5

Root canals

Relief for deep tooth pain, saving your natural tooth whenever possible.

6

Extractions

Teeth that cannot be saved, removed as gently as possible with aftercare guidance.

7

Emergency care

Sudden pain, swelling, or a broken tooth: call (410) 633-4649 and we will get you in as soon as we can.
