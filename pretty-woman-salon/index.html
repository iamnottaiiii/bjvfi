our story

A little boutique salon
in Mill Basin

Pretty Woman Salon has quietly become one of the most loved beauty spots in the neighborhood.

how it started

Built on brows,
loved for everything

Pretty Woman Salon is a boutique salon on Avenue N in Mill Basin, Brooklyn. It began with a simple idea: do brows really, really well, and treat every client like a regular.

Word travels fast when the work is this good. Clients come from different Brooklyn neighborhoods and even from other states for the eyebrow work alone, and many of them have been coming back for years.

Inside, the mood is calm and welcoming. The salon is known for a clean, relaxing, positive environment and staff who are caring, friendly, and professional, taking the time to make every visit a good one.

  • Famous for precise, perfect eyebrow work
  • Nails, lashes, and waxing all in one visit
  • Clean, relaxing space with caring staff
