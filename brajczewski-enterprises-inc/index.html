What we do

Services

Plumbing and contracting services for homes and businesses. Call to discuss your project and get a straight answer on scheduling.

01

Leak repair

Dripping faucets, supply line leaks, and visible pipe leaks tracked down and repaired. Stopping a leak early protects walls, floors, and cabinets from water damage.

02

Drain cleaning and clogs

Slow drains, backed up sinks, and clogged toilets cleared. If a drain keeps coming back, ask about what is causing the repeat blockage.

03

Fixture installation

Faucets, toilets, sinks, and shower fixtures installed and set correctly so they work the first time and keep working.

04

Water heaters

Water heater troubleshooting, repair, and replacement. No hot water or not enough of it is worth a phone call the same day.

05

Pipe repair and replacement

Damaged, corroded, or burst pipe sections repaired or replaced, with attention to the rest of the line so the fix lasts.

06

Commercial and contract plumbing

Ongoing and project based plumbing work for commercial properties, handled with a contractor’s eye on schedule and scope.

Our story

A Jersey City plumbing company

Brajczewski Enterprises Inc is a plumbing and contracting company based at 84 Morris St in Jersey City, NJ. The company works across the residential and commercial sides of the trade, serving homes and businesses in Jersey City and the wider Hudson County area.
