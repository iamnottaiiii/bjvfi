Who we are

The contractor behind the pavement.

Most drivers never think about who built the road beneath them. Around Hebron and Boone County, there is a good chance the answer is the Michels family. Since 1983, Paul Michels & Sons has manufactured asphalt paving mixtures and put them down on roads, lots, and driveways across Northern Kentucky.

We are not just pavers. The company works in water, sewer, and pipeline construction, plus power line and communications work, the heavy civil jobs that keep a growing county running. One family, one yard on Youell Road, and a crew that treats every job like it has the family name on it. Because it does.

When the company name is your family name, every job is personal.

Our story

Three generations.
One yard on Youell Road.

Paul Michels & Sons Inc began in 1983 in Hebron, Kentucky, and it has stayed a family affair ever since. Kentucky Secretary of State records show the Michels family still running the company decades later: Joseph E. Michels as president, Catherine Michels as secretary-treasurer, Paul J. Michels as vice president, with Vincent, John, and Pete Michels among the directors.

The company grew from paving into a full heavy-civil contractor. The asphalt plant on Youell Road manufactures the paving mixtures the crews lay, and the same family name stands behind water, sewer, and pipeline work across the region. When your name is on the trucks, the license, and the plant, shortcuts are not an option.

1983 to today: same family, same yard, same standard.

What the records tell us: the company is verified as licensed with the Kenton County license board, holds a BuildZoom score of 90, and is classified under heavy and civil engineering construction. The rest of the story, the jobs, the crews, the decades of Northern Kentucky pavement, is best heard from the family themselves.
