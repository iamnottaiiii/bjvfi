Our story

Riverview concrete,
done like neighbors

Hardscapes Innovation Inc is a concrete contractor based in Riverview, Florida, pouring driveways, patios, and pool decks across the Tampa Bay area.

i

Prep is the product

Anyone can pour concrete. The difference is what happens before the truck arrives: the base, the forms, the drainage plan. We obsess over the parts you will never see, so the parts you do see stay beautiful.

ii

Fair prices, kept promises

Customers mention two things again and again: good customer service and good prices. That is not an accident. We quote honestly, show up when we say we will, and finish what we start.

iii

Local, start to finish

Based at Tall Palm Pl in Riverview, we work where we live. When your driveway is our reputation on your street, every pour matters.
