When to call an electrician

Frequent breaker trips

A breaker that keeps tripping is telling you something. Get it checked.

Flickering or dimming lights

Often a sign of a loose connection or an overloaded circuit.

Warm outlets or odd smells

Heat, buzzing, or a burning smell near outlets calls for prompt attention.

Remodels and additions

New space usually needs new circuits, planned before the drywall goes up.

Working with a local electrician

Questions worth asking any electrician

  • Are you licensed and insured for this work?
  • How do you price the job: flat rate or time and materials?
  • Will the work require a permit or inspection?
  • What is the expected timeline from start to finish?
  • How do you handle surprises found during the work?
  • Do you guarantee your workmanship?
