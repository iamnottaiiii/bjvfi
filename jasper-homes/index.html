Our story

A family business, building where we live

Rooted in Cincinnati

Jasper Homes, Inc. describes itself as a family owned business that builds and remodels homes throughout the greater Cincinnati area. The company is based at 904 Winged Foot Way in Cincinnati, Ohio.

Family builders work differently. There is no corporate office three states away and no rotating cast of project managers. The name on the truck is the name on the contract, and reputation is built one home, and one neighbor, at a time.

“We build the way we would want our own home built.” Sample line
