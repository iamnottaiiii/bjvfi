A small botanica, kept with devotion

Judas Botanica is a modest neighborhood shop of velas, remedios, yerbas and devotional goods. We keep simple hours and a quiet shop, and we are here on Saturdays for whoever comes by.

Plan your Saturday

Saturday is the day we open the doors, from 10 AM to 6:30 PM. The other six days the shop is closed. If you are coming from across town, call ahead and we will confirm the hours.
