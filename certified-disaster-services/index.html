Our Restoration Process

A clear, step-by-step plan from the first call to the final walkthrough.

1

Emergency Call

Call (513) 583-0668 any time. We assess the situation and dispatch a crew to your property.

2

Inspection and Moisture Detection

Technicians inspect the damage and measure hidden moisture behind baseboards, walls, and flooring.

3

Water Extraction

Standing water is extracted quickly using powerful pumps and truck-mounted equipment.

4

Drying and Dehumidification

Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure completely to prevent mold growth.

5

Cleaning and Sanitizing

Affected areas are cleaned and sanitized to remove contaminants and odors.

6

Restoration

Damaged drywall, flooring, and finishes are repaired, returning your property to pre-loss condition.
