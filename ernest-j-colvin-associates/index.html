Our Story

Founded on service

Dr. Ernest James Colvin, a native of Rock Hill, South Carolina, earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Howard University in 1968 and built a thriving practice in Baltimore over nearly five decades. A pioneer in dentistry and in business, he was known as a visionary in the city of Baltimore.

Patient reviews note the office continues today under the care of Dr. Delwynn Chavis, DMD, who longtime patients say has served them for more than 25 years.
