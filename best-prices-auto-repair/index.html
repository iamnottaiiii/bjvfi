Best prices auto repair sits on Loisdale Rd in Springfield, VA, doing what independent shops do best: real mechanical work at prices regular people can handle.

What we're about

No frills. No markup. Just the fix.

Dealerships charge dealership prices. We are the alternative. Best prices auto repair is a working shop for working people: engine jobs, mechanical repairs, and everyday maintenance, explained in plain language and priced to be fair.

Reviewers describe the work as professional and good mechanical, and customers have trusted the shop with serious engine jobs. That trust is the whole business: every car that leaves running right is another driver who comes back.

We will also be straight with you about this: like any shop, we have not pleased everyone. Our reviews page shows the full picture, the good and the bad, so you can decide for yourself.
