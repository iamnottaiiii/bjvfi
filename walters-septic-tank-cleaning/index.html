Why pumping matters

Out of sight should not mean out of mind

A septic tank that is never pumped is a backup waiting to happen. Regular pumping protects your drain field, your yard, and your wallet.

  • Removes built-up solids and sludge before they clog the system
  • Helps the drain field absorb water the way it was designed to
  • Catches small problems during service before they become big ones

How often should you pump?

Most households pump every 3 to 5 years, but tank size, household size, and water use all change the math. The surest schedule is the one a septic pro sets for your system. Call us and we will help you figure it out.
