Hardwood Installation and Refinishing

Solid and engineered hardwood for living rooms, bedrooms, and hallways. We install, sand, stain, and seal to a smooth, lasting finish.

  • Site finished and prefinished hardwood
  • Sanding, staining, and refinishing of existing floors
  • Subfloor prep and moisture testing
Get a Hardwood Quote

Luxury Vinyl Plank and Tile

Waterproof vinyl plank and tile that mimics wood and stone at a friendlier price. A smart pick for kitchens, basements, and busy family homes.

  • Rigid core waterproof planks
  • Glue down and click lock installation
  • Basement and below grade approved options
Get a Vinyl Quote

Laminate Flooring

Scratch resistant laminate in wood and stone looks, installed floating over most existing subfloors for a fast, clean upgrade.

  • Wide plank and hand scraped styles
  • Underlayment and sound dampening
  • Quick installs, often in a single day
Get a Laminate Quote

Ceramic and Porcelain Tile

Tile for kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and entryways. Level, sealed, and grouted for wet areas and heavy traffic.

  • Floor tile and backsplashes
  • Shower and tub surrounds
  • Heated floor mat installation
Get a Tile Quote

Carpet Installation

Soft, stain resistant carpet for bedrooms, stairs, and family rooms, with premium padding for comfort and noise control.

  • Stain and pet friendly fibers
  • Stair runners and full stair installs
  • Furniture moving available
