Our Story

A Buffalo concrete contractor built on a simple idea: pour it right, prep it right, and stand behind it.

Built for Buffalo, By People Who Live Here

Fun Boys Enterprises is a concrete contractor based in downtown Buffalo, NY. We work across Buffalo and Erie County on the projects other crews rush: driveways, sidewalks, patios, garage floors and the repairs that keep them going.

Western New York is one of the toughest places in the country for concrete. Freeze-thaw cycles, road salt and heavy lake-effect snow punish shortcuts. That is why we are stubborn about base prep, drainage and the right mix for the job. A slab is only as good as what is under it.

When you call (716) 240-9920, you talk to the people doing the work. We show up when we say we will, keep the site clean, and finish on schedule.
