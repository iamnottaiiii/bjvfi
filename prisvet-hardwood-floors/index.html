Services

One trade, done properly

Choose a service to see what is included. For pricing on your rooms, call for an estimate.

Hardwood installation

Solid and engineered hardwood, installed over a properly prepped subfloor so boards stay flat and quiet.

  • Oak, maple, walnut, and other domestic species
  • Solid and engineered plank options
  • Subfloor prep, leveling, and moisture checks
  • Stairs, transitions, and trim to match

Refinishing and resurfacing

Worn hardwood does not always need replacing. Sanding and a fresh finish can bring tired floors back.

  • Sanding and screening of existing hardwood
  • Stain color changes and natural finishes
  • Scratch, gap, and board repairs
  • Dust-conscious sanding process

Laminate flooring

A practical wood look for busy households, at a friendlier price point than solid hardwood.

  • Wood-look laminate in popular tones
  • Moisture-aware product guidance
  • Underlayment and quiet-step options
  • Clean transitions to adjoining rooms

Porcelain tile and tiles

For kitchens, baths, and entries, porcelain tile that stands up to water and daily wear.

  • Porcelain and ceramic tile installation
  • Kitchens, bathrooms, and entryways
  • Proper underlayment and waterproofing
  • Grout and layout planning
