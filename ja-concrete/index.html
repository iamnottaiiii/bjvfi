From first call to final cure

What to expect when you hire J&A Concrete for a project. These are typical stages of a residential concrete job.

1

Call and Describe

Reach (818) 398-7829 with the size of the area and what you want built. Photos of the site help.

2

Site Visit and Estimate

The space is measured and you get a written-style quote covering prep, materials, and finish.

3

Prep Day

Old concrete comes out, the base is graded and compacted, forms and rebar go in.

4

Pour and Finish

Concrete is placed, screeded, and finished to the texture you chose, with clean control joints.

5

Curing

The slab cures undisturbed for the recommended time so it reaches full strength.

6

Walkthrough

A final look together to confirm the finish, drainage, and edges are right.

7

Aftercare Tips

Simple guidance on sealing and care to keep the surface looking good for years.

8

Future Projects

One contractor who already knows your property makes the next job easy.
