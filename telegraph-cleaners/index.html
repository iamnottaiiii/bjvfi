Our story

The little shop at Telegraph Village

Telegraph Cleaners has been part of the Telegraph Road corridor for 38 years, a true mom and pop shop in the Shops at Telegraph Village where Telegraph Road meets South Kings Highway. No chain, no call center, just the people behind the counter who know the regulars by name.

What keeps people coming back is simple: the work is done right and the price is fair. Independent consumer ratings give the shop an 83% superior rating across 12 reviews, and price comparisons found it about 14% lower than the average Alexandria dry cleaner.

The stories customers tell say the rest. One had at least eight suits altered here over three years. Another needed a last minute fix and the shop stayed late to finish it. That is the kind of place this is: the one you trust with the suit for the big day and the shirts for every day.
