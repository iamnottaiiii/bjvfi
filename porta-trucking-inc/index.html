Our Story

An independent carrier, rooted in Pittsburgh

Porta Trucking Inc has been hauling freight out of Pittsburgh since 2010. We started small at 291 Gibb St with a simple plan: run legal, run on time, and treat every shipper like the only one that matters.

Still independent, still hands on

More than a decade later, that is still how we operate. We are an active FMCSA registered carrier (USDOT 906192, MC 419326) running interstate for hire. The person who answers when you call (412) 292-4349 is the person who schedules your truck. There is no maze between your freight and the driver moving it.

Pittsburgh manufacturers, distributors, and warehouses trust Porta because we know this city: the mill schedules, the bridge detours, the docks that open early and the ones that never answer. Local knowledge is a service, and we provide it free with every load.
