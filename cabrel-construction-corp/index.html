Services

One trade, done thoroughly

Pick a service area to see what is included. Every service starts with an on-site look and a written estimate.

Repair and patching

Targeted repairs for damage that does not need a full re-stucco. Patches are built up in layers and hand-textured to blend with the existing wall.

  • Hairline and settlement crack repair
  • Impact chips and gouges
  • Hollow or delaminated patch replacement
  • Stucco around windows and doors
  • Foundation line stucco repair
  • Patch texture blending

Re-stucco and new stucco

When repairs alone will not cut it, we take the elevation back to a sound surface and build it back up with a full stucco system.

  • Full elevation re-stucco
  • Three coat stucco systems
  • Stucco over block and masonry
  • New construction stucco
  • Addition and dormer tie-ins
  • Texture selection: sand, dash, smooth

EIFS and specialty finishes

Exterior insulation and finish systems plus decorative finishes that give you insulation value and a crisp, modern look.

  • EIFS installation and repair
  • Insulation board and base coat
  • Acrylic finish coats
  • Decorative bands and trim
  • EIFS damage patching
  • Finish recoating

Color match and refinishing

The part of the job that decides whether a repair disappears. Color is matched to your weathered wall, not to a fresh sample card.

  • On-site color matching
  • Full-wall recoating for even tone
  • Integral color finish coats
  • Paint-ready stucco prep
  • Faded elevation refresh
  • Texture-matched spot refinishing

Cracks and waterproofing

Water is stucco's worst enemy on Long Island. We address the cracks and the moisture paths behind them, then seal the surface properly.

  • Crack mapping and evaluation
  • Flexible crack fillers
  • Waterproof coatings and sealers
  • Flashing and penetration sealing
  • Efflorescence treatment
  • Moisture damage repair

Inspections and estimates

  • Written repair scope
  • Straightforward pricing
  • Pre-purchase stucco check
  • Insurance documentation photos
  • Maintenance advice

Why stucco matters here

Long Island is hard on exterior walls

Freeze-thaw cycles, nor'easters, and salt air open up cracks and push moisture behind the finish. Small stucco problems become big wall problems when they are ignored. We fix the small ones properly and rebuild the big ones from a sound base.
