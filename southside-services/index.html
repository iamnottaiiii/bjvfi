A country garage on Route 125, run the old way.

The ledger says: Southside Services sits at 2887 OH-125, just west of Bethel in Clermont County, Ohio. Public business records list the shop as founded in 1977, which puts decades of wrenches behind it. One public listing describes a small crew of 1 to 4 people.
How they work: Verified customers describe a shop that keeps all records on their vehicles, gives a call in the morning with what it will do and a quote, and has the car ready the same day. The words that come up again and again are honest, fair, and timely. One reviewer writes that the shop is consistently honest with customers and very fair with prices, and that the work is top tier.
Known for: Custom exhaust work. A longtime verified customer says to ask for Eric or Rob for any work you need on your vehicle. Another reviewer brought an engine-swapped Land Rover Discovery for exhaust, praised the bends and placement, and warns to call ahead because the shop stays well booked with exhaust jobs.
