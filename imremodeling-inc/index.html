Our story

How IMREMODELING INC approaches every job, from the first phone call to the final walkthrough.

A contractor that treats your home like our own

IMREMODELING INC is a general contractor based in Burbank, California. We focus on residential remodeling, the kitchens, bathrooms, additions, and updates that make a house work for the people living in it.

We keep our crews small and our communication direct. You get a written scope, a real timeline, and one person to call with questions. No handoffs, no surprises.

Burbank homes range from 1920s bungalows to mid-century ranches, and we plan every project around the bones of the house in front of us.

  • Written estimates, alwaysScope, materials, and timeline in writing before work begins.
  • Clean job sitesDust control, daily cleanup, and protection for the rooms we are not touching.
  • One point of contactYou talk to the person running your job, not a call center.
  • Permits done rightWe handle city paperwork so inspections pass the first time.
