Our story

A local crew with heavy-duty tools and a simple promise: leave every surface better than we found it.

Blasius e Souza Cleaning is a Lynn, Massachusetts cleaning and blasting outfit built on hands-on work. We started with pressure washers and elbow grease, then added blasting equipment when customers kept asking for a way to strip paint and rust without the grind.

Today we handle the jobs most cleaners turn down: corroded trailers, graffitied walls, driveways black with oil, and siding green with mildew. We show up on time, protect what should not be blasted, and clean up when we are done.

We serve Lynn and the surrounding North Shore, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. If your project needs a surface stripped, washed, or prepped for a fresh coat, we would like to hear about it.

Why blasting?

  • Reaches pits and pores that sanding misses
  • Strips paint and rust in a fraction of the time
  • Dustless media keeps the work area cleaner
  • Leaves the right surface profile for new coatings
  • Ideal before repainting or sealing
