The Editorial, A Builder's Story

Brick by brick, tradition by tradition.

Great Traditions is a Cincinnati home builder known for neighborhoods that fit their place. The company's Stetson Square development in Corryville earned Community of the Year from the Home Builders Association of Greater Cincinnati in 2006, and the company has been recognized as an urban visionary and city builder by the Niehoff Urban Studio at the University of Cincinnati.

Today the Traditions team builds townhomes, condos, and single-family homes in communities like Vintage Club in Montgomery, Mosaic in Mason, and the Village at Rivers Pointe in Hebron, Kentucky. Every detail counts because a dream home is not just a house. It is a master-crafted home that reflects your lifestyle, tastes, and personality in your favorite location.

About Great Traditions

Over 45 years of great places and great spaces.

Great Traditions, part of the Traditions Building and Development Group, has been creating great places and spaces for people to live in Greater Cincinnati for over 45 years. The company builds custom homes and whole communities, from urban infill in Corryville and Northside to luxury neighborhoods in Montgomery, Mason, Madeira, and Hebron, Kentucky.

The builder's Stetson Square development in Corryville earned the Community of the Year award from the Home Builders Association of Greater Cincinnati in 2006, and the company was recognized by the Niehoff Urban Studio at the University of Cincinnati for its leadership as an urban visionary and city builder. Traditions Building Group has also been named the No. 1 small volume home builder for customer experience in the United States.
