About the salon

A Liverpool studio built
on happy clients

Infinity Salon is a boutique hair salon on Old Liverpool Road, known across the area for color expertise and genuine hospitality.

Led by Heidi

Clients describe Heidi as kind, professional, and intuitive, with a gift for seeing both what suits you and what you are dreaming of. Many have followed her chair for over a year and recommend her to everyone they know.
