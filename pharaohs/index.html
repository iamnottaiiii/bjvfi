Services

Every service includes a consultation so you leave with exactly the cut you pictured.

At Pharaohs, the menu is built around what a barbershop should do best. Precision cutting, careful beard work, and the kind of hot towel shave that slows the day down.

  • Men's Haircut. Consultation, precision cut, wash and style.
  • Skin Fade. Seamless fade blended down to the skin.
  • Beard Trim. Shape and detail with hot lather edging.
  • Hot Towel Shave. Traditional straight razor shave with hot towels.
  • Kids Cut. Patient, friendly cuts for young gentlemen.
  • Lineup / Edge Up. Crisp lines, sharp temples, clean neckline.
  • Head Shave. Smooth razor shave with soothing finish.
  • Gray Blending. Subtle color service that softens gray.

Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments keep the wait short. Call (323) 453-7460 to book your chair.

About Pharaohs


Pharaohs serves Lynwood with precision haircuts, beard work, and classic shaves. Bring a photo or just an idea, take a seat, and let the barbers handle the rest.
