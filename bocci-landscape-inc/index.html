Our story

Rooted in Woodmere
since 1999

BOCCI LANDSCAPE INC is a small, locally owned landscape design company. Big enough to handle full property projects, small enough that the person who designs your yard knows your name.

Who we are

A designer led crew, not a mow and go

Public records list Carmelo Bocci as president of the company, and the work reflects a designer led approach: plan first, plant second, maintain always. Since 1999 the company has served homes and businesses around Woodmere and greater Nassau County.
