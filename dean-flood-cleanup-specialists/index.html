What to do right now
Flood emergency? Call us.

Until we arrive, these steps limit the damage:

  1. 1Shut off the water source if you can do it safely.
  2. 2Stay out of rooms with sagging ceilings or standing water near outlets.
  3. 3Move valuables, rugs and documents to dry ground.
  4. 4Do not use household vacuums on standing water.

Our process

From soaking wet to bone dry

Dean Flood Cleanup Specialists serves Malden and the greater Boston area with a step by step recovery process. No guesswork, no half dried walls, no callbacks for mold.

Why speed matters
Mold can start in 48 hours

The faster standing water comes out and drying begins, the less damage you end up repairing. That is why our first step is always rapid extraction.

  1. 1

    Inspect and assess

    We find the water source, map how far the moisture spread, and check for hidden pockets with meters.

  2. 2

    Extract the water

    Pumps and extraction units remove standing water from floors, basements and crawl spaces.

  3. 3

    Remove what cannot be saved

    Soaked carpet pad, ruined drywall and unsalvageable materials come out so they cannot harbor mold.

  4. 4

    Dry and dehumidify

    Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure completely, verified with moisture readings.

  5. 5

    Clean and disinfect

    Affected surfaces are cleaned, disinfected and deodorized to a safe, healthy condition.

  6. 6

    Restore and verify

    We confirm everything is dry and clean, and walk you through the finished work.
