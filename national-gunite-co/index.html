How a gunite pool comes together

A general overview of the gunite construction process. Your project plan will be confirmed with you directly.

i.

Layout and Excavation

The pool shape is laid out on your property and excavated to depth.

ii.

Steel Reinforcement

A steel rebar cage is tied to give the shell its structural strength.

iii.

Gunite Application

Concrete is sprayed at high velocity over the steel, forming a monolithic shell.

iv.

Cure and Finish

The shell cures, then tile, coping, and interior finish complete the pool.

About National Gunite Co

A Southfield pool builder

National Gunite Co is a swimming pool contractor based at 26460 Eight Mile W in Southfield, Michigan. The company works in gunite pool construction, a method prized for its strength and design freedom.

Unlike liner or fiberglass pools, a gunite pool is formed on site, so curves, depths, and features are limited only by the design.
