Our Story

Who we are

Gate specialists, not generalists

Expert Electric Gate Repair Burbank does one thing: electric gates. Automatic swing gates, slide gates, the motors and operators that drive them, and the access systems that control them. That focus shows in the reviews, where customers single out technicians by name for fast, knowledgeable diagnosis.

The approach is simple. Arrive when promised. Find the actual fault. Quote a fair price. Fix it cleanly and leave the site tidy. It is why every review on record so far is five stars.

How we work

The visit, step by step

You call and describe the problem. A technician arrives, often the same day, and calls ahead so you know when to expect them. They test the system, find the fault, and explain the repair and the price before touching a tool.

Then the work: clean wiring, solid welds, calibrated sensors. You test the gate yourself before they pack up. That is the whole story, and it is why customers call the service outstanding.
