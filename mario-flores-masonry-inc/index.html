How we work

First, a conversation: what you want, where it goes, and what the site looks like. Then a straight estimate. Then the work, done to the standard a licensed mason signs his name to.

Masonry fails when corners get cut: shallow footings, weak mortar, no drainage. We build like the wall has to stand for fifty years, because it should.

The trade

Brick, block, stone, and concrete are unforgiving materials. They reward patience and punish shortcuts. Three decades in the trade is how you learn the difference between a wall that looks finished and a wall that is finished.
