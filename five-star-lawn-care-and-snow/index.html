About

Built for Kentucky weather, all of it.

Five Star Lawn Care & Snow (also listed as Five Star Lawn Care & Snow Removal LLC) is based at 5364 Cody Rd in Independence, KY. The company grew out of a simple observation: homeowners needed one reliable crew for the yard in summer and the driveway in winter, not two different phone numbers.

Today the work spans lawn care, snow removal, excavation and hardscapes. Estimates are free, communication is direct, and payment is simple: cash or check.

Listed categories include excavation contractor, snow removal service, landscape contractor, landscape designer and gardener, serving Independence and the surrounding Northern Kentucky area.
