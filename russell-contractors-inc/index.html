Our story

since 1997

Russell Contractors, Inc. has been a Florida-registered corporation for close to three decades, building and remodeling across the Jacksonville area.

1997

Registered in Florida

Russell Contractors, Inc. registered as a Florida corporation, based at 10125 Terrell Pappy Rd in Jacksonville. The company has been building in Northeast Florida ever since.

Steady work, straight dealing

Close to three decades in this business teaches you what matters: show up, do what the contract says, and answer the phone. Russell Contractors, Inc. is a working general contractor, not a sales operation, and the jobs run the way the plans say they will.

Homeowners hire us for additions and remodels that have to blend with the existing house. Business owners hire us for build-outs that have to open on schedule. Both get the same thing: one accountable team, one written scope, and a punch list that actually gets finished.

The office sits on Terrell Pappy Rd in Jacksonville, and the crews work across Duval, Clay and St. Johns counties.
