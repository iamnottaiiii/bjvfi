Who we are

Small footprint,
long view

We are not a volume builder. Our work is concentrated in Pasadena, where we know the blocks, the building stock, and what it takes to keep a residence in good shape for decades.

That focus lets us do the unglamorous work well: solid renovations, responsive stewardship, and honest conversations with the people who live in our homes.

If you are looking for a developer that treats a residence like a long-term commitment rather than a transaction, that is the idea.
