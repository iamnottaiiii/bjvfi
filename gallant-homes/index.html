Our story

A builder with
Northern Kentucky roots

Gallant Homes, LLC is a home builder based in Florence, Kentucky, working across the Northern Kentucky and Greater Cincinnati region.

Gallant Homes was founded on a simple belief: a home should be built like the builder plans to live in it. That means straight framing, honest materials, and details that do not cut corners.

As a local Florence builder, we know Northern Kentucky lots, codes, and neighborhoods firsthand. We work with trusted local trades, keep jobsites clean and safe, and communicate clearly from the first meeting to the final walkthrough.

Whether it is a custom home on your own land, an addition that gives your family room to grow, or a remodel that finally makes the house feel like yours, we bring the same standard to every project: build it right, the first time.
