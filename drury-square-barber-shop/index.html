About

Our Story

One chair, decades of trust

Drury Square Barber Shop has served Auburn from the same square for decades, listed at 42 years in business. It is the kind of shop that does not need to change with every trend, because the fundamentals never go out of style: a sharp blade, a steady hand, and genuine care for the person in the chair.

Visitors describe a very nice little shop with a very rustic barber feel. The owner is known for being polite and considerate, taking time for real conversation. Regulars come monthly, some for years, and bring their families along, including moms in their 80s who would not trust their hair to anyone else.

Middle-aged professionals worried about looking their age, young sons getting their first real cuts, longtime locals who have been coming for years: they all end up saying the same thing. They would not go anywhere else.
