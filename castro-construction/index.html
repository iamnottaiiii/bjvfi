How we work

Remodeling is disruptive enough. Our approach keeps it simple: show up when we say we will, keep the site clean, explain what is happening and why, and finish what we started. That is how a small contractor earns repeat calls and referrals in its own neighborhood.

  • Direct communication with the person running your job
  • Estimates based on an in-person look at the space
  • Clean, respectful job sites in occupied homes
  • Final walkthrough before the job is called complete
