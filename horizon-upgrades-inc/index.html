Services

Roofing and whole-home upgrades

Services below are per the company's public Porch profile. Call (818) 817-6060 to confirm scope and current availability.

Full roof replacement with composite shingles, the workhorse of Southern California roofing: affordable, durable, and available in a wide range of colors. Includes tear-off, underlayment, flashing, and cleanup.
Proper attic insulation is one of the fastest paybacks in home efficiency. Installed to recommended depth for our climate zone to keep cool air in during Glendale summers.
Central AC installed and sized to the home, so the system runs efficiently instead of fighting a leaky envelope. Pairs naturally with new insulation and roofing work.
Full exterior repaints with proper prep: scraping, priming, and quality coatings that protect siding and stucco from sun and weather. Whole-home and partial projects.
Complete exterior transformations: color consultation, full prep, and uniform coverage across every elevation. A fresh envelope that also seals out the elements.
The company's signature approach: bundling roofing, insulation, and cooling into one efficiency plan. Ask how combined upgrades can lower monthly energy costs.

About

50+ years of combined experience, one clear mission

Horizon Upgrades Inc. is a roofing contractor based at 3900 San Fernando Rd in Glendale, CA 91204. The company describes itself this way: with over 50 years of combined experience, the team is dedicated to providing homeowners the tools they need to save money, become environmentally aware, and be energy efficient.

That mission shapes every job. A roof is not just shingles; it is the top of your home's energy system. The management team brings deep experience in energy-efficient upgrades, and the engineering and project management teams keep projects on time, on budget, and built to a high standard of quality.
