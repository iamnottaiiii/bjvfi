AboutSpringtown, PA

A small shop
with a big following

"Always fast and very short wait time for a great haircut. Also you can get all the local news."Paraphrased from public reviews

Reflections Hair Studio is the kind of barber shop that does not need a big sign. Sitting on PA-212 in Springtown, it has earned a 4.9 rating across 42 reviews the old-fashioned way: one good haircut at a time.

The shop is owner-operated. Reviewers note that the owner cuts your hair herself, and that she aims to please. That personal touch is what regulars mention most: quick service, fair prices, and a chair that regulars trust with their kids' haircuts too.

Families are a big part of the story. Parents call the studio perfect for boys' haircuts, from first trims to lightning-bolt designs that make a school week. It is wheelchair accessible, easy to reach on Route 212, and open four days a week, Wednesday through Saturday.

Come for the cut. Stay for the conversation. Leave looking like the best version of yourself.
