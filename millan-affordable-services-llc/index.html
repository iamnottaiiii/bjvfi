Our story

A Hamden team,
cleaning since 2012

Millan Affordable Services LLC has been a registered Connecticut business for over a decade, built on careful work, fair prices, and neighbors who keep calling back.

The short version

Careful work at a price
that makes sense

Millan started in Hamden in 2012 with a simple idea: a cleaning service does not have to choose between thorough and affordable. The team shows up on time, works a real checklist, and treats every home, and every office, like it matters.

Customers describe a professional, efficient crew that is proactive about the work: they know exactly what to do and how to do it. Houses, family members homes, and office workspaces all get the same attention to detail.

Beyond cleaning, Millan is a neighborhood multi service shop, the same friendly team neighbors also visit for tax preparation and travel help. One door on Dixwell Ave, a lot of ways to help.
