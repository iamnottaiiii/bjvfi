Our story
Featured project

The Grand Vista waterfall

Grand Vista, a 125 unit luxury apartment complex in Studio City, asked ARTBOX to design and build a signature landscape feature. The result: an illuminated woven wire mesh waterfall that doubles as a projection screen for films and multimedia, filtering daylight and creating the sound of rain when at rest.

The installation was covered by the trade press for its blend of art, engineering and sustainability, using recycled water, low voltage power and high efficiency equipment.

Our story

Where art meets construction

Founded in 2001, ARTBOX Construction is a Los Angeles based design, build and fabrication company specializing in projects that demand a high level of design sophistication and technical expertise.

Our in house team of designers and fabricators works out of more than 4,000 square feet of studio space, exploring the relationship between art and construction on everything from concept pieces to full buildings.

We collaborate with a network of architects, engineers, interior designers and lighting consultants, using computer aided drafting and 3D software to realize each client's vision.

Our portfolio spans commercial and residential work. Commercial projects include corporate offices, restaurants, studios and creative spaces. Residential projects include new homes, remodels and additions designed to artfully enhance living spaces.

One example: the illuminated woven wire waterfall we designed and built for the Grand Vista apartment complex in Studio City, a landscape feature that doubles as a projection screen and was covered by the trade press.
