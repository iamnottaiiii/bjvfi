About

Wired into Shrewsbury
since 1987

R Shepard Electric Inc is a family-run electrical contractor based in Shrewsbury, Massachusetts, and has been keeping local homes powered safely for nearly four decades.

The company was started in 1987 and remains a hands-on, owner-led operation. Reviewers describe a father-and-son team: efficient, professional, honest, and capable, the kind of crew that installs your ceiling lights and leaves the room brighter in every sense.

What sets the work apart is diagnosis. Customers consistently report that Bob Shepard quickly finds the real cause, like the visit where a dead deck outlet led to the discovery of serious irregularities inside the breaker panel, fixed before they could cause real damage.

The business is listed with the Better Business Bureau (file opened 2002), and carries public review profiles on Google, Birdeye, and Angi.
