Our story

A Syracuse favorite on University Ave

Prince & princess Design sits at 404 University Avenue, in the heart of the university neighborhood, serving students, families, and professionals alike.

Our approach

Skill you can see, warmth you can feel

We built our reputation the old-fashioned way: one great haircut at a time. Clients describe our stylists as talented, sweet, professional, and detail-obsessed, and our prices as genuinely affordable.

Being near Syracuse University, we see a lot of first-timers far from home. We take extra care to listen, explain, and make sure you leave happy, whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth.

Open six days a week until 7:30 PM, with Sunday hours too, so great hair fits your schedule, not the other way around.
