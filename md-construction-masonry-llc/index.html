About MD Construction & Masonry LLC

MD Construction & Masonry LLC is a Plainfield, New Jersey construction company and contractor serving local homeowners and small businesses. We focus on the work that holds a property together: masonry, concrete, and general construction repairs and improvements.

Our approach is simple. Show up on time, give an honest price, do the work the right way, and leave the site clean. No pressure, no surprises, just solid construction work you can see and feel for years.

From a cracked stoop to a new retaining wall to an addition that gives your family room to grow, we treat every project with the same care, whether it takes a day or a month.
