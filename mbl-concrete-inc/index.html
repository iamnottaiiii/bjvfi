Driveways

Your driveway takes more abuse than any other concrete on the property. We build them thick, reinforced, and sloped to drain, so they stay flat and crack-free for decades.

  • New driveways and complete tear-outs
  • Compacted base and steel reinforcement
  • Proper slope away from the garage
  • Control joints cut to manage cracking

MBL CONCRETE INC is a concrete contractor based in Mission Hills, serving homeowners and small businesses across the San Fernando Valley.

Our approach

Prep is the job.
The pour is the reward.

Anyone can order a mixer truck. What makes concrete last is everything that happens before the truck arrives: the excavation, the compacted base, the forms set dead true, the steel exactly where the engineer put it.

That is where we spend our time. We show up, we measure twice, we tell you what your concrete actually needs, and then we pour and finish it like it is going in our own yard.

We are easy to reach and easy to work with: one phone number, (661) 264-6650, answered by someone who knows the work.
