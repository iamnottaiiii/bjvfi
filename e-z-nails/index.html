Why E Z Nails

Nail Care Without the Fuss

E Z Nails is a neighborhood nail salon focused on doing the basics well: neat manicures, relaxing pedicures, durable acrylics, and fun nail art.

No appointments needed for most services. Walk in, pick your color, and leave with nails that look great and last.

Most services are available on a walk-in basis. Calling ahead is smart on weekends and evenings when the salon is busiest.
