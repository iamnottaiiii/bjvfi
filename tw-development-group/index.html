What we develop.

Facility types from the company's stated project history. Select a row to see how a typical engagement works.

Headquarters projects start with the client's program: headcount, growth plans, image, and budget. The development team coordinates site selection, design, financing, and construction so leadership gets one accountable partner.

  • Site selection and land analysis
  • Design and budget coordination
  • Schedule management through delivery

Distribution work is about throughput: dock configuration, trailer parking, clear heights, and labor access. TW Development has repositioned existing buildings into modern fulfillment space, including an 87,000 sq ft facility in Kettering, Ohio.

  • Building repositioning and conversion
  • Dock modifications and trailer courts
  • Parking expansion and site work

Data center development demands rigorous site diligence around power capacity, fiber routes, and redundancy. The team's finance and construction expertise supports the long lead items these projects require.

  • Power and utility coordination
  • Secure, resilient design standards
  • Phased delivery planning

Call center clients typically need large floor plates, robust parking ratios, and fast occupancy. The development approach keeps budget and schedule as the two governing constraints from day one.

  • Large floor plate layouts
  • Parking and access planning
  • Accelerated delivery schedules

Retail development lives or dies on location analytics: traffic counts, demographics, and co-tenancy. The team has repositioned retail assets, including a former grocery building in Cincinnati, for new commercial use.

  • Site selection and market analysis
  • Asset repositioning
  • Sale or lease marketing support

Multi-family projects pair development discipline with long term asset thinking: unit mix, amenity programming, and durable construction that performs for owners and residents alike.

  • Unit mix and amenity planning
  • Full development and financing coordination
  • Long term asset performance focus

About

A Cincinnati developer with national reach.

TW Development Group is a commercial real estate development company based at 5533 Fair Lane in Cincinnati, Ohio. The firm works with corporate clients of all sizes to develop customized, high performance facilities throughout the United States.

The team's diversified talents and decades of experience bring a unique and measured perspective to every project. For many clients, the deciding factors are the project budget and a condensed schedule, and the firm is built to manage both on the client's behalf.

Across its history the company has developed retail, multi-family, call centers, data centers, fulfillment centers, and corporate headquarters facilities in locations across the country. The work is grounded in three commitments: dedication to clients above all else, the highest levels of excellence in every project, and demonstrated honesty and integrity in all that it does.

The firm is also active in the local community. TW Development Group served as an Eagle sponsor of the First Tee of Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky annual golf tournament, supporting youth programs across the region.
