Why modular

Housing that arrives ready, and stands up fast.

Our story

Rethinking how a home gets built.

uPad Cabana began with a question: what if a well-built, comfortable home could be manufactured like a precision product and set up in hours instead of built over months?

The answer became the Smart Pad, a 120 sq ft insulated modular building, and a system of kitchen and bathroom modules that connect to it. UPad Cabana Home LLC was formed in California in December 2022, and the team has been refining the panelized system from its Lake View Terrace workshop ever since.

Alternative housing for real needs: instant shelter, backyard living, and large-scale development.

Every unit is designed for solar integration and off-grid locations, because a home should work wherever it is needed most.

  1. Design and engineering

    Each panel is engineered for strength, insulation, and fast connection.

  2. Manufacturing

    Units are built in a controlled shop, not exposed to weather delays.

  3. Erection in hours

    Panelized parts arrive ready and go up fast on a prepared site.

  4. Built to grow

    Add kitchen and bath modules as needs change, without starting over.
