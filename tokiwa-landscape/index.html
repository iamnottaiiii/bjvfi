Our story

A Northridge landscaper with staying power.

Tokiwa means evergreen, and the name fits. We have been shaping landscapes in the San Fernando Valley for years, building a reputation one yard at a time.

In 2004, the California Landscape Contractors Association recognized Tokiwa Landscape with its President's Choice Award for craftsmanship on a residential project, part of the chapter's annual Beautification Awards. It is the kind of recognition you earn with careful hands, not shortcuts.

Today we are still a hands-on crew: the same people who design your planting are the ones in the beds making it happen. Customers mention us by name in reviews, and that is exactly how we like it.
