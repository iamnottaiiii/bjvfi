About the outfit

Pittsburgh Trucking Services is a cargo freight carrier based at 2526 Library Rd in Pittsburgh, PA 15234, right in the South Hills.

We are a local hauler, not a national fleet. That means the person who quotes your job is close to the person who drives it, and every load gets treated like it matters, because to a small carrier it does.

Based on Library Road with Pittsburgh in our name and in our routes, we serve shippers who want a direct line, a fair quote, and freight delivered without the runaround.
