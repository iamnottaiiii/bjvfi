About

A Syracuse nail staple

Paris Nail has been part of the Western Lights Shopping Center for years, serving Syracuse with a full range of nail services. The team handles everything from everyday manicures to detailed nail art, and the salon welcomes walk-ins alongside appointments.

With 166 reviews on record, clients know the technicians for their craft. If you like precision work and a wide polish selection, this is your spot on Onondaga Boulevard.
