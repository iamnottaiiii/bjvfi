Our story

A Pittsburgh contractor,
plain and simple

Mc Donald Remodeling & Construction is a general contractor based on Bernice Street in Ross Township, serving Pittsburgh's North Hills.

WHO WE ARE

Local, hands-on, accountable

We are a Pittsburgh general contractor, not a call center. When you hire us, you talk to the people doing the work. That keeps communication short, decisions fast, and accountability exactly where it should be: with us.

WHERE WE WORK

Rooted in the North Hills

Based at 213 Bernice St in Ross Township, most of our projects are in Pittsburgh's northern neighborhoods. Being local means faster site visits, familiar suppliers, and a reputation we have to live with.

HOW WE WORK

No surprises, just the plan

Remodeling goes wrong when scope is vague and communication stops. So we do the opposite: written estimates, scheduled start dates, daily tidy-ups, and updates you do not have to chase. Then a final walkthrough where we fix anything that is not right.
