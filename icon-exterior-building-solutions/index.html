Curtain wall systems

Our core craft. We design, extrude and install aluminum framed curtain walls, including unitized insulated glass units, for office buildings, cultural venues and commercial developments.

Landmark reference: the timber backed glass curtain wall with krypton filled insulated glass units at Arena Stage in Washington, D.C., a system we custom designed and fabricated.

Included

  • Aluminum framed curtain walls
  • Structural glass curtain walls
  • Unitized insulated glass units
  • Design assist and engineering coordination

Storefronts and entrances

Aluminum storefront framing, commercial entrances and exterior glass doors. We handle the full opening, from framing and hardware to weather sealing, so doors operate smoothly from day one.

Included

  • Aluminum storefront systems
  • Commercial entrances and glass doors
  • Entrance hardware and closers
  • Weather sealing and adjustment

Our story

Glass specialists for commercial Maryland

Icon Exterior Building Solutions is a commercial construction company based in Glen Burnie, Maryland, focused on one discipline: the exterior glass envelope. Our main lines of business are curtain walls and glass and glazing contracting, and we have been listed with the Blue Book of Building and Construction since 2011.

Landmark work

We custom designed and extruded the aluminum framed curtain wall, and installed the entrances and exterior glass doors, for Arena Stage at the Mead Center for American Theater in Washington, D.C. A timber backed glass facade with krypton filled insulated glass units, hanging from structural steel.

Where we work

From our Glen Burnie base we serve Baltimore and the surrounding counties, plus the Washington, D.C. metropolitan region, on commercial new construction, renovation and maintenance projects.

How we work

Early design assist, precise fabrication and our own field installation crews. One accountable team from shop drawings to the final seal.
