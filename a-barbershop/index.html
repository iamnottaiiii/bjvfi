Our Story

A Long Beach Boulevard fixture

At 3520 Long Beach Boulevard, A Barbershop has become a neighborhood fixture by doing the simple things well. Customers describe walking into a casual, cool shop on a hot day and walking out happy with the cut. It is the kind of place where the barbers remember your name and your usual.

The chairs hold a mix of experience and energy. Raymond brings professionalism and precision, especially with cuts and beards. Eli brings friendliness and skill that turns first time visitors into regulars. Jack is an icon to his loyal clients, handling everything from formal to modern styles. Marcos takes his time and listens, making sure the final look is right.

The shop opens early, Tuesday through Saturday at 7:30 AM, keeps prices inexpensive, and welcomes everyone, including families. That combination is rare, and it is why the reviews keep landing at 4.6 stars.
