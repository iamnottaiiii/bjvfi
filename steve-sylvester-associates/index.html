Founder

Steve Sylvester

Moeller High School · Notre Dame · Oakland Raiders

Steve Sylvester is a hometown graduate of Moeller High School, where he learned that discipline and hard work will get you everywhere. It earned him a football scholarship to the University of Notre Dame, where he helped the Fighting Irish win a National Championship in 1973.

Steve stayed focused on academics, earning a Bachelor's degree in both Economics and Secondary Education. He went on to play football for the Oakland Raiders, helping earn World Championship titles in 1976, 1980 and 1983.

Steve began selling real estate during his NFL offseason and started selling homes full time at the close of his football career. Since then he has received numerous awards for sales excellence and superior client service, including the prestigious Circle of Excellence award from the Cincinnati Area Board of Realtors every year since 1985.

Steve and his wife Laurie reside in Montgomery, where they raised three daughters and enjoy visits from their grandchildren.

Circle of Excellence, Cincinnati Area Board of Realtors. Awarded every year since 1985, as published by the team.
