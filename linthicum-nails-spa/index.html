Nail Salon in Linthicum Heights, MD

Your neighborhood spot for beautiful nails.

Linthicum Nails & Spa is a full-service nail salon in the Shipley Linthicum Shopping Center at 507 S Camp Meade Road. Guests praise the friendly staff, the clean salon, and a huge selection of gel colors.

Call (443) 960-4327 See Services

Manicures, pedicures, and more

Choose from classic manicures, gel manicures, relaxing pedicures, acrylics, and dip powder. Walk-ins are welcome, and the team is known for double-checking that you love your nails before you leave.

Brows and waxing too

Beyond nails, the salon offers eyebrow services and waxing, making it a one-stop stop for a quick refresh. Call ahead to ask about same-day availability.
