About Muddiman Maintenance

A family trade, Cincinnati grown

Muddiman Maintenance is a Cincinnati electrical and handyman business built the old way: show up, do the work right, and leave the place cleaner than you found it.

Customers who have hired the Muddimans talk about decades of hands-on experience. One long-time customer writes that Joe Muddiman is a certified electrician with over 40 years in the trade, and that a concrete driveway he poured more than 35 years ago has not cracked once. The same reviews mention painting and general repairs, all left clean as can be.

That is the whole pitch. No franchises, no call centers. Just a local tradesman family serving Cincinnati homes, one honey-do list at a time.
