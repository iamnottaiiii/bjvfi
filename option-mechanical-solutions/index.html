Our story

Built around the right options for your building.

Option Mechanical Solutions is an HVAC contractor based on 148th Drive in Rosedale, Queens. The name says it all: when something goes wrong with your heating, cooling or refrigeration, you deserve clear options, honest pricing and work done right the first time.

We serve residential and commercial customers, and our customers value the neatness of our work and the affordability of our service. Whether it is a home AC that quit in a heat wave or a commercial refrigeration unit that needs attention, we diagnose first, explain your options, and get to work.
