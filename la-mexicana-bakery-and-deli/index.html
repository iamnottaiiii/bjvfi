Pan dulce

And the deli side

More than a bakery

Half the shop is a deli counter for a quick lunch: sandwiches built on the same bolillos and teleras that come out of the oven each morning, plus shelves of Mexican staples to grab on the way home.

Swing by at 7 AM for warm bread, or at noon for a sandwich. The door is open from 5 in the morning, every single day of the week.

Bakery & Deli

Two counters, one door on Eastern Ave

La Mexicana Bakery and Deli keeps it simple: a panaderia case that fills up before dawn, and a deli side for a fast, filling lunch.

Sweet bread by the piece

Pick up a tray and tongs at the pan dulce case: conchas, orejas, cuernos, empanadas, and whatever else came warm off the rack that morning.

Bolillos & teleras

Our daily bread. Perfect for tortas, molletes, or just butter. Baked in batches all day so the racks rarely sit empty.

Cakes for the occasion

Tres leches, birthday cakes, and custom orders. Give us a few days of lead time and we will have it boxed and ready for pickup.

Seasonal & holiday bread

Special trays around the holidays, from seasonal sweet breads to the loaves that only show up once a year. Ask what is in the oven this month.

The deli counter

A quick lunch, made on our own bread

The deli side keeps the neighborhood fed through the afternoon: sandwiches and quick bites built on fresh bolillos and teleras, plus grab-and-go staples on the shelves. No need to plan ahead, just walk in.
