Good to know

Straight answers about roofs

How do I know if I need a repair?

Water stains on ceilings, granules in the gutters, curled or missing shingles, and daylight in the attic are the classic signs. An inspection settles it fast.

Repair or replace?

It depends on age, material and damage spread. A localized issue on a younger roof usually means repair. Widespread wear on an aging roof usually means replacement.

How long does a replacement take?

Most homes take one to three days of on-roof work, weather permitting. You will get a timeline in writing before we start.
