Our story

The stylist Staten Island trusts

Blessing Beauty hair Salon sits at 151 Lafayette Ave in Staten Island, between Henderson and Cassidy, where one stylist has built a loyal following one head at a time.

Clients describe Blessing as the kindest, sweetest stylist they know: gentle, patient, and so creative with color that trying new styles is half the fun. Some have been in her chair for four years; others for more than a decade.
