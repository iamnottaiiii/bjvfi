How it works

Straightforward auto repair in Newark

Fran's Auto Repair on North 6th Street keeps Newark drivers moving with honest diagnostics and fair prices. No upsells, no mystery charges, just the repair your car needs.

Step 1: Drop off

Pull in or call ahead. We take a full history of the problem before anything else.

Step 2: Diagnose

We run the diagnostics and show you exactly what we found.

Step 3: Approve

You get a written quote first. Nothing happens without your okay.

Step 4: Repair

Factory-grade parts, careful work, road-tested before pickup.

Step 5: Drive out

We walk you through what was done and what to watch for next.
