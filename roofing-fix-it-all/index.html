What we do

Roofing services,
done completely

Residential roof repair and replacement across Pasadena and the LA Metro area. Open a section to see what each service covers.

Most roof problems start small and get expensive fast. We find the source and fix it right the first time.

  • Leak detection and targeted leak repair
  • Missing, cracked, curled or wind-lifted shingles
  • Flashing repair around chimneys, vents and skylights
  • Storm and wind damage repair
  • Tile repair and replacement for cracked or slipped tiles

When a roof is at the end of its life, patching costs more than replacing. We do full tear-offs and new installations.

  • Complete tear-off and replacement
  • Shingle roof installation
  • Tile roof installation and replacement
  • Flat and low-slope roof systems
  • Wood shingle roofing
  • Decking repair and underlayment replacement

Buying a home, selling one, or just want to know where your roof stands? A proper inspection looks at every layer.

  • Full roof condition assessments
  • Pre-purchase and pre-sale roof inspections
  • Attic and interior leak-sign checks
  • Written findings with repair or replacement options

Pasadena homes wear a lot of different roofs. We work with all of them.

  • Asphalt and architectural shingles
  • Clay and concrete tile
  • Flat and low-slope roofing systems
  • Wood shingles and shakes
