About

A Chatsworth remodeling shop since 1989

Euro-Tech Kitchens has been designing and building kitchens and bathrooms in the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles for nearly four decades. The company is built on a simple policy: integrity, listening, clear communication and precise attention to detail.

The approach

Design first, then build.

It starts with a visit to your space: dimensions, discussion of the project, and computer generated floor plans. Cabinets are designed around those plans, built, then installed by the same team that designed them. Removal of existing cabinetry, prep work, installation and final cleanup are all part of the process.

What happy customers mention

Reviewers praise creative kitchen design that thinks outside the box, custom cabinetry workmanship down to the last detail, and a team that walks the homeowner through every choice. Several describe kitchens that look like they came out of a magazine.

What unhappy customers mention

Some reviewers report projects running far past promised timelines, incomplete punch list work, and slow communication when follow up was needed. Euro-Tech Kitchens takes scheduling and follow through seriously on every job.
