General dentistry,
done right

Dr. Gastaldi provides complete general dental care for adults and families. If something needs a specialist, he will explain exactly why and refer you to one he trusts.

Dental exams

Comprehensive checkups with digital X-rays to catch small issues before they become big ones.

Cleanings and polish

Gentle professional cleanings that patients describe as comfortable and thorough.

Tooth colored fillings

Natural looking composite fillings that blend with your tooth and stop decay in its tracks.

Crowns

Durable, well fitted crowns that protect weakened teeth and restore full chewing strength.

Root canals

Pain relieving treatment for infected teeth, with care taken to keep you comfortable throughout.

Extractions

Careful, unhurried tooth removal when a tooth cannot be saved, with clear aftercare guidance.

Dentures

Full and partial dentures fitted for comfort, so you can eat and smile with confidence again.

Cosmetic whitening

Professional whitening treatments for a visibly brighter smile, tailored to your teeth.

Gum care

Evaluation and treatment of gum disease, plus coaching on home care that actually works.

About

keeps recommending

Dr. Michael Gastaldi, DDS has practiced general dentistry on Staten Island for nearly four decades, earning the loyalty of patients who have been coming for years, and bringing their families too.

Meet the dentist

Experience you can feel

Dr. Gastaldi graduated from New York University in 1987 and holds an active New York dental license. In an era of corporate dental chains, his McClean Ave office has stayed small, personal, and focused on one thing: the patient in the chair.

Patients mention that he takes time, explains what he is doing, and makes sure they are comfortable. The office speaks both English and Spanish, and the front desk team is known for appointment reminders and keeping visits on track.

  • Graduated NYU, 1987, active NY license
  • 39 years of general dentistry experience
  • English and Spanish spoken
  • Most insurance accepted, including EmblemHealth
