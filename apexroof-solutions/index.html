About ApexRoof.

A roofing company based in Los Angeles, California, serving homes and businesses.

Quality craftsmanship, roof after roof.

ApexRoof Solutions. provides roofing services from its Los Angeles base at 5542 York Blvd. The company works with asphalt shingles, metal, flat roofs, and tile, covering installation, repair, replacement, and inspections.

The approach is straightforward: show up, assess honestly, and do the work well. Estimates are free, options like energy efficient materials are on the table, and storm damage gets prompt attention.

Whether it is a home in Highland Park or a commercial building across the city, every roof gets the same standard of care.
