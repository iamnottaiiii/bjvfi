Our story

A trucking company that answers its own phone

Glenshaw Auto Transport started with a simple idea: vehicle shipping works better when the people quoting the job are the people running the job. No broker chains, no mystery carriers, no fine print.

From our base in Shaler Township, we serve Glenshaw, the greater Pittsburgh area and long haul routes across the United States. Dealerships trust us with inventory, families trust us with the car they drive every day, and collectors trust us with the ones they do not.

We are open seven days a week, 7 AM to 8 PM, because moves and deliveries do not keep office hours either.
