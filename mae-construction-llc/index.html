About

MAE Construction LLC is a general contractor based in Northfield, Ohio, working residential and commercial projects across Northeast Ohio. The company is known publicly for the regulated environmental trades: lead abatement and mold remediation.

1998

In business since 1998

Public contractor records date the company's operation to 1998, with a track record of permitted residential and commercial projects across the Cleveland area.

2013

Blue Book listed since 2013

The company has carried a Blue Book ProView listing since 2013 under lead assessment, lead abatement, and mold remediation trade codes.

Today

Full service construction

Current public materials describe a full service construction company handling both commercial and residential construction, with licensed lead abatement contracting in the state of Ohio.

How we describe the work
  • Regulated trades first. Lead abatement and mold remediation are the company's public specialty, performed to health department standards.
  • Residential and commercial. Public records show both home improvement and commercial construction scopes.
  • Compliance work. A large portion of the published project record is city violation corrections in Cleveland and the suburbs.
  • Insurance coordination. The company publicly describes routine work with insurance companies on claim repairs. Call to confirm.
