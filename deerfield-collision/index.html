About

A Blue Ash fixture since 1969.

Deerfield Collision Center, LLC is a family owned auto body shop at 11067 Deerfield Rd in Blue Ash, Ohio. The facts below come from the shop's Better Business Bureau profile and public directory listings.

1969

The shop opens its doors

The BBB lists the business start date as January 1, 1969, making it one of the longest running body shops in the Blue Ash area.

1995

BBB file opened

The BBB opened its file on the business on July 12, 1995, and lists it under Auto Body Repair and Painting and Auto Repairs.

2002

Incorporated as an LLC

Deerfield Collision Center, LLC was incorporated on December 31, 2002, per BBB records.

Today

Family leadership

The BBB lists Mr. Joel T. Lauman as owner, and the shop continues to offer collision repair and minor mechanical repair from its Deerfield Road location.

57 years in business

Years in business as reported by the Better Business Bureau, a rare run for an independent shop.
