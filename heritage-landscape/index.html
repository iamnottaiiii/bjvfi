Our Story

Rooted in Cincinnati

More than 25 years of transforming yards across the Cincinnati area, per the business listing.

A name built on trust

Based in Union Township at 687 Marieda Drive, Heritage Landscape is a landscape company with over 25 years of experience serving homeowners and businesses. The team is known for going above and beyond on every project, whether it is a small residential refresh or a full commercial property.

Your ideas, our expertise

What sets the process apart is the consultative approach. Instead of handing you a fixed plan, the designers work closely with you to bring your own ideas to life, combining professional knowledge and installation expertise with the vision you have for your space.

Designs you can see

The experienced design staff uses computer-aided layouts to develop fully colored and scaled digital images of proposed projects. You get a clear representation of the finished work before installation begins, from patios and fire pits to outdoor kitchens and full landscape overhauls.
