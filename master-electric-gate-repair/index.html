Signs you need us

Your gate is telling you something

Gates rarely fail all at once. These early warnings mean it is time to call before a small fix turns into a stuck gate and a security problem.

  • The gate stops halfway or reverses on its own
  • Grinding, scraping, or popping noises when it moves
  • Remotes or keypads that only work some of the time
  • A gate that sags, drags on the ground, or will not latch

Services

Gate work, done like it matters

Every gate is different, so every job starts with an honest diagnosis. These are typical jobs customers call us for. Call to confirm what you need and get a quote.

Examples

Gate repair

Broken wheels and rollers, bent tracks, loose hinges, sagging frames, latches that will not catch. If your gate drags, grinds, or stalls, this is where we start.

Examples

Opener and motor service

Gate opener repair and replacement, remotes and keypads, wiring and sensor issues. We service electric gate equipment so the whole system moves smoothly again.

Examples

New gate installation

Need a replacement? We measure carefully and match your current gate and fence line, then install a gate built to a higher standard than the one it replaces.

Examples

Adjustments and tune-ups

Alignment checks, track cleaning, lubrication, and small hardware corrections. A little maintenance goes a long way toward keeping an electric gate quiet and reliable.

Service examples only. Every property and gate is different, so the final scope and price are confirmed on the call and again on site before work begins.
