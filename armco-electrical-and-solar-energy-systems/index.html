About

A local crew for wired-up homes.

Armco Electrical and Solar Energy Systems is based at 9942 Terhune Ave in Shadow Hills, California. We are an electrician shop that grew into solar, which means your rooftop system is designed by people who understand the panel it connects to.

Shadow Hills and the surrounding San Fernando Valley neighborhoods are horse country and hillside homes: older panels, long wire runs, and roofs with great sun exposure. That mix is exactly what we work on every week.
