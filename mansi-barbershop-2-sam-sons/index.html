Our story

Sam, his sons,
and a second chair

The Mansi family opened their first shop in Cliffside Park and built a loyal following one careful cut at a time. Edgewater is chapter two.

From Anderson Ave to River Road

A family trade,
grown the honest way

Mansi Barbershop started on Anderson Avenue in Cliffside Park, where regulars came back week after week because the cuts were consistent and the vibe was right. When the family opened a second shop at 880 River Road in Edgewater, they brought the same crew, the same standards, and the same promise: listen first, cut second.

Today the Edgewater shop is known for its stylish, comfortable interior, an easy walk-in flow, and barbers who remember your name and your fade. It is the kind of place where a first visit turns into a standing appointment.

  • Second location from the trusted Cliffside Park original
  • Barbers praised by name for fades, low fades, and detail work
  • Clean shop, cool vibe, plenty of parking on River Road
