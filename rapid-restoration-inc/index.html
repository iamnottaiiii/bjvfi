Our Services

Full-service restoration for homes and businesses in Howard Beach and across Queens.

Water Extraction

Rapid pump-out of flooded basements, burst pipes, appliance leaks, and storm intrusion. Every gallon removed early saves downstream repair.

Drying and Dehumidification

Commercial air movers and dehumidifiers with daily moisture readings, so drying is verified, not guessed.

Mold Remediation

Inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, removal, and antimicrobial treatment to make the space safe again.

Fire and Smoke Damage

Soot and smoke cleanup, odor removal, and repair of water damage left behind by firefighting efforts.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Safe removal of contaminated water, sanitizing of affected areas, and disposal of unsalvageable materials.

Complete Rebuild

Drywall, flooring, trim, and paint. We return rooms to their pre-loss condition with documented, insurance-ready records.
