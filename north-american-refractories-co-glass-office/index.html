What a refractory company actually does

Refractories are the ceramic materials that line the inside of glass furnaces and other extreme heat equipment, so the molten glass never touches the steel shell.

Batch 01

Furnace linings heritage

NARCO grew into one of the largest U.S. makers of ceramic refractory materials used to line high temperature equipment, including glass furnaces.

Batch 02

Engineered for the melt

Refractory products are designed to resist thermal shock, thermal cycling, and corrosive attack inside the hottest industrial processes on earth.

About North American Refractories Co

What public records tell us about this company and the Milford office. History below is drawn from published sources, including Wikipedia's HarbisonWalker International article and industry reporting.

  • 1929

    North American Refractories Co (NARCO) is created in Ohio through the merger of seven manufacturers of refractory solutions.

  • 1965, 1990s

    NARCO is acquired by Eltra Corp, later becomes part of Allied's chemical division, then Didier-Werke AG, and in the mid 1990s joins the Austrian group Veitsch-Radex, a subsidiary of RHI.

  • 2000s

    NARCO becomes part of the ANH Refractories family of companies alongside A.P. Green Refractories. The ANH website is listed as anhrefractories.com in business directories.

  • 2015

    ANH Refractories becomes HarbisonWalker International (HWI), combining the Harbison-Walker, A.P. Green, and North American Refractories legacies. Yellow Pages listings show Harbison-Walker Refractories at 400 Techne Center Dr, Milford, OH 45150 with phone (513) 947-8400.

  • 2023

    HarbisonWalker International combines operations with Calderys and becomes the group's brand for the Americas region.
