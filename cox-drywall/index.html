Meet the family
The difference between good drywall and great drywall is the last ten percent: the sanding, the light check, the final skim.

That last ten percent is where we spend our time. Flat seams under raking light, tight corners, texture that matches the existing wall so a patch disappears. It is slower work, and it is the reason our walls photograph well and paint evenly.

What we do

Services

Full-service drywall for homes and light commercial work around Silver Grove and Campbell County. Tap a service to see what is included. Call to confirm any project not listed.

New walls and ceilings hung to code: measured, cut, and screwed to studs and joists with tight butt joints. Includes garages, basements, additions, and remodels. Moisture-resistant board available for baths and kitchens.
Embed tape, three-coat joint compound build-up, and sanding to a Level 3 or Level 4 finish. Corners finished with metal or paper-faced bead for crisp, durable edges.
A full skim coat over the entire surface for walls under critical lighting: great rooms with big windows, glossy paint, or smooth plaster-look walls.
Knockdown, orange peel, and skip-trowel textures, plus patch blending so repairs disappear into the existing wall.
Doorknob holes, anchor damage, cracked seams, and nail pops. Small patches to full sheet replacement, finished and texture-matched.
Cut-out and replacement of stained or softened drywall after leaks, with stain-blocking primer so the repair does not bleed through. Call to confirm scope.
Ceiling patches, drywall for soffits, and light repair work such as handrails and trim. Published customer feedback mentions step and handrail repair work, so ask about your project.

Our story

A family trade, twenty years in
