About

The name on the lane

Kremer Lane is named for the family behind the company. That tends to keep a contractor honest.

Kremer's Excavating Company Inc was established in 1981 and incorporated in Kentucky. For more than four decades the company has operated from 2468 Kremer Lane in Villa Hills, serving Northern Kentucky with excavation and fill work.

Public records list Kenneth W. Kremer with the business, and the company holds an active contractor license through Kenton County with a BuildZoom score of 90.

What "excavation and fill" covers

  • Cutting ground to plan for foundations and basements
  • Placing and compacting structural fill in lifts
  • Grading sites so water drains away from structures
  • Trenching for utilities and drainage

It is the work nobody photographs and everybody depends on. Kremer's has been doing it on the same hills since 1981.
