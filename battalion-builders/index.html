OUR STORY

Old Pasadena roots

Battalion Builders operates out of 107 S Fair Oaks Ave in the heart of Old Pasadena, California.

The name reflects the way the company runs a job site: organized crews, clear orders of operation, and nobody standing around. Every project gets a plan, a schedule, and a foreman who owns the outcome.

Whether it is a historic home near the Arroyo or a commercial space on Colorado Boulevard, the standard does not change: build it like it matters, because to the client it does.
