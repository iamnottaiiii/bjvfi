About us

A Long Island asphalt name, on Johnson Road

Rason Materials is part of the Rason Asphalt family, supplying and recycling asphalt across Long Island for decades.

Family of plants

Rason operates asphalt facilities across Long Island, including Inwood, Melville, Glen Cove, and North Lawrence. The Inwood yard is our materials and recycling hub.

Permitted + documented

Our recovery operations report to the NY State DEC as a registered construction and demolition debris handling facility. Paperwork your GC and your inspector will accept.

Built on recycling

Asphalt is one of the most recycled materials on earth. We take in old pavement and put it back to work, which keeps costs down and trucks full both ways.
