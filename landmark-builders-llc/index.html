Our Story

Three Decades of Building in Severna Park

Landmark Builders LLC is a construction company based at 15 Robinson Rd in Severna Park, Maryland. With over three decades of experience, the company has built its reputation one residential project at a time across greater Anne Arundel County.

The focus has always been residential: custom home construction, whole-home renovations, kitchen and bath remodels, and additions tailored to the needs of local homeowners. Over 30 years in the industry, Landmark has developed a process that emphasizes clear communication and steady project management on every build or remodel.

That experience matters when a project gets complicated. Efficient execution from planning through completion is what keeps a renovation on track and a custom build moving forward, and it is what Landmark brings to every job.
