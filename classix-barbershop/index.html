About Classix Barbershop

Classix Barbershop sits at 9 W Charlton St in the heart of Cincinnati, Ohio, steps from the University of Cincinnati campus. Established in 2014, the shop grew out of a lifetime around the chair: the owner's mother has been a hairstylist for over 50 years, and that barbering heritage runs through everything the shop does.

Regulars describe a fun, welcoming atmosphere with barbers known for attention to detail, clean tools, and consistent cuts. The crew keeps clients in and out efficiently while making everyone feel at home, and the shop serves walk-in friendly cuts in a classic, no frills barbershop setting.

Whether you are a student on campus or a longtime local, Classix aims to send every client out the door looking sharp.
