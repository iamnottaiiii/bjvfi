Our story

A Burbank supplier built around industrial doors

ADVANCE INDUSTRIAL CONTRACTOR, INC. operates from Burbank, California, supplying and servicing the doors that commercial and industrial facilities depend on every day. The company is listed as a garage door supplier with a focus on industrial door work, from new installations to ongoing service.
