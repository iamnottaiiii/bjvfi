Our story

A tranquil retreat on Buffalo Road

Heaven Nails & SPA II is the large, bright, wide-open salon where guests are treated to chair massages, hand paraffin dips, snacks at the drying station and a complimentary clear top coat. Technicians prioritize precision over speed, take plenty of staff on hand so waits stay short, and ask each guest what they want before they begin.
