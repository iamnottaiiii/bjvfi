Why a fortress wall

Built like it matters. Because it does.

A retaining wall is structural work: it holds back tons of soil, manages water, and protects everything above and below it. We build walls the engineered way, with proper footings, geogrid reinforcement where needed, and drainage systems that keep hydrostatic pressure from ever becoming a problem.

From the San Fernando Valley to the Westside, Los Angeles hillsides demand walls that respect soil, slope, and seismic reality. That is the whole job.

About

Los Angeles walls, built for Los Angeles ground.

Fortress Retaining Wall Contractors is a Los Angeles retaining wall company based in Studio City. The focus is narrow on purpose: retaining walls and the earthwork around them, done with structural discipline.

Every wall starts with the site: slope angle, soil behavior, water flow, and what sits above and below. Footings go in level and true, reinforcement goes where the loads are, and drainage goes in before backfill, because a wall without drainage is a wall with a countdown.
