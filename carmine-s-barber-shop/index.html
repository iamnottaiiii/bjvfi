Our Story

An old school shop
with a Bloomfield heart

Carmine's is the kind of barber shop people write reviews about: unhurried, personal, and proud of the craft.

From Franklin Street
to Broad Street

Carmine's moved from Franklin Street to its current home at 1290 Broad St, taking over another barber shop along the way. Longtime customers followed, and new regulars keep finding it.

It is generally a father and son operation, and reviewers describe both generations the same way: up on the latest styles, soft spoken, and taking their time on every single person in the chair.

The shop is clean and friendly, a place where a guy can get a haircut, catch the latest score, and get a shave or a beard trim. Good conversation is part of the service.
