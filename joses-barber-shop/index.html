Our Story

A Wilmington fixture

BARBER
POLE
Since way back

The chairs stay busy for a reason

Jose's Barber Shop has been cutting hair on North Avalon Boulevard for decades. Directory listings note more than 40 years in business, and generations of Wilmington families have come through the door.

The formula has not changed: keep a full team of barbers and stylists on the floor so walk-ins rarely wait long, charge prices the neighborhood can afford, and treat every client with respect. Reviewers say the service has stayed fast, reliable, and professional year after year.

Whether it is a classic gentleman's cut, a sharp skin fade, or a first haircut for a young kid, the goal is the same: you walk out looking right and feeling right.
