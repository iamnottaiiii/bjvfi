Our story

Seventeen years
of soulful cuts

A neighborhood barbershop that has been part of the Peninsula community for nearly two decades.

Rooted in the community

Soulful Shears has been cutting hair for 17 years and counting. In 2013 the shop moved into a newly renovated location in Palos Verdes Plaza on South Western Avenue, where it has been a fixture ever since.

The owner has been serving the San Pedro and Peninsula area for over 20 years, and regulars follow the shears wherever they go.

★★★★★

"An outstanding barber shop. I would recommend this shop to all women and men. High quality service."

Paraphrased from public reviews

Known for

Fades, tapers, regular cuts and designs, delivered in a warm, often funny environment where everybody feels at home.

The regulars say

Clean shop, knowledgeable barbers, reasonable prices, and a team that takes its time to get it right. Many clients say they will never go anywhere else.

Bring the whole family

Kids, parents, grandparents, everyone gets the same care. First-time visitors are welcomed like old friends, and the shop is wheelchair accessible with a private lot.

Tip from regulars: call the day of to check the wait, especially on Saturdays.
