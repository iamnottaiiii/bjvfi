Our story

Forty-one years of growing things right

Smith's Camargo Landscape has been serving the Amelia area since 1984. President Steve Smith built the company on a simple idea: a landscape company should be staffed by people who actually understand plants.

That is why the team includes employees with degrees in horticulture and forestry, as well as certified arborists. When a customer asks about a tree or shrub, Steve can walk through the pros and cons of just about every option, and clients say he answers every question patiently.

The crew has been with the company for years, and it shows in the work: stone walls laid with obvious care, beds edged cleanly, and lawns kept thick and green season after season.
