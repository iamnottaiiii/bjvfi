Safe Digging Procedure

How A Locate Ticket Works

Three steps between your project and a safe dig. Every excavation that hits an unmarked line risks injury, outages, and fines.

01

File The Ticket

Call 811 or file online at least 48 hours before digging (lead time varies by state). The one-call center creates your locate ticket and notifies the utility owners.

02

We Mark The Ground

A USIC technician visits the dig site and marks every buried line with paint and flags using the APWA uniform color code, so each utility type is instantly recognizable.

03

You Dig Safe

Respect the tolerance zone around each mark, hand-dig near lines, and keep marks visible. If marks fade or the scope changes, call for a refresh before you dig.

Company File

About USIC

USIC, LLC is America's largest provider of underground utility damage prevention services, headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana, with operations in 48 states.

USIC's team of more than 10,000 locate technicians protects the underground infrastructure of over 1,400 telecommunication, electric, gas, water, and sewer utilities and municipalities nationwide, performing more than 84 million locates each year.

Every technician completes six weeks of training to become certified, followed by up to six months of on-the-job training before working independently. The company's Safe-Life culture puts safety and quality above all else, because an accurate locate is what keeps excavators, the public, and critical services safe.

USIC is owned by private equity firms Partners Group and Kohlberg & Company, and its affiliate companies, Blood Hound, Reconn Utility Services, and On Target Utility Services, provide specialized infrastructure protection and compliance services.

Marking Discipline

White marks the proposed dig. Pink marks survey points. Red, yellow, orange, blue, green, and purple mark the utilities themselves. Every USIC technician marks to the APWA uniform color code, so anyone on site reads the ground the same way.
