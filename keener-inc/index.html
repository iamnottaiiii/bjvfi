What we do

Masonry that holds its line

A wall is only as good as the hands that laid it. Keener Inc lays brick, block, and stone with the straight courses, clean joints, and solid footings that keep a building standing straight for decades. From a front stoop to a commercial shell, the standard is the same.

How we work

Straight lines, solid work

Good masonry is patient work. Bed joints level, head joints plumb, mortar mixed right for the weather that day. It is not glamorous, and it is exactly what keeps water out and walls standing.

Keener Inc works across the Mechanicsburg and Camp Hill area on both new construction and repair. If your project involves brick, block, or stone, it is worth a conversation.
