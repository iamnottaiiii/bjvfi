Services

What a licensed landscaper does

Design, installation, and ongoing care. Pick a tab to see the work, then call for a current service list and a free look at your yard.

Landscape design

A plan before a shovel. We design for Valley sun, water-wise planting, and yards people actually use.

  • Residential landscape design and planting plans
  • Front yard curb-appeal makeovers
  • Drought-tolerant and native planting schemes
  • Outdoor living layouts: patios, paths, gathering spaces
Good to know: with a landscape architecture license on the team, plans can go beyond planting into grading, drainage, and hardscape layout.

Build and installation

The plan, made real. Planting, hardscape, and the irrigation that keeps it all alive through August.

  • Planting: trees, shrubs, perennials, sod and groundcover
  • Irrigation installation and smart controller upgrades
  • Patios, walkways, edging, and retaining walls
  • Drainage fixes and grading corrections
Good to know: permits pulled where the work requires them. The business has pulled permits locally since 1998.

Care and upkeep

A designed yard needs a steady hand. Seasonal care that keeps the planting healthy and the design intact.

  • Pruning, shaping, and seasonal cleanups
  • Irrigation checks, repairs, and seasonal adjustments
  • Mulching, soil care, and replanting tired beds
  • Ongoing maintenance plans for homes and HOAs
Good to know: service lists change with the season. Call (818) 366-2064 for what is currently offered.
