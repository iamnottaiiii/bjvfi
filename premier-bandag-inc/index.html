// HOW RETREADING WORKS

From Worn Casing to Road-Ready Tire

The Bandag retread process starts with a strict casing inspection. Only sound casings move on to buffing, where the old tread is removed to precise dimensions. New tread rubber is applied and the tire is cured under controlled heat and pressure, then final-inspected before it ships back to the fleet.

A single casing can often be retreaded more than once, which is why retreading is one of the most cost-effective and sustainable choices a fleet can make.

// WHO WE ARE

A Bandag Franchise Built on Quality

Premier Bandag Inc is a Bandag franchise retreader serving commercial fleets, with retread plants across Indiana, Kentucky, Missouri, and Ohio, including the Milford, Ohio location at 5997 Meijer Dr.

The Premier name dates to 1997, when the company now known as Premier Bandag was formed from the combination of Ken's Bandag Inc. and Huber Tire Co. President Rich Elliott chose the name "Premier" to make a strong statement about the firm's commitment to quality, a standard the company still builds its reputation on.

As a Bandag dealer, Premier works within the Bridgestone retread system, one of the most widely used commercial retread processes in North America.
