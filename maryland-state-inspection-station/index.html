Who Needs an Inspection

Maryland law requires a state safety inspection before you can register a used vehicle, or within 60 days of moving into Maryland. A passing grade means an official inspection certificate for the MVA.

1

Bought a Used Vehicle

State law requires the vehicle to be inspected within 90 days of purchase before it can be registered. Bring it in and leave with your official inspection certificate.

2

New to Maryland

Recently moved to the state? You must have your vehicle inspected and registered within 60 days.

3

Thorough, Standardized Check

Every Maryland state inspection covers the same safety systems: brakes, steering, suspension, lights, exhaust, seat belts, glass, and more. Certified technicians check each one.

4

Certificate for the MVA

Pass the inspection and you receive the official certificate required to complete your vehicle registration with the Maryland MVA.

Our Story

Maryland State Inspection Station is an authorized vehicle safety inspection station on Brandywine Road in Clinton, Maryland, listed in the state inspection station directory.

Its work centers on one thing: the official Maryland state safety inspection. That inspection exists to make sure vehicles on Maryland roads meet the state's safety standards before they are registered.

If you just bought a used car or just moved to Maryland, the station is where the inspection gets done, with the official certificate you take to the MVA.
