Our story

A small, owner operated cleaning company built on showing up and doing the job right.

Crystal Clean has been serving Burlington and Eastern Massachusetts since 2011. It is a one person operation, which is exactly the point: the owner, Chris Hickey, answers the phone, does the work, and stands behind it.

Customers keep calling back for the same reasons. Jobs start on time, the work is thorough and detailed, and the price quoted is the price paid. The company holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau and reviews frequently mention polite, fast, honest service with attention to the details.

Whether it is a family living room, a favorite armchair, or a whole office floor, Crystal Clean treats every job like it is happening in their own home.
