The salon

A neighborhood salon with a loyal following

Cutting Edge Salon on Genesee Street in Westvale is known for friendly, accommodating stylists and a clean, relaxing space. Clients come for everything from wash, cut and blow dry appointments to full color transformations, and many say they keep coming back for the personalized experience.

Walk-ins are welcome when chairs are available, and the team works with all hair types, from curly hair to locs to keratin smoothing.
