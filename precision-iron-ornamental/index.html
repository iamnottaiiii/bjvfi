Our story

IRONWORK IS A TRADE. WE TREAT IT LIKE ONE.

Precision Iron Ornamental is a fence contractor based in Pasadena, California, focused on ornamental iron: fences, gates, railings, and the repair work that keeps them standing.

The name says the approach. Ironwork lives or dies on precision. A gate that is an eighth of an inch off will drag for its whole life. A weld done in a hurry will crack. So we measure carefully, weld cleanly, and finish every job like our name is on it, because it is.

When you call (626) 797-4810, you reach the people who do the work. That is how we like it.
