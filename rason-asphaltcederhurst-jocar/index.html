Our story

From the plant
to your pavement

Rason Asphalt is connected to an asphalt production operation in the Johnson Road area of Inwood and North Lawrence, producing hot asphaltic paving mixtures to customer specifications. That plant-side roots matter: the mix going down on your driveway or lot is fresh and mixed to spec, not trucked in cold from far away.

The Cederhurst Jocar side of the name ties to Jocar Asphalt LLC, part of the local asphalt supply network serving Nassau County, including state and municipal paving contracts. It is a working asphalt outfit, not a broker, with real equipment and a real crew.

Homeowners get a contractor that understands base, drainage and compaction. Property managers get a crew that can handle a full lot: milling, paving, patching and striping.
