How a project runs

1. Walkthrough and estimate. We visit, listen, measure, and put a written scope and price in your hands.

2. Scheduling. Materials ordered, permits pulled, and a realistic timeline you can plan around.

3. The build. Professional staff on site, close coordination with you, and a clean workspace daily.

4. Final walkthrough. We go room by room together until every detail is right.

About

Quality and affordability, without compromise.

DNM Contracting INC - Remodeling and Renovations is a full service general contractor based on Paxton Street in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. With decades of experience, the company has built its name on quality craftsmanship, honest pricing, and treating every home like it matters.

From kitchens and baths to additions, custom homes, and fire restoration, the professional staff brings dedication to the details: careful cost control, close job supervision, and constant coordination with the homeowner. That is how efficient, successful building practices happen, and how projects finish on time and on budget.

They finished the project on time, used quality materials and their price was fair. From a customer review on Birdeye

Whether you are dreaming up a kitchen that finally feels like yours or rebuilding after the unexpected, DNM is the crew Harrisburg homeowners call when they want it done right.
