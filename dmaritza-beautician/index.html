Our Story

A neighborhood salon with a personal touch

D'Maritza Beautician is a beauty salon at 5920 Glenwood Rd in Brooklyn, NY. Day to day, we focus on one thing: hair care done with patience, skill, and genuine warmth.

Directory reviewers describe our team as friendly, professional, and kind, and mention services including hair washing, perms, and bleaching and coloring. Those words mean a lot to us, because they describe exactly the salon we try to be.
