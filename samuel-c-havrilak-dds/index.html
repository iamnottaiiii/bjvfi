Services

The notes below describe general dentistry in general terms. Call the office to ask which treatments are offered here.

Routine exams and cleanings are the foundation of preventive dental care, helping catch small issues before they become painful or costly. Ask the office about scheduling a checkup.

When decay or damage affects a tooth, general dentists commonly repair it with fillings or crowns. Call to ask what restorative options this office provides.

General dentists check gum health at exams and advise on brushing, flossing and professional care. Ask about gum health at your next visit.

Missing teeth raise questions about bridges, dentures and implants. A general dentist can evaluate your situation and explain options. Call the office to ask.

Severe toothaches, broken teeth and swelling should not wait. Call (410) 668-5035 promptly if you have an urgent dental problem.

Service descriptions are general information about dentistry, not a list of this office's offerings. Call (410) 668-5035 to confirm what Havrilak Samuel C DDS provides.
