Our story

A supply store that speaks epoxy

Low Cost Epoxy opened on Lankershim Boulevard with a simple idea: contractors and DIYers in the Valley deserve a local shelf stocked deep with coating products, without driving across town or paying contractor supply markups.

We keep the focus narrow on purpose. Floors are the most abused surface in any building, and a coating is only as good as the prep, the product and the plan behind it. That is why every visit comes with straight advice, not a sales pitch.

Whether you are coating your first garage or your fiftieth warehouse, you will leave with the right products in the right amounts, and a number to call if anything comes up mid project.
