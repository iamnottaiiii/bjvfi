Our story

Keeping foothill
homes kept.

UpKeep started in 2012 with a toolbox, a truck, and a simple observation: La Cañada Flintridge homes are beautiful, and beautiful homes still get dings, drips, and stuck doors.

SINCE 2012

Built on the small stuff

Big remodelers chase big projects. We built UpKeep for everything else: the running toilet before guests arrive, the shelf that has leaned for a year, the front door that never quite latched. Small jobs, done like they matter, because in your home they do.

A

Respect for your home

Shoe covers, drop cloths, and a vacuum pass at the end. Foothill homes deserve white-glove treatment even for a patch and paint.

B

Respect for your time

Arrival windows we actually keep, and a text when we are on the way. Your day should not revolve around a handyman.

C

Respect for your wallet

Sample rates published up front, exact quotes before work, materials at cost. The bill should never be a surprise.

D

Know the limits

We handle the handyman scope with pride. When a job truly needs a licensed electrician or plumber, we say so and point you right.
