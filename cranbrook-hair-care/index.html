About

A neighborhood barbershop

Cranbrook Hair Care sits at 2503 West Maple Road in Bloomfield Hills, Michigan, serving the community with classic barbering. The shop keeps the traditional feel: careful clipper work, sharp edges, and barbers who listen before they cut.

Guests visit for everyday maintenance and for cuts that need to look right the first time. Walk in, take a seat, and leave looking sharp.
