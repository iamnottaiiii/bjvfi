About Falcons Towing & Roadside Assistance

Falcons Towing & Roadside Assistance is a veteran owned and operated company based at 1501 Flanders Ln in Harwood, Maryland, providing safe and professional towing and roadside assistance services.

The company is a registered active motor carrier with the FMCSA (DOT 3693828) and serves drivers across southern Maryland.
