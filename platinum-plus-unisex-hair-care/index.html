Our story

Three Decades on Mott Avenue

Platinum Plus Unisex Hair Care has served Far Rockaway for 31 years, per public business listings. That kind of run does not happen by accident. It happens one chair, one regular, one first haircut at a time.

Parents bring toddlers in for their first cut and keep bringing them back for years. Reviewers describe a clean, quiet shop with good vibes, a family oriented team, and barbers and stylists who know their regulars by name.

Barbershop on one side, full salon on the other: fades and line ups next to braiding, perms and straightening. Everyone walks out better than they walked in.
