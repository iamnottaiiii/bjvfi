Development philosophy

Planned like a city, built like a home

Before entering private development, Folkers leadership spent years in public planning and development for the cities of Lansing, Michigan; Toledo, Ohio; and Cincinnati, Ohio. That civic foundation shapes every project: developments are evaluated not only for returns, but for what they give back to the neighborhood around them.

The result is a portfolio approach grounded in patience. Commercial properties and apartment buildings selected carefully, structured soundly, and managed for the long term.

About the firm

From city hall to the skyline

Folkers Associates & Folkers is a Cincinnati real estate development firm led by its president and owner, Winston Folkers. The firm develops commercial property and apartment buildings, with a special focus on public/private ventures and the syndication of limited partnerships.

Before entering the private sector, Folkers was responsible for planning and development activities for the cities of Lansing, Michigan; Toledo, Ohio; and Cincinnati, Ohio. That public-service foundation is rare among developers, and it is the reason the firm's projects are known for working as well for their communities as they do for their investors.

Folkers is a member of the Urban Land Institute, the National Association of Real Estate Boards, the Chamber of Commerce, the National Trust for Historic Preservation, and the National Association of Housing and Redevelopment Officials. He holds a master of public administration from the University of Minnesota and a bachelor of arts in economics and political science from Hamline University.
