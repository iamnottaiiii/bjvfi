Our story

Licensed since 1995. Still answering the phone.

A Sun Valley contractor with staying power

Century Construction Co. has held California contractor license 704262 since March 1995, operating from 10175 Elkwood St in Sun Valley. Three decades in this trade teaches you what matters: show up when you say you will, write down what you promised, and build it like your name is on the sign. It is.

The company is bonded through North River Insurance Company and carries workers compensation coverage, because a legitimate contractor protects the homeowner as well as the crew. License status is public record with the California Contractors State License Board.
