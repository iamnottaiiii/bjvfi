02 · Why we started

Big companies skip small jobs. We don't.

Most contractors won't return your call for a sticking door or a wobbly shelf. But those little annoyances are what make a house feel unfinished. house husbands was built for them.

We're based right here in Northborough, which means short trips, quick scheduling, and a neighbor showing up at your door, not a call center.

We show up when we say we will. We protect your floors, clean up when we're done, and tell you the price before the work starts, not after.
