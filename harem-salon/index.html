The history

Sixty-plus years of good hair days

Harem Salon has been listed as a Syracuse beauty salon since 1960, a remarkable run that spans generations of clients. Few salons in the city can claim that kind of continuity.

Through the decades, the formula has stayed simple: listen to the client, cut with care, and treat hair health as the foundation of every style.
