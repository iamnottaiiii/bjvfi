Our story

A neighborhood shop with two crafts

MD Auto Care & Audio sits on Cady Dr in Fort Washington, in the middle of one of the area's busiest auto corridors. Drivers know it as the place that does the honest maintenance work, oil changes, brakes, AC, filters, without pushing repairs you do not need.

The audio side grew out of the same idea: most drivers spend hours in their cars, so the sound should be worth it. From simple speaker swaps to full subwoofer setups, installs are done cleanly and tuned before you leave.

Open Monday through Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM. Call (240) 338-7393 to book.
