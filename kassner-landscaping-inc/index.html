About

A Milford fixture since 1977

Kassner Landscaping Inc has operated from 1343 US Route 50 in Milford, Ohio for decades, growing into a nursery, garden center and full-service landscaping company serving Clermont County and the greater Cincinnati area.

Established 1977

Public business records list the company as established in 1977 and incorporated in Ohio. Yellow Pages notes 49 years in business.

Nursery & Crew

Records estimate a small team of about 8 staff, and reviewers describe the people as knowledgeable and friendly.

Local Roots

One address for decades: 1343 US Route 50, Milford, OH 45150, serving customers across Clermont, Hamilton and Warren counties.
