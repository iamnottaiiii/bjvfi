About

Repair shop and tow yard,
one address

Mitchell's Auto Services Center sits at 9700 Old Perry Highway in Pittsburgh's North Hills, running both service bays and tow trucks from the same lot.

The shop

A working shop,
not a waiting room

Mitchell's is listed as an auto repair shop with a towing operation attached, a combination you notice in McCandless Township and the surrounding North Hills neighborhoods. The same address is also associated with Randy Mitchell Towing, so the trucks you see staged on the lot are part of the business, not a coincidence.

The model is simple: diagnose it, fix it, or tow it. Drivers who break down nearby can get their car towed straight into the bays instead of hunting for a second shop after the tow.

The area

North Hills, right off
Perry Highway

Old Perry Highway runs through the heart of Pittsburgh's northern suburbs, and the shop is easy to reach from McCandless, Ross Township, and the Route 19 corridor. Plenty of commuters pass the lot daily, which is exactly why the towing side of the business stays busy.

Hours run Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with Sundays closed. If you are unsure the shop can help, a phone call costs nothing: (412) 608-8111.
