Our story

An All Star Shop, Run Like a Neighbor

ALL STAR AUTO CENTER INC sits at 3005 Niagara Falls Blvd in Amherst, a full service auto repair shop built on one simple idea: treat every car like it belongs to a neighbor, because it usually does.

The heart of the shop is Joe. Customers describe a mechanic who takes the time to explain the repair, walks out to the car, and shows you the worn or broken part with his own hands. No jargon, no pressure, no mystery line items. You see what is wrong, you understand the fix, and you approve it.

That approach is why reviewers say they book in advance now. The word is out in Amherst: if you want it done right the first time, you call All Star.
