Our story

A North Hollywood original

Inferred details

Rooted on Mary Ellen Avenue

Weight Construction Co is registered at 7863 Mary Ellen Avenue in North Hollywood, and the name has history on that street. Public records link the address to Orville H. Weight, once registered as a motor carrier doing business as Weight Construction Company. That is the kind of lineage you cannot buy: a working name carried across generations in the same neighborhood.

Today the company works as a general contractor across the Valley, keeping the same straightforward approach the name was built on: show up, do solid work, stand behind it.
