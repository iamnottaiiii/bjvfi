Our story

Built around your punch list

The idea

Every homeowner knows the list: the drawer that sticks, the paint scuffed by the couch, the faucet that drips just enough to annoy. MetroGuard exists so one trusted visit can clear the whole thing, instead of three different trades on three different days.

The standard

  • Quote before we lift a tool
  • Shoe covers and drop cloths, every visit
  • Everything tested before we leave
  • Photos of finished work on request
  • Honest advice when a job needs a specialist
