Our Story

A new chapter,
written by old hands

Made The Cut opened its doors in July 2016, and it was new in every way that shows: modern design, modern equipment, modern products. But the people holding the scissors were anything but new.

The staff brought 25 years of experience with them, most recently from Shine Hair Salon on 8th Avenue. For their longtime clients, Made The Cut was not a gamble. It was a new chapter with the same trusted stylists, better tools and a brighter room.

That combination is the whole story: veteran hands, current techniques. The salon specializes in coloring and Asian hair, uses Olaplex to keep hair healthy through chemical services, and takes both walk-ins and appointments.

"You can tell the whole team works well together and really cares about the customer experience."

paraphrased from a Yelp review
