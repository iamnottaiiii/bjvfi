Our story

A family roofing business in Alexandria, Kentucky, since 1973.

Leonard P Lauer Roofing Co began in 1973, and it has served Northern Kentucky from the same Alexandria address ever since: 10419 Pleasant Ridge Rd. The company incorporated in 1993, and today President Jeffrey Lauer leads the operation.

The Better Business Bureau has kept a file on the company since 1989 and awards it an A+ rating, its highest. The BBB business profile lists roofing, window, and siding services, and notes 53 years in business.

Decades in one town means something simple: our reputation is local, and we plan to keep it that way. When you call Lauer Roofing, you get a contractor your neighbors have used for generations.
