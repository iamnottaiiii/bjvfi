How we work

Owner-managed,
team-driven

Platinum image barbershop opened in 2018 at 6661 Belair Rd in Baltimore. From day one, the owner has managed the shop personally, building a team of nine barbers who share one standard: every client leaves sharper than they came in.

Nine chairs means short waits and a barber whose style fits yours. Whether you want a skin fade, a classic scissor cut, a sculpted beard or a straight razor shave, there is a specialist on the floor for it.

Walk-ins are welcome, and the shop keeps evening hours on weekdays plus full weekends, so a fresh cut fits your schedule, not the other way around.
