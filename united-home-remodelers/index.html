Our story

Building in the Valley
since 2001

United Home Remodelers began as Home Energy Masters, helping homeowners cut energy waste. Twenty-five years later, we are a full-service general contractor, and efficiency is still in everything we build.

Roots

From energy audits
to full builds

We started by making homes cheaper to run: insulation, windows, efficient systems. Homeowners kept asking us to do more, so we grew into additions, ADUs and custom homes, always with that same efficiency-first mindset.

Today we are based at 9701 Lurline Ave in Chatsworth, serving the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles with one accountable crew.
