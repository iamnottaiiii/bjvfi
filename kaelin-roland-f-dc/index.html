What we treat

Focused care for backs, necks, and nerves.

Chiropractic care centers on the relationship between the spine, the nervous system, and the rest of the body. Dr. Kaelin most often helps patients with the conditions below.

Spine

Back and neck pain

Persistent lower-back pain, upper-back tension, and stiff or painful necks are among the most common reasons patients visit a chiropractor.

Disc

Herniated and cervical discs

Disc bulges, herniations, and cervical disc disease can refer pain, numbness, or tingling into the arms and legs.

Head

Headaches

Tension-type headaches and migraines are frequently connected to posture and cervical spine mechanics.

About

One doctor. Decades of practice.

Illustration

Original illustration created for this site.

Dr. Roland F. Kaelin, D.C., has practiced chiropractic in Pitman, New Jersey for 47 years. He graduated from Sherman College of Straight Chiropractic in 1979 and has run his own solo practice on Pitman Avenue ever since.

His approach reflects the straight-chiropractic tradition: a focus on spinal alignment, the nervous system, and the body's own ability to function well when the spine moves well.

Education

Sherman College of Straight Chiropractic

Doctor of Chiropractic, class of 1979. Sherman is known for its focus on vertebral subluxation and hands-on technique.
