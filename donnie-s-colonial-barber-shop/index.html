A Westborough staple

Donnie’s Colonial Barbershop is a classic, old-style shop on South Street, known for great haircuts and a friendly, family-welcome atmosphere. Kids, dads and grandfathers all take the same chair here.

Walk-ins are welcome, the prices stay below average, and the conversation is always good. It is the kind of shop Westborough has trusted for years.
