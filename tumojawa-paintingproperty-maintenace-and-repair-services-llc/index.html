Service dossier

What the crew
actually does

Straightforward trades, done in the right order: repair first, prep second, paint last. Call (267) 237-4806 to scope your job.

01

Interior Painting

Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, hallways and stairwells. Includes the prep that makes paint last: patching nail holes and dings, sanding rough spots, caulking gaps, and cutting clean lines at ceilings and trim. Walls, ceilings, doors and trim, finished in the sheen that fits each room.

02

Exterior Painting

Siding, brick accents, porches, railings, shutters, doors and trim. Surfaces get scraped, sanded and primed where needed before the topcoat goes on, so the finish holds up to sun, rain and Philadelphia winters instead of peeling by spring.

03

Property Maintenance

Ongoing upkeep for homes and rental properties: paint touch-ups between tenants, recaulking windows and trim, tightening and refastening loose fixtures, and seasonal walk-throughs that catch small problems before they turn into repair bills.

04

Repair Services

The damage and wear that painting alone cannot fix: drywall cracks and holes, damaged trim and molding, water-stained ceilings, scuffed and worn surfaces. Repaired, skimmed and sanded smooth, then painted to blend with the surrounding finish.

Good to know: every job starts with a look at the actual property, because paint and repair quotes mean nothing without seeing the surfaces. Call (267) 237-4806 to set one up.
