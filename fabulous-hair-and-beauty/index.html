Our Story

A Bay Ridge staple on 5th Avenue

Fabulous Hair & Beauty has been part of the 5th Avenue block in Bay Ridge for years, known for friendly staff and stylists who take the time to get it right.

Customers say they can bring in reference photos and get straight, helpful advice, including honest guidance about what will work with their hair and what will not. The salon is good for kids, accepts credit cards, and welcomes walk-ins.

Visit us at 8709 5th Avenue near 86th Street, or call (718) 680-6996 to book your chair.
