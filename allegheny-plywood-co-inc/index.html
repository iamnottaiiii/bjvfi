About

Allegheny Plywood Co Inc is a wholesale distributor of hardwood plywood and panel products in Brook Park, Ohio. Founded in 1952 and run by the Huber family, it operates from three warehouse locations and serves cabinet shops and industry, per its public website alleghenyplywood.com.
