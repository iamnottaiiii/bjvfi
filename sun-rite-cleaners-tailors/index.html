Our story

Belmont's neighborhood
cleaners and tailor shop

On Leonard Street, minutes from Belmont Center, we've built our name on careful work and straight talk.

How we work

Careful with your clothes,
honest with you.

We're the kind of shop where your garments are inspected before they're cleaned, and where a tailor tells you plainly what can and can't be done. One reviewer put it best: they looked at a shirt, decided an alteration would damage it, and said so. That honesty is the whole business model.

Dry cleaning and tailoring under one roof means your suit can be cleaned, pressed, and adjusted without a second trip. Drop off in the morning on your way through Belmont, pick up crisp and ready.

Good to know
