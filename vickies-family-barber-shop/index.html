Our Story

Vickie's Family Barber Shop has been part of the Kent community since 1988. Owner Vickie Knox built the shop the old-fashioned way: one honest haircut at a time, at prices working families can afford.

This is a true family shop. Vickie has cut hair for grandparents, parents, and kids from the same families, sometimes all in the same week. Folks describe her style as no-nonsense and her manner as warm: she asks the right questions, listens to what you actually want, and delivers.

The shop sits at 524 W Meeker St #5 in Kent, a small independent barbershop with a big reputation: 4.8 stars across public reviews and regulars who say they would not trust their hair to anyone else.
