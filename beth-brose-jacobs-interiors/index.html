The Approach

Arches, mosaic, and color with a point of view.

Signature moments run through the work: arched moldings, ceramic mosaic tile, and bold hues that give each space its own sense of place. The studio draws from a deep bench of trusted vendors, marrying residential warmth with commercial rigor.

Every project begins with listening. The goal is never a showroom, it is a home, whether it belongs to one family or three hundred neighbors.

Our Story

A Cincinnati studio, grown by word of mouth.

Since 2003.

Beth Brose Jacobs Interiors began in 2003 as a one designer studio working from home. Early residential clients and a local developer relationship helped the firm grow organically, first into custom homes, then into multifamily communities across the region.

Today the studio counts nineteen team members, led with design director Stephanie Fulkerson Walker, one of the firm's earliest hires. Separate commercial and residential teams share vendors, standards, and a single philosophy: design that feels like home.

The founder's eye was shaped early, raised in Paraguay in a family of Mennonites, where her mother and grandmother taught her to notice the natural beauty around her every day.
