Services

Cleaning that fits your home

Pick a service below. Every job starts with a free quote, call (857) 204-3559 to plan yours.

Deep Cleaning

The reset your home needs. A top-to-bottom detailed clean that reaches the spots daily life misses.

  • Detailed dusting, baseboards and trim
  • Bathrooms scrubbed and sanitized
  • Kitchen degreased, inside and out
  • Floors vacuumed and damp mopped
  • Light fixtures and vents wiped
  • Interior windows and mirrors polished

Recurring Home Cleaning

Weekly, biweekly or monthly visits that keep your home consistently fresh without you lifting a finger.

  • Same trusted team each visit
  • All rooms dusted, vacuumed, mopped
  • Bathrooms and kitchen refreshed
  • Beds made, linens changed on request
  • Priority checklist tailored to your home
  • Flexible scheduling around your life

Move In / Move Out Cleaning

Ceiling-to-floor cleaning for empty homes, so you hand over keys with confidence or settle into a fresh start.

  • Inside refrigerator, oven and microwave
  • Cabinets and drawers wiped inside and out
  • All bathrooms deep cleaned and sanitized
  • Baseboards, doors and switch plates
  • Closets, laundry areas and storage nooks
  • Final floor detail, vacuum and mop

Kitchen & Bath Detailing

The two rooms guests notice most. We give them the deep, detail-first treatment they deserve.

  • Grout, tile and fixture detailing
  • Shower glass and tub descaling
  • Stovetop, range hood and backsplash
  • Counters, sinks and faucets polished
  • Appliance exteriors wiped and shined
  • Trash areas cleaned and deodorized
Sample pricing: deep cleans from $180, recurring visits from $120, move cleans from $250. Every home is different, so final pricing is confirmed with a free quote before any work begins.
