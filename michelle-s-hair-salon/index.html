Our story

Twenty years of good hair days

How we got here

Michelle’s touch, trusted across Northern Virginia

For more than two decades, families across Northern Virginia have trusted Michelle with their hair: first haircuts, wedding-day styles, color transformations and everything between.

Today the salon sits on McWhorter Pl in Annandale, a tidy, welcoming space with plenty of parking and a waiting area stocked with filtered water, coffee and tea. It is the kind of place where appointments are easy to book, the team is accommodating, and nobody is ever rushed.

The philosophy is simple: listen first, cut second. Bring a photo or just a feeling, and Michelle will shape it into a style that fits your hair, your face and your life.
