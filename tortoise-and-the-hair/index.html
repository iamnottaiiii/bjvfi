How the tortoise got its name

The fable got it right

In the old story, the tortoise beats the hare not by rushing, but by refusing to rush. Tortoise & The Hair was named in that spirit: a salon that would rather spend an extra twenty minutes getting your cut exactly right than hurry you out of the chair.

On Hylan Boulevard in Staten Island, that philosophy built something rare. Paraphrased from public reviews, clients describe stylists Ali and Susan as masters of their craft and perfectionists, the kind of people you trust with your hair for twenty years and counting.

One reviewer has been coming since 2000. Another calls the team family without hesitation. A third says simply: the absolute best. When people talk about a salon like that, you know the tortoise is winning.

I have been getting my hair done here for years. To say you become family is an understatement. Paraphrased from Google reviews

What to expect on your first visit: a real consultation, not a rushed greeting. Bring a photo of the cut you want; your stylist will study it with you and tell you honestly how it will work with your hair. Then settle in. The pace here is deliberate, and that is the point.
