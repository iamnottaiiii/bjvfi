Our Services

Oral surgery, explained simply

Select a service to learn more about what it involves.

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Third molars often arrive crowded, angled, or trapped under the gum. Removing them before they cause pain, infection, or damage to neighboring teeth is one of the most common oral surgery procedures.

  • ✓Evaluation with imaging to map tooth position
  • ✓Removal of impacted or partially erupted molars
  • ✓Clear aftercare instructions for a smooth recovery

Dental Implants

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone that acts as an artificial tooth root. It supports a crown that looks, feels, and works like a natural tooth. Patient reviews specifically praise the implant work done here.

  • ✓Permanent replacement for one or more missing teeth
  • ✓Preserves jawbone and neighboring teeth
  • ✓Natural look and full chewing strength

Tooth Extractions

When a tooth is too damaged to save, careful removal protects the rest of your mouth. The practice handles straightforward and surgical extractions with attention to comfort.

  • ✓Removal of damaged, decayed, or broken teeth
  • ✓Planning for replacement options afterward
  • ✓Post procedure care to support healing

Consultations and Referrals

Not sure whether you need surgery? A consultation gives you imaging, an honest assessment, and a clear recommendation. The practice coordinates with referring general dentists so your records travel with you.

  • ✓Honest evaluations with no pressure
  • ✓Second opinions on recommended surgery
  • ✓Coordination with your regular dentist

Meet the practice

Dr. Emory Ross Meyer, DDS

Meyer E Ross DDS is the oral surgery practice of Dr. Emory Ross Meyer, DDS, an oral and maxillofacial surgeon practicing at 6130 Oxon Hill Road, Suite 305, in Oxon Hill, Maryland. His public NPI record lists Maryland dental license 9868 in oral and maxillofacial surgery.

A surgical focus

Rather than general dentistry, this practice concentrates on surgical care: wisdom tooth removal, dental implants, and extractions. That focus is exactly what patient reviews highlight, with praise for implant results and comfortable wisdom tooth procedures.
