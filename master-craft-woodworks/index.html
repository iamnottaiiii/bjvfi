Our story

A neighborhood woodshop

Master Craft Woodworks is a woodworker based at 9025 Sunland Blvd in Sun Valley, California, serving the surrounding San Fernando Valley community. The shop focuses on custom woodworking: pieces planned around your space, your measurements, and how you will actually use them.

Good woodwork is honest work. Solid materials, careful joinery, and a finish that holds up. That is what this shop aims for, one project at a time.
