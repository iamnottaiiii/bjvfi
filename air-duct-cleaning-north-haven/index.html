How it works

Our cleaning process, step by step.

No mystery, no upsells at the door. Here is exactly what happens when you book a visit.

Inspect

We walk your home or business, check vents, returns and the main trunk, and note trouble spots.

Protect

Work areas get drop cloths and vent covers, and your furnace is secured before cleaning begins.

Clean

High-powered vacuum equipment and agitation tools pull built-up dust and debris from every accessible run.

Review

We walk the results with you, reinstall grilles and filters, and leave the space as clean as we found it.

Know the signs

Time for a cleaning? Check your home.

Dust keeps coming back. Surfaces need dusting again just days after you cleaned them.

Musty smells from vents. The HVAC kicks on and an odor fills the room.

Uneven temperatures. Some rooms never feel as warm or cool as the rest of the house.

Allergy flare ups indoors. Sneezing and congestion ease up noticeably when you leave the house.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Complete lint removal from the dryer vent line, outside cap and transition hose. Shorter dry times, lower energy use and a real reduction in lint fire risk.

Sample pricing: $99 to $175 per vent line.

Duct Inspection

A close look inside the ductwork with camera or scope where accessible. We document buildup, leaks, disconnected runs and moisture issues, then give you a plain language report.
