Our Story

A Pasadena studio with an old-soul approach

IThe Beginning

Rooted in Pasadena

Henry Johnstone & Co began with a simple conviction: that a well-designed room should feel inevitable, as if it had always been that way. From the studio at 95 San Miguel Rd, the firm serves homeowners across Pasadena and the San Gabriel Valley.

IIThe Philosophy

Timeless over trendy

Pasadena's architecture, from Craftsman bungalows to historic estates, rewards restraint. The studio favors classic proportions, honest materials, and color palettes drawn from the landscape, so rooms age gracefully instead of dating.

IIIThe Craft

Designed room by room

Each project moves through consultation, concept, development, and installation with close attention to how you actually live: where the light falls, how a room is entered, what a chair needs to invite.

IVToday

An invitation

Whether it is a single room that never quite came together or a whole home awaiting its character, the studio would be glad to walk it with you. Consultations are by appointment.
