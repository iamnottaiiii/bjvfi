Your roof takes the heat so you don’t have to

Valley summers are brutal on shingles, flashing and flat roof membranes. CCS Inc Roofing Contractor works where the wear happens: on the roof itself. Public listings describe the company as a roofing contractor and construction company serving the Woodland Hills area.

One trade, done properly: roofing.

Why call a roofing specialist

Leaks rarely start where the stain appears. A roofer traces water back to its source, whether that is a cracked boot, lifted flashing or worn valleys, and fixes the cause instead of the symptom. If you see stains, missing shingles or granules in the gutters, it is worth a call.
