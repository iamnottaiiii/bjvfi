01 · Why us

A contractor who plans before the first hammer swings.

Most remodel pain comes from weak planning, not weak craftsmanship. Tile ordered before waterproofing is confirmed. Cabinets arriving before floor elevations are set. Windows sized off old measurements. Howard Shermer Co runs a tight preconstruction process so the field work stays orderly: drawings reconciled, permits handled, lead times tracked, and trades scheduled in the right order.

The cheapest bid is rarely the cheapest project. Good management is what protects the budget.
  • Written scopes with line-item pricing, no vague allowances
  • Permit coordination with the City of Glendale and LA County
  • Daily site cleanup and dust protection for occupied homes
  • Direct line to the person running your job, not a call center

Services

Four specialties, one standard: done right the first time.

Kitchen and bath remodels

The two rooms where layout, plumbing, and electrical all collide. We map every trade before demo so cabinets, tile, fixtures, and lighting land exactly where the plan says.

  • Full kitchen reconfigurations and open-plan conversions
  • Bathroom gut remodels, showers, and accessible upgrades
  • Countertops, cabinetry, tile, and fixture installation
  • Plumbing and electrical coordination with licensed subs

What is included

  • On-site measurement and written line-item estimate
  • Permit drawings coordination where required
  • Dust barriers and daily cleanup in occupied homes
  • Final walkthrough with a written punch list

Room additions and ADUs

Adding square footage means foundations, framing, roofing tie-ins, and systems that must marry to the existing house. We handle the structural side and the finish side under one contract.

  • Primary-suite and family-room additions
  • Detached and attached accessory dwelling units
  • Garage conversions permitted to current code
  • Foundation, framing, roofing, and stucco tie-ins

What is included

  • Feasibility review against local zoning setbacks
  • Permit submittal coordination with the city
  • Structural engineering referrals when required
  • Staged schedule so the existing home stays livable

Whole-home renovations

Older Valley homes hide surprises: old wiring, undersized beams, past leak damage. We budget for discovery, open walls carefully, and resolve what we find without derailing the schedule.

  • Complete interior renovations and layout changes
  • Flooring, drywall, paint, and trim throughout
  • Electrical panel and plumbing system upgrades
  • Exterior refreshes: stucco, windows, and doors

What is included

  • Phased plan so you can stay in the home when possible
  • Weekly progress updates with photos
  • Change orders priced in writing before work proceeds
  • Closeout binder with warranties and paint records

Commercial tenant improvements

Office, retail, and light-industrial build-outs on a schedule your lease can live with. We coordinate off-hours work to keep neighboring tenants happy.

  • Office and retail space build-outs
  • Demising walls, storefronts, and restroom cores
  • Lighting, flooring, and finish upgrades
  • ADA path-of-travel corrections

What is included

  • Schedule built around your move-in date
  • After-hours and weekend work options
  • Coordination with building management
  • Certificate of insurance on request

Our story

A Glendale builder with an old-school work ethic.

Howard Shermer Co operates out of 1450 Norton Ave in the heart of Glendale. The company works the way good Valley contractors have always worked: show up on time, write down what you promised, and stand behind the finished job. Public records show the company posting contractor bonds with neighboring cities, a sign of a shop that takes its licensing obligations seriously.

One crew, one foreman, one phone number that actually gets answered.

Homeowners hire us for the unglamorous middle of a project, the part between a beautiful rendering and a finished room. Permits pulled correctly. Inspections passed the first time. Floors protected, neighbors respected, and a site that is swept at the end of every day. That is the whole story, and it is enough.
