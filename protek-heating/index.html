What we do

Heating and cooling services

Repair, replacement and maintenance for furnaces, boilers and central air. Call (774) 276-7894 for honest advice on your system.

Furnace and Boiler Repair

No heat, strange noises, short cycling or rising bills. Protek diagnoses the problem and fixes it, often the same day you call.

Heating System Replacement

When repair stops making sense, get a right-sized, efficient replacement quoted upfront with no pressure.

Central AC Diagnosis and Repair

Weak airflow, warm air, leaks or a unit that will not start. Careful troubleshooting finds the real cause, not a guess.

AC Installation

New central air systems sized for your home and installed cleanly, with the old equipment hauled away.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Heating checkups in the fall, cooling checkups in the spring. Catch small issues before they become mid-season breakdowns.

Safety and Efficiency Checks

Combustion safety, refrigerant levels, airflow and thermostat calibration, all verified and explained.
