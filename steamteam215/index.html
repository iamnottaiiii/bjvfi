Our story

One craft, done well

Steamteam215 is an upholstery cleaning crew based in South Philadelphia, built around a simple idea: do one thing, and do it right.

Who we are

Furniture people, through and through

We started Steamteam215 because we saw too many rushed cleaning jobs: surface wipes that looked fine for a week, then the stains came back. We do it differently.

Every job gets the same treatment: a careful look at the fabric, targeted pre-treatment for stains and odors, deep steam extraction, and a final walkthrough with you before we pack up.
