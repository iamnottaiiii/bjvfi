About

A roofing contractor built around management

Roof Management & Systems Inc. is a California corporation based in Arleta, providing commercial and residential roof services across the San Fernando Valley.

The name says it all. Too many roofs fail because nobody is watching them: small leaks go unnoticed, flashing loosens, and by the time water shows inside, the damage is done. We take a management approach instead, documenting roof condition, making targeted repairs, and maintaining the system over its full life.

As a licensed roofing contractor, we handle everything from a single repair to a full building re-roof, with one company accountable from inspection to final walkthrough.
