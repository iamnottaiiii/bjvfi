The shop

Old-school care
on Liberty Rd

Tony's Family Hair Center sits on busy Liberty Rd in Windsor Mill, and it has earned its 4.9-star reputation the old-fashioned way: one great haircut at a time.

Public reviews paint a consistent picture. Customers praise the reasonable prices, the lack of a wait, and a barber named Dino who keeps a written record of exactly how each customer likes their hair, so regulars can sit down and relax.

Parents call it a great family-friendly place, bringing kids in for trims and even first haircuts. It is the kind of shop where everybody knows your cut.
