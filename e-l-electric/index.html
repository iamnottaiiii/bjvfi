Our Story

A family shop wired into the neighborhood

E&L Electric is a family-owned electrical contracting business based on North Raymond Avenue in Altadena, California. BestProsInTown describes the company as having served Los Angeles and nearby areas for over forty years, and customers treat us like the neighborhood electrician: the number they keep on the fridge.

That reputation was built one job at a time. Reviews mention the same pattern: a prompt visit to look at the problem, a quote that stays put, and electricians who are polite and easy to deal with while the work gets done. From installing fans and reconnecting lights to switchboard upgrades and replacing old wiring, the approach does not change.

We keep the disruption low and the communication high. You will know what we found, what it will cost, and how long it will take before we start. When we finish, we walk you through the work and test it with you.

If your lights are flickering, your panel is maxed out, or you are planning a project that needs real power behind it, call the shop that Altadena has trusted for decades.
