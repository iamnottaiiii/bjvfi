Our story

A neighborhood chair on Avenue X

Maval Beauty Salon is a full-service beauty salon in Sheepshead Bay, Brooklyn, where hair, color and nail care happen in one relaxed visit.

A

One salon, every treatment

The idea behind Maval is simple: knock out all of your beauty treatments in one location. Instead of running across the neighborhood for a cut, color and pedicure, you settle into one chair and the team takes care of it all.

It is the kind of practical luxury busy neighbors appreciate: one booking, one visit, one very good hair day.

  • Hair, color and styling
  • Manicures and pedicures
  • A calm, comfortable chair
B

Detail you can see

Public reviews keep coming back to the same words: neat, careful, professional, kind. Guests praise the attention to detail, from the first consultation to the final mirror check, and say the atmosphere makes the time fly.

That is the standard every appointment is held to, whether it is a quick trim or a full color transformation.

  • Careful, detail-first work
  • An unhurried, comfortable visit
  • Stylists who listen before they cut
C

Part of Sheepshead Bay

Maval sits on Avenue X in the heart of Sheepshead Bay, serving a wonderfully diverse neighborhood. Reviews arrive in more than one language, and the salon's door is open to everyone.

New to the area? Introduce yourself at your first visit. Regulars will tell you this is the kind of place that remembers you.

  • 624 Avenue X, easy to reach
  • A multilingual, welcoming team
  • Weekday hours that fit a workday
