Roofing services

Full roof replacement

When repair no longer makes sense, we tear off to the deck and rebuild: new underlayment, flashing, vents, and shingles or tile rated for Southern California sun.

  • Architectural asphalt shingles, concrete tile, and metal options
  • Decking repairs priced by the sheet, shown before work starts
  • Cool-roof rated materials to cut summer heat gain

Sample pricing: typical valley replacements start around $9,500 for a standard single-story home. Your exact quote follows the free inspection.

Targeted roof repairs

Leaks, lifted shingles, cracked boots, and tired flashing. We find the source, not just the stain on the ceiling.

  • Leak tracing with attic and roofline inspection
  • Pipe boot, chimney, and skylight flashing replacement
  • Shingle and tile patchwork matched to your roof

Sample pricing: most leak repairs fall between $350 and $1,200. Complex flashing work is quoted on site.

Flat and low-slope roofing

Garages, additions, and commercial roofs get torch-down, TPO, or coating systems with sealed seams and proper drainage.

  • New flat roof installs and full tear-offs
  • Elastomeric coatings to extend roof life
  • Ponding water and drain corrections

Sample pricing: coatings from about $4 per square foot; full systems quoted per project after measurement.

Storm damage response

Wind-lifted shingles, fallen limbs, and sudden leaks get priority scheduling and temporary weatherproofing the same visit.

  • Emergency tarping and temporary seals
  • Wind and impact damage documentation for insurance
  • Full repair or replacement to code

Sample pricing: emergency tarping starts around $400. Insurance documentation included at no extra charge.

The Summit standard

Every job follows the same field checklist, whether it is a two-shingle patch or a full tear-off.

  1. Walk and photograph the full roof
  2. Inspect attic side for hidden moisture
  3. Present options: repair, partial, or replace
