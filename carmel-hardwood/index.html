Our story

A Northridge shop for real wood floors

Carmel Hardwood is a flooring contractor based at 17340 Nordhoff St in Northridge, California. We work in one material and know it deeply: how different species move, how subfloors behave in San Fernando Valley homes, and how a finish should wear over decades, not years.

Why wood, and only wood

Flooring is full of generalists. We chose the opposite path. Hardwood installation and refinishing is all we do, which means every estimate, every board and every finish coat benefits from years of focused practice.

The result is floors that lie flat, stay quiet, and look better with age. That is the whole business plan.

Measured, not guessed

We measure moisture, check the subfloor, and acclimate the wood before a single board goes down.

Finished to last

Dust-conscious sanding and a proper finish schedule mean your floors wear gracefully for years.

Straight talk

If your floors are candidates for refinishing instead of replacement, we will tell you. It costs us the bigger job and earns us the repeat call.
