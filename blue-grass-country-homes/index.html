Why a log home

A log wall is structure, insulation, and finish in one. It ages beautifully, holds heat through a Kentucky winter, and every home we raise has a character no stick-built house can copy.

There is a reason log homes have stood in Kentucky for two hundred years. Thick timber walls breathe with the seasons, shrug off weather, and settle into the landscape instead of sitting on top of it. When we raise a home in Hebron, we are building in that same tradition, with modern sealing, chinking, and energy detailing underneath the rustic look.

Every project starts with your lot and your life: which way the porch should face, where the morning light lands, how many boots need a place by the door. Then we draw a plan that fits, cut the package, and raise it with a crew that knows timber.

"Build it once, build it from timber, and your grandchildren will argue over who gets it."

What we build. Full custom log homes, smaller cabins and weekend retreats, and timber additions like great rooms and wraparound porches. Shell packages for owner-finishers are available too. Ask about current lead times when you call, since every home is cut to order.

Where we build. Our shop is in Hebron, and we raise homes across Northern Kentucky and the surrounding region. If your lot is within a day's drive of Boone County, call us and we will talk it through.

How it starts. One phone call. Bring your lot, your wishlist, or just an idea. We will walk you through plans, pricing, and timeline with no pressure.

Our story

A small Kentucky crew with a big-timber craft.

Blue Grass Country Homes grew out of a simple belief: a home should be built from real materials by people who know their names. From our Hebron shop, a small crew designs, cuts, and raises log homes across Northern Kentucky, keeping the old craft alive with modern building science underneath.

We stay small on purpose. A handful of builders means the person who draws your plan is on site when the walls go up. It means every corner notch, every chink line, and every porch post gets the same care, because our name is on the beam.

"Kentucky was built from timber. We are just keeping the tradition standing."

The craft. Full-log walls, saddle-notched corners, timber roof systems, and stone or timber foundations. We seal and chink for modern energy performance while keeping the hand-hewn character that makes a log home a log home.

The promise. Line-by-line pricing before we build. A crew that treats your land like their own. And a walkthrough at the end where we do not leave until you love it.
