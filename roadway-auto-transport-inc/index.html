Based in Pittsburgh, shipping everywhere

Roadway Auto Transport Inc. operates from 355 Fifth Ave in Pittsburgh, PA, coordinating vehicle transport across the country. As a trucking company focused on auto transport, we connect shippers with carriers that move cars, trucks, and SUVs safely from pickup to delivery.
