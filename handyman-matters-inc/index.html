Our Story

Built on one promise: help you love your home

Handyman Matters Inc started with a simple observation. Homeowners in Washington County had a long list of small jobs, and no one reliable to finish them. The big contractors were too busy. The solo handyman was too unpredictable.

So we built the middle path: a professional team of multi skilled craftsmen, each with 20 to 25 years of experience, background checked, uniformed, and backed by a real warranty. Today we have completed more than 17,000 home repairs and remodels across the South Hills of Pittsburgh, Washington and Canonsburg, the Mon Valley, and Uniontown.

The awards followed the work: ten straight years of the Angie's List Super Service Award, an A+ Better Business Bureau rating, and a one year warranty on all labor and materials that few in the trade will match.
