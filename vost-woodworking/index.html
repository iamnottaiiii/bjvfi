The shop

Wood remembers. A good board has grain that tells you where it wants to go, and the job of a woodworker is to listen before cutting. At Vost Woodworking, that is the whole approach: measure twice, cut once, and finish by hand so the piece ages as well as the day it leaves the shop.

"Furniture should outlive trends. We build it to."

Every project gets a fixed conversation up front, honest timelines, and joinery chosen for strength, not speed. Call (508) 380-8501 to talk through an idea.

About

A one-shop operation in Jefferson

Vost Woodworking is a woodshop at 564 Quinapoxet St in Jefferson, Massachusetts, serving Worcester County and the surrounding towns. The shop builds custom furniture, cabinetry, and millwork one piece at a time, with an emphasis on solid joinery and honest materials.

The approach is straightforward: talk through the idea, pick the right wood for the job, build it to outlast its first home, and finish it so it looks better with age. No catalog, no assembly line, no shortcuts in the joinery.

"Measure twice, cut once, finish by hand."

The craft

What goes into every piece

Solid hardwoods

Oak, maple, walnut, cherry, and other domestic hardwoods, chosen per project for grain, strength, and color.

Real joinery

Mortise and tenon, dovetails, and other mechanical joints, so pieces stay tight for decades, not seasons.

Hand finish

Oil and film finishes applied by hand and rubbed out smooth, easy to maintain and repair over the years.
