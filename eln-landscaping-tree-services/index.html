About us

Locally owned, and invested in our clients

ELN landscaping & tree services is a landscaping and tree service business based at 131 Chatham St in Lynn, Massachusetts, locally owned and operated.

Our experience comes from years of hands-on work in tree care, landscaping and construction. We keep learning industry best practices for tree trimming, removal, safety procedures and proper equipment operation, because tree work done wrong is dangerous and tree work done right is an art.

We believe in fair, honest and competitive pricing, great communication, integrity and quality work. You are one call away from relief, whatever the problem may be.

🌲

Our promise

Great communication, integrity and quality work on every project, no matter how big or small. We look forward to earning your business.

Safety first
