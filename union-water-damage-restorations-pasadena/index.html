Why speed matters

Water does not wait. Neither do we.

Within hours, water migrates behind baseboards and into wall cavities. Within days, damp materials can start growing mold, turning a manageable cleanup into a major rebuild.

Our process starts with stopping the source and extracting standing water, then mapping hidden moisture so drying targets the structure, not just the surface.

How a restoration runs

A structured process keeps surprises, and costs, down.

01

Inspect and assess

Find the source, map affected areas, and check for hazards before work begins.

02

Extract water

Pumps and vacuums remove standing water fast, before it soaks deeper.

03

Dry and dehumidify

Air movers and dehumidifiers pull moisture from inside walls and floors.

04

Clean and restore

Sanitize, deodorize, then repair drywall, flooring, and paint.

What we handle

Emergency water extraction

Burst pipe, appliance leak, or flooding: standing water comes out first, with powerful truck-mounted and portable extraction units.

  • Rapid response for active leaks
  • Standing water removal from floors and carpets
  • Water shutoff assistance on arrival
Sample photo

Structural drying and dehumidification

Surface dry is not dry. We track moisture inside walls, subfloors, and cavities with meters until readings confirm the structure is truly dry.

  • Thermal imaging and moisture mapping
  • Commercial air movers and dehumidifiers
  • Daily moisture readings with documentation
Sample photo

Mold inspection and remediation

Where water sat too long, mold follows. We contain the area, remove affected materials, and treat surfaces to stop regrowth.

  • Containment to stop spore spread
  • Removal of unsalvageable materials
  • Antimicrobial treatment and prevention plan
Sample photo

Sewage backup cleanup

Sewage is a biohazard, not just a mess. We remove contaminated water and materials, then sanitize and deodorize the area thoroughly.

  • Safe removal of contaminated materials
  • Deep sanitizing and odor treatment
  • Protective handling of your belongings

Damage assessment and repairs
