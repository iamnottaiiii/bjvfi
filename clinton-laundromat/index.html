Our story

A neighborhood workhorse on Piscataway Road

Every neighborhood needs a place where the machines work, the dryers run hot, and nobody overcomplicates wash day. On Piscataway Road in Clinton, that place is Clinton Laundromat, a self service coin laundry serving Prince George's County families and regulars who have been coming for years.

No frills, no gimmicks. Just clean machines and fair prices.

Directory listings note the shop is small, with a team of just a few people, and directory records name Tom Cook as the contact. Some listings also show the shop under the name Cook's Laundrymat, a nod to the family behind the counter.

Reviewers keep coming back to the same themes: the place is clean, the customer service is friendly, and drying is fairly cheap. One reviewer washed patio furniture pillows here and called it one of the best laundromats they had ever visited. Another noted the on site change dispenser and the plastic tops for carrying quarters.

Honest notes from the reviews: a few visitors wish the hours ran longer, and prices can run a bit higher than some expect for the area. The dryers do the job, though, and the folding space gives you room to finish properly.
