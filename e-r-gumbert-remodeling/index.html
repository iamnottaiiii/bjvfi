About the company

A Williamsburg remodeler, 21 years in

E R Gumbert Remodeling Ltd. works out of Williamsburg, Ohio, remodeling homes across Clermont County and the Cincinnati area.

E R Gumbert Remodeling Ltd. has spent 21 years doing one thing well: remodeling homes. Kitchens and bathrooms are the specialties, with decks, porches, basements, and total house remodeling rounding out the trade.

Based at 4399 Dela Palma Rd in Williamsburg, the company serves homeowners across Clermont County, including Amelia, Batavia, Bethel, Felicity, Goshen, Loveland, and Milford, plus the wider Cincinnati area.

The record speaks plainly: a 5.0-star rating across 7 Angi reviews, every one of them five stars. Homeowners keep the number because the work holds up.

The way we work

  • Specialists, not generalists. Kitchens, baths, decks, porches, basements: the rooms we remodel every week.
  • One accountable crew. From first demo day to final walkthrough, you know who is in your house.
  • Built for daily life. Waterproofing, ventilation, and framing done right, because pretty fades and solid lasts.
  • Local and reachable. Based in Williamsburg, on the phone when you need us.
