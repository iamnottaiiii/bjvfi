Our story

Supplying western PA since 1968.

An independent supply house with a long memory.

Pyramid Supply and Equipment Co opened its doors in 1968 on Noblestown Road in McDonald, Pennsylvania. The region was full of shops, mines, mills and contractors who needed parts fast and advice they could trust.

More than five decades later, the formula has not changed. Stock the right inventory. Know your customers by name. Get them the part that actually fixes the problem, not the closest thing on the shelf.

Today the counter serves machine shops, maintenance departments, fleets, farms and contractors across Washington and Allegheny counties. Owner John Zirwas and the team keep the tradition going: independent, knowledgeable and easy to do business with.
