Our story

38 years of showing up
and doing it right

A small, accountable crew in Alexandria, KY, built on referrals, repeat clients, and work that holds up.

1988

The beginning

Olympic Contracting Inc opens its doors in Alexandria, Kentucky, a small general contracting crew with a simple standard: do it right, or do not do it.

The 2000s

Restoration expertise

The company grows into disaster restoration, handling fire, flood, and water damage cleanup and put-back, and adds ACAC certified mold remediation personnel to the team.

2018

Recognized for service

Pulse of the City News awards Olympic Contracting its Excellence in Customer Satisfaction award, a 5 out of 5 rating drawn from reviews, surveys, and customer feedback.

Today

Still referral-driven

38 years in, most customers are referrals or repeat clients. The company maintains an A+ BBB rating and serves homes, businesses, and HOAs across the Tri-state area.
