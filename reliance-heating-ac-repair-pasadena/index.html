What we do

Heating & cooling services in Pasadena

Pick a category to see what is covered. Every job starts with a diagnosis and a straight answer about repair versus replacement.

AC repair & service

When the cool air stops, the clock starts. Technicians trace the fault to its source so the fix lasts.

  • AC not cooling or blowing warm air
  • Weak airflow and hot spots in rooms
  • Refrigerant checks and leak diagnosis
  • Capacitors, contactors, and electrical faults
  • Thermostat diagnosis and replacement
  • Condensate drain and coil cleaning

Good to know

A system that runs constantly but never quite cools is working too hard. That shortens equipment life and raises bills. A tune-up or repair now usually costs far less than a compressor later.

Furnace & heat repair

Pasadena winters are mild but real. Heat that will not start on a cold night is an emergency, and treated like one.

  • Furnace will not start or keeps cycling
  • Ignition and pilot problems
  • Heat pump diagnosis and service
  • Blower motor and airflow issues
  • Safety and carbon monoxide checks
  • No-heat and partial-heat troubleshooting

Safety first

If you ever smell gas near your furnace, leave the area and call your gas utility before calling anyone else. Never try to relight or repair a gas appliance yourself.

Seasonal maintenance

The cheapest repair is the one you never need. Regular maintenance keeps efficiency up and catches wear before it fails.

  • Full system tune-ups, cooling and heating
  • Filter replacement guidance
  • Electrical connection tightening and testing
  • Coil and blower cleaning
  • Refrigerant level verification
  • Performance and efficiency checks

When to schedule

Book cooling maintenance in spring and heating maintenance in fall, before each season puts your system under load. One call covers both sides of the system.

System installation & replacement

When repair no longer makes sense, a right-sized new system pays you back in comfort and lower bills.

  • Full system replacement planning
  • Load calculation and right-sizing
  • High-efficiency equipment options
  • Ductwork evaluation
  • Thermostat and control upgrades
  • Old equipment haul-away

Repair or replace?

General rule of thumb: if the repair costs more than half of a new system and the equipment is past 10 years, replacement is usually the smarter money. Ask for both numbers before you decide.
