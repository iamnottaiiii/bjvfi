How refinishing works

Four firings of finish, done in a day or so

Refinishing is a layered process: clean, prep, bond, and spray. The exact steps vary from shop to shop, so ask Perry what their process includes when you call.

01
Stage one

Strip and deep clean

Old coatings, soap film, and grime come off so the new finish has a clean surface to grip. Sample

02
Stage two

Repair and mask

Chips get filled and the surrounding tile, fixtures, and floors get masked for protection. Sample

03
Stage three

Etch and bond

The surface is etched and a bonding agent goes down so the glaze coat adheres for the long haul. Sample

04
Stage four

Spray and cure

Fresh glaze coats are sprayed on and left to cure into a hard, glossy shell. Sample

Good to know: this four-stage outline describes refinishing in general. Call (513) 541-5151 to confirm exactly what Perry Porcelain Refinishing includes in their jobs.
