When it's urgent, call first

Dental emergencies rarely keep office hours. A cracked tooth at dinner, a lost crown on the weekend, swelling that won't quit. Calling right away is the fastest path to relief and the best chance of saving the tooth.

  • Severe toothache or sensitivity that won't ease up
  • Chipped, cracked or knocked-out tooth
  • Lost filling, crown or denture breakage
  • Swelling, abscess or bleeding gums

Urgent dental care, when it matters most

Urgent Care Dentist 24/7 exists for one reason: dental problems don't wait, so help shouldn't either. Based at 412 Mother Gaston Blvd in Brooklyn, the practice focuses on fast relief and complete denture care.

Built around the emergency, not the schedule

A toothache at midnight feels very different from a routine cleaning. Urgent dental care means prioritizing pain relief, same-day help and clear answers, so patients know exactly what is wrong and what comes next.

As a denture care center, the practice also serves patients who depend on dentures every day. A cracked plate, a sore spot or a loose fit can ruin a week. Repairs, relines and new dentures are handled with the same urgency as a toothache.

The approach is simple: call, describe what hurts, and get a straight answer about what can be done and what it will cost. No jargon, no surprises.

  • Pain-first triage: the worst pain gets seen fastest
  • Clear treatment plans explained before work begins
  • Denture expertise: repairs, relines, full and partial dentures
