Our story

The little bakery with the big oven

Up before the sun

Our day starts in the dark so the first trays are warm by 5 AM. That early-morning smell of fresh bread is our hello to Lansing.

From La Rosa to Diana's

This bakery began as La Rosa de Guadalupe Bakery, and today Diana's Mexican Bakery carries that same tradition forward at 19124 Burnham Ave. The name changed, but the recipes and the welcome did not.

We are a small, quaint, cash-only bakery, and proud of it. What we do not have in size we make up for in freshness: shelves and cases lined with cookies, muffins, brownies, cakes, and pan dulce, all baked right here.

A big part of our work happens behind the scenes. We bake bread for local stores and restaurants, so when you order a torta at a neighborhood taqueria, there is a good chance the bread came from our ovens.
