Popular services

Heating, cooling, and everything in between

From emergency AC repair to full system replacement, one call covers your whole home comfort system.

AC Repair

Blowing warm air, strange noises, or a unit that will not start. Our technicians diagnose the fault, explain your options in plain language, and get cold air flowing again, usually the same day.

  • Refrigerant leak detection and recharge
  • Capacitor, contactor, and fan motor replacement
  • Thermostat troubleshooting and upgrades
  • Emergency after hours AC service

Heating Repair

Furnaces and heat pumps that short cycle, blow cold, or refuse to start. We service gas, electric, and heat pump systems so your home stays warm through every cold snap.

  • Furnace ignition and burner service
  • Heat pump repair and defrost issues
  • Carbon monoxide safety checks
  • Pilot light and flame sensor service

System Installation

When repair no longer makes sense, we size and install high efficiency systems matched to your home and budget, with clean workmanship and full startup testing.

  • Central AC and furnace replacement
  • Heat pump installations
  • Ductwork evaluation and sealing
  • Smart thermostat setup included

Maintenance Plans

Twice yearly tune ups catch small problems before they become breakdowns, keep efficiency high, and extend the life of your equipment.

  • Spring cooling tune up
  • Fall heating tune up
  • Filter and coil cleaning
  • Priority scheduling for members

Our story

A local shop built on showing up

HVAC Solutions Heating & AC Repair started with a simple observation in Lake View Terrace: when heating and cooling equipment fails, people do not need a sales pitch, they need a technician who answers, arrives, and fixes it.

So we built the business around response time and straight talk. We answer around the clock, we quote before we wrench, and we treat every home like it belongs to a neighbor, because in the foothill communities we serve, it usually does.

Thirty two verified reviews and a 4.9 star average later, most of our work comes from repeat customers and referrals. That is the whole business plan, and we are sticking with it.
