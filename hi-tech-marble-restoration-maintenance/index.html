Why restoration beats replacement

Your marble is not ruined. It is just tired.

Etch marks from lemon and vinegar, dull traffic lanes, grout that never looks clean. These are surface problems with surface solutions. Restoration costs a fraction of replacement and keeps your original stone in place.

01

Marble floors

Traffic lanes, scratches, and lost shine are diamond-honed and polished back to a mirror or satin finish, your choice. Lippage between tiles can be flattened so the floor feels as good as it looks.

02

Kitchen and bath stone

Etch rings, water spots, and dull patches on counters and vanities are removed and re-polished in place. No tear-out, no dust storm, no weeks without a kitchen.

03

Travertine and limestone

Holes filled, grout deep-cleaned, and surfaces honed to an even finish. Sealing locks in the result so the stone stays easy to live with.

04

Care plans

Stone stays beautiful with the right routine. Scheduled maintenance visits keep floors and counters polished year after year, with products that are safe for natural stone.

Our story

A stone-only shop for the San Fernando Valley.

Sample Company background below is representative of an independent stone restoration business; details confirmed at booking.

01

Why we exist

Most contractors treat stone like tile. It is not tile. Marble, travertine, limestone, and granite each react differently to abrasives, acids, and sealers, and the wrong call can ruin a floor in an afternoon. Hi-Tech Marble Restoration & Maintenance was built to do one thing well: bring natural stone back to life and keep it that way.

02

How we work

Every job starts with testing, not guessing. We identify the stone, the finish, and the damage, then choose the diamond grit sequence and chemistry for that exact situation. Dust-controlled grinding, careful masking, and a walk-through at the end are standard on every visit.

03

Where we work

Based at 7657 Winnetka Ave in Winnetka, we serve homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley, including Canoga Park, Reseda, Woodland Hills, Northridge, and Encino, plus greater Los Angeles by appointment.

04

What we promise

Honest assessments, written quotes, stone-safe methods, and finishes we stand behind. If restoration is not the right answer for your stone, we will tell you that too.
