Our story

A concrete crew, not a call center

Rogers Custome Concrete Construction is based on Western Reserve Road in Crescent Springs and works across Northern Kentucky and Cincinnati.

Built on strength. Poured with pride.

That is how the company describes itself, and it shows up in the work: residential and commercial concrete from a local crew that does its own forming, pouring, and finishing.

Public business listings put the company at about three decades in the trade, serving the full Northern Kentucky and Cincinnati area: Boone, Kenton, and Campbell counties and across the river.

Residential

Driveways, patios, garage floors.

Commercial

Slabs, flatwork, cutting.
