How we work

Design and construction, side by side

Mega Group Builders Inc. is a full service construction company based at 5900 Sepulveda Blvd in Sherman Oaks. Since 1984 we have served homeowners and businesses across the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles.

Our model is design-build: trusted architects and engineers work with our construction team from concept and pre-construction through every phase of the build. A designer helps you plan the space, then our field crew brings it to life, with one team accountable for the result.

Custom homes, remodels, room additions, commercial spaces: one process, tuned to the project in front of us.

Whether the project is a single bathroom, a whole house, or a commercial tenant improvement, the approach is the same: listen first, plan carefully, build cleanly, and finish like it is our own name on the door. Because it is.
