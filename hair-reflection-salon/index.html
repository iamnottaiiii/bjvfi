Our story

A family salon since 2008

Hair Reflection Salon opened on Lyman Street in 2008 as a small family business, and that is still exactly what it is. Two stylists, a warm room, and a steady stream of neighbors who keep coming back.

Owner Anh Tram and the team built the salon on a simple idea: you do not need a luxury price tag to get a great haircut. You need a stylist who listens, takes her time, and remembers your name.

  • Stylists with 10+ years behind the chair
  • Known for affordable, honest pricing
  • Kid friendly and welcoming to all ages
  • Walk-ins welcome when a chair is open
  • Personal service from stylists who know you

Why regulars stay

No fuss. Just great hair.

Guests describe the salon as charming and relaxing, the kind of place where a ten-minute haircut turns into a standing appointment for years. It is the opposite of a chain: here, you know exactly who will be cutting your hair, and she knows exactly how you like it.
