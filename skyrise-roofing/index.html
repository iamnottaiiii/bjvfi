Services

Roofing work, organized by what your roof needs.

Pick a category to see what is included. Every job starts with an inspection so you know the full picture before any work begins.

Roof repairs

Targeted fixes for leaks, damage, and wear. We trace the source, not just the stain, and repair the affected area with materials matched to your existing roof.

  • Leak detection and source tracing
  • Shingle, tile, and slate spot replacement
  • Flashing repair around chimneys and vents
  • Flat roof patching and seam repair
  • Gutter and downspout repair
  • Skylight leak sealing

Full roof replacement

When repairs stop making sense, we replace the whole system: tear off, deck inspection, underlayment, and new roofing installed to manufacturer spec.

  • Asphalt shingle systems
  • Clay and concrete tile
  • Flat and low slope membranes
  • Underlayment and deck repair
  • Ventilation upgrades
  • Final walkthrough and cleanup

Roof maintenance

Regular checkups catch small problems while they are cheap. A maintenance visit covers the full roof surface, drainage, and every penetration.

  • Full roof surface inspection
  • Debris and gutter clearing
  • Sealant and flashing check
  • Moss and buildup treatment
  • Written condition report
  • Priority scheduling for repairs found

Emergency service

Storm damage, fallen branches, sudden leaks: we respond fast, stop the water intrusion with temporary protection, then plan the permanent fix.

  • Rapid leak response
  • Emergency tarping and weatherproofing
  • Wind and storm damage assessment
  • Insurance documentation photos

Our story

A North Hollywood roofer that treats your home like its own.

SkyRise Roofing is a roofing contractor based at 6150 Simpson Ave in North Hollywood, serving homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley. We got into this trade because a roof is the one part of a house you cannot afford to get wrong, and too many homeowners get talked into work they do not need.

So we run the business on a simple rule: inspect first, explain clearly, and recommend only what the roof actually needs. You get photos of what we find, a plain language explanation, and a written scope before any work starts.

When the job is done, we walk the property with you, clean the site like we were never there, and leave you with a roof you can stop worrying about. That is the whole business model.
