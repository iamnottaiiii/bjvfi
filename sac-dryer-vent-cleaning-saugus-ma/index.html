Fire Safety First

Lint packed into a dryer vent is one of the most common household fire hazards. A professional cleaning removes the buildup you cannot reach from inside the laundry room.

Dryers That Finish Faster

Clogged vents make dryers work twice as long. Clearing the line restores airflow, so loads dry in one cycle and the machine stops running hot.

Cleaner Indoor Air

Dust, pet dander, and debris collect inside ductwork and recirculate through the house. Air duct cleaning helps every room breathe easier.

Offerings

Services built around airflow.

Every job starts with a look at your vent path and ends with airflow you can feel. Here is what SAC handles.

  • 01Dryer Vent CleaningResidential
    Complete cleaning of the dryer vent line, from behind the machine to the outside cap. Clears packed lint, bird nests, and crushed or disconnected sections that choke airflow. Ideal when dry times have crept up or the dryer runs hot.
  • 02Air Duct CleaningWhole home
    Cleaning of supply and return ducts to remove years of settled dust, pet hair, and debris. Helps HVAC systems move air freely and keeps recirculated air cleaner for the whole household.
  • 03Vent Inspection & Safety CheckAdd on
    A walkthrough of the full vent path: checking for disconnected joints, crushed flex hose, blocked exterior caps, and improper materials. You get a plain-English report on what is safe and what needs attention.
  • 04Rental & Multi-Unit PropertiesLandlords
    Vent and duct cleaning for apartments, condos, and rental turnovers. Keeping dryer vents clear across units protects the property and keeps tenants safe. Ask about scheduling multiple units in one visit.

About

A Saugus crew for the air you breathe.

SAC Dryer Vent Cleaning and Air Duct Cleaning Service is based at 12 Pleasant St in Saugus, Massachusetts, and serves homeowners, landlords, and property managers across the area.

The business keeps its focus narrow on purpose: dryer vents and air ducts. That specialization means every visit is about airflow, safety, and indoor air quality, nothing else competing for attention.

Dryer vent fires are a real and preventable hazard, and most start with lint that no vacuum or brush from inside the house can reach. SAC's work targets exactly that buildup, along with the dust that settles into ductwork over the years.

Open six days a week with long hours, the service is built around real schedules: early calls before work and evening appointments after it.
