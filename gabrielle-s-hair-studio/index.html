Our story

The chair feels like family
because it is

Gabrielle's Hair Studio is a small, independent hair studio on Genesee Street in Auburn, built one loyal client at a time.

Since day one

A studio, not a chain

No rushed appointments, no upsell scripts. At Gabrielle's, the appointment is personal: easy conversation, careful work, and stylists who remember what you asked for last time.

Clients talk about Thom by name, a stylist who has been cutting hair in Auburn for a very long time, and about Jen, who has cared for some families' hair for more than thirty years. That kind of loyalty is earned one appointment at a time.

It is the kind of studio where a birthday hair coloring becomes a celebration, and where a first-time client can walk in nervous and walk out a regular.
