Why refinish instead of replace

A quality reglaze costs a fraction of a full tear-out, takes about a day, and keeps your existing plumbing, tile and walls exactly where they are. No dumpster, no dust, no weeks without a bathroom.

Tri State Tub & Tile grew out of Perma Ceram of Cincinnati, a porcelain enamel repair name local homeowners have trusted for decades. The approach is still the same: meticulous prep, honest advice, and a finish built to last.

Services

Every service is a reglazing service: repair, refinish and protect, all in place. Estimates are always free, call (513) 474-6896 to schedule yours.

01

Bathtub Reglazing

The flagship service. Cast iron and steel porcelain tubs are etched, repaired and recoated. Reviewers consistently praise how dramatically better an old tub looks after a reglaze, especially with the full sanding prep option.

02

Tile Reglazing

Wall tile, shower surrounds and wainscoting refinished without removing a single tile. Grout lines get sealed and unified for a fresh, modern look at a small fraction of retiling cost.

03

Sink Refinishing

Porcelain and cast iron sinks with stains, chips or dull finishes are resurfaced to a hard, glossy coating that wipes clean. A smart add-on during a tub or tile job.

04

Fiberglass Shower Refinishing

Acrylic and fiberglass shower stalls, tub surrounds and shower pans repaired for cracks, then refinished. Reviewers note the clean, even finish and the thousands saved versus replacing the unit.

05

Porcelain Enamel Repair

The original Perma Ceram craft: chips, rust and cracks in porcelain enamel filled, color-matched and blended, then sealed under the reglaze coating for a seamless result.

About the company

From Perma Ceram to Tri State Tub & Tile

According to the Better Business Bureau, this company previously operated under the name Perma Ceram of Cincinnati, a porcelain enamel repair name with deep local roots. Today it trades as Tri State Tub & Tile, still focused on the same craft: restoring tubs, tile, sinks and showers instead of replacing them.

It is an owner-operated shop. Reviewers describe the owner as professional, honest and punctual, someone who explains the work, gives solid advice without pushing upgrades, and takes pride in the finish. The result, in their words: a 42-year-old tub that looks brand new, and thousands of dollars saved versus replacement.

Based in the Forestville area of Cincinnati at 8356 Jakaro Dr, the company serves homeowners across the tri-state region. Estimates are free, and the phone is answered by the people who do the work.
