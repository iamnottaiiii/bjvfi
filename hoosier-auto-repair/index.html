A neighborhood shop, not a chain

Hoosier Auto Repair is an independent auto repair shop, the kind where the same people who answer the phone do the work. Customers call it Kevin's Hoosier Auto Repair, and reviews stretching back years describe a plainspoken crew that troubleshoots until a problem is actually solved, charges fair prices, and stands behind the work. One long-time customer wrote that the shop had been fixing their cars for 17 years. Another called Kevin and the crew a breath of fresh air after four or five other shops would not even try to diagnose their problem.

The shop is open weekdays, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday, and closed on weekends, per public directory listings.
