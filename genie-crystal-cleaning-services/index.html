Services

Three wishes, endless sparkle.
Pick your clean.

From weekly upkeep to full reset deep cleans, every service follows the same crystal standard. Free estimates on everything.

01

Recurring Home Cleaning

Weekly, biweekly or monthly visits that keep the whole house consistently clean. Kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas, dusted, vacuumed, mopped and polished on a schedule you set once and forget.

Sample pricing

Biweekly plans often start around $140 per visit for a typical 3 bedroom home. Final quote depends on size, pets and condition.

02

Deep Cleaning

The full reset. Inside cabinets and appliances, baseboards, window sills, grout lines, ceiling fans and every corner a routine clean misses. Ideal before guests, after renovations or once a season.

Sample pricing

Deep cleans often start around $280 for a typical home. We walk the space first and quote exactly.

03

Move In / Move Out Cleaning

Empty-home detailing that makes move day easier. We clean inside every cabinet and drawer, scrub baths and kitchens to a shine, and leave floors ready for furniture, or ready for the next tenant.

Sample pricing

Move cleans often start around $250 depending on home size and condition. Deposit friendly checklists available.

04

Commercial and Office Janitorial

Reliable janitorial service for offices and workplaces across Connecticut. Restrooms, break rooms, lobbies and workspaces kept presentable on a daily, weekly or custom schedule that fits your business hours.

Sample pricing

Custom quoted per site. We tour your space, learn your schedule and price it fairly. No long contracts required to start.

About pricing: rates above are samples to set expectations. Every home and business is different, so your exact quote comes from a quick call or walkthrough. Estimates are always free.
