Our story

A Kings Highway institution in the making

Excellent Cleaners is a neighborhood dry cleaner at 334 Kings Hwy in Brooklyn, built on careful work and honest service.

i.

Inspected, every time

Each garment is checked when it arrives and again before it goes on the rack. Stains are treated, buttons secured, hems reviewed.

ii.

Finished by hand

Machine pressing gets you most of the way. Our finishers take it the rest, with crisp collars, sharp creases and a natural drape.

iii.

Honest and local

We are on Kings Highway, not in a call center. Ask us anything at the counter, or call (718) 645-0033, and you will get a straight answer.
