About Vinyl Kraft Inc

A Cincinnati window manufacturer since 1993

Three Decades of Glazing

Vinyl Kraft Inc started in Cincinnati on March 12, 1993, and has spent 33 years manufacturing vinyl windows, patio doors, storm windows and screens for the replacement market. Our BBB profile lists the business as a corporation offering window installation and service, led by owner Larry Hoff.

  • Business started: March 12, 1993 (per BBB)
  • Owner: Larry Hoff (per BBB)
  • Entity: Corporation
  • 7725 Reinhold Dr, Cincinnati, OH 45237
  • Official site: vinylkraft.com

Please note: Vinyl Kraft Inc is not a BBB accredited business, and BBB does not have sufficient information to issue it a rating. History details above come from the BBB business profile.
