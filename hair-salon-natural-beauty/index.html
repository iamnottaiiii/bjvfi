About

A neighborhood salon

Hair Salon Natural Beauty is a hair salon, beauty salon, and hair styling shop in Brooklyn, NY.

Straightforward care, calm setting

You will find us at 950 66th St in Brooklyn, a no-frills salon with a clean, orderly space and a calm atmosphere. The focus is simple: good hair care at friendly prices.

Guests note the straightforward approach, cash and cards are accepted, and street parking is available nearby. Listed as an inexpensive salon, it is the kind of place you can visit regularly without a second thought.

Whether it is a trim, color, or a fresh style for the weekend, the team is here to help you look and feel your natural best.
