How every build runs

A four phase method keeps your project on paper before it goes up in the air. Typical sequence shown; your plan is confirmed on the call.

PHASE 01

Walkthrough

We visit, measure, and listen. You describe the end result, we flag what the structure will actually allow.

PHASE 02

Blueprint

Scope, materials, and milestones go into one written plan so there are no surprises mid build.

PHASE 03

Build

Crews execute phase by phase with check ins at every milestone, not just at the end.

PHASE 04

Punch List

We walk the finished work with you and close out every detail before sign off.
