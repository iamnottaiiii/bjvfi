About

Dr. Shabnam Mazhari, DDS

Dr. Shabnam Mazhari earned her Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Maryland in 2005, her Master’s degree at Johns Hopkins University in 2007, and her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Maryland in 2012. She is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD), and the Maryland State Dental Association. Her Baltimore office is listed at 300 N Charles St. Outside the office she enjoys yoga, jogging, new music, cooking, and travel.
