An independent, locally owned auto repair shop serving Lawrenceburg and Dearborn County from Business Center Drive.

A local shop with a loyal following

Bright Automotive is listed as a general automotive repair shop at 2075 Business Center Dr, Lawrenceburg, IN 47025. Business listings name Mark Moorwessel as the owner contact, and reviewers mention mechanics Bob and Mark by name.
