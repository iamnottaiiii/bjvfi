About the Loveland Yard

A working supply yard with deep roots in southwest Ohio

Doing the Dirty Work Since 1975

Evans Landscaping has served the Cincinnati area since 1975, growing from a local operation into a full service landscape company with retail and wholesale supply yards. The Loveland location at 9743 E Kemper Rd is the supply store side of the business: bulk mulch, topsoil, gravel, stone, boulders, and firewood, plus delivery and landscape services.

The company emphasizes American made products and keeps a working fleet of trucks and heavy equipment, so most jobs, from a few yards of mulch to excavation and demolition, can be handled in house.

Why Fall Works

Cooler air plus warm soil is ideal for planting trees and shrubs, and fresh mulch locks in moisture before winter. Ask the yard crew what is in stock for fall planting.

What reviewers criticize
