Our story

Two businesses,
one address

Proctor put a full barbershop inside a car wash and detailing shop, because nobody has time for two errands.

The one-stop idea

In their own words: you can get your haircut and your car detailed at the same time. That is the whole pitch, and customers love it.

Drop the car in the bay, walk ten steps to the barber chair, and by the time the fade is done, the interior is spotless and the wax is gleaming.

The waiting room is a real movie theater lounge, so the wait never feels like waiting. Bring the kids, pick a movie, and leave with a fresh cut and a fresh ride.
