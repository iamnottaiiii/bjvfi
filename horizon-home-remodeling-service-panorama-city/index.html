Our story

Built on Van Nuys Boulevard

Horizon Home Remodeling Service Panorama City grew out of years of hands-on construction work across the San Fernando Valley. We watched too many homeowners get burned by contractors who vanished mid-job, padded the bill, or left a mess behind.

We started this company to be the crew we would want in our own homes. That means written scopes, fixed pricing, permits pulled properly, and a site left clean every single night. From our shop at 8155 Van Nuys Blvd, we serve Panorama City, Van Nuys, North Hills, Arleta, and the surrounding Valley neighborhoods.

Most of our work comes from referrals, and we plan to keep it that way: one honest remodel at a time.
