About · Our story

A neighborhood favorite on Main Street

Pink Nail Salon has earned its regulars the steady way: careful work, fair prices, and a salon that always feels clean and calm.

pink
Our approach

Done right, not done fast

Plenty of salons rush you through the chair. Pink Nail Salon built its name on the opposite: technicians who listen, take their time with shaping and cuticle work, and check the details before you leave. Guests mention it again and again in reviews, the nails come out exactly how they pictured them.

The salon keeps a full range of colors and finishes, from everyday neutrals to bold seasonal shades, plus gel systems for long wear and acrylics when you want length and drama.

Clean first

Hygiene you can see

Sanitized tools, fresh pedicure liners, wiped-down stations between every guest. It is the detail reviewers notice most.

The space

Calm by design

Soft colors, comfortable pedicure chairs, and a relaxed pace. Many guests pair their appointment with a little downtown Torrington wandering before or after.

First visit?

Here is how it goes

Walk in or call (860) 618-2970 to book. Tell your technician what you have in mind, or bring a photo. They will talk through options, confirm the price up front, and get to work. Most guests are in and out feeling pampered within the hour.
