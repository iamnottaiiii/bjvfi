Why hire licensed

Maryland requires home improvement contractors to hold a current license, carry liability insurance, and stand behind the Home Improvement Guaranty Fund that protects homeowners.

Insurance in place

Licensed Maryland contractors must show proof of $50,000 in liability insurance to hold their license.

Guaranty fund protection

Hiring a licensed contractor means you are covered by the state's Home Improvement Guaranty Fund.

Permit history

Public permit records show residential and commercial work completed across Pasadena, Annapolis, Severn, and Crownsville.
