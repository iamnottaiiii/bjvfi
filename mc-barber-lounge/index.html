About MC Barber Lounge


MC Barber Lounge is a neighborhood barbershop in Brea built around sharp cuts and honest service. Walk in for a classic cut, a skin fade, or a hot towel shave, and leave looking sharp.


Every appointment starts with a consultation: what you like, what you do not, and how much upkeep you want. The result is a cut that looks sharp on day one and grows out clean.


Find us at 1211 Imperial Hwy. Unit 103-A, Brea, CA 92821, or call (562) 846-9666 to book your chair.
