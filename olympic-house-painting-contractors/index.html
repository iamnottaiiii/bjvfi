Our story

About Olympic House Painting Contractors

Olympic House Painting Contractors is a painting company based on North Lake Avenue in Pasadena, California. The focus is straightforward: interior and exterior painting for homes and businesses, done with careful prep and a clean finish.

Pasadena homes have character, from craftsman bungalows to Spanish revivals to mid-century ranches, and each surface asks for the right prep and the right product. Our approach stays the same on every job: protect the space, fix the surface, prime it right, and finish it in color you will love for years.
