The ancient art

Threading: precise, gentle, chemical free

Threading uses a twisted cotton thread to lift hair straight from the root. It is an old technique from South Asia and the Middle East, and it is still one of the most precise ways to shape brows.

  • Clean, defined brow lines with single-hair precision
  • No heat, no wax, no chemicals on your skin
  • Gentle option for sensitive and acne-prone skin
  • Quick visits and easy upkeep every few weeks

Why threading

Precision you can see,
gentleness you can feel

Threading removes hair from the root with nothing but twisted cotton thread. No hot wax, no chemicals, no tugging at the skin, which makes it a favorite for delicate facial skin and for anyone who wants crisp, clean brow lines.

At Komal Threading Salon on 3rd Ave in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn, our specialists shape brows to suit each face. Quick visits, friendly prices, and results that grow out beautifully.

Whether it is your first threading or your fiftieth, you will be in practiced hands. Come as you are; leave a little more polished.

Great brows should not hurt your skin or your wallet.
