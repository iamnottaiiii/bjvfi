Our story

From Seoul, 1956, to Annandale

Seventy years of hair artistry, one philosophy: create styles that suit the individual.

1956 · Seoul

Five chairs in Myeong-dong

Kim Sun Young Beauty Salon began in 1956 as a small five-member salon in Myeong-dong, Seoul, Korea. Through relentless investment in education and equipment, and a passion for creating hairstyles suited to each individual, KSY grew into a worldwide family of hundreds of designers.

Founder Kim Sun Young, born in Cheorwon, Korea in 1938, began her career at 19 after graduating from the ChungHa hair institute. She went on to invent new perm and cutting techniques, including the original Straight Perm, and today leads the worldwide KSY salons from her office in Los Angeles.

56
1990 · Los Angeles, then Annandale

A world leader arrives in America

KSY opened its first American salon in Los Angeles in 1990, bringing Korean hair fashion to the United States. The Annandale salon carries that same legacy: unique haircuts and hair color that set trends, and perm expertise trusted across Los Angeles, New York, Chicago and Seoul.

Every designer is trained to do one thing above all: design the right hairstyle for you, so you leave fully satisfied.
