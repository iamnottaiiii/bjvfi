The difference

A dock is only as good as what is under it

Anyone can nail boards together. The part that matters is below the waterline: pilings set deep, framing squared, hardware that will not rust out in two seasons.

That is where 34 years of marine contracting shows. We build for storms, tides and the long haul, so your dock is an asset, not a annual project.

Our story

Locally owned, water tested

Custom Marine Systems Inc is a locally owned dock building and marine contracting business in East Rockaway, proudly serving Nassau County and surrounding areas for 34 years.

34 years in business

Decades of dock building on Long Island waterways, through calm seasons and rough ones.

East Rockaway roots

Based on Oxford Road, working the same bays, inlets and canals we know by heart.

Satisfaction first

Quality workmanship and customer satisfaction has been the main focus for years, and still is.

We build what we would tie our own boats to

That is the whole philosophy. Marine construction fails in quiet, expensive ways when it is rushed. So we do not rush it. Deep pilings, honest materials, and a finished dock that is still standing strong when the next storm rolls through.
