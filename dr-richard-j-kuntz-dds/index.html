Why periodontal care

Gum health affects more than your smile

Periodontics is the dental specialty devoted to preventing, diagnosing, and treating problems with the gums and the supporting structures of the teeth. When gums are inflamed or receding, the whole mouth pays the price.

Early, conservative care is almost always simpler than waiting.

Dr. Kuntz takes a measured, explain-first approach: an honest assessment of your gum health, a clear treatment plan, and straightforward answers about costs and options before anything begins.

Periodontal Care

Care for gums, bone, and implants

A periodontist is a dentist with advanced training in the tissues that hold your teeth in place. If a general dentist has referred you, or your gums are telling you something is wrong, call the office to discuss next steps.

01

Gum Disease Evaluation

A thorough look at gum depth, bleeding, and bone support, so you know exactly where your gum health stands and what, if anything, needs attention.

02

Non-Surgical Gum Treatment

Deep cleanings and scaling that remove buildup below the gumline and give irritated gums a chance to heal.

03

Gum Surgery and Grafting

When advanced care is needed, ask Dr. Kuntz about surgical options to restore gum tissue and support around teeth.

04

Dental Implants

Periodontists are implant specialists. Call (410) 356-9640 to ask whether implant treatment is right for you.

Treatment plans are made after an in-office exam. The descriptions above explain what periodontic care covers in general; your plan will be tailored to you.
