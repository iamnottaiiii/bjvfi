Our services

Electrical services for homes and businesses

Every job is done by skilled electricians, to code, with minimal disruption to your day. Call for a quote on your project.

Electrical Panel Upgrades

Older panels are one of the most common electrical risks in Valley buildings. We replace outdated panels and upgrade service capacity so your wiring can safely handle modern loads.

  • Main breaker panel replacement and service upgrades
  • Circuit breaker installation and dedicated circuits
  • Permitted work, inspected and brought up to code

Commercial Electrical

Our specialty. We wire, maintain, and repair electrical systems for commercial buildings across North Hollywood and the San Fernando Valley, with scheduling that respects your business hours.

  • Office, retail, and restaurant wiring and buildouts
  • Interior and exterior lighting installation
  • Troubleshooting that keeps equipment up and running

Code Compliance & Safety

Compliance with safety codes and regulations is built into every job we do. If your property needs corrections before a sale, a remodel, or an inspection, we handle it end to end.

  • Electrical safety inspections and hazard assessments
  • Code corrections for inspections, sales, and remodels
  • Grounding, bonding, and surge protection review

Repairs & Fire Restoration

From dead outlets to full restoration after an electrical fire, we diagnose the problem fast and fix it right. One local restaurant is back serving customers today because we got their power restored quickly and safely.

  • Electrical troubleshooting and repair
  • Post-fire electrical restoration and rewiring
  • Outlet, switch, and fixture repairs and replacements
