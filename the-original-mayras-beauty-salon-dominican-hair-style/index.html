Our Story

A neighborhood Dominican salon, true to its roots.

Staten Island

Why "The Original"

The name says it all. This is a classic Dominican hair salon on Campbell Avenue, keeping the traditional techniques that made Dominican blowouts famous: patience at the wash bowl, a meticulous roller set, and a blowout that respects the hair. Clients come back for the consistency, the friendliness, and hair that stays silky for days.

Walk ins are welcome, and regulars know to call ahead on busy days. Reach us at (917) 225-7867.

The experience

What to expect

A chair, a stylist who listens, and hair that leaves the salon moving. Expect honest advice about what your hair needs, from deep conditioning to the right cut for your texture.
