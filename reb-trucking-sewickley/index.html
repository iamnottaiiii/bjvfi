Services

Built around the lanes you run

Reb runs regional freight out of Sewickley, PA. Our services are simple: show up on time, secure the load right, and keep the customer informed from pickup to delivery.

Flatbed Trucking

Open deck hauling for loads that do not fit in a box: steel, lumber, pipe, building materials, and machinery. Tarps, chains, straps, and edge protectors on every run.

General Freight

Palletized goods, crated equipment, and commercial commodities moved on regional lanes. We confirm pickup and delivery windows in writing before the truck rolls.

Cross Dock & Short Haul

Dock to dock transfers, freight staging support, and short haul moves for warehouses, contractors, and manufacturers around the Pittsburgh region.

Scheduled Runs

Repeat customers can lock in standing pickup days. Your lane gets a regular truck and driver who knows your dock, your paperwork, and your people.

Securement & Tarping

Every load is secured to spec with proper dunnage, edge protection, and tarps where weather exposure matters. Your freight arrives the way it left.

Contractor & Job Site Hauling

Material delivery to active job sites, including tight access locations. We coordinate with site contacts so the truck is not sitting while the crew waits.

About us

A Sewickley carrier that answers its own phone

Reb is based at 113 Ritter Rd in Sewickley, Pennsylvania, serving shippers across western Pennsylvania and nearby states. We are a working carrier: the same people who quote your lane are the ones who make sure the load is picked up on time and secured right.

We built this business on a simple idea. Shippers want reliability, not runaround. So we keep our service area tight, our equipment maintained, and our communication direct. When you call, you reach dispatch. When something changes, you hear it from us first.

Operating principles

  • On time pickup and delivery, confirmed in writing
  • Load securement checked before every departure
  • Direct dispatch communication, no middlemen
  • Equipment inspected and maintained on schedule
  • Honest pricing with no surprise accessorials
Service area

Western PA and regional lanes

Our home base is Sewickley, just northwest of Pittsburgh. We run freight throughout Allegheny County and the surrounding region, with regular lanes into Ohio and West Virginia. Ask about your lane and we will give you a straight answer on coverage.
