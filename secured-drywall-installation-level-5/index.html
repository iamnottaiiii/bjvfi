Why Level 5 matters

The finish most painters wish every wall had

Level 5 is the highest grade of drywall finish: a skim coat over the entire surface that hides joints, fasteners, and texture differences. It is the finish for glossy paints, strong lighting, and walls you actually look at.

Secured Drywall brings that standard to residential remodels, new construction, and commercial spaces across Sun Valley. Every seam taped, every screw covered, every wall sanded smooth.
