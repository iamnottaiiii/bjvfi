Our story

Three decades of
good nails

Sook Nail Salon has served Avenue T in Brooklyn since the 1990s: a calm, clean, professional neighborhood salon.

Since the 1990s

Integrity and comfort,
since day one

Sook Nail Salon opened on Avenue T back in the 1990s, and it has been a fixture of the Gravesend and Sheepshead Bay neighborhood ever since. It is a women owned and ethnically owned small business, built on steady, honest work.

Clients describe the same things, year after year: a calm and clean space, a professional and welcoming team, and manicures and pedicures they call excellent. Not fancy, not frilly, just a salon that treats people with integrity and comfort.

When one reviewer scouted salons from East 69th Street and kept walking past dirty windows and unkempt shops, she found Sook on East 26th Street and felt at home instantly, "like I had been going there for years."
