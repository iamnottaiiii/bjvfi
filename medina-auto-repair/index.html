Today's Diagnostic Checklist

Every visit starts the same way: a careful once over, honest findings, and a clear repair plan. No guesswork, no mystery charges.

  • Listen to the symptoms. Tell us the noise, the leak, or the light on the dash. We take it from there.
  • Run full diagnostics. Computer scans plus a hands on inspection before any wrench turns.
  • Write the repair prescription. A plain language plan of what your car needs, and what it does not.
  • Road test the cure. Every repair is verified on the road before we hand back the keys.

Doctor's orders: never ignore a check engine light.
