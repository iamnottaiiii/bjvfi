Services

Truck Services

Heavy-duty repair and maintenance for work trucks, tractors, and trailers. Below are representative services common to a heavy-duty truck shop.

Diesel Engine Repair

Diagnostics and repair for diesel powerplants, from hard starts and power loss to full engine work. Call with your symptoms and we will point you the right way.

Brake Service

Air brake inspection, adjustment, and repair. Brakes are the one system you never gamble on, so we check them like it.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled service, fluid changes, filters, and grease work that keeps small wear from turning into a roadside breakdown.

Electrical and Diagnostics

Charging systems, starters, lighting, and wiring faults traced with proper diagnostics instead of guesswork.

Tires and Wheels

Truck tire service, mounting, and wheel checks. The right rubber at the right pressure saves fuel and blowouts.

Trailer Work

Lights, landing gear, suspension, and general trailer repair so the whole combination is road ready.

Representative list only. Service offerings change, so call (301) 383-6528 to confirm what the shop handles today.

Our Story

A Shop That Speaks Truck

MD Truck Repair is a truck repair shop in Brandywine, Maryland, set up for the vehicles that keep freight and work crews moving through Prince George's County and beyond.

The work here is practical: diagnose it right, fix it once, and get the driver back on schedule. No upsell, no mystery line items, just the repair the truck actually needs.

Owner-operators, small fleets, and anyone whose living depends on a diesel starting every morning will find a shop that treats downtime like the emergency it is.

  • Heavy-duty trucks, tractors, and trailers
  • Straightforward estimates before work begins
  • Brandywine, MD location with easy truck access
  • Open six days a week, Monday through Saturday
