Services

Heating and cooling, covered

Tap a service to see what a typical visit includes. This list reflects standard HVAC contractor work, call to confirm what Hikar AC Service offers for your system.

When the air goes warm, weak, or stops altogether, a technician tracks the fault to its source instead of guessing.

  • Diagnosis of no-cool, weak airflow, and strange noises
  • Capacitor, contactor, and thermostat fixes
  • Refrigerant checks and leak inspection

For systems past their prime, a properly sized replacement cuts energy waste and actually keeps up with August.

  • Load sizing for your home, not a guess
  • Full system changeouts and new installs
  • Startup testing and airflow balancing

Pasadena winters are mild, but a dead furnace on a 45 degree night is still miserable.

  • Furnace repair and no-heat diagnosis
  • Heat pump service
  • Pre-season safety checks

A yearly tune-up catches small problems while they are cheap, and keeps the system running efficiently.

  • Coil cleaning and filter replacement
  • Electrical and refrigerant checks
  • Performance report after every visit

Sometimes the system is fine and the thermostat is the liar. Smart controls can also trim the power bill.

  • Thermostat diagnosis and replacement
  • Smart and programmable thermostat setup
  • Zoning guidance for multi-story homes

Leaky or crushed ducts can waste a third of your conditioned air into the attic.

  • Duct inspection and leak sealing
  • Airflow testing room by room
  • Vent and return placement advice
