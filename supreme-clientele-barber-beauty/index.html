Our story

Buffalo's newest one-roof shop

Supreme Clientele Barber & Beauty opened on Genesee Street with a straightforward idea: barbering and beauty, side by side, for everybody.

The shop calls itself Buffalo's newest barber shop and beauty salon, and it acts like it, with a clean, modern space and a team that covers both sides of the mirror.

On the barber side: fades, lineups, beard work and classic cuts. On the salon side: styling, color and finishing work for women. Kids are welcome in both chairs.

The goal is simple and stated plainly in the shop's own words: service for men, women and children, in one convenient stop on the East Side.
