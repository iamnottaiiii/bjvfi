What we do

Painting and repair,
done the right way

Interior rooms, whole homes, exteriors, and the drywall repair that makes paint look its best. Every job starts with a look and an honest plan.

Interior house painting

Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, hallways, ceilings and trim. We protect floors and furniture, patch the walls, and leave crisp lines where wall meets ceiling and trim.

Exterior painting

Siding, doors, shutters, trim and porches. Proper scraping, caulking and priming first, so the finish stands up to New Jersey weather.

Drywall repair Customer favorite

Holes, cracks, water stains and rough patches. Patched, sanded and primed until the repair disappears, then painted to blend. This is the work our reviewers praise most.

Trim, doors and touch ups

Baseboards, crown molding, interior doors and the little scuffs and marks that make a room look tired. Small jobs welcome.

Move in and move out painting

Fresh, neutral walls for a sale or a clean start in a new place. Fast scheduling when closing dates are tight.
