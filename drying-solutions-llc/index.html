The technology

The Press-Relax Dryer,
up close

A patented machine with a simple idea: press the water out, let the sludge relax, then finish it dry. Here is how it works and why plants choose it.

The Press-Relax Dryer is patented waste-treatment equipment that reduces moisture in solid waste sludge from 80% liquid or more down to 8-10%, while sanitizing the sludge at the same time.

It is designed to fit seamlessly into your plant's operations as the final process after the standard belt press stage. No plant rebuild, no exotic utilities, just a smarter last step.

Press. Sludge enters the machine and hydraulic force presses the free water out, the way a belt press does, but harder and more completely.

Relax. The pressed cake is allowed to relax, releasing bound moisture that straight pressing alone cannot reach. This is the step conventional equipment skips.

Dry. The cycle finishes the material at 8-10% moisture, sanitized and ready for far cheaper hauling or reuse, instead of 80% water headed to a landfill.

The short version

Founded in 2015 by R. Murray Hough, after years of investigation and research into current practices and problems in the sewage treatment industry.

Based in Cincinnati, Ohio. Serving municipalities of all sizes across the United States and around the world.

One patented product that does the job better: the Press-Relax Dryer.

Why this company exists

Hough looked at the equipment municipalities used to remove moisture, aeration equipment, lagoons, centrifuges, and found they had only minimal effects on the moisture content of waste treatment sludge.

Truckload after truckload was leaving plants at 80% liquid or more, and cities were paying to haul water to landfills. So he built a better final step, and patented it.
