About

The cozy neighborhood shop

Ray's Barbershop feels like the living room of Pico Rivera barbering: warm, unhurried, and personal. Reviewers talk about the friendly atmosphere as much as the cuts, and beard trims get special mention.

It is the kind of shop where the barber learns your name and your usual. Stop by the Rosemead Blvd location and settle into a chair that feels like it has been waiting for you.
