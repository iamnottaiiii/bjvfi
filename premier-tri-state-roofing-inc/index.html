About us

Two decades of keeping Cincinnati dry

Premier Tri-State Roofing Inc. is a roofing contractor based at 4792 Rapid Run Rd in Cincinnati, Ohio, serving homeowners across the Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana tri-state area.

The company's HomeAdvisor listing reports 19 years of experience, a 4.9 out of 5 rating across 26 reviews, and services ranging from asphalt shingle work to natural slate, traditional tile, box gutters, and chimney repair.
