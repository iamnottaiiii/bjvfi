Services

Everything your yard needs, handled.

Residential and commercial landscaping in Waltham, MA. Service details below are described from customer reviews and public business listings; call to confirm availability for your project.

1

Lawn Renovation and Reseeding

Tired, patchy lawns get a full reset. Existing grass is removed, topsoil is added, and the lawn is reseeded so it comes back thick and even.

Good for: lawns with bare spots, weeds taking over, or grass that never quite recovered from a rough season.

2

Mulch and Edging

Clean mulched edges around beds and trees, plus fresh mulch that keeps beds neat, holds moisture, and sets off the plantings.

Good for: flower beds, tree rings, and walkways that need a crisp, finished look.

3

Planting and Shrub Work

Old, overgrown shrubs removed and hauled away, and new shrubs and plants installed to refresh the front of the house.

Good for: dated foundation plantings and bare beds that need new life.

4

Seasonal Cleanups

Spring and fall cleanups that clear leaves, branches, and winter debris so the yard starts each season fresh.

Good for: keeping the property maintained year round without the weekend work.

5

Ongoing Lawn Maintenance

Regular mowing, trimming, and upkeep visits that keep the property looking wonderful and well-kept all season.

Good for: homeowners and businesses that want one crew handling the yard on a schedule.

6

Garden Design and Hardscaping

Design help for beds and borders, plus hardscape touches that give the yard structure and shape.

Good for: properties that need more than maintenance: a plan for how the yard should look and function.
