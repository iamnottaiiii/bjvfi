Our Story

A Staten Island braiding shop

Sisters African Senagalese has served Staten Island from 104 Canal St for about 15 years, according to business directory listings. The salon focuses on African hair braiding, a craft passed down through generations.

Braiding is both protection and expression, and the team takes time with every head: neat parts, secure braids, and styles that hold.

The shop also carries clothing, making it a small neighborhood stop for more than hair.

Good to know

Open Monday through Saturday, 9:30 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed Sundays. Braiding appointments can take several hours, so calling ahead is recommended.
