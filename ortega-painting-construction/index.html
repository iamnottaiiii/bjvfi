Interior Painting

Rooms, whole homes, apartments and offices. We move and cover furniture, protect floors, and leave every room cleaner than we found it.

  • ✓ Whole-home and single-room repaints
  • ✓ Walls, ceilings and trim
  • ✓ Drywall patching and crack repair
  • ✓ Door and window casing painting
  • ✓ Color changes and accent walls
  • ✓ Move-in and move-out repaints

Exterior Painting

New England weather is rough on siding. We prep properly so your exterior paint lasts: scraping, priming, and the right paint for the surface.

  • ✓ House exteriors and siding
  • ✓ Scraping, sanding and priming
  • ✓ Trim, shutters and doors
  • ✓ Deck and fence staining
  • ✓ Caulking and weatherproofing
  • ✓ Garage and shed painting

Repairs & Carpentry

Paint over damaged surfaces never lasts. We fix what is underneath first, so your new paint has a solid foundation.

  • ✓ Rot and water damage repair
  • ✓ Trim and molding replacement
  • ✓ Drywall installation and finishing
  • ✓ Siding and clapboard repair
  • ✓ Minor carpentry fixes
  • ✓ Caulking and sealing

Light Construction

  • ✓ Room updates and refreshes
  • ✓ Finish carpentry
