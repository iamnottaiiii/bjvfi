About

Sianni cleaning LLC

A janitorial service based in Florence, Kentucky, with a simple philosophy: care for every space as if it were your own.

Sianni cleaning LLC provides janitorial service from Mt Zion Rd in Florence, Kentucky. Our work centers on the everyday spaces where people spend their days: offices, restrooms, breakrooms, and the hallways in between.

We believe a clean space is a calm space. Our approach is unhurried and methodical, with attention given to the small details that make a room feel genuinely cared for rather than simply tidied.

Because every building is different, we talk through your needs first and shape the cleaning plan around your space, your schedule, and your priorities.
