Our Story

A Glendale shop focused on comfort

West Coast Heating & AC Repair is an air conditioning contractor based on West Glenoaks Boulevard in Glendale, California. The focus is simple: keep local homes cool in summer and warm in winter, with honest work at a fair scope.

Every job starts with listening. What is the system doing, when did it start, and what does comfort look like for your household? From there the team diagnoses, explains, and fixes, so you always know where you stand.

No upsells, no jargon, no disappearing acts. Just heating and cooling done right, by people who live and work in the same community.
