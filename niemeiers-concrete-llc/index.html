About the crew

Honest concrete from Kyle Niemeier

Niemeier's Concrete, LLC is a concrete contractor based in Cincinnati, Ohio. The company motto is simple: high quality work at a fair price.

Built on the job, not in a boardroom

Owner Kyle Niemeier has spent more than 15 years in the construction field. That experience shows up in the details customers notice: a polite crew, a clean job site, practical suggestions before the first form is set, and a finish that holds up to Ohio weather.

OSHA 30 NCCER ACI
  • ACI certified concrete knowledge, the industry standard for quality placement and finishing.
  • NCCER credentialed craft training for safe, professional job sites.
  • OSHA 30 safety training, so your property and our crew are protected.
  • Residential focus: driveways, steps, pads, and the small jobs big companies skip.
