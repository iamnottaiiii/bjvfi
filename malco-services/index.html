Our story

A neighborhood landscaping crew

Malco Services is a landscaping business based on Sherwood Drive in Independence, Kentucky, serving residential and commercial clients across Northern Kentucky.

The company was set up to do straightforward, dependable yard work: professional workmanship, competitive prices, and jobs delivered on time and within budget. That is the whole pitch, and it has kept the crew busy serving homes and businesses throughout the area.

From weekly mowing routes to seasonal cleanups and mulch jobs, Malco handles the full outside of a property so owners do not have to juggle three different contractors.
