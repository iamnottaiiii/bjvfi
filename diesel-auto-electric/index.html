Core service

Starter Repair

Clicking instead of cranking? Slow turnover on cold mornings? Your starter may just need repair, not replacement. We open it up, find the fault, and fix it.

  • Solenoid, brush, and commutator diagnosis
  • No-crank, slow-crank, and click issues traced
  • Quality replacement parts, tested before pickup

Old-school specialty

Starter Rebuilds

Rebuilding is our craft. Your starter is disassembled, cleaned, fitted with new wear parts, reassembled, and bench tested, so it comes back like new instead of heading to a landfill.

  • Full teardown, cleaning, and reassembly
  • New brushes, bearings, and solenoids as needed
  • A fraction of the cost of a new unit

Alternator Repair & Rebuilds

Dim headlights, a battery light on the dash, a battery that keeps dying. Charging system faults start here. We repair and rebuild alternators with quality parts and verify output before they go back on your vehicle.

  • Diode, regulator, and stator diagnosis
  • Full rebuilds for cars, trucks, and equipment
  • Charging output verified on the bench

Bench Testing & Diagnosis

Not sure whether it is the starter, the alternator, or something else? Remove the part, bring it in, and we will test it on the bench. You get a straight answer before spending a dollar on parts.

  • Starters and alternators tested off the vehicle
  • Straight answer on repair vs. rebuild vs. replace
  • No mechanic work here, just the electrical specialty
