Services

What we work on

Typical heating and cooling work for homes in and around Hummelstown. Tap a service for details, then call to schedule.

AC repair+
Air conditioners that blow warm air, short cycle, leak, or will not start at all.
  • Diagnosis of no-cool and weak-cool problems
  • Common part replacements such as capacitors, contactors, and fan motors
  • Refrigerant checks and leak detection
Furnace and heating repair+
Furnaces, heat pumps, and other heating equipment that will not keep the house warm.
  • No-heat troubleshooting and repair
  • Ignition, blower, and thermostat issues
  • Heat pump service and repair
System installation and replacement+
When repair no longer makes sense, a full or partial system changeout.
  • Air conditioner and furnace replacement
  • Heat pump installation
  • Sizing guidance based on the home, not guesswork
Seasonal tune ups and maintenance+
Routine checkups that catch small problems before they become no-heat or no-cool emergencies.
  • Pre-season heating and cooling inspections
  • Filter replacement and airflow checks
  • Cleaning of coils and accessible components
Thermostats and controls+
Upgrades and repairs for the controls that run the system.
  • Thermostat replacement and programming
  • Wiring and control troubleshooting
Indoor air quality+
Options for cleaner, more comfortable air moving through the ductwork.
  • Air filtration upgrades
  • Ductwork inspection and sealing guidance
