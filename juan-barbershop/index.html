Why regulars stay

A chair that feels like home

For years, families across Allentown have trusted Juan Barbershop with their hair. Parents bring their little boys, and the barbers take real care with the kids. It is friendly, respectful, old-school service at prices that make sense.

One longtime customer wrote that they have been getting their haircut here since 2011. That kind of loyalty is earned one cut at a time.

Our story

A Ninth Street institution

Juan Barbershop has served downtown Allentown from 100 N Ninth St for years, building its reputation the slow way: one satisfied customer telling the next.

Reviewers call it the most respectful and friendly barbershop in town, with A1 service guaranteed. Others praise the fun atmosphere and the fair prices. It is the rare shop that feels both classic and completely current.

"The most respectful and friendly barbershop in this town. A1 service guaranteed."

Published customer review. More on the reviews page.

The experience

What a visit feels like

Walk in and you are greeted by name if you are a regular, and like a future regular if you are not. The clippers hum, the conversation flows, and you leave sharper than you arrived.
