Our story

A neighborhood nail spot on Forest Ave

Golden rose nail salon sits at 635 Forest Ave in the West New Brighton neighborhood of Staten Island, a New York State licensed nail salon serving the block with steady, careful nail care.

Placeholder photo

What keeps clients coming back

Based on 57 reviews, the word that comes up most is clean. Reviewers praise the tidy stations, the absence of the strong chemical smell you get in some salons, and techs who take their time instead of rushing.

It is a no-frills kind of place in the best sense: friendly staff, professional service, and a wide shelf of polish colors to choose from.
