Curator’s Statement

About The Gallery

Gumbo is the unprejudiced way a variety of ingredients add their own nuances, yet coexist, and provide sustenance.

That idea gave the gallery its name. Gallery At Gumbo opened as Cincinnati’s first barbershop and art gallery, founded by Rico Grant, who built it as a welcoming space to get your groom on, view and support local art, and engage in open dialogue.

The open floor plan pairs artisanal emerald barbershop chairs with a 900 square foot gallery in the rear of the shop. Only local artists’ works are featured and for sale, an explicit bet on the entrepreneurial side of creativity, and on artists being able to make art full time.

The intent to represent every facet of the community is printed right on the walls. One of the slogans, a nod to the 1990s sitcom Martin, reads: “If you’re racist, sexist, or have any ounce of prejudice in your body, get ta’ steppin’.”

History above is drawn from a Cincinnati Magazine profile and the gallery’s own public description. Hours confirmed via public listings: Tue to Sat, 10 AM to 6 PM.
