Our story

One bay, one mechanic,
one reputation

Kurt's Auto Repair Center has been on Terminal Road since 1999, built the slow way: one honest repair at a time.

The shop

Kurt's is a small, independent shop at 8415 Terminal Rd # A, run by Kurt Thornburg. No service writers, no commission-based upsells, no waiting-room coffee bar. When you call, you talk to the person who will actually turn the wrench.

That shape has a trade-off the reviews are upfront about: the schedule fills up, and most repairs are not while-you-wait. But the payoff is the work itself, and a mechanic who has spent decades getting good at the hard stuff.

The specialty that defines the place: solutions to difficult and unusual automotive problems. The failure that two shops could not explain. The noise that only happens on Tuesdays. That is the work this shop was built for.
