Services

Drywall services, done by hand

The list below covers the core drywall work offered. Every project is quoted individually, so call to confirm scope, pricing, and scheduling for your job.

Drywall Hanging

New drywall installation for rooms, additions, basements, and remodels. Board hung plumb with tight joints for a clean finish.

Tape and Mud Finishing

Taping, bedding, and multiple finish coats with careful sanding between passes for flat, paint ready walls.

Smooth and Skim Finishes

Skim coating and high level smooth finishes for a refined, modern look with no visible texture.

Texture Application

Orange peel, knockdown, and skip trowel textures matched to existing walls or applied fresh. Sample panels shown on the home page are illustrative.

Repair and Patching

Small holes, corner damage, settling cracks, and water damaged sections cut out, replaced, and blended invisibly.

Ceilings

Ceiling board installation, retexturing, and repair work, including matching existing ceiling textures.
