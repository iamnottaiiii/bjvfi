The brief

Roofs are systems. We treat them that way.

A roof is not just shingles or tile. It is flashing, underlayment, ventilation, gutters and drainage working together. When one part fails, the rest pay for it.

That is why every job we do starts with a full look at the system, not just the symptom. A leak over the garage gets traced to its source. A replacement gets specified for how your building actually sits in the sun and wind, not just what is cheapest per square.

We work with both homeowners and commercial property clients across Sylmar, because the principle is the same at any scale: find the real problem, fix it properly, and stand behind it.

The company

Roofing is a trade of details

The difference between a roof that lasts and one that leaks in five years is rarely the material. It is the details: flashing cut and set right, underlayment lapped the correct way, fasteners in the right pattern, ventilation balanced so heat and moisture have somewhere to go.

We built this company around getting those details right and proving it. Every job is surveyed before it is quoted, specified in writing before it starts, and walked with you when it is done. That discipline is what lets us work on both a family home and a commercial building with the same standard.

Based at 12688 Norris Ave in Sylmar, we are a local contractor, reachable by phone, accountable in person.
