Why YoungNails

Small details,
done beautifully

YoungNails opened its doors on Chili Avenue to give Rochester a nail salon that treats the little things as the big things: even shaping, careful cuticle work, and polish lines so clean they look painted on.

Whether you are here for a quick polish change before the weekend or a full set for a special occasion, the approach is the same: take the time, do it right, and make sure you love the result before you leave the chair.

  • Sanitized tools and fresh linens for every client
  • A wide wall of colors, from classic nudes to bold seasonal shades
  • Walk-ins welcome, and call-ahead seating when you are in a hurry
