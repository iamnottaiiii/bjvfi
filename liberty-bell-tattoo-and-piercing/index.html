The Record · August 2026

A world record set right here on Gay Street

Owner and artist Brannan Clift spent 76 consecutive hours tattooing the same person, a three day, four hour marathon of black and gray biblical scenes, David and Goliath, Daniel in the lions den, moments from the life of Jesus, plus designs honoring Americas 250th anniversary.

The session ran from noon Tuesday to 4 PM Friday and surpassed a standing Guinness World Record. It was not his first: Clift had previously held the record himself with a 52 hour session. Twenty six years into his career, he called the finish both exhausting and cathartic.

That same steady hand is the one that does your tattoo or piercing. Clients describe Brannan as calm, professional, and easy to talk to, the kind of artist who will talk you through the process and make sure you are comfortable at every step.

Meet the artist

Liberty Bell Tattoo and Piercing is a faith based shop run by owner and artist Brannan Clift.

Brannan Clift · Owner and Artist

Twenty six years of ink,
one steady hand

Brannan Clift has spent 26 years in the craft, building a shop where faith, professionalism, and artistry sit in the same chair. He runs Liberty Bell as a faith based shop and asks that record setting artwork honor God, which is how a 76 hour marathon of biblical scenes came to be.

In August 2026 he attempted a Guinness World Record by tattooing the same person, friend Christian Eshbaugh, for 76 consecutive hours, from noon Tuesday to 4 PM Friday. The black and gray canvas covered David and Goliath, Daniel in the lions den, and moments from the life of Jesus, alongside designs marking Americas 250th anniversary. An official witness, Rev. Dr. Glenn Dietz, stayed up the whole time.

Clients know a different side of the same man: the calm professional who talks piercings through with nervous first timers, who once helped a hospital patient remove dermals for an emergency MRI, and who will fit new jewelry instead of selling you a re pierce you do not need.
