Field checklist

Storm damage spotting guide

After heavy wind or hail in Brunswick, walk the ground and look up. This checklist is informational only, not a diagnosis.

1

Missing or lifted shingles

Bare patches on the slope, or shingles scattered in the yard after wind.

2

Granules in gutters

Piles of sandy granules at downspouts can signal worn shingle surfaces.

3

Hail bruising

Hail can bruise or crack shingles without tearing them, so look closely.

4

Bent or loose flashing

Check the metal around chimneys, vents, skylights, and valleys.

5

Ceiling stains or attic dampness

Water spots inside often trace back to a breach up on the roof.

6

Debris impact

Fallen limbs can crack shingles, dent gutters, and lift flashing.
