Care that starts with the full picture

Neurological concerns are rarely simple. Dr. Contractor's background in both general neurology and headache medicine means evaluations consider sleep, stress, medication, and medical history together, not in isolation.

Dr. Contractor holds subspecialty certification in Headache Medicine and has served as Medical Director of The Affinity Center in Cincinnati. Headache visits focus on identifying headache type and triggers, then building a practical treatment plan.
