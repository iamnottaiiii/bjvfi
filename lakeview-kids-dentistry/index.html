Services

Pediatric dental services.

General descriptions of pediatric dental care. Call (410) 771-5824 to confirm which services this office currently offers.

  1. First exams and routine checkups

    Welcoming first visits and regular six-month exams that track your child's dental development.

  2. Cleanings, fluoride, and sealants

    Preventive care that keeps young teeth strong and cavity-resistant.

  3. Tooth-colored fillings

    Comfortable repair of cavities in primary and permanent teeth.

  4. Growth and development monitoring

    Watching how teeth and jaws develop to time any orthodontic referrals well.

  5. Urgent care for dental injuries

    Prompt help for knocked-out, chipped, or aching teeth after falls and sports.

Service descriptions are general information about pediatric dentistry, not promises of specific treatments at this office. Fees are not published online.
