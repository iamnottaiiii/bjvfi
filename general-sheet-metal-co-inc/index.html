Our Story

Three Generations of Metalwork

1950

Lawrence "Larry Sr." Tomasino and Charlie Walsh found General Sheet Metal Co. in Worcester, Massachusetts, building a shop known for quality sheet metal craftsmanship.

The Next Generation

Larry's three sons join the business. Over time, Charlie is bought out and the Tomasino brothers grow the company into three divisions: General Sheet Metal, General Mechanical Contractors, and HVAC and refrigeration service contractors.

Listed Since 1987

The company joins the Blue Book of Building and Construction, where its main lines of business are listed as air duct cleaning, heating contractors, and sheet metal contractors and fabricators.

Today

Now based at 29 Sword St in Auburn, MA, the company employs more than 100 people, many of them trade school graduates who have been with the team for 20+ years. It serves residential, commercial, design and build, and public markets across New England.
