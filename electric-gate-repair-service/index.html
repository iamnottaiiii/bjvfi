Gate trouble? We have seen it all.

Openers that hum and do nothing. Gates that stop halfway. Remotes that quit. Most electric gate problems come down to a short list of causes, and we diagnose them on site before quoting a price.

Repair first, replace only when it makes sense

A lot of "dead" gates just need a new capacitor, a realigned sensor, or a chain that is back on its sprocket. We fix the part that failed instead of selling you a whole new system you do not need.

When replacement is the right call, we install current-model openers and set limits, force, and safety devices to spec.

Services

Electric gate repair is the core of what we do, with fence work alongside it. Residential and commercial, swing and slide.

Electric gate repair

Gates that will not open, will not close, stop halfway, or run noisy. We trace the fault and fix it, from wiring to worn hardware.

Opener service and replacement

Slide and swing gate operators serviced, repaired, or replaced. Limits, force settings, and safety devices adjusted to spec.

Access control

Keypads, remotes, clickers, telephone entry, and intercom systems. We add, replace, and reprogram entry devices.

Fence installation and repair

As a fence contractor we also handle the fence around the gate: repairs, section replacement, and new installs.

Safety sensors

Photo eyes, loop detectors, and edge sensors that keep gates from closing on cars, kids, or pets. Tested on every visit.

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled tune-ups: lubrication, chain tension, battery backup checks, and safety tests that catch failures before they strand you.
