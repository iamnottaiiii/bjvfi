Welcome In

Sit back, settle in, and leave the rest to us

Take Tyme Hair Salon is a full service hair salon serving Nottingham and the surrounding Baltimore County area. From everyday cuts to protective styles and special occasion looks, every appointment is one on one, unhurried, and tailored to your hair goals. Call ahead to book your chair.

Services

Tap a service to learn more, then call to book

Precision cuts, blowouts, and everyday styling for every hair type and length. Walk in with an idea or let the stylist shape a look that fits you.

Single process color, highlights, and dimensional color work that keeps hair healthy and the shade true.

Protective braiding styles and extension installs, from sew ins to quick weaves, finished clean and built to last.

Silk press finishes, deep conditioning, and restorative treatments for smooth, touchable hair.

Updos, event styling, and polished finishes for weddings, proms, photos, and nights out.
