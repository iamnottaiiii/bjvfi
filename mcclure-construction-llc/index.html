Our story

Veteran-owned.
Walton-based. Here to stay.

McClure Construction LLC is a Kentucky company built on military discipline and jobsite pride, pouring concrete across Northern Kentucky and Southwest Ohio.

The short version

Show up. Do it right. Stand behind it.

McClure Construction LLC was organized in Kentucky in March 2021 and is veteran-owned and operated. We are a small crew, which means no layers of salespeople between you and the people pouring your concrete.

We carry a BBB A+ rating and run our own trucks and equipment, so your schedule is not waiting on a subcontractor's schedule. Our service area covers Boone, Kenton, Campbell, and Grant counties in Kentucky, plus Butler, Hamilton, and Warren counties in Ohio.

Concrete is one of the few things you cannot redo cheaply. We treat every pour like we will be driving past it for the next twenty years. Because we will.
