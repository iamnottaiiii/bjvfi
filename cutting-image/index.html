Our story

A Harford Road institution

Cutting Image has been holding it down on Harford Road in Baltimore, one chair, one conversation, one sharp cut at a time.

The shop

Where Baltimore gets kleaned up

This is a classic neighborhood barbershop: walk in, take a seat, and get a cut from people who treat you like a regular from day one. Reviewers talk about Al and Rich the way people talk about old friends, because that is what the shop feels like.

The menu runs deep, from fades and military cuts to hot towel shaves, beard dyeing, and hair coloring. Kids get patient, careful cuts, and the shop entrance is wheelchair accessible, so everyone in the neighborhood is welcome.

  • Full service barbering: cuts, shaves, beard work, color
  • Wheelchair accessible entrance, restroom, and parking
  • A shop where conversation is part of the service
