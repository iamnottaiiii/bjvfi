About Daves collision repairs

Dave's Collision Repairs has been a family run shop on West 51st Street since 1929, now in its third generation. Customers describe it as the small town repair shop in the middle of the big city.

Repairs are known for invisible paint matching and fast turnaround, often a day and a half, with free estimates and help on insurance paperwork.
