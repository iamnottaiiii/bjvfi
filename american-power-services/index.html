What we do

One call for heaters, coolers, condensers, and evaporators.

When process heat exchangers foul, leak, or lose performance, downtime gets expensive fast. We diagnose the problem, prove it with testing, and fix it, in our shop or in your plant.

  • Shell and tube heat exchangers, fixed tube sheet and more
  • Surface condensers and process heat exchangers
  • Phone troubleshooting through complete turnkey rebuilds
  • Documented testing: eddy current, hydrostatic, ultrasonic
