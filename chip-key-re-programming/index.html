Our story

A key shop built for the chip key era

Chip Key Re-Programming started on High Street with a simple observation: car keys stopped being metal a long time ago, but most key shops still treated them that way.

We built the business the other way around. The programming bench came first, stocked with the diagnostic and syncing tools dealerships use. Key cutting came second. That order matters, because nine times out of ten, the blade is fine and the chip is the problem.

Today our mobile unit covers Pittsburgh with a rolling workshop: key machines, a full fob inventory, and the software to talk to nearly every immobilizer on the road. Most jobs finish in one visit, in your driveway or parking lot, at hours dealerships closed long ago.
