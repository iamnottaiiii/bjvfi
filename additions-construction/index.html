Our story

About Additions Construction

Additions Construction is a general contracting business serving Westfield, New Jersey, led by John Killoran. The work centers on home additions and remodeling projects where fit, finish, and structural sense matter.

Because the business is local, every project gets direct attention from the person whose name is on the work. No layers, no handoffs, just a builder who answers the phone.

Hours are Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call to start the conversation about your project.
