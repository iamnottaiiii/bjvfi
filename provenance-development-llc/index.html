What We Do

Five divisions, one ledger.

Every engagement runs through the same disciplined process. Open a division to see typical scope.

Finding and securing the right site is the whole game. We underwrite each opportunity before it ever reaches your desk.

  • Off-market and listed site sourcing in the San Gabriel Valley
  • Feasibility studies: zoning, density, and buildable area
  • Pro forma budgets and schedule modeling
  • Purchase negotiation and due diligence management

From concept sketches to a stamped permit set, we shepherd the project through every city counter and review board.

  • Architect and consultant team assembly
  • Planning applications and design review
  • Permit submittals and plan-check responses
  • Neighborhood and stakeholder coordination

Ground-up builds managed against budget and schedule, with weekly reporting you can actually read.

  • General contractor bidding and selection
  • Construction administration and site oversight
  • Budget tracking and change-order control
  • Quality walkthroughs at every milestone

For existing properties with good bones and bad floor plans, we reposition assets for modern buyers and renters.

  • Property condition and code assessments
  • Scope development for maximum return
  • Phased renovation to protect cash flow
  • Lease-up and disposition support

Own a lot or a building and not sure what it could become? We act as your development department.

  • Highest-and-best-use analysis
  • Entitlement pathway mapping
  • Owner representation through design and permitting
  • Capital planning and lender-ready packages

Our Story

A developer built like a ledger: every line accounted for.

Provenance Development LLC is a real estate development company based at 141 E Santa Clara Street in Arcadia, California. California business records list the company's line of business as new real estate development, and that is exactly what we do.

The name says it all. Provenance means origin, the documented history of where something comes from. We believe a building should have a clear provenance too: a clean site history, honest numbers, permitted work, and a finished product we would put our own name on.

We work in Arcadia and the surrounding San Gabriel Valley, the neighborhoods we know lot by lot. If you own land, are considering a ground-up project, or want a development partner who answers the phone, start with a conversation.
