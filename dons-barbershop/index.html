About

A barbershop
the old way

DON’S BARBERSHOP serves Allentown from 1101 Hamilton St, keeping classic barbering simple and honest.

The shop on
Hamilton St

Walk into DON’S BARBERSHOP and you get the essentials done right: a careful cut, a steady hand with the clippers, and a chair that does not rush you out. It is the kind of shop where regulars are known by name and first-timers are treated like regulars.

Whether you want a classic gentleman's cut, a modern fade, beard detailing or a full hot lather shave, the goal stays the same: you leave looking sharper than when you walked in.

  • Careful, unhurried workNo rushed chairs. Your cut gets the time it deserves.
  • Classic and modern stylesFrom timeless tapers to today's fades and designs.
  • Easy to reachRight on Hamilton St in Allentown. Call (610) 554-3672 before you head over.
