About Tri-State Tire

A Westwood Avenue tire shop serving the Ohio, Kentucky, Indiana corner.

A Neighborhood Tire Stop with a Tri-State Reach

Tri-State Tire sits at 1669 Westwood Ave in Cincinnati, Ohio. As the name suggests, it draws drivers from across the tri-state region: Southwest Ohio, Northern Kentucky, and Southeast Indiana.

Local directory listings note the shop has been around for about 20 years, and online reviewers highlight competitive prices, a strong tire selection, and knowledgeable, fast mechanics.
