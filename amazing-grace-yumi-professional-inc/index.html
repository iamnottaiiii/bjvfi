Our Story

One chair, done right.

Amazing Grace Yumi Professional Inc is a neighborhood nail salon in Bath Beach, Brooklyn, built around the careful work of Yumi, its skilled professional. Clients describe a fast, efficient experience in a clean and welcoming space, with friendly, hospitable service that keeps them coming back.

The salon specializes in SNS dipping powder, alongside gel manicures, acrylic fill, pedicures, body waxing, blowouts and haircuts. Reviewers praise the detailed care, beautiful shape and finish, and the comfortable, unhurried atmosphere of a single-professional studio.
