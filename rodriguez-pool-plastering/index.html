Replastering

Rough, stained, or flaking? Start over the right way.

Pool plaster wears out. Years of sun, water chemistry, and use leave the surface rough on feet, stained, or flaking into the water.

Replastering means removing the worn layer and applying a fresh finish bonded to the shell, so the pool looks freshly installed and the water stays cleaner.

  • Smooth finish that is easy on feet and swimsuits
  • Stains, etching, and discoloration covered with new plaster
  • Free on-site look and written estimate before any work

Finishes

Pick the finish that fits your pool and your budget.

Not every pool needs the same surface. Classic white plaster is the timeless, budget-friendly choice. Quartz and pebble finishes cost more up front and stand up to heavy use and hard water for years longer.

Good to know

Before you book anyone

Ask about licensing

Pool work in California should be done by a licensed contractor. Ask us for license and insurance details when you call, and verify anyone you hire.

Timing matters

A replaster takes several days plus cure time. Plan it for the off-season if you can, and never right before a big pool party.

Start-up care

The first week of water chemistry decides how the finish cures. We walk you through it, step by step, before we leave.
