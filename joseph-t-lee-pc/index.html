When a tooth needs a specialist.

General dentists refer patients to endodontists for complex root canal cases, retreatments, and difficult diagnoses. Here is what that specialty covers.

+

Tooth pain diagnosis

Endodontists use specialized testing to find the source of persistent tooth pain or sensitivity.

◈

Root canal treatment

Removing infected pulp from inside the tooth, relieving pain while keeping the natural tooth in place.

⟳

Retreatment

When a previous root canal does not heal as expected, retreatment gives the tooth a second chance.

✦

Traumatic injuries

Endodontists treat teeth damaged by accidents, including dislodged or fractured teeth.

◎

Cracked teeth

Specialized imaging and experience help diagnose cracks that are hard to see on standard x-rays.

✓

Saving natural teeth

The core goal of endodontics: relieve pain and keep your own tooth whenever possible.

Endodontic treatment is often done by referral from your general dentist. If you have severe tooth pain, call the office directly at (410) 750-1200 to ask about availability.

Services

Endodontic services, explained.

The notes below describe what endodontic practices typically provide. Call (410) 750-1200 to confirm current services at this office.

01

Root canal therapy

The signature endodontic procedure: the inflamed or infected pulp inside the tooth is removed, the canal is cleaned and sealed, and pain is relieved. Modern root canals are routinely completed with local anesthesia and are often described by patients as no more uncomfortable than a filling.

02

Diagnosis of tooth pain

Not all tooth pain needs a root canal. Endodontists perform detailed diagnostic testing to determine whether the pulp is healthy, inflamed, or infected, and recommend treatment accordingly.

03

Endodontic retreatment

A tooth that was previously treated can sometimes develop new problems. Retreatment reopens the tooth, addresses the issue, and reseals it, often avoiding extraction.

04

Dental trauma care

Teeth injured in accidents may need specialized care quickly. Endodontists are trained to manage dislodged, fractured, and avulsed teeth in both children and adults.
