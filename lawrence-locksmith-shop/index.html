About the shop

Pittsburgh's neighborhood key counter

Lawrence Locksmith Shop serves Pittsburgh from our storefront at 316 Grant Ave in Millvale. We do the everyday lock and key work that keeps homes, shops, and cars moving: lockouts, rekeys, fresh keys cut while you wait, and locks installed the right way the first time.

What sets a neighborhood shop apart is simple: you can walk in. Bring a lock that sticks, a key that needs copying, or a question about securing a rental. You will get a straight answer and a fair price from the person who will do the work.

We answer our own phone, we show up when we say we will, and we treat every door like it belongs to a neighbor. Because around here, it does.
