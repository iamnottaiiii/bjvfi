Our Story

A studio built on listening

Eclips Hair Design sits on Hylan Blvd in Staten Island, a working studio where the consultation matters as much as the cut. Before the scissors come out, the stylist listens: what you like, what you do not, how you actually wear your hair on a Tuesday morning.

That conversation shapes everything. Color is mixed for your skin tone and your maintenance appetite. Cuts are built around your texture, not a magazine photo. And when you leave, you know how to recreate the look at home, because a great cut you cannot repeat is not a great cut.

New to the studio? Bring reference photos and an honest budget. Call (718) 967-5600 and we will tell you plainly what your hair needs and what it will cost.

House rules

  • 01. Consultation before every major service.
  • 02. Honest pricing quoted up front.
  • 03. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
  • 04. You leave knowing how to style it.
