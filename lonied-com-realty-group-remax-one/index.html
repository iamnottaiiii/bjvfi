About

Meet Lonie Dachnowski

Lead agent of LonieD.com Realty Group /Remax One, backed by one of the most recognized names in real estate.

LD

Lonie Dachnowski

Lead Agent · RE/MAX One

California real estate license 01253228.
Member, National Association of Realtors and California Association of Realtors.

Lonie received his real estate license in the late 90s and started as an assistant to one of the area's top producers, learning early that success in this industry takes a lot of hard work.

He was later recruited by one of the top builders in Los Angeles and Ventura County, where he marketed and sold high end semi-custom residential homes in one of the most competitive markets in California.

Today his team specializes in luxury residential real estate, built on three promises: the best customer service in the industry, hard work to earn your trust, and aggressive negotiation on your behalf. Knowledgeable, friendly, and helpful, from the first call to the final signature.
