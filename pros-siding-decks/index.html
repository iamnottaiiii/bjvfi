Services

Two trades, done properly. Pick a lane to see what a typical project includes, then call for a free written estimate on your home.

▮Vinyl siding

Full replacement and new installs. Affordable, low maintenance, and available in a wide range of colors and profiles.

▮Fiber cement siding

Hardie-style lap and panel siding built for wet Northwest winters. Rot and pest resistant with a long service life.

▮Cedar and wood siding

Natural cedar lap, shingle, and board-and-batten for the classic Northwest look. Stained or painted to your spec.

▮Repairs and partial reside

Storm damage, rot repair, and single-wall replacement that blends with your existing siding.

▮House wrap and flashing

Proper weather barrier, flashing, and trim detailing underneath every install, because that is what keeps water out.

▮Soffit and fascia

Vented soffit and clean fascia to finish the roofline and protect the eaves.

▮Composite decks

Low-maintenance composite decking in wood-look colors. No staining, no splinters, built for decades of summers.

▮Cedar and wood decks

Real cedar and pressure-treated builds with proper spacing, flashing, and fasteners that hold up to rain.

▮Railings and stairs

Wood, aluminum, and cable railing systems plus code-correct stairs with solid footings.

▮Deck resurfacing

If the framing is sound, keep it. New decking, rail, and stairs over your existing structure at a fraction of full rebuild cost.

▮Covers and pergolas

Patio covers and pergolas that turn a deck into an outdoor room you can use in the rain.

▮Repairs and safety fixes

Ledger attachment checks, rotted joists, loose railings, and wobbly stairs made safe and solid again.

▮Exterior trim and accents

Corner boards, window and door wraps, gable accents, and shutters that give the whole house a finished look.

▮Board-and-batten features

Accent walls and gable details in vertical board-and-batten for modern farmhouse and craftsman style.

▮Skirting and under-deck

Lattice and solid skirting to close in decks and porches, plus dry under-deck drainage options.

▮Caulking and sealing

The unglamorous work that keeps water out: re-caulked joints, sealed penetrations, and painted cut ends.

Our story

A Kent contractor with a simple philosophy: do two things, and do them better than anyone else in the neighborhood.

Two trades, zero distractions

Plenty of contractors do a little of everything. Pros Siding & Decks stays in its lane on purpose. Siding and decks are related trades that share the same enemies: water, sun, and time. Specializing means the crew, the tools, and the materials are right for every job that rolls in.

Built for Puget Sound weather

Kent gets real rain, and siding and decks take the beating. That is why installs here start with proper house wrap, flashing, and drainage thinking, not just the pretty outer layer. A deck in this climate lives or dies on its ledger attachment and footings, and we treat both like the job depends on it. It does.

In writing, before we start

Every project gets a written estimate with the full scope, the materials, and the colors spelled out. What you sign is what gets built. Then we finish with a walkthrough, and anything that is not right gets fixed on the spot.
