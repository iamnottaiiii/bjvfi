Our story

A family feel on Mountain Road

Suffield Nail Salon was built to be the kind of place where you are greeted by name. Guests describe a clean, organized salon with a welcoming team and technicians who take their time to get the shape and color right.

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, the goal is the same: a relaxing visit and nails that hold up to real life.

Good to know

Open Monday through Saturday, 9:30 AM to 7:00 PM, and closed Sundays. Walk-ins are welcome, and calling ahead for an appointment is the easiest way to get the technician and time you want.
