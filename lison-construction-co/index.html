Our Story

Three decades on Staten Island

According to public business records, Lison Construction Co has been listed as a Staten Island construction company since 1993. That is more than 30 years of building, remodeling, and repairing homes in the borough.

The company is a small, single-location operation, which means the person who estimates your job is close to the crew that builds it. No layers, no call centers, just a local builder with a long paper trail.
