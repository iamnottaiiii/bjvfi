Who we are

Known by name,
trusted by routine

Festival Cleaners is the kind of local business that runs on relationships. Customers describe a shop where the staff know the families who walk in, where the counter is clean and organized, and where a promise about pickup day is a promise kept.

It sits on Branch Ave in Clinton with plenty of parking out front, an easy stop on the way to or from the day. Walk in with an armful of clothes, walk out with a plan for when they come back crisp.

From everyday laundry to military uniforms that need to look inspection-ready, the work is the same: careful, consistent, and priced fairly for the neighborhood it serves.
