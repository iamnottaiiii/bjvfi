About

A neighborhood cleaner
that treats clothes like its own

Best Dry Cleaners serves Lexington from its shop on Massachusetts Ave, with the kind of careful, personal service a neighborhood deserves.

Good cleaning is in the details

A great dry cleaner notices what others miss: the loose button before it is lost, the spot that needs a second treatment, the crease that should be sharp and the one that should not exist at all.

At Best Dry Cleaners, every order is checked in with you at the counter, tagged carefully, and inspected again before it is pressed and bagged. Stains get pre-treatment, delicates get gentle cycles, and structured garments come back holding their shape.

Located at 189 Massachusetts Ave, we are an easy stop on your way through Lexington, whether you are dropping off a week's worth of shirts or a single treasured piece.

Inspected twice

Checked at drop off and again before pressing, so nothing slips through.

Gentle on delicates

Silk, lace, beading and heirloom pieces handled with a light touch.

Ready on time

Straightforward turnaround times, and rush service when you ask for it.

Neighbors first

A local shop where regulars are known by name and new faces are welcome.

We treat every garment like it is going somewhere important, because usually it is.
