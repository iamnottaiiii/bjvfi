Why Potterhill

A builder that thinks about
your energy bill, too

From the start, the company was founded to offer affordable, environmentally friendly housing, with geothermal heating and cooling and energy-first design.

Energy-first design

Known for geothermal heating and cooling, thoughtful insulation, and efficient layouts that help keep monthly costs down over the life of the home.

Affordable by design

Founded on the idea that a healthy, efficient home should not require a luxury budget. Smart plans and standard features that matter most.

Family-founded

Founded in 2002 by Carolyn Rolfes with her father. A local, family-rooted builder, not a national production machine.

Our Story

Family-founded.
Future-focused.

Potterhill Homes began in 2002 with a simple idea: an affordable home should also be a healthy, energy-smart home.

2002

A family business begins

Carolyn Rolfes founded Potterhill Homes with her father, setting out to build homes in the Greater Cincinnati area.

Source: Indianapolis Business Journal, 2010

Green from the start

Affordable, environmentally friendly housing

The company specialized in energy-efficient building, including geothermal heating and cooling, and explored solar panels and LEED certification, long before green building was mainstream.

Source: Soapbox Cincinnati, 2009

Growing communities

Hundreds of homes across the region

Permit records show more than 300 permitted projects, with communities including Aosta Valley in Walton, KY and neighborhoods across Northern Kentucky and Greater Cincinnati.

Source: BuildZoom permit records
