Our Story

The Shelton's Barbershop story

Shelton's Barbershop sits on South Vermont Avenue in Los Angeles, a straight-up neighborhood shop built around the basics done well. No gimmicks, no rush, just steady hands and a sharp eye.

Come in for a classic haircut, a skin fade, or a beard trim and lineup. The chairs are comfortable, the conversation is easy, and the cut is the whole point.

What we believe

A great haircut is equal parts skill and listening. Tell us what you want, and we will tell you honestly what will work.
