Our story

Treated like our own property

Who we are

Hugo landscaping and maintenance is a landscaping company based at 15751 San Fernando Mission Blvd in Granada Hills, CA. We do the steady, unglamorous work that keeps a property looking its best: the mowing, the edging, the trimming, the clean-ups.

Good landscaping is mostly consistency. A yard looks great not because of one big project, but because someone careful shows up again and again.

How we work

We walk the property, agree on a plan, and then keep to it. Edges get edged, beds get weeded, and everything gets blown clean before we leave. If something needs attention between visits, we say so plainly.

The goal is simple: a yard you are proud of, without you having to think about it.
