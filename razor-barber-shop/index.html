Our Story

West Long Beach runs through this shop

Razor Barber Shop sits on West Pacific Coast Highway, serving the west side of Long Beach with a crew of three barbers who treat the chair like a craft. Reviews call them gifted craftsmen, hardworking, and gentlemen, and describe an atmosphere that feels like family.

The shop has a reputation that travels. Customers write about driving in from Los Angeles and Inglewood because the cuts are worth the trip. The straight razor is a point of pride here: reviewers single out all three barbers, including Roy and Johnny, as skilled with the blade.

No appointments, no fuss. Walk in, take a seat, and walk out sharper than you walked in. That is the whole philosophy, and it has kept the chairs full and the reviews glowing.
