Services

What we build and remodel

Pick a project type below. Every job starts with a walkthrough and a written scope of work, so you know exactly what is included.

Kitchen remodeling

The kitchen is the hardest working room in the house, and a remodel touches almost every trade. We sequence demolition, plumbing, electrical, cabinets, counters, and tile so the project moves steadily and you are never guessing what happens next.

Whether you want a full layout change or a focused refresh of cabinets and finishes, we build around how you actually cook and gather.

Typical scope includes

  • Cabinet installation or refacing
  • Countertops, backsplash, and tile work
  • Plumbing and electrical updates
  • Flooring and lighting
  • Layout changes and wall modifications

Bathroom remodeling

Small space, big detail. A bathroom remodel lives or dies on waterproofing, tile setting, and fixture placement. We handle the full tear-out and rebuild, from the shower pan to the final bead of caulk.

Hall baths, primary suites, and powder rooms all get the same care: proper prep behind the tile and finishes that hold up to daily steam and splashes.

Typical scope includes

  • Showers, tubs, and shower pans
  • Tile floors and walls
  • Vanities, toilets, and fixtures
  • Waterproofing and moisture protection
  • Ventilation and lighting upgrades

Room additions

An extra bedroom, a home office, or a larger family room, an addition gives you the space without the cost and chaos of moving. The trick is tying the new structure into the old: foundation, framing, and rooflines have to meet cleanly.

We plan the tie-in carefully so the addition feels like it was always part of the house, inside and out.

Typical scope includes

  • Foundation and framing
  • Roof tie-in and exterior siding match
  • Electrical, plumbing, and HVAC extensions
  • Drywall, flooring, and paint
  • Permits and inspections coordination

Accessory dwelling units

ADUs are one of the smartest uses of a San Fernando Valley lot: space for family, a home office with a door that closes, or long term rental income. We build detached and attached ADUs as complete, code compliant homes in miniature.

From the slab to the kitchenette, one crew handles the whole unit so nothing falls between trades.

Typical scope includes

  • Detached and attached ADU builds
  • Full kitchens and bathrooms
  • Separate electrical and plumbing systems
  • Insulation, drywall, and finishes
  • Permits and inspections coordination

Flooring and interior paint

Sometimes the biggest transformation comes from the surfaces. New flooring and fresh paint can reset a whole house, and they are often the finishing layer of a larger remodel. We install, patch, and finish with an eye for the details you will see every day.

Typical scope includes

  • Laminate, vinyl plank, and tile flooring
  • Interior painting, walls and ceilings
  • Drywall repair and texture matching
  • Baseboards and trim
  • Whole house repaints

Exterior upgrades

Curb appeal is also protection. Exterior paint, siding repairs, and stucco patching keep weather out and keep the house looking sharp. We prep properly, because exterior work fails at the prep, not the paint.

Typical scope includes

  • Exterior painting and stucco repair
  • Siding and trim replacement
  • Fascia, eaves, and minor roofing repairs
  • Patio covers and outdoor structures
  • Fences and gates

Our story

A Valley construction company, built on straightforward work

Best Home Construction is based on Atoll Ave in North Hollywood and serves homeowners across the San Fernando Valley with remodels, additions, and ADUs.

How we work

No drama, just the job done right

Construction has a reputation problem: vague bids, crews that vanish mid-job, and punch lists that never get punched. We run our projects the opposite way.

It starts with a walkthrough where we listen more than we talk. Then you get a written scope that spells out the work, the materials, and the order of operations. During the build, one point of contact keeps you posted, and the site gets tidied every day so you can still live in your home while we work in it.

We would rather turn down a job than start one we cannot finish well. That is how a local company earns the next referral, and the one after that.
