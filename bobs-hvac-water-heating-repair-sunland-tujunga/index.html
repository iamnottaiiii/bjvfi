Our Story

A local name on Foothill Blvd

Bob’s HVAC & Water Heating Repair Sunland Tujunga is a heating contractor based at 6589 Foothill Blvd in Tujunga, California, right in the corridor that connects Sunland and Tujunga at the foot of the San Gabriels.

The name says the whole business plan: heating and cooling on one side, water heaters on the other. Foothill households deal with real winter nights and real summer heat, plus hard-working water heaters that rarely get attention until they quit. Bob’s exists to be the one local number for all three.
