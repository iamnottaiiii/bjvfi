Seasonal Comfort Calendar

One Simple Plan for All Four Seasons

Heating and cooling systems work best with a visit in the shoulder seasons. Here is the rhythm most Richfield homes follow.

Fall, September to November

Furnace Tune Up

Get heating ready before the first freeze.

  • Inspect burners, heat exchanger, and ignition
  • Test thermostat and safety controls
  • Replace or clean filters
Winter, December to February

Heating Repairs and Priority Service

No heat calls get top priority in winter.

  • Fast diagnosis of furnace and boiler issues
  • Uneven heat and cold spot troubleshooting
  • Replacement quotes for aging systems
Spring, March to May

AC Tune Up

Wake the cooling system up before summer.

  • Clean condenser coils and check refrigerant
  • Test capacitors, contactors, and drain lines
  • Confirm even cooling room to room
Summer, June to August

Cooling Repairs and Comfort Fixes

Keep the house cool when humidity climbs.

  • AC not cooling or blowing warm air
  • Condenser replacement when repair no longer pays
  • Air duct cleaning for better airflow
