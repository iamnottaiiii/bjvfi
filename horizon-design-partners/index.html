Why Horizon

Remodeling without the runaround

Most renovation headaches come from handoffs: designer says one thing, contractor does another. We keep design and construction under one roof, so the plan you approve is the home you get.

Single point of accountability

One contract, one crew leader, one phone number. When a question comes up mid-project, there is no finger pointing, just an answer.

Realistic schedules

We scope honestly and sequence trades tightly, so your kitchen is not a construction zone a week longer than it has to be.

Permits handled

Additions and structural work need city sign-off. We prepare the plans and shepherd the permit process for you.

Clean, livable job sites

Dust control, daily cleanup and clear communication. Many of our clients live in the home while we remodel it.

Services

Full-service general contracting

From a single bathroom to a full addition, one team handles design, permits and construction. Call (818) 722-6889 for a project consultation.

Kitchen Remodeling

Full or partial kitchen renovations: layout reconfiguration, cabinetry, stone counters, backsplashes, lighting plans and appliance integration.

Bathroom Remodeling

Walk-in showers, tub-to-shower conversions, tile work, vanities, plumbing fixture upgrades and proper waterproofing.

Room Additions

Seamless additions that match your home's architecture, with foundations, framing, roofing and finishes handled in-house.

ADUs

Accessory dwelling units for multigenerational living or rental income, from garage conversions to detached builds.

Interior Renovations

Flooring, interior painting, drywall, doors, trim and lighting updates that refresh the whole home.

Project Management

Permits, inspections, subcontractor scheduling and budget tracking, managed for you from start to finish.
