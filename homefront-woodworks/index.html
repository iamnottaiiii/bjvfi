Our story

The shop on Main Street

Homefront Woodworks started with a simple idea: make wood pieces that feel personal. From our shop at 1232 Main St in Holden, we build custom decor, display pieces and personalized gifts, one at a time, by hand.

Along the way our Etsy shop grew to more than a hundred five-star reviews, with customers praising the craftsmanship, the communication and the care put into every order. The reviews mention fast turnarounds, even for last-minute parties, and pieces that arrive better than the photos.

Whether it is a custom stand for a collection, a personalized gift with a name carved in, or a themed keepsake for a fan, every piece gets the same treatment: solid wood, clean joinery and a smooth hand finish.
