Our Story

A neighborhood salon,
run like family

Susan's Nails and Salon

Tucked into the parking lot at 6325 Avenue N in Bergen Beach, Susan's Nails and Salon has been part of the neighborhood's beauty routine for years. The business has held its New York appearance enhancement license since 1995.

Owner Susan and her team built the salon around a simple idea: walk in as a neighbor, walk out feeling polished. Regulars come for the nails and stay for the hair, the facials, and the easy conversation.

Longtime guests describe a clean, welcoming space where the staff takes its time and treats first-timers like regulars. One reviewer of 30 years calls the staff wonderful and the store very clean.
