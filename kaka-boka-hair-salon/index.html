Our story

A neighborhood
staple in Annandale

Tucked into the John Marr Professional Center, Kaka Boka has been quietly earning regulars for years.

How we got here

Small salon,
big reputation

Kaka Boka Hair Salon is the kind of place people find by chance and then never leave. New ownership a few years back kept the warm service and skilled stylists that regulars loved, and the chairs have stayed full since.

Clients come for the value: salon-quality cuts, color and perms at prices that keep them coming back. They stay because the stylists remember them, remember their hair, and get the style right without a long explanation.
