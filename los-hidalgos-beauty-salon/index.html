About Los Hidalgos Beauty Salón

Los Hidalgos Beauty Salon on 10th Avenue offers cuts, color, texturizing, extensions, hairstyling, makeup, manicure, pedicure and acrylic nails. Open seven days a week, clients praise the friendly staff and great prices.

We are located at Los Hidalgos Beauty Salón, 342 10th Ave, Paterson, NJ 07514. Call (973) 279-8080 for hours and appointments.

Personal care, friendly prices, and a shop that feels like home.
