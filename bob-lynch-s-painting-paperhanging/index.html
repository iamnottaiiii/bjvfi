Our story

Six decades of walls, one family name

Bob Lynch's Painting & Paperhanging has been a Gloucester County, NJ based company for over 65 years. We are fully insured and family operated.

From day one the promise has been simple: show up on time, prep like it matters, and leave every room cleaner than you found it. That is how a local painting business survives for three generations in the same county.

The craft

Painters who hang paper

Plenty of companies paint. Fewer still hang wallpaper, and fewer still do it well. Paperhanging is the trade this family built its name on, and it still sets our work apart: measured twice, matched at every seam, and trimmed with a steady hand.

Today we pair that heritage craft with full interior and exterior painting for homes and businesses across Southern New Jersey. NJ Lic#13VH04748700.

The promise

What you can expect

Free estimates, clear pricing, protection for your floors and furniture, and a crew that answers the phone. When the job is done, you get our family name on the finish.
