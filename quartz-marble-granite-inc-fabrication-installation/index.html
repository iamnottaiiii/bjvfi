Materials

Three stones, one shop.

Quartz

Engineered stone with consistent patterning. A practical pick for busy kitchens that see daily cooking.

Marble

Classic veined stone with a high-end look. Best for baths, bars, and spaces where appearance leads.

Granite

Hard natural stone with one-of-a-kind movement. Holds up to heat and heavy use in kitchens.

Not sure which fits?

Bring your cabinet color and your cooking habits. The shop can walk you through trade-offs at (818) 960-6195.

Our story

A stone shop on Branford Street.

Quartz Marble & Granite Inc. Fabrication & Installation works out of 12278 Branford St in Sun Valley, on a stretch of the San Fernando Valley known for stone and building-material shops. The business is listed as a marble contractor and granite and marble supplier, and its whole pitch fits in its name: fabrication and installation of quartz, marble, and granite.

Public records describe the offering plainly: kitchen countertops, bathroom countertops, BBQ stonework, and commercial or residential projects. It is a working shop, not a showroom chain, which means the people you talk to are the people cutting and setting your stone.

How a project runs

Measure, cut, set.

  1. 1. Talk through the project.
    Call the shop and describe the space. Kitchens, baths, BBQ islands, commercial counters.
  2. 2. Pick the stone.
    Choose quartz, marble, or granite, and settle edges and layout details with the fabricator.
  3. 3. Fabrication and installation.
    The slab is cut and finished, then the install crew sets it in your home or business.
