Our story

Danvers' friendly neighborhood laundromat

A clean start, every visit

Scrubbles is built around one simple standard: a laundromat should feel clean, safe, and welcoming. The facility is kept tidy, the machines are dependable, and the staff take pride in helping customers have a smooth visit, whether it is your first load or your five hundredth.

Part of the Pine Street neighborhood

Located at 142 Pine St in Danvers, Scrubbles serves neighbors, commuters, and travelers passing through. Regulars come back for the friendly faces and the quick turnaround on drop-off laundry, and newcomers are always shown the ropes.
