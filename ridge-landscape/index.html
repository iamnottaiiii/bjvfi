Our story

Built on the ridge, for the valley

Ridge Landscape is a general contractor based at 22944 Califa St in Woodland Hills, California, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley. The name comes from the ridgelines that frame this part of Los Angeles: long views, tough terrain, and yards that have to work with the land, not against it.

We started with a simple frustration: too many outdoor projects get split between a designer who never visits the site, a crew that cuts corners on the base, and nobody to call when something settles. So we do it differently. One crew, one plan, one person accountable from the first sketch to the last wheelbarrow.

Do the base right, pick plants that belong, and treat the property like it is our own.

That is the whole philosophy. Proper grading and drainage before the pretty stuff. Materials chosen for California sun and Santa Ana winds. And a cleanup at the end of every day, because a job site should never be a burden on your week.
