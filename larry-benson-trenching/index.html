Why Larry Benson Trenching

The machine is only half the job. The operator is the rest.

Trenching for sewer, water, electrical, and foundations is exacting work. A straight trench at the right depth and proper compaction keeps pipes from settling and walls from cracking. Larry Benson Trenching runs experienced operators on backhoes, bobcats, skip loaders, augers, excavators, and dump trucks, so residential and commercial jobs in Winnetka get done cleanly and safely.

Our Story

A Valley dirt man, still answering his own phone

Larry Benson Trenching has been working the San Fernando Valley from 8529 Comanche Ave in Winnetka for decades. Directory records show owner Larry Benson operating as an excavating contractor with California license 392098, and the listing still reads: open for business, call day or evenings.

The business model is simple and old-fashioned. No call center, no sales team. You call, you talk to the person who will run the machine, and the trench gets dug straight, at grade, and compacted right.
