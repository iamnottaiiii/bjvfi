Our story

A Sylmar roofer,
measured by the work

Superior Roof Solutions is a roofing contractor based in Sylmar, California, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley and the wider L.A. area.

How we work

Thorough first,
fast second

The pattern in customer feedback is consistent: the assessment is the product. David and the team are known for walking the entire roof, explaining what they find, and writing it all down in a detailed quote that stands up next to any other bid.

On the job, crews are described as respectful and efficient, protecting pools, lawns, and landscaping from debris, and leaving a finished roof that looks sharp from the street.

Flat and low-slope roofs are a particular strength. When ponding water keeps defeating patch repairs, the fix is drainage geometry, like the metal crickets that redirect water to the drain, and that is the kind of problem this team is known for solving.
