Why we exist

A local crew for a tricky climate

Montrose weather does not sit still. A September afternoon can hit the 90s while a January night dips near freezing, and many Foothill homes run the same system hard in both seasons. That is a lot to ask of one machine.

We started Elite HVAC Montrose to give this neighborhood the kind of service we would want for our own homes: a tech who shows up on time, explains what is wrong in plain language, and prices the work before touching a wrench.

Repair first is our rule. Plenty of companies lead with replacement quotes because that is where the money is. We lead with the repair, and we only talk replacement when the age, condition, and math genuinely point that way. It is a slower way to grow a business, and a better way to keep one.
