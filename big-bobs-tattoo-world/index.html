What we do

STYLES & SERVICES

Pick a lane. Every service below is grounded in what clients actually get done here. Call for pricing and availability.

01Custom color tattoos

The signature. Saturated, vibrant color work that clients say looks as good years later as the day it healed. Bob works with you on the design so the piece fits the placement and your idea, never a copy paste job.

From client reviews: "Everything Bob has done looks as good as day one even years later."

02Japanese & large scale work

Japanese designs, snakes, full sleeves, big compositions planned across multiple sessions. Large scale work done right: clean lines, smart flow with the body, and a plan you agree on before the first session.

From client reviews: snake pieces, Japanese designs, and sleeve work, all with great satisfaction.

03Cover ups & rework

Old tattoos, faded work, pieces from other shops you have fallen out of love with. Bob reworks and covers them into tattoos you will be proud of, and he enjoys the challenge.

From client reviews: "I have gotten new ink, reworkings, cover ups, filler, you name it."

04Consultations

Bring photo ID showing you are 18 or older and a clear idea of placement and style. Talk through the design, the size, the sessions, and the estimate before anything is permanent.

ABOUT BOB

THE ARTIST

Bob McCarty is the artist behind Big Bob's Tattoo World on Greentree Rd in Blackwood. Clients describe him as creative, ambitious, and up for a challenge, the kind of artist who makes every visit feel easy and every piece turn out better than expected.

People get tattooed by Bob for years, five, eight years running, and keep coming back. That kind of loyalty is earned one clean, vibrant tattoo at a time.

THE SHOP

A clean, organized shop where clients say they feel comfortable from the first minute. Courteous, professional, and straight up about what a piece needs.
