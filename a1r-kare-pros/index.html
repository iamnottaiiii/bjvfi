Services

Pick your comfort problem. We will handle it.

Select a category to see what is included. Every visit starts with a real diagnosis, not a sales pitch.

Central AC service and repair

When the cold air stops, we find out why and fix it right. Our techs service all major makes and models of central air conditioning.

  • No cooling or weak cooling diagnosis
  • Refrigerant charge and leak checks
  • Capacitors, contactors, and fan motors
  • Compressor troubleshooting
  • Frozen coil and drainage issues
  • Emergency repair service

Heating and furnace systems

Valley winters get cold enough to matter. We keep furnaces, heat pumps, and boilers running safely and reliably.

  • Furnace repair and service
  • Heat pump diagnosis and repair
  • Boiler repair and maintenance
  • Ignition and pilot issues
  • Safety and carbon monoxide checks
  • Uneven heating solutions

Maintenance and tune-ups

Regular maintenance is the cheapest comfort insurance there is. A seasonal visit catches small issues before they become big bills.

  • Full system inspection
  • Coil and filter service
  • Electrical connection checks
  • Refrigerant level verification
  • Thermostat calibration
  • Efficiency and airflow review

System installation and replacement

When a system is beyond sensible repair, we help you choose the right replacement and install it properly, backed by extended warranties.

  • Load calculation and proper sizing
  • High quality equipment options
  • Extended 10 plus year warranties
  • Thermostat install or replacement
  • Old equipment haul away
  • Post install testing and walkthrough

Our story

Sixteen years of showing up for the Valley

LicensedCA HVAC license 1014152, verified active
Top ratedTop 6% of California licensed contractors

A1r Kare Pros began serving the community in 2010, and sixteen years later the formula has not changed: answer the phone, arrive when promised, and treat every home like it is your own.

What started as a small Valley HVAC outfit has grown into one of the area's most reviewed contractors, with 189 HomeAdvisor reviews at 4.5 out of 5. Customers keep coming back for the same reasons: knowledgeable technicians, clear explanations of the work and the charges, and response times that respect how miserable a broken AC feels in a heat wave.

We are a licensed California HVAC contractor (license 1014152), and we handle the full life of your comfort system: emergency repairs, seasonal maintenance, and full installations with extended 10 plus year warranties.

And because comfort should never get lost in translation, se habla español. Our team serves English and Spanish speaking households across Van Nuys, the San Fernando Valley, and the greater Los Angeles area.
