Our Story

A Gravesend salon with heart.

Welcome, and enjoy your stay.

Thank you for selecting Rafi Salon and Spa and trusting our personal service. We are dedicated to providing a memorable experience for every guest.

You will be provided with a relaxing, positive, and motivational atmosphere while receiving the highest quality services. We are committed to your complete satisfaction, and we will sincerely do our best not only to anticipate your needs, but to exceed your expectations.

Your loyalty is important to us, and we want you to visit frequently and to regard us as your home away from home.

Experience you can trust.

Rafi has been serving the Brooklyn community since 2012, building a reputation for exceptional quality of service and a team of skilled hairdressers.

One of our specialties is preparing brides for their big day. With hundreds of brides under our belt, our team has perfected the art of bridal hair and makeup.
