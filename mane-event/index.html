The story in brief

01. A shop built on the chair, not the gimmick

Walk into Mane Event and the first thing you notice is the calm. No rushing, no upsell speech. Owner and master barber Paul Castricone has been cutting hair for decades, and his shop on Seneca Street runs on the old virtues: listen first, cut precisely, keep the shop spotless.

Guests come for classic men's cuts and fades, crisp beard trims, and full color services from a team of stylists, including Nichole, who is known for both natural and vivid color work. Reviewers keep saying the same thing: they asked for exactly what they wanted, and they got exactly that.

Our StorySince 2014

The shop

A barber shop the old way

Mane Event has been serving Buffalo and West Seneca since 2014, when owner and master barber Paul Castricone opened the doors at 2500 Seneca St. A New York State licensed appearance enhancement business, the shop blends classic barbering with full salon services under one roof.

Paul is the kind of barber guests stay with for decades. Reviewers describe him as friendly, precise, and a shop owner who keeps everything spotless. Around him is a team of stylists guests praise by name: Cindy, Nichole, Cathy, and Michelle, covering cuts, color, and styling for the whole family.

Public reviews highlight a few themes again and again: stylists who listen and deliver exactly what was asked, excellent color services from natural to vivid, and a friendly shop where people feel like regulars fast. It is a walk in friendly shop with a loyal following built one great cut at a time.
