MOD-01

Hood Degreasing

The canopy is the first grease trap in the system. We scrape heavy buildup, apply degreaser, pressure wash the interior, and polish the exterior stainless.

  • Canopy interior and grease trough
  • Plenum chamber behind the filters
  • Exterior stainless wipe-down
MOD-02

Grease Duct Cleaning

Ductwork carries the highest fire risk because grease coats every inch of it. We clean the full run from the hood collar to the roof curb.

  • Vertical riser and horizontal runs
  • Access panels opened and resealed
  • Grease containment at every opening
MOD-03

Exhaust Fan Service

A grease-loaded fan loses draw and strains the motor. We service the rooftop unit so the whole system breathes.

  • Fan blades and wheel degreased
  • Housing and drain/grease cup emptied
  • Belt and vibration visual check
MOD-04

Filter Service

Baffle filters catch the bulk of airborne grease. We pull them, soak them, degrease them, and reseat them so airflow stays even across the cook line.

  • Filters removed, soaked, and degreased
  • Filter track wiped clean
  • Filters reseated and aligned
MOD-05

Scheduled Maintenance

High-volume kitchens need cleaning on a set interval. We set a schedule matched to your cooking volume and keep a service record for inspectors.

  • Interval set to cooking volume
  • Service record kept per visit
  • Reminder before each cleaning

WHY EXHAUST CLEANING MATTERS

  • Grease buildup in hoods and ducts is one of the leading causes of restaurant kitchen fires.
  • Fire marshals and health inspectors check exhaust systems during routine visits.
  • Clean fans and ducts move more air, which keeps the cook line cooler and clearer.
  • Regular service records show inspectors and insurers the system is maintained.
