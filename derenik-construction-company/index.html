Our Story

A Licensed Builder You Can Actually Reach

Derenik Construction Company is a California licensed General Building contractor based in Burbank, led by Derenik Ghadimi. The company has held CSLB license #1018165 continuously since September 2016, and BuildZoom ranks it in the top 4% of California licensed contractors.

The way we work is simple: show up, listen first, put everything in writing, and build it like it is our own home. Most of our work comes from people who called once, watched how we operate, and called again for the next project.

If you are comparing contractors, we encourage it. Check our license at cslb.ca.gov, ask for references, and read the written scope line by line. A good contractor welcomes the scrutiny.
