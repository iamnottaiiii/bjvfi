Services

Residential and commercial electrical work, from quick repairs to full upgrades. Pick a category to see what we do.

Panel Upgrades

Fuse box to breaker panel conversions, 100 to 200 amp upgrades, and subpanels for additions, EVs, and hot tubs.

Wiring and Rewiring

Whole-home rewiring, new circuits, outlet and switch installation, and code corrections for older homes.

Lighting Installation

Recessed lighting, ceiling fans, dimmers, landscape lighting, and LED retrofits that cut your electric bill.

Electrical Repairs

Troubleshooting dead outlets, tripping breakers, flickering lights, and storm or surge damage. We find the fault.

Smoke and CO Detectors

Hardwired detector installation and replacements to keep your home up to current safety codes.

Surge Protection

Whole-home surge protectors installed at the panel to guard appliances and electronics.

Office and Retail Wiring

New circuits, dedicated computer circuits, and power for buildouts and renovations.

Commercial Lighting

LED retrofits, parking lot and security lighting, and emergency and exit lighting.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled panel checks, thermal scans, and tightening programs that catch failures early.

Electrical Inspections

Full-system inspections for home purchases, insurance, and peace of mind, with a clear written report.

Code Corrections

Fixing violations flagged by home inspectors or insurance companies, done right the first time.

Generator Hookups

Transfer switch installation so your backup generator connects safely, never backfeeding the grid.
