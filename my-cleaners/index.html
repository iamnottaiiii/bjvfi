About us

Lexington's family cleaner since 2006

My Cleaners is a family owned dry cleaning and laundry business at 400 Lowell St. For nearly two decades, neighbors have trusted the shop with everything from work shirts to designer pieces, and the reviews keep coming back to the same themes: exceptional work, genuine friendliness, and care you can feel.

It is the kind of place where the team knows the regulars and treats every garment like it matters, because to the person wearing it, it does. Alterations are handled in house, drop off is easy with a private lot, and the atmosphere stays positive even on a busy Saturday morning.
