Chapter Three · The craftsman

Ken Miller and crew

Home Remodeling Specialist is run by Ken Miller out of 4309 Mayhew Ave in Cincinnati, Ohio. The company describes itself plainly: a full service home remodeler with the best and fairest prices around, covering exterior work to interior work, all of it.

Professional, timely, and accommodating, with craftsmanship customers notice the moment they walk in.

The business is BBB accredited with an A+ rating, is insured, and holds Cincinnati registration CBRC007790. Estimates are free, and the crew is known for responsiveness: quick to answer questions, provide updates, and accommodate changes along the way.

Recent projects include full master bathroom renovations, kitchen remodels, vinyl plank flooring installations, mudroom rebuilds on historic homes, cabinet painting, and stair and lighting work. One crew, accountable from first call to final walkthrough.
