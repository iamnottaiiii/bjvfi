Why clean your ducts

Dust in your ducts ends up in your air

Over time, supply and return ducts collect dust, pet dander, and debris. A professional cleaning removes that buildup from the runs, registers, and main trunk.

Dust and debris removal

Rotary brushing and high-powered vacuuming pull years of accumulated dust out of your duct runs, so less of it circulates through your rooms.

Help your HVAC breathe

Clearer ducts mean steadier airflow. Your system does not have to push as hard, which can support efficiency and more even comfort room to room.

Fresher indoor air

Removing musty buildup and debris from registers and vents helps reduce stale odors and the particles that trigger sneezing and stuffy noses.
