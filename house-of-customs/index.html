About

One craft, done properly

House Of Customs is an auto upholstery shop at 628 Burbank St in Cincinnati, Ohio. The shop describes itself simply: "Quality Automotive Interior Stitch-craft and Reupholstering service." That is the whole mission, and it shows in the work.

Public business listings categorize the shop under furniture repair and automotive customizing, but the reputation it has earned is narrower and stronger: interiors. Seats, door panels, and vintage cabins that get finished with unusual care.

Customers who leave reviews keep naming the same person: Ross. Reviewers describe attention to detail that "can't be topped," and a willingness to pick up jobs other shops left unfinished and make them look seamless.
