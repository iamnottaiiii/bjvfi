Divisions

Four ways we build

Each division is handled by the same design/build team, so design intent carries straight through to construction. Tap a division to see the typical scope.

Ground-up homes built from a single coordinated plan set.

  • Architectural design and engineering coordination
  • Grading, foundation, and framing
  • Full MEP systems: electrical, plumbing, HVAC
  • Interior and exterior finishes, landscaping rough-in

More room on the lot you already love: second stories, rear additions, garage conversions, and accessory dwelling units.

  • Feasibility review and lot coverage planning
  • ADU layouts for rental income or multigenerational living
  • Tie-in detailing so new work matches the existing home
  • Separate metering and utility planning where required

The two rooms that define a home, rebuilt around how you use them.

  • Layout reconfiguration and wall relocation
  • Custom cabinetry, stone, and tile
  • Lighting plans and fixture upgrades
  • Plumbing and electrical brought up to current code

The paper trail, handled: drawings, calculations, and city submittals for your project.

  • As-built measurements and existing-condition plans
  • Permit-ready drawing sets and structural details
  • Plan-check correction responses
  • Inspection scheduling and sign-off coordination

Scopes above describe typical design/build work. Final scope and pricing are set after a site walk and written proposal.

Our story

Built on the design/build idea

One contract, one team, one point of responsibility, from the first sketch to the final walkthrough.

CL Builders & Development Inc. is a design/build company based in Arcadia, California, working across the San Gabriel Valley. In the design/build model, the same team that draws the plans builds the project, which keeps design intent, budget, and schedule under one roof instead of split across separate designers and contractors.

That single point of responsibility is the heart of the company: when the people framing the walls are the same people who drew them, details survive the journey from paper to jobsite. Projects move through design, permitting, construction, and finish work as one continuous process, with clear communication at every stage.
