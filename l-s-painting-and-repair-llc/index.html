The Story Behind the Sound

A Family Act, Three Generations Strong

L & S Painting and Repair, LLC. is a family-owned business with a passion for creating beautiful and functional living spaces. As a third-generation contractor, the crew takes pride in delivering exceptional results on every job, no matter the size.

Their commitment to open communication and customer satisfaction means you can feel confident and at ease through the whole process. They believe in honest, reliable, affordable service, and they even encourage customers to get three estimates before starting any work. That is confidence.

The crew happily services the Cincinnati, Dayton, and Kentucky areas.
