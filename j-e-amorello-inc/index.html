About J E Amorello Inc

A Worcester contractor built on underground work done right the first time.

Three decades underground

J E Amorello Inc was incorporated in Worcester on December 26, 1995, and has spent 30 years in the trades most people never see: excavating, drainage, sewer, and water main construction.

Led by president J. Edward Amorello, the company serves commercial, residential, educational, and industrial clients across Central Massachusetts, delivering site development and utility work tailored to each project.
