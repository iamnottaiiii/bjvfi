How it works

From first call to final cure

Concrete work is all about the steps before the pour. Here is how we run every job.

01

Free on-site estimate

We measure, check the grade and drainage, and give you a straight price in writing. No pressure, no obligation.

02

Site prep and base

Old concrete out, ground compacted, forms set true. The part you never see is the part that decides how long it lasts.

03

The pour

Fresh mix, screeded level, control joints cut in the right spots so cracks happen where we plan them.

04

Finish and cure

Broom, smooth, or stamped finish, then proper curing so the slab reaches full strength.

Our story

Walton roots,
concrete results

Cats Construction Services is a concrete contractor based right on S Main St in Walton, Kentucky, serving homeowners and small businesses across Boone County and Northern Kentucky.

KY

Local to Walton

We are based at 37 S Main St, Walton, KY. When you call, you are talking to people who drive the same roads and know the same clay-heavy soil you are building on.

01

Concrete is all we do

We are a concrete contractor, not a jack of all trades. Driveways, patios, slabs, stamped work, walkways, foundations and repairs. Specialists pour better slabs.

Built for Kentucky weather

Freeze-thaw cycles are brutal on bad concrete. We spec the right mix, cut joints where slabs need to move, and prep bases that drain, so your concrete survives winter after winter.
