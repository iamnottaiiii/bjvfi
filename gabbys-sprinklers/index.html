Why it matters

One bad head can waste
thousands of gallons

A cracked head, a sunken nozzle, or a valve stuck half open can quietly flood a sidewalk for months. Most sprinkler problems are cheap to fix and expensive to ignore.

  • Brown spots and puddles usually mean a head or valve issue, not a dead lawn
  • Most repairs are finished in a single visit
  • A tuned timer waters deeper with less runoff
