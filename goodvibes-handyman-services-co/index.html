Our story

A handyman with a sunny outlook

GoodVibes Handyman Services Co is a Covington, Kentucky handyman business built around a simple idea: the small jobs matter. A door that finally latches, a wall that looks new again, a faucet that stops dripping at 2 a.m. These are the fixes that make a house feel like home.

The approach is friendly and low-pressure. You make the list, you get a straight answer on price, and the work gets done tidily. If a job turns out to need a licensed specialist, you will hear that honestly instead of a guess.

Based on Hanser Drive in Covington, GoodVibes serves homeowners across Northern Kentucky who would rather spend their weekends doing anything other than home repair.
