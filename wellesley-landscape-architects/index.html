Start with the site, not the style.

Every project begins on the ground: light, soil, drainage, and how you actually live outside.

01

Site study

A careful read of the property: sun and shade, water movement, existing trees, and the views you want to keep or frame.

02

Design development

Concept sketches grow into a working plan, so patios, plantings, and paths fit together before anything is built.

03

Built to endure

Materials and plants chosen for New England seasons, so the landscape looks better in year five than in year one.

A good landscape feels inevitable, as if it could never have been any other way.

That is the aim of every plan drawn at Wellesley Landscape Architects: outdoor space that fits the house, the land, and the people who use it.
