Meet the chair

Owner-barber AP is the name reviewers know. Public write-ups describe AP as incredibly personable, keeping the shop spotless and the atmosphere relaxed while turning out sharp low taper fades and clean beard trims.

Reviewers also praise the wider crew, including barbers known in reviews as Showcase and Jeremy, for fast confirmations, family-warm service, and detail work that brings kids and parents back. Unverified
