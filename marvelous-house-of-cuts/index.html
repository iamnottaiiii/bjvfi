The House of Marvelous Cuts

Marvelous House Of Cuts is a neighborhood barber shop on Warsaw Avenue in East Price Hill, Cincinnati. The shop's public listings highlight classic barbering: men's haircuts, hot towel shaves, head shaves, and beard trims.

It is the kind of shop built around classic barbering and a welcoming chair. If you are new to the neighborhood or just due for a fresh look, the marquee is lit.

Shop history details are not published online. Call to confirm anything you would like to know before your visit.
