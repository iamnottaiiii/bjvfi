Our story

Built on fair prices and finished lists

L. Q. S Handyman Solutions is based at 2525 Lake Ave in Altadena, California, serving Altadena and the surrounding San Gabriel Valley neighborhoods. The business exists for one reason: the jobs too small for a contractor and too big to ignore.

Every home collects them. A door that will not close right. Steps that need paint. Dry rot creeping in around a window. A countertop waiting to be mounted. Each one is small on its own and overwhelming as a list.

The crew's reputation, a 5 star average across 24 Birdeye reviews, comes from treating that list with respect: showing up when promised, charging a fair price, communicating clearly, and doing skillful work that holds up. Customers say they would recommend the crew to friends and family. That is the whole business model.
