Refinishing that makes old floors look new again

  • Stain color changes, from natural to rich walnut tones
  • Board replacement and spot repairs before finishing

Our Services

Hardwood floor refinishing, installation, repair and staining for Pasadena area homes.

◆

Hardwood Floor Refinishing

Our signature service. We sand down to fresh wood, then stain and seal for a like new surface that can add years of life to your floors.

☆

Screen and Recoat

For floors with a sound finish that just look tired. A light abrasion plus a fresh topcoat, usually done in a single day.

▦

New Floor Installation

Solid and engineered hardwood installed with tight joints, proper acclimation and clean transitions at every doorway.

⬢

Repairs and Board Replacement

Damaged, cupped or squeaky boards replaced and blended before refinishing, so the repair disappears into the floor.

◉

Custom Staining

From light natural to deep espresso. We test stain samples on your actual floor so you approve the exact color first.

▮

Stairs and Railings

Treads, risers and handrails refinished to match your floors for one continuous look through the home.

✦

Water Damage Repair

Assessment and repair of water stained or warped sections, with honest advice on what can be saved.

☎

Free Phone Consultations

Describe your floors and get straight answers on options, timing and cost. Call (410) 766-0666.

How it works

A simple, predictable process

1

Call and describe your floors

Tell us the rooms, the condition and what you want to change. We will give you a realistic range right on the phone.

2

On site estimate

We look at the floors in person, test the finish and leave you a written estimate with a firm price.

3

Sand, stain and seal

Most refinishing jobs take two to four days. We protect your home, keep dust down and clean up daily.

4

Walkthrough

We walk every room with you before final payment. If something is not right, we fix it.

About us

Owner operated, floor obsessed

Affordable Hardwood Flooring is owned by Tony Woo and based on Royal Star Court in Pasadena, Maryland. It is a small, owner operated company, which means the person who quotes your job is the person who stands behind it.

We hold a Maryland Home Improvement license and focus on a single trade: hardwood floors. That focus keeps our overhead low and our skills sharp, and it is how we keep quality high while staying true to the "affordable" in our name.
