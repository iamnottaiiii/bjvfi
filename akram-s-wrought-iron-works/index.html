Why iron, why us

A well-built iron fence lasts 50 to 100 years with simple care. Here is what you are buying when you buy it from a dedicated iron shop.

Fabricated, not assembled. Your gate is cut and welded to your exact opening, not bolted together from stock panels. That means it swings true and locks clean on day one and year twenty.

Finished against rust. Proper prep, rust-inhibiting primer and a durable topcoat are the difference between iron that ages gracefully and iron that flakes in three summers.

Repairs, not just replacements. A cracked weld or sagging gate does not have to mean a new gate. We weld, re-hang and re-finish existing iron, which is usually a fraction of replacement cost.

One shop, one accountable name. The person who quotes your job is the owner of the shop that builds it. Listed with the Blue Book since 1996.

An iron shop, the old way

Akram's Wrought Iron Works is a single-location Northridge shop: owner Ziad Kassab, a small crew, and a trade directory listing that goes back to 1996.

Measure twice, weld once

Akram's Wrought Iron Works sits at 18736 Parthenia St in Northridge, in the industrial heart of the San Fernando Valley, surrounded by machine shops and fabricators. It is the right neighborhood for a business like this one: a working shop, not a showroom.

The shop's main lines of business are ornamental and miscellaneous ironwork, plus welding and cutting equipment work. In plain terms: gates, fences, railings, guards and repairs, cut and welded on site by people who do nothing else.

It has been listed in the Blue Book, the construction industry's trade directory, since 1996, serving Los Angeles and the surrounding counties from this one Northridge location.

"A gate is the first thing people touch at your home and the last thing they see leaving. We build them to be worth touching." Ziad Kassab, Owner · Sample quote

How a project runs

From first call to final coat, here is the rhythm of a typical custom job.

1. Measure and sketch. We visit, measure your opening, talk design, and leave you a written quote. No obligation.

2. Fabricate in the shop. Steel is cut, fitted, welded and ground smooth at our Parthenia St shop.

3. Finish against the weather. Rust-inhibiting primer and a durable topcoat, with galvanizing available for the longest life.

4. Install and adjust. Set, leveled, hung and tested on site, with hinges and latches adjusted until the swing is perfect.
