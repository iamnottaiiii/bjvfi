About Los Amigos Barber Shop


A local shop for classic and modern cuts in Downey. Los Amigos Barber Shop focuses on the details: clean lines, even fades, and a chair you will not want to leave.


Every appointment starts with a consultation: what you like, what you do not, and how much upkeep you want. The result is a cut that looks sharp on day one and grows out clean.


Find us at 12936 Paramount Blvd, Downey, CA 90242, or call (562) 923-8485 to book your chair.
