Our story

A Glendora cabinet shop built on repeat customers

R B's Fulltime Cabinetry works out of its shop on East Gladstone Street in Glendora, California, building custom cabinetry for homes across the area.

2013

Rooted in Glendora

R.B.'s Fulltime Cabinetry, Inc. is a California corporation registered in 2013 and led by owner John C. Fulton, operating from 2021 E Gladstone St, Unit H. It is a small, full-time cabinet shop: design, build, and install under one roof, with the kind of personal accountability that earns repeat business room after room.

🏠
