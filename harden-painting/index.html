Our Story

A Morton painting name since 1979

Public business records list Harden Painting as established in 1979 and based on Yale Avenue in Morton, Pennsylvania. That is more than four decades of brushes, rollers, and ladders across Delaware County.

The work has stayed simple on purpose: show up when promised, prep the surface properly, use good paint, and leave the place cleaner than we found it. Most of our work comes the old fashioned way, from neighbors telling neighbors.

Whether it is a single bedroom or the whole outside of the house, you deal directly with people who take the craft seriously.
