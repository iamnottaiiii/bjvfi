About Restoreroofing

A Cincinnati roofing company built on a simple idea: treat customers right, every step of the way.

Why We Started

Our owners spent years working inside the roofing industry and watched how some companies operate: owners getting greedy while customer excitement turns to disappointment. We did not want to work for a company like that.

So we started Restoreroofing to be different: totally upfront and honest, no hidden costs or fees, working directly with each customer for their satisfaction from the initial contact to the final payment. Both owners hold state certifications, bringing the education and experience to get the job done right.

What we enjoy most is helping people in our communities restore their homes to the comfort they once knew, and saving them real money along the way.
