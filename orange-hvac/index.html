About

A Granada Hills crew with a simple standard.

Orange HVAC is a heating and air conditioning contractor based at 10605 Balboa Blvd in Granada Hills, CA, serving homes across the San Fernando Valley.

The standard customers describe is straightforward: show up fast, explain the charge before starting, give options that fit the budget, and leave the place clean.

Reviewers mention lead tech Michael by name for confident, no-pressure expertise, and crews including Dillon and Isaiah who installed a Mitsubishi mini split in a garage in one visit, tidied up, and were gone.
