Why folks come in

Classic shop, modern edge

Reviewers call Handsome Devil Barber Shop a hidden gem on Route 66: a chill vibe with good music at a reasonable volume, a beautiful shop atmosphere, and seasoned barbers who take the cut personally.

Precision cuts

Fades, tapers, lineups, and classic scissor work. Bring a photo of the cut you want and the barber will dial it in.

Beard and shave

Beard trims and hot towel shaves that finish the look, plus clean head shaves for the low maintenance crowd.

No rush chair

Personalized service and an easy atmosphere. Call ahead on busy weeks; Saturdays fill fast, so midweek calls get you in.

Our story

A Route 66 original

Handsome Devil Barber Shop sits in a strip mall on historic Route 66 in Glendora. Inside, the shop keeps the classic barbershop promise: a beautiful space, music at a comfortable volume, and barbers who treat every cut like it matters.

Seasoned barbers

Reviewers name barbers Anthony and Manny for great personalized haircuts and excellent service, and call the shop the best local spot in town.

Chill vibe

A hidden gem feel: clean shop, nice music, reasonable volume. The kind of place where you settle in and come out sharper.

Fair prices

Reviewers consistently mention reasonable prices alongside the quality of the cut. Great cut, great price is the house reputation.
