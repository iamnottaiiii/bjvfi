What we do

A bathroom remodeler, not a handyman with a truck.

Bathrooms are the hardest working rooms in the house: water, steam, tight clearances, and finishes that have to look good ten years from now. We plan the plumbing, the waterproofing, and the layout before a single tile comes off the wall.

Because we also handle kitchens, homeowners often book both rooms together and save a second round of dust, permits, and scheduling.
