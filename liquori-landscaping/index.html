Our story

A Framingham landscape firm
since 1988

Liquori Landscaping Company, Inc. has been designing, building and maintaining landscapes in the Framingham area for well over three decades.

Who we are

One company, every part of the yard

Liquori Landscaping is a landscape construction and maintenance firm based at 74 Hastings St in Framingham. Rather than splitting design, build and upkeep across contractors, everything stays with one accountable team.

The company takes on work at every scale, from a $100 yard cleanup to a $100,000 full-property build, and accepts credit cards for convenience.

Public listings describe the firm as having decades of experience, with a Better Business Bureau rating of A+ and a 4.36 rating across 11 online reviews.
