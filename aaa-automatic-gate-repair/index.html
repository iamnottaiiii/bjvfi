AAA Automatic Gate Repair is a Burbank fence gate contractor focused on one thing: automatic gates that work every time you press the button.

How we work

Diagnose first.
Pressure never.

A gate is a system: motor, power, controls, track and hardware all have to agree. We look at the whole system, find the real cause, and explain it plainly before we touch a wrench.

Customers notice the difference. One reviewer wrote that even with an old gate, our technician did not pressure a replacement, just fixed what was broken and gave an honest number for the future. That is the standard we hold on every job.

  • On-time arrival and courteous, professional techs
