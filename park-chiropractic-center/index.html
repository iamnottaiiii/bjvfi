Services

Care for the way you live

Chiropractic care focuses on the spine, joints, and nervous system. Below are the kinds of concerns patients commonly bring through our door. Call and tell us what is going on, and we will tell you honestly if we can help.

01

Spinal Adjustments

Precise, hands-on adjustments to restore motion and ease tension in the spine. The cornerstone of chiropractic care.

02

Back Pain Care

Lower and upper back pain from desk work, lifting, or just life. We find the source and work it out with you.

03

Neck Pain and Stiffness

Tech neck, whiplash-type strain, and morning stiffness. Gentle mobilization to get your head turning freely again.

04

Headache Relief

Tension headaches that start in the neck and shoulders often respond well to chiropractic care and postural work.

05

Posture and Mobility

Rounded shoulders, limited rotation, that permanent hunch. Practical correction you can feel and keep.

06

Wellness and Maintenance

Stay-ahead-of-it visits for people who would rather keep moving well than wait for something to seize up.

About

A neighborhood practice on Park Avenue

Park Chiropractic Center sits at 1120 Park Avenue in the heart of Carnegie Hill on Manhattan's Upper East Side, a short walk from Central Park and Museum Mile.

We are a chiropractic practice built around a simple idea: listen first, adjust second. Every patient gets a real conversation, a real exam, and a plan that makes sense for their life, whether that means weekly tune-ups or a short course of care to get past an injury.

New to chiropractic? You are in good company. Most of our first-time patients arrive a little nervous and leave wondering why they waited so long. Call and ask us anything.
