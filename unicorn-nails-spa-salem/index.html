Our Story

A newer Salem salon that earned its reputation the honest way: one careful set of nails at a time.

A little magic, made in Salem

Since 2023, UNICORN NAILS & SPA has been Salem's go-to for nails done right. What started as a bright new salon on Hartford Road quickly became a local favorite, thanks to a team that treats every appointment like it matters.

Guests notice the difference immediately: a bigger, brighter space than the storefront suggests, a team that greets you by name, and technicians who slow down instead of rushing through. Powder dip, gel, builder sets, and spa pedicures all get the same careful touch.

From hot towel finishes to shoulder massages while your color sets, every visit is designed to feel like a small retreat from the everyday.

Clean by conviction

Sanitation is a point of pride, not a checkbox. Guests consistently call out the spotless space.

Detail over speed

Technicians take their time on shaping, cuticles, and art, because the little things are the big things.

Honest and kind

Fair pricing, thoughtful advice (like flagging thin nails before a service), and a free rewards program.
