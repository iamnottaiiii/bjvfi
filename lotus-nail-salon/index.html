Our story

Blooming in Syracuse since 2001

Lotus Nail Salon opened its doors in Syracuse in 2001 and has been a neighborhood favorite ever since. The salon is run by Kim and Joe, who bring a combined 25 years of experience in the nail business, with knowledge and techniques gathered across many different salon settings.

From classic pink and white to ombre powder, UV and LED gel, acrylics and spa pedicures, the menu covers everything from everyday upkeep to special occasion sets. Guests consistently mention the calming atmosphere, the wide range of colors, and staff who take real time with every client.

Some regulars have been coming for 8 to 15 years. That kind of loyalty is earned one careful set at a time.
