Curbing styles and services

Pick a profile, pick a pattern

Every style below is extruded as one continuous concrete border, then colored and stamped by hand. Typical for this trade Service details below describe typical landscape curbing work. Confirm specifics for your yard when you call.

Mower edge curbing

The low, flat profile that mower wheels glide over. It is the workhorse choice for long bed runs and tree rings.

  • Continuous pour, no joints to shift
  • Stamped stone or brick patterns available
  • Integral color so it never needs paint

Slant and stone profiles

Taller faces that show off a hand-troweled finish. Slant sheds water away from the bed; stone patterns mimic cut rock.

  • Deeper barrier against roots and washout
  • Natural stone and slate stamp options
  • Great around slopes and downspouts

Tree rings and bed rebuilds

Perfect circles around trunks, plus full bed reshaping when the old edge has sunk or spread.

  • Clean rings that protect bark from trimmers
  • Old plastic or metal edging removed
  • Beds re-cut to a crisp new line

Commercial and HOA curbing

Long runs for entries, parking islands, and common areas where a tidy edge cuts maintenance hours.

  • High-visibility, low-maintenance borders
  • Consistent color across large pours
  • Call to discuss scope and scheduling

Our story

The edge is the whole job

Premier Landscape Curbing is a Alexandria, Kentucky landscaping outfit focused on one craft: continuous concrete curbing that gives yards a clean, lasting frame.

Why we lead with curbing

Most landscape projects spend the budget on plants and mulch, then outline the beds with whatever edging was on sale. Within a season or two the line heaves, fades, or disappears under the grass.

We do the opposite. The border goes in first, as one unbroken concrete ribbon, and everything inside it looks better for years. It is a small trade with an outsized payoff, and it is all we focus on.

Built for Kentucky weather

Freeze and thaw, spring downpours, summer mowing crews. A poured curb flexes with the ground instead of popping out of it, which is why it outlasts segment edging.

One trade, done well

Specialists, not generalists. Curbing is the service, so the profiles, colors, and patterns are dialed in.

Clean job sites

Typical for this trade: the crew cuts the line, pours, finishes, and leaves the lawn as tidy as they found it.

Straightforward pricing

Jobs are usually priced by the linear foot. Call for pricing on your layout, no obligation.
