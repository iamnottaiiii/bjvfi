Services

Roofing, from the ridge down

Every job below is core work for C & C Contractors. Call (717) 561-9917 and tell us what your roof is doing: we will tell you what it needs.

  1. 01Roof ReplacementFull tear-off

    When repairs stop adding up, we strip the old roof to the deck and rebuild it: underlayment, shingles, flashing, ridge vent, and a final inspection you can walk through with us.

  2. 02Shingle Roof SystemsNew installs

    Architectural and three-tab shingle installation with proper felt underlayment, ice and water shield where it counts, and ventilation balanced from eave to ridge.

  3. 03Roof RepairsLeaks and wear

    Missing shingles, lifted flashing, nail pops, and active leaks. We trace water to its source and repair it so it stays repaired, and we will say so plainly when a patch is not enough.

  4. 04Aluminum Window CappingExterior trim

    Custom-bent aluminum capping over window trim that locks out weather and drafts and gives older woodwork a crisp, maintenance-free finish.
