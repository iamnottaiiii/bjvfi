Services

Flooring services, start to finish

Every job starts with a look at your subfloor and a written estimate. Call (818) 946-0163 to talk through your project.

Hardwood Installation

Solid and engineered hardwood, nailed or glued, with transitions and shoe molding finished to match. Stairs and landings included.

Laminate and Luxury Vinyl Plank

Floating and glue down LVP and laminate for kitchens, baths, and high traffic areas. Water resistant options for busy households.

Tile and Stone

Ceramic, porcelain, and natural stone floors set on proper backer board, grouted and sealed for wet areas and entries.

Refinishing and Sanding

Bring worn hardwood back to life: sanding, staining, and sealing without the cost of a full replacement.

Floor Repair

Squeaks, cupped boards, cracked tile, and water damaged sections repaired or replaced to blend with the existing floor.

Commercial Flooring

Offices, retail, and rental units: durable materials installed on schedule to keep your business moving.

Our story

Local floors, honest work

Flooring & Construction North Hollywood is a flooring contractor based on Lankershim Boulevard in the heart of North Hollywood, CA. We spend our days in homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley, measuring rooms, matching materials, and laying floors one careful row at a time.

We believe a flooring contractor should be easy to reach, clear about price, and proud enough of the finished floor to walk it with you before leaving. That is how we run every job, from a single bedroom of new laminate to a full commercial install.

  • Written estimates before any work begins
  • Respect for your home: shoe covers, drop cloths, daily cleanup
  • Guidance on materials that fit your budget and lifestyle
  • A local phone number answered by real people
