HOW A SHOTCRETE JOB RUNS

Typical project flow for structural shotcrete work.

1

Excavate & Form

The shape is dug and reinforcement steel is set to spec.

2

Place Shotcrete

Concrete is sprayed at velocity, compacting on contact.

3

Shape & Finish

Crews cut, shape, and finish the shell to line and grade.

4

Cure & Handoff

The shell cures and is handed off for plaster or coping.
