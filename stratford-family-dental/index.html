Services

General dentistry, described plainly.

Stratford Family Dental is a general dentistry practice. The notes below describe the kinds of care general dentistry practices typically provide. Call the office to confirm which services are currently offered.

01

Exams and cleanings

Routine checkups and professional cleanings form the backbone of preventive dental care. Patients of this practice often note that cleanings are thorough and that the dentist explains each step.

02

Fillings and restorative care

When a tooth is damaged by decay, restorative treatment such as a filling returns it to comfortable function. Reviewers have mentioned same-week attention for small filling issues.

03

Dentures

For patients missing teeth, dentures restore the look and function of a full smile. One long-time patient review notes upper dentures made by Dr. Stratford that others mistook for natural teeth.

04

Cosmetic dentistry

Cosmetic dental care focuses on the appearance of the smile. Online listings categorize this practice under cosmetic dentistry as well as general dentistry. Ask the office about available options.

Service descriptions are general information about dental care categories, not a promise of specific treatments. Fees are not published online; call (844) 817-0343 for current information.
