Hiring a mason? Ask these four questions.

Good advice for any masonry contractor, including us:

  1. What is under the wall? Ask about footing depth, steel and drainage, not just the pretty face.
  2. In California, most projects over $500 require an active CSLB license. Ask for the license number and verify it at cslb.ca.gov.
  3. How will new work match the old? Ask to see how they blend mortar color and brick or stone.
  4. What is the warranty on the work? Get it in writing along with the scope and price.
