Our story

Three decades of spotless in West Chester.

Painters Crossing Cleaners has been part of the Village at Painters Crossing for more than 30 years, at the corner of Route 1 and Route 202 where Chadds Ford meets West Chester. Generations of customers have trusted the shop with their suits, their shirts, and their special occasion garments.

The formula never changed: careful inspection, honest stain treatment, a proper press, and a friendly counter. That is why the same families keep coming back season after season.
