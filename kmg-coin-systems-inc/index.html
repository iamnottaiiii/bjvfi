Our story

Saugus's Main Street laundromat

KMG Coin Systems Inc is a coin-operated laundromat serving Saugus and the nearby North Shore communities. The store keeps things simple: working machines, fair prices, and a convenient Main Street location.

Saugus is one of Greater Boston's oldest towns, and Main Street has long been its commercial spine. KMG sits right in that mix, near familiar local landmarks like Boston Barre and the historic Boardman House, an easy stop for anyone doing laundry in the area.

Whether you are a weekly regular, an apartment dweller without in-unit laundry, or just tackling a stack of comforters before the season changes, this is a place built for one thing: getting your laundry clean without a fuss.
