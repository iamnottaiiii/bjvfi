Triage, not guesswork

How fast should you call?

Plumbing trouble falls into three tiers. Find yours below, then hit the call button. A dispatcher (not a phone tree) sorts out the rest.

Critical

  • Sewage backing up into the home
  • Burst or flooding pipe
  • No heat in freezing weather
  • Gas smell near a line (call 911 first)
Call immediately

Urgent

  • Slow or gurgling drains everywhere
  • Water heater leaking or cold
  • Running toilet or hidden leak
  • Sump pump acting up before rain
Call today

Routine

  • Fixture installs and upgrades
  • Bath and kitchen remodel plumbing
  • Preventive maintenance checks
  • Second opinions on big quotes
