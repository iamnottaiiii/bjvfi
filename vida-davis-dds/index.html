Exams and cleanings

Routine exams and professional cleanings are the foundation of preventive dentistry. Public reviews of this practice mention both routine visits and the friendliness of the front desk and hygienists.

Crowns and fillings

Damaged teeth can be restored with fillings or crowns. Note that some public reviews of this office raise concerns about treatment recommendations, including a crown a later dentist said was unnecessary. As with any practice, a second opinion is always your right.

Emergency appointments

For tooth pain or injury, prompt care matters. One reviewer reports being fit in for an emergency appointment just before a holiday, with the office coordinating the needed care.

Care for children

General dentists commonly treat patients of all ages. Reviews mention children being seen at this practice; parents may wish to ask the dentist directly about their child's treatment plan during the visit.
