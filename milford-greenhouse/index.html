About

A family greenhouse, rooted in Clermont County

Milford Greenhouse is part of a family greenhouse operation in Clermont County, Ohio. The family grows annuals, perennials, vegetables, and herbs in its own greenhouses to sell in its retail stores, including this Milford location on Lila Avenue.

Danny and Ellen Grant started the business in 1978. It is currently owned and operated by their daughter Leah Grant, carrying the family growing tradition forward for local gardeners.

1978
The beginning. Danny and Ellen Grant start the family greenhouse business in Clermont County.
Today
Still family-run. Daughter Leah Grant owns and operates the greenhouses, growing plants for retail stores in the area.

Source: the family business website, grantsgreenhouses.com. History summarized from public information.
