Our story

A neighborhood salon, refreshed and reimagined.

Since 2016

Under Kim's management

Kim's Nail & Spa has been a local favorite since new ownership took over in June 2016. Under the skilled management of Kim, a highly qualified nail technician and beauty expert, the salon underwent significant renovations to create a space that is both modern and inviting.

The atmosphere is one of calm and comfort: a place to escape the everyday and indulge in some much-needed relaxation, with a team dedicated to individualized care.
