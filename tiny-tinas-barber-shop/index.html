Our little story

Small shop, warm chairs

Tiny Tina's Barber Shop is a neighborhood barber shop at 634 Elm St in Ludlow, Kentucky. It is the kind of place where folks stop by on a Saturday morning, swap stories in the waiting chairs, and walk out looking sharp.

We keep things simple: classic hair cuts and facial grooming in a friendly, family-welcoming shop. No fuss, no rushing, just good barbering and a warm hello from the neighborhood.
