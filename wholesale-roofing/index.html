BUILT ON STRAIGHT TALK

Roofing has a reputation problem: big promises, vague bids, crews that vanish. We started Wholesale Roofing to do it the other way. You get a written scope, a firm price, a start date, and a crew that treats your yard like their own.

We work on the roofs Cincinnati actually has: steep asphalt shingle colonials in Anderson Township, low-slope commercial roofs along the east side, and everything in between. Ohio weather is rough on a roof, so we build for it.
