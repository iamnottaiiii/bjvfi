About

A team salon
East Syracuse trusts

East End Hair Co brings together experienced stylists under one roof on West Manlius Street.

Our story

Many chairs,
one standard

East End Hair Co is a team salon: a group of qualified stylists sharing one shop, so clients can find the right fit for their hair and their schedule. Many clients have been coming for years, and some travel a long way to keep their appointments.

The salon is a licensed New York State appearance enhancement business, and it is listed as a DevaCurl certified salon for curl-specific cutting and color.

  • Licensed NY appearance enhancement business
  • DevaCurl certified curl services
  • Cuts, color and styling for men and women
