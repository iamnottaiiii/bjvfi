Our story

An Alexandria trucking outfit, plain and simple

Creektrace Transport LLC is a freight and trucking company based on Creektrace Road in Alexandria, Kentucky. We run local and regional hauls out of Campbell County, serving shippers across Northern Kentucky and the nearby tri-state area.

We keep the operation lean on purpose. When you call (859) 445-8074, you talk to the people who schedule the truck and the driver behind the wheel. No call centers, no load boards of strangers, no wondering where your freight is.

Our promise is short: show up when we say we will, handle the freight like it is our own, and charge what we quoted. That is how a small Kentucky carrier earns repeat business, one on-time delivery at a time.
