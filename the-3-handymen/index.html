Services

What we can
help with.

Every home is different, so start with a call. Describe the job and we will tell you straight whether it is something we do.

  1. 01

    Small repairs

    The everyday fixes that keep a house feeling right: doors, drawers, handles, trim, and the other little things that add up. Tell us what is broken and we will tell you if we can fix it.

  2. 02

    Installs and assembly

    Shelves, curtain rods, ceiling fans, flat pack furniture, TV mounts. If it came in a box with confusing instructions, that is exactly the kind of job to hand off.

  3. 03

    Home maintenance

    Caulking, weatherstripping, minor drywall patches, and the seasonal upkeep that protects your home. A little maintenance now saves a bigger repair later.

  4. 04

    The miscellaneous list

    Got a list of five small things and no Saturday to spare? Bundle them into one visit. One call, one trip, one tidy result.
