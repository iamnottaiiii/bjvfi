The Feature

One builder, one point of contact, zero guesswork.

Custom building goes wrong when too many hands touch the plan. We keep the chain short: you talk to us, we run the site, and every trade answers to one schedule.

From lot evaluation and architectural coordination to framing, finishes, and the final punch list, we stay on site and on the details. Clear budgets, written change orders, and a build calendar you can actually read. That is the whole philosophy, and it is why our clients recommend us to their neighbors.

A custom home should feel inevitable, as if the house could not have turned out any other way.

We build in Media and the surrounding Delaware County communities, where walkable streets, mature trees, and historic housing stock reward a builder who sweats the small stuff: true divided lites, honest trim profiles, and foundations that stay dry for decades.

Services

Custom building, end to end.

One contract, one builder, one accountable schedule. Here is the full scope of what we take on in Media and Delaware County.

01

New Custom Homes

Complete ground-up construction: lot evaluation, plan coordination, permitting, excavation, framing, roofing, systems, and interior finishes. We manage every trade and keep you updated with a written build calendar and regular site walks.

02

Home Additions

Primary-suite additions, second stories, rear expansions, and in-law suites. Structural tie-ins, rooflines, and exterior materials are matched to the existing home so the addition looks original.

03

Whole-Home Renovations

Full gut renovations: layout changes, new electrical and plumbing, kitchens, baths, flooring, and trim. Ideal for older Media homes that need modern systems behind classic character.

04

Kitchens & Baths

Custom cabinetry, stone and tile work, fixture upgrades, and lighting design. We handle the messy middle (plumbing, electrical, waterproofing) as carefully as the visible finish.

05

Outdoor Living

Decks, porches, patios, and finished lower levels that extend the home. Built to handle Pennsylvania weather and permitted to code.

06

Design Coordination & Permitting

We work directly with your architect or help you find one, then manage drawings, engineering, and the full permit process with the borough and county.

Pricing is project-specific. Every job is estimated line by line after a site visit, so the number you approve is the number we build to.

Our Story

A builder rooted in Media.

West End Walk Media, LLC is a custom home builder headquartered at 630 W End Walk in Media, Pennsylvania. We work where we live: the walkable streets, stone colonials, and new infill projects of Delaware County are our everyday job sites, not a service area on a map.

The company was founded on a simple frustration with how custom building usually goes: vague allowances, trades talking past each other, and homeowners finding out about problems after the drywall is up. We run the opposite playbook. One contract, one schedule, one builder answering the phone.

Whether it is a ground-up home, a second story, or a whole-house renovation, the goal is the same: a house that fits your household, built to outlast trends and weather alike.
