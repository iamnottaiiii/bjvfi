Our story

Cabrera's Auto Body is a full-service auto body shop on NW 2nd Street in Des Moines, run by owner Juan Cabrera.

The shop handles major jobs like fender replacement, engine repair, and paint jobs, plus minor work like dent repairs and bumper replacement to restore vehicles to pre-accident condition. Reviewers praise the fair prices and quality work.
