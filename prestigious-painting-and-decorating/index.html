About

A Society Hill shop with 31 years on the wall.

Prestigious Painting & Decorating is a Philadelphia painting and decorating company based at 636 Pine St in Society Hill. For 31 years the company has specialized in custom residential painting and historical restorations, serving Center City and the Main Line.

It is imperative to us that each of our clients feels understood, and that the future of their surroundings is in capable, trustworthy hands.

The company is listed on the Society Hill Preferred Contractor's list and earned the Angie's List Super Service Award in 2011. It is a member of the Painting and Decorating Contractors of America, and accepts major credit cards.
