Chapter 01

The firm on Colorado Boulevard

City of Los Angeles business records list John V Hole at 1125 Colorado Boulevard with a start date of April 1998. That is more than two and a half decades of general contracting rooted in one Eagle Rock address, serving homeowners and businesses across Northeast Los Angeles.

A general contractor is the single point of responsibility on a construction project: scheduling the trades, pulling the permits, keeping the inspections on track, and standing behind the finished work. When you hire one firm to run the job, you stop playing phone tag with five different crews.

“A good contractor does not just swing a hammer. He runs the calendar, the permits, and the punch list.”

Chapter 02

How projects run here

The process is deliberately old school. You call, you talk through the project, and somebody comes out to see the space in person. No web forms standing between you and the person running your job.

From there you get a written scope: what is included, what order the work happens in, and what it costs. Change orders, when they come up, get discussed before they get built. That is the whole philosophy, and it fits on an index card.

“Measure twice, permit once, and never leave a punch list unfinished.”
