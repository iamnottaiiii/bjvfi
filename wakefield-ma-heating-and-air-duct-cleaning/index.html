Why duct cleaning matters

Dust, pet dander and everyday debris collect inside ductwork over time. A professional cleaning clears that buildup so your heating and cooling system can move air the way it was designed to.

Cleaner ducts can mean less dust settling around the house, fewer musty odors from vents and a system that does not have to work as hard to keep every room comfortable.
