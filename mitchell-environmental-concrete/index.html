Our story

A Sun Valley concrete crew with structural chops

Mitchell Environmental Concrete operates out of Sun Valley, California, serving homeowners, builders and property managers across the San Fernando Valley. The business lists itself as a structural concrete specialist, a step above the typical flatwork-only outfit.

That distinction matters. Structural concrete carries loads, resists earthquakes and has to meet engineering specs. It is work where shortcuts show up years later as cracks, settlement or failed inspections.
