A note on safety

Electricity does not give second chances, so the rule here is simple: if something smells hot, sparks, or trips repeatedly, stop using it and call an electrician. DIY fixes on panels and wiring are where small problems become dangerous ones.

Smell burning plastic or see scorch marks around an outlet? Cut power to that circuit and call right away.
