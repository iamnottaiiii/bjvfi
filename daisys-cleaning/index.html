What we do

Beach cleaning, the careful way

From one-time deep cleans to recurring shoreline care, every job is priced on its scope. Call for a free quote.

01

Shoreline Litter Removal

Hand and rake pickup of litter, plastics, bottle caps, wrappers and washed-up debris along beaches and coastal walkways.

02

Post-Event Beach Cleanup

Weddings, cookouts, festivals and gatherings. We clear the site the same day so the shoreline looks untouched by morning.

03

Storm Debris Clearing

After wind and high water, we remove driftwood, tangled line, seaweed mats and scattered debris from your stretch of coast.

04

Beachfront Property Care

Clean steps, rails, decks and access paths at beachfront homes and rental properties, so guests arrive to a spotless setting.

05

Recurring Shoreline Visits

Weekly or biweekly cleanups for communities and businesses that want their shoreline consistently guest-ready.

06

Deep Detail Cleanups

The fine work: micro-plastics, cigarette filters and hidden litter in dunes and grass lines. The details that make a beach feel pristine.

Sample pricing: small one-time cleanups from $120, event cleanups from $250, recurring plans quoted by scope. Final pricing is confirmed on the call and always in writing before work begins.
