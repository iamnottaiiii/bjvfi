Our Story

Green Acres began as a landscaping business in Revere and grew into the place Revere families come for their Christmas tree. Owner Mike Colecchia, a lifelong Revere resident and Revere High class of 1975 graduate, has been selling Christmas trees to Revere families for over 25 years.

Christmas was always a big part of growing up in the Colecchia house, with Italian family traditions like Sunday dinners and, on Christmas, antipasto salad and macaroni. And always a real balsam tree, never a fake one.

That tradition continues on the lot today. About 500 customers visit every holiday season, and every child who comes gets a free candy cane and a special tree ornament. For Mike, making Christmas tree shopping fun for families is the whole point.

Green Acres is also known around Revere for its landscaping work, including grounds care for local institutions. Call (781) 284-4277 any time of year.
