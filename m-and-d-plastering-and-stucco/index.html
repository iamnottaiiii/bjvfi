Why stucco

Built for California weather

Stucco is the finish of the Valley for a reason: it shrugs off sun and heat, resists fire, and keeps homes cooler when summer runs long. Applied right, it lasts for decades with almost no upkeep.

We match textures and color so repairs disappear into the existing wall, and we prep every surface, lath, paper and flashing, so the finish bonds for the long haul.

The process

Prep first, finish forever

A stucco job is only as good as what is underneath. That is why every project starts with the layers nobody sees: sound substrate, proper lath and paper, correct flashing around every opening.

  • Thorough prep, lath, paper and flashing done right
  • Proper curing between coats for a crack-resistant finish
  • Texture and color matched to blend with existing walls

The crew

Plasterers by trade, perfectionists by habit

Stucco is unforgiving. Every trowel mark shows, every shortcut cracks. This crew works the old way: proper prep, full curing between coats, and a finish that is checked in raking light before we call it done.

We start early, keep the site tidy, and treat your home like our own. Most jobs come from referrals and repeat calls, which is exactly how we like it.
