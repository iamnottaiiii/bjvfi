Our Story

A contractor from your own neighborhood

Crestline Contracting is a general contracting business based on Vista Place in La Cañada Flintridge. We started with a simple idea: do the job the way we would want it done in our own home, and treat every client like a neighbor, because most of you are.

La Cañada Flintridge homes have character, from mid century ranches to hillside builds with real structural quirks. We know these houses, the soil they sit on, and the permitting realities of building here. That local knowledge keeps projects on track and budgets honest.

Show up

On time, every time. If a schedule slips, you hear it from us first, not last.

Communicate

One point of contact, plain language updates, and answers when you call.

Stand behind it

If something we built is not right, we come back and make it right. That is the whole policy.
