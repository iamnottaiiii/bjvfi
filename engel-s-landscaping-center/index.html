About Engel's

Engel's Landscaping Center is a garden center and landscaping business at 15810 Lockbourne Eastern Rd in Ashville, Ohio. Public records show the business established in 1981 and owner Kurt Engel. The Pickaway County visitors bureau notes the center opens each spring with perennials, annuals, hanging baskets, herbs, and more.
