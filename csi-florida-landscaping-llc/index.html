Who we are

Decades in the dirt,
still showing up

CSI Florida Landscaping LLC is a family-owned company based at 1104 Harmony Dr N in Jacksonville. Its business profile notes more than 36 years of experience providing landscaping services to residential and commercial clients across the area.

That experience shows in the range of the work: hardscaping and tree services alongside everyday lawn care, and emergency storm cleanups when Florida weather makes its own plans.

The throughline is the company's own four-word creed: reliability, great communication, integrity, and quality work. Simple words, kept job after job.

We are experts in our trade and will do our best to keep you as educated as we can on your particular task or project.
