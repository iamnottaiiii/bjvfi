Our story

About Johnson Brothers Inc

A Staten Island electrical contractor with roots going back to 1940.

Rooted in Staten Island

Johnson Brothers Inc is headquartered at 496 Mosel Ave, Staten Island, NY 10304. Public records show the company in business since 1940, making it one of the borough's longest running electrical contractors.

Built for Bigger Work

The company is qualified for union work and takes on projects up to $2,000,000, serving both residential customers and commercial clients across the five boroughs.

The Way We Work

Clear proposals, proper permits, and tidy job sites. We scope the work in writing, pull NYC DOB permits where required, and leave your property clean when we leave.
