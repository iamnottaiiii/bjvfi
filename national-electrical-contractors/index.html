Straight answers, safe work

Electrical work is not a guess and check trade. Here is how to get the most out of your first call.

  • Describe the symptom: what stopped working, when it started, and which breakers are involved.
  • Ask whether the job needs a permit or inspection in Hamilton County. Call to confirm
  • Request the estimate in writing before work begins. Call to confirm
  • For anything sparking, smoking, or hot to the touch, call right away and cut power if it is safe to do so.
