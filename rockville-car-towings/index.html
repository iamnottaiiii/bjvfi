Our story

A Local Tire Shop, Built for Rockville Drivers

Rockville Car Towings is a tire dealer serving Rockville and the surrounding Montgomery County area from Piccard Drive. The shop focuses on one thing and does it well: getting you the right tires at a fair price, fitted the right way.

Whether you need a single replacement after a blowout, a full set of new tires, or a budget-friendly used option, the team works to get you back on the road fast, without the dealership runaround.

  • New and used tires in common sizes
  • Same-day mounting and balancing when stock allows
  • Honest advice on repair versus replacement
  • Easy to reach location off Piccard Drive
