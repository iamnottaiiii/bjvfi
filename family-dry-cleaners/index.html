Our story

A family shop
on Main Street

Family Dry Cleaners is a neighborhood dry cleaner in Somerville, built on careful work, fair prices, and customers who keep coming back.

Who we are

Skilled hands,
honest prices

We are a family-run dry cleaner at 5 Main St, near the corner of Central St and Broadway. People know us for two things: garments that come back looking their best, and a staff that treats every item like it matters.

Our seamstresses are the heart of the shop. Customers praise their ability to repair and alter clothing, even specialized pieces like ski suits, and describe the owner as a wonderful, professional seamstress who listens carefully and delivers quality work on time.

Loyalty runs deep here. Plenty of our regulars have been with us for years, and some still make the trip even after moving out of the neighborhood.
