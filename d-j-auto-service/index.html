Our Story

A real neighborhood shop on Black Horse Pike

D&J Auto Service has built its name the slow way: one honest repair at a time. Located at 107 S Black Horse Pike in Mt Ephraim, the shop serves drivers across South Jersey who want straight answers and fair prices.

Customers know the crew by name. Regulars mention Dan and Jose in their reviews, the kind of detail you only get from a shop that treats people like neighbors instead of work orders.
