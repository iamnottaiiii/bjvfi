About

not a clinic for patients

Empire Dental Labs is a dental laboratory. We do not see patients. We serve the dental offices that do.

The laboratory

Behind every great restoration

Empire Dental Labs operates from 76 Hancock St in the Midland Beach section of Staten Island, New York. It is classified in industry records as a dental laboratory, the trade that fabricates the crowns, bridges, dentures, and implant prosthetics dentists place every day.

Our work is business to business. Dental practices send us prescriptions and impressions, and we return finished restorations ready to seat. Patients never sit in our chairs, because we do not have any. That focus is the point: every hour goes into the bench work your cases depend on.

We measure our work in microns and in remakes avoided.
