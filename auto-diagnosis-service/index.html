Our story

An independent repair shop built around one idea: know before you fix.

The shop on Terminal Road

Auto Diagnosis Service operates out of 8409 Terminal Rd Ste A in Lorton, Virginia, in the heart of the area's automotive corridor. Drivers come in with noises, warning lights, and worries, and leave with a clear answer and a car that behaves.

The approach is simple and it has not changed: diagnose first, explain in plain language, and repair only what the evidence points to. That is how a small shop earns repeat customers one accurate fix at a time.

What we stand for

  • Diagnosis before dollars
  • Honest answers, even when the answer is "it can wait"
  • Clear estimates with no jargon
  • Respect for your time and your car
