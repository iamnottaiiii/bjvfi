About the company

A contractor rooted in Batavia

Watson Contracting works out of 2065 US-50 in Batavia, Ohio, building across Clermont County and the greater Cincinnati area.

General contracting, minus the runaround

Watson Contracting is a general contractor serving residential and commercial clients. Trade records list the company in new multifamily housing construction, and the day-to-day work spans new builds, residential projects, and commercial jobs.

Being based on US-50 in Batavia means the crew is local: on local roads, working with local suppliers, and reachable by phone during business hours when you need an answer.

The approach is simple. Scope the work honestly, coordinate the trades tightly, keep the site clean, and finish with a walkthrough the owner signs off on.

How we work

  • Straight scoping. Clear plans and honest pricing before work begins.
  • Tight coordination. One accountable contractor managing the trades on your job.
  • Clean sites. Work areas kept orderly while the job is underway.
  • Signed-off finish. Final walkthrough with the owner, no loose ends.
