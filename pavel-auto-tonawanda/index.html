Services

What the shop handles.

Tap any service for details. These are the repairs a full service shop like Pavel handles week in and week out. Have something specific in mind? Call (716) 818-3567 and ask.

Check engine lights, warning lights, rough running, and mystery noises. Modern diagnostics find the fault fast, and experience confirms it before any part gets replaced.

Pads, rotors, calipers, and brake lines. If your pedal feels soft, your steering shakes when you stop, or you hear grinding, bring it in before a small wear item becomes a big bill.

Oil changes, transmission fluid, coolant, and filter service. Routine maintenance is the cheapest repair there is, and it is the one most drivers put off too long.

Shocks, struts, tie rods, and alignments. If the ride feels loose or the tires are wearing unevenly, the suspension is telling you something.

Air conditioning service, radiators, thermostats, and hoses. Buffalo area weather punishes both systems, so we keep them ready for July and January alike.

Batteries, alternators, starters, belts, and spark plugs. The parts that leave you stranded get checked before they fail, not after.

Our story

Licensed in 1999. Still turning wrenches.

1999
Original NY repair shop license issued
25+
Years at 112 Delaware Street

Pavel Auto Repairs has been a New York State licensed repair shop at 112 Delaware Street in Tonawanda since 1999. The licensed owner on public record is Pavel Mokiyenko, and the shop has carried his first name ever since.

Shops come and go on Delaware Street. This one stayed, because it was built on the oldest business plan there is: do the work properly, charge a fair price, and treat every driver like a neighbor. In Tonawanda, most of them are.

There is no service writer upselling you at the counter and no revolving door of strangers under the hood. It is a craftsman shop, the kind where the person who diagnoses your car is the person who fixes it.
