Good to know

When to schedule a cleaning

Most homes do well with a dryer vent cleaning about once a year, and air duct cleaning every few years, or after a renovation, a move, or a stretch of heavy dust. If it has been a while, now is a fine time.

  • Yearly dryer vent cleaning keeps airflow strong
  • Duct cleaning after renovations clears construction dust
  • New to the home? Start fresh with both
