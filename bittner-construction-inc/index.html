Three Generations of Concrete and Craft

Bittner Construction, Inc. has been pouring, building and remodeling around Cincinnati since 1951. It is still family-led today.

1951

The business opens its doors in the Cincinnati area. Concrete work is the foundation trade from day one.

1975

Bittner Construction, Inc. is formally incorporated as an Ohio corporation.

1994

The BBB file opens on the company, beginning a long public record of accredited work.

2014

Bittner Construction earns BBB accreditation and holds an A+ rating to this day.

Today

Led by President Ross Bittner and Vice President Todd Bittner, the company handles concrete work, new construction, remodels, additions and custom steel buildings from 7420 Harrison Ave in Mt Healthy.
