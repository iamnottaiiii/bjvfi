About East Los Angeles Barber Shop

East Los Angeles Barber Shop sits right on Cesar E Chavez Avenue, serving the neighborhood with straightforward, no-nonsense barbering. Walk in for a clean cut from a shop that knows its regulars.

Every visit follows the same simple rhythm: a real consultation about what you want, careful work in the chair, and a finish you can feel good about. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
