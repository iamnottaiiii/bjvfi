About PBC

A design-build contractor based in Williamsburg, Ohio.

Design and construction, one team

Pbc Design and Construction, operating as Parks Brothers Construction, LLC, is based at 15998 Eastwood Rd in Williamsburg, Ohio. The firm works as a design-build contractor, which means the same team that draws up your project is the team that builds it.

That matters when the plan meets the real world. Decisions get made on site, budgets stay tied to the drawings, and you always know who to call.

PBC serves homeowners and businesses across Clermont County and the Greater Cincinnati area, from single-room remodels to ground-up construction.

How a project runs

  1. Consult

    We walk your property, hear what you need, and talk honestly about budget and scope.

  2. Design and estimate

    Drawings and a written estimate, revised until the plan and the price both make sense.

  3. Build

    Permits, schedule, trades, and daily cleanup, with updates as the work progresses.

  4. Walkthrough

    We go through the finished work together and make sure every detail is right.
