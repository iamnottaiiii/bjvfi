Our story

A Riverdale flooring store since 1994.

Jessie's Floors opened its doors on Highway 85 in Riverdale, Georgia in 1994. For three decades it has been a locally owned flooring store and contractor serving homes and businesses across Clayton County and the south metro Atlanta area.

The store carries a wide selection of carpet, hardwood, laminate, and luxury vinyl plank to suit different styles and budgets, with professional installation by an experienced team. Services include residential and commercial installation, carpet binding and serging, and free estimates.

Customers consistently praise the staff by name, the clear communication through each project, and work that gets done quickly without cutting corners. The business holds a 4.8 rating across 36 reviews on Birdeye.
