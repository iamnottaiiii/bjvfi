Our Story

The neighborhood laundry of Eagle Hill

Eagle Hill Laundry serves the Eagle Hill section of East Boston from its Trenton Street storefront: a quiet, well-kept shop built around one promise, laundry that is convenient and affordable.

Rooted in the neighborhood

Named for the Eagle Hill neighborhood it serves, the shop is a practical fixture for East Boston residents, students, and professionals. It sits on Trenton St in the 02128, close to daily life and an easy stop on the way home.

Time back in your week

The Wash & Fold service is the shop's answer to the busiest weeks: drop off your laundry and pick it up fresh and folded, cleaned with high-quality products and proper care for every garment.
