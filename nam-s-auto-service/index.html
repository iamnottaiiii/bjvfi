Our story

Springfield’s own shop, since 1999

For more than 25 years, NAM’S AUTO SERVICE has been the shop Springfield drivers recommend to each other. Not the biggest shop in town. Just the one people trust.

A neighborhood fixture

NAM’S AUTO SERVICE opened its doors on Boudinot Dr in 1999 and has been serving Northern Virginia drivers ever since. The shop has grown the old-fashioned way: one honest repair at a time, and customers who keep coming back for five, eight, ten years and tell their friends.

Reviewers describe the same shop over and over: quick, efficient work at a reasonable price, with mechanics who explain what they are doing and never push repairs you do not need.

Verified and accountable

NAM’S AUTO SERVICE is a AAA Approved Auto Repair facility, which means it meets AAA’s standards for quality, and AAA members can get discounts and AAA Dollars here.

The shop holds a BBB A+ rating, and takes complaints seriously, with a named contact handling customer concerns.

How we work

1. Diagnose honestly. We find the real cause of the problem, and we tell you exactly what we found.

2. Quote fairly. A straight estimate before any work starts. Customers regularly note our prices beat other quotes.

3. Fix it once. Reviewers say the same thing: they never have to bring the car back for the same repair.

The kind of shop we are

We have lent customers a car to get them through a tough repair. We have diagnosed a compressor problem for free while recharging an AC. We hand out free advice on things that might go wrong down the road.

That is what a neighborhood shop does. If that sounds like the kind of shop you want working on your car, we would love to meet you.
