Welcome

A warm, welcoming shop for beautiful braids

Mimi's describes its approach simply: constantly learning the most current haircare techniques and creating a warm, welcoming environment for every client. Braiding is the pride of the shop.

Services

Braiding services

Every style starts with a consultation. Call ahead for pricing and to book your chair.

Box braids, knotless braids, and cornrows in the length and size you want. Bring a reference photo and the stylists will match it.

Senegalese twists, passion twists, and two-strand twists, braided gently so there is no pulling or pain.

Sew-in weaves and extension installs for added length and fullness.

Loc starts and maintenance for natural, long-lasting styles.

Hair replacement services and hairpiece work. Call to discuss your needs and book a consultation.
