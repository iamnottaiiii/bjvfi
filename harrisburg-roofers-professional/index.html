Roofers who live where you do.

Harrisburg Roofers Professional is a roofing contractor headquartered at 3315 Walnut Street in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. We focus on one thing: keeping homes in Dauphin County dry, safe, and looking good from the curb.

Roofing is a trust business. You cannot watch every nail, so you need a crew that works like you are watching. That is how we run every job: clear scope, careful installation, honest communication, and a site left clean.
