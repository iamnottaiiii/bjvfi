Our story

Rooted in Cincinnati

The personal-care lawn crew

Blue Spruce Lawn Care grew out of a simple idea: a lawn company that treats every yard like it is the only one on the route. Based on State Road in Cincinnati, we serve homeowners who want steady, honest work from people who notice the details.

Our clients stay with us for years. They tell us we are less expensive than the big companies they left, and that we actually care about the result. That is the whole business model: show up, do it right, and keep the yard looking excellent.

We also share roots with Blue Spruce Nursery, which is why planting, beds, and mulch are second nature to us.

What we believe

  • Personal care beats a big route sheet
  • Fair pricing, honest recommendations
  • Healthy lawns start with healthy soil and beds
  • A yard should look excellent every visit, not just the first
