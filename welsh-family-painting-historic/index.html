Our story

A family trade, kept the old way

Welsh Family Painting/Historic began with a simple observation: old houses reward patience, and most painters are in a hurry.

We are a family crew based in Berlin, New Jersey. The same professionals have been with the owner for years, and that continuity shows up in the work: careful prep, honest color advice, and a finish that respects the architecture instead of covering it up.

Historic homes are our specialty because they are where craftsmanship matters most. A 100 year old porch railing cannot be rushed. A Victorian paint scheme cannot be picked from a single fan deck at noon. We take our time, and our clients tell us the neighborhood notices.

One reviewer put it best: neighbors stopped by, people slowed their cars to comment, and the house became the envy of the street. That is the outcome we paint for.
