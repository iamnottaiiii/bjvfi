The short version

Two services, one address

Garment Care Cleaners sits at 5603 Avenue L in Brooklyn, and it does double duty: a dry cleaner for the garments you dress up in, and a laundromat for the laundry that never stops.

Local listings describe dry cleaning and laundry plus self-service machines, which means one trip can cover the suit for Monday and the mountain of towels from the weekend.

The model is straightforward: bring in what needs care, tell the counter what you need, and get it back clean, pressed, or folded. When in doubt, call ahead: (718) 444-0544.
