Our story

A neighborhood shop
with a sharp reputation

Klean Kuts Barbershop sits on Belair Road in Baltimore, doing what a good barbershop does: great cuts, straight talk, and chairs that stay busy.

The shop

Early doors, high standards

Klean Kuts opens at 7 AM on weekdays, built for people who want a fresh cut before the workday begins. It is a working barbershop, not a lounge: you come in, you get a sharp cut, you leave looking right.

Reviewers keep returning to the same themes: quality fades, careful line ups, and barbers worth asking for by name. One customer put it plainly: if you care about quality, this is the place to go.
