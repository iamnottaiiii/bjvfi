About

A Cincinnati company that builds what it sells.

Manufacturer, distributor, and service company for projection and presentation gear.

We make the projector. Then we stand behind it.

Innovative Data Communications is based at 3786 Hopper Hill Road in Cincinnati, Ohio. We are listed as a manufacturer, distributor, and service company for projectors and presentation equipment.

We are the North America distributor for the Conference Pro™ multimedia projector and the Mobile Pro™ complete presentation system, the portable package that puts a projector, computer, VCR, stereo speakers, and wireless microphone into one heavy-duty travel case.

Schools, businesses, and houses of worship across the region rely on rooms where the picture is bright and the audio carries. That is the whole job, and it is the only job we do.
