Our Story

A Valley Roofing Crew, Plain and Simple

ER Roofing, also listed as Edgar Romero Roofing, is a roofing contractor based in Van Nuys, California. In the company's own words, it has offered top-quality roofing services to the local community for over 20 years, building trust by adapting each job to the customer's needs and budget.

That approach shows up in how the work gets done: show up, look at the actual roof, say what it needs, and do the work right the first time. No upsells, no mystery line items.

The crew works on the roofs Valley homes actually have, clay tile, asphalt shingle and flat systems, and handles everything from a single leak repair to a full replacement.
