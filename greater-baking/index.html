Our story

Baked with heart on 71st Street

A small cupcake bakery with a big mission: community, joy, and bringing people together.

A South Shore home

Greater Baking opened its doors at 2300 E 71st St in the former Sarah's Bakery space, and made it its own: a clean, kid-friendly café where neighbors gather over cupcakes and lemonade.

Be happy, stay humble

That is the owner's motto: "Be happy, stay humble, and be great in all that you do." Customers describe a wholesome, hardworking owner who is restoring faith in the South Shore community, one cupcake at a time.

More than sweets

Greater Baking partners with Too Good To Go to rescue surprise bags of gourmet cupcakes, cutting food waste while sharing the love. Community is the whole point.

Come be part of the story.

Open Thursday through Saturday, 11 AM to 7 PM. Walk in for a cupcake, stay for the couches and the lemonade.
