About

Kitchen Remodeler San Marino is a kitchen remodeling business based at 2516 Huntington Dr in San Marino, California. The focus is single and simple: kitchens. Design, cabinetry, countertops, tile, and the full build that turns an aging kitchen into the room the house revolves around.
