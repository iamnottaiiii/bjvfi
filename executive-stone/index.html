Our story

Two decades of fine stone in the Valley

Executive Stone Trading (EST) is an importer, manufacturer and distributor of natural stones, specializing in limestone known as Jerusalem Stone, based in North Hollywood, California.

Working with leading developers, architects, designers and builders, we have supplied stone for prestigious and renowned projects including the Jewish Federation in Los Angeles, the Bellagio Hotel in Las Vegas, Cartier in Beverly Hills, the San Jose Library and the Oxnard Library.

We are a fully licensed and insured company providing Southern California with exceptional ceramic, stone, mosaic and stone veneer installation for over 20 years, with a goal of unparalleled customer satisfaction.
