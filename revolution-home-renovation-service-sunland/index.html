Our story

Born on Foothill Boulevard

Revolution Home Renovation Service Sunland is a construction company based at 7842 Foothill Blvd in Sunland, California, in the heart of the Sunland-Tujunga foothills. We grew up around these ranch homes and mid century houses, and we know how they are built, where they fail, and how to bring them up to date without losing their character.

Our philosophy is simple: revolutionize the renovation experience by doing the unglamorous things right. Show up on time. Protect the floors. Keep the site clean. Answer the phone. Finish when we said we would.

A renovation should be exciting, not stressful. We take the stress so you get the excitement.

Whether it is a kitchen that finally works for your family, a bathroom that feels like a retreat, or a whole home brought into the modern era, we treat every project like it is our own house on the line.
