About

Two trades. One local crew.

T&S Painting & Dryer Vent Cleaning Services Co is a Vincentown, New Jersey business built around a simple idea: the painter already in your home is the right person to spot and clear a dangerous dryer vent.

We serve Southampton Township and the surrounding Burlington County area with interior and exterior painting, plus dedicated dryer vent cleaning and inspections. No invented history, no inflated claims, just straightforward work quoted by phone and finished on schedule.
