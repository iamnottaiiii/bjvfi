Our story

Three decades of tile in the Valley.

Petros Tile Interior Improvement has been listed as a working tile and interior improvement business in Van Nuys for 33 years, according to its long-standing Yellow Pages listing. That kind of tenure in the trades comes from one thing: work that holds up.

Based at 7855 Burnet Ave in Van Nuys, the company serves homeowners across the San Fernando Valley who want tile installed by people who treat the subfloor, the waterproofing, and the layout with the same care as the finished surface.

The grout lines are the signature. Straight, even, and clean, every time.

Kitchens, bathrooms, and floors are the core of the business. Ceramic, porcelain, and natural-stone-look tile go in with an eye toward how the room will be used ten years from now, not just how it photographs on day one.
