Why insulation matters here

The Valley runs hot. Your house does not have to.

In Chatsworth and across the San Fernando Valley, summer heat pours through under-insulated attics and walls, and winter nights leak warmth right back out. The right insulation levels out temperatures room to room and takes real load off your air conditioner.

Attics
01

Cooler upstairs rooms

Most heat enters through the attic. Bringing attic insulation up to proper depth is one of the highest-return upgrades a Valley home can get.

Walls
02

Quieter, steadier rooms

Wall insulation steadies temperatures and dampens outside noise, so bedrooms and offices feel calm even on busy streets.

Bills
03

Lower energy costs

A tight thermal envelope means shorter AC cycles and less wasted heat, which shows up on the utility bill month after month.

Where the heat gets in

Attic insulation

The attic is the front door for Valley heat. Topping up thin or compressed attic insulation, sealing air leaks around penetrations, and making sure soffit and ridge vents can breathe changes how the whole house feels.

  • ✓ Blown-in and batt attic insulation
  • ✓ Attic air sealing around ducts, wires, and fixtures
  • ✓ Ventilation checks so insulation can perform
  • ✓ Old or damaged insulation removal and replacement
Ask about your attic

Wall insulation

Older Valley homes often have little or nothing inside the walls. Adding wall insulation steadies room temperatures, cuts drafts, and noticeably quiets street noise.

  • ✓ Retrofit insulation for existing walls
  • ✓ New-construction and remodel wall cavities
  • ✓ Draft and air-leak reduction
  • ✓ Sound dampening between rooms and from outside
Ask about your walls

Whole-home upgrades

The best results come from treating the house as a system: attic, walls, floors, and the air leaks between them. A whole-home plan prioritizes the work by payback, so the biggest comfort gains come first.

  • ✓ Full thermal-envelope assessment
  • ✓ Prioritized scope: biggest gains first
  • ✓ Crawl space and floor insulation
  • ✓ Coordination with HVAC and roofing work
Plan your upgrade

Commercial buildings

Offices, shops, and warehouses bleed energy through roofs and walls just like homes do, at much larger scale. Commercial insulation work targets the envelope so HVAC systems stop fighting the building.

  • ✓ Office and retail insulation
  • ✓ Warehouse and industrial spaces
  • ✓ Scheduled work to limit business disruption
  • ✓ Written scope and timeline up front
