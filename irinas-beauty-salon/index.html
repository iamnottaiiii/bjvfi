Welcome

One salon, every ritual of self care

Tucked on Quentin Road, Irina's Beauty Salon brings together the services most women schedule across three different appointments: a cut or color, a manicure, a facial, makeup for an event, and smooth skin that lasts. Here you can do them all under one roof, with one team that gets to know you.

The salon lists hair, nails, makeup, laser hair removal, facials and waxing among its specialties, so whether you are preparing for a wedding or just keeping up your routine, you can plan the whole visit in a single call.

A beauty studio should feel like your own vanity, just with better lighting and someone else doing the work.

Call ahead to ask about availability, current services and pricing. The team is happy to help you plan a single service or a full day of pampering.
