Our story

A luxury salon with
a neighborhood heart

Since 2020

Built on Avenue X

Keratin E&D Salon opened its doors in 2020 with a simple promise: high-quality beauty services and exceptional care, all in one welcoming space. The salon describes itself as a luxury salon for hair, nails, skincare and makeup, and it has become a go-to for locals who want the full treatment without hopping between appointments.

Guests highlight the warm ambiance from the moment they step inside, and the little extras: the salon is known for giving gifts to clients, a small touch that turns a routine appointment into something memorable.

The team

Specialists, not generalists

Stylists stay current with the latest trends and techniques, from dimensional balayage to meticulous Russian manicures. Guests praise team members by name for listening closely and replicating exactly the look they asked for, whether that is a precise brow shape, a soothing scalp treatment, or a full hair transformation.

Personalized service is the house style: every visit starts with what you want, and ends with you feeling pampered and valued.
