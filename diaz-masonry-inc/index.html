Why masonry

The trade that holds buildings together

Good masonry is part structure, part finish work. A well laid brick wall, a level paver patio, or a clean stone facade adds value you can see and durability you cannot, season after season.

Diaz Masonry Inc brings that craft to residential and commercial projects around Stockbridge, GA. From repairs to full installations, every course is laid with care for line, level, and the look of the finished work.
