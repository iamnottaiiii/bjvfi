A Neighborhood Original

An independent barbershop on Monmouth Street, not a franchise. The regulars say the quality and the work are always on point, which is why it became their go-to.

The Shop

Klassicos Barbershop sits at 641 Monmouth St in Newport, KY, serving Northern Kentucky with classic cuts, straight razor finishes, and beard work. Reviewers describe a clean, professional shop where appointments run about 30 minutes and walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free.

It is the kind of place with a pool table by the waiting area, free Wi-Fi, and barbers who remember your name. One long-time reviewer put it simply: "I always look for independent barbershops instead of the generic franchises, and these guys always deliver."
