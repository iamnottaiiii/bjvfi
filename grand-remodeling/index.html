Our story

The Grand Remodeling ledger

A few honest chapters about who we are and how we work.

01CHAPTER

Run by Danny Jarvis

Grand Remodeling is a Cincinnati construction company led by Danny Jarvis. No call centers, no layers of sales reps. You talk to the person who runs your job, and he treats your home like it is his own.

02CHAPTER

Kitchens and bathrooms are our craft

We specialize in kitchen and bathroom remodels, the two rooms where quality either shows or it does not. From the first teardown to the last bead of caulk, the work is handled with care.

03CHAPTER

A tidy site, every day

Clients notice this again and again: the workspace is left neat at the end of every day, floors and carpet protected, dust controlled. Remodeling is disruptive enough without living in a mess.

04CHAPTER

Open to your ideas

Change your mind mid-project? Speak up. We stay open to ideas and changes as the work unfolds, and we finish in a timely manner without cutting corners on the details.

05CHAPTER

Fair price, finished product

Good quality at a good price is the goal on every job. When the work is done, it should feel second to none, because in your home, it is.

Grand Remodeling is grand: from teardown to finish work, clients describe a five-star experience.
