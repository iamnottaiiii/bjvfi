FILE 01

Remodels live or die on the details: the reveal lines on a cabinet run, the grout joints in a shower, the way a door closes after the last coat of paint. A Installation Inc. exists for those details. Homeowners across the San Fernando Valley call when they want a contractor who shows up, communicates, and finishes. About the company

FILE 04

A Installation Inc. is a contracting company based in Woodland Hills, California, working in kitchens, bathrooms, and finish installation. The name says it plainly: installation is the trade, and doing it well is the business.

Contractor work is a trust business. A crew is inside your home, around your family, handling the rooms you use every day. That is why the approach here stays simple: clear scope before work starts, realistic scheduling, tidy job sites, and communication when plans change. No mystery line items, no vanishing acts mid project.

The company serves Woodland Hills and surrounding San Fernando Valley neighborhoods, from Tarzana and Encino to Calabasas and Canoga Park. If your project involves cabinets going in straight, tile meeting cleanly, or a kitchen or bath that needs to work harder, that is the right kind of call to make.
