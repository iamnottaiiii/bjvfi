The Shop

About

A small Cincinnati woodworking shop with a simple creed: build it right, build it local, build it to last.

Our Story

Adams Custom Woodworking is a small custom shop on Wyoming Avenue in Cincinnati, Ohio. Public business records list it as established in 2008 with a crew of one to four craftspeople. The shop focuses on custom designed furniture and woodworks made from locally sourced materials, with an emphasis on environmental sustainability: mature hardwoods harvested ethically from local stands.

Our Method of Work
