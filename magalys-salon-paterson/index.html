About

Magaly's Salon is a hair salon at 254 Union Ave in Paterson, NJ, owned and operated by master stylist Magaly Tejada.

The salon focuses on haircuts and hair color using quality natural products, with a specialty in curly hair through the Rezo Cut technique, plus shampoo and blowout services.
