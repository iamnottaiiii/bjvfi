About

Built on reputation, one home at a time

Pyramid Properties Inc is a construction company based at 6129 Pond View Terrace in Bath, Pennsylvania, serving the Lehigh Valley. The company focuses on custom home building and additions and remodels, including exterior renovations, attic conversions, and garage additions.

Ask past clients what sets the company apart and the answer is consistent: communication. David Miller has guided first-time builders through four-year projects, answering question after question with patience, and clients describe him as decent, ethical, and easy to work with. Several say he became a friend by the time the keys changed hands.

Recognition

In 2019, Pyramid Properties Inc received the Pulse of the City News Award for Excellence in Customer Satisfaction, with a top rating of 5 out of 5 stars, based on independent research into reviews, surveys, and customer experience.

What to expect

  • Direct communication with the builder through the project
  • Written scope and schedule before work starts
  • Careful coordination of trades and inspections
  • Final walkthrough to confirm every detail
