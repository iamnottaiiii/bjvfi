Our story
03 · SINCE 1997

One license, one standard, three decades of Queens plumbing.

Pal Mar Plumbing & Heating Inc was established in 1997 and incorporated in New York. The company is led by Vito Palmieri, a New York City Licensed Master Plumber, license number 1533, and a member of the Master Plumbers Council of the City of New York.

NYC
LMP
Vito PalmieriPresident and Master Plumber, LMP License 1533

Master plumber status in New York City is not handed out. It is earned through years in the trade and a licensing exam that covers the full plumbing code. When Pal Mar signs off on a job, it carries that license behind it.

The shop works across the Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Queens, on everything from apartment water heaters to commercial heating plants, violation removals, and new construction.
