Our Story

Méndez masonry Corp is a Staten Island concrete and masonry contractor based at 162 Benedict Ave. We are licensed by the New York City Department of Consumer Affairs (license 2101345-DCA, active) as a home improvement contractor.

Our work covers the full range of concrete and masonry: driveways, sidewalks, steps, walls, repairs, and restoration for residential and commercial properties. We price jobs clearly, show up when we say we will, and stand behind the finished work.

Why hire a licensed contractor

A New York City DCA license means the business is registered and accountable. It protects homeowners and keeps every project on the right side of local requirements.
