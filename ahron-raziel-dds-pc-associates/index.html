About the practice

Four decades of
Brooklyn smiles

Dr. Ahron Raziel, DDS

General Dentist · Brooklyn, NY

Dr. Raziel has practiced dentistry for more than four decades, caring for generations of Brooklyn families. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Maryland Baltimore College of Dental Surgery in 1984.

Patients describe a dentist who takes time to answer questions and explain treatment, supported by a friendly front desk and skilled hygienists.

  • DDS, University of Maryland Baltimore College of Dental Surgery, 1984
  • 42+ years of clinical experience in general dentistry

The practice

A team, not just a dentist

The "& Associates" in our name means you are cared for by a full team: associate dentists, hygienists and front-office staff who keep visits running smoothly and comfortably.

Our approach is simple: prevent what we can, fix what we must, and always explain what is happening and why. No jargon, no pressure, just honest dentistry.

We welcome patients of all ages, from a child's first checkup to dentures that bring back a confident bite.
