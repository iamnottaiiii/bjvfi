How it works

Convertible tops and custom interiors

Branch Brook Auto Top on Broadway has been Newark's upholstery and convertible-top shop for generations: new tops, seat covers, headliners, and full custom interiors.

Step 1: Show us the car

Drive in or send photos of the top, seats, or interior you want done.

Step 2: Pick materials

Vinyl, canvas, or leather. We show you real samples, not catalog guesses.

Step 3: Free quote

One written number for the whole job, parts and labor.

Step 4: We build it

Cut, stitched, and fitted in our own shop by career trimmers.

Step 5: Final fit check

You inspect every seam before the car leaves.
