Our story

A Panorama City crew with valley roots

Future Construccion & Remodeling started with a simple idea: give San Fernando Valley homeowners a contractor who answers the phone, shows up when promised, and stands behind the work. From our shop on Van Nuys Boulevard in Panorama City, we take on the projects other crews rush through, kitchens, baths, additions, and ADUs, and we finish them like they are our own homes.

We keep our operation lean on purpose. One crew per job means the people who start your project are the people who finish it. You get a written scope, a real schedule, and a foreman you can reach directly.

The first call

Free consultation at your home. We listen, measure, and talk honestly about budget and timeline.

The written plan

You get a detailed scope of work and a fixed estimate before anything is demolished.

The build

A dedicated crew, a tidy jobsite every evening, and updates you do not have to chase.

The walkthrough

We walk every room together and close out the punch list before final payment.
