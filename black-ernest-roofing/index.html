Why Black Ernest Roofing

The small job that protects the big investment

Clogged gutters push water where it does not belong: under shingles, behind siding, and against your foundation. We keep the whole system flowing so one rainy season never turns into a repair bill.

Full cleanouts

We scoop, flush, and clear every foot of gutter and downspout, then haul the debris away. Your yard looks the same as when we arrived, only drier.

Honest repairs

Loose hangers, leaky seams, sagging runs. We fix what is worth fixing and tell you straight when a section should be replaced instead.

Gutter guards

Quality guards keep leaves and pine needles out while letting water through, which means fewer cleanings and fewer ladder days for you.

Our Story

A local crew that treats your roofline like its own

Black Ernest Roofing is a gutter cleaning service based on Fair Oaks Avenue in Altadena, California. We started with a simple observation: most of the water damage we saw on local homes traced back to gutters that were clogged, sagging, or simply never cleaned.

So we built the business around that one job and learned to do it right. Every visit means ladders set safely, every foot of gutter cleared by hand, downspouts flushed until they run clear, and a walkthrough at the end so you can see the difference. No upsells, no mystery line items, just water flowing where it should.

Altadena and the surrounding foothill neighborhoods are full of mature trees, which means gutters here work harder than most. That is our home turf, and we know exactly what the seasons do to a roofline. Homeowners keep calling us back, and many of our reviews mention the same things: on time, thorough, and easy to deal with.

Whether it is a first cleanout in years or a standing seasonal plan, we show up with the same care every time. Call us and see what a clear gutter system feels like.
