Our story

A neighborhood counter on Old Keene Mill

Cosmo Cleaners has served the Cardinal Forest neighborhood of Springfield from its storefront at 8316 Old Keene Mill Road. It is the kind of shop where the staff know the regulars, remember how you like your shirts, and notice when a hem needs attention before you do.

The work is unglamorous and exacting: sorting by fabric and color, treating spots before they set, pressing collars until they lie flat. Done well, nobody notices the cleaning at all. They notice you.

Detail work is the point

Ask around Springfield and the name comes up for alterations and difficult spots, the jobs other counters turn away. That bench, with its pins and chalk and patience, is the heart of the shop.

Customers praise fast, kind counter service and alterations they stay satisfied with.

What customers praise

Reviewers who had a good experience point to fast, kind counter service and strong alteration work. Several mention difficult spots successfully treated and garments coming back looking sharp, with one customer saying shirts left for a week still came back wrinkle free.

What customers criticize

Other reviewers report serious dissatisfaction, including garments returned with discoloration or damage, stains not fully removed, pressing they felt was incomplete, and poor handling of complaints. A few say they switched to other Springfield cleaners after their experience.
