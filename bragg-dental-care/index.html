Our story

Optimum oral health care in a pleasant, comfortable atmosphere

Bragg Dental Care. is a family dental practice in Alexandria, Virginia, founded in 2007. Our goal is to be proficient in rendering optimum oral health care using the latest technology and dental equipment available in the industry.

We will listen to your concerns and work together as a team to provide the best possible treatment plans to achieve your optimal dental health. Protecting the well-being of our valued patients is one of our top priorities, which is why our office meets and surpasses all OSHA and CDC standards.

We are confident you will feel right at home, because we welcome all patients as if they were family.

About Dr. Tesfaye Hailemariam, D.D.S.

Dr. Hailemariam is a graduate of Howard University College of Dentistry and has been practicing dentistry for over 18 years. He has completed numerous continuing education courses across general, restorative, periodontic, orthodontic, endodontic, cosmetic, and implant dentistry. His emphasis on patient comfort and on keeping up to date with the latest advances in dentistry enables him to provide high quality care to every patient.
