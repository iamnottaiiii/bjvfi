Our story

One trade. Done right.

DMendez Drywall LLC is a Plainfield drywall contractor built on a simple idea: do one trade extremely well and treat every customer like a neighbor. Because around here, most of our customers are.

Why drywall only

Drywall is the surface everything else sits on. Bad taping shows through paint, bad hanging cracks within a year. We focus on this single trade so the finish coat is always right: flat seams, crisp corners, and walls that stay that way. We are licensed in New Jersey (Home Improvement Contractor license 13VH10394000) and carry general liability and workers compensation coverage.

What working with us feels like

Tidy by default

Floors covered, dust controlled, and the room cleaner than we found it when we leave.

Honest scheduling

Real arrival windows and real completion dates. If something slips, you hear it from us first.

Paint-ready guarantee

We do not call a wall finished until a painter could roll right over it.
