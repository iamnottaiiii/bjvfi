What we do

Full-service chimney care

Choose a category below. Every service starts with an honest assessment, not a sales pitch.

Sweeping & Cleaning

  • Fireplace and wood stove sweeping with creosote removal
  • Furnace and boiler flue cleaning
  • Smoke chamber cleaning and soot removal
  • Animal and debris blockage removal
  • Full HEPA-vacuum cleanup after every visit

Inspections

  • Annual safety inspections for fireplaces and stoves
  • Flue condition and creosote buildup evaluation
  • Crown, cap, and flashing condition checks
  • Draft and smoke-draw problem diagnosis
  • Written findings with recommended next steps

Repairs & Masonry

  • Tuckpointing and mortar joint repair
  • Chimney crown repair and rebuilds
  • Flashing repair and replacement
  • Waterproofing to stop freeze-thaw damage
  • Partial and full chimney rebuilds

Caps & Installs

  • Stainless steel chimney cap installation
  • Top-sealing damper installation
  • Chase cover replacement
  • Fireplace grate and accessory guidance
  • Rain, animal, and spark protection
