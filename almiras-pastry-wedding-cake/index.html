Our story

A neighborhood bakery with a wedding cake reputation.

The shop

On Indianapolis Blvd

Almira's Pastry and Wedding Cake sits at 4725 Indianapolis Blvd in East Chicago, serving the neighborhood fresh pastries, bread, and celebration cakes six days a week, plus Sunday mornings.

The craft

Decorated by hand, baked with care

The team is known for careful, personal cake decorating: listening to what you want, sketching the design with you, and sweating the details until the cake matches the vision in your head.

The promise

Beautiful, delicious, and fair

Reviewers keep saying the same three things: the cakes look stunning, they taste delicious, and the price is reasonable. That is the whole business model, and it works.
