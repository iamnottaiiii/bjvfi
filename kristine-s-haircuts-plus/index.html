Our story

Kristine cuts hair the way you want it cut

Kristine's Haircuts Plus is a full service salon and barber shop on Kinne Street in East Syracuse. For years, Kristine has built a loyal following by doing one thing well: listening to each client and delivering the cut they asked for.

One reviewer put it simply: Kristine is the best, she cuts your hair the way you want it cut. Another has trusted her since childhood and came back as an adult because she is the only traditional cut they trust.

Walk-ins are welcome, appointments are easy, and the atmosphere is warm. Men, women and children are all at home in the chair here.
