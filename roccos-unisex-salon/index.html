Our Story

Three decades of chairs,
one family of stylists

Rocco's Unisex Salon is owned by Christine Newcomb, who brings 36 years of experience behind the chair, and a team clients treat like family.

On Kinne Street in East Syracuse sits a salon that has earned something money cannot buy: loyalty measured in decades. Clients first walked through the door as kids in the late 90s and still come in for every haircut, bringing their own children now. That is what happens when a salon is run by people, not by a brand.

Christine Newcomb owns Rocco's and leads the floor with 36 years of experience. Alongside her, Sue is praised by name in review after review for cuts clients love, Heather is known for extraordinary patience, including with clients who need extra gentleness, and Rocco rounds out the family team. Walk-ins are welcome, regulars are family, and the prices stay fair. That is the whole story, and it is a good one.
