Our Story

A family trade, a family business, and a simple promise: treat every roof like it is over our own heads.

Roofing is the family trade.

Cabrera Family Roofers LLC grew out of years of hands on roofing work across Los Angeles. What started as a trade learned on the job became a family business with a simple operating rule: do the work you would want done on your own home.

That means honest inspections, clear explanations in plain language, and crews that clean up like they were never there, except for the roof.
