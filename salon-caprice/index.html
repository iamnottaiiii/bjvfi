Our story

North Buffalo's friendly chair

Salon Caprice sits inside Marshalls Plaza on Delaware Avenue, where it has become a go to for North Buffalo hair care. It is a New York State licensed salon run by people who genuinely love the craft and the neighborhood.

Guests describe a warm, welcoming atmosphere and stylists with serious experience, including stylist Noris, who reviewers praise as an expert with natural and relaxed hair, known for healthy, beautiful blowouts that do not damage the hair.

Public reviews tell the story best. One guest with thinning, gray hair wrote that they walked out feeling like a million dollars, praising the stylist's special technique for keeping fullness on top. Another called it the best hairdresser they have ever been to, pointing to the friendly atmosphere and hardworking team.

That is the Salon Caprice formula: real skill, honest prices, and a chair you will want to come back to.
