One company, every kind of fence

Most fence companies pick a lane. We built our name working all of them, from backyard gates to power plants.

Five Star Fence began its story as early as 1977 and formalized operations in 1985. In the decades since, it has become a go-to fence contractor for commercial, industrial, government, and residential customers, plus leading general contractors throughout Southern California.

The work ranges from office buildings, shopping centers, and parking lots to manufacturing plants, warehouses, utility plants, and water reservoirs. Public agencies trust the crew too, including the Los Angeles Department of Recreation and Parks, LAUSD, and the City of Beverly Hills.

Great work brought us our customers. Great work keeps them.Five Star Fence

Homeowner associations across Southern California call when old, damaged fencing needs replacing with something that upgrades appearance, raises property value, and improves security. And plenty of homeowners have simply wanted a better fence, installed right the first time.

Credentials matter on jobs like these. Five Star Fence holds CA License #1022201 (C-13), a Public Works DIR contractor registration, and certification as a California Small Business Enterprise.

About

From a small fencing outfit to a Southern California institution.

Five Star Fence began its story as early as 1977 and formalized operations in 1985. For more than 35 years since, the company has grown into a premier provider of fences for commercial, industrial, government, and residential customers, as well as leading general contractors throughout Southern California.

The formula never changed: professional design, reliable crews, great quality, fair price. Great work brought in customers, and great work kept them coming back with the next project.

Over 35 years of fences that hold their ground.The Five Star record

Today the company continues that journey, one satisfied customer at a time, from Pacoima to every corner of the Southland.
