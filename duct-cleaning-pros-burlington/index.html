About us

The Pros Behind the Name

A licensed, Burlington-based duct cleaning company with one simple goal: air you can feel the difference in.

Licensed Work You Can Trust

Duct Cleaning Pros operates as a licensed Massachusetts Home Improvement Contractor (HIC License #209973, verified active on BuildZoom). That means real accountability: a state license on file, and a company that stands behind every visit.

We do one thing and do it well. No bait pricing, no scare tactics, no pushing services you do not need. When we inspect a system, you get the straight answer, backed by photos.

Why Ducts Matter

Your HVAC system moves air through the same ductwork dozens of times a day. When that ductwork is loaded with dust and debris, so is the air your family breathes. Professional cleaning every few years keeps the system efficient and the air noticeably fresher.

How We Work

We show up on time, protect your floors and furniture, clean the system at the source, and walk the results with you before we leave. If something looks off, we tell you. If it does not need cleaning, we tell you that too.

Local to Burlington
