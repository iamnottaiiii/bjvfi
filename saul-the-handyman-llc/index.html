Who we are

Saúl the Handyman L.L.C.

Saúl the Handyman L.L.C. is a handyman business based at 10670 Haddon Ave in Pacoima, California, listed as a contractor with handyman and kitchen and bath specialties. The work is the everyday kind: repairs, refreshes, and small projects that keep a home running.

The philosophy is simple. A home has a hundred small things competing for attention, and most of them take a skilled hour or two, not a renovation crew. One call, one visit, one shorter list.
