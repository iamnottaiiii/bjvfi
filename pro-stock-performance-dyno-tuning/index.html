About the Shop

A performance shop, not a parts counter

Pro Stock Performance & dyno tuning is an independent motorcycle repair shop in Cincinnati, Ohio, built around one big advantage: its own in house motorcycle dyno. The shop handles the full spread, from general maintenance to engine building, dyno tuning to paint and body.

Reviewers describe the crew as knowledgeable, fast, and fair on price, with multiple riders calling it the only shop they will take their bike to. A team member named Kenny comes up by name in customer feedback for knowing motorcycles inside and out.
