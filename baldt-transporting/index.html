OUR STORY

A Glenshaw family in the freight business.

Baldt Transporting is the DBA of Baldt Associates Inc, a Pennsylvania corporation led by president Doreen F. Baldt and based at 1467 Glenn Ave in Glenshaw.

We are a deliberately small carrier: four power units and four drivers running as an authorized for-hire interstate motor carrier. That size is the point. Our customers are not account numbers in a brokerage system; they are the manufacturers, distributors, and local businesses whose freight we have hauled for years.

When you call (412) 486-6385, you reach the people who dispatch the trucks and know the lanes. That is how we have always done it, and it is how we plan to keep doing it.
