Our story

Nearly four decades of keeping freight moving.

Pacific Atlantic Transport Inc earned its property broker authority in 1988, when the Pittsburgh region was rebuilding its industrial identity. We set up on Neville Road in Neville Township, close to the river terminals and the freight corridors that feed western Pennsylvania.

We stayed small on purpose. A compact team means every shipper gets a direct line to someone who knows their lanes, their deadlines, and their freight. That is how long relationships are built in this business: one delivered load at a time.

Today we broker general freight, household goods, building materials, fresh produce, and refrigerated food across the country, with the same promise we started with: honest rates, vetted carriers, and freight that arrives as promised.
