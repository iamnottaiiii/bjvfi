Walk into the office on Route 168 and you will find a practice built the old fashioned way: one doctor, one patient, one careful adjustment at a time. Dr. Michael J. Miranda has been practicing chiropractic since 1982, and his approach reflects that depth of experience. He evaluates the spine and the nervous system, explains what he finds in plain language, and treats what he can help.

Patients come in with the familiar complaints of modern life: the stiff neck from a long commute, the low back that acts up after yard work, the headache that will not quit. Others arrive with extremity problems, a wrist that aches at the keyboard, an ankle that never quite recovered, a shoulder that complains on the ladder. This is the daily work of the practice, and it is done without rush and without pressure.

The office is wheelchair accessible, appointments are recommended, and most questions start with a simple phone call to (856) 374-1200.

Services of the practice

What Dr. Miranda treats, in the practice's own terms, drawn from its published descriptions.

Core service

1.Chiropractic adjustments

Spinal manipulative treatment aimed at restoring motion and easing interference in the nervous system. The foundation of the practice since 1982.

Head and neck

2.Headache and neck pain care

Evaluation and treatment of headaches and neck pain, including tension type complaints and stiffness from posture, work, or injury.

Back

3.Upper and lower back pain care

Care for persistent back pain, from the upper back between the shoulders to the low back and hips, with attention to how the whole spine moves together.

Extremities

4.Carpal tunnel and wrist complaints

Treatment of extremity conditions including carpal tunnel syndrome and related hand and wrist discomfort.

Extremities

5.Foot and ankle conditions

Care for foot and ankle complaints, where alignment and gait can play a larger role than patients expect.

Extremities

6.Shoulder injuries

Treatment of shoulder injuries and related loss of motion, common in work and sport alike.

Injury

7.Whiplash and collision injuries

Evaluation and care after auto collisions and similar injuries, where whiplash and soft tissue complaints are common.

Work and sport

8.Occupational and sports injuries

Care for injuries tied to work and athletics, aimed at returning patients to full activity.

Also treated

9.Sciatica, scoliosis, and more

The practice's published descriptions also name sciatica, scoliosis, fibromyalgia, TMJ disorders, balance difficulties, and digestive complaints as areas where chiropractic care may help.

About Dr. Miranda

A practitioner biography, from the public record.

Dr. Michael J. Miranda, DC graduated from Logan College of Chiropractic in St. Louis, Missouri in 1982. That places him among the most experienced chiropractors in the Turnersville area, with more than four decades of hands on practice behind him.

His practice at 501 Route 168 operates as Future Chiropractic Center, where Dr. Miranda serves as president. The practice is registered in New Jersey under license 38MC00257300, and its national provider record lists NPI 1487812111 for the organization and NPI 1992770622 for Dr. Miranda individually.

He accepts the Medicare approved amount, so Medicare patients are not billed beyond the deductible and coinsurance. The office keeps things simple: call ahead for an appointment, arrive as you are, and get a straight answer about your spine.
