Residential Development

Homes and communities designed for Central Florida living. We look for sites near employment centers, schools, and everyday conveniences, then plan neighborhoods people are proud to call home.

  • Site selection driven by local demand and growth patterns
  • Entitlements, permitting, and county coordination handled in-house
  • Build quality that protects long-term value for owners

Commercial Projects

Retail and mixed-use developments positioned where people already drive, shop, and gather. We build for visibility, access, and tenants who stay.

  • Corridor analysis: traffic counts, demographics, and trade areas
  • Site plans designed around tenant needs and customer flow
  • Delivery to spec, on schedule, ready to lease

Land Acquisition

The right parcel at the right time is where every good project begins. We actively evaluate land across Osceola County and move decisively when the numbers work.

  • Off-market and listed parcels evaluated with local knowledge
  • Due diligence: zoning, utilities, access, and environmental review
  • Straightforward offers and clean, timely closings

Development Partnerships

Own land or capital and want a local developer who knows the ground? We structure partnerships with clear roles, shared goals, and straight communication from day one.

  • Joint ventures with landowners on well-positioned parcels
  • Investor relationships built on transparency and reporting
  • Single point of contact through every phase of the project

How we think

Local ground,
long view

Development rewards patience and punishes guesswork. Our approach is to know the ground first: the corridors, the county plans, the neighborhoods, and the people who will live and work in what we build.

From our Kissimmee base, we evaluate every opportunity against the same standard: does this project make the area stronger, and does the math hold up over decades, not just quarters?

We would rather build one right project than ten rushed ones.

Parkway Center LLC.
