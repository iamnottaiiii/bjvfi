Why call a handyman first

A dripping faucet, a sticking door, a wobbly shelf, a room that needs fresh paint. Small jobs like these do not need a general contractor bid and a three week wait. A handyman visit bundles them into one efficient trip, often at a fraction of the cost and hassle.

How a visit works

1. Call with your list. Walk through the house and note everything that bugs you.

2. Get a clear quote. We look at the jobs and agree on scope before work starts.

3. One visit, many fixes. Repairs get done in a single trip whenever possible.
