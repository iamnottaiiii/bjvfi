Our story

Beauty with heart,
on Van Dorn Street

Rugish Beauty salon is a neighborhood beauty salon in Alexandria, built on personal care, honest advice, and styles that last.

The idea

A chair of your own

Every great salon starts with a simple promise: sit down, be heard, leave beautiful. Rugish Beauty salon was created to be that kind of place for the Van Dorn neighborhood, a salon where every client gets real attention.

The craft

Skilled hands, healthy hair

From protective braids to silk presses to color, the work centers on hair health first. Careful prep, quality products, and techniques that protect your hair while delivering the look you want.

The community

Neighbors, not numbers

Regulars are remembered, new faces are welcomed warmly. It is the kind of salon where appointments feel like catching up, and first-timers become regulars.
