About

The walk-in shop Allentown relies on

"We are a walk-in barber shop serving the Allentown area and beyond!"

Parkway Center Barber Shop sits at 1507 Lehigh St in Allentown, inside the Parkway Center area, and it keeps things simple: skilled barbers, fair service, and a chair that is open when you walk in.

Customers stick with their favorite barbers for years, and new faces are always welcome. The team is known for patience and expertise, especially with children, including kids on the autism spectrum.

It is a classic neighborhood barbershop experience: quick turnaround, honest work, and a friendly atmosphere where everybody leaves looking their best.

Questions about a cut or timing? Call the shop at (610) 797-0851 and ask for the team, they are happy to help.
