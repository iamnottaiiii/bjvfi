Our story

A Glendale contractor with tile in the name

Arttig tile is a general contractor based on Justin Avenue in Glendale, California. The name says it plainly: tile is what we do, and doing one thing well beats doing ten things halfway.

Most of our work is kitchens and bathrooms, the two rooms where sloppy tile shows the most. We take the time to prep surfaces, waterproof what needs waterproofing, and set tile so the lines stay true across the whole room.

No website hype, no pressure tactics. Call us, we show up, we measure, and we give you a straight price. That is the whole pitch.
