Our story

Bold by name, bold by nature

Hot Heads Hair Salon earned its name. We are the Depew salon where stylists have real opinions, real skill, and a real good time doing hair.

Guests come for the talent and stay for the vibe: a fun atmosphere where stylists listen, go above and beyond, and make sure you leave happy. Families have been coming for years, and new guests become regulars fast.

Curly hair? That is our specialty. Our stylists are the ones curly-haired guests across Western New York specifically seek out.
