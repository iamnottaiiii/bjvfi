Our Story

A North Hollywood sheet metal shop

Holland Industries operates from 13215 Leadwell St in North Hollywood, California, serving the San Fernando Valley and the wider Los Angeles area. The shop is listed as a sheet metal work contractor and as a manufacturer of sheet metalwork, with business directories noting capabilities in precision metal fabrication, aerospace work, and CNC-equipped production.

The focus is practical: fabricated sheet assemblies, electronic chassis and cabinets, and certified welding for customers who need parts made right and delivered on time. Prototype work and production runs are handled under the same roof, so a design can move from first article to full quantity without changing hands.

If you are an engineer, contractor, or purchasing manager looking for a local fabrication partner, the fastest path is a direct call to the shop.
