About

A neighborhood dentist on Braddock Road.

Nam Johnny K DDS is a general dental practice located at 9561 Braddock Rd in Fairfax, Virginia.

Dr. Johnny K. Nam is a licensed general dentist in Virginia. A general dentist is the primary dental care provider for patients of all ages, responsible for the diagnosis, treatment, management and overall coordination of services related to oral health needs.

Patients visit for routine exams and cleanings as well as everyday restorative care. If you are new to the area or overdue for a checkup, call the office to introduce yourself and book a first visit.
