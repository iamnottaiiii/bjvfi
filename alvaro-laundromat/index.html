Our story

A neighborhood laundry on Sumner St

Why we are here

Alvaro Laundromat serves the East Boston community with straightforward, self-service laundry. No gimmicks, no fine print: just clean machines, fair prices, and doors open every day of the week.

We are located at 341 Sumner St, a short walk or ride for neighbors across East Boston. Whether it is a quick weekly load or a mountain of family laundry, our space is set up to help you finish faster.
