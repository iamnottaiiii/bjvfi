The shop, in brief

Walk in. Walk out
with something permanent.

Ill Skillz Tattoo Shop has been serving North Philadelphia from 4948 N 5th St, offering walk-in tattoos, cover-ups, piercings, black and grey work, eyebrow tattoos, and microblading. Customers describe attentive, polite, professional service in clean facilities, and reviewers keep mentioning the good vibes from the owners to the staff.

Whether it is your first small piece or a cover-up you have been putting off, the process starts the same way: bring your idea, talk it through with an artist, and get honest guidance on size, placement, and price.
