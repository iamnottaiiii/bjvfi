TEAM HISTORY

Decades in the Starting Lineup

The Shop

Salem Barber Shop has served Cincinnati from 6397 Salem Rd for generations. Listings note the shop established in 1948, family owned since 1962, and reviewers say barbers have been cutting hair there for more than 50 years. The vibe is classic and inviting, with a comfortable waiting room and a staff that makes you feel right at home.

Public listings describe three barbers, each with 30 or more years of experience, known for listening to what customers want and working with them to get the look. Walk ins are welcome, pricing runs below average, and bike parking is available.
