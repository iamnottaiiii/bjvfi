About

A contractor built
for show week

Based in Orlando, one of the busiest convention cities in the country, we exist for the days when everything has to happen at once.

The work behind the show

F & M Convention Contractors is a general contractor serving the trade show and convention industry from 2481 Principal Row in Orlando, Florida. Our crews handle the physical side of exhibiting: booth installation and dismantle, material handling, freight coordination, and on-site labor across the run of an event.

Orlando hosts hundreds of conventions a year, and exhibitors fly in from everywhere. That is who we work for: the out-of-town team that needs a reliable local crew to receive freight, build the booth, and be there when something changes at the last minute.

Public feedback on our work points to the same thing in both of our reviews: attentive, good service. We plan to keep earning it, one show at a time.
