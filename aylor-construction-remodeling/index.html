Our story

A family trade, run like a family business.

Aylor Construction & Remodeling is a family-owned business led by Robert Aylor, who has been in the construction industry since he was 17. His experience across multiple trades and project management shows up in the details: an eye for how a house actually goes together, and how a renovation should fit into it.

We focus on residential clients in Palmdale and the Antelope Valley, helping them get more function and more value out of the homes they already love.
