The neighborhood

Small town feel, fifteen minutes from downtown

Sayler Park is a river neighborhood with green space, sidewalks, and a redeveloping business district. Winding Walks was designed to bring people together in it.

Development

About 70 homes in four phases

Developed by David Sehlhorst of Sehlhorst Equipment Services on just over 26 acres his family purchased off Hillside Avenue. Buyers can build a custom home or choose from plans by JNB Custom Homes, Roell Building and Remodeling, and CW Custom Homes.

Tax savings

A 15 year city tax abatement

The incentive saves homeowners thousands per year on taxes for the first fifteen years, helping buyers qualify for more home or a shorter mortgage.

Green building

LEED certified homes

Lifestyle

At a glance

Why Winding Walks

Built to bring neighbors together

The community plan centers on shared space: walking trails, gathering areas, green space, and sidewalks. Sayler Park itself offers farmers' markets and a small town feel on the west side.

A 15 year tax abatement

All houses are eligible for a 15 year city tax abatement. Published descriptions of the community note the incentive has saved homeowners thousands per year for the first fifteen years on homes starting around $325,000. Confirm current figures and eligibility with the developer before buying.

Custom or planned, your choice

Buyers can build a fully custom home or choose from plans offered by the community's participating builders:

  • JNB Custom Homes
  • Roell Building and Remodeling
  • CW Custom Homes

On Hillside Ave in Sayler Park

The community sits at 6735 Hillside Ave, Cincinnati, OH 45233, about a 15 minute commute to downtown, with larger lots than many previously developed tax abated neighborhoods in the city.

The vision

A community that showcases Sayler Park

Developer David Sehlhorst grew up in Sayler Park and, with his family, set out to build a neighborhood that highlights what makes it special: green space and sidewalks, farmers' markets, and the small town feel.

Gathering

Shared spaces

Gathering spaces and walking trails are woven through the phases so neighbors meet on the path, not just in the driveway.

Outdoors

Green space and sidewalks

Larger lots than many tax abated neighborhoods, with green space throughout and sidewalks connecting it all.

Sayler Park

A redeveloping district nearby

The community sits close to Sayler Park's redeveloping business district, farmers' markets, and the riverfront small town life of the neighborhood.

Your home

Custom homes in Sayler Park

About 70 homes are planned across four phases. Bring your own plan or start from a builder's design.

Options

Custom or builder plans

Work with one of the community's participating builders, JNB Custom Homes, Roell Building and Remodeling, or CW Custom Homes, or bring a custom design. Homes are built for LEED certification and all are eligible for the 15 year city tax abatement.
