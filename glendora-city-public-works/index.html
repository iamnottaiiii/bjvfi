Services

Divisions & services

Select a division to see what Public Works covers. For anything not listed, call (626) 914-8246 and the office will point you the right way.

The streets crew keeps Glendora's roadways safe and drivable, from major corridors to neighborhood streets.

  • Asphalt resurfacing and pavement rehabilitation
  • Pothole and crack repair
  • Street sweeping and lane striping
  • Traffic signal and streetlight maintenance
  • Sidewalk and curb repairs

The water division operates the city's water and sewer systems around the clock.

  • Drinking water production and distribution
  • Water quality testing and reporting
  • Water main and service line repairs
  • Sewer collection system maintenance
  • Water meter services and conservation programs

Trash, recycling, and organics collection are coordinated through the city's contracted hauler and Public Works oversight.

  • Residential and commercial refuse collection
  • Recycling and organics (SB 1383) programs
  • Bulky item and special collection information
  • Household hazardous waste guidance

The engineering division plans capital projects and reviews work in the public right-of-way.

  • Capital improvement project design and construction
  • Encroachment and excavation permits
  • Grading and improvement plan review
  • Traffic studies and safety reviews

Crews maintain the public spaces and buildings the community shares.

  • Park landscaping and irrigation
  • Street median and tree maintenance
  • Public building upkeep and repairs
  • Graffiti abatement on public property

Glendora sits at the foot of the San Gabriels, so storm readiness matters.

  • Storm drain and catch basin cleaning
  • Flood preparedness and sandbag information
  • Stormwater quality and pollution prevention
  • Debris basin coordination
About

The department behind the streets.

What Public Works does

Glendora City Public Works is the municipal department responsible for the city's physical infrastructure: streets and paving, water and sewer systems, storm drains, parks, public buildings, and the capital projects that keep them all in good shape. The department's offices are at Glendora City Hall.

Divisions include engineering, street maintenance, water utilities, environmental services, and parks and facilities maintenance.
