Our story

Nearly Four Decades of Building in Maryland

Pioneer Contracting Inc opened its doors in Glen Burnie in 1988. In the years since, we have watched neighborhoods grow, buildings change hands, and trends come and go. What has not changed is how we work: show up, do it right, and stand behind it.

As a general contractor with deep roots in Anne Arundel County, we have handled everything from careful interior demolition to full home renovations. Our A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau reflects the way we treat clients, with clear scopes, honest pricing, and follow through.

The company is led by owner B. B. Patel, who stays personally involved in the work. When you call Pioneer, you talk to someone who knows your project, not a call center.
