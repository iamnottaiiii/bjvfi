About Our Family Company

A Pittsburgh Family Business

Kelvin D Ferguson & Sons, LLC Moving Company is a family owned mover based at 100 Hafner Ave in Pittsburgh, PA. Kelvin started the business with hauling and moving work, and his son Djuan now works alongside him, leading moves for families and businesses across the region.

The company describes its work with a simple slogan: No Job Too Small. Whether it is a single room or a whole house, the crew shows up every day ready to move families and businesses safely from point A to point B using modern equipment and vehicles.
