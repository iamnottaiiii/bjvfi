Our Story

Rooted in Batavia.

Chapter One

A local nursery, grown over decades

Neff Landscape & Garden Center has served the Batavia and Clermont County community for decades as both a retail garden center and a landscaping company. What started as a local landscaping operation grew into a full-service nursery where homeowners can buy the plants and hire the crew to install them.

Today the center stocks trees, shrubs, annuals, perennials, flowers, mulch, rock, pine straw, topsoil, fertilizer, seed, and lawn chemicals, and the landscaping team handles at-home service and installations across the area.

Chapter Two

Why neighbors keep coming back

Reviewers mention healthy plants at fair prices, friendly and knowledgeable employees, and advice that actually fits the project, from plant recommendations to mulch delivery that shows up when and where it’s needed.
