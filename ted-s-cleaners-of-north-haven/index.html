Our story

Five decades on Whitney Avenue.

Ted's Cleaners has served North Haven and greater New Haven since 1976. Generations of families have trusted the same counter with their Sunday best, their wedding days, and their everyday laundry.

1976

The doors open

Ted's Cleaners starts serving North Haven with old school dry cleaning: inspect everything, pretreat by hand, press with care.

1990s

A full service counter

Alterations and tailoring join the menu, so a suit can be cleaned, fitted, and finished without leaving the building.

2000s

Heirlooms and restoration

Wedding gown preservation and water and smoke damage restoration become specialties, alongside leather, suede, and UGG care.

Today

Still the neighborhood cleaner

Under current ownership, the shop keeps its reputation for fast turnaround, courteous counter service, and garments that come back looking as good as new.

Illustration

A reputation to stand on

The shop's own motto says it plainly: pride in the work, and a reputation built one garment at a time. Regulars mention the polite counter service, the speed, and the way a favorite dress or suit returns looking new again.
