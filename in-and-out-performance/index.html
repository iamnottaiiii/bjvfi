Our Story

Run by people who race what they build

In and Out Performance is an automotive maintenance shop at 14711 Baltimore Ave, Suite 23 in Laurel, Maryland. Since around 2010 it has served the DC metro area with repair, preventive maintenance, and performance installs.

Customers describe the shop in the same words: down to earth mechanics, responsive and straightforward communication, and work done at the agreed price. Steve and Shelby get named by name in reviews for taking care of people and their cars.

The team races sports cars themselves, which is why enthusiasts trust them with installations and customizations. Mustang owners especially, the shop loves late model Mustangs, but the same care goes into any vehicle that rolls in.
