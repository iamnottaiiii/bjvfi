About

Meet the shop

S & E Car Care is a family-owned auto repair and tire shop at 327 East Northfield Road in Livingston, NJ. Mechanic Sergey has been servicing cars for as long as he can remember, and the shop runs on a simple rule: treat everyone like family.

That means honest advice when the car does not need work, clear explanations when it does, and prices that keep neighbors coming back. Foreign and domestic vehicles welcome.
