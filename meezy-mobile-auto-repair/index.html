How it works

Your repair, mapped out like a road trip

From first call to final key turn, here is the route we take on every job. Simple, straight, no detours.

Stop 1

You call or message us

Tell us what your car is doing (or not doing) and where it is parked. We listen, ask a few questions, and get your visit on the route.

Stop 2

We roll your way

Our van carries the tools, parts, and diagnostics to your driveway, office, or parking spot anywhere around Cincinnati.

Stop 3

We check it out

We diagnose the problem right in front of you and explain what we find in plain language, with an upfront price before any wrench turns.

Stop 4

We fix it on the spot

Most jobs are done right there at your curb. You can watch, work from home, or just go about your day.

Finish line

You drive away happy

We test everything, tidy up, and hand your keys back. No tow bill, no ride home from the shop, no waiting room coffee.

About

Meet the van behind Meezy.

Meezy mobile auto repair is a mobile mechanic based on Vine Street in Cincinnati, Ohio. Instead of a waiting room, we have a van. Instead of a shuttle, we bring the repair to you.

Why mobile

Car trouble is stressful enough

The old way means arranging a tow, finding a ride, sitting in a lobby, and hoping the bill matches the quote. We built Meezy around a simpler idea: the mechanic comes to you. You keep your day, we handle the car.

  • No tow truck needed. If your car can be fixed where it sits, we fix it where it sits.
  • Upfront, plain language pricing. We explain the problem and the price before any work starts.
  • Your schedule, not ours. We work around your day, at your home, office, or parking spot.
  • Local to Cincinnati. Based at 8201 Vine St, serving the surrounding neighborhoods.
