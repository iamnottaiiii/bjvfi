Why an endodontist

When a toothache needs a specialist

Endodontists complete additional years of training focused on the dental pulp and root canal treatment. If you have lingering sensitivity to hot or cold, pain when biting, or swelling around a tooth, a referral to an endodontist is often the next step.

The practice welcomes emergency appointments, so sudden or severe tooth pain can be evaluated promptly. Call the office to describe your symptoms and find the soonest available visit.

Services

Endodontic care, all in one place

As an endodontics practice, treatment centers on the dental pulp and the tissues surrounding the roots of the teeth. Below are the kinds of care endodontists commonly provide.

  • 01

    Root Canal Therapy

    Treatment for infected or inflamed dental pulp, designed to relieve pain and save the natural tooth.

  • 02

    Endodontic Retreatment

    Re-evaluation and further treatment for a tooth that has not healed as expected after a prior root canal.

  • 03

    Surgical Endodontics

    Procedures such as apicoectomy for cases where non-surgical treatment is not sufficient.

  • 04

    Cracked and Traumatized Teeth

    Diagnosis and care for cracked teeth and teeth injured by trauma or sports injuries.

  • 05

    Emergency Endodontic Care

    Prompt evaluation for severe toothache, swelling, or dental emergencies. Emergency appointments are accepted.

  • 06

    Referrals and Consultations

    Works with your general dentist on diagnosis and treatment planning for complex cases.

Call the office to confirm which treatments are available at your visit and whether a referral is needed.
