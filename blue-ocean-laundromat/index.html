How a visit goes

In with the pile,
out with it folded

Sort your loads, pick your machines, and let the big commercial washers do the heavy lifting. While they run, grab a coffee or run an errand on Broad St. Come back, dry, fold, and go.

  1. 1
    Sort and load

    Separate lights, darks, and delicates. Big machines handle comforters and bulky loads with room to spare.

  2. 2
    Wash and dry

    Run washers and dryers in parallel. Most visits take about an hour, door to door.

  3. 3
    Fold and go

    Fold it fresh out of the dryer and head home with the whole week handled.

About the shop

A neighborhood laundromat,
right on Broad St

Blue Ocean laundromat serves Staten Island from 211 Broad St, a straightforward, no-fuss place to wash, dry, and fold. The idea is simple: keep the machines in good shape, keep the space clean, and keep the hours long enough for real life.

Open seven days a week, with early weekend starts for the Saturday-morning crowd, the shop fits around work schedules instead of the other way around. Whether it is a weekly routine or a one-time mountain of laundry, the doors are open.
