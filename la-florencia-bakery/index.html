Our Story

A family bakery blooming on Ewing Avenue.

La Florencia, "the flowering," has been part of Chicago's East Side since 2011.

What started as a neighborhood panaderia has grown into a bakery and cake shop that regulars trust with both their daily bread and their biggest celebrations. Every morning the case fills with fresh conchas, butter cookies, eclairs, and sweet breads, and every week custom cakes leave the shop for birthdays and family parties.

Customers describe great service, honest prices, and bread that tastes like it was made for family. One reviewer called our Rosca de Reyes the best around South Chicago and Northwest Indiana. Another praised a tropical themed custom cake as a divine masterpiece. Those are the moments we bake for.

Whether you stop in for a warm concha on your way to work or you call ahead for a cake that will steal the show at the party, you will be met with the same care. This is your neighborhood bakery, and you are always welcome.
