Services

Five areas of care, one convenient office

The practice describes its scope as general, cosmetic, endodontics, orthodontics, and prosthodontics. Here is what that means for you.

01

General Dentistry

Exams, cleanings, fillings, and everyday care that keeps your whole mouth healthy. The foundation everything else builds on.

02

Cosmetic Dentistry

Whitening, veneers, and smile design for a brighter, more confident look. Ask what is possible at a consultation.

03

Endodontics

Root canal therapy that relieves tooth pain and saves natural teeth, handled with a gentle touch.

04

Orthodontics

Options for straighter teeth and better bites at any age. Talk through the approach that fits your life.

05

Prosthodontics

Crowns, bridges, dentures, and implants planning that rebuild full function and a natural appearance.

Treatment plans are always personalized. Call (718) 273-1101 to discuss your goals.
