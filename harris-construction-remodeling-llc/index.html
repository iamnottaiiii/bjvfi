How a project runs

The stitched process

Four steps, sewn in order. You always know which stitch we are on.

  1. 1

    Walkthrough , we listen first

    We visit, look closely, ask questions, and take real measurements. You describe the finished room; we write down everything it will take to get there.

  2. 2

    Plan on paper , measure twice

    Scope, materials, and sequence go into a written plan you approve before work starts. No surprises hiding in the margins.

  3. 3

    The build , cut once

    Demolition through finish work, kept clean and on sequence. One point of contact answers your questions while the sawdust flies.

  4. 4

    Final walkthrough , seated square

    We walk every inch together, check the details against the plan, and only call it done when you would show it off.

Timelines and pricing are set per project, call to confirm
