Chapter 02

Why the name says green

A well-tuned system does more than keep you comfortable. It uses less energy, costs less to run, and puts less strain on the equipment, which means fewer breakdowns and a longer life for your unit. We lean into maintenance and right-sized, efficient equipment because it is simply the smarter way to heat and cool a home.

Regular maintenance, clean filters, sealed ducts, and smart thermostat settings add up to real savings on summer electric bills and a smaller footprint all year.
