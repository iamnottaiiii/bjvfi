Our story

Estética,
every day

ESTÉT takes its name from the idea of beauty as a daily practice. On Avenue U, that means showing up, chair by chair, client by client.

The name

ESTÉT, a promise in five letters

Short for estética, the art of looking and feeling beautiful, ESTÉT is a salon built on the belief that great hair is not a luxury reserved for special occasions. It is a Tuesday. It is a job interview. It is the everyday version of you, at your best.

Tucked into the Gravesend stretch of Avenue U, the salon serves the neighborhood it sits in: regulars who pop in for a trim, brides getting ready down the block, and anyone who has ever walked out of a salon thinking, this is exactly what I asked for.

How we work

Slow down, look closely

Hair is personal, so we treat it that way. Appointments are never rushed through an assembly line. Your stylist takes the time to understand your hair’s texture, history and habits, then builds the cut or color around you, not a template.

Listen first.
Cut second.
