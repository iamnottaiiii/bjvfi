Our story

Building across Southern California since 1994

Redkorp, Inc. started in the San Fernando Valley with a simple idea: run every job like it is your own home. Three decades later that is still how we work.

From our office at 22709 Victory Blvd in West Hills, we take on residential and commercial work across greater Los Angeles, Ventura County and Orange County. New construction, full renovations, additions and A to Z remodeling, all under one roof.

We have pulled 46 building permits since 1995, and directory listings show a five star customer rating average. Our clients come back and send their neighbors, and that is the reputation we protect on every job site.
