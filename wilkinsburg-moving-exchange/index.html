About

Forty one years on the road.

Wilkinsburg Moving Exchange is a moving and storage company headquartered at 900 Middle Ave in Wilmerding, Pennsylvania. The company has held active federal motor carrier authority since 1985, hauling household goods with a small, dedicated driving team.

You may also see the business listed under the name Dorr's Wilkinsburg Moving Exchange in some directories. Both names refer to the same Wilmerding operation at 900 Middle Ave.

For a moving company, longevity is the resume. Four decades of continuous operating authority means thousands of household moves handled the careful way, and a crew that knows the Mon Valley and the greater Pittsburgh area.
