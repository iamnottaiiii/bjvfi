Doc HT-003 · Company background

Built on a global foundation

Holcim is a global leader in innovative and sustainable building solutions, with 60,000 employees on a mission to decarbonize building while improving living standards. Its purpose is to build progress for people and the planet.

In the United States, Holcim operates nearly 350 sites across 43 states with 7,000 people. Customers rely on Holcim US for cement, concrete, aggregates, roofing and low-carbon solutions from ECOPact to ECOPlanet, backed by a net-zero strategy with 1.5 C targets validated by SBTi.

Public details about this specific Cincinnati office are limited to its listing: a building materials and wholesale supply facility at 3270 Southside Ave. For the full company story, visit holcim.us.
