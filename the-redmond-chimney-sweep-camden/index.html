The craft

Old trade.
Modern care.

Chimney sweeping is one of the oldest trades in the book, and the reason for it has never changed: a clear flue burns cleaner, drafts better, and keeps smoke and sparks where they belong.

Whether you burn wood every winter night or light the fireplace twice a year, regular sweeping is the simplest way to protect your home.

Our services

Everything your chimney needs,
nothing it does not

Tap a category to see what is included. Every visit starts with a look and ends with honest advice.

Full chimney sweep

The core of what we do: brushes and vacuums from the crown down to the firebox, clearing the flue of everything that should not be there.

  • Soot and creosote removal
  • Firebox and smoke chamber cleaning
  • Debris, leaves, and nest removal
  • Drop cloths and HEPA vacuum cleanup

Wood stove and insert care

Stoves and inserts work harder than open fireplaces and collect buildup faster. We service the stove, the connector pipe, and the flue.

  • Stove pipe and connector cleaning
  • Baffle and gasket check
  • Burn-efficiency advice for your model

Chimney inspection

A thorough, top-to-bottom look at the condition of your chimney, inside and out, so small issues get caught early.

  • Flue liner condition check
  • Firebox, damper, and smoke chamber review
  • Crown, cap, and exterior masonry look-over

Pre-purchase and seasonal checks

Buying a home with a fireplace, or firing one up for the first time in years. We make sure it is safe before the first match is struck.

  • Home-buyer chimney condition reports
  • Start-of-season safety checks
  • Draft and smoke-spillage diagnosis

Masonry and crown repair

Water and weather wear chimneys down over time. We seal cracks, repoint tired mortar, and restore crowns before small damage spreads.

  • Mortar repointing and brick touch-ups
  • Crown sealing and rebuilds
  • Waterproofing treatments

Caps and dampers

The simplest upgrades with the biggest payoff: a cap keeps rain and animals out, and a working damper keeps heat in.

  • Chimney cap installation and replacement
  • Damper repair and replacement
  • Animal and nest prevention

Dryer vent cleaning

Dryer vents clog with lint and quietly become one of the most common household fire risks. We clear the full run, vent to termination.

  • Full-length lint removal
  • Faster drying, lower energy bills
  • Reduced fire risk

Smoke and CO guidance

A sweep is a good time to make sure the rest of your fire safety setup is in order. We check placement and give practical advice.

  • Detector placement recommendations
  • Safe burning practices for your setup
  • Wood selection and storage tips
