Our story

Twenty five years
in the dirt

Martinez Landscape Co., Inc has operated out of 12357 San Fernando Rd in Sylmar since its CSLB license was issued in 2000.

The company

Built like a contractor,
not a gardener

From the start, Martinez Landscape was set up as a construction company that happens to specialize in landscapes. That is why the license book reads like a general contractor's: landscaping, tree service, concrete, plumbing, earthwork and paving, and masonry.

Over the years the company has grown into public and commercial work, bidding LA County Public Works projects and listing with the Blue Book of construction since 2017. The fleet runs its own trucks and crews out of the San Fernando Road yard.

For property managers, GCs, and agencies, that means one accountable contractor for the whole exterior scope.
