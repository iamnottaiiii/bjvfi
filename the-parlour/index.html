A little salon with a big
reputation on Deereco Rd

The Parlour is a salon and barbershop on Deereco Road where women's cuts, men's cuts and beard shaping, color, corrective color and extensions all happen under one roof.

Walk in off Deereco Road and the first thing you notice is the hum of a working salon: clippers, conversation, the smell of color processing. The Parlour has been at it since 2013, and in that time it has become the kind of place guests plan their calendars around.

The team is a mix of independent stylists and barbers, each with a following. Guests mention blondes done right, without brass or fried ends, beard shaping that actually takes its time, and extension work from stylists who know multiple methods and help you pick the right one.

Good to know. The Parlour is home to Baltimore-area stylists and small business owners, so you book with the artist whose work fits you. Call ahead and ask who is the best match for your hair.

It is a working salon, not a spa palace. Reviews describe it as a hidden gem: adorable, personal, and run by people who treat your hair like it matters, because to them it does.

Our Story

Twelve years of good hair
on Deereco Road

The Parlour opened its doors in 2013. A decade-plus later, it is still a second home to some of Baltimore's most dedicated stylists and small business owners.

In 2013, The Parlour welcomed its first guest. Fast forward and the little salon is a hub: a diverse, talented team of stylists and barbers, many of them independent business owners, sharing one roof and one reputation.

Guests describe a place that feels personal. Stylists who take the time to get to know you, who can improvise when you are not sure what you want, who listen and then tell you honestly what will look good.

From blondes without brass to beard shaping done right, from grey-blend transformations to extension work in multiple methods, the thread through every review is the same: passion for the work, visible in the results.
