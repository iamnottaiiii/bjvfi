About Duley's Service

Duley's Service is located at 11611 Lanham Severn Rd in Bowie, Maryland. Public business records list the company as operating since 1948, making it one of the area's longest-running towing and service station businesses.

The business operates multiple tow trucks and maintains an on-site impound location for towed vehicles.
