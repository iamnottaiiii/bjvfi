Our Story

A Chestnut Street classic

Serving Emmaus since 2010.

The shop

Jonny's opened its doors on Chestnut Street in 2010 and has been the neighborhood's barbershop ever since. Four stations, a lively atmosphere with festive music, and barbers who take pride in every cut. It is the kind of shop where regulars are known by name and first timers leave as regulars.

Directory listings describe Jonny's as a local favorite for quality shaves, trims, and haircuts for men and boys, with an inexpensive price point that keeps the chairs full.
