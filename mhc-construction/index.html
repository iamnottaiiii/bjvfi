Our Story

A Staten Island builder with a mason's eye

MHC CONSTRUCTION works out of Staten Island, NY, serving homeowners across the borough. The work that built our name is the kind you can see from the street: brickwork with clean lines, pavers laid dead level, concrete finished smooth.

Customers mention the same things again and again: friendly crew, clean sites, fast turnarounds, and professional results. One homeowner had basement walls seeping during heavy rain; MHC dug eight feet down around the perimeter and reinforced the exterior walls. Problem solved.
