01. Why Cooling Systems Fail

Heat is the enemy of every engine. A leaking radiator, a stuck thermostat, or a tired water pump can turn a normal commute into a breakdown on the highway. The warning signs are easy to miss: a slowly dropping coolant level, a temperature gauge that creeps higher than usual, or a sweet smell under the hood.

At Eddy's, cooling systems are our specialty. We pressure-test the system, trace leaks to their source, and fix only what is actually failing, so you do not pay for parts you do not need.

02. What We Service

We work on radiators, water pumps, thermostats, radiator hoses, heater cores, and cooling fans, plus coolant flushes that keep the whole system healthy. We regularly service Chevrolet, Ford, Honda, Jeep, Nissan, Toyota, and most other makes.

Beyond cooling, we handle general repairs too, so one visit can take care of the radiator and the brakes, the oil, or whatever else your car needs.
