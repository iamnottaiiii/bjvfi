The Workshop

A Lifetime at the Workbench

Krippendorf Custom Woodworks is the workshop of Heiko Krippendorf, a California licensed cabinet, millwork, and finish carpentry contractor. Since 1989 the shop at 7230 Greeley St in Tujunga has produced one-off pieces built to fit the home, not the other way around.

Every project starts with a conversation about how you live and ends with joinery made to last decades.
