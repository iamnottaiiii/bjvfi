Our Story

The corner barbershop
of Quinsigamond Village.

Pro cuts, no pretense.

Pro Cutz sits on the corner of Whipple St and Blackstone River Rd in Worcester, and it runs on a simple promise printed right on its own page: we take pride in the way you look.

It is a working neighborhood shop. Walk-ins are welcomed, the chairs turn over fast, and the barbers keep up with the latest trends without losing the classic touch. Clients praise Berthony for excellent, detail-driven haircuts and Dee for jumping in on last-minute requests.

The hours tell the story: open seven days a week, late into the evening, because a haircut should fit around your life, not the other way around. Cash App is accepted, walk-ins are welcome, and the value keeps the regulars coming back.

Rated 4.6 stars across 65 reviews, Pro Cutz is the barbershop equivalent of a firm handshake: straightforward, reliable, and sharp.
