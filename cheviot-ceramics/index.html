What we do

Tile services, one bubble at a time

As a tile contractor in the Cincinnati area, our work centers on ceramic and porcelain tile. Service details below are typical of the trade.

Bathroom Tile

Floor and wall tile for full bathrooms, powder rooms, and tub surrounds.

Shower Tile
