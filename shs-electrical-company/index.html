Services

Electrical, end to end.

The core work SHS performs for homes and businesses. If your issue is not listed, call and ask: troubleshooting is the starting point for everything.

Service calls

On site visits for anything electrical that stopped working or never worked right.

  • Dead outlets and switches
  • Breakers that keep tripping
  • Flickering or dead lighting

Troubleshooting

Lighting and wiring issues traced to the source with proper testing, not guesswork.

  • Circuit tracing and fault finding
  • Wiring inspections
  • Load and panel checks

Installation and upgrades

New circuits, fixtures, and equipment installed to code and tested under load.

  • Lighting and ceiling fans
  • Outlets, switches, and dimmers
  • Appliance and equipment circuits

Rewiring

Old, damaged, or undersized wiring replaced so the system is safe and reliable.

  • Partial and full rewires
  • Knob and tube era upgrades
  • Panel to device runs

Code correction

Violations and inspection punch lists resolved for sales, remodels, and rentals.

  • Inspection failure repairs
  • GFCI and AFCI protection
  • Grounding and bonding fixes

Repair and maintenance

Ongoing care that keeps small issues from becoming outages or hazards.

  • Preventive checkups
  • Storm and surge damage repair
  • Commercial maintenance visits
