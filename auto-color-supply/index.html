About the shop

Cincinnati's paint-match counter

Auto Color Supply is an auto body parts supplier located at 15 E Vorhees St in Cincinnati, Ohio 45215. Public directory listings and customer reviews describe it as an automotive paint and refinish supply store, with paint matching as its signature strength.

Reviewers going back several years describe the person behind the counter as exceptionally knowledgeable, the kind of shop owner who takes real time to get a paint match right and explains the process along the way.

More recent public reviews suggest a possible change: reviewers mention the long-time owner stepping away and the building's contents being sold, with some visits finding the store closed. We present this honestly because it matters if you plan to visit. Call first.
