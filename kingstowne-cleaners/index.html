Our story

Kingstowne's neighborhood cleaner

A local dry cleaner in the heart of Kingstowne Town Center, serving the commuters, families and professionals of Alexandria's Kingstowne neighborhood.

Who we are

One counter, one neighborhood

Built for the Town Center routine

Kingstowne Cleaners sits at 5965 Kingstowne Towne Ctr, right where the neighborhood already shops, banks and grabs coffee. The idea is simple: dry cleaning should fit into your errands, not the other way around.

Morning drop offs before the commute, evening pickups on the way home, and Saturday hours for the weekend reset. That rhythm is the whole business.

Garments treated like they matter

The work suit that closes the deal. The dress for Saturday night. The coat that has seen ten winters. Every item is tagged, inspected and finished with the same care, because to you it was never "just a shirt."

Got a stain, a tear or a tricky fabric? Bring it to the counter and ask. Straight answers beat surprises.
