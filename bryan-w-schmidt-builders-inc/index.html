Services

How your build works, step by step

Every project follows the same five steps. Click through the steps below to see what happens at each stage of your build.

Step 1: Consultation

We start with a conversation, not a sales pitch. Tell us what you want to build or change, and we will tell you honestly what it takes.

  • On-site or office meeting at 3210 Milverton Ct
  • Review of your goals, lot, and timeline
  • Straight talk about budget ranges

Step 2: Design and Estimate

Your ideas become drawings and a written estimate. You see the full picture before a single board is ordered.

  • Floor plans and selections guidance
  • Itemized written estimate
  • Material and fixture choices locked in

Step 3: Permits and Prep

We handle the paperwork and the groundwork so the build starts clean and stays on schedule.

  • Permit applications filed for you
  • Site preparation and scheduling of trades
  • Confirmed start date in writing

Step 4: Construction

The build itself, managed by one point of contact from foundation to finishes. You always know what is happening and what is next.

  • New builds, additions, kitchens, and baths
  • Regular progress updates
  • Clean, supervised job sites

Step 5: Walkthrough

We walk every room together before closing. If something is not right, it gets fixed before you move in, not after.

  • Room-by-room final inspection
  • Punch list completed before closing
  • Keys, manuals, and our phone number

About us

Cincinnati built, since 1989

Bryan W Schmidt Builders Inc has been building homes in Greater Cincinnati for 37 years. We started in 1989 as a local corporation, and we have stayed local ever since.
