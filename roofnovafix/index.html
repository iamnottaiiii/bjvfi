Field notes from the crew

A roofer that runs like a dispatch desk

RoofNovaFix treats every call like a work order: logged, scheduled, and closed out clean. Here is how a typical job moves through our hands in Pasadena.

01

The call

You reach us at (956) 489-1374 and describe what you are seeing: a stain on the ceiling, a lifted shingle, a gutter that overflows in every storm. We log it and set a time to look at it in person.

02

The inspection

We walk the roof, check flashing, vents, valleys, and the attic side where possible. You get a plain-language read on what is wrong, what can wait, and what cannot.

03

The fix

Repairs are scoped to the problem, replacements are scoped to the whole system. We clean up daily and close the work order only when you are satisfied with the result.

About

A Pasadena roofing crew, run like a service desk

RoofNovaFix is a roofing contractor based at 1942 E Woodlyn Rd in Pasadena, CA. The idea behind the name is simple: bring the clarity of a work order system to a trade that too often runs on vague promises.

Every job gets logged with a scope, a schedule, and a closeout checklist. You know what is being done, when it is being done, and what it costs before the first ladder goes up.

We serve Pasadena and surrounding San Gabriel Valley neighborhoods with repair, replacement, inspection, and gutter work for residential roofs.
