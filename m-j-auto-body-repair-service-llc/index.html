Why drivers choose M and J

Straight answers and bodywork that disappears

M & J Auto Body Repair Service LLC is the kind of body shop where the estimate comes before the work and the price matches the quote. At 4126 Bergen Turnpike, North Bergen, customers bring everything from scraped bumpers and broken door handles to full collision damage.

How a job runs here

It starts with a careful look at the damage and a plain-language explanation of what it will take to fix. Reviewers praise how Emilio talks through the repairs and costs before starting, so there are no hidden fees and no mystery line items. Approval comes from you, then the work begins.

Fast without cutting corners

Multiple reviewers describe multi-day turnarounds on jobs other shops stretch out, with paint and panel work finished so cleanly the car looks brand new. After an accident they can even handle the tow and the insurance paperwork, which is exactly what you want when the day is already going badly.

Minor mechanical work gets the same treatment: brakes, oil changes and diagnostics from a team that does it all day, at prices reviewers call fair over and over.
