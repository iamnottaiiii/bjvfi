The process

From bare subfloor to sealed grout.

01 Measure and plan

Call to talk through the project. Steve measures, talks tile choices and layout, and maps the pattern so cuts and grout lines fall in the right places.

02 Set with care

Tile is set level and true, with attention to the tricky spots: niches, thresholds, uneven walls. Difficulties get creative solutions, not shortcuts.

03 Grout and seal

Clean grout lines, sealed where it counts, and a walkthrough so you know exactly how to keep it looking new.
