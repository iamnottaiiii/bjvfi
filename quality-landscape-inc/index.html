Our story

A family business, rooted in Revere

Quality Landscape Inc. is a family owned and operated landscaping company based at 42 Hutchinson St in Revere, Massachusetts. For more than 20 years, our crew has kept lawns and yards beautiful for homeowners and businesses alike.

We are a second-generation business, and that shows in how we work: the same care on every visit, honest advice about what your property actually needs, and results that hold up through every New England season.

Our services reach residential and commercial clients throughout Suffolk County, including Boston. From scheduled lawn maintenance to retaining walls, patios, and full landscape design, we can give you the yard you have always wanted.
