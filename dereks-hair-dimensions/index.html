SEC. C

About The Shop

SHOP HISTORY PER PUBLIC REVIEWS AND LISTINGS. UNVERIFIED DETAILS ARE LABELED.

Plan View

Derek's Hair Dimensions is a barber shop at 9272 Cincinnati Columbus Rd, West Chester Township, OH 45241. Public listings also carry it under the categories of barber shop, beauty salon, and spa.

Reviewers describe a no frills, neighborhood shop: normal guys cutting the hair of normal guys, in one customer's words. Multiple reviews mention Derek as the current owner and barber, and note that the previous owner, Denny, retired. One longtime customer reports visiting since the early 1990s.

Public listings mark the shop as inexpensive on price, and reviewers repeatedly mention reasonable pricing and quick, personable service. Walk-ins are listed as welcome.
