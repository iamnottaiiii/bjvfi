Why it matters

Concrete fails when
the prep is rushed

Cracking, settling and spalling almost always trace back to what happened before the pour: the base, the forms, the mix, the cure. A contractor that also runs a materials yard lives in those details every day.

Get the subgrade right, order the right mix, finish it clean, cure it patiently. That is the whole job, and it is what this shop is built around.

Done once

Concrete is forever, or it is a tear-out. We aim for forever.

  • Proper base and compaction before every pour
  • Mixes matched to the load and the finish
  • Clean finishing and patient curing

    One address, two trades

    Standard Concrete Products sits at 8401 Tujunga Ave in Sun Valley, California. The business is listed both as a concrete contractor and a building material store, and that dual role defines it: the same company that pours your slab can supply the materials for your crew.

    That matters on a job site. When the contractor and the yard are the same operation, the mix shows up right, the quantities are honest, and the advice at the counter comes from people who pour.

    The shop serves residential and commercial customers across the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles: homeowners fixing a driveway, contractors running a crew, anyone who needs concrete done properly.
