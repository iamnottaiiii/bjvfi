Our Story

Clean cars, the touch-free way

Clean2o brings touch-free car washing to Cohoes. The idea is simple: a great wash should not need brushes rubbing your paint. High-pressure water and soap do the work, and the car rolls out clean with no swirl marks and no scratches.

Drivers around Cohoes rate the Columbia Street location 3.9 stars across 85 Google reviews. The themes come through again and again: it is economical and efficient, the touchless tech clears mud and dirt from wheel wells and the undercarriage, and the staff steps up with friendly customer service when anything goes wrong.

Clean2o operates multiple locations across the Capital Region, and the Cohoes bay keeps long, driver-friendly hours: 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM, every day. Whether it is salt after a winter storm or dust from a dry summer week, there is a wash waiting whenever you are.
