Our story

A Valley crew that treats concrete like a craft

Big Valley Concrete works out of Northridge, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley. We are the crew neighbors call when a driveway is crumbling, a patio needs to become livable space, or a commercial slab has to be right the first time.

Concrete is unforgiving. Once it sets, every shortcut is permanent. That is why we obsess over the parts you never see: the compacted base, the steel placement, the joint layout, the cure. The finish you walk on is only as good as the groundwork under it.

We keep our operation lean and local. When you call (818) 643-3933, you talk to the people who will actually show up with the forms and the mixer. One crew, one standard: pour it like it is our own driveway.
