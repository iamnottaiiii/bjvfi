Our Story

Bristol's go-to nail salon

Star Nails of Bristol CT is a place where guests say they can truly be pampered. Set on Farmington Avenue, the salon has become a Bristol favorite through skilled work and genuine customer care.

Cleanliness is a point of pride. Reviewers describe sterilized tools, a tidy shop, and high-quality products, the kind of details that turn a first visit into a habit.

The team is the other half of the story. Guests name their favorite techs and book ahead to see them, praising gentle hands, honest advice, and nail art skills that handle everything from classic French to intricate character designs for kids.

Come for a quick touch-up or a full set of something stunning. Either way, expect a warm welcome, a complimentary drink, and nails that get compliments.

What guests love

Clean salon, kind staff, skilled techs, competitive prices, and results that keep people coming back.

Family friendly

Parents and kids are regulars here. The team is patient with young guests and happy to tackle fun designs.
