Three Acts, One Stage

Every visit follows the same crowd pleasing routine: talk it through, draw it up, ink it clean.

Act I: The Consultation

Tell the artist what you want, show a reference, or start from scratch. You will get honest advice on size, placement and what will age well on skin.

Act II: The Design

Your artist draws a custom piece built around your idea. Nothing gets inked until you love the design on paper.

Act III: The Session

Clean station, single use needles, steady hands. Then aftercare guidance so your tattoo heals bold and bright.
