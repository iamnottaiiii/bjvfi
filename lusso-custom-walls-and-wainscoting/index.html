About

Detail-obsessed wall craft

Lusso Custom Walls and Wainscoting is based on Puce Rd in Belle River, Ontario, serving homeowners across Essex County who want more from their walls than flat paint.

The work centers on custom wainscoting and decorative wall treatments: measured, cut, and installed to fit each room, then finished cleanly for a tailored look.
