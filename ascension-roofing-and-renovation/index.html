Your roof is the first line of defense. We treat it that way.

A roof does not fail all at once. It fails one shingle, one flashing, one storm at a time. Ascension Roofing and Renovation exists to catch those small failures before they become big ones, and to rebuild the whole system right when the time comes.

Our story

Named for the climb

Ascension Roofing and Renovation is a roofing contractor based in Alexandria, KY, serving Campbell County and the surrounding Northern Kentucky area. The name reflects the work itself: climbing up, doing it right, and leaving every home better protected than we found it.

A roof is a system, not a surface. Underlayment, flashing, ventilation and shingles all have to work together, or none of them work at all.

Too many roofing jobs fail at the details: flashing reused instead of replaced, vents left choked, valleys cut short. We built Ascension around the opposite instinct. Every replacement gets full tear-off, new flashing, proper ventilation and a documented walkthrough, so the roof performs for its full rated life.

Because the name also says renovation, we finish what roofing starts: gutters, siding, soffit and fascia, so the whole exterior reads as one finished project instead of a patchwork of trades.
