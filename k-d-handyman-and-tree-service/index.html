Our Story

Taught Young, Built to Help

K&D Handyman and Tree Service started in the spring of 2021, but the story starts much earlier. Our founder was taught how to fix things and build as a young kid, and helping others in need was always the passion. Starting this business was simply the best way to turn that passion into a living.

"I was taught how to fix things and build as a young kid. It has always been a passion to help others in need, so what better way than to start my own business."
Early years
Learning the trade. Fixing and building alongside family, picking up the skills that still show up on every job today.
2021
K&D opens its doors. Launched in the spring of 2021 in East Pennsboro Township, serving Enola and the surrounding area with handyman and tree work.
Today
One crew, two trades. From tree takedowns to drywall patches, neighbors call one number and the job gets done right.
