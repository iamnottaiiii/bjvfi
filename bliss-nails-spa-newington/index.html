Our story

A fresh chapter on the Berlin Turnpike

Bliss Nails and Spa sits at 2195 Berlin Turnpike in Newington, CT. The salon is under new ownership and says it plainly: new owner, service upgrade, and more types of manicure and pedicure to choose from.

What guests notice

Clean, friendly, and genuinely relaxing.

Guests describe a neat and pretty space with soothing music, polite staff, and little touches that make a visit feel like a break: hot tea on arrival and a complimentary shoulder massage with select services.

Technicians get personal shout outs in reviews for careful, detailed work and a gentle touch, whether the ask is a simple polish change or a full statement set.

The neighborhood

Right on the Turnpike, easy in and out.

The Berlin Turnpike location puts the salon minutes from central Newington with free lot parking and walk-in availability, which reviewers say makes it an easy pick for a last minute manicure or pedicure.
