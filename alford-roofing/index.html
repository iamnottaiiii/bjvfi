Our story

A Campbell County roofer,
since 1998

Alford Roofing Inc was founded in Melbourne, Kentucky in 1998 by Kurt Alford, and it is still run the way it started: family-owned, owner-supervised, and word-of-mouth driven.

The short version

Kurt L. Alford started Alford Roofing in 1998 and has been on roofs in Campbell County ever since. The company is a Kentucky corporation, based at 166 Truesdell Road in Melbourne.

There is no sales team and no revolving cast of subcontractors. When you hire Alford Roofing, the owner shows up, supervises the work, and answers his own phone. One reviewer put it plainly: supervision on site, 100 percent of the job.

The business runs on referrals. One customer said they would never have found Alford if not by word of mouth, and were glad they did. That is the whole marketing plan, and it has worked for more than 25 years.

How we work

Show up. We come out, look at the roof, and give you a straight answer.

Prove it. Proof of insurance on request, every time. No one should have to ask twice.

Do the work. Kurt supervises the crew himself, start to finish.

Clean up. Driveway swept, magnet run for nails, every single day. Not just the last one.

Stand behind it. If something is not right, call. It gets taken care of.
