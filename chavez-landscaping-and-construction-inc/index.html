About

A Lynn crew for yard and build.

Chavez Landscaping & Construction Inc works out of 33 Laighton St in Lynn, Massachusetts. We are a contractor built around a simple idea: the same crew that cares for your landscape should be able to build on it too.

That means no juggling a landscaper and a mason who have never met. One call covers the lawn, the beds, the patio, the wall, and the walkway, planned together so the finished property feels like one project, not two.

We keep it straightforward: show up when we say we will, do what we said we would do, and leave the site cleaner than we found it.
