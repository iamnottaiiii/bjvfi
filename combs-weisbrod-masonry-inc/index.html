About

A Cincinnati-Area Masonry Contractor

Combs & Weisbrod Masonry Inc is based at 6065 OH-128 in Cleves, Ohio, and works as a masonry specialty contractor across the greater Cincinnati tri-state area.

The company is listed with the Mason Contractors Association of America and on the Procore Construction Network as a masonry specialty contractor. Public county project reports show our crews performing brick and stone veneer installation on the Greene County Gene Fischer Justice Center, working through winter months inside heated tents to hold the construction schedule.

That is the kind of contractor we aim to be on every job: the masonry sub that shows up, lays clean courses, and keeps the project moving.

How we work

The Way We Lay It

1. Review the Drawings

Send plans and specs. We scope the masonry package honestly and price it straight, no padding, no surprises.

2. Mobilize the Crew

Materials staged, scaffolding up, and masons on the wall. We coordinate with your superintendent and other trades.

3. Lay It Clean

Plumb walls, consistent joints, and a finished face you can put your name on. Then we clean up and demobilize on schedule.
