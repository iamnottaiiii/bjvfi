Joinery that holds the frame

Post-and-beam building is all about the joint. These are the classic connections behind the timber-frame tradition, shown as illustrative diagrams.

Illustrative

Mortise & Tenon

The workhorse of timber framing. A shaped tenon locks into a mortise pocket and is pinned with a hardwood peg.

Illustrative

Dovetail Joint

A flared tenon resists pull-out. Traditional carpenters use it where a tie beam must stay locked under load.

Illustrative

Scarf Joint

Two timbers spliced end to end to make a longer beam, pegged so the splice behaves like one solid piece.
