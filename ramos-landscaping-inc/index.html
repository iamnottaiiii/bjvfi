Our services

Built for homes
and businesses

Ramos Landscaping Inc runs two crews: one for maintenance, lawn mowing and pruning, and one for building gardens, planting trees and creating walkways and green walls.

Lawn care and mowing

Routine mowing with professional commercial mowers, plus edging and blowing, so the whole property looks finished.

Pruning and trimming

Shrubs, hedges and ornamentals shaped with proper pruning equipment, timed for healthy regrowth.

Landscape design

Designs for new plantings and full yard refreshes, planned around sun, soil and how you actually use the space.

Garden builds and planting

New garden beds, tree planting and green walls, installed by the construction crew with the right gear for the job.

Walkways and hardscaping

Walkways and outdoor surfaces built to handle New England freeze and thaw, with clean lines and solid bases.

Spring and fall cleanup

Seasonal cleanups that reset the property: leaves, branches, bed edging and fresh mulch where it counts.

Snow plowing

Winter plowing service for driveways and lots, so the same crew that cares for your yard keeps it clear.

Commercial maintenance

Scheduled upkeep for offices, retail and multi unit properties, with the trucks and trailers to scale.
