Our Story

A barbershop with a point of view.

The name says it all. Unique Barber Shop set out to do one thing differently: treat every head of hair like it matters.

01

Old school values, modern cuts.

Walk into our shop on Ridge Road and you will notice the difference right away. The chairs are classic, the conversation is easy, and nobody is watching the clock. We believe a haircut is a small ritual worth doing well.

From timeless side parts and pompadours to skin fades and textured crops, our barbers keep up with what is current while respecting what is classic. Bring a photo, describe a feeling, or just say "make it look good." We will take it from there.

Lyndhurst is our neighborhood. Many of our clients live a few blocks away, and plenty drive in from Rutherford, North Arlington and beyond. First visit or fiftieth, you get the same care.
