Entry No. 03 : About the Shop

A Sharonville repair shop, on the record.

Brown's NAPA Auto Care, Inc. is an auto repair shop at 11444 Reading Rd in Sharonville, Ohio, serving drivers in the northern Cincinnati area.

The shop appears in the NAPA AutoCare directory of independent auto care centers. NAPA AutoCare centers are locally owned shops; this site is a demo concept for the Sharonville shop itself, not for NAPA corporate.

Public reviews paint a consistent picture: an honest diagnosis, an honest estimate, and work done on time. Multiple reviewers single out Ken, the mechanic, for straight talk and quality repairs ranging from routine maintenance to engine replacement.
