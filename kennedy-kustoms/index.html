/// About the shop

Kennedy Kustoms is Des Moines' home for vehicle customization. The shop builds custom LED light kits for motorcycles and autos, sells custom wheels and tires, and fabricates custom parts.

The business is also a certified Intoxalock ignition interlock location, offering installation, calibration, and monthly service from certified technicians. Reviewers praise the professional, friendly crew and the clean, welcoming shop.
