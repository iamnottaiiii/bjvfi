Our story

Why "stressfree" is in the name

Most dental practices treat teeth. We set out to treat the whole experience, because we believe nobody should dread the dentist.

I

A different kind of dental office

Advanced Stressfree Dentistry was founded on a simple observation: a huge number of people avoid the dentist not because of cost or convenience, but because of stress. The sounds, the pace, the pressure. So we built the practice in reverse, starting from the question: what would make a nervous patient feel safe?

The answer shapes everything here, from unhurried appointment times to the way treatment is explained before it ever begins. No lectures, no judgment about how long it has been since your last visit. Just a fresh start, on your terms.

II

Advanced, but never cold

"Advanced" means modern tools and current techniques. "Stressfree" means we never let technology get in the way of being human. You will always know what is happening, why it matters, and what it will feel like.

III

Right here in Burlington

We are part of the Cambridge Street community, serving Burlington and the surrounding towns from our office at 281 Cambridge St, Suite 5. Easy to reach, easy parking, and a team that treats neighbors like neighbors.

IV

Our promise

Explain first. Rush never. Pressure never. Whether it is your first cleaning in years or a full smile restoration, you will be heard, respected, and cared for. That is what stressfree means to us.
