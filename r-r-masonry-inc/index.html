Chapter One · The Company

Six decades of brick, block, and stone

From university campuses to civic landmarks, R & R Masonry has spent generations building the masonry backbone of Southern California. Their portfolio includes UCLA and USC facilities, UCI buildings, Irvine City Hall, the Cal Poly Performing Arts Center, Biltmore Tower, the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library, Disney projects, and the Walt Disney Concert Hall.

Structural brick and block for schools, universities, government buildings, offices, and large commercial projects. The core of the business since 1963.

Custom stonework installation, decorative wall construction, stone restoration, and glass block installation for projects that need a finish as good as the structure.

Brick repair, bricklaying, and brickwork services that match existing masonry, from small repairs to chimney construction.

Services

What R & R Masonry does

Service areas below combine the company's stated commercial focus with specialties listed on public contractor directories. Call to confirm scope for your project.

Structural masonry for commercial, industrial, and institutional projects. This is the company's primary line of work, per their own statement of 55+ years of quality masonry on complex commercial projects.

Repair and new installation of brick walls, facades, and architectural brick details. Matching existing brick for seamless repairs.

Custom stonework installation, decorative wall construction, and stone walls for projects where the finish matters as much as the structure.

Restoration of aging stonework on established buildings, preserving the original character while renewing structural integrity.

Glass block walls and windows for light, privacy, and architectural detail in commercial and residential settings.

Brick and masonry chimney construction and rebuilding, built to code for safety and long service life.

Services above are based on the company's website and public contractor directories. Confirm details when you call.
