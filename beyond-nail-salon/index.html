About

The story

Beyond Nail Salon keeps things simple and does them well. The room is calm, the stations are immaculate, and the technicians take their time, no rushing, no conveyor belt.

Ask about the early-bird special: a manicure and pedicure for just $25 Monday through Thursday before 3 PM. It is the kind of deal that turns first-timers into regulars.

From everyday polish to special-occasion sets, the team works in gel, acrylic, dip and hybrid systems, and they will talk you through which one suits your nails and your week.
