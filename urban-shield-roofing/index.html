About the company

A roofing contractor rooted in Tujunga

Urban Shield Roofing is a roofing contractor based at 6915 Greeley St in Tujunga, California, serving homeowners across the foothill communities. The name reflects the job: your roof is the shield between your home and the weather, and keeping that shield strong is the whole business.
