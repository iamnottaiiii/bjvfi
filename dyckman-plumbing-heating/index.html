Services

Plumbing and heating, done right.

Pick a category to see what the Dyckman crew handles. Final pricing is confirmed on site before work starts.

Leak Repair

Fast diagnosis and repair for leaking pipes, joints, and supply lines in kitchens, baths, and basements.

Pipe Replacement

Partial or full pipe replacement for corroded, frozen, or burst lines in older Manhattan buildings.

Fixture Installation

Faucets, toilets, sinks, and shower fixtures installed cleanly and tested before we leave.

Toilet Service

Repairs and replacements for running, clogged, or rocking toilets, including wax ring and flange work.

Drain Cleaning

Clearing slow and blocked drains in sinks, tubs, showers, and floor drains throughout your home.

Sewer Line Work

Cleaning and repair for main sewer lines, including roots and buildup that back up the whole building.

Grease Line Clearing

Kitchen line clearing for restaurants and food prep spaces with heavy grease use.

Drain Camera Inspection

Video inspection to find the exact cause of repeat clogs before digging or cutting anything.

Water Heater Installation

Tank and tankless water heater installation sized to your household and your space.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, strange noises, or leaks? We diagnose and repair before recommending a replacement.

Tankless Service

Descaling and maintenance that keeps tankless units efficient and extends their life.

Safety & Code Checks

Venting, pressure relief, and expansion tank checks to keep your system safe and up to code.

Boiler Service

Repair and maintenance for steam and hot water boilers common in prewar Manhattan buildings.

Radiator Work

Noisy, cold, or leaking radiators diagnosed and bled, repaired, or replaced as needed.

Heating Tune-Ups

Seasonal checkups that catch small issues before the first cold snap turns them into emergencies.

Thermostat & Controls

Replacement and setup of thermostats and heating controls for steadier, more even heat.
