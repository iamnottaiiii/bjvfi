Our story

The Avenue O barber

Sahak Style Salon is a family style barber shop at 10 Avenue O in Brooklyn's Bensonhurst edge. It was built the old fashioned way: one satisfied client telling the next.

The shop serves a loyal, multigenerational clientele. Fathers bring their sons. Neighbors stop in on their way home. The atmosphere is relaxed, the conversation is easy, and the work is consistently sharp.

Clients describe unbeatable quality for the price and a barber who remembers exactly how you like it.

What we stand for

Consistency

The same sharp result every visit. No surprises, just a cut you can count on.

Value

Top tier barbering at a fair neighborhood price. Clients say it cannot be beat.

Community
