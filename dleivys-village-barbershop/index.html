Our story

A village shop in the heart of Worcester

Dleivys VIllage Barbershop sits on Greenwood Street in Worcester, and it carries the village in its name on purpose. This is a neighborhood shop: regulars by name, kids who grow up in the chair, and conversations that run as long as the clippers.

The barbers are known for being friendly, charismatic, and genuinely skilled, handling everything from modern fades to classic cuts across all ages. Many clients say their children will not trust anyone else with their hair.

Come in for the cut. Stay for the atmosphere. Leave looking sharp.
