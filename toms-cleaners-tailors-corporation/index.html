31Years of Service

Three decades on Avenue S

Tom's Cleaners & Tailors Corporation has served Brooklyn for 31 years. That kind of longevity comes from doing the small things right: checking every pocket, matching every button, and remembering how you like your shirts.

Combining dry cleaning with in-house tailoring means your alterations are done by people who understand fabric, not outsourced to strangers. Suits come back sharper. Dresses fit the way they should. And it all happens at one counter on Avenue S.
