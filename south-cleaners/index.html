Services

Full-service garment care under one roof.

South Cleaners handles the pieces you wear every day and the ones you save for special occasions. Every order is inspected on drop-off and checked again before pickup.

01

Dry Cleaning

Professional dry cleaning for suits, blazers, dresses, skirts, and outerwear. Stains are pre-treated, garments are cleaned in quality solvents, and everything comes back finished and pressed.
02

Laundered Shirts

Dress shirts and blouses washed, starched to your preference, and hand-finished. Choose folded or on hanger at pickup.
03

Wash and Fold

Drop off your everyday laundry and pick it up washed, dried, and neatly folded. A simple way to get a week of laundry off your list.
04

Pressing and Finishing

Expert pressing for trousers, pleated skirts, silk blouses, and linen. Sharp creases, smooth collars, no shine marks.
05

Household Items

Comforters, duvets, blankets, bedspreads, curtains, and table linens. Bulky pieces get the same careful cleaning and thorough drying.
06

Stain and Spot Care

Bring in stained garments and point out the spots at drop-off. Pre-treatment gives the best chance of lifting them before cleaning.

Pricing depends on garment, fabric, and finish. Call (215) 923-2177 for a quote on your items.

Our story

A South Street staple for clean clothes.

South Cleaners sits on South Street in Philadelphia's Bella Vista neighborhood, one of the most walkable corridors in the city. Neighbors drop off their suits, shirts, dresses, and everyday laundry here and pick them up cleaned, pressed, and ready to wear.

The shop keeps it simple: careful cleaning, honest service, and garments returned the way you would want to wear them. Regulars know the staff by face, and the 4.6-star rating across 18 Google reviews reflects a steady record of getting it right.

Clean clothes, pressed right, ready on time. That is the whole philosophy.
