ABOUT AIM

Aim Construction Inc is a 3rd generation construction company with over 35 years of experience working in the construction industry, serving the Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky areas.

The pitch is simple: when you need an experienced handyman who can handle just about any construction project, call Aim. They can install, repair, or build anything from the foundation of the house to the roof.

They work with homeowners and investment property owners, doing repair, remodeling, painting, and roofing. The promise is high quality work and personal attention at a price that is fair for the pocketbook.

Serving: Northern Kentucky and the Greater Cincinnati area. Based at 313 E 3rd St, Melbourne, KY 41059.
