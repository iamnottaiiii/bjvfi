Our Story

A supply yard built by people who have carried the bundles

NovaEdge Contractors started with a simple observation: roofers in the San Fernando Valley were driving too far for basic material. Big-box aisles do not stock a contractor's list, and the big distributors do not answer the phone on a Friday afternoon.

So we opened a focused roofing supply store on Lemarsh Street in Mission Hills: one building, the products roofers actually buy, and a counter that answers on the first ring.

  • Independent, locally run supply yard
  • Stock chosen around Valley roofers' daily needs
  • Contractor pricing with no membership cards
  • Delivery trucks staged the same day you call
