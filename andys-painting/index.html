what we do

Painting, done properly

Interior, exterior, and all the careful prep in between. Every job starts with a free estimate and straight answers.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, and doors. Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, whole-house refreshes. We keep you educated on your project, so you know exactly what is getting painted and how.

Sample pricing: rooms from $350

Exterior painting

Siding, brick, stucco, porches, and shutters. Curb appeal that survives Pennsylvania weather, with the prep work that keeps it sticking for years.

Sample pricing: exteriors from $2,500

Drywall repair and prep

Patching, crack repair, and wallpaper removal before a single drop of paint goes on. A smooth wall is half the finish.

Sample pricing: patch jobs from $120

Trim, doors, and detail work

Crisp baseboards, window casings, crown molding, and doors with a furniture-smooth finish. The details are where quality shows.

Sample pricing: trim packages from $200
