The Shop

A cozy corner of Justice

Sweet House Bakery sits at 8405 S Roberts Rd in Justice, Illinois, a homey little bakery with warm decor, a welcoming counter, and the smell of something good always in the air.

The Craft

Cakes and tables, done with pride

The team takes visible pride in the work: red velvet cake like butter, strawberry cake bursting with flavor, cannoli, cupcakes, and banana pudding guests drive across town for. Nothing tastes heavy or overly sweet, just right.

The Events

Chicagoland's dessert-table secret

Word spread fast. Bridal showers, baby showers, weddings, graduations, engagements. Families book Sweet House for every milestone, then come back for the next one. Cake stand rentals, tastings, and on-time delivery make the big day easy.

The People

Service guests rave about

Review after review mentions the same thing: knowledgeable, patient, professional staff who calm wedding-week nerves and go the extra mile. Walk in with an idea, walk out with a plan.
