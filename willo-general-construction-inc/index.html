Our story

Family owned. Job site raised.

Willo General Construction Inc. is a family owned construction business founded in Chatsworth in 2011, built on work ethic and commitment to clients.

Who we are

Willo is a family oriented business that aims to work in a timely manner and give clients the finest quality and satisfaction. That is not a slogan on our trucks, it is how the company describes itself, and how it has operated since 2011.

Over the years the company has grown from residential remodels into commercial work and licensed asbestos abatement, while staying headquartered at 9621 Canoga Ave in Chatsworth.

Licensed and accountable

Willo holds California contractor licenses including General Building (CSLB 946732), plus electrical, plumbing, and cabinet, millwork and finish carpentry classifications.

The company carries a $25,000 contractor bond and workers compensation coverage, and is registered with Cal/OSHA for asbestos work. BuildZoom ranks Willo in the top 8% of California licensed contractors, with 345 building permits pulled since 2011.

A note on licenses: license numbers and registrations shown here come from public records (CSLB, Cal/OSHA, BuildZoom). Always verify current license status with the Contractors State License Board before hiring any contractor.
