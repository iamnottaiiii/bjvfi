Our Story

22 years of roofing and remodeling, built on a simple promise: give our absolute best in all the work we perform.

Jessup Roots, Maryland Work Ethic

T.N.T Home Improvements serves Jessup, Odenton, and nearby Maryland communities from its home base on Anderson Lane. The company describes itself plainly: a team that prides itself on reliability, communication, integrity, and quality work. That is not a slogan on the truck. It shows up in review after review, where customers mention punctual crews, clean job sites, and projects finished on time and on budget.

Roofing is the heart of the business. New roofing system installations, asphalt shingle work, and storm damage repair headline the services, supported by deck restoration, kitchen remodeling, and exterior sealing. With 22 years of experience behind it, the team educates homeowners about the work process as it goes, so every decision is an informed one.
