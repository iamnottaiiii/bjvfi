Our Story

A Wallingford contractor built on the estimate, not the upsell.

Connecticut CASE Handyman & Remodeling (Case Handyman and Remodeling Services, LLC) is a handyman and remodeling contractor based at 40 N Plains Industrial Rd in Wallingford, Connecticut. The business handles the full spread of home work: everyday handyman repairs, vinyl siding, exterior fixes, and remodeling projects.

The way the company works is the story. One reviewer describes the process in detail: a representative walked the entire house explaining what had to be done, laid out the differences between materials, colors, and styles, delivered a detailed written estimate, then came back to explain it again before any work started. Repairs for squirrel damage and an air conditioner support were folded into the same job, and everything was completed in a timely fashion.

No pressure tactics, no vague numbers. Just a walkthrough, a written estimate, and the work as promised. That is how CASE earns its 5.0 star rating, and how it plans to keep it.

The CASE approach

  • Walk it: every project starts with a full walkthrough
  • Explain it: materials, colors, and styles laid out plainly
  • Write it: a detailed estimate, reviewed with you in person
  • Do it: work completed in a timely fashion, as estimated
