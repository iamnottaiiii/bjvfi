What we do

Full service heating and cooling

From a strange noise to a full system replacement, one call covers it.

AC repair

Warm air, weak airflow, odd noises, leaks, or a unit that will not start. We track down the cause and get cold air moving again.

Heating repair

New installation

Ductwork

Indoor air quality

Filtration upgrades, humidity control, and ventilation improvements for cleaner, healthier air inside your home.

Services

Heating and cooling, covered end to end

Repair, installation, and maintenance for residential and light commercial systems across Van Nuys and the San Fernando Valley.

Air conditioning repair

Diagnosis and repair for central air, ductless mini-splits, and heat pumps: compressors, capacitors, contactors, refrigerant issues, frozen coils, and drainage problems.

Heating repair

Furnace and heat pump service: ignitors, flame sensors, blower motors, thermostats, and gas components checked and repaired safely.

System replacement

When repair stops making sense, we size and install efficient replacement equipment, handle the old unit, and commission the new one properly.

Preventive maintenance

Seasonal tune-ups: clean coils, check refrigerant charge, test controls and safety devices, tighten electrical, and replace filters.

Duct repair and sealing

Duct inspection with sealing and repair of leaks, disconnections, and crushed runs so conditioned air reaches every room.

Thermostats and controls

Thermostat replacement and calibration, including smart thermostats, plus zoning evaluations for multi-level comfort.

Our story

Why we do this work, and how we do it differently.

Who we are

Local techs, not a call center

Superior HVAC Repair Van Nuys is based at 6239 Halbrent Ave in Van Nuys, right in the middle of the neighborhoods we serve. When you call, you talk to people who know the Valley's homes: the older ductwork in postwar ranch houses, the rooftop package units on small commercial buildings, and what a 100-degree September does to an aging condenser.

We started this shop on a simple idea: most HVAC frustration is not about the equipment, it is about the experience. Unclear pricing, upsells, techs who will not explain. So we built the visit around the opposite: listen first, explain plainly, fix what is actually broken, and prove the fix before we leave.
