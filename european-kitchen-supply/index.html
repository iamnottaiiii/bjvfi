Services

Kitchen remodeling, start to finish.

As a kitchen remodeler serving Burbank and surrounding areas, we handle the pieces that make a kitchen work: layout, cabinetry, surfaces, and the details that tie it together.

01

Full Kitchen Remodels

Complete renovations managed in one place, from the first design sketch to the finished room. Layout changes, new cabinetry, countertops, and fixture coordination.

European Cabinetry

Our Story

A Burbank showroom built around European kitchen design.

European Kitchen Supply is a kitchen remodeler located on South Victory Boulevard in Burbank, California. Our focus is straightforward: bring clean, functional European kitchen design to local homeowners.
