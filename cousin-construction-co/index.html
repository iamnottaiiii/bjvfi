About

A Cincinnati contractor
worth knowing

Cousin Construction Co is a general contractor based at 2930 Highland Avenue in Cincinnati, Ohio, certified by the City of Cincinnati as a Women-Owned Business Enterprise and a Small Business Enterprise.

Built on accountability,
not just concrete

A general contractor's real product is not the building. It is the certainty that the building gets finished right, on time and on budget. That is the job we signed up for.

We focus on the markets where that certainty matters most: commercial and industrial work where downtime costs money, health care work where lives depend on the details, and institutional work where the public is watching.

Our design/build capability means owners can hand us a goal instead of a stack of drawings, and get a finished building back, with one team answerable for all of it.
