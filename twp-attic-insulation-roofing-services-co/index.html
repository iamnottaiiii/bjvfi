Why the top half matters

Most heat escapes through the attic. Most leaks start at the roof.

Insulation and roofing are usually sold by two different companies. TWP was built around a simple idea: treat them as one job, and the house stays drier, warmer in winter, and cooler in summer.

The TWP approach

One inspection, roof to attic floor

When we look at a home, we check the shingles and flashing up top and the insulation depth, air leaks, and ventilation down below. A new roof over a leaky attic still wastes energy. Fresh insulation under a failing roof still gets wet. We look at both before we quote anything.

Lower energy bills

Proper attic insulation and air sealing slow the heat escaping in winter and creeping in during summer, so your furnace and AC run less.

Dry, quiet, comfortable

A sealed attic means fewer drafts, less moisture feeding mold, and a noticeably quieter house when it rains or the wind picks up.

Blown-in insulation

Loose-fill cellulose or fiberglass blown over the attic floor to fill gaps around joists, wiring, and recessed lights. A common fix for older west-side homes with thin original insulation.

Typical for this trade

Fiberglass batt insulation

Pre-cut batts fitted between joists and studs in attics, knee walls, and bonus rooms. A straightforward option for accessible, even spaces.

Typical for this trade

Spray foam insulation

Closed or open-cell foam that seals and insulates in one pass, especially useful at roof decks, rim joists, and hard-to-reach attic corners.

Typical for this trade

Attic air sealing

Sealing the gaps, chases, and penetrations where conditioned air leaks into the attic. Often the cheapest comfort gain in the whole house.

Typical for this trade

Radiant barrier

Reflective barrier installed under the roof deck to bounce summer heat away before it reaches the attic floor.

Typical for this trade

Ventilation check

Soffit, ridge, and gable ventilation reviewed together with the insulation, since insulation without airflow can trap moisture.

Typical for this trade

Roof inspections

A close look at shingles, flashing, valleys, and penetrations, with photos and a plain-language read on remaining roof life.

Typical for this trade

Shingle repair

Replacing wind-lifted or missing shingles, resealing flashing, and stopping small leaks before they reach the attic insulation below.

Typical for this trade

Roof replacement

Full tear-off and replacement with new underlayment and shingles when a roof is past patching.

Typical for this trade

Ridge vents

Continuous ridge ventilation that lets hot, moist air escape the attic while the new insulation stays dry.

Typical for this trade

Soffit and fascia

Repair or replacement of the eave boards and vents that protect the roof edge and feed attic airflow.

Typical for this trade

Storm damage review

Post-storm walkthroughs documenting hail and wind damage, so you know whether to call your insurer.

Our story

The top half of your home deserves its own crew

A cross-section of the idea

Roofing keeps the weather out. Insulation keeps the comfort in. TWP treats them as one system instead of two separate jobs.

Based at 460 Wilke Dr in Delhi Township, serving the west side of Cincinnati and surrounding Hamilton County neighborhoods.

Walk into most Cincinnati homes built before 1990 and climb into the attic, and you will find the same story: a few inches of tired insulation, daylight peeking around a pipe chase, and shingles overhead that have seen better decades. The furnace runs, the bills climb, and nobody connects the two halves of the problem.

TWP Attic Insulation & Roofing Services Co was started to close that gap. Instead of hiring an insulation crew that never looks at your shingles, or a roofer who never checks your attic floor, you get one company that does both and plans them together.

Insulation without a sound roof gets wet. A new roof over a leaky attic still wastes energy. Do both, in the right order, once.

We are a newer listing, so our public story is still being written. What we can promise is simple: we show up, we look at the whole top half of your home, and we tell you straight what it needs.
