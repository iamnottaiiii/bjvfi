Full Bathroom Remodels

A complete transformation, from layout to lighting. We take tired, dated, or poorly laid out bathrooms and rebuild them into spaces that work beautifully every day.

  • Complete bathroom tear-outs and rebuilds
  • Layout improvements for better flow
  • New plumbing fixtures and fittings
  • Lighting, ventilation, and electrical updates
  • Finishing touches down to the last detail

Our story

A remodeler with a simple promise.

On Time Builders specializes in bathroom remodeling using 3D design rendering. We started with a straightforward belief: nobody should have to imagine their new bathroom and hope for the best.

So we built our process around showing instead of telling. You see your bathroom in 3D, you approve it, and then we build exactly that. It is a small idea that changes everything about how a remodel feels.

We are dedicated to providing the highest quality workmanship and customer service on every project. Based in Woodland Hills, California, we serve homeowners across the surrounding area.
