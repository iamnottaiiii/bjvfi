Our Story

A beauty room built on relationships

I.G Glam Room is a beauty and aesthetic center on the second floor of 1510 Avenue M in Midwood, Brooklyn. The salon describes itself simply: a place where results matter.

Instead of sending clients to four different appointments across the city, the Glam Room brings nails, facials, laser hair removal and hair services together in one warm, professional space. Reviewers note the strong team relationships and the sense of care and pampering that runs through each visit.

What to expect

Appointments are recommended to ensure availability. Plan to spend at least an hour for most treatments.

The salon accepts credit cards and cash. Call ahead and the team will help you choose the right service.
