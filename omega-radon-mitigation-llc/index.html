Mold Mitigation

Damp basements and crawl spaces often bring radon and mold together. Mold mitigation service to address both problems at the source.

Why radon matters in Ohio

Radon is a colorless, odorless gas that forms naturally in soil and rock. Ohio sits on geology that puts many homes at risk, and you cannot smell it or see it. The only way to know your level is to test.

Services

Radon and mold services

Straightforward, focused work. Testing tells you where you stand, mitigation fixes it, and mold service rounds out the indoor air picture.

Sample detail

Radon Testing

We place calibrated test devices in the lowest livable level of your home, retrieve them after the testing window, and report your reading with plain-English next steps. Call for scheduling.

Sample detail

Radon Mitigation Systems

Sub-slab depressurization is the standard fix: a small, quiet fan draws radon-laden soil gas out from beneath the slab and exhausts it above the roof. We size the system to your foundation.

Sample detail

Mold Mitigation

Moisture that feeds mold often shows up in the same basements and crawl spaces where radon collects. Mold remediation to help dry out and clean up problem areas.

How a typical job goes

Every home is different, but most projects follow the same path.

1. Test

Measure your current radon level so the plan matches the problem.

2. Design

We look at your foundation type, slab, and sump layout, then design the right system.

3. Install and verify

Install the mitigation system, then retest to confirm levels dropped.
