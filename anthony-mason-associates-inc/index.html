The approach

A method born on a real job site

In 1984, on the Four Seasons Hotel development in Los Angeles, founding principal Anthony Mason created the Intent of Design IGMP project delivery method as an alternative to design-bid-build, after the traditional approach produced disagreements among the project team.

The method became the subject of a Harvard Graduate School of Design publication in 2009 on the LMU William H. Hannon Library, and was renamed by Harvard GSD as the Integrated Project Management Approach (IPMA) to project delivery.

Services

Project management,
done from the owner's side

AMA is an independent consultancy: no design fees, no construction markups, no divided loyalties. The firm represents the client's interests through every phase of a project.

01

Owner's representation

Acting as the client's in-house project team: managing architects, engineers, general contractors and consultants; controlling budgets, schedules and quality; and reporting to ownership with one clear voice.

02

Project planning and controls

Feasibility, budgeting, scheduling, procurement strategy and risk management, established before the first drawing is issued and maintained through closeout.

03

Intent of Design IGMP delivery

The firm's proprietary Initial Guaranteed Maximum Price method: a holistic delivery approach created in 1984, used on approximately 130 projects, and studied and published by Harvard GSD in 2009.

04

Master planning and entitlements

Campus and site master plans, entitlement strategy and development advisory, from studio lots to university campuses to resort destinations.

05

Construction administration

Submittals, change management, field coordination and commissioning oversight, keeping complex builds on budget and on schedule through completion.

06

Capital project consulting

Advisory for institutions, non-profits and public agencies on major capital expenditures, including UN consulting work in Malawi, Central Africa.
