About

Two decades in the woods.

Harrington Hardwood has met firewood, land clearing, and logging needs across Massachusetts and Northern New England for twenty years, operating from Jefferson, MA since 1997.

Our story

Small crew, honest wood.

Harrington Hardwood is a small, family-run operation. We cut our own timber, season our own firewood, and stand behind every cord we sell. Landowners call us for clearing and logging because we treat their land like it matters: clean landings, careful work, and no mess left behind.

We are also certified under the Massachusetts Asian Longhorned Beetle compliance program for the Worcester area, so regulated wood moves legally and safely.
