Our story

Greater Cincinnati's trusted water and wastewater contractor since 1958.

What started in 1958 as Winston Electric Company has grown into Winelco Inc. Environmental Contractors, a full service water and wastewater treatment contractor, manufacturer, and service company.

Today Winelco serves residential, commercial, and municipal customers across Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana. The company designs, builds, and maintains treatment facilities, installs and services sewer and water lines, operates plants under contract, and runs a certified EPA laboratory for permit testing.

For homeowners, Winelco has offered preventive maintenance service agreements on aerobic wastewater systems since 1958, providing security and convenience against health department compliance issues, backups, and odors.
