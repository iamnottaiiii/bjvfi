Our story

A Valley Glen company with a simple standard

G and K Construction is based on Whitsett Ave in Valley Glen, working on homes across the San Fernando Valley.

How we work

Listen first, plan clearly, build carefully

Most remodeling stress comes from surprises: unclear scope, shifting schedules, and nobody answering the phone. We run our jobs to avoid all three.

It starts with a walkthrough where we ask questions and listen. Then you get a written plan that spells out the work in plain language. During the build, you have one person to call, and the site gets cleaned up every day so your home stays livable while we are in it.

We take on the projects we can finish well, and we finish what we start. That is how a local construction company earns its reputation, one neighbor at a time.

Plain talk

No jargon, no pressure. We explain the options, the trade-offs, and the costs in words that make sense.

Respect for your time

We show up when we say we will, and we tell you right away if a schedule has to move.

Pride in the finish

The last ten percent is what you see every day. We do not rush it, and we do not skip it.
