About us

A contractor Amelia can call by name

McCart Contracting LLC is a general contractor based in Amelia, Ohio, serving homeowners across Clermont County and the surrounding area.

The idea is simple: one contractor who answers the phone, shows up when promised, and treats your home like it matters. No sales teams, no handoffs between strangers, no disappearing mid-project.

Good work is the whole business plan. Everything else follows.

Whether it is a kitchen that finally works the way your family lives, a deck that survives Ohio weather, or a repair done right the first time, the standard is the same: built right, finished clean.

Why hire local

A local contractor stakes their name on every job. We live and work here, so every project has to be one we would be proud to drive past.

Licensed trades coordinated

Where a project needs electrical or plumbing work, we coordinate licensed tradespeople so every part of the job is done to code.
