J and M Automotive is a full-service gas and inspection station, owned and operated by a couple who has spent more than three decades servicing all makes and models.

How we got here

A neighborhood institution,
run like family

Ask around Ingomar and you will hear the same thing: John and Melody do great work. Reasonably priced, punctual and thorough, the rare combination every driver is looking for.

With over 30 years of experience across all makes and models, there is very little this shop has not seen. That experience shows in the diagnosis, in the repair, and in the bill.

And because J and M is also a full-service gas station, it is woven into the daily rhythm of the neighborhood: fill up on the way to work, get the inspection done on Saturday, and know the people behind the counter by name.
