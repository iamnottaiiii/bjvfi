Our story

A contractor that stays on top of every phase

Fiojo Construction Inc. is a full-service construction company based in Van Nuys, California, with over 8 years of experience in new construction and remodeling.

Our goal is simple: provide efficient, beautiful work that fits your vision and your budget. We bring meticulous attention to detail to each job and maintain thorough, open communication with you throughout the process.

As your dedicated contractors, we stay on top of each phase of construction to ensure the highest professional standards are practiced, and we work efficiently to finish your project on schedule and within budget.
