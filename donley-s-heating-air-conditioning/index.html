Services

The full story on what we fix, service, and install

Heating, cooling, air quality, and water heating, explained the way our techs explain it at your kitchen table.

Chapter 01 · Cooling

Air conditioning that keeps up with August

When the humidity rolls in off the Susquehanna, your AC has to work overtime. We repair central air systems, track down refrigerant and airflow problems, and install efficient replacements when a unit has given its last summer.

If your system is short-cycling, freezing up, or just blowing warm, call before the next heat wave.
  • Central AC diagnostics and repair
  • Full system replacement and installation
  • Seasonal tune-ups and refrigerant checks
Chapter 02 · Heating

Furnaces and boilers built for Pennsylvania winters

We install furnaces, and we service and repair boilers, the workhorses of older Central PA homes. Annual maintenance keeps them safe, efficient, and ready for the first cold snap.

A furnace that is over 15 years old deserves a second opinion before every winter.
  • Furnace installation
  • Boiler installation, repair, and maintenance
  • Heating tune-ups and safety inspections
Chapter 03 · Air & water systems

The quiet work: ducts, ventilation, and balancing

Uneven rooms, dusty vents, an attic that bakes all summer. Air duct cleaning, attic ventilation installation, and air and water systems balancing even things out so your equipment does not have to fight the house.

  • Air duct cleaning
  • Attic ventilation installation
  • Air and water systems balancing
Chapter 04 · Water heating

Hot water, on demand, without drama

We service and replace water heaters, so a cold shower is a one-morning problem, not a one-week problem.
