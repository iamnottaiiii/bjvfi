The cord ticket, explained

A cord is 128 cubic feet of stacked wood: 8 feet long, 4 feet high, 4 feet deep. When you order by the cord, you know exactly what is coming off the truck.

  • Full cord: 4 ft x 4 ft x 8 ft stacked
  • Face cord: one row of a full cord Sample
  • Half cord and smaller loads available Sample

No short stacks. Measure twice, deliver once.
