The Assembly Line

Kutt Factory is a barber shop in the College Hill Plaza area of Cincinnati, Ohio. The verified facts are the address, the phone number, and a 4.8 star rating across 48 reviews. The stations below describe how a typical visit runs; they are a general picture, not claims about this shop's history.

Station 01

Intake Inspection

You walk in and state the target: fade, trim, shave, or full transformation. The barber checks the current state and confirms the plan.

Station 02

Main Production

Clippers, shears, and razors do the heavy work. Clean lines, even blends, and sharp edges come off the line here.

Station 03

Detail and Finish

Beard shaping, line ups, hot towel work, and styling product lock the cut in. Nothing leaves the floor half finished.

Station 04

Final Quality Check

The mirror test. You inspect the work from every angle before the cape comes off and the unit ships out the door.
