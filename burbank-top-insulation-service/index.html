The problem

Your AC works hard. Your attic works against it.

Many Burbank homes were built decades ago with thin, settled, or missing attic insulation. In summer, heat pours through the ceiling. In winter, warmth escapes the same way.

The fix is straightforward: proper insulation at the right R value, installed cleanly, with air sealing where it counts. The result is a home that holds its temperature, an AC that cycles less, and bills that drop.

  • Lower heating and cooling bills
  • Even temperatures, room to room
  • Quieter rooms with sound dampening
