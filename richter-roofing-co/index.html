Why roofs fail here

Foothill homes take sun, heat and Santa Ana winds. Shingles curl, flashing lifts, and flat roofs pond. Most problems are cheaper to fix early, which is why we push inspections before the rainy season.

  • Free estimates on replacements and repairs
  • Shingle, metal and flat roof experience
  • Storm damage assessments with photos
Get a free estimate

Spot the signs early

Missing shingles, granules in the gutters, stains on the ceiling, or a roof past 20 years. If any of that sounds familiar, a look is free and takes under an hour.

How we approach a roof

We start on the roof, not at a desk. Every estimate begins with an actual inspection: photos of what we see, plain-language explanation of what matters, and a written scope before any work is scheduled.

No pressure tactics, no mystery line items. If a repair will buy you five more years, we will say so. If a replacement is the honest answer, we will show you why.

  • Inspections with photos you can keep
  • Written scopes before work starts
  • Site left clean, nails picked up with magnets
