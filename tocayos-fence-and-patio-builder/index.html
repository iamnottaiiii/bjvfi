What we build and fix

Five focused services. Every job starts with a free estimate: call (626) 756-1877.

Fence Installation

New fences measured to your lot, posts set in concrete, and every section built plumb and level. We handle the full tear-out of the old fence when a replacement is needed.

  • 01 Wood privacy and board on board fences
  • 02 Picket and ranch style fences
  • 03 Full fence replacement with haul away of old material
  • 04 Posts reset and reinforced in concrete
Pricing is by the foot and depends on height, style, and ground conditions. Every quote is free and in writing before work begins.

Fence Repair

Storm damage, rot, and years of leaning can usually be fixed without replacing the whole line. We find the failing parts and rebuild just what is needed.

  • 01 Leaning and broken post replacement
  • 02 Rotted and damaged board replacement
  • 03 Sagging section re-hang and re-level
  • 04 Concrete footing repair and reinforcement
Repairs are quoted per section after an on-site look. Call with photos of the damage for a faster ballpark.

Gate Installation and Repair

A gate is only as good as its frame and posts. We build gates that do not sag and repair the ones that already do, with latches and hardware that last.

  • 01 New walk gates and side gates
  • 02 Drive gate installation and re-hang
  • 03 Sagging gate diagnosis and re-frame
  • 04 Hinges, latches, and hardware replacement
Gate pricing depends on width, style, and hardware. Bring your opening measurements when you call for the fastest quote.

Our story

A local crew for fences and patios

Tocayos Fence and Patio Builder works out of Duarte, California, building and repairing fences, gates, and patios for neighbors across the San Gabriel Valley. The focus has always been simple: do one kind of work, and do it well.

When the same crew installs and repairs fences every week, small details stop being small. Post depth, concrete mix, how a gate hangs in year three: that is the craft that keeps a fence standing straight long after the invoice is paid.

No sales team, no call center. You call, you talk to the people who will be on your property, and you get a price you can understand before anything is built.
