Residential electrical

Common projects in Tujunga homes include:

  • Outlet, switch, and dimmer installation and repair
  • Indoor and outdoor lighting installation
  • Ceiling fan installation
  • Dedicated circuits for appliances and home offices
  • Smoke and carbon monoxide detector wiring
  • Whole-home safety inspections

Commercial electrical

Common projects for local businesses include:

  • Office and retail lighting and power layouts
  • Troubleshooting outages and faulty circuits
  • Dedicated circuits for equipment
  • Scheduled maintenance and repairs
  • Tenant improvement electrical work
  • Responsive service for ongoing vendor relationships

Troubleshooting and repair

Flickering lights, dead outlets, breakers that keep tripping. Common diagnostic and repair work includes:

  • Circuit tracing and fault finding
  • Breaker and fuse replacement
  • Wiring repairs and code corrections
  • GFCI and AFCI protection upgrades
  • Storm and surge related repairs

Panels and service upgrades

When your electrical system needs more capacity or a fresh start:

  • Main panel upgrades and replacements
  • Subpanel installation
  • Service mast and meter work coordination
  • Whole-home surge protection
  • EV charger circuit installation

Our story

A local shop built on showing up

Cornerstone Electrical is an electrical contractor based on Foothill Boulevard in Tujunga, California, in the foothills of the San Fernando Valley. It is the kind of shop the neighborhood runs on: local, reachable by phone, and known for doing the job right.

The public record is short but telling. Customers describe the company as reliable, with one business customer calling Cornerstone a great vendor partner for their electrical needs. That is the reputation of a contractor that answers, arrives, and finishes clean.

Residential and commercial work both run through the shop, from home repairs and lighting to ongoing electrical support for local businesses. No call centers, no runaround: you call the number, you talk to the electrician.

If you are in Tujunga, Sunland, or nearby and need electrical work done properly, this is your neighborhood crew.
