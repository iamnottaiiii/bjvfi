About

Bethany’s own
one-shop plumber

Perry Plumbing & Heating is an owner-operated shop based on Miller Rd, built on repeat customers and word of mouth across New Haven County.

The shop

Big-company skills,
small-shop accountability

This is not a franchise and not a call center. It is a working plumber who answers the phone, shows up when promised, and stands behind every joint, valve and fitting.

Because the owner is the technician, nothing gets lost between the estimate and the work. What gets quoted is what gets done, and the person who quoted it is the one turning the wrench.
