Our Story

A Pasadena shop built on showing up.

Best AC Repair Pasadena started with a simple observation: when an air conditioner fails in a Pasadena heat wave, people do not need a sales pitch, they need a technician who shows up, finds the fault, and fixes it right the first time.

From our shop at 33 East Green Street in Old Pasadena, we have built the business the old way: one careful repair at a time. Customers describe our team as professional and knowledgeable, quick to identify and resolve issues, friendly in the home, and fair on price.

We service central air systems, ducted and ductless units, and the heating equipment that carries you through winter. Repair is always the first option; replacement is recommended only when the math favors you.
