Built on the idea in our name

Integrity Home Improvements is a Lancaster, New York contracting business founded on a simple standard: say what you will do, then do what you said. That is what keeps a roofing contractor in business in a town where neighbors talk.

We serve homeowners across Lancaster, Depew, Cheektowaga, and the wider Buffalo area with roofing, exterior painting, and home improvement work. Every project gets the same treatment: an honest inspection, a clear written quote, a clean job site, and a final walkthrough before we call it done.

Western New York weather is hard on roofs. Snow loads, ice dams, and freeze-thaw cycles punish shortcuts. We build for that reality, with materials and methods chosen for this climate, not for a brochure.
