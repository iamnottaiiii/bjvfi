Our process

How a typical job runs.

Every home is different, but most floor restoration jobs follow the same four stages from first call to final walk-through.

01

Walk-through

We look at your floors in person, talk about what you want, and agree on the scope and finish before any work starts.

02

Sanding

Old finish and surface damage are sanded away in passes, from coarse to fine, with careful attention to edges and corners.

03

Stain and finish

Stain goes on if you want a color change, then protective finish coats are applied evenly and left to cure.

04

Final check

We walk the floors with you, make sure every room looks right, and share simple care tips to keep the finish lasting.
