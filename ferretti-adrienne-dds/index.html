About the dentist

Dr. Adrienne Ferretti, DDS

Dr. Adrienne Ferretti is a general dentist practicing in Staten Island, New York. Licensed in New York State, she provides preventive, restorative and surgical dental care for patients of all ages from her office at 11 Ralph Pl.

Patients describe Dr. Ferretti as gentle, kind and professional, noting a warm and welcoming manner that puts nervous patients at ease. Her practice has a particular strength in comfortable extractions, with reviewers praising careful, pain-free experiences.
