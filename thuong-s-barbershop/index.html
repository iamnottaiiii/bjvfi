Our story

THUONG'S BARBERSHOP is a classic barber shop on Main Street in Worcester, MA, serving the neighborhood seven days a week.

The shop keeps barbering simple and honest: a good consultation, careful clipper work, and a clean finish. Whether it is a regular fade, a first haircut for a young client, or a quick shape up, every client gets the same attention in the chair.

Listed hours run 9:30 AM to 7:00 PM every day. Walk ins are welcome, and calling ahead is the best way to check chair availability.
