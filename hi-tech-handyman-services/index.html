Two people, one truck,

Hi-Tech Handyman Services is a husband-and-wife team working out of Union Township, east of Cincinnati. No call centers, no subcontractor roulette, the people who quote your job are the people who show up.

THE TEAM

Rick and Marie

Customers know them by name, and they ask for them by name. Reviewers describe them as professional, trustworthy, and easy to work with.
