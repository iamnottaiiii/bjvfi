Our story

Family owned. Customer obsessed.

Marriott & Sons Roofing was founded in Glen Burnie, Maryland on a simple idea: treat every customer the way family should be treated. Our name is our promise. Like our name says, our goal is to go above and beyond expectations on every roofing project, big or small.

Founder Zachary Mathis built the company around experienced roofing technicians who lead every crew, so you get strong workmanship and strong customer service on the same visit. We are factory certified with GAF, accredited with the BBB, and licensed and insured to operate in the state of Maryland.

We make sure the customer is happy with the end result, every single time.
