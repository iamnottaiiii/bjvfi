Our Story

Welcome to Lucky Nail & Spa

We are dedicated to the passion and artistry of nail care. Our team provides professional nail care and waxing services in a sincere, professional atmosphere, and our goal is to create a beautiful and relaxing environment for all of our customers to enjoy.

Experienced staff, passionate about the work, proud of the service you expect.

Located at 2086 Flatbush Ave in Brooklyn, we keep things simple and calm: reasonable prices, a quiet space, and first come, first served walk in service. For appointments, please call (718) 692-1988. Walk ins are always welcome.
