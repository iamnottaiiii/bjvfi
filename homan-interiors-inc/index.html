About

The shop on Wooster Pike.

Homan Interiors Inc works out of 5648 Wooster Rd in Cincinnati, Ohio, a hands-on studio where upholstery, refinishing, and design happen under one roof. It is the kind of place people find once and then send their friends to.

What the record shows

Facts, not folklore.

Here is what public listings and reviews actually say about the business. Where the record is thin or conflicts, we say so.

A craft-first business

Directory listings categorize Homan Interiors Inc as interior designers and decorators, upholsterers, and furniture repair and refinishing, with antique restoration among its listed specialties. Reviews center on the same thing: upholstery and refinishing done to a high standard.

4.2 stars, five reviews

On Birdeye the shop holds a 4.2 star rating across 5 reviews. Praise calls it the best high end upholstery and refinishing in the Cincinnati area. One reviewer described a long wait for a small job, which we show honestly on the Reviews page.

Weekday hours call to confirm

Most listings show Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, closed weekends. One directory lists a 9:00 AM opening instead. Hours can change, so call (513) 271-2921 before visiting.

Practical details

Listings show the shop accepts American Express, Mastercard, and Visa. A fax line is listed at (513) 271-2922. The business profile is not claimed on some review sites, so details may lag behind reality.

Keep, don’t replace

Good bones deserve a second act. The shop’s whole ethos is restoration over the landfill.

Done right, not rushed

Custom work takes the time it takes. Expect honest timelines, not fast ones.
