Our story

A Jamesville staple, one haircut at a time

Cutter's Corner has been serving the Jamesville community from East Seneca Turnpike with the same simple formula: friendly people, fair prices, and hair you love.

Small salon, big heart

The corner salon neighbors trust

Tucked into the corner of East Seneca Turnpike and North Street in Jamesville, Cutter's Corner is the kind of salon where the stylists know your name and your usual. No rush, no attitude, just good work at a fair price.

Clients mention the same things again and again: friendly staff, well-done haircuts, and prices that make sense. Some have followed our stylists for years, and new faces quickly become regulars.

Whether you need a quick trim, a fresh color, or styling for a big occasion, you will leave the chair feeling like the best version of yourself.
