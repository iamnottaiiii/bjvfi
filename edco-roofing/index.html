Services

What We Do on the Roof

Every service starts with a free inspection and a written estimate. No pressure, no surprise line items.

Asphalt Shingle Roofing

Architectural and 3-tab shingle installation and replacement. A typical re-roof takes two to three days.

Tile Roofing

Clay and concrete tile installation, repair, and underlayment replacement for the long haul.

Flat and Low-Slope Roofs

Torch-down, single-ply, and foam systems for flat and low-slope sections, common on Valley homes.

Leak Repair

We trace leaks to the source, not just the stain on your ceiling, and repair flashing, vents, and valleys.

Storm Damage Response

Wind and rain damage assessment, emergency tarping, and insurance-friendly documentation.

Gutters and Flashing

Seamless gutters, drip edge, and flashing details that keep water where it belongs.
