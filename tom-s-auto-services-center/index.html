Our Story

Tom's Auto Services Center has been a fixture on White Horse Pike in Atco for decades. Reviewers trace their relationship with the shop back to 1984, and many have now trusted Tom's with their cars, their kids' cars, and their grandkids' cars.

The formula never changed: tell people the truth about their car, charge a fair price, and do the job right the first time. When another shop quoted a customer thousands for work the truck didn't need, Tom's found the real fix and earned a customer for life.

We service all makes, including trucks and SUVs, and we're known for being the shop people call when they want a second opinion they can believe.
