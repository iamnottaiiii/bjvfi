Our Story

A FORT MITCHELL ORIGINAL

Sixty plus years on Dixie Highway. This is the skyline-era story of a neighborhood institution.

1963

THE DOORS OPEN

Plaza Barbers describes itself as a traditional barber shop established in 1963, opening its chairs on Dixie Highway in Fort Mitchell, Kentucky. The formula was simple from day one: an honest cut, a fair price, and a chair that felt like yours.

THE PLAZA YEARS

A NEIGHBORHOOD ANCHOR

Through the decades the shop became a fixture of the corridor, the kind of place where fathers bring sons and regulars are greeted by name. Vintage chairs, buzzing clippers, and easy conversation kept generations coming back.

TODAY

OLD SCHOOL, STILL SHARP

Under owner Eric Hehman, the shop holds the line on tradition: walk-ins welcome, real barbering, and a family friendly floor where even first-timers settle in fast. Fades, straight razor finishes, and hot towel shaves remain the signatures reviewers name again and again.

THE PLAZA PROMISE

Walk in, sit down, walk out looking sharp. No appointments, no fuss, just the craft the way it has been done here for more than six decades.
