Our philosophy Sample narrative

We believe a building's scars are its resume. Every spalled brick, every weathered joint, every streak of patina is a record of weather survived and generations served. Restoration is not about making an old building look new; it is about giving an old building the repairs it needs to keep being old, beautifully, for another hundred years.

We work slowly where the building asks for slowness: lime before cement, repair before replace, and always a mortar softer than the brick it holds.
