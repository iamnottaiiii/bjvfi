The studio

A calm room, a steady hand

Sarah tattoos at Cardinal Tattoo Studio, a custom shop in the Priscilla Town Center, owned and operated by Rich Bevilacqua and Lee Cramer with over 30 years of combined experience. Every artist is registered with the Gloucester County Board of Health, and the shop follows strict sterilization and aseptic practices.

Between Philadelphia and the Jersey shore, Cardinal is known for a welcoming, family-like feel: an open studio where first timers feel at ease and collectors feel at home. Free and ample parking sits right outside in the court lot.

Sarah's clients come for the small things done beautifully: a sprig of lavender on a wrist, a word in fine script, a symbol only they understand. Each piece starts with a conversation and a consult.

The shop

Cardinal Tattoo Studio

Sarah's home base, a custom, state of the art shop in the Priscilla Town Center.

Cardinal Tattoo Studio is owned and operated by Rich Bevilacqua and Lee Cramer, with over 30 years of combined experience. The team is a collection of versatile artists who each work across a range of styles, from veteran collectors to first time clients.

Located at 5101 Route 42, the shop sits centrally between Philadelphia and the Jersey shore, with free and ample parking in the court lot. All employees are registered with the Gloucester County Board of Health and follow strict sterilization and aseptic techniques.
