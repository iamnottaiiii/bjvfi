Our Story

A family name on every truck.

Espenscheid Plumbing Inc was established in 1954 in Southgate, Kentucky, and has been family led ever since, with Fred F. Espenscheid at the helm of today's crew.

We are a small shop, about seven people, and we like it that way. It means the person who answers the phone knows the tech coming to your door, and the tech knows your street. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and word of mouth across Campbell County.

Our approach is simple: show up fast, diagnose honestly, price fairly, and leave the place cleaner than we found it. After more than 70 years, it still works.
