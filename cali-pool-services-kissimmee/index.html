What great pool care looks like

The checklist we run on every weekly visit.

Test

Chlorine, pH, alkalinity, and stabilizer checked against healthy ranges.

Balance

Chemicals adjusted so water is safe, clear, and gentle on eyes and equipment.

Clean

Surface skimmed, walls brushed, floor vacuumed, baskets emptied.

Inspect

Equipment, water level, and visible plumbing given a quick once-over.
