About the Salon

Where Everybody Gets the House Blend

Beauty & the Beard Salon sits at 93 School Rd in Dry Ridge, Kentucky, serving Grant County with a full menu of salon services. The name says it all: a place for her beauty ritual and his beard trim, plus the whole family in between.

The salon describes itself simply: haircuts, color, perms, facial waxing, and special event hair and makeup, with appointments recommended. Guests consistently describe a clean, friendly, relaxing space where the staff listens, same day appointments are often possible, and prices stay fair.

In public reviews, guests praise stylists by name, including Melissa, Morgan, Liz, Amberly, and Kelly, for careful listening and precise work, especially with kids cuts.
