The artist

ABOUT INTUITION

A small studio with a big reputation on South Broadway in Pitman.

BRIAN GORMLEY.
CUSTOM TATTOO ARTIST.

Intuition Tattoo is the studio of artist Brian Gormley, who trained under veteran South Jersey tattooer Billy Webb before setting up his own shop on South Broadway in Pitman.

His approach is simple: listen first, draw second, tattoo third. Clients describe him as thorough, efficient, respectful, and kind, the kind of artist who focuses on the work and treats every piece like it matters, because it does.

  • Custom designs developed from your ideas
  • Light handed technique clients praise by name
  • Clean, comfortable, professional studio
  • Upfront estimates and easy scheduling

THE SHOP.

A comfortable, friendly atmosphere on Pitman's main drag. One on one service means your appointment is about you: your design, your placement, your pace.

Wheelchair accessible. Street parking nearby on South Broadway. Walk ins are handled when the schedule allows, but booking ahead is the sure move.
