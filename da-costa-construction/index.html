Our story

Two generations, one standard

Da Costa Construction Inc was incorporated in Florida on June 15, 2006, and has been an active company ever since, based at 2159 27th Ave N in St. Petersburg. The company is led by Fernando M. Da Costa, and jobs are run by a father-and-son crew with Fernando and Carlos Da Costa.

Twenty years in, the formula has not changed: show up when promised, communicate clearly, pour quality concrete, and leave the job site spotless. That is why customers keep hiring the crew again and sending their neighbors over.
