Our Story

Dominican technique, Brooklyn heart

Dominican Beauty Salon brings the Dominican Republic's famous hair traditions to East New York: the meticulous wash, the deep-conditioning ritual, the roller set and the silk blowout that leaves hair moving like water.

Reviewers describe a welcoming place that feels like home, with stylists who give honest, personalized advice on color and style, and service that keeps hair looking great until the next visit.
