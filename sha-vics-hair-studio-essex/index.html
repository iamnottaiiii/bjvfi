Our Story

A studio built on craft and care.

Essex's neighborhood studio

Sha Vics Hair Studio Essex sits on Mace Ave, serving Essex and the surrounding Baltimore County neighborhoods. Our stylists specialize in color, smoothing and texture services, from balayage and keratin to perms and curly hair care.

We believe great hair comes from listening first. Tell us what you want, show us a photo, or let us guide you, and we will create a plan that keeps your hair healthy, shiny and easy to love.
