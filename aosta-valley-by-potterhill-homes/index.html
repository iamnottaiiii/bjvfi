Our Story

A valley named for the Alps,
built with Kentucky roots

Aosta Valley takes its name from a beautiful valley in Italy. Here in Walton, it means rolling land, friendly streets, and homes built to last.

The community

Why the valley works

Aosta Valley was planned around the way families actually spend their days: a pool for summer, a playground within shouting distance, a court for evening games, and streets that feel safe to wander.

Set just off Highway 16 with easy access to I-71, it keeps Walton's small-town character while staying close to shopping, dining, and the commute into Cincinnati.

Potterhill Homes builds here as part of the community's mix of builders, bringing the family-founded, energy-efficient approach it has used since 2002.

A new home is one of the largest investments a family will ever make. It should be built to respect that, in cost, in comfort, and in energy use.
The Potterhill approach

Part of the Potterhill Homes family. Founded in 2002 by Carolyn Rolfes with her father, Potterhill Homes is known for affordable, energy-efficient building, including geothermal heating and cooling. Aosta Valley is one of its Walton communities.
