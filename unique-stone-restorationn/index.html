How a restoration visit works

A clear four step process, explained before any work begins.

01

Assess

We look at the stone type, the damage, and the finish you want, then quote the job on the spot.

02

Restore

Diamond abrasives grind out scratches, lippage, etching, and old coatings.

03

Polish

We refine the surface to a honed satin or a mirror polish, your choice.

04

Seal

A penetrating sealer protects the finish, with care instructions before we leave.

Stone care is a trade, not a mop job

Marble, travertine, limestone, and terrazzo each need different abrasives and chemistry. The wrong cleaner etches marble in seconds. We match the method to the stone.

Our story

A stone shop with one specialty

Unique Stone Restoration is a marble contractor based at 303 Jessie St in San Fernando, California. We do one thing: bring natural stone back to its best. Floors, counters, showers, and vanities in marble, travertine, limestone, terrazzo, and slate.

Restoration is the practical choice. It costs a fraction of replacement, finishes in days instead of weeks, and keeps good stone out of the landfill.

  • Focused trade: stone restoration only
  • On site quotes with clear, written pricing
  • Dust controlled equipment for indoor work
  • Care guidance so the finish lasts
