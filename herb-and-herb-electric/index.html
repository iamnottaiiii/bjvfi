Our story

A neighborhood electrician, wired into the community

Herb And Herb Electric is a locally run electrical shop based on Banks Street in Penbrook, serving Harrisburg and the surrounding Central PA communities. We do the unglamorous work that keeps homes and businesses safe: panels, circuits, code corrections, and the troubleshooting jobs nobody else wants to touch.

We are also part of the neighborhood beyond the jobsite. Our crew has spent time with the Penbrook Leo Club summer program, teaching local kids about magnets, forces, and how electricity works, because a trade shop should give back to the block it works on.

Residential or commercial, big project or a single dead outlet, you get the same deal: a registered PA Home Improvement Contractor, straight talk before the work starts, and a jobsite left clean.
