Our story

A Van Nuys crew that treats concrete like a craft

Concrete Van Nuys is a concrete contractor based on Victory Boulevard in Van Nuys, California. We focus on one trade and do it well: forming, pouring and finishing concrete for homes and small commercial properties across the San Fernando Valley.

Most of our work comes from referrals and repeat customers. That happens when the slab is level, the edges are straight, the crew shows up when promised and the yard is clean when we leave.
