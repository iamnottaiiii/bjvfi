How a build comes together

Four steps to your front door

Building a home is a big decision. A clear process keeps it straightforward, on budget, and on schedule.

Step 01

Consult and plan

We talk through your goals, your lot, your budget, and your timeline. No pressure, just a straight conversation.

Step 02

Design and permits

Plans are drawn up and detailed, and we handle the paperwork so the build can start right.

Step 03

Build

Foundation to roof to finishes, with regular updates so you always know where your home stands.

Step 04

Walkthrough and keys

We walk every room together, punch list anything that needs attention, and hand over the keys.

What we can build for you

New home construction

Complete start-to-finish construction of single-family homes in Fleming Island and the surrounding Clay County area. One team, one contract, one point of contact from permits to punch list.

  • Full ground-up construction management
  • Floor plans for Florida living: open, bright, breezy
  • Energy-conscious materials and systems
  • Regular build updates with photos
Call for a consultation
Good to know: timelines and pricing depend on the plan, the lot, and current material costs. Call (904) 278-2545 for a realistic estimate for your project.

Custom home building

Have land, a plan, or just an idea? We work with you to shape a home that fits your lot, your budget, and your life, then build it with a craftsman's eye for detail.

  • Design guidance from first sketch to final plan
  • Finish and fixture selections, curated for you
  • Permits and inspections handled for you
  • Final walkthrough before you move in
Call for a consultation
Good to know: already working with an architect? We are happy to build from existing plans. Bring them to the first call.

Home additions

When your home is almost right but needs more room, we add it: bedrooms, bonus rooms, expanded living areas, and second stories, matched to your existing home inside and out.

  • Bedroom and bath additions
  • Family room and living space expansions
  • Garage and storage additions
  • Exterior matching: roof, siding, and trim
Call for a consultation
Good to know: additions must work with your lot, HOA rules, and local setbacks. We review all of that with you before anything is drawn.

Remodeling and renovation

Keep the address, change everything else. We rework kitchens, bathrooms, and whole floor plans so your current home finally feels like yours.

  • Kitchen remodels and expansions
  • Bathroom renovations
  • Whole-home layout reconfigurations
  • Flooring, finishes, and fixture updates
Call for a consultation
Good to know: we can phase larger remodels so you can keep living in your home while we work. Ask about phasing on your call.
