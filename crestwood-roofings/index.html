Know the signs

Is your roof asking for help?

A quick look from the ground can tell you a lot. If you spot any of these, it is time for a professional eye.

  • Missing, curling, or cracked shingles
  • Water stains on ceilings or walls
  • Granules piling up in the gutters
  • Roof older than 20 years, or sagging anywhere
