Our Story

A neighborhood dry cleaner with a seamstress's touch.

Ace Dry Cleaners at 4468 Walt Stephens Rd has become a trusted stop for garment care in Stockbridge. It is a small, friendly shop where the work is personal: careful cleaning, crisp pressing, and alterations done with a master seamstress's eye.

Customers come back year after year for the same reasons: the owner is kind and helpful, the staff is courteous and professional, prices are fair, and turnaround is quick. It is the kind of place where your name is remembered and your clothes come back better than you left them.

Whether it is a suit for an interview, a dress for an event, or a favorite coat that needs a refresh, bring it to Ace Dry Cleaners and see the difference careful hands make.
