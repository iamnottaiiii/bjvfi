About

The Craftsman Behind
the Name

Hank Zureick Carpentry is a carpentry and general contracting shop based at 8988 Livingston Rd in Cincinnati, Ohio. Public listings classify the business as a carpenter and contractor serving residential and commercial clients.

The approach is simple and old fashioned: listen carefully, measure honestly, and build as though the work will be inspected by the next generation. Whether the job is a single built in or a full renovation, the finish work receives the same unhurried attention.
