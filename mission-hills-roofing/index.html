Why Mission Hills Roofing

Roofs built for Valley weather.

Santa Ana winds, summer heat, and the occasional downpour all punish a roof. We install systems rated for real Southern California conditions, and we repair the damage when weather wins.

Local and reachable

Based right on Sepulveda Blvd in Mission Hills, open every day from 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM, so help is close when a leak shows up.

Repair first, replace when right

An honest inspection tells you whether a repair will buy years or whether replacement is the smarter money. No pressure either way.

Clean, safe jobsites

Nails picked up with magnets, landscaping protected, and debris hauled away. Your property looks the same, minus the roof problems.
