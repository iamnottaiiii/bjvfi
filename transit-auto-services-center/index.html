Our story

The shop Transit Road relies on

Transit Auto Services Center sits at 10710 Transit Road in East Amherst, NY, serving drivers across Amherst, Clarence and Williamsville. It is a true full-service center: mechanical repair bays on one side, auto body and paint on the other.

Customers describe a team that knows them by name, explains the work in plain language and prices it fairly. Several reviewers mention bringing their cars here for well over a decade, which says more than any advertisement could.

The formula is simple. Diagnose carefully, quote honestly, fix it right, and stand behind the work. Whether it is a routine oil change, a brake job, new tires or bodywork after a fender bender, the goal is the same: hand back keys to a car you can trust.

Open weekdays 9 to 5 and Saturday mornings for the jobs that cannot wait until Monday.
