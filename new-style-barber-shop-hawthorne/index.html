The story of New Style Barber Shop

New Style Barber Shop in Hawthorne welcomes the whole family, from first haircuts to weekend touch-ups. The team includes barbers Tony, Anthony and Martin, and clients praise the personal attention.

Located at 11918 Hawthorne Blvd, Hawthorne, CA 90250, the shop serves Hawthorne and the surrounding neighborhoods. Walk-ins and regulars are both welcome, and the team is happy to talk through the cut before the clippers come out.
