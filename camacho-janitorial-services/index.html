Services

Janitorial services for every corner of your building

Mix and match services into a recurring plan, or book a one-time deep clean. Every job starts with a walkthrough and a clear written quote.

Scheduled Office Cleaning

Daily, weekly, or biweekly service covering dusting, vacuuming, trash, and restrooms. The backbone of a consistently clean workplace.

Commercial Floor Care

Machine scrubbing, mopping, buffing, and polishing for tile, vinyl, concrete, and other hard floors in high-traffic spaces.

Restroom Cleaning and Sanitizing

Disinfection of toilets, sinks, floors, mirrors, and partitions, plus restocking of soap, paper, and liners.

Trash and Recycling

Collection, bag replacement, and proper sorting of trash and recyclables from offices, breakrooms, and common areas.

Window and Glass Cleaning

Interior glass, partitions, and entryway windows cleaned streak-free for a brighter, more professional look.

Post-Construction Cleanup

Dust, debris, and residue removal after renovations or build-outs, so the space is move-in ready on day one.
