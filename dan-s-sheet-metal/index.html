Our story

A sheet metal shop
with staying power

Dan's Sheet Metal has been working the San Fernando Valley out of the same Sun Valley shop since 1995.

Built on metal, kept on word of mouth

Dan's Sheet Metal is a small sheet metal contractor at 11142 Fleetwood St in Sun Valley, California. The shop has been in business since 1995, a single location serving residential and light commercial customers across the San Fernando Valley.

The trade is architectural sheet metal: roofing, gutters, flashing and custom fabrication. The public record backs up the experience, with 15 permitted projects on file with California building departments and a $15,000 contractor bond on record.

Being small is the point. One shop, one call, and the same people measuring, cutting and installing your job.
