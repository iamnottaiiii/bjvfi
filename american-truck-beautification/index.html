About The Shop

American Truck Beautification, Inc. has been fixing and beautifying trucks in Harrison, Ohio since 1993.

Three Decades Under
The Spotlight

What started in 1993 as a small auto body and painting operation on Kilby Road has grown into a trusted local shop for truck and van owners across the Harrison area. Our name says it plainly: we beautify trucks.

Our reputation is built one panel at a time. Reviewers consistently mention the quality of our paint work and how well we match factory colors, which is the hardest part of the job to get right.
