Our story

Raised in the trade, raised to a standard

LTS Builders, Inc. was formed in late 1992 when Steve and Ted Leonard came together with the goal of setting the bar higher in the building industry. Both brothers have been in the construction business most of their adult lives.

After working together for a number of years, the brothers decided to form LTS Builders, Inc. after Ted graduated from Purdue University in the construction management program. Steve was a lead carpenter for various frame and trim crews until he formed his own business in the late 80s in search of better quality.

Our emphasis is on quality and family. Each home and project is ours until the project is complete and ready to present to our clients. We will finish our promise to you before we move on to the next task at hand.
