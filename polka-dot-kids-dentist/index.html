For growing smiles

Care that grows with your child

Baby teeth matter. They hold space for adult teeth, help kids chew and speak clearly, and build habits that last a lifetime.

We focus on preventive, routine kids dental care and on teaching brushing and flossing in ways kids actually remember. When something needs attention, we explain the plan in plain language for you, and in kid language for them.

About us

A dental home for Baltimore kids

Polka dot Kids Dentist is a pediatric-focused dental practice at 3333 N Calvert St in Baltimore, built around one idea: dental visits should feel safe, kind, and maybe a little bit fun.

Our approach

Small patients, big patience

Children are not small adults, and kids dentistry should not feel like it. Visits here move at kid speed, with clear explanations, gentle hands, and room for wiggles.

We believe the best dental care starts with trust. That is why parents stay involved, questions are encouraged, and no one is ever rushed through the chair.

Gentle first

Comfort comes before everything. We earn trust one visit at a time.

Parents included

You know your child best. We plan visits together, with you in the loop.

Habits that stick

We teach brushing and flossing in ways kids remember and actually enjoy.
