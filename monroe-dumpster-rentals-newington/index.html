How long can I keep it?

Most rentals include 7 to 14 days. Need longer? Extensions are available. Call before your time runs out and we will keep you covered.

Where should it go?

A flat, paved surface is best: driveway, parking lot, or compacted gravel. The truck needs about 14 feet of overhead clearance, so watch for wires and branches.

Do I need to be there for delivery?

Usually not. Just tell us exactly where to place the dumpster when you book, and mark the spot if you can.

What about weight limits?

Each size includes a weight allowance, with overages billed per ton. Heavy loads like concrete, dirt, or roofing may need special handling. Mention them when you call.
