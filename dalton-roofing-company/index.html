Roof replacement

When a roof is past patching, we tear it down to the deck and build it back right with high-definition shingles. Most residential replacements are finished in a single day.

  • Full tear-off and deck inspection
  • High-definition architectural shingles
  • Underlayment, flashing, and ventilation
  • Supervisor on site for the whole job
  • Complete cleanup when we are done

Good to know: customers say our written quote matches the final bill. Call for a written quote on your home.

Roof repair

A leak, a missing shingle, or storm damage does not always mean a new roof. We fix what is broken, seal what is worn, and tell you honestly when repair is enough.

  • Leak detection and patching
  • Missing or damaged shingle replacement, often within 24 hours
  • Flashing repair around chimneys and vents
  • Storm damage assessment
  • Honest advice when a repair will do

Fast response

One customer called in the morning about missing shingles and we replaced them within 24 hours. For leaks, sooner is always cheaper.
