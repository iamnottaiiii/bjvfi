Our story

The oil heating expert Central MA calls

Begin Quality Heating operates out of 45 Lakeview Dr in Leicester, MA, serving homeowners across Central Massachusetts as both a discount heating oil company and a full heating contractor.

Customer reviews tell the story best: Randy is described as an oil heating expert who knows boilers better than anyone, up to speed on natural gas and the newest modulating-condensing boilers. One long-time customer had him manage a full changeover from an oversized oil boiler to a properly sized high-efficiency gas unit, plus oil tanks, water heaters, gas lines, and radiators over the years.

The approach is simple: fair discount pricing on heating oil, expert work on heating systems, and the kind of punctual, informative service that earns 5-star reviews.
