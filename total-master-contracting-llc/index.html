About

A lifetime tradesman

Vinny, the owner of Total Master Contracting Llc, has spent his whole working life in the trades. He attended the New York City District Council of Carpenters technical vocational college and worked as a carpenter in NYC for 17 years, then studied plumbing, electrical, and HVAC at the Technical Career Institute.

When his daughter was born, he went into business for himself, focusing on residential home improvements, mostly bathrooms and kitchens. After the COVID years made help hard to find, he started working solo and liked it better. He now concentrates on smaller jobs, cares about his customers and his work, and tries to be fair on every price.

Customers describe him as professional, punctual, courteous, and friendly, with real troubleshooting skill. One review credits him with solving a plumbing mystery a family had dealt with for two years.
