HOW TO READ A SHOP WITH FEW PUBLIC REVIEWS

POSITIVE SIGNS

Look for reviewers who name the exact repair, mention fair pricing, and say the rig was back on the road fast. Specifics beat star counts.

MIXED SIGNS

Good work but slow turnaround, or fair prices but limited communication, are the most common mixed patterns at independent truck shops.

NEGATIVE SIGNS

Watch for repeated complaints about surprise charges, work that had to be redone, or rigs held longer than promised. Patterns matter more than one bad day.
