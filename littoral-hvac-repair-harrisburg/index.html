What we do

Full service heating and cooling

Littoral HVAC Repair Harrisburg covers the whole lifecycle of your home comfort system. Call for a full list of services and current availability.

01
Most called

Heating repair

Cold air from the vents, a furnace that will not start, a boiler losing pressure: we find the fault in furnaces, boilers and heat pumps and fix it properly.

02
Summer essential

Air conditioning repair

Central air and ductless systems that blow warm, freeze up, leak or rattle. We restore cooling fast so your home stays livable in the heat.

03
Prevent breakdowns

HVAC maintenance and tune ups

Twice a year checkups: coils cleaned, filters changed, electrical tightened, refrigerant and airflow verified. Small issues fixed before they strand you.

04
Long term fix

System replacement and installation

When repairs stop making sense, we size the right system for your home, install it to spec, and commission everything before sign off.

05
Breathe easier

Indoor air quality

Filtration upgrades, humidity control and ventilation fixes for dusty, dry or stuffy homes.
