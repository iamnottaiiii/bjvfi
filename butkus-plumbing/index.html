About

A family shop, not a franchise.

Butkus Plumbing is run by Joe Butkus, a Southbury plumber customers describe as professional, honest, and fair. His son Zack works alongside him, so the person on your job is family, not a rotating crew.

Reviews keep coming back to the same things: fast callbacks, on-time arrivals, clear explanations of the work, and pricing that feels fair. One customer put it simply: the new filter basket is much nicer than the old one, connected to a metal pipe instead of PVC, and the repair took very little time.
