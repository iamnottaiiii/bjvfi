About the company

A Winchester painting contractor you can trust

Blue Jay painting is a painting contractor based at 61 George Rd in Winchester, Massachusetts. The company focuses on residential interior and exterior painting for homeowners across Winchester and the surrounding Middlesex County towns.

The approach is straightforward: careful prep, quality paint, honest pricing and a tidy job site. Floors and furniture get protected, surfaces get primed where they need it, and the crew does not pack up until the walkthrough is right.

What to expect

  • Written quotes. Scope, paint and timeline in writing before work starts.
  • Real prep work. Washing, scraping, sanding, caulking and priming as needed.
  • Clean job sites. Drop cloths, masking and daily cleanup while the crew is in your home.
  • Final walkthrough. You approve every wall before the project is called done.
