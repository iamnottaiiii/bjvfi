La Panaderia

Baked here, sold here

A small neighborhood bakery and tortilleria. The trays fill up early and the tortillas go fast, so a quick call ahead never hurts.

Flour Tortillas

The signature item. Soft flour tortillas made by hand, bagged warm and ready for your table.

Tamales

Savory tamales from the tamale side of the kitchen. Ask what is in the steamer when you call.

Sugar Donuts

Classic sugar donuts, dusted heavy and boxed to go. A morning favorite on W 70th Ave.

Conchas

Pan dulce with the cracked cookie shell on top. Grab a bag and mix the colors.

Cakes

Cakes for birthdays and get-togethers. Call the shop to talk flavors and sizes.
