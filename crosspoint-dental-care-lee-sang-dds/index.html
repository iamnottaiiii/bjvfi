Our story

Care that feels like family

At CrossPoint Dental Care, dentistry is personal. Dr. Sang W. Lee, DDS, and Dr. Hannah Lee lead a small, caring team that treats patients the way they would want to be treated: listened to, never rushed, and told honestly what their teeth need.

The practice welcomes patients of all ages and has a reputation for putting anxious visitors at ease. Whether it is a routine cleaning or a full ceramic crown, the goal is the same: careful work in a relaxing environment, and results that look natural.

Conveniently located at 7535 Little River Turnpike, Suite 310-C in Annandale, the office serves families across the Little River corridor and beyond.
