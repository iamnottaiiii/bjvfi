No. 01 · The Studio

A quiet room where big ideas become permanent art.

At a glance

  • ✓Custom design for every client, drawn from your ideas.
  • ✓Color and black and grey work in many styles.
  • ✓Cover ups and rework of older tattoos.
  • ✓Strict hygiene with single use needles.
  • ✓Free consultations to plan placement and pricing.

A tattoo is a collaboration. At Blended Ink Studio, your artist sits down with you before any needle touches skin, sketching and refining until the design feels like it was always meant to be there. That unhurried process is the whole point of the studio: custom work, done carefully, in a space that puts you at ease.

“The best tattoos start as conversations. We listen first, then we draw.”

From bold color pieces to soft black and grey shading, the studio handles a wide range of styles. Bring reference images, a rough concept, or simply a feeling, and the artist will translate it into line and tone.

Cover ups and reworked older tattoos are a specialty. If you have ink that no longer fits, a consultation can map out how to transform it into something you love again.

Hygiene is non negotiable. Single use needles, hospital grade sterilization, and a spotless workstation come standard with every appointment.

First timers are welcome. Your artist will walk you through placement, sizing, pain expectations and aftercare, so you leave the chair confident as well as inked.

No. 03 · About

Small studio, serious craft.

Blended Ink Studio keeps things personal. As a boutique studio on Young Avenue, every appointment gets real one on one time: a proper consultation, a design drawn for your body, and an artist who stays with your piece from stencil to healed result.

“Custom means yours. No two pieces leave this studio alike.”

The studio works across styles rather than forcing one look. Color, black and grey, fine line and illustrative work all share the same foundation: careful drawing, honest advice about what will age well, and meticulous execution.

Clients are encouraged to bring ideas in any form, from polished references to rough sketches to a story they want told in ink. The consultation shapes those raw materials into a design with the right scale and placement.

Cleanliness and comfort round out the experience. Expect a calm room, clear communication about each step, and aftercare you can actually follow.
