Our story

A local contractor,
built for a city of microclimates.

TOP AC Inc was founded in 2011 by Itai Kolet, with a simple observation: Los Angeles is not one climate, it is many. Northridge runs hotter than Pasadena. Studio City has different building stock than North Hills. So the company built a service operation that understands those differences and responds to them.

Today the company is headquartered at 21201 Victory Blvd, Suite 102, in Canoga Park, and provides residential and commercial HVAC repair, installation, and maintenance across more than 30 Los Angeles neighborhoods. TOP AC Inc is a certified Mitsubishi Electric Diamond Dealer and a CSLB-licensed contractor, license numbers 959168 and 1086117.

Customers know the company for transparent pricing and same-day availability: the technician explains the real problem, fixes it properly, and charges what was quoted.
