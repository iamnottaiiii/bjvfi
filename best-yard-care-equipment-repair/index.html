Our Story

A Local Shop Since 2009

Two trades, one promise: done right.

Best Yard Care & Equipment Repair is a Newtown, Ohio business with roots going back to 2009. What started as yard care grew into a shop that also keeps the mowers and machines behind those yards running strong.

Customers describe the team as professional, punctual, and fair, saying the company finishes on time and genuinely cares about doing a great job and what the customer thinks.

The business holds an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau and a 4.8 star rating across 22 online reviews. It is listed locally as a landscaper and gardener, and its name says the rest: if it cuts grass or keeps the grass cut, they handle it.
