Our story

A little shop with
a lot of stories

Odd-N-Commen's sits on Pike Street in Bromley, Kentucky, a small river town where everybody's stuff has a past and deserves a future.

Who we are

Secondhand, first-rate

We are a consignment shop, which means the shelves are stocked by the community itself. Your neighbor's dining set, someone's barely-used lamp, a box of vinyl from a very cool basement: it all passes through here on its way to a second life.

Consignment keeps good things in circulation. It keeps money in Bromley instead of sending it to a warehouse across the country. And honestly, it is just more fun than buying new.

Stop by, dig around, and ask us about anything with a tag on it. If we know its story, we will tell you. If we don't, you get to make one up.

Buy the weird lamp. You will regret the ones you left behind far more than the ones you brought home.
