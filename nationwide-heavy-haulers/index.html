About Us

The short version of who we are and what we do.

Who We Are

Nationwide Heavy Haulers is a transportation service based at 524 Prestley St in Carnegie, Pennsylvania. We focus on the loads other carriers turn down: heavy, oversized, and hard to move.

What We Do

We haul heavy equipment and oversized freight across the country. Every move is planned around the load, with the right trailer, the right permits, and clear communication from pickup to delivery.

Why It Matters

Your equipment is your livelihood. We treat every load like it is the most important one on the road, because to you, it is.
