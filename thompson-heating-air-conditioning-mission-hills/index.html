Our Story

The local shop that treats your house like its own

Thompson Heating & Air Conditioning Mission Hills grew out of a simple frustration: too many HVAC companies in the Valley sent techs who upsold first and diagnosed second. Our founders wanted a shop where the phone gets answered by someone local, the tech arrives in a marked truck, and the price on the estimate is the price on the invoice.

Today we serve Mission Hills and the surrounding San Fernando Valley with the same rule we started with: explain the problem, show the options, and only recommend what the home actually needs.
