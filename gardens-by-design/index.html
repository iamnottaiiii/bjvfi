About Gardens By Design

A landscape design and build company in Shrewsbury, MA, known for low-maintenance, creative and native landscape solutions.

Our approach

Artistry, grounded in horticulture

Gardens By Design specializes in low-maintenance, creative and native landscape solutions for residential properties and public spaces. The work pairs an artist's eye for color and composition with deep knowledge of perennials and plants that genuinely thrive here.

Clients describe an easy, collaborative process: you are brought into the design of your own garden, so the result feels personal, not imposed.

  • Innovative designs with attention to detail
  • Unusual plant applications and perennial expertise
  • A collaborative process, from first sketch to final planting
Artistic preview
How we work

Two ways to build your garden

Full-service design and build: we design the garden and see it through, hardscape, planting, furniture and artistic elements, to a finished landscape.

Design you install yourself: prefer to do part of the work on your own? We are happy to create the plan and plant palette while you take on the parts you enjoy.

Either way, the garden is designed for the long term: plants that suit the site, color through the seasons, and maintenance you can actually keep up with.
