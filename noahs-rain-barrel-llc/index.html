Why rain barrels

Your plants prefer it, and so does your water bill

Rainwater is naturally soft and free of the chlorine and minerals in tap water. Many gardeners find their plants respond better to it, and every gallon you catch is a gallon you do not pay for.

It also keeps stormwater out of the storm drains, which helps local creeks and rivers stay cleaner.
