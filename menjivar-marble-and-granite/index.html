About the business

A licensed stone shop in Sun Valley

What public records tell us about Menjivar Marble And Granite, stated plainly. No invented history, no filler.

Menjivar Marble And Granite is a California licensed contractor (CSLB license #1019957, issued October 2016) operating a marble and granite shop at 12281 Branford St Ste B in Sun Valley, California. Branford Street sits in the heart of the San Fernando Valley stone trade, surrounded by slab yards and fabrication shops serving greater Los Angeles.

The shop works in natural marble and granite: kitchen countertops, bath vanities, fireplace surrounds, and custom cut pieces. Fabrication happens in-house, which means templates, cuts, and polish are all handled by the same crew that installs the finished stone.
