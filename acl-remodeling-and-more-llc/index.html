How a job runs here

Every project moves in phases. You always know what stage your house is in and what comes next.

01PHASE

Tear out

Worn roofing, tired siding, failing decks, dated rooms: the old stuff comes off cleanly so the rebuild starts on a solid base.

02PHASE

Rebuild

New roof, new siding, new deck, rewired rooms. The listed trades (remodel, roofing, siding, decks, electric) go in during this phase.

03PHASE

Finish and walkthrough

Final fit, cleanup, and a walkthrough with you before signoff. Details like exact scope, pricing, and scheduling are confirmed by phone, so call to confirm.
