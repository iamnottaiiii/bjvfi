What we do

The service index

Six lines of work, one standard. Numbers below match the index on the home page.

01 Custom deck design

Your yard, your home, your budget. We plan the deck around how you will actually use it: morning coffee, weekend gatherings, room for the grill.

  • Layout, elevation and material planning
  • Wood and composite options walked through side by side
  • Permits and inspections handled for you

02 Deck construction

Ground-up builds with proper framing, footings and fastening. Built to hold up through Glendale summers and everything after.

  • New decks, deck expansions and multi-level designs
  • Wood and low-maintenance composite builds
  • Built-in benches, planters and lighting options

03 Patio covers

Shade that holds up. Solid and lattice patio covers that keep your deck or patio usable through the heat of the day.

  • Attached and freestanding patio covers
  • Solid-roof and lattice shade options
  • Low-maintenance materials that resist the elements

04 Deck repair and resurfacing

Not every tired deck needs to be torn out. We repair what is worth saving and resurface when the frame is still good.

  • Board replacement, structural repairs and re-fastening
  • Resurfacing over sound existing frames
  • Stain, seal and finish refresh work

05 Railings, stairs and gates

The details that make a deck safe and finished. Railings, stair builds and gate work that match your deck, not fight it.

  • Wood, metal and cable railing options
  • Stair builds and rebuilds
  • Gates and access points

06 Pergolas and shade structures

Open-air shade with structure behind it. Pergolas that define the space without closing it in.

  • Attached and freestanding pergolas
  • Arbors, trellises and garden structures
  • Retractable shade-ready framing
Typical project ranges (samples, call to confirm): deck repairs often start in the low hundreds; resurfacing and small builds commonly land in the low thousands; full custom decks and covers vary widely by size and material. Every project gets its own quote.
