Services

Flooring for work and home

Commercial flooring installation

Built for foot traffic, deadlines and budgets. We work with facility managers, contractors and business owners across Greater Cincinnati.

  • Carpet tile for offices and open workspaces
  • Sheet vinyl and LVT for retail and healthcare
  • Fast turnarounds, including short notice jobs
  • Transitions, base and finish details handled

Residential flooring installation

The same installation standards we bring to commercial jobs, sized for your home.

  • Carpet for bedrooms, stairs and living spaces
  • LVP and laminate for kitchens and basements
  • Tile for baths, entries and laundry rooms
  • Hardwood installation and refinishing options

We supply and install all of the following for commercial and residential projects.

  • Carpet and carpet tile
  • Vinyl sheet and luxury vinyl tile
  • LVP, luxury vinyl plank
  • Ceramic and porcelain tile
  • Hardwood flooring
