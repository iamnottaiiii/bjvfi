Our story

Decades of metal, one shop in Van Nuys

EDWARD'S SHEET METAL SUPPLY, Inc. is a full line duct fabrication company with over 39 years of experience in the trade. Our specialty is custom round and rectangular duct fabrication, and our shop at 7810 Burnet Ave has served contractors across Los Angeles and vicinity for decades.
