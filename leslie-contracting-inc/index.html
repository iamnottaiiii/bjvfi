A Contractor, Not a Broker

Leslie Contracting was founded in 1990 by Wm. Wayne Leslie, an Auburn University building science graduate with a decade of commercial construction experience before starting the firm.

We are a general contractor in the true sense: we employ skilled field personnel and own and operate our own equipment. We work as part of the owner, architect, and contractor team to bring every project online on schedule and within budget.

Why Owners Choose Leslie

  • Licensed in Georgia, Alabama, Florida, South Carolina, North Carolina, Tennessee, Mississippi, and Kentucky
  • Experienced project managers and commercial superintendents on every job
  • Self-performing crews with company-owned equipment
  • Church, school, medical, retail, and industrial project experience

Commercial Construction Capabilities

Churches and Houses of Worship

Schools and Educational

Classroom buildings, gymnasiums, and campus improvements delivered around academic calendars and tight summer windows.

Medical and Office

Retail and Commercial

Industrial and Warehouse
