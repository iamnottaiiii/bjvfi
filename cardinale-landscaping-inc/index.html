Our Story

A local crew that treats your yard like their own

Cardinale Landscaping Inc is a landscaping and gardening company based at 37 Croak Avenue in Staten Island, NY. Led by Pete and his crew, the company serves homeowners across the borough with lawn care, cleanups, and garden work.

Customers know Cardinale for reliability and thoroughness. Reviewers describe the crew coming out after a renovation and a long winter, cleaning the backyard completely, going above and beyond, and leaving no corner untouched.

The company holds a 5.0 rating from customer reviews and operates Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Call (718) 605-7500 to talk through your yard.
