Our story

A 49th Street original

Maya's Beauty Salon, Inc. was incorporated in New York in 2004, and we have been welcoming Borough Park into our chairs at 1573 49th St ever since. We are a full-service beauty salon: haircuts, styling and color, manicures and pedicures, facials and skincare, all under one roof.

i.

Experienced stylists

A skilled, steady-handed team that listens first and cuts second, so you leave with the look you actually asked for.

ii.

Welcoming to all

A family salon in the truest sense: comfortable for kids, parents and grandparents alike, with walk-ins always welcome.

iii.

Fair neighborhood prices

Quality work at prices that make sense for the neighborhood. Ask about package pricing when you call.
