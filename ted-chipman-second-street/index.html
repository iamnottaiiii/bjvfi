A look inside the shop at 103 George St

Ted Chipman Second Street is a barber shop on Second Street in Aurora, Indiana, at 103 George St. It is a one-barber operation: Ted himself, cutting hair in the traditional style.

Public reviews paint a consistent picture. Customers describe a friendly, talkative barber who gives great traditional haircuts and does the cut you ask for. Several reviewers have been coming since they were kids, and one credits Ted with giving his son his very first haircut.

The shop is listed as a barber shop, hair salon, and hair stylist in public directories. Tips from directory listings mention walk-ins are welcome and the shop is good for kids. Call to confirm
