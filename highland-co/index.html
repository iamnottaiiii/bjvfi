What We Do

Heating and Cooling Services

Pick a category below. Every job starts with a clear diagnosis and an upfront price before work begins.

Air Conditioning Repair

Diagnosis and repair for systems blowing warm air, short cycling, leaking, freezing up, or not turning on. We service most major brands and explain repair versus replace honestly.

AC Installation and Replacement

Right-sized new systems for replacements and new construction, installed with proper refrigerant charge, airflow checks, and thermostat setup.

AC Tune-Ups

Seasonal checkups covering coils, refrigerant levels, electrical connections, condensate drains, and controls so summer does not catch you off guard.

Furnace Repair

No heat, weak heat, odd smells, or a furnace that keeps shutting off. We troubleshoot gas and electric furnaces and get heat back safely.

Heat Pumps

Heat pump service and replacement for year round heating and cooling in one system, a popular fit for Maryland shoulder seasons.

Heating System Replacement

When repairs stop making sense, we size and install efficient replacements and haul the old equipment away.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled seasonal visits that keep warranties valid, efficiency high, and surprise breakdowns rare.

Thermostat Upgrades

Smart and programmable thermostats that cut energy waste and let you control comfort from your phone.

Indoor Air Quality

Filtration upgrades, humidity control, and duct evaluations for cleaner, more comfortable air inside.

Our Story

A Local HVAC Crew in Annapolis Junction

Highland Co. is a heating and air conditioning contractor based at 10140 Junction Dr in Annapolis Junction, Maryland. We serve homes and businesses in the surrounding area with repair, maintenance, and installation work.

Our approach is simple: show up on time, diagnose carefully, explain the options in plain language, and stand behind the work. We open our doors seven days a week, from 7:30 AM to 7:00 PM, because heating and cooling problems do not check the calendar.

Whether it is a furnace that quit on a January night or an AC that gave up in August, the goal is the same: get your comfort back with honest pricing and clean, careful workmanship.
