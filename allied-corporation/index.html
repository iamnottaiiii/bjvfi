About

Allied Corporation

Allied Corporation began as a material production company of Northern Ohio Paving and has grown into a leading Ohio producer of asphalt, ag lime, limestone, ready mix, and sand and gravel. Since joining The Shelly Company in 2000, Allied has expanded to multiple plants and mines across Ohio, with a stated commitment to sustainability, quality, and community engagement.
