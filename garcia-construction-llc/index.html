Our story

A local crew with an old fashioned work ethic

Garcia Construction llc is a locally owned and operated company based in Glen Burnie, Maryland. With 11 years of experience in the trades, we have built our name one project at a time, earning repeat calls and referrals across Anne Arundel County.

We offer a variety of home improvement services customized to each individual project, from remodeling and flooring to painting, decks, doors, and trim. No two homes are the same, so no two jobs get the same cookie cutter treatment.

What has not changed in 11 years is the standard we hold ourselves to: we value honesty and integrity in all aspects of our business, we pride ourselves on the quality of our work, and we guarantee your satisfaction. We look forward to building a lasting relationship with you and your home.

Se habla espanol. We are glad to walk your project with you in English or Spanish.
