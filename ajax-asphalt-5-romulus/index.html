About the Plant

AJAX ASPHALT #5 ROMULUS

AJAX ASPHALT #5 ROMULUS is the Romulus, Michigan hot mix asphalt facility of Ajax Paving Industries, located at 8744 Inkster Rd on an 18-acre site near I-94 and Detroit Metro Airport. The plant produces paving-grade hot mix asphalt and is the company's highest-producing asphalt facility. Ajax Paving Industries was founded in 1951.
