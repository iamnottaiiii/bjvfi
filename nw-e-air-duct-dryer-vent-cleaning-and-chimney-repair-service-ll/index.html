Most homeowners never see inside their ductwork, but it touches every breath the family takes. Dust, allergens, and debris collect in the runs over the years, dragging down airflow and indoor air quality. NW&E exists for one job: to open those hidden pathways and leave them clean.

Clean ducts, clear vents, and a sound chimney are three of the cheapest ways to protect a home.

Air duct cleaning. A detailed cleaning of supply and return ducts that standard household cleaning cannot reach, supporting better airflow and heating and cooling efficiency.

Dryer vent cleaning. Clothes taking longer to dry and heat building up in the laundry area are classic signs of lint buildup. Clearing the full vent run helps the dryer work safely and use less energy.

Chimney repair. Cracks, blockages, and worn components affect draft and safety. Inspection and repair work restores proper function and helps guard against smoke backup, moisture, and carbon monoxide.

Signs it is time to call

Dust returns to furniture days after cleaning, or some rooms never quite warm up. Both point to ducts that need attention.

The dryer needs two cycles for one load, or the laundry room feels hot and damp. That is lint telling you the vent is restricted.

A fireplace that smokes into the room, a cracked crown, or a missing cap means the chimney deserves an inspection before the next fire.
