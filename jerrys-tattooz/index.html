The oldest working tattoo shop in Bucks County

"Jerry has the oldest working tattoo shop in Bucks county and maybe all of Pennsylvania." That is how the shop describes itself, and the review history backs up the longevity: clients who started in the 80s and 90s still trust Jerry with their ink.

We do all styles, in color to black and grey: custom pieces, traditional work, and cover-ups that turn old regrets into new favorites.

  • Cover-up specialist. Old tattoos reworked into art you will want to show off.
  • Color that lasts. Clients report bright, crisp color years later.
  • Clean and comfortable. A welcoming shop, good for first-timers.

Jerry

"Jerry has been creating my tattoos since the 80's. He does a great job. The shop is clean and comfortable." That is a client speaking, not an ad. It is the theme of nearly every review the shop has: decades of experience, a steady hand, and ink that still looks fresh years later.

Jerry does all styles, in color to black and grey, and is especially known for cover-ups: turning old, tired tattoos into pieces clients are proud to show off.

  • Decades of experience. The oldest working tattoo shop in Bucks County, in the shop's own words.
  • Listens first. Clients praise his willingness to work with them until the design is right.
  • Fair prices. High-quality work without breaking the bank, per client reviews.
