About

A Cincinnati plastering family since 1968

History below is based on the business's own description in public directory listings.

The trade, passed down three generations

Ken Hall Plastering has been offering quality plaster, stucco, and ornamental repair to Cincinnati and surrounding areas since 1968. With over 40 years in the business, the family brings what it calls third-generation skills, trade, and expertise to every job.

The work is residential and hands-on: stucco repair for the home, crack repair, and ornamental repairs that are meant to look like new when finished. Homeowners describe the service in public reviews as timely, clean, and affordable.

The business is listed as a plastering contractor at 3335 Glenhurst Pl in Cincinnati's Hyde Park area. Directory listings carry the full name Ken Hall Plastering.
