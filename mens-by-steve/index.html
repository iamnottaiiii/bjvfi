About

Steve’s chair, Topton’s barbershop

Mens By Steve is a full service barber on North Home Avenue in Topton, PA, known for great cuts at low prices.

THE SHOP

A neighborhood barber, the real thing

Mens By Steve sits at 16 N Home Ave in Topton, PA, a classic full service barbershop where the work does the talking. Guests describe great haircuts at low prices, the kind of value that turns first timers into regulars.

It is the kind of shop whole families end up at: one reviewer started bringing his son, and then his husband started going too. The verdict from the chairs: nobody has been disappointed.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Straightforward and skilled

No gimmicks, no upsell, just solid barber work. Come in, say what you want, leave looking sharp.
