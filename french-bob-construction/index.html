About the builder

Robert French, mason and builder

Public contractor records list Bob French Construction Inc as holding California General Building and Masonry licenses, with a BuildZoom score in the top quarter of California licensed contractors. The Blue Book has carried the listing since 1992.

The work is straightforward: reinforced concrete, flatwork, brick and block, the structural masonry that homes and yards are built on. No franchise, no rotating crews, the person who quotes your job is the person who lays your block.

Based on Ocean View Blvd in Glendale's foothill neighborhoods, the company works where hillside masonry matters most: retaining walls, foundations, and drainage that have to be right the first time.
