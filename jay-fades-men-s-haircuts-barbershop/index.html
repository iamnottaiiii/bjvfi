About Jay Fades: Men's Haircuts & Barbershop

Jay Fades on West 6th Street keeps the focus where the name says it: men's haircuts and fades done right. A straightforward shop for a sharp, modern cut.

Every visit follows the same simple rhythm: a real consultation about what you want, careful work in the chair, and a finish you can feel good about. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
