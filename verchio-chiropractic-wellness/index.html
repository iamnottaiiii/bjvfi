About

The doctor behind the cottage door

Dr. Julia Verchio opened Verchio Chiropractic and Wellness with a simple idea: chiropractic care works best when patients feel at home. Her office on Fries Mill Road is known locally as the Chiropractic Cottage, a private, well-lit space with a home-like waiting room and plenty of parking.

As a Certified Chiropractic Sports Physician, Dr. Verchio brings advanced training in athletic injury care to everyday patients too. Whether you are chasing a personal record or just chasing your kids around the yard, you get the same careful, evidence-minded approach.

Excellent care for migraines, rehabilitation, pain management, nutritional guidance, and exercise therapy, for all ages and body types.

Treatment blends hands-on adjustments with modern therapies like electrical muscle stimulation and ultrasound, plus practical rehab exercises and nutrition guidance you can actually stick with.
