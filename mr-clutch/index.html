The work

Built around the drivetrain

Most shops do a little of everything. We focus where it counts: the parts that put power to the pavement.

01 · Clutch replacement

Slipping, grabbing, or gone

A worn clutch shows up as slipping under load, a pedal that grabs at the top, grinding gears or a burning smell on hills. We diagnose it, pull the transmission, and fit a complete clutch kit.

  • Clutch disc, pressure plate and release bearing
  • Pilot bearing and rear main seal inspection
  • Road tested before you pick it up

02 · Flywheel resurfacing

Machine shop precision

As an auto machine shop, we resurface flywheels in house so the new clutch mates to a perfectly flat surface. That means smoother engagement and longer clutch life.

  • Flywheel resurfacing and inspection
  • Hot spots, cracks and warpage checked
  • Replacement when resurfacing is not enough

A Clutch service

  • Complete clutch replacementCall for priceDisc, pressure plate and release bearing, fitted as a matched kit.
  • Clutch diagnosisCall for priceSlipping, chatter, hard pedal or no disengagement, we find the cause.
  • Performance and heavy duty clutchesCall for priceUpgraded kits for towing, tuned cars and hard use. Ask what fits your car.

B Flywheel & machining

  • Flywheel resurfacingCall for priceMachined flat in house for smooth engagement and long clutch life.
  • Flywheel replacementCall for priceWhen a flywheel is cracked, heat checked or past spec, we replace it.
  • Pilot bearing & rear main sealCall for priceReplaced while the transmission is out, the smart time to do it.

C Hydraulics & linkage

  • Clutch master cylinderCall for priceDiagnosis and replacement when the pedal goes soft or stays down.
  • Clutch slave cylinderCall for priceLeaks and failures fixed, system bled and tested.
  • Clutch cable serviceCall for priceAdjustment and replacement for cable operated clutches.

D Drivetrain support

  • Transmission mountsCall for priceWorn mounts cause clunks and vibration on shifts.
  • Axles & CV jointsCall for priceClicking on turns or torn boots, inspected and replaced.
  • Manual transmission serviceCall for priceFluid changes and linkage checks for stick shift gearboxes.
