About Atlantis Beauty Salon & Barber


Atlantis Beauty Salon & Barber is a full service beauty salon and barbershop in Maywood. From precision haircuts and color to styling for special occasions, walk in and leave looking your best.


Every appointment starts with a consultation: the look you want, the upkeep you prefer, and the details that matter to you. The result is hair that looks great on day one and grows out clean.


Find us at 6025 S Atlantic Blvd, Maywood, CA 90270, or call (323) 773-2405 to book your chair.
