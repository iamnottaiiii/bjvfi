Services

Roofing services, done completely.

One reviewer noted that where other roofers wanted a separate contractor for fascia, starter boards and sagging rafters, Aloha included all of it in one fair price. That is the standard here: the whole job, not just the shingles.

New Roof Installation

Complete tear-off and replacement, including related carpentry like fascia and starter boards so the new roof sits on solid wood.

Roof Repairs

Targeted repairs for leaks and storm or age damage, plus fixes to underlying boards and rafters when they need attention.

Roof Inspections and Estimates

A real look at your roof's condition with honest findings. Estimates are free, call to schedule one.

Re-Roofing

When a roof is past patching, a full re-roof done in one coordinated project, mindful of your landscaping along the way.

Maintenance

Periodic checkups that catch small issues early and extend the life of your roof.

Related Carpentry

Fascia, starter boards and rafter repairs handled in-house as part of the roofing job, not handed off.
