OUR STORY

A Glenshaw carrier with a simple promise.

PA

Rooted on William Flinn Hwy

BMB Logistics operates from 1101 William Flinn Hwy in Glenshaw, Pennsylvania, minutes from the Pittsburgh freight corridors. We started with a simple observation: shippers were tired of chasing brokers for answers. So we built a carrier where the person who quotes the load is the person who moves it.

Today we run full truckload and regional freight for manufacturers, distributors, and businesses that cannot afford late deliveries. Our dispatchers know the lanes, our drivers know the docks, and our customers know exactly who to call.

How we work

Every shipment starts with a written quote: rate, fuel, and accessorials spelled out. Then one dispatcher owns the load from pickup to delivery.

What we believe
