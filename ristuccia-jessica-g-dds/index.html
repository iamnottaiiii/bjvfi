About Dr. Ristuccia.

A dentist Bedford families stay with for decades.

Dr. Jessica G. Ristuccia, DMD, is a licensed Massachusetts dentist (license DN1855706) practicing at 56 Great Road in Bedford. As a general and family dentist, she is the primary dental care provider for patients of all ages, responsible for diagnosis, treatment and coordination of each patient's oral health needs.

Her approach is personal and unhurried: explain the procedure, put patients at ease, and pay attention to every detail. Reviewers call her a painless dentist and praise how she makes dental stress disappear.
