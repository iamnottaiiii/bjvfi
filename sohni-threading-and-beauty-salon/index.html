Why threading

An ancient technique, loved for modern reasons. Here is what makes it special.

✦

Gentle on skin

Threading uses no chemicals and no heat, just a twisted cotton thread. It is a favorite for sensitive skin.

◉

Precise lines

Each hair is targeted individually, so brows come out crisp, clean, and shaped to your face.

❤

Lasts for weeks

Hair is removed from the root, so smooth results typically last two to four weeks.

☼

Quick visits

Most threading services take only minutes, easy to fit into a lunch break or errand run.

✨

Full face care

Pair threading with waxing and facials for complete, glowing results in one visit.
