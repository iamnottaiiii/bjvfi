About us

A Mass Ave neighborhood staple.

J & D Cleaners serves Arlington from 144 Massachusetts Avenue, a few blocks from the town center corridor. It is the kind of shop where the work is steady, the turnaround is reliable, and the counter service is personal.

Dry cleaning and laundry are simple services that only work when the details are right: stains treated before cleaning, garments inspected before bagging, and orders ready when promised. That is the standard we hold ourselves to.
