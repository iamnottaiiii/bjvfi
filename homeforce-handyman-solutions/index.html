About HomeForce

A Glendale handyman service built on a simple idea: show up, do careful work, and leave the place better than we found it.

The short version

HomeForce Handyman Solutions serves homeowners in Glendale, California, from our base on Aristo Street. We take on the repair and improvement jobs that are too small for a general contractor but too important to ignore: the sticking door, the wall that needs patching, the TV that still is not mounted, the room that needs fresh paint.

Our approach is practical. We look at the whole list, not just one job, so a single visit can clear several items at once. We bring our own tools, protect your floors and furniture while we work, and clean up before we leave.

  • Residential repairs and installations
  • Painting, drywall, and finish work
  • Furniture and fixture assembly
  • Straightforward scheduling by phone
