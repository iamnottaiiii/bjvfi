About the Shop

An atelier for everything you sit on

Quality Upholstery & Trim Shop is a Cincinnati upholstery shop at 7021 Harrison Ave, serving Cincinnati and the surrounding areas. The shop handles furniture, automotive, and marine upholstery under one roof, along with convertible tops, headliners, window tint, and car audio.

Customers know the team by name. Reviews repeatedly praise Mo, Joey, and Joe for master-level reupholstery, careful fabric matching, quick turnarounds, and for answering the phone every time.
