Good to know

Signs your roof
wants attention

Catching these early is almost always cheaper than catching them late.

Missing or lifted shingles

Wind and age break the seal. Exposed underlayment is a countdown to a leak.

Stains on ceilings

Brown rings inside mean water is already getting through. Trace it back to the source fast.

Granules in the gutters

Shingle grit washing off in quantity means the weathering layer is wearing thin.

Sagging or soft spots

Decking that gives underfoot signals rot or structural trouble underneath.

Flashing pulling away

Chimneys, vents, and valleys are leak magnets when flashing loosens or cracks.

Roof older than 20 years

Most asphalt roofs are on borrowed time past two decades. An inspection tells you where you stand.
