Our story

A Jacksonville builder, plain and simple

Superior Contractors is a construction company based at 11017 Hood Rd in Jacksonville, Florida. We work on homes: building them, adding to them, and bringing older ones back to life.

The public record tells part of the story. Jacksonville permit filings show Superior Contractors LLC as the contractor on residential projects valued at $750,000 and $950,000, the kind of high-value custom work that only happens when a crew can be trusted with the details.

Our approach has not changed: show up, communicate clearly, build it right the first time. No sales games, no disappearing acts, no surprise charges. Just construction work done properly.
