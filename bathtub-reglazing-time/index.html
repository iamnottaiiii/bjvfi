Our process

Strict guidelines, quality materials, and a tidy job site. Here is what a typical reglazing visit looks like.

1

Deep clean and prep

Every trace of soap scum and residue comes off, and the surface is etched so the new coating bonds.

2

Repair chips and cracks

Damage is filled and sanded perfectly smooth so it vanishes under the new finish.

3

Mask and ventilate

Your bathroom is masked off and ventilated to protect everything except the tub.

4

Prime and coat

Professional bonding agent and top-grade coating are applied in smooth, even layers.

5

Cure and final check

The finish cures to a hard, glossy surface, then we walk it through with you before we go.
