Our story

one promise

Cunningham Cleaning Inc started in Medford in 2011 with professional steam cleaning equipment and a simple idea: treat every carpet like it is in your own home. Fifteen years later, that is still the whole business plan.

We are a small local team based at 11 Wildwood Rd in Medford, serving homes across the area, Monday through Friday. When you call (781) 396-7823, you talk to us, not a call center.
