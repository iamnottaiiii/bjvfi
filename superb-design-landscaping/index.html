Who we are

Small crew, big transformations

Superb Design & Landscaping is a landscaping business based on Northwoods View Road in West Hills, California. We take on the projects that change how a home feels: the dead backyard, the unusable slope, the front garden that should have been a driveway all along.

What customers notice, in their own words: speed without sloppiness. One backyard went from dry dirt to a green sitting area in 3 days. Another job, a front garden converted to a driveway, was finished fast with a team described as professional, reliable, helpful, and friendly.
