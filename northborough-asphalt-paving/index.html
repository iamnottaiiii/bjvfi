Our services

Asphalt paving & sealcoating

Residential driveway work done right: solid base, proper drainage, clean edges, and a finish that holds up to New England winters.

Driveway Installation

Complete new-driveway builds: excavation, gravel base, grading for drainage, and fresh hot-mix asphalt, rolled and compacted.

Resurfacing & Replacement

Overlay a worn surface with a new top course, or tear out and replace a driveway that is past saving. We will tell you honestly which one you need.

Sealcoating

A fresh sealcoat protects against sun, water, and freeze-thaw damage while restoring that rich black look. Recommended every few years.

Crack Repair & Patching

Cracks and potholes filled and sealed before small problems become big ones. Targeted repairs that extend the life of your asphalt.

Driveway Extensions

Need more parking space? We widen or extend your existing driveway and blend the new asphalt seamlessly with the old.

Grading & Drainage

Water pooling against your foundation or garage? We regrade and correct the slope so water runs off instead of sitting on your asphalt.

Our approach

Do the base right, and the top lasts

Anyone can dump hot asphalt on the ground. What makes a driveway last is what is underneath it: a compacted gravel base, the right pitch, and drainage that moves water away instead of letting it pool.

That is how we work every job. We take the time on prep, we finish edges by hand, and we leave your property clean when we roll out.
