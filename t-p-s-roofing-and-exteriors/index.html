How we work

Straightforward, start to finish

Step one

We look at the whole roof and tell you what it actually needs. Repair when repair makes sense, replacement when it does not. No pressure, no mystery line items.

Every step is checked with the homeowner before we move on. When the crew leaves, the yard is spotless and the roof is under warranty.

Our story

A veteran owned crew with a simple standard

T.P.'s Roofing & Exteriors started with a simple idea: treat every roof like it is over your own family. That means showing up on time, explaining the work in plain language, pricing it fairly, and leaving the property cleaner than we found it.

The business is veteran owned, and that discipline shows in the work. Customers describe Tommy and the team as communicative, detail focused, and easy to work with, from trim and flashing to full roof replacements and gutters.

Based in Florence, KY, the crew serves homeowners across Northern Kentucky, Northern Cincinnati suburbs, and nearby Indiana towns.
