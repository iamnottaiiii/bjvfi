Our story

An Amelia shop with forty years of winters behind it

Amelia Mechanical Services has been working on heating and cooling systems in the Amelia area since the 1980s, according to our public Porch business profile. That is four decades of furnaces, air conditioners, boilers, and ductwork across the east side of Cincinnati.

We are a BBB-accredited HVAC contractor based at 1250 W Ohio Pike in Amelia. Homeowners call us for honest diagnostics, careful installations, and maintenance that keeps systems running year after year.

No upsells, no scare tactics. Just a local crew that treats your home like it is our own and answers the phone when it matters.

For repairsNote the brand and model of your unit and what it is (or is not) doing. A photo of the nameplate helps.
For replacementsKnow the age of your current system and any comfort issues, like hot or cold rooms.
