About Us

A contractor built for complex buildings.

Our Story

Twenty-plus years of safer, smarter buildings.

FEI Enterprises, Inc. is a California-based contractor specializing in full-service low voltage systems, fire alarm installations, BDA/DAS/ERRCS communication systems, and smart building technology for residential, commercial, and mixed-use development.

The company was founded on the belief that complex infrastructure demands smart, scalable, and reliable solutions. From luxury custom homes to large multi-family developments and high-rise commercial buildings, FEI delivers end-to-end services covering system design, permitting, installation, testing, inspection, and long-term support.

Our background in electronics, construction, and development lets us approach every project holistically, blending functionality, technology, and long-term value.

Who We Serve

Trusted across the project team.

  • Developers planning residential and mixed-use projects
  • General contractors needing a low voltage partner
  • Property owners upgrading safety and automation
  • Public agencies with strict code and timeline demands

What sets us apart

FEI is especially known for fire alarm and emergency responder communication systems. Our team navigates the latest code requirements and jurisdictional approvals, including signal surveys and engineered drawings, for seamless implementation and approval from local authorities.
