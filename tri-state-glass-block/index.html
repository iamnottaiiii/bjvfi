Why glass block

Glass block lets daylight flood in while keeping your home private, secure, and energy smart. It never needs painting, never fogs like old single panes, and turns ordinary windows and walls into a design feature.

Natural light, real privacy

Frosted and patterned blocks pour in daylight while obscuring the view in. Ideal for bathrooms, basements, and street facing windows.

Security built in

Solid glass blocks set in mortar are far tougher to breach than a standard window, a favorite upgrade for basement and ground floor openings.

Low maintenance

No rot, no rust, no repainting. Glass block stands up to Cincinnati weather and wipes clean with glass cleaner.
