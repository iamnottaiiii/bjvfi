About us

A Rutland company,
built on heavy work

Martelli Construction Co is an excavating contractor based at 87 Main Street in Rutland, MA, serving the surrounding towns with excavation, site work and utility installation.

How we work

Excavation is unforgiving work, so we do it carefully: we mark utilities before we dig, we bench and slope for safety, we compact in lifts, and we leave the site graded and clean.

Permitted work like septic installs and utility lines gets done to plan and to code, because a failed inspection costs everyone time and money.
