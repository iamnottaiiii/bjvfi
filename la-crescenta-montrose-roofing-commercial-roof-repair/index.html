The local difference

A roofer who actually knows these neighborhoods

From the older ranch homes off Foothill to the commercial buildings along Honolulu, Crescenta Valley roofs face sun, Santa Ana winds, and winter rain. We work on them every day.

Most roof problems start small. A lifted shingle after a windstorm, a slow drip around a vent, ponding water on a flat commercial roof. Left alone, each one turns into sheathing damage, interior stains, and a bill that is several times larger than the original fix.

That is why we built this company around repair first. If your roof can be repaired, we will tell you. If it is time for replacement, we will show you exactly why, with photos and a written estimate, so you can decide with confidence.

"A good roof is the quietest part of your home. Our job is to keep it that way." La Crescenta-Montrose Roofing & Commercial Roof Repair

We handle both sides of the valley: family homes and the shops, offices, and warehouses that keep Montrose running. Commercial clients get the same straight answers, plus maintenance plans that catch small issues before they interrupt business.
