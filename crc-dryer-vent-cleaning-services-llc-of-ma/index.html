The problem

Lint is a fire hazard hiding in your laundry room

Every load pushes fine lint past the trap and into the vent line. Over time it packs the duct, chokes airflow, and forces the dryer to run hotter and longer. Clogged dryer vents are one of the leading causes of home laundry fires.

02
The fix

One thorough cleaning restores safe airflow

We open the vent line, remove packed lint from end to end, and verify air is moving freely again. Most homes only need this done about once a year, and the difference in drying time is immediate.

Warning signs

Call us if your dryer is telling you something

Clothes still damp after a full cycle, the top of the dryer hot to the touch, a musty smell in the laundry room, or lint building up around the outside vent hood. These are all signs the vent line is restricted.

Why it matters

Small vent, big consequences

A restricted vent makes your dryer work harder, dry slower, and run hotter than it should. Regular cleaning is one of the simplest fire-prevention steps a homeowner can take.

We keep it simple: show up, clean the vent thoroughly, check the airflow, and leave your laundry room tidy. If you'd like a quote or have questions about your vent, call and we'll talk it through.
