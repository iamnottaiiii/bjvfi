Our story

A Valley mason you can reach by phone

Lopez Mansonry is a masonry contractor based on Saticoy Street in Van Nuys, serving homeowners and builders across the San Fernando Valley.

The trade is simple and honest: mix the mortar right, lay every course level, and stand behind the work. That is how Lopez Mansonry approaches every block wall, brick facade, chimney and paver patio.

When you call (818) 625-7017, you talk to the people who will show up with the trowels. Estimates are free, scopes are written down, and jobsites are left clean.
