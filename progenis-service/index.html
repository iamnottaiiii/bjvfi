Regular Home Cleaning

The recurring clean that keeps your home guest-ready. Available weekly or biweekly.

  • Full dusting of all reachable surfaces
  • Vacuuming and mopping every room
  • Kitchen counters, sink, and appliance fronts
  • Bathroom toilets, tubs, and mirrors
  • Bed making and linen straightening
  • Trash removal and bin wipe-down

Deep Cleaning

The top-to-bottom reset for homes that need extra attention. Great before recurring service starts.

  • Baseboards, door frames, and trim
  • Ceiling fans, vents, and light fixtures
  • Window sills, tracks, and blinds
  • Grout and tile detail in baths
  • Behind and under furniture where reachable
  • Cabinet fronts and switch plates

Move In / Move Out

Empty-home deep cleans for renters, landlords, and anyone starting fresh in Lynn.

  • Full deep clean of the empty home
  • Inside cabinets, drawers, and closets
  • Appliance interiors on request
  • Deposit-ready finish for rentals

Extras and Add-ons

Small upgrades that make a big difference. Add any of these to your visit.

  • Inside the oven
  • Inside the refrigerator
  • Interior window washing
  • Laundry: wash, dry, and fold
  • Garage or basement tidy
  • Pet-area detail
