Our story

Comfort Pro's Heating & Air Conditioning Mission is a heating and air conditioning contractor based in Mission Hills, California, serving the San Fernando Valley.

The name says it all: comfort is the product. Valley summers push cooling systems to their limits, and winter nights still call for reliable heat. We built this business around being the local crew neighbors can call when the system quits, and the crew they keep calling for tune-ups year after year.

Every visit follows the same promise: show up, diagnose honestly, explain the options in plain language, and stand behind the work. No pressure, no jargon, no surprises on the invoice.
