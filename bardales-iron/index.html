Security that looks like it belongs.

A fence is the first thing people see and the last line of defense. Since 2012 we have designed and installed custom ironwork that does both jobs: steel gates that glide, fences that stand straight, and pedestrian gates sized to the inch. We serve homes and businesses throughout the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles County.

We put our customers first. You can count on us to make sure you are taken care of every step of the way.

It starts with a free quote and a site visit. We measure your openings, talk through styles, and recommend the steel and finish that fit your property and your budget.

Then we fabricate. Every piece is welded in our Panorama City shop, fitted, and finished before it ever reaches your property.

Installation is clean and precise. Gates are hung true, motors are calibrated, and our work is backed by a one-year warranty on fabrication and installation.

From wall toppers and security boxes to full motor gate systems, if it is iron, we build it.

Our Story

A fabrication shop built on trust and clean welds.

Fabricating in the Valley since 2012.

Bardales Iron is a metal fabrication shop at 9132 Kester Ave in Panorama City. Since 2012 we have served Los Angeles County as licensed, insured metal fabricators, and our rule has never changed: do not go the cheaper route. We build ironwork the right way, the first time.

We know what we are doing. We have been in this business for years, so we know how to get it done correctly and on time.

Most of our work is custom. A gate is only as good as its measurements, so we visit, we measure, and we fabricate each piece for its exact opening.

We keep pricing honest. A free quote up front, no surprise charges, and a one-year warranty on defects caused by fabrication or installation.

From the San Fernando Valley to Simi Valley, Calabasas, and Beverly Hills, our gates and fences are working every day across Los Angeles County.
