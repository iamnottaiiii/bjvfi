Our story

A local crew with a simple rule: do it right

Pacos Painting LLC is a Wilmington painting company built on word of mouth, one careful room at a time.

Pacos Painting LLC started the way good contractors start: with a brush, a truck, and a reputation that had to be earned house by house. Based on Sycamore Avenue in Wilmington, the crew serves homes across New Castle County, and most of its work still comes from customers who tell their neighbors.

The business is led by Luis, whose name comes up again and again in customer reviews for the same reasons: he answers fast, he shows up when he says he will, and he runs a crew that treats a stranger's home with care. Reviewers describe the team as professional, friendly, hard working, and respectful, and several mention that the crew moved all their furniture and covered everything before starting.

Four years in, the company carries a 5.0 rating on Thumbtack across 25 reviews, Top Pro honors in 2023 and 2024, and a background-checked badge. Six employees strong, it has been hired dozens of times for projects from single-room refreshes to whole-home repaints finished in two days.

The work itself stays classic: thorough prep, quality paint, crisp lines, and a walkthrough at the end. No shortcuts in the middle. That is the whole business plan, and the reviews suggest it is working.
