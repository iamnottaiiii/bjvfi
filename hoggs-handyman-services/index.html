Services

One call covers
the whole list

Typical handyman work we take on around Lemoyne and the West Shore. Every job starts with a clear estimate.

01Drywall and paint touch-ups+

Nail holes, dents, and damaged patches repaired and blended, then finished with matching touch-up paint. Good for: move-out repairs, scuffed hallways, rooms that need a refresh without a full repaint.

02Mounting and assembly+

TVs, curtain rods, shelving, mirrors, and bathroom accessories mounted securely to studs or proper anchors. Flat-pack furniture assembled square and sturdy. Good for: new move-ins and rooms that finally need to feel finished.

03Doors, locks, and hardware+

Doors that stick or will not latch, loose handles, new deadbolts, hinge adjustments, and doorbell or knocker installs. Good for: rentals, older homes, and quick security upgrades.

04Minor carpentry+

Baseboards, trim, closet shelving, small repairs to stairs and railings, and built-ins that need a steady hand. Good for: finishing the details a remodel left behind.

05Caulking and weatherproofing+

Old caulk cut out and replaced around tubs, showers, windows, and exterior trim. Drafts and drips stopped at the source. Good for: bathrooms, kitchens, and pre-winter prep.

06Small exterior fixes+

Fence boards, mailbox posts, loose siding pieces, gutter re-securing, and entryway repairs. Good for: curb appeal and the little things neighbors notice.

About

A steady hand
for Lemoyne homes

Hogg's Handyman Services is a local handyman service working out of Lemoyne, Pennsylvania, serving homeowners and renters across the West Shore.

How it works

1. Call or reach out. Tell us what is broken, loose, or half-finished.

2. Get a straight estimate. Online estimates are available, so you know the plan before we arrive.

3. We fix it. Most to-do lists get knocked out in a single visit, clean and done right.

Why a handyman

Big contractors pass on the small stuff. We built this service around it: the odd jobs, the punch lists, the repairs that are too small for a crew but too important to ignore.

One person you can call for all of it, who shows up, communicates clearly, and leaves the place tidy.
