Savings Tips

Call first

Tire inventory turns over fast. A 60-second call confirms your size is on the rack before you drive over.

Ask about used pairs

Matching used pairs give even wear at a steep discount versus a single new tire. Ask what pairs are in stock today.

Check air monthly

Proper pressure adds thousands of miles to any tire, new or used, and saves gas money every week.

Rotate on schedule

Regular rotation evens out wear so a full set lasts longer. Ask the shop to rotate while your tires are off.
