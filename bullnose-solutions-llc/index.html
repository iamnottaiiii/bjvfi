The bullnose difference

Standard drywall corners are sharp and chip easily. Bullnose corners round the edge with a smooth radius that resists dings and gives rooms a softer, more finished look.

It is our namesake specialty, and it changes how a whole house feels.

What we do

Services

Every project below gets the same finish-first approach: straight studs, tight seams, and corners you can run your hand along.

01

Drywall installation

New drywall hung for additions, basements, garages, and full remodels. Hung tight, screwed to spec, and ready for a level finish.

02

Drywall repair and patching

Holes, cracks, water damage, and doorknob dents repaired so the patch disappears into the surrounding wall.

03

Tape and finish

Seams taped, coated, and sanded to a smooth, paint-ready surface. The finish work is where good drywall becomes invisible.

04

Bullnose corners

Rounded corner bead installed for soft, chip-resistant edges throughout a room or a whole home. Our signature detail.

05

Texture matching

Existing wall texture matched on repairs and additions so new work blends with old.

06

Ceiling repair

Sagging, stained, or damaged ceilings repaired and refinished to a clean, even surface.

How a project runs

1. Walkthrough. We look at the space, the existing walls, and what you want changed.

2. Straight quote. One number for the work described, before anything starts.

3. Clean work. Floors and furniture protected, dust kept down, debris hauled out.

4. Final look. We walk the walls with you in good light before we call it done.
