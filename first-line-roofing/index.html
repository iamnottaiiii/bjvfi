Our Story

A local roofing crew with a simple standard: inspect honestly, quote honestly, build honestly.

Valley born

First Line Roofing is based at 11780 Snelling St in Sun Valley, right in the middle of the San Fernando Valley. The roofs we work on face the same Valley heat, Santa Ana winds and winter rains we live with ourselves, so we build for exactly those conditions.


The name is the mission: your roof is the first line of defense for everything underneath it. We treat it that way.

No pressure, ever

Too many roofers lead with scare tactics and same-day-close pricing. We lead with a ladder, a camera and the truth. Our free inspection comes with photos of what we actually found, a written scope, and a price that does not change mid-job.


If your roof has years left, we will tell you that too.
