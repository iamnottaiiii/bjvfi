Our story

A Local Wrench, Not a Waiting Room

Bubbs Mobile Auto Repair was built on a simple idea: most car trouble happens away from the shop, so the shop should come to the car. Based in Niagara Falls, NY, we roll out to homes, workplaces, and roadsides across the area.

Customers know us as honest, down-to-earth, and persistent. Those are their words, pulled from public reviews: fair prices, straight explanations, and a willingness to go above and beyond, like helping a stranded traveler on a holiday or squeezing in a weekend starter replacement so someone could make it home.

We handle general repairs and maintenance plus tougher jobs like engine work, and we explain everything in plain language before we touch a bolt. One mechanic, one phone number, no runaround.
