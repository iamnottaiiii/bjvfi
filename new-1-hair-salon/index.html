Our story

An 11th Ave staple

New 1 hair salon sits at 6417 11th Ave in Brooklyn, NY 11219, serving the neighborhood along 11th Avenue. It operates as New 1 Hair Salon Inc and holds a New York State appearance enhancement business license.

Over the years it has built a loyal following. One guest writes that her family and friends have been coming for 13 years, calling the shop clean, the service great, and the team very welcoming. Others travel across the borough for a blowout, saying it lasts for days.

The details make the difference: long, comfortable hair washes with a scalp massage clients describe as unique, skilled cutting and styling, and color work, from balayage to all over color, that guests say always lands on point.
