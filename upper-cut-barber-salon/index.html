Our Story

San Pedro's unpretentious barbershop

Upper Cut Barber & Salon sits on Gaffey Street in the heart of San Pedro, a port town that likes things straightforward. So does the shop: no loud murals, no inflated prices, no pretense.

What you get is experience. The kind that comes from cutting thousands of heads over the years, regulars who have been coming for a decade or more, and a chair that treats everybody the same.

How We Work

Say what you want, get what you asked for

The barbers here cut a lot of heads every day. The house rule, learned from loyal clients: speak up. Show a photo, name the length, describe the fade. Clear direction in means a clean cut out.

It is a two-way street, and it is why regulars keep the chair warm.
