Materials

We use paints worth your money

Premium acrylic and enamel coatings from brands like Sherwin Williams and Benjamin Moore go further, cover better, and hold their color years longer than builder grade paint. We match the product to the surface and the exposure, and we are happy to explain why.

Low VOC and low odor options are available for homes with kids, pets, or sensitivities.
