About

Meet Dr. Mahmuda Hussain

Verified credentials

Dentist, Jamaica NY

Dr. Mahmuda Hussain, DDS is a general and cosmetic dentist practicing at The Dental Corner Care PC in Jamaica, Queens. She graduated from dental school in the mid 2000s and has more than 20 years of clinical experience.

Her clinical focus includes restorative, cosmetic, reconstructive and family dental procedures, with advanced training in dental implants, endodontics, prosthodontics and veneers, dentures, whitening and Invisalign.

She is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry and the Queens Dental Society, and completes over 100 continuing education credits each year.
