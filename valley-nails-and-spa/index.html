The experience

A quiet chair, just for you

Because visits are by appointment, you get real one-on-one time. No rushing, no waiting room crowd, just careful work on your hands and feet.

Why clients stay

Ten years of loyal regulars

Tammy built her following at other salons before opening Valley Nails & Spa, and clients followed. They praise the fair prices, the huge color wall, and how last-minute requests get a yes whenever possible.
