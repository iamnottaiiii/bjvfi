What we treat

Back pain, neck pain, and related musculoskeletal complaints are the heart of chiropractic care. Bring your films, your history, and your questions; we will start from what we can see and measure.

What to expect

First visits center on examination and diagnosis. Treatment plans are explained before they begin, and the friendly staff is always available to answer questions or address concerns along the way.

About the Practice

A neighborhood chiropractic office on the Upper West Side.

Our approach

West 79th Street Chiropractic practices a drug free, hands on approach to health care. Care may include patient examination, treatment through spinal manipulation, in office X Ray, blood work, and modern physical therapy modalities.

Our friendly and helpful staff is always available to answer any questions or address any concerns you may have. We provide flexible hours to accommodate your busy schedule, and appointments are recommended.
