Our story

The Queen City's neighborhood barber

Queen City Cuts is a Cheektowaga barber shop built around one thing: a great haircut from a barber who treats the chair like a craft. Don Lange runs the shop, and the client list reads like a neighborhood roll call.

"Best haircut in America. Don is the best."

Clients praise the later hours, the easy appointment availability, and the atmosphere. It is the kind of shop where regulars bring their sons, visitors stop in during tournaments, and everybody leaves with a plan to come back.
