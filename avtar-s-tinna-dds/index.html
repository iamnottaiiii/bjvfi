A practice patients recommend to friends

Online reviewers describe Dr. Tinna and his staff as wonderful, professional, and gentle. One parent wrote that her young son had never been to the dentist before and the team made him feel completely comfortable. Another longtime patient said the office treats people like people rather than just another body in the chair, and that she has told all her friends about it.

Honest about costs, respectful of your time

Patients say the front office shares cost estimates before treatment begins, and that final bills have come in at or below the estimate. The office is described as spotless, well lit, and kid friendly, with digital x-rays and TVs to keep you comfortable during longer visits.

Serving a diverse neighborhood

The office directory lists English, Bengali, Hindi, Punjabi, Spanish, and Urdu among the languages spoken at the practice, reflecting the Jamaica community it serves.
