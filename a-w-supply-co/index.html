Our story

Wilmington's fast, friendly tire stop

A & W Supply Co sits on Governor Printz Boulevard in Wilmington, serving drivers who need tires, brakes and repairs without the runaround.

The shop's reputation was built the simple way: good prices, quick turnaround, and a crew that is accommodating and patient with every car that rolls in. Reviews describe tire and brake changes finished in under 25 minutes, honest advice about car and truck parts, and service that is fast, polite and helpful.

Whether it is a pair of used tires to get you through the month or a full brake job, the approach is the same: walk in, get a straight answer, and get back on the road.
