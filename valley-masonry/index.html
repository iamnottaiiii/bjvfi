Repairs are half the trade

Not every job is new construction. Repointing crumbling mortar, rebuilding a leaning section, or fixing a chimney crown can add decades to existing masonry.

Repair work

Fix it before it fails

Masonry problems rarely get cheaper with time. A small crack this year is a rebuild in five. If your wall, chimney, or patio is showing its age, have it looked at while the fix is still small.
