What we do

One contractor for every wire in the building.

Phone lines, data drops, fiber backbones: most offices end up juggling three vendors for one network. Turn Around Communications Inc covers the full low-voltage scope, so moves, adds, and new build-outs stay on one ticket.

Based at 760 Arrow Grand Circle in Covina, the crew works across offices, retail, warehouses, and multi-tenant buildings.
