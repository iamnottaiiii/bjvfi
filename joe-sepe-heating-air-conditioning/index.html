About

Family owned in Chatsworth since the 1990s

Joe Sepe Heating & Air Conditioning is a family owned and operated HVAC contractor based at 10417 Oklahoma Ave in Chatsworth, serving the San Fernando Valley and Ventura County.

The company reports over 30 years of experience in the HVAC trade, with technicians certified to repair, service, and install all brands of heating and air conditioning equipment.
