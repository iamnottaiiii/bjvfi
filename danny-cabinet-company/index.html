About

Danny Cabinet Company LLC operates from 11983 Abbey Road in Cleveland, Ohio, building custom cabinetry and millwork for homes and businesses.

What the records say

The business is registered as Danny Cabinet Company LLC. Per its BBB profile, the business started on March 12, 2012, and BBB lists Misho Milovanovic as the owner. The shop describes its own work this way: commercial casework, exhibit manufacturing, production carpentry, luxury kitchens, baths, fitted furniture and millwork, servicing the commercial and residential industries for over 35 years.

The craft

Public listings describe a custom cabinet shop producing custom kitchens and baths in wood, with categories spanning cabinetry, countertops, kitchen and bath contracting, carpentry, and home improvement. Angi notes the business offers free estimates and emergency services, and describes over 25 years of building custom cabinetry and countertops for commercial and residential applications.
