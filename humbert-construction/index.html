Our Story
About Humbert Construction

A Shadow Hills Contractor Since 1981

Humbert Construction has been based at 9701 Sunland Blvd in Shadow Hills, California since its founding in 1981, when the firm earned California contractor license #415598. The company remains a single-location operation led by owner Humbert Arteaga.

Over the years the focus has settled on what the firm does best: multifamily renovations, capital improvement projects, and property maintenance repairs. The team partners with property management companies, developers, and investors across Los Angeles County, San Diego, and Orange County to deliver large-scale renovation and rehabilitation projects.

The operating philosophy is straightforward: quality work, compliance with local building codes, transparent communication, accurate cost control, and on-time project delivery.
