Welcome In

A salon with warmth in every chair

Walk through the doors at 700 Main St and you are greeted by name. Our stylists speak English and Spanish, and every visit is personal, from the first consultation to the final touch.

  • Family friendly salon and barbershop under one roof
  • Blowouts, color, cuts, styling and barber services
