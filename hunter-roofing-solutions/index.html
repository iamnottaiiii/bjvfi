Our story

Built on straightforward work.

Hunter Roofing Solutions is a general contractor based in Altadena, California, serving the San Gabriel Valley foothills. No sales games, no disappearing acts. Just honest roofing and contracting work.

One contractor for the whole roof, and what surrounds it.

Too many roofing jobs stall because the roofer will not touch the fascia, the painter will not touch the roof, and nobody coordinates. We started Hunter Roofing Solutions to be the contractor who handles the full picture.

As a general contractor with a roofing focus, we inspect honestly, estimate clearly, and show up when we say we will. Homeowners across Altadena, Pasadena, and the nearby foothill communities call us when they want the job done once and done right.

Every project starts with a free inspection and a written plan you can actually understand. That is the whole business model.
