Our Story

A Straightforward Shop for Everyday Laundry

Candid Cleaners has served North Haven from 104 Washington Ave for years, listed in local directories as a dry cleaning and laundry shop with delivery and alterations. The idea is simple: give the neighborhood one reliable place for both self service machines and full service garment care.

No gimmicks. Clean machines, fair dealing, and a counter that knows what it is doing. That is the whole business plan, and it works.
