Starting a Project

What to expect when you call

Every project starts the same way: a conversation about what you want the space to do. Bring your ideas, your must-haves and your budget range. From there, a good builder walks the property, talks through what is realistic, and puts a clear plan and price on paper before work begins.

Permits, scheduling, dust control and daily cleanup are the unglamorous parts that decide whether a project feels smooth. Ask any contractor how they handle each one, and listen for specifics.

Questions worth asking

  • Are you licensed and insured for this work?
  • Who will be on site day to day?
  • How do you handle permits and inspections?
  • What does the payment schedule look like?
  • How do you handle changes mid-project?

Working Together

Straightforward from day one

The builders worth hiring are the ones who answer plainly. What will it cost, how long will it take, who is on site, and what happens when something unexpected turns up behind a wall. Those answers should come before you sign anything, in writing, with room for your questions.

If you are comparing bids, compare the scope line by line, not just the bottom number. The cheapest bid with the vaguest scope is rarely the cheapest project.

Good signs in a builder

  • Detailed written scope and fixed milestones
  • Clear license and insurance documentation
  • A named person on site every workday
  • Written change-order process
  • References from recent, similar projects
