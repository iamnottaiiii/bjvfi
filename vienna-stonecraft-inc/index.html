Our story

A masonry specialist in Van Nuys

Vienna Stonecraft Inc is a masonry contractor based at 14654 Raymer St in Van Nuys, California. We are specialists: brick repair, bricklaying, and brickwork are our core, alongside custom stonework installation, decorative wall construction, brick flooring, paver installation, and concrete construction.

Masonry is one of the oldest trades, and it still rewards patience. We mix mortar the old way, check every course with a level, and prep the base before a single unit is set. The result is work that looks right on day one and stays right for decades.

  • Masonry specialist serving Van Nuys and the San Fernando Valley
  • Brick, stone, block, pavers, and concrete under one roof
  • Written bids before work begins
  • Residential repairs to full outdoor builds

How we work

1. Site visit. We come look, measure, and talk through options.

2. Written bid. Scope, materials, and price in writing before we start.

3. Craft. Base prep, level courses, clean joints, tidy site.

4. Walkthrough. We review the finished work with you, course by course.
