The designer
Placeholder portraitFL

Fiona Lau

After completing her Architecture and Interior Design program at UCLA, where she was selected as the winner of the James R. Northcutt Scholarship, Fiona's passion for interior design grew stronger than ever.

Before starting the company, she worked at a residential development company as a designer, an experience that broadened her view of construction and shaped her practical, buildable approach to design.

She draws inspiration from books, magazines, shops, and the people around her, and believes that by being flexible and a good listener, creative ideas are born. That openness lets her see each client's needs so designs can be tailored to meet their goals.
