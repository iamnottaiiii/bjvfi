Our story

One shop, one name,
since 1993

Dan Levesque Auto Body of Troy has been fixing cars in Troy for over three decades, as a small owner operated shop where the name on the door is the person doing the work.

The shop

Small by design

Big collision centers run on volume. Dan Levesque Auto Body runs on reputation. With a small crew at a single location on Wetsel Rd, every car that comes in gets personal attention from the owner himself.

The business has been at it since 1993, which means Dan has seen just about every kind of damage a Capital Region winter, a parking lot, or a highway can do to a car. That experience shows up in the estimate: what can be saved, what should be replaced, and what it will really cost.

It is the kind of shop where you talk to the person holding the wrench, and where the finished repair gets a walkaround before you drive away.
