About Aliso Barbershop DTLA

Aliso Barbershop DTLA sits on East 3rd Street in downtown Los Angeles, a convenient stop for anyone working or living nearby. The shop keeps things focused: clean cuts, steady hands, and a chair that is ready when you are.

Every visit follows the same simple rhythm: a real consultation about what you want, careful work in the chair, and a finish you can feel good about. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
