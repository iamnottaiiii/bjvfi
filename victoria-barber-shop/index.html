About Victoria Barber Shop

Victoria Barber Shop on East Garvey Avenue in Monterey Park is home to Dao, a barber with decades of experience. Reviewers describe quick, affordable cuts with a friendly touch, finished with the shop's well-known shoulder massage.

Every visit follows the same simple rhythm: a real consultation about what you want, careful work in the chair, and a finish you can feel good about. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
