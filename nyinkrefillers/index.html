What happens when you call

One call sets the whole recovery in motion. Here is the process, start to finish.

  1. 1
    You call, we listenWhere is the water, how fast is it spreading, and what is at risk. We talk you through shutting off the source.
  2. 2
    We inspect and measureOn-site moisture readings in walls, floors, and cavities, not just a glance. That tells us how far the water traveled.
  3. 3
    We extract and dryPumps and extractors pull standing water, then industrial air movers and dehumidifiers finish the job.
  4. 4
    We clean and restoreSanitizing, odor control, and repair of damaged materials, bringing the space back to pre-loss condition.

Mold starts fast. So do we.

Within 24 to 48 hours of standing water, mold can begin to grow inside walls and under floors. That is why the first call matters more than anything else. Shut off the water if you can, stay out of flooded electrical areas, and call us. We will take it from there.

What we do

Full water damage restoration

From the first pump to the final coat of paint. Pick a service below to see what is included.

Emergency Water Extraction

Standing water is the emergency. We remove it fast with truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment, then move straight into drying so damage does not spread.

  • Basement and crawl space pump-outs
  • Burst pipe and appliance leak response
  • Carpet, pad, and flooring water removal
  • Contents protection and move-out
Call for water extraction

Structural Drying and Dehumidification

Water hides in drywall, subfloors, and framing long after the surface looks dry. We place industrial air movers and dehumidifiers and verify with moisture meters until readings are back to normal.

  • Moisture mapping with meters and imaging
  • Industrial dehumidifiers and air movers
  • Daily drying logs and progress checks
  • Hardwood floor and cabinet drying
Call about drying

Mold Remediation

Where water sat, mold follows. We contain the affected area, remove compromised materials, HEPA-filter the air, and treat surfaces so the growth does not come back.

  • Containment to stop cross-contamination
  • Removal of affected drywall and materials
  • HEPA air filtration and surface treatment
  • Post-remediation moisture verification
Call about mold

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Sewage water is contaminated and unsafe to handle without protection. We remove the waste, disinfect every affected surface, and dry the area to sanitary condition.

  • Safe waste removal and disposal
  • Antimicrobial disinfection of surfaces
  • Removal of unsalvageable materials
  • Odor treatment and air cleaning
Call about sewage cleanup

Storm and Flood Damage

Heavy rain, coastal surge, and wind-driven water hit the Five Towns hard. We respond after storms with extraction, drying, and temporary protection so the next rain does not undo the work.

  • Post-storm flood water removal
  • Roof leak and window intrusion drying
  • Temporary board-up and tarping coordination
  • Debris clearing in affected areas
Call about storm damage

Repairs and Build-Back

Once everything is dry and clean, the rebuild starts: drywall, flooring, trim, and paint, so the room looks like the damage never happened.

  • Drywall replacement and finishing
  • Flooring and subfloor repair
  • Trim, baseboards, and paint
  • Final walkthrough and documentation
