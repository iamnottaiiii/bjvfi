Our story

A family company built on repeat work

Since 2015, Moretti Remodeling and Restoration has served Severn and Anne Arundel County with one simple standard: quality work at a fair price.

From investor rehabs to dream kitchens

Owner Leone Moretti started the company doing full property rehabs for real estate investors, work where budgets are firm, schedules are tight and there is no room for excuses. That discipline carried into residential remodeling, where the same crews now handle kitchens, bathrooms and complete home alterations.

We work with homeowners, property managers, real estate agents and structural engineers. Many of our projects come from repeat clients and referrals, which we take as the highest compliment in this business.

Our goal is straightforward: deliver quality work at an affordable price in an industry that can be unfair to customers. Free estimates, clear scopes, warranties on completed work, and one point of contact who answers.
