About the Shop

Suder Engine Services is an independent engine rebuilding machine shop at 7089 Thompson Rd in Monfort Heights, Cincinnati, Ohio. The business is listed publicly as an engine rebuilding service, with additional categories of fuel injection services, diesel repair, and gas engines.

Public records show the business has been operating in the area for many years, with a Better Business Bureau file dating to 2007. Over that time it has built a base of repeat customers, some reporting 15 plus years of bringing in cylinder heads, blocks, and full engines.

The shop's reputation in public reviews centers on three things: honest advice about whether an engine is worth rebuilding, careful diagnostic work, and fair pricing on machine shop labor.
