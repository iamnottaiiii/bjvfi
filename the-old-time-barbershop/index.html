Harrison's Own Old Time Barbershop: Classic Cuts on Stone Drive

Harrison, Ohio | Barbershop Gazette Staff

Right here on Stone Drive, The Old Time Barbershop keeps the classic barbershop tradition alive for Harrison, Ohio. Neighbors know the shop for a friendly chair, a steady hand, and haircuts that keep folks coming back week after week.

Public listings give the shop a strong 4.8 star rating across 145 reviews, with customers praising great service, great cuts, reasonable prices, and a welcoming atmosphere. The shop is noted as a favorite for children's haircuts and is known for its support of veterans.

Whether you need a trim before the work week or a fresh look for a special occasion, give the shop a call or stop by during business hours. Walk-ins are welcome at this neighborhood barbershop.

About the Shop: A Harrison Institution

Harrison, Ohio | Feature Story

The Old Time Barbershop sits at 1373 Stone Dr #2 in Harrison, Ohio, serving the community from a storefront that lives up to its name. It is the kind of place where the barbershop tradition still means something: a good chair, a straight answer, and a haircut done with care.

Public review listings paint a consistent picture. Customers describe great service and great cuts at reasonable prices, with particular praise for the shop's skill with children's haircuts. The shop is also noted for its support of veterans and a welcoming atmosphere that keeps regulars returning.

With a 4.8 star rating across 145 reviews on Birdeye and additional positive mentions across local directories, the shop has earned its reputation one haircut at a time. Some reviewers mention they wish the shop kept longer hours, a fair note for a small shop with a loyal following.

What we could not verify, we will not print: the shop's founding date, its full history, and the names of the barbers are not published in public sources we could confirm. What is confirmed is the address, the phone number, the hours as listed by public directories, and a community that speaks well of the place.
