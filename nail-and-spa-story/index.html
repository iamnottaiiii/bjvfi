Our Story

A calm corner on Clove Road

Nail and Spa Story describes itself as dedicated to personal nail care in a clean, friendly, and safe atmosphere. Open since 2014, it has become a regular stop for Staten Island customers who come back for years.

The space is small and welcoming, with a separated room for waxing, several pedicure chairs, and nail stations. Tools are sanitized in a special machine, and the team is known for treating everyone like family.
