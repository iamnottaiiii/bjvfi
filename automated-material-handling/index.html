How we work

Process first, equipment second.

The catalog answer is rarely the right answer. We start with what you move, how fast, and where it has to end up, then specify equipment that fits. That includes paper recycling and refuse handling lines, where material behavior drives every design choice.

  • Site review and throughput analysis up front
  • Custom engineering when catalog equipment falls short
  • Installation and commissioning by people who built it
  • Ongoing service and parts support
