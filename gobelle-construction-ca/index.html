Field notes

A straightforward way to build

Construction projects go smoothly when the plan is clear. Here is how to start one with us.

  • ✓
    Call and describe the project. Tell us what you want built, fixed or remodeled, and where the property is.
  • ✓
    Walk the site together. We look at the space, talk through options and flag anything to check first.
  • ✓
    Agree on scope and price. You get a clear written scope before any work begins.
  • ✓
    Build, with updates. Steady communication from first day to final walkthrough.
