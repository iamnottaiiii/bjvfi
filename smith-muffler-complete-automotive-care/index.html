SHOP RECORD

The story behind the bays: a family owned Covington institution, running since 1924.

HISTORY

Smith Muffler is a third generation family owned and operated business serving Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky. Per the company's own public statements, it has been operating since 1924 and grew from the area's leading muffler shop into a full service automobile repair service.

The company states its mission is to provide every guest with the best customer service, quality parts and exceptional repairs, with the goal of becoming its customers' automotive service provider for life.

Source: company press release published December 2020. Facts stated as published by the business.
