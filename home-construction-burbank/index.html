Chapter 01 · Who we are

A Burbank builder focused on the details that last.

Every project starts with a conversation, not a contract. We walk your property, listen to how you live, and put together a plan that fits your home, your timeline, and your budget.

From single room remodels to larger additions, our crews handle the work start to finish: demolition, framing, electrical, plumbing coordination, drywall, paint, and final finishes. You deal with one point of contact the whole way through.

Measure twice, cut once is not a slogan here. It is how the schedule stays on time and the budget stays honest.

Chapter 02 · What we build

Remodels, additions, and whole home work.

Kitchens and bathrooms, room additions, ADUs, and full interior renovations. If it involves framing, finishes, and fixtures, it is the kind of project we take on.

About · Home Construction Burbank

Built in Burbank, for Burbank homes.

Home Construction Burbank is a construction company based at 840 N Hollywood Way in Burbank, California. We serve homeowners across Burbank and the surrounding San Fernando Valley with remodeling, additions, and home construction work.

Our process is deliberately simple: walkthrough, written scope, scheduled work, and a final walkthrough before we call the job done. You always know what is happening, what it costs, and when it finishes.

Written scopes

Every project starts with a clear written description of the work, so there are no surprises mid job.

One point of contact

You talk to one person from estimate to final walkthrough. No phone tag across subcontractors.

Tidy job sites

Daily cleanup and dust control, because you still have to live in your home while we work.
