Services

What we dig, move, and grade.

Core excavation services from our public listings. Call (818) 767-3366 to talk through your project and get a straight answer on whether we are the right crew.

  • Excavation

    Residential and commercial

    General digging for foundations, footings, pools, additions, and new construction. We read the plans, mark the layout, and dig to spec.

  • Grading

    Rough and finish

    Rough grading to shape the site and finish grading to leave a smooth surface with proper slope for drainage, ready for concrete, pavers, or landscaping.

  • Earthwork

    Cuts and fills

    Moving, placing, and compacting soil for pads, driveways, and building sites. Balanced cuts and fills keep hauling costs down.

  • Trenching

    Utilities

    Trenches for water, sewer, electrical, and gas lines, cut to required depth and backfilled with compaction so the ground stays put.

  • Backfilling

    Compaction included

    Backfill around foundations, walls, and trenches, placed in lifts and compacted to reduce settling later.

  • Septic Drain Field Installation

    Leach lines

    Septic and leach drain field installation for properties that need onsite wastewater systems, dug and bedded to plan.

Our Story

A Sun Valley crew that lives for groundwork.

Triangle Enterprises is an excavating company based in Sun Valley, California. We are the crew that shows up before everyone else: the ones who read the soil, move the dirt, and leave the site ready for the builders, plumbers, and landscapers who come after us.

Our public listings describe a company focused on the fundamentals: septic and leach drain field installation, trenching, grading, and earthwork. No gimmicks, just equipment operators and laborers who know what a good subgrade feels like under a boot.

Every job gets the same treatment: a walk of the site, a clear scope, and a crew that treats your property like it is the only one we have. When the ground is prepared right, everything built on top of it goes smoother, and that is the reputation we are building, one site at a time.
