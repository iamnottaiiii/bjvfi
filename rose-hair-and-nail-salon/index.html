Everything you need, one friendly team

Located in Sheepshead Bay at 1675 East 16th Street, Rose Hair & Nail Salon is built around a simple idea: one visit should take care of all of it. A haircut and a manicure. A pedicure and a massage. A prom look from head to toe.

Guests describe the team as skilled and attentive, from careful color matching at the nail bar to thorough keratin treatments at the hair station.

  • Hair salon and nail salon in one location
  • Massage specialties: deep tissue, Swedish, hot stone, cupping
  • Walk ins welcome, call ahead for weekends
✦

Easy to reach

Right on East 16th Street in the 11229 zip, close to Sheepshead Bay's shops and transit. Call (718) 627-9666 and we will get you on the schedule.

✦

Open seven days

Monday through Thursday 9 AM to 8 PM, Friday and Saturday 9 AM to 10 PM, and Sunday 11 AM to 8 PM. Evening appointments are available on weekends.
