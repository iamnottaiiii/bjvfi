What we do

Services, numbered for clarity

Every treatment below is general dentistry performed at the Sand Lane office. Call (718) 273-5006 to ask what is right for you.

  1. 01

    Exams and x rays

    Routine checkups, digital x rays and oral cancer screening. The starting point for every treatment plan.

  2. 02

    Cleanings

    Professional hygiene visits that remove tartar, polish teeth and keep gums healthy between appointments.

  3. 03

    Tooth colored fillings

    Natural looking repairs for cavities, matched to your tooth so the work blends in.

  4. 04

    Crowns

    Protective caps for cracked, worn or heavily restored teeth, shaped to fit your bite.

  5. 05

    Root canal therapy

    Saving an infected or painful tooth from the inside, so you can keep your natural tooth.

  6. 06

    Extractions

    Gentle removal of teeth that cannot be saved, with clear aftercare guidance.

  7. 07

    Dentures and partials

    Removable replacements for missing teeth, fitted for comfort and a natural look.

  8. 08

    Cosmetic whitening

    Brightening treatments that lift years of stains for a fresher smile. Ask at your visit

Our story

Meet Dr. Lyubov Krut

Dr. Lyubov Krut, DDS is a general dentist with more than four decades of experience caring for patients on Staten Island. She practices at Sand Lane Dental, the office she calls home at 128 Sand Ln Fl 1.

Her approach is old school in the best way: listen first, explain everything, and treat people the way you would treat family. Patients mention her polite, courteous manner and the way she takes time to explain treatment in full, along with a friendly front desk and x ray team.

She speaks English and Russian, and welcomes new patients of all ages.
