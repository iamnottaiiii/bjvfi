Our story

A quieter kind
of nail salon

Canaan Beauty Nail Spa Inc. is a neighborhood nail salon and spa on 13th Avenue in Dyker Heights, built around calm, cleanliness and careful work.

The approach

Slow down.
We have got your nails.

Most nail appointments feel rushed. Here, the pace is different on purpose. The room stays quiet and relaxed, the team takes its time with shaping and cuticles, and nobody is watching the clock while your polish sets.

That patience shows in the results. Clients describe work that looks clean and professional, and gel and dip sets that hold up well over time. Cleanliness is taken seriously too, because a space that feels cared for makes for a visit you can trust.

It is a practical kind of pampering: tidy hands, smooth feet, and an hour that feels like a break from a busy schedule.
