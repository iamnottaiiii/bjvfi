The journey so far

Family Owned, Cincinnati Rooted

Landtech Designs began as a landscape installation and lawn maintenance company more than 30 years ago, and has grown into a full service landscape and hardscape company with its own garden center and greenhouse.

A Family Company

Landtech describes itself as a family owned company with more than 30 years of experience in commercial and residential landscaping and landscape maintenance. The BBB lists 34 years in business.

Garden Center and Greenhouse

The retail garden store at 9741 Hamilton Ave in Mt. Healthy sells annuals, perennials, trees, shrubs, mulches, and houseplants, with different crops by season. The greenhouse also grows cut flowers, supplied wholesale to tri-state florist shops.

Where We Work

Based in Mt. Healthy, a suburb north and west of Cincinnati near I-275, Landtech serves Southern Ohio, including the Cincinnati, Dayton, and Columbus areas.

What We Build

Landscape construction, interlocking pavers, retaining wall systems, planting, and landscape maintenance, backed by a garden center that grows much of what gets planted.
