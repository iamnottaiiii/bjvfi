A window is only as good as its install

Drafts, sticking sashes, and fogged panes usually trace back to the install. Our work focuses on the details that keep a window working for decades: square frames, proper shimming, quality sealant, and clean exterior capping.

Services

Window work is our trade. Every service below is available to Staten Island homeowners; call for a free assessment of your project.

Window installation

Full-frame and insert installation of new windows for renovations and additions. We verify every opening is plumb and square before the window goes in, then insulate, seal, and cap for a weather-tight finish.

Replacement windows

Swap aging, drafty, or fogged windows for modern energy-efficient units sized to your existing openings. We help you weigh repair against replacement honestly.

Window repair and restoration

Sash repair, balance replacement, glass replacement, hardware restoration, and draft sealing for wood, vinyl, and aluminum windows.

Custom and specialty windows

Odd sizes and shapes get the same careful treatment: exact measuring, careful ordering, and a true fit on install day.

Exterior capping and trim

Clean aluminum capping and exterior trim work that protects the window frame and keeps the facade looking sharp.

General home improvements

As a licensed home improvement contractor we also take on related interior and exterior improvement work. Call to discuss your project.

Our Story

A Staten Island shop built on craft.

Mirek Old World Craftsman Inc is a window installation service and licensed home improvement contractor based on Leona Street in Staten Island. The name says the quiet part out loud: we believe in the old world way of doing things, with patience, precision, and pride in the finished work.

Window work rewards attention to detail. A quarter inch here, a bead of sealant there, and a window that should last thirty years becomes a callback in three. We built this business on getting those small things right, on measuring carefully, fitting truly, and standing behind the work.

Homeowners across Staten Island call us when they want one person to see the job through, from the first measurement to the final wipe-down. That is the whole business model: show up, do it right, and leave a window that works the way a window should.
