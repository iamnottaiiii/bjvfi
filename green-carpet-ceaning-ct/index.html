Stairs and Upholstery

Staircases take a beating, and so do sofas and chairs. We deep clean stair carpeting tread by tread, and refresh upholstered furniture so it looks and feels new again.

When water gets in, speed matters. We respond fast, extract standing water, and run a careful drying and moisture control process so carpets, padding, and subfloor recover properly.

Good for: leaks, flooding, burst pipes, and any carpet that has taken on water.

Our story

Built on showing up, and doing it right

Green Carpet Ceaning CT started with a simple belief: a carpet cleaner should leave your home noticeably cleaner than they found it. Not a rushed pass with a machine, but a careful, thorough job you can see and feel.

From our base at 160 State St in North Haven, we serve homes and businesses across the area, one room at a time. Our customers keep coming back, and they keep telling their neighbors. That is how we grow.

When customers review us, the same words come up again and again: professional, thorough, dependable, and easy to work with. We intend to keep earning them.
