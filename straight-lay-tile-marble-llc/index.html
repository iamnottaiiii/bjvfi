Our story

The one-crew remodeler

Straight Lay Tile & Marble LLC works out of Cincinnati's east side, remodeling bathrooms and kitchens with tile and marble set straight and true.

Built on a simple idea

Most remodels turn your home into a revolving door of different contractors. Straight Lay works differently: reviewers describe a crew certified across many fields, so one company and one crew stays on your job from start to finish.

That shows up in the details people notice. A bathroom customer wrote that the crew put the family first and everyone was put at ease. Another kitchen customer wrote that the porcelain tile, tumbled marble, and marble border together look gorgeous, and that the kitchen looks amazing.

And when the dust settles, the house is left cleaner than they expected: one reviewer called it the cleanest remodel job they had ever done.

What customers say

  • "Professional," "amazing job," and "would recommend," in the words of reviewers.
  • Family first: customers say the crew puts the household at ease.
  • Clean job sites: one reviewer called it cleaner than any remodel they had done.
  • One crew for the whole job, not a rotating cast of contractors.
Why the name? "Straight lay" is the tile trade's term for the cleanest, most honest layout: tiles in a straight grid, every corner aligned. It is also a promise: the work stays on the level, and so does the crew. No bait, no mess, no strangers in your house.
