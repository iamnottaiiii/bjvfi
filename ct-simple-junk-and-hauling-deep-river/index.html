How we work

The name is the business plan

Simple is not just in the name; it is the whole idea. Junk removal is stressful enough without confusing pricing or crews that ghost you. We answer the phone, we tell you the price before we lift a thing, and we leave the space cleaner than we found it. That is the entire pitch.

Because we are local, not a national franchise, the person quoting your job is part of the crew doing it. We know the transfer stations, we know the donation spots, and we know the back roads between Deep River and the shoreline towns we serve. That local knowledge keeps prices fair and pickups fast.

Donate what we can, recycle what we can, landfill only what is left.
