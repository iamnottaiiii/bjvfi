The studio

Ink is a craft.
We treat it like one.

A neighborhood studio on Edgmont Ave where every piece starts with a conversation and ends with clean, lasting work.

Every tattoo starts as an idea in your head and a blank space on skin. Our job is to bridge the two with lettering that reads from across the room and lines that stay crisp for years. From a single scripted name to a full custom piece, each design is drawn for you, not pulled from a wall of flash.

We keep the studio clean, the needles single use, and the vibe easy. First tattoo or fiftieth, you get the same care: a real consult, honest advice on placement and size, and aftercare guidance so your ink heals right.

The artist

The hand behind
the name

Jei tatts gifted handz is built around one artist's hand: steady, practiced, and known for lettering that reads like it was always meant to be there.

Jei is the tattoo artist behind Gifted Handz, working out of the Edgmont Ave studio in Brookhaven. The name says it plainly: the work speaks through the hands. Script and lettering are the calling card, with blackwork and fine line rounding out the book.

The approach is simple. Listen first, draw second. Every piece is designed with the client in the chair, placement is talked through honestly, and nobody gets rushed. That is how you get ink you still love in ten years.

Piercing services are offered in the same studio, with sterile technique and straightforward aftercare so everything heals clean.
