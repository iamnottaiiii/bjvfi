Craft process

How a repair moves through the shop.

A typical job follows these stages. Exact steps and timing vary by vehicle, so call to confirm.

Stage 01Walk-in estimate

Bring the car by or call ahead. The damage is looked over and the price is put in writing before any work begins.

Stage 02Disassembly and assessment

Damaged panels and trim come off so hidden damage can be found and the true scope is known up front.

Stage 03Metal shaping and bodywork

Dents are worked out and panels straightened by hand, then filled, blocked, and sanded until the surface is true.

Stage 04Paint and color match

Color is matched to the factory finish and the repair is painted so the blend disappears. Reviewers say it looks brand new.

Stage 05Reassembly and detail

Trim goes back on, panel gaps are checked, and the car is cleaned up for handoff.

Stage 06Final walk-around

You look the car over with the shop before it leaves. Reviewers describe being very happy with the finished result.
