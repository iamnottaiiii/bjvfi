About us

A Highland Park roofer with a paper trail

We let the permits do the talking

Mrk Roofing Services Inc operates out of 6123 Strickland Ave in Highland Park, Los Angeles. The company holds a California roofing contractor license, which BuildZoom verified as active, and scores 110 on BuildZoom, ranking in the top 4% of more than 336,000 California licensed contractors.

Over the past three years, building permit records show at least 39 roofing projects, mostly full re-roofs on homes, duplexes and apartment buildings across Northeast LA, the San Gabriel Valley and Studio City, with typical job values around $6,300 and projects ranging from small repairs to large commercial re-roofs.
