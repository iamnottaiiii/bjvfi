Who we are

Sandi Apel Interior Design

Sandi Apel Interior Design is a boutique design firm based in Glendale, California, specializing in creating personalized and functional spaces for residential clients. Blending style and function, the studio works closely with each client to bring their vision to life through thoughtful, considered design.

No two homes are alike, and no two projects are either. The focus is always on how you actually live: the light you wake up to, the chair you reach for, the room where everyone ends up.

How we work

Three steps to a home you love

01

Listen

We start with a conversation about your home, your habits, and what is not working. Bring photos, Pinterest boards, or just a list of frustrations.

02

Design

We build the concept: floor plans, color and material palettes, and 3-D renderings. You review and refine until it feels exactly right.

03

Deliver

Furnishings are ordered and installed, and the final styling pulls it all together. You walk into a finished room, not a to do list.
