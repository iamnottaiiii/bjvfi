Refinishing

Bring tired floors back to life

Scratched, dull or discolored floors do not always need replacing. Our sanding and refinishing process strips away years of wear and rebuilds the finish, often for a fraction of the cost of new wood.

Fix the damage, keep the floor

  • Water damage and pet stain remediation

Our story

A craftsman shop on Parthenia Street

G.M. Hardwood Floors is a working craftsman shop at 21001 Parthenia St in Canoga Park. We do one thing and we do it well: wood floors.

Installation, repair and refinishing for homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley. No showroom markups, no subcontractor roulette. When you call (818) 731-1789, you reach the people who will sand, stain and seal your floors.

Wood is honest material. It shows every shortcut and rewards every careful pass. That is why we prep subfloors properly, acclimate every board, and finish with products we would put in our own homes.

We are open Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Stop by the shop, bring a photo of your floor, and let us talk through what it needs.
