About

The side work specialists

Cincy Side Work is a Cincinnati based handyman service for the jobs that fall through the cracks.

Big contractors pass on small jobs. DIY weekends turn into month long sagas. Cincy Side Work exists for everything in between: the repairs, installs, and punch lists that keep a home running.

We work by appointment, show up with the right tools, and treat a two hour visit with the same care as a two week remodel. One call, one visit, one less thing on your list.

What we stand by

  • Clear scope agreed before any work begins, so the price and the plan match.
  • Appointment time blocks instead of all day windows.
  • Clean work areas. We leave the room better than we found it.
  • Honest advice. If a job needs a licensed specialist, we will tell you.
