Who we are

One contractor, start to finish.

Hiring a contractor should not feel like a gamble. We show up when we say we will, keep the jobsite clean, and explain every line of the estimate.

Pro Construction LLC is a general contracting company based on Patterson Street in Newport, Kentucky, serving homeowners across the river towns and the greater Cincinnati area. We take on the projects that matter most to a household: the kitchen that needs a second life, the addition that makes room for a growing family, the roof and siding that protect everything under them.

No sales teams, no pressure tactics. You talk to the people doing the work, and you get a written estimate before anything starts. Read our story.

Why hire a general contractor?

  • Single point of responsibility for every trade on your project
  • Permits and scheduling handled, not handed to you
  • One written estimate instead of five conflicting quotes
  • Warranty mindset: we stand behind finished work

Our story

From Patterson Street, for the river cities.

Pro Construction LLC is a Newport-based general contracting company serving the Northern Kentucky river towns and the Cincinnati metro.

We started the company on a simple frustration: too many homeowners getting vague quotes, missed start dates, and jobsites left a mess. Pro Construction was built to be the opposite of that experience.

We keep the operation lean on purpose. The person who estimates your project is involved in building it. That keeps communication short, accountability clear, and the finished work something we are willing to sign our name to.

Newport, Bellevue, Dayton, Fort Thomas, Covington, and across the river into Cincinnati: these are our neighborhoods. We would rather do great work close to home than chase jobs we cannot stand behind.
