Services

What we work on

A full-service neighborhood repair shop. If it rolls, rattles, or refuses to start, bring it by. Call to confirm a specific job.

  1. 01

    General Repair and Maintenance

    The everyday work that keeps a car reliable: scheduled maintenance, fluid services, belts, hoses, and the small jobs that prevent big ones. Reviewers mention respectful turnaround times and work finished when promised.

    Book it
  2. 02

    Engine Diagnostics

    Check-engine light on, strange noise, mystery stall? We track down the real cause instead of guessing. One reviewer came in after another shop misdiagnosed the problem, and drove out with the job done right.

    Book it
  3. 03

    Brakes, Steering, and Suspension

    Pads, rotors, calipers, shocks, and struts, the parts that keep you safe and the ride smooth. We will show you what is worn and explain what can wait.

    Book it
  4. 04

    Oil Changes and Fluids

    Quick, honest oil changes plus transmission, coolant, brake, and power steering fluid services. No mystery upsells, just what your car actually needs.

    Book it
  5. 05

    Tires, Alignment, and Rotation

    New tires, rotations, balancing, and alignments to keep wear even and handling true. Ask us to check tread depth at your next visit.

    Book it
  6. 06

    Starters, Alternators, and Electrical

    Dead battery, slow crank, flickering lights, we chase electrical gremlins to the source. Reviewers mention starter work done properly and at a fair price.

Our story

The shop Main St trusts

Downtowne Auto Center is an independent auto repair shop at 188 Main St in North Tonawanda, NY, the kind of place built on repeat customers, not advertising.

How we work

Call first, approve everything

Reviewers praise one habit above all: the shop calls before doing any work and gets your okay first. No surprises on the invoice, no work you did not agree to. That is the whole philosophy, written on every job.

The crew

Mechanics who know your car

Customers mention the owner and the techs by name, and describe a small, hardworking team that treats regulars like neighbors. One family has been coming for over 20 years. Another sends their elderly friends here with total confidence.
