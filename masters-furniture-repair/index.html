Chair and table repair

Loose joints reglued and clamped properly, broken legs and rungs replaced and matched, split table tops closed up.

Wood refinishing

Water rings, sun fade and scratched varnish stripped, sanded and refinished in a tone that suits the piece.

Antique restoration
