Services, Tab by Tab

Pick a tab to inspect each service. These are the jobs Mullis Septic Services publicly lists: pumping, jetting, maintenance, and inspections. Anything beyond that, call to confirm.

Septic Tank Pumping

The core of the business. Mullis specializes in pumping septic tanks for homes and properties around Cincinnati, clearing out sludge and scum so the system can breathe again.

  • Full pump out of residential septic tanks
  • Waste hauled by an MSD permitted hauler
  • Crew checks tank condition during service
  • Straightforward scheduling by phone
Heads up on pricing: pump out cost depends on tank size, access, and condition. Mullis is reviewed as fair on price. Call (513) 446-0404 for a quote before booking.
Exact pricing: call to confirm

Jetting

High pressure water jetting to blast through buildup and blockages in septic lines. When drains slow down but the tank itself is fine, jetting is often the fix.

  • High pressure line clearing
  • Restores flow without digging, in many cases
  • Often paired with a pump out for a full reset
Not sure if you need jetting or pumping? Describe the symptoms on the phone and the crew will tell you which job fits. No pressure, just a straight answer.

Tank Maintenance

Ongoing maintenance keeps a septic system out of trouble. Mullis provides maintenance service on tanks to catch small problems before they turn into backups.

  • Routine tank maintenance visits
  • System health checks between pump outs
  • Guidance on pump out intervals for your household
Good to know: most households pump every 3 to 5 years, but usage and tank size change the math. Ask the crew what schedule fits your home.

Tank Inspections

Buying a home with a septic system, or just want to know where you stand? Mullis provides inspections of tanks so you know the condition before it becomes a surprise.

  • Tank condition inspections
  • Honest findings on what needs attention
  • Useful before a home purchase or sale
Inspection reports: ask when you call whether the visit includes a written summary. call to confirm
