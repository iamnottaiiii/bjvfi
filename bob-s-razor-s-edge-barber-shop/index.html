Our Story

Old school is
the whole point

Bob's Razor's Edge Barber Shop sits at 36 N Main St in Millbury, a classic barber shop in the truest sense. The chairs are real barber chairs, the tools are clippers and straight razors, and the standard is simple: every man leaves looking sharper than when he walked in.

Reviewers describe Bob as a straight OG legend, a barber who works harder than almost everyone they know. That work ethic shows in every fade, every line-up, every hot-towel finish. This is not a salon that happens to cut men's hair. It is a barber shop, full stop.

Whether you want a military fade, a classic taper, or just a damn good haircut, pull up a chair. The pole is spinning and the clippers are warm.

Great place for a military fade. Great place for a haircut.
