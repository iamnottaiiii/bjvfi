Services

The workbench: what we do.

Chiropractic care centers on the spine, joints, nerves, and muscles, and how they work together. Below is what the practice offers. Every plan of care is fitted to the individual, so call to discuss your needs.

01

Chiropractic Adjustments

Controlled, hands on adjustments to spinal joints that are not moving well, with the aim of restoring motion and easing pain.

Core care
02

Orthopedic Chiropractic Evaluation

A structural assessment of bones, joints, discs, muscles, ligaments, and tendons to find the source of a complaint, not just the sore spot.

Specialty focus
03

Spinal Manipulation & Manual Therapy

Hands on techniques applied to the spine and surrounding tissues to improve mobility and relieve stiffness.

Hands on
04

Back & Neck Pain Care

Care for common neuromusculoskeletal complaints, including back pain, neck pain, and related stiffness from work, sport, or daily life.

Common visits
05

Movement & Mobility Support

Guidance on movement, posture habits, and self care between visits to help improvements hold.

Between visits
06

Consultations & Referrals

When a problem needs another kind of specialist, Dr. Morgan refers patients to the right provider rather than treating blindly.

Honest care
