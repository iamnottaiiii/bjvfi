The story so far

Thirty-five years of making homes look new again

Dirt, algae, mold, and weather wear every home down. For decades, Splish Splash has been the crew Wallingford calls to wash it all away and handle the odd jobs and repairs that come with it.

Walk around any house in Connecticut after a few New England seasons and you will see it: green film creeping up the siding, gray decks, streaked gutters. Power washing is not just cosmetic. Mold and mildew left on siding eat into paint and trim, and a dirty deck gets slippery and rots faster. A thorough wash protects the investment underneath the grime.
