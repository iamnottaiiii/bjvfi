Our Story

Taught by dad. Trusted by neighbors.

David grew up learning the trade from his father, who has worked this industry for over 30 years. Together they built Sod & Turf Pros on a simple idea: treat every lawn like it is your own family’s yard.

“My dad always told me a green lawn is where precious memories are made.” Those words stuck. This is a family that genuinely loves lawns, and it shows in the work: careful prep, honest pricing, and crews that communicate clearly from quote to cleanup.

Over 100 lawns installed across the San Fernando Valley. Free quotes. Phenomenal customer service, because they truly care.
