Why simple wins

The uncomplicated way to get rid of junk

Renting a dumpster means you do the loading, the scheduling, and the waiting. CT Simple Junk And Hauling flips that around: our crew does the lifting, the loading, and the hauling, and you get your space back the same day.

Every job starts with a free quote and an upfront price. You approve it before anything moves, and there are no surprise fees when the truck pulls away.

We are based right on Boston Post Road in Old Saybrook, serving the shoreline towns we know well. Homes, rentals, small businesses, and contractors all call us when there is debris to move.

What we take gets sorted responsibly. Usable items are donated when possible, recyclables are recycled, and the rest is disposed of properly.

Our story

Simple by name, serious about the work

CT Simple Junk And Hauling is a shoreline debris removal crew based on Boston Post Road in Old Saybrook. We started with a simple observation: getting rid of junk should not be complicated, expensive, or a whole weekend project.

So we keep it simple. You call, we quote, we haul. One crew, one truck, one upfront price, and a swept-clean space when we are done.

We serve the shoreline communities around Old Saybrook, the towns where we live and work. Homeowners clearing out garages, landlords turning over rentals, contractors with debris piling up, we treat every job with the same straightforward care.

And we dispose of it all responsibly: donating what still has life in it, recycling what can be recycled, and handling the rest the right way.

Show up on time, charge a fair price, leave the place cleaner than we found it.
