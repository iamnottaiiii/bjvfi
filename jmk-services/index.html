Our Story

Built on consistency in Lynn

JMK Services is a Lynn, Massachusetts janitorial company serving offices and commercial spaces across the North Shore. We started with a simple observation: most cleaning complaints are not about skill, they are about consistency. The crew that shows up every time, cleans to the same checklist, and answers the phone when you call is the crew worth keeping.

That is the standard we hold ourselves to. Scheduled service, clear communication, and a clean that looks the same on visit one hundred as it did on visit one. If you manage an office, a retail space, or a commercial building in Lynn or nearby, we would like the chance to walk your space and show you what a reliable janitorial partner feels like.
