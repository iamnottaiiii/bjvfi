Why We Started

CT Simple Junk And Hauling was built on a frustration every homeowner knows: getting rid of junk should not be a project of its own. Renting a dumpster is a hassle. Borrowing a truck means favors and weekends lost.

So we keep it simple. One call, one quote, one pickup. We are a local crew out of Farmington, CT, and we treat every driveway like it is our own: show up on time, work carefully around your home, and sweep up before we leave.

We also sort what we haul. Items in good shape get a second life through donation, recyclables go where they belong, and the rest is handled responsibly. Clearing your space should not come at the planet's expense.
