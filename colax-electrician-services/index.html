Why call Colax

Electrical work you do not have to think twice about

Electrical problems are stressful enough. We keep it simple: clear communication, tidy work, and respect for your home.

Repairs and troubleshooting

Dead outlets, flickering lights, breakers that keep tripping. We find the cause and fix it properly, not just patch it.

Panel upgrades
