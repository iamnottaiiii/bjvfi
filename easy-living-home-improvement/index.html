How a project goes

Simple, straight, no surprises

1. Walkthrough and measure

We come out, look at the siding, windows, or woodwork in question, take measurements, and talk through what you want the finished result to look like.

2. Plain-language plan

You get a clear rundown of the work, the materials, and the price. No jargon, no pressure. If a repair makes more sense than a replacement, we will say so.

3. The work itself

We show up when we say we will, protect your landscaping and walkways, and keep the job site tidy while we install, repair, or rebuild.

4. Final walkthrough

We walk the finished work with you, piece by piece, and make sure you are happy before we call it done.

Good siding is the difference between a house you maintain and a home you are proud of. We treat every project like it is our own street, because it usually is.

Who we are

Small crew, straight answers, finished exteriors

We are Easy Living Home Improvement, a contractor based at 4276 Amelia-Olive Branch Rd in Batavia, Ohio. Our listings describe us as a siding contractor, with carpentry and home window services alongside it.

Being small is our advantage. You talk to the contractor, not a sales department. The person who measures your house is the person who stands behind the work.
