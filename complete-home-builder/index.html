Our story

A West Hills crew built on word of mouth

Complete Home Builder works out of West Hills, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley. The team grew the way good contractors do: one kitchen, one bath, one addition at a time, with most new jobs coming from referrals and repeat customers.

Reviewers mention the same names again and again: responsive project leads, courteous crews, and clear explanations at every step. The approach is simple. Show up when promised, keep the job site tidy, communicate through each phase, and stand behind the finished work.

Whether it is water damage that needs fast, honest repair or a full mid century modern remodel planned with your architect, you get one accountable team from the first walk through to the final walk through.

How we work
  1. 1. Walk through. We see the space, hear your ideas, and talk budget honestly.
  2. 2. Written estimate. Clear scope and fair pricing before anything starts.
  3. 3. The build. Tidy crews, regular check ins, and updates at every phase.
  4. 4. Final walk through. We review every detail together until it is right.
