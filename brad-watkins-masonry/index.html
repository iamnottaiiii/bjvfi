Common questions

In most LA-area cities, walls over a certain height or retaining walls require permits and engineering. We handle the permit paperwork and coordinate inspections as part of the job.
A standard residential block wall often takes one to two weeks including footings and cure time. Larger retaining walls and fireplaces run longer. Your bid includes a written schedule.
Veneer costs less and goes up faster, and looks nearly identical from the street. Full-depth stone is the choice for structural walls and historic matches. We will show you both at the site visit.
