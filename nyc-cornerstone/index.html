About us

The cornerstone of a good remodel is the crew

NYC cornerstone is a construction company based at 1111 Richmond Rd in Staten Island, NY. We specialize in the work that transforms how a home feels day to day: kitchen and bathroom remodels, whole-house painting, and the finish carpentry that pulls it all together.

Our customers describe us the same way, again and again: professional, quick, and clean. Crews that respect your home. Work that looks sharp when we leave. That reputation is why most of our new jobs come from people who were already happy customers, or from their neighbors.

Call (718) 351-1111 and tell us what your home needs. We will come look, talk it through, and give you a straight answer.
