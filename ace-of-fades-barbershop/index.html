About the shop

All cards on the table

Ace of Fades Barbershop on Irene Street in Bethlehem is built on a simple hand: great cuts, honest prices, and a room full of energy. Walk in a stranger, walk out a regular. That is the whole business plan, and the reviews say it works.

The crew takes pride in precision work, from skin fades to beard sculpting, and in keeping the shop the kind of place people recommend to their friends.
