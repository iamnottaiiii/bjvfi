About

A mason's shop on York Street

Brick Master is a masonry contractor based at 1008 York Street in Newport, Kentucky, right in the heart of one of the city's most historic brick neighborhoods. Around here, masonry is not a side trade. It is the trade that built the streets.

Newport's brick rowhouses, river-town storefronts, and Victorian homes were laid by hand over a century ago, and they need masons who understand lime mortar, soft brick, and historic bonds. That is where a dedicated brick specialist earns the name.

From chimney rebuilds to custom patios, Brick Master handles residential masonry across Newport, Bellevue, Dayton, Fort Thomas, and Covington. Estimates are free.
