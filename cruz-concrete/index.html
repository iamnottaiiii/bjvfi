About

A North Hills concrete crew with the paperwork to prove it

Cruz Concrete is based at 9658 Haskell Ave in North Hills and serves the San Fernando Valley. The company is a licensed California contractor, CSLB license #930480, issued in 2009, with workers compensation insurance and a surety bond on file with the state.

We handle residential and commercial concrete: driveways, patios, walkways, slabs and replacements. The focus is simple, honest work: a solid base, straight forms, and a finish that lasts.
