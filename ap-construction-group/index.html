Since 1989, the reliable bid on public work.

Established in 1989, AP Construction Group Inc. is one of California's most reliable general contracting firms specializing in modernizations, new construction, MEP, and tenant improvement projects.

"The company has grown by building excellent customer relationships with clear and open communication, cost-conscious pricing, efficient and professional services, and most importantly, hassle-free quality construction."

The firm has successfully completed over $50 million of publicly funded projects, including school modernizations and HVAC work for public agencies across Southern California. Staff hold industry credentials including NADCA membership and Air Systems Cleaning Specialist certification.
