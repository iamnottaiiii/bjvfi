Our Story · No. 04 about the family
About

A family name on every brushstroke.

Family Ties Painting is a family-run painting company based in Philadelphia, PA. The name says it all: we work the way family takes care of a home, with patience, pride, and attention to every last detail.

When you hire us, you are not getting a rotating crew of strangers. You are getting people who treat your walls, your trim, and your time with respect, because our family name is on the finished work.

We serve homeowners and businesses across Philadelphia from our home base at 944 Carver St, Philadelphia, PA 19124.

Our promises

How a family crew does it.

Simple promises we keep on every job, big or small.

Prep Like It Matters

Because it does. Cleaning, patching, sanding, and priming come before a single finish coat goes on.

Clean Lines, Clean Site

Sharp edges on the walls and a tidy workspace every day. We protect your floors and furniture like they are our own.

Honest Quotes

One clear price for the agreed scope. If something changes mid-job, you hear about it before we lift a brush.

Walkthrough Finish

We walk every room with you at the end. Nothing is done until you are happy with it.
