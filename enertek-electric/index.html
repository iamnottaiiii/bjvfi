Field notes

General answers, not specific advice. Every home is wired differently, so describe your situation when you call.

When should a breaker panel be replaced?
Common signs include frequent tripping, warm breakers, corrosion, or a panel that is decades old and full. An electrician can inspect and advise. Sample guidance, call to confirm.
Can I add a circuit for a home office or workshop?
Often yes, if the panel has capacity and space. A load calculation tells the full story. Sample guidance, call to confirm.
Why do my lights flicker when an appliance starts?
Large motors draw a surge on startup. Occasional brief dimming can be normal, but persistent flicker deserves a look. Sample guidance, call to confirm.
What should I do before the electrician arrives?
Clear access to the panel and the work area, and note when the problem happens. Photos of labels help. Sample guidance, call to confirm.
