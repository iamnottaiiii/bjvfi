Services

What a landscape designer does for you

Directory listings describe Carson-Magness Landscaping's services as landscaping and landscape architecture. Browse the three areas below, then call to discuss your property.

Landscape design and architecture

A good garden starts on paper. Design services plan the layout, planting, and structure of your outdoor space before any digging begins.

  • Site planning for yards of any size
  • Plant selection suited to Glendale's climate
  • Beds, borders, and focal points
  • Hardscape coordination, such as walls and paths

Landscape installation

Once the plan is set, the build brings it to life: plantings, soil work, and structural elements installed with an eye for the finished look.

  • Planting of trees, shrubs, and perennials
  • Garden bed preparation and edging
  • Structural touches like retaining walls
  • Clean, respectful work on your property

Ongoing landscape care

Landscapes mature and change. Regular care keeps plantings healthy and the design intent intact through every season.

  • Seasonal pruning and shaping
  • Bed cleanup and refreshing
  • Plant health check-ins
  • Call to ask about maintenance options
