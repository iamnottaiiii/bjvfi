About

Built on showing up

Barnett Pro Contractor is a contracting business based at 40 Bullard Ave in Worcester, MA, serving homes and businesses across Worcester County.

Who we are

Local, lean, accountable

We are a Worcester-based contractor working out of Bullard Ave. We keep the operation intentionally small: the person who scopes your job stays accountable for it through the final walkthrough.

That is the whole business model. No layers of salespeople, no disappearing subcontractors, no fine print. Just contracting work done the way it should be done.

Residential remodels, commercial fit-outs, repairs and full project builds, if it involves a building in Worcester County, we can probably handle it. If we cannot, we will tell you straight.
