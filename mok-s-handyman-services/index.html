About

Small business. Big reliability.

MOK'S Handyman Services is a Pacoima-based handyman business at 13919 Del Sur St, serving homes and rentals across the San Fernando Valley. The workday starts early, at 6:00 AM most mornings, because repairs should fit your schedule, not the other way around.

The idea is simple: be the number you call when something around the house needs fixing. Show up when promised, quote the job before starting, clean up when done, and leave the place better than you found it.

From drywall patches and paint touch-ups to door repairs, fixture installs, and the dozen small jobs on every homeowner's list, one visit can clear the backlog.
