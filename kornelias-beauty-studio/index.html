Our story

A Bensonhurst staple for beautiful hair

Kornelias Beauty Studio has served the 20th Avenue neighborhood as a licensed New York beauty salon since 2014.

Built on trust, one client at a time

The studio was founded by Kornelia, a stylist who believes a great appointment is about more than the cut. Clients describe leaving with hair they love and the know how to recreate the look at home, with extra volume and easy styling that fits real life.

Over the years, the chair has become a neighborhood fixture. Regulars return not just for the results, but for the friendly, easygoing atmosphere: a place where you are remembered, listened to, and never rushed.

As a licensed New York State appearance enhancement business, the studio meets the state's professional standards for cleanliness and care, so you can relax into every visit.
