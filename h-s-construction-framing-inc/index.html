Framing Is the Backbone of Every Build

A house is only as good as its frame. We specialize in wood framing and rough carpentry, then carry projects through to finished construction so one crew owns the whole job.

From new home framing packages to room additions and full remodels, H & S Construction & Framing Inc handles structural work with the precision it demands.

Every wall is plumbed, every header is sized right, and every subfloor is nailed down tight before we move on. That discipline carries through siding, decks, and interior finish work, so the finished project looks right and lasts.

Why framing specialists matter

Most callbacks on a house trace back to the frame: doors that will not latch, floors that creak, drywall that cracks. A framing-first contractor gets the bones right, and everything after it goes faster.

Our Story

A framing-first general contractor serving Essex and Baltimore County, Maryland.

We started with hammers and nail guns, and we never lost respect for the frame underneath the finishes.

H & S Construction & Framing Inc is a general contractor based at 1222 Four Winds Way in Essex, MD. Framing is our trade and our foundation: the crew cut its teeth on wood framing and rough carpentry before expanding into full general contracting, additions, remodeling, decks, and siding.

That order matters. When the same company that frames your addition also finishes it, there is no finger pointing between trades and no gaps in accountability. We measure twice, we build square, and we treat your home like the jobsite it is: clean, safe, and on schedule.

Square and True
