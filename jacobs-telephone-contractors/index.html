About

Jacobs Telephone Contractors was established in 1983 as a subcontractor installing telephone systems in the Dayton and Cincinnati areas, and has grown into a full-range cabling installation company. The company is a BICSI corporate member with RCDD staff, and runs offices in Dayton, Columbus, Cincinnati, Indianapolis, and Atlanta. Its website is jacobstelephone.com.
