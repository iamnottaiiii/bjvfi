Our story

A Florence deck company with one job: outdoor spaces people actually live on.

Built on Florence backyards

Decked-Out, LLC. is based at 8471 Village Drive in Florence, Kentucky, serving homeowners across Boone County and Northern Kentucky. The company is listed under Decks and Porches, with a trade lineup that covers custom deck builds, TREX composite decking, awnings, and handicap ramps.

No showroom theatrics, no pressure. We measure your yard, sketch the design with you, and build it like it is our own backyard, because our name is on every board.

What guides the build

  • Fit first. The deck follows your house lines and your habits, not a catalog template.
  • Materials with a reason. Pine, cedar, or TREX, chosen for budget, maintenance, and look.
  • Built for weather. Northern Kentucky freeze-thaw cycles get real footings and real flashing.
  • Access for everyone. Ramps and step-free entries designed in, not bolted on later.
