About

Built around one idea: own the outcome.

Franks Realty Investment And Construction LLC is a real estate development company based on Van Nuys Boulevard in Pacoima, California. The name says what the company does: invest in real estate, and build.

Pacoima sits at the heart of the northeast San Fernando Valley, a neighborhood of single-family homes, small apartment buildings, and busy commercial corridors. It is the kind of market where local knowledge, realistic construction budgets, and steady execution decide which projects succeed.

That is the lane this company works in: finding property with potential, planning the construction honestly, and seeing it through to a finished building.
