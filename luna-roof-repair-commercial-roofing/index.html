SMALL LEAK TODAY, BIG BILL TOMORROW.

Water finds the weak spot and keeps working. A slipped tile, a cracked boot, a failed flashing: each one lets moisture into decking, insulation, and framing. We find the source, show you photos, and fix it before it spreads.

  • Leak detection with photo documentation
  • Tile, shingle, and flat roof repairs
  • Flashing, vent boot, and skylight resealing
  • Storm damage assessment and repair
  • Full tear-off and re-roof replacements
  • Commercial flat and low-slope systems
  • Preventive maintenance plans for buildings
  • Written scope and warranty on every job

BUILT FOR LEAK SEASON.

Luna Roof Repair and Commercial Roofing works out of 12029 Miranda St in North Hollywood, serving homes and commercial buildings across the San Fernando Valley. The shop focuses on the work most roofers rush past: careful leak diagnosis, honest repair-versus-replace advice, and commercial systems done to spec.

RESIDENTIAL FIRST

Most calls start the same way: a stain on the ceiling or a drip during rain. We trace it to the source instead of guessing, show you the photos, and fix what is actually broken. If the roof has life left, we say so.

COMMERCIAL GRADE

Flat and low-slope commercial roofs live a harder life: ponding water, foot traffic, rooftop equipment. We repair seams, penetrations, and drains, and we set up maintenance plans that catch problems between storms.
