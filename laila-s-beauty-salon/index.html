About

The story

Since 2008, Laila's has been the all-in-one answer for Iselin families: mom gets a facial while dad gets a haircut, and the brows get threaded on the way out.

With more than 800 Google reviews, the salon is known for threading with a gentle hand, haircuts that are both quick and careful, and staff who explain options instead of rushing decisions.

Open seven days, it fits around work, school and wedding season. Walk in for one service or book the full head-to-toe refresh.
