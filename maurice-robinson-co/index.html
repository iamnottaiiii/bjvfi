Our story

A concrete crew from Pedretti Ave

Maurice Robinson Co is a concrete contractor based at 325 Pedretti Ave in Delhi Township, on Cincinnati's west side. Public listings show the company working weekdays, pouring and repairing concrete for homes and businesses across the area.

Good concrete work is unglamorous and exacting: forms set true, base compacted, mix placed at the right slump, finish timed to the weather. It is the kind of trade where the difference between a slab that lasts five years and one that lasts fifty is all in the doing.

That is the standard this crew works to. If your driveway is cracking, your steps are settling, or you are ready to pour that patio, call for a free estimate and talk it through with the people who will actually do the work.
