About us

A family company that treats your house like its business card

The notes below are paraphrased from USA Builders public contractor profiles. They describe how the company presents itself, not verified company history.

Built on quality and schedule

The company says there is only one way to succeed: deliver professional quality work, use only quality materials, and stay on schedule. Every project, big or small, gets an assigned project manager who supervises the job from A to Z and keeps the customer in the loop until the project is complete.

Licensed, bonded and insured

Public profiles list USA Builders as licensed, insured and bonded, holding General Building and Roofing licenses in California. The company says it works with state programs, energy-efficiency programs, insurance work, and both commercial and residential clients.

Recognized in the top tier

BuildZoom gives USA Builders a score of 120, ranking in the top 1% of California licensed contractors, with 64 projects and 130 project photos listed. The company also carries an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau.
