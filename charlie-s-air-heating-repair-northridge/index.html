Valley summers are brutal on AC systems. When yours blows warm, cycles constantly, leaks, or quits entirely, we find the fault fast, from capacitors and contactors to refrigerant and compressor issues.

  • Refrigerant leak detection and recharge
  • Capacitor, contactor, and electrical repairs
  • Frozen coil and drainage fixes
  • Thermostat replacement and calibration

Based at 16933 Parthenia St in Northridge, serving homes across the San Fernando Valley.

Charlie's Air & Heating Repair Northridge started with a simple observation: too many HVAC visits end with a confusing quote and a problem that comes back. We built our shop around the opposite idea, that a technician should explain what failed, fix it properly, and test it in front of you.
