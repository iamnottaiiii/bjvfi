The basics

A working laundromat,
nothing fancy

DonCamillo Broadway Laundromat is a self-service laundry at 1833 Revere Beach Pkwy in Everett, Massachusetts. Public business records show it started in 2012, and it has been a regular stop on the Pkwy ever since.

The setup is the classic one: rows of washers and dryers, folding tables, and supplies from the vending machines. Payment is flexible, with cash, major credit cards, and mobile wallets all accepted. There is a free parking lot and the space is listed as wheelchair accessible.

It is the kind of place built for weekly wash days: pull up to the free lot, run your loads through the big machines, fold, and go.
