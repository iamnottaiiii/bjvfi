Our story

Powering Northern Kentucky since 2000.

WEBSTER ELECTRICAL CONTRACTING has been wiring homes and businesses around Walton, Kentucky for over two decades. Local directory listings trace the company back to 2000, and permit records show steady residential and commercial electrical work across Boone, Kenton, and Campbell counties ever since.

The shop sits at 12004 Green Rd in Walton, and the work ranges from service upgrades and load center replacements to full wiring for new single family homes, church construction temps, and commercial service poles. It is the kind of contractor neighbors recommend: show up, diagnose honestly, wire it clean, and leave the lights on.

No job too small, and no shortcut taken on the big ones.
