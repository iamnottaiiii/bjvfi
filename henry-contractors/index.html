OUR STORY

Built by hand, run by name

Henry Contractors started in the mid-90s in Cincinnati's East End, founded by Henry Heidlage, who still leads the company today. Thirty years on, it remains an owner-led shop: the person whose name is on the door is the person accountable for the work.

The trade at the core is concrete: construction and repair of the slabs, driveways, foundations, and flatwork that everything else stands on. Around that core, the company grew into full general contracting for residential and commercial projects across the Cincinnati area.

Public business records trace the company back to a BBB file opened in 1997, and three decades of continuous operation in the same East End neighborhood.
