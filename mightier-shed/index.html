About the shop

A Shed Shop With Standards

Built the Old Way

Mightier Shed is a shed builder based in Harrison, Ohio. We design and build sheds and storage buildings for homeowners who want something stronger than a kit and better looking than a metal box.

Every project gets the workbench treatment: solid framing, weather-tight roofing, doors that actually latch, and trim that is cut clean. If it leaves our shop, it carries our name, so it leaves right.

We serve Harrison and the surrounding southwest Ohio communities. Bring us a rough sketch, a photo of what you like, or just a list of everything you need to store, and we will turn it into a plan.
