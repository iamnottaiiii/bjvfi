About G & G Construction Co Inc

G & G Construction Co Inc is a general contracting company based at 3411 Clifton Avenue in Cincinnati, Ohio, serving homeowners and businesses in the surrounding neighborhoods.

As a general contractor, the company takes responsibility for the full scope of a project: estimating, scheduling, managing subcontractors and trades, sourcing materials, and seeing the work through to a finished result.

If you are planning a remodel, an addition, or a commercial build-out in the Cincinnati area, start with a conversation about scope, timeline, and budget.
