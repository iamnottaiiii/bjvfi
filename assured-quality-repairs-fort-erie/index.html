About Assured Quality

A Fort Erie contractor with a simple promise: repairs you can trust and renovations finished like they matter.

Built on the details

Assured Quality Repairs and Renovations serves homeowners in Fort Erie and the surrounding Niagara communities from its base on Parkdale Avenue. As a general contractor with carpentry at its core, the business takes on the jobs other contractors skip: the repair lists, the half-finished projects, and the rooms that need a careful hand.

The approach is straightforward: show up when promised, price the work honestly, keep the site tidy, and finish every detail. That is what "assured quality" means in practice.
