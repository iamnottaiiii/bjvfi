Why a yearly sweep matters

Every fire leaves creosote behind. Left alone, that buildup narrows your flue, hurts your draft, and raises the risk of a chimney fire. A professional sweep clears it out and catches small problems before they turn into big repairs.

  • Removes creosote, soot, and blockages from the flue
  • Checks the chimney structure, cap, and flashing
  • Keeps fireplaces, inserts, and stoves drafting right
  • One appointment covers cleaning and a safety check
