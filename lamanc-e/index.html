Our process

How your repair moves through the shop.

LaManCïe sits at 48 W 120th St in Harlem. Here is the exact path your car takes from drop-off to pickup.

Intake and estimate

We photograph the damage, check panel gaps and paint depth, and write the estimate with you present.

Teardown and parts

Damaged panels come off so we can see what is hiding underneath. Parts are ordered to factory spec.

Body work

Metal is pulled, shaped, and smoothed. Gaps are set before anything sees primer.

Prime and paint

Surfaces are sealed, color is matched to your code, blended, and cleared.

Reassembly and polish

Trim, lights, and moldings go back on. The finish is polished and the car is detailed.

Your walkaround

We go over every repaired panel together. You drive away only when it is right.
