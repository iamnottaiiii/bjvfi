About

The story

Transitions earns its name. Clients come in ready for a change, a new cut, a new color, a new chapter, and leave with hair that matches the moment.

With a 4.9-star reputation, the salon is known for stylists who listen first and cut second. Evening hours Wednesday through Friday and Saturday mornings make booking around work easy.

Set on Green St in Woodbridge with accessible parking, it is a relaxed, professional room where regulars are known by name.
