Chapter 01

Built on a simple idea

A house is never finished. It grows, it settles, it asks for attention in small ways before the big ones arrive. We started Great House Restoration Corp. to answer that call with honest work: repairs done right, renovations planned carefully, and craftsmanship that respects the home you already love.

We treat every project like a story worth telling well, from the first measurement to the final walkthrough.

Our Story

A young company with an old fashioned standard

Great House Restoration Corp. was filed as a California corporation in May 2023 and is based in San Marino, California. We are a construction company, which means our license to operate comes with a responsibility we take seriously: to build safely, to keep our word, and to leave every home better than we found it.

New on paper, traditional in practice. Measure twice, cut once, clean up like it was your own home.

San Marino is a city of beautiful older homes, and older homes reward patient contractors. We chose restoration as our focus because it demands the full craft: diagnosing what failed, preserving what still works, and rebuilding what does not. That is the work we want our name on.
