Our Story

Built on Plans, Kept by Word of Mouth

Home Top Construction & Design Inc is a general contractor based in Van Nuys, California.

The company describes itself as a general contractor and home remodeling expert covering room additions, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, roofing, demolition, home building, construction and water damage restoration, and describes its operation as fully licensed and insured.

It also lists itself as a member of the Better Business Bureau since 2006 with an A+ rating, and its office sits at 6931 Van Nuys Blvd, Suite 307, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley.
