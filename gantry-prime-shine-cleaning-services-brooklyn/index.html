Our story

Born in Brooklyn, built on detail.

Gantry Prime Shine Cleaning Services started with a simple observation: a truly clean home changes how the whole week feels. From its Brooklyn base, the company set out to deliver house cleaning with the care of a much smaller outfit and the reliability of a much bigger one.

The team serves homes across Brooklyn and the five boroughs, hiring cleaners for reliability and a genuine eye for detail. Every visit runs on a room-by-room checklist, professional supplies ride along with the crew, and the goal is always the same: a home that shines when you walk back through the door.

Whether it is a weekly standard clean, a seasonal deep clean or a full move-out reset, Gantry Prime Shine treats your home like its own reputation depends on it, because it does.
