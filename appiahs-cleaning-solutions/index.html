Rooted on Young Street

Appiah's Cleaning Solutions started with a mop, a checklist, and a standard no one else in the neighborhood was meeting.

Based at 10 Young St in New Haven, CT, we are a house cleaning service that treats every home like the first one. The idea was never complicated: show up on time, clean like it matters, and leave the place better than you found it.

That standard turned one-time visits into standing appointments, and standing appointments into neighbors recommending us to neighbors. This site is our front door. The work inside is the same as it has always been: careful, consistent, and done right.
