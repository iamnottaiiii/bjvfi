Why it matters

The parts of your home
you never see need attention too

Dryer vents and air ducts work hardest where you cannot look. Here is what a professional cleaning takes care of.

01

Lint is fuel for dryer fires

Clogged dryer vents trap heat and highly flammable lint inside the line. A full cleaning removes the buildup from the dryer to the exterior cap, helping your laundry room stay safe and your dryer run cooler.

02

Dust, dander, and debris recirculate

Air ducts collect years of dust, pet dander, and fine debris that your HVAC system then pushes through every room. Cleaned ducts mean cleaner air moving through your home.

03

Blocked vents make dryers work overtime

Clothes taking two cycles to dry, a laundry room that runs hot, a dryer that shuts off early: these are classic signs of a restricted vent. Clearing the line restores airflow and shortens dry times.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Lint builds up along the entire vent run, not just in the trap you can see. We clean the full line from the back of your dryer to the exterior cap, using rotary brushes and vacuum extraction that reach every foot of the run.

  • Full vent line cleaning, dryer to exterior cap
  • Exterior vent cap inspection and clearing
  • Airflow check after cleaning
  • Signs of a clogged vent: clothes still damp, hot laundry room, burning smell, dryer shutting off mid-cycle
Sample pricing: most single-family dryer vent cleanings fall in the $129 to $249 range. Final price depends on vent length and access. Call (475) 326-6389 for a free, exact quote.

Illustration: airflow restored through a cleared vent line. Artwork, not a job photo.

Air Duct Cleaning

Your ductwork is the lungs of your home. Over time, supply and return ducts collect dust, pet dander, and fine debris that your system keeps pushing back into the rooms you live in. We clean the accessible ductwork so your air starts cleaner.

  • Supply and return duct cleaning
  • Vent register and grille cleaning
  • Great after renovations, moves, or years without service
  • Pet owners and allergy-sensitive households see the biggest difference
Sample pricing: typical whole-home air duct cleanings start around $299 and up, depending on home size and duct count. Call (475) 326-6389 for a free, exact quote.

Illustration: clean air moving through serviced ductwork. Artwork, not a job photo.

Vent Repair & Rerouting

Sometimes a cleaning reveals a bigger problem: crushed flexible duct behind the dryer, a vent that runs too long, a disconnected joint, or a cap that birds keep nesting in. We fix the run so the airflow stays strong after we leave.

  • Crushed or kinked duct replacement
  • Disconnected vent reconnection and sealing
  • Exterior vent cap replacement
  • Rerouting long or inefficient vent runs
Sample pricing: repairs are quoted on site after inspection, since every vent run is different. The inspection itself is free when you book a cleaning.
