Our story

A Malden crew with
a builder's eye

Deleon's Landscaping & Construction Inc. has served Massachusetts homes and businesses for over a decade, pairing expert design with solid craftsmanship.

Two trades, one team

  • Most companies do lawns or builds. Deleon's does both, so your patio, plantings, and masonry all come from one plan.
  • Homeowners and businesses across the Massachusetts area trust the crew with full-property projects.

What we stand on

  • Reliability and professionalism on every visit, from a seasonal cleanup to a full build.
  • Customer satisfaction first: reviewers call the crew prompt, easy to work with, and worth recommending.
  • Affordable pricing, so a beautiful outdoor space is within reach.
