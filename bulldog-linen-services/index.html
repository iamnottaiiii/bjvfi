Bulldog Linen Services is a Hamden, Connecticut business supply company serving the food and beverage world: restaurants, bars, caterers, and hospitality.

Who we are

Built for the dining room

Hospitality runs on details, and few details are more visible than linen. A crisp tablecloth, a clean napkin, a fresh apron: these are the small signals that tell guests they are in good hands.

Bulldog Linen Services exists to make those details effortless. We are a business supply service based at 109 Sanford St in Hamden, CT, focused on commercial linen for the food and beverage industry. Our routes run Monday through Friday, delivering fresh stock and collecting soiled linens on a schedule built around your business.

"Steady routes, honest pricing, and linens that show up ready to work."

Whether you run a white-tablecloth dining room, a busy bar, or a catering operation, we keep your linen inventory handled so your team can focus on service.
