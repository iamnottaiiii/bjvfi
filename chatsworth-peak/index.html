Services

Fence services and sample pricing

Every property is different, so final pricing comes from a free on-site estimate. The figures below are samples to help you budget.

Wood fence installation

Cedar and redwood privacy, picket, and ranch rail fences built on pressure treated posts set in concrete.

  • 6 ft cedar privacy fence, the Valley standard
  • Picket and spaced picket styles
  • Cap rails and trim details included
  • Staining available as an add-on
Sample: from about $45 per linear ft installed

Vinyl fence installation

Maintenance free privacy and semi-privacy vinyl that keeps its color in the California sun.

  • Solid privacy and lattice top styles
  • Never needs paint or stain
  • Wind rated panel options
  • Matching gates and hardware
Sample: from about $65 per linear ft installed

Iron fence and gates

Ornamental iron panels and custom gates for front yards, pool enclosures, and commercial perimeters.

  • Powder coated for rust resistance
  • Pool code compliant heights
  • Custom drive and walk gates
  • Repairs to existing ironwork
Sample: from about $90 per linear ft installed

Fence repair

Storm damage, leaning sections, rotted posts, and gates that drag. We repair what is sound and replace what is not.

  • Post replacement and re-setting
  • Rail and picket replacement
  • Gate re-hang and hardware upgrades
  • Honest advice on repair vs replace
Sample: most repairs quoted per site visit
