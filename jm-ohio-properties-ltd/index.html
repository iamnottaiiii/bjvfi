Our Story

About JM Ohio Properties

JM Ohio Properties, Ltd. is an Ohio real estate development and investment company headquartered in Cincinnati. The company was incorporated in Ohio on June 11, 2007, and focuses on the acquisition, improvement, and long term holding of residential properties.

Public records show a portfolio concentrated in Cincinnati neighborhoods including the 45227 corridor, with recorded activity spanning from 2014 through 2024. The company's financing history includes lending relationships with Stock Yards Bank and Trust and Cincinnatus Savings and Loan.

Recorded real estate documents associate investor Jeremiah Kraus with the company's entities and property activity in the Cincinnati market.
