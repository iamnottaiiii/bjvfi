Our story

A Panorama City company with a simple standard

Set in stone California started with a straightforward belief: the best construction work is the kind you stop noticing, because it simply holds. From our base on Crosnoe Avenue in Panorama City, we serve homeowners and businesses across the San Fernando Valley with stone, concrete, and masonry work built to that standard.

How we work
  1. Walk the site

    We look at grade, drainage, sun, and access before talking materials, because the ground decides what lasts.

  2. Say it straight

    You get a clear scope, a clear timeline, and a clear number. If a cheaper path would serve you better, we say so.

  3. Build like it is ours

    Clean lines, honest materials, and finishes we would put our name on. That is the whole quality program.
