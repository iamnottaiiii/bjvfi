Our Story

A Valley roofing shop built on straight talk

Panorama City Roofing & Commercial Roof Repair started with a simple observation: too many roofers in Los Angeles sell fear. We sell facts. We climb the roof, take the photos, and show you what is actually up there.

From our shop at 8741 Van Nuys Blvd, we serve homeowners and property managers across Panorama City, Van Nuys, North Hills, and the wider San Fernando Valley. Residential crews handle shingle, tile, and shake systems. Our commercial team specializes in flat and low-slope systems: TPO, modified bitumen, and reflective coatings that cut cooling costs.

We believe a roof is a system, not just a surface. Ventilation, underlayment, flashing, and drainage all matter as much as the shingles you see from the street. That is why every replacement includes an attic ventilation check and upgraded underlayment as standard, not as an upsell.
