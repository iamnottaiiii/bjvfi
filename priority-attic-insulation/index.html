What We Do

Insulation services

Priority Attic Insulation is an insulation contractor based in Glendora, CA. Below are the typical services offered. Call (626) 654-1732 to confirm details for your project.

A

Attic Insulation

Full and partial attic insulation installs using batt or rolled materials, fitted to your roofline and joist layout.

B

Blown-In Insulation

Loose-fill blown insulation that fills irregular spaces and tops up existing coverage for a consistent thermal barrier.

C

Insulation Removal

Removal of aged, compressed, or soiled insulation so a clean, effective layer can go in its place.

D

Air Sealing

Targeted sealing around attic hatches, recessed lights, duct chases, and plumbing penetrations to cut drafts.

E

Crawl Space Insulation

Insulation for crawl spaces and rim joists to reduce cold floors and moisture-related comfort problems.

F

Attic Ventilation Review

A look at intake and exhaust ventilation so insulation and airflow work together instead of against each other.
