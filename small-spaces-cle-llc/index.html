About

Small Spaces CLE, LLC designs and builds compact dwellings and mobile commercial structures in Valley View, Ohio, shipping homes across the United States.

Homes are built with the company’s proprietary TurboShell building system: high-strength structural steel and EPS foam that is 40 percent lighter than wood and resists rot, mold, bugs and critters, with full encapsulation insulation rated R20 to R60.

TurboShell: steel + EPS, R20-60, 100% recyclable
