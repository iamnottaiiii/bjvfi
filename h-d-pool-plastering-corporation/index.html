Our trade

Plaster is a craft

A pool surface has to hold water, stand up to chemicals and sun, and feel good underfoot. That takes prep, mix, timing, and a practiced trowel.

We focus on the surface: replasters that seal and smooth, remodels that bring old pools back, and new construction finished right the first time.

If your pool is stained, rough, or losing water, the surface is usually the story. Call us and we will take a look.
