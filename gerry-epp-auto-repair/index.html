Our story

A family name
on the door since 1998

Gerry Epp Auto Repair is a family-run shop in White Plains, built the old-fashioned way: one honest repair at a time.

Gerry Epp has spent more than four decades under the hood. When he opened his own shop on Printers Court in 1998, he brought a simple philosophy with him: tell the customer the truth, charge a fair price, and do the job like it is your own car.

That philosophy is why the shop is busy. Drivers from across Charles County and Southern Maryland bring their cars here, and keep bringing them back. Many of our customers have been with us for years, trusting Gerry and his team with everything from high-mileage daily drivers to newer cars like a 2020 Ford Fusion, and even Audis that other shops turn away.

We are not a chain, and we do not work on commission. When we say a part can wait, it can wait. When we say it cannot, we will show you why. That is how a family shop earns a 4.8-star reputation, one straight answer at a time.

Come by, meet the crew, and see what honest auto repair feels like.

Shop milestones
