Our Story

A cottage builder with a simple belief

tate modern cottage is a construction company based at 9441 Main St in Montgomery, Ohio, in the heart of Greater Cincinnati.

"We build eco-friendly cottage homes for those who embrace amazing design and quality."

That sentence is our whole philosophy. We believe a home does not need to be big to be beautiful, and that building with care for the environment and care for the homeowner are the same job.

From custom cottage builds to thoughtful remodels, we bring storybook charm and modern building sense to every project we take on.
