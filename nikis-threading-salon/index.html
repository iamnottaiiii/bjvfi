Our story

A neighborhood studio built on trust.

Niki’s threading salon opened on Elton Street in Brooklyn and quickly became a go to spot for threading and waxing in East New York. The business is registered in New York as Niki’s Threading Salon Inc. and is in active standing.

What guests notice

Guests describe a clean and elegant space with a comfortable, natural energy. Reviews highlight affordable prices, good quality products, and an owner who is attentive, professional and welcoming.

Why threading

Threading is precise, fast and gentle on skin, which is why so many regulars trust Niki’s with their brows month after month. First time visitors are walked through the process so they feel safe and comfortable.
