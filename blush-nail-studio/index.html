Her Story

From a home studio to a dream space

Jasmine started Blush Nail Studio years ago, working from her home and building a loyal following one perfect set at a time. Guests who found her then have never left, following her through every chapter.

Now she has expanded into a newly renovated studio space that guests describe as absolutely beautiful and welcoming. It is still the same one-on-one experience, just in a room that feels as special as the work.

What keeps people coming back is the way she works: she listens to exactly what you want, takes her time, and sweats every detail. Wild inspiration photos are welcome here.

She is also known for looking after guests with sensitive skin and fragrance allergies, going out of her way to make every appointment comfortable. It is the kind of care you remember.

She is so talented with her nail designs, no matter how crazy they may be. I constantly get compliments and always recommend her.
