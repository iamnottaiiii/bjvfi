Our story

A cookie shop with
a candy-store imagination

Beka's Cookie Co. is a South Side cookie shop in Hegewisch, Chicago, baking by the dozen for birthdays, classrooms, offices, and serious cookie people.

What the menu tells us

Beka's built a menu the way kids build a dream dessert table: nearly 50 flavors, real candy folded into the dough, stuffed centers, and brownie-cookie hybrids.

The shop also thinks in parties: decorate-it cookie kits with frosting bags and sprinkles, chocolate-dipped Oreos and pretzels, and custom themes for birthdays and school events, all with about a day of lead time.
