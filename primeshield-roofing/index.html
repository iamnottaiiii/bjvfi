Our story

The name is the method.

PrimeShield Roofing started with a frustration we kept hearing from homeowners: a roof is the most expensive part of a house to guess about. Guessing leads to overspending or under protecting, and both end with water where it should not be.

So we built the company around evidence. Every job starts with a camera: photos of every slope, every flashing, every soft spot. The quote reads like a report because it is one. And the workmanship warranty is handed over in writing at the final walkthrough, not promised over the phone.

"Prime means the best version. Shield means it holds. We try to be both, every job."

We are based at 965 Foothill Blvd in La Cañada Flintridge, and most of our work is within a short drive: the Foothill corridor, the hillside streets, and the neighborhoods where tile and shingle roofs take the brunt of wind season.
