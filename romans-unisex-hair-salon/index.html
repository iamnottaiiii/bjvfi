OUR STORY
ROMAN'S UNISEX HAIR SALON

A Bay Ridge institution, one haircut at a time

Roman's Unisex Hair Salon sits on 4th Avenue in the heart of Bay Ridge, Brooklyn, serving men, women, and kids from across the neighborhood. Owner Roman leads a skilled team known for friendly service and consistent, fashionable results.

Reviewers tell the story best: clients who have trusted the salon for nearly a decade, stylish updos that hold through the night, and color work done with patience and care. It is the kind of place where walk-ins become regulars and regulars become friends.
