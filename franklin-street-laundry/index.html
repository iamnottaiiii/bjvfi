How it works

Three steps,
one clean bag.

No appointments, no frills. Self-service laundry is as simple as it gets, and the routine takes about as long as a coffee run.

  • Sort lights and darks, pick a machine that fits the load, and load it up.
  • Wash, move to a dryer, and use the folding stations to sort it neatly.
  • Large-capacity machines take comforters and bulky items in one go.

Bring your own supplies

Bring detergent, softener, and dryer sheets from home, or pick them up nearby. Travel light and the machines do the rest.

First visit tips

Separate lights and darks before you arrive, choose a machine that fits the load with room to tumble, and move clothes to the dryer promptly so the next person can wash.
