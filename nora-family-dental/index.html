About us

A family practice
on Jamaica Ave

Nora Family Dental is a general dental practice in Jamaica, Queens, serving families across the 11423 neighborhood and nearby Hollis and Queens Village.

Our approach

Dentistry that talks
to you, not at you

Going to the dentist should never feel like a mystery. At Nora Family Dental, the focus is on clear communication: what is happening with your teeth, what your options are, and what comes next, explained in words that make sense.

The practice serves patients of all ages, from kids coming in for their first checkup to adults keeping up with routine preventive care. Weekday hours, Monday through Friday from 10 AM to 6 PM, make it easier to fit a visit into a busy week.

Located at 182-41 Jamaica Avenue in Jamaica, Queens, the office is convenient for families in Hollis, Queens Village, St. Albans and the surrounding neighborhoods.

General Dentist

Dr. Flora Mounessa, DDS

Dr. Mounessa leads the practice with a soft-spoken, patient-centered style. Reviewers describe her as polite, a great listener, and clear about treatment options.
