Our story

29 years on Cincinnati rooftops.

Budget Construction and Remodel is a chimney cleaning and repair company based in Cincinnati, Ohio. For 29 years we have focused on one craft: keeping chimneys safe, sound, and standing straight.

We specialize in repairs and rebuilds, the jobs other companies pass on. Cincinnati weather is hard on masonry, with freezing winters, heavy rain, and humid summers stressing every joint and crown. That is why so much of our work is preventative: catch the crack early, repoint the joint properly, and the chimney lasts for decades.

We serve homeowners across the city, from Hyde Park and Anderson Township to Westwood and neighborhoods near downtown, and throughout Hamilton County.
