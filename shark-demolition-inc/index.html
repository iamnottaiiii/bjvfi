01Who we are

Licensed. Bonded. Ready to work.

SHARK DEMOLITION INC is a California licensed contractor (CSLB license 1073478) headquartered in Winnetka, in the San Fernando Valley. The company has been licensed with the Contractors State License Board since 2021 and carries a surety bond and workers compensation coverage. Its registered trade covers demolition of buildings, house and building demolition, and advisory services relating to building demolition.

"Demolition is the first trade on a rebuild site. Done right, it saves the whole project time and money."

The Shark Demolition approach
02What the record shows

Work across Los Angeles County

Public building permit records show Shark Demolition completing demolitions of single family homes, detached garages, restaurants, car wash canopies, and a manufacturing building across Los Angeles County.
