Our story

Built for drivers
who hate dealership games

Speed Pro Motors started with a simple frustration: getting a car fixed, or buying a used one, should not feel like a negotiation with a stranger. So we built the shop we wanted to find.

Why we exist

A repair shop that sells cars, not a lot that guesses at repairs

Plenty of used car lots cannot tell you what is under the hood of what they sell. We can, because the same techs who inspect our inventory are the ones turning wrenches in the service bay.

That changes the conversation. When you ask about a car on our lot, you get maintenance history and an honest read on its condition, not a sales pitch. When you bring your own car in, you get the same honesty about what it actually needs.

The house rules
  • Diagnose before we quote, quote before we work
  • Show the old parts, explain in plain English
  • If it does not need fixing, we say so
  • Custom work with a written quote, always
