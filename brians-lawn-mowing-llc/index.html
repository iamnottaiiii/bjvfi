Brian's Lawn Mowing LLC is owned and run by Brian Watts out of 4905 Springdale Rd in Cincinnati's Groesbeck neighborhood. The business started in 1995 and became an LLC in 2005, according to its BBB profile. That is 30 plus years of mowing the same west side neighborhoods.
