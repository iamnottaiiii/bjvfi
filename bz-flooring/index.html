What a flooring contractor does for you

From tearing out the old to the final walk-through, here is how a full flooring project runs.

01

Measure and choose

You walk the rooms together, talk about how you live, and pick the material that fits the budget and the wear. Solid hardwood for the long haul, laminate or vinyl for busy households.

02

Prep the subfloor

Old flooring comes out, the subfloor gets leveled and cleaned, and moisture gets checked. The part nobody sees is the part that decides how long the floor lasts.

03

Install

Planks or tile go down square, tight, and true, with transitions and trim finished cleanly at every doorway.

04

Finish and walk-through

Baseboards back on, dust cleaned up, and a final pass with you to make sure every room looks the way it should.
