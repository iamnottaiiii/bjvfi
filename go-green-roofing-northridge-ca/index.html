Our story

A Northridge roofer with green in the name and the work

Go Green Roofing is a roofing contractor based on Reseda Boulevard in Northridge, California. The name says the mission: roofing work that protects the home and respects the environment around it. Cool roof coatings are the signature example. Instead of tearing off a roof that still has structure left, a reflective coating renews its surface, keeps more material out of landfills, and bounces solar heat away from the house.

“A roof should protect the home and the neighborhood around it.”

Public records list the company with a California C39 roofing contractor license and show permitted cool roof coating projects in the Los Angeles area.
