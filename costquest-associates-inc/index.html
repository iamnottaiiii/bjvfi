What we do

Consulting, data and economics for the broadband industry

Public-sector and private clients rely on CostQuest for cost modeling and related analytics that support efficient distribution of broadband deployment funds across the United States.

  1. 01

    Broadband consulting and advisory

    Strategic guidance for service providers and public agencies on deployment planning, funding programs and broadband policy.

  2. 02

    BroadbandFabric data suites

    Address-level location data that identifies every broadband serviceable location, tied to demand, demographics, service availability and economics.

  3. 03

    Cost modeling and network economics

    Network economics, profitability analysis and asset valuation that inform network investment and funding decisions.

  4. 04

    Mapping and data science

    Geospatial mapping, programming and data science that turn raw locations into deployment-ready insight.

Why it matters

Federal broadband funding runs on data. Ours is the reference.

CostQuest is recognized as the provider of the official data behind the Broadband Serviceable Location Fabric for the FCC's Broadband Data Collection and NTIA's BEAD programs, a reference that informs federal broadband funding and deployment decisions nationwide.

FCC Broadband Data Collection

Our Fabric data underpins the national broadband map that determines where funding is needed most.

NTIA BEAD program

The Broadband Equity, Access, and Deployment program allocates funds using Fabric-backed location data.

Providers and agencies

Internet service providers and government entities use our data for planning, qualification and reporting.

Services

Advisory and data for broadband decisions

Four practice areas, one goal: helping providers and agencies deploy broadband where it counts.

  1. 01

    Broadband consulting and advisory

    CostQuest provides consulting and advisory services to customers in the broadband industry, including service providers, as well as government entities. Engagements cover deployment strategy, funding program support and policy analysis.

  2. 02

    BroadbandFabric data suites

    The BroadbandFabric Data Suites give broadband providers the critical data to make deployment and business planning decisions. The data set provides accurate Broadband Serviceable Location coordinates that tie to demand and demographics, service availability and competition, and engineering and construction profiles.

  3. 03

    Cost modeling and network economics

    Network economics, profitability analysis and asset valuation. CostQuest's cost models support efficient distribution of broadband deployment funds across the United States.

  4. 04

    Mapping, programming and data science

    Geospatial mapping, programming and data science services that weave together address points, imagery and parcel data into a locational fabric used to identify broadband availability and gaps.

How engagements work: CostQuest serves both private broadband providers and public agencies. For specifics on consulting engagements or data licensing, please call (513) 941-9009.

The company

What CostQuest Associates does

CostQuest Associates is a specialized broadband consulting firm that provides consulting and advisory services as well as proprietary data to customers in the broadband industry, including service providers, and to government entities. The company is headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, with additional offices in Seattle, Washington, and Washington, D.C.

  1. 2024

    Employee ownership

    The company was sold to the CostQuest Employee Stock Ownership Plan, giving ownership to the company's employees.

  2. 2023

    NTIA Broadband Fabric contract

    CostQuest was awarded a federal contract for NTIA Broadband Fabric mapping data, valued at up to $47,918,524 over five and a half years, to support national broadband mapping initiatives.

  3. 2026

    Fixed wireless partnership

    CostQuest announced a commercial agreement bringing its Fabric data to fixed wireless access operators nationwide, enabling address-level coverage prediction and service qualification.
