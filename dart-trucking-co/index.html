Our Story

A Coraopolis trucking company that answers the phone

Dart Trucking Co runs out of 5 Thorn St in Coraopolis, right in the middle of the Pittsburgh freight corridor near the airport and the major interstates. We are a working trucking company, not a call center, and that is the point.

When you call (412) 269-0244, you talk to dispatch. When you book a load, a real truck shows up. We built the business on the simple idea that shippers want reliability they can reach, so we keep our operation lean and our word good.

Local manufacturers, warehouses, and distributors count on Dart for regional hauls, local deliveries, and dedicated runs. We know the docks, the back roads, and the rush hour patterns of Western Pennsylvania because we drive them every day.
