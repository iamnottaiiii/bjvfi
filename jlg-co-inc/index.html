Our story

A neighborhood flooring store with national muscle

JLG Company Inc is an independent flooring retailer with a showroom at 419 Wards Corner Road in Florence, Kentucky. The store carries everything from ceramic tile and hardwood to carpet, luxury vinyl, and countertops.

JLG is a member dealer of Flooring Plus, a national buying group of independent retailers. That membership means bulk pricing from major manufacturers, plus the accountability of a group whose reputation rides on every store.

The team is known for practical, pressure-free guidance: considering the room, the traffic, the kids, the pets, and the light before recommending a single sample.
