About

A Sylmar concrete crew

Stone Horse Concrete Co is a concrete contractor based at 12117 Havana Ave in Sylmar, California, working across the San Fernando Valley.

Concrete is unforgiving: once it sets, the work is the work. That is why the unglamorous parts matter most, the base compaction, the form lines, the joint layout, the cure. We spend the time there so the slab spends decades not moving.

Call (818) 447-4072, Monday through Friday, 6:30 AM to 8:00 PM, and tell us what you are pouring.

How we work

  • On-site measure. Every quote starts with a tape measure, not a guess.
  • Base first. Compacted base and proper thickness under every slab.
  • Straight forms. Form lines set with a string line, checked twice.
  • Control joints. Joints cut where concrete wants to crack, so it does not crack where you look.
  • Cure and clean. We protect the finish while it cures and leave the site broom-clean.
