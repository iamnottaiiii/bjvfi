How we help

One specialist, one plan, your home

Our Independent Living Specialists are specifically trained to help you regain independence at home. It starts with listening, then a plan built around your life.

Home Evaluations

A thorough, in-home look at barriers and risks, so nothing important is missed. Recommendations consider current needs and what may come next.

Accessibility Consultations

Personalized guidance on what to change, what to add, and what can wait, grounded in universal design principles.

Fall Prevention

Practical steps to make daily routines safer, from lighting and layout to the right equipment in the right place.

Wheelchair Assessment

Matching mobility equipment to both the person and the home, so movement stays smooth and safe.

Medical Equipment and Assistive Technology

Help choosing and acquiring products that truly fit, plus training so everyone is confident using them.

Caregiver Training

Family and caregivers learn safe, sustainable ways to support daily life without burning out.

Our care process

From first call to confident living

Every engagement follows a clear, numbered path. You will always know what step you are on and what comes next. Details and timing are confirmed together, never assumed.

1

The Conversation

It begins with a call. We listen to your story, your goals, and your worries, and answer your questions honestly. If we are not the right fit, we will tell you.

Start here: (513) 276-0515
2

Home Evaluation

An Independent Living Specialist visits your home to survey barriers, observe how you move through your day, and identify safety risks, including fall hazards.

In person or via telehealth where appropriate
3

Your Game Plan

You receive a thorough, personalized report: a game plan for today, tomorrow, and the future. It prioritizes what matters most and sequences changes so they build on each other.

Written report you can share with family and providers
4

Modifications and Equipment

Universal design remodeling guidance, accessibility changes, wheelchair assessments, and assistive technology: we help you select the right products and coordinate the work.

Product knowledge and acquisition support
5

Caregiver Training

Family members and caregivers learn practical, sustainable ways to support daily routines safely. Everyone leaves with confidence, not confusion.

Training for the whole support circle
6

Follow Up

Needs change, and plans should keep up. We follow up to make sure you are satisfied and adjust recommendations as life evolves.

A relationship, not a one-time visit

About us

A specialist who shows up for your family

Rebuild Independence LC was founded on a simple belief: with the right plan, most people can keep living at home, safely and with dignity.

Marnie Renda

OTR/L · CAPS · ECHM
Independent Living Specialist

Owner and occupational therapist. Marnie has spent her career helping elders and persons with disabilities figure out how they can continue to live at home, combining clinical training with deep product and home-modification knowledge.

The Destination Home program

When disability or illness strikes, Destination Home helps you pick up the pieces. Independent Living Specialists are specifically trained to help you regain independence at home through a personalized accessibility consultation, universal design remodeling, caregiver training, and assistive technology solutions.

We help you create a game plan for today, tomorrow, and the future.
