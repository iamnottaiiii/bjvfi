The craft first

Paperhanging is a trade of millimeters. Paste the wrong weight, miss a seam by a hair, and the whole wall tells on you. Mike Conway has been hanging paper and brushing paint in Delaware County homes for more than four decades, and the work shows it: crisp seams, smooth walls, and rooms that look finished instead of just painted.

Conway Paperhanging and Painting was established in 1981 and is run and owned by Mike Conway, a home improvement contractor. The business is built on honesty, courtesy, reliability and hard work, and on the simple promise of stress free, positive results. Fully insured, professional and licensed, serving the tri state area.

Services

One call. Every surface.

From a single accent wall to a full exterior repaint, every job starts with prep and ends with a walkthrough. Estimates are always free.

  1. Paperhanging

    Common wallpaper, borders, decals, murals, vinyl wallpaper and pre pasted high end papers. Pattern matched, seams invisible.

  2. Interior painting & priming

    Walls, ceilings and trim painted with premium products chosen for your room, with Mike's expert opinion on products and design included.

  3. Exterior painting & priming

    Siding, trim, doors and fences, prepped with scraping, caulking and power washing for a finish that lasts.

  4. Drywall & sheetrock

    Wall scraping, drywall and sheetrock repairs and installation, spackling and full wall prep before paint or paper.

  5. Staining

    Staining for floors, decks and fences that protects the wood and brings out the grain.

  6. Power washing

    Exterior surfaces washed clean so paint and stain bond properly and look sharp.

Our story

A family trade, done right.

In 1981 Mike Conway picked up the trade and never put it down. More than forty years later, Mike Conway Paperhanging & Painting is still run and owned by Mike himself, a home improvement contractor based in Springfield, PA, in Delaware County.

It is a locally owned family business that stakes its name on honesty, courtesy, reliability and hard work. For more than 25 years Mike has served the tri state area, dedicated to client satisfaction and superb work. Fully insured, professional and licensed, the promise is simple: stress free, positive and timely results.
