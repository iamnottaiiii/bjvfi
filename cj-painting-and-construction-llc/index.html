About

Prep is the product

C&J Painting and Construction LLC started in 2020 in Fairview, New Jersey, and built its reputation one careful job at a time. The philosophy is simple: most paint jobs fail in the prep, so the crew spends the time there, scraping, sanding, patching, and priming before the finish coats go on.

That approach shows up in the reviews. One customer watched the crew spend four days prepping a weather-beaten deck and stucco foundation before two days of painting, and called the end product great. Another hired the crew to finish condo renovations on a deadline, and praised them as responsive, professional, and punctual at every step.

The business offers free estimates, emergency services, and Spanish-language service, and takes on residential and commercial work across Bergen County.
