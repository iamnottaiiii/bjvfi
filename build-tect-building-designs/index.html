About

A design-build practice in the Valley

Build-Tect Building Designs is a general contractor based in North Hills, in the San Fernando Valley, combining building design with construction.

The idea

Design that knows how to build

A beautiful drawing that cannot be permitted or built is not a plan, it is a wish. Build-Tect was built around a simple idea: the people who design your project should also know how to construct it.

That is why design, plans, permits, and construction are handled together here. Decisions made on paper account for cost, structure, and schedule from the start, so there are fewer surprises once work begins.

Based at 9707 Woodley Ave in North Hills, the company serves homeowners across the San Fernando Valley and the greater Los Angeles area.

The practice

Accountability, end to end

When one company designs and builds, there is no finger-pointing between the architect and the contractor. Questions get answered in hours, not weeks, because the answer is in the same office.

It also keeps the budget honest. Design decisions are weighed against real construction costs while they can still be changed on paper, which is always cheaper than changing them in the field.
