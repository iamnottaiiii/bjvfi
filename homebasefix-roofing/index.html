Our story

An Altadena crew,
roofs only

We are roofers, not generalists. That focus is the whole business model.

HomeBasefix Roofing is based on Fair Oaks Ave in Altadena, and just about everything we do happens within a short drive of home. We know these roofs: the 1920s bungalows with original tile, the mid century ranches with low slopes, the hillside homes that catch every Santa Ana gust.

Foothill roofs live a harder life than most. Blazing summer sun bakes shingles, winter rains find every weak flashing, Santa Ana winds lift whatever is loose, and fire season puts ember resistance at the top of every homeowner's mind. We build and repair for all of it, not just the rain.

How we work

We get on the roof and look. Then we tell you what we see, in plain language, with photos. Then we put the scope in writing before any work starts. Repairs get the same care as replacements: proper materials, proper flashing, proper cleanup.

And when we leave, the yard is clean. Nails get magnet swept, gutters get cleared of debris, and you walk the finished work with us before we call it done.

Why roofs only

A crew that does a little of everything masters nothing. We chose roofs because a roof is the one part of your house you cannot afford to get wrong. Specialization means we have seen your problem before, we stock the right materials, and we know what lasts in this climate.

"A roof is the one part of your house you cannot afford to get wrong."
