About the Practice

Meet the dentist and the office behind the name.

Dr. Linh Q. Nguyen, DDS

Dr. Nguyen is a general dentist practicing in Annandale, Virginia, and the dentist behind Confident Dentistry Corporation. Licensed in Virginia, Maryland, and Pennsylvania, Dr. Nguyen provides general dental care including preventive, restorative, and diagnostic treatment for patients of all ages.
