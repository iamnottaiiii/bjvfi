About

Full service, friendly shop

Fade In Full Barbershop is a Whittier shop where the welcome is as warm as the hot lather. Reviewers highlight the friendly crew and the easy, unpretentious atmosphere that makes first visits feel comfortable.

The specialty is right there in the name: fades done in full, from subtle tapers to high skin blends. Follow the shop on Instagram for fresh cuts and shop updates.
