About

A contractor you can actually reach

Kilroy Construction is a general contracting business based in Shrewsbury, MA, run by people who pick up the phone.

Who we are

Local crew, licensed trade

Kilroy Construction works out of 40 Browning Rd in Shrewsbury, serving homeowners across Worcester County. The business is led by Timothy J. Kilroy, who holds an active Massachusetts Construction Supervisor license (CS-103252).

We keep the operation lean on purpose: the person who estimates your job is connected to the crew that builds it. That is how small contractors stay honest, and it is how we plan to stay.

Why hire us

Straight answers beat slick sales

Big remodeling companies sell you with a showroom. We would rather win the job with a straight estimate, a realistic timeline, and work that holds up when the inspector leaves.

If a job is not a fit, we will tell you that too, and point you in a better direction. We would rather lose a bid honestly than win one we cannot stand behind.
