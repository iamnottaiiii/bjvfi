Why Happy Home

Forty years of floors that still turn heads

Most floors do not need replacing. They need a craftsman with a sander, a good eye for stain, and the patience to do every pass right. That is the whole business, and it has been for four decades.

We sand down to fresh wood, repair what is damaged, then build the finish back up, coat by coat, until the grain glows again. Or we lay new hardwood from subfloor to final finish, flat, tight and quiet underfoot.

Call for a free estimate or just for advice on your hardwood floors. We will tell you honestly whether your floors should be refinished or replaced.

Sanding & Refinishing

Scratches, dull finish and wear patterns sanded away, then stained and sealed like new.

New Installation

Solid and engineered hardwood, laid flat and tight, finished to match your home.

Repairs

Damaged boards, squeaks and gaps fixed properly, blended into the surrounding floor.

A good refinish doubles the life of your floors at a fraction of replacement cost.

The Happy Home approach

Services

Everything your hardwood floors need

Four decades of sanding, staining and laying wood across Los Angeles. Here is the full menu.

01

Hardwood Sanding & Refinishing

Our signature work. We sand worn floors down to fresh wood, then stain and seal them back to life. Scratches, pet wear, sun fading and dull traffic lanes disappear.

  • Dust-controlled sanding
  • Custom stain matching
  • Matte, satin or gloss finish
02

New Hardwood Installation

Solid and engineered hardwood installed over a properly prepped subfloor, flat, tight and quiet. We help you pick the species, width and finish that suits the room.

  • Solid & engineered wood
  • Oak, maple, walnut & more
  • Transitions & thresholds
03

Repairs & Board Replacement

Water damage, deep gouges, squeaky boards and gaps. We cut out the bad, lace in the new and blend the repair so it disappears into the floor.

  • Board replacement
  • Squeak repair
  • Gap filling
04

Stain, Seal & Finish Options

Go darker, go natural, or match an existing floor. We test stain colors on your actual wood so there are no surprises, then lock it in with durable finish coats.

  • Color consultations
  • Sample boards on site
  • Low-VOC finish options
