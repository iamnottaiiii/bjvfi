About Jo-Med Contracting Corp.

Jo-Med Contracting Corp. is based at 300 S Second St in Elizabeth, New Jersey. Industry directory records list the company as operating since 1998, led by president John Medeiros, specializing in concrete work including sidewalks and flat concrete, plus sewer and underground utilities.

In 2018 the company received the Pulse of the City News award for excellence in customer satisfaction, based on independent research into customer service performance.
