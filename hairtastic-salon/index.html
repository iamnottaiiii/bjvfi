Our story

Making clients feel HairTastic since 2016

Owner Jessica Baumgartner has been a licensed cosmetologist since 2007, trained at EMCC, a vocational school in Fairport, NY.

Jessica started her career at Hairzoo, where she built speed and skill behind the chair, then moved to the JCPenney Salon, learning from fellow stylists and continuing her education through the on site education program. After six years with a corporation, she was ready for her own chair, on her own terms.

The opportunity came as a rental: the first floor of a house that was already a salon. After the sale of the building, renovations began. Floors were redone, walls got a fresh coat of paint, and on January 15, 2016, HairTastic Salon opened for business. The work of making it beautiful and inviting has never really stopped.

Today HairTastic is known for all things hair: precision cuts, dimensional and vivid color, perms, bridal styling, and facial waxing, delivered with the relaxed, personal touch of a true neighborhood salon.
