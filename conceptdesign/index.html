Services

The concept-phase timeline

Every project follows the same five phases, from first sketch to final walkthrough. Scope of services below is typical for this trade and Sample call to confirm.

01

Concept Sketch

We meet on site or by phone, listen to what you want, and put the first ideas on paper: rough sketches, measurements, and a plain-language scope of work.

Deliverable: sketch + written scope Sample
02

Design Development

Sketches become working drawings. Layouts, materials, and finishes are selected and priced so the estimate reflects real choices, not guesses.

Deliverable: drawings + line-item estimate Sample
03

Permits & Planning

We prepare the paperwork, coordinate permits with the local jurisdiction, and sequence the job so materials and crews arrive in the right order.

Deliverable: permit set + schedule Sample
04

Build

Construction proceeds against the plan: framing, systems, finishes, and regular check-ins so you always know what happens next and when.

Milestone updates as work progresses Sample
05

Walkthrough & Punch List

A final walkthrough with you, a written punch list, and sign-off only when every item on the original scope is complete.

Deliverable: completed punch list Sample
