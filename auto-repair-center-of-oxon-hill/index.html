Our story

The Livingston Road shop with a reputation

On Livingston Road in Oxon Hill sits a shop that drivers describe with the same words over and over: honest, professional, transparent. Auto Repair Center of Oxon Hill, ARC to its regulars, built that reputation one repair at a time.

It is primarily a mechanical repair shop, the kind of place people find after a bad experience somewhere else. Reviewers tell that story repeatedly: money wasted at another shop, then someone pointed them to ARC, and the problem finally got fixed right.

The front desk gets its own share of the praise. Customers call the staff helpful and kind, the kind of people who keep you informed on wait times and work with you on price. Names that come up in reviews include Jackie and Deuce, the team members regulars ask for by name.

"All mechanics were professional, transparent and honest about all repairs, from the initial inquiry to the finished job."

Paraphrased from public reviews
