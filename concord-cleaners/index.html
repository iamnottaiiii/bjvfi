About us

A neighborhood cleaner on Sudbury Road

Concord Cleaners sits at 191 Sudbury Rd in Concord, Massachusetts, serving neighbors who want their clothes handled with care and returned on time. Customers have been trusting us with their wardrobes for years, from everyday work shirts to the suits and dresses that matter most.

What keeps people coming back is simple: polite service at the counter, garments that come back clean and sharply pressed, and alterations that are done well at prices that feel fair.

Stop by with a bag, or call ahead and we will talk through what your items need.

  • Dry cleaning for everyday and special occasion garments
  • Laundered and pressed shirts
  • Alterations, hems, and repairs
  • Comforters, blankets, and household items
  • All major credit cards accepted
  • Free lot parking at the door
