Our Story

Remodeling the tri-state since 2009.

Robinson Remodeling, LLC started in Amelia, Ohio with a simple idea: one crew that can handle an entire remodel, from the first sketch to the final coat of paint. More than fifteen years later, that is still how we work.

Instead of juggling a plumber, an electrician, a painter, and a tile setter from four different companies, our customers deal with one contractor and one schedule. Our own crew covers kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, tile, decks, painting, plumbing, and electrical, which keeps projects moving and accountability clear.

Homeowners across the Cincinnati tri-state area, in Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana, call us when they want the job done right the first time and a crew that communicates. We offer free estimates on every project and military discounts for those who serve.
