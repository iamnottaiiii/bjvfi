About

A Carlisle company that
treats old houses right

Restoration Painting & Carpentry Services, Inc. is based at 13 Lowell St in Carlisle, MA, a BBB-accredited company built around careful prep and honest work.

Our approach

Prep first,
paint second

Anyone can roll paint onto a wall. The difference is what happens before: the scraping, the sanding, the rot repair, the caulking. That is where a paint job becomes a restoration.

One accountable crew

Because we do both carpentry and painting, the wood gets repaired properly before it gets painted. Nothing falls between two contractors.

Recognized for service

A 2018 Pulse of the City News award winner for excellence in customer satisfaction, with a 5-out-of-5 rating in their research.

Older-home specialists

Carlisle and the surrounding towns are full of antique homes. We are set up for exactly that work: lead-safe care, wood repair, and period detail.
