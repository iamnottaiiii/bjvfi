Who we are

Earthwork is the whole business

Richard Record & Son General is an excavating contractor based at 709 South St in Shrewsbury, Massachusetts. Construction directories list the company's main lines of business as excavating contractors and site development, with Worcester County as its home service area.

That focus matters. Excavation is the first trade on site and the one everything else depends on: foundations, drainage, driveways, and utilities all start with ground that was moved correctly.

If you have a project that starts with earthwork, call and talk it through. You will get a direct answer about whether the job is a fit, and a quote based on the actual site.
