Our Story

Marblehead born, Marblehead raised.

Dr. Terence Alan Criswell, DDS, opened his dental practice at 40 Pleasant Street in 1984. Forty-two years later he is still there, still licensed, and still practicing the way dentistry was meant to be done: carefully, honestly, and with real relationships.

In an era when dental offices consolidate into chains, this practice has stayed independent and personal. The Massachusetts Board of Registration in Dentistry shows Dr. Criswell holding an active general practice license, and the Better Business Bureau profile reflects 42 years in business with a small, steady team.

Patients come for cleanings and stay for the trust. There is no upsell culture here, just a dentist who has spent a working lifetime keeping Marblehead smiles healthy.
