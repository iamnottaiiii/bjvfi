Our story

A ranch heart on Main Street

The sun comes up early here

Our ovens warm up before the street does, so the first tray of conchas is ready when East Chicago wakes up. That is the rhythm we have kept from day one.

The neighborhood's bakery

La Rancherita Bakery sits right on Main Street in East Chicago, and it shows. Folks stop in once a week, sometimes more, for pastries they say they cannot find anywhere else, at prices that make it an easy habit.

The cakes are what put us on the map. Neighbors call them the best in town, freshly baked and finished with strawberries, bananas, and whipped cream. Bring us an idea for a special-order cake and we will make it better than you pictured it.

Come hungry after dinner too. More than a few guests make an evening of it: a meal next door, then dessert with us before the lights go out.
