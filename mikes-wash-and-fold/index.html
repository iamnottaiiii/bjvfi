Our story

Laundry, the neighborhood way.

Mikes Wash and Fold opened on Amity Road with one idea: make laundry day painless for the people of New Haven. No memberships, no apps you have to download, no fine print. Just clean machines, a fair wash and fold service, and somebody at the counter who will actually help you.

We know the regulars. We know the students with a mountain of hoodies, the parents with a week of kid clothes, the small business owners with uniforms that never stop. Whoever you are, your clothes get the same care we would give our own.

Why wash and fold?

Because your weekend is worth more than a laundromat chair. Drop your bag, run your errands, and come back to laundry that is washed, dried, and folded. It is the easiest chore you will ever outsource.

Why do it yourself here?

Because sometimes you like your laundry your way. Our self-service floor gives you the machines, the supplies, and the space, without the laundromat horror stories. Clean, bright, and looked after.
