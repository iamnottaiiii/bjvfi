About the Department

The Springfield Twp Services Road Department is the public works arm of Springfield Township, Hamilton County, Ohio, headquartered at 952 Compton Road.

What We Do

The department maintains and improves township roads, and supports other township infrastructure needs. Work includes routine road maintenance and repairs, coordinated resurfacing projects, and service to township public spaces. Verified

Springfield Township government operates under the Ohio Township form of government, with services funded through township levies including the Road and Bridge levy. Verified

Where We Serve

The department serves township-maintained roadways within Springfield Township, Ohio. County and state routes are handled by their respective agencies. If you are unsure whether a road is township-maintained, call (513) 522-4004 to confirm.
