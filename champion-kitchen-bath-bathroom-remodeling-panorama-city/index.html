Our Story

A remodeling crew rooted in Panorama City

Champion Kitchen Bath Bathroom Remodeling Panorama City grew out of a simple idea: remodeling goes better when one accountable crew handles the whole job. From our shop at 14350 Arminta St, we serve homeowners across the San Fernando Valley who want honest work at a fair price.

We specialize in the rooms that matter most. Kitchens and bathrooms are where a home shows its age first, and where a good remodel pays back every single day. Our crew does the demo, the rough work, and the finish detail, so nothing gets lost between subcontractors.

What clients notice most is communication. You will know the schedule, you will know the cost before we start, and you will know who to call with a question. That is how a remodeling company earns its name in its own neighborhood.
