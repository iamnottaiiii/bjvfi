Why sealcoat

Sealcoating is cheap insurance.

Sun and water break asphalt down over time. A proper sealcoat locks out moisture, slows cracking, and keeps the surface looking new. It is one of the simplest ways to make a driveway or lot last years longer.

  • Protects against water and UV damage
  • Restores a deep, like new finish
  • Costs far less than repaving
