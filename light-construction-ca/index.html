Our story

A North Hollywood crew

Inferred details

Based on Rhodes Avenue

light Construction CA works out of Rhodes Avenue in North Hollywood, right in the middle of the neighborhoods it serves. Being local is not a marketing line here. It means we are ten minutes away when something needs a second look, and our reputation travels by word of mouth on the same streets we build on.

The crew takes on the full spread of residential work: the kitchen that finally gets done right, the bathroom that stops leaking, the addition that gives a growing family room to breathe.

How we work

1. Walk and talk. We come out, look at the project, and listen first.
2. Scope in writing. You get a clear list of what is included and what it costs.
3. Clean build. Drop cloths down, debris out daily, neighbors respected.
4. Final walk. We do not leave until you have looked at everything.

Most of our work comes from repeat clients and referrals. That only happens one way: by leaving every job better than we found it.
