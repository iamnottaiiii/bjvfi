About us

Masons by trade,
neighbors by choice

Old Stone Masonry is a family owned masonry contractor based in West Boylston, Massachusetts, serving homeowners and businesses across the region.

Our approach

Old craft, honest work

Masonry is one of the oldest building crafts in the world, and we are proud to carry its traditions forward. Dry stone stacking, running bond brickwork, careful repointing: techniques refined over centuries, executed with modern materials.

As a family owned, insured and bonded contractor, we answer our own phone, show up when we say we will, and stand behind every wall we build.

  • Family owned and locally operated
  • Insured and bonded for your protection
  • Serving Massachusetts, New Hampshire and Rhode Island
What drives us

Preserving what was built to last

We take special pride in restoring historic masonry, the stone foundations, brick facades and chimneys that give New England its character. Repairing them properly takes patience and respect for the original craft.

Whether it is a crumbling garden wall or a full historic restoration, we bring the same standard: precision, durability and an eye for how the finished work should look fifty years from now.
