Our story

A neighborhood cleaner,
an old-fashioned standard

British Imperial Cleaners is a locally owned dry cleaner on South Juniper Street in Center City, serving the neighbors around Rittenhouse and Washington Square West.

Who we are

Care you can see in every seam

The name says it all. We hold ourselves to a high, almost old-world standard: garments inspected piece by piece, stains treated individually, and pressing done by hand so the finish looks natural, not stiff.

We are a small, locally owned shop, and we like it that way. When you drop off your clothes at 1326 S Juniper St, you are dealing with people who know the craft and know the neighborhood.

Whether it is a weekly stack of shirts, a suit before the big meeting, or the coat that has seen one too many winters, we treat it like it matters. Because to you, it does.

"From the counter to the press, everything is done with the kind of care you rarely see anymore."

The Imperial standard
