01Our story

A lumber yard for the Valley's builders

CALIFORNIA LUMBER & HARDWARE sits at 8907 Woodman Ave in Pacoima, in the industrial heart of the northeast San Fernando Valley. It is a working lumber yard and hardware store, the kind of place where the parking lot holds work trucks by 8 AM and the conversation at the counter is about board feet and hinge overlay.

The yard's focus is narrow on purpose: hardwood plywood, dimensional hardwood lumber, panel goods, cabinet hardware, edge banding, adhesives, and storage systems. Everything a cabinet shop or serious remodeler needs, nothing they do not.
