Direct line, straight answers.

There is no online quote form and no middleman. You call the shop, describe what you need fabricated or installed, and get a direct answer on whether the job fits.

Bring drawings or dimensions if you have them; if not, describe the application and the shop will tell you what information is needed for a quote.

Services

Sheet metal capabilities.

Representative sheet metal work for commercial and industrial clients. Exact capabilities, materials, and project minimums are confirmed on your call.

  1. 01

    Custom Sheet Metal Fabrication

    One-off and short-run parts cut and formed to specification: brackets, panels, housings, and machine guards for commercial and industrial use.

  2. 02

    Cutting, Forming, and Bending

    Sheet stock sheared, bent, and formed to dimension, including brake work for angles, channels, and enclosures.

  3. 03

    Welding and Assembly

    Joining and assembly of fabricated components, from tacked subassemblies to finished welded units ready for install.

  4. 04

    Repair and Rework

    Repair of damaged sheet metal components and rework of existing parts: patches, reinforcement, and refabrication of worn sections.
