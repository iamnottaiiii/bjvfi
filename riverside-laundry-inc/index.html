Our Story

A neighborhood laundry on Poplar Street

Riverside Laundry Inc is a locally run laundry at 281 Poplar Street in New Haven, Connecticut. Listed as a laundromat, dry cleaner, and commercial laundry, it serves both neighborhood households and local businesses that need reliable, no drama laundry service.

Run by people who know laundry

This is a working laundromat, not a franchise counter. The team on site knows the machines, the cycles, and how to get a stubborn load truly clean.

Right in the neighborhood

Located on Poplar Street in the 06513 area of New Haven, Riverside is an easy stop for surrounding blocks and nearby workplaces.

Built for businesses too

Listed as a commercial and linen supply laundry, Riverside handles repeat business loads like uniforms and linens. Call to talk through regular service.

Our promise

Clean machines, honest service

We keep it simple: working equipment, a tidy store, and straight answers about price and timing. If something is not right with your load, tell us and we will make it right.
