About

A neighborhood institution since 1983

The station, the standard, and the people behind the counter.

Dick's Service Station has sat at 24-62 49th St in Astoria since 1983, operating as a gasoline service station and auto repair shop. That longevity was not bought with advertising. It was earned with honesty.

Woody and Chris

Customers name them directly. Woody, who has been the family mechanic for some drivers for over 35 years, and Chris, who together treat everyone like VIPs. Reviews describe being welcomed with smiles, offered a complimentary water bottle while waiting, and kept informed if a job runs long.

The honesty standard

The story reviewers tell most: being talked out of a service. One customer came in expecting a tune-up and was told the car did not need it. That moment is why loyal customers return year after year for inspections, oil changes and repairs, and why the station is called the best mechanic shop in the area.

Routine maintenance to complex repairs, done right at fair prices. That is the whole pitch, and it has worked since 1983.
