Who we are

Construction with a craftsman's care

Supreme Construction Service Pasadena is based at 775 E Green St in Pasadena, serving homeowners across Pasadena and the San Gabriel Valley. We take on the projects that matter most to a household: kitchens, baths, additions, and the repairs that keep a home in good shape.
