Why homeowners call us

A deck is not lumber. It is where your summers happen.

Anyone can screw boards together. A good deck takes layout, patience, and a carpenter who cares about the details you cannot see as much as the ones you can. We frame every deck square and true, flash every ledger, and set every fastener with intention, because a deck should still be solid decades from now.

We build the deck we would want on our own home, and we do not cut corners to get there.

From the first sketch to the final walkthrough, you deal directly with the person building your project. No sales teams, no handoffs, no surprises. Just honest carpentry and a straight answer about what your project needs.

Services

No. 01

Custom Decks

New deck construction from the ground up. We design layouts that fit your yard, your house, and how you actually live outside, then frame and finish them for decades of use.

  • Wood and composite decking options
  • Multi level and wraparound layouts
  • Proper footings, ledgers, and flashing
No. 02

Deck Repair and Resurfacing

Not every tired deck needs a full rebuild. We replace rotted boards, reinforce framing, rebuild stairs, and resurface sound structures so you get a like new deck for less.

  • Board and railing replacement
  • Structural reinforcement
  • Stair rebuilds and safety fixes
No. 03

Porches and Patios

Covered porches, front stoops, and ground level patios that connect your home to the yard. Built to match your house and stand up to Maryland seasons.

  • Covered and open porches
  • Front steps and stoops
  • Patio framing and borders

Railings, Stairs, and Details

The parts you touch every day deserve the most care. Custom railings, wide comfortable stairs, built in benches, and trim details that make a deck feel finished.

  • Custom wood and composite railings

  • Lighting ready framing and trim

Small Carpentry Projects

Our story

Carpentry is a craft. We treat it like one.

Cote's carpentry is a deck builder based on Ridge Chapel Road in Hanover, Maryland, serving homeowners who want outdoor spaces built with real care.

A deck is one of the few parts of your home you buy with your eyes wide open. You stand on it, you lean on the rail, you feel every board under your feet. That is why we build the way we do: measure twice, frame it true, and finish every detail as if we were going to live with it ourselves.

Cote's carpentry grew out of a simple belief that good work speaks for itself. We keep our operation lean and personal so the person you talk to on the phone is the person swinging the hammer. No runaround, no shortcuts, no mystery about where your money went.

Build it once. Build it right. That is the whole business plan.

When you hire us, you get a clear quote, a realistic schedule, and a deck that is built to code and built to last. And when we leave, your yard is clean and your new favorite room has no roof.
