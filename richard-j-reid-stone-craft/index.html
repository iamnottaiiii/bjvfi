Our Craft

Masonry is a slow trade, and that is the point

Richard J Reid Stone Craft operates out of Chatsworth, California, serving the San Fernando Valley with stone and masonry work. The business is listed in local contractor directories under masonry and stonework, and keeps its focus narrow: stone, block, and mortar done right.

The approach is simple. Choose the right stone for the exposure, cut it to fit tight, and lay it so the wall reads as one piece. No shortcuts in the mortar bed, no loose veneer, no mess left behind.

Materials

Natural stone, manufactured stone, honest advice

Natural ledgestone and fieldstone for timeless faces. Manufactured stone when budget and weight call for it. We will tell you which suits your wall, your climate exposure, and your wallet, then stand behind the choice.

  • Stone selection help at the yard or from samples
  • Proper flashing and drainage behind every veneer
  • Color matched mortar for a unified finish
