About

Local concrete, done the right way.

Dan's Concrete Services is a concrete contractor based in Fruit Cove, Florida, serving St. Johns County and the greater Jacksonville area. We are a working crew, not a sales team: the person who quotes your job is on site when it gets poured.

Why prep matters more than the pour.

Anyone can order a truck. The difference is what happens before it arrives: soil compacted in lifts, forms set dead level, steel placed where the load actually goes. That is the unglamorous work that keeps your driveway flat in year ten.

We quote honestly, show up when we say we will, and leave the site clean. If a repair makes more sense than a replacement, we will tell you that too.

How we work

1. Walk and measure. We visit, listen, and measure the real scope.

2. Written quote. Itemized price, timeline, and finish options.

3. Prep and pour. Base, forms, steel, then a clean pour day.

4. Cure and clean. Proper cure time guidance and a full cleanup.
