From Our Fryers

No mixes, no shortcuts. Just flour, sugar, and a family recipe that has fed Chicago's South Side since 1972.

Why "old fashion" is a promise, not a theme

At Old Fashion Donuts and Cafe, every batch starts from scratch before sunrise. The dough is mixed, cut, and fried fresh each morning, then glazed by hand while it is still warm. That is how the family's original Roseland shop earned its name, and it is how we do it on Harwood Avenue.

The apple fritter is the legend: fried hot, craggy, and packed with apple, famously "the size of your head." The glazed rings are light and fluffy enough to melt in your mouth. And the glazed croissant, flaky and dipped in sweet glaze, is the sleeper hit regulars swear by.

"Get there early, because the freshest picks go fast and the case empties by afternoon."

Pair it all with fresh-brewed diner coffee, and you have the classic American morning, done right.

Our Story

A South Side donut institution, now frying in Homewood.

From Roseland to Harwood Avenue

The Old Fashion Donuts family story starts in 1972 on Chicago's South Side, where the original Old Fashioned Donuts on Michigan Avenue in Roseland became a neighborhood legend. Generations of Chicagoans grew up on its apple fritters, glazed rings, and old-fashioned cake donuts, all made fresh by hand every morning.

That tradition is carried on by Burritt L. Bullock, known to everyone as "Mr. B.", owner of the original shop. In June 2026, Mr. B. brought the family recipes to Homewood with the grand opening of Old Fashion Donuts and Cafe on Harwood Avenue, where hundreds of neighbors lined up for free donuts to celebrate.

The promise has never changed: every batch from scratch, fried fresh daily, glazed by hand. That is the old fashion way.
