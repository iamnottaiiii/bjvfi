Our story

A Neighborhood Market With an Oven at Its Heart

Orland Market & Bakery serves Orland Park and the surrounding southwest suburbs with Mediterranean meals and groceries, fresh falafel, pita, salads, poultry, meats, and household needs.

The idea is simple: one stop for the week. The market shelves carry Middle Eastern groceries and everyday essentials, the butcher counter keeps fresh meats and poultry, and the kitchen serves the food people crave, from falafel sandwiches to a full mixed grill.

At the center of it all is the bakery. Every morning starts with the baker firing up the ovens so the pita, zaatar pies, and cheese pies are fresh when the doors open at 9. It is the smell regulars talk about and the reason many say the bread alone is worth the trip.

Whether you are stocking the pantry, grabbing lunch, or planning a family gathering, this is a place built for the neighborhood: welcoming, well stocked, and proud of what comes out of its ovens.
