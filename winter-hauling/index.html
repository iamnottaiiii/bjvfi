About Winter Hauling

Winter Hauling is a dump truck business based at 2301 Dorsey Road, Suite 201, in Glen Burnie, Maryland.

The company hauls aggregate material to Maryland, Virginia, Washington DC, Delaware, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey, running a daily fleet of dump trucks. Drivers carry TWIC cards and government clearance, and trucks hold ACT-90 permits.

Public carrier data: USDOT 870712, interstate operation. See the FMCSA carrier record and the Manta business profile.
