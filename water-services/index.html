Common questions

What should I do first when I find water damage?
If it is safe, stop the source: shut off the water valve to the leaking fixture or appliance. Then call us at (818) 867-8506. Move valuables and electronics away from the water, but leave heavy or risky work to the crew.
How much does water damage restoration cost?
It depends on how much water there is, how far it spread, and what materials are affected. Small cleanups can be a few hundred dollars; larger jobs with drying and rebuild run higher. We give an exact, written quote before any work begins.
Will my homeowner's insurance cover this?
Sudden water damage, like a burst pipe or appliance failure, is often covered, while slow leaks and neglect usually are not. Every policy is different. We document the damage thoroughly to support your claim.
How long does drying take?
Most homes take three to five days of active drying, depending on the extent of the water. We monitor moisture levels daily and only finish when readings are back to normal.
Do I need to worry about mold?
Mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours after water exposure, which is why fast extraction and thorough drying matter so much. We check for it on every job and remediate it when we find it.
