About us

The handy crew behind the name

Nailed It Home Repair LLC is a Worcester-based home repair company built for homeowners who want the job done right.

Big to small,
we do it all

Nailed It Home Repair LLC is a licensed and insured home improvement contractor, handyman, and remodeler serving Worcester, Massachusetts and the surrounding area.

Homeowners hire us because one call covers the whole list: the leaky faucet, the wobbly railing, the light fixture that never got installed, and the room that needs a refresh. Small jobs get the same care as big ones.

We show up on time, communicate clearly, and leave the work area clean. That is the whole business model: dependable repairs, fair dealing, and craftsmanship you can see.
