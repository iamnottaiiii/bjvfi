About S & L Auto Collision Auto Repair

S & L Auto Collision Auto Repair covers both sides of car trouble: mechanical repair and collision body work. One stop in the City of Orange for everything from fender benders to brake jobs, with repairs explained in plain language.

  • Collision Repair Accident damage repaired, from scraped panels to full body restoration.
  • Mechanical Repair Brakes, suspension, and engine work handled alongside body repairs.
  • Oil & Fluids Oil changes and fluid services to keep everything running smooth.
  • Paint & Finish Refinishing that matches your car's factory color.
  • AC Service Air conditioning diagnosis and recharge for comfort all summer.
  • Inspections Safety and maintenance inspections that catch small problems early.
