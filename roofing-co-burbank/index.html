Services

Roofing, start to finish

Pick your project type. Every service starts with an inspection and a straight answer about cost.

Full Roof Replacement

Tear off the old system and install a new one: underlayment, flashing, shingles or tile, ridge caps, and vents. We match materials to your home and your budget.

Shingle Installation

Architectural asphalt shingles installed with proper nailing patterns, starter strips, and ridge ventilation for a roof that lasts.

Tile Roof Service

Concrete and clay tile repair and replacement. Broken tiles swapped, slipped tiles reset, and underlayment issues addressed.

Roof Inspections

A full walk of the roof and attic with photos, so you can see exactly what we see before you decide anything.

Flat & Low Slope Roofs

TPO, modified bitumen, and built up systems for offices, retail, and industrial buildings, installed with proper drainage.

Roof Coatings

Reflective elastomeric coatings that extend the life of a flat roof and cut heat gain on Burbank summers.

Preventative Maintenance

Scheduled cleanings, drain checks, and seam inspections that catch small problems before they become shutdowns.

Leak Detection & Repair

We trace water intrusion to the real entry point, repair it, and verify with the surrounding area, not just the stain.

Flashing Repair

Chimney, skylight, vent, and valley flashing resealed or replaced so water stays outside where it belongs.

Gutters & Downspouts

Cleaning, rehanging, and replacement of gutters and downspouts so water drains away from your foundation.

Storm Damage Response

Wind lifted shingles, fallen branches, and emergency tarping to keep your home dry until permanent repairs are done.

Our story

Roofers rooted in Burbank

A local roofing contractor on Empire Ave, doing right by neighbors one roof at a time.

Built on ladders and handshakes

Roofing Co Burbank is a roofing contractor based at 2507 Empire Ave in Burbank, California. We serve homeowners and businesses across Burbank and the surrounding San Fernando Valley with roof repair, replacement, and maintenance.

Our approach is simple: climb the roof, look closely, take photos, and tell you the truth. Some roofs need a full replacement. Many just need a proper repair in the right place. We will tell you which one yours is, and we will put the price in writing before any work starts.

Burbank weather is kinder to roofs than most places, but sun, Santa Ana winds, and the occasional downpour still take their toll. Regular inspections and small repairs are the cheapest roof you will ever buy, and we are happy to be the crew that keeps yours in shape.
