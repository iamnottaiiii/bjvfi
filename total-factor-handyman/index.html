Our story

A handyman service built for Encino.

Total Factor Handyman started with a simple observation: most homes do not need a full remodel, they need someone reliable to handle the steady stream of small jobs that keep a house in good shape.

So that is what we do. We serve Encino and the nearby San Fernando Valley neighborhoods with straightforward repairs, carpentry, painting and installations. No upselling, no disappearing acts, just a full tool bag and a promise to leave your place better than we found it.

If you have a growing fix list, we would like to be the first call you make.
