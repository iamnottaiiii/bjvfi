About the agency

Small agency, personal service

The Marlow Company Inc. is a real estate agency rooted in Northridge, California, built on the idea that buying or selling a home deserves a human guide, not a machine.

Our approach

Guidance you can trust

Real estate moves fast in the Valley, but a good decision should never feel rushed. We take the time to understand your timeline, your budget and your must-haves, then we do the legwork: comparable sales, honest pricing advice, careful negotiation, and steady communication from offer to keys.

As a smaller, local agency, we answer our own phones. When you call The Marlow Company Inc., you speak with the people who will actually show up at the inspection, the appraisal and the closing table.
