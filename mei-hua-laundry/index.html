Why neighbors pick Mei Hua

A small shop that keeps things simple: clean machines, fair prices, and hours that fit real laundry days.

Spotless and fresh

Clean machines, clean floors, fresh smell. It is the first thing customers mention when they walk in, and we work to keep it that way every day.

Card-friendly machines

The washers and dryers take credit cards, so there is no coin scavenging before you head over. Bring your basket, tap, and go.

Open early, open late

Doors open at 6 AM daily. Weeknights we close at 11 PM, and on Friday, Saturday and Sunday you can wash until midnight.

Late load? Friday, Saturday and Sunday we run until midnight. Monday through Thursday the last wash starts before 11 PM. Stop by Cedar Ave and see why folks call this their go-to laundromat.

The Wash · Self service

Everything is self-serve.

Bring your baskets and your detergent, grab a machine, and you are in and out. No sign-ups, no drop-off, no waiting on anyone else's schedule.

Washers

A row of self-service washers, kept clean and ready all day. Small loads and big family loads both get through.

Dryers

Plenty of dryers so you are never standing around with a wet basket. Warm, quick, and easy to check on.

Pay your way

Machines take credit cards. Coins still work too, if that is what is in the jar.

Open 7 days

Every day starts at 6 AM. Weeknights until 11 PM, weekends until midnight.

Kept tidy

Folding surfaces, floors, and machines get regular attention. Customers notice, and reviews say so.

A friendly counter

Questions about a machine or a payment? Someone friendly is around to help you sort it out.

Beat the weekend rush. Weekday mornings are the quietest stretch. Doors open at 6 AM, and the full row of washers is usually free until mid-morning.
