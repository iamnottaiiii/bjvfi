About At the Buzzer

The old-time barbershop feeling, alive on Main Street.

Remember the old-time barbershop that Dad took you to when you were a kid? Most of them have vanished. At the Buzzer brings it back: a clean, comfortable haven where you can relax, watch TV, talk some sports, and just be guys.

Located at 64 E Main St in West Jefferson, Ohio, the shop offers full service barbering with the newest styles and trends, plus timeless touches like the straight razor neckline cleanup and the steamy hot towel shave.
