About us

Lincoln's neighborhood cleaner

A local dry cleaner on Lewis Street, known for good quality work, friendly faces, and reliable service, drop after drop.

Our story

Good quality, friendly, reliable

That is how our customers describe us, and we work hard to earn it every day. Lincoln Town Cleaners has served Lincoln from our Lewis Street shop with careful dry cleaning, sharp pressing, and alterations done right.

Our location is part of the charm: we are just across the railroad tracks from the inbound platform for the train to Boston, so commuters can drop off on the way in and pick up on the way home. Neighbors know us, and we know their wardrobes.

Whether it is a weekly stack of shirts, a suit before a big meeting, or a hem before the weekend, every garment is inspected, treated, and finished by hand.
