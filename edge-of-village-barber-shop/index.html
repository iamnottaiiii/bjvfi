About the Shop

our story

One Chair, Done Right

Edge of Village Barber Shop is a sweet little one-chair shop at 557 Main St in Groveport, Ohio. Neat, clean, and open by appointment, it is the kind of place where the barber knows your name and your usual cut.

Neighbors praise the personal, professional service and the calm, unhurried pace. No crowds, no rush, just a great cut in the heart of the village.
