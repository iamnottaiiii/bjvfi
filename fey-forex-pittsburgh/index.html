What a freight forwarder does

A freight forwarder arranges the transportation of goods on behalf of shippers: planning the route, coordinating carriers, and handling the details so cargo gets from origin to destination without the shipper juggling every leg.

Fey Forex Pittsburgh serves shippers from its North Versailles Township office. Listed hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, per public directory listings.
