About

Meet Dr. Anzhela Tkachuk

Dr. Anzhela Tkachuk, DDS is a general dentist licensed in New York, caring for patients of all ages at her Sand Lane office in the Arrochar neighborhood of Staten Island.

Her practice

Dr. Tkachuk is the primary dental care provider for patients of all ages, responsible for the diagnosis, treatment and overall coordination of their oral health. She shares the Sand Lane office with Dr. Lyubov Krut, so the practice has deep roots in the neighborhood.

Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments can be made by calling the office directly.
