When to call a roofer

A few warning signs mean your roof needs attention sooner rather than later.

  • Water stains on ceilings or walls after rain
  • Missing, cracked, or curling shingles
  • Granules collecting in gutters or downspouts
  • Daylight visible through the attic roof deck
  • A roof older than 20 years that has never been inspected
