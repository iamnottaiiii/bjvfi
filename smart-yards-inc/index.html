Our story

A Glendale studio built around smarter yards

Smart Yards, Inc. is a landscape design company based in Glendale, California. We started with a simple observation: a lot of yards in this city fight the climate instead of working with it, and homeowners pay for that fight in water bills and weekend hours.

So we design the other way around. We pick plants that like heat and lean soil, lay out irrigation in tight efficient zones, and shape outdoor rooms you will actually use: a shady dining corner, a low care front yard with real curb appeal, a backyard the kids and the dog can enjoy.

Design comes first on every job, big or small. That is the whole idea behind the name.

What guides us

Three rules for every design

Right plant, right place

Plants matched to sun, soil, and water reality, so the yard thrives instead of surviving.

Water with intent

Every drop should land on a root. Zoned, efficient irrigation is part of the plan, not an afterthought.

Rooms, not leftovers

We shape outdoor space the way you would shape indoor space: for cooking, gathering, playing, and quiet.
