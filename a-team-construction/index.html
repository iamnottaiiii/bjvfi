§Our Story

A family trade, carried forward. In the owner's own words:

I am a team leader in a business I created many years ago with my father. We used to work daylight to dark. Now I have taken my experience and used it to make homeowners happy. I do roofing, siding, gutters, flooring, painting.

Daylight to dark, like always

A Team construction started as a father-and-son operation, built on long days and honest work. The owner took what he learned working side by side with his dad and turned it into a crew of his own in Florence, Kentucky.

The idea is simple: one team leader runs the whole job. Roof, siding, gutters, floors, paint. Homeowners deal with one person who knows every corner of their project, instead of a chain of subcontractors who never talk to each other.
