Services

What we do

Air conditioning work for homes and light commercial spaces: repairs, maintenance, and new installs. Choose a tab to see what each visit covers.

Repair

AC diagnostics

Systematic troubleshooting for units that blow warm, cycle oddly, leak, freeze up, or will not start at all.

Repair

Component repairs

Capacitors, contactors, fan motors, and controls: the parts that fail most, repaired or replaced on the visit when possible.

Repair

Refrigerant service

Leak checks and proper charging. Low refrigerant is a symptom, so we look for the leak, not just the refill.

Repair

Airflow problems

Weak vents, hot rooms, and noisy ducts traced to their source: filters, blowers, coils, or duct restrictions.

Maintenance

Seasonal tune-ups

Pre-summer checkups that catch worn parts, dirty coils, and weak electrical connections before the heat arrives.

Maintenance

Coil and filter care

Cleaning that restores efficiency and airflow, the highest-value maintenance there is for an aging unit.

Maintenance

Electrical inspection

Connections, capacitors, and controls checked and tightened so the system starts reliably all season.

Maintenance

Thermostat checkups

Calibration and settings review so the system runs when it should and rests when it should not.

Install

System replacement

When repair stops making sense, a right-sized replacement matched to your home, not just the old unit's tonnage.

Install

New AC installs

Installations for additions, remodels, and homes upgrading from window units to central cooling.

Install

Thermostat upgrades

Modern controls that make the system you already own run smarter and more predictably.

Install

Post-install walkthrough

We show you the new system, the filter schedule, and what normal sounds like, so nothing is a mystery.

A

A repair-first AC shop

Best Choice Air Conditioning Repair Service works out of Van Nuys, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley, where air conditioning earns its keep about eight months a year. We are built around the service call: show up, diagnose honestly, fix it clean.

No invented history here, just a working contractor. What we promise is process: a real diagnosis before a quote, plain-language explanations, and work we stand behind.

B

How a visit goes

1. You describe the symptom. Warm air, noises, no start, weak vents.
2. We diagnose on site. Testing, not guessing.
3. You get options. Repair now, maintain later, or replace when the math says so.

C

Where we work

D

What we will not do

We will not sell you a new system when a repair will do, and we will not patch a dying unit without telling you it is dying. Straight answers, either way.
