About the Shop

Brooklyn Cuts Barbershop sits on Chestnut Street in Emmaus, PA, bringing old-school barbershop craft to the Lehigh Valley.

The Shop

Brooklyn Cuts is a full-service barbershop built around the classics: sharp fades, careful scissor work, and straight razor finishes. The shop keeps the atmosphere relaxed and the standards high, so every client leaves looking sharp.

What to Expect

  • Walk-ins welcome during shop hours
  • Clean, comfortable shop on Chestnut Street
  • Fades, tapers, and classic cuts
  • Beard trims and hot towel shaves
  • Service for men and boys of all ages
