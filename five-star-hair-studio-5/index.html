About

A neighborhood shop
with five star standards

Five Star Hair Studio 5⭐️💈 sits on MacArthur Road in Whitehall, and the name is a promise the shop keeps every day. It is a family friendly barber shop built on the basics done right: greeting every client, starting on time, and sweating the details of every cut.

The barbers, including Luis, Cynthia, and Keury named in client reviews, cover the full range of modern barbering: skin fades, blow outs, tapers, beard work, and kids cuts. Reviewers call out the reliability, the fair prices, and how comfortable the shop feels for first timers and regulars alike.

Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments can be booked by phone or through Booksy. However you come in, you will be in the chair fast and out looking sharp.
