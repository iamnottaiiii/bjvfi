Dental Services

General dentistry services for everyday needs. Call to confirm what is offered during your visit.

Routine examinations of teeth and gums to catch small issues early. Your dentist will talk through any findings in plain language.

Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing misses, helping protect against cavities and gum disease.

Treatment for cavities and minor tooth damage to restore the shape and function of the tooth.

Protective restorations for teeth that are cracked, worn, or weakened, built to blend with your natural smile.

Careful removal of teeth that cannot be saved, with clear aftercare guidance for a smooth recovery.
