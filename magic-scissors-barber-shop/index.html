Our story

A neighborhood shop built on patience and precision

Magic Scissors Barber Shop sits at 805 W Chew St in Allentown, PA, in the Old Allentown neighborhood. It is the kind of shop where the barber takes the conversation as seriously as the cut, and where reviewers say the small details never get skipped.

Customers praise the fast, fair service and the welcoming atmosphere. On busy days every chair is tended and the wait stays short, and families keep coming back: one reviewer noted the barber did a great job with a six-year-old and offered to fix anything until everyone was satisfied.

"My favorite barber in the Lehigh Valley. Any style, any twist, that man knows what he is doing."

That line, from a public review, captures the shop's reputation: versatile, skilled, and personal. Whether it is a clean everyday cut, a sharp skin fade, or creative design work, the standard is the same. Based on Google reviews.
