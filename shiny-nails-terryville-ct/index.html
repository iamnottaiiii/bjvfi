Our story

Terryville's bright spot
for beautiful nails

Tucked inside Adams Hometown Market on Main Street, Shiny Nails has become the salon Litchfield County recommends to friends.

A salon built on
cleanliness and kindness

Guests describe Shiny Nails as extraordinary clean, very comfortable, and elegant. That did not happen by accident. Every station is kept immaculate, the room smells fresh, and hygiene is treated as part of the service, not an afterthought.

The team is just as careful with people as with polish. Reviewers call the staff sweet, pleasant, and welcoming, praising the relaxing vibe and the way technicians take time to get designs exactly right. Many clients have been coming since the salon opened, and they bring their friends.

Fair pricing rounds out the reputation. Guests call Shiny Nails a hidden gem, proof that a top tier manicure does not need a top tier price tag.
