What We Do

General contracting covers a wide range of home projects. Pick a topic below to see how the conversation usually starts. Every job begins with a call to confirm scope, timing, and budget.

Home Repairs

Small problems become big ones when they wait. We look at the issue, explain the options, and fix it so it stays fixed.

  • Walkthrough of the problem area
  • Clear explanation of what caused it
  • Repair plan with an upfront scope

Remodeling Projects

Updating a kitchen, bath, or living space takes planning. We help you think through layout, materials, and sequencing before work starts.

  • Goal setting and priority list
  • Material and finish guidance
  • Phased work plan to limit disruption

Home Additions

Need more room? An addition is a major project that deserves careful planning from day one.

  • Feasibility discussion for your lot and home
  • Scope, timeline, and budget planning
  • Coordination through completion

Outdoor Projects

Decks, porches, and exterior improvements add living space and curb appeal. We talk through what makes sense for your home and yard.

  • Site review and measurements
  • Design direction and material options
