About us

ATLAS Concrete & Terrazzo Polishing provides concrete polishing, stains and dyes, terrazzo restoration, and surface preparation for commercial and industrial customers throughout the Midwest.

Our concrete flooring services include joint and crack repairs, epoxy and other specialty coatings, logo placement and stenciling, overlays, and safety striping, plus regularly scheduled maintenance programs. We service warehouses, labs, manufacturing facilities, parking garages, hospitals, offices, retail stores, restaurants, car dealerships, distribution facilities, churches, and select high-end residential properties.
