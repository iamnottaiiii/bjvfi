Our story

A Clinton shop built on doing right by people

N&E Auto Repair is a full-service auto repair and auto body shop in Clinton, Maryland. The shop has been serving local drivers for over 30 years, and the approach has never changed: honest advice, fair prices, and work the team is proud to stand behind.

Root-cause repairs

Instead of guessing, the mechanics dig until they find what is actually wrong, from electrical gremlins to suspension wear. Customers say that patience is what keeps them coming back.

Budget conscious

The shop works to stay affordable and offers special consideration for loyal customers, according to long-time reviewers.

Modern equipment

Experienced technicians use up-to-date equipment so repairs are done to a high standard, whether it is a routine service or a full transmission job.
