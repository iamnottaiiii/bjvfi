Razor 1 Kutz Barbershop sits at 7218 Montgomery Rd in Silverton, Ohio. Online it describes itself simply: "cutting a taper." Customers praise the barbers for being on time, professional, and skilled at delivering the look you ask for, with clean and fresh results.
