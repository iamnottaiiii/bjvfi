A neighborhood with a view of history

The homes at 3 S Grand Ave sit in one of Pasadena's most storied settings, above the Arroyo Seco with the Colorado Street Bridge as the backdrop. The association exists to keep the shared spaces, grounds, and day-to-day details of the community running smoothly.

This site is a simple information hub: how to reach the association, where the community is located, and what a community association typically handles for its residents.

What the association handles

Typical duties The duties below are typical for a condominium community association. Exact responsibilities, fees, and rules for this community are confirmed by calling (626) 396-1186.

Community associations generally oversee shared walkways, landscaping, parking areas, and building exteriors so the property stays maintained for everyone.

Associations typically set and enforce community guidelines covering noise, parking, pets, and exterior changes. Ask the association for the current rule documents.

Meeting notices, community updates, and maintenance schedules are generally shared with residents by the association office. Confirm the best contact method by phone.

Community information

Typical duties This page describes what community associations like this one generally handle. Nothing below is a confirmed policy of Vista Del Arroyo Hoa; confirm details at (626) 396-1186.

Common questions for the association office include maintenance requests, parking assignments, amenity access, and community rule documents. Office hours per the directory listing are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM; call to confirm.

Owners typically receive dues statements, meeting notices, and community financial updates from the association. Contact the association directly for account or document requests.
