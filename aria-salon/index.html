Our story

Meet the stylists of Syracuse

Aria Salon is a hair and beauty studio on West Genesee Street, built on professional products, honest consultations and stylists clients ask for by name.

The studio

Come meet the best stylists of Syracuse

That is how Aria Salon introduces itself, and the reviews agree. Jessica, Ryan and Ali are the names clients repeat: colorists and cutters known for talent, professionalism and attention to detail, and a makeup artist brides trust with their wedding day.

The salon handles all your beauty needs in one chair: color, cuts and waxing, with professional products and service as the house standard. The philosophy is simple. Listen to what the client wants, enhance their image, and send them out the door feeling like the best version of themselves.

"Color, cuts, and waxing. Let our top stylists enhance your image with only the best professional products and service."
The team

Jessica · Ryan · Ali

Jessica and Ryan lead the chair work: color, cuts, styling and texture services. Ali brings the finishing touch with makeup, from polished everyday looks to full bridal parties. Call and schedule your appointment with any of them today.
