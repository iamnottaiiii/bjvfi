Granada Hills is home. Tile is the craft.

TheMagicTileCompany is a tile contracting crew based on Gothic Ave in Granada Hills. We spend our days in kitchens and bathrooms across the San Fernando Valley, setting tile that stays put and grout that stays clean.

We keep the crew small on purpose. The person who measures your room is the person who sets your tile, and the person you call with a question is the person who answers.
