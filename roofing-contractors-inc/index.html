The Feature

An 80 year roofing practice, one roof at a time.

Roofing Contractors Inc began contracting in California in 1946, and the family has never left the trade. Today a third generation owner runs the company from 8500 Tunney Ave in Northridge, serving homes and small commercial properties across the Valley.

Every installer is a veteran of the roofing business. The company carries workers compensation and liability insurance as a matter of course, pulls building permits on all projects where the city requires them, and stands as a Los Angeles city certified contractor.

Our Story

Contracting in California since 1946.

The family behind Roofing Contractors Inc has been contracting in the State of California since 1946. Three generations later, the company still operates the old fashioned way: career roofers, real insurance, and permits pulled on every job that needs one.

Roofing Contractors Inc serves residential properties and small commercial buildings in Northridge and the surrounding Valley. The company is a Los Angeles city certified contractor.

"No day labor. Veterans of the roofing business on every crew, with workers compensation and liability insurance on every project."

When you call (818) 201-6635, you reach a family that has been on roofs for eighty years. That is the whole pitch.
