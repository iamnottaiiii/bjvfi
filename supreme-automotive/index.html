Our Story

Supreme Automotive is a neighborhood auto repair shop on Cherry Tree Crossing Road in Brandywine, Maryland. We built our reputation the old-fashioned way: one honest repair at a time.

Drivers here tell us the same thing, that honest work is hard to find nowadays. That is exactly what we set out to give. When your mechanic takes the time to explain the problem, show you what is wrong, and charge you fairly, you stop dreading the shop and start trusting it.

Today our customers bring us all of their vehicles, from daily commuters to family cars, because they know the work will be done right and explained clearly.
