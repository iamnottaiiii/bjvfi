Our story

Remodeling Harrisburg since 1998

Matt Ormsbee Remodeling Contractor, LLC has provided custom interior remodeling, home improvements and repairs to the Harrisburg area since 1998. Based at 6000 Linglestown Rd in Harrisburg, the company focuses on the projects that change how a home lives: bathrooms, kitchens and basements.

In 2018, Pulse of the City News awarded Matt Ormsbee Remodeling Contractor, LLC its award for Excellence in Customer Satisfaction, with a top rating of 5 out of 5 stars, based on independent research into customer reviews, surveys and business-rating services.

Licensed in Pennsylvania as PA038826, the company also handles additions, remodels and light commercial interior work.
