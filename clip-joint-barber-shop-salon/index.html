Our Story

Six decades, one chair, countless sharp heads.

Clip Joint Barber Shop and Salon has served San Pedro for over 64 years, making it one of the longest-running barbershops in the South Bay. Generations of families have sat in its chairs.

The shop keeps the classic barbershop feel alive: a clean, nicely decorated interior with framed photos and art posters, a welcoming atmosphere, and barbers known for fades, tapers, and flat tops at prices that stay honest.

Regulars praise the friendly, professional crew and the no-fuss experience. Walk in or book ahead, either way you leave looking sharp.
