Services

// PICK A LANE

Tire Sales

New and used tires for daily drivers, work trucks, and SUVs. Sizes and brands vary with stock, so call ahead with your tire size and we will check the rack.

  • New passenger and light truck tires
  • Used tires, inspected before sale
  • Special orders by size
Call to confirm availability

Mounting and Balancing

Tires mounted with proper seating and spin balanced to cut vibration and uneven wear. Most jobs turned around same day.

  • Tire mounting
  • Computer spin balancing
  • Valve stems and TPMS checks
Call to confirm availability

Flat and Puncture Repair

Bring the flat in and we will inspect the tread and sidewall. If the tire can be repaired safely, we patch or plug it. If not, we will tell you straight.

  • Puncture inspection
  • Patch and plug repairs
  • Sidewall damage assessment
Call to confirm availability

Rotation and Pressure

Routine tire care that stretches tread life: rotations on schedule and pressure set to spec, not to guess.

  • Tire rotation
  • Air pressure check and fill
  • Tread wear inspection
Call to confirm availability
