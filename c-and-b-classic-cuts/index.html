The shop

No gimmicks. Just sharp work.

C and B Classic Cuts is a traditional barbershop on Harford Road, and it shows in everything: the clean chairs, the unhurried pace, the way regulars settle in like they own the place. Haircuts, shaves and beard trims are the trade here, done the classic way.

Reviewers call it one of the cleanest, most professional shops around. They mention the great conversation, the occasional chess game, and barbers who treat every head of hair, young or old, with the same care.

Our story

A neighborhood institution on Harford Rd

C and B Classic Cuts keeps the old school alive. This is the kind of barbershop where the conversation is as good as the cut, where regulars have been coming for years, and where a game of chess might break out while you wait.

Reviewers describe the shop as clean, professional and upbeat. Longtime clients praise the steady, reliable work. One reviewer has trusted the same barber since age fifteen. Another brings an elderly friend and says the gentlemen there are always kind and professional.

That is the C and B way: classic cuts, honest service, and a chair that feels like it has been waiting for you.
