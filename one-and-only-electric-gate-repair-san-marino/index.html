Services

Core service

Electric Gate Repair

Gates that will not open, will not close, or stop halfway. We trace the fault and get the gate moving again.

Common culprits include worn operators, broken chains or belts, bent tracks, and storm damage. Call for hours.

Operators

Gate Opener Service

Diagnosis and repair of automatic gate openers, including motors that hum but do not move and openers with no power.

We check power supply, control boards, limit switches, and mechanical drive parts before recommending replacement.

Safety

Sensors and Wiring

Photo-eye sensors, safety loops, and low-voltage wiring faults that cause phantom reversals or dead gates.

Misaligned or failed safety devices are a leading cause of gates that refuse to close. We realign, rewire, or replace them.

Entry

Access Controls

Keypads, remotes, and intercom entry systems that no longer talk to the gate.

From a dead keypad to an intercom that will not release the latch, we restore the full entry chain.

Upkeep

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled checkups that catch worn parts before they strand you outside your own driveway.

Lubrication, alignment, battery checks, and safety-device testing keep the system reliable year round.

Install

New Gate Installations

Automation for an existing manual gate, or a full operator setup on a new installation.

We match the operator to the gate weight, duty cycle, and power available at your property.
