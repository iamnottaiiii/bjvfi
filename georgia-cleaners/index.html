Our Story

The neighborhood cleaner on Old National Highway, keeping Atlanta sharp.

A cleaner that knows your name

Georgia Cleaners has served the Old National corridor from 5081 Old National Hwy for years, becoming the go-to stop for commuters, airport workers and families across south Atlanta and College Park.

We are the kind of cleaner where the regulars get a wave and the new customers get a walkthrough. Every garment is checked at the counter, every stain gets honest advice, and every order leaves with a promised pickup day.

Whether it is a weekly shirt bundle or a once-a-year suit rescue before a wedding, we treat it all with the same care.

Look sharp, feel sharp. We will handle the cleaning.
