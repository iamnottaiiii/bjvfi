Services

Concrete work, done right

From residential driveways to commercial flatwork, every project starts with a site visit and a written quote. No surprises, no shortcuts.

Concrete Driveways

New pours, replacements, and extensions. Proper base, correct thickness, control joints cut in, and a broom or decorative finish.

Patios and Walkways

Patios, sidewalks, front walks, and steps. Designed to fit your yard and drain away from your home.

Foundations and Footings

Foundation walls, footings, and slabs for additions and new builds, formed square and poured to plan.

Stamped and Decorative

Stamped patterns, exposed aggregate, and colored concrete that look like stone or brick without the upkeep.

Commercial Flatwork

Parking areas, loading pads, dumpster pads, and sidewalk runs for businesses and properties of all sizes.

Repair and Replacement

Cracked, settled, or spalling concrete evaluated honestly: repair where it makes sense, replace where it does not.

How a job runs

A simple process, the same on every project.

1. Site visit

We walk the site, measure, and talk through what you want and what the ground will allow.

2. Written quote

A clear scope and a firm price before anything is scheduled. Questions welcome.

3. Prep and pour

Base prep, forming, and reinforcement done properly, then a clean, well finished pour.

4. Cure and cleanup

We protect the fresh work, clean the site, and walk you through curing and care.

About us

A local concrete crew

S C Concrete is a concrete contractor based on Guilford Road in Annapolis Junction, Maryland, serving homeowners and businesses across the Baltimore-Washington corridor.

What we believe

Concrete is unforgiving: once it sets, the work speaks for itself. That is why we take prep, grading, and finishing seriously on every pour, large or small. A driveway should drain right, a patio should sit level, and a foundation should be square. We do the unglamorous parts correctly so the finished work lasts.

How we work

You talk directly to the people doing your job. We show up when we say we will, keep the site tidy, and explain what is happening at each step. If something in the ground changes the plan, you hear about it first, with options and honest pricing.

Where we work

Our yard is in Annapolis Junction, and our trucks run throughout Anne Arundel and Howard counties and nearby communities, including Laurel, Columbia, Jessup, Savage, Odenton, Severn, Elkridge, and Fort Meade.
