Who we are

About B & B Pole Buildings

A Hummelstown builder

B & B Pole Buildings is located at 602 W High St in Hummelstown, Pennsylvania. We are a general contractor focused on one craft: post-frame buildings done well.

Community roots

Our people have long been part of Hummelstown civic life, including service on the borough's downtown master planning committee. We build here because we live here.

Public trust

When Hummelstown Borough needed a new salt shed at the Public Works Building, the borough council awarded the construction bid to B&B Pole Buildings.
