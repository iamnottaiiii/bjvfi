Styles to ask about

The shop does not publish a style menu, so here is a plain-English guide to common tattoo styles. Call and ask what the shop's artists do best.

Guide

Black and Grey

Shading built from black ink and skin tone. A workhorse style for portraits, realism, and pieces that age gracefully. Ask the shop about availability.

Guide

Traditional

Bold lines, solid color, classic imagery like roses, eagles, and daggers. Built to read clearly from across the room and hold up for decades.

Guide

Fine Line

Delicate single-needle work, script, and minimalist designs. Precise and subtle. A good first tattoo for many people.

Guide

Lettering and Script

Names, dates, quotes, and memorials. Placement and sizing matter more than most people think, so ask for the shop's advice.
