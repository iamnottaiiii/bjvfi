Our story

Weedsport's family salon since 1988

Over 30 years of experience, one small town, and generations of great hair days.

Since 1988

Built on trust, one appointment at a time

Heather's Hair Salon has been part of Weedsport life since 1988. The salon's own description says it best: family hair care with over 30 years of experience.

That experience shows in the details reviewers mention: cuts that come out exactly as asked, color that lasts, perms done right, and a team that treats longtime clients like family. Some guests have been in the same chair for 10 or 16 years.

The salon is licensed by New York State for appearance enhancement services, with current licenses held by the salon and its stylists, and it stays sharp with continuing education, from master classes to new technique training.

  • Heather: the salon's founder, praised for cuts, color and wedding styling
