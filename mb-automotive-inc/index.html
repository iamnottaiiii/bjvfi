Our story

A straight-shooting shop on the Boulevard

M&B Automotive Inc. sits at 3950 Niagara Falls Boulevard in North Tonawanda, and it runs the way an independent shop should: small crew, experienced hands, and no pressure. The shop is led by Rich, a mechanic reviewers describe as knowledgeable and honest, the kind who tells you what the car needs and what it does not.

It is a complete automotive repair facility, which in plain terms means one stop for the whole car: diagnostics when a light comes on, maintenance to keep it healthy, tires and alignments, and official NYS inspections. Imports and domestics both get the same treatment, and regulars mention VW, Audi, and BMW among the cars the crew knows well.

The waiting room keeps it simple too: free Wi-Fi and a quiet corner, because nobody chose to spend their afternoon at a repair shop. Most jobs go out the same day, and the quote you get is the bill you pay. That is the whole business model.
