Our Process

How a project moves forward

A straightforward way of working, from first call to finished roof.

1. First call

Tell us what you are seeing: a leak, missing shingles, an aging roof. We will ask a few questions and set up a time to look.

2. On site inspection

We walk the roof, check flashing, vents, and drainage, and document what we find with photos you can keep.

3. Clear written scope

You get a plain language scope of work and a price before anything starts. No jargon, no pressure.

4. Scheduled install and cleanup

Work happens on an agreed schedule, with daily cleanup and a final walkthrough when the job is done.
