About New Image

A Philadelphia painting and remodeling contractor, and the people and principles behind the name.

Paint is the finish.
Remodeling is the craft.

New Image Remodeling and Paint, L.L.C is a Philadelphia contractor working at the intersection of two trades. Where many companies do one or the other, this one was set up to handle both: the remodeling work underneath, drywall, bathrooms, bathtubs, and the paint that finishes it.

The company is led by president and CEO Felix Gonzalez and serves the Philadelphia area from the 19107 zip code. Its public profiles list interior painting for homes and businesses, bathroom remodels, bathtub installation and replacement, and drywall installation and repair, with additional services on request.
