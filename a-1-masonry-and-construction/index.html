Services

The full masonry menu

Brick, stone, tile, and remodeling. The core services below come from the company's public trade listings; typical masonry services round out the list.

01Brick Repair

Cracked, spalling, or loose brickwork repaired and repointed to match the surrounding wall.

02Bricklaying & Brickwork

New brick walls, veneers, piers, and structural brickwork laid level and true.

03Brick & Stone Driveways

Installation of brick and stone driveways, plus repair of settled or broken sections.

04Chimney Rebuilds

Rebuilt chimney stacks and crowns, like the company's documented Cincinnati rebuild.

05Retaining Walls

Block and stone retaining walls that hold back grade and define the landscape.

06Paver Patios & Hardscapes

Patios, walkways, and full hardscapes with fountains, benches, and dry creek beds.

07Stonework

Natural and manufactured stone for walls, facades, and landscape features.

08Concrete Flatwork

Typical for this trade: slabs, walkways, and pads to pair with the masonry work.

09Tuckpointing

Typical for this trade: fresh mortar joints that renew old brickwork and stop water entry.

A-1 Masonry & Construction is based at 4551 S Park Forest Cir in Batavia, Ohio. The company's own profile describes "a unique team of professionals" in brick, stone, tile, and remodeling, with 15 years of experience in the field.
