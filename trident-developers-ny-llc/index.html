Our Story

Trident Developers NY, LLC grew out of a belief that the best development in Brooklyn starts with respect for what is already here. The borough's prewar apartment buildings were built to last a century, and with the right stewardship they will.

Our work centers on Fort Hamilton and Bay Ridge. At 128 Marine Ave, families have made their homes with us for ten years and counting, and that is the measure we hold ourselves to: would a family stay here for a decade?

Beyond restoration, we develop new housing too, including a 27-unit residential building at 585 6th Ave in Park Slope. Whether we are restoring a 1926 classic or building new, the job is the same: solid construction, honest management, and buildings Brooklyn can be proud of.
