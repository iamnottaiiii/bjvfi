The trade

Small signs deserve a professional look.

Most electrical trouble starts quietly: a breaker that trips now and then, an outlet that went dead, lights that flicker when the air conditioner kicks on. Left alone, the small stuff becomes the expensive stuff.

A good electrician reads those early signs, traces them to the source, and fixes the cause rather than the symptom. That is the whole job, and it is what we do.

.

  • Breakers that trip again and again
  • Outlets or switch plates that feel warm
  • Lights that flicker or dim without reason
  • Buzzing sounds near the panel or fixtures
  • A remodel or addition that needs new circuits
  • An EV charger or new appliance on the way
Safety first, always.

If you smell burning, see sparking, or find a hot panel, stay clear and call right away. Do not open the panel yourself.
