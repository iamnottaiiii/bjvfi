The craft

Drywall is the surface every room is judged by

Light shows everything. A wavy seam or a rough patch of compound telegraphs through paint and catches the eye every day. We hang board flat, tape tight, and sand to a finish that disappears under paint, so your rooms look crisp from every angle.

Repairs

Holes, cracks and water damage, gone

Doorknob holes, settling cracks, stains from a fixed leak: we cut out the damage, patch with matching board, and blend the texture so the repair melts into the surrounding wall. Most patches are done in a day, with finish coats scheduled to match your timeline.

Services

Everything drywall, start to finish

One contractor for the whole wall system, from bare studs to paint-ready finish.

01

Drywall Installation

New board for rooms, additions, basements and full-house projects. Hung plumb and tight, with fasteners set just below the surface for a clean tape coat.

02

Drywall Repair

Patches for holes, dents, cracks and water damage. We match the surrounding surface so the repair is invisible once painted.

03

Taping & Finishing

Seams taped and coated to a smooth, flat finish. Multiple thin coats, sanded between passes, for joints that stay hidden.

04

Ceiling Work

Ceiling board, repairs and refinishing, including popcorn texture removal prep and smooth ceiling upgrades.

05

Texture Matching

Orange peel, knockdown and smooth finishes matched to your existing walls so patches blend in.

06

Paint-Ready Prep

Final sanding, touch-ups and priming guidance so your painter starts on a flawless surface.
