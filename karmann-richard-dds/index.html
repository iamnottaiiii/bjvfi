The Practice

A neighborhood dentist patients have trusted for decades.

Dr. Richard Karmann has cared for smiles in Jersey City from his Bergen Avenue office for many years. Patients describe him as the gentlest, most calming dentist they have ever met, and longtime patients say they hold the utmost respect for him.

The office keeps things personal. One reviewer noted that even without an appointment, Dr. Karmann will take care of you. The front desk staff earn the same praise: always friendly, always helpful.

As a general dentistry practice, the office handles the everyday work of keeping teeth healthy: exams, cleanings, fillings, and restorative care. The focus stays on doing careful, honest work rather than rushing patients through the chair.

"Patients call him gentle, calming, and considerate, and they keep coming back."
