Our Story

Part of Wilmington

A neighborhood shop serving Anaheim Street for years.

The shop

Barber craft, salon care

Avalon Barber & Beauty Salon sits at 224 E Anaheim St in the heart of Wilmington. Corporate records show the salon organized as a California corporation, and the shop has served local families for years with both barbering and beauty services.

The idea is simple: one welcoming place where men can get a sharp haircut and a shave, kids can get a friendly first cut, and anyone can get color and styling. Directory listings note below-average prices, walk-ins welcome, appointments available, credit cards accepted, and a kid-friendly shop.

Reviewers over the years have praised the personal touch, mentioning barbers and stylists by name and returning for years at a time. That kind of loyalty is earned one cut at a time.
