How we work

It starts with a walk around your yard.

Good landscapes are not ordered from a catalog. We start by walking your property with you, listening to how you use the space, and sketching a plan that fits your home, your soil, and your budget.

From there, our crew handles the build: beds and plantings, mulch and stone, patios and edging, lighting, and the seasonal care that keeps it all looking sharp. One team, one plan, no hand-offs.

Our story

A small Kentucky crew
with a sketchpad habit.

Crafted Outdoors LLC is a landscape design company based on 4 Mile Road in Melbourne, Kentucky. The name says the plan: outdoor spaces that are crafted, not just installed, with a design drawn for your property before a single shovel hits the ground.

We work across Melbourne, Silver Grove, and the surrounding Northern Kentucky river towns, where yards range from shady lots to wide-open new builds. Every one of them gets the same treatment: a walkthrough, an honest conversation about budget, and a hand-drawn plan.

We would rather talk you out of a project than sell you one you will regret.

A note on what we know: public records confirm our name, address, and phone number. Details about our team and history come from the business itself, so the best source is a phone call. We would love to tell you in person.

Field notes

Why we sketch first:

  • A drawing catches problems a quote cannot: drainage, sun angles, that corner that floods every April.
  • You see the whole plan before you spend a dollar on plants.
  • Phases become obvious: what to do now, what can wait a season.

No sketch, no surprises.
