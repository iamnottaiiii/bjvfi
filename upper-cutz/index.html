    Our story

    NEVER LEAVE
    UNHAPPY AGAIN

    upper cutz started with a simple frustration: too many people walking out of barber shops unhappy with the cut or the experience. So Victor built the shop he always wanted to sit in.

    Victor runs the chairs with a simple standard: listen first, cut second. Regulars book him every three weeks like clockwork, because the result is the same every time, sharp, clean, and exactly what they asked for.

    Darvin is the kids-cut whisperer and a fade specialist. Parents say their sons are comfortable in his chair, and grown regulars say the same about his consistency: no rush, always on time, great work.

    The shop keeps it modern and no-drama: online booking with real availability, straightforward prices, and a team that would rather get it right than get you out the door.

    THREE RULES
