About Dr. Kan

Dr. Maryann Kan, DDS

Dr. Maryann Kan is a general dentist serving patients of all ages in Alexandria, Virginia. Licensed in Virginia, she is the primary dental care provider for her patients, overseeing diagnosis, treatment and the overall coordination of their oral health.

Her practice is built on a simple philosophy: listen carefully, treat gently, and never rush. Patients consistently praise her attentiveness, describing a doctor who takes great care with every procedure and makes visits pleasant.

The office at 6389 Little River Turnpike welcomes new patients by appointment. Because it is a small practice, every visit gets personal attention.
