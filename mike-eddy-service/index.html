The shop

A full-service shop with a first-name feel

Set back off Gibsonia Road, the shop has been a fixture of the community for decades. Business records trace it back to 1987, and the reviews read like a family album: customers who started coming in the 1990s and never left.

What keeps them coming back is not fancy waiting rooms or coupons in the mail. It is Mike himself: knowledgeable, straight-talking, and famous among reviewers for talking customers out of repairs they do not need.

From full rebuilds to everyday brake jobs, the shop handles complex mechanical work that other places send away, all at prices reviewers consistently call fair.
