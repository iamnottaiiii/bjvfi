Why SecureTop

Services

Everything your roof needs,
nothing it does not.

Inspections, repairs, maintenance, and full replacements for homes and small businesses across Arleta and the San Fernando Valley.

  • 01
    Roof inspections

    A full walk of the roof: shingles or tile, flashing, vents, valleys, and drainage. You get photos and a plain-English summary of what needs attention now and what can wait.

  • 02
    Leak repair

    We trace leaks to the actual entry point, not just the ceiling stain below it. Then we repair the source and check the surrounding area so the fix holds.

  • 03
    Shingle repair and replacement

    Lifted, cracked, or missing shingles get replaced and sealed, with matching materials wherever possible.

  • 04
    Tile roofing

    Tile holds up well in Valley heat, but underlayment ages. We repair broken tile and address what is underneath when it is time.

  • 05
    Full roof replacement

    Tear-off and re-roof for roofs at the end of their life. Materials chosen for high sun exposure, heat, and wind.

  • 06
    Flashing, vents, and gutters

    The details that keep water moving off the roof: flashing at chimneys and walls, vent boots, and gutter runs cleared and secured.

  • 07
    Storm and wind damage

    After Santa Ana winds or heavy rain, we assess damage, document it, and get the roof sealed up again fast.

  • 08
    Roof maintenance

    Seasonal checkups and small fixes: debris clearing, sealant touch-ups, and early warnings before problems grow.

SecureTop Roofing works out of Arleta, in the heart of the San Fernando Valley. Our roofs are the ones you drive past every day.

Who we are

Roofers, not salespeople.

SecureTop Roofing is a roofing contractor based at 13170 Bracken St in Arleta, CA. We do one trade and we do it in the neighborhoods around us: Arleta, Pacoima, Panorama City, North Hills, Mission Hills, and San Fernando.

The name says the plan: a secure top over your head. That means showing up when we say we will, putting in writing what we will do, and standing behind the work when the next storm rolls through.

Most of our jobs come from one street to the next. A repaired leak on one block becomes a full replacement referral two doors down. That is the reputation we are building, one roof at a time.

We know Valley roofs

Decades of sun bake shingles brittle, underlayment under tile wears out, and sudden winter rain finds every weak flashing joint. We have seen all three, on streets all around Arleta.

Straight talk, in writing

Every job starts with an on-roof look and ends with a written scope and price. If something can wait a year, we say so. If it cannot wait a month, we say that too.
