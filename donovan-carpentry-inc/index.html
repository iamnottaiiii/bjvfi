About

A Cincinnati carpentry shop with sawdust in its veins

Donovan Carpentry Inc is a small family operation based at 3749 Hazel Ave in Cincinnati. The shop has served customers across the Greater Cincinnati area for over 20 years, and its craftsmen bring 35 years of experience to every job, from home additions to built-in bookshelves.

35 years of experience

Listed across major directories with 35 years in the trade. That is three and a half decades of learning what holds, what warps, and what lasts.

Perfect five-star record

5.0 from 21 reviews on Porch and 5.0 from 19 reviews on Angi, with 100 percent of Angi ratings at five stars.

Rooted in Cincinnati

Based in the 45212 zip code and working across the Greater Cincinnati area, including documented projects in Cincinnati neighborhoods.
