About V10

A licensed Pacoima contractor with a decade of permitted work behind it.

Our story

V10 Construction is a California corporation headquartered at 13301 Louvre St in Pacoima. First licensed by the Contractors State License Board in 2014, the company carries General Building, Glazing, and General Engineering classifications, which lets one crew take a project from foundation to finish.

Public permit records show 21 permitted projects over the last three years, ranging from whole apartment buildings to bathroom and electrical remodels. The company holds a BuildZoom score of 108, placing it in the top 5% of California licensed contractors.

The company pledge is straightforward: the highest quality service in an honest, efficient, and professional way, building trust by treating every project with integrity and exceeding expectations. Se habla español.
