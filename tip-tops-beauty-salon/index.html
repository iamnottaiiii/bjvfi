Our Story

Twenty years of great hair days

Tip Tops Beauty Salon sits on Orchard Park Road in West Seneca, a small salon with a loyal following. Clients talk about coming here for 10, 15, even 20 years, and never walking out with a bad cut or color.

The secret is simple: individual attention. This is a by appointment only salon, which means your stylist's focus is entirely on you from the moment you sit down. The atmosphere stays quiet and relaxed, a calm break in a busy week.

Whether you need a trim, a whole new color, or an updo for a big day, you will leave feeling like the best version of yourself. That is the Tip Tops promise, and it is why regulars recommend us to everyone they know.
