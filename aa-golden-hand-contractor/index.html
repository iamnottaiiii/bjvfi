The story

One contractor for the whole project

Remodeling a home usually means juggling a plumber, an electrician, a painter, and a framer, each with their own schedule. AA Golden Hand Contractor takes the other approach: one construction company that manages the job from first walkthrough to final cleanup, so the homeowner makes one call instead of five.

Based at 7565 Woodman Pl in Van Nuys, the company serves homeowners across the San Fernando Valley who want kitchens, bathrooms, additions, and full renovations done without the runaround.

One call, one crew, one finished project. That is the whole pitch.

Public records show an active California limited liability company under a matching name (Aa Golden Hand Contractor LLC), on file with the Secretary of State since 2020. For a homeowner, that paper trail matters: it means a real, registered business stands behind the work.
