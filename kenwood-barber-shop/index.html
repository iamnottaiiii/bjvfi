Our story

A Spencer original

Tucked on Mechanic St, Kenwood Barber Shop is the neighborhood shop regulars recommend by name.

The Kenwood way

One chair. Full attention.

Kenwood runs the way barbershops used to run: you sit down, you talk through what you want, and the barber takes the time to get it right. No assembly line, no rushed clippers.

Regulars describe the shop as clean, professional and very reasonably priced, the kind of place where the barber remembers your usual and the conversation is as good as the cut.

  • Consultation before every cut
  • Clean tools, clean shop, every client
  • Honest cash pricing, no surprises
