What we do

Services

Quiptech Machinery INC. is a truck repair shop on 41st Street in Astoria, serving commercial trucks and working fleets. A DMV-licensed repair facility, the shop opens early so rigs get back on the road before the day gets going.

Diesel Engine ServiceDiagnostics, repair, and overhaul for diesel powerplants.
Preventive MaintenanceScheduled PM programs that keep fleets rolling.
Air Brake ServiceAir brake inspection, repair, and adjustment.
DOT InspectionsInspection prep and compliance checks.
Suspension and Axle WorkSprings, shocks, axles, and alignment for heavy rigs.
Electrical and LightingWiring, lighting, and charging system repair.
Transmission and ClutchClutch jobs and transmission service for trucks.
Fleet AccountsPriority scheduling and consolidated billing for fleets.
