Our story

A neighborhood salon with a personal touch

I opened Jessie’s Salon because I believe great hair should come with a great experience. No rushing, no guessing, just a real conversation about what you want and the skill to deliver it.

Licensed in New York since 2018, our little suite on Walden Avenue has grown the best way a salon can: one happy regular at a time. Guests come in for a cut or color and stay for years, bringing their families and friends along.

Whether you want a bold new color, a keratin-smooth finish, or simply a stylist who listens, my chair is open to you.

  • Licensed NY appearance enhancement business
  • Open by appointment only, so you are never rushed
  • Color, cuts, keratin, braids, locs and curly hair care
