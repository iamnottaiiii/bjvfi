Before you call

Two quick checks that sometimes solve it in five minutes.

Check the thermostat

Make sure it is set to COOL, the set point is below room temperature, and fresh batteries are in if it takes them.

Check the breaker

A tripped breaker on the outdoor unit stops cooling instantly. Flip it fully off, then back on, and give the unit a few minutes.

AC repair that finds the cause

Warm air, ice on the lines, constant running, or a system that will not start: we test refrigerant charge, capacitors, contactors, and airflow to pinpoint the failure.

  • Refrigerant leak detection and recharge
  • Capacitor, contactor, and fan motor replacement
  • Frozen coil and drain line clearing
  • Uneven cooling and hot-spot fixes
SAMPLE pricing only: diagnostic visit sample $89; common repairs typically land between $150 and $600 depending on parts. Final quote always before work.

Heating service and repair

Georgia winters are short but sharp. We keep furnaces and heat pumps starting reliably and heating evenly through every cold snap.

  • Furnace no-heat diagnosis and repair
  • Heat pump defrost and reversing valve service
  • Ignitor, flame sensor, and control board fixes
  • Carbon monoxide safety checks

System replacement and installs

An aging, inefficient system costs more every month it runs. We measure, size, and install equipment matched to your home, not just the cheapest box that fits.

  • Load calculation for correct sizing
  • Condenser, air handler, and furnace installs
  • Ductwork evaluation and sealing
  • Thermostat setup and walkthrough

Maintenance plans
