Our Story

A Bailey Ave staple.

Profile Barber Shop is a barber shop at 2955 Bailey Ave in Buffalo, New York, known for skilled, confident barbering and fair prices. Over the years it has built a loyal base of regulars who trust the chairs here with their look.

Reviewers describe barbers who are skilled and confident in their cutting technique, a shop where clients feel comfortable, and pricing that stays reasonable. Add easy parking near the highway, and it is a shop built for real life, not just special occasions.

Whether you need a sharp edge up, a full restyle, or your regular maintenance cut, the goal is the same every time: walk out looking and feeling your best.
