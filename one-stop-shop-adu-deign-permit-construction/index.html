Our story

One shop for the whole ADU.

Most ADU projects stall in the gaps: the designer finishes, the permit expediter waits on drawings, the builder prices from plans he did not draw. one-Stop-Shop ADU Deign Permit & Construction exists to close those gaps.

The idea is simple and it is right there in the name. Design the unit, carry it through permitting, then build it, all with one team accountable for the outcome.

Based at 9624 Donna Ave in Northridge, the shop serves homeowners across the San Fernando Valley who want a backyard unit without managing three separate vendors.

The one-stop promise

When one team owns the plans, the permit, and the build, surprises shrink. Decisions made at the drawing board already account for what the city will require and what the crew can actually construct.

That continuity is the whole business model: fewer handoffs, fewer delays, and a finished ADU that matches the approved drawings.
