Our story

A flooring crew from San Fernando

Bfloor solutions is a flooring contractor based in San Fernando, California, serving homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley. The focus is simple: honest measurements, quality materials, and installation done right the first time.

Every project starts with a free on-site estimate, real samples you can see in your own light, and one clear written price. From tear-out and subfloor prep to trim, transitions, and the final walkthrough, one crew handles the whole job.

  1. Local and reachable

    Based at 11219 Rincon Ave in San Fernando. When you call, you talk to the crew doing the work.

  2. Prep before planks
