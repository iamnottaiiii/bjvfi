Services

Everything your project needs, under one roof

Residential and commercial work across the North Shore. Every job starts with a free estimate.

Interior painting and staining

Single rooms or whole homes, with meticulous prep: patching, sanding, caulking, and primer where it counts.

  • Walls and ceilings
  • Trim, baseboards, and crown molding
  • Doors and built-ins
  • Cabinet painting and refinishing
  • Small projects and touch-ups
Color help included. We will test swatches on your walls before a drop of paint goes on.

Exterior painting and staining

Curb appeal that survives New England winters: power washing, scraping, priming, and durable topcoats.

  • Siding and shingles
  • Trim, soffits, and fascia
  • Front doors and shutters
  • Decks, porches, and railings
  • Garages and outbuildings
Exterior work is scheduled seasonally. Call early in spring for the best availability.

Kitchen and bath remodeling

Our specialty. From quick refreshes to full remodels, we coordinate the whole project.

  • Bathroom remodels, our specialty
  • Kitchen refreshes and remodels
  • Tile and backsplash installation
  • Drywall, patching, and texture
  • Finish carpentry and trim
One crew handles paint, tile, and finishes, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Specialty finishes

Decorative work that turns plain walls into the focal point of the room.

  • Venetian plaster
  • Wallpaper installation and removal
  • Accent walls and decorative painting
  • Faux finishes and glazing
Specialty finishes are priced per project. Ask for a free estimate and samples.
