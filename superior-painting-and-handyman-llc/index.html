Our story

Two trades, one standard

Superior Painting & Handyman LLC was built on a simple idea: the painter should be able to fix the wall, and the handyman should be able to paint it.

The crew your to-do list has been waiting for

Based in Cincinnati, Ohio, we serve homeowners who are tired of juggling specialists. A fresh coat of paint usually reveals a few repairs, and a repair usually needs a coat of paint. We show up ready for both.

That means careful prep before every brush stroke, honest pricing before every job, and a clean workspace when we leave. No upsells, no disappearing acts, just the list getting shorter.
