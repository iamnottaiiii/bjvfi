About JC Beauty & Spa

JC Beauty & Spa on Broadway in Fair Lawn is known for a warm, family-like atmosphere and stylists who handle all hair types, including curly, Asian and African-American hair. Services span hair, nails, facials and lashes.

We are located at JC Beauty & Spa, 24-04 Broadway, Fair Lawn, NJ 07410. Call (201) 796-9496 for hours and appointments.
