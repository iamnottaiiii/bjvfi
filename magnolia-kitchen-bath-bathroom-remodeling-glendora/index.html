What we remodel

Bathroom Remodeling

Our core specialty. Whether it is a hall bath that needs a refresh or a primary suite you want to turn into a retreat, we plan the layout, the finishes, and the schedule with you.

  • Full bathroom remodels and partial updates
  • Walk-in showers and tub replacements
  • Tile floors, walls, and shower surrounds
  • Vanities, sinks, toilets, and fixtures
  • Lighting, mirrors, and storage planning

Kitchen Remodeling

The kitchen is the center of the home, so we treat it that way. Layout, storage, and surfaces get planned around how your household actually cooks and gathers.

  • Kitchen remodels from refresh to full redesign
  • Cabinets, hardware, and storage solutions
  • Countertops and backsplashes
  • Sinks, faucets, and fixture upgrades
  • Layout planning for better flow

Details and Finishes

Remodels live or die on the details. These finishing touches can be part of a larger project or, where scheduling allows, a smaller standalone job.

  • Tile installation and regrouting
  • Flooring updates for wet areas
  • Fixture and hardware replacements
  • Painting and trim touch-ups tied to a remodel
