Our story

Your Avenue T dry cleaner.

White On T Cleaners is a neighborhood dry cleaning shop on Avenue T in Marine Park, Brooklyn. The name says it plainly: this is the cleaner on T, and it takes pride in keeping the block looking sharp.

Walk in with a bag of laundry or a suit that needs rescuing and you will get the same treatment: careful tagging, honest stain assessment, thorough cleaning and a crisp press. No upsells, no mystery fees, just clean clothes returned on time.

For neighbors juggling work, school runs and everything in between, having a reliable cleaner a few blocks away makes the week run smoother. That is the job White On T shows up to do every day.
