Who we are

One crew for the whole yard

Hurtado Landscape And Concrete Inc is registered with the California Secretary of State and operates out of Duarte, serving homes and businesses across the San Gabriel Valley.

The idea is simple: most outdoor projects are not just landscape or just concrete, they are both. A patio needs planting around it. A new lawn needs edging and walks that fit. Doing both in-house keeps the design coherent and the schedule in one pair of hands.
