Reading Reviews Well

Three Habits for Hiring

Look for Patterns

One bad review among many good ones means less than a repeated complaint about the same issue. Read for themes, not outliers.

Watch the Responses

How a business replies to criticism tells you how it handles problems on the job. Silence and defensiveness both say something.

Verify Offline Too

Reviews help, but references, photos of finished work, and a written contract matter more. Use all three before you commit.
