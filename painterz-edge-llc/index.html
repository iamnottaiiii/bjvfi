About

Painting is the trade. Trust is the business.

Painterz Edge LLC. was founded in Pemberton, New Jersey in 2012. The owner brings more than 15 years of hands-on painting experience across residential, commercial, and industrial work, including years with home builders, property managers, and general contractors.

The mission is simple: provide the finest quality paint jobs at the best possible prices. That means personal coordination and daily supervision on every project, no subcontractors, and a commitment to see each job through from start to finish until the customer is 100 percent satisfied.
