What we do

Excavation services

Pick a service below to see what is included. Every job starts with a site walk and a clear price, so you know exactly what you are getting.

Site Excavation

Full and partial excavation for foundations, footings, basement digouts, pool shells and additions. We dig to plan, protect what stays, and haul spoil off site so the next trade can start clean.

Grading & Leveling

Rough grade for new construction and finish grade for lawns and landscaping. Proper slope directs water away from your building, which is the cheapest insurance a foundation can get.

Trenching

Trenches for water, sewer, electric and drainage lines, cut straight and to depth. We backfill and compact in lifts so the trench does not settle later.

Land Clearing

Clearing overgrown lots of brush, small trees, stumps and debris to open up usable land for building, parking or storage.

Selective Demolition

Tearing out old concrete, sheds, slabs and small structures, then removing the debris so the lot is ready for what comes next.

Driveway & Pad Prep

Excavation and base prep for new driveways, parking pads and equipment pads, graded for drainage and compacted for a long lasting surface.
