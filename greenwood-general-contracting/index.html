Our Story

A family business, built on word of mouth

Greenwood General Contracting is a family owned general contractor based in West Hempstead, NY. For 31 years, we have helped Long Island homeowners reimagine their kitchens, bathrooms, and whole homes with honest pricing and craftsmanship that holds up.

Our work is led by Rick and a crew of skilled, courteous professionals. Clients tell us the same thing over and over: we show up, we communicate, and we finish on time. That is why so many of our projects come from repeat customers and their neighbors.

We are known for paying close attention to detail and making customer satisfaction the top priority on every job, large or small. From a single bathroom refresh to a full home addition, you get one point of contact and a team that treats your home like their own.
