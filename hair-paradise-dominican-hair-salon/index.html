Our Story

A Port Richmond staple for Dominican hair care.

Care over speed

Hair Paradise has built its name on patience: stylists who take time with long, curly, and recovering hair instead of rushing through appointments. Clients describe being treated with genuine care, and results that keep them coming back as regulars.

From first blowout to full hair restoration journeys, the salon meets every head of hair where it is.

The experience

Expect a welcoming, clean, organized salon where the team explains what your hair needs. Many clients arrive with damaged or hard to manage hair and leave with a plan, not just a style.

Walk ins welcome when chairs are open. Calling ahead at (347) 466-4649 guarantees your spot.
