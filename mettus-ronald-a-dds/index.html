What We Do

General dental services

Tap a topic to read more. The office is a general dentistry practice for patients of all ages.

The topics below describe standard care at a general dental practice. For the exact services offered by this office, please call (724) 898-1155.

Checkups and Cleanings

Routine exams and cleanings are the foundation of dental health. Regular visits help catch cavities, gum changes, and other issues early, when they are simplest to treat. Ask the office about a recall schedule that fits your needs.

Fillings and Repair

When a cavity or small crack is found, a filling restores the tooth and stops the damage from spreading. Early repair keeps small problems from becoming big ones.

Crowns and Bridges

Crowns protect and strengthen teeth that are cracked, worn, or repaired with large fillings. Bridges replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the teeth on either side.

Root Canal Care

When infection reaches the inner nerve of a tooth, root canal treatment removes the infection and saves the tooth, usually relieving a painful toothache in the process.

Gum Health

Healthy gums hold healthy teeth. Deep cleanings and gum care treat early gum disease and help keep the foundation of your smile stable for years.

Dentures and Partials

When teeth cannot be saved, dentures and partial dentures restore chewing, speaking, and appearance. Fittings and adjustments keep them comfortable over time.
