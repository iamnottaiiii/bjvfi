Our story

A Local Elizabeth Contractor

P&S Associates Roofing Gutters Masonry Contractors is based at 740 Pennington St in Elizabeth, New Jersey. We are a licensed NJ Home Improvement Contractor (plus an additional NJ trade license) serving homeowners across Elizabeth and Union County.

The business is led by Walter Lozano and his crew. Customers know us for showing up fast, explaining the options in plain language, and leaving the work area clean. Many of our jobs come from repeat callers who first hired us for a single leak and now call for every structural odd job around the house.

What sets us apart is the combination of trades under one roof. Roofing, gutters, and masonry are connected systems, and a contractor who understands all three finds the problems that single trade shops miss. That is why a leaking roof, a sagging gutter, or a crumbling chimney can all be solved with one phone call.

Our hours: Monday through Friday 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM (Wednesday and Thursday from 6:30 AM), Saturday 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, closed Sunday.
