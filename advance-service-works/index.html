Hiring any contractor? Run this checklist.

Good advice no matter who you hire, including us:

  1. Get the scope in writing before work starts, what is included and what is not.
  2. In California, most projects over $500 require an active CSLB license. Ask for the license number and verify it at cslb.ca.gov.
  3. Ask who will actually be on site each day, and who your single point of contact is.
  4. Agree on a payment schedule tied to completed milestones, never full payment up front.
