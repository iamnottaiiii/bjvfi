About the Shop

Our Story

Small Shop, Serious Standards

Three Rivers Body Shop is an auto body repair shop at 390 Three Rivers Pkwy in North Bend, Ohio. Public business directories list the shop as operating since 1987, and customer reviews consistently mention the owner, Dan, by name.

Reviewers describe careful paint work, honest pricing that came in under competing estimates, and vehicles returned on time. One customer brought in a 1962 Lincoln and called the result amazing. Another praised a perfect three-part paint match on a repair a different shop had left unfinished.
