The feature story

A builder, not a brand

John Parker is the John in Carpenter John Construction, LLC. He is a certified building contractor, born and raised in St. Petersburg, and a third generation builder who has been in the construction business for decades. Directory profiles put the company's roots at 1976, and the work on record backs it up: everything from door replacements and crown molding to room additions and new construction.

The shop is small on purpose. One owner, one phone number, one standard. The listings that track the company describe a "hands on contractor" and put it plainly: good with wood. It is accredited by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, and past customers describe quality, dependable construction delivered by someone who treats the job like his own name is on it, because it is.

The business is registered in Florida as Carpenter John Construction, LLC, with its principal place of business at 3101 17th St N in St. Petersburg. Reviews across Birdeye and Porch rate the work 4 to 5 stars, and the themes repeat: first class work, dependable, the very best.

Hire Carpenter John and you get Carpenter John. That is the whole pitch, and around here, it is enough.

"Good with wood. Hands on contractor, born and raised in St. Pete, 3rd generation builder."
