Our process

How a project runs

No mystery, no surprises. Here is what working with RWF Connect looks like from first call to final walkthrough.

01

The call and the walkthrough

You call (513) 515-7592 and tell us what you need. We schedule a walkthrough, look at the real conditions, and ask the questions that matter before anyone talks numbers.

02

Clear scope and pricing

You get a written scope that spells out what is included, what is not, and what it costs. If something is unclear, we explain it before you sign, not after.

03

Permits and scheduling

We pull the permits, line up the trades, and give you a schedule you can plan around. Materials get ordered so nobody is waiting on a truck.

04

The build

Our crews and subs show up when they say they will. The site stays as clean as a jobsite gets, and you get updates when anything changes.

05

Walkthrough and punch list

We walk the finished work together. Anything that is not right goes on the punch list and gets fixed. Then we shake hands.
