Our Story

Roy B. Construction Company, LLC is a Brooklyn-based general contractor registered as a New York limited liability company since 2004. For more than two decades, the company has served Brooklyn homeowners and property managers with hands-on contracting work.

Based in the Canarsie neighborhood at 1153 E 81st St, the company keeps things personal: direct communication, clear expectations, and work that is planned carefully before a single tool comes out.
