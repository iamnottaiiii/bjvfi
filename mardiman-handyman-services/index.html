Who we are

Our Story

Mardiman Handyman Services is a local handyman based on Allen Ave in Altadena, helping neighbors keep their homes in good shape.

The guy you call
before small problems
get big

Every house has a list: the door that never quite closes, the patch of drywall from the doorknob, the faucet that drips just enough to be annoying. Mardiman Handyman Services exists for that list.

Based at 1938 Allen Ave in Altadena, we work across the neighborhood and nearby communities, one visit at a time. The approach is simple: show up when we say we will, quote the job honestly, do the work carefully, and leave the place cleaner than we found it.

No upselling, no mystery fees, no half-finished jobs. Just a local handyman who treats your home like it matters, because in Altadena, neighbors talk.

  • Free estimates on every job, before any work starts
  • On time, with our own tools, and we clean up after ourselves
  • Honest advice: if a job needs a licensed specialist, we will say so

Our promise

We treat every home like it is our own. If something is not right, we come back and make it right.

Altadena homes have character: older construction, hillside quirks, and repairs that need a steady hand rather than a rush job. That is exactly the work we do best.
