About

A Granada Hills builder

Ohan Construction is a general contractor based at 11822 Susan Dr in Granada Hills, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley. The business is built on a simple idea: a remodel should have one accountable contractor, not a rotating cast of trades you have to manage yourself.

From the first walkthrough to the final walkthrough, every phase is scheduled in writing, permits and inspections are handled, and the jobsite is left clean at the end of each day.

Whether it is a single bathroom or a full addition, the approach is the same: clear scope, honest pricing, and craftsmanship that holds up.
