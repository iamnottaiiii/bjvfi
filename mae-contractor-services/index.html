Our Story

A contractor who picks up the phone

Mae Contractor Services is a general contracting business based on West 144th Street in Harlem, New York. The work is straightforward: show up on time, do what the estimate says, and leave the place cleaner than we found it.

Most of the projects are the kind that make a home livable again: a kitchen that finally works for a family, a bathroom that stops leaking, walls and floors that look the way they should. Small jobs get the same care as big ones, because the punch list matters as much as the remodel.

We also handle appliance installation and repair, so the crew that renovates your kitchen can keep its appliances running too.
