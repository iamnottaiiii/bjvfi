Services

Electrical work, done to code

Residential and commercial electrical services for Northford and surrounding Connecticut communities. Every job starts with a conversation. Call (203) 484-0126.

Troubleshooting and repairs

Dead outlets, flickering lights, tripping breakers, faulty switches. We trace the problem to its source and fix it right.

Lighting installation

Recessed cans, ceiling fans, chandeliers, dimmers, and under-cabinet lighting that transforms a room.

Kitchen and bath wiring

Dedicated circuits for appliances, GFCI protection, exhaust fans, and vanity lighting wired safely.

Outdoor and landscape lighting

Security floods, pathway lights, deck and patio wiring built to handle Connecticut weather.

Remodel and addition wiring

New circuits and full rough-in wiring for finished basements, additions, and whole-home remodels.

Smoke and CO detectors

Hardwired, interconnected detectors installed to current code for real peace of mind.

Office and retail build-outs

Power, lighting, and data rough-in for new spaces, tenant fit-outs, and renovations.

Lighting retrofits

Energy-efficient upgrades that cut operating costs and improve workspaces.

Dedicated equipment circuits

Reliable power for machinery, servers, and commercial equipment, sized and permitted correctly.

Maintenance and repairs

Scheduled upkeep and fast response repairs that keep a business running without surprises.

Breaker panel replacement

Swap aging fuse boxes and overloaded panels for modern breaker panels with room to grow.

Service upgrades

Upgrade service amperage to support EV chargers, hot tubs, additions, and all-electric homes.

Sub-panels

Add sub-panels for workshops, garages, and additions without overloading the main panel.

Whole-home surge protection

Panel-mounted surge protection that guards appliances and electronics from spikes.
