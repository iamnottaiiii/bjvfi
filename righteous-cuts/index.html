Our story

A Parkville staple
on Harford Road

Righteous Cuts has been holding down its corner of Harford Road in Parkville for years, building a loyal base of regulars who drive past other shops to get here. Listings describe a comfortable, welcoming shop where the barbers know their regulars by name.

The name says it all. The shop built its reputation one head at a time: precise fades, honest service and cuts that keep customers coming back week after week. Reviewers consistently single out barber Lamont for his skill and attention to detail, and many customers say they will not trust anyone else with their cut.

Men and boys are the core clientele. Fathers bring their sons, regulars bring their friends, and the chairs keep a steady rhythm through the week.
