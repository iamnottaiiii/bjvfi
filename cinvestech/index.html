Services

Everything from the outlet to the on-screen picture

Residential installations across Cincinnati and Anderson Township. Call (513) 808-3937 for a quote on your project.

TV Wall Mounting

Flat, tilt, and full-motion mounts on drywall, brick, stone, or above the fireplace. We locate studs, level precisely, and anchor for your exact TV size and weight. In-wall wire routing available so the finished wall shows nothing but the screen.

Projector and Screen Installation

Ceiling mounts, throw-distance planning, and fixed-frame or motorized screens sized to your room. We align geometry and focus, then connect your sources so the first movie night just works.

Home Theater and Audio

Soundbars, AV receivers, and surround speakers wired and calibrated. One remote, one input list, zero confusion. We stay until every source plays through every speaker.

Cable Concealment

Power and HDMI routed inside the wall or inside paintable cord channels that blend into the room. Game consoles, streaming sticks, and cable boxes stay connected and out of sight.

Streaming and Cord-Cutting Setup

Antenna placement, streaming device setup, and app logins configured on your new display. We explain the monthly savings in plain numbers and make sure you can find every show you watch today.

Electrical Installation Service

New outlets behind the TV, dedicated circuits for theater gear, and lighting that sets the mood. Installed safely, neatly, and ready for inspection.

About

Cincinnati's clean-install crew for everything with a plug

CinvesTech is an electrical installation service based on Beechmont Avenue in Anderson Township, Cincinnati. The specialty is the part most installers rush: the finish. TVs mounted dead level, wires routed out of sight, and every device tested and explained before the truck leaves.

Customers describe quick scheduling, techs who arrive well equipped with the right mounts and hardware on hand, and the kind of plain-language guidance that makes cutting cable or setting up a projector feel simple.
