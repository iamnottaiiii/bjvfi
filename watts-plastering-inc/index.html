Sample services

Typical plaster work a contractor like Watts Plastering Inc. may offer. Call to confirm which services are available for your project.

Sample scope

Interior plaster work

  • Smooth trowel finishes for living rooms, bedrooms, and hallways
  • Textured finishes such as skip trowel or knockdown
  • Ceiling plaster and re-skimming over old surfaces
  • New drywall skim coats for a level paint-ready surface
Sample scope

Exterior stucco work

  • Three coat stucco systems for new walls and additions
  • Re-stucco over existing siding or old stucco
  • Color coat finishes in sand, smooth, or dash textures
  • Stucco crack repair and water damage restoration
Sample scope

Repairs and patching

  • Hairline crack stitching and re-floating
  • Patching holes and damaged sections to blend with existing texture
  • Delamination and hollow spot repair
  • Touch ups after plumbing or electrical work
