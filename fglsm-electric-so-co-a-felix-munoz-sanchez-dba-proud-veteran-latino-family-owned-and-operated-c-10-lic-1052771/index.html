Our story

Veteran owned.
Family operated.

FGLSM Electric is the trade name of Felix Munoz Sanchez, a proud veteran running a Latino family owned and operated electrical contracting business out of Pacoima. The full business name says it plainly: FGLSM ELECTRIC SO CO. A Felix Munoz Sanchez DBA. Proud Veteran, Latino Family Owned and Operated, C-10 Lic# 1052771.

A C-10 license is California's electrical contractor license, and it means the person doing your electrical work is tested, bonded, and held to code. When you call (818) 271-9391, you reach the family behind the license, not a dispatch center.

We serve Pacoima and the surrounding San Fernando Valley with residential and light commercial electrical work.
