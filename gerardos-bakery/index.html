Our Story

A neighborhood panaderia on the East Side of Chicago.

Gerardo's Bakery sits at 10125 S Ewing Avenue, in the heart of Chicago's East Side, and it has become a local institution for traditional Mexican pan dulce. The ovens start early, the doors open at 4 in the morning, and the first trays of conchas and puerquitos come out warm.

What customers talk about most is the feeling of the place. Traditional music plays from the bakery in the back. The staff is courteous and hard working. The bread is light and fluffy, the way pan dulce is supposed to be. For many regulars, it is a taste of home and a reason the East Side feels like a neighborhood.

The bakery has also catered events, bringing its sweet bread to family parties and celebrations around the community. Whether you stop in for a single concha on your way to work or you order trays for a gathering, Gerardo's treats every order with the same care.
