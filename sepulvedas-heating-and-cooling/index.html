About us

A North Hills shop with a simple habit

Sepulveda’s Heating and Cooling is an air conditioning contractor based in North Hills, California. The habit our reviewers keep mentioning: our technicians find every possible way to make a system work, and they stay friendly while they do it.

We handle installation, repair and maintenance for heating and cooling equipment, plus the airflow work around it like dryer vent cleaning. One crew, whole system, straight answers.
