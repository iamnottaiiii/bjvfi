About

Lucky's Barber Shop is a good old fashioned barber shop at the Zettler Center in Columbus, serving the neighborhood since 2017. The shop was formerly known as Mike's on the Pike before Pat and Dan took over and gave it the Lucky's name.


The barbers cut all types of hair and are known for sharp razor work, clean razor fades, and taking their time so every client leaves satisfied. Appointments are encouraged since the shop runs a tight book.
