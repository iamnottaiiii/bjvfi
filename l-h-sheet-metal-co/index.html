The shop

A working metal shop, not a warehouse.

L & H Sheet Metal Co has operated out of Jonesboro since 1987, cutting, bending, and assembling sheet metal for HVAC and construction work. Contractors bring drawings and specs; the shop turns them into finished ductwork and fittings.

Materials run the range of the trade: steel, aluminum, zinc, iron, and more, formed into the ducts and fittings your system calls for.
