About

Three decades of building in the Lehigh Valley

Jrn Construction Co is a construction company based in Slatington, Pennsylvania, with 34 years of experience in the construction and real estate industry. From new home construction, remodeling, repairs, additions, kitchens, and baths, our goal has always been simple: provide quality craftsmanship at an economical price.

We bring highly skilled employees to every job and partner long term with some of the finest subcontractors in the Lehigh Valley. That combination gives us the ability to fulfill all of your needs, from a single repair to a full new build.
