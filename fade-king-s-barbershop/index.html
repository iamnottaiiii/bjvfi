About

Worcester's fade specialists.

The shop

On Grafton Street, in the heart of Worcester.

Fade King's Barbershop sits at 867 Grafton St #8 in Worcester, MA, and has built its name on one thing: consistency. The team is known for taking their time on every cut, double checking the blend, and making sure the lineup is sharp before you stand up. The shop keeps a classic barbershop feel with big TVs and a clean, welcoming space.

Walk-ins are welcome, and the shop keeps long hours, open seven days a week including Mondays, which regulars say is hard to find anywhere else in town.

The crew

Barbers who sweat the details.

Reviewers call out barbers including Stephan, Shayne, and Desharn for their attention to detail and focus on making each cut perfect. The vibe is friendly and unhurried: good conversation, honest advice on what suits your hair, and no rushing the chair.

"Reasonable prices, top quality, and a shop you actually want to hang out in."Paraphrased from online reviews
