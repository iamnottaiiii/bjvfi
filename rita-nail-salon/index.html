Our Story

A Kings Highway staple

Rita Nail Salon has served the Kings Highway neighborhood of Brooklyn for years, building a loyal base of regulars who come back for the same reason: reliable, affordable nail care in a clean, professional salon.

Guests praise the salon's cleanliness, the professional staff, and the team's ability to recreate the nail designs clients bring in. Prices stay reasonable, the massage chairs are new, and walk-ins are welcome every day.
