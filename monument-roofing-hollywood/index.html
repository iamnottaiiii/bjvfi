Our story

A North Hollywood roofer with a simple rule.

Monument Roofing Hollywood is a roofing contractor based at 6945 Farmdale Ave in North Hollywood, California.

"Treat every roof like it is over your own family."

That rule shapes every job. It means inspections with photos you can see, quotes you can understand, and recommendations we would give our own neighbors. If a ten dollar tube of sealant and an hour of labor will buy your roof five more years, that is what we will tell you.

What we believe

Most roofs do not fail all at once. They fail at the details: flashing, valleys, vents, and edges. We sweat the details.

How we communicate

Photos before, during, and after. You see what we see, so every decision is an informed one.

How we leave a job

Nails picked up, gutters cleared, debris hauled. The only sign we were there is the roof itself.
