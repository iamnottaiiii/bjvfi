About Independence Cleaners

Independence Cleaners has served the Independence, Kentucky community from 1832 Declaration Drive for around two decades. Public business listings show the company established around 2005, and it remains a small, locally run shop with a handful of employees.

What sets it apart is not scale but heart. Customer reviews tell story after story of personal care: a suit kept safe and unclaimed for four years, a tuxedo hand-delivered to a customer's back door on New Year's Eve, a ruined comforter brought back to life. The staff, including folks reviewers name as Jim, Katie, and Colin, treat garments and the people who wear them with real respect.

No standalone website was found for the business in public listings; the shop is active on Facebook, which is listed as its web presence in directory records.
