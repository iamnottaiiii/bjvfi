Services by room

Pick a room. Picture the reveal.

Each tab walks through a typical renovation focus: what usually changes, and the notes that shape a good result. Scope lists are samples to start the conversation.

Kitchens

A kitchen refresh centers on the pieces you touch every day: cabinets, counters, layout, and light.

  • Cabinet refacing or replacement
  • Countertop updates
  • Backsplash and fresh paint
  • Lighting and fixture updates
  • Flooring refreshes
Sample scope list · Call to confirm

Transformation notes

Paint and cabinet color carry most of the visual change in a kitchen; hardware and lighting finish the story. Before you call, note what bothers you most about the current layout, since that shapes every estimate.

Bathrooms

Bathrooms reward focus: a few well chosen surfaces and fixtures can make a small room feel entirely new.

  • Vanity and storage updates
  • Tile for floors, showers, and walls
  • Fixture and faucet updates
  • Ventilation and lighting improvements
  • Paint and trim refreshes
Sample scope list · Call to confirm

Transformation notes

In baths, light color and good ventilation do quiet heavy lifting. Snap a photo of your current vanity and shower before calling; it helps any contractor scope the job faster.

Basements

A finished basement adds living space without adding footprint. The order of work matters more here than anywhere else.

  • Moisture and drainage review first
  • Framing, insulation, and drywall
  • Flooring suited to below grade spaces
  • Lighting plans that beat low ceilings
  • Paint, trim, and finishing touches
Sample scope list · Call to confirm

Transformation notes

Basements change most when light and flooring are handled together. Decide how you will actually use the space, office, playroom, guest suite, before picking finishes.

Whole home updates

Sometimes the biggest reveal comes from consistent, home wide refreshes rather than one big room.

  • Interior painting, room by room or whole home
  • Flooring updates across levels
  • Interior doors, trim, and casing
  • General repair and improvement work
  • Punch lists before a sale or move in
Sample scope list · Call to confirm

Transformation notes

Whole home work reads best when paint, flooring, and trim share one palette. Walk the house with a notepad and rank rooms by priority; bring that list to your call.

Scope lists above are starting points, not quotes. The fastest path to a real answer is a conversation.
