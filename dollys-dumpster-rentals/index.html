What goes in the bin

Most non hazardous household and construction debris is welcome. When in doubt, call and ask before you load.

Household junkFurniture, clutter, old belongings
Renovation debrisDrywall, lumber, flooring, tile
RoofingShingles, underlayment, gutters
Yard debrisBranches, brush, leaves, sod
AppliancesWashers, dryers, stoves, fridges
Move out junkMattresses, carpet, boxes, misc
Not accepted

Hazardous materials are never allowed in roll off dumpsters: wet paint, chemicals, solvents, asbestos, batteries, tires, and fuel tanks. Ask us for safe disposal guidance for these items.
