Full roof replacement

When a roof is past patching, replacement is the honest answer. We tear off to the deck, replace damaged sheathing, install proper underlayment and flashing, then lay the new roof to manufacturer spec.

  • Architectural shingles, tile, and flat roof systems
  • Deck inspection and damaged wood replacement
  • New flashing at valleys, chimneys, and walls
  • Full cleanup, magnetic nail sweep, haul-away

Sample pricing: full replacements on typical Valley homes often fall in a wide range depending on size, pitch, and material. Call for a free on-site estimate. No sample figure is a quote.

Roof repair

Most roofs do not need replacing. They need the actual problem fixed: the cracked flashing, the lifted shingles, the dried-out sealant around a vent. We find the source and repair it properly.

  • Leak detection and leak repair
  • Missing, cracked, or wind-lifted shingles
  • Flashing repair at chimneys, skylights, and walls
  • Vent boot and pipe collar replacement

Sample pricing: minor repairs often start around a few hundred dollars; larger section repairs run higher. Final price quoted after we see the roof.

Roof inspections

The cheapest roof work is the work you do not need. Our inspections document the real condition of your roof with photos, so you can plan instead of panic.

  • Pre-purchase and pre-sale roof inspections
  • Post-windstorm damage checks
  • Maintenance plans for aging roofs
  • Written findings with photos

Sample pricing: inspections are typically modest in cost and often credited toward repair work. Call for current inspection pricing.

Gutters & more

A roof is a system. Gutters, downspouts, and attic ventilation all decide how long it lasts. We handle the pieces around the shingles too.

  • Gutter installation and repair
  • Downspout rerouting away from foundations
  • Attic ventilation assessment
  • Skylight leak repair

Sample pricing: priced per job after a look. Call (818) 352-3271 and describe what you need.
