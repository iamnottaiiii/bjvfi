Our story

A family name on every job

Huber & Behle Inc has been remodeling homes in Northern Kentucky for decades, as a small company where the owners name is on the work.

Built on craftsmanship,
kept honest by reputation

When your company carries your family name, every job is personal. That is how Huber & Behle has worked since the beginning: a small crew of five, trusted subcontractors we have used for years, and homeowners who call us back and send their neighbors.

The Better Business Bureau lists 66 years in business for the company, and Angi reviewers give the team a perfect 5.0 across 7 reviews. Numbers aside, the pattern is the same: careful work, clear communication, and fair hourly pricing with no travel charges.

Dennis Behle is the contact many customers know by name. Free estimates and warranty coverage come standard, because that is simply how the company does business.
