Our Story

A Family Trade, Three Decades Strong

C Moore Builders LLC is a family business with 35 years in the building trade, based on Jumpers Hole Road in Pasadena, Maryland. We grew the old fashioned way: by doing most of the work ourselves and keeping the subcontractors we have trusted for many years.

That is why customers call us true professionals and why 100 percent of Angi customers say they would recommend us. We treat every home like it carries our name, because it does.

  • Family owned and operated, 35 years
  • Most work performed by our own crews
