Our story

A Main Street cleaner, rooted in Middleton

Middleton Dry Cleaners has been pressing shirts and keeping this town sharp from the same Main Street storefront for over a decade.

On the record since 2013

Public business records show Middleton Dry Cleaners operating at 232 S Main St since 2013, a small local shop serving the commuters, families, and professionals of Middleton and the surrounding North Shore towns.

The formula has stayed simple: look every garment over at the counter, treat each one like it matters, and have it pressed and ready when promised. No gimmicks, just clean clothes done right.
