Our Story

A Childhood Dream on Kingston Avenue

Sarah dreamed of owning her own salon when she was still a child. Years later she made it real, opening Hair Lounge by Sarah as a storefront on Kingston Avenue in the heart of Crown Heights, Brooklyn.

A decade into running the business, she told a community interviewer what keeps the doors open: the relationships. Clients recommend their friends, new brides come in glowing, and regulars are greeted by name. "I want people to leave happier than when they came in," she said, "and that is what has kept my doors open all these years."

A Storefront That Feels Like Home

Being a storefront on a busy avenue matters to Sarah. People can walk in at any time, and with two stylists in the salon at all times, someone is always there to help. The salon is centrally located in the neighborhood she lives in, and she counts it a privilege to work in her own community and be her own boss.

"I love the fact that we are a storefront. People really love and appreciate that they can walk in at any time."

Wigs With a Personal Touch

The salon grew around its wig clients. Sarah sells and services wigs with the eye of someone who wears the craft personally: cutting, highlighting and styling each piece until it looks like it grew there. Clients mark milestones in the chair, from new wigs for the holidays to wedding day updos, and the lounge loves being part of those simchas.

Whether you are buying your first wig or your fifteenth, the goal is the same great service and beautiful results, every single visit.
