Our story

A boutique salon on Avenue S

Seasoned professionals, quality products, and a warm room to unwind in. That is the whole idea behind Nonpareil Salon Group.

Seasoned hands, personal touch

Nonpareil Salon Group is a full service boutique salon in Sheepshead Bay, Brooklyn. The team is made up of experienced beauty professionals who are passionate about their craft, and each stylist brings their own skills and expertise to the chair.

That means no cookie cutter cuts. Every client gets personal attention and a customized approach, whether it is a simple trim or a complete transformation.

The salon is frequented by both men and women, and the room is designed to feel relaxing and comfortable, the kind of place where you can unwind and enjoy a little pampering.
