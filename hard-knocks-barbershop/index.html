Our Story

A classic shop for
a classic town.

Built on hard knocks and clean cuts

Hard Knocks Barbershop sits at 1279 Providence Rd in Whitinsville, serving Northbridge and the surrounding Central MA towns. The formula is simple: skilled barbers, honest prices, and a laid-back shop you actually want to hang out in.

Reviewers call it a unique hangout with a no-nonsense, laid-back atmosphere. Sons feel at ease in the chair, regulars become lifelong customers, and new clients keep showing up because someone told them this is the spot.

  • Skilled barbers for cuts, fades, tapers and beard work
  • Kids cuts and father and son appointments, all ages welcome
  • Online booking plus walk-ins, parking on site, cards accepted
