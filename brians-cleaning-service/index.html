About

Bayonne's detail-obsessed cleaners

A local service built on showing up

Brian’s Cleaning Service is based at 72 W 17th St in Bayonne, NJ, serving homes and small businesses across the area. Our reputation comes from the basics done right: arriving on time, cleaning thoroughly, and treating your home with care.

Regular customers know the routine. After an initial deep clean, recurring visits keep the home maintained while rotating one area into the spotlight each time: the refrigerator one week, the cabinets the next. It is a simple system, and it works.

Scheduling stays flexible. If your week changes, we work with you to find a new slot. That is how a cleaning service earns 4.8 stars across its reviews.
