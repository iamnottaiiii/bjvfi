Our Story

A neighborhood workhorse for laundry day

City Laundromat has become the go-to self-service laundry for Somerville residents who want clean machines, fair prices, and a straightforward visit.

The store runs on a simple idea: keep the machines newer, keep the floor clean, and keep laundry day painless. About 100 machines, including 135 lb jumbo washers for bedding and bulky loads, mean you rarely wait long for an open machine.

Regulars describe friendly attendants, a tidy space, and a card system that beats carrying a bag of quarters. The dedicated parking lot is a small luxury on Somerville Ave, and outlets for charging make the wait more pleasant.

The team describes its own mission this way: bring your clothes to us, or we will pick them up for you, whatever suits you best. Drop-off service and pickup are available for anyone who would rather skip the chore entirely.
