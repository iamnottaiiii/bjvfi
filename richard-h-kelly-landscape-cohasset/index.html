The practice

No. 01

Good landscape architecture starts with reading the land. Coastal light, native plantings, stone, and grade all shape how a property feels and functions through the year.

Richard H. Kelly Landscape works with homeowners on residential landscape design, from concept and planting plans through coordination of installation. Each project begins with a conversation about the site and how you want to live in it.

Services

Landscape architecture, from concept to completion.

Residential landscape design services for Cohasset and surrounding coastal communities. Every engagement begins with a site visit and conversation.

i.

Site analysis and concept design

Reading the property: grades, drainage, views, light, and existing plantings, then developing a design direction that fits the site.

ii.

Planting plans

Detailed planting plans with plant selections suited to salt air, ledge soils, and New England seasons.

iii.

Hardscape and outdoor living design

Terraces, steps, walkways, and walls designed to work with the architecture of the home and the slope of the land.

iv.

Project coordination

Coordination with contractors during installation so the built landscape matches the design intent.
