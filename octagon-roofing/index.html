Our story

A Valley roofing company since 1989.

Octagon Roofing Company Inc. has worked on San Fernando Valley roofs for more than three decades, growing from a small local crew into a full service roofing company with a dedicated skylight division.

Led by a master roofer.

Owner Charlie Ford is a career roofer with decades in the trade. When customers kept calling about leaking skylights installed by someone else, he built a dedicated skylight division inside Octagon Roofing to fix them properly, work that became one of the companys best known specialties.

The company describes itself as locally owned and operated, valuing honesty and integrity, and treating each home as if it were its own. Pricing is competitive, details are watched closely, and the goal is lasting relationships, not one time jobs.

  • In business since 1989 in Van Nuys, CA
  • About 600 customers served per year
  • Residential roofing focus across the Valley
