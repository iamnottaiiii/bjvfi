THE VIBE

Blenderz Barbershop sits at 4351 E Main St in Whitehall, Ohio. The shop's own motto says it best: walk-ins welcome, all hair welcome. It is a laid-back spot where the barbers blend classic technique with modern style.

From adult haircuts and buzz cuts to kids cuts, lineups, and beard shaping, the menu covers the everyday grooming routine.
