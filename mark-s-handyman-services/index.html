Our Story

The man behind the toolbox

Mark's Handyman Services is a handyman business based at 17 Pinney Rd in Somers, Connecticut. Public business records list the company as established in 2004, with Mark Barpofiak as owner.

In the owner's own words, shared in a public review response: every job is recorded, backed by a guarantee, and comes with a receipt. No cash only jobs, no guesswork.

Customers mention fair pricing and fence work they were proud of, and several say they would hire Mark again.
