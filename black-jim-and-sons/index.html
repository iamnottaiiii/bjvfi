Our story

Family roofing since 1982

Jim Black & Sons Roofing & Sheet Metal: a Silver Grove family company, BBB accredited since 1995.

The company

One county, one family,
four decades

Jim Black & Sons started in 1982 as a family roofing business on Mary Ingles Highway in Silver Grove, Kentucky. More than forty years later, it is still a family operation: owner Douglas Black runs the company, and much of the work still arrives the old-fashioned way, by referral from satisfied customers.

The company joined the Better Business Bureau in 1995 and holds an A+ rating, a record built over decades of roofing, sheet metal, and gutter work across Campbell County and Northern Kentucky.

The specialty

Sheet metal is a craft,
not a commodity

Plenty of roofers do shingles. Fewer do sheet metal well, and fewer still will touch box gutters on older homes. That is where this company made its name: relining box gutters, forming seamless gutters on site, and keeping historic rooflines watertight without changing how they look.

It is the kind of work that does not photograph well but matters enormously. A relined box gutter can outlast the roof around it.
