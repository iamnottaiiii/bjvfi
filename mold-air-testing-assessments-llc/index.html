From first call to clear answers, in three steps

Mold problems hide behind walls, under floors, and in HVAC systems. Testing takes the guesswork out, so you only fix what actually needs fixing.

  • 1
    Inspection & sampling. A visual assessment plus air and surface samples taken where it matters most.
  • 2
    Accredited lab analysis. Samples are analyzed by an independent lab for spore types and levels.
  • 3
    Written report & protocol. Clear findings, plus a remediation scope when conditions are elevated.

Water damage? Test before you remediate.

After a leak or flood, drying fast matters, but so does knowing whether microbial growth started. An assessment documents moisture and mold conditions now, giving you evidence for insurance or remediation decisions later.

Our approachNo. 01

Data first, sales pitch never

In Florida, the same company cannot legally assess mold and remediate it. That rule exists to protect you, and it is exactly how we work: independent testing, objective lab results, and a written report that says what was found and what it means.

Customers describe the service as professional and reasonably priced, with reports delivered quickly so decisions about remediation or real estate can move forward without waiting.

When to call us

A musty smell with no visible source. Allergy symptoms that ease when you leave the house. A recent leak, flood, or roof repair. Buying a home and wanting documentation. After remediation, to verify the job worked.
