Our Story

A Mexican bakery on Pulaski Road, built on early mornings and honest bread.

Marcella's Bakery is the kind of panaderia that regulars plan their mornings around. The ovens are lit while Alsip is still dark, and by the time the sun is up the cases hold conchas, bolillos, pan fino, and tamales oaxaqueños wrapped in banana leaves.

Guests describe it as the best Mexican bakery on the south side, a place worth a 10 to 15 minute drive out of the way. The bread is fairly priced and consistently fresh, and the service keeps families coming back with two bags instead of one.

The rhythm here is simple: bake through the night, sell through the morning, and start again. When the bread is gone, it is gone, which is why the regulars arrive early.

Before dawn: ovens lit, dough shaped, tamales steaming.

4 AM: doors open, first bolillos hit the case.

Morning rush: pan dulce for coffee, tamales for the table.

Evening: custom cakes planned for the next celebration.

Why "desde temprano"?

It means "since early morning," and it is the whole philosophy. Freshness is not a claim here, it is a schedule. The bread you buy at 6 AM was shaped a few hours earlier, not a few days earlier.
