Services

Garment care,
start to finish

Everything below is priced per item at the counter. Call ahead for rush orders or anything unusual.

01

Dry cleaning

Suits, blazers, trousers, dresses, coats, and delicates. Each piece is inspected, pre treated where needed, cleaned, and finished by hand. Buttons are checked, seams are pressed flat, and everything comes back bagged and ready to wear.

Priced per item · ask at the counter
02

Shirt laundry & pressing

Dress shirts and blouses laundered, starched to your preference, and pressed crisp. Choose hangers or folded and boxed. A standing weekly order keeps the drawer full without thinking about it.

Priced per item · ask at the counter
03

Alterations & repairs

Hems shortened or let out, waists taken in, zippers replaced, buttons sewn, and small tears mended. Bring the garment in and we will tell you honestly what can be done and what it will cost.

Quoted in person
04

Wash & fold

Everyday laundry by the load: sorted, washed, dried, and folded neatly. Drop it off in the morning and cross laundry day off your list entirely.

Priced per load · ask at the counter
05

Household & bulky items

Comforters, duvets, blankets, curtains, drapes, tablecloths, and slipcovers. The things that do not fit in a home machine get the full treatment here.

Priced per item · ask at the counter
06

Stain treatment

Wine, grease, ink, coffee: bring it in as soon as you can and point out the spot. The sooner we see it, the better the odds. No promises on set-in stains, but we will give it our best.

Ask at drop off

About

A Bay Ridge
mainstay on 3rd Ave

Belt French Cleaners serves the Bay Ridge neighborhood from 9217 3rd Avenue, keeping the block looking sharp one garment at a time.

§

The shop

Belt French Cleaners is a neighborhood dry cleaner in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn, at 9217 3rd Ave. Walk in with an armful of clothes, walk out with a ticket and a pickup date. It is the kind of local business the avenue runs on: practical, personal, and consistent.

§

The standard

Every item is ticketed and tracked from drop off to pickup. Stains get pre treated when you point them out. Pressing is done by hand, not rushed through. If something is not right when you pick up, say so at the counter.

§

The neighborhood

Bay Ridge runs on its avenues, and 3rd Ave is one of the busiest. We clean for commuters, families, restaurants, and anyone else who needs to look sharp. Regulars welcome, walk ins always welcome too.
