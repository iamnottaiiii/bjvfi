Our Story

One mechanic, one standard

The shop

Teta's Auto Repair sits right on US-9 in Clifton Park, an independent shop where the person who diagnoses your car is the person who fixes it. Owner Robert Teta, known to customers simply as Bob, runs the bays with a simple rule: tell the truth about what a car needs, charge a fair price, and explain the work.

Teta's is a licensed New York State vehicle safety and repair facility, so inspections and repairs happen under one roof.

The reputation

With a 4.9 star rating across dozens of reviews, the shop has become a word-of-mouth favorite in the Clifton Park area. Customers describe years of service across multiple vehicles: routine oil changes, brakes and rotors, tire repairs, water pumps, transmission service, and even full engine rebuilds.

The recurring theme is trust. Drivers say Bob explains the work, quotes it clearly, and often comes in under the estimate.
