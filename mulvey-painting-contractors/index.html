What we do

Painting services

Interior, exterior, and whole-home repaints for the North Grafton area. Every job starts with a firm, written quote.

Interior painting

Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, hallways, ceilings, trim, and doors. We protect your floors and furniture, prep every surface, and leave crisp lines.

  • Walls, ceilings, and trim
  • Doors, moldings, and built-ins
  • Color changes and accent walls

Exterior painting

Siding, shutters, porches, and trim painted to stand up to New England weather. Power washing, scraping, priming, and quality exterior coatings.

  • House siding and trim
  • Porches, decks, and shutters
  • Prep, priming, and weatherproof coatings

Whole-home repaints

Moving in, moving out, or just ready for a change. Full interior repaints and exterior makeovers handled room by room or all at once.

Detail and finishing

Staining, transparent finishes, cabinet touch-ups, and small repair-and-paint jobs that make a room feel finished.
