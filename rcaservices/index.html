Why a fridge specialist matters

General handymen fix a little of everything. Refrigeration is its own trade: sealed systems, refrigerant handling, defrost controls, and ice maker plumbing all in one box. A specialist sees your exact failure mode every week, not once a year.

That focus shows up in the visit. Faster diagnosis. The right part the first time. And an honest answer when a repair costs more than the fridge is worth.

How we treat your kitchen

Appliance repair happens in the middle of your home, so the bar is simple: show up when promised, explain the problem in plain language, quote before working, and leave the kitchen as clean as we found it.

Based at 2029 Verdugo Blvd in Montrose, serving local homes. When you call, you talk to the people who will do the work.
