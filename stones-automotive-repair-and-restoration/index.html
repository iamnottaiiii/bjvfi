No. 01The Atelier

One shop, two crafts: restoration and repair.

Some shops fix cars. Some shops restore them. Stones does both, with the same patience, under the same roof in Bryantown.

There is a particular kind of care that goes into bringing an old car back. It is slow work, part detective, part craftsman. Stones Automotive Repair and Restoration was built around that kind of care, and it shows in everything from a full restoration down to a routine repair on a daily driver.

Neighbors describe the owner simply: respectful, helpful, the kind of person you recommend without hesitation. Many customers have been coming for years, which in this trade is the highest compliment there is.

No. 02In the shop

Restoration patience, applied to every repair.

A shop that restores classics learns things a regular repair shop never has to: how to be patient with old metal, how to source what no longer exists, how to make something right rather than just make it run. That discipline carries into every brake job and every diagnostic.

It is also why the pricing stays fair. Restoration customers watch every dollar across months of work, so honesty is not a policy here, it is a habit. Bring the daily driver or bring the dream car. Both get the same treatment.
