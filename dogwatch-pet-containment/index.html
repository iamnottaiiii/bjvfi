Our Story

Northern Kentucky's DogWatch dealer since 2010

DogWatch of Northern Kentucky is the local dealer for DogWatch hidden fence systems, serving dog owners across the Northern Kentucky area. The business is owned and run by Mary Jo Mentink, known to customers simply as MJ.

Since opening in 2010, the team has built its reputation on two things: professional installation and hands-on training. Rather than dropping off a box of equipment, MJ comes to your home, meets your dogs, surveys the yard, and personally trains your dog over multiple sessions.

Why customers choose a local dealer

  • On-site service: consultations, installation, and training all happen at your home.
  • Owner-led training: the person who sells you the fence is the person who trains your dog.
  • Real follow-up: customers describe prompt answers, extra time when needed, and ongoing support after install.
  • Trusted standing: BBB A+ rated, with 16 years in business in Northern Kentucky.

The DogWatch brand itself is known for its Pet-Friendly training approach and SmartFence connected systems, and this local team brings that technology home with a personal touch customers remember by name.
