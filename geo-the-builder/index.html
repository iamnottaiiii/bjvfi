Our story

A Valley Village handyman built on word of mouth

Geo started fixing things the way most handymen do: for family, then for neighbors, then for neighbors of neighbors. The name on the truck stayed the same because the work did too.

Based on Chandler Blvd in Valley Village, Geo The Builder handles the jobs that fall between the cracks: too small for a general contractor, too annoying to live with. Drywall dings before a move-out, a faucet that will not stop dripping, the TV that has sat in its box for a month.

The approach is simple. Show up when promised. Price it straight. Do it like it is your own place. Clean up like you were never there.

Valley Village homeowners, renters and property managers all get the same deal: one reliable number to call when something needs fixing.
