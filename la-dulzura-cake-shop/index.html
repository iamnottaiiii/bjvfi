Our story

La Dulzura means "the sweetness," and sweetness is what we are all about. We are a custom cake shop in Hammond, Indiana, built around the celebrations that bring families together.

"Pasteles y postres personalizados para tu evento especial."

That is how we describe ourselves: custom cakes and desserts for your special event. Weddings, quinceañeras, birthdays, and everything worth gathering for. Every cake is designed around your theme, your colors, and your people.

Alongside our custom work, our case holds the desserts our neighbors love most: tres leches cake, coconut tres leches, chocoflan, gelatinas, and turtle cheesecake, made fresh and ready to take home.

Se habla español. Come tell us about your celebration, and let's bake something unforgettable.
