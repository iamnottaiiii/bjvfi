Our story

A fabrication shop, not a showroom.

BNS Marble & Granite Inc has operated as a stone fabrication shop in North Hollywood since 2003, according to its public business profiles. The work is custom by definition: imported and domestic granite, marble, limestone, and quartz, cut and finished to each customer's specifications.

For over 15 years, Beni has provided quality work for great prices, with quick turnaround and craftsmen who build to last.

That description comes from the shop's public profile, which credits owner Beni and a team of dedicated craftsmen. Directory listings add that the shop offers free estimates and warranty on its work. California BuildZoom records show the company holds an active Tile (Ceramic and Mosaic) contractor license and ranks in the top 22% of licensed California contractors by BuildZoom score.
