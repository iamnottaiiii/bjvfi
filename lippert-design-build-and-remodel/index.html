Why design build

The designer and the builder should never be strangers

When one company owns both the plans and the construction, budgets stay honest and details do not get lost between trades. That is the whole idea behind Lippert Design Build & Remodel.

Design with intent

Your project starts with plans shaped around your lot, your budget, and the way your family actually lives. No stock plans forced to fit.

Build with care

The same team that drew the plans swings the hammers. Details get the attention they were promised on paper.

Finish with pride

Walkthroughs, punch lists, and follow through. Customer satisfaction is the top priority on every single job.

Our story

A builder who treats your home like his own reputation

The Lippert way

Lippert Design Build & Remodel is a custom home builder and remodeling company based on Pleasant Ridge Road in Alexandria, Kentucky. The approach is simple and old fashioned: pay exquisite attention to detail, and make customer satisfaction the top priority on every job.

Because design and construction live under the same roof, nothing gets lost between the drawing board and the jobsite. One team answers for the plans and the finished work.

Experience you can lean on

With 38 years of experience, Homer Lippert has guided homeowners through custom home builds, full remodels, and the hundred small decisions in between. Clients describe him as fantastic to work with, knowledgeable, and the reason their project felt easy.

That steady guidance is what a design build firm is for: one knowledgeable partner from the first sketch to the final walkthrough.
