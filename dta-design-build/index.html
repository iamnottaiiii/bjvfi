One team from sketch to keys

DTA Design Build is a general contractor based in Woodland Hills, California. The name says the model: design and build under one roof, so the people who draw the plans are the people accountable for building them.

A

Design-build, explained plainly

In a traditional remodel you hire a designer, then bid the plans to a contractor, then hope the two agree on what anything costs. With design-build, one company owns the drawings, the budget and the build. When a question comes up mid-project, the answer comes from the same team that priced it.
