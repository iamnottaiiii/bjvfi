Our story

A working carpentry crew in Orlando, Florida.

Sample art

Built on the basics.

TS Carpentry LLC is a working carpentry business based in south Orlando. We grew the way most good trades do: one referral at a time, from kitchens and baths to full trim packages and outdoor builds.

Our crew handles the full arc of a job, from the first measurement to the last coat of caulk. The owner is on site, the crew knows the plan, and the customer knows the price before we start.

What guides the work.

  • Measure twice, cut once, every time
  • Clean job sites at the end of each day
  • Honest timelines, honest pricing
  • Finish work we would sign our name to
