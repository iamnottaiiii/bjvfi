Our Story

A Harrison Main-Street Fixture

Ballard's Barber Shop has served the Harrison community from its storefront at 226 Harrison Avenue for decades. Public business listings trace the shop back to 1979, making it one of the longstanding small businesses on the avenue.

It is a traditional barbershop in every sense: a place where men and boys get quality haircuts and grooming services from experienced barbers, and where neighbors catch up while they wait their turn.

Owner Brenda Ballard keeps the shop's classic character alive, offering the kind of dependable, fair-priced cuts that keep families coming back year after year.
