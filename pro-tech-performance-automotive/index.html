The Shop

John’s shop, built on trust

Pro-Tech Performance Automotive is the kind of shop that earns customers one car at a time, and keeps them for years.

Walk into Pro-Tech Performance Automotive and the first thing reviewers mention is not the equipment. It is the owner. John is the name that comes up again and again: friendly, knowledgeable, easy to talk to, and honest in a business where honesty is hard to find.

The shop itself reflects that care. Customers describe a clean, orderly shop and office, appointments that get scheduled quickly, and cars treated as if they were John’s own.

Then there is the performance side. This is not a shop that dabbles in go-fast parts. Supercharger pulley upgrades, fuel injectors, dyno tuning: customers bring their Corvettes and their CTS-Vs here and drive home grinning.
