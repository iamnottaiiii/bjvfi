Our Story

A foothill company, built yard by yard.

Ambiente Landscape has worked the La Crescenta foothills for decades, holding California contractor license #730001 since 1996. We grew the slow way: one yard at a time, with most new work coming from neighbors who saw a finished project down the street.

Staying design-build-maintenance under one roof was a deliberate choice. The person who draws the plan knows what the soil, the slope, and the sun actually allow, because they are the same person who has to build it and keep it alive.
