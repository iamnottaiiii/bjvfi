The process

Simple, start to finish

Four steps. No mystery, no runaround.

1 · Walk it

We come look at the project in person, listen to what you want, and flag anything we see that you should know about.

2 · Scope it

You get a written scope with the work, the timeline, and the price. Questions welcome. Pressure tactics, never.

3 · Build it

We show up, keep the site clean, and keep you posted. If something changes, you hear it from us first.

4 · Walk it again

Final walkthrough together. Punch list, if any, gets finished. We are done when you say we are done.

About

The name says it all

Just In Construction, Inc. is a construction company in Quartz Hill, California, working across the Antelope Valley.

Just in time. Just right. Just honest. However you read the name, that is how we try to work.

We are based on 52nd St W in Quartz Hill, and most of our work is within a short drive: Lancaster, Palmdale, Littlerock, and the surrounding valley. Being local is not a marketing line for us. It is the reason our phone keeps ringing.

Construction has a trust problem, and we know it. So we keep things simple: we look at the job, we tell you what it will take, we put it in writing, and then we do exactly that. If we would not do it on our own homes, we do not recommend it on yours.

Big remodel or small repair, you get the same treatment: respect for your time, respect for your property, and a finished job you can be proud of.

Our standard

On time. In writing. Clean site. Final walkthrough. If every contractor did those four things, nobody would dread hiring one.
