About

An Oxford shop with deep roots.

Bob Paige Plumbing & Heating Nc works out of Old Church Road in Oxford, handling the two trades every Connecticut home depends on.

Small shop. Big winters.

Public business directories trace this shop back to 1970, making it one of the longer-running plumbing and heating names in the Oxford area. It is a small operation: a crew that knows the local housing stock, from older colonials to newer builds, and the heating and plumbing quirks that come with each.

The pitch is simple. One call covers pipes and heat. Honest assessments, fair pricing, and work that holds up. Customers who have used the shop describe a local plumber who shows up and shoots straight.
