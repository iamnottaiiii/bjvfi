Services

Siding services, start to finish.

Every job begins with a free quote and a walkaround of your home. Below is what a typical project with Kyle Construction LLC includes.

01

Siding installation

Full replacement of worn or outdated siding. We strip the old material, inspect and repair the sheathing underneath, then install new siding with proper housewrap and flashing.

  • Vinyl siding in a range of profiles and colors
  • Fiber cement siding for extra durability
  • Housewrap and flashing done to code
Sample photoPlaceholder: new vinyl siding installation.
02

Siding replacement

When patch repairs stop making sense, a full replacement resets your home's protection and curb appeal in one project. Most homes are completed in a matter of days.

  • Tear-off and disposal of old siding
  • Sheathing inspection and rot repair
  • Color and style consultation included
Sample photoPlaceholder: full exterior replacement, gable detail.
03

Siding repair

Targeted repairs for cracked, loose, or storm-damaged sections. We match panels to your existing siding so the repair blends in.

  • Panel replacement and re-securing
  • Caulking and sealant touch-ups
  • Storm and wind damage repairs
Sample photoPlaceholder: repairing a damaged siding section.
04

Trim, soffit, and fascia

New siding deserves clean edges. We replace rotted trim boards, vented soffits, and fascia so water stays out and the whole exterior looks finished.

  • PVC and wood trim replacement
  • Vented soffit panels
  • Fascia boards and drip edge
