Our Story

A North Hollywood fence and gate shop

Utopia Gates & Fences is a fence contractor based at 7331 Varna Ave in North Hollywood, California. The business is listed as a fence gate contractor, serving the San Fernando Valley and the greater Los Angeles area.

The name says the ambition plainly: the ideal version of the boundary around your home. A gate that closes with one hand. A fence line that runs straight from corner to corner. Work that looks as good in five years as it does on installation day.

Fences and gates are deceptively simple work. The difference between a fence that lasts and one that leans is in the parts nobody sees: post depth, concrete, drainage, and hardware sized for the weight it carries. That is where a dedicated fence contractor earns the call.
