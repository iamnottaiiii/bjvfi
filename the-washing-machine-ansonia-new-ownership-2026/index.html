Our story

An Ansonia original, with new energy.

The Washing Machine has been part of Division Street for years, and locals know it as one of the oldest family owned laundromats in Ansonia. In 2026, new ownership took over with one mission: keep everything neighbors loved, and make the rest better.

That means the big machines stay. The wash and fold service that regulars rave about stays, and gets even more attention. Around it, the new owners are refreshing the space, keeping it attended and welcoming, and making sure every visit feels easy from the moment you walk in.
