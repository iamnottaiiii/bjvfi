The experience

Small salon, personal service

Pro Touch Nail Salon is owned and run by Michelle and Terry, a local couple who treat every appointment like a visit with a friend. With only three chairs, the room stays peaceful and every client gets unhurried attention.

Guests rave about the neck and shoulder massage while nails dry, and the little details like candy and soothing music.

Quality that lasts

Reviewers say their nails still look brand new a week later, with no chips or lifting. The team takes its time and checks every nail before you leave.

Reasonable prices

Guests consistently call the pricing very reasonable for the quality, with spa pedicures and dip manicures among the favorites.

About

A family business in Severna Park

Pro Touch Nail Salon is a mom and pop salon run by Michelle and Terry, who have created a welcoming space where customers can relax and unwind while getting pampered. Near the library on McKinsey Road, it has served the community for years.

Small place, kind people, and nails done right the first time.

Why three chairs

The intimate size is the point. Fewer clients at once means a quiet room, no waiting on a crowd, and a technician who is never watching the clock.

Appointments recommended

Walk ins are welcome when chairs are open, but calling ahead guarantees your spot with your favorite technician.
