ABOUT

A Calabasas contractor built on straight talk.

Flawless Design Group Inc works out of Calabasas, California, at 22287 Mulholland Highway, and builds across the greater Los Angeles area, from the San Fernando Valley to the Westside and the coast. As a general contractor, the company handles both the design side and the construction side of residential projects, which means fewer handoffs and fewer surprises.

The services list tells the story: bathroom remodeling, full design build projects, accessibility construction and design, basement waterproofing, and even barn and outbuilding construction. It is a crew comfortable with both fine finishes and structural work.

Fewer handoffs. Fewer surprises.

How a project runs

  1. The call. You describe the project; we ask the questions that matter.
  2. The walkthrough. We see the space, take measurements, and talk budget honestly.
  3. The estimate. Itemized, in writing, with allowances spelled out.
  4. The build. Scheduled crews, tidy site, regular updates.
  5. The walkthrough. Punch list completed before final payment.
