About

Cheveux means hair.
We take that personally.

A boutique salon on South Main Street in the village of Pittsford, built around skilled stylists and unhurried appointments.

IThe name

French for hair, serious about craft

Cheveux chose its name as a promise: this is a salon that thinks about hair the way a tailor thinks about cloth. Cut, color, and texture are planned around your features, your routine, and how the style will look in six weeks, not just six hours.

"Talented stylists, a relaxing visit. I will be back."Paraphrased from verified guest reviews

IIThe village

On South Main, in the heart of Pittsford

Our salon sits at 7 S Main St, steps from the canal and the village shops. Guests also find us at our sister location, Cheveux Salon Deux, serving the Greece area. Two doors, one standard of care.

IIIThe standard

4.9 stars, earned one head at a time

Across 130+ verified reviews, guests describe great suggestions, careful listening, and results they love enough to return for. That is the review we work for at every single appointment.
