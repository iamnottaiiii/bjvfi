What we do

Full-service septic care, start to finish

From routine pumping to locating a buried tank, American Sewerage Septic Tanks handles the jobs most homeowners only think about when something goes wrong.

Septic tank pumping

Routine tank cleaning and pumping to keep sludge and solids from reaching your drain field. The single most important maintenance your system needs.

System inspections

Thorough inspections for home sales and peace of mind, including Massachusetts Title V style evaluations. We check tank, distribution box and field.

Tank locating

No record of where your tank is buried? We locate unmarked tanks and dig to expose them, so service day goes smoothly and your yard stays tidy.

Repairs

Baffles, covers, distribution boxes, broken lines. We fix the parts that fail most often, and tell you straight when a repair beats a replacement.

Backup response

Slow drains, gurgling pipes, sewage smells. Call at the first sign. Fast diagnosis can be the difference between a pump-out and a system failure.

Maintenance plans

Set-and-forget pumping schedules based on your tank size and household. We keep track so a forgotten tank never becomes an emergency.

Services

Septic services, done right

Residential septic work for Rochdale, Leicester, Oxford and nearby Worcester County towns. Every job explained in plain language, every price quoted before work begins.

Septic tank pumping and cleaning

Complete pump-out of your tank, including a check of baffles, sludge levels and inlet and outlet tees. Routine pumping is the cheapest insurance your system can get.

Title V style inspections

Selling or buying a home with a septic system? We perform thorough system inspections in the style of Massachusetts Title V evaluations, with a clear written rundown of what we find.

Tank locating and uncovering

Bought a house with no septic records? We probe, locate and dig to expose unmarked tanks, then can note the location so you never have to guess again.

Component repairs

Cracked covers, broken baffles, crushed distribution boxes, damaged lines. We repair the parts that fail, and we will tell you honestly when replacement is the smarter move.

Drain field assessment

Soggy spots, sewage odors, or lush green stripes over the field are warning signs. We assess what is going on and lay out your options in plain terms.

Real estate and lender work

Coordinating with buyers, sellers, agents and lenders on inspection timing and paperwork. One less headache in an already stressful process.

Not sure what your system needs?

Describe the symptoms by phone and we will point you in the right direction, even if the answer is "you do not need us yet."
