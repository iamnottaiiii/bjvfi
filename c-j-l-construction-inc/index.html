About Our Kitchen

The story behind the recipe card.

A family recipe for building

C J & L Construction, Inc is a general contracting company based at 11980 Runyan Drive in Cincinnati, Ohio. Directory listings trace the company back to 1989, which means decades of Cincinnati job sites, Midwest weather, and projects finished the honest way.

Like any treasured family recipe, the formula is simple: show up when you say you will, use the right materials, and treat every project like it has your own name on the mailbox. Around here, it literally does.
