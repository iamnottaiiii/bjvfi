Why it matters

The air you breathe passes through your ducts every day

Dust, pet dander, pollen, and lint build up inside ductwork over time. Every time your system kicks on, that buildup circulates through your home.

A professional duct and vent cleaning removes years of accumulation from supply and return ducts, registers, and dryer vent lines, helping your HVAC system breathe easier and your home feel fresher.

Dryer vent safety

A clogged dryer vent is a fire risk

Lint is extremely flammable, and it collects exactly where heat builds up: inside your dryer vent line. Long, clogged, or bird-nested vents force your dryer to work harder and run hotter.

Regular dryer vent cleaning is one of the simplest safety upgrades a homeowner can make. Most appointments take under an hour.
