About the shop

A Southside shop built on flow and detail

Tito's Flow is a walk-in barber shop at 1932 S 4th St in Allentown, PA, where the motto is simple: walk in welcome.

Clients describe experienced barbers, short waits, and cuts that hold their shape. The shop is clean and tidy, with a massage chair for customers, and reviewers regularly say they leave feeling like artists.

The crew serves Allentown's Southside in English and Spanish, and regulars keep coming back to the same chairs year after year.
