Why pool plumbing matters

Your pool is only as good as the plumbing behind it.

Leaks, low pressure, and failing equipment usually trace back to one place: the pipes, valves, and fittings that move water through your pool. Because we are licensed for both plumbing and swimming pool work, we diagnose the whole system instead of guessing at parts.

From our Arleta shop we serve homeowners across the area with straightforward repairs and clear communication. One call, one licensed contractor, and a system that runs the way it should.

Berger Pool Plumbing Inc has held California contractor license #752692 since 1998. We are based at 12817 Branford St in Arleta, and we built our reputation on one specialty: the plumbing that keeps pools running.
