About

A Cincinnati excavation company that keeps it simple.

JCM Excavating Inc is based at 2655 8 Mile Rd in Cincinnati, Ohio. The company is registered with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (USDOT 1693584, active status) and has operated under its authority since October 2007.

As a lean, intrastate operator, JCM stays focused on the work itself: moving earth precisely, keeping sites clean, and finishing on schedule. Customers deal directly with the people on the machines, which means straight answers and no runaround.

Public directory listings describe the company as a reliable excavating contractor known for precision, with specialties including site preparation, land clearing, and utility trenching for the Cincinnati area.
