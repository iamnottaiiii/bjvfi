About Us

A Central Florida general contractor with more than two decades of builds behind it, trusted from family homes to theme park landmarks.

The company

CEC General Contractors Inc is a general contracting firm based at 3456 North Bonnet Creek Rd in Lake Buena Vista, serving Orlando, Kissimmee and surrounding Central Florida communities.

With over 20 years in the industry, the company delivers residential and commercial construction: new home construction, additions and remodeling, plus light commercial build-outs. Owners and developers rely on CEC to coordinate every phase, from initial planning to final inspections.

The track record speaks loudly in public records: CEC General Contractors Inc appears as the listed contractor on Walt Disney World projects, including restaurant construction at Disney's Hollywood Studios and exterior rehab plus roof shingle replacement at Disney's Yacht Club Resort.

Licenses & credentials
