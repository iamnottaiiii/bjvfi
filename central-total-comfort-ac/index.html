Good to know

Signs your AC needs attention

Weak airflow

Some rooms never get cool, or the air coming out feels lukewarm. Often a quick fix if caught early.

Strange noises

Grinding, squealing, or banging from the unit means something mechanical needs a look.

Rising bills

Cooling costs climbing with no change in use? Efficiency drops are a classic warning sign.
