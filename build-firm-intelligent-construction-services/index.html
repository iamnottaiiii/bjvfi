About Build Firm

Intelligent construction, honestly delivered.

Build Firm, Intelligent Construction Services is a general contractor based at 2495 E Harrisburg Pike in Middletown, Pennsylvania. We handle interior remodeling, exterior construction, and commercial design/build for clients across the region.

We are registered with the Pennsylvania Attorney General as a home improvement contractor (HIC PA105033) and fully insured. Our mission guides every estimate, every schedule, and every finished detail: transform the ordinary into the extraordinary by delivering exactly what we promise, and a little bit more.

Incorporating high ethical standards, character, and integrity into daily work gives our clients comfort. The pathway to your finished product is paved by operating within your budget while maintaining quality of presence, craftsmanship, and innovative technology.
