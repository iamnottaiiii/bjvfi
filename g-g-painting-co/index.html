Services

Pick your project.
We handle the rest.

Every job starts with a free estimate and ends with a walkthrough. Switch tabs to see what each service covers.

Walls & ceilings

Full-room repaints and single-wall refreshes with crisp cut lines, careful masking, and even coverage.

Trim, molding & doors

Baseboards, crown, window casings and interior doors finished smooth with durable enamel.

Cabinet refinishing

Kitchen and bath cabinets degreased, sanded, primed and sprayed for a factory-smooth finish.

Drywall repair & patching

Holes, cracks and water stains repaired and skimmed so the paint goes on like the damage never happened.

Wallpaper removal

Steamed off, adhesive neutralized, and walls skimmed smooth before the first coat of paint.

Color consulting

Help choosing shades that suit your light, your trim, and the way you actually live in the rooms.

Full house painting

Siding, clapboard and shingles prepped with scraping, spot priming and caulking, then coated for the long haul.

Trim, shutters & doors

The details that make the house: fascia, soffits, shutters and entry doors in high-adhesion exterior enamel.

Deck staining & sealing

Decks, porches and railings cleaned, brightened and stained to stand up to New England weather.

Pressure washing

Pre-paint washing that strips mildew, chalk and grime so coatings bond the way they should.

Carpentry touch-ups

Rotted clapboard and trim boards swapped out before painting, so you are not painting over problems.

Masonry & foundation paint

Brick, block and foundation walls cleaned and coated with breathable masonry finishes.

Accent walls & finishes

Statement walls, two-tone schemes and clean geometric work for rooms that need a focal point.

Small commercial jobs

Offices, storefronts and rental turnovers painted on schedule with minimal disruption.

Move-in / move-out refresh

Fast, clean repaints between tenants or before a sale, with neutral colors that photograph well.

Seasonal scheduling

Exterior work timed for the right weather windows; interior work year round. Book early for spring.

Who we are

Led by George.
Backed by a crew that shows up.

G & G Painting Co is a painting contractor based in Salem, Massachusetts, working homes across Salem and the nearby North Shore. The crew is led by George, the name customers mention when they talk about the work.

The reputation we hear about most is simple: friendly people, careful prep, and follow-through. When clapboard had to be repaired mid-job, the crew came back and finished the exterior right. That is the standard every project gets.

Consumer checkbook ratings show every surveyed customer recommending the work, and reviewers call out the care taken on tall, older homes, the kind Salem has plenty of.
