Services

Care for every stage of life.

Choose a category to see what the practice offers. Call the office for details, pricing, and to confirm what is right for you.

Preventive care

Regular checkups that catch small issues before they become big ones, for patients of all ages.

  • Routine exams and cleanings
  • Digital x rays as needed
  • Oral health screenings
  • Kids and family checkups
  • Gum health monitoring
  • Fluoride guidance
The best dental work is the kind you never need. Most patients do well with a checkup and cleaning twice a year.

Restorative care

Repair and rebuild damaged teeth with natural looking, long lasting results.

  • Tooth colored fillings
  • Crowns
  • Bridges
  • Dentures and partials
  • Root canal therapy
  • Extractions when needed
Every restorative plan is explained in plain language before any work begins, including options and costs.

Cosmetic dentistry

Refresh the look of your smile with conservative, natural results.

  • Professional whitening
  • Veneers consultation
  • Cosmetic bonding
  • Smile makeover planning
Cosmetic consultations start with your goals. Call to talk through what is possible for your smile.

Urgent care

Toothache, chipped tooth, or lost filling? Call the office and the team will get you seen as soon as possible.

  • Severe tooth pain
  • Chipped or broken teeth
  • Lost crowns or fillings
  • Dental injuries
