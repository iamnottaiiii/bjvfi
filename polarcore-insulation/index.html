Why it matters

Pasadena homes lose the most energy through the attic.

Many homes in the 91103 area were built before modern insulation standards. Thin or settled insulation means your heater and air conditioner work harder than they should, and some rooms never feel quite right.

A proper insulation project can even out temperatures room to room, cut down on drafts, and help lower monthly energy costs. PolarCore Insulation focuses on exactly this kind of work for Pasadena homeowners.

Signs your insulation may need attention

  • Some rooms stay hot or cold no matter the thermostat
  • Energy bills keep climbing with no change in usage
  • Drafts near outlets, vents, or attic access points
  • Attic insulation looks flat, thin, or uneven
  • Old insulation damaged by moisture or pests

Services

Insulation work, done for Pasadena homes

Below are the kinds of projects insulation contractors in Pasadena commonly take on. Every home is different, so call PolarCore Insulation at (626) 653-4597 to confirm the right scope for your project.

01

Attic Insulation Installation

Adding or upgrading insulation in the attic, where most residential heat loss happens. Options generally include blown-in, batt, and blanket materials matched to the space.

02

Attic Insulation Removal

Removing old insulation that has settled, been damaged by moisture, or been contaminated, so clean new material can be installed on a sound surface.

03

Wall Insulation

Improving insulation inside exterior walls to reduce heat transfer and even out temperatures between rooms.

04

Crawl Space Insulation

Insulating crawl spaces and floor systems to keep ground-level rooms warmer and reduce moisture-driven energy loss.

05

Air Sealing

Sealing gaps around penetrations, top plates, and attic access points so insulation can do its job without air leaking past it.

06

Insulation Inspection and Advice

Having a look at what is up there now and explaining, in plain language, what would actually help and what would not.
