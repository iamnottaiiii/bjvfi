The plant

Valley Asphalt Corporation, Plant 19.

Plant 19 is Valley Asphalt Corporation's hot mix asphalt facility on Mehring Way in Cincinnati, Ohio. The site sits in the Queensgate area near the Ohio River, a working industrial corridor that also feeds the Brent Spence Bridge corridor projects. In 1997 the Mehring Way facility was named one of five winners of the National Asphalt Pavement Association's Ecological Awards, recognizing hot mix plants that operate cleanly and fit in with their neighbors.

How a mixing plant works

From aggregate to hot mix.

An asphalt mixing plant heats and dries aggregate, blends it with liquid asphalt cement, and loads the finished hot mix into trucks for paving jobs. Crews use it for road resurfacing, parking lots, driveways, and patching, and the mix has to be placed while it is still hot, so timing a pickup with the plant matters.

For load sizes, available mixes, and the day's production schedule, the plant line is the fastest answer: (513) 784-1476.
