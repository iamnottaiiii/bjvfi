Why Call Us

Gate trouble has a short list of causes.

We have seen all of them. That is why most repairs are diagnosed over the phone in minutes and finished in one visit.

Openers That Quit

The most common call. Dead motors, stripped gears, bad boards, and sensors knocked out of alignment. We repair or replace most major opener brands.

Gates That Drag or Stick

Bent tracks, worn rollers, and sagging hinges put strain on the whole system. We realign and rebuild before it burns out the opener.

Open Daily

6:00 AM to 9:00 PM, seven days a week. A stuck gate is a security problem, so we keep long hours.
