The problem

Water finds every crack. We find them first.

New England freeze and thaw cycles punish foundations. A hairline crack today becomes a flooded basement in April.

We inspect how water moves around and through your foundation, then build a system that handles it: interior perimeter drainage, sealed sump basins, crack injection, and exterior membranes where they matter most.

Stop mopping. Start solving. Most basement water problems trace to two or three fixable entry points.
Sample photo

The approach

Inside, outside, or both. Never guesswork.

Every home is different, so every estimate starts with a walk-through, not a sales pitch.

Interior systems manage water that is already coming in. Exterior waterproofing stops it at the wall. We will tell you which one your home actually needs, and when a simple grading or gutter fix will do the job instead.
