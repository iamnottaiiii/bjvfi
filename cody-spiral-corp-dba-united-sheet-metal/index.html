Services

Sheet metal services

Public listings describe United Sheet Metal as a sheet metal contractor offering flashings, gutters and downspouts, and roofing materials. Select a tab for details, then call to confirm your project is a fit.

Sheet Metal Flashings

Fabricated metal flashings for roofs, walls, and penetrations, formed to the dimensions your detail calls for.

  • Counterflashing and wall flashings
  • Drip edge and metal trim
  • Flashing for repairs, reroofs, and new construction
  • Call to confirm gauges, finishes, and turnaround

Gutters and Downspouts

Metal gutter systems fabricated and installed to move water away from the structure cleanly.

  • Seamless and sectional style metal gutters
  • Downspouts sized to the roof area
  • Replacement sections and repairs
  • Call for a site look and estimate

Roofing Materials

Sheet metal components for roofing assemblies: panels, copings, and accessory metals.

  • Copings, caps, and edge metals
  • Metal roof accessories and trims
  • Coordination with roofing contractors
  • Call to confirm materials in stock

Custom Fabrication

One-off and small-run fabrication for contractors, roofers, and builders who need it cut right the first time.

  • Contractor pickups at the Glendale shop
  • Patterns and templates accepted
  • Jobs sized from single repairs to full runs
  • Call (818) 241-8831 to discuss your drawings
