Our story

A neighborhood salon
with a big heart

Young Nails & Spa has become a go-to on Jefferson Road by doing the simple things right: clean stations, careful work, and genuine kindness.

Who we are

Dedicated, friendly, and obsessed with the details

Guests describe the team as dedicated and friendly, the kind of staff who stay late so a last-minute guest still leaves happy, and who add little hand-drawn touches just because.

The salon itself is big and bright, with roughly ten pedicure chairs and a huge polish wall, so there is almost always room for you, even on busy weekends.

Whether it is your first set or your fiftieth, you get the same care: careful cuticle work, precise shaping, and color that lasts.
