Why itty bitty

Small pieces, made to be loved forever.

Not every tattoo has to be a sleeve. Itty Bitty Ink specializes in the kind of work that fits in the palm of your hand: fine line florals, tiny symbols, script, and minimalist designs. As a tattoo and piercing shop, they keep things intimate, careful, and unhurried.

Services & styles

The itty bitty menu.

Every piece is custom to you. These are the specialties the shop is known for. Final pricing is always set in person, based on size, detail, and placement.

01

Fine Line Tattoos

Hair-thin lines, soft shading, and elegant restraint. The shop's signature: tiny florals, delicate script, and whisper-light details.

02

Tiny & Minimalist Tattoos

First tattoo? Start small. Symbols, initials, constellation dots, and micro designs that fit behind an ear or on a wrist.

03

Custom Ink

Bring your reference or your idea. Consult with the artist to refine the design, the size, and the perfect placement.

04

Body Piercing

Itty Bitty Ink is listed as a tattoo and piercing shop. Call to confirm piercing availability and aftercare guidance.

05

Touch-Ups & Aftercare Advice

Fresh ink needs good care. Ask the shop about healing, moisturizers, and when a touch-up makes sense.
