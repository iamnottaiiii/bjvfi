About

The neighborhood shop
on Clinton Ave

L & S Auto & Tire Services: tires, repair, parts, and fuel, from a crew that knows Irvington drivers by name.

What we are about

We are a tire dealer and auto repair shop at 1029 Clinton Ave in Irvington, New Jersey. Drivers come to us for tires, brakes, oil changes, and the everyday repairs that keep a car dependable.

Being a neighborhood shop means something to us: explain the problem in plain words, quote the job honestly, and do the work like it was our own car. That is how regulars are made, and that is how we have kept ours.

Why drivers pick us

Tires and repair under one roof, plus a fuel pump out front. One stop, one call, one bill, and a crew that would rather earn your next visit than oversell this one.
