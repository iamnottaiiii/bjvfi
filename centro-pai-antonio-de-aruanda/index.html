Three simple things guide everything that happens at our center.

Fé

Gatherings centered on prayer, gratitude, and light, kept in the Umbanda tradition.

Caridade

Comunidade

New to Umbanda?

You do not need to know anything to walk in

Umbanda is a Brazilian spiritual tradition built on faith, charity, and respect for all people. If you are curious, just come by. Our community is happy to answer questions, share a prayer, and help you feel at home - exactly as you are.

Life at the Center

Gatherings & Community

Prayer, spiritual works, and charity fill our week. Times can vary from week to week, so the kindest thing to do is call ahead or stop by and ask what is coming up.

I

Faith Gatherings

Umbanda gatherings with prayer, song, and spiritual works of charity. Whether it is your first time or your five hundredth, you are welcome at the table.

II

Charity in Action

Caridade guides what we do. From acts of service to supporting neighbors in need, the work of the center reaches beyond our doors.

III

Everyone Welcome

You do not need to be a member or speak Portuguese to feel at home here. Come as you are and our community will greet you warmly.
