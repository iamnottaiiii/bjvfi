LA NOSTRA STORIA

Set in stone, as a family trade should be

NICOLA FAMILY BARBERING

On Glenway Avenue in Cincinnati stands a barber shop run the old way: by family, for neighbors. Nicola Family Barbering is an independent, family-owned barberia where the craft is passed down rather than franchised.

Directory records list the shop as a single-location, family-run business with a small team. Public reviews praise the shop as an alternative to the corporate chop shop, noting decades of combined cutting experience behind the chairs.

Men and women, young and old, all take the same chair here. That is the whole philosophy: honest barbering, done well, for the people of the west side.
