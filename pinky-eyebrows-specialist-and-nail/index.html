Our story

The 86th Street regulars

A one stop beauty chair

Pinky Eyebrows Specialist And Nail grew up around a simple combination that neighborhood clients love: expert eyebrow shaping plus full nail care under one roof. The salon specializes in eyebrow waxing and threading, manicures and pedicures, gel and acrylic nails, nail art, skin care, body scrubs, and eyelash services.

Cleanliness as a habit

Clients travel from far neighborhoods because the salon uses new files and scrubs for every client and keeps the space constantly cleaned and wiped down. It is the kind of detail regulars notice and mention again and again.

Loyal for a reason

Many reviewers have been clients for 10 to 15 years. They describe Pinky as a master of brow shaping, nail technicians who nail the coffin shape and recreate photo references exactly, and fair prices that keep them coming back.
