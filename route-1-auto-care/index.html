Our story

Route 1 Auto Care is an independent repair shop on Richmond Highway in Alexandria, the kind of place where the mechanic knows your car by heart.

Who we are

Small crew, big standards

Route 1 Auto Care sits right on Richmond Hwy (Route 1), a few minutes south of Old Town Alexandria. It is a small shop, and that is the point: fewer cars in the bays means your car gets real attention.

Ray, the mechanic customers mention by name, has built the shop's reputation one honest diagnosis at a time. People drive in from Fairfax and Southern Maryland just to have him look at their cars, and some have trusted him with their vehicles for eight years or more.

The philosophy is simple: tell the customer what is actually wrong, fix it for a fair price, and call back to make sure everything is right.
