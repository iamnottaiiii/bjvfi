The Neighborhood

A tight knit community with room to breathe

Del Mar Club sits in Dacula, in the heart of Gwinnett County, one of the signature communities built by Crown Communities. For nearly two decades the builder has put its name behind neighborhoods known for thoughtful layouts and craftsmanship, and Del Mar Club carries that same character block to block.

Homes here typically pair split bedroom floor plans with sitting rooms, stone countertops, walk in pantries, kitchen islands, and vaulted master suites. Two car attached garages, level fenced lots, and screened porches built for grilling season round out the classic suburban picture.

Tight knit streets, an active homeowners association, and a full slate of community amenities.

Residents have access to a community swimming pool, tennis courts, a clubhouse, a playground, and a residents-only park. Sidewalks and street lights run through the neighborhood, and schools and shopping sit within easy reach.

Amenities

One membership, a full calendar of living

Community dues cover maintenance of the swim and tennis facilities and on-site security, with access to every amenity below.

Swimming Pool

The community pool is the centerpiece of summer in Del Mar Club. Lap lanes, open swim, and plenty of deck space make it the natural gathering spot from Memorial Day through September.

Call (678) 973-1923 for current season hours.

Tennis Courts

Well kept courts give players of every level a place to practice, play pickup games, or join friendly round robins through the warm months.

Clubhouse

The community clubhouse hosts homeowners association meetings, neighborhood events, and private resident gatherings, making it the social anchor of Del Mar Club.

Park and Playground

A residents-only park with a playground and open green space gives kids a safe place to play and families a quiet place to unwind without leaving the neighborhood.

Streets and Safety

Sidewalks and street lights line the neighborhood, and community dues cover on-site security, so evening walks and early morning runs feel comfortable year round.

The Community

Del Mar Club, by the details

Del Mar Club is one of the Gwinnett County communities built by Crown Communities, a builder that spent nearly two decades earning a place among the region's home building elite by insisting homes be built the right way from the ground up.

That philosophy shows in the details residents live with every day: arched doorways, coffered ceilings, covered front porches, and great rooms with masonry fireplaces. Outside, level fenced lots, large screened porches, and extended patios make the most of Georgia evenings.

An active homeowners association keeps the shared spaces sharp and the calendar full.
