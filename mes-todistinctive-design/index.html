Our story

Distinctive by name, distinctive by nature

Mes Todistinctive Design is a landscaping and design business based in Walton, Kentucky, with a simple belief: no two yards should look alike.

How we work

Design first, dirt second

We start by walking your property with you, listening to how you live outdoors, and noting what the land is already telling us: where the sun falls, where water collects, which views matter.

Only then do we pick plants and materials. The result is a landscape that fits your home and your habits, not a catalog page dropped onto your lot.

Listed locally as a landscaper, gardener, and interior designer, we bring an eye for design to everything from a front bed refresh to a full outdoor living plan.

Planted to last, not just to photograph
