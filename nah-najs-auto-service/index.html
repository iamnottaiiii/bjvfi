Our story

Small shop, deep specialty

Many shops list transmission on a long menu. At Nah Najs, it is the headline: the shop describes itself as providing a wide range of service from bumper to bumper, specializing in transmission.

That focus matters. Transmission work is complex, expensive and hard to redo, so it belongs in the hands of a shop that does it every day.

Around that specialty, the shop covers the everyday essentials: oil changes, tune-ups, engine diagnostics and brake work, all at competitive, moderate prices.
