About the Company

PRIME ROOFING and WATERPROOFING INC. is a Brooklyn based roofing contractor incorporated in New York in 2022. The company focuses on residential and commercial roofing plus full waterproofing services across New York City.

Being both a roofer and a waterproofing contractor matters: the same crew that installs your roof seals the parapets, flashings, and penetrations that cause most leaks, so nothing falls between two trades.
