Our story

A Brooklyn masonry crew for 21 years

Prestige Construction has spent more than two decades working on the homes that define Brooklyn: brownstones, brick row houses, and the apartments inside them. The specialties we built our name on, brownstone stoop and facade restoration, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, and full interior and exterior renovation, are still the work we do every week.

We keep our operation straightforward. You talk to the people doing the work, you get a clear scope and price before we start, and we stand behind the result. Most of our jobs come from repeat clients and neighbor referrals, which is exactly how we like it.
