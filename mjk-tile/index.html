Who We Are

Tile is all we do, and we do it carefully

MJK Tile is a tile contractor based in Van Nuys, California. We focus on one trade, tile installation, so every project gets a specialist's eye: layout planned around your fixtures, cuts that land where they should, and grout lines you can run a finger across.

Homeowners call us for bathrooms, kitchens, and floors, and they call us back because the work holds up. We show up when we say we will, keep the job site tidy, and walk every finished project with you before we call it done.

If you are comparing tile contractors in the San Fernando Valley, give us a call. We will give you an honest read on your project, even if the honest answer is that you do not need us yet.
