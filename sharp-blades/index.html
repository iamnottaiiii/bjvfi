Our story

About SHARP BLADES

SHARP BLADES on East Washington Boulevard is known for friendly customer service and a shop that welcomes kids as well as regulars. Reviewers also note the shop stocks serious barber supplies, a sign the team truly lives the craft.

Every visit follows the same simple rhythm: a real consultation about what you want, careful work in the chair, and a finish you can feel good about. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
