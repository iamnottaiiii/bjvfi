Our story

A Baltimore painting company
built on prep work

ADL Painting LLC is a Baltimore-based painting company serving homes and businesses with interior and exterior work.

The finish is only as good as the prep.

Anyone can roll paint on a wall. ADL Painting LLC starts earlier: cleaning, sanding, caulking, and repairing surfaces so the topcoat bonds, covers evenly, and lasts.

That prep-first approach runs through every project, from a single bedroom to a full commercial exterior. Pair it with a custom color consultation, and you get a result that looks intentional from every angle.

  • Interior and exterior painting
  • Residential and commercial projects
  • Free estimates on every project
