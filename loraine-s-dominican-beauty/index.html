Our story

Loraine's Dominican Beauty is a local family-owned business on Lake Avenue with a straightforward system refined over years: wash and set, dryer, blowout, press, and style. Reviewers describe a no-frills shop where the craft does the talking, transforming even the most kinky and curly hair into a silky, straight style. Many clients have come for years and tip generously for the results.
