About

The story

Threading is kinder than waxing: nothing touches the skin but twisted cotton thread, pulling hair from the root with precision no strip can match. Star Threading has made it their specialty.

The salon sits right on Hylan Blvd with hours every day of the week, so a brow refresh fits between errands, no appointment needed.

Brows frame everything. A ten-minute threading can lift your whole face, and the results last two to five weeks.
