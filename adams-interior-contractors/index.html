Our Story

A Florida contractor with deep local roots.

Adams Interior Contractors, Inc. has operated out of Orange Park, Florida for more than 25 years, building homes, offices, and storefronts across the Jacksonville area.

1999

Founded in Orange Park

Adams Interior Contractors, Inc. is incorporated in Florida and sets up at 2018 Smith Street in Orange Park, where the company still operates today.

2000s

Commercial work across Jacksonville

The company takes on commercial contracting, remodeling, and tenant improvement projects across the greater Jacksonville market, building a repeat client base of property owners and businesses.

2010s

Residential remodels join the roster

Kitchen and bathroom remodeling, interior renovations, and finish carpentry become a core part of the business for homeowners throughout Clay County and the First Coast.
