About

Why "Bernoulli Principle"?

Because heating and cooling is physics you live inside. The name is a promise to respect the science.

The principle, in one sentence

In a moving fluid, faster flow means lower pressure. It is why airplane wings lift, why perfume atomizers spray, and why a poorly designed duct run starves the far bedroom of air while the hallway vent roars.

Most comfort problems are airflow problems. We start there.

Temple

Of what, exactly?

A temple is where you go to get something right. Ours is devoted to the unglamorous craft of moving air well: sealed ducts, balanced rooms, equipment sized to the load.
