Why we started

Too many homeowners told us the same story: a roofer who would not return calls, a quote that doubled mid-job, a leak that came back with the next rain. PrimePeak Roofing Co. was built to be the opposite of that story. Based at 7812 Geyser Ave in Reseda, we work the neighborhoods we live in.

How we work

Every job starts on the roof with a camera, not a clipboard. You see what we see. The scope is written down, the materials are named, and the crew lead stays on site until the magnetic nail sweep is done. If weather delays us, you hear it from us first.
