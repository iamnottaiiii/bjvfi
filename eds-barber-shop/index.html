About

Three decades on Western Avenue.

Ed's Barber Shop has been part of Gardena for around 30 years, and it shows in the best way. The shop is small and unpretentious, the kind of old-style barbershop where the craft comes first.

Two experienced barbers, each with more than ten years in the trade, handle classic cuts, modern fades, tapers and beard trims. Appointments and walk-ins are both welcome, though regulars recommend calling ahead since the chairs stay busy.

"He's been cutting my hair for 20 years. I trust him. Good guy, nice old style barber shop."

Paraphrased from public review themes.

The pace is deliberate. Ed takes his time with each client, which means you may wait a little, and it also means you leave with a cut done right. Mornings are the most relaxed time to visit.

One practical note: the shop operates cash only, so plan accordingly before you come by.
