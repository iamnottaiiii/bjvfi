Styles and Services

Every style below is work the shop is known for, drawn from what clients and directories report. Styles range from delicate linework to bold color pieces. Call ahead to match your idea with the right artist.

Fine Line

Delicate, precise linework for minimalist designs, script, and small detailed pieces. One of the shop's most requested specialties.

Color Tattoos

Bold, saturated color work that stays vivid, from expressive neo-traditional pieces to bright custom designs.

Black and Grey

Smooth shading and clean gradients for portraits, realism, and timeless monochrome work.

Portraits

Memorial and likeness pieces built on careful consultation so the likeness carries the meaning you need.

Traditional and Old School

Bold lines, classic motifs, and the timeless American traditional look that never goes out of style.

New School and Tribal

Exaggerated color and movement for new school fans, plus clean tribal designs done with precision.

Tattoo Cover-Ups

Strategic rework that turns old or unwanted tattoos into something you will actually love wearing.

Body Piercing

Nose, labret, belly, and ear piercings with quality jewelry, sterile technique, and real aftercare support.

Walk-Ins and Flash

Walls of flash to pick from plus custom work by appointment. Walk-ins welcome when artists are available.
