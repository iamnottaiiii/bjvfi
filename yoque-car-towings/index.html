Our story

Rockville's Used Tire Specialist

Yoque Car Towings is a used tire shop on Shady Grove Road in Rockville, Maryland. The idea is simple: a tire does not have to be brand new to be safe, dependable, and a great value.

Every used tire in stock is inspected before sale, with tread depth and sidewall condition checked. If a tire does not meet the standard, it does not go on a customer's car. That is the whole business model: good rubber, honest grading, fair prices.

Drivers across Rockville and Montgomery County stop in for single replacements, full sets, mounting, balancing, and quick flat repairs, usually with same-day turnaround when stock allows.
