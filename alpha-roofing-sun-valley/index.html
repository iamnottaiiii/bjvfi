Our Story

A Sun Valley crew for Sun Valley roofs.

Alpha Roofing Sun Valley is a roofing contractor based at 11332 Saticoy St in the heart of Sun Valley. We are the crew your neighbors see on ladders around the block, not a call center three counties away.

We got into roofing because a roof done right is one of the few home projects where craftsmanship is literally visible from the street.

Our approach is simple. We answer our own phone at (818) 201-2241. We show up when we say we will. We use materials rated for real Valley heat. And we clean up like the job site was our own driveway, because around here, it usually belongs to someone we will see again.

Whether it is a full replacement on a ranch house off Saticoy or a quick flashing repair before the rainy season, every job gets the same care: inspected honestly, priced in writing, and finished on schedule.
