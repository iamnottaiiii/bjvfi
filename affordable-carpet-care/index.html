Our story

Small, local, and proud of it. Affordable Carpet Care is the kind of business that earns customers for life, one living room at a time.

A one-person standard of clean

Affordable Carpet Care is based at 37 Batt Ln in East Haven, Connecticut, and it is run the old-fashioned way: by the person whose name is on the work. Customers know their technician as Mike, and reviews describe him as meticulous, professional, and respectful of every home he steps into.

The approach is simple. Arrive on time. Work quietly. Treat spots, stains, and odors until they are gone, not until the clock runs out. Put the furniture back. Leave the carpet looking like it has new life again, which is exactly what long-time customers say after every visit.

Loyalty like that does not come from ads. It comes from fifteen years of showing up and doing the job right. That is the whole business model, and it has worked from day one.
