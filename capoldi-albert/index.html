A Springfield family business since 1970

Capoldi Albert, listed in local directories as Al Capoldi's Carpentry and Painting and Al Capoldi and Son Painting, is a small family owned business at 1152 Providence Road in Springfield, PA. According to directory listings, the business has been serving Springfield and the surrounding Delaware County area since 1970.

The approach is simple: prep the surface right, use good paint, and treat the home like it matters. Estimates are free, and cash or checks are accepted.
