Projects in the Greater Cincinnati Region

Publicly documented developments from 360 Property Partners. Project details below come from industry reports and regional news coverage.

Echelon

Eastgate, Cincinnati, OH

A 187-unit luxury multifamily community developed by 360 Property Partners in the Summerside submarket of Eastgate.

  • 187 luxury apartment homes
  • Four-story midrise design
  • 4,200 SF interior amenity space
  • Outdoor courtyard and private porches

Source: 2017 Cincinnati multifamily market overview (CBRE/GCNKAA).

Braxton Barrel House

Fort Mitchell, KY

An adaptive reuse redevelopment of the former Remke Market building at 2501 Dixie Highway into Braxton Brewing Co.'s barrel-aging facility and taproom, opened September 27, 2019.

  • Former grocery store transformed
  • 20,000 SF subterranean rickhouse
  • 1,500 to 2,000 SF patio and taproom
  • Redevelopment by 360 Property Partners

Source: Cincinnati Business Courier, September 2019.
