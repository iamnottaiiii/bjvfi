Flooring options

Pick your surface. We handle the rest.

Every install includes careful measuring, subfloor prep, clean transitions, and a final walkthrough. Call for hours and scheduling.

Luxury Vinyl Plank

Waterproof, scratch-resistant, and comfortable underfoot. The go-to for kitchens, basements, rentals, and homes with kids or pets.

  • Floating click-lock and glue-down installs
  • Moisture barrier and underlayment
  • Stairs, transitions, and trim included

Hardwood

Solid and engineered hardwood installed to last generations: acclimated, nailed or glued right, and finished with tight seams.

  • Solid and engineered hardwood
  • Proper acclimation and moisture testing
  • Sanding-line-free, even plank layout

Laminate

The budget-smart way to get a wood look that stands up to daily life: fast installs, easy care, and a clean finish.

  • Click-lock floating installs
  • Underlayment for sound and comfort
  • Great for bedrooms and living areas

Tile and Stone

Ceramic, porcelain, and stone tile for kitchens, baths, and entries: level beds, straight lines, and grout that stays clean.

  • Ceramic, porcelain, and natural stone
  • Waterproofing for wet areas
  • Clean grout lines and sealed finish

Carpet

Soft, quiet, and warm: professional carpet installation with proper stretch, sharp seams, and tidy edges.

  • Bedrooms, living rooms, and stairs
  • Quality pad for comfort and life
  • Power-stretched, no ripples

Removal and Prep

Great floors start underneath. We remove old flooring, level and repair subfloors, and set the stage for a flawless install.

  • Old flooring and adhesive removal
  • Subfloor leveling and repair
  • Moisture checks before install day
