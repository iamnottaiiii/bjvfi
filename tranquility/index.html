About

A quiet corner
of Skaneateles

Tranquility Salon offers clients a peaceful retreat where exceptional hair and service is our focus. Tucked into a historic building on East Genesee Street, the salon pairs expert hair care with lake views and a genuinely relaxing atmosphere.

Guests describe long, unhurried appointments, a gentle touch and results they love, from soft natural blondes to complete multi-hour makeovers. It is the kind of place where the conversation flows as easily as the coffee, and where regulars bring friends.

The salon holds a New York State appearance enhancement business license and welcomes new clients. Saturday appointments make it easy to fit self-care into a busy week.

Calm by design

A light-filled, peaceful space above the village shops, made for unwinding.

Licensed and experienced

A New York State licensed salon with years of experience in cut and color.

Saturdays open

Weekend hours for busy schedules, just call ahead to reserve.
