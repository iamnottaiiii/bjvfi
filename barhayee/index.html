About Barhayee

Barhayee is a Windsor, Ontario woodworking studio. Its public shop describes handcrafted wooden gifts and home pieces made by a third-generation woodworker who traded an IT career for the craft. The studio sells artisan wood creations for home and gifting, including an online Etsy shop opened in 2024.
