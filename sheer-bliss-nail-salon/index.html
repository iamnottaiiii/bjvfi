About

A Kings Highway favorite

Sheer Bliss Nail Salon sits at 523 Kings Hwy in the Gravesend section of Brooklyn. The salon describes itself as high end, hygienic and customer friendly, and treats clients like family.

Reviewers back that up. Locals praise the cleanliness of the salon, the skill of the technicians and the relaxing massages that come with spa services. Many clients come in for regular pedicures and stay for the calm, welcoming atmosphere.

Whether you need a quick polish change or a full set of acrylics with hand painted art, the team takes the time to get the details right.
