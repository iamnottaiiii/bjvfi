How a replacement day goes

The typical full replacement described in reviews follows the same four beats. Timelines vary by roof size and weather, so treat this as the shape of the day, not a promise.

1

Tear-off down to the deck. Two layers come off if that is what your roof carries.

2

Deck inspection. Damaged boards are replaced before anything new goes down.

3

New shingles installed while the crew lead monitors quality on site.

4

Full cleanup of nails, shingles, and debris so the yard looks like a job never happened.
