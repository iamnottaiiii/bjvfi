Our story

A family name on every job

Owned and operated by Patrick and Kerry Hourican, this is a small local contractor where the owner is on the job, not behind a desk.

Owner Pauric Hourican pays close attention to every customer and their project

With over 10 years of experience in the field, Pauric built Hourican Plumbing & Heating around a simple idea: top quality workmanship, materials, and equipment, plus the kind of attention only a small family business gives. From small plumbing services to additions, remodels, and new construction, the team handles residential and commercial work across Arlington and nearby towns.

The review record backs it up. Customers describe quotes that arrive fast, crews that leave basements tidy, and techie questions answered with patience. One neighbor recommendation led to a water heater replacement on a holiday weekend; another led to a full heating system install where "every member of his team were true professionals."
