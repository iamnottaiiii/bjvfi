Our Story

The Pico's Finest Barbershop story

Pico's Finest Barbershop is a Black-owned shop on Pico Boulevard where every fade is treated like the main event. Owner T-Baby is praised by reviewers for blend work that never needs painted-on hairlines and for treating every client like royalty.

The shop works with all hair types and all ages. Call ahead at (323) 332-8112 and you will never wait; walk in to 5764 W Pico Blvd and see why reviewers call it the best on this side of Pico and Fairfax.
