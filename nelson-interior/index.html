About

Built on the details

Nelson Interior, LLC is a Cincinnati based general contractor led by owner Jermall Nelson. The company serves both residential and commercial clients across the Cincinnati area, from single room refreshes to full apartment turnovers.

The business is known for its move in ready apartment remodels, a specialty that property managers rely on between tenants, alongside kitchen and bath renovations, cabinet installation, tile, painting, and handyman work.

In business since 2019 and rated A+ by the Better Business Bureau.
