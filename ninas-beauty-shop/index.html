About the salon

A neighborhood institution
on 49th Street

Nina's Beauty Shop is the kind of salon where customers become regulars, and regulars become family.

Some salons do hair. Nina's takes care of people. Based on 39 Birdeye reviews, guests describe a stylist with skilled, patient hands, honest advice, and a warm way with kids and longtime customers alike.

One reviewer writes that Nina treats all her customers like family, and that she has been coming since she was ten, now married with kids of her own. Another calls her work "hands of gold," praising her patience and honesty above all.

The salon keeps weekday hours, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, in Borough Park, Brooklyn. Walk-ins are welcome when the chair is free; calling ahead is the surest way to be seen on time.
