Our Story

Metal shaping is our whole business.

Empire Manufacturing Co is a sheet metal contractor operating from 11003 Penrose St in Sun Valley, California, in the heart of one of the Valley's busiest industrial corridors.

Trade directories list the company among Sun Valley's sheet metal and metal fabrication shops, serving general contractors, remodelers, and commercial customers who need real parts made to real dimensions.
