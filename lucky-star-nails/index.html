Our Story

A neighborhood salon built on skill, kindness and fair prices.

⭐

A fresh start in Chili Center

Lucky Star Nails opened its doors in Chili Center with one simple idea: give every guest a luxurious pampering experience without luxury prices. Today the salon is known across Rochester's west side for its friendly team and consistently beautiful results.

👩‍🎨

Techs who treat you like family

Guests mention Lily and Ann by name, praising their speed, precision and eye for the latest trends. Many visitors arrive every two weeks and leave with playful new designs they designed together with their tech.

🏡

A salon that feels like home

The interior was freshly painted and reorganized so every color is easy to browse. Free lot parking, wheelchair access and a calm, friendly atmosphere make every visit easy from the moment you walk in.
