About

Built on service, quality work and word of mouth

JMK Landscape PA is a New Cumberland landscaping company serving homes and businesses across Dauphin, Cumberland, Perry and York counties.

Our story

From a mowing company to a full landscape team

JMK Landscape PA started as a small mowing company and grew into a full-service landscape business providing residential and commercial landscape design, installation and maintenance services.

The growth came the honest way: unmatched service, quality work and word of mouth, with attention to detail from the first design conversation all the way through the completed project.

JMK is affiliated with the Pennsylvania Landscape and Nursery Association (PLNA), the state trade group for landscape professionals.
