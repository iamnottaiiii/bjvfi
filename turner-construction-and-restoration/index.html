How we work

From first walkthrough to final walkthrough

Restoration is detective work as much as construction. Here is the path every project follows with us.

Step 1

Walk the property

We come out, look at what is standing, what is failing, and what matters most to you. You get an honest read, not a sales pitch.

Step 2

Plan the repair

Scope, materials, and schedule in writing. For restorations, we document what can be saved and what must be replaced.

Step 3

Do the work

Installation, repair, and maintenance handled by one accountable crew. Clean site, clear communication, every day.

Step 4

Stand behind it

Final walkthrough together. If something is not right, we come back and make it right. That is what restoration means to us.

Our story

A Butler County crew that restores, not just replaces

Turner Construction & Restoration is based on KY-154 in Butler, Kentucky, serving Pendleton County and the surrounding river counties.

Where we work

Rooted on KY-154

Our shop sits at 3996 KY-154 in Butler, which means short drive times, local suppliers, and crews who know Pendleton County homes, from farmhouses to newer builds.

What we believe

Save what can be saved

Anyone can tear out and start over. Restoration takes judgment: knowing which beam is still good, which wall has another fifty years in it, and when replacement is honestly the safer call.

How we operate

One crew, accountable

Installation, repair, and maintenance under one roof. When you call, you talk to the people doing the work, not a call center three states away.

When to call

Before small becomes big

A soft spot in the floor, a stain spreading on the ceiling, siding pulling loose after a storm. The earlier we see it, the cheaper the fix. Walkthroughs are how every project starts.
