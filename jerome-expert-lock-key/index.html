Our story

Expert is a promise, not a slogan

From Pius Street outward

Jerome Expert Lock & Key grew out of a simple frustration: locksmithing had become a game of call centers and upsells. We set up on Pius Street in the South Side Slopes with the opposite model. One expert. One fully stocked van. One honest price quoted on the phone.

Neighbors noticed. Landlords with a dozen units noticed. Now the van spends its days between the Slopes, South Side Flats, and the rest of Pittsburgh, doing the unglamorous work well: opening doors without damage, rekeying between tenants, and cutting keys that actually work the first time.

Why the name matters

Putting "Expert" on the door is a dare. It means picking the lock instead of drilling it, telling you when a repair beats a replacement, and standing behind every cylinder we touch.

How a visit goes

You call and describe the problem. We quote the price and the arrival window. We fix it with the gentlest method that works, you test everything, and we leave you with a working key and a straight receipt.
