About

A neighborhood beauty destination.

T J Hair Station is a beauty supply store with an in-house hair station, located at 8643 Philadelphia Rd in Baltimore, near Golden Ring Plaza.

Shoppers come for wigs, extensions, braiding hair, and everyday hair care, and stay for the salon-side services. The team keeps the shelves stocked and the chairs ready.
