Our story

Small Shop, Big Standards

The Barber Code sits on Imperial Highway in South Gate, a humble barbershop built around one idea: take your time and do the cut right.

There is no pretense here, just skilled barbers, sharp tools, and a steady stream of regulars. Clients describe walking in for a simple cut and walking out with a barber they trust.

Reviewers name their barbers: Smooth, known for taking his time on a real clean fade, and Kid, whose cuts earn repeat visits. That kind of loyalty is not bought. It is earned one chair at a time.
