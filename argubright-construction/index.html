Our story

A Northridge contractor since the eighties

Argubright Construction Inc has held California General Building license 493620 since 1986. That is nearly four decades of framing, wiring, plumbing, and finish work across Northridge and the greater Los Angeles area.

Permit records show 44 building permits pulled since 1996: kitchens, additions, renovations, and new builds, each one run with organized project management and practical solutions for jobs of every size.

The reviews tell the rest. Homeowners describe kitchens that feel completely transformed, condos rebuilt from the studs, and floors refinished so well they hired the crew a second time. Dependable, friendly, and easy to work with comes up again and again.
