Bathroom Remodeling

Full and partial bathroom renovations, from a dated guest bath to a primary-suite retreat. We handle demolition, plumbing coordination, tile, fixtures, and finishes under one contract.

  • Full bathroom gut renovations
  • Tub-to-shower conversions
  • Tile, grout, and waterproofing
  • Vanities, mirrors, and lighting
  • Accessibility upgrades
  • Permit coordination with the city
Good to know: bathroom projects are bid per job after an in-person walkthrough. Call for a free estimate.

Kitchen Remodeling

The heart of the home, rebuilt around how you cook and gather. Layout changes, cabinetry, counters, and the trades behind them, managed as one project.

  • Full kitchen renovations
  • Cabinet installation and refacing
  • Countertops and backsplashes
  • Flooring and lighting
  • Open-concept wall removal (engineered)
  • Plumbing and electrical coordination
Good to know: kitchen projects are bid per job after an in-person walkthrough. Call for a free estimate.

Home Additions

When the house you love needs more house. Room additions, second stories, and ADU-adjacent work, from plans and permits through framing and finish.

  • Room and bump-out additions
  • Garage conversions
  • Structural framing
  • Foundation and concrete work
  • Plans, permits, and inspections
  • Finish carpentry and trim
Good to know: additions are the biggest projects we take on, and they start with a conversation, not a calculator. Call to talk it through.

Commercial & Tenant Improvement

Build-outs, remodels, and ongoing facility work for offices, retail, and restaurants. Construction management that keeps tenants, landlords, and schedules aligned.

  • Office and retail build-outs
  • Tenant improvement (TI) work
  • Construction management
  • New commercial construction
  • ADA and code upgrades
  • Scheduled maintenance repairs
Good to know: commercial work is bid from plans or a site walk. Call to set up a walkthrough.

Concrete & Carpentry

The trades that hold everything else up. Our concrete license and carpentry crews mean the structural work never has to be handed off and hoped for.

  • Concrete flatwork: driveways, patios, walkways
  • Foundations and footings
  • Rough framing
  • Finish carpentry, doors, and trim
  • Major home repairs
  • Structural corrections
Good to know: structural work starts with an assessment. Call to schedule a site visit.

Austin Contracting & Associates Inc has been a California corporation since 2004, a locally owned general contractor with over two decades of industry experience.

How we work

General contracting, the accountable way

We are a certified appraisal company and a full-service general contractor, which means we measure twice in more ways than one. Estimates are clear, permits are pulled, and the crew that starts your job finishes it.

Our work spans home improvement projects of every size: concrete work, carpentry, bathroom and kitchen remodels, additions, and major home repairs, plus commercial tenant improvements and construction management for businesses.

We pride ourselves on the quality of the work and on building lasting relationships. Our guarantee is simple: your satisfaction.
