About

A local shop, not a call center.

Tupper Plumbing LLC is a plumbing shop run from Hart Road in Guilford, Connecticut. The business was registered in Connecticut in 2001 and has served Guilford, Madison, and the surrounding shoreline ever since.

Customers describe the same experience again and again: a plumber who shows up promptly, explains the work in plain language, and charges a reasonable price. Much of the work is repeat business and word of mouth, including seasonal care for summer cottages along the shore.

The formula has not changed in over two decades. Answer the phone. Show up when promised. Do the job right. Charge fairly.
