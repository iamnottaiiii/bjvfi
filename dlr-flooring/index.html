Services

Flooring services, start to finish

Every project starts with a free on-site visit: we measure, check the subfloor, and give you a written estimate. Call (818) 939-1087 to schedule.

Hardwood Installation

Solid and engineered hardwood, nailed or glued down, with transitions and shoe molding finished to match.

Laminate Installation

Click-lock laminate over a proper underlayment. Fast, affordable, and kind to busy family life.

Luxury Vinyl Plank

Waterproof LVP and LVT, floating or glue-down, ideal for kitchens, baths and pet-friendly homes.

Tile Installation

Ceramic and porcelain tile set on a level bed, with straight grout lines and sealed finishes.

Floor Repairs

Squeak fixes, damaged plank replacement, re-grouting and subfloor repairs that stop problems at the source.

Refinishing

Sanding and refinishing that brings tired hardwood back, for a fraction of replacement cost.

Subfloor Prep

Leveling, moisture testing and underlayment, the invisible work that decides how long a floor lasts.

Stairs and Trim

Stair treads, risers, baseboards and transitions matched to your new floor for a finished look.

About us

A Pacoima crew for Valley floors

DLR flooring is a flooring contractor based at 12887 Filmore St in Pacoima, California. We work the way a good neighbor should: show up when we say we will, explain the options in plain language, and leave the house cleaner than we found it.

Most of our work comes from repeat customers and referrals across the San Fernando Valley, people who needed one room done right and then called us back for the rest of the house.

We install hardwood, laminate, luxury vinyl plank and tile, and we stand behind the prep work nobody sees: leveling, moisture checks and underlayment. That is what keeps a floor quiet and flat for years.
