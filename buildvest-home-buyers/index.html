How it works

Selling, minus the hard parts

A direct cash sale to Buildvest Home Buyers is built around one idea: your house, sold on your terms, without the months of prep and uncertainty.

We buy houses as-is

Forget the repair list. When Buildvest buys your house, you do not fix anything, clean anything, or stage anything. Peeling paint, dated kitchens, tired roofs, cluttered basements: we have seen it all, and none of it scares us off.

This is the single biggest difference from a traditional sale, where every flaw becomes a negotiation, an inspection objection, or a reason for a buyer to walk away. With us, the condition of the house is simply a fact we work with, not a problem you have to solve.

What this means for you: no contractor quotes, no weekends lost to repairs, no money spent on a house you are leaving.

As-is means as-is

Homes with major repairs needed, code issues, or years of deferred maintenance are exactly the houses we buy. You will never hear us ask you to fix something first.

A fair cash offer, in plain terms

After you tell us about the property, we evaluate it and make you a cash offer you can actually understand. No pages of fine print, no junk fees buried in the paperwork, no last-minute price drops before closing.

Our offers are based on the property, the local market, and the work the house needs. We show our math and answer your questions. If the numbers do not work for you, say so. There is no pressure and no hard feelings.

What this means for you: you know exactly what you will walk away with, before you commit to anything.

No obligation

Getting an offer costs nothing and commits you to nothing. Plenty of sellers get our number just to know their options. That is a perfectly good reason to call.

Close on your timeline

Traditional sales close when the buyer's lender says so. Cash sales close when you say so. Need to move fast? We can move quickly. Need an extra month to find your next place? We can wait.

Because there is no bank involved, there is no appraisal contingency, no underwriting delay, and no financing falling apart a week before closing. The date we agree on is the date it happens.

What this means for you: certainty. Plan your move around a closing date you chose, not one a lender imposed.

You pick the date

Fast closings for urgent situations, relaxed timelines when you need breathing room. Either way, the schedule serves you, not the other way around.

A note on pricing: every property is different, so we do not publish one-size-fits-all offer formulas. The only way to know what your house is worth to us is a quick conversation. Call (774) 287-5689 and we will talk it through.

About

Buildvest Home Buyers is a real estate developer and home buyer based at 12 Jane St in Shrewsbury, MA, focused on direct, as-is home purchases.

What we do

Buildvest Home Buyers purchases homes directly from owners in Shrewsbury, Massachusetts and the surrounding area. Instead of listing a property and hoping the market cooperates, sellers come to us for a straightforward alternative: a fair cash offer, an as-is purchase, and a closing date they choose.

We handle the houses other buyers walk away from: the ones needing major repairs, the inherited properties across the state, the rentals that stopped making sense. Every property has a path forward, and our job is to find the simplest one.

Our approach is deliberately low-pressure. We explain how we reach our offer, answer every question, and leave the decision entirely with you. A good deal for us is only a good deal if it is also a good deal for you.

How we are different

We are buyers, not agents. There is no listing agreement, no commission, and no marketing your home to strangers. When we make an offer, we are offering our own money.

How we work with sellers

It starts with a conversation. You tell us about the property and your situation, and we listen. Then we do our homework: evaluating the home, the neighborhood, and the numbers. Then we make you an offer, in plain terms, with no expiration-date pressure tactics.

If you accept, we handle the paperwork and coordinate the closing around your schedule. If you decline, we part on good terms. Either way, you will have real information about your options, which is more than most sellers start with.

Straight answers

Wondering what your house could fetch, what repairs really matter, or whether selling as-is makes sense? Call and ask. We would rather give you an honest answer than a sales pitch.
