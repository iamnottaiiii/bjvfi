Safety & upgrades

Older wiring and maxed-out panels are the most common hidden hazards we find. We make them safe.

  • Panel upgradesReplace old fuse boxes and undersized panels with modern breaker panels.
  • Safety inspectionsA thorough look at your wiring, panel, and grounding for peace of mind.
  • Surge protectionWhole-home surge protection to guard your electronics.
  • Smoke & CO detectorsHardwired detector installation and replacement.
Older home? Homes with fuse boxes or two-prong outlets are good candidates for an inspection. Call and ask.
