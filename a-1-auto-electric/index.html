A-1 Auto Electric is an auto repair shop at 905 Edwards Rd in Erlanger, Kentucky. Public review sites list it under automotive repair, and the reviews make the specialty clear: car electronics. Customers bring electrical problems, charging faults, and alternator failures here, not general body work.

Customers describe a small, family-run shop. One reviewer calls it "a family owned small shop" with an owner who is "a decent person, nice to chat with." Several reviews name Jamie and his crew, thanking them by name for electrical work on trucks and cars. Another praises the front office staff for making customers feel welcome.

The shop rebuilds alternators in its own workshop. Reviews describe a workspace inside where old alternators are fixed up, and customers report fast turnarounds on rebuilds. That in-house rebuild capability is what reviewers recommend the shop for.
