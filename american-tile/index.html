Our story

Vinyl flooring roots that go back to 1950.

The business at 10720 Kimberly Drive is the U.S. home of Vinylasa American Tile. Vinylasa’s story begins in 1950, when its founders started a company pioneering the manufacture and marketing of vinyl flooring, built on honesty, hard work, and responsibility (per industry profiles). Today that heritage shows up as commercial-grade vinyl tile trusted in schools, hospitals, offices, and stores.

In the trade press, the Union location is listed under point of contact Glen Frost, with a toll-free line at (888) 688-2608 (Floor Covering Weekly directory).
