Our Services

Pick a service to see what a typical visit includes. Every job starts with a free inspection and a written recommendation.

Attic Insulation

Add or upgrade the thermal barrier above your living space so your heating and cooling stays where it belongs.

  • Blown-in cellulose and fiberglass for full coverage in irregular attics
  • Fiberglass batts for standard joist bays
  • Spray foam for high R-value plus air sealing in one step
  • Removal of damaged or compacted old insulation when needed
Typical scope, confirmed at your free inspection

Roofing

A tight roof protects everything below it, including your new insulation.

  • Roof inspections with written findings
  • Leak and storm damage repairs
  • Full roof installs and replacements
  • Ventilation checks so the attic breathes properly
Typical scope, confirmed at your free inspection

Air Sealing

Insulation cannot do its job where air leaks through. We seal the paths heated and cooled air uses to escape.

  • Sealing attic bypasses around chimneys, ducts, wiring, and plumbing
  • Attic access door and hatch sealing
  • Recessed light and top-plate sealing
Typical scope, confirmed at your free inspection

Attic Inspection

A detail-by-detail evaluation covering every part of your attic system: insulation levels, radiant barriers, ventilation, and moisture. Customers note our inspections are thorough and our advice straightforward and cost-effective.

  • Insulation depth and coverage measurement
  • Radiant barrier evaluation
  • Heating and cooling system overview with maintenance suggestions
  • Written findings with prioritized recommendations

Mold & Moisture

Moisture in the attic ruins insulation and indoor air quality. We find the source and give you a cost-effective plan to fix it.

  • Thorough mold inspection, including the spots homeowners never check
  • Moisture source identification
  • Straightforward mold removal guidance
  • Ventilation and insulation fixes to prevent recurrence
