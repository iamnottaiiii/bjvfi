Our story

A cleaner way
to do laundry

Green Laundry opened on Dixwell Ave with a simple bet: a laundromat can be spotless, modern, and even a little bit pleasant.

Why Green

Green by name,
green by machine

The name is a promise. Our all new high efficiency machines use less water and less energy than the old coin op workhorses, which is better for your clothes, your wallet, and the planet.

But being green is also about the room itself. Spotless floors, machines that actually work, and a calm, quiet space with comfortable seating and TVs. Customers tell us it is the cleanest laundromat in the area, and we work hard every day to keep it that way.

  • All new high efficiency washers and dryers
  • Free card system, no coins to hunt
  • Free WiFi, TVs, ATM, and plenty of parking
