About

A roofing contractor
rooted in Pacoima

BlueSky Roofing & Repair Solution works out of Goleta St in Pacoima, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley.

Roofs are all we think about

We are a roofing contractor, full stop. No handyman side gigs, no jack-of-all-trades menu. Roofs leak, roofs age, roofs take a beating from Valley sun and winter storms, and keeping them watertight is the whole job.

Our approach is simple: get on the roof, find the real source of the problem, and fix it in a way that lasts. When a repair will not hold, we tell you that too, and we show you exactly why.

Based at 13157 Goleta St in Pacoima, we are close enough to respond quickly when the weather turns, and we know how Valley roofs age: the sun-baked shingles, the flat-roof ponding, the flashing that gives up before the shingles do.

What you can expect

  • An actual inspection before any quote, not a guess from the driveway
  • Plain-language findings: what is wrong, what it takes, what it costs
  • Repairs sized to the problem, replacements only when the roof is done
  • A tidy job site and debris hauled away when we leave
