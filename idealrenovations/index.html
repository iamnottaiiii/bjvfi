Size, layout changes, and material picks. Moving plumbing or walls adds scope; keeping the footprint the same keeps costs in check.

How we work

A process with no surprises

Remodeling horror stories almost always come down to fuzzy scope and vanishing crews. Here is exactly how your project runs.

Consultation & measurements

We visit, listen, measure, and talk budget honestly. You will know right away if your vision and your budget are in the same room.

Design & fixed proposal

Layout, materials, fixtures and finishes chosen together. Then a written, fixed-scope proposal: what is included, what it costs, how long it takes.

Permits & scheduling

We pull the permits and line up the trades. You get a start date and a milestone schedule before demo day.

Build, clean

Demo to final coat with dust control, daily tidy-ups, and photo updates. One point of contact from start to finish.

Walkthrough & warranty

We walk every inch together and fix the punch list before final payment. Then you cook in it.

Fair questions

Before you ask

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Most full remodels run 4 to 8 weeks depending on scope and material lead times. Refreshes and refacing are typically under two weeks. Your proposal includes a written timeline.

Do I need to move out?

Usually not. We seal off the work zone, keep dust down, and set up a temporary kitchenette so daily life keeps moving.

Can I supply my own materials?

Yes, and we will tell you honestly which items are safe to source yourself and which ones we prefer to warranty through our suppliers.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Sunland and work across the nearby foothill communities. Call and ask, if we can reach you reliably, we will.
