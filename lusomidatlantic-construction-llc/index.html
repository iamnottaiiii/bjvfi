Who We Are

LusoMidAtlantic Construction, LLC

We are a construction company based at 1365 Rosanna Dr in Odenton, Maryland, specializing in concrete flat work for homes and businesses. Driveways, sidewalks, patios, stamped concrete, pavers, curb and gutter, ADA ramps. That focus is our advantage: we do concrete all day, every day.

Our name reflects our roots in the Mid-Atlantic and the Portuguese heritage behind the crew. Our approach is simple and old school: show up on time, prep the base right, pour quality concrete, finish it clean, and stand behind the result.

Quality workmanship, reliability, and lasting results at competitive pricing. Contact us today for a free estimate.
