Services

Every sewer service under one roof

Service descriptions below are starter wording the owner can adjust. Call (818) 606-9260 for exact pricing and availability.

Sewer line repair and replacement

Spot repairs for cracked or offset sections, and full line replacement when the pipe is beyond saving. We handle permits and backfill.

Trenchless pipe lining

A new pipe cured inside the old one, no trench across your yard or driveway. Ideal for root-damaged clay and cast iron lines.

Sewer camera inspection

High-resolution video inspection that pinpoints breaks, bellies and root intrusions, with footage you can keep for your records.

Drain cleaning and root removal

Professional snaking and root cutting for main lines and branch drains, cleared and flow-tested before we leave.

Hydro jetting

High-pressure water jetting scours grease, scale and roots from the inside of the pipe, restoring full flow without chemicals.

Emergency sewer service

Backups and overflows cannot wait. Call (818) 606-9260 and we will get a crew scheduled as fast as possible.

Warning signs to watch for

Multiple slow drains at once, gurgling sounds, sewage smells, or water backing up in tubs and showers. These point to the main sewer line, not a single fixture.
