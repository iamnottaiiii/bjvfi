Our story

The neighborhood
fix-it crew

Handyman General Services is based on Burnet Ave in North Hills, serving homes across the San Fernando Valley.

Every home has a list. The door that sticks in summer, the bathroom caulk turning pink, the TV still leaning against the wall because nobody wants to drill into it.

We exist for that list. Handyman General Services is a home repair and maintenance business in North Hills, CA, built around one simple idea: small jobs deserve the same care as big ones.

We show up on time, we protect your floors and furniture, we clean up when we are done, and we price the job before we start. If something is not right, we come back and make it right.

No job too small is not a slogan here. It is how the calendar fills up: one happy customer at a time, one fixed door at a time.

  • Drop cloths, shoe covers, and a broom finish on every job
  • One visit for most jobs, scheduled around your day
