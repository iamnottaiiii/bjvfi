Our Story

Five decades of getting it right.

A family business built tile by tile on Foothill Boulevard.

1975

The shop opens

Crescenta-Canada Ceramic Tile starts as a family tile business in the Crescenta Valley, built on a simple promise: if you want your job done correctly, hire experts in the field.

1984

Putting down roots

The business is formally established on Foothill Blvd in La Cañada Flintridge, where it still serves homeowners today.

Today

One team, whole projects

Still family owned and operated, the crew now handles all of your construction needs: tile, plumbing, drywall and final finishes, coordinated by one team that shows up when stated.

The family standard

Licensed, bonded and insured. Never hire someone who is not.

That has been the family advice for fifty years, and it is how they run the business: licensed general building and tile contractor, CA license 304397, standing behind every grout line.
