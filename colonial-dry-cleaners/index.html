Our story

Twenty plus years on Park Street.

Colonial Dry Cleaners has been in business for over 20 years, providing expert dry cleaning and shirt services to North Reading and the surrounding towns.

We are a neighborhood shop in the truest sense. We know our regulars by name, we remember how you like your shirts, and we treat a communion dress with the same care as a CEO's suit.

Thank you to everyone who voted us the number one dry cleaner in North Reading and Lynnfield. We work every day to keep earning it.
