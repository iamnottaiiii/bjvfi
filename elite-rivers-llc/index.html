Our story

Neglected homes, renewed streets

Elite Rivers LLC was founded on a straightforward idea: the Worcester area is full of homes that have been neglected and outdated, and those homes deserve a second chance.

Since 2015, our work has centered on remodeling those properties and returning them to the community as affordable, quality homes. Every project is a small bet on the neighborhood around it.

Our goal has never changed: revitalize neighborhoods, help people out of difficult real estate situations, and provide quality homes at their best price.

We want to be a part of Team Worcester. Revitalized neighborhoods mean stronger streets, and stronger streets mean a stronger city.
