About the practice

Led by Dr. Salvatore Lanza, DDS

State of the Art Dentistry is a Staten Island dental practice owned and operated by Dr. Salvatore Lanza, DDS. The practice is built around a simple idea: use current techniques and technology, and treat every patient with expertise and compassion.

Patients consistently mention the same things: thorough explanations, gentle hands, and a team that makes the visit feel personal rather than rushed. Whether you are coming in for a routine cleaning or a more involved procedure, the goal is a healthy, beautiful smile you can feel good about.

The practice aims to maintain the oral health and overall well-being of every patient it serves.Practice philosophy

First visit basics

What to bring
