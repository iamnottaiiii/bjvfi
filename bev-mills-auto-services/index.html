About Bev & Mills

A Fairfield fixture

Bev & Mills Auto Services is a family run auto repair shop at 3171 Cunagin Dr in Fairfield, Ohio. Public listings describe it as an automotive repair shop also handling auto body and parts work. What we know for sure comes from the people who bring their cars here:

  • Customers describe it as run by three brothers.
  • Reviewers report 17 to 25+ years of loyal visits.
  • The house rule, per a longtime customer: "I treat your money like it was my money."
