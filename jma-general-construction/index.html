Our story

A North Hollywood crew that shows up

JMA General Construction is a construction company based at 5834 Vineland Ave in North Hollywood, California. We serve homeowners across the San Fernando Valley with remodels, additions, and renovations of every size.

Our approach is simple: listen carefully, plan thoroughly, and build like it is our own home. That means clear scopes in writing, honest timelines, and a clean jobsite at the end of every day.

Whether it is a single bathroom or a full home renovation, you deal with one accountable crew from the first call to the final walkthrough.

Honest pricing

Clear scopes and fair numbers, agreed in writing before work begins.

Clean jobsites

We protect your home and tidy up daily, so living through a remodel stays livable.

Real communication

You always know what is happening, what is next, and who to call with questions.

Built to last

Proper prep, quality materials, and craftsmanship that holds up for years.
