Chapter 01 · Craft

Four decades on Pasadena rooftops.

Devon Roof Company is a family owned and operated roofing contractor based at 102 S San Marino Ave in Pasadena. For more than 30 years we have served Pasadena and the greater San Gabriel Valley, earning a reputation for clear contracts, competitive pricing, and crews that clean up after themselves. We are known for Spanish tile work, custom fascias, and careful sheet metal detailing that other roofers skip.
