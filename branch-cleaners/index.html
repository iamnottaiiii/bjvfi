Our Story

The cleaner on Old Branch Ave

Branch Cleaners is a neighborhood dry cleaner in Clinton, Maryland, the kind of counter where the staff knows the regulars and the garments by name.

04

A Clinton mainstay

Set in the shops along Old Branch Ave, Branch Cleaners has served Clinton and the surrounding Southern Maryland community with dry cleaning, laundry and garment care.

Customers rate the shop 4.4 out of 5 across 14 Google reviews, with friendly counter service and careful finishing among the things people mention.

It is a practical business built on repeat customers: work shirts that come back crisp, suits ready for Monday morning, and gowns treated like they matter.

The neighborhood counter on Old Branch Ave
05

Care in every press

Good dry cleaning is mostly attention: reading the care label, choosing the right solvent and temperature, pretreating the stain, and pressing by hand where it counts.

That is the standard behind every ticket that crosses the Branch Cleaners counter, from a single shirt to a full wedding party.
