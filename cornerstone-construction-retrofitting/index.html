Services

Structural work, three ways

Tap a specialty to see what is included. Every project starts with an inspection and a written report.

Earthquake Retrofitting

Seismic upgrades that tie your home to its foundation so walls do not slide or collapse in a quake.

  • Cripple wall bracing with engineered plywood panels
  • House bolting: sill plates anchored to the foundation
  • Full perimeter retrofits for raised-foundation homes
  • Retrofit plans for insurance and escrow requirements
Buying or selling? For homes in escrow we charge $125 for the inspection and written report, then deduct the $125 from the job if we earn your business. Homeowners living on the property get the inspection free.

Foundation Repair

From hairline cracks to full change outs, we restore the base your house stands on.

  • Foundation crack strengthening and repair
  • Foundation change out (full replacement)
  • Settlement and drainage-related foundation problems
  • Foundation inspections for insurance requirements
Not sure your foundation is the problem? Doors sticking, cracks in stucco and sloping floors are common early signs. Call and describe what you see.

Structural Support

Reinforcement for the under-house structure that keeps floors level and walls plumb.

  • Post and pier strapping
  • House leveling
  • Underpinning support for added loads
  • Written reports for insurers and real estate transactions
All structural work is performed by a licensed General B contractor and documented with photos before, during and after.

Our story

One trade. Three decades of it.

Cornerstone Construction & Retrofitting is a General B contractor that chose a specialty instead of a little of everything: earthquake retrofitting and foundation work. For 28 years we have focused on the part of the house nobody sees but everybody depends on.

We work with homeowners, buyers in escrow and insurance carriers across Northridge and the San Fernando Valley. Our reports are plain-spoken: what we found, what it means, what it costs to fix, and what can wait.

The name is the philosophy. A cornerstone holds the whole structure. We make sure yours can hold.

  • 28 years
    Focused experience in retrofitting and foundation repair, per our Angi business profile.
  • Licensed General B
    Structural work by a licensed California contractor. Always verify current license status with the CSLB.
  • Escrow specialists
    Fast $125 inspections with written reports for homes changing hands, credited back when we do the work.

How a project runs

No surprises, no jargon. Four steps from first call to final walkthrough.

1Inspection. We crawl the foundation and document everything with photos.
2Written report. Findings, options and firm pricing in plain English.
3Permitted work. Bolting, bracing and repairs to code, kept clean.
4Walkthrough. Final photos and paperwork for your records or insurer.
