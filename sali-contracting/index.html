Our Story

A Staten Island contractor since 2000

Sali Contracting Inc. was established as a New York business corporation in 2000 and operates from 9 Woodcrest Road in Staten Island. The company is a licensed home improvement contractor with the New York City Department of Consumer Affairs and is fully insured.

Over more than two decades, Sali Contracting has handled masonry, roofing, siding, and gutter work across Staten Island homes and businesses. The approach is straightforward: show up, do the work the right way, and stand behind it. All work is guaranteed, and estimates are free.

When you call (718) 720-8594, you talk directly to the contractor who will show up at your door.
