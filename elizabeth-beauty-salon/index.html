Who we are

Elizabeth Beauty Salon is a full service beauty salon on 5th Avenue in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn. The salon describes its work in its own words: peinados, color, cortes, permanentes, extensiones, keratina, rayitos. Styling, color, cuts, perms, extensions, keratin, and highlights.
