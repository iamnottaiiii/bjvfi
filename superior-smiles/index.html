Services

Care for every stage of your smile

As a general dental practice, Superior Smiles covers the everyday care most smiles need, plus options to repair, replace, and brighten teeth.

01

Preventive Care

Exams, professional cleanings, and digital x-rays to catch problems early and keep gums healthy.

02

Restorative Dentistry

Tooth-colored fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures that repair damage and restore comfortable chewing.

03

Cosmetic Options

Whitening and cosmetic treatments to brighten stained teeth and improve the look of your smile.

04

Root Canals

Treatment for deep decay or infection inside the tooth, aimed at relieving pain and saving the tooth.

05

Extractions

Gentle removal of teeth that cannot be saved, including problem wisdom teeth, with aftercare guidance.

06

Emergency Care

Prompt attention for toothaches, broken teeth, lost fillings, and other urgent dental problems.

Call the office to confirm which treatments are offered and to discuss your specific needs.
