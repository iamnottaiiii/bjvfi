Our story

B & H Painting is a local painting contractor based on East Woodland Avenue in Springfield, Pennsylvania, serving homeowners across Delaware County.

The idea is simple: most painting jobs go wrong in the prep, not the painting. So we spend the time up front, cleaning, repairing, sanding, caulking, and priming, and the finish coats go on smooth and stay put.

Customers rate the work 5 out of 5 on public review profiles, and the business keeps a straightforward weekday schedule, Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. When you call, you talk to the people who will actually do the work.

Local

Based in Springfield, working across Delco towns like Morton, Media, and Swarthmore.

Careful

Clear
