Our Story

A Kentucky Company Since 1978

Built on Northern Kentucky Roads

Lawrence Construction and Leasing, Inc. was formed in Kentucky on May 1, 1978, according to Kentucky Secretary of State records. For nearly five decades it has specialized in heavy highway construction across Northern Kentucky.

The company is led by President Timothy L. Mahoney and headquartered at 233 Shorland Drive in Walton, Kentucky. Its LCL Construction Machinery Sales and Rental division keeps a fleet of well-maintained heavy equipment working on local job sites, available for sale or rent.

From Industrial Road to US 42 in Union and Turfway Road, the roads Lawrence builds are the roads the region runs on.
