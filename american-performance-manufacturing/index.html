The Shop That Shows Up

American Performance Manufacturing grew out of a simple idea: Buffalo's truck owners deserved a repair shop that treats a work truck like the livelihood it is. From our location at 180 Irwin Pl in Buffalo, we serve owner-operators, contractors, and fleets across Erie County and Western New York.

Our approach is old school in the best way: honest diagnostics, straight talk on pricing, and repairs done once and done right. No upsells you do not need, no mystery charges, no truck sitting in the lot for weeks.

  • Heavy Truck FocusDiesel and heavy duty truck repair is our core. If it earns its keep on the road, we know how to fix it.
  • Fleet TrustedLocal fleets count on us for scheduled maintenance that keeps inspection records clean and trucks rolling.
  • Honest EstimatesEvery job starts with a clear written estimate. You approve the work before we touch a bolt.
