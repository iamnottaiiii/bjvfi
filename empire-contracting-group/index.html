What negative reviewers report

The following themes appear in public reviews. These are allegations made by reviewers, not findings of fact, and the company may dispute them.

Scam and theft allegations

Multiple reviewers accuse the company of stealing from homeowners, taking money and doing little or no work, and using different names or accounts. One reviewer urges homeowners to go to a different company and claims there are multiple lawsuits against the business.

Unfinished siding and roof jobs

Reviewers describe siding jobs left unfinished for many months, including one account of a year-long partial siding job that was never completed. Before you hire any contractor

  • Get a written contract with scope, timeline, and payment schedule.
  • Verify the contractor's license and insurance independently.
  • Never pay the full amount before work is complete.
  • Read full reviews on Birdeye and other platforms, not just the summary.
  • Check for complaints with the Ohio Attorney General and the BBB.
