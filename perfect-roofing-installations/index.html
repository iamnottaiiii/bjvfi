Our story

Flawless and reliable, on purpose.

Perfect Roofing Installations is a roofing contractor based on Dyer Street in La Crescenta, serving La Crescenta-Montrose and the surrounding Foothill communities. Our name is our standard: every installation should be flawless, every project reliable.

We bring a skilled crew and close attention to detail to each job. Whether you are building a new home or replacing an old roof, we use high-quality materials and proven techniques to deliver roofs that are structurally sound and good looking. The result is a roof that elevates the protection and value of your property.

We keep the process simple: show up on time, communicate clearly, keep the site clean, and stand behind the work.
