Our story

Thirty-plus summers in Amelia.

A local, family-run pool company that never left Ohio Pike.

Rainbow Pools opened its doors in 1994, and the shop at 1139 Ohio Pike has been the center of it ever since. What started as a local pool builder grew into a full pool and spa shop: construction, liner replacement, covers, hot tubs, and the chemical shelves regulars rely on.

The formula has not changed much in three decades. Show up, do the liner right, answer the phone, and stock what people actually need. Public records show inground pool permits filed across the Amelia area year after year, and the Better Business Bureau gives the company an A+ rating.

It is still owned and run locally. When you call, you talk to people who know the local water, the local weather, and what both do to a pool.

4.0out of 5 · Birdeye
★★★★☆

Based on 34 reviews on Birdeye. Other public listings show similar scores: around 4.3 out of 5 on ecohome (28 reviews) and 3.8 on Porch (28 reviews). Taken together, the picture is a well-liked local shop with a few rough edges on scheduling.

What customers praise

Reviewers frequently describe friendly, fast service at the Ohio Pike store and strong liner replacement work. Several mention buying chemicals there regularly and call the staff helpful and good to deal with.

What to watch for

A few reviewers report frustrating scheduling and communication experiences, including missed appointment windows and slow follow-up on service calls. Worth pinning down arrival times in writing when you book.
