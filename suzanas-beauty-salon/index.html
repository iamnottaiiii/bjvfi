Our Story

A neighborhood salon with staying power

Suzana's Beauty Salon has served Castleton Corners on Staten Island for over a decade, operating as a New York State licensed appearance enhancement business since 2014.

Clients describe walking in and feeling at home: a family atmosphere, reasonable prices, and stylists who stay current with the latest styles and products. Many have returned for ten or twenty years, traveling from across New Jersey and Staten Island just for their appointments.

Suzana, the lead stylist, is known for making clients happy with their hair, and for standout work with formal hair and updos. The team is also praised for blonde balayage, highlights, and clean, careful color.

Since 2014
