About Beast Carbon

Beast Carbon is a truck accessories and automotive customizing shop based at 607 Shepherd Dr in Cincinnati, Ohio. The shop specializes in one thing: real carbon fiber parts for trucks.

Where most "carbon look" accessories are plastic with a printed pattern, Beast Carbon parts are built from genuine continuous tow carbon fiber in a 2x2 twill weave, finished matte for scratch and glare resistance, and assembled in the USA.

The shop's motto says it plainly: no plastic, no apologies, just authentically tough carbon fiber attitude. Every product carries the chrome Beast Carbon logo as a mark of the real weave underneath.

What Sets It Apart

  • Genuine carbon fiber, not printed plastic
  • Made in the USA
  • No-drill installs with 3M automotive bonding
  • UV and corrosion resistant construction
  • Cincinnati based shop, real people on the phone
