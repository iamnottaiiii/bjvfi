About G O P

A Granada Hills general contractor building homes and commercial spaces across Los Angeles since 2012.

Who we are

Small, certified, and equipped

G O P Construction Inc is headquartered in Granada Hills, California, and was founded in January 2012. We describe our work simply: a residential and commercial general contractor specializing in ADU builds, remodeling, and new construction.

We run our own equipment, including excavators, skid steers, trucks, and trailers, so site work does not wait on rentals. Our crew handles projects across Los Angeles and the surrounding areas, from family homes to school renovations.

How we work

Trust is the biggest thing we build

Construction is a people business. We keep leadership accessible, flag problems early, and solve them before they cost you money. Clear scopes, written estimates, and schedules we stand behind.
