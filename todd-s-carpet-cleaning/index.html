Our story

Two decades of cleaner carpets on Route 38

Todd's Carpet Cleaning has been cleaning carpets around Pennsauken and Merchantville for about 23 years, built on one simple promise: a deeper clean that is healthier for your home.

Who we are

The local Chem-Dry, run like a neighbor

Todd's is a locally operated Chem-Dry carpet and upholstery cleaning service based at 5626 Route 38. For more than two decades, we have cleaned carpets, upholstery, and tile for families and businesses across the region.

We chose the Chem-Dry method because it is better for the home: a green certified cleaning solution that is safe for kids and pets, one fifth the water of old-fashioned steam cleaning, and dry times measured in hours instead of days.

Our service area covers Bucks, Montgomery, Philadelphia, and Delaware counties in Pennsylvania, plus Burlington, Camden, and Gloucester counties in New Jersey.

  • Locally operated, not a call center: you talk to the people doing the work
  • Free estimates before any work begins
  • Residential and business appointments

The carbonation difference

Instead of flooding carpet with soapy water, Chem-Dry's hot carbonation uses millions of tiny bubbles to lift dirt from the base of the fiber. Less water means less chance of mold and mildew, and carpets that dry in one to two hours.

  • Green certified core cleaner
  • No sticky, dirt-attracting residue
  • Safe around children and pets
