good to know

Before you come in

Walk-ins welcome

Walk-ins are welcome when artists are available. For larger pieces, call ahead at (215) 452-1751 so an artist can block out time.

Tattoos and piercing

Custom tattoo work plus body piercing, including popular picks like nose and ear piercings, all in one shop.

Easy to reach

Street parking nearby, wheelchair accessible entrance, and credit cards accepted. Call for hours and same-day availability.

A note on prices: sample pricing is not listed because tattoo pricing depends on size, detail, and placement. Reviewers consistently describe the shop as inexpensive and fair. Call (215) 452-1751 for a quote on your idea.
what we do

Tattoo styles
and services

Bring your own design or pick from the flash wall. Every piece starts with a conversation with your artist.

Custom Tattoos

Bring reference images or a rough idea and the artists will draw it up with you. Sizes from small flash to larger custom pieces.

Flash Designs

Classic ready-to-go designs on the wall. Quick, bold, and a great first tattoo or spontaneous addition.

Body Piercing

Nose, ear, tongue, and more. Reviewers praise quick, clean piercings and a staff that puts first-timers at ease.

Cover-Ups and Touch-Ups

Got ink you want reworked or covered? Talk to an artist about turning an old piece into something you love.

Community note: the shop has hosted charity tattoo events in the past, including a breast cancer awareness "Get ink for pink" event praised by clients. Ask in store about upcoming events.
