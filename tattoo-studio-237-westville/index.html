How it works

From idea to ink in four steps

  1. 01

    Call or walk in

    Tell us what you want, where you want it, and roughly what size. We will match you with the right artist and give you an honest quote.

    No pressure
  2. 02

    Consult and design

    Your artist sketches a custom design and refines it with you until it feels right. 18 plus with valid ID required.

    Free consult
  3. 03

    Get tattooed

    Settle in. Sterile setup, sharp lines, solid color. Big pieces are scheduled in sessions with breaks built in.

    Clean shop
  4. 04

    Heal it right

    You leave with clear aftercare instructions and a shop you can call if anything looks off during healing.

    Aftercare included

Styles and services

Pick your lane

The crew covers the full range, from fine line florals to bold custom color. Pricing is by piece and session. Call for a quote on your idea.

  1. 01

    Custom Tattoos

    One of a kind designs drawn for your body and your story. Bring references or just a rough idea and let the artist build it.

    Most booked
  2. 02

    Blackwork and Linework

    Crisp black ink, fine lines, dotwork shading. Clean geometry and illustrative pieces that age well.

    Ages great
  3. 03

    Color and Neo Traditional

    Saturated color, bold outlines, illustrative pop. Character work, florals, and vibrant custom color pieces.

    Bold color
  4. 04

    Sleeves and Large Scale

    Half sleeves, full sleeves, and back pieces planned across multiple sessions. Reviewers praise the shop's long form floral and themed work.

    Multi session
  5. 05

    Cover Ups

    Turn old ink into something you actually want to show. Consult required so the artist can plan around the existing tattoo.

    Consult first
  6. 06

    Body Piercing

    Ears, nose, and more from a piercer reviewers rate among the best in NJ. Sterile jewelry and clear aftercare guidance.

    Walk in
  7. 07

    Touch Ups

    Keep existing work sharp. Ask the shop about touch up policy when you book your original piece.

    Ask in shop
