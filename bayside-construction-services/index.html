Our story

A St. Pete contractor
built on doing it right

Bayside Construction Services is a general contractor based on 51st Ave N in St. Petersburg, serving homeowners and businesses across the Tampa Bay area.

Local, not a franchise

We are based right here in St. Petersburg, not dispatched from a call center three states away. When you call, you talk to someone who knows your street.

Craft over shortcuts

Tile lines straight, grout lines even, paint cut clean. The details nobody notices are the ones we obsess over, because that is what makes a remodel last.

Straight talk

If a wall cannot move, we say so. If a cheaper material will do the job, we say that too. You get honest answers, even when they cost us the upsell.
