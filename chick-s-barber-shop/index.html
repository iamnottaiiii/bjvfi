How the shop describes itself

Chick's has been cutting hair in Whitinsville since 1954, and the formula has not changed: welcome everybody, cut carefully, and treat regulars like family. It is the rare shop where a grandfather, his son, and his grandson can all be regulars at once.

Reviewers call it a great local place with a friendly demeanor and short waits. Plenty of parking, good conversation if you want it, and barbers with decades of experience behind the chair.

Our story

Since 1954,
on Church Street

A family barbershop that has served Whitinsville for more than seventy years.

Chick's Barber Shop opened its doors in 1954, and it has been a Whitinsville fixture ever since. Today the shop is known as a warm, welcoming place where experienced barbers take pride in their work. First haircuts, senior haircuts, and everyone in between: that has always been the promise, and it still is.

Walk-ins are welcome, appointments are available, and the door on Church St has been open to neighbors for over seventy years.
