About the Shop

A small shop with a big reputation

Doug's Auto Service sits on Port Union Rd in Hamilton, Ohio, doing what a neighborhood repair shop should do: honest diagnostics, fair prices, and work done right the first time.

Across public review sites, the same themes come up again and again. Customers say they are never taken advantage of, prices are fair and clearly quoted, and the shop works hard to squeeze in urgent repairs even on busy days. Several reviewers say their families have trusted the shop for years, even decades.

The shop holds a 4.9 star rating across dozens of reviews on independent listing sites, with customers recommending it as a reliable, honest mechanic for the Hamilton area.
