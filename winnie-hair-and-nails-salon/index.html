Our story

A family shop on 20th Avenue.

Winnie Hair & Nails Salon is a small, family run beauty salon in Dyker Heights, Brooklyn, right across from the Parkville post office. It is the kind of place where the staff takes the time to listen, and where neighbors come back because the results are consistent.

Hair and nails under one roof means one trip covers the full routine: a cut and color in one chair, a manicure or pedicure in the next, and waxing when you need it.

The salon is a New York State licensed appearance enhancement business.
