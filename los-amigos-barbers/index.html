Services

Pick your service

Walk-ins only, so pick a lane and come on by. Not sure what to ask for? The barbers will talk it through with you in the chair.

Haircuts

High quality haircuts for men, cut to suit your hair type and your routine. Fades, tapers, scissor cuts, and classic shapes.

  • Consultation in the chair before the first snip
  • Works with all hair types
  • Finished with a clean neckline

Straight Razor Shaves

Traditional straight razor shaves done with patience and a steady hand. The close, clean finish you cannot get at home.

  • Classic straight razor technique
  • Beard shaping and cleanup
  • A great add-on to any haircut

Kids Cuts

Haircuts for kids with the patience young clients deserve. Reviewers bring their sons here for everything from tidy school cuts to creative designs.

  • Patient, friendly approach
  • Designs and detail work on request
  • A shop the whole family uses

Lineups and Eyebrows

Crisp lineups and eyebrow cleanup to keep you sharp between full cuts. Quick visits, big difference.

  • Shape ups and edge ups
  • Eyebrow cleanup
  • Perfect between-cut maintenance
