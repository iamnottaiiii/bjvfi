About

The rock crew
of West Boylston

Atomic Rock Splitting is a West Boylston, Massachusetts contractor focused on one of the toughest trades there is: making rock and concrete disappear.

What we do

A specialty trade

Most contractors pour concrete. We break it, cut it, split it and haul it away. Rock splitting, ledge removal, concrete breaking and sawing, and demolition are the core of the business, not an afterthought.

How we work

Controlled and clean

We work mechanically, not with explosives, so jobs stay controlled around homes, utilities and existing structures. When we leave, the rock is gone and the site is clear.

Who we serve

Homeowners to highways

From a boulder in a backyard to concrete breaking for streets and highways, we take residential, commercial and municipal work across the West Boylston area.
