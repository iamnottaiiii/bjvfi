Our story

One mason, one standard, since 1987.

Ponce Masonry has held California contractor license 520793 since November 14, 1987. That is nearly four decades of block, brick, stone, and concrete across Los Angeles County, all from the same Northridge shop at 8725 White Oak Ave.

The craft

Masonry is unforgiving work. Mortar joints do not hide mistakes, and concrete gives you one chance to get it right. Pascual Ponce built this business on doing it right the first time, which is why customers describe him as their first and only call.

The job site

Neatness is a recurring theme in reviews: a tidy site, clear communication, and respect for the client's home. Masonry projects are messy by nature, but the site does not have to look that way when the crew leaves each day.

The license

CSLB license 520793, issued 1987, current through November 30, 2027. Workers compensation coverage in place and a $25,000 surety bond through Western Surety Company. Ask us for current documentation any time.
