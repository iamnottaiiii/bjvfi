About

A Milton landmark
on Randolph Ave

Eagle Farms brings together a farm store, a nursery and a landscaping crew in one local business.

One address, three ways to help

At 1126 Randolph Ave you will find a farm store stocked with fresh produce and groceries, an outdoor nursery full of plants and shrubs, and a landscaping crew that works yards across the area.

It is the kind of local place people stop at on the way home: grab dinner ingredients, pick up a flat of flowers, and ask about the yard, all in one trip.

The crew handles the seasonal rhythm of a New England yard, from spring cleanups and plantings to fall leaf removal and winter snowplowing.
