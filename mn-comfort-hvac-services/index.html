Our story

Mn Comfort HVAC Services. is a local heating and air conditioning contractor based in Burbank, California.

We started with a simple observation: most HVAC headaches come from rushed diagnoses and oversized equipment. So we built the business the other way around. We measure first, quote in writing, and size equipment to the house, not to the sales target.

Burbank homes put real demands on a system: long hot spells in summer, chilly valley nights in winter, and dust that never quite quits. We service what is here and recommend replacement only when repair stops making sense.

  • Diagnosis before dollarsWe find the actual failure and explain it in plain language before quoting.
  • Right-sized equipmentLoad calculations on every replacement, so the system runs efficiently instead of short-cycling.
  • Tidy work, every visitDrop cloths, shoe covers, and a vacuum pass before we leave.
  • One local crewThe people who answer the phone are the people who show up.
