The practice

Care you can count on

Choice of Dental Care is a general dentistry practice at 3234 Nostrand Avenue in Brooklyn, led by Dr. Aleksandr Izrailov, DDS. The practice provides complete dental care for adults and families: preventive checkups and cleanings, restorative work like fillings, crowns and root canals, tooth replacement with dentures, and cosmetic whitening.

Evening and Sunday hours make it easier to fit dental care around a busy schedule, and new patients are always welcome. The approach is simple: listen carefully, explain clearly, and recommend only what your teeth truly need.
