LINER NOTES

About The Shop

A Corryville Institution

Irbys Barber Shop sits at 2828 Short Vine St #1 in Cincinnati, close to the University of Cincinnati and right next door to Island Fridays, where plenty of customers grab a snack after their cut.

Reviewers name barbers Phil Irby and Charles again and again: careful with every head, clear about who is next in line, and never rushing a cut. Customers describe a traditional barbershop feel, fair and affordable prices, and a room where real conversation happens.

Barber names and shop details above come from public customer reviews. No founding date or ownership history is published online, so none is claimed here.
