What to do before you arrive.

Quick first-aid guidance for common dental emergencies. This is general information, not medical advice.

Toothache

Rinse with warm water, floss gently to remove anything trapped, and call for guidance. Do not place aspirin directly on the tooth or gums.

Knocked-out tooth

Hold it by the crown, rinse it without scrubbing, and try to keep it moist in milk. Time matters, so call immediately.

Broken denture

Save every piece in a safe container and stop wearing it. Do not try to glue it yourself. Bring the pieces with you.
