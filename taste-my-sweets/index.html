Our Story

A family bakery with a whole lot of heart

Taste My Sweets started with a simple belief: a good cookie can make anybody's day better. From our kitchen in Country Club Hills, we bake small batches of cookies, cupcakes, and our now-famous Peach Cobbler Egg Rolls, the way homemade treats are supposed to taste.

"You can't eat just one, two, or three."

Word travels fast in a neighborhood. Ours traveled through church picnics, ball games, birthdays, and family tables, anywhere people gather and something sweet is welcome. That is what our customers tell us, and we take it as the highest compliment.
