Our Story

A Dominican salon with Port Richmond heart.

Yolandas Magical Scissors brings Dominican hair traditions to Staten Island: thorough washes, careful sets, and blowouts with real staying power.

The salon sits on Port Richmond Avenue, where it has become a go-to for clients who value stylists who listen. Regulars describe easy communication with the team, reasonable prices, and results they can count on week after week.

Dominican Techniques

Roller sets and blowouts rooted in Dominican salon culture, adapted to every hair type that walks through the door.

Clients for Years

Many guests have trusted the salon for years and refer friends and family who stay just as happy.

It is a busy shop, which says plenty on its own. Plan ahead, call to book, and come ready to leave with hair you love.
