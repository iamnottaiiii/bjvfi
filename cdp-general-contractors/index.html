  1. Walk-through and scope. We see the space, hear what you want, and define exactly what is included.
  2. Clear proposal. A written plan with the work spelled out before anything begins.
  3. Managed build. Trades scheduled in order, site kept orderly, progress communicated.
  4. Final walk-through. We check every detail together before the project closes.

About

A Brooklyn contractor built on accountability

CDP General Contractors LLC is registered in Brooklyn, New York, and has been operating from 1005 E 29th St since 2007. As a general contractor, the company takes responsibility for the whole job: planning, trades, schedule, and finish quality.

New York renovation work comes with real constraints: building rules, permits, shared walls, and deadlines that matter. CDP plans around those realities from the first walk-through, so the build runs cleanly and the result holds up.
