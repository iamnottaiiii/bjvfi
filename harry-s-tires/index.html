About the shop

A Blackwood staple for affordable tires

Harry's Tires is a used tire shop at 20 Hilltop Ave in Blackwood, New Jersey. Drivers across Gloucester Township know it as the place to go when a tire gives out and a brand-new set is not in the budget.

The formula is simple: keep a huge inventory of inspected used tires, work fast, and price fairly. Regulars mention being in and out in minutes, with honest advice about whether a tire is worth repairing or replacing.

It is a no-frills, cash-only shop. Judge the tires, not the building, and call ahead so the crew can pull your size before you arrive.
