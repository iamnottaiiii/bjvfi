About

The salon where everybody knows your hair

Hair It Is opened its doors in Camillus in 2014 and has been a West Genesee Street staple ever since. It is the kind of salon where stylists remember your last cut, ask about your family, and tell you honestly what your hair needs.

Clients praise the team for friendliness and skill: stylists who listen, explain options clearly, and even fix color issues from elsewhere. The space is clean, quiet and welcoming, and appointments are booked with a real person on the phone, not a robot.

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, you will be greeted like a regular.
