About

A neighborhood salon, run with care

Nhan Le's Hair Salon sits at 315 Fayette Street in the heart of Manlius. It is a small, close-knit salon where owner Nhan Le and the team build their reputation one head of hair at a time.

People

A small team, personal service

With just a handful of stylists, every visit feels personal. Owner Nhan Le is known for careful cuts and perms, and guests single out stylist John as a true hair artist who instinctively knows what cut suits you.

Place

Calm, clean, and green

Reviewers repeatedly mention the salon's beautiful plants, spotless stations, and friendly atmosphere. It is the kind of place where regulars are greeted by name.
