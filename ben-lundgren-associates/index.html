Our story

A San Marino practice,
on the public record

Ben Lundgren & Associates is a landscape design practice based at 2605 Fairfield Pl in San Marino, California, led by consultant landscape architect Ben Lundgren, California license #3109.

Rather than tell you a story we cannot verify, here is what the record shows: the firm's name appears on landscape and irrigation plan sets filed with Los Angeles County for projects in Arcadia, Pasadena, and Torrance, work that passed agency review and went to construction.

Ben Lundgren has also served his city directly, sitting as an alternate member of the City of San Marino Design Review Committee, the body that reviews the design quality of new construction in one of Southern California's most design-conscious cities.

On our history: one directory listing describes the firm as established in 1982. We could not independently confirm that date, so take it as a directory claim, not a verified fact. What is verified: the license, the address, and the filed plans.

The short version

Licensed landscape architect. San Marino based. Plans on file with the county. If you want a landscape that is designed, documented, and buildable, start here.
