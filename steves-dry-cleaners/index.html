Our story

A Saugus family business, pressing forward since 1977

In 1977, Rodney and Rita Stevens Sr., together with their son Rodney Jr., opened Steve's Cleaners in Saugus. Daughter Rhonda joined the business four years later. It started with one store and five employees, and it grew the honest way: one well-cleaned garment at a time.

Over the decades the industry changed around us. Manual presses gave way to electric air-driven presses, and transfer units gave way to a state of the art computerized dry cleaning machine. We changed with it, because the goal never changed: quality first, every single load.

Rodney Sr. loved his family, and all seven of his grandchildren have worked in the business at one time or another. Since his passing, Rodney Jr. and Rhonda have carried it on the way he would have wanted: with passion, confidence, and pride.

Today we serve Saugus from our shop on Essex Street with the same promise we started with. Quality is our number one goal.
