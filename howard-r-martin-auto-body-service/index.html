Our story

Howard R Martin Auto Body Service has been in business since 1936. Family owned and operated, the shop prides itself on customer care and professional work on all makes and models, American and foreign.

From minor dents to major collision damage, the team handles paint, body, and undercoating work. Reviewers praise the timely, friendly, professional service, with several noting the shop went above and beyond after deer collisions and daily mishaps.
