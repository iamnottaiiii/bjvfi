How we got here

Grew with the customers

Goodfellas Tire Shop began in Columbia, CT, where the Oquendo family built a loyal following over about a year and a half. When the time came to grow, they moved to 82 Columbia Ave in Willimantic: a larger space with a waiting room, ample parking and better visibility.

The move was for the customers as much as the family. Much of the clientele already came from Willimantic, and the new shop puts Goodfellas right where its people are.

Owners Jesus Oquendo and Brenda Rodriguez run it as a family business: honest advice, fair prices, and the kind of care that turns first-time visitors into regulars.
