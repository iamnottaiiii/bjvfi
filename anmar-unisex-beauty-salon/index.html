Our story

A Foster Ave fixture

Anmar Unisex Beauty Salon sits at 418 Foster Ave in Brooklyn, NY 11230, serving neighbors from Kensington, Windsor Terrace and beyond. It is a unisex shop by name and by nature: every age and every hair type is welcome in the chair.

The salon is known for an old school value: a genuinely great haircut at a price that does not sting. One guest says her family has trusted Anmar for more than 30 years, which is the kind of loyalty no advertisement can buy.

Walk in, take a seat, and let the stylists handle the rest.

Why regulars stay

Simple, honest salon care

No rush, no upsell, no fuss. Guests describe a warm, family friendly shop where the focus stays on the cut and the person in the chair.

Affordable by design, rated 5.0 across directory reviews, and easy to reach on Foster Ave. That is the Anmar formula.
