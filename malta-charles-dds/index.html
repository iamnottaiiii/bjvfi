Checkups & cleanings

Regular exams and professional cleanings keep small problems small. Most patients are seen about twice a year.

Digital x-rays

Modern imaging that spots decay, bone loss and hidden issues early, so treatment stays simple.

Gum care

Screening and treatment for gingivitis and gum disease, because healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile.

Family dentistry

Care for teens, adults and seniors in one familiar office. A good fit for the whole household.

Tooth-colored fillings

Natural-looking composite fillings that repair decay and blend in with your smile.

Crowns & bridges

Restore cracked, worn or missing teeth with sturdy crowns and natural-looking bridges.

Root canals

Relieve deep tooth pain and save teeth that would otherwise need extraction, with a gentle hand.

Extractions

When a tooth cannot be saved, straightforward and comfortable removal with clear aftercare guidance.

Teeth whitening

Brighten years of stains for a fresher, more confident smile. Ask the office about options.

Veneers & bonding

Correct chips, gaps and uneven teeth with thin, natural-looking veneers or simple bonding.

Our story

Dentistry done the
old-fashioned way:
with care

Dr. Charles W. Malta, DDS, has built his Stoneham practice around a simple idea: treat every patient like a neighbor, because most of them are.

Public records show a general dentistry practice at this same Main St address for over 30 years, a rarity in modern dentistry. Patients describe a dentist who is knowledgeable and gentle, and a team that is courteous, helpful and kind to all.

One longtime reviewer puts it simply: after decades of care for themselves and their adult children, they consider Dr. Malta a good friend besides their dentist. That is the kind of practice this is.

Dr. Malta is great. He really understands my concerns and treats me with respect. His staff is wonderful, very helpful and kind to all his patients.

Experience you can feel

A Boston University dental graduate with decades of general dentistry practice. Patients say his experience shows in every careful, unhurried appointment.

Plain-language dentistry

No jargon, no pressure. Dr. Malta explains conditions and treatments clearly, answers every question, and keeps patients informed throughout care.

Family-first practice

Adults, teens and seniors are all welcome. Many patients have sent their children and grandchildren here over the years.

There when it matters

One reviewer notes Dr. Malta has come in on his day off to care for patients who needed him. That level of dedication is rare.
