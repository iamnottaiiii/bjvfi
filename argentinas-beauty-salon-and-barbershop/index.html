Services

Every service includes a consultation so you leave with exactly the cut you pictured.

At Argentinas beauty salon and barbershop, the menu covers what a full service salon should do best. Precision cutting, rich color, and styling for every occasion, all in one welcoming shop.

  • Signature Haircut. Consultation, precision cut, wash and style.
  • Blowout. Smooth, polished finish with volume that lasts.
  • Hair Color. Single-process color matched to your skin tone.
  • Highlights. Dimension and brightness, foil or balayage.
  • Kids Cut. Patient, friendly cuts for young clients.
  • Deep Conditioning. Restorative treatment for soft, healthy hair.
  • Special Occasion Styling. Updos and styling for weddings and events.
  • Beard Trim. Shape and detail with hot lather edging.

Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments keep the wait short. Call (323) 635-0190 to book your chair.
