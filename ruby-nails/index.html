Our story

New Dorp's neighborhood nail salon

Ruby nails opened on New Dorp Plaza with a simple idea: great nails should be easy to get. No appointment gymnastics, no uptown prices, just a clean, welcoming salon where the work speaks for itself.

Our technicians take their time. Prep is never rushed, tools are kept spotless, and we will tell you honestly what your nails need, whether that is a simple polish change or a full set for a big event.

From everyday manicures to wedding parties, we are proud to be part of the New Dorp routine.
