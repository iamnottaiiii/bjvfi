Our story

Forever Young Beauty Salon is a full-service beauty hub on Spencerport Road in Rochester: hair, color correction, lash extensions, laser hair removal, waxing, and makeup all under one roof. The team includes stylists Jenna, Brianna, and Abby for hair and color transformations, plus esthetician Maddy for lashes, laser, and waxing. Clients praise the clean, professional, punctual shop where hair "always turns out beautiful."
