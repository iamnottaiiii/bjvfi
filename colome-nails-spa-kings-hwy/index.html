Our story

A calm corner
of Kings Highway

Colome Nails Spa built its name the slow way: one careful set of nails at a time, in a room people describe as peaceful.

The approach

Careful hands,
quiet room

What sets Colome apart, according to the people who keep returning, is the combination of skill and atmosphere. Technicians with more than ten years of experience take their time: checking the shape with you as they go, paying attention to detail, and never rushing the finish.

The room itself does half the work. Bright and beautifully decorated, with soft music, strict cleanliness, and pedicure stations with massage chairs, it is built for unwinding. The polish wall is wide, the tools are sterilized or disposable, and the staff is known for being welcoming and honest about wait times.

Come in for a quick polish change or settle in for the full manicure and pedicure. Either way, the goal is the same: you leave calmer than you arrived, with nails to match.

What reviewers highlight

  • Strict hygiene: sterilized or disposable tools
  • Massage chairs at the pedicure stations
  • Wide selection of polish colors
