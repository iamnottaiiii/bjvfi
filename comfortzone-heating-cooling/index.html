About Us

A Local Shop, Not a Call Center

ComfortZone Heating & Cooling is locally owned and operated, and has been servicing the Greater Cincinnati area for more than 25 years.

We are a small HVAC contractor built on word of mouth. Customers know us by name: reviewers praise Steve for clear communication, reasonable pricing, and work done right, from water heater replacements to full AC and furnace upgrades.
