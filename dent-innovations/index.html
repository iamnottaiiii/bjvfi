PORTRAIT OF THE CRAFTSMAN · Photo coming soon

The Craft

Paintless dent repair is bodywork at its most restrained. Using specialized tools and techniques, dents, creases, and larger damage are worked out from behind the panel. No paint is sprayed, no filler is spread, and the vehicle keeps its original finish, which means no depreciation from a repaint and a gentler footprint on the environment.

That restraint is the whole philosophy of the atelier: remove only the damage, and leave everything else exactly as the factory made it.
