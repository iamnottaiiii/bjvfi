How a Job Runs

Four steps. No mystery.

Step one

You call

Tell us what the walls look like now and what you want them to look like. We will talk it through and set a time to look.

Step two

We assess

On-site look at the drywall damage or the paint scope, then a straight, itemized quote. No vague numbers.

Step three

We work

Prep, protect your floors and furniture, then hang, mud, sand, and paint. You will know the plan each day.

Step four

You inspect

We walk the finished work with you. If a seam or edge is not right, we fix it before we leave.

One crew for the wall and the paint

Philly Drywall and Paint Pros is a Philadelphia contractor working in two trades: drywall installation and repair, and interior and exterior painting. The idea is simple. Most wall jobs need both.
