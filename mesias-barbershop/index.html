Our Story

A neighborhood shop on Webster St

Mesias Barbershop serves the Webster Street neighborhood of Worcester with classic barbering and a personal touch. It is the kind of shop where the barber knows your name and remembers your cut.

The shop keeps things simple: a clean chair, sharp tools, and honest work. Clients come for fades, beard trims, and straight razor shaves, and they come back because the details are right.
