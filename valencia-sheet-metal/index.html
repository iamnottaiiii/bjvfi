About

A Pacoima sheet metal shop

Valencia Sheet Metal Inc. is a specialty contractor on Ilex Avenue, licensed in California since 1986.

The shop

Licensed, bonded, and local

Valencia Sheet Metal holds California CSLB License 500855, first issued in November 1986. That is decades of sheet metal work out of the same Pacoima shop.

The company is listed as a specialty contractor serving commercial and institutional market sectors, with a crew of about nine people who fabricate and install under one roof.

General contractors, facility managers, and building owners across the San Fernando Valley call Valencia when the metal has to fit the first time.
