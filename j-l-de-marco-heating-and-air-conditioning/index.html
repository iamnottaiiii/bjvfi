Heating services

Furnace installs, replacements, and repairs that keep New England winters comfortable.

  • Furnace repairNo heat, strange noises, short cycling, we diagnose and fix it.

  • Thermostat upgradesSwap an old dial for a modern programmable or smart thermostat.

Cooling services

Central air conditioning that actually keeps up with August.

  • Central AC installationNew air conditioning systems paired with your furnace and duct work.
  • AC repairWarm air, weak airflow, leaks, or a unit that will not start, we troubleshoot it.
  • AC replacementWhen the old condenser is done, we size and install its replacement.
  • Seasonal startup checksPre-summer inspection so the first hot week does not surprise you.
Good to know: estimates are free and straightforward. Call (508) 829-6676 to talk through your cooling needs.

Heat pump systems

Efficient year-round heating and cooling, with gas backup for the coldest days.

  • Heat pump installationFull systems for first and second floor zones, air handlers mounted to suit your home.
  • Dual-fuel setupsHeat pump paired with a gas furnace that takes over when temperatures drop.
  • ERV ventilation unitsEnergy recovery ventilation to keep indoor air fresh without wasting heat.
  • Heat pump serviceRepairs and tune-ups for existing heat pump equipment.
Good to know: permitted projects include multi-zone heat pump installs with basement and attic duct runs. Call (508) 829-6676 to see if a heat pump fits your home.

Ductwork and airflow

The hidden half of comfort, ducts and vents that move air where it should go.

  • Duct installation and modificationNew duct runs for additions, remodels, and system upgrades.
  • Bathroom fan ventingProper exhaust venting for bathrooms to control moisture.
  • Dryer ventingSafe, code-compliant dryer exhaust runs.
  • Airflow problem fixesHot and cold spots, weak registers, and noisy ducts diagnosed and corrected.
Good to know: sheet metal and HVAC permits on record cover duct work for multi-story homes. Call (508) 829-6676 for an on-site assessment.
