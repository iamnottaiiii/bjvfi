The story, as the customers tell it

A Newport Original

The shop has no official website or published history, so this timeline is pieced together from public reviews and directory listings, not from the shop itself.

The Setting

Home Base: Newport Plaza

The shop sits at 40 Carothers Rd in the Newport Plaza shopping center, on the Kentucky side of the Ohio River, minutes from the water and across from Cincinnati.

The Trade

A Family Tradition

Public reviews describe a family trade: a father who cut hair here for years, and a son who carries on the work today. Barbers Tim and Anthony are praised by name again and again.

The Style

Good Old-Fashioned Barbering

Reviewers call it a "good old fashion Barber": unhurried cuts, sports talk, jokes, and a chair where everybody feels welcome. Some regulars say they have been coming for over 20 years.

The Word

Word of Mouth on the Riverbank

With about 4.7 stars across roughly 79 public reviews, the shop runs on reputation. Customers say they will not go anywhere else, and they tell their neighbors the same.

What Folks Praise

  • Fair, affordable prices, called the best deal in town by longtime customers
  • Unhurried, old-fashioned haircuts with attention to detail
  • Barbers Tim and Anthony, praised by name in many reviews
  • Friendly, welcoming atmosphere with sports talk and good humor
  • Loyal regulars, some visiting for 20 years or more

Fair Notes

  • One reviewer visited twice expecting an 8:30 opening shown on a sign, found the shop closed, and waited with other customers
  • One reviewer noted that prices have been raised over time
  • Hours vary slightly between directory listings, so calling ahead is wise
