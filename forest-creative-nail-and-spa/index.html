Our story

A neighborhood studio with loyal regulars.

Some guests have been coming to Forest Creative Nail & Spa for over fifteen years. That kind of loyalty does not happen by accident. It comes from careful work, honest service, and a team that takes the time to fix anything that is not right.

Reviewers describe quick, smooth visits with friendly, attentive staff.Based on 129 Google reviews

The salon sits on Forest Avenue in Staten Island's West Brighton area, serving the surrounding neighborhood with everyday nail care: manicures and pedicures, gel and acrylic, dip powder, nail art, and waxing. Booking listings note the salon welcomes both women and men.

Guests praise the salon for simple, beautiful manicures at reasonable prices, and many recommend calling ahead because popular days fill up fast.
