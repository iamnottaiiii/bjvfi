Behind the marquee

About Us

A neighborhood laundromat with a five star name

Five Star Laundromat is a self service laundromat on US-42 in Florence, Kentucky, near the intersection of US Highway 42 and Hopeful Church Road. Customers come for the clean machines, the extra large washers for bulky loads, and the friendly attendants who keep the place running.

Pay your way: the store accepts credit cards and tap to pay alongside cash, so you can skip the coin hunt. Between the washers, dryers, drop off service, and folding stations, most laundry days wrap up in a single visit.
