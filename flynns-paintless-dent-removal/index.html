The Craft

Your factory finish stays put

Paintless dent removal is the least invasive way to fix a dent. Instead of grinding, filling, and repainting, the technician works from behind the panel, massaging the metal back to its original shape with specialized rods and hand tools. When the paint is unbroken, the repair is invisible.

It costs far less than a traditional body shop visit, it is done in a fraction of the time, and it keeps your original factory finish intact, which protects your resale value. It is also the greener option: no paints, no chemicals, no body filler.

Why it matters

A repainted panel never quite matches the factory. PDR avoids the paint booth entirely, so your car keeps its original finish, its value, and its story.

Most small dents are finished the same day, and many take less than an hour.

Our Story

One technician, one standard

Flynn's Paintless Dent Removal is the work of David Flynn, a PDR specialist based in Cohoes, New York. The business is built on a simple idea: most dents do not need a body shop. They need a technician who knows how metal moves and takes the time to move it back.

Paintless dent removal is a craft of patience and precision. Working from behind the panel with rods, picks, and light, a technician reads the dent the way a reader reads a page, then eases the metal home a fraction of a millimeter at a time. Done well, there is no evidence the damage ever existed.
