The story of Oscars barbershop

Oscars barbershop on Atlantic Avenue in South Gate is a neighborhood shop with loyal, long-time clients. Barber Oscar is known for clean, professional cuts at a fair price in a tidy shop.

Located at 10251 Atlantic Ave, South Gate, CA 90280, the shop serves South Gate and the surrounding neighborhoods. Walk-ins and regulars are both welcome, and the team is happy to talk through the cut before the clippers come out.
