Our story

A builder Boone County
knows by name

Todd Helm Construction, LLC is built around one thing: a builder whose clients stick with him for decades.

The builder

Todd Helm runs a general contracting company out of Burlington, Kentucky, at 7359 East Bend Road. The company's public record tells the story in one line: an association with a client spanning decades, built on being well organized, hard working, and conscientious.

This is not a volume operation. It is a builder who shows up, manages the work himself, and produces quality that keeps people calling back years later. In a trade where trust is everything, that reputation is the whole business.

How every project runs

1. Walk it. Todd looks at the project in person and talks through what you actually need.

2. Plan it. Clear scope, clear timing, clear expectations. No surprises mid-project.

3. Build it. Organized job sites, conscientious work, quality you can inspect.

4. Stand by it. The kind of builder clients recommend for decades does not disappear at final payment.
