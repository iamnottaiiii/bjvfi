Our Story, In Chalk

a neighborhood lot on Reading Road

The Lot

Stricklen Auto sits at 8410 Reading Rd in Reading, Ohio, just north of Cincinnati. It is a used car dealership with its own repair shop on the same lot: sales up front, mechanics in the back, and a rental counter for when your car is down.

The business describes itself as having plenty of great cars to choose from, monthly specials, a great staff, and tons of repeat customers. The lot board above is where the current picks live.

How They Do Business

  • "We work with you" approach to financing
  • Vehicles inspected before they go up for sale
  • Repeat customers make up a big share of buyers
  • Sales and service handled at one address
  • Open six days a week, closed Sundays
