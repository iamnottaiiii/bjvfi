The salon

A little retreat in the middle of the mall

Step off the concourse and into a quieter space. Nail Trix & Spa pairs detailed nail work with genuine spa touches: warm towels, careful shaping, and pedicures guests describe as spotless and relaxing.

Dip powder is the house specialty, loved for its durability and natural look. Prefer gel or classic polish? The technicians will walk you through what suits your nails best.
