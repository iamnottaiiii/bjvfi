The Surface Edit · No. 01

A salon that feels like self-care, run by people who treat hair like an art form.

Walk into Surface Salon on South Ave and the first thing you notice is the mood: beautiful, calm, and a little bit lux, with great music on and a team that genuinely loves what they do. Clients call it their favorite self-care stop in Rochester, and the reviews back it up.

The stylists here are known for getting it exactly right, the haircut you described, the color you pictured, the brows you have been nervous to trust anyone with. They keep their training current, their prices fair, and their welcome warm. This is not a rush-you-out chair. It is a sit-back, tell-us-everything, leave-gorgeous kind of place.

Our story

A beautiful room where
great hair happens

Surface Salon is an independent beauty salon on South Ave in Rochester, built on talent, training, and treating every guest like a regular.

The space

Lux but never stuffy. Chill but never careless.

Guests describe Surface as beautiful, lux, and chill, a space with awesome music where you can actually relax. It is the kind of salon people call their favorite self-care activity, not just an errand. Street parking out front, a warm welcome at the door, and a chair that feels like yours from the first visit.

The team takes training and customer service seriously and stays current with style trends. They are, in the words of one guest, kind-hearted people who are gifted both artistically and technically. Fair prices, a lovely shop, and stylists who remember what you asked for last time.

They also stock excellent Davines Italian hair products, so the good hair days continue at home.
