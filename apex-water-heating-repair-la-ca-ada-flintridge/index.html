Signs You Need Us

Is your water heater trying to tell you something?

Rumbling or Popping

Sediment baked onto the tank floor makes the burner work overtime. A flush and inspection usually quiets it down.

Rusty or Smelly Water

Discolored hot water or a rotten-egg odor points to a failing anode rod or tank corrosion. Catch it early and you may save the unit.

Rising Gas or Electric Bills

A heater fighting mineral buildup burns more energy for the same hot shower. Maintenance restores efficiency fast.
