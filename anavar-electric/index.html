Our Story

Licensed in 1995. Trusted ever since.

ANAVAR ELECTRIC began as a California State Licensed Electrical Contractor in 1995, License 711638, and never left the foothills. From the Rockland Pl shop in La Cañada Flintridge, the company serves homes and businesses across La Cañada Flintridge and surrounding cities within about 15 miles.

The reputation was built on four words the company still stands behind: knowledge, experience, fairness, and respect. That shows up in the way work gets done: continuous study of electrical codes and the latest industry trends, honest estimates that look for ways to save you money, and communication that keeps you informed from the first call to the final check.

Small jobs are welcomed and considered just like any other size work project.

Troubleshooting and repairs are the specialty, the cases other contractors find difficult. New installs and remodeling projects round out the work, across both residential and commercial environments. Whether it is a flickering circuit, a full panel upgrade, or wiring for a remodel, the standard is the same: safe, code compliant, and done to your satisfaction.

Customers know the owner by name. Rafi answers, shows up, suggests the cleverest placement for a new charger or fixture, and calls back in the evening to make sure everything works. That personal accountability is the whole business model.
