01 · Who we are

The handy neighbor Cincinnati calls first

Urban Heart Enterprises is a handyman business serving Cincinnati from 731 E McMillan St. We take on the everyday jobs that keep a home running: patching, hanging, tightening, sealing, and sorting out the little things before they become big things.

No job is too small to deserve care. One visit often clears a whole list: the door that sticks, the shelf that never got mounted, the caulk that gave up last winter. You describe it, we handle it, and we clean up after ourselves.

Fix the small stuff now, and the big stuff never gets a chance to start.

Because every home is different, we quote each job on its own merits. Call (513) 402-8784, tell us what is going on, and we will talk through options and scheduling.
