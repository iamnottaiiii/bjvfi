Our story

A local painter who treats your home like his own

Nick's Painting & Drywall Repairs is a locally owned Canoga Park business built on more than 25 years in the trade. The promise is simple: answer every question, show up on time, and leave the job cleaner than we found it. Whether the work calls for a sprayer, a roller, or a fine brush, the crew matches the method to the surface, and every drywall repair gets a texture match so it blends right in.

Customer satisfaction is the top priority on every job, from a single patched wall to a full exterior repaint.
