Our story

A Main Street mainstay

Serranos Barbershop has earned its place on Main Street in Worcester one cut at a time. It is the kind of shop where the clippers hum, the conversation flows, and every client leaves sharper than they arrived.

What sets the shop apart is simple: pride in the work. Reviewers notice it, from the careful detail of each cut to the relaxed, welcoming atmosphere that makes first-timers feel like regulars.
