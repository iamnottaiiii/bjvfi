About

A contractor,
not a manufacturer.

First, the name question: we are Armstrong Ceilings & Walls, a local drywall contractor in Amelia, Ohio. We are not affiliated with Armstrong World Industries, the ceiling product manufacturer. We just share a name and a love of good ceilings.

Who we are

Drywall is the whole business.

We are a drywall contractor based at 3737 Redthorne Dr in Amelia, Ohio, serving Clermont County and the Greater Cincinnati area.

We don't do plumbing, electrical, or roofing. We hang, tape, finish, texture, and repair drywall and ceilings. That narrow focus means the crew on your job does this work every day, and the finish shows it.

  • Dust control and floor protection on every job
  • Finishes checked in raking light before we call it done
  • Written estimates, clear schedules, full cleanup

What to expect

1. Measure. We walk the job, measure everything, and note access, heights, and existing conditions.

2. Written estimate. You get an itemized price in writing. No verbal guesses, no surprise add-ons.

3. Clean work. Protected floors, controlled dust, and a crew that treats your home like a job site, not a construction zone.

4. Final walk. We check every seam in raking light with you before we pack up.
