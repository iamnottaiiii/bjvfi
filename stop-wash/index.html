How it works

STEP 1

Sort and load

Bring your laundry, pick a washer that fits the load, and add detergent.

STEP 2

Wash and dry

Run the cycle, then move clothes to a dryer while you wait or step out.

STEP 3

Fold and go

Fold at the counter, bag it up, and head home with clean laundry.
