Our story

Worcester's full-service shop

Headliners Barbershop and Salon brings barbershop culture and salon craft together on Grafton Street.

How we work

Quality service at an honest price

The shop's mission is simple: quality service at an affordable price, from a team of certified barbers and stylists who genuinely love hair.

With a large crew on the floor, Headliners handles walk-ins all day while still giving regulars the option to book their favorite barber or stylist by name. Reviewers call out the crew by name, from crispy fades to patient kids cuts.

It is a community shop in the truest sense: laid-back, welcoming, and proud to serve Worcester families of every background and hair type.

The Headliners promise

  • Certified barbers and stylists on every chair
  • Barbershop and salon services in one visit
  • Walk-ins welcome seven days a week
  • Family friendly, from toddlers to grandparents
