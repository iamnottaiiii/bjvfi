Know the signs

Time to call an HVAC tech?

If any of these sound familiar, your system is asking for attention.

!

Weak or warm airflowVents blowing, but the house never quite gets comfortable.

!

Strange noises or smellsBanging, grinding, or musty odors coming from the unit or ducts.

!

Rising energy billsCosts climbing even though your usage has not changed.

!

Short cyclingThe system turns on and off constantly instead of running steady cycles.

!

Uneven temperaturesSome rooms freezing while others stay warm.

!

System age 12 plus yearsOlder equipment breaks down more and costs more to run.

AC Repair and Service

Georgia summers do not wait, and neither should you. When your air conditioning struggles, we diagnose the real cause and get cold air moving again.

  • No cool or weak cooling diagnosis
  • Refrigerant level checks and leak detection
  • Capacitor, contactor, and electrical repairs
  • Condensate drain clearing and float switch service
  • Thermostat troubleshooting and replacement
  • Heat pump cooling mode service

Heating Repair and Service

Cold snaps hit fast. We keep furnaces and heat pumps running safely so your home stays warm all winter.

  • Furnace no heat diagnosis and repair
  • Heat pump heating mode service
  • Ignition, burner, and blower repairs
  • Safety and carbon monoxide checks
  • Thermostat calibration and replacement
  • Airflow and duct performance checks
