Recurring home cleaning

The regular visit that keeps your home consistently fresh.

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, appliance exteriors, spot clean cabinets
  • Bathrooms: toilets, tubs and showers, mirrors, fixtures shined
  • All rooms: dusting, vacuuming, mopping hard floors

Top to bottom

Deep cleaning

A detailed reset for the whole home, inside and out of the usual routine.

  • Everything in a recurring clean, plus baseboards and door frames
  • Grout lines, tile edges and hard to reach corners detailed
  • Inside microwave, oven fronts and fridge exteriors wiped down
  • Window sills, blinds and light fixtures dusted
