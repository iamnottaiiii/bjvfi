About

A masonry specialist, not a middleman.

Gamon Construction Co., INC. is based at 4101 Avenue J in Brooklyn and has focused on masonry work for close to twenty years. Business directories describe the company as a general contractor specializing in masonry, with projects ranging from $2,000 to $1,500,000 performed within the five boroughs and Westchester for select construction managers and private owners.

The firm is listed as a minority business enterprise certified firm in NY business directories. Its documented job history includes large masonry contracts for established builders, the kind of repeat work that only comes from getting the details right.
