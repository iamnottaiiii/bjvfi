What we do

More than a paintbrush

Prep is everything. We patch, sand, caulk, and prime before a drop of paint goes on, so the finish lasts for years.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and cabinets. We help you pick the perfect shade, then protect your floors and furniture like they are our own.

  • Drywall patching, crack and hole repair
  • Two full coats on every project
  • Baseboards, casings, and door refinishing

Exterior painting

Stucco repair, pressure washing, and waterproof caulking around every window before we spray or roll a weatherproof finish.

  • Stucco patching and crack repair
  • Full pressure wash and gutter cleaning
  • Gutters, fascia, and trim included

Flooring and trim

Finish the room completely: laminate and vinyl flooring, baseboards, casing, and door hardware, installed and painted to match.

  • Laminate and vinyl plank installation
  • Baseboard, casing, and molding
  • Carpet removal available

Why SunShine
