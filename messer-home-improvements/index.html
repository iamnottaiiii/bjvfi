About

Meet Larry

Messer Home Improvements is a one-man home repair shop in Newport, Kentucky, built on showing up and doing honest work.

One person. One phone number. One standard.

Larry runs Messer Home Improvements the old-fashioned way: he answers his own phone, looks at the job himself, and gives you a straight price. No sales team, no upsells, no mystery.

Public reviews paint a consistent picture. Customers describe him as honest about what he can and cannot do, prompt to the point of showing up early, and fair on price. Landlords trust him with tenant work. Neighbors recommend him to family.

From gutter cleanouts to porch steps to a fresh coat of paint, the through line is care: the small jobs get the same attention as the big ones, because for Larry there are no small jobs.
