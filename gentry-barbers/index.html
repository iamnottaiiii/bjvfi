About

The story of Gentry Barbers

Gentry Barbers, also known as Arnold's Gentry Barbers, is a straightforward shop on Paramount Boulevard in Downey. Barbers Phil and Arnold keep the focus on quick, inexpensive, no-nonsense haircuts.

Located at 12248 Paramount Blvd, Downey, CA 90242, the shop serves Downey and the surrounding neighborhoods. Walk-ins and regulars are both welcome, and the team is happy to talk through the cut before the clippers come out.
