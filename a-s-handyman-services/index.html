Our Story

The handyman Butler calls when it has to be right

A/S handyman services started with a simple idea: most homes do not need a big contractor, they need someone skilled who actually shows up. From our base on Carbon Street in Butler, we have built our name one fixed door, one patched wall, and one happy neighbor at a time.

We are a local, independent shop. When you call (724) 822-2888, you talk to the people who will do the work. That means honest timelines, fair pricing, and craftsmanship we would put our own name on, because we do.

Whether it is a single leaky faucet or a whole house punch list before a sale, we bring the same care to every job.
