About

A local landscaping business in New Cumberland, Pennsylvania.

Hmt Lawn Care & Design is based at 705 3rd St in New Cumberland, PA, serving yards across the West Shore and the Harrisburg area. The business is listed as a landscaping company, with gardening work among its listed categories.

The approach is simple: show up, do the work carefully, and leave every yard better than it was found. Mowing is done at a proper height, edges are cut clean, and design projects start with a plan that fits the property instead of a one-size-fits-all layout.

"Do the work carefully, and leave every yard better than it was found."

Why local matters

A local crew knows the soil, the seasons, and the grass types that grow well around Cumberland County. That knowledge shows up in healthier lawns and beds that thrive instead of just surviving.
