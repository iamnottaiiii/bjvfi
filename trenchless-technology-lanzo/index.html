About

Trenchless Technology Lanzo is connected with the Lanzo family of companies. Per the Lanzo website, Lanzo Construction was founded in Roseville, Michigan in 1968 by Quirino "Gary" D'Alessandro, and Lanzo Trenchless Technologies grew from Lanzo Lining Services, formed in 1993 to rehabilitate aging pipelines.

Trenchless methods include cured-in-place pipe lining, sequential carbon fiber wrap and structural polyurea, with projects totaling more than nine million linear feet of lining in pipes from 4 to 144 inches in diameter.
