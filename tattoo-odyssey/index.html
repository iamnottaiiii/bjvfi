Services

What the shop does

The shop describes its full service as custom, traditional, and cover-up tattoos. Styles named in client reviews and listings include traditional, neo-traditional, black and grey, and color.

Custom tattoos

Original designs drawn for you. Gil is praised for listening carefully and delivering exactly what clients envisioned, often exceeding it.

Traditional

Bold classic work in the American traditional vein, built to hold up for decades.

Cover-ups

Old ink reworked into new art. Clients bring twenty year old pieces in for a facelift and leave with work they love again.

Black and grey

Smooth shading and fine detail, from portraits to illustrative pieces.

Color

Vivid color work that clients say still looks amazing ten and twelve years later.

Body piercing

The shop is listed as a tattoo and piercing shop in business directories.

Gil

Clients call Gil an amazing, detailed artist and a fine line talent. Reviewers credit him with dragon reworks, twin dragon back pieces, a musical tattoo with notes that spell DAD, and tattoos that still look amazing a decade later. One client writes he has been going to Gil for twenty years.
