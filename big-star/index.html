About

A Boston cleaning company since 1998

Big Star began serving the community in 1998 from Everett, Massachusetts, and has spent 28 years doing one thing well: keeping commercial spaces clean. Today the company serves the entire Greater Boston area with a team of nine in-house employees and zero subcontractors.

That structure is deliberate. When the people who quote your job are backed by the same crew that cleans it, quality stays consistent and accountability stays simple. Clients get a 5.0-star experience on Angi, free estimates, and pricing with no travel charges and no after-hours charges.
