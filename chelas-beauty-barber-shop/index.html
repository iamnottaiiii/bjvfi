Our story

A family behind
every chair.

Chela's Beauty & Barber Shop is run by Chela and her daughters Angela and Susie. For more than 15 years they have been cutting hair in San Pedro, building the kind of trust that turns first-timers into regulars and regulars into family.

How we work

Listen first. Cut second.

Guests say it again and again: the stylists here ask questions before they begin, so you and your barber are on the same page about what you are expecting. That simple habit is why parents trust them with their kids, why seniors feel at home, and why so many clients have been coming for a decade or more.

Walk-ins welcome

No appointment.
No problem.

Busy day? Just walk in. Chela's runs on a walk-in basis, so you can get a great cut on your schedule. The shop keeps things spotless too, with chairs and tools cleaned after every guest.
