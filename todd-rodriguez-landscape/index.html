About

Rooted in Woodland Hills

Todd Rodriguez Landscape is a landscape design and gardening service based in the heart of Woodland Hills, California.

i

A designer, not just a mower

The focus is on how the whole landscape works together: plants, beds, lawn, and layout, planned as one composition.

Built for Valley conditions

Hot summers, clay soils, and water awareness shape every recommendation, so plantings survive and thrive.

iii

One point of contact

Design, installation, and ongoing care from the same local team, so nothing gets lost between visits.
