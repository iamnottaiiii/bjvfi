Our story

Fabricators first, salespeople never

Renovva Stone Master is a stone fabrication and installation company based in San Fernando, California, with showrooms and project footprints across Southern California, including Calabasas and Thousand Oaks.

Their own site says it plainly: the warehouse holds nearly 250 slabs and the showroom carries more than 40 top brands, from Cambria and Silestone to Vadara and MSI. That inventory is the point. You pick real stone in person instead of choosing from a swatch book and hoping.

On Thumbtack, where the company holds Top Pro status for 2024 and 2025, the profile describes a simple standard: quality first, no shortcuts, even when it takes longer. The license is verified as a California B General Building Contractor, the background check is passed, and every project carries a one-year labor warranty.

Reviewers describe an easy showroom experience and an owner who picks up the phone and makes things right. That is the whole business model: real slabs, careful fabrication, and standing behind the install.
