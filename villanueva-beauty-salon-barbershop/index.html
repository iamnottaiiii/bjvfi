Our Story

A unisex shop for the whole block

Villanueva Beauty Salon & Barbershop sits on Willis Avenue in Mott Haven, serving the Bronx as a true unisex shop. Where many storefronts pick a lane, barber or salon, Villanueva keeps both chairs turning.

The idea is simple: a household should be able to handle every appointment in one visit. He gets a fade and a beard shape up. She gets a cut, color, and nails. The kids get patient first cuts. Everyone leaves from the same door on Willis Ave.

Reviewers describe the shop as an excellent unisex place for women and men, and the service list reads like a full beauty menu: cortes de dama y caballero, tintes, permanentes, manicure, pedicure, unas, aplicacion de pestanas, and depilaciones.
