Our story

A Harrisburg yard built for people who dig in the dirt

Sunrise Farms Landscape Co started as a working landscape operation and grew into the supply yard neighbors rely on. Sitting on Atticks Lane in Harrisburg, the yard serves everyone from first time gardeners to seasoned contractors running multiple crews.

The idea has always been simple: stock quality material, price it fairly, and give straight advice. When you pull in, you are talking to people who load trucks, spread mulch, and plant beds for a living.

Whether you are refreshing one flower bed or breaking ground on a full landscape, we will help you get the material right the first time.
