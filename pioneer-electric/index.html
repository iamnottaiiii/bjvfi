Our Story

A family trade, done the honest way

Established in 1990, Pioneer Electric is a family owned and operated electrical contractor serving Burbank and the surrounding San Fernando Valley. For more than 30 years we have wired homes, apartment complexes, commercial spaces and industrial sites with the same simple model: the customer comes first.

Owner Haik leads every job with old school work ethic. We arrive on time, keep the work area clean, explain the work in plain language and guarantee what we install. No surprises, no shortcuts, just fair pricing and craftsmanship our customers recommend to their neighbors.

That is why so many of our customers have called us for years. When you find an electrician you trust, you keep the number. Ours is (818) 221-5240.
