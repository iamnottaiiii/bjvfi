Our story

Digging in Northern Kentucky since 1999

Since 1999, Brass Eagle Excavating has been providing a valuable service to the area at a great price. It is a locally owned and operated company, and the crew takes real pride in the work.

The whole excavation process is handled in house: dirt, sand and gravel removal and transportation, swamp or pond removal, and residential or commercial excavation, all at competitive prices.

The company describes itself in five words: fast, professional, knowledgeable, reliable, and honest. If you are looking for a dependable excavation company with years in the business, that is the pitch.
