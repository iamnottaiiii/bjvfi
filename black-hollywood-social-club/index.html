Our Story

Built like a social club, run like a studio

On South Western Avenue in Los Angeles, Black Hollywood Social Club grew into something bigger than a barbershop: a creative meeting ground for the neighborhood and the industry alike.

I

The room

A space where clients relax, connect, and even work while waiting. Free wi-fi, good energy, and a crew that treats newcomers like regulars from day one.

II

The craft

Barbers known by name and reputation: sharp line-ups, clean fades, and an eye for detail that has clients booking weekly. Stylist Roni handles cuts, color, and styling.

III

The community

Black-owned and women-owned, open to everybody. From first-time visitors who found it on Google reviews to locals who never go anywhere else, it is a neighborhood institution.

Also in the family

The BHSC name also runs a DTLA location at 128 W 7th St, same phone, same standard. Downtown regulars, that one is for you.
