About

Norris Brothers Co is a Cleveland industrial contractor established in 1867. The company describes its work as moving the impossible every day.

Services include machinery moving and erecting, rigging, heavy transportation, warehousing, concrete installation, and equipment installation and maintenance. The company also operates norrisbr.com.
