Why it matters

Your attic decides how hard your AC works.

In the Valley, summer heat pours through an under-insulated attic straight into your living space. The right insulation slows it down.

Heat rises, then lingers

An attic can run far hotter than the rooms below. Without enough insulation, that heat radiates down all evening.

Old insulation settles

Insulation compresses and thins over the years. If you can see the tops of your joists, coverage is probably thin.

Comfort you can feel

Even temperatures room to room, a quieter house, and an HVAC system that cycles less. That is the payoff.

Services

Attic work, done properly.

Typical attic services we offer. Every home is different, so call to confirm what fits yours.

Blown-in insulation

Loose-fill fiberglass or cellulose blown to an even depth across the attic floor. The best fit for topping up existing insulation and reaching tight corners.

  • Fiberglass or cellulose loose-fill
  • Even depth to target R-value
  • Fills gaps around pipes and wiring
  • Fast install, usually one day

Batt insulation

Pre-cut fiberglass batts fitted snugly between joists and rafters. A clean, predictable option for open, accessible attics.

  • Fiberglass batts and rolls
  • Fitted between joists, no gaps
  • Kraft or unfaced, as the job needs
  • Good for new and retrofit work

Air sealing

Insulation slows heat; air sealing stops the drafts. We seal the gaps around lights, pipes, ducts, and the attic hatch before insulation goes in.

  • Sealing around can lights and fixtures
  • Pipe, duct, and wire penetrations
  • Attic hatch weatherstripping
  • Top plates and chase openings

Radiant barrier

A reflective layer that bounces radiant heat back out of the attic. Especially effective in Southern California, where the sun does most of the damage.

  • Reflective foil under the roof deck
  • Cuts radiant heat gain in summer
  • Pairs well with blown-in insulation
  • Keeps attic temps noticeably lower

Removal and cleanup

Old, compressed, or contaminated insulation has to come out before the new layer goes in. We remove it, clean the space, and leave it ready.

  • Old insulation vacuum removal
  • Debris and droppings cleanup
  • Sanitizing before reinstall
  • Vent and soffit check
