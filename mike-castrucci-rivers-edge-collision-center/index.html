About Rivers Edge

The Shop

Mike Castrucci Rivers Edge Collision Center is the collision repair arm of the Mike Castrucci dealer group in Milford, Ohio. The shop sits at 691 US-50, near the East Fork of the Little Miami River, alongside the group's Ford and Chevrolet dealerships.

Public listings describe it as a trusted auto repair shop specializing in collision repair, with a commitment to customer satisfaction and reliable repairs. Reviewers repeatedly praise the knowledgeable, friendly staff, thorough damage assessments, regular updates, and vehicles returned in showroom condition.
