01 · The problem

Lint hides where you cannot see it

The lint trap catches the easy stuff. The fine stuff sails right past it and packs into the vent line, year after year. That buildup chokes airflow, so clothes stay damp, the dryer overheats, and energy bills creep up.

Worst case, it is a fire starter. The U.S. Fire Administration links thousands of home fires a year to clothes dryers, and lint buildup is the top cause.

How we got here

We saw the problem too many times to ignore

We started Daxon after seeing the same story over and over: a dryer that takes two cycles, a laundry room that runs hot, a vent line packed solid with lint nobody knew was there. It is a quiet hazard, and it is completely preventable.

So we built the whole business around doing this one job properly. Full cleaning, real airflow measurements, honest quotes, tidy work. That is the entire business plan.

Our promise

If the numbers do not improve, the cleaning is on us

Every visit ends with an airflow test. If the reading does not get meaningfully better, we rework the line until it does, or we do not charge for the cleaning. It is that simple.

We would rather talk you out of a visit you do not need than sell you one you do. Call and describe the symptoms; we will tell you straight whether your vent needs us.
