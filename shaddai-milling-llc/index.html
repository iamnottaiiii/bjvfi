What milling does

The right base makes the road last

Asphalt milling removes the worn top layer of pavement without disturbing the sub-base, leaving a clean, level surface ready for fresh asphalt. It is faster and more cost-effective than full removal, and the milled material is recyclable.

Why us

Straightforward paving prep, done right

Road work lives or dies on preparation. A properly milled surface bonds better, drains better and holds its grade longer, which means fewer callbacks and a longer-lasting road.

We keep the process simple: walk the site, price it clearly, mill it clean, and leave the area ready for the paving crew.

About the company

Local crew, heavy equipment, honest work

Good roads start below the surface. Shaddai Milling focuses on the part of road construction that determines how long a pavement lasts: careful, precise milling and preparation before the asphalt goes down.
