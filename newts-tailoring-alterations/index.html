What we take in

Bring it on a hanger if you can. We will tell you honestly what it needs and what it will cost before any pin goes in.

1.

Hemming

Pants, jeans, skirts, dresses and sleeves, finished to match the original stitch where it matters.

2.

Resizing

Taking in or letting out shirts, slacks and dresses so off-the-rack finally sits right.

3.

Repairs

Torn seams, open hems, zippers and patches, fixed so the garment goes back to work.

4.

Custom Fits

Adjustments shaped to your frame, the difference between worn and worn well.

5.

Formal and Wedding Wear

Suits, dresses and bridesmaid gowns fitted on a schedule that respects the event date.

6.

Uniforms and Workwear

Customers have trusted us with their military uniforms for years, plus the everyday work wardrobe.
