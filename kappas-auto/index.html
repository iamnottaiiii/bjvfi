Our Story

One mechanic,
one garage, one way of doing things.

Kappas Auto is a main-street garage on Duss Ave in Ambridge, built on a simple idea: tell people the truth about their cars.

Pete runs Kappas Auto the old way. When you bring your car in, he looks at it, tells you what is wrong, explains what the fix involves, and gives you a fair price. Then he does the work, and he stays in contact with you the whole time so you always know what is happening and why.

Customers say he will show you the worn part, teach you what it does, and talk you out of work you do not need. That is why whole families have trusted him for twenty years, and why the reviews sound like they were written by friends.

"Pete is always straightforward and honest. He stays in contact with you so you understand why and what he is doing at all times, so you are not surprised when you get your bill."
