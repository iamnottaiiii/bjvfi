Services

What Strong Arm installs and repairs

Pick a trade to see what is included. Every job starts with a free estimate from the owner.

5″ and 6″ seamless gutter installation

Continuous aluminum gutters formed on site to your exact roofline. The 6 inch profile handles heavier Long Island downpours and pairs well with larger roofs.

  • On site roll forming, no factory seams
  • Hidden hanger system, screwed not spiked
  • Proper pitch set for full drainage
  • Colors to match trim, siding, and brick

Leaders, downspouts, and gutter guards

Leaders sized to the gutter system carry water safely away from the foundation. Gutter guards cut cleaning frequency and keep debris out.

  • Leader placement planned for drainage away from the home
  • Elbows and extensions routed around landscaping
  • Gutter guard options to reduce clogs

Gutter cleaning and repair

Seasonal cleanouts and honest repairs: resealed corners, re-pitched runs, replaced sections, and storm damage fixes.

  • Full debris removal and downspout flushing
  • Leak sealing at corners and end caps
  • Re-securing loose or pulling gutters
  • Storm and ladder damage repair

Siding, soffits, fascia, and capping

The surfaces your gutters attach to matter. We replace rotted fascia, install siding, and wrap trim in aluminum capping so the whole roofline lasts.

  • Vinyl and fiber cement siding installation
  • Soffit and fascia board replacement
  • Aluminum capping over wood trim
  • Power washing for siding, decks, and driveways

Roofing and trim carpentry

Gutters only work when the roof edge is sound. We handle shingle roofing and the carpentry behind it, from drip edge to fascia.

  • Shingle roof installation and repair
  • Drip edge and ice and water shield
  • Fascia and rake board carpentry
  • Coordinated gutter and roof projects

Not sure what you need?

Describe the symptom: overflow in heavy rain, water behind the gutter, peeling paint on the fascia, or a section pulling away. The owner will diagnose it and quote it free.

Our story

Strong Arm: built on the roofline

Strong Arm Construction is an owner owned and operated construction company based at 29 Phipps Ave in East Rockaway, NY. The name on the truck is the person who answers the phone, measures your roofline, and stands behind the work.

The specialty is 5 inch and 6 inch seamless gutters and leaders, installed to what the company calls the highest standard: formed on site, hung on hidden hangers, and pitched to drain. Around that core, the crew handles the full exterior envelope, including siding, roofing, soffits, fascia board, aluminum capping, and power washing.

Strong Arm holds a Nassau County Home Improvement Contractors license. Public contractor records give the company a BuildZoom score of 93, placing it in the top 27% of licensed New York contractors, and its Google profile carries a 5.0 rating.
