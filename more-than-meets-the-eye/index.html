About the studio

Not always as they seem:
rooms with hidden depth

More Than Meets the Eye began with a simple belief, the best rooms are the ones that reveal themselves slowly.

Our story

Designed around
the life inside

We are an interior design studio in Villa Hills, Kentucky, working with homeowners across Northern Kentucky and Greater Cincinnati. Our name is our promise: we look past the obvious and design for the way you actually live.

Some designers decorate. We study. How you gather, where the light falls at breakfast, which chair everyone fights over. Then we build the room around those truths, with color, texture, and pieces chosen to last.

We love mixing the new with the storied: a tailored sofa beside a weathered antique, modern art over a vintage chest. The contrast is where the magic lives, and where a house starts to feel like it could only be yours.

We design for the Tuesday mornings, not just the dinner parties.
The studio

Lived-in, not looked-at

Beautiful matters, but livable matters more. Every choice has to survive real life: kids, pets, guests, and quiet Sundays.

Honest guidance

We will tell you what is worth the investment and what is not, and we will never push a trend that does not suit you.
