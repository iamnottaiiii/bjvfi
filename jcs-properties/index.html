Our Story

A Cherry Grove fixture since 2004

JCS Properties has been operating in the Anderson Township business community since 2004, most notably in the area known as Cherry Grove. The building at 8595 Beechmont Ave was built in 1973 and has stood as a prominent structure and business environment in the neighborhood, and it continues to earn that reputation today.

1973

The building at 8595 Beechmont Ave goes up in Cherry Grove, a prominent new business address on one of Anderson Township's busiest corridors.

2004

JCS Properties begins operating in the Anderson Township business community, managing and leasing office space in the building.

Today

Decades of tenants later, the building remains a well kept, locally managed home for professional offices, with on-site management and a Cherry Grove address tenants are proud to print on their cards.
