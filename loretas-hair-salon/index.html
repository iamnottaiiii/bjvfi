Our story

A neighborhood chair
with a personal touch

Loreta's Hair Salon has been part of the Richmond Ave corridor in Staten Island, a place where clients become regulars and regulars become friends.

Why we do it

Hair is personal.
So are we.

A salon is not just chairs and mirrors. It is the place you come before the wedding, after the breakup, and every six weeks in between. We treat that trust seriously.

Loreta's was built on a simple idea: take the time, do the consultation, and never let a client leave unsure. The result is a loyal neighborhood following on Staten Island's Richmond Ave, and a chair that regulars refuse to give up.

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, you get the same care: honest advice about what your hair needs, skilled hands, and a finish you are proud to wear home.

Listen first, cut second. Great hair starts with understanding the person in the chair.

The Loreta's way

Clients first

We would rather talk you out of a service that is wrong for your hair than take your money for it.

Skilled hands

From classic cuts to modern balayage, our stylists keep their techniques sharp and current.

Neighborhood roots
