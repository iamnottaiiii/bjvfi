About us

A cleaning company
with its name on the door

C Ruiz Cleaning Company is based in North Plainfield, NJ, and built on a simple idea: do the job right, every single visit.

When our name is on the door, our name is on the work. That is the whole philosophy: show up on time, clean like it is our own place, and leave every room better than we found it.

We keep our checklists consistent and our standards high, so the tenth visit looks just like the first. No cutting corners, no rushing out the door.
