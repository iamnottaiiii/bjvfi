Rooted on Ninth Street

Elegancia Barber Shop serves the neighborhood from 550 N Ninth St in Allentown. It is a shop built on relationships: clients who first came in as kids now bring their own children to the same chairs.

The name says it all. Elegancia means elegance, and that shows in the details: crisp lines, careful fades, and a calm, respectful atmosphere where every customer is treated well.
