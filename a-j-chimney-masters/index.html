The business

A J Chimney Masters is a roofing contractor based at 9426 Telfair Ave in Sun Valley, California. The business focuses on the two systems that meet at the top of every house: the chimney and the roof around it.

That pairing matters. A leaking chimney is often a flashing problem, and a stained ceiling near a fireplace can be either system. A crew that handles both can diagnose the real cause in one visit instead of sending you between two contractors.
