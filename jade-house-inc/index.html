Our story

Quiet work since 1974.

i.

Rooted in Monrovia

Jade House Inc has been a California corporation since 1974, operating from 407 W Maple Ave in Monrovia. We are a small, local outfit, and we like it that way: it keeps us close to every project and every client.

ii.

How we work

We take on a limited number of projects at a time. One point of contact, plain-language updates, tidy job sites, and a final walkthrough where nothing is rushed. If something is not right, we make it right.

iii.

What we believe

Good construction is quiet. It does not call attention to itself; it simply works, year after year. We choose materials and methods for the long term, not the listing photo.
