Our story

A small crew with a careful standard.

M & T landscaping services LLC started with a simple idea: do the job the way it was agreed, on time, and treat every yard like it is your own. That means walking the property before we start, protecting the plantings that matter to you, and finishing the day with a yard that looks better than we found it.

Our review history says it best. A first-time customer wrote that we were the first landscapers so careful and meticulous that not a single fragile new sprout or bloom was damaged, and that their yard was the most beautiful it had ever been. We hold every job to that bar.
