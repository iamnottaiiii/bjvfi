Chimney sweeps

Creosote is the tar like residue of wood smoke, and it is the leading cause of chimney fires. A yearly sweep removes it along with soot, nesting debris and blockages, and keeps your fireplace drafting the way it should.

  • Full flue and liner brushing with rotary equipment
  • Smoke chamber, shelf, firebox and damper
  • Home protection: sealed opening, drop cloths, HEPA vacuums
  • Wood stoves, fireplaces and pellet units
PricingSweep visits are quoted per chimney and condition. Call for a same day estimate. Prices shown on this page are samples, not final quotes.

Chimney inspections

Whether you just bought a New Haven home, had a chimney fire, or simply have not looked up the flue in years, an inspection gives you a clear read on condition and what needs attention first.

  • Visual inspection included with every sweep
  • Flue tile, mortar joint and liner condition
  • Crown, flashing and exterior masonry review
  • Written findings with prioritized recommendations
PricingStandalone inspection pricing depends on roof access and chimney height. Call for a quote. Sample prices only.

Masonry and structural repairs

Freeze thaw cycles are brutal on brick. We repoint failing mortar, rebuild deteriorated crowns, replace spalling brick and shore up leaning stacks before small damage becomes a rebuild.

  • Tuckpointing and mortar joint repair
  • Crown rebuilds and waterproofing
  • Brick replacement and partial rebuilds
  • Flashing repair to stop roof leaks
PricingRepair work is quoted on site after inspection. Sample pricing on this page is illustrative only.

Chimney caps and crowns

An uncapped chimney is an open invitation to rain, raccoons, nesting birds and downdrafts. We fit stainless and copper caps sized to your flue, and rebuild cracked crowns so water sheds instead of soaking in.

  • Stainless steel and copper chimney caps
  • Animal and rain entry prevention
  • Crown rebuilds with proper drip edges
  • Draft improving cap options for smoky fireplaces
PricingCap pricing varies by flue size and material. Call for a quote. Sample prices only.

Flue relining

Cracked or missing flue tiles let heat and gases escape into the structure of your home. We install stainless steel liners that restore a safe, correctly sized flue for your fireplace or stove.

  • Stainless steel liner installation
  • Right sized for fireplace, wood stove or insert
  • Insulated liner options for cold exterior chimneys
  • Full inspection and draft test after install
PricingRelining is quoted per job after measuring the flue. Call for an on site assessment. Sample pricing only.
