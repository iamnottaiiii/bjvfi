Good to know

Before you hire any builder

Check the license

Ask for the CSLB license number and verify it at cslb.ca.gov before signing anything. It takes two minutes.

Get it in writing

Scope, price, payment schedule and timeline belong in a signed contract. Verbal promises are not a plan.

Expect the permit talk

Structural, electrical and plumbing work needs permits and inspections in Los Angeles. A builder who skips them is a red flag.
