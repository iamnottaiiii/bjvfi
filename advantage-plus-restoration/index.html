Our story

Built on exteriors.

Advantage Plus Restoration is a Cincinnati contractor focused on the outside of your home: siding, gutters, roofing, and the restoration work that keeps everything weather-tight.

We're based on Beechmont Avenue in Anderson Township, working across the Greater Cincinnati area. As a general contracting shop, we take on the multi-part jobs too: when a storm tears up the siding and the gutters with it, one call covers the whole repair.

Our approach is simple: look at the job, quote it straight, and do the work like it's our own house.
