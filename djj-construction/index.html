About

The neighbor
with the toolbox

DJJ Construction is a handyman outfit based on Halsey St in Granada Hills, CA, helping homes run right.

Our way

Show up, fix it, clean up

No upsells, no mystery pricing. Just honest repair work and a tidy job site when we leave.

DJJ Construction works out of Granada Hills, serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley. The business is built on the simple idea that small jobs deserve the same care as big ones: the door that sticks, the wall that needs patching, the carpet that has seen better days.

Because we also do carpet cleaning, one visit can cover both the repair list and the refresh. Bundle the work, save the trip.

On time

We give you a window and keep it. Your day matters as much as the job.

Straight pricing

You know the price before we start. No surprises when the work is done.

Done right

We treat your home like our own and stand behind the work we leave behind.
