About

Family owned for over 24 years

The shop, the standard, and the people behind the wrenches.

JRM Auto Service, Inc. is family owned and managed, and it shows. For over 24 years, drivers across North Bergen have brought their cars to 5209 John F. Kennedy Blvd for work they can trust.

Owner Jose and technicians like Junior lead a team known for professionalism and attention to detail. Reviewers call the environment professional and welcoming, the pricing competitive, and the service quick, often completed ahead of schedule.
