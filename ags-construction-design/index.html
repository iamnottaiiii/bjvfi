Our story

A Van Nuys contractor built around design

AGS Construction & Design is a general contractor on Victory Boulevard in Van Nuys, California. Per business directory listings, the company was established in 2013 and provides construction service and architectural design for both residential and commercial clients.

The idea behind the name is simple: construction and design should not be two separate vendors pointing at each other. When the people who draw the plans work alongside the people who build them, estimates are honest, schedules hold, and the finished space matches the vision.

Today the team serves Van Nuys and the wider Los Angeles area, from single room remodels to additions, ADUs, and commercial build outs.
