About CutMasters of Lathrup Village

CutMasters of Lathrup Village is a family friendly barbershop at 28630 Southfield Rd, Suite 202C. Appointments are preferred and walk-ins are welcome when chairs are open. The shop is known for precision line work, shear cuts, and hot towel finishes.
