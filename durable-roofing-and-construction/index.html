Our Story

Durable Roofing and Construction is a Pasadena roofing contractor at 1308 E Colorado Blvd. The name says the plan: roofs and the construction work around them, built to last and priced straight.

Roofing jobs often uncover more than a bad shingle: rotted decking, tired fascia, flashing that never sealed right. A combined roofing and construction crew means those finds get fixed by the same people, instead of handed off to someone new.

Our approach stays simple: inspect thoroughly, write the scope down, do the work clean, and stand behind it. No pressure, no mystery line items.
