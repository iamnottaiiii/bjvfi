Nails that keep up with your week.

KT Nail is a neighborhood nail salon on Hylan Boulevard in Staten Island, the kind of place where a quick polish change and a full set both get the same careful attention. Manicures, pedicures, gel, acrylic and nail art are all on the menu, so whether you are stopping in on a lunch break or settling in for a full afternoon reset, you leave with hands that look finished.

A good salon visit is part craft, part calm. The team at KT Nail focuses on clean, healthy nail care first, then the color and design you came in for. Walk-ins are welcome whenever there is a free chair, and appointments keep things quick for regulars. Call (718) 447-9900 to book your chair.

Our story

KT Nail is a local nail salon in the heart of Staten Island, built on careful work and clients who come back.

Our approach

Healthy nails first, pretty nails always

A beautiful set starts with healthy natural nails underneath. That means careful cuticle work, proper prep, and honest advice about what your nails can handle.

From a simple polish change to a full acrylic set with hand painted art, every service gets the same patience. No rushing, no shortcuts, just nails that look great when you walk out and still look great two weeks later.

Care

Sanitation matters

Clean tools and fresh liners for every client, every time. Your health comes before anything else.
