Abrasive blasting

The core service: pressurized abrasive media strips unwanted material off a surface without cutting into the base. Faster than grinding, cleaner than chemicals.

  • Steel beams, tanks and structural steel
  • Trailers, truck beds and farm equipment
  • Fences, railings and gates
  • Concrete and masonry surfaces
Free project quotes: pricing depends on material, size and condition. Call (301) 523-1632 and describe the job.

Rust and paint removal

Rust and peeling paint are signs the coating failed. Blasting removes both completely, so the new finish bonds to clean metal instead of loose rust.

  • Heavy rust and scale taken to bare metal
  • Peeling, chalking or failed paint stripped off
  • Even finish profile, ready for primer
  • No chemical strippers, no residue
Good to know: blasting reveals the true condition of the metal. If a section is too far gone, we will tell you straight.

Surface preparation

Most coating failures start with bad prep. Blasting creates the clean, textured surface profile that primers and paints are designed to grip.

  • Prep for industrial paint and epoxy
  • Concrete prep for sealers and coatings
  • Mill scale removal on new steel
  • Containment to protect surrounding areas
Timing matters: freshly blasted steel should be coated promptly. Ask about scheduling prep and paint together.

About

The crew behind the blast

Gutierrez Cleaning Services is a blast cleaning service based in Upper Marlboro, Maryland, built around one idea: do the prep right, and everything after it lasts longer.

Straightforward by design

This is a phone-first, weekday operation. You call, describe the job, and get a straight quote. No upsells, no mystery line items.

We show up with the blasting rig, strip the surface clean, contain the mess, and leave the site ready for its next coat. That is the whole business model.

Blasting is honest work: you can see the result the moment the dust settles. We like it that way.

Why prep matters

Paint does not fail on clean steel. It fails on rust, mill scale and old coatings that were never fully removed. Abrasive blasting is the most reliable way to get down to a surface that coatings can actually grip.

Whether it is a rusted trailer, a peeling fence line or a shop floor going under epoxy, the prep decides how long the finish lasts. That is the part we handle.
