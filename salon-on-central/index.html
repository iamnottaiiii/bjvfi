About the Salon

A neighborhood salon with a loyal chair

Salon On Central sits on New York Central Road in Port Byron, New York. In the salon's own words, it is a complete hair care salon, and it also offers sunless spray tanning.

The team is known for listening. Reviewers describe stylists who take their time, explain the plan, and deliver cuts and color that match what was asked for, including patient work with children who can be nervous in the chair.

The space itself gets mentioned again and again: clean, cozy, and welcoming, with reasonable prices that keep families coming back.
