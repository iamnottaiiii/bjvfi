Our Story

The newest lounge
on Ralph Avenue

Avery's Nail Lounge opened its doors in Brooklyn with one goal: to redefine what getting pampered feels like. The idea was simple. Take the nail appointment out of the rushed, fluorescent routine and turn it into a lounge experience: comfortable, unhurried, and built around great customer service.

The lounge offers the full range of modern nail care, from classic manicures and spa pedicures to acrylics, gel extensions, dip powder, Russian manicures, and custom nail art. Everything happens in one welcoming space on Ralph Avenue.

Avery's Nail Lounge LLC has held its New York business license since 2022, and the team keeps building its name one set of nails at a time. Stay tuned: there is more news coming from the lounge.
