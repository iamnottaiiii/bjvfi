About Sheri

Three decades of rooms that work beautifully

Placeholder portrait · Sheri Molder

Sheri Molder (ASID, CID) is a Southern California native who earned her Interior Design degree from the College of Art and Design at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo, then built a practice that has now spanned more than 30 years.

As a professional member of the American Society of Interior Designers, where she has held a board position, and a Certified Interior Designer in the State of California, Sheri has been a participating designer in the Pasadena Showcase House, The Bennett Idea House and the Descanso Gardens Hospitality House Christmas Showcase. Client homes have also been included in the Pasadena ASID Showcase of Kitchens.

Her interiors have been featured in national publications including Home Gym and Fitness, Family Circle, Designers West, Glendale Magazine and Better Homes and Gardens. An avid outdoor enthusiast, she draws inspiration from the Southern California landscape, and her projects range from small scale accessorizing to planning and executing large scale remodels.
