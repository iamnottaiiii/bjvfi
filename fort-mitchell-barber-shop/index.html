About the Shop

What public listings and the community say about this Dixie Highway staple.

From public listings

An Old School Barbershop

Coming from a long line of barbers, brother and sister team Donna and Jack have been in the industry for decades. Their shop on Dixie Highway is an old school barbershop that reflects years of hard work, sweat, snips, and clips, with affordable, clean cuts in a cozy shop filled with vintage memorabilia and classic leather chairs.

The atmosphere is talkative and fast paced, and reviewers consistently mention the vintage decor, the spinning barber pole outside, and a welcoming, no frills experience.
