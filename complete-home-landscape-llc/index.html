About us

A local crew that does water and dirt, start to finish.

Complete Home & Landscape LLC is a pond contractor based in Bath, PA, serving homeowners across the Lehigh Valley.

One company for the pond and the yard around it.

Most pond projects stall because the water part and the landscape part are handled by different people. We built this company to do both. When we design your pond, we are already planning the beds, the edging, and the plantings that will frame it.

The result is a finished yard that looks designed instead of assembled. You deal with one crew, one schedule, and one phone number from the first sketch to the last load of mulch.

We work on residential properties across Bath and the surrounding Lehigh Valley communities. Every project starts with a free on-site consult so we can see the yard, hear what you want, and give you an honest price.
