Our story

The craft
behind the crease

Pressed Dry Cleaning Services is a neighborhood dry cleaner on Calamo St in Springfield, built around one simple standard: everything leaves sharper than it arrived.

Why pressed

Cleaning gets it fresh.
Pressing makes it yours.

Anyone can run a machine. The difference is in the finishing: the hand on the iron, the eye for a collar, the patience to set a pleat so it stays. That is the craft we practice every day.

Bring us your workweek suits, your event dresses, your everyday shirts. We inspect each piece on arrival, treat spots individually, and finish every garment by hand.

Inspected on arrival

Every garment is checked at drop-off. Stains are noted, loose buttons flagged, and special instructions recorded.

Finished by hand

Machine cleaning, human finishing. Collars, cuffs, pleats, and creases get individual attention before bagging.

We treat a ten-dollar shirt with the same care as a tailored suit.

The Pressed standard
