What we do

Services, by what your teeth need

Mid City Dental Group is a general dentistry practice. Pick a category below to see the everyday treatments offered. Specific options and pricing vary by patient, so ask for a written estimate before treatment.

Preventive care

Regular visits keep small issues from becoming big ones. A typical checkup includes an exam, cleaning and a review of what the dentist sees.

  • Dental exams and checkupsA full look at teeth, gums and bite.
  • Professional cleaningsPlaque and tartar removal, polish and floss.
  • Dental x-raysTo see decay and bone health between visits.
  • Fluoride treatmentsExtra enamel protection, especially for kids.

Restorative care

Decay, cracks and worn teeth can be repaired. The dentist will explain which option fits your tooth and your budget.

  • Tooth-colored fillingsDecay removed and filled to match your tooth.
  • Dental crownsA cap that protects a weakened or broken tooth.
  • Root canalsTreatment to save an infected tooth from extraction.
  • ExtractionsRemoval of teeth that cannot be saved.

Cosmetic care

Want a brighter smile for photos, work or just yourself? Ask what is realistic for your teeth before you decide.

  • Professional whiteningIn-office brightening for stained or yellowed teeth.
  • BondingTooth-colored resin to fix chips and small gaps.
  • Veneers consultThin shells over front teeth, ask if you are a candidate.

Dentures and bridges

Missing teeth affect chewing, speech and confidence. Several replacement options exist, and the right one depends on your mouth and budget.

  • Full denturesA complete removable set when all teeth are missing.
  • Partial denturesReplaces several missing teeth, anchored to the rest.
  • Dental bridgesA fixed replacement held by crowns on nearby teeth.

Dental emergencies

Dental pain rarely fixes itself. If you are hurting, call (718) 951-2261 and describe what is happening so the team can advise you.

  • Severe toothacheCall promptly, do not wait for it to pass.
  • Chipped or broken toothSave any pieces and call the office.
  • Knocked-out toothKeep it moist and call immediately, time matters.
  • Lost filling or crownCall to have it checked and re-cemented.
Before you agree to treatment: specific services, materials and pricing vary by patient. Always ask for a written estimate and a clear explanation of your options first.
