About

Sun Valley concrete, built on civil work

A M Concrete Inc is based at 9050 Norris Ave in Sun Valley, California. Public records associate the company with highway, street and bridge construction, the heavy civil side of the concrete trade. That background shapes how the crew approaches every job: engineered base prep, proper reinforcement and finishes built for real traffic.

Today the company serves homeowners and businesses across Sun Valley and the San Fernando Valley with driveways, slabs, precast work, resurfacing, sealing and cleaning.
