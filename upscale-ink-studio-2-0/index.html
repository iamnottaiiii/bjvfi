The atelier

An ink studio built like a private gallery

Upscale Ink built its name in South Jersey on realism, black and grey, cartoon and portrait work in a bright, spotless shop. Studio 2.0 brings that same craft to Gloucester City, in a calm, appointment focused space where your piece gets the time it deserves.

Every tattoo starts with a one on one consultation. Bring references, a rough idea, or just a feeling. Your artist draws for you, sizes for your body, and places with you in the mirror before a single line goes down.

How it works

From first idea to healed art

Consult. Call or walk in to talk through your idea, placement and size with an artist. Deposits secure your appointment date and go toward the final price.

Design. Your artist draws a custom piece for your body. Nothing off a wall unless you want flash. Revisions happen before ink, never after.

Session. Hospital grade hygiene, single use needles, and a calm chair. Aftercare guidance goes home with you in writing.

First timers

Never been inked? Good. Start here.

Most first tattoos at Upscale Ink are small, meaningful, and over before the nerves settle. We will talk placement honestly, including what hurts more, what ages well, and what does not.

You must be 18 or older with a valid photo ID for tattoos. Questions about sizing, pain, or healing? Call the shop and ask for real answers, not a sales pitch.
