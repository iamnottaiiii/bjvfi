About

A neighborhood chair on Liberty Street

New Styles BarberShop sits at 1452 Liberty St in Allentown, PA 18102, serving the neighborhood with straightforward, quality barbering. The shop keeps things simple on purpose: one standard of work, fair treatment, and a chair that feels like your own.

Walk ins are welcome and calling ahead at (484) 743-9377 is the fastest way to lock in a time. The shop is open Monday through Saturday.

If you are new to the area or new to the chair, come by, say hello, and see the standard for yourself.
