Our Salon

A neighborhood salon with loyal clients

Mia Bella Salon sits on 159th Avenue in Howard Beach, Queens, serving clients who come in for regular blowouts and stay for color, cuts, and special occasion styling. The salon holds a New York State appearance enhancement business license.

What clients mention most is the personal attention: stylists who follow the inspiration photos you bring, work with you on color corrections, and keep appointments running on time. Many describe it as fast, friendly, and reasonably priced, the kind of place you recommend to friends.

Whether it is your weekly blowout, a fresh balayage, or hair and makeup for an event, you will be greeted warmly and leave feeling polished.
