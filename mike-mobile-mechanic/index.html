Our story

A shop on wheels,
built for real life.

mike mobile mechanic started with a simple observation: most car repairs do not need a building. They need a good mechanic, the right tools, and a parking spot.

Why we went mobile

Your car broke down.
Why should your day?

The old way: call a tow truck, wait for the tow truck, ride to the shop, wait at the shop, find a ride home, find a ride back. A two-hour brake job eats your entire day and your wallet.

The mobile way: you call, we drive to you, and the brake job happens in your driveway while you answer emails, make lunch, or just stay warm inside. Same quality parts, same careful work, none of the logistics.

We are based on State St in Schenectady, and our service area is anywhere your car happens to be: home, the office lot, the grocery store parking lot, the side of the road. If we can safely reach it, we can fix it there.

We bring the shop to the problem, not the problem to the shop.The whole business model in one sentence.
