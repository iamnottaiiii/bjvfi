About us

A Jefferson family business since 1962

Leahy Excavating Co., Inc. is a family owned business that has been working locally since 1962. From our yard at 1403 Wachusett St in Jefferson, MA, we run backhoes, bulldozers, and dump trucks on residential and commercial job sites across Central Massachusetts.

Our main lines of work are excavating contracting, septic tank installation and service, and topsoil. We are listed among permitted septic installers by local Boards of Health and we are a BBB accredited business with an A+ rating.

Three generations of customers have called the same number. We plan to keep answering it.
