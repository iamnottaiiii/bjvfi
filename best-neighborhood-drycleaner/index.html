About the shop

Best Neighborhood Drycleaner has served Medford from 570 High Street for about 20 years. It is a small, family-style operation, four people strong, built on careful work and honest dealing rather than volume.

Guests single out two things again and again. First, the shop is genuinely eco-conscious: reviewers describe posted information about the green grants behind its environmentally friendly operation. Second, the staff treats hard jobs as a point of pride, from a wedding dress left uncleaned for a year to specialty suede work shipped in during the pandemic.

The result is the kind of cleaner where people know your name and your clothes come back right.
