Our story

The short version

Built on soil sense

Based at 1694 Pickett Run in Union, KY, C S Earth Innovations is an excavating contractor with 25 years in business, per its BBB profile. The company works three trades that belong together: excavation, basement waterproofing, and hardscaping.

The logic is simple. The same crew that cuts the grade should understand where the water goes, and the crew that understands the water should build the patio on top. One accountable team, from raw dirt to finished stone.
