Our story

A fixture on Avenue U.

Paul's Hair Salon Inc. has served the Homecrest neighborhood of Brooklyn for years from its storefront at 2213 Avenue U. It is the kind of shop that earns regulars slowly: a decent cut, a fair price, and chairs that keep moving.

"Fast and efficient, and they know my cut by now."

Reviews describe a no-frills atmosphere where the focus is the work itself. Many customers have followed their favorite stylists here for years, and new walk-ins are always welcome alongside appointments.

A

Affordable by design

Reviewers call the prices the cheapest in the area. The salon keeps overhead low and passes the savings to the neighborhood.

B

What regulars say

Professional, efficient, little waiting. The shampoo wash comes with a relaxing head massage that reviewers single out again and again.

C

An honest note

Reviews are mixed on consistency: some cuts come out rushed or shorter than asked. The regulars' advice is to find the stylist who understands your taste and book with them.
