Our story

A Hartford workhorse on Locust St

Mid-Town Tire & Supply Inc is an independent tire dealer and auto parts supplier serving Hartford drivers, local shops, and fleets.

The business

Three jobs, one address

"Tire dealer, parts counter, repair shop."

Listed as a tire dealer with auto parts and automotive repair, Mid-Town runs the kind of operation Hartford's working vehicles depend on: tires moving through the warehouse, parts ordered and picked up at the counter, and repair bays turning wrenches.

The people

Crew that knows the stock

People who have worked here describe a hands on crew: warehouse staff loading and stacking tires by brand, size and style, front counter advisors helping customers order parts and communicate with techs, and drivers running deliveries to other shops and stores.

It is the kind of place where the more you know, the more you do, and where customers send their friends because the counter treats them right.
