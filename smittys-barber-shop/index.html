Our Story

A century-strong barbershop on Fort Thomas's main drag, still cutting hair the neighborly way.

Four owners, one hundred years

By the shop's own account, Smitty's Barber Shop has been around for over 100 years, serving Fort Thomas through four different locations. Brian, the barber and fourth owner, marked his 30th year at the shop in December 2025, and customers say he is the only person they will let cut their hair, even after moving 40 minutes away.

It is an old-school shop in the best sense: no appointments needed in years past, fair prices, family-friendly talk, and a cut delivered exactly how you asked. In late 2025 the shop went through a big remodel, and two new barbers, Anthony Lawson and Nate Kidwell, joined Brian in the chairs, so there is more room for neighbors old and new.
