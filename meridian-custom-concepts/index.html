Why build this way

Efficiency pays you back every month

An efficient home costs less to heat, cool, and maintain. Meridian plans the envelope, the systems, and the finishes as one system so the house performs as designed.

Envelope

Tighter shells

Air sealing and insulation detailing that keep conditioned air where it belongs, cutting drafts and hot or cold spots room to room.

Systems

Right-sized equipment

Heating, cooling, and ventilation matched to the actual load of the house, so equipment runs less and lasts longer.

Value

Lower cost of ownership

Utility savings, fewer callbacks, and durable assemblies add up to a home that is cheaper to live in year after year.

Our story

A builder measured by performance

Meridian Custom Concepts grew out of a simple conviction: a house should be judged by how it performs, not just how it photographs. That conviction earned the company a place among Ohio's ENERGY STAR partner builders.

Based on Woodsedge Drive in Anderson Township, Meridian worked as a custom construction company serving Cincinnati's east side, with an emphasis on energy-efficient building practices recognized by the ENERGY STAR program.

Public records show David Kaylor, a University of Cincinnati graduate, served as the company's president from 2014 through 2021, bringing experience in renovation and operations management to every project.
