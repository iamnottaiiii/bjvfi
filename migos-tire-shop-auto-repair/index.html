About Migos

Migos Tire Shop & Auto Repair is a family owned tire shop and auto repair business at 2458 Harrison Ave in Cincinnati, OH, serving the west side neighborhoods including Cheviot and Westwood.

Public reviews paint a consistent picture: a shop known for low tire prices, fast service, and a friendly, communicative crew that works with customers to get the job done right. Customers also note a bilingual, welcoming atmosphere.

The shop is open six days a week, Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and closed on Sundays.
