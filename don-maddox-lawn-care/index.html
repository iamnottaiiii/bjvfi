Our story

A Florence name since 1985

Don Maddox Lawn Care is a locally owned, family-run company serving Florence and Northern Kentucky. The company describes itself simply: sit back and relax, let us take care of all your lawn care needs.

1985

The company begins

Don Maddox Lawn Care starts serving Florence lawns. In the company's own words, they have been in business since 1985, growing through repeat customers and word of mouth.
