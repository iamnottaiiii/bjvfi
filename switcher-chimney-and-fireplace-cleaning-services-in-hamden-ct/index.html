Safety first

When to call the sweep

Most chimney pros recommend a sweep every year, or every cord of wood burned, whichever comes first. If you notice any of these, do not light another fire until a pro takes a look.

Heavy soot buildup, fires that burn poorly, smoke rolling into the room, or critters in the flue all mean it is time.

The Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection reminds homeowners to verify credentials before hiring any chimney pro. Ask for references, read reviews, and never sign under pressure.
