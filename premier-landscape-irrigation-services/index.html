The company

Strong attention to detail, guaranteed work

Premier Landscape & Irrigation Services describes itself as a full service company with a strong attention to detail. The team is professional and meticulous, and all services are performed in-house, from design to installation and maintenance.

The company has been listed in the Blue Book of building and construction since 2009, with main lines of business in irrigation systems and equipment, landscape construction and retaining wall systems. Service areas include Cincinnati, Columbus and Dayton across Southern Ohio.
