Our story

HAR Masonry is a working masonry crew based in North Hills, California. No showroom, no sales team, just masons who show up with levels, mixers, and a habit of doing it right the first time.

The trade list is short on purpose: block walls, retaining walls, driveway concrete, interlocking pavers, saltillo tile, and stamped concrete. Specializing means every pattern, pour, and course gets the attention it deserves.

Homeowners across the San Fernando Valley, from North Hills to Northridge, Van Nuys, and Granada Hills, call HAR Masonry when they want masonry that stays put and looks like it belongs.

"A wall should look like it grew out of the ground, not like it was dropped on it."

The HAR Masonry approach: straight lines, solid footings, clean finishes.
