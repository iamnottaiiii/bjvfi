Before you hire anyone

Good questions for any carpet installer.

  • Is the estimate free, and does it include pad, tear-out, and haul-away?
  • Do you power-stretch wall-to-wall installs, and how are seams placed?
  • What is the lead time from measuring to install day?
  • Can you show photos of recent stairs or whole-room work nearby?
