About

A Monroe electrician who sweats the details

Partyka Electric LLC is led by Stanley Partyka, a Connecticut licensed electrician working out of Monroe. It is a small, hands on operation: the person who answers the phone is the person who shows up and does the work.

Customers point to the same thing again and again. He looks closer than expected. Breakers installed backwards by a previous owner, a generator surging because of stale fuel, outlets that were never brought up to date. Problems like these get found and fixed right, not patched over.

The company serves residential and light commercial customers in Monroe, Trumbull, Shelton, Newtown, Bridgeport, and Fairfield, handling everything from quick repairs to full rewiring projects.
