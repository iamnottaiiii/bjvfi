About the practice

Meet Klein Dental Care

A general dentistry practice on Voorhies Avenue in Brooklyn, built around careful work and honest advice.

The practice

Led by Dr. Steven Klein, DDS

Klein Dental Care is a general dentistry practice at 1513 Voorhies Ave, Suite 2, Brooklyn. Dr. Klein provides comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages, from routine checkups and preventive cleanings to restorative work like fillings, crowns and root canals.

Patients describe him as knowledgeable and thorough, with a calm chairside manner that puts anxious patients at ease. The office focuses on personalized attention: listening first, explaining options clearly, and recommending only what your teeth actually need.

Whether you are due for a cleaning, dealing with a toothache, or thinking about brightening your smile, the first step is a simple phone call.
