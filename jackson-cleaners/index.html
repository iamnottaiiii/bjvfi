The Shop

A tailor's eye, a cleaner's care

By the Jackson Cleaners counter

Most dry cleaners press clothes. Jackson Cleaners finishes them. Tucked into downtown Melrose on Essex Street, this small shop has spent more than three decades doing the fussy work other places rush: hems that fall straight, suits altered to the inch, and dresses repaired so the fix disappears.

Customers drive across town for the alterations alone, then stay for everything else.

Bring in the suit that never quite fit, the dress that needs saving, or the week's wash and fold. The team will tell you honestly what can be done, do it on the promised day, and stand behind the result.

Our Story

Three decades on Essex Street

Est. 1994 · Downtown Melrose

Jackson Cleaners opened its doors in 1994, when a pressed suit still meant something and a tailor's word was a contract. More than thirty years later, the shop still runs on the same small-team promise: two people, one counter, and garments finished like they matter.

In an era of drop off lockers and apps, this is a shop where someone looks you in the eye and tells you when your suit will be ready.

Owner Thanh Dinh has built the business the slow way, one alteration at a time. Regulars bring in the suit from the big interview, the dress from the wedding, the coat that survived another winter. Many arrive anxious about a deadline and leave as customers for life, because the work is ready when promised and the fit is right.

The reviews tell the same story in different words: quick turnarounds, reasonable prices, and repairs so clean you cannot find the seam. That is what thirty years of practice looks like.
