About

Our story

The long-running shop on Petersburg Road

Ruehl Elec Co works out of 1654 Petersburg Road in Hebron, Kentucky, minutes from the Boone County neighborhoods we serve. Directory listings put us at 46 years in business, which makes us one of the longest-running electrical shops in the area.

We are an electrician and electrical repair shop, and cabling contractor. That combination means we handle the everyday electrical life of a home or small business: the fault that needs finding, the panel that needs attention, the lights and outlets that need adding.

No call centers, no upsell scripts. You call the shop, you talk to someone who has wired houses since before most panels were born.
