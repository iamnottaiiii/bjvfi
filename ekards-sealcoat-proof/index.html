Services

Built up in coats, like good pavement

Every job follows a layered process, from bare prep to a sealed, protected finish. Here is how each coat earns its place.

01Prep

Surface Prep and Cleaning

Debris is swept, surfaces are cleaned, and drainage issues are addressed before anything touches the asphalt. Prep prevents standing water and protects the binder, so every coat bonds like it should.

  • Debris removal
  • Drainage check
  • Surface cleaning
02Crack Fill

Crack Filling

Hot rubberized compounds seal cracks and stop water infiltration before cracks grow into potholes. This coat protects the sub-base, which is where the real expensive damage starts.

  • Hot rubberized fill
  • Stops water intrusion
  • Prevents potholes
03Seal Coat

Sealcoating

The signature coat: a protective liquid barrier that guards against UV oxidation, moisture, oil, and gas. Applied every 2 to 3 years, it restores the deep black finish and extends pavement life for a fraction of resurfacing cost.

  • UV and moisture barrier
  • Deep black finish
  • Every 2 to 3 years
04Cure

Cure and Protect

The seal sets into a rich, uniform surface that is ready to take traffic and weather. Cure timing: call to confirm

  • Uniform sealed surface
  • Weather-ready finish
