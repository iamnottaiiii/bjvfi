About

A Bronx shop built for Bronx buildings

Norwind air condition and heater is a local HVAC contractor based at 593 Prospect Ave in the Bronx. We handle air conditioning, heating and plumbing for residential and commercial customers, so one call covers the systems that keep a building running.

Old walk-ups, new renovations and corner stores all have their quirks. We size repairs to the building, explain the options in plain language, and leave the place clean when we are done.
