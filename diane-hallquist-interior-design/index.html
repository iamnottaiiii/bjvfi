In Loving Memory

Diane Hallquist passed away on January 21, 2026, leaving behind a design legacy built over more than 40 successful years in interior design. Her work touched countless Indianapolis homes with warmth, color and grace.

The Blue Door, her beloved design studio and shop, was a landmark for clients seeking timeless style and personal guidance.

Her Story

A full-service interior design studio that ran for over forty years, known for vivid color, distinctive lighting, and many appearances at the St. Margaret's Guild Decorators' Show House. The studio also operated The Blue Door home accents shop.

Based at Diane Hallquist Interior Design, 5215 N Washington Blvd, Indianapolis, IN 46220 in Indianapolis, Diane built The Blue Door into a trusted name over four decades. She is remembered by clients, colleagues and friends for her talent and generosity.
