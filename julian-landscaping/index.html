Our story

Three clients and a mower.

That is how Julian landscaping began. Three lawns, one worker who genuinely loves mowing grass, and a standard he would not lower for anyone.

Word got around. Three clients became more, and more became a full route across Rockville, Gaithersburg, Silver Spring and North Potomac. Ten years in the trade later, the formula has not changed: show up on time, work hard, and clean up like the job is not done until the sidewalk is clear.

Julian likes to play soccer and football when the mowers are parked, but ask what he loves most about the industry and the answer is simple: he loves to mow the grass.

"Something that I do better than other companies is that I do them the best that I can."

That is Julian in his own words, and it is the promise behind every visit.
