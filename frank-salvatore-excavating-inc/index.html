How A Site Gets Dug Right

Every project starts with reading the ground. Here is the general order of operations on a typical excavation job.

01

Survey The Lot

Stakes go in, grades get marked, and the plan is confirmed against the property before any machine moves dirt.

02

Cut To Grade

Topsoil is stripped and the site is cut or filled to the elevations the build needs, layer by layer.

03

Leave It Level

Trenches, pads, and drive areas are left compacted and ready for the next trade to roll in.
