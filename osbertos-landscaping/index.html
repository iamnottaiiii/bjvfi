Our story

A Lynn business built on steady work

Osberto's landscaping is a local landscaping business based at 387 Eastern Ave in Lynn, Massachusetts. We started with a simple idea: do the yard work the right way, show up when promised, and treat every property like it is our own.

Most of our work comes from neighbors telling neighbors. That only happens when the mowing lines are straight, the hedges are even, and the cleanup is complete. So that is the standard we hold on every visit, whether it is a weekly mow or a full spring cleanup.

We serve Lynn and the surrounding North Shore area. If your yard needs attention, call (781) 692-1050 and we will take it from there.
