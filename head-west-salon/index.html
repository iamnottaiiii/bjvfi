About the salon

Rooted in Auburn,
grown by word of mouth

Head West Salon has been part of the Auburn community for well over a decade, built one happy client at a time. The salon sits in a spacious, easy-to-find spot on Arterial West with plenty of parking, and inside you will find a calm, welcoming team that treats regulars like family and first-timers like friends.

Clients drive in from across the Finger Lakes, some from as far as Rochester, because they trust the stylists here with cuts, color and big transformations alike. Reviews consistently mention the friendly atmosphere, fair pricing and stylists who listen before they pick up the scissors.

Beyond hair, the salon has grown into a full beauty destination: a day spa with massage, facials, manicures and pedicures, plus lash, brow and spray tan specialists, so you can plan a whole day of self-care in one place.
