About

A small Blackwood practice, focused on recovery

Back To Health Chiropractic is a chiropractic practice located at 69 S Black Horse Pike in Blackwood, New Jersey. Public business records show the practice was established in 2007 and incorporated in New Jersey, operating as a small office with a close-knit staff.

The practice centers on a simple belief: that the relationship between the spine and the nervous system affects overall health, and that careful, hands-on care can help the body heal. Patients are treated as neighbors, not numbers, with time taken to explain conditions and treatment plans in plain language.

Whether you are dealing with a new injury, a long-standing ache, or simply want to move through your days with less stiffness, the team at Back To Health Chiropractic welcomes the conversation.
