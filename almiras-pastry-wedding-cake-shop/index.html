Our story

A Hammond bakery built on celebration

Almira's Pastry and Wedding Cake Shop has earned its place as one of Hammond's go-to bakeries for life's biggest moments. Our name says it plainly: pastry and wedding cakes are what we do, and we do them well.

"The cake was beautiful, tasted absolutely delicious, and was set up on time."

That sentiment, paraphrased from a real customer review, captures what we aim for with every order. Our decorators are known for their attention to detail, sketching designs around your vision and bringing them to life in buttercream and fondant.

But we're not only a special-occasion shop. Every morning our cases fill with fresh donuts, including the chocolate-frosted favorites with sprinkles, plus cupcakes, pies, and fresh bread and rolls. Stop in for a dozen, stay for the smell of a real bakery.

With a 4.3 average across public reviews and a reputation for reasonable prices, we're proud to be part of Hammond's celebrations, big and small.
