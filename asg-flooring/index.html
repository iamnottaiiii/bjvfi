Our story

A local flooring crew that treats your home like its own.

ASG Flooring is a flooring contractor based in Enola, PA, serving homeowners and businesses across the greater Harrisburg area. The work is straightforward: show up on time, prep the subfloor properly, install carefully, and clean up before leaving.

Reviewers consistently mention the quality of the installation and how easy the crew is to work with. That reputation is earned one room at a time, and it is the standard every job is held to.

What we believe

Prep is everything. A beautiful floor on a bad subfloor is a short lived floor, so leveling, moisture checks and acclimation are never skipped.

How we work

Free written estimates, clear timelines, and one point of contact from measure to walkthrough. You always know what is happening and when.
