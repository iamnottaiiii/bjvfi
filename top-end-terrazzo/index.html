Services

Terrazzo systems and industrial coatings.

Select a service to see what it involves. Every project starts with consulting: we help you decide whether terrazzo is the right floor for the building, then install and maintain it.

Epoxy Terrazzo

Our most specified system. High-grade epoxies are blended with marble, glass, or plastic chips, then poured over the existing concrete or wood floor. After a short cure period, we grind the floor until the chips are exposed, polish it with high-speed polishers, finish it with a high-gloss sealer, and wax it to a lasting luster.

  • Seamless, chip-blended floors in custom colors and patterns
  • Thin-set installation over existing concrete or wood substrates
  • High-gloss sealer and wax finish for a durable shine
  • Suited to hospitals, schools, showrooms, airports, and labs

Cement-Based Terrazzo

The classic terrazzo system. It follows the same installation steps as our epoxy terrazzo, integrating cement instead of epoxy as the binder. The result is the traditional, time-tested terrazzo floor with its own distinct character.

  • Traditional cement binder system
  • Same grind, polish, and seal process as epoxy terrazzo
  • Colors vary with your design and preference
  • A fit for heritage-style and institutional projects

Industrial Coatings

Alongside terrazzo, we install industrial coating systems for commercial and industrial floors that need chemical resistance, cleanability, and long service life.

  • Coating systems for chemical rooms and industrial floors
  • Specified for labs and manufacturing environments
  • Installed by supervised, experienced field crews

Consulting and Maintenance

Terrazzo is a long-term investment, and we stay with it after installation. Our crews are skilled craftsmen, well supervised and equipped to reach any field supervisor or estimator when a project needs attention.

  • Project consulting: is terrazzo right for your building?
  • Installation by supervised, experienced crews
  • Ongoing maintenance guidance for a lasting finish

Our Story

Three decades of pouring stone-strong floors.

Top End Terrazzo began in California and has grown over 35+ years into a terrazzo contractor with offices in Northern and Southern California, handling business across North America, and a global team based in Hong Kong serving international projects.

What has not changed is the craft. Our work crews are skilled craftsmen, qualified to deliver a job well done. Crews are well supervised and equipped to reach any field supervisor or estimator if the need arises.

We specialize in the installation of terrazzo floors and industrial coatings, and we are a member of the National Terrazzo and Mosaic Association: fully licensed and insured to perform in California, Arizona, Nevada, Washington, Oregon, and Texas.
