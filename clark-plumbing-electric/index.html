Our Story

Clark Plumbing & Electric is an independent plumbing, electrical and heating contractor based at 9926 Silvergate Ln in Cincinnati, Ohio. It is a small operation, which means the person you talk to is the person doing the work.

Directory records list William Bishop as the owner. The business is known locally for handling the everyday jobs other shops overlook: the stubborn clog, the dead outlet, the furnace that quits on the coldest night of the year.
