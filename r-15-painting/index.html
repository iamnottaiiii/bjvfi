Our story

A Baltimore painting crew with a simple rule

Do it like it's your own house

R 15 Painting is a local painting crew based on Quantril Way in Baltimore, serving homes across the city.

Our rule is the one we'd want from any contractor in our own home: show up when you say you will, protect everything you are not painting, and don't leave until the lines are clean and the floors are swept.

We keep things personal on purpose. Small crew, direct communication, and a final walkthrough where you point at anything that isn't perfect and we make it right.

Ready when you are

Tell us about the rooms, the siding, or the cabinets that need attention. We'll take a look and give you a straight number.
