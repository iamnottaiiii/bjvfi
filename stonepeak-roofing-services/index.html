Our story

Plainfield roofers, plain and simple

StonePeak Roofing Services started with a straightforward idea: show up on time, quote honestly, and do roofing work that stands up to New Jersey storms. Based on Park Avenue in Plainfield, we serve homeowners who want a contractor they can reach, a crew that respects the property, and a roof they do not have to think about again.

We keep the operation lean on purpose. Fewer layers between you and the person on your roof means faster answers, clearer pricing, and accountability on every job, from a single leak repair to a full replacement.
