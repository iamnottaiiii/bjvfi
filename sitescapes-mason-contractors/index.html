The groundwork matters as much as the finish work

Most masonry problems start below the surface: bad grading, soft fill, water with nowhere to go. Because this crew does sitework and excavation as well as masonry, the base gets built right before a single block is laid.

That means retaining walls with real drainage behind them, patios on compacted bases instead of loose soil, and foundations excavated clean so the block work starts true.

Our story

Sitescapes Mason Contractors is a Garwood, New Jersey contractor working at the point where the ground meets the building. The company specializes in sitework, excavation and masonry, the three trades that decide whether a structure stays straight and dry.

The approach is practical: walk the site, read the grade, plan the drainage, then build. Homeowners get one crew accountable from the first dig to the final joint, instead of three subcontractors pointing at each other.

If you are planning an addition, a wall, or a yard that keeps flooding, start with a conversation about the ground itself.
