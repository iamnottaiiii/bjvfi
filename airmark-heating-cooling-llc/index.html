The climate lab

How a heating contractor thinks

Three readings every good HVAC visit checks. This panel is illustrative, based on standard trade practice.

Delta T

Temperature difference across the coil. Too small means low refrigerant or weak airflow; too big means restricted air.

Static pressure

Resistance inside the duct system. High readings point to clogged filters, crushed ducts, or undersized returns.

Combustion safety

On gas furnaces, techs check heat exchangers and venting for cracks and carbon monoxide risk. Never skip this one.

How to judge any heating contractor

Ask about load calculation

Reputable installers size equipment with a Manual J calculation, not a rule of thumb. Oversized systems short cycle and waste energy.

Ask about combustion safety

On gas furnaces, a heat exchanger inspection and carbon monoxide check should be part of every tune up.

Get it in writing

Scope, equipment model numbers, warranty terms, and total price belong on the proposal before work starts.

Check airflow, not just the box

Many comfort complaints are duct problems. A contractor who measures static pressure is doing real diagnostics.
