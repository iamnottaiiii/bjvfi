Our story

From a Family Recipe to Hammond's Cake Spot

That's A Cake started with a family recipe for double butter pound cake and a simple idea: bake it the way home bakers do, soft, moist, and generous, then share it with the neighborhood.

Word of mouth did the rest. Guests started driving in for the deep fried pound cakes, the thick slices, and the flavors you do not see everywhere, like the Double Butter Fried Churro Cake and the Strawberry Deep Fried. Food media and local foodies took notice, and the little shop on Gostlin Street became a destination.

Today it is a Black-owned, family-feel bakery where the staff treats you like a regular on your first visit. Reviewers talk as much about the warm welcome as they do about the cake, and that is exactly how the shop likes it.
