ABOUT THE SHOP

Latinos AllStar Barbershop is a Latino-owned barber shop at 4888 Dixie Hwy in Fairfield, Ohio. In the shop's own words from its public business profile: they are here to give the best service to all public, children, adolescents, adults and seniors, and no appointment is needed because walk-ins are welcome.

Public review aggregators show a 4.1 star rating across 83 reviews (Birdeye), with reviewers praising good value, skilled barbers and a friendly environment, while some note long waits and occasional scheduling hiccups. You can read the unfiltered mix on our Reviews page.
