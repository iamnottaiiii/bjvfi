Our Story

A roofing shop on Foothill Boulevard

Jewel Roofing La Cañada Flintridge is a roofing contractor based at 484 Foothill Blvd, right in the heart of La Cañada Flintridge. We chose to specialize in one trade and do it well: roofs, and everything that keeps water out of your home.

The foothills are hard on roofs. Summer sun bakes shingles, Santa Ana winds lift edges and flashing, and winter storms find every weak spot. Because we work these streets every day, we know the failure patterns of local roofs and how to prevent them.

Inspect first

We look before we quote. Photos, measurements, and an honest read on whether you need a repair or a replacement.

Do it once

Proper underlayment, flashing, and ventilation. The parts you never see are the parts that matter most.

Leave it clean

Landscaping protected, nails swept with magnets, and the site tidier than we found it.
