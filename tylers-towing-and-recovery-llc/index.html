How To Read Any Towing Review

General guidance for evaluating tow companies. Not customer reviews of this business.

Positive signals

Fast arrival, a firm price quoted before the hook, the truck number and driver name given over the phone, and no damage at drop-off.

Mixed signals

Good work but slow arrival, or a price that changed from the quote. Response times often depend on call volume and distance, so ask for an ETA and a written total.

Negative signals

Refusal to quote a price upfront, pressure to sign blank paperwork, or new damage discovered after the tow. Photograph your vehicle before and after.
