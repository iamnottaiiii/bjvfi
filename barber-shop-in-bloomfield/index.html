How we work

The chair comes first

Every visit starts the same way: a real conversation about what you want. Not a rushed nod, a consult. Because a great cut is a collaboration between the barber and the person in the chair.

From there it is steady, careful work. Clean sections, blended fades, crisp lines, and a finish that looks as good walking out the door as it does a week later.
