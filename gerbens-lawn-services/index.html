Our story

Family owned, and proud of it

Gerbens Lawn Services started in Severn and never left. For over 21 years we have mowed, mulched, cleaned up, and plowed for the same neighborhoods, and a lot of our customers have been with us for most of that time.

The difference is simple: we do the work ourselves. No subcontractors, no rotating strangers. The family that quotes your job is the family that does your job, which is why our work looks the same in July as it does in October.
