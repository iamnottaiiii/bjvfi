The heritage parlor

❦Our Story

Mr. & Mrs. Grote Traditional Barber and Beauty Salon is a traditional barber and beauty salon on Cincinnati's West Side. Public listings describe a genuine old school shop with classic barbering craft: cream and straight edge detailing, hot towel shaves, and the kind of easy conversation that turns first visits into long friendships. Guests call it a West Side staple, and many have been coming for years.
