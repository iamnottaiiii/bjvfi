About

A Northfield name in landscaping

The details below come from public business listings. Anything not listed here is best confirmed with a quick call.

Stewart Landscaping is a landscaping company based at 7641 Chaffee Rd in Northfield, Ohio. Public listings note the company has been serving the community since 1993, working on residential and commercial grounds care.

Listings describe a small, local operation handling lawn service and maintenance, landscape design, sod installation, retaining walls, patios and walks, landscape construction, and seasonal snow removal.

The company serves portions of Cuyahoga, Portage, and Summit counties, including towns such as Northfield, Aurora, Bedford, Brecksville, Hudson, Macedonia, Solon, Streetsboro, and Twinsburg. It holds a BBB rating of A+ and a 4.5 out of 5 rating on Angi from 15 reviews.
