About

An owner-operated Topanga company with deep local roots.

Labor of Love Home Repair and Remodel is run by owner Ken Bass, a contractor customers describe as kind-hearted and honest, bringing a local Topanga feel to every job.

Public records list Kenneth Edward Bass as holding a California General Building license, and BuildZoom ranks the company in the top 5% of licensed California contractors with a score of 109 out of 336,931. Angi lists 35 years of experience and notes the business is owner operated.

Past customers describe a range of work from bathroom remodels to second-story additions and hillside foundation work, and say the owner takes time to meet clients and go over every question and concern.
