What we paint

Every project, done like
it has our name on it

Interior, exterior, and everything in between. Free estimates, meticulous prep, and a finish you will brag about.

01

Whole-Home Interior Painting

Most requestedWallsCeilings

Full interior repaints for homes across Peabody and the North Shore. Customers say George repainted entire interiors, even new-construction ADU and garage conversions, with results friends and family immediately noticed.

  • Living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, hallways, and baths
  • Ceilings, closets, and fresh color consultations
  • Furniture protected, floors covered, site left clean
02

Exterior House Painting

SidingShuttersPorches

New England weather is hard on exteriors. We do the full system: wash, scrape, caulk, prime, and two coats of weather-rated paint, so your curb appeal lasts.

  • Siding, shutters, fascia, soffits, and porches
  • Power washing and peeling-paint repair included in prep
  • Weather-rated exterior paints, applied in the right season
03

Cabinets, Trim, and Doors

Detail workSmooth finish

This is where a detail-obsessed painter earns the title. Cabinets, baseboards, crown molding, and doors get the sand-prime-spray treatment for a factory-smooth finish.

  • Kitchen and bath cabinet refinishing
  • Baseboards, casings, crown molding, and interior doors
  • Crisp lines where walls meet trim, every time
04

Drywall Repair and Prep

PatchingCaulking

Great paint jobs are made before the paint comes out. We patch holes, repair cracks, re-caulk, and sand everything smooth so the topcoat looks flawless.

  • Drywall patching, crack repair, and texture blending
  • Caulking gaps in trim and woodwork
  • Stain-blocking primers where needed
05

New Construction and Conversions

ADUsGaragesBuilders

From new-construction ADU interiors to garage conversions, we handle fresh drywall and new builds with the same care, on your timeline.

  • New drywall priming and finish coats
  • ADU and garage conversion interiors
  • Coordination with contractors and schedules
