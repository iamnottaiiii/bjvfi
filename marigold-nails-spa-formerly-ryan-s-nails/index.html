Our story

Same beloved salon,
a brand-new bloom

The salon Syracuse knew as ryan's Nails has a new name: Marigold. Same Eastside address, same caring team, a fresh look to match the care inside.

What stayed the same

The team and the craft you trust

A name change does not change the hands behind the work. Guests who followed the salon from ryan's Nails to Marigold kept coming for the same reasons: technicians who take their time, honest advice on what your nails need, and results that last until the next fill.

The marigold is a flower that stands for warmth and devotion. We liked that. It felt like the right name for a place where people come to feel cared for.

Spotless, every day

Cleanliness is one of the most-mentioned compliments in our reviews. We work hard to keep it that way.

Calm by design

Relaxing, quiet, unhurried. Guests describe the room itself as part of the treatment.

Booking made easy

Call us, walk in, or send a message on Instagram. We will find you a chair.
