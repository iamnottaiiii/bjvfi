About the shop

A Walnut Hills barbershop with a 4.7 rating across 50 public reviews.

On Point Barbershop sits at 1200 Lincoln Ave in Cincinnati's Walnut Hills neighborhood, in a mixed-use building where the shop has been the ground-floor tenant for years. It is a straightforward, community barbershop: inexpensive prices, walk-in friendly, and known for attentive barbers.

Reviewers consistently name the barbers and the atmosphere: a great cut, a good vibe, and patience with kids. The shop's own posts keep it simple, haircuts, kids cuts, beard trims, at prices that have stayed low.
