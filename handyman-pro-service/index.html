Our story

The handyman Encino calls first

Handyman Pro Service started with a simple observation: most homes do not need a big contractor, they need someone reliable who shows up, does careful work, and treats a small job like it matters. So that is what we built.

"Treat every home like it is our own, and every list like it is the only one."

We are based on White Oak Ave in Encino and serve homeowners, renters and property managers across the San Fernando Valley. From a single leaky faucet to a full turnover punch list, one call gets it handled.

What sets us apart is not fancy equipment. It is the basics, done consistently: clear quotes before work starts, shoe covers at the door, drop cloths on the floors, and a full cleanup before we leave.
