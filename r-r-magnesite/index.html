Our Story

Since 1982, R & R MAGNESITE has been a small family business run by the same owner family, the Torrez family, out of North Hollywood. While bigger outfits chased every trade, this shop stayed with one: magnesite waterproofing.

Ask around Laurel Canyon and Strathern and you will hear the same thing: they show up, they do the work they promised, and they treat you like family. That family-style customer service is the whole business plan, and it has carried R & R MAGNESITE for more than four decades.
