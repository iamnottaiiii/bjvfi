About

Rooted in Cincinnati since the 1960s

Frank Sullivan Landscaping Inc is a landscape design and installation company based at 3441 Zumstein Avenue in Cincinnati's East End. Public directory listings trace the business to the 1960s: Angi describes it as independently owned and operated since 1966 with 60 years of experience, while business directories record an opening date of 1967.

The company specializes in custom designed landscaping and installation for both residential and commercial clients. Business records list Francis Sullivan as president and a small crew of about five people.

  • Custom design focus. Landscapes planned around each property rather than one-size-fits-all packages.
  • Residential and commercial. Homes, businesses, and commercial grounds across the Cincinnati area.
  • Lighting expertise. Outdoor lighting plans, installation, and repair alongside planting work.
  • Independently owned. A local, independently operated firm, not a franchise.
