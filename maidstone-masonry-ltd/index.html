Brick, Block & Stone, Laid to Last Generations

Maidstone Masonry Ltd is a masonry contractor on 10th Concession Road in Maidstone, Ontario, serving homes and farms across Essex County.

Masonry is the quiet backbone of a building. Done well, it outlives everything around it: the roof gets replaced, the paint fades, but good brick and stone stand for a century. That is the standard this trade is measured against, and it is the standard we hold every course to.

Straight joints, true corners, and mortar mixed for the job at hand.
