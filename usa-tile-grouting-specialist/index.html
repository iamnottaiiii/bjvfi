Why grout matters

Dirty or damaged grout makes good tile look old.

Grout lines collect dirt, stains, and moisture over time. Professional cleaning, repair, and sealing restore the original look and protect the tile underneath.

Services

Tile and grout services

Tap a service to see what is included. Every job starts with a look at your tile so the work matches what it needs.

Deep cleaning that lifts years of dirt, stains, and buildup out of grout lines.
  • Kitchen and bathroom tile
  • Floors, walls, and backsplashes
  • Showers and tub surrounds
Fixing cracked, crumbling, or missing grout so water stays out and tile stays put.
  • Cracked grout line repair
  • Missing grout replacement
  • Loose tile re-bonding
Removing old, stained grout and applying fresh grout for a like-new finish without replacing the tile.
  • Full regrouting of rooms
  • Shower and bath regrouting
  • Color-consistent finish
Protective sealing that keeps grout cleaner longer and resists stains and moisture.
  • Penetrating grout sealers
  • Shower and wet-area sealing
  • Maintenance advice
Removing old, moldy caulk and applying fresh sealant where tile meets tubs, sinks, and fixtures.
  • Tub and shower caulking
  • Sink and countertop joints
  • Mold-stained caulk removal
