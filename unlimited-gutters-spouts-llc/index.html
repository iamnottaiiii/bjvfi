Why clean gutters matter

NOTE A

Protect the foundation

Overflowing gutters dump water right where it does the most damage. Clean gutters move it away.

NOTE B

Stop fascia rot

Standing water and packed debris rot out fascia boards and invite pests into the roofline.

NOTE C

Twice a year

Most homes do best with spring and fall cleanings. Heavy tree cover means checking more often.
