Our story

Pool people,
through and through

Swimming Pools Services & Remodeling is a pool contractor rooted in La Cañada Flintridge, built on the simple idea that pool ownership should feel like a luxury, not a chore.

We know foothill pools

Hillside lots, canyon winds, oak leaves in the skimmer by October, and summer heat that eats chlorine for breakfast. Local water and local weather shape how we service every pool.

Chemistry you can trust

Clear water is chemistry done right. We test, balance, and document every visit, so your water is safe for kids, kind to equipment, and easy on the eyes.

Remodels without drama

A resurfacing is a construction project in your backyard. We keep it tidy, keep you updated, and finish with water you can swim in the day we hand it back.

The pool you wanted
when you bought the house

Whether it needs a weekly visit or a full rebirth, we will get it there. One contractor, one phone number, zero pool stress.
