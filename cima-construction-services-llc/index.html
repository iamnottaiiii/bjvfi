Our Story

Twelve years of building.
One promise kept.

cima construction services, llc grew out of more than a decade of hands-on construction work across Florida, and now serves the whole state as a general contractor and subcontractor.

Then

12 years in the construction business

The team behind Cima spent over a decade working job sites across Florida, learning every phase of commercial construction from the ground up before putting a name on the door.

2021

cima construction services, llc is formed

The company was established as a Florida limited liability company on October 27, 2021, based at 1101 Miranda Ln in Kissimmee, and is active and in good standing with the State of Florida.

Now

General contractor and subcontractor, statewide

Today the company works as both general contractor and subcontractor across all of Florida. Recent client work spans Rockbox Rockledge, Tapville Florida Mall, Tapville Premium Outlet Tampa, KFC Jacksonville, Taco Bell Winter Garden, and Smokey Bones locations from Plantation to Melbourne.
