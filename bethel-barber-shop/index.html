Hometown Story

A Barber Shop Bethel Can Call Its Own

Bethel Barber Shop is a classic American barbershop on West Plane Street in Bethel, Ohio. The shop describes itself on social media as a place run by Fran and Paul, who opened their doors right next door to Gramma’s Pizza and look forward to seeing new faces walk in.

It is the kind of shop where customers become regulars. Reviewers write about Fran never disappointing, about kids getting their first great fade, and about an atmosphere that feels like the barbershops of an earlier era.

Hours listed publicly show the shop open Tuesday, Wednesday, and Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., and Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to noon. Past reviewers also note the shop is cash only. Both are worth a quick call to confirm before you visit. Call to confirm
