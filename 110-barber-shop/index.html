Our story

San Pedro born,
San Pedro raised

One shop, one street, one promise: a good cut, a fair price, and a chair that feels like home.

Est. 2010

A staple since day one

110 Barber Shop opened its doors on Gaffey Street in 2010, founded by local owner Steve Guevara. From the start, the formula was simple: skilled barbers, reasonable prices, and an atmosphere where everybody feels welcome.

Fifteen-plus years later, that formula has made 110 a staple of the San Pedro community, the kind of shop people recommend to newcomers and bring their kids to.

The owner

Built by Steve Guevara

Steve is a San Pedro local through and through: a hardworking father of seven, a husband, and a community guy who also serves as a firefighter. That work ethic runs through the whole shop.

His dedication to the neighborhood shows in the details: fair prices, a relaxed shop, and a crew that treats every customer like a regular.

Making San Pedro look good, one haircut at a time.
