The C stands for the cut.

Unique "C" Barber Shop is a neighborhood barbershop at 826 Findlay Street in Cincinnati, Ohio. The monogram is the whole philosophy: one initial, one promise, no clutter.

Reviewers describe the chair as patient and laid back, the kind of shop where a first haircut or a nervous kid gets the same care as a regular. That is the brand in practice, not on paper.

Details like founding date and team bios are not published yet, so we will not guess at them. What is verified: the address, the phone number, the hours on the public profile, and a 4.2 rating across 13 public reviews.
