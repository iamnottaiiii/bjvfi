Our story

Duarte's gate specialists

Elite Gate Services is a gate and fence contractor based at 960 Hamilton Rd in Duarte, California, serving homes and businesses across the San Gabriel Valley.

The work centers on one craft: entries that are beautiful, secure, and dependable. From a first sketch of an iron gate to the final test of the operator, every detail gets the same attention, because a gate is the one part of your property that moves every single day.

Whether it is a new automated entry, a repair that gets a stuck gate moving again, or fencing to complete the perimeter, the goal is simple: open smoothly, close securely, and look good doing it.
