Our story

Meet Pamela

Pamela's Hair Salon is a neighborhood beauty salon on James Street in Syracuse, where the appointment feels personal and the results keep families returning for years.

Clients describe Pamela as an outstanding colorist and a stylist who truly listens. Whether it is a precision cut, a full color refresh, or styling for a big night out, the goal is the same: you leave the chair feeling like the best version of yourself.

The salon keeps things simple and welcoming. Friendly faces, experienced hands, and a relaxed chair where you are listened to first and styled second.

Find us at 2901 James St, Syracuse, NY 13206, open Monday through Saturday from 10 AM to 7 PM. Call (315) 432-9922 to book your visit.
