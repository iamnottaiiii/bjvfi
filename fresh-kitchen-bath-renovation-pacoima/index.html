Our process

From first call to final wipe-down

Remodeling is disruptive enough. Our process keeps it predictable.

1

Free consultation

We visit, measure and talk budget. You get honest advice on what is worth doing and what is not.

2

Design and selections

Cabinets, tile, fixtures and finishes picked with our guidance, so everything arrives on time.

3

Written bid

Line-item pricing and a schedule in writing. The price we sign is the price you pay, barring changes you approve.

4

Permits and prep

We handle permits and inspections, and protect the rest of your home before demo starts.

5

Build

Demo, plumbing, electrical, tile and finish work by our own crew, with daily cleanup.

6

Walkthrough

We walk every inch together, fix the punch list and hand over a space you love.
