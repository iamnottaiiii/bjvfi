Our trade

Underground utilities are our main line of business

Our main lines of business are underground utilities contracting: the water, sewer, and drainage infrastructure that sits beneath roads and neighborhoods. That focus is why towns and cities bring us in for sewer separation, rehabilitation, and water main replacement work.

Recent public projects include sewer separation and rehabilitation contracts for the City of Fitchburg and the Route 20 Sewer Expansion for the Town of Shrewsbury, where J.A. Polito and Sons was the low bidder. Our company profile has been carried in the Blue Book construction directory since 2009, and we serve clients throughout Worcester County and greater New England.
