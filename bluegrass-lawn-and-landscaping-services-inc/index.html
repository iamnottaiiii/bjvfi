Our story

A Melbourne, Kentucky company with deep local roots

Bluegrass Lawn & Landscaping Services, Inc. is based on Mary Ingles Highway in Melbourne, Kentucky, in the heart of Campbell County. Public business listings trace the company back to 2004, and it has appeared in the Blue Book of Building and Construction, a directory of vetted commercial contractors, since 2010.

The company's listed lines of business are landscape construction and retaining wall systems, and public profiles show it serving Campbell County in Kentucky plus the Cincinnati, Columbus, and Dayton areas of Southern Ohio.

That mix, residential lawn care backed by commercial-grade construction experience, is what sets the crew apart on everything from a weekly mow to a engineered retaining wall.
