Our story

A supply counter
for New Haven's work

WireProMax Electrician is an electrical supply store serving New Haven, Connecticut, built around one idea: the right part, on the shelf, when the job needs it.

01

Why a supply store matters

Every electrical job, from a tripped breaker to a full panel swap, comes down to parts. A local supply counter means no waiting on shipping, no second trips across town, and a real person to double check you grabbed the right breaker before you leave.

That is the job we do at 630 Chapel St: keep the shelves stocked with the wire, devices, and gear the work demands, and answer the counter with straight answers.

02

Who we serve

Licensed electricians stocking the van between calls, maintenance crews keeping buildings running, and homeowners who would rather fix it right than fix it twice. Everyone gets the same counter.

03

Where to find us

We are at 630 Chapel St, New Haven, CT. Reach us at (912) 872-1555 for stock checks, pricing, and special orders.

04

How we work

Call ahead, and we will confirm the part is on the shelf and have it waiting. Walk in with a question, and we will help you match the part to the job. Simple as that.
