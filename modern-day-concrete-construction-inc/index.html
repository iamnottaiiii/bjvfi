What the paper trail says

The Blue Book of Building and Construction lists Modern Day Concrete Construction Inc as established in 1964, with a company listing active since 1998. The Better Business Bureau profile reports 62 years in business and describes the work as concrete forming and commercial construction.

The company is based at 9773 Crosby Rd in Harrison, Ohio, and industry records place its service area across the Cincinnati, Columbus, and Dayton region of southern Ohio, with a listed crew size of 10 to 19 employees.
