Services

Tile work, room by room

Ceramic, porcelain, mosaic and stone, installed with level lines and tight joints. Pick a room to see what is included.

Kitchen tile

The kitchen takes the most abuse in the house. We set tile that handles heat, splatter and daily scrubbing, and looks sharp doing it.

  • Kitchen backsplashes, subway to large format
  • Full-height backsplash walls behind ranges
  • Kitchen floor tile in porcelain or ceramic
  • Tile transitions to hardwood and doorways
  • Grout color consulting for the look you want
  • Sealing for natural stone installations
Good to know: every project is measured and quoted individually. Call for an estimate and we will walk through tile choices and timing.

Bathroom tile

Bathrooms live or die on waterproofing. We prep properly, then set tile with falls to drains and corners that meet clean.

  • Shower surrounds and tub surrounds
  • Shower pans and curbless shower floors
  • Bathroom floor tile with proper slope to drain
  • Accent stripes, niches and mosaic insets
  • Wainscot and half-wall tile
  • Re-grout and refresh of tired bathrooms
Good to know: every project is measured and quoted individually. Call for an estimate and we will walk through tile choices and timing.

Floor tile

A floor has to stay flat, stay put and stay beautiful. We check deflection, use the right underlayment, and lay out patterns so cuts land where they should.

  • Porcelain and ceramic floor tile
  • Wood-look plank tile layouts
  • Entryways, hallways and laundry rooms
  • Natural stone floors, sealed for protection
  • Tile over existing floors where appropriate
  • Thresholds and transition strips
Good to know: every project is measured and quoted individually. Call for an estimate and we will walk through tile choices and timing.

Backsplashes and mosaic

Small tile, big impact. Mosaic and decorative tile turn plain walls into the part of the room everyone photographs.

  • Glass, stone and ceramic mosaic
  • Decorative and patterned cement tile
  • Fireplace surrounds and accent walls
  • Medallions and border insets
  • Laundry and mudroom feature walls
  • Outdoor-rated tile for patios

Why tile, and only tile

Specialists beat generalists at the details

Tile looks simple until you live with bad tile. Lippage under bare feet. Grout lines that wander. A shower niche that never quite drains. We started Acosta Tile Works to do one trade and do it right.

As a California licensed contractor in the Tile (Ceramic and Mosaic) classification, we are held to the state's standards for the trade, including the experience, examination and bonding the license requires.

  • Licensed Tile (Ceramic and Mosaic) contractor, California
  • Based in North Hills, serving the San Fernando Valley
  • Residential kitchens, bathrooms, floors and backsplashes
