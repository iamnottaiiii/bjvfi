Our Story

A neighborhood salon since 2018

A Beautiful Nails has served Staten Island from 102 Canal St since 2018. What started as a small neighborhood salon has grown into a local favorite with a 4.0 star rating across 152 Google reviews.

The team focuses on careful, unhurried work: clean tools, shaped nails, and color that lasts. Whether you come in for a quick polish change or a full set of acrylics, the goal is the same, nails that look and feel beautiful.

Walk ins are welcome, and the salon is open seven days a week.
