Our Story

Newly renovated, newly managed,
same warm welcome

Top Ten Nails on Belair Road in Perry Hall is under new management by Vincent Lam, with a freshly renovated space: new massage chairs, a stylish boutique feel, and the same hospitality guests have praised for years.

Longtime guests say they have been coming for over a decade, some for fifteen years. They stay because the people take their time, stand behind their work, and make the salon feel like home.
