Spot it. Shield it. Seal it.

About

Built to defend Pasadena roofs

BattleShield FixRoofing is a roofing contractor based at 2241 Brigden Rd in Pasadena, CA. The name says what we do: we treat your roof like the shield it is, and we fix it like it matters.

Our approach is direct. We inspect first, explain what we find in plain language, and scope every repair or replacement in writing before work starts. No mystery line items, no pressure tactics.

We serve Pasadena and the surrounding San Gabriel Valley with residential roof repair, replacement, inspection, and gutter work.
