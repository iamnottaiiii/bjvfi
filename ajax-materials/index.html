About us

Ajax Materials

Ajax Materials Corporation operates six leading-edge, high-capacity drum mix asphalt plants in southeast Michigan, including the Taylor location at 6871 Inkster Rd. The plants produce hot mix and warm mix asphalt at up to 700 tons per hour, with commercial-grade, Superpave, Polymer Modified, Porous, and high-performance cold asphalt mixtures. All plants hold AASHTO accreditation for Quality Management and Asphalt Mixture.
