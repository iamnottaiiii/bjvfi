Our story

A small salon with a loyal crowd

Illustration · Owner portrait placeholder

Ladies and Gents Hair House is a small, friendly salon owned and operated by Candice Lavoie on Southbridge Street in Auburn, Massachusetts. Since 2010 it has been the kind of place you hear about from a neighbor and keep coming back to.

The philosophy is simple: quality work at economy-friendly prices, delivered by stylists who treat your time and your budget with respect. Walk-ins are always welcome, appointments are available for your convenience, and men, women and children are all welcome in the chair.

A high percentage of business comes from repeat customers and referrals, and reviewers regularly call out the friendly atmosphere, the efficient service and the fair pricing, including discounts for veterans and senior citizens.

2010

The salon opens its doors on Route 20 in Auburn, built on honest pricing and neighborly service.
