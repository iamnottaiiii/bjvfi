REF 01, WHY THIS SHOP

A Specialist, Not a Generalist

Transmissions are the most complex assembly in your car. A shop with "trans" in the name focuses where it counts.

A

Diagnosis First

Shifting trouble is often electrical or fluid related, not mechanical. A proper diagnosis keeps small problems from becoming rebuilds.

B

Repair Before Replace

Valve bodies, solenoids, seals, and sensors can often be serviced without pulling the whole unit. Ask what options fit your car.

C

Local and Reachable

Right on Colerain Avenue in Cincinnati. One phone number, one shop, no call center: (513) 300-5680.

Warning signs your transmission needs attention

SymptomWhat it can mean
Slipping gearsWorn clutches or low fluid
Delayed or harsh shiftsSolenoid or valve body issue
Burning smellOverheated fluid, needs service
Fluid leaks under the carPan gasket or seal failure
Check engine lightStored transmission fault codes

Services, Called Out Like Parts

Typical work for a transmission focused auto repair shop. Availability varies by vehicle and by day.

01

Transmission Diagnostics

Computer scan for fault codes, fluid condition check, and a road test to pinpoint whether the problem is mechanical, electrical, or fluid related.

Call to confirm
02

Automatic Transmission Repair

Targeted repairs to valve bodies, solenoids, seals, gaskets, and sensors, fixing the failed component without unnecessary teardown.

Call to confirm
03

Transmission Rebuild

Full disassembly, inspection, and rebuild of the unit with new friction materials, seals, and any hard parts that fail inspection.

Call to confirm
04

Fluid and Filter Service

Drain and refill with the correct fluid for your vehicle, plus a new filter and pan gasket where the design allows it. The single best preventive service for a transmission.

Torque Converter Service
