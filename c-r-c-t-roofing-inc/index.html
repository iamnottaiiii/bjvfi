Storm damage checklist

Spot trouble before it spots you

Tap each card that matches what you see on your roof. Then call us and read off the count. This checklist is general guidance, not a diagnosis.

Missing or lifted shingles

Bare patches or tabs curling up after wind.

Granules in gutters

Sandy grit washing out means shingles are wearing thin.

Ceiling water stains

Brown rings indoors usually start at a roof leak.

Cracked flashing

Metal around chimneys and vents splits with age.

Dented vents or caps

Hail leaves dimples on soft metal first.

Sagging ridge line

A wavy ridge can signal deck or structure issues.
