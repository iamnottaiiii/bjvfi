Why HoCo homeowners call us

A duct cleaning done properly protects your air quality and your HVAC investment.

Allergy relief starts at the vents

Dust, pollen and pet dander collect in ductwork and recirculate with every cycle. A deep cleaning removes years of buildup.

Lower energy bills

Clean ducts move air freely, so your system does not work as hard to heat and cool your home.

Dryer safety

Lint packed dryer vents are a leading fire risk. Clearing the full vent line is one of the simplest safety upgrades a homeowner can make.

Honest recommendations

We inspect first and tell you plainly whether a cleaning will help. No pressure tactics.

How a visit goes

Most whole home cleanings finish in a single visit.

1. Walkthrough and estimate

We look at your system, count vents and give you a firm price before any work begins.

2. Protected, contained cleaning

Floor coverings go down, then each vent and main line is cleaned under vacuum collection.

3. Final check with you

We walk the home together, confirm every vent was serviced and tidy up completely.

4. Maintenance guidance

You get simple tips on filters and cleaning intervals so results last longer.
