About the Shop

A Trim Shop Built on Reputation

Anthony's Trim Shop is an auto upholstery shop at 8519 US-42 in Florence, Kentucky. Public reviews describe Anthony as a nice guy who does great work at a very reasonable price, with several customers calling him their go to for all things automotive trim.

The shop's public reputation rests on a handful of specialties: convertible tops, seat foam and rebuilds, vinyl tops, and classic car interiors. Reviewers mention cars kept secure in the shop overnight and same-day measurements on convertible tops.
