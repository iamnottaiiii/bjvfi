Our story

A welcoming chair
in Colchester

Hyacinth Nail & Spa is a locally loved nail salon on Westchester Road, known for careful work, a clean space, and a team that treats your free time as precious.

What we believe

Relax. We have
your nails.

In the salon's own words, Hyacinth exists to give you the best place to relax and enjoy your precious free time. That is the standard behind every appointment: unhurried, careful, and genuinely friendly.

Guests notice it. Reviews describe technicians who are meticulous rather than rushed, who fix a repair patiently, who shape and polish with real attention. Many visitors come back monthly, and plenty bring their mothers, sisters, and daughters along.

Walk in for a quick polish change, or settle in for the full spa pedicure with the famous warm neck wrap. Either way, you leave a little lighter.

Careful, clean, and kind. My favorite nail spot in town, every visit feels like a treat.
