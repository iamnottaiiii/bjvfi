Services

Yard work, top to bottom

Tap a service to see what is included. Every job ends with a full clean up. Call (717) 382-8746 for a quote.

Regular mowing visits keep the grass even, edged, and healthy through the growing season. Choose one-time cuts or a recurring schedule.

  • Mowing at the right height for the season
  • String trimming around fences and beds
  • Sidewalk and driveway edging
  • Clippings blown off walks and porches

Spring and fall clean ups reset the whole property. We clear the winter or summer mess so beds and lawns start fresh.

  • Leaf and debris removal
  • Stick and branch pickup
  • Bed reshaping and re-edging
  • Perennial and shrub cutbacks

Fresh mulch and tidy beds change the look of a house fast. We install new beds or refresh tired ones.

  • Mulch delivery and installation
  • Bed edging and redefining
  • Shrub and flower planting
  • Weed barrier installation

Overgrown shrubs get shaped back to size without butchering them. We trim for shape and for plant health.

  • Hedge shaping and shearing
  • Shrub size reduction
  • Deadwood removal
  • Seasonal pruning timing

Sprinklers that spray the driveway or skip the lawn waste water and money. We check and repair systems so coverage is even.

  • Head adjustment and replacement
  • Leak checks and repairs
  • Spring start-up and fall winterizing
  • Timer and coverage tuning
