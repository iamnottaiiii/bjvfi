Our Story

A mobile scrap metal buyer serving Hartford County for over two decades.

Since day one

21 Years Hauling Metal Across Connecticut

A & S Scrap Metal & Recycling started with a simple idea: make selling scrap metal as easy as a phone call. Instead of asking customers to haul heavy loads to a yard, we built the business around coming to you.

Our trucks carry certified scales, so every pound of copper, brass, aluminum, or steel is weighed on the spot and paid for in cash. That on-site honesty is what keeps contractors, homeowners, and auto shops calling us back.

From junk cars and catalytic converters to warehouse cleanouts and heavy equipment removal, we handle the hard work so you can turn clutter into cash.
