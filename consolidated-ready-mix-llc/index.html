PRODUCTS

Consolidated Ready Mix LLC is a ready mix concrete supplier in Azusa. This page describes the operation in plain terms, based on the plant description in public City of Azusa planning documents.

Ready Mix Concrete

Concrete batched at the plant and delivered by mixer truck to construction sites. Materials are weighed at the plant and mixed during transit, the standard transit-mix method.

Call for mix options and delivery scheduling.

Aggregates and Materials

Sand, rock, and cement are the core materials handled at the plant. City planning documents describe weighing batchers with digital or manual scales feeding the charging chute.

Call to discuss material and batching needs.

Plant Capacity

Published plant documents filed with the City of Azusa cite an average output of about 50 cubic yards of concrete per day, with a maximum of 125 cubic yards per day.

Figures are from 2019 planning documents. Call to confirm current capacity.

Fleet and Delivery

Published plant documents describe a fleet of up to 7 mixer trucks plus a dump truck and a semi-truck delivering to designated construction sites in Southern California.

Figures are from 2019 planning documents. Call to schedule delivery.
