Community and builder

About

Quarry Reserve at Boulder Brook is a Pulte Homes residential community in Twinsburg, Ohio, set on the Hunters Ridge extension. The community was planned with sidewalks on both sides of the street, wooded views, and winding roads, in a thriving community with a strong school district.

The development offers a variety of new home designs, including two-story, ranch, and multi-level floor plans, sized from about 1,935 to 2,875 square feet with 2 to 4 bedrooms. Its location offers easy access to schools, shopping, and employment centers in the Cleveland area.

Availability: call to confirm

Third-party listing sources report this community as sold out. Call the sales center at (330) 892-9596 to confirm current availability.

Pulte Homes is one of the country's leading new home builders. Its homes feature Life Tested design touches, Smart Home options, and energy-efficient features, and buyers can follow construction through the Owner's Entry portal. Pulte pairs a 10-year limited structural warranty with coverage of materials and workmanship and key mechanical systems.
