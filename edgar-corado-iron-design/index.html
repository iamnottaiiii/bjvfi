The shop behind the sparks

Edgar Corado has spent more than 16 years serving the San Fernando Valley with honesty and professionalism in every weld. What started as a one-man welding service on Balboa Blvd grew into a full iron design shop, but the rule never changed: measure twice, weld once, stand behind it.

Neighbors know the shop as the place that answers the phone, shows up when promised, and speaks your language. Hablamos Español. From a first sketch to the final coat of paint, you deal with the people building your gate, not a sales crew.

How a project works

1. Call and measure

Free on-site visit. We measure, listen, and sketch what fits your opening and budget.

2. Build in the shop

Cut, welded, ground smooth, primed, and painted at 7752 Balboa Blvd.

3. Install and stand behind it

Set in concrete, aligned, and tested. If a weld ever fails under normal use, we make it right.
