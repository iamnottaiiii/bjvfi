Our story

One family, one trade, since 1983

Habco Masonry Inc was incorporated in Kentucky on July 26, 1983, and it has been a Brueggeman family business ever since. Thomas H. Brueggeman serves as president and James A. Brueggeman as secretary, running the company out of 275 St Agnes Circle in Fort Wright.

State records list Habco as a small business in the special-trade construction category. That is the whole story, really: a small crew that does one thing, masonry and concrete, for homes and businesses around Covington, Fort Wright and Northern Kentucky.

Forty-plus years in one trade teaches you what lasts. Proper footings. Mortar mixed for the weather it will face. Courses laid plumb. That is the Habco standard, and it has not changed since 1983.
