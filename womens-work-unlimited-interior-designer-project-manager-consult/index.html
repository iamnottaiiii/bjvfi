About the Atelier

Design is only half the job

Women's Work Unlimited was founded on a simple belief: a beautiful plan means little without someone to carry it through. As an interior designer, project manager, and consultant based in Cincinnati, Ohio, the studio pairs creative vision with practical, on the ground management.

From first consultation to final walkthrough, you have one devoted partner coordinating the details, so you can enjoy watching your home come together instead of chasing down deliveries and contractors.

  • Atelier approach. Every palette and plan is made for you, never pulled from a template.
  • Managed delivery. Orders, trades, and timelines are tracked and communicated clearly.
  • Honest counsel. Straightforward advice on where to invest and where to save.
