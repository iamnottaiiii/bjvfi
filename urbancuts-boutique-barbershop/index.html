The shop

A boutique cut, without the boutique attitude.

Bright, relaxing, and run like clockwork. Clients describe it as very professional, very clean, and very consistent.

Urbancuts has been cutting hair in Buffalo since 1997, most recently from its Esser Avenue home in Riverside after years on Grant Street. The formula has not changed: sharp, modern cuts in a bright, welcoming space where families feel comfortable bringing the kids.

"Best haircuts in the city of Buffalo. Family oriented, safe environment, and the barbers are on their game with all the updated styles."

Reviewers keep coming back to three things: professionalism, cleanliness, and consistency. Crispy fades, detailed lineups, and cuts that look the way you asked.

Booking an appointment is the smoothest way in. The barbers run on schedule, so calling ahead means your chair is waiting and your cut starts on time.

From classic gentlemen's cuts to the latest fade work, beard shaping and hot towel finishes, the service list stays focused on what a great barbershop does best.

About

Nearly three decades of sharp.

Urbancuts Boutique Barbershop opened its doors in 1997 and has been a Buffalo staple ever since. Longtime clients remember the Grant Street years; today the shop serves Riverside from 126 Esser Avenue with the same steady hand.

The "boutique" in the name shows up in the details: a bright, relaxing space, an appointment book that actually runs on time, and barbers who keep up with every updated style instead of cutting the same three looks all day.

"The barbers are on their game when it comes to all the updated styles. Totally worth it."

Families are a big part of the clientele. Parents bring their boys knowing the shop is clean, professional, and welcoming, and the appointment system means nobody sits around waiting with restless kids.
