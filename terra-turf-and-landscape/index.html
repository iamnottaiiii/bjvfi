Services

The full field sheet.

Terra Turf and Landscape is a full-service lawn and landscape management company serving residential and commercial properties in the Cincinnati area. Our specialties are landscape maintenance and installations.

Spec 01 · Residential and commercial

Lawn Care

Regular mowing, edging, and trimming that keep turf even, edges crisp, and the whole property reading neat from the curb.

  • Routine mowing visits
  • Edging and trimming
  • Leaf and debris removal
Spec 02 · Our specialty

Landscape Maintenance

Ongoing care for beds and plantings: pruning at the right time, fresh mulch, weed control, and seasonal cleanups that reset the property.

  • Pruning and shaping
  • Mulch refreshes

About

A local crew, on the plan since 2016.

Terra Turf and Landscape was founded in 2016 by owner Dan Wanstrath and his wife Aimee. It is a family-run, full-service residential and commercial lawn and landscape management company based in Cincinnati, Ohio.

We specialize in landscape maintenance and installations.

That focus is the whole business: keep existing landscapes healthy and sharp, and build new ones correctly from the start. No sprawling menu of side services, just lawn and landscape work done by people who do it every day.

Based at 3753 N Berkley Cir in Cincinnati, the crew serves homes and businesses across the area. For scheduling, pricing, or project questions, the fastest route is a phone call.
