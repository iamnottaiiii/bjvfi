our story

A neighborhood salon,
kept personal

Polished Image is a small, independent nail salon on Farmington Ave in Kensington, built on repeat guests and word of mouth.

The shop

Cute, clean, and welcoming

Guests describe a cute shop that is kept clean, a place where you can browse the color wall while you wait and leave with nails you are happy to show off. It is the kind of salon where walk-ins are greeted warmly and regulars are remembered.

The team

Personable and professional

Reviews highlight techs who take their time, explain the options, and treat every set of nails like it matters. That unhurried, personal touch is why so many guests come back.

The craft

Color that lasts

From careful Shellac removal to a flawless gel finish, the focus is on healthy natural nails and color that holds up to real life.

The promise

Leave polished

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, the goal is simple: you walk out feeling a little more put together than when you walked in.
