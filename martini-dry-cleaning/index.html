Services

Three crafts, one counter

Dry Cleaning

Careful cleaning for suits, dresses, coats, and delicates.

  • Suits and separates - cleaned and pressed to a sharp finish
  • Winter coats and outerwear - heavy garments handled with care
  • Dresses and fine fabrics - attention for delicate materials
  • Stain treatment - targeted care for spots before cleaning

Shirt Laundry

Washed, starched to your liking, and pressed crisp.

  • Laundered shirts - folded or on hangers, your choice
  • Quick turnaround - ready on a schedule you can plan around
  • Consistent finish - collars and cuffs pressed sharp

Alterations and Repairs

Small fixes and adjustments from experienced hands.

  • Button replacement - missing buttons sewn back on
  • Rip and seam repair - small tears mended neatly
  • Hems and adjustments - ask at the counter for your garment
