About the shop

A family shop with
barbershop roots

Welcome to a family friendly barbershop and unisex salon in the heart of Allentown. The chairs are staffed by 10+ year licensed barbers and a cosmetologist, so the whole family can get sharp in one stop: precision fades, classic cuts, beard sculpting, and hot towel shaves.

The Sneaker Studio in the name is the culture. The shop blends old school barber craft with sneakerhead energy, a spot where the conversation is as fresh as the lineup.

Practical stuff: free parking on site, credit cards accepted, Wi-Fi, and a kid friendly space. Appointments are preferred and walk ins are always welcome.
