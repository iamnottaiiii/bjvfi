Why design first

Start with a drawing,
not a truckload of mulch

A landscape design gives every plant, bed, and path a reason to be where it is. It saves rework, keeps the budget honest, and makes the yard easier to care for year after year.

How we work

Site-first thinking

Sun, shade, soil, and drainage come before plant picks. The plan fits your lot, not a catalog photo.

Plants that suit PA

Selections chosen for Central Pennsylvania seasons, so the yard looks intentional in April and in October.

Phased for your budget

Big vision, practical steps. A good plan can be built in phases as time and budget allow.

About

MICHAEL LEHMAN is a landscape designer based in Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania, focused on plans that make yards work better and look better.

The approach

Measure twice,
plant once

Too many yards are built plant by plant with no plan, and it shows: crowded beds, the wrong plant in the wrong light, and money spent twice on the same spot.

The work here starts on paper. Your lot is studied for sun, shade, slope, soil, and drainage, and the design grows out of what the site is actually doing. The result is a yard that is easier to install, easier to maintain, and better looking for years.
