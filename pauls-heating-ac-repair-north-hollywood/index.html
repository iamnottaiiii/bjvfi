Our story

Named Paul's, run like a neighbor.

Paul's Heating & AC Repair North Hollywood is an HVAC contractor based at 5920 Colfax Ave in North Hollywood, serving the San Fernando Valley. The business is built on a simple observation: most HVAC calls are stressful, and stressed homeowners deserve a technician who explains instead of pressures.

Diagnose honestly

We find the actual failure point and tell you what it will take to fix it, including when the honest answer is that a repair is not worth it.

Fix it cleanly

Shoe covers, drop cloths, and a full cleanup. Your home should look untouched except for the comfortable temperature.

Stand behind it

We test every repair before we leave and we come back if something is not right. That is what a local reputation is built on.
