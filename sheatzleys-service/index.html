About the Shop Storefront Ledger

An old fashioned general store page about a modern hometown repair shop.

Photo coming soon A picture of the storefront will be pasted here.

Sheatzley's Service is an automobile repair shop at 465 Old State Rte 74 in Cincinnati, Ohio, reachable at (513) 528-0488. Public listings describe it as a family run business, and that reputation shows up again and again in what customers write about it.

Reviewers describe honest work at a fair price, knowledgeable service, and a welcoming shop with a comfortable place to wait. Customers say they get accurate estimates and straight explanations about which repairs are truly needed, which is the whole reason a neighborhood shop earns trust.

Brake and rotor replacements are among the jobs reviewers mention by name, completed efficiently. Cars and trucks alike roll through the bays, and reviewers say they keep coming back, some for many years and many vehicles.

The short version: treat people square, fix cars right, charge fair.

Hours currently posted are Monday to Thursday 8 AM to 6 PM, Friday 8 AM to 5 PM, closed Saturday and Sunday. Call to confirm
