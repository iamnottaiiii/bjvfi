A Hometown Salon With Heart

Tucked on Lincoln Road West in Crystal Beach, Patricia's Hair Salon is the kind of place where clients become regulars and regulars become friends. It is a neighborhood salon in the truest sense: warm, unpretentious and focused on one thing, making you love your hair.

Patricia works with every client personally, taking the time to understand what you want before a single snip. That care shows up in the reviews, where longtime clients rave about walking in with a shaggy mess and walking out a brand new person.

"Came out of there a new person. She did such an amazing job. I love my new look."

From first cuts for kids to full color transformations, every appointment gets the same friendly, detail obsessed treatment. Come see why Crystal Beach keeps coming back.
