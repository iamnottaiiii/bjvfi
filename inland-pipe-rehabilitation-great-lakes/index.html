About

Inland Pipe Rehabilitation - Great Lakes is the Detroit-area office of Inland Pipe Rehabilitation (IPR), a national provider of underground infrastructure rehabilitation solutions.

The Great Lakes region office at 4086 Michigan Ave in Detroit serves Michigan, Ohio, Indiana, and Pennsylvania with trenchless sewer and pipe rehabilitation work, including emergency rehabilitation of large-diameter interceptors.
