Water Extraction

Standing water is removed quickly with high-capacity extraction equipment, stopping damage from spreading to dry areas.

Drying and Dehumidification

Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers dry walls, floors, and framing. Moisture meters verify everything is truly dry.

  • Odor control where needed
