Our Story

A small shop with a big reputation

Commonwealth Garage started with a simple idea: run a repair shop the way it ought to be run. Owner Raul works the floor himself, answers the phone, and treats every car like it belongs to a neighbor. Because in Springfield, it usually does.

Try, not upsell

Years of loyalty

Drivers who found us once now bring every vehicle they own, and send their friends.

What sets us apart is not fancy equipment. It is patience. We take the time to explain what is wrong, show you the worn part, give you a realistic timeline, and deliver exactly what we promised. Stranded travelers have been squeezed in on a Friday afternoon. Extended warranty claims have been fought and won. Dashcams have been installed as carefully as engines have been rebuilt.

Customers call us their one-stop shop, their honest mechanic on speed dial, and the best garage they have ever been to. We plan to keep earning that.
