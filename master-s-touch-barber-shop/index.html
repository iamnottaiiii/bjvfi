Our story

A neighborhood chair with a master's touch

Master's Touch Barber Shop sits at 1283 E Delavan Ave in Buffalo, and it earned its name the hard way: one head at a time. The shop built its reputation on special hair designs, the kind of detailed line work and custom cuts that turn a regular appointment into a statement.

Inside, it feels like a classic Buffalo barbershop should. Sports and TV talk fill the room, the chairs stay busy, and kids are welcome in the chair just like the regulars. Reviewers call out the professional, efficient service and the community feel, and many mention fair prices that keep them coming back week after week.

Beyond the cut, the shop offers full beard treatments, hot towel service and steam facials, so you can walk out with the whole look finished, not just the haircut.
