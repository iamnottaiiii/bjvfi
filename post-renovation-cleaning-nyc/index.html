Scope of work

Post-renovation services

A house cleaning service focused on one thing: getting your home livable again after construction. The checklist below is a SAMPLE of what a typical post-renovation visit covers.

SAMPLE checklist, call to confirm
  • Fine dust removal from all reachable surfaces, including walls, ledges, and trim.
  • Floor detail: vacuum, damp mop, and spot treatment for paint flecks and residue.
  • Kitchen reset: cabinet fronts and interiors wiped, counters and backsplash detailed, appliances wiped down.
  • Bathroom reset: fixtures, tile, mirrors, and grout haze cleaned after tile work.
  • Vents, sills, and baseboards: the dust traps standard cleanings skip.
  • Light fixtures and switch plates dusted and wiped.
  • Debris pickup: small construction leftovers bagged and removed from the work area.
SAMPLE: this checklist is illustrative, not a confirmed menu. Call (646) 640-0388 to confirm exactly what is included in your booking.
