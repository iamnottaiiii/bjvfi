Our Story

Rooted in a Cincinnati landmark

Tri-State Building Ltd operates from Suite 212 of the Tri-State Building at 432 Walnut Street in downtown Cincinnati.

The building itself is a piece of the city's history. Designed by the noted firm D.H. Burnham and Company and completed in 1903, the fifteen-story building originally served as headquarters of the Cincinnati Traction Company and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

As a general contractor, we bring that same long-view thinking to every job: build it well, manage it honestly, and leave every property better than we found it.
