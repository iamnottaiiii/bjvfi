Our Story

Pouring concrete in La Cañada Flintridge since 1986

Boc Construction, Inc. has worked out of the same Foothill Boulevard corridor for decades. Listing records date the company to 1986, and it remains a locally owned specialty contractor focused on one trade: concrete.

The approach has not changed much in that time. Show up, look at the site, tell the customer the truth about what the concrete needs, and pour it once, properly. The company's trade listings cover slip forming, exposed aggregate and grooved finishes, plus cast-in-place curbs and gutters, which is the unglamorous infrastructure work that keeps neighborhoods draining and walkable.

Residential, commercial and industrial clients across the Los Angeles area call the same number they have called for years: (818) 266-0985.
