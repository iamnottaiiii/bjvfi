About

A Queens plumbing name with staying power

James J Kump Plumbing & Heating is based at 6-66 Seneca Ave in Flushing, Queens, and has been registered in New York since 1990. Owner James Kump runs a licensed plumbing and heating operation serving homes and businesses across the borough.

As a smaller, long-standing shop, the business runs on reputation: show up, do the work properly, and charge a fair price. That is the whole pitch, and it has kept the doors open for decades.
