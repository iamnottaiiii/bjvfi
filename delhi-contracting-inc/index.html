Our Story

Digging in since 1966

Delhi Contracting Inc. has operated from Rapid Run Road in Cincinnati since 1966. That is six decades of excavating through every kind of Ohio ground: river clay, hillside rock, and everything in between.

The company stays deliberately small, with a tight crew led by owner Carl Kappen, and works mainly contractor to contractor as well as directly with property owners. The model is simple: show up, move the dirt right, and stand behind the grade.

Sixty years in one trade in one town does not happen by accident. It happens one honest job at a time.
