Our story

Two names, one yard

Original Cargo Cabana is listed as a modular home dealer in Lake View Terrace, California. It shares its address at 11415 Ruggiero Ave and its phone line, (818) 480-0909, with uPad Cabana, listed as a modular home builder, and with UPAD DESIGN LLC at the same location. In practice that means the dealer and the builder are the same operation: the people who answer the phone are the people who raise the walls.

Panel-first building

The builder's project notes describe SIP wood frame wall panels installed on a slab foundation in about four days. Panels arrive ready, the crew sets them, and a small home takes shape in under a week.

Turnkey or DIY

Buyers can take a finished unit installed on their slab, or choose the affordable do-it-yourself home kit and assemble it themselves. The notes name two models: the 240 sq ft legacy unit and the uPad Cabana Home studio unit.

Why "cargo"?

The name nods to the shipping container: a steel box engineered to travel the world and survive it. The cabana takes that same rugged logic and turns it into a small, honest home.
