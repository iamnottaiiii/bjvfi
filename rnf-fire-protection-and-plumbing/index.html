Our story

A specialty contractor with two crafts

RNF FIRE PROTECTION AND PLUMBING was formed as a California corporation in 2023 and remains in active standing. From our Glendale base, we serve commercial and residential clients across Los Angeles County as a licensed specialty contractor.

Fire protection and plumbing are both life safety systems in their own way. One guards against fire, the other keeps water where it belongs. We built a company around doing both with the same discipline: plan carefully, install to code, test everything, and stand behind the work.

Our team of skilled professionals handles installation, maintenance, and repair, so property owners and general contractors have one accountable partner for both systems.

What we stand for

Code first

Fire and plumbing work lives or dies by compliance. We install and test to the standard, every time.

One accountable crew

Both trades under one roof means clear responsibility and no finger pointing between subs.

Built to maintain

We install systems we are proud to service for years, and we offer the maintenance to prove it.
