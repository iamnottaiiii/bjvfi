01Lawn Mowing and Care+

Regular mowing to keep your grass at a healthy height, with clean edges along walks and driveways. Recurring schedules keep the lawn consistent through the growing season.

One-time mows are available too. Call to describe your yard and set up the first visit.

02Landscaping+

Planting and yard improvements that change how the whole property feels: new plants, refreshed layouts, and features sized to your space and budget.

Bring your ideas or ask the crew for suggestions. Call to walk the yard together and plan the work.

03Flower Bed Care+

Weeding, planting, and bed refreshes that turn overgrown beds into tidy, colorful ones. Customers specifically mention beds transformed from weedy to beautiful.

Bed edging and mulch touch-ups keep the finished look crisp. Call to add bed care to your visits.

04Hedge and Shrub Care+

Trimming and shaping for bushes and hedges, keeping them neat, healthy, and clear of paths and windows.

Overgrown shrubs can be cut back and shaped over one or more visits. Call for an assessment.

05Landscape Design Help+

Planning help for new beds, plantings, and layout changes: which plants fit the spot, where beds should go, and how the pieces work together.

A design conversation is the first step before any planting work. Call to start with a walkthrough.

06Scheduling Options+

Set up recurring visits through the season, or book one-time service when the yard needs attention.
