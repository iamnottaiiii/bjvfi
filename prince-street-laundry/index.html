Our story

Isabel's, to the neighbors

Walk into Prince Street Laundry on any given day and you will find the same thing North Enders have found since the early 1990s: a corner laundromat that doubles as the neighborhood's living room.

Locals know the shop as Isabel's, after owner Isabel, who runs the place with the kind of personal attention you cannot franchise. A local writer recalled her using her own quarters to buy a customer extra detergent, then standing over the machine to make sure the load would actually come out clean.

That spirit never left. Today the team is praised in reviews as gracious, informative, and extremely kind. The machines are spotless, the folding is precise, and delicate items get treated like they belong to family.

It is, as one reviewer put it, a great local family business and an absolute gem.
