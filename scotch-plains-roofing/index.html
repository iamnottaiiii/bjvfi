About

A local roofer Scotch Plains trusts

01

Decades on local roofs

Scotch Plains Roofing is a roofing and siding company based at 1764 Front Street in Scotch Plains, NJ, serving homeowners across Union County. With decades of experience in the industry, the team handles everything from new roof installations to repairs of damage found underneath.

Homeowners describe a professional, knowledgeable, and trustworthy crew: projects start as promised, finish on schedule, and the team stays quick to answer questions along the way.

02

Fair prices, clear details

After windstorm damage, one homeowner compared several estimates and found Scotch Plains Roofing both competitive and thorough: the team squeezed the job into a busy schedule, explained every detail, and replaced a flat roof without problems.

Small commercial roofs get the same treatment. One business owner called a tough commercial project worry-free and completed on time, then asked the team back for a home roof.
