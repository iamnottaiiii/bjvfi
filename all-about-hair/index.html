No. 01 · About

A Neighborhood Institution

On Dixie Highway since 1997.

All About Hair opened its doors in Erlanger in 1997, according to business gift listings, and it has been a Dixie Highway fixture ever since. The shop blends a classic barbershop with a full beauty salon, serving men, women, and children side by side.

Public reviews describe a warm, welcoming shop where stylists take their time and clients never feel rushed. Longtime customers write about bringing their kids, and even their grandkids, to the same chairs, a sign of a shop that treats people like neighbors.

Today the shop carries a 4.9 star average across more than one hundred public reviews, ranking it among the top-rated hair salons and barbershops in Erlanger.
