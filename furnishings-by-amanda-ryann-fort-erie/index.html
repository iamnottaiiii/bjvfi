Our story

One craftsperson, every detail.

Furnishings by Amanda Ryann is a small Fort Erie workshop where furniture gets the attention mass produced pieces never do.

The workshop

Made by hand, not by assembly line.

Amanda Ryann refinishes, repairs, and reimagines wood furniture from her workshop in Fort Erie, Ontario. Every piece passes through her hands alone, from the first inspection to the final coat of finish.

That is why customers notice the details. Drawers that glide again. Stain colors matched to the room. Farmhouse dressers built around exactly what a family asked for.

It is slow work, on purpose. A piece done right does not need doing again.
