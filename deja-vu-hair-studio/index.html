Our story

A studio built on
regulars, not walk-bys

Deja Vu Hair Studio is a neighborhood studio in Fairport's Town Centre Plaza, where clients come for years and bring their families along.

The studio

Relaxing chairs,
real relationships

There is a reason clients keep the same stylist for years here. The studio is calm, the chairs are unhurried, and the team takes the time to understand what you want before the first snip.

Parents bring kids who grow up in the chair. Brides book makeup and then come back for every trim after. It is the kind of place where they remember your name, your usual, and the story you were telling last time.

Easy to reach in Town Centre Plaza with free lot parking, Deja Vu is the Fairport studio neighbors recommend to neighbors.
