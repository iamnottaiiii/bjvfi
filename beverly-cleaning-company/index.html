About us

A local crew,
not a franchise

Beverly Cleaning Company works out of 800 Cummings Center in Beverly, serving homes and businesses across four counties.

Beverly Cleaning Company is a cleaning service based at 800 Cummings Center, Suite 150R, Beverly, MA. The crew handles both sides of the business: recurring home cleaning for local families, and commercial work for offices and larger business spaces.

What sets the team apart, according to recent public reviews, is flexibility. They have deep cleaned large business spaces at very late hours, stayed on schedule, and left places spotless. Clients describe them as prompt, friendly, and thorough, with a genuine desire to do a good job.

The company is open every day, 8 AM to 8 PM, and serves Essex, Middlesex, Norfolk, and Suffolk counties. One call is all it takes to get a quote and a schedule.
