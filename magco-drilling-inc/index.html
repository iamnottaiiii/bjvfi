About Magco Drilling

A Western U.S. drilling contractor based in Azusa, California, built on decades of foundation work.

Our story

Formed by Mike Maggio. Built on hard ground.

Magco Drilling, Inc. was formed by Mike Maggio and has performed technical and challenging foundation projects in the Western U.S. for over 30 years.

Company records date the founding to 1972, and the business has established crews and customers throughout California, Nevada, and Arizona, working in the commercial, civil, and industrial markets with a stated commitment to safety and quality.

The company is headquartered at 501 W Foothill Blvd in Azusa, California. Note: public records show the business was acquired by Aldridge; call the office to confirm current operations and branding.
