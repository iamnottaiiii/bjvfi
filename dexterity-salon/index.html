Services

What we do
best

Hair services for everyday life and special days alike. Tap a category to explore, then call for pricing and availability.

Precision cuts

A great cut is the foundation of everything. Dexterity Salon shapes each cut around your face, your hair texture, and how much effort you want to put in on a Tuesday morning.

Consults come first, clippers and shears second. Walk out with a cut that grows out gracefully.

  • Women's cuts, layered, blunt, textured, or classic, finished with a style.
  • Men's cuts, clean and sharp, tailored to your look.
  • Kids' cuts, patient and friendly, in a chair that does not feel scary.

Color with dimension

Whether you want to cover grays, add warmth, or completely reinvent yourself, color starts with an honest conversation about what your hair can do and what upkeep you are signing up for.

Call ahead for color services so enough time is set aside for your transformation.

  • All-over color, rich, even coverage from roots to ends.
  • Highlights and dimension, brightness placed exactly where it flatters.
  • Root touch-ups, seamless grow-out maintenance.

Styling and finishing

Some days call for more than a cut. Blowouts, event styling, and special-occasion looks that photograph beautifully and hold through the last dance.

Booking ahead is recommended for wedding parties and events.

  • Blowouts, smooth, bouncy, polished.
  • Special-occasion styling, updos and formal looks for weddings, proms, and parties.
  • Everyday styling, learn how to recreate your salon look at home.

Hair health and care

Healthy hair takes color better, holds style longer, and simply looks better. Ask about treatments that restore moisture, strength, and shine between services.

Not sure what your hair needs? That is what the consultation is for.

  • Deep conditioning treatments, moisture and repair for stressed hair.
  • Scalp care, the foundation healthy hair grows from.
  • At-home guidance, product and routine advice that fits your budget.
