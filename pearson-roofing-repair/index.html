Roofing Services

Roof leak repair

Water stains grow and wood rots when leaks wait. We find the entry point and fix it at the source.

  • Attic and roof inspection to trace the leak path
  • Flashing repair around chimneys, vents, and skylights
  • Seal and boot replacement on pipe penetrations
  • Water test to confirm the fix before we leave
Sample scope · exact pricing confirmed on site

Shingle repair and replacement

Wind and age lift, crack, and strip shingles. We restore full coverage without touching what still has life in it.

  • Matching replacement shingles to your existing roof
  • Ridge cap and starter course repair
  • Nail pop and lifted shingle reseating
  • Granule loss assessment and honest lifespan guidance
Sample scope · exact pricing confirmed on site

Storm and wind damage

After heavy weather we secure the roof fast, then make permanent repairs.

  • Emergency tarping and weatherproofing
  • Documented damage report for insurance
  • Permanent repair of lifted and missing materials
  • Follow up inspection after the next heavy rain
Sample scope · exact pricing confirmed on site

Flat and low slope roof repair

Ponding water and seam failures are the top killers of flat roofs. We address both.

  • Membrane patching and heat welded seam repair
  • Ponding and drainage correction guidance
  • Flashing and coping repair at parapet walls
  • Coating options to extend service life
Sample scope · exact pricing confirmed on site

Full roof replacement

When repairs stop making sense, we replace. Straight talk on materials, timeline, and cost, in writing.

  • Tear off and disposal of old roofing layers
  • Deck inspection and replacement of rotted wood
  • New underlayment, flashing, and ventilation
  • Workmanship backed by a written warranty
