Our Story

A Brooklyn contractor shaped by masonry, built to last.

Est. 2001

Two Decades on Brooklyn Job Sites

S.SAHAB CONTRACTOR CORP. was registered in New York in July 2001 and has worked across Brooklyn ever since. We started in masonry contracting, the trade that teaches precision and patience, and grew into a full-service general contractor. Our permit history includes facade restoration and alteration work in Brooklyn and Manhattan, including jobs at 382 5th Avenue and 5 Doyers Street.
