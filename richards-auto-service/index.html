Our Story

Richards auto service is a car repair and maintenance shop at 7512 Commerce Ln in Clinton, Maryland. The business of fixing cars well has never been about the newest lift or the shiniest waiting room. It has always been about the same things: listening to the customer, finding the actual problem, fixing it right, and charging a fair price.

That is the work this shop does for drivers across Clinton and Prince George's County. Maintenance that keeps cars dependable. Repairs that are diagnosed before they are quoted. And straight answers when you ask what your car needs and what it does not.

Reviewers who recommend the shop say the same things: good service, fair prices, and a mechanic who fixes what others could not. That reputation is earned one car at a time, and it is the standard every job here is held to.
