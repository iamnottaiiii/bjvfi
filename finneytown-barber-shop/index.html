The Lead Story

Finneytown's Own Barbershop, Still Holding Court on Hempstead Drive

Reported from the shop floor | Cincinnati, Ohio

Finneytown Barber Shop has long been part of the daily rhythm of Springfield Township. Located at 924 Hempstead Dr in Cincinnati, Ohio 45231, near the corner of Hempstead Dr. and Winton Rd., the shop opens early, keeps steady hours, and treats a haircut the way it has always been treated here: as a small ritual of the week.

According to its public listings, the shop is open Monday through Friday from 6:30 AM to 3:00 PM, and Saturday from 6:30 AM to 2:00 PM, with Sundays closed. Patrons in public reviews describe a welcoming atmosphere, easy conversation, and a barber who takes his time with every head of hair. Walk in or call ahead at (513) 522-9090.

Profile

The Man Behind the Chair: Bill

A profile drawn from public reviews | Cincinnati, Ohio

Photo coming soon: the shop floor on Hempstead Dr.

Ask around Finneytown about the barbershop on Hempstead Drive and one name keeps coming up: Bill. Public reviews describe him as a warm conversationalist, a collector of antique radios with a few on display in the shop, and a barber patrons have trusted for decades. One longtime customer notes more than 30 years in the chair.

Customers mention the shop's easygoing Saturday morning atmosphere, with conversation that stretches from old cowboy shows to local news. Reviews also mention he is one of the last of a traditional breed of neighborhood barbers. A business directory record dates the shop's listing to 2001, though the shop itself has not published an official history.
