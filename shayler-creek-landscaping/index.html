Our story

Rooted in Batavia, Ohio.

Shayler Creek Landscaping is based at 1292 Old State Route 74 in Batavia, Ohio, and serves homes and businesses across Southern Ohio, including the Cincinnati, Columbus, and Dayton regions.

Our main line of work is landscape construction: building planting beds, preparing soil, and installing plantings that are meant to last. We have been listed in The Blue Book, the construction industry directory, since 1998.

Alongside construction, we handle the everyday care a landscape needs to stay healthy: mowing, pruning, fertilizing, and watering. One crew, start to finish, for the whole yard.
