The short version

Small crew. Big standards.

SimpleFix Handyman operates under the Meraki Contractors umbrella in Walton, KY. Meraki builds and remodels; SimpleFix exists for everything smaller: the door that will not latch, the light fixture sitting in its box for three months, the shelf that is still leaning against the wall.

We are members of the Building Industry Association of Northern Kentucky, and we show up like it: on time, in uniform, with drop cloths and a plan. Customers tell us the crew goes above and beyond, works fast without cutting corners, and is one of the few companies they fully trust inside their home.
