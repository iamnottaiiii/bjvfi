Our story

The name on the truck
is the name on the job

Thomaq & Sons, Inc is led by Renaldo Qafzezi and operates as a general contractor serving Worcester and the surrounding Central Massachusetts communities. When a family puts its name on the business, every job becomes personal.

That shows up in the way we work: clear estimates before we start, tidy job sites while we are there, and honest communication when plans need to change. No disappearing acts, no surprise line items.

From kitchens and baths to full renovations and additions, we take on residential and commercial projects of all sizes, and we treat a one-room update with the same care as a whole-house build.
