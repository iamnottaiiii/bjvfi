Our story

Sample illustration

A shop built on craft

Class Of Our Own Barbershop sits on Pleasant Street in Worcester, a neighborhood shop where the work speaks for itself. The name says it all: this is a place in a class of its own.

Reviewers describe a warm, welcoming, and professional atmosphere, and an owner who takes real pride in the craft, combining skill, precision, and artistry in every chair.

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, you get the same thing: a careful cut, an honest conversation, and a finish you can feel.
