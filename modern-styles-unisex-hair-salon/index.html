Our Story

A neighborhood salon on West Genesee

Modern Styles Unisex Hair Salon sits at 4112 W Genesee St in Syracuse, serving the west side and the surrounding towns. It is the kind of shop where regulars book by first name and newcomers are greeted like regulars.

Reviewers point to the same things again and again: stylists who genuinely know hair, a clean shop, fair prices, off-street parking, and a wheelchair accessible entrance. Some guests have been coming for years and keep coming even after moving away.

Beyond cuts and color, the salon also runs an in-shop nail studio, so you can handle hair and nails in one visit.

  • Experienced, friendly stylists
  • Clean, comfortable shop
  • Reasonable, honest pricing
  • Good for kids and families
