Who we are

The yard on Marshall Rd

Peninsula Auto Recycling Ltd is an auto wrecker and recycler serving Niagara Falls and the surrounding region from 3516 Marshall Rd.

What the yard does

Every week, end-of-life vehicles roll through our gates. Skilled hands strip what is still good, engines, transmissions, electrical parts, body panels, and those parts go back on the road at fair prices. What is left is recycled for its metal.

Peninsula Towing and Recovery works out of the same address, so the truck that picks up your car belongs to the same crew that processes it. Reviewers mention helpful, fair dealing and a towing service they recommend to neighbors.

Why recycling matters

A car is one of the most recycled products on earth, and for good reason. Reusing a good engine keeps it out of a landfill and keeps your repair bill down. Recovering steel and aluminum takes far less energy than mining new metal.

When you sell us your vehicle, you are not junking it. You are feeding a cycle: parts reused, metals recovered, fluids handled properly. That is the whole business, done right.
