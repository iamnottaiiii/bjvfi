About

About K&R Towing LLC

K&R Towing LLC is a family owned and operated towing company based at 7927 Old Jessup Rd in Jessup, Maryland. Since 2008, the company has served Howard County and Anne Arundel County, including work alongside state police barracks in the region.

The fleet includes flatbeds and wheel lifts, and the team provides towing plus roadside services such as lockouts, fuel replacement, and tire changes.
