Insulation installation

The core trade. New insulation for attics, walls, and hard to reach spaces, installed to stop heat from escaping in winter and entering in summer.

  • Attic and wall insulation upgrades
  • Qualifying customers have received insulation at no cost through program rebates
  • Work scheduled and completed within the promised timeframe, per reviews

Home energy audits

A full walkthrough of how your home uses energy, with a clear priority list of fixes. Reviewers say the team made even remote audits easy.

  • Room by room efficiency review
  • Simple written findings you can act on
  • Guidance on which improvements pay back fastest

Air sealing and upgrades

Sealing the gaps around wiring, pipes, and framing before insulation goes in, so the new material performs at full value.

  • Targeted sealing of attic and basement leaks
  • New LED lighting as part of efficiency visits
  • Draft reduction customers notice immediately
