What we do

In-house capabilities, end to end

From first article inspection to production runs, every step happens in our Chatsworth facility. Short runs, prototypes, and full production quantities.

CNC Punching

High speed, highly accurate turret punching with extensive tooling in every shape, plus custom tooling where the job demands it.

Precision Forming

Press brakes from 4 to 10 feet and up to 100 tons, with tooling for every common bend radius plus custom tools for intricate bends.

Shearing and Notching

Clean, accurate shearing and corner notching to get every blank started right, held to tight tolerance from the first cut.

Welding and Assembly

Full welding and mechanical assembly: PEM press hardware, fasteners, latches and rivet assemblies built to print.

Machining and Deburring

In-house machining and thorough deburring so parts arrive clean, safe to handle and ready for finishing or assembly.

Design, Inspection and Finishing

Product design and engineering with cost saving methods built in, first article inspection, and outside processing including anodize, powdercoat, chem film and silkscreen.

Capabilities

Every process in-house

One of Southern California's premier precision job shops for electronics and aerospace. Shearing, punching, forming, machining, welding, deburring and assembly, backed by first article inspection and quality control.

CNC Punching

Amada CNC turret punch press with extensive tooling: single-D, double-D, keyways, trapezoids, corner radii, countersinks, and custom tooling options.

Precision Forming

Press brakes 4 to 10 feet long, up to 100 tons. Tooling for bend radii from sharp to 1.000 inch, plus custom tools for intricate bends.

Shearing and Notching

Amada shear and corner notcher for clean blanks in aluminum, steel, stainless, copper and brass from .010 through .250 inch.

Machining

In-house machining for secondary operations that need to hit the print exactly.

Welding and Assembly

PEM press nuts, studs and standoffs, Southco hardware, latches, blind, tubular, solid and pop rivet assemblies.

Deburring

Every part deburred and clean, safe to handle and ready for finish.

Design and Engineering

Product design services with cost saving methods built in before the first chip is cut.

Quality Control

First article inspection and quality control processes in place on every job.

Finishing

Outside processing managed for you: anodize, chem film, nickel, tin, paint, powdercoat and silkscreen.

About us

A Chatsworth job shop since 1986

Design Tech Industries was established in Chatsworth, California in 1986 and has remained one of Southern California's premier precision job shops. For over 39 years it has been at the same location with the same phone number, serving the electronics and aerospace industries.
