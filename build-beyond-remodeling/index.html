Services

Remodeling services, detailed

Select a service to see what a typical project includes. Every engagement starts with a free consultation and a detailed written plan.

Kitchen remodeling

The kitchen sets the tone for the whole home. We design kitchens around your cooking habits, storage needs, and style, then build them with precision craftsmanship.

Typical timelines run from a few weeks for focused updates to longer for full reconfigurations. You receive a detailed schedule during your free consultation.

  • Custom layouts and cabinet design
  • Countertops, backsplashes, and islands
  • Lighting, flooring, and fixture upgrades
  • Plumbing and electrical coordination

Bathroom renovation

From efficient hall baths to spa like primary suites, we turn dated bathrooms into spaces that feel clean, calm, and built to last.

A standard bathroom remodel typically takes two to four weeks depending on scope, with a detailed timeline provided up front.

  • Tile, shower, and tub installations
  • Vanities, toilets, and fixtures
  • Ventilation and waterproofing done right
  • Accessibility friendly design options

ADUs and room additions

Add living space and long term value with an accessory dwelling unit, garage conversion, or room addition. We handle permits, design, and full construction.

ADU projects include feasibility review, plans, permitting with the city, and complete build out.

  • Detached and attached ADUs
  • Garage conversions
  • Bedroom and living space additions
  • Permits and plans managed for you

Outdoor living

Make the backyard your favorite room. Decks, patio covers, pavers, and exterior refreshes built for Southern California living.

Outdoor projects are planned around drainage, sun exposure, and how you actually use the space.

  • Custom decks and patio covers
  • Pavers, driveways, and walkways
  • Stucco, paint, and exterior facelifts
  • Artificial turf and low water landscaping prep

Whole home redesign

When the vision is bigger than one room, we coordinate architecture, interior design, and construction into a single seamless project.

You work with one team from concept through completion, with daily updates and a single point of accountability.

  • Full home renovations
  • Floor plan reconfigurations
  • Coordinated interior finishes
  • Phased work to fit your life

Our story

Built by two brothers, trusted across the Valley

Build & beyond remodeling was founded by two brothers with a passion for quality craftsmanship and innovative design. For more than 16 years the company has served Southern California homeowners with kitchen and bathroom renovations, ADUs, cabinet facelifts, decks, and full home transformations.

The approach is deliberately customer first: complimentary consultations, transparent planning, daily project updates, and meticulous attention to detail. An in house team brings together specialists in construction, architecture, and interior design, so projects move from concept to completion without the usual handoffs and finger pointing.

The company holds California Contractor License #1119334 and is fully licensed, bonded, and insured, serving communities across Los Angeles County including Canoga Park, Woodland Hills, West Hills, Calabasas, Chatsworth, Tarzana, Reseda, and Northridge.
