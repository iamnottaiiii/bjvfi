Our story

A family crew with one rule: leave it better than we found it.

Turcios Brothers Contracting is a family-run remodeling company based at 211 W 2nd St in Silver Grove, KY, serving homeowners across Northern Kentucky. In our own words: our number one goal is to leave a great job, leave, and know that our customers are happy with the job.

We take on the work other crews pass over, from a single door in a basement room to full rental rehabs with roof repair, fencing, painting and concrete. One reviewer hired us for three different houses and called the work stellar every time.

What customers notice most is simple: we work hard, we respond fast, and we finish on time at a reasonable cost. That is the whole business plan.
