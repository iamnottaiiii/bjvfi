Services

Straightforward yard work, priced by the job and done on schedule. Service list below is based on the public business listing. Call to confirm exactly what is offered.

Regular mowing to keep your grass at a healthy height all season.

  • Mow, trim, and blow clippings off walks and drives
  • Weekly or every other week schedules
  • Gate latches closed and yard left tidy

The detail work that makes a mow look finished.

  • Crisp edges along sidewalks, driveways, and curbs
  • String trimming around fences, trees, and beds
  • Debris blown clean when done

Fresh mulch keeps beds neat and helps hold moisture through Cincinnati summers.

  • Old mulch turned or hauled out
  • New mulch laid at an even depth
  • Bed edges re-cut for a clean line

Keep hedges shaped and shrubs off the house and walkways.

  • Shearing for formal hedges
  • Selective pruning for flowering shrubs
  • Trimmings hauled away

Spring and fall cleanups that reset the yard for the season ahead.

  • Leaf and stick removal
  • Bed and corner cleanout
  • Gutter-line and fence-line clearing

Ohio clay compacts fast. Aeration opens the soil so seed and water can get in.

  • Core aeration in spring or fall
  • Overseeding to thicken thin spots
  • Best scheduled ahead of the rainy season
