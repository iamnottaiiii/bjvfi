Our Story

Counsel you can count on.

FIQ Services Inc is a real estate consulting firm based in Woburn, Massachusetts, in the heart of Middlesex County. The firm works with buyers, sellers, and investors across Greater Boston, offering guidance that is practical, respectful, and grounded in the facts of each situation.

Rather than a one-size-fits-all playbook, every engagement begins with your goals: what you are trying to accomplish, what the numbers say, and what the next right step looks like. Clients consistently describe working with the team as straightforward and dependable, a reputation earned one consultation at a time.

If you are weighing a move, an investment, or a sale, FIQ Services Inc is here to help you think it through.
