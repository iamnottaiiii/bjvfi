Our story

A neighborhood shop
on Eastern Ave.

Z & Z Auto Service is a family-run repair shop in the Vale neighborhood of Schenectady, owned by Ismael Kareem and licensed by New York State since 2013.

Since 2013

Licensed and accountable

Z & Z operates as a New York State DMV licensed repair shop, which means regulated standards, real accountability, and paperwork you can trust. It is also a licensed used car dealer, so the cars on the lot meet the same bar as the cars on the lift.

Hands-on owner

The owner is the mechanic

Reviewers keep mentioning the same thing: the owner is professional, present, and personally invested in the work. When the person quoting your repair is the person turning the wrench, corners do not get cut.

Word of mouth

Recommended daughter to father

One reviewer came because his daughter recommended the shop after years of overpaying elsewhere. That is how this place grows: one fixed car, one fair bill, one family member telling another.

Fair by reputation

Beating the big names on price

The most repeated theme in our reviews is simple: great quality work without the big-shop markup. Drivers who used to brace for the bill now just call Z & Z first.
