Our story

The Spa Pointe family,
in Glastonbury Center

Spa Pointe Too is the Hebron Avenue sister location of Spa Pointe, bringing the same careful, guest first approach to a cozier corner of town.

Small room, big exhale

Guests describe Spa Pointe Too as the calmer sibling: the same skilled hands as the original Spa Pointe, with a quieter atmosphere and an easier time getting in. Many visitors say they never have to wait long, and walk-ins are welcome when the schedule allows.

The philosophy is simple. Keep the rooms spotless, take your time with every guest, and use quality products that are kind to skin. The Spa Pointe family is known for an eco friendly, cruelty free approach with natural and organic spa products, and that care runs through everything here.

Whether it is your wedding week or just a Tuesday that needs rescuing, you are welcome here.

What guests notice first

Cleanliness, every time. Then the calm. Then the way the staff remembers what matters to you, from a sensitive nail bed to a favorite polish shade.

  • A quieter alternative to the busy original location
  • Walk-ins welcome when the book allows
  • Attentive to individual needs and preferences
  • Eco friendly, cruelty free, natural products
