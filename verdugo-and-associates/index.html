A South Pasadena builder since 1999

Verdugo & Associates is a residential construction company based at 1015 Hope Street in South Pasadena, California. The company works as a general contractor, with a focus on additions and remodels, garage conversions, and home additions.

Owner Michael Verdugo has run the company as a small, hands on operation from the start. With a crew of one to four people, the business stays intentionally local: every project gets direct owner attention instead of being handed down a chain of subcontractors.

That local focus shows in the work. South Pasadena homes are often older, well built, and full of character, and the company specializes in expanding and updating them without losing what makes them special.
