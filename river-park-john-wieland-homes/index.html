The Community

River Park, Fayetteville

01The Neighborhood

River Park sits on Briergate Place in Fayetteville, GA 30215, a residential pocket with the easy rhythm buyers look for in Fayette County. Homes here mix modern Craftsman styling with traditional brick, and most were built within the last 10 to 15 years, giving the neighborhood a fresh, updated character.

Inside, the plans emphasize family living: open-concept great rooms, gourmet kitchens with granite countertops and stainless appliances, large center islands, crown molding, and hardwood floors. Master suites typically include walk-in closets and spa-inspired baths, while bonus rooms and finished basements add flexibility for home offices, media rooms, or guest space.

02The Builder

John Wieland Homes has built communities across Georgia for decades, earning a reputation for thoughtful design and consistent quality that keeps resale interest strong. Details like high ceilings, large windows, gas fireplaces, and well-proportioned rooms show up plan after plan.

That track record matters at resale. A John Wieland name on the neighborhood carries weight with buyers who know the brand and trust what it stands for.

03The Location

Fayetteville puts you close to the things that run your week. Downtown Fayetteville's shops and restaurants are nearby, Peachtree City is a short drive, and Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport is reachable without the worst of Atlanta traffic.

Fayette County schools serve the area, which keeps River Park on the short list for relocating families. For specifics on current availability, floor plans, and pricing, call the community office at (770) 996-6065.
