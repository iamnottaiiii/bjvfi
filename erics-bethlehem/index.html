About Eric’s

A neighborhood barber shop in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania.

Our shop

Small shop, serious craft

Eric’s is a barber shop at Eric’s, 2499 Willow Park Rd, Bethlehem, PA 18020. The shop is built around one idea: give every person in the chair your full attention. Reviewers keep coming back for the genuine skill and care that goes into every haircut, the clean and welcoming space, and a barber who listens to what you are asking for and makes sure you leave with exactly the cut you wanted.

Whether it is a classic crew cut or a more modern style, the goal is the same: solid craftsmanship, fair prices, and a friendly visit from start to finish.
