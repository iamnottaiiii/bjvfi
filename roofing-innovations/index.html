Signs you should call

Small signs, big savings
if you catch them early

!

Water stains inside

Brown spots on ceilings or walls mean water is already getting in. Every rain makes it worse.

!

Shingles in the yard

Granada Hills winds lift shingles loose. A missing shingle is an open door for the next storm.

!

Clogged or sagging gutters

Overflowing gutters push water where it should never go: behind siding and under the roof edge.
