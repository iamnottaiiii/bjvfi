Dental care at Apple Dental

General dentistry for patients of all ages. Call the office to ask which services are currently offered and to schedule your visit.

  1. 01

    Routine exams and cleanings

    Regular checkups are the foundation of lifelong oral health. Dr. Shah examines your teeth and gums, and talks through what she finds in plain language.

  2. 02

    Diagnosis and treatment planning

    From a sensitive tooth to a full smile refresh, your care starts with an accurate diagnosis and a plan built around your needs and priorities.

  3. 03

    Restorative care

    When teeth need repair, general dentistry covers fillings, crowns, and other restorations that bring back comfort and function. Ask the office what is available.

  4. 04

    Ongoing oral health guidance

    Prevention beats repair. Get practical guidance on brushing, flossing, diet, and the habits that keep small issues from becoming big ones.

Good to know: this list describes general dental care. Services and availability can change, so please call (301) 592-9224 to ask what is offered before your visit.

Meet the dentist

Dr. Kinjal Shah, DDS

Dr. Kinjal Shah is a general dentist practicing at Apple Dental in Severn, Maryland. She is a graduate of the New York University School of Dentistry and brings over a decade of experience in general dentistry to her patients.

She is licensed to practice dentistry in Maryland and is listed in the national provider registry as a general practice dentist (NPI 1104003854).
