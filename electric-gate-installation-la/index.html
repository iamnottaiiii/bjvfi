Our story

A Valley crew that does one thing well

Electric Gate Installation LA started with a simple observation: plenty of handymen will hang a gate, but few understand the operator, the wiring, the safety devices, and the access control as one system. We built the business around that whole system.

From our shop on Stagg St in Van Nuys, we serve homeowners, property managers, and small businesses across the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles. Open seven days a week, 7 AM to 9 PM, because gates do not keep office hours.
