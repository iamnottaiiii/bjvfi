About the Shop

A neighborhood salon and barbershop with Latin roots on Dixie Highway.

Beauty Salón & barbershop Latin is a hair salon and barbershop located at 5353 Dixie Hwy G in Fairfield, Ohio. It serves the local community with salon and barber services in a welcoming, bilingual setting.

Guests can expect a family friendly atmosphere where English and Spanish are both spoken. For details about the team, history, and current offerings, the best source is the shop itself.

  • Bilingual service: Se habla español.
  • Salon plus barbershop: services for men, women, and kids under one roof.
