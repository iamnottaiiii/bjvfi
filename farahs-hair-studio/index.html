Our story

A Bay Ridge fixture since 2007

Farah's Hair Studio opened its doors in April of 2007, but Farah had been in the field since 1994. After working 13 years in another hair salon, opening her own studio was a dream come true, earned one happy client at a time.

Clients describe walking in like walking into a home. The atmosphere is warm, the staff is attentive, and Farah brings a focus to every head of hair she touches that reviewers have called magical.

Alongside Farah, stylist Nigina is a client favorite for precise layered cuts, beautiful color work, eyebrow threading and natural looking brow tattooing.
