Our story

A Jamaica Ave trade counter since 2011

Carpen’s Plumbing & Heating Supplies INC. opened its doors on Jamaica Avenue to serve one audience: the people who fix buildings. New York corporate records show the company was incorporated in 2011, and the storefront at 175-09 Jamaica Ave has been its home ever since.


Supers from the apartment blocks along Jamaica Ave, licensed plumbers on rush jobs, and weekend DIYers all walk up to the same counter. The promise is simple: real inventory on real shelves, fair trade pricing, and staff who know a sweat joint from a shark bite.

The record

Incorporated as a New York domestic business corporation in 2011. NYC consumer-protection inspections of the hardware-retail operation in 2016 and 2017 both closed with no violation issued. Sources: NY DOS filing 4118886 via nybizdb.com; NYC DCA inspection records.
