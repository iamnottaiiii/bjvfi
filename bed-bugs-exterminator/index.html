Why call us

We do one thing: bed bugs.

Bed bugs hide in mattresses, box springs, bed frames, baseboards, and furniture seams. Finding them early and treating the whole problem at once is what gets a home back to normal.

  • Full inspection of bedrooms, living areas, and common hiding spots
  • Treatment plans built around how bad the infestation is
  • Follow up visits to confirm the problem is resolved
  • Discreet service with unmarked scheduling on request

Seeing signs of bed bugs?

Itchy bites in lines or clusters, rust colored spots on sheets, or tiny shed skins near the mattress are all reasons to get an inspection soon. The earlier the call, the easier the fix.

What happens when you call.

A bed bug job done right follows the same order every time. Here is exactly what to expect.

01

The call

We ask what you have seen: bites, spots on bedding, or live bugs. Then we book an inspection time.

02

The inspection

Mattress, box spring, frame, headboard, nightstands, baseboards, and nearby furniture get checked for live bugs, eggs, and droppings.

Before we arrive

Easy prep checklist.

  • Wash and dry bedding and clothing on the hottest safe setting, then bag them
  • Reduce clutter around the bed so every seam and crack is reachable
  • Pull the bed a few inches from the wall if you can
  • Make arrangements for people and pets to be out during treatment
