Kitchen Remodels

A kitchen remodel is the project homeowners mention most in reviews, from full tear-outs to smart layout changes like removing a wall or rebuilding storage.

  • Layout changes: opening walls, moving islands, new work zones
  • Custom and semi-custom cabinetry with modern storage
  • Countertops, backsplashes, sinks, and fixtures
  • Lighting plans: task, ambient, and under-cabinet
  • Flooring, drywall, paint, and finish carpentry

Bathroom Remodels

Primary bathrooms and guest baths rebuilt for comfort and lasting value, with attention to tile work, waterproofing, and fixtures.

  • Walk-in showers and tub-to-shower conversions
  • Tile floors, walls, and niches with proper waterproofing
  • Vanities, mirrors, lighting, and hardware
  • Ventilation and plumbing updates
  • Accessibility upgrades on request

Home Additions

When your home no longer fits your life, an addition beats a move. We build additions that look like they were always part of the house.

  • Family rooms, primary suites, and home offices
  • Roof tie-ins and siding matched to your home

  • Interior finishes consistent with the existing home

About

A father and son team with deep roots

Kiessling Construction is a father and son general contracting team based in Hummelstown, Pennsylvania, bringing over 40 years of combined experience to home renovations across Central Pennsylvania.

The company is rooted in the trade since 1984 and operates today as Kiessling Construction, LLC. Being a small team is the point: the people who plan your project are the people who build it.

  • Family-run: direct contact with the builders, not a sales office
  • Residential focus: kitchens, baths, additions, basements, outdoor living
