About us

Groesbeck's collision crew

Bratfish Auto Collision Repair Inc. is an independent auto body shop at 4776 Blue Rock Rd in Groesbeck, Ohio. Despite its modest appearance, the shop has built a loyal following for high-quality work and genuinely friendly service.

The team is known for going above and beyond: helping stranded drivers, changing a tire at no charge, fixing small problems free of charge, and pushing insurance companies to act faster on claims.

BBB Accredited with an A+ rating, Bratfish keeps customers updated during every repair, even when delays are beyond the shop's control.
