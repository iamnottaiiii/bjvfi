Our Story

A Tire Shop, Plain and Simple

What Salvage Systems is about, and why Rockville drivers keep coming back.

Tires are all we do. That is the point.

Salvage Systems is a tire shop in Rockville, Maryland. We do not do engines, we do not do transmissions. We do tires: new, used, repaired, rotated, and balanced.

Specializing means we see more tires in a week than most shops see in a month, and it means our prices stay lean. A used tire that safely gets you another year, a new set at a fair price, a flat fixed right instead of sold as a replacement you did not need.

Call ahead with your tire size and we will tell you what we have in stock and what it costs. No appointment needed for most work during business hours.
