Our story

A roofer raised
by roofers

Gio Roofing is a family run business in North Hollywood, founded by Giovanni Orantes in 2017, and built on two decades of learning the trade from the ground up.

2005

Learning
the trade

Giovanni Orantes started working as a roofer in 2005, taught by his dad and a roofing mentor with more than 35 years of experience. He learned the unglamorous way: carrying bundles up ladders, watching how water actually moves across a roof, and seeing which shortcuts fail five years later.

Twelve years of that schooling became the foundation of the company. Every crew member at Gio Roofing works to the same standard Giovanni was taught: do it like it is your own home, because someone's home is exactly what it is.

2017

Going out
on our own

In 2017 Giovanni founded Gio Roofing Inc. on Vineland Ave in North Hollywood. A family run business from day one, it grew the way good roofing companies grow: one neighbor telling another.

Today the crew serves residential and commercial customers across Los Angeles County, from Compton to Lancaster, handling re-roofs, repairs, leak detection and gutters. The approach has not changed since 2005: high quality labor, honest pricing, and a finished roof we are proud to sign our name to.
