About

Local crew, honest work

Niagara Landscape Services is a Fort Erie landscaping company with a simple motto: lawns to landscape.

Our story

We take care of yards like they are our own

Based on Englewood Court in Fort Erie, we are the crew your neighbors call when the lawn needs more than a quick mow. We handle the routine, the seasonal and the full redesigns.

Landscaping is detail work. Straight edges, even mulch, shrubs pruned at the right time of year, grass cut at the right height for the season. That is what a cared-for yard looks like, and it is what we deliver every visit.

We work across Fort Erie and the surrounding Niagara area: Ridgeway, Crystal Beach, Stevensville and beyond. If your yard is in our reach, we will come take a look for free.

  • Locally based at 8 Englewood Ct, Fort Erie
  • Free on-site estimates, no obligation
  • Residential and light commercial properties

What you can expect

We show up. Scheduled visits, rain or shine, with a heads-up if weather moves us.

We clean up. Clippings bagged or blown, gates latched, beds left tidy.

We communicate. Straight talk about what the yard needs and what it costs.
