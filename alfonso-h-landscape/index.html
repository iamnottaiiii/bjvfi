Our story

A Pasadena landscaper, plain and simple

Alfonso.H Landscape is a landscaping business based in Pasadena, California, working from Summit Ave in the 91103.

The approach is straightforward: show up on time, listen to what the yard actually needs, and leave it better than we found it. Lawns get cut with clean edges, beds get planted with the local climate in mind, and irrigation gets tuned so water goes where it should instead of onto the sidewalk.

It is hands on work, and we like it that way. No rushed crews, no corners cut, just steady care for the outdoor spaces people live in every day.

If you are in Pasadena and want a yard that looks cared for, call and tell us what you are picturing. We will take it from there.
