Our story

A neighborhood salon with staying power

Jenae's Hair Designs has been a Union Road fixture for years, building its name the old-fashioned way: one great haircut at a time. Clients write about following their stylists here, staying for decades, and bringing the whole family along.

"Reinvent how you look in a trendy, undeniably inviting atmosphere."

The team keeps the vibe professional but comfortable, the kind of place where you walk in a stranger and walk out feeling like a regular. Evening hours Tuesday through Thursday make it easy to fit great hair into a busy week.
