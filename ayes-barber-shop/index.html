Our Story

The Ayes barber shop story

Ayes barber shop pairs quality barbering with a curated vintage store in the back, making it a true Walnut hidden gem. Barbers Aaron, Kevin, and Manny handle the chairs while the racks hold hard-to-find sneakers and apparel.

Walk-ins are welcome at 18736 Amar Rd, right by the 24 Hour Fitness. Call (909) 374-7702 to check the wait, then browse the vintage selection after your cut.
