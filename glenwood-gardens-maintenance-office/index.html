Who We Are

The Office Behind the Park

We are the maintenance and operations office for Glenwood Gardens, the 338-acre Great Parks of Hamilton County park in the Woodlawn and Cincinnati area.

Our Story

Rooted in Glenwood Gardens

Glenwood Gardens is one of the most loved parks in the Great Parks of Hamilton County system, with scenic trails, playful garden spaces, and wide-open natural areas reminiscent of England's Cotswold region. Our office at 377 Sheffield Road coordinates the daily work that keeps those landscapes peaceful and beautiful: mowing, planting, tree care, trail upkeep, and seasonal cleanup.

We are not a retail landscaping company. We are the park's own grounds team, and our neighbors know our work by walking through it.

  • Office: 377 Sheffield Rd, Cincinnati, OH 45240 (Woodlawn area)
  • Phone: (513) 771-4055
  • Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM; closed weekends
  • The park itself is open daily from dawn until dusk

How We Care

The quiet principles behind our daily work.

We work with the land's natural character, keeping meadows, woodlands, and gardens healthy year round.
