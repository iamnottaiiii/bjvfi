Services

Custom solutions for every corner of the yard.

From brand new construction to the routine care that protects your investment, here is what the crew at Franklin A. Bream Landscaping can take on.

01

Patios and Walkways

A new patio or walk changes how you use your yard. We install patios and walks sized to the property, graded for drainage, and built to handle Pennsylvania weather for years to come.

02

Retaining Walls

Slopes and grade changes do not have to be problems. We build retaining walls that hold back the hill, manage water flow, and add clean lines to the landscape.

03

Gardens and Plantings

New gardens designed around your soil, sun, and the look you want. We can also improve existing beds, refreshing tired plantings without starting over.

04

Seasonal Maintenance

The routine work that keeps a property sharp: shrubbery trimming, weeding, mulching, and seasonal cleanups in spring and fall. Set it up once and the yard stays handled.

05

Excavation and Drainage Control

Water pooling where it should not? We do excavation and drainage control problem solving, finding where the water goes wrong and fixing the grade so it drains right.

About

A local landscaper with a hands-on approach.

Franklin A. Bream Landscaping is based at 31 W Locust St in Mechanicsburg, PA, serving homeowners across the area with custom landscaping solutions and dependable outdoor maintenance.

Our approach

Custom work, not cookie cutter.

Every property is walked first and quoted honestly. Whether the job is a new patio, a retaining wall, a fresh garden, or regular upkeep like trimming, weeding, and mulching, the goal is the same: outdoor spaces that look right and last.

We also take on the trickier jobs, like excavation and drainage control problem solving, for yards where water or grading has been an issue.
