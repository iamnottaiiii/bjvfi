Flagship work

Driveways that take the beating

Your driveway is the most abused slab on your property. We spec the right thickness, reinforcement and joints for Northern Kentucky freeze-thaw cycles, then finish it smooth and seal it tight.

  • Tear-out and haul-away of failed concrete
  • Compacted stone base and proper drainage slope
  • Rebar or wire mesh reinforcement as needed
  • Control joints saw-cut on a real layout

Why Vigilante

The name is a promise

Vigilante Concrete Contractors is a Florence, KY concrete crew with a simple code: show up, prep it right, pour it straight, and stand behind the work.

How we work

Prep is the job. The pour is just the payoff.

Most concrete failures start under the slab: soft base, bad drainage, skipped joints. We spend the time on excavation, compaction and layout so the concrete you see stays flat and crack-controlled for years.

  • Local crew based in Florence, KY
  • Proper base prep on every pour, no shortcuts
  • Written quotes with clear line items
  • Jobsite left clean at the end of each day

A/ Straight talk

If a repair will not hold, we say so. If a full replacement is overkill, we say that too.

B/ Built for Kentucky

Mixes, thickness and joint layouts chosen for freeze-thaw cycles and clay soils, not generic specs.

C/ One crew start to finish

The people who form your slab are the people who finish it. No hand-offs, no excuses.
