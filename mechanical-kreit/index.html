Our story

Three generations of mechanical work

Mechanical Kreit, known as Kreit Mechanical Associates, Inc., has been in continuous family ownership for over 65 years. Today the company is managed by the third generation while training the fourth.

That longevity shapes how the work gets done: extensive QA and QC methods, rigorous training and continuing education, proper communication, and documented controls on every project. The result is quality installation, durable systems, and cost-efficient construction.

Clients work directly with executive leadership, not layers of middle management. The structure keeps decisions fast, support personal, and problem-solving agile.
