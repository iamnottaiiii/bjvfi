Why hydroseeding works

A sprayed slurry of seed, fiber mulch, fertilizer, and tackifier locks moisture against the soil so grass starts faster and grows in more evenly than dry seeding.

Faster Germination

The mulch blanket holds water right where the seed sits, giving new grass a head start in Ohio growing seasons.

Even Coverage

Spray application coats slopes and bare patches uniformly, so you avoid the thin spots dry seed leaves behind.

Budget Friendly

A fraction of the cost of sod, with strong root development from seed grown in your own soil.
