Color My World Painting Co is a small, well-established painting and decorating company based at 101 Fellswood Dr in Moorestown, NJ. We specialize in residential design work and take on projects of every size, from a single room refresh to large whole-house rehabs.

What clients remember most is the experience: a knowledgeable, professional team that shows up on time, communicates clearly, and treats the home with respect. Many of our jobs come from repeat customers and their neighbors.

From the first color conversation to the final walkthrough, the goal is simple: a finish you love and a process you would recommend.
