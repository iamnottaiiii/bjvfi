About the Studio

Design that respects the way you actually live.

Great design is not about filling a room with expensive things. It is about flow, light, color, and the small details that make a space feel effortless. Revamp Designs brings a designer's eye to homes across Jonesboro, helping you avoid costly mistakes and get the look right the first time.

Whether you are refreshing one room or reimagining your whole home, every project starts with listening: your style, your budget, your timeline.

How We Work

A simple process, from first call to final reveal.

1. Consultation. We walk your space, talk through goals, and agree on budget and timeline.

2. Design plan. You get a clear concept: layout, palette, furnishings, and finishes.

3. Styling and install. We source, coordinate, and style every detail into place.

Design Services

Choose the level of help you need

Every project is scoped to your home and budget. Call for exact pricing.

Full-Service Interior Design

End-to-end design for one room or your whole home. We handle the plan, the sourcing, the ordering, and the final styling.

  • In-home consultation and measurements
  • Floor plans and furniture layouts
  • Finish, fabric, and lighting selections
  • Purchasing and project coordination
  • Final styling and reveal walkthrough

Room Refresh

Your room has good bones but feels stuck. We keep what works and update what does not, with paint, textiles, art, and layout tweaks.

  • Style assessment of your existing pieces
  • Paint and accent color recommendations
  • Textile, rug, and window treatment plan
  • Shopping list within your budget

Color & Space Planning

About to buy furniture or repaint? Get the plan right before you spend. Layouts and palettes that fit how you live.

  • Whole-home or single-room color palettes
  • Furniture arrangement and traffic flow
  • Lighting placement recommendations
  • Scale and proportion guidance

Styling & Decor

The finishing layer: art, accessories, greenery, and shelf styling that makes a house feel like a home.

  • Shelf, mantel, and tabletop styling
  • Art selection and hanging
  • Seasonal refresh options
  • Move-in styling for new homeowners
