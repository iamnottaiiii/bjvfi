The practice

A family practice in the heart of Swampscott

Drs. Rogers & Rogers, PC is a general dentistry practice at 25 Railroad Ave, Suite 2, in Swampscott, Massachusetts. It is led by two doctors: Dr. C. Bradford Rogers, DMD, and Dr. Dara K. Rogers, DMD.

Public records confirm the practice address and phone number, and patients consistently describe a small office where the doctors know each patient well and take a thorough, unhurried approach to care.

Appointments are recommended, and new patients are welcome to call the office to ask about joining the practice.
