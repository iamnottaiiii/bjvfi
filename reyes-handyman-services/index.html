Repairs that protect the house

Small problems grow. A loose bit of caulking lets water in, a sticking door chews its frame, a cracked tile turns into a tripping hazard. Getting them fixed early is the cheapest home insurance there is.

Reyes focuses on the fixes that keep a home sound: drywall patches, door adjustments, sealing and caulking, and the installs that finish a room properly.
