Our story

Alexandria born and raised.

Laker's Lawn Care is a family-run crew out of Alexandria, Kentucky, led by Adam Laker. We started with a mower, a trailer, and a simple idea: treat every yard like it is the one on our own street.

Today we care for lawns across Campbell County, from weekly mowing routes to full landscape projects. The tools got bigger, but the idea never changed.

Fast facts
  • Based at 2160 Grandview Rd, Alexandria, KY
  • Serving Alexandria and surrounding Campbell County
  • Crew a small local team, not a franchise
  • Phone (859) 635-5211, Mon to Fri

What we believe

Show up on time. Leave the gate latched. Stripe it like you mean it. A lawn is the first thing people see when they pull up, and we take that personally.

When you call Laker's, you talk to the people who will actually be in your yard. That is how we like it, and it is how we plan to keep it.
