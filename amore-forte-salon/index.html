About

Amore Forte Salon sits at 241 E Seneca St in Manlius, NY, a welcoming space where clients become regulars and regulars become friends.

Our story

Full service, personal touch

Amore Forte describes itself as a full-service salon in Manlius, NY, led by owner Laurie Loveless Armstrong. The reviews tell the rest: a friendly staff, a clean salon, and stylists clients trust for decades.

It is the rare salon where someone can sit down, say "surprise me," and walk out happy. That kind of trust is earned one appointment at a time, and Amore Forte has earned it many times over, with a 5.0 rating across 11 reviews.
