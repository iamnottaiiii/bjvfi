An Old Shop in the Best Sense

Olde Media Tattoo has been in the tattooing business in Media, Pennsylvania since 2009. The shop calls itself a guaranteed source for tattooing, and the reviews agree: a clean, professional studio where the artists take the time to understand each client's idea before a single line is drawn.

"Roddie makes you feel at ease, especially for a first timer. Super clean shop, and the tattoo came out wonderful."

Whether you walk in with a finished design or nothing but a notion, the artists here collaborate with you until the drawing is right. That is the whole philosophy, printed plain: a good tattoo ain't cheap, and a cheap tattoo ain't good.

The Services, Set in Type

An editorial list of what the shop does, drawn from the studio's own description and customer reviews.

I

Custom Tattoos

The heart of the shop. Bring your idea and the artists will work up a design with you until it is right, then tattoo it in a style that suits the piece: black and gray, color, fine line or bold traditional.

Quoted per piece after consultation. Call (484) 444-0133.

II

Cover Up Work

Old ink you regret becomes new art you show off. Reviewers give Mike's cover ups top marks, and the shop is known for thorough consultations that lay out every option before the needle moves.

Bring the existing tattoo in for an evaluation, in person or by photo.

III

Portrait Tattoos

Shane's portrait portfolio turned skeptical clients into believers. Likeness work demands experience, and this is where the shop's long record since 2009 shows.

Book by appointment. Bring a clear, high resolution photo.

IV

Small Tattoos and Walk Ins

First tattoo? The shop is a favorite for first timers, with a calm studio and artists who explain everything. Small pieces and simple designs can often be done same day.

Call ahead at (484) 444-0133 to check same day openings.

V

Consultations and Aftercare

Talk through placement, size and style with an artist before you commit. Every session ends with clear aftercare guidance so the work heals clean.

Consultations are the best first step. Free to ask, easy to book.
