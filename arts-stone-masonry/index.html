Our story

The mason behind the stone

Based in Allison Park and serving the Pittsburgh area since 2004, Arts Stone Masonry is built on one craftsman's reputation, earned one fireplace at a time.

How it started

Twenty-two years of
fitting stone by eye

Art founded Arts Stone Masonry in 2004 with a simple idea: be the mason who shows up, listens, and fits every stone like it matters, because it does. Two decades later, that is still the whole business plan.

Local builders trust him with their clients' homes, year after year. Homeowners write about the detail, the guidance from first call to final cleanup, and the finished work that draws compliments from every guest who walks in.

No sales team, no handoffs. When you call (412) 726-8902, you talk to the mason.
