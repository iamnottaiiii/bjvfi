About Us

A Pittsburgh transport company, plain and simple

Great American Auto Transport is a trucking and vehicle shipping company based on Ellsworth Ave in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. We focus on one thing: getting vehicles from point A to point B without the runaround.

Auto transport is not like shipping a package. Pickup windows depend on routes, weather, and road conditions, so we keep our communication straight: honest timelines, a real person on the phone, and no fine print surprises.

Whether you are relocating for work, buying a car out of state, or sending a vehicle to a family member, we handle the logistics so you can keep your schedule.

Call us at (412) 274-9506 to talk through your move.
