Our craft

The Dominican difference

Dominican salons are known for the blowout: hair washed, deeply conditioned, and blown out smooth with serious body and shine. Stephanie's brings that tradition to Essex, and the silk press takes it further. Thick rollersets, steam treatments, and trims that respect your length goals all happen here, in a family-run shop where the staff know their regulars by name.

“My silk press comes out perfect every time.”Client words
Good to know

Silk press, done right

A great silk press is about prep: a clarifying wash, real heat protectant, and section-by-section work. That is why clients say their presses stay smooth and bouncy for weeks.
