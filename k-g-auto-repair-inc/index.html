Since 1990

Three decades under the same roof

K and G Auto Repair has served the South Bronx from 878 East 149th Street since 1990. Owner Alex and the crew have earned a 4.8 star rating across 47 Google reviews by doing honest work at fair prices, year after year.

Electrical diagnosis is a particular strength here, along with brakes, fuel systems, and the everyday repairs that keep Bronx drivers moving. Generations of customers, including many off duty officers and city workers, trust this shop with their cars.
