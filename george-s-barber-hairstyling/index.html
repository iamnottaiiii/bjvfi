About George's Barber & Hairstyling


George's Barber & Hairstyling is a neighborhood barbershop in Culver City built around sharp cuts and honest service. Walk in for a classic cut, a skin fade, or a hot towel shave, and leave looking sharp.


Every appointment starts with a consultation: what you like, what you do not, and how much upkeep you want. The result is a cut that looks sharp on day one and grows out clean.


Find us at 5586 Sepulveda Blvd, Culver City, CA 90230, or call (310) 398-0011 to book your chair.
