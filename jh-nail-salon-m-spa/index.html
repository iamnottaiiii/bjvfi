Our story

A neighborhood nail spa on Avenue M

Care first, always

Jh Nail Salon M Spa serves the Midwood neighborhood of Brooklyn with manicures, pedicures, nail enhancements, nail art, and waxing. The focus is simple: clean tools, careful work, and service that fits into your day.

Whether you need a quick polish change before the weekend or a full set of acrylics for a special occasion, the team takes the time to get the details right.
