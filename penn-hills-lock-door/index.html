Our Story

Three decades behind the counter

Penn Hills Lock and Door opened its doors in Verona in 1989. It is still family owned, still run out of the same kind of real neighborhood shop you can walk into, and still built on the same idea: know the trade, charge a fair price, and treat people right.

The team serves commercial, residential, industrial, and automotive customers across the greater Pittsburgh area. That means rekeying an office building in the morning, cutting a car key at lunch, and repairing a front door before dinner.

Reviewers keep coming back to the same three words: quick, competent, reasonable. People mention advice they did not expect from a locksmith, quotes that came in under competitors, and charges that came in under the quote itself.
