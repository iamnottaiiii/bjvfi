About

Local hands, honest work

MNJ Home Solutions is a Worcester-based handyman business built on creativity, integrity, and getting projects done on time and within budget.

Our story

Making your house
your dream home

MNJ Home Solutions was founded in 2017 by owner Joel B. Bienvenu, right here in Worcester, Massachusetts.

The idea was simple: one dependable handyman for everything a house needs. No runaround, no surprise charges, just solid work and straight talk.

Today MNJ handles home repairs, improvements, and consultations for homeowners and businesses across the Worcester area, with the same focus it started with: creativity in the work, integrity in the pricing, and an eye on finishing on time and within budget.
