Born in New Haven

Scrappy & Pappys Junk Removal is a Connecticut LLC based right here in New Haven. We are the crew neighbors call when the basement floods, the tenant moves out, or the old couch finally has to go. Small team, big truck, bigger work ethic.

The reviews tell the story better than we can: people mention the fast replies, the same-day turnarounds, and prices that beat every other quote they got. That is the whole business model. Show up fast, charge fair, do it right.

How we work

Call or text (203) 506-3150. Tell us what is going where it should not be. We quote it straight, schedule it fast, and haul it carefully, protecting your doorways, floors, and walls on the way out.
