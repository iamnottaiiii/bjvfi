The craftsmanship behind the counter

A body shop is a craft shop. The work lives in the details: a panel gap that lines up, a paint edge you cannot find with your fingertip, a bumper that looks like it was never touched. That is the standard Kleiman Auto Body has held itself to on Norwood Avenue since 1984, per the shop's own business description.

This is a small, hometown business, and that is deliberate. Smaller means the person writing your estimate is close to the person doing the work, and it means your car is not just another ticket on a line. The shop's technicians are ASE Certified, and the shop advertises a warranty on paint and body repairs for as long as you own your vehicle. Ask for the current written terms when you bring your car in.

Beyond the bench, the shop pitches in where it can: negotiating with your insurance company so you do not have to, offering pickup and delivery as needed, and always giving free estimates with no appointment necessary. Saturdays are by appointment.
