Our story

A Bay Ridge nail salon built on care

New Betty Nail Inc. serves the Bay Ridge neighborhood from its home at 7311 13th Avenue in Brooklyn. The salon focuses on what regulars value most: technicians who take their time, listen to exactly what each client wants, and deliver careful, clean work.

Clients come in for everything from a quick polish change to dip powder manicures, gel sets and spa pedicures. Many are repeat visitors who book a standing visit every couple of weeks.
