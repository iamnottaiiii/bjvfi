About

Jolesz Marta DDS

Dr. Marta Jolesz, DDS is a pediatric dentist practicing at 2200 Kernan Drive in Gwynn Oak, Maryland.

She provides both primary and comprehensive preventive and therapeutic oral health care for infants and children through adolescence, including those with special health care needs. To book an appointment, call (410) 448-2500.
