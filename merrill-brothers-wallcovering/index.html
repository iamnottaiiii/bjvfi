Why wallpaper

Paint covers a wall. Wallpaper finishes a room.

Wallpaper changes how a whole space feels: pattern, texture, and depth that paint alone cannot give. The catch is that it has to be hung perfectly, with clean seams and careful prep underneath.

The difference

It is all in the preparation

Reviewers keep coming back to the same thing: the prep. Walls that had roller lines, flaws, and old damage came out looking smooth and even once the paper went up, because the surface was done right first.

That is the whole philosophy. The pattern gets the compliments, but the prep is what makes it last.
