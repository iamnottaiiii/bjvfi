Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy is for bjvfi (SiteDesk), operated by Business Identity LLC. It covers the bjvfi.com website and the SMS text messaging program run by bjvfi (SiteDesk), operated by Business Identity LLC. It describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We collect information that is needed to build websites, send messages, and run the service, including:

  • Name and business name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Business address
  • Website and business details
  • Message content, including texts you send us and replies you send to our messages
  • SMS opt-in data and consent records
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages visited and device and browser information

How we use it

We use the information we collect to:

  • Build and provide website services
  • Send SMS messages about a business prebuilt website preview and related follow up
  • Respond to replies, questions, and support requests
  • Maintain opt out and consent records
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the service

SMS and text messaging

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, sold to, or bought by third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

When you take part in our text messaging program, we use your phone number and your opt-in and consent records to send and manage those messages. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that are needed to deliver messages and run the service, for example messaging providers and hosting providers, and only so they can do that work for us. We may also share information when required by law, or to protect the service and the rights of others.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as it is needed to provide the service, to maintain consent and opt out records, and to meet legal obligations. When information is no longer needed for those purposes, we delete it or remove the parts that identify a person.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. That stops future text messages from our program. For privacy requests, including asking what information we hold about you or asking us to delete it, contact us by email at contact@bjvfi.com.

Cookies and website data

The website may use basic cookies and similar tools to keep the site working, remember simple preferences, and understand how pages are used. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Turning cookies off may make parts of the website work less well.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact@bjvfi.com.
