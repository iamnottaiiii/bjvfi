The practice

An artist's eye, a builder's discipline

Perry Bush Residential Designs is known for creative and unique home designs across the Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky area. Perry Bush Custom Homes has built more than 420 homes, each carrying the signature "special effects" style: unusual ceiling and wall treatments, unique architectural appointments, and details composed piece by piece.

The firm is a longtime member of the Home Builders Association of Greater Cincinnati and has designed and constructed 27 award-winning show homes. Energy efficiency runs through the work as well, with EPA ENERGY STAR certification and LEED certified homes among its builds.

About

The artist behind the homes

Perry Bush Residential Designs is operated by an accomplished artist and former college art instructor. Perry Bush holds a Master of Fine Arts degree from the University of Cincinnati and the University of North Carolina, and he comes from a family with experience in home construction, having worked alongside his father to learn the building trade when he was younger.

That combination, fine art training plus hands-on construction knowledge, is what makes the firm's homes both aesthetically striking and structurally sound. His signature style is often described as "special effects": meticulous attention to detail in each individual piece of a project, from ceiling treatments to one-off architectural appointments.

Based at 88 Watch Hill Ln in Newport, Kentucky, the practice serves the Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky area.
