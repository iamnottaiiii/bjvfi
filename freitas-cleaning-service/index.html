Our story

Thirteen years of spotless.

Freitas Cleaning Service started with one belief: treat every home like it is your own, with love and affection for the details. Thirteen years later, that is still the whole business plan.

We are a small, background-checked team based on Walnut Street in Saugus. No call centers, no rotating strangers. The people who answer the phone are the people who show up with the supplies. We are punctual, we avoid delays, and we focus on the small details that make a house feel cared for.

Our customers say it best: quality work, professional attitude, real value. One reviewer called us the cheapest option available and still the best service they could have expected. Two people working their best for over two hours, cleaning floors, carpet, windows, walls, kitchen, and bath, even lifting stains they had given up on. That review could be about any of our jobs. We aim to earn it every time.
