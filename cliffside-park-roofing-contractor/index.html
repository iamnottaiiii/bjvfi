Our story

A roofing name you can read at a glance

Cliffside Park Roofing Contractor Repair and Installation does exactly what the name says: repairs and installs roofs in Cliffside Park, New Jersey. Based at 207 Edgewater Rd, the company serves the homes along the Palisades where wind, hail, and old shingles keep roofers busy.

Public reviews paint a consistent picture: replacements completed in the time promised, thorough cleanup afterward, and clear, responsive help when storm damage strikes. Customers describe reasonable pricing and crews that are easy to work with from estimate to final walkthrough.

We are not going to invent a founding date or a family saga. What we can say is that the themes in the reviews - on time, clean, responsive - are the ones that matter when the roof over your head needs work.
