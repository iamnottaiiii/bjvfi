Our story

A classic chair
on Worcester St

Mc Clure's Barber Shop is a neighborhood barbershop in North Grafton: the kind with a steady clientele, a friendly team, and fair prices on the wall.

The shop

Old-school, in the best way

Set at 210 Worcester St, Mc Clure's keeps the barbershop tradition alive in North Grafton. Customers talk about the great environment, the friendly team, and cuts that are fast without feeling rushed.

It is the sort of place where regulars keep coming back for years, and where first-timers say they wish they had found it sooner.

Walk-ins are part of the charm: no booking app, no phone tag. Just a chair, a cape, and a barber who knows what they are doing.

✂

Known for

A team customers call friendly, easygoing, and efficient. Quick trims or a whole new style, they take care of it. And at $14 for a regular cut, the value speaks for itself.

The team

Good with grown-ups,
great with kids

Customers single out the staff for patience and professionalism, including with kids who need a little extra care in the chair.

That is the measure of a neighborhood shop: it takes care of everybody who walks through the door.
