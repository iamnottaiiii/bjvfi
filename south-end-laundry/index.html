In store
How it works

Walk in, walk out clean

No account, no app, no appointment. Bring your laundry, pick a machine, and the staff will help you get started if it is your first visit.

1. Sort and load

Choose a washer size that fits your load. Big loads go in the triple-load machines.

2. Add detergent

Bring your own or buy from the in-store selection, including bleach and softeners.

3. Dry and fold

Move to a dryer, then fold at the tables. Or hand it all to the wash and fold counter.

Our Story

A South End
laundry staple.

Rooted on Columbus Ave

South End Laundry serves the South End from 565 Columbus Avenue, near Symphony station and the New England Conservatory of Music. Students, young professionals, families, and longtime residents all share the folding tables here.

What regulars say

Reviewers describe a clean facility, machines that work, fair pricing, and staff who are straightforward and polite. Many call it their preferred choice for both self-service and full-service laundry in the neighborhood.

Built for real life

Laundry is a chore, so the goal is simple: reliable machines, honest prices, and help when you need it. That is the whole story, and it has worked for years.

Neighborhood

South End, Boston

One of the city's most walkable neighborhoods, with Victorian row houses, restaurants, and galleries. The shop sits close to the Green Line E branch at Symphony, making it an easy stop on the way home.
