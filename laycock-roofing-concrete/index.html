Our story

Alexandria born, roof raised

Laycock Roofing & Concrete is a locally owned roofing contractor based at 3661 Walnut Park Drive in Alexandria, Kentucky. We started with a simple idea: bring big-city roofing craftsmanship to our own neighbors, with small-town honesty on every invoice.

Steel roofing is our specialty. A well installed metal roof can protect a home for 40 to 70 years, shrug off hail and high wind, and cut cooling costs in Kentucky summers. We install standing seam and metal panel systems for homes and commercial buildings, and we service what we sell.

Alongside roofing, our concrete crew pours driveways, patios, walkways, and slabs, so one call can handle the top and the ground of your project. We show up on time, protect your landscaping, and leave the site cleaner than we found it.
