How it goes

From first call to final walkthrough

  • Walkthrough and estimate. We see the space, talk colors, and give a clear written price.
  • Prep day. Patch, sand, caulk, mask, and prime. The unglamorous part that makes it last.
  • Paint. Clean coats, sharp lines, daily tidy-up.
  • Final walk. We inspect every wall with you and touch up anything on the spot.

Our story

A Pasadena paint crew with a simple rule

SharpLine Colorworks started with one belief: the line where the wall meets the ceiling tells you everything about a painter. We hold that line to a higher standard on every job, from a single bedroom to a full exterior.

Based on Mataro Street in Pasadena, we serve homes and businesses across the San Gabriel Valley. Most of our work comes from referrals, which is exactly how we like it.
