Our Story

The salon where detail is the whole point.

Angie's Salon is built around one idea: great hair takes time, and you deserve every minute of it. Tucked into the Annandale Professional Center on Little River Turnpike, this boutique beauty salon has earned a five-star reputation the old-fashioned way, one careful appointment at a time.

Guests describe Ms. Angie as meticulous and caring, someone who treats the shampoo and conditioner step as part of the craft, complete with a warm-towel massage reviewers call worth the visit alone. Clients with Asian hair especially praise her precise, flattering cuts.

The space itself is clean, organized, and calm, a place to unwind while your hair gets the attention it deserves. Whether it is a routine trim or a full transformation, you will leave feeling like yourself, only better.
