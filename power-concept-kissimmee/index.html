Safety

Safety is the service

Most electrical fires start small and preventable. Here is what we check on every visit.

What we look for

Overloaded circuits, loose connections, scorched outlets, missing GFCI protection near water, and panels with double-tapped breakers. Small issues now beat emergencies later.

When to call fast

Burning smells, hot outlet covers, breakers that trip repeatedly, or lights that dim when appliances kick on. Do not wait on these. Call (407) 305-0429.
