Our Story

Nagle Brothers is a family-run masonry shop based at 2205 Richmond Terrace in Port Richmond, Staten Island. For 30 years the company has served the borough as both a masonry and marble contractor, and owner John Nagle still puts his name on the work.

Staying small was a choice. A one-shop operation means the person who looks at your project is the person who does it, and that keeps the quality consistent from the first brick to the final washdown.

If you need marble set right, a stoop rebuilt, or joints repointed before winter, call and talk it over. There is no pressure and no obligation, just straight answers about what the job needs.
