Our story

A Pasadena crew with a simple standard

Maryland LP Home Improvement LLC is a locally owned contracting company based on Duvall Highway in Pasadena, Maryland. We do the work most homeowners put off: the tired bathroom, the unfinished basement, the peeling trim, the deck that has seen better days.

Our reputation in the neighborhood comes from the basics. We show up, we listen to exactly what you asked for, we price it honestly, and we leave the place clean. Customers rate us 4.4 stars across 12 public reviews and keep pointing to the same things: reasonable quotes, helpful crews and professional, non-sloppy work.

Big or small, every job gets a written estimate before we start and a walkthrough when we finish. That is the whole business model.
