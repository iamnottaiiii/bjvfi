About the company

Michael F. Schloss Roofing is a family roofing company based at 4734 Whetsel Ave, Cincinnati, OH 45227. Its own public description notes three generations in business and a specialty that sets it apart: the company is the roofing slate distributor for the Cincinnati area.

Alongside roofing, the crew handles the details other roofers skip: chimney flashing, chimney caps and tuck pointing, gutter cleaning, and roof inspections for real estate sales. In winter, they do snow removal.

Public reviews name Mike and Thomas, describing them as professional, hardworking, and totally trustworthy.
