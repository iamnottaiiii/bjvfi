Our Story

Built one
haircut at a time

Magna Carter I earned its reputation the slow way: loyal clients, meticulous work, and a shop people are proud to bring their families to.

The heritage

A barber's chair
is a kind of trust

Roger is the name that comes up again and again. Clients describe years of great cuts, a meticulous eye for detail, and the ability to understand exactly what you want before the clippers come out.

The shop itself reflects that standard: clean, spacious, and comfortable, with a team of barbers who keep regulars coming back and newcomers converting after a single visit. Some clients travel from out of state rather than trust anyone else with their hair.

It is a family-friendly shop in the truest sense, kids get their first cuts here, dads bring their sons, and mothers have found a trusted chair with Mr. Kenny for trims of their own.
