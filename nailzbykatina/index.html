About

One artist. One studio. Your nails.

NAILZBYKATINA is the studio of nail artist Katina in St Charles, Maryland. Guests find her at 1144 Smallwood Dr W, Suite F2, a quiet appointment only setting built around personal attention.

The approach is simple: take the time to get the shape right, keep the nail healthy underneath, and finish with polish or art that lasts. Guests praise the careful work and the relaxed one on one atmosphere.

Walk in lines rush. Here the appointment belongs to you, from prep to the final top coat.
