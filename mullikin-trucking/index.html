Our story

Family-run, neighbor-approved

Mullikin Trucking is a small, family-run heating oil company on Old Fort Smallwood Road in Pasadena, Maryland. No call centers, no corporate scripts, just people who take pride in keeping local homes warm.

The way we work

When a customer runs out of oil late and calls us, we do not shrug. One reviewer wrote that we came out on very short notice, bled the lines, waited while they got home, and walked them through the work that was done. That is simply how we operate.

Reviewers mention Corey and his grandfather by name: timely, polite, personable, and willing to go above and beyond. Around here, that kind of service is the whole business plan.

Rooted in Pasadena

Our trucks roll out of 1442 Old Fort Smallwood Road, right in the community we serve. Most of our customers are repeat neighbors, some for over five years, who say they have never had a problem and see no reason to change.

Competitive pricing, honest communication, and a crew that treats your home like their own: that is the Mullikin way.
