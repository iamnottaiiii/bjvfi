Our Story

A Brooklyn crew that treats your space like their own

Sterling Clean Co. started with a simple observation: most cleaning problems are really consistency problems. A missed trash run here, a skipped restroom there, and a building starts to feel neglected.

So we built our service the other way around. Written checklists for every account. The same crew each visit. A direct phone line instead of a maze. From our base on Avenue S in Brooklyn, we serve offices, stores, and buildings that want cleaning they can stop thinking about.
