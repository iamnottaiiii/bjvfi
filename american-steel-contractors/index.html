Our story

A Valley steel shop with documented roots

American Steel Contractors operates from 9900 Glenoaks Boulevard, Suite 7, in Sun Valley, California, in the industrial corridor of the San Fernando Valley where steel, fabrication, and construction have worked side by side for decades.

The company is categorized as a steel distributor and warehouse, and it appears on the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety fabricators roster under listing FB00044 for lightweight steel and hollow structural sections. That listing puts the shop on the documented map of the region's steel supply chain.

It is a small operation, roughly a handful of people, serving contractors and builders who need material close to the jobsite. No corporate maze: you call the number, you talk to the yard.
