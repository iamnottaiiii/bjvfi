About

Twenty years of loyal clients

Nina’s Nails on North Eutaw Street has served downtown Baltimore for over two decades, building a base of loyal, long term clients.

Reviewers consistently praise Nina herself as an excellent nail technician, with professional and courteous staff, convenient hours, and nails that hold up without lifting or breaking.

The salon accepts cash and credit cards. Ask for Nina specifically; regulars say the wait is worth it.
