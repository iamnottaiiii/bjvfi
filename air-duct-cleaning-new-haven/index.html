What we do

One visit. Every vent. Noticeably fresher air.

Our full system cleaning covers the supply and return ducts, registers, grilles, blower and coils. No shortcuts, no upsell circus.

  • Whole home duct cleaning. Agitation tools and high suction clear dust, dander and debris from every run.
  • Dryer vent cleaning. Packed lint is a fire risk and an energy drain. We clear the full vent path.

How you know it is time

Dust puffing from vents when the system kicks on. Musty smell on startup. Allergy symptoms that ease when you leave the house. Visible buildup on registers. If any of that sounds familiar, your ducts are overdue.

Why it matters

Your ducts are the lungs of your home

New Haven homes work hard through humid summers and long heating seasons. All that air, and everything floating in it, passes through your ductwork again and again.

When ducts are packed with dust and debris, your system strains to push air through. That means higher energy bills, uneven temperatures from room to room, and more particles landing on every surface you just cleaned.

A professional cleaning resets the whole system. Airflow improves, the blower works less, and the air coming out of your vents is the air you actually want to breathe.
