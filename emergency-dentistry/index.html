Is it a dental emergency?

If you are in serious pain, have swelling, a knocked-out tooth, or a broken tooth with sharp edges, call right away. Fast care can save a tooth and relieve pain sooner.

In pain right now?

Call us and describe what happened. We will advise you on the next step.

What to do before you arrive

For a knocked-out tooth, keep it moist and get to a dentist quickly. For swelling or severe pain, avoid chewing on that side and call us as soon as possible.

Emergency Services

Tap a topic to learn what we can help with. Call (410) 632-8797 for your specific situation.

Severe toothache

A toothache that keeps you up at night or does not respond to over-the-counter relief may signal infection or deep decay. We examine the tooth, diagnose the cause, and plan relief.

Broken or cracked tooth

Cracks and chips can expose the inner tooth and lead to infection. We evaluate the damage and restore the tooth when possible.

Dental injury

Sports injuries, falls, and accidents can loosen or knock out teeth. Quick treatment gives the best chance of saving the tooth.

Lost filling or crown

A lost filling or crown leaves the tooth vulnerable. We can replace it and protect the tooth from further damage.
