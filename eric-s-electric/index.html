Our storyThe electrician Glendale recommends

Eric's Electric is a Glendale-based electrical shop run the old-fashioned way: one job at a time, done right. The business operates out of 3531 Clifton Pl and serves homeowners and small businesses across Glendale and the surrounding foothill communities.
