Our story

30+ years behind the chair

Alex Hair Salon has been part of Bay Ridge life for over three decades: a unisex salon where skill, honesty and friendly faces keep clients coming back.

Since the beginning

A neighborhood institution on 5th Ave

For more than 30 years, Alex and his team have offered all unisex hair services, plus makeup and eyebrows, from their salon at 7923 5th Ave in Brooklyn.

Generations of Bay Ridge families have sat in these chairs: first haircuts for kids, prom updos, wedding mornings, and the standing appointments that keep it all running. The team is known for top professional service and for treating regulars like friends.

Brides especially have found their way here. Alex's elaborate updos and the team's occasion makeup have dressed countless wedding parties across Brooklyn.
