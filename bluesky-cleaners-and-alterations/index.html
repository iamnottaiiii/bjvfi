  • No chemical odor when you pick up

Dry cleaning

Classic dry cleaning for suits, dresses, coats and anything the label says to treat with care.

  • Dresses and formal wear

Our story

A small shop with
a careful hand

Bluesky cleaners and Alterations is a locally run shop on Whalley Ave where the owners still do the measuring, the pinning and the inspecting themselves.

How we work

Measured twice,
cleaned once

Every garment that comes over the counter gets looked at by a person, not just a tag gun. Stains get spotted before cleaning. Alterations get pinned while you are standing there. If something looks off, the owners call you before they do anything.

That is the whole philosophy: pay attention at the start so there are no surprises at pickup. It is why reviewers talk about switching from other cleaners and never looking back.

And it is why the shop chose a chemical-free wetcleaning method: cleaner for your clothes, cleaner for the people handling them all day.
