The shop

Built on regulars

A barber shop lives or dies by its regulars, and Executive Cuts has them: clients who have been coming back for decades, and newcomers who drive in from as far as northern Virginia once they find the chair they like.

The formula is simple. Experienced barbers. A friendly, easygoing atmosphere. Fair prices posted up front. And a location right on Liberty Road with plenty of parking, easy to get to and easy to get back from.

Walk in for a shape-up between cuts, or settle in for the full works: haircut, beard sculpt, and a hot towel finish. Either way, you leave looking like an executive.
