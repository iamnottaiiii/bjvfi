Our story

A roofing crew that never says "that is not our department."

Miguel Multiservices started with a simple observation: most exterior problems arrive in pairs. A roof leak sits under a branch that caused it. A new roof looks wrong next to an overgrown yard. So the business was built to cover the whole exterior, with one crew, one quote, and one standard for cleanup.

Where we work

Based in Altadena, on the job across the foothills.

Our shop sits at 2111 N Fair Oaks Ave in Altadena, CA 91001. From there we serve Altadena, Pasadena, and the surrounding San Gabriel Valley neighborhoods, homes with mature trees, older roofs, and yards that need a steady hand.

Roofing first

Roofing is the core trade. Every other service exists because roofs live in a yard, under trees, and above gutters.

One point of contact

You talk to the person doing the work. No call centers, no handoffs between three subcontractors who blame each other.

Cleanup is the job

A roof repair that leaves nails in the driveway is not finished. Hauling, sweeping, and gutter clearing are part of every visit.
