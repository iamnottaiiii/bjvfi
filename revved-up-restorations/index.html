About the Shop

Revved up Restorations operates out of 1669 Westwood Ave in Cincinnati, Ohio. Listed publicly as a tire repair shop with roots in automotive restoration and body work, the shop has recently added towing to its services and offers junk car pickup in the community. What follows is how a restoration project typically moves through a shop like this.

Sample The timeline below describes a typical restoration process. It is not a claim about this shop's history or past projects. Call the shop to ask about their actual work.
Phase 1: Intake

Roll In and Inspection

The car arrives at 1669 Westwood Ave. It gets a full walk-around: rust, panels, tires, brakes, and everything in between are documented before a single wrench turns. Sample

Phase 2: Teardown

Strip Down to the Bones

Trim, glass, and interior come out. What is solid stays, what is tired goes. Every part is cataloged so nothing gets lost on the way back. Sample

Phase 3: Body and Metal

Rust Out, Strength In

Panels are repaired or replaced, metalwork is finished, and the body is prepped for paint. This is where a tired car starts looking like itself again. Sample

Phase 4: Mechanicals

Engine, Tires, and Running Gear

Engine work, brakes, suspension, and fresh rubber go in. A restored car has to drive as good as it looks, and that includes reliable tires. Sample

Phase 5: Detail and Roll Out

The Final Walkthrough

Detail, final inspection, and a last walkthrough with the owner. Then the keys change hands and the car rolls back onto Cincinnati streets. Sample
