About

Meet Dr. Stanek.

Dr. Andrew H. Stanek, DDS practices endodontics in Columbia, Maryland, as part of Delgado and Stanek, at 10632 Little Patuxent Pkwy.

As an endodontist, Dr. Stanek focuses on diagnosing tooth pain and performing root canal treatment. Patients describe an empathetic chairside manner and step-by-step explanations that make even difficult procedures feel manageable. He is a member of the Maryland State Dental Association.
