The studio

Isabelle Richard, designer and founder

Blue Lotus Home Designs was founded by Isabelle Richard, an interior designer with 13 years of experience creating homes across Southern California. Her philosophy is simple: inspiring, custom interiors that suit your true nature.

"Less is more. An understated, elegant, casual style, tailored to each client's comfort, with natural elements in every room."
