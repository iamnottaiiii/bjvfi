Our story

Three decades on S Salina St

A downtown Syracuse nail salon with a long history and a reputation for fearless nail art.

Since way back

Serving Syracuse for over 30 years

Oriental Nail Salon has been part of the downtown Syracuse landscape for more than three decades, a rare kind of staying power in the beauty business.

What keeps clients coming through the door is the artistry. Reviewers describe designs they have never seen anywhere else, from intricate patterns to bold 3D work, alongside the everyday essentials: clean manicures, relaxing pedicures, and careful lash application.

Walk-ins are welcome, and the salon keeps generous hours, Monday through Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM.
