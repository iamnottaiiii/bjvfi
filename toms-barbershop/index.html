About the Shop

A corner barbershop in the heart of Covington, Kentucky.

Toms Barbershop sits at 804 Scott St in Covington, KY, serving the neighborhood with classic barbering: haircuts, beard trims, head shaves, and hot towel shaves at honest, inexpensive prices.

Customers describe the shop as welcoming and personal. One public review thanks a barber named Jerry by name, and several reviewers mention the shop is great with children. A common tip from customers: call ahead for an appointment.

The shop has also been featured in local press (LINK nky, 2015) for its owner's story of turning his life around and becoming a role model for Covington kids.
