Who We Are

Comfort you can feel, service you can trust

Wacold Inc is an air conditioning contractor based in Van Nuys, California. We serve homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley with the full range of heating and cooling work: repairs, installations, and the routine maintenance that keeps systems running strong.

We believe good HVAC service is simple. Show up on time, find the actual problem, explain the options honestly, and stand behind the work. That approach is what keeps our customers calling us back season after season.

If your AC is struggling or your heater is acting up, reach out. We will give you a straight answer and a fair price.
