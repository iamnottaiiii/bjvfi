About

Ridgefield roots, contractor discipline

C&C General Contractors operates from Church St in Ridgefield, NJ, serving homeowners across Bergen County. We are a general contractor in the classic sense: one company accountable for the design, the trades, the schedule, and the finished work.

One point of contact

You talk to us, not a chain of subcontractors. We coordinate electricians, plumbers, and finish crews on a schedule you can see.

Written scopes

Every project starts with an on-site visit and a written estimate. No vague allowances, no surprise line items at the end.

Clean job sites

Dust control, daily cleanup, and respect for your home and neighbors while the work is underway.
