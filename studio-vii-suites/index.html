Our story

A suite of
independent artists

Studio VII Suites brings together beauty specialists in one welcoming Windsor space, so your whole routine can happen in a single visit.

The idea

Salon suites,
done right.

Instead of one chair doing everything, Studio VII Suites hosts specialists who each do their own thing beautifully: nail techs who obsess over shape, stylists who know texture, lash artists with a light, steady hand.

You get the focus of a private suite and the convenience of a full-service salon. Walk in for a fill, walk out with your hair done too.

i.

Specialists, not generalists

Each service is handled by someone who focuses on it, from acrylic sculpting to retwists to lash mapping.

ii.

A calm suite setting

Private-suite comfort with a friendly, professional atmosphere that regulars keep coming back to.

iii.

Details that last

Guests say their sets still look amazing weeks later. Careful prep and quality products make the difference.

iv.

Easy to find

Right on Broad Street in Windsor with street parking, minutes from Hartford and the surrounding towns.
