Our story

Nearly two decades on Nostrand Ave.

A neighborhood institution

In the salon's own words, they have been in this Sheepshead Bay neighborhood for almost two decades. That longevity shows in the clientele: reviewers describe coming here for 10, 14, even 16 years, and recommend it to everyone they know.

Clean, calm, and careful

The place is described as always spotless and sanitized, and the nail techs as sweet, talented, patient, accommodating, and interactive. It is the kind of environment where you can relax while your nails get the full treatment.

Precision you can see

Reviewers call the technicians perfectionists who execute requested styles down to a T. From dip powder to gel extensions to detailed nail art, the work holds up, and clients travel from far neighborhoods to keep their appointments here.

An honest note

A few reviewers mention rushed visits when arriving near closing time, or gel polish peeling within a week. Booking earlier in the day and asking for the right technician goes a long way.
