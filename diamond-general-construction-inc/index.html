Our story

Built by determination, one unit at a time

Diamond General Construction Inc is a woman-owned, full-service general construction company based in the San Fernando Valley, founded by Roxana Brito.

From door-knocking to 95 permits

Roxana started the company without a formal business education, knocking door-to-door on property managers' offices and working late nights and early mornings. Eight years later, the business had scaled from interior and exterior renovations to building new dwelling units.

Today the company serves homeowners, property managers, and business owners across North Hollywood and surrounding Los Angeles neighborhoods, with 95 building permits pulled since 2017, per BuildZoom.

As an immigrant, queer Latina in a predominantly male industry, Roxana has spoken about breaking barriers and hopes her story inspires young women to believe in themselves. The company remains family-rooted, built with her wife's support.
