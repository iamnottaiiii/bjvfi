Our Story

A neighborhood salon with a loyal chair.

Trendsetters sits on the main street in Weedsport, and regulars describe it the same way: easy to find, comfortable inside, and staffed by stylists who listen before they cut. Guests mention the friendly atmosphere, the water and snacks on offer, and color work that keeps them coming back visit after visit.

Reviewers praise fair, reasonable pricing with no hidden upcharges, and stylists who set realistic expectations and take their time. Whether it is a first balayage, a curly-hair specialist who finally gets it, or prom-day styling for the whole family, the goal is the same: you leave feeling like the best version of yourself.
