About

Medford's friendly
neighborhood cleaner

Lee's Cleaners serves Medford from Salem Street with careful work, honest prices and a counter that knows its regulars.

Care you can see in the finish

A good cleaner notices the details: the spot that needs a second treatment, the button tightened before it is lost, the crease pressed exactly right. At Lee's Cleaners, every order is checked in with you at the counter, tagged carefully, and inspected before it is pressed and bagged. That is how a neighborhood shop earns regulars, one sharp crease at a time.

Honest prices

Fair, straightforward pricing with no surprises at pickup. What we quote is what you pay.

Ready on time

Clear turnaround times and rush service when your calendar will not wait.

Neighbors first

A local shop on Salem Street where regulars are greeted by name and newcomers are welcome.
