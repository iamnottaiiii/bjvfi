Chapter 01 - Who we are

A local HVAC contractor built around one promise: your home, comfortable.

When the San Gabriel Valley heat rolls in, a failing air conditioner is more than an inconvenience. ABR Heating & Air Conditioning is an Arcadia-based HVAC contractor focused on fast, honest heating and cooling service for homes and light commercial spaces.

From 127 La Porte St in the heart of Arcadia, we diagnose, repair, install, and maintain the systems that keep you comfortable year round. No upsells you do not need, no jargon, just clear answers and work done right the first time.

Chapter 02 - How it works

Three steps from uncomfortable to comfortable.

1. Call or visit. Reach us at (626) 671-1255 or stop by 127 La Porte St g, Arcadia, CA 91006. Tell us what your system is doing, or not doing.

2. Honest diagnosis. We inspect the system, explain what is wrong in plain language, and lay out your options with clear pricing before any work begins.

3. Fast, clean repair. Most repairs are completed the same visit. We test everything before we leave and clean up our workspace.

Emergency breakdown? Call right away. We prioritize no-cool and no-heat situations because nobody should sit in a broken home.

What we do

Services

Tap any service to see what is included. Pricing is quoted before work begins. Call (626) 671-1255 for a free estimate on installs and replacements.

When your AC blows warm, cycles constantly, leaks, or quits altogether, we find the root cause and fix it.

  • Full system diagnosis and honest repair options
  • Capacitors, contactors, fan motors, and compressors
  • Refrigerant leak detection and recharge
  • Thermostat troubleshooting and replacement
Most repairs same visitFree estimate on replacement

Cold nights should not mean cold rooms. We service furnaces, heat pumps, and wall heaters.

  • Furnace ignition, burner, and blower repair
  • Heat pump service and reversing valve diagnosis
  • Carbon monoxide and safety checks
  • Pilot light, flame sensor, and control board fixes

When repair no longer makes sense, we size and install a new system matched to your home and budget.

  • Load calculation so the system is sized correctly
  • Central AC, furnace, and heat pump installs
  • Permit coordination and code-compliant setup
  • Startup testing and walkthrough on day one
Free in-home estimate

Seasonal tune-ups keep systems efficient, extend equipment life, and catch small issues early.

  • Coil cleaning and airflow checks
  • Electrical connection and safety inspection
  • Filter replacement reminders
  • Efficiency report after every visit

Dust, hot and cold spots, and stale air often trace back to ducts and filtration.

  • Duct inspection, sealing, and repair
  • High-efficiency filter and media cabinet installs
  • UV light and air purifier options
  • Vent and register balancing
