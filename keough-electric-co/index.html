Why Keough

A crew you can trust with the lights on

Keough Electric Company was founded in 1992 by Doug Keough and now operates from the Airport Industrial Park in Sun Valley. With more than 30 years of electrical contracting behind us, our team handles commercial, industrial and residential jobs with the same standard: safe, on time, and up to code.

  • Safety first on every jobsite, every project built to the National Electric Code
  • Commercial, industrial and residential experience under one roof
  • Design build and custom home specialists, from plan to final inspection
  • Trusted across restaurants, retail, hospitality, clean rooms and more
