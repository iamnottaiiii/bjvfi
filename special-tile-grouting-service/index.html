Good to know

Grout questions, answered.

Should I clean my grout or just replace it?

If the grout is intact but dirty, professional cleaning plus sealing usually brings it back. If it is cracked, crumbling, or missing, regrouting is the right fix. We will tell you which one yours needs.

How long does grout sealing last?

It depends on traffic, cleaning products, and whether the area stays wet. Sealed grout stays cleaner far longer than unsealed grout, and we can advise on maintenance during the visit.

Can you match my existing grout color?

We color-match replacement grout to your tile as closely as current products allow, and we can discuss color sealing options if you want a uniform new look.

Do I need to move furniture or empty rooms?

For floors, clear small items and we handle the rest. For showers and backsplashes, just clear the immediate area. We will confirm exactly what to prep when you book.

About

Specialists, not generalists.

We do one thing and we do it well: making existing tile look its best without tearing it out.

Why grout first

Most tile that looks "old" is actually fine tile with failing grout. Retiling means demolition, dust, and weeks of disruption. Cleaning, regrouting, and sealing usually delivers the transformation at a fraction of the cost and mess. That is the whole idea behind our service.

How we work

We look at the grout in person before quoting anything. You get a clear scope: what gets cleaned, what gets replaced, what gets sealed, and the exact price. Then we protect your home, do the work neatly, and walk the finished job with you.
