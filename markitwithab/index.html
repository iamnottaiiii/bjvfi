Our story

A little shop with a big B

The name says it all

MarkItWithA”B” started with a simple idea: a cake should look like it was made for exactly one person, because it was. Every order gets the full treatment, sketched, baked, and finished by hand, then boxed up with the shop stamp on top.

Oak Forest proud

On Margie Ln

You will find us at 5503 Margie Ln in Oak Forest, Illinois, baking weekdays from 9 to 6. We are a made-to-order shop, which means the ovens run on your schedule, not ours.

What we believe

Fresh beats fancy. A simple cake baked this morning beats a complicated one from last week, every time. That is the whole philosophy, written in frosting.
