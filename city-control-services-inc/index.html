Our story

A San Fernando cleaning company built on showing up

City Control Services Inc started with a simple observation: most cleaning complaints are not about the cleaning, they are about reliability. Missed visits, rotating crews, checklists that shrink over time. We built the company to be the opposite of that.

Based at 820 San Fernando Rd in the heart of San Fernando, we serve offices, property managers and homeowners across the Valley with small crews and written checklists that stay the same visit after visit.

We are early in our public review history, and we intend to earn every star the slow way: by being the crew that shows up, communicates, and leaves the space better than we found it.

What we believe
  • Reliability beats everything else
  • Written checklists, every visit
  • Small crews, consistent faces
  • Plain pricing, confirmed up front
