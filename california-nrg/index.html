Our story

A Van Nuys contractor built on clear plans.

California NRG is a general contracting team based on Delano Street in Van Nuys. We focus on the projects where planning matters most: bathrooms, kitchens, and garage to ADU conversions. Homeowners know us for explaining the work in plain terms, showing up when we say we will, and helping with the parts of a remodel that have nothing to do with a hammer, like plans, permits, and financing options.

Our name reflects the other half of what we do: energy smart upgrades, from efficient windows to solar, that keep cutting costs long after the remodel dust settles.
