About the shop

A Main Street barbershop, run the personal way.

Erich's Cutting Edge has been part of the Oxford community for years, known for friendly faces, low wait times, and cuts that keep people coming back.

The story

Comedy Central vibes, barbershop precision.

Ask longtime customers about Erich's and the word "warm" comes up again and again. The shop has the easy banter of a place where everybody knows your name, paired with barbers who take the craft seriously.

Reviewers praise the personal, professional service: one says he has never had a bad cut there, another calls it the best haircut she ever got. Families are welcome, prices stay low, and the chairs keep turning without long waits.

It is the kind of shop people recommend over any chain, and mean it.
