Services

Underground construction, end to end

From excavation to final paving, our crews handle the full scope of pipeline construction for cities, agencies, and commercial clients in the Los Angeles area.

01

Water main installation

New water main construction and replacement for municipal systems, including valves, fittings, hydrant laterals, and service connections.

02

Sewer pipeline construction

Gravity sewer and force main installation with proper bedding, compaction, and mandrel or air testing per agency specifications.

03

Storm drain systems

Storm drain pipe, catch basins, and related drainage infrastructure for streets and public right of way projects.

04

Underground utilities

Trench excavation, shoring, pipe laying, and backfill for underground utility corridors serving commercial and public sites.

05

Fuel distribution utilities

Scheduling, instrumentation, and control systems for fuel distribution utility work.

06

Pipeline rehabilitation

Repair and replacement of aging water and sewer lines, with street restoration to match existing conditions.

About us

A pipeline contractor since 1972

Williams Pipeline Contractors, Inc. is a California corporation registered in 1972 and based in Mission Hills. For more than five decades we have performed commercial contracting focused on water and sewer pipeline work for public works projects.
