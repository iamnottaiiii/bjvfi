Our story

Meet Kathy,
the heart of the salon

Styles & Smiles Dog Salon is owner-operated, which means your dog is groomed by the person whose name is on the door.

One groomer, all heart

A salon built on patience

Kathy opened Styles & Smiles with a simple belief: grooming should never be scary. Before every appointment she takes time to understand each dog's needs, whether that means a nervous rescue pup, a wiggly puppy, or a senior who just needs things taken slowly.

Clients notice. Reviews keep coming back to the same themes: the salon is spotless, the atmosphere is warm and inviting, and dogs that used to dread grooming now walk in happily.

Based in the Towne Plaza on Buffalo Road in North Chili, the salon serves families across Chili, Gates, and the greater Rochester area.
