Our story

A neighborhood chair,
run with care

Anna Unisex Salon is the kind of place reviews describe as "a great little neighborhood salon": small, honest, and full of regulars.

Who we are

The boss lady's chair

Reviewers know the owner by name: Anna. She personally cuts many of the heads of hair that walk through the door, from kids getting their first proper trim to regulars who have trusted her for years.

The salon describes itself simply: a family-friendly establishment that provides haircuts for a great price. No pretension, no upsell, just a careful cut and a fair bill.

With a small team and a loyal crowd, weekend lines can form, a sure sign that the neighborhood would rather wait for Anna than go elsewhere.

Mom and pop shop

Family first

Kids are welcome regulars here. The team is known for patience with little ones who won't let anyone else cut their hair.

Fair prices

An inexpensive price level and a reputation for good value. Great hair should not cost a fortune.

Swift and careful

A small team that works quickly without cutting corners. Top notch haircuts, the reviews say, again and again.
