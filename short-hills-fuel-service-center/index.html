Who we are

Gas station by day,
trusted garage by reputation

Short Hills Fuel & Service Center is a family-owned business at 46 Chatham Rd, in the middle of Short Hills, minutes from the train station. It is the rare stop that does both jobs: fill the tank and fix the car.

Customers describe a friendly, helpful, and experienced team that goes above and beyond. Free tire pressure checks, a clean windshield with every fill-up, and a follow-up call after service are the touches that turned one-time drivers into long-term regulars.

  • Family owned and operated
  • Fuel plus full repair bays on one lot
  • Serving Short Hills and the Millburn area
