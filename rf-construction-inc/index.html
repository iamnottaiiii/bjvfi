Our story

A Panorama City builder, block by block

RF Construction Inc is a general contracting company based on Roscoe Boulevard in Panorama City, California, serving homeowners and businesses across the San Fernando Valley.

We started with a simple belief: construction goes better when the contractor answers the phone, shows up on time, and tells you the truth about cost and schedule. That is still how we run every job.

From kitchen remodels to full home renovations and commercial buildouts, our crews treat each property like it is our own. Clean job sites, licensed trade partners, and a finished product you can point to with pride.
