About

A Newport crew for Newport homes

GONZA CONSTRUCTION LLC operates out of 14 W 10th Street in Newport, Kentucky, serving Campbell County and the Northern Kentucky river cities. We are the kind of contractor who answers the phone, shows up when we say we will, and treats your home like it is our own jobsite.

Newport's housing stock is a mix of historic brick, river-town frames, and newer infill, and each one asks for different skills. We work across all of it: renovations, additions, roofing, siding, decks, concrete, and the punch lists that keep a house standing strong.
