Our story

Canoga Park roots, contractor standards

Atlas Construction Inc is a Canoga Park handyman and home improvement business now in its sixth year. It is a one-person operation by design: the person you talk to on the phone is the person who shows up and does the work.

That means no handoffs, no subcontractor roulette, and no surprises. Homeowners across the West Valley call on Atlas for dependable repairs and upgrades, and the background-checked profile on Thumbtack reflects the same reputation: show up, do careful work, leave the place better than you found it.
