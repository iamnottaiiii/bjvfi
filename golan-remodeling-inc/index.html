Our story

A family name on every job

Golan Remodeling Inc is a family-owned and operated contractor based in Winnetka, California. For more than twenty years we have remodeled kitchens and bathrooms across Los Angeles and Ventura Counties, building a reputation one careful project at a time.

Before running our own jobs, our team worked as subcontractors for some of the finest construction firms in the industry. That background taught us what separates good work from great work: the details nobody sees, the prep nobody photographs, and the honesty to say what a space really needs.

We keep everything in house. The crew that plans your remodel is the crew that builds it, which means consistent quality, clear accountability, and a team that treats your home with respect. Homeowners tell us they notice the difference from the first estimate visit.

Reasonable pricing, quality workmanship, and professional service. That is the whole business plan, and it has carried Golan Remodeling Inc for over two decades.
