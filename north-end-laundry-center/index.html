Our story

A North End laundry shop that does it all

On Salem Street, between the cafes and the corner markets, North End Laundry Center has become the go-to for neighbors who want laundry done right.

What we are about

One shop for wash day and wardrobe care.

Most laundromats wash. Most tailors sew. North End Laundry Center does both, along with dry cleaning and shirt service, all handled on site. That is why reviewers keep calling it the best choice in the North End for laundry and alterations.

The shop is known for a clean, well-kept space, a staff that stays helpful even on busy days, and tailoring that comes back fast without cutting corners. It is the kind of neighborhood place people recommend to friends who just moved in.

Walk-ins are welcome for self-service and drop-off. For tailoring with a deadline, like a wedding or an event, call ahead and the team will tell you what is possible.
