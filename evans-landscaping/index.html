Our Story

About Evans

Since 1975

A Cincinnati Original, Built on Dirt

Evans Landscaping is a family-owned company that has served Greater Cincinnati since 1975. What started as landscaping grew into one of the area's best-known bulk material yards: mulch, topsoil, sand, gravel, decorative stone and firewood, produced and stocked right here in Ohio.

The company's own description says it best: mulch, topsoil, sand, gravel, decorative stone, firewood, landscaping, demolition, site clearing and excavating in Cincinnati, OH. Retail and wholesale outlets serve homeowners and contractors alike, and the business is BBB accredited with locations on Round Bottom Road and in Loveland.

Proudly Local

What customers praise

  • Fast bulk delivery, often next day, with clear itemized delivery fees when ordering online
  • Friendly, helpful staff and an easy ordering process by phone or online
  • Wide variety of products and fair, decent pricing on mulch, stone and soil
  • Drivers who place loads close to where they are needed, saving customers real effort
  • Firewood that is easy to order and consistently delivered on time

What customers criticize

  • Inconsistent delivery experiences, including loads dropped in the wrong spot
  • Product quality complaints, including contaminated topsoil and subpar mulch batches
  • Complaints about truck driving behavior on public roads
  • Disputes over contracted project work such as retaining walls, with missed deadlines alleged
  • Some reviewers say they would not do business with the company again
