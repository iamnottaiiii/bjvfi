About Pavex Inc

Digging since 1972

Pavex Inc is a site development and excavating contractor based in Camp Hill, Pennsylvania, established in 1972. Led by president Robert Kalbach Jr., the company specializes in large scale excavation and site work for commercial and industrial clients.

Over the decades, Pavex crews have prepared ground for warehouse and distribution facilities, hospitals, parks, military bases, and power plants, including high-profile work for clients such as Target, the Giant Center, and Johns Hopkins.

Landmark job
The Target warehouse in Chambersburg was a 1.3-million-square-foot facility on a site that was 50 to 60 percent rock. Pavex moved more than a million cubic yards of material.
