Our Story

A neighborhood spot for self care

BEAUTY SPOT is a beauty salon and nail salon located at 2079 Coney Island Avenue in Brooklyn's Gravesend neighborhood. We bring hair and nail care together under one roof, so a refresh can be simple and close to home.

Our focus is straightforward: careful work, a comfortable chair, and service that treats regulars and first timers the same way. Walk out feeling like the best version of yourself, without the trek across town.
