Our story

A family shop with decades behind the chair.

Mario's Hair Designs is a family-owned and operated hair salon in the heart of Manlius. We offer the latest styles and services for men, women and children, and we do it the old-fashioned way: by listening first and rushing nothing.

Our clients tell our story best. Many have been coming for 10, 20, even 30 years. They mention our stylists by name, bring their families, and send their friends. That kind of loyalty is earned one appointment at a time.
