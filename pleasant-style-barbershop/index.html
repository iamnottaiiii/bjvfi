About the Shop

A family favorite on Pleasant Street, built on steady hands and honest work.

Pleasant Style Barbershop is the kind of place Worcester keeps coming back to. Tucked into the Pleasant Street corridor at 809 Pleasant St, it is a cozy, family friendly shop where walk-ins are welcome and the wait is always worth it.

Reviewers describe barbers who listen first and cut second, delivering clean fades, sharp lineups, and styles that photograph well long after you leave the chair. The shop works with clients of all ages and all hair types, and the atmosphere stays relaxed even on a packed Saturday morning.
