About

The neighborhood shop on Pleasant Street

Robidoux's Hair Styling For Men & Women serves Rochdale and the surrounding towns with classic barbering and a genuinely warm welcome.

Our Story

Old-school barbershop, open to everyone

Robidoux's is the kind of shop reviewers describe as a throwback to the days of the local barbershop: skilled hands, honest work, and a room where patrons talk with each other while they wait.

Men and women alike take a seat here, for everything from a quick cleanup to a full restyle. The goal is simple: you leave looking sharp and feeling like a regular.
