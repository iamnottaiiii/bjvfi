How it works

Your brows, in three visits

Permanent makeup is a two session process, and the consultation is where it all begins.

  • Consultation: talk through your goals, map your ideal shape, and choose a pigment that suits your skin and hair.
  • Initial session: the shape is drawn, approved by you, then implanted with a licensed PMU artist.
  • Perfecting touch up: after healing, a follow up session refines color and density for a lasting finish.
