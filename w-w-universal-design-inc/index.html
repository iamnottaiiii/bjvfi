Services

What we build

Tap a service to learn more. Every project begins with a consultation at your home or ours.

Accessibility Construction

New construction and additions designed from the ground up around universal access: no-step entries, wide hallways, reachable kitchens, and roll-in showers that look like a design choice, not a compromise.

  • Zero step entries and ramps integrated into the design
  • 36 inch doorways and open circulation paths
  • Accessible kitchens with varied counter heights
  • Roll-in showers and comfort height fixtures
Free consultation, call (516) 457-5515

Accessibility Design

Good accessibility starts on paper. We plan spaces that work for grandparents, kids, and everyone in between, so your home adapts as life changes.

  • Aging in place planning
  • Floor plans with future adaptability built in
  • Lighting, contrast, and wayfinding details

Accessibility Retrofitting

Love your home but need it to work better? We retrofit existing houses with the access features you need, often without major structural changes.

  • Stair lifts and platform lift preparation
  • Bathroom conversions: tub to walk-in shower
  • Grab bars, railings, and support blocking
  • Doorway widening and threshold removal
Free consultation, call (516) 457-5515

Baluster Installation

Staircases are a focal point and a safety feature. We install balusters, newels, and railings that are beautiful to look at and solid to hold.

  • Wood, iron, and cable baluster systems
  • Handrail replacement and reinforcement
  • Full staircase refinishing coordination

Barn Construction

From classic post and beam barns to modern outbuildings, we build sturdy, handsome structures for storage, workshops, and gatherings.

  • Custom barn design and construction
  • Outbuildings, sheds, and workshops
  • Repairs and restoration of existing barns

Need something else? As a full service construction company we take on a wide range of remodeling and carpentry work. Ask us about your project.

Our Story

Built on the belief that good design includes everyone

W & W Universal Design, Inc. was incorporated in New York in 2007 and is led by William Christiansen from our Woodmere, Long Island office. For nearly two decades we have helped homeowners across the Five Towns, Nassau County, Queens, and Brooklyn build and remodel with accessibility in mind.

2007

W & W Universal Design, Inc. is incorporated in New York, founded on universal design principles.

The work

Accessibility construction, design, and retrofitting become our signature: homes that welcome every age and ability.

Today

Still based at 1074 Mora Pl in Woodmere, still led by William Christiansen, still building homes that work for everyone.

Design first

We plan carefully before we build, so accessibility feels intentional and beautiful, never bolted on.

Craft matters

From a single baluster to a full barn, every detail gets the same care and precision.

Built for life

Homes should adapt as life changes. We build in the flexibility to age in place gracefully.
