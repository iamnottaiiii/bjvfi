The process

From first call to final inspection

A roofing project should be straightforward. Here is how it typically works when you reach out.

  • 1. Call us. Describe what you are seeing: leaks, missing shingles, age, storm damage.
  • 2. Get an inspection. We assess the roof's condition and explain what it needs.
  • 3. Approve the plan. You see the scope and pricing before any work begins.
  • 4. We get to work. Professional installation or repair, then a final walkthrough.
