About the salon

Located in the Timonium Square shopping center on York Road, All Nails and Spa is known for its friendly staff, sanitized tools, and updated pedicure chairs. Regulars say the team remembers their names and makes every visit feel personal.

Why clients return

Clients praise the long-lasting gel manicures, comfortable pedicure chairs, and a calm, clean environment. Walk-ins are welcome and appointments are recommended.
