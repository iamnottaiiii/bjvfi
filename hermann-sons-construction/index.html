Our story

Three decades in Bristol

Hermann & Sons Construction is a general contractor based at 245 Vera Rd in Bristol, Connecticut. The company was registered in Connecticut in May 1997, and community listings credit the business with 29 years of experience in the trade.

As a general contractor, the company takes on residential renovations, commercial builds and repair work across the Bristol area. Reviewers describe a crew that goes above and beyond for each client and call the business a go-to construction service in town.

The business is led by Christopher Hermann, listed as principal on the Connecticut business registry.
