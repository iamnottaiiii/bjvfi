Services

The full interior scope, under one roof

Choose a trade to see what ATO Contracting Corporation brings to the jobsite.

Drywall Installation

Commercial-grade drywall hung by career mechanics. We handle everything from single office alterations to full floor fit-outs.

  • Metal and wood stud partition walls
  • Shaft walls and area separation walls
  • Sound-rated and fire-rated assemblies
  • Coordination with mechanical, electrical and plumbing trades

Taping and Finishing

Taping is our signature trade. Our finishers deliver flat walls and sharp corners that paint beautifully the first time.

  • Taping and joint finishing, levels 3 through 5
  • Skim coating and re-skimming over old plaster
  • Crisp inside and outside corners
  • Dust-controlled sanding and jobsite cleanup

Carpentry

Rough and finish carpentry from the same crew that hangs your drywall, so the schedule never stalls between trades.

  • Wood and metal framing
  • Blocking, backing and furring
  • Door frames, trim and finish carpentry
  • Wood backing for heavy fixtures and cabinetry

Ceilings

Drywall and acoustic ceilings installed level and true, ready for lights, diffusers and sprinklers.

  • Suspended acoustic tile ceilings
  • Drywall ceilings on framing or furring
  • Soffits, bulkheads and light coves
  • Ceiling repairs and tile replacement

Painting

Interior painting as the natural final step of our drywall and taping work, or as a standalone service.

  • Interior painting for offices, retail and homes
  • Priming and finish coats over new drywall
  • Repaints and color changes
  • Neat, protected, low-odor work practices

Plaster Work

Traditional plaster repair and restoration for older Brooklyn buildings, alongside modern veneer systems.

  • Plaster patching and crack repair
  • Skim coats over damaged plaster
  • Ceiling medallion and molding repair coordination
  • Matching existing textures

Our story

A Brooklyn trade firm built on craft

Since 1994

ATO Contracting Corporation was founded in 1994 and has spent three decades as a full-service carpentry, drywall and taping firm. Based on 71st Street in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn, the company serves commercial and residential clients across the five boroughs.

As a New York State certified woman-owned business enterprise (WBE), ATO brings both union craftsmanship and diverse ownership to public and private projects alike.

Sample image

Union signatory

ATO is signatory to union agreements with the New York City District Council of Carpenters and the painters union. That means trained, insured mechanics on every job and labor standards your project team can count on.

From the first day of protection work to the final taped seam and coat of paint, one accountable firm carries the interior scope.

Sample image

What clients can expect

No job is too small or too big for the firm. Crews show up with laborers to protect the site, hang and finish the work to spec, and leave the space clean. Clear communication with general contractors, owners and property managers is part of every project.
