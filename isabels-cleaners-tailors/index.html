Our story

A neighborhood cleaners and tailors on Chestnut St, keeping Lynn looking sharp one garment at a time.

Clean clothes, honest work

Isabel's Cleaners & Tailors is a dry cleaner and alteration shop at 155 Chestnut St in Lynn, MA. The shop describes itself simply: dry cleaner and alteration. That is exactly what it does, carefully and consistently, six days a week.

Bring in the suit before the interview, the dress before the event, the pants that never fit quite right. Cleaning and tailoring under one roof means fewer trips and clothes that come back better than they arrived.

Clean

Garment-by-garment dry cleaning and careful pressing, not a conveyor belt.

Alter

Hems, tapering, and repairs by people who sew, done in the shop.

Return

Clothes back on time, ready to wear, with a fit that feels right.
