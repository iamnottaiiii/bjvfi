Questions

Before you call

How does pricing work?
The studio charges an hourly fee. Consultation-only projects are priced based on scope of work, and quotes are provided for purchases with a down payment before purchase. Per public listings, the initial meeting is at no charge.
What happens after the first meeting?
After the first meeting, a Scope of Work is drawn up detailing the work you wish the studio to perform.
Which areas do you serve?
The studio is based in North Royalton, OH and serves the greater Cleveland and Akron areas, including Strongsville, Brunswick, Medina, Hudson, Solon, Westlake, and many more. Call to confirm your town.

About the studio

Personal, professional, practical

Chris Roblin Interior Design, Inc. is an interior design studio based at 6471 Cady Rd in North Royalton, Ohio. The stated goal: provide personal, professional, practical solutions by using creative, innovative, and timely ideas to meet your design needs.

The studio works on one room or entire homes, new construction or renovation, and also takes on commercial projects including ADA-compliant spaces. Detail orientation, time consciousness, and staying within your budget are described as strong points of the practice.
