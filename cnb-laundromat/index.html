Services

Self serve or full service, the choice is yours. Bring your laundry to Broad St and leave with it fresh.

Self Serve Laundry

Washers and dryers for every load size, from a quick everyday load to big family weeks. Run multiple machines at once and finish in one visit.

Wash and Fold

Drop off your laundry and we will wash, dry, and fold it for you. Pick up fresh clothes without spending your afternoon in the laundromat.

Cash and Cards

Pay the way you like. CNB Laundromat accepts cash and credit cards, so you never have to hunt for the right payment.

Easy to Wait

Kid friendly space with arcade games, a massage chair, and a vending machine, making the wait go by while your loads run.
