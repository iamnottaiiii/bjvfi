Why Start With the Soil

Most lawn problems are soil problems. Thin, sandy, or compacted soil starves roots, wastes water, and makes every lawn chore harder. A fresh layer of quality top soil rebuilds the root zone so grass, gardens, and plantings can thrive.

Filippone Top Soil brings the material to your driveway or job site, so you can skip the bags and the guessing.

How Much Soil Do You Need?

A quick guide for common projects. Final yardage is confirmed on your call.

Topdressing a lawn

A thin layer to level and feed an existing lawn. Most lawns need 1 to 3 yards per 1,000 square feet.

Raising a garden bed

New beds usually need 4 to 8 inches of soil. Measure length times width times depth to get cubic yards.

Filling a low spot

Low areas settle, so order a little extra and compact in layers. We can help you estimate.

Our Story

A Local Soil Business on Bayview Avenue

Filippone Top Soil is a local landscaping and material supply business based at 598 Bayview Ave in Inwood, New York, right on the Nassau County line.

The business grew out of a simple observation: Long Island yards live or die by their soil. Between sandy native soils, salt air, and heavy clay pockets, a lot of lawns struggle no matter how much seed and fertilizer go down. Bulk top soil, delivered where it is needed, fixes the foundation instead of the symptoms.

Today Filippone Top Soil serves homeowners, property managers, and contractors with bulk soil delivery and hands on landscape services, all from one local phone call.
