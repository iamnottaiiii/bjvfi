Large-Item Pickup, Scheduled in One Call

Trenton residents can schedule special pickups for household trash, brush, and bulky items that do not fit the regular route. Call (734) 675-5496 and leave a message to book your stop.

Call (734) 675-5496

How to Schedule

  1. Call the message line at (734) 675-5496.
  2. Leave your name, address, and a description of the items.
  3. Put items at the curb for your scheduled pickup.
  4. Watch your mailbox: you will be billed for the service.
See Pickup Fees

What We Pick Up

Three pickup types, each billed per load.

Regular Special Pickup

Household trash items that exceed normal collection: furniture, bulky waste, and large loads.

Brush Pickup

Branches and brush bundled for the special route.

Loader Stops

Heavy loads handled with the front-end loader, priced per cubic yard.

Pickup Fees

Fees are billed to the resident by mail and can be paid through the city.
