Our story

A neighborhood shop
on Darlington Drive

Afroliners sits in the Oakleigh Shopping Center in Parkville, a unisex barbershop built on skill, consistency, and good conversation.

What we are about

Expert barbers,
cool vibe

That is how regulars describe Afroliners, and it is the whole point. A real barbershop is more than a chair and clippers. It is the conversation, the music, the way the cut comes out consistent every single time you sit down.

Unisex is in the name and in the practice. Men, women, and kids all get the same care in the chair, from sharp fades and lineups to cuts and styles for the whole family.
