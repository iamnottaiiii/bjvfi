What we do

Design, planting and care for residential yards. Pick a category to see what is included.

Landscape Design

A plan for the whole yard before anything goes in the ground. Layout, plant selection and hardscape ideas shaped around the site and how you use it.

  • On-site walkthrough and design consultation
  • Planting layouts and bed designs
  • Yard redesigns and refreshes

Plantings

Trees, shrubs, perennials and annuals, selected for New England conditions and placed for long term health.

  • New beds and foundation plantings
  • Privacy plantings and hedges
  • Mulching and bed edging

Lawn Care

Ongoing care that keeps the grass thick and the edges crisp through the growing season.

  • Regular mowing and trimming
  • Edging and bed maintenance
  • Seasonal lawn refreshes

Seasonal Cleanups

Spring and fall visits that reset the yard for the season ahead or put it to bed for winter.

  • Spring debris removal and bed prep
  • Fall leaf removal and cutbacks
  • Pruning and shaping

About

Design with
a local eye

Gregory Morgan Landscaping Services is a landscape design and yard care business based in Hyde Park, MA.

One crew, from plan to planting

Too many yards are designed by one person, planted by another, and maintained by nobody. Gregory Morgan keeps it simple: the same crew that designs your yard plants it and cares for it.

That means the design is built to be maintained, the plantings are chosen to thrive here, and the follow through is part of the plan from day one.

Based on Bradlee St in Hyde Park, serving homeowners across the Boston area who want a yard that looks considered, not just cut.

The best yard is the one that still looks good in October.
