About

Dr. Allan V. Garfinkel, DDS

Dr. Allan Victor Garfinkel practices oral and maxillofacial surgery in Glen Burnie, Maryland. His office at 300 Hospital Drive, Suite 237, serves patients referred for surgical dental care across Anne Arundel County.

Oral surgeons diagnose and treat diseases, injuries, and defects involving the functional and esthetic aspects of the hard and soft tissues of the oral and maxillofacial region.
