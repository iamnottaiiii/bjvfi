A Working Salvage Yard In Whitewater Township

Miami Auto Parts is a used auto parts store at 4625 E Miami River Rd in Cleves, Ohio, listed publicly under junkyard and automotive parts categories. The yard sits between Miamitown and Cleves along the Great Miami River, serving the greater Cincinnati area.

It is the kind of place mechanics and DIY builders know by reputation: rows of donor vehicles, a parts counter that knows its inventory, and prices that keep project cars on the road. Public listings note the yard for engines, body panels, glass, wheels, and electrical parts.

If you are hunting a hard to find component, the fastest route is a phone call. Bring your year, make, model, and trim, and the counter will tell you straight whether the part is on the lot.
