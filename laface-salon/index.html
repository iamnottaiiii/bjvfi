About

A neighborhood salon with a loyal following

Laface Salon sits in Empire Plaza on Erie Boulevard East in Syracuse, serving guests from Syracuse and De Witt. The salon brings together independent hair stylists and nail artists who each run their own book, so appointments are personal and unhurried.

Reviewers describe the work as meticulous and passionate, and many say the people at Laface have become like family. That word of mouth has earned the salon a 5 star rating across dozens of reviews.
