Our story

About Gomez Barber Shop

Gomez Barber Shop on Garvey Avenue in South El Monte is run by a husband-and-wife team with an old-school approach: take your time, listen well, and cut it right. Regulars praise the personal service and fair prices.

Every visit follows the same simple rhythm: a real consultation about what you want, careful work in the chair, and a finish you can feel good about. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
