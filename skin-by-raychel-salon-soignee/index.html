Our story

Skin by Raychel @ Salon Soignee is Raychel's licensed-esthetician practice on Long Pond Road in Rochester, specializing in corrective skincare. Clients praise the microdermabrasion that leaves skin squeaky-clean with barely any reddening, and the eyebrow waxing that keeps clients loyal for over 15 years. Diamond dermabrasion, corrective peels, acne correction, and organic spray tanning round out the menu.
