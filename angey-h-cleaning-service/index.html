Office and workspace cleaning

Routine cleaning that keeps desks, common areas, and reception spaces presentable for staff and visitors.

  • Dust desks, shelves, ledges, and fixtures
  • Wipe light switches, door handles, and touch points
  • Vacuum carpets and mop hard floors
  • Clean kitchen and break room surfaces
  • Empty desk and common-area waste bins

Floor care

The floors people notice first: lobbies, hallways, and sales floors kept clean and scuff-free.

  • Sweep, mop, and detail hard flooring
  • Vacuum carpeted offices and waiting areas
  • Entry mat cleaning for tracked-in dirt
  • Spot cleaning for spills and stains

Restroom sanitation

Thorough restroom service on every visit, stocked and ready for the next workday.

  • Disinfect toilets, sinks, and countertops
  • Clean mirrors and polish fixtures
  • Restock soap, paper towels, and tissue
  • Mop and sanitize floors
  • Empty sanitary waste bins

Trash and recycling

Full waste handling so the building never starts a week with full bins.

  • Collect and remove trash from all rooms
  • Sort recycling per building rules
  • Replace liners and wipe bins as needed
  • Break down and haul cardboard

Windows and glass

Streak-free glass for storefronts, partitions, and entry doors.

  • Interior window and partition cleaning
  • Entry door glass, inside and out
  • Wipe frames, sills, and smudges
  • Display case and counter glass

Move in and move out cleaning

Deep one-time cleans for unit turnovers, new leases, and post-renovation spaces.

  • Full top-to-bottom deep clean
  • Inside cabinets, closets, and storage
  • Baseboards, vents, and light fixtures
  • Bathroom and kitchen detail

Our story

A Lynn janitorial service built on showing up

Angey H cleaning service is a janitorial business based at 112 Johnson St in Lynn, Massachusetts. The whole model is simple: businesses need their space cleaned reliably, on time, and without drama, so that is exactly what we sell.

When you hire us, you deal directly with the person who cleans your building. That keeps communication short and accountability clear. If something was missed, one call fixes it. If your hours change, one call reschedules it.

We bring our own supplies and equipment to every visit, work around your schedule including evenings and weekends, and keep pricing flat and predictable. Reliable, local, and easy to reach, that is the pitch.

What we believe

A clean building makes staff prouder and customers more comfortable. That is worth doing carefully, every visit.

How we work

Walk the space with you, agree on a checklist, clean to it, and check in regularly. Adjustments are always free.
