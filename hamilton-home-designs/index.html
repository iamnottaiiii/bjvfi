Our story

A local builder, by design.

Hamilton Home Designs is based in Fleming Island, Florida, building and designing custom homes for neighbors across Clay County and the Jacksonville area.

The approach

Measure twice, build once.

The name says it all: design comes first. Too many builds go sideways because the plans were rushed. We take the time to get the drawings right, price the work honestly, and communicate clearly while the sawdust flies.

As a local, independent builder, you work directly with the person responsible for your home. No call centers, no hand offs, no surprises at closing.
