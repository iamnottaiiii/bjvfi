Our story

A local crew with a simple standard

K’s Landscaping & Masonry Inc. is a locally owned and operated landscaping and masonry company based at 50 Allen Ave in Lynn, Massachusetts. For six years, our six-person crew has served Lynn and the surrounding North Shore communities.

We are led by Edvin Mendez, whose standard is simple: do every job the way you would want it done on your own home. That is why neighbors hire us once and keep calling us back, for weekly mowing, for spring and fall cleanups, and for bigger masonry projects like patios, driveways and steps.

We are background checked on Thumbtack, we show up when we say we will, and every estimate is free.
