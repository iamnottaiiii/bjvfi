About

The contractor neighbors recommend

d Sullivan Contracting is a Marlborough-based general contractor led by Don Sullivan, known for steady communication and workmanship that earns repeat clients.

Our Story

Twenty-one years of showing up

d Sullivan Contracting has been remodeling and repairing homes in the Marlborough area for over two decades. The business is built on a simple reputation: interior and exterior renovations done carefully, with the homeowner kept in the loop from day one.

Clients describe the quality of the work, the care in the process, and the communication along the way as excellent, and several have hired Don for project after project over ten years or more. That kind of loyalty is not bought. It is earned one finished room at a time.

Based at 172 Winter St in Marlborough, the company serves Marlborough and surrounding MetroWest communities, including Southborough and Sudbury, where past projects include deck builds and full home renovations.
