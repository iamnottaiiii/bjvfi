What we handle

The usual suspects

Queens homes and businesses deal with the same handful of pests again and again. These are the ones we see most.

Cockroaches

German and American roaches in kitchens, basements, and boiler rooms. We treat harborages and follow up until activity stops.

Bed bugs

Mattresses, box springs, and baseboards. Careful inspection first, then targeted treatment of every hiding spot we find.

Rodents

Mice and rats in walls, ceilings, and storage areas. Trapping, exclusion advice, and monitoring to confirm they are gone.

Ants

Trailing lines in kitchens and bathrooms. We track them back to the colony instead of just spraying the trail.

Termites

Quiet damage is the danger. We look for mud tubes, frass, and hollow wood, then recommend the right next step.

Wasps & hornets

Nests under eaves, in sheds, and around yards. Removed carefully so your outdoor space is yours again.

What we do, and how each visit works

Open any section for a plain description of the service. Every job starts with an inspection and a clear explanation before any treatment begins.

A full-home visit for apartments, houses, and multi-family buildings in St. Albans and across Queens.

  • Room-by-room inspection for roaches, ants, spiders, silverfish, and pantry pests
  • Targeted treatment of harborages: behind appliances, under sinks, along baseboards
  • Entry-point notes so you know exactly where pests are getting in
  • Simple prevention steps tailored to your home

For restaurants, bodegas, offices, and storefronts where a pest sighting costs more than a treatment.

  • Discreet scheduling around your business hours
  • Documentation-friendly service notes for health inspections
  • Focus on kitchens, storage rooms, and waste areas where problems start

Bed bugs hide well, so the inspection matters most. We check mattresses, box springs, headboards, and nearby furniture before treating.

  • Detailed inspection of sleeping areas and adjacent rooms
  • Targeted treatment of cracks, crevices, and harborage points
  • Prep checklist so your treatment day goes smoothly
  • Follow-up guidance to confirm the problem is resolved

Mice and rats leave droppings, gnaw marks, and noise in walls. We find how they enter, remove the active ones, and help you shut the door behind them.

  • Trapping and removal plan for the current population
  • Entry-point survey: gaps around pipes, doors, and foundations
  • Exclusion and sanitation recommendations

Termites work quietly. An inspection looks for mud tubes, damaged wood, and swarm evidence around the foundation and crawl spaces.

  • Visual inspection of accessible wood and foundation areas
  • Clear findings report in plain language
  • Treatment recommendations if activity is found

Nests under eaves, in wall voids, sheds, and trees. We assess the nest location and species, then remove it safely.

  • Safe removal scheduled to minimize risk to people and pets
  • Advice on deterring rebuilds in the same spot

For landlords, supers, and homeowners who handle the small stuff themselves between professional visits.

  • Traps, monitors, and glue boards
  • Roach and ant baits and gels
  • Sealing materials: caulk, steel wool, door sweeps
