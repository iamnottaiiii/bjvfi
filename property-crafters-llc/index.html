The Craft Benches

Services by Station

Four stations cover what the workshop does best: premium painting and complete drywall services. Each station lists a sample scope and a sample process. Call to confirm details for your project.

Sample scopeSample processCall to confirm
Station 01

Interior Painting

Premium interior painting with thorough prep work, careful masking, and crisp lines at every edge.

  • 1Survey. Walk the rooms, note repairs, and confirm colors and sheen.
  • 2Prep. Protect floors and furnishings, fill and sand as needed.
  • 3Craft. Prime where needed and apply even finish coats.
  • 4Walkthrough. Inspect every wall with you before the tools come down.
Station 02

Exterior Painting

Exterior painting built for Ohio weather, from surface prep to a durable top coat.

  • 1Survey. Check siding, trim, and trouble spots up close.
  • 2Prep. Scrape, sand, caulk, and prime failing areas.
  • 3Craft. Apply weather-ready coatings in the right conditions.
  • 4Walkthrough. Final review of every elevation with you.
Station 03

Drywall Installation

Complete drywall installation: hung straight, taped tight, and finished smooth and ready for paint.

  • 1Survey. Measure the space and plan sheet layout.
  • 2Hang. Set boards plumb with tight, clean joints.
  • 3Finish. Tape, mud, and sand to a smooth, even surface.
  • 4Walkthrough. Check the finish in raking light before sign-off.
Station 04

Drywall Repair and Finishing

Repairs that disappear: patches, crack fixes, and texture matching that blend into the existing wall.

  • 1Survey. Assess the damage and the surrounding finish.
  • 2Patch. Cut, back, and set the repair solid.
  • 3Blend. Tape, mud, sand, and match the texture.
  • 4Walkthrough. Confirm the repair vanishes into the wall.
