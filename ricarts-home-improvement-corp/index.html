About Ricart's Home Improvement Corp.

Based in Linden, New Jersey, Ricart's Home Improvement Corp. has spent 10 years becoming the crew local homeowners call first. What started as general home repairs grew into a full home improvement service: decks, plumbing, pressure washing, and winter snow removal, all under one roof.

The formula has not changed. Answer fast, show up ready, do clean work, and charge a fair price. That is why the company holds Top Pro status on Thumbtack, with a 4.9 rating across 168 reviews and more than 200 hires.

The team is background checked and license verified, and every project gets the same care, whether it is a single leaky faucet or a full deck rebuild.
