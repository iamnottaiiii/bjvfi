Why earthwork matters

Every solid project starts below grade. Good digging is invisible, bad digging is not.

The part of the job nobody sees.

Foundations, driveways, drainage, septic lines: they all depend on what happens before the concrete truck shows up. Proper depth, proper slope, proper compaction. That is the whole business, and it is the business Dedden Excavating is in.
