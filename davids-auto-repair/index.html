Our story

One man, one shop,
one standard

Tired of messy shops, he built his own

David Robey has been working on automobiles for 35 years. It is his only passion. Over the years he worked in many repair shops, and in his words, they were all a mess.

So in 2003 he opened his own shop at 8238 Gregory Dr in Alexandria, a nice clean shop where he does the work himself. He treasures his customers and treats them well.

The reviews tell the rest: a spotless garage, upfront quotes, and customers who have trusted him with their vehicles for 17 years and counting.

"I treasure all my customers and treat them very well. I do the job right the first time."

David Robey, Owner
