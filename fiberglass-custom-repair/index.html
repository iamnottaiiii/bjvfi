The Yard's Record

The shop's public business listing describes Fiberglass Custom Repair as a premier leader in fiberglass and gelcoat repair, with one guiding rule: quality over everything else.

The shop sits at 128 Wilmer Ave in Cincinnati, close to the Ohio River, and works on boats of many kinds. The shop's own public posts show mornings at the shop running boats and days spent installing composite transoms.

When the river rose in 2019, the shop posted that they would save customers' boats, clean up the shop, and have everyone repaired in time for summer, writing that looking after customers through high water is simply what they do.

  • Specialty shop. Listed as a boat service and marine service station, not a general marina. Fiberglass and gelcoat are the core trade.
  • Quality-first rule. The shop's own public description: "Quality over everything else."
  • River country. Based at 128 Wilmer Ave, Cincinnati, OH 45226, serving boaters on and around the Ohio River.
  • Hands-on crew. Public posts from the shop show the crew at work on hulls, transoms, and engines, season after season.
