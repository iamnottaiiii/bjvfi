Why Ometeotl

A studio with soul,
not a flash factory

Named for the Aztec concept of duality and creation, Ometeotl Tattoo brings deep cultural roots to South Philly ink.

Custom, never copied

Every piece starts as a conversation. Your story, your symbols, drawn fresh for your skin. No catalog tattoos, no shortcuts.

Clean and licensed

A professional, licensed studio with strict hygiene. Single-use needles, hospital-grade sterilization, and aftercare guidance for every client.

Walk-ins welcome

What we do

Styles and services

From bold Chicano black and grey to delicate fine line, every piece is drawn custom. Large scale work welcome.

  1. 01 Black and Grey RealismSmooth shading, deep contrast, portraits and large scale pieces built to age beautifully. from $100sample
  2. 02 Chicano StyleFine line black and grey with cultural soul. Lettering, portraits, and iconography with roots. from $120sample
  3. 03 Fine LineDelicate single needle work. Minimalist symbols, script, botanicals, and small meaningful pieces. from $80sample
  4. 04 Custom LetteringNames, dates, and phrases in hand drawn script. Old English, calligraphy, and original styles. from $80sample
  5. 05 Color TattoosBold reds, deep blues, rich contrast. Statement color work designed to stay vivid for years. from $150sample
