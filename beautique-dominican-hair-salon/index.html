Our story
A neighborhood salon where great hair days are the routine.

Beautique Dominican Hair salon is a Dominican hair salon on Avenue H in Brooklyn, built around the techniques Dominican stylists are famous for: careful washing, thorough conditioning, and blowouts with lasting silk and bounce.

Guests know the space for being very clean and the wash service for being a cut above. It gets busy on weekends, so regulars arrive early, and newcomers quickly become regulars too.

Whether you want a classic wash and set, a sleek silk press, or a deep conditioning reset, you will be welcomed, listened to, and sent out the door with hair that turns heads.
