The way we build

Start with the lot. End with a home that could only be yours.

A Christopher Robin home begins with a walk of the land: where the light falls, which trees stay, how the driveway should curve. The plan is drawn to fit the lot and the family, not the other way around.

That is how homes end up with beamed ceilings under a tree canopy, covered decks facing the woods, and lower levels finished as real living space instead of afterthoughts.

The details are instantly evident: the open floor plan, beamed ceilings, and views in every direction.

Our story

Twenty years of building in one township

Christopher Robin Homes has spent two decades doing one thing: building custom homes in Anderson Township, on Cincinnati east side. No subdivisions of lookalikes, no far-flung jobs. Just homes drawn for their lots, built by people who know the ground, the codes and the neighbors.

The company is listed as an ENERGY STAR partner in Ohio, reflecting a long-standing attention to how a home performs, not just how it photographs.

We build the house. Then we help you finish the setting, down to the last planting.
