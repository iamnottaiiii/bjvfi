Our story

The shop Accokeek
keeps coming back to

The service station at 201 Bryan Point Rd is operated by the MTH Auto Services team, a shop reviewers describe as honest, knowledgeable, and genuinely on the customer's side.

Not about scamming people.
About fixing cars.

That is how customers describe this shop: a place that cares, wants to help, and sticks with you. Drivers say the techs take more time than expected, explain the work in plain language, and price it fairly.

The team works on all makes and models, from daily-driver Hondas to work trucks, and handles everything from a quick tire patch to full engine diagnostics with ASE certified training behind it.

Many customers have been coming for years, bringing in second and third vehicles, and sending family and friends. In a town this size, that kind of loyalty is earned one repair at a time.
