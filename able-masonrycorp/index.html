Our Story

Able Masonrycorp is a masonry contractor based in East Rockaway, New York, working across Nassau County. The name says the approach: show up, do the work right, and stand behind it.

Masonry on Long Island takes a beating from freeze-thaw cycles, salt air, and sandy soil. We build with that in mind: proper footings, correct mortar, real drainage, and joints tooled to shed water.

Whether it is a crumbling chimney, a sunken walkway, or a new stone facade, you get a written scope, a fair price, and a crew that cleans up at the end of every day.

What We Believe

  • The right material for the job, never the cheapest shortcut
  • Straight lines and clean joints on every course
  • Honest timelines, kept
