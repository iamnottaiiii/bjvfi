Our Story

About the Shop

A quality auto body shop on Cincinnati's west side, backed by the Walt Sweeney dealer family.

Part of the Walt Sweeney Family

Walt Sweeney Harrison Ave Quality Auto Body sits at 6315 Harrison Ave in Cincinnati, Ohio, on the west side of town. It operates as part of the Walt Sweeney dealer group, a longtime Cincinnati Ford dealer with locations including 5400 Glenway Ave.

The 6315 Harrison Avenue site has deep west side roots. The property previously housed Wullenweber Motors and was purchased by Sweeney Investments, owner of Walt Sweeney Auto Center, in 2020. Today the location is home to Walt Sweeney's Harrison Ave operations, Glenway Auto Body, and Smyth Automotive.

Reviewers consistently describe a stress free, friendly experience: quick turnaround times, honest communication, and a team that treats customers well.
