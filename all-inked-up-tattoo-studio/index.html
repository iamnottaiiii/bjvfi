Tattoo styles

Every style,
one shop

The artists cover all styles imaginable. These are the lanes the shop is known for, with sample pricing to set expectations.

  1. 01

    Traditional and neo-traditional

    Bold lines, classic motifs, eagles, roses, daggers, anchors. The shop's bread and butter since 1988.

  2. 02

    Black and grey realism

    Portraits, wildlife, religious imagery and smooth shaded blackwork with depth that holds up over time.

  3. 03

    Fine line and minimalist

    Delicate script, small symbols and single-needle style work. A favorite for first tattoos.

  4. 04

    Color pieces

    Vibrant color packing for new school, illustrative and floral work that stays bright.

  5. 05

    Lettering and script

    Names, quotes, memorial pieces. Clean lettering drawn to fit the body, not stamped from a font book.

  6. 06

    Cover-ups

    Honest consults on what an old tattoo can become. No promises that cannot be kept.

Pricing: sample guide only. The shop is known for fair prices and giving you the most for your money. Final quotes depend on size, placement and detail, always confirmed before the needle touches skin. Call (302) 654-4421 for a quote.
