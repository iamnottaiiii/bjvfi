Vertek Construction Management, LLC is a construction firm based at 7171 Airport Road in Bath, Pennsylvania, serving the Lehigh Valley. Since 2014, the company has provided construction management, consulting, and general contracting services with a hands-on approach to every project.

Rather than a one-size-fits-all model, Vertek customizes its resources to meet the demands of each client, providing a start-to-finish solution from project inception to completion.
