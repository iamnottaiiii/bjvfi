Our Story

Thirty Years of Style

Sense of Style has been part of the Elma community for over 30 years, growing from a neighborhood nail studio into a full service beauty salon on Transit Road. The formula never changed: treat every client like a regular, keep the studio spotless, and make sure the work speaks for itself.

Today the salon offers total hair services, including expert coloring and updos, alongside its signature nail work: silk and fiberglass wraps, gel nails, spa pedicures and manicures. Massages and waxing round out a menu built for head to toe pampering.

What clients remember most is the feeling. A cool, cozy shop. A down to earth owner and a team that takes walk ins when they can and sends everyone out looking their best.

The interior of the salon is absolutely beautifully designed and super clean.

Paraphrased from client reviews.
