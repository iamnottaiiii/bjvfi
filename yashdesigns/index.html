Our story

Design rooted in Bay Ridge

YashDesigns is an interior design studio based at 10031 4th Ave #2G in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn. The studio works with homeowners across Brooklyn who want spaces that are warm, functional, and unmistakably theirs.

The approach is practical and personal: understand how you live, plan around it, and source pieces that fit your budget and your taste. No showroom pressure, no one-size-fits-all looks.

Clients rate YashDesigns 5.0 stars on Google, and the studio keeps weekday hours, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
