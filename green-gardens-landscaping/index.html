Our story

A Silver Spring crew with deep roots.

Green Gardens Landscaping grew out of a simple observation: plenty of Silver Spring yards had potential, but few had a crew they could truly count on. We started small, earned trust one lawn at a time, and kept growing on referrals.

Today we serve homes and small businesses across Montgomery County. The formula has not changed: show up on time, do the work properly, and treat every property like it belongs to a neighbor. Because it does.
