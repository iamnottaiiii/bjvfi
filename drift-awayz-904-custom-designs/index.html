Our Story

Built on Parkside Ave

Drift Awayz 904 Custom Designs is a small custom woodworking shop in Orange Park, Florida, where every piece starts as raw lumber and an idea.

Custom work, the slow way

The name says it all: pieces with a drift, a flow, a character you cannot mass produce. We build custom wood designs, home decor and furniture one piece at a time, choosing the boards, cutting the joints and applying the finish by hand.

Because the shop is small, every project gets real attention. You talk to the person building your piece, not a sales desk.

Whether it is a table your family will eat around for decades or a sign that makes a house feel like home, the goal is the same: woodwork worth keeping.

Why custom?

Store-bought furniture is built to a price. Custom woodwork is built to a person: your space, your style, your life. It costs more than flat-pack, and it outlives it by decades.

That is the whole philosophy of the shop, in one paragraph.
