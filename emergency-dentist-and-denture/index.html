Emergency Care

Toothaches and dental pain

Severe tooth pain can signal infection, decay, or nerve damage. The first step is a careful exam to find the cause, then treatment aimed at relieving pain and protecting the tooth.

If you have swelling, fever, or pain that will not ease, do not wait. Call (410) 364-8470 right away.

Emergency Care

Broken, chipped, or knocked-out teeth

Accidents and injuries can crack, chip, or dislodge teeth. Quick care gives a damaged tooth the best chance of being saved. If a tooth is knocked out, keep it moist and call immediately.

Denture Services

Denture repairs and adjustments

A cracked or ill-fitting denture makes eating and speaking difficult. The practice offers repairs and adjustments to restore comfort and function, so you can get back to your day.

Denture Services

Full and partial dentures

For patients missing teeth, dentures offer a practical way to restore a complete smile. The process starts with a consultation to understand your needs and options.
