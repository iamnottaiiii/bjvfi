Services

Full-service accounting,
contractor edition

Bookkeeping, payables, receivables, payroll, and tax, all coded the way construction actually works. Select a tab.

Monthly bookkeeping & job costing

Your books kept current and coded to the job level, so every report tells you something useful.

  • Transaction entry and coding to jobs and cost codes
  • Bank, credit card, and loan account reconciliations
  • Monthly financial reports: P&L, balance sheet, job profitability
  • New job setup in the accounting software with schedule of values

Accounts payable & compliance

Vendor bills paid on time and subs kept compliant, so nobody holds up your job.

  • Invoice entry with job costing and vendor statement reconciliation
  • Lien waiver collection from vendors before payment
  • Subcontractor W-9 management and compliance tracking
  • 1099 preparation and filing at year end

Accounts receivable & pay applications

Get billed, get followed up, get paid. Cash flow is the business.

  • Invoicing and pay applications, AIA and non-AIA formats
  • Retention tracking and invoicing when it comes due
  • Pre-lien notice requests and lien management support
  • Follow-up on late payments and customer communication

Payroll & certified payroll

Crew pay handled right, including the public-works paperwork most bookkeepers run from.

  • Time coded to jobs and cost codes, payroll submitted on schedule
  • Payroll tax filings and payments made on time
  • Certified payroll reporting for prevailing-wage work
  • Year-end W-2 preparation and filing

Tax & financial advisory

Beyond the books: planning that keeps more of what you earn and bids that cover what you spend.

  • Tax return preparation for contractors and related entities
  • Overhead and budget planning for the year ahead
  • Work-in-progress reporting and financial health reviews
  • Construction accounting training for your office staff
