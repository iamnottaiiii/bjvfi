Our Story

A neighborhood place, through and through

Long Nails is owned and operated by LeeAnn and Ricky, neighborhood people running a neighborhood salon. That shows in everything clients say about it: polite, friendly staff, honest service, and nails that last.

Regulars for over a decade

Clients describe coming back for 12 years or more, calling it the best nail salon they have ever visited. Their nails last and come out beautiful, they say, and the owners and workers could not be more polite.

More than a quick manicure

Regulars rave about the special pedicure with its magnificent massage, perfect gel manicures, and brows done right. It is the kind of place where you arrive a customer and leave a regular.
