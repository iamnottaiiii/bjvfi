Our story

A Tujunga low-voltage specialist with a decade in the trade.

VDV Systems Inc is an active California corporation based at 6630 Elmhurst Dr in Tujunga. For more than ten years the company has focused on one lane: the low-voltage systems that homes and businesses depend on every day, from security to fiber to automation.

"Low-voltage is not an add-on. It is the backbone."

The team serves Tujunga, Sunland, La Crescenta, Glendale, Pasadena, Burbank, and communities across the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles, bringing the same care to a single camera install as to a full structured-wiring project.

Voice, data, and video. The three low-voltage signal types that carry your calls, your internet, and your cameras.
