Our story

A neighborhood nail studio on Avenue U

Sparkle On You has been part of the Gravesend routine for years, a friendly stop for regulars and first timers alike.

Where regulars are made

Some clients have been coming for over fifteen years, and they point to the same things: a sparkling clean space, a refreshing atmosphere that feels like home, and friendly owners and workers who remember you.

The menu stays focused on what a neighborhood nail salon should do well: careful manicures and pedicures, sturdy acrylic sets, and nail art with a personal touch, from classic French tips to the cute floral designs clients show off in reviews.

Walk-ins are welcome and the kettle is metaphorically on. Come in for a quick polish change or settle in for the full works.
