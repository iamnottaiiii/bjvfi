Attic insulation

Your attic is where comfort is won or lost

Most heat gain and heat loss in a home happens through the attic. Old, thin, or damaged insulation forces your HVAC system to work overtime, and your bills show it.

We install and upgrade attic insulation the right way: sealed, even, and matched to your home, so every room feels the way it should.

  • Blown-in and batt insulation installed to proper depth
  • Old or damaged insulation removed and replaced
  • Attics, walls, and crawl spaces covered

Energy and air quality

Ducts matter as much as insulation

Leaky or damaged air ducts can waste a large share of the air your system conditions, dumping it into the attic or crawl space instead of your rooms.

Our air duct repair and replacement service seals the leaks and restores proper airflow, so the comfort your insulation creates actually reaches you.

  • Air duct repair and full replacement
  • Better airflow in every room of the home

Our story

The eco choice, plain and simple

Eco Choice Remodeling is a Tarzana-based insulation contractor. Eco Choice Insulation Inc is registered as a California corporation in the insulation business, and the Eco Choice name carries an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau under Eco Choice Attic Insulation, Inc.

The idea behind the name is straightforward: the eco choice is the smart choice. A well-insulated home uses less energy, costs less to heat and cool, and stays comfortable through LA summers and chilly winter nights.

We focus on doing a small set of things well: insulation installation and upgrades, insulation removal and replacement, and air duct repair and replacement, for homes and commercial buildings alike.
