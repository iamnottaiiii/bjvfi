What we clean

Six specialties, one standard: thorough, careful work on every visit.

Carpet Cleaning

Deep cleaning for wall to wall carpet. Lifts embedded dirt, revives traffic lanes, and leaves fibers soft and fresh.

Stairs

Staircases collect the most wear in any home. We clean each tread carefully so the whole staircase looks even again.

Upholstery

Sofas, loveseats, armchairs, and dining chairs. We refresh fabric so your furniture looks and feels new again.

Pet Stains and Odor

Targeted treatments for pet spots and lingering odors, so your home stays inviting for guests and family.

Area Rugs

Area rugs get expert care to remove buildup and bring back color and texture, handled gently by fiber type.

Move In and Move Out

Empty homes and apartments deep cleaned so you move in fresh or hand over keys with confidence.

About us

A cleaner clean for Hamden homes

ECO CARPET CLEANING CT is a Hamden, Connecticut carpet cleaning service built around one idea: your carpets can be deeply clean without filling your home with harsh chemicals.

Based in Hamden

We are local to Hamden, CT, at 1800 Aspen Glen Dr, and we serve Hamden plus surrounding New Haven County communities.

Eco by name, eco by method

Low moisture, eco conscious cleaning methods mean less water, faster drying, and no harsh residue left in your carpets.

Open when you need us

Every day from 5 AM to 10 PM. Early morning, evening, or weekend appointments that fit your schedule.

How every job goes

1. Walkthrough

We walk the areas with you, note stains and high traffic spots, and agree on the plan and price first.

2. Deep clean

Thorough, careful cleaning with eco conscious methods, treating the spots that bother you most.

3. Final check

We review the results with you before we leave, so you see the difference firsthand.
