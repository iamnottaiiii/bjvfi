About the Shop

Young's Barber Shop has been cutting hair on Price Pike in Florence, Kentucky for years, and the reviews tell the same story again and again: a relaxed, old-school atmosphere, barbers who take their time, and prices that stay reasonable.

Customers mention the shop by name for proper fades, friendly conversation, and a crew that remembers you. One reviewer wrote that the staff cared enough to remember his upcoming wedding from a passing mention, and that the cut before the big day was perfect. Another noted it's been around for so long it's no wonder, and that the professional staff and relaxed atmosphere are why folks keep coming back, some for years at a stretch.

What we do

Traditional men's haircuts. Walk-ins welcome. That's the whole sign, and it's the whole philosophy.

How it works

Walk in, get your name in the ledger, and take a seat. The shop stays busy, so showing up early on a Saturday is the wise move.

Who's in the chair

Reviewers name barbers like Caleb, Craig, and Matt for clean fades and careful work. In a hurry? Folks say any chair is a safe bet.
