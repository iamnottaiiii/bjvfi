Storm damage checklist

Spot trouble from the ground before it becomes a leak

After any strong storm, walk your property and check these five things. Try the interactive version on the Services page and bring your score to the call.

D.01

Missing or lifted shingles

Wind can peel tabs or tear shingles off entirely. Check the yard and the roofline.

D.02

Hail bruising

Look for dents on gutters, vents, and flashing. Shingle bruising often hides until it leaks.

D.03

Fallen debris

Branches and limbs can puncture membranes and crack shingles on impact.
