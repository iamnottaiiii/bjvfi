Our story

A local shop built on doing the job right

Coastal Climate Heating & AC Repair started with a simple frustration: too many homeowners in the Valley were getting upsold on systems they did not need. We built our shop around the opposite idea. Show up, diagnose honestly, fix what is broken, and only recommend replacement when the math truly favors it.

From our base at 8477 Canoga Ave in Canoga Park, we serve homes across the West San Fernando Valley. Our technicians live here too, which is why our reputation on every street matters more to us than any single invoice.

  • Locally owned and operated in Canoga Park
  • Technicians trained on all major equipment brands
  • Straight answers, written upfront pricing
  • Respect for your home: shoe covers and cleanup, every visit
