About

Our story

Founded 2017 in La Crescenta

Fernandez Project Services, Inc. was founded in 2017 and is led by Chief Executive Officer Darrell Fernandez. Listed in the Blue Book since 2018, the firm supports public and private building industries with program, project, and construction management and facility maintenance.

As a certified disadvantaged business enterprise, the firm partners on public infrastructure and building work across Los Angeles County, including teaming on major transit civil works.
