Our story

Two decades of keeping corrections connected, now employee-owned.

Combined Public Communications was founded in 2000 with a single focus: reliable, cost-effective communications technology for correctional institutions.

From its headquarters at 100 Aqua Drive in Cold Spring, Kentucky, CPC grew to serve hundreds of state, county and municipal facilities across the United States, becoming one of the largest providers in the industry.

Today the company is employee-owned through an Employee Stock Ownership Plan, so the people who install, support and answer for the technology are the people who own it.
