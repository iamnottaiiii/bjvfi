Our story

A Lynn crew that treats your home like its own

J&H Construction Inc. is a home building and general contracting company based at 38 Forest Hill Ave in Lynn, Massachusetts. In business since 2018, we are a small local crew, which means the people you meet at the estimate are the people on your job site.

We focus on single-family home construction, roofing, and renovations across Lynn and the surrounding North Shore communities. Our approach is simple: show up when we say we will, keep you updated every day, and leave the place cleaner than we found it.

Homeowners hire us for the work, and recommend us for how we work. Recent customers have called the crew professional, courteous, and hardworking from the minute they arrived.
