Signs you need us

Hear It Early, Fix It Cheaper

Slipping Gears

RPMs climb but speed does not. A classic early warning.

Grinding or Whining

New noises under acceleration or while shifting deserve a look.

Delayed Shifting

A long pause before the gear engages usually means low or burnt fluid.

Fluid Leaks

Red or brown puddles under the middle of the car. Do not top off and ignore it.
