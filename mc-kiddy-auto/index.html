Services

Typical work for a neighborhood auto repair shop. Call to confirm what the shop currently offers before you come in.

Oil changes and fluids

Routine oil changes, fluid top offs, and filter swaps to keep things running smooth.

Brake service

Pads, rotors, and inspections when your stopping feels soft or squeaky.

Engine diagnostics

Check engine light on? We read the codes and explain what they mean in plain words.

Tires and alignment

Rotations, balancing, and alignment checks for even wear and a straight drive.

Batteries and electrical

Testing, replacement, starters, and alternators when the car will not turn over.

Transmission service

Shifting trouble? Ask about transmission inspection and service options.

Heating and A/C

No cold air in July or no heat in January, we will chase it down.

Pre purchase inspections

Thinking about buying a used car? Bring it by for a once over first.
