About

A Boone County excavation company

Small, local, and still here after nearly three decades. That is the whole pitch.

Since 1999

G & T Excavation, Inc. has been in business in Burlington, Kentucky since 1999, incorporated in 1998 and led by president Tessie Finke, according to public business records.

Twenty seven years in the dirt business means we have seen just about every kind of Boone County ground: clay that holds water, rock that fights back, and slopes that need a careful hand.

How we work

We are a small crew, not a call center. When you call, you talk to someone who knows the work and will give you a straight answer about scope and timing.

Commercial or residential, big pad or small trench: if it involves moving earth the right way, it is what we do.
