Our story

A Harrisburg electrical contractor since 1993.

Built on the trade, based on Forster Street.

Upa Inc is a Pennsylvania electrical contracting company headquartered at 2401 Forster Street in Harrisburg. Since 1993 we have wired, repaired, and upgraded homes and commercial buildings across Dauphin County and the surrounding region.

We are a small, independent shop, and that is by design. The people who answer the phone understand the work, and the people who show up take pride in it. Our listing in the Blue Book of building and construction since 2006 reflects the commercial-grade capability we bring to every job, large or small.
