System Types

Furnaces

Gas and electric furnaces are the workhorses of Cincinnati winters. Common service needs include ignition, blower, and thermostat issues.

  • Typical repair: ignition and sensor faults
  • Typical install: full furnace replacement
  • Typical upkeep: annual safety check
Call to Confirm Availability

Central AC

Split-system air conditioners cool the whole house through ductwork. Low refrigerant, frozen coils, and capacitor failures are common summer calls.

  • Typical repair: not cooling or short cycling
  • Typical install: condenser and air handler
  • Typical upkeep: coil cleaning and filter changes
Call to Confirm Availability

Heat Pumps

Heat pumps heat and cool in one system, popular for milder shoulder seasons. Defrost and reversing-valve issues are the usual service calls.

  • Typical repair: stuck in one mode
  • Typical install: full heat pump system
  • Typical upkeep: outdoor unit clearing
Call to Confirm Availability

Ductless Mini-Splits

Wall-mounted heads condition single rooms or additions without ductwork. Great for garages, sunrooms, and older homes.

  • Typical repair: leaks or weak airflow
  • Typical install: single or multi-zone heads
  • Typical upkeep: filter rinsing
Call to Confirm Availability

Thermostats

The control center of the system. Upgrades and wiring fixes can solve comfort swings and high bills.

  • Typical repair: blank screen or wrong readings
  • Typical install: programmable or smart upgrade
  • Typical upkeep: battery and settings check
