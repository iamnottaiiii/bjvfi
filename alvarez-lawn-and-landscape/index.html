Our story

Rooted in Burbank

Alvarez lawn and landscape grew the honest way: one yard at a time, one neighbor telling the next.

We started with a mower, a trailer and a simple promise: show up when we say we will, and leave the yard better than we found it. Burbank noticed.

Today we run weekly routes across the neighborhood from our base on N Keystone St, plus cleanups, plantings and the bigger landscape projects that transform a property.

What has not changed is the standard. Edges stay crisp. Gates get closed. Clippings get blown off the walk, not left on it. The small things are the whole job.

Treat every yard like it is the one the whole street is judging.
