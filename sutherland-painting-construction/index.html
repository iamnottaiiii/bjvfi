About Us

A Salem crew you can call by name

Our story

sutherland painting & construction is run by owner John Sutherland and has served Salem and the North Shore since 2011. We are a small, hands-on crew, which means the person you talk to on the phone is the person who cares about your job.

We started as painters and added construction skills along the way, because too many paint jobs were hiding rotted trim, tired shingles, or failing gutters. Now we handle the whole picture: paint plus the repairs that make the paint last.
