Why Triple Crown

A community built around the good life

Triple Crown is one of Northern Kentucky's signature addresses: a master-planned golf community in Union with resort-style amenities and quick access to shopping, the airport, and downtown Cincinnati.

GolfTriple Crown Country Club's course winds through the neighborhood, minutes from the model center.
Pools + tennisCommunity pools, tennis courts, and a clubhouse anchor summer weekends, per community listings.
TrailsWalking trails thread the community for morning miles without leaving the neighborhood.
Local color

Streets named for legends

Man O War Boulevard, Justify Drive, Affirmed Avenue. The street signs read like a Kentucky Derby winners list, a nod to the region's racing heritage that gives the community its name and its character.

The community

Union, Kentucky's front porch

What the public record says about life at Triple Crown: a premier Northern Kentucky community built around its golf course, with everyday conveniences close by.

Amenities, per public listings

Resort, meet routine

Community pools, tennis courts, a clubhouse, and walking trails are listed as Triple Crown amenities across MLS and community profiles. Triple Crown Country Club and its golf course sit nearby, and a new Publix serves daily errands.

Location, per public listings

Connected, not crowded

Community profiles note convenient access to shopping, restaurants, and the Greater Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky airport, with downtown Cincinnati about 30 minutes away. Boone County schools serve the area.
