How a table gets its life back

Restoration, step by step

Every table is different, but a careful restoration follows the same patient sequence.

I

Assess

Slate, rails, cushions, and frame are inspected so you know what the table truly needs.

II

Strip and repair

Old cloth comes off; wood is repaired, re-glued, and refinished where needed.

III

Level and cloth

Slate is leveled true, then new cloth is stretched tight and even.

IV

True up

Cushions, pockets, and sights are set so the table plays the way it should.

The craft

Billiards and woodwork, under one roof

Nahrgang Billiard Co is listed as a billiard table repair shop and as a custom furniture and woodworking company. The services below reflect those listings; call to confirm current offerings.

01

Billiard table repair and restoration

The core of the business. Structural repairs to rails, legs, and frames, plus full restorations that bring tired tables back to true.

  • Rail and cushion rubber replacement
  • Frame and leg repairs
  • Wood refinishing and touch-up
  • Pocket replacement
02

Refelting and leveling

New cloth, stretched and fitted by hand, over slate leveled to play true. The single biggest upgrade an old table can get.

  • Cloth replacement
  • Slate leveling and shimming
  • Cushion facing and sights
03

Table moving and setup

Tables are heavy, fragile, and unforgiving. Professional disassembly, transport, and reassembly keep slate intact and level.

  • Disassembly and reassembly
  • In-home setup and leveling
04

Custom wood furniture

The woodworking side of the shop: custom-built and repaired furniture with traditional joinery, from game-room pieces to household cabinetry.

  • Custom furniture design and build
  • Furniture repair
  • Carpentry and cabinetry work

About

Three decades at the workbench.

Public records list Nahrgang Billiard Co as established in 1990 and incorporated in Ohio, a two-person operation run by owner Hugh Nahrgang. It has kept the same Rockhurst Lane address in Cincinnati's 45255 for years, the kind of shop neighbors find by word of mouth and keep calling for decades.

"A pool table is furniture first. It should play true and look like it belongs in the room."

That philosophy runs through both halves of the business: the billiard work demands precision measured in fractions of an inch, and the custom woodworking brings that same precision to the rest of the home.
