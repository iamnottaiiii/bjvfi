About

Meet Dr. Michael E. Fanelli

A Mullica Hill chiropractor with more than three decades of clinical experience.

Sample photo

Dr. Michael E. Fanelli, DC

Dr. Michael E. Fanelli is a licensed chiropractor practicing in Mullica Hill, New Jersey. He graduated from the New York Chiropractic College in 1990 and has been in clinical practice for more than 36 years.

Patients describe him as honest, informative, and thorough. One reviewer has been under his care since 1999; another, an elderly patient with multiple diagnoses, praised his honesty about what chiropractic care could and could not do for them.

The practice name, Fanelli Chiropractic & Muscle, reflects its whole-body approach: treating the spine together with the muscles, posture, and movement patterns that support it.
