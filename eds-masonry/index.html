The Story Behind the Trowel

There is a rhythm to laying brick that you cannot fake. Butter the brick, set it, tap it true, strike the joint. Do it ten thousand times and your hands learn things no classroom can teach. That is the kind of work Ed's Masonry was built on.

Based on Merrittview Lane in Cincinnati, Ed's Masonry is a one-name, one-reputation kind of business. The Better Business Bureau has carried the company's file since 2007, and it holds an A+ rating. In a trade where fly-by-night operators come and go, that kind of staying power says something.

Ed's Masonry takes on the jobs other contractors walk past: the leaning chimney, the spalling brick, the retaining wall that has seen better decades. The philosophy is simple. Show up when you say you will, do the work the right way, and leave the place cleaner than you found it.
