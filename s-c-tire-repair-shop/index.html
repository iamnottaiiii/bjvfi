Our story

A small shop with a big reputation

S & C Tire Repair Shop sits on South Colony Street in Meriden, and it runs on a simple idea: fix the tire, charge a fair price, treat people right.

Ask around town and you will hear the same stories. A driver with an urgent issue late in the day, and the owner staying after hours to get the tire fixed. A flat on New Years Eve, when every other shop was closed, and one phone call got it handled. A slow leak that came back a week later and was swapped out with no questions asked.

That is the whole business model. No upsell, no runaround. Just fast tire work at prices regular people can afford, from a shop that remembers its customers.

New or used tires, flat repairs, leak fixes, rim work: if it rolls, we can probably help. And if we cannot, we will tell you straight.
