How ordering works

From phone call to finished pour

Ordering concrete is straightforward. Tell us what you are pouring and when, and we handle the batching, the timing and the truck.

01

Call the plant

Ring (516) 371-3555 with your yardage, strength and pour date. Weekday orders welcome.

02

We batch it fresh

Your mix is batched to spec right before the truck rolls, so it arrives workable.

03

Truck arrives on site

Our driver positions the chute where your crew needs it and discharges at your pace.

04

You finish and cure

Your crew floats, finishes and cures while the concrete is at its best.

Our story

A local plant since 1998

Colonial Transit Mix Corp has been batching ready mix concrete in Inwood, New York since 1998. We are a small, family scale operation: a six truck fleet, a crew that knows the local job sites, and a plant that contractors in the Five Towns call when the pour has to go right.

Our own fleet, our own schedule

Because we run our own trucks, we control the timing. Your concrete is batched fresh and driven straight to your site, not passed through a broker.
