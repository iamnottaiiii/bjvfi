About the shop

A Main Street fixture

Village Barber Shop sits at 228 W Main St in Williamsburg, Ohio, a small shop with a long history on the street. Public records list the business as established in 1969, and a reviewer notes one barber with 45 years on the job.

It is the kind of place where the barber takes his time, knows the regulars by name, and sits right across from The Old Firehouse Brewery, according to a longtime customer review.

  • 1969: business listed as established (per Manta public records).
  • Today: a two-chair style shop serving Williamsburg and Clermont County.
  • The neighborhood: Main Street neighbors include Holtman's Donut Shop, Peoples Bank, and Main Street Pizza.
