About

The story of So Cali Barbershop

So Cali Barbershop on Martin Luther King Jr Boulevard serves the Lynwood neighborhood with everyday cuts. Barber Anthony, also known as June bug, is a familiar face in the chair.

Located at 3535 Martin Luther King Jr Blvd, Lynwood, CA 90262, the shop serves Lynwood and the surrounding neighborhoods. Walk-ins and regulars are both welcome, and the team is happy to talk through the cut before the clippers come out.
