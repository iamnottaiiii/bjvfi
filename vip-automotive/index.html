Our story

A Clinton shop with a loyal following

VIP AUTOMOTIVE sits at 9127 Piscataway Rd in Clinton, Maryland, serving drivers across Prince George's County. The shop has built its name on a simple formula: explain everything before work starts, charge a fair price, and treat every customer with respect.

Reviewers describe years of trust in the shop's work, bringing in every car in the household and sending friends and family along. Many mention that the team helps manage repair budgets, tackling the most important work first so drivers stay safe without overspending.

Whether it is routine maintenance, a tricky electrical problem, or collision damage, the goal is the same: your car leaves driving like new, and you leave knowing exactly what was done.
