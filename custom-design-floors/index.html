New floors, installed clean

Hardwood, laminate and luxury vinyl plank installed with tight seams and a level finish. We handle the subfloor prep, the transitions and the trim so the floor looks like it grew there.

Refinishing that brings floors back

Worn hardwood gets sanded, stained and sealed for a fresh surface without a full replacement. A refinish costs less than new wood and keeps the character of the original floor.

Tile for kitchens and baths

Durable, water friendly tile laid straight and grouted clean. Kitchens, bathrooms, entryways and laundry rooms get floors that stand up to daily life.

About Custom Design Floors

Custom Design Floors is a flooring contractor based at 301 Commerce Rd in Linden, New Jersey. The work is straightforward: measure carefully, prep the subfloor properly, and install floors that stay flat, quiet and good looking for years.
