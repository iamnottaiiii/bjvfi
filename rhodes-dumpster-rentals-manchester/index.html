Dumpster Sizes

Typical industry sizes and what they handle. Call (959) 250-2404 to confirm availability and pricing for your project.

10Yard

Small cleanouts

Garage cleanouts, small basement clearances, minor remodel debris, and yard waste. Fits a standard driveway.

Typical: about 12 ft long, 8 ft wide, 4 ft tall. Holds roughly 3 pickup truck loads.
15Yard

Medium projects

Roofing tear-offs on smaller homes, flooring removal, and mid size cleanouts with bulky furniture.

Typical: about 16 ft long, 7.5 ft wide, 4.5 ft tall. A step up when 10 yards will not cut it.
20Yard

Remodels

Kitchen and bathroom remodels, whole home flooring, deck demolition, and larger cleanouts.

Typical: about 22 ft long, 8 ft wide, 4 ft tall. The most popular homeowner size.
30Yard

Big jobs

Major renovations, whole home cleanouts, large roofing jobs, and light commercial work.

Typical: about 22 ft long, 8 ft wide, 6 ft tall. Holds roughly 9 pickup truck loads.
40Yard

Construction

New construction, full tear-outs, commercial cleanouts, and large demolition debris.

Typical: about 22 ft long, 8 ft wide, 8 ft tall. Maximum capacity for the biggest projects.

Who we are

About

A Manchester dumpster company built for one thing: getting the box to you on time.

CT

Based in Manchester

Rhodes Dumpster Rentals Manchester is a garbage collection and dumpster rental service based at 12 Summit St in Manchester, CT. We serve homeowners, contractors, and property managers across the Manchester area.

We keep it simple: a local number, a real person on the phone, a dumpster that shows up when promised, and pickup when the job is done.

For homeowners

Cleanouts, remodels, roofing, and yard projects. One box in the driveway beats ten trips to the transfer station.

For contractors

Jobsite boxes on your schedule, swaps when they fill, and clear terms so your bid stays accurate.

For property managers

Unit turnovers, eviction cleanouts, and renovation debris handled with one call and reliable pickup.

What goes in

Household junk, furniture, construction debris, roofing, yard waste, and general trash. Hazardous materials, tires, and electronics are restricted, ask us if you are unsure.
