What we build

Our development activity, drawn from the public record, centers on residential construction and property repositioning in Staten Island.

  1. Two-Family Residential Development

    Ground-up filings for two-family homes, such as the seven-building program on Wandel Avenue in Fox Hills. Design, permitting, and delivery from concept to certificate of occupancy.

  2. Alterations and Repositioning

    Alteration-type filings and modernization work on existing residential and mixed-use properties across the borough, based on Department of Buildings permit history.

  3. Demolition and Site Preparation

    Full demolition and fence/site applications for redevelopment parcels, preparing lots for new residential construction.

  4. Project and Permit Management

    End-to-end handling of the NYC approvals path: plans, filings, and coordination with architects of record and city agencies.

About Klacken Corporation

A Staten Island development company with a public track record going back to the early nineties.

At a glance

  • Domestic business corporation, New York
  • Filed 1993; active status per NY DOS
  • Principal office: 362 St Marks Place, Staten Island, NY 10301
  • Leadership: David J. Berman, Chief Executive Officer
  • Focus: residential development and property repositioning

Three decades on the Island

Klacken Corporation was incorporated in New York in 1993 and has kept its principal executive office in Staten Island ever since. The company's permit history runs from late-nineties alterations through 2014 demolition and site filings, and its development filings include the multi-building Wandel Avenue residential program in Fox Hills covered by New York YIMBY.

The firm is led by David J. Berman, who serves as chief executive officer.
