What we do

Roofing services

Tap a service to see what is included. Every job starts with an honest assessment and a written estimate. Pricing is quoted per project, call for a free estimate.

Leaks, missing shingles, damaged flashing, wind-lifted sections: CJP diagnoses the real cause and fixes it, without selling you a roof you do not need.

  • Leak detection and targeted repairs
  • Flashing, vent and pipe boot replacement
  • Storm and wind damage repair

When a roof has reached the end of its service life, CJP handles the complete tear-off and installation with top-tier materials and a clean, supervised jobsite.

  • Complete tear-off, underlayment and new shingle systems
  • New ridge cap, drip edge and flashings
  • Eligible for the 50-year Golden Pledge Warranty

CJP is a commercial roofing specialist for the LA area, certified by the manufacturers whose systems they install.

  • Polyglass and Dura-Last certified installations
  • NDL (No Dollar Limit) warranties in 10, 15 or 20 year terms
  • Minimal disruption planning for occupied buildings

A full replacement is not always necessary. For qualifying low-slope roofs, a manufacturer-backed restoration can extend roof life at a fraction of replacement cost.

  • Coating and restoration systems with material and labor coverage
  • Honest evaluation of whether restoration is right for your roof

After a covered sudden loss, CJP works with your insurance company and with you to get the repair or replacement approved and done right.

  • Damage documentation for your claim
  • Coordination with adjusters through completion

Regular inspections catch small problems before they become expensive ones: clearing debris, checking for leaks, and seasonal upkeep.

  • Professional roof health inspections
  • Seasonal maintenance before the rains

Our story

Not storm chasers.

In their own words: "We're not a profit-driven national roofing corporation, nor are we the transient roofers who show up only after major storms. Our commitment is simple: we're 100% local to LA, a family-owned, family-operated, and family-oriented business."

With a foundation going back over a decade, CJP brings that experience to every project, residential or commercial. The philosophy is quality without compromise: proven processes, passionate professionals, and warranties that are among the strongest in the industry.
