Our Craft

Slow work, on purpose.

Custom woodworking is the opposite of fast furniture. Here is how a project moves through the shop.

From idea to installed

It starts with a tape measure, not a catalog. The shop measures your space, talks through wood species and finishes, and builds to those exact numbers. Then it gets installed like it grew there.

Good joinery outlives trends. A cabinet built right is still square when the paint color is not.

The project steps

1. CallDescribe the space and what you need
2. MeasureSite visit and exact dimensions
3. QuoteWood, finish, and timeline in writing
4. BuildCut, joined, and finished in the shop
5. InstallFitted and finished on site

Typical custom-shop workflow. Your project timeline is confirmed by phone.
