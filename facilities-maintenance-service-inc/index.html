Our story

A veteran-owned shop with a painter's pride.

The owner

Facilities Maintenance Service Inc is owned by James Piper, Sr., known around Florence as Jim. The business is listed in the Veteran Owned Business Directory as a veteran-owned small business, with Mr. Piper identified as a Marine Corps veteran and member since 2015.

How we work

This is a small, owner-run painting business. When you call, you talk to the person who will show up, prep your walls, and roll the paint. That keeps communication simple and the work accountable.
