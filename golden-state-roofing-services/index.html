Why roofs fail in the Valley

Heat, Santa Ana winds and the occasional hard rain are tough on roofs. Most leaks we see start small: a cracked tile, dried-out flashing, a clogged valley. Caught early, they are a repair. Ignored, they become a replacement. That is why we push inspections before storm season, not after the ceiling stains.
