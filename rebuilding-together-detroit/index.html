About

Neighbors rebuilding together

Rebuilding Together Detroit is a home builder organization located at 3239 Croissant St in Dearborn, Michigan, serving the greater Detroit community.

The organization mobilizes volunteers and partners to provide critical home repairs for neighbors in need, helping residents stay safe in the homes they love.
