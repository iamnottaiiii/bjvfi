Who we are

Construction, remodeling and alterations, done locally

Forest Hills Properties is a construction company and contractor based at 6 Forest Hill Ct in Whippany, NJ, serving Morris County homes and businesses. Our listed trade categories include altering and remodeling contracting and general building contracting, and that is where we focus: kitchens, baths, additions, interior reconfigurations and full project management.

Hiring a contractor is about trust. You are inviting someone into your home or your business. Our approach is simple: show up when we say we will, keep you informed, price the work honestly, and finish every detail. That is how a local company earns repeat calls and neighbor-to-neighbor referrals.

Rooted in Whippany

We are based right in town, so scheduling is easy, site visits are quick, and we treat every project like the neighbors will see it, because they will.

Written estimates

Every project starts with a clear written scope and price. If conditions uncover something unexpected, you approve the change before we do it.

Respect for your space

Construction is disruptive enough. We keep work areas tidy, protect finished surfaces, and leave the site clean at the end of each day.

Meet us on your project.

Tell us what you are planning and we will give you an honest read on cost, timeline and options. No pressure, no sales pitch.
