Meet your neighborhood shop

Jerry Day's Auto Services sits at 4436 Vine St in Cincinnati, OH, and has built its name the old-fashioned way: one honest repair at a time.

The short version

Jerry Day's is a locally rooted auto repair shop where the owner is known by name and the pricing is known for being fair. Customers describe the team as down to earth, professional, and quick to find the real problem. Many have trusted the shop with their cars for years.

The shop's reputation rests on a simple idea: only do the work a car truly needs, charge a fair price for it, and treat every driver with the same respect. Neighbors say the lot stays busy, so calling ahead is a smart move.
