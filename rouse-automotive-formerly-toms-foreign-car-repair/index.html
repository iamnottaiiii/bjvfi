Our Story

Two Names, One Standard

The Legacy Name

Drivers across Cincinnati came to know Tom's Foreign Car Repair at 5300 Rapid Run Rd. The name stood for dependable work on everyday cars and imports alike, and public reviews still speak of an honest, fair shop.

The Rouse Standard

Today the shop operates as Rouse Automotive, formerly Tom's Foreign Car Repair. It describes itself as a full service automotive repair shop, and keeps the same street, the same phone line, and the same commitment to straight dealing.

The Neighborhood

Located on Rapid Run Road on Cincinnati's west side, the shop serves local drivers who value a familiar face under the hood. As listed online, the shop is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
