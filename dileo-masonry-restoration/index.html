The Journal

Why old masonry fails, and how we bring it back.

A short field guide from the crew that spends its days on scaffolds and ladders across Delaware County.

Chapter 01 // Diagnosis

Every wall tells you what is wrong, if you read it.

Spalling faces, crumbling joints, white efflorescence blooming across a chimney. These are not cosmetic problems. They are a masonry wall asking for help in the only language it has.

We start every restoration with a close read of the wall: mortar hardness, brick condition, water paths, past repairs. Only then do we write the prescription.

"Match the mortar, respect the brick, and the repair outlives us all."
Our services
Chapter 02 // Repointing

Tuckpointing is surgery, not caulk.

Grinding out failed joints and packing in new mortar looks simple from the sidewalk. Up close it is slow, dusty, exacting work, and the mortar has to be softer than the brick or the brick loses.

We mix for the era of the building, tool the joints to match the original profile, and clean as we go. Done right, you cannot find where the old wall ends and our work begins.

Services

The restoration ledger.

Six services, each one a craft. Call (215) 594-3921 for an on-site assessment and a straight price.

01

Tuckpointing & Repointing

Deteriorated mortar joints are ground out to sound depth and repacked with mortar matched to the original in strength, color and texture. The single most effective repair for an aging brick wall.

 → 02

Brick Repair & Replacement

Spalled, cracked or missing bricks are cut out and replaced with units matched for size, color and character, then pointed to disappear into the surrounding work.

 → 03

Chimney Restoration

Chimneys take the worst weather a house sees. We rebuild crowns and caps, repoint stacks, replace flue liners where needed and make the whole assembly watertight again.

 → 04

Stone Restoration

Fieldstone and cut-stone foundations, walls and facades cleaned, repointed and repaired with mortars formulated for stone, not modern brick.

 → 05

Masonry Waterproofing

Breathable sealers and water repellents that keep rain out while letting trapped moisture escape, plus flashing and drainage corrections at the real sources of leaks.

 → 06

Historic Masonry Restoration

Older and historic buildings get lime-based mortars and period-correct techniques, so the repair honors the original construction instead of fighting it.

 →

Service descriptions reflect standard masonry restoration trade practice. Pricing varies by scope: every job starts with a free on-site assessment.

Our story

We learned on old brick, and we stayed there.

New construction rewards speed. Restoration rewards patience. We chose patience. DiLeo Masonry Restoration exists for the houses and buildings that deserve a second century: the brick rowhomes, the stone foundations, the chimneys that have outlasted three roofs.

Our work starts with looking, not selling. We read the wall, tell you what is actually wrong, and quote the repair it needs, not the replacement that pays better. When a wall can be saved, we save it. When it cannot, we rebuild it the way it was built.

"The best restoration is the one nobody notices."

Based in Darby and working across Delaware County, we keep our jobs local so we can stand behind them in person.
