Our story

A neighborhood salon that respects your time

IT Nails Salon sits at 1564 Jefferson Road in Rochester, an easy stop for the neighborhoods around Henrietta and Brighton. It was built for real life: walk-ins welcome, appointments that run on time, and technicians who remember your name and your shape.

The team blends speed with craft. Guests describe quick, efficient service for busy schedules alongside careful detail work: cuticle attention, clean shaping and designs that hold up. It is the rare salon where you can squeeze in a fill on a lunch break and still leave with nails you want to photograph.

Fair prices keep it a regular stop rather than a special occasion. Come see why so many reviewers call it their go-to.
