What a visit looks like

Every job at our shop follows the same checklist, from the moment you hand over the keys to the moment you drive away.

  • Drop off and listen

    Tell us what you hear, feel, or smell. We write it down on the ticket, word for word, and confirm the plan before any wrench turns.

  • Inspect and diagnose

    We check the complaint plus the basics around it, so a simple fix does not hide a bigger problem.

  • Call you first

    You approve the work and the cost before we start. No surprises on the bill, ever.

  • Fix it and road test

    Repairs are completed, then the car gets a road test to confirm the original complaint is gone.

  • Hand back with notes

    You get plain talk on what was done and what to watch for next. Call to confirm

About the shop

Marks Complete Auto Repair is an independent auto repair shop at 1322 Hicks Blvd in Fairfield, Ohio. Business directory records place the shop at this address since 2010.

Rooted in Fairfield

Same address on Hicks Blvd for over a decade, serving drivers across Fairfield and the surrounding area.

Community backed

In 2017, after a burglary wiped out about $20,000 in shop tools, neighbors organized a public fundraiser breakfast to help the owner get back on his feet. The tools were how he provided for his family, and the town showed up.

Independent by choice

No franchise scripts here. General automotive repair, one car at a time, with the owner on the shop floor.
