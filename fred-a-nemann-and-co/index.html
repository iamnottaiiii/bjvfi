Company

A Cincinnati contractor with deep roots

Fred A. Nemann Company has operated as a heavy highway contracting business in the Cincinnati area since 1960, working on public infrastructure across the region.

What public records show

The company’s principal Fred (Butch) A. Nemann, Jr. led the heavy highway contracting operation from 1960 until his passing in 2020. The firm appears in City of Cincinnati purchasing records as a bidder and contractor for water main repair and maintenance work for Greater Cincinnati Water Works, operating out of its yard at 6480 Bender Road.

The business is listed under contractor, plumber, and heating/ventilating/air conditioning categories, and holds long-standing FMCSA operating authority for its equipment fleet.

1960

1993

FMCSA operating authority granted for the company’s trucking and equipment fleet, active since.

2007-08

The company constructs a sewer pump station for the Metropolitan Sewer District of Greater Cincinnati.

Operating from 6480 Bender Rd, Cincinnati, performing water main and utility work for public agencies.
