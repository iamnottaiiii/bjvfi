Our story

Ledger entry: the company
A

Who we are

Jast Construction & Management is a Pasadena-based construction company working out of 177 E Colorado Blvd. The firm's own public posts describe a crew that has been setting concrete since 1994, specializing in commercial construction and large-scale builds.

B

How we work

Every project opens as a work order with a written scope and closes with a signed-off ledger. Foundations get the same documentation discipline as full commercial builds, because that is where the company's reputation was poured.

C

What we believe

Quality is not a line item, it is the whole job. As the firm's own posts put it: no difference between getting a job done and getting a job done well.
