Our story

Six decades of the golden touch.

Golden Touch Cleaners has been part of the Woodbridge landscape since 1965. That is generations of first communion suits, wedding dresses, job interview outfits and everyday work shirts, each one treated like it matters, because to its owner it does.

The shop stays deliberately small. With a tight team and a steady hand, every garment passes under the same careful eyes. The focus has always been simple: fair pricing, quality work and the kind of personal service that turns first time customers into regulars.

Walk in with a stained jacket or a pair of trousers that need taking in, and you will talk to someone who knows the fabric, knows the fix, and will give you an honest answer about what is possible.

"A personal touch and fair pricing, with a long standing commitment to the community."
