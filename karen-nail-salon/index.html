Our Story

A Flatbush Ave favorite

Karen Nail Salon is a neighborhood nail salon at 2078 Flatbush Ave in Brooklyn, serving Marine Park and the surrounding blocks with manicures, pedicures, gel polish and nail art.

Guests describe the salon as affordable and unpretentious, with quick and efficient service and technicians who take the time to get custom designs right. It is the kind of place regulars return to week after week: not fancy, but dependable, friendly, and easy to get to.

Services include classic and Russian manicures, spa pedicures, nail art, and polish finishes, with walk-ins welcome during open hours.
