Services

Everything your bathroom needs, nothing it doesn't

Each project is designed around your space, your budget, and how you live. Free consultations on every job.

01

Walk-In Showers

Open, airy showers with frameless glass, rainfall or handheld fixtures, built-in niches, and low or curbless entries for easy access.

  • Frameless glass
  • Curbless entries
  • Rainfall fixtures
  • Built-in niches

Shower remodels are our most requested project. Most take one to two weeks from demo to first shower, depending on tile and fixture choices.

02

Tub-to-Shower Conversions

Trade a tub you never use for a shower you will love. We handle plumbing, waterproofing, tile, and glass as one coordinated project.

  • Plumbing rework
  • Waterproofing
  • Custom tile
  • Glass doors

A conversion typically reclaims several square feet of visual space and makes the room feel noticeably larger.

03

Full Bathroom Remodels

The complete transformation: layout, tile, vanity, toilet, lighting, and finishes, designed together and built start to finish by one team.

  • Layout redesign
  • Floor and wall tile
  • Vanities
  • Lighting

Full remodels begin with a design conversation in your home, so the plan reflects how you actually use the room.

04

Tile, Vanities & Fixtures

Targeted upgrades with outsized impact: new floor or shower tile, a vanity that fits the room, modern faucets, mirrors, and lighting.

  • Shower tile
  • Floor tile
  • Vanity installs
  • Faucets and mirrors

A focused refresh can be the right call when the layout already works and the finishes are simply tired.

05

Accessible & Safe Bathrooms

Comfort and safety without a clinical look: grab bars that match your fixtures, walk-in tubs, comfort-height toilets, and slip-resistant flooring.

  • Grab bars
  • Walk-in tubs
  • Comfort-height toilets
  • Non-slip tile

Thoughtful for aging in place or recovering from an injury, and designed to look like it was always meant to be there.

Our story

Built on calm, from the first visit to the final wipe-down

Photo soon · Handset tile detail

Serenity Bathroom Remodeling Cincinnati started with a simple observation: bathroom remodels are stressful mostly because of how they are run, not what they involve. Dust everywhere, vague timelines, crews you never meet twice.

So we run ours differently. One project lead from the first consultation to the final walkthrough. A written plan with real dates. Floors protected, dust controlled, and a job site left tidy every single day.

"Anyone can install tile. We take pride in how the whole experience feels."

We are based in Cincinnati and serve homeowners across the metro area, from compact hall baths to generous primary suites. Whether it is a one-week shower conversion or a full transformation, the goal is the same: a bathroom that feels like a deep breath.
