Our story

A neighborhood salon on 60th Street

Lady Nail Salon Inc serves the Dyker Heights neighborhood of Brooklyn from its storefront at 1973 60th St. It is the kind of salon regulars find once and keep coming back to: straightforward service, steady hands, and prices that make regular nail care easy.

The team focuses on the classics. Manicures that look clean and last, pedicures that leave you feeling reset, and enhancements for the moments that call for something extra. Everything is done in house, one client at a time.

The classics, done well, one client at a time.

What to expect

Walk in or call ahead. Pick your service and your color. Relax while the work gets done, and leave with nails ready for the week.

Good to know

The salon is closed on Saturdays and opens at 9:30 AM every other day. Sunday hours match weekdays, so weekend nail care is easy.
