The Royal Charter

About Kingdom Cleaners LLC

A Cincinnati house cleaning company built on thoroughness, trust, and care.

Kingdom Cleaners LLC serves Cincinnati, Ohio from its home at 4466 Andreas Ave. The company name says it all: every home is treated like a castle, and every client like royalty.

Clients describe a professional, friendly team that listens. Before a deep clean or move-out clean, the team asks about your home, its square footage, and your problem areas, then gives a realistic quote and gets to work. Visits are thorough enough that homes look and smell brand new, and flexible enough to work around busy families and high-energy dogs alike.

Many clients start with a single deep clean and stay for recurring visits, comforted by familiar faces and consistently excellent results. That is the knightly standard: show up, listen, and leave every castle better than you found it.
