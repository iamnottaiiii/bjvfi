How a project runs

Prep. Pour. Protect.

STEP 01

Walkthrough & spec

We walk the floor with you, test the slab, and spec the right system for the traffic, chemicals, and abuse it will see.

STEP 02

Prep the slab right

Diamond grinding or shot blasting to open the concrete, plus moisture mitigation where the slab needs it. Coatings fail without prep; ours do not.

STEP 03

Install & cure

Epoxy, urethane, polish, or stain goes down to spec, with line work, joint filling, and sealing to finish. Then we hand you a floor that outlasts the lease.

The company

J. Wesselman Specialty Flooring, LLC

Based in Covington, Kentucky, J. Wesselman Specialty Flooring is a specialty contractor serving commercial and industrial clients across Northern Kentucky and the Greater Cincinnati area. The company has been in the flooring trade since 2010.

The scope is deliberately narrow: epoxy and urethane floor systems, concrete polishing, staining and sealing, surface preparation, and the line work and joint filling that finish a facility floor. It is the kind of work general contractors hand off to people who do it every day.

On the commercial construction side, the company works within contractor networks (including Procore) on projects across the region, handling flooring and coating scopes for larger builds.

Timeline

Fifteen-plus years on the slab.

2010 · FOUNDED

The company opens its doors

Founded in Northern Kentucky in 2010 (per company directory records), offering stripping and waxing, epoxy and urethane installation, and wood floor refinishing. The requested brand colors from day one: hunter green, orange, and white.

2010s · SCOPE GROWS

Into commercial and industrial systems

The work expands into full commercial and industrial epoxy floor systems, moisture mitigation, self-leveling underlayments, and concrete surface preparation including diamond grinding and shot blasting.

2020s · CONTRACTOR NETWORKS

Working commercial projects region-wide

The company joins contractor networks such as Procore, taking on flooring, coating, and finish scopes for commercial construction projects across the region.
