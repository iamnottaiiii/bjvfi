Our story

A Barbershop the Neighborhood Grew Up In

Lawrence Unisex has been cutting hair on Rockaway Turnpike in Cedarhurst for years, serving regulars who came in as kids and now bring their own. The name says it all: a chair for everyone, with barbering on one side and full hair styling on the other.

Reviewers call out the good vibe in the shop, the steady hands behind the chair, and the kind of loyalty you only earn one haircut at a time.

  • ✓Unisex shop. Mens cuts, shaves, beard work and hair styling for all.
  • ✓Open 7 days. Monday through Sunday, 8 AM to 6 PM.
  • ✓Card and mobile payments accepted, wheelchair accessible throughout.
