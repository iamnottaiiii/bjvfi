About the Establishment

Worthington Barber Shop occupies 24 W New England Ave in the heart of Worthington, Ohio, steps from the village green. It is a traditional, walk-in barbershop in the classic mold: multiple chairs, skilled barbers, and an atmosphere reviewers describe as lively, friendly, and community driven.

Customers praise the consistent quality, the affordable prices, and barbers who know their craft. It is the rare shop that feels exactly like a barbershop should: unpretentious, welcoming, and excellent at the fundamentals.
