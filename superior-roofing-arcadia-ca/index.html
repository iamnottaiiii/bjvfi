Repairs Most called for

Most roof calls are repairs, not replacements. A leak caught early is a patch; a leak ignored is a ceiling, drywall, and paint job.

  • Active leak repairs and water-intrusion tracing
  • Missing, cracked, or wind-lifted shingles and tiles
  • Flashing, vent, and chimney-adjacent resealing
  • Santa Ana wind and storm damage response

Replacement Big projects

When repairs stop making sense, a full replacement resets the clock. Expect a tear-off, underlayment, new material, and a thorough cleanup.

  • Full tear-off and re-roof
  • Tile and architectural shingle systems
  • Underlayment and ventilation addressed with the job

About

The roofer on Huntington Drive

Superior Roofing Arcadia CA is a roofing contractor based at 440 Huntington Dr #204 in Arcadia, serving homes across Arcadia and the surrounding San Gabriel Valley.

Roofing is a trust business: you cannot see most of the work from the ground, so you hire the crew that explains what they found, fixes what is actually broken, and leaves the place clean. That is the whole pitch.

Arcadia roofs take a particular beating: long hot summers, Santa Ana wind events that lift shingles and tiles, and winter rains that find every weak seam. A local roofer who has seen all three seasons of damage is worth more than a low bid from across the county.
