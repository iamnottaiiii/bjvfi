About Us

A working contractor in Orlando, FL, focused on doing the job right and treating your home like it matters.

Who we are

EM Contractors Corp is a general contracting company based in Orlando, Florida. We specialize in the work homeowners ask for most: kitchen and bath remodeling, concrete flatwork, and the repairs and updates that keep a home in good shape.

We are a hands-on crew, not a call center. When you call, you talk to the people who will actually do the work.

How we work

Every project follows the same straightforward process: we look at the job in person, give you a written scope and price, show up when we say we will, and walk the finished work with you before we call it done.

If something is not right, we fix it. That is the whole policy.
