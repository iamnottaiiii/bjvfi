Our story

Built on the jobsite

Dragon Contractors LLC started with a simple observation: most remodeling headaches come from coordinating too many people. The plumber does not talk to the tile guy, the painter shows up late, and the homeowner becomes the project manager.

Dragon took the other path. One crew, trained across the trades that matter in a remodel, from drywall and paint to plumbing and electrical. The result is fewer handoffs, fewer surprises, and a job that moves.

Their public HireRush listing puts it plainly: the crew has deep experience in every service they offer, and the business is licensed and insured. The contact on that listing is Larry, who fields calls and estimates himself.

Based on Cincinnati's west side, Dragon serves homeowners, landlords, and property managers across the area, from single-room refreshes to full apartment turns.

What we believe

Show up ready. Tools loaded, plan in hand, crew on time.

Do every trade right. A remodel is only as good as its weakest trade, so there is no weak trade on our crew.

Price it straight. Free estimates and honest numbers, before the work starts.

Finish clean. Walkthrough with you, not just a wave from the driveway.
