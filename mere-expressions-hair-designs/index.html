Our story

Three decades of great hair

Mere Expressions Hair Designs was started in 1992 by Lisa and Dee. Through many life changes over the years, they have kept the same pride in their work and in their salon, and the business is still going strong.

Owners, not employees

When you sit in our chair, you are with the people who built this salon. Lisa and Dee run the business themselves, which means every appointment gets an owner's attention to detail.
