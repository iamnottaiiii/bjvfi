About

A downtown salon built on trust

Profile

Small salon, devoted regulars

The Concept @ 235 operates from 235 East Water Street in downtown Syracuse as a licensed New York appearance enhancement business. The salon describes itself simply: salon services and nail care for men and women, featuring Aveda products.

What sets it apart, according to guests, is consistency. Clients write about going for years, about a stylist who remembers exactly how their hair behaves, and about an atmosphere that is professional without being stiff.

"Master stylist, delightful setting, amazing Aveda. Parking never a problem."

Whether it is a quick trim or a full restyle, the promise is the same: honest advice, careful work, and hair that is easy to live with.

Good to know

Cash and credit cards accepted.
Easy downtown parking.
Closed Sunday and Monday.
