Our Story

Chicago's First Infused Bakery

What began as a small CBD coffee shop in Chicago rolled into a TikTok-famous, all-inclusive joint, and Homewood got its own slice of the vibe.

Healing

Tranquility in Every Treat

Relaxation and mental repose are the keys to healing. Every treat is crafted to create tranquility and all-around well-being.

Opportunity

Lifting Up Our Neighbors

We lift up our team and our neighbors, from the candy store down the street to local collaborators across Homewood.

Mindfulness

Made with Intention

We ebb and flow with intention. Every bake and every brew is made mindfully, with consistency you can count on.

The Homewood Hangout

Our Dixie Hwy spot is built for lingering: good music, plenty of relaxing areas, and a crew that is patient, helpful and knowledgeable about every item on the menu. Come for a latte, stay for bingo night, leave with your new favorite cookie. Vegan and vegetarian options are available, just ask.
