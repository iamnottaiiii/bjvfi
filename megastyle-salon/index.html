Our story

A cozy corner of Seminary Hill since 2011

Megastyle Salon has been part of the East Building on Kenmore Ave for over a decade, building a loyal following one great haircut at a time.

Rooted in the neighborhood

Established in 2011 and tucked into Seminary Towers, Megastyle is the kind of salon where neighbors become regulars and regulars become friends.

Kindness is the house style

Reviewers keep coming back to the same words: cozy, relaxed, kind. Guests love the service and especially the gentleness of the people who serve them.

Stylists worth asking for

From precise haircuts that earn compliments to eyebrow threading guests will not trust to anyone else, the team has earned its reputation review by review.
