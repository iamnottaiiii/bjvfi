About

The shop on Skokorat Street

Tanner's Plumbing & Heating is a local plumbing and heating business in Beacon Falls, Connecticut.

Based at 699 Skokorat St, the shop serves Beacon Falls and neighboring towns across the Naugatuck Valley. As an independent local business, the approach is personal: the person who answers the phone is close to the person who shows up with the wrench.

The work covers both sides of home comfort. Plumbing repairs, water heaters, drains, and water softeners on one side; boilers and heating service on the other. One call, one crew, one standard of care.
