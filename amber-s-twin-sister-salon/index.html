Our story

A twin act on Sycamore Street

Amber's Twin Sister Salon is a small-town hair salon in New Richmond, Ohio, where public reviews describe twin stylists, friendly conversation, and a homey feel.

Public reviews mention "the twins" by name, and guests describe quick, skilled work on cuts and color with plenty of friendly conversation along the way. The salon's own public description keeps it simple: hair salon, colors, perms, haircut.
