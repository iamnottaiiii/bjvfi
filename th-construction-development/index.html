Our story

A builder's builder,
based in Reseda

TH Construction & Development is a general contractor working out of Canby Ave in Reseda, building across the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles.

Development is in the name for a reason

We are not a handyman service and we are not a design studio that subs out the build. TH Construction & Development is a general contractor set up to take a project from an idea to a finished, inspected building.

That means we think in structures, not just surfaces. When a wall needs to move, we know what is holding the house up. When a room needs to be added, we know what the city needs to see on paper before a single footing is poured.

Our home base is 6251 Canby Ave in Reseda, right in the middle of the Valley we build in. Most of our work is residential: new construction, additions, ADUs, and full remodels, with commercial build outs for local businesses.

Every job gets the same deal: a written scope, a written estimate, permits where the work requires them, and a walkthrough before we call it done.

"Build it like you have to live with it."

The way we run every job

Written everything

Scope, price, and schedule in writing before work starts. If it is not on paper, it is not part of the job.

Permitted work

When the work triggers permits, we pull them. Inspections passed and documented, not skipped and hoped for.

One point of contact

You talk to us, we handle the trades. No playing phone tag with five subcontractors.
