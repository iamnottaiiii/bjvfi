Roof repair

Targeted fixes that stop leaks and extend the life of your current roof.

  • Leak detection · Trace stains and drips to the true entry point.
  • Shingle and tile replacement · Match and swap damaged shingles or tiles.
  • Flashing repair · Reseal and replace flashing at chimneys, vents, and valleys.
  • Emergency patching · Fast temporary seals to stop active leaks.
  • Vent and pipe boot repair · Fix cracked boots, the most common leak source.

Our story

Straight-line roofing, no games.

Placeholder

Pro Line Roofing Systems was built on a simple frustration: too many roofers sell what is easy instead of what is right. The crew works out of North Hollywood with a systems mindset, looking at decking, underlayment, ventilation, and drainage as one connected job.

That means honest repair-or-replace advice, photo documentation of what was found, and a crew that cleans up like it was their own driveway. Homeowners across the San Fernando Valley call (818) 873-4337 for everything from a single lifted shingle to a full tear-off.

The rule on every job: do it like the next storm is already on the radar. Because in the Valley, it always is.
