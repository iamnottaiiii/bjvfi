Our story

About Kaybran Auto Diagnostic Inc

Kaybran Auto Diagnostic Inc opened on 51st Avenue to give Woodside drivers a repair shop that starts with the facts, not the guesswork.

Modern cars talk in codes, and the shop is built to listen: scanners, test equipment, and technicians who verify a fault before quoting a fix.

Alongside diagnostics, the shop is a licensed New York State inspection station, so the same careful eye that finds a problem also keeps your car legal on the road.
