Our story

Rooted in Pasadena

Leones Tree Care and Landscaping Inc is a Pasadena tree care and landscaping company located at 1584 El Sereno Ave in the 91103 area. We are a local crew, not a distant chain, which means the people who answer your call are the people who show up at your yard.

Trees are the biggest investment in most Pasadena yards. Mature canopies shade homes, cool streets, and shape the whole feel of a property. Our work starts from a simple idea: care for the tree first, and the yard follows.

Whether it is a single overgrown tree, a yard that needs regular attention, or a full landscape refresh, we bring the same approach to every job: show up on time, do the work carefully, and leave the place cleaner than we found it.
