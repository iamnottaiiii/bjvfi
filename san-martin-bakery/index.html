Our story

A Neighborhood Panaderia on 149th Street

San Martin Bakery is the kind of place every neighborhood needs: a panaderia where the bread is fresh, the prices are fair, and somebody greets you like they have known you for years.

Listed as both a panaderia and pasteleria, the bakery covers the full spread, from morning conchas and donuts to tres leches and custom celebration cakes. Reviewers describe the smell of fresh bread drifting out the door, and a case full of pan dulce that keeps regulars coming back, some say hundreds of times.

What stands out most in the reviews is not just the food. It is the warmth. Customers talk about welcoming service, generous portions of kindness, and a shop that feels like part of the family rhythm of East Chicago.
