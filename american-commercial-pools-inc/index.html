Our story

Family owned, referral driven

American Commercial Pools, Inc. is a family owned and operated business in Sylmar, California. We believe customer satisfaction is the key to our success. In fact, most of our work comes from referrals.

We have the ability and desire to help customers with every aspect of pool design and construction, including remodeling and upgrading existing pools and spas. Our staff plan, manage, and complete the entire project. We use the best equipment, the best materials, and our own highly trained crews for each job.

Most importantly, we stay with you long after the job is completed. Each pool also comes with a full warranty to ensure many years of enjoyment in your swimming pool paradise.
