General dental care

Everyday dentistry for adults and families. Call the office to ask which services are currently offered.

  1. 01

    Exams and cleanings

    Regular visits keep small problems small. Dr. Exposito examines teeth and gums and discusses findings with you directly.

  2. 02

    Diagnosis and treatment planning

    From a chipped tooth to ongoing sensitivity, care begins with a clear diagnosis and a plan that fits your priorities.

  3. 03

    Restorative dentistry

    Fillings, crowns, and other repairs that restore comfort and chewing function. Confirm availability with the office.

  4. 04

    Preventive guidance

    Practical advice on home care and habits, so you can protect your smile between visits.

Good to know: this list describes general dental care. Services and availability can change, so please call (410) 768-8220 to ask what is offered before your visit.
