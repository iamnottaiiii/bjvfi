Our story

Built for the connected home

California IOT started in Chatsworth in 2020 with a simple observation: homes were getting smarter, but electricians and tech installers rarely talked to each other. David Betesh built a company that does both, so a remodel gets its panels, its low voltage, and its smart systems from one crew.

From the shop at 10758 Cozycroft Ave, the team handles everything from full rewires to camera systems, and reviewers keep coming back to the same words: fast, easy to communicate with, clean, and on time.
