Foundation repair

The core of the business. Foundation repair addresses the structural problems that threaten a home: cracks, settlement, bowing walls, and shifting.

  • Inspection of cracks, bowing, and settlement
  • Stabilization and structural repair options
  • Written plan before any work begins

Sealing and waterproofing

Water is the most common enemy of a foundation. Sealing keeps it out of basements and crawl spaces.

  • Interior and exterior sealing approaches
  • Drainage and water management
  • Damp and musty basement solutions

Crawl space services

Crawl spaces are out of sight, but moisture there causes rot, mold, and sagging floors above.

  • Moisture and drainage assessment
  • Encapsulation and vapor barrier options
  • Structural support for sagging floors
