Process

What to expect

A plain-language walkthrough of how an ADU project typically unfolds with a design-build contractor. Your project may vary.

1

First conversation

Talk through your goals: rental income, family space, a home office. Ask anything; reviewers say the team takes time with questions.

2

Site visit

Someone walks the property with you, looks at access, setbacks and utilities, and talks through options for the lot.

3

Design and estimate

A design takes shape around your budget. You review drawings and a scope of work before anything is signed.

4

Permits

Plans go to the city. Timelines depend on the jurisdiction, and the team handles the back-and-forth.

5

Construction

Framing, systems, finishes. Reviewers mention regular updates on how the build is going.

6

Walkthrough

A final pass through the finished unit together, checking details before handoff.

This is a typical flow, not a promise of how your project will run. Confirm each step when you call.
