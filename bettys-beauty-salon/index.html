About

A Baltimore institution
since 1987

Betty's Beauty Salon has been part of the Pimlico Road neighborhood for decades. Reviewers say it is the only place they have gone since 1987, and some have been clients for over 30 years.

The salon is known for a cozy, clean, well-kept space and a staff that listens first. One reviewer calls a stylist a magician with scissors. Another simply says: the best cosmetologist.

Whether you are a first-timer or a regular of twenty years, you will be welcomed the same warm way.
