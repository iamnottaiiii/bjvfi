Our Story

The story behind the suds

A locally owned dog wash with one simple promise: no cages, ever.

Dirty Hairy's Self and Full Service Dog Wash is a locally owned dog wash in Fort Thomas, Kentucky, run by owner Lisa Kelly. The shop got its start on North Fort Thomas Avenue and now welcomes pups at 1118 S Ft Thomas Ave, in a renovated building it shares with Pet Wants, the fresh pet food shop Kelly co-founded.

What makes wash day different here is the house rule: dogs are never caged. Every visit means one-on-one attention, potty breaks, and play time, which is why nervous pups and rescue dogs tend to do so well here. Reviewers regularly mention that their dogs come home happy, soft, and smelling great for weeks.

Prefer to wash your own dog? The self-serve stations were built for it: ergonomic raised tubs with ramps and doors, several types of shampoo, brushes, towels, and a specialty dryer, all for $15 no matter the size of the dog. Want the full treatment instead? The crew offers full-service washes and grooming by appointment, including nail trims and ear cleaning.
