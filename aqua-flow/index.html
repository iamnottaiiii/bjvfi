How we help

From soaked to solid, step by step

Water damage moves fast, and so do we. Here is the restoration process, start to finish.

Step 01

Assessment

We find the source, map the moisture with meters and imaging, and lay out a plan before a single pump starts.

Step 02

Water extraction

Standing water comes out fast with professional pumps and extraction equipment, limiting further damage.

Step 03

Drying and dehumidification

Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers pull hidden moisture from walls, floors, and framing.

Step 04

Cleaning and sanitizing

Affected areas are cleaned, disinfected, and deodorized to stop mold and remove contaminants.

Step 05

Mold remediation

If mold testing finds growth, we contain and remediate it with industry-standard practices.

Step 06

Repair and rebuild

Damaged drywall, flooring, and finishes are repaired or replaced, restoring the space.

Step 07

Fire and smoke repair

Soot, smoke odor, and char damage are addressed alongside any water damage from firefighting.

Step 08

Final walkthrough

We verify everything is dry, clean, and documented, then hand your space back to you.

Water emergency? Do this first

Before we arrive, these steps can limit the damage:

  • Stop the source if you can do it safely, like shutting the main water valve
  • Turn off power to wet areas if outlets or wiring are affected
  • Move valuables, rugs, and furniture out of the water
  • Take photos for your records, then call us
