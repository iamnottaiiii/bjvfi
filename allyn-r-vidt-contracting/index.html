How we work

We visit your home or lot, listen to what you want, and look at what the structure will allow. You get honest feedback, including when an idea is not worth the money.

02

About

Rooted in Shaler Township

Allyn R Vidt Contracting is a general contractor based at 105 Hartle Road in Glenshaw, in the heart of Shaler Township. The business has deep local ties: the Vidt name is connected to decades of hands-on trade work in the area, including seamless flooring and epoxy work alongside home building and contracting.

That background shows in the work. A contractor who started in the trades understands what is behind the walls, not just what is on the surface, which is exactly what you want when a remodel opens up a 1920s farmhouse or a 1960s split-level, the housing stock that fills these neighborhoods.

The approach is simple and old-fashioned: show up, do what you said you would do, and stand behind it. One point of contact from estimate to final walkthrough, written scopes, and a job site kept in order while the work is underway.
