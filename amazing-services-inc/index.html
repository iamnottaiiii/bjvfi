About Amazing Services Inc.

A local Revere crew focused on one thing: the outside of your building, kept clean and sharp.

Half ladder, all business

Customers joke that our technicians are half ladder, and honestly, the boots on your roof and the squeegees on your glass are where we spend most days. Amazing Services Inc. works across Revere and nearby North Shore towns, handling residential and commercial jobs with the same care.

The crew is fully insured, which matters when someone is working at height on your property. Our core services, window cleaning, gutter cleaning, custom gutter capping and power washing, cover nearly everything the weather does to the outside of a building.

Longtime clients keep coming back for the finish: streak free glass, flowing gutters and concrete that looks new again.
