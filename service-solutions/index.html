Our story

The straightforward
way to do HVAC

Service Solutions is an air conditioning contractor based on W Victory Blvd in Burbank.

The name is the whole business plan: solutions, not sales pitches. Too many HVAC calls end with a tech pushing a new system for a problem a repair would fix. We built this company to do it the other way, diagnose first, explain clearly, and only recommend replacement when the numbers genuinely favor it.

We serve Burbank and the surrounding San Fernando Valley, Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM. When you call (310) 351-3446, you reach someone local who can actually help, not a phone tree.
