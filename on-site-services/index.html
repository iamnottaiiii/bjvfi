Why On Site Services

A paving crew that shows up ready

Good paving is mostly preparation. We grade the base, fix drainage problems, and compact before a single load of asphalt or concrete is placed. That is how a driveway or lot stays smooth year after year.

  • Residential driveways and walkways
  • Parking lots and commercial surfaces
  • Concrete installation and replacement
  • Repairs, resurfacing, and ongoing maintenance

What we do

Paving and concrete services

On Site Services handles new installs, replacements, repairs, and maintenance for asphalt and concrete surfaces around Jonesboro, GA. Every job starts with a site visit and a written quote. Typical projects are below. Call to discuss yours.

Asphalt PavingCore service

New asphalt driveways, parking areas, and private roads built on a properly prepared and compacted base.

Concrete InstallationCore service

Concrete driveways, walkways, pads, and slabs, formed and finished for strength and a clean look.

Repairs and ResurfacingPopular

Crack repair, patching, and overlays that extend the life of worn asphalt and concrete surfaces.

SealcoatingMaintenance

Protective sealcoating that shields asphalt from sun, water, and wear, keeping it dark and flexible.

Commercial PavingBusinesses

Parking lots and commercial surfaces scheduled to keep your business running with minimal disruption.
