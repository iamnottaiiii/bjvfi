The Vogt Family Story

A small, family-run Cincinnati garage where your car is treated like it belongs to one of their own.

Vogt Auto Inc is a family-operated auto repair and restoration shop on Cleneay Avenue in Cincinnati. Public business records trace the shop back to 1987, when it was incorporated in Ohio, and it remains a small team of just a few people to this day.

What the shop is known for goes beyond routine repairs: full mechanical and body restoration for all models, with a deep specialty in early Volkswagens. Customers describe being led through every step of an engine rebuild, and treated like part of the family while their vehicle is in the shop.

The shop describes itself as serving both local drivers and clients from further afield, people who bring cherished vehicles to Cincinnati specifically for this kind of careful, old-school work.

History above is drawn from public business records and the shop's own public description. Details beyond that are part of the family's own story, best heard over the counter.
