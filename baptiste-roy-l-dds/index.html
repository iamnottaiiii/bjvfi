Our services

General dentistry, done well

Everyday dental care for adults and families, from prevention to restoration. Choose a category to see what is offered.

Exams and Cleanings

Routine checkups and professional cleanings to keep teeth and gums healthy year round.

Oral Health Screenings

Thorough exams that check for decay, gum disease, and other issues early, when they are easiest to treat.

Gum Care

Evaluation and care for gum health, including guidance on home hygiene that works for your schedule.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Natural-looking fillings that repair decayed teeth and blend in with your smile.

Crowns, Bridges and Dentures

Restorations that rebuild damaged teeth and replace missing ones, full or partial.

Extractions and Root Canals

Relief for damaged or infected teeth, including extractions and root canal therapy when a tooth can be saved.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening to brighten stained or discolored teeth for a fresher smile.

Cosmetic Restoration

Repair for chipped, cracked or discolored teeth with bonding and other cosmetic techniques.

Smile Consultations

Talk through your goals and get a clear, no-pressure plan for the smile you want.

Comfort-First Visits

Patients mention feeling comfortable during visits. Ask about options if you feel anxious about dental work.
