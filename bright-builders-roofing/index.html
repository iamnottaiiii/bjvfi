Roofing for Valley weather

Encino roofs take heat, Santa Ana winds, and the occasional hard rain. We build and repair roofs to handle all three, with materials suited to Southern California homes.

1

Inspect

We walk the roof, check flashing, vents, and valleys, and photograph what we find so you see exactly what we see.

2

Explain

You get plain-language options: what must be fixed now, what can wait, and what each choice costs. No pressure.

3

Build

Tear-off, underlayment, flashing, and new roofing installed in clean, staged layers with daily cleanup.

4

Stand behind it

Final walkthrough with you on the details, plus straightforward answers if anything ever looks off. Call to ask about current workmanship terms.
