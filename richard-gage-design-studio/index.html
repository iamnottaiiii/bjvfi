About the Studio

The Richard Gage Design Studio was established to offer a wide variety of art services to Hazel Park and the community at large, including fabricating difficult or unique decorative architectural elements and sculpture with hand craftsmanship using contemporary and historic techniques.

The studio has been in business since the late 1990s and works with architects, municipalities, developers, planners, designers, and homeowners. Past work has included historical repair and replacement of the copper roof on the Rose Garden Tea House at the Henry Ford Estate in Dearborn, along with ironwork in the main house.

Over the years the studio has also supported other artists and builders by offering access to larger equipment, space, and expertise.
