Services

From the base up,
done right

Typical concrete work we take on around Lemoyne and the West Shore. Every job starts with site prep and a clear estimate.

01Driveways+

New concrete driveways and full replacements. Includes: excavation, compacted stone base, proper thickness and reinforcement, control joints, and grading that moves water away from the house.

02Patios and slabs+

Backyard patios, shed pads, hot tub pads, and utility slabs. Includes: layout, base prep, forming, and a broom or smooth finish to suit the use.

03Sidewalks and walkways+

Front walks, side paths, and curb-adjacent sidewalks. Includes: removal of failed sections, new base, and clean tooled joints for a safe walking surface.

04Garage and basement floors+

Interior flatwork finished smooth and level. Includes: surface prep, proper mix for interior use, and sealing options to resist oil and road salt.

05Steps, porches, and entries+

Entry steps, stoops, and porch caps repaired or rebuilt. Includes: solid footings, code-minded rise and run, and finishes that shed water.

06Repairs and resurfacing+

Crack repair, spall patching, and overlays for tired but sound slabs. Good for: extending the life of existing concrete without the cost of a full replacement.

About

Local concrete,
poured to last

Pennsy Concrete is a concrete contractor working out of Lemoyne, Pennsylvania, serving homes and small businesses across the West Shore.

How we work

1. Site visit. We look at the ground, the drainage, and what is failing, then measure it out.

2. Straight estimate. You get a clear price for the full job: prep, pour, and finish.

3. Prep, pour, finish. Base compacted, forms set, concrete placed and finished, then cured properly.

Why prep matters

Most concrete problems start underground: soft soil, no base, water with nowhere to go. We put the work into what you cannot see, so what you can see stays flat and crack-free for years.

Proper thickness, control joints in the right places, and a finish matched to the job. That is the whole philosophy.
