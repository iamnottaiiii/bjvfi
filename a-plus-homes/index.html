Why modular

Built indoors. Assembled on your land.

Modular construction builds your home in sections inside a controlled factory, then sets those sections on your foundation. Ask A Plus Homes how the process works for your lot.

01

Precision building

Sections are built indoors to plan, away from weather and delays that slow traditional builds.

02

Your floor plan

Ranch, cape, and two-story layouts are common in modular building. Ask what plans are available.

03

Faster timeline

Factory work and site work can happen at the same time, which can shorten the path to move-in day.

04

Built to code

Modular homes are assembled to local building codes like site-built homes. Ask how inspections work.

05

One point of contact

A builder who coordinates the moving parts, from plan selection to the finished home.

06

Local to Harrisburg
