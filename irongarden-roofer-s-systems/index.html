The craft

A roof is a system, not just shingles

Water finds the weak point. That is why we treat every roof as a complete system: decking, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, and the outer layer all working together.

In New Providence, that means accounting for heavy rain, snow load, and summer heat in every detail, from the drip edge to the ridge vent.

Do it once, do it right, and you will not think about your roof for decades.
