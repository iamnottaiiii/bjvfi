Our Story

Main Street, since way back

A vintage barbershop in the heart of Old Town Seal Beach, keeping classic barbering alive one chair at a time.

The shop

An Old Town original

Tucked into Main Street alongside local favorites, the shop is easy to spot: look for the spinning barber poles out front. Inside you will find vintage chairs, old school character, and the unhurried pace of a classic neighborhood barbershop.

One longtime reviewer notes the shop has been part of Main Street since 1961, and the room wears its history well.

EST.
SHOP LORE
1961

Per a longtime reviewer. Either way, this is the real old school deal.

The barbers

Fun, professional, entertaining

Reviewers describe a great group of people behind the chairs: skilled with clippers and straight razors, generous with conversation, and quick to make a first timer feel like a regular. Out of town visitors praise the expert shaves; locals praise the atmosphere.

The vibe

Like Cheers, with clippers

If there was a barber shop version of Cheers, one reviewer wrote, this place would be it. Enough said. Come for the cut, stay for the company, and leave looking sharp.
