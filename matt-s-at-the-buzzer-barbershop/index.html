About

A Millbury original with game-day energy.

Matt's at the Buzzer has built its name on two things: great cuts at prices everyone can afford, and an atmosphere people actually enjoy. Sports talk fills the room, the service is fast and friendly, and the barbers treat every head in the chair like it matters.

Parents bring kids because the shop is patient and quick. Regulars stay because the cuts are consistent and the prices never creep. And when someone wants something bolder, Matt's signature hair designs turn heads all over town.

With hundreds of published reviews averaging 4.8 stars, it is one of the most talked-about barbershops in the Millbury area.
