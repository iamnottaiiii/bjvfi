about us

Syracuse's calm corner
for beautiful nails

Serving the east side of Syracuse since 2013, with a steady team, honest prices and a genuinely relaxing room.

A salon built on steady craft

Since opening in 2013, Syracuse Nails & Spa has kept the same simple formula: a clean, spa-like room, technicians who keep learning, and service that treats every guest like a regular.

The team stays current with new colors, tools and techniques, and manager Chae keeps the focus where it belongs, on attentive customer care from the moment you sit down.

Relaxed by design

Soft lighting, roomy stations and an unhurried pace. Whether you are in for a quick polish change or a long restorative pedicure with extended massage, the visit feels like a break, not an errand.

Families book together, bridal parties get ready together, and students keep coming back for the friendly prices.
