About the Workshop

The story of a Cleves, Ohio restoration shop, told through its work.

A Curator’s Eye for Old Iron

At 32 Porter Street in Cleves, Ohio, Classic & Antique Restoration treats classic and antique automobiles the way a museum treats its collection: with research, patience, and an insistence on correctness.

The shop’s reputation was built the hard way, one concours car at a time. Its repaint of a 1957 Triumph TR3 in British Racing Green, with renewed brightwork, reupholstered Stone vinyl interior, Wilton wool carpets, and a fully rebuilt drivetrain, carried the car to Best in Class at the Cincinnati Concours d’Elegance at Ault Park, Best of Show at the 2016 Triumph Register of America meeting, and a 99.5 point score at the 2017 meet.

The team is trusted by the collector community itself. The Queen City chapter of the National Corvette Restorers Society held its July 2026 meeting at the shop, where Pete, Mike, and Dave showed members their current projects, including an original 1963 Mickey Thompson Indy Car (one of three built) and a 1959 Corvette Tri-Power.

What we don’t claim: no founding date, no owner biography, and no staff list beyond the names published in the Corvette club’s newsletter appear in public records, so we won’t invent them here. The work speaks first.
