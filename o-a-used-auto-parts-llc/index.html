Our Story

O&A Used Auto Parts LLC is a working salvage yard on Brandywine Road in Clinton, Maryland, the kind of place where a little elbow grease saves you a lot of money.

We are set up for the do-it-yourselfer: come browse the yard, pull the part you need, and pay yard prices instead of dealer prices. And if you are not sure what fits your vehicle, our team has a reputation for going beyond to help, with customers calling out the friendly service again and again.

Whether you are patching up a daily driver or hunting a hard-to-find piece for an older car, start here before you pay full price anywhere else.
