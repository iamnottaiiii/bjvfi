Our story

Since 1987, one name
on the truck

Floriano's Plumbing, Inc. was incorporated in Connecticut in 1987, and it has operated out of 1798 Dixwell Ave in Hamden ever since. That kind of staying power is rare in the trades, and it was earned the slow way: one satisfied customer at a time.

Today the company is led by president Elio Floriano. The business remains family-run, which means the people answering the phone and the people doing the work share the same last name on the invoice.
