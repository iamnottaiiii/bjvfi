About the practice

One dentist, one neighborhood

Fulop Gabriel DDS is an independent dental practice on Lefferts Blvd in Jamaica, Queens, serving patients with general dentistry and endodontic care.

Meet Dr. Fulop

Dr. Gabriel Fulop, DDS, leads this practice with a focus on careful diagnosis and conservative treatment. Directories list his practice for general dentistry and endodontics, including root canal therapy.

Two languages

Dental directories list English and Romanian as spoken at this practice, so more neighbors can describe their symptoms in the language they are most comfortable in.

Small and personal

This is a small, independent practice, not a chain. Patients deal directly with the dentist and the office team, which keeps communication clear and appointments personal.
