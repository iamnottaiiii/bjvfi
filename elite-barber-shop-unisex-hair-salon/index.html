ABOUT
The Story

About

A neighborhood barber shop and unisex salon on Amsterdam Avenue, serving the Upper West Side from a single storefront.

The Shop

Amsterdam Avenue, 10025

Elite Barber Shop Unisex Hair Salon sits at 662 Amsterdam Ave # B in Manhattan's Upper West Side, a block-level neighborhood shop rather than a downtown chain.

Public business records list the shop under the name Elite Unisex Hair Salon Barber Shop & Nail & Spa, licensed in New York State. The storefront itself presents as Elite Barber Shop Unisex Hair Salon.

The Craft

Two Chairs, One Standard

The barber side handles fades, lineups, and beard work. The salon side handles women's and men's cuts, blowouts, and styling. Clients come for one side and stay for both.

Walk ins are part of the culture here. For a guaranteed chair at a specific time, call ahead at (212) 877-1459.
