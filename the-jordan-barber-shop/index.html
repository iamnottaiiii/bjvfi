Our Story

A Harbor City
original.

The Jordan Barber Shop grew up the way the best neighborhood shops do: one great haircut at a time. Located at 1111 W Pacific Coast Hwy in Harbor City, the shop has become a trusted stop for the South Bay community, known for its cleanliness, welcoming atmosphere, and barbers who treat every head like it matters.

Regulars will tell you the secret. Customers praise the friendly, professional staff who deliver clean, precise haircuts tailored to each person's style. Barbers like Ernesto have earned loyal followings, with clients who come back year after year and bring their family and friends along.

The shop is a family destination. Children get patient first cuts, seniors get the classic service they remember, and everyone gets fair prices in a shop that stays spotless.

What we stand for

Clean tools. Honest prices. Haircuts that look sharp when you leave and still look sharp two weeks later. That is the whole philosophy, and it has carried The Jordan Barber Shop for years.
