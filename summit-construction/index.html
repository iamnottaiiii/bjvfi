Center for Precision Manufacturing Renovation

In 2023, Summit Construction was selected to provide general contracting services for the Center for Precision Manufacturing renovation on the University of Akron's main campus. The work transformed the existing Akron Polymer Training Center into the Timken Foundation Center, with demolition, infrastructure updates, new HVAC, a new roof, classrooms, a conference room, computer lab, precision manufacturing lab, offices, and modern furnishings.
