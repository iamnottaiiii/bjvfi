Our Story

Two decades of stucco in the Valley

Del Toro Plastering Co has been providing quality lathing and plastering service to the San Fernando Valley, Los Angeles, and surrounding areas for over two decades. From family homes to commercial buildings, the focus has stayed the same: proper preparation, clean work, and finishes that hold up.

As a California licensed contractor (CSLB Lic. #936289), we bring licensed, insured professionalism to every estimate and every job. Residential or commercial, the quote is free and the work speaks for itself.
