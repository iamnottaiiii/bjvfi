01 · Systems

Formwork and shoring systems

Steel formwork and shoring equipment for walls, columns, cores, slabs, beams and heavy civil structures. Systems like PLATE GIRDER assemble into gangs and cycle efficiently pour after pour.

  • Double-sided and tall wall forming
  • Gang forms for fast cycling
  • Shoring for slabs and structures
  • Equipment rental and sales
02 · Engineering

Engineering and drawings

EFCO engineers design the forming approach for your project: which system, how it cycles, and how to save cost without sacrificing the finish. Form erection drawings are supplied with the equipment.

  • Project-specific forming design
  • Form erection drawings
  • Cycling and cost analysis
  • Finished-concrete quality planning
03 · Field service

Field supervisors on site

EFCO field supervisors work alongside your crew: helping with formwork assembly and cycling, walking through the drawings, and developing methods that keep the job safe and productive.

  • Job-site assembly assistance

  • Safety and productivity methods
  • Crew training on the system

About

Forming concrete
since 1934

W. A. Jennings started Economy Forms Corporation in 1934. Nine decades later, EFCO is an employee-owned company with operations in 12 countries.

The story

From a 1934 startup
to a global forming company

1934 · The beginning

W. A. Jennings founds Economy Forms Corporation, building a business around better ways to form concrete.

Generations of leadership

EFCO remains privately held and employee-owned, now led by its second, third and fourth generations of leadership, with Scott Walter, the founder's great-grandson, serving as president and CEO.

12 countries

EFCO operates in 12 countries, with manufacturing in Des Moines, Iowa and Georgetown, Ontario, Canada, and a headquarters in Des Moines.

Edgewood, Kentucky

The Edgewood office at 3005 Dixie Highway serves contractors across Northern Kentucky and the surrounding region with EFCO systems, engineering and field service.

How we work

Engineered, not improvised

EFCO is known for high-quality, innovative products and engineered solutions for concrete forming and shoring. The engineering team tackles some of the industry's most challenging projects, focusing on design, equipment cycling, cost savings and the quality of the finished concrete.

That engineering comes to the job site: field supervisors help crews assemble and cycle the forms using EFCO drawings, and develop methods that promote safety and productivity.
