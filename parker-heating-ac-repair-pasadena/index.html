About us

Our story

Parker Heating & AC Repair Pasadena is a heating and air conditioning contractor based at 650 Sierra Madre Villa Ave in Pasadena, CA, serving homes across Pasadena and the San Gabriel Valley.

A

Repair-first mindset

We started Parker around a simple frustration: too many homeowners being sold new systems when a honest repair would do. We fix what can be fixed and replace only when it truly makes sense.

B

Pasadena is home turf

From historic Craftsmans to new builds, we know the housing stock and the climate swings of the San Gabriel Valley, and we stock parts for the systems common here.

C

Customers, not tickets

We answer our own phone, remember your system, and follow up after the visit. Most of our work comes from neighbors telling neighbors.

Service area

Pasadena, San Marino, Arcadia, Altadena, South Pasadena, San Gabriel, Sierra Madre, and nearby communities.
