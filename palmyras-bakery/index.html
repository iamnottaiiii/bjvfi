Our story

A bakery with the neighborhood at heart

Palmyra's Bakery sits on 171st Street in Hammond, the kind of corner shop built for regulars: the morning coffee crowd, the after-school cookie run, the family picking up a birthday cake on Saturday.

We believe a bakery should smell like butter and sugar when you open the door, and that the best advertising is a warm loaf handed across the counter. No shortcuts, no pretense, just honest baking made fresh for the people who live nearby.

Whether you need a dozen rolls for Sunday dinner or a three-tier cake for the wedding, you will get the same care in every box. Come see us on 171st St, we will save you something good.
