Services

Everything your heating and cooling needs

One call covers your whole system. Every job starts with an honest diagnosis and a clear explanation of your options.

AC Repair

Warm air, weak airflow, strange noises, leaks, frozen coils, or a system that will not start. We track down the cause and fix it, from capacitors and contactors to compressors and refrigerant issues.

Heating Repair and Service

Furnace and heat pump troubleshooting and repair. No heat, short cycling, pilot and ignition problems, and uneven heating room to room.

Maintenance and Tune-Ups

Seasonal service that keeps your system efficient and dependable: coil cleaning, filter changes, electrical checks, refrigerant levels, and thermostat calibration.

System Installation and Replacement

When repair no longer makes sense, we help you choose a properly sized, efficient replacement and install it to manufacturer spec.

Thermostats and Controls

Thermostat replacement, smart thermostat setup, and zoning controls that put every room exactly where you want it.

Ductwork and Air Quality

Duct inspection and sealing, airflow balancing, and indoor air quality upgrades like better filtration for a healthier home.

Not sure what your system needs? Call (747) 221-9211 and describe the symptoms. We will point you in the right direction.
Our story

A neighborhood HVAC shop, built on showing up

Van Nuys AC Service Repair started with a simple observation: on the hottest days of the year, too many Valley families waited too long for help, or got help that did not last. We set out to be the crew that answers the phone, arrives when promised, and stands behind the work.

From our spot on Van Nuys Blvd, we serve homes and small businesses across the San Fernando Valley. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and neighbors telling neighbors, and that is exactly how we want it.

  • Local technicians who know Valley housing stock and its quirks
  • Repair first: we fix what is fixable and explain when it is not
  • Straightforward communication, before, during, and after the job
