Our story

About Dominican Hair Creations

Dominican Hair Creations is a family-run shop where stylist Juana is known for bringing damaged hair back to life, with silk-press blowouts, careful color, and scalp treatments.

The salon makes its own organic products, including a praised hair growth oil, skin scrub, and cream, all available in the shop. Note: the salon is cash-only, so plan accordingly.
