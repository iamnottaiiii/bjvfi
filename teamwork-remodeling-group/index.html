Our story

Two brothers, one standard

Teamwork Remodeling Group was founded by two brothers with a passion for construction and innovative design. More than a decade later, the company has grown into a go to remodeling contractor for West Hills and the greater Los Angeles area.

The name says it all. Remodeling is a team sport: designers, carpenters, tile setters, and project managers all pulling in the same direction. Our clients feel the difference in the details, the communication, and the finished rooms they live in every day.

From kitchen and bathroom remodels to whole home renovations and ADUs, every project gets meticulous attention and a commitment to customer satisfaction from the first estimate to the final walkthrough.
