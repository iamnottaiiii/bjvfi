Our story

A boutique salon with a personal touch

Charne Hair Salon is a boutique salon on Flatbush Avenue in Brooklyn, known for an immaculate, tidy space and unhurried, careful work. Guests come for the exceptional blow-dry techniques, the thoughtful braiding and coloring, and an atmosphere that feels efficient, affordable, and genuinely welcoming.

Every appointment starts with a real conversation about your hair, your routine, and the look you want, so you leave with a style that works in real life, not just in the chair.
