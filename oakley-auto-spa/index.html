Our Story

A Detail Studio in the Heart of Oakley

Oakley Auto Spa is a car detailing service at 3525 Cardiff Ave, serving the Oakley and Hyde Park neighborhoods of Cincinnati, Ohio. Our promise is simple: a true auto spa and salon experience for your vehicle, from the moment you step into our calm waiting room to the final walk around of your freshly detailed car.

We go piece by piece, removing paint overspray, embedded debris, stains, and odors that other shops miss. Customers trust us with daily drivers, luxury sedans, and heavily soiled work trucks alike, and we treat every one like it is our own.

With pick up and delivery service, gift cards, and same day availability when our schedule allows, keeping your car immaculate has never been this easy.
