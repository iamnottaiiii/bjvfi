Our story

BUILT ON WRENCHES

LMR Auto Repair Services is a mechanic shop on Terminal Rd in Lorton, Virginia.

LMR Auto Repair Services sits on Terminal Rd in Lorton, in the middle of one of Northern Virginia's busiest corridors of independent auto shops. It is the kind of place drivers find by word of mouth and keep coming back to because the work holds up.

We keep it simple: listen to what the car is doing, diagnose it properly, fix it right the first time, and charge a fair price. No upsells you do not need, no jargon to hide behind.

Fix it right the first time, and the customer comes back. That is the whole business plan.

Whether it is a commuter that needs to make it to work tomorrow, a family SUV heading out on a road trip, or a work truck that cannot afford downtime, every vehicle gets the same careful attention.
