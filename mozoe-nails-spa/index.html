Our story

An upscale environment,
without the upscale attitude

Mozoe Nails Spa opened on Milton Avenue in Camillus with one guiding idea: quality services, reasonable prices, and an upscale environment can all live under one roof. That philosophy still drives everything, from the calm of the salon to the care taken with every set of nails.

Guests know us for detailed nail work, honest advice and a team that never rushes. First time visitors become regulars, and regulars bring their friends.
