Our process

From first call
to final approval

A septic project moves through the same clear steps every time. Here is how we take you through them.

Talk it through

Call (508) 839-5113 and describe your project: a new build, an addition, or a system that needs replacing. We will tell you honestly what the path looks like and what information we need.

Soil evaluation and site investigation

Mark M. Santora, a licensed Massachusetts Soil Evaluator (#2138), comes to your lot. Deep observation holes and percolation testing establish exactly what your soil can handle.

Engineered design

Your system is designed around your lot, your soil, and your plans, then stamped by a Massachusetts Registered Professional Engineer (#40167). The design is checked against installation experience, so it builds as drawn.

Permits and filings

We prepare complete application packages for your Board of Health and, where needed, the conservation commission. A former town Health Agent prepares them, which keeps reviews moving.

Construction support

During installation, we stay available for field questions and site visits, so the system in the ground matches the system on the plans.
