Our Story

How a small shop in Old Town Torrance became the neighborhood chair of choice.

Headlinerz Barbershop opened its doors in 2016 in the heart of Old Town Torrance, at 1217 El Prado Ave, and has been a local fixture ever since. The mission was simple: professional, clean fades and shaves in a shop where everyone feels like a regular.

Over the years the shop has earned a 4.8 rating across more than 100 Google reviews. Clients mention the welcoming atmosphere, the reggae on the sound system, the friendly shop dog, and barbers who take their time and get the details right. Walk-ins stumble in during the Old Town antique fair and leave as regulars.

Today Headlinerz remains what it set out to be: a skilled, affordable, family-friendly barbershop in the heart of Torrance.
