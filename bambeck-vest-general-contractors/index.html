How we build

A team approach, on every project

Bambeck & Vest keeps its own labor force of carpenters, drywall finishers, and laborers, and pairs them with subcontractors chosen for quality. Every project, large or small, gets weekly job meetings where budget and work in progress are reviewed with the whole team, so costs stay where they were promised.

Each estimator and project manager brings 25 or more years of experience, a combined 150+ years across the estimating and management staff. The result: projects from very small to multimillion-dollar, delivered on time and within budget.

Our story

Six decades, two names

Bambeck & Vest was founded in 1964 by Andrew C. Bambeck and William H. Vest, and incorporated in the State of Ohio in 1984. What began as two builders' names on a door is now a thirty-person company run the way the founders ran it: with your own people, and with the client in the room.

1964

The founding

Andrew C. Bambeck and William H. Vest start the company in Cincinnati, building on the belief that a contractor's reputation is earned one finished project at a time.

1984

Incorporated in Ohio

The company incorporates in the State of Ohio, formalizing two decades of steady growth in commercial and institutional construction.

Today

Second-generation leadership

The management team is headed by CEO Dianne Bambeck, President Ed Roark, Vice President David Dooley, and Secretary/Treasurer Nicole Benintendi, with over 125 years of combined construction experience. About thirty full-time office staff, estimators, project managers, carpenters, drywall finishers, and laborers carry the work.

The company is a certified Women's Business Enterprise and a member of Associated Builders and Contractors, the National Federation of Independent Businesses, and the Greater Cincinnati Chamber of Commerce. Most of the business comes from repeat customers and their referrals, which the company counts as its real credential.
