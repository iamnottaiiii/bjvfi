About us

Rooted in Cincinnati construction

Jindal Builders & Restoration is a general contracting company based in Cincinnati, Ohio. Company records indicate the business was established in the mid 2000s, and public records list Ram Jindal as president.

From our office at 510 Old State Rte 74, we take on residential and commercial projects throughout Greater Cincinnati. Our work spans new construction, renovations, and the restoration of buildings that need a second life.

  • General contracting for homes and businesses
  • Renovation and restoration focus
  • Local team serving Greater Cincinnati
