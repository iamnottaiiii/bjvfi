Our story

Rooted in Clifton Heights, built on hard work

D'Antonio Excavating Inc is a locally owned excavation contractor operating out of 364 E Madison Ave in Clifton Heights, Pennsylvania. Our background is in water, sewer, and pipeline construction, the kind of underground work where there is no room for shortcuts.

That discipline carries into every job we take: residential digs, grading, drainage, demolition, and hauling across Delaware County and the surrounding Philadelphia area. We run a tight crew, keep our equipment maintained, and treat every property like it is our own.

Customers describe us as the hardest working group around, and we plan to keep earning that. If your project needs earth moved, lines run, or a site made ready, talk to us first.
