Our story

On Main Avenue since 1970

Krebs Inc is a family-owned excavating contractor based in Highland Heights, Kentucky. The company was formed as Krebs Construction, Inc. on April 10, 1970, and has been rooted on Main Avenue ever since.

Today the company is owned and operated by Joseph A. Krebs. The work has stayed close to its roots: site preparation, land clearing, grading, trenching, and demolition for residential and commercial projects across Northern Kentucky.

Public business listings describe Krebs Inc as a full-scope dirt-work contractor covering excavation, general contracting support, utility work, and real estate development projects. When a project needs ground that is cleared, level, and ready to build on, that is the call Krebs Inc answers.

Fifty-plus years in the same town means the reputation travels by word of mouth. If you are planning a build, a teardown, or a lot that needs shaping, start with a conversation.
