100% waterproof click-lock and glue-down vinyl. The fastest growing floor in Cincinnati homes for good reason.

  • Waterproof SPC and WPC cores
  • Wood-look and stone-look styles
  • Great for kitchens, baths, and basements
  • Low maintenance, high scratch resistance

Soft underfoot and quiet. We help you pick the right fiber and pad weight for each room.

Our story

A local crew that treats your home like its own.

Modern Flooring Solutions LLC is a flooring contractor based in Cincinnati, Ohio. We focus on one thing and do it well: beautiful, durable floors installed with care.

Every job starts with the subfloor, because a floor is only as good as what sits under it. We level, repair, and prep before a single plank goes down, then we install, trim, and clean up like we were never there.

Homeowners call us for honest measurements, straight answers about which floor fits their budget and lifestyle, and installs that hold up to kids, pets, and everyday life.
