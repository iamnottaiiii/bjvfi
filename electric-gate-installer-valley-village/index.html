What we do

Gates that open on command

From new automated driveway gates to repairs that get a stuck gate moving again, this is the core scope of a fence gate contractor.

New gate installation

Sliding and swing gates sized to your driveway, with posts set and hardware aligned for smooth travel.

Openers and automation

Electric operators, remotes, keypads, and exit sensors so the gate works with a tap or a code.

Gate repair

Diagnosis for gates that will not open, drag on the ground, or make grinding and clicking sounds.

How it works

From first call to first open

A typical gate project follows these steps. Timing depends on the gate style and site conditions.

01

Call and describe

Share your driveway width, gate style, and what is or is not working.

02

On-site visit

Measurements, slope check, and power access are confirmed in person.

03

Install or repair

The gate is set, wired, and adjusted for smooth, quiet travel.

04

Test and hand over

Remotes and keypads are programmed and the system is tested with you.
