How it works

From first walk to final planting.

Every project follows the same calm rhythm. You always know what happens next and what it costs before we break ground.

Our story

Design in the name, care in the work.

Kho DesignScapes started with a simple observation: plenty of contractors can install a yard, but few design one. We built the business around the design first, because a thoughtful plan is what separates a yard that survives from a landscape that thrives.

Based on Gledhill Street in Northridge, we work across the San Fernando Valley, designing and building outdoor spaces for homes of every size, from compact courtyards to full-property transformations.

We keep the process personal. You talk to the people doing the work, the plan is yours to approve line by line, and we do not consider a project finished until the last bed is mulched and you know how to care for it.

"A good landscape should look inevitable, like the house and the garden grew up together."
