About Colerain Auto Center

A family owned used car dealership serving Cincinnati and Colerain Township.

Colerain Auto Center has been family owned and operated for over 30 years, according to the business's own description. The dealership sits right on Colerain Avenue in Cincinnati, Ohio, one of the busiest automotive corridors on the northwest side of town.

As an independent used car dealer, the lot focuses on pre-owned cars, trucks, SUVs, and vans at prices aimed at working families. Customers in public reviews describe a straightforward buying experience: walk the lot, test drive, talk numbers, and drive home.

The business is also listed as an automotive services and repair provider, so the team can speak to vehicle condition from hands on experience, not just a spec sheet.

Colerain Auto Center holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau. It is not BBB accredited.
