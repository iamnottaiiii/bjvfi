What we do

Waterproofing, done by a general contractor

Georgia clay soil and heavy rains push water toward foundations, basements, and crawl spaces. We handle the waterproofing side of the house: finding where water gets in and stopping it with practical, proven methods.

Because we are a general contractor, we can also take care of related repairs around the project instead of sending you to three different companies.
