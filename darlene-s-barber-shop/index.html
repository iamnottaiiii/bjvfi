About Darlene

The barber behind the chair

Darlene runs a classic one-chair style neighborhood shop on Riverlin Street in Millbury, where the work speaks for itself. Clients describe her as friendly, accurate, and quick: she takes your mop and makes you look human again, as one reviewer put it.

The approach

Old school, done well

No gimmicks, no rushed chairs. A proper consultation, a precise cut, and honest advice about what works for your hair. That consistency is why clients keep coming back and why new ones arrive on word of mouth.

Because it is a personal, appointment-based shop, every visit gets full attention. Call ahead, sit down, and leave looking like a million bucks.
