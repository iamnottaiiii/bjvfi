Welcome

City Nails & Spa has been a Federal Hill favorite for over a decade. The salon specializes in manicures, pedicures, and dip powder nails using professional brands like OPI, CND Shellac, and Gelish, plus waxing, facials, and massage. Walk-ins are welcome and the shop is wheelchair accessible.
