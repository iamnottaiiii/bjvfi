About

Seven Hills Properties

Seven Hills Properties (also known as Seven Hills Development) is a Cincinnati real estate developer and property company based in the Oakley neighborhood at 4382 Marburg Avenue.

We focus on residential development and property management on the city’s east side, keeping every decision local: local contractors, local tenants, local accountability.
