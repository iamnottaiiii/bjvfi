About us

Three decades of clean on Riverside Ave

Grace Cleaners has been part of the Medford community since 1988, a neighborhood dry cleaner built on careful work and honest service.

Our story

Since opening its doors in 1988, Grace Cleaners has served Medford from its home at 33 Riverside Ave. Under president Hyung Lee, the shop has stayed true to a simple idea: treat every garment like it matters, because to its owner, it does.

Thirty-eight years in, that approach still shows up in every pressed collar and every freshened coat. Customers come back because the work is consistent, the service is personal, and the results speak for themselves.

  • 88

    1988: doors open

  • 06

    2006: a local institution

  • 26
