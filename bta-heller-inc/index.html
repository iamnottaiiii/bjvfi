About

BTA Heller Inc. began in 1952 in Troy, Michigan, as American Heller, with roots in a German tooling tradition more than a century old. The company became BTA Heller in 2007.

BTA Heller designs and manufactures deep hole drilling tooling and systems, including gun drilling, single tube, BTA, and double tube/ejector drilling systems for holes from 0.5 in to 36 in diameter. In 2018 the company was acquired by Sunnen Products Company.
