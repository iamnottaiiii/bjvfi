About

A Norwood shop with deep roots

Kaleco Plumbing is a locally owned plumbing company at 2437 Williams Avenue in Norwood, run by owner John Kalebain. The shop serves residential and commercial customers across Cincinnati, Loveland and the surrounding counties.

Permit records show more than 200 permitted plumbing projects, from service repairs to full remodels and commercial fit-ups. The team handles everything from a dripping faucet to a whole-house re-pipe.

Walk-ins and calls welcome Monday through Friday, 8 to 4, and Saturday mornings 9 to 1.
