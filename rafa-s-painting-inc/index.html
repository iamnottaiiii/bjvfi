Our story

A Pikesville painting crew with a simple standard

Rafa's Painting inc. is a local painting contractor based on Kahn Drive in Pikesville, serving homes across the Baltimore area.

The standard is simple: prep like it matters, paint like someone will look closely, and leave the place clean. Most painters can open a can. Fewer will spend the extra hour on the wall before the can opens. We are the second kind.

We keep crews small and communication direct, so the person who quotes your job is around while the job gets done. If something is not right, you say so, and we fix it. That is the whole business model.
