Our story

Thirty-four years of asphalt, one owner, zero shortcuts.

Rack Seven Paving Co is a certified women-owned business led by its founder, Kimberly Rack. We are fully insured with bonding capabilities in good standing, and we set out to exceed expectations on every job.

From 1992 to today

1992

Kim Rack starts Rack Seven Paving Co in Cincinnati, Ohio, focusing on residential and commercial asphalt work.

1999

The company incorporates as Rack 7 Paving Co., Inc., and grows into roads, cart paths, courts, excavation, and gravel.

2021

Rack Seven celebrates 30 years in business, still independently owned and operated by its founder.

2025

The company earns BBB accreditation and holds an A+ rating, with the file open since 1993.

Today

Based at 7208 Main St, we serve Ohio, Northern Kentucky, and Eastern Indiana from our central Cincinnati shop.
