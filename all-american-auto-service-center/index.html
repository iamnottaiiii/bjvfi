Our story

done right

All American Auto Service Center is an independent, full-service repair shop on Cremen Road in Temple Hills, built on fair prices and straight talk.

How we got here

Fair and just,
since day one

That phrase is the shop's own description of itself: a repair center for cars and body work at a fair and just price. It is also exactly what customers say they get, visit after visit, for the better part of two decades.

Priced to be fair

Customers regularly report getting multiple estimates and finding All American the best price, without giving up quality workmanship.

Explained, not sold

Open communication and knowledgeable explanations come up again and again. You will understand what is wrong and why it costs what it costs.

Every kind of vehicle

Daily drivers, BMWs, Tahoes, diesels, classics and work fleets. If it has wheels, this crew has probably fixed one like it.
