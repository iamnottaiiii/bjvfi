The shop

Good wood, honest joints, no shortcuts.

Every project that leaves the shop starts as rough lumber and an idea. Scott Nailed It builds furniture and woodwork the slow way: measured twice, cut once, and finished so it can take daily life. Tables that host holidays, shelves that hold the heavy books, trim that finally makes a room feel finished.

Bring a sketch, a photo, or just a problem you want solved in wood. You will get a straight answer on what it will take and what it will cost before any sawdust flies.

What the shop builds

Five ways wood can fix your home.

Custom Furniture

Tables, benches, beds, bookshelves, and storage pieces built to your exact measurements. Pick the wood, the finish, and the details; the piece is built for your room, not a warehouse shelf.

  • Dining and coffee tables, benches, and desks
  • Beds, nightstands, and dressers
  • Bookshelves and display shelving
  • Entryway pieces: hall trees, consoles, mudroom storage

Rough starting points, for planning only: a simple bench from about $400, a farmhouse dining table from about $1,200. Your quote depends on size, wood species, and finish.

Built-ins and Cabinetry

Custom storage that fits your walls to the inch. Built-in bookcases, window seats with storage, closet systems, and cabinetry that looks like it was always part of the house.

  • Built-in bookcases and entertainment walls
  • Window seats and banquette seating with storage
  • Closet and pantry organization
  • Laundry and mudroom cabinetry

Rough starting points, for planning only: a single built-in bookcase from about $900. Final pricing follows an on-site look at the space.

Trim and Millwork

Finish carpentry that tightens up a whole room. Crisp baseboards, crown molding, wainscoting, and casing installed with tight miters and clean paint lines.

  • Crown molding and baseboards
  • Wainscoting, board and batten, accent walls
  • Door and window casing
  • Stair treads, risers, and handrails
Sample
Rough starting points, for planning only: single-room trim packages from about $600. Priced by linear footage and profile.

Repairs and Refinishing

Good bones deserve a second life. Structural repairs for wobbly chairs and tables, plus stripping, sanding, and refinishing that brings tired wood back without hiding its character.

  • Furniture repair: joints, legs, drawers, and veneer
  • Refinishing and re-staining tables and cabinets
  • Touch-up and color matching
  • Hardware replacement and upgrades
Sample
Rough starting points, for planning only: simple chair re-gluing from about $75, table refinishing from about $500. Send photos for a faster estimate.
Call for a quote

Outdoor Projects

Yard and porch woodwork built for Georgia weather: pressure-treated and sealed pieces that stand up to sun and rain, from planters to pergolas.

  • Planters, raised garden beds, and potting benches
  • Porch swings, adirondack chairs, and outdoor tables
  • Pergolas and privacy screens
  • Deck and fence repairs

Rough starting points, for planning only: cedar planters from about $150, porch swings from about $450. Weather-rated finishes included.
