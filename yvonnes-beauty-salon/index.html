Meet Yvonne

One chair, years of trust

Yvonne's Beauty Salon has been a trusted spot for hairstyling in South Ozone Park over the years. Regular clients, including whole families, appreciate her skill with haircuts, coloring and eyebrow shaping.

What keeps people coming back is the personal touch: she remembers how you like it, she is honest about what works, and the visit feels warm and unhurried.
