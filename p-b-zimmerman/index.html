Our story

A Reseda excavating contractor

P.B. Zimmerman is an excavating contractor based at 18855 Vanowen St in Reseda, California. We focus on one thing and do it well: moving earth so construction projects can start on solid ground.

Dirt is our trade

We are not a jack of all trades. Excavation, trenching, grading and hauling is what we show up to do every day.

Plans come first

We work from your plans and permits, dig to spec, and keep the site marked and organized for inspectors and other trades.

Neighbors matter

Most of our work happens in residential neighborhoods. We keep noise, dust and street impact to a minimum.

Service area

Proudly serving the Valley

Based in Reseda, we travel to job sites across the San Fernando Valley and nearby communities, including Van Nuys, Northridge, Tarzana, Woodland Hills, Canoga Park, Encino and North Hollywood. Working on a project farther out? Call and ask.
