Our story

A small builder with a green mission

CFS Homes, LLC was founded by Charles Soucek, a seasoned construction professional who set out to build homes that are as healthy as they are efficient. Based on Markley Road in Anderson Township, the company has specialized in LEED-certified green homes since its early days.

Staying intentionally small, with a team of one to four people, CFS Homes keeps every project personal: one builder, one family, one home at a time. That focus shows in the details, from safe material choices to floor plans shaped around natural light.

Public records also connect founder Charles Soucek with Forest Trails Development LLC, a related Ohio development company.
