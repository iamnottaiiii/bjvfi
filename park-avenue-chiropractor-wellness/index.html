Services

What we do

The practice describes its work as chiropractic, sports medicine, and nutritional services. Below is how those disciplines show up in patient care.

01

Chiropractic Care

Hands-on spinal adjustments and joint mobilization to restore motion, ease pain, and take pressure off irritated nerves. Care for back pain, neck pain, stiffness, and related complaints.

02

Sports Medicine

An athlete-minded approach to strains, sprains, overuse injuries, and getting back in the game. Treatment and guidance tuned to the demands of your sport and your training schedule.

03

Nutritional Services

Practical nutritional guidance to support recovery, manage inflammation, and keep your energy steady. Fuel is part of the treatment plan, not an afterthought.

04

Wellness and Prevention

Ongoing care for people who want to stay mobile, resilient, and ahead of the next flare-up. Maintenance visits built around your body and your calendar.

Every patient is different. Call (212) 838-6600 to discuss your needs and find out whether our approach fits.

About

Wellness, the Park Avenue way

Park Avenue Chiropractor Wellness P.C. practices at 580 Park Avenue in Manhattan's Upper East Side, steps from some of the city's best running routes, gyms, and offices.

The practice brings together three disciplines that are usually sold separately: chiropractic care for the structure, sports medicine for the active body, and nutritional services for the fuel. The idea is simple. The body is one system, so care should treat it like one.
