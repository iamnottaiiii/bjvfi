Our story

Fifteen years of
great hair days

Carlitos Barbershop opened on Main St in 2009 and never looked back. Owner Carlos built a shop with a healthy, totally familiar environment where clients feel comfortable, confident and relaxed, and a staff with over fifteen years of combined experience.

The team

Carlos leads a skilled crew: Alex and Jeyson on the clippers, praised for meticulous fades, and Kathia keeping long hair looking its best.

Family first

A family-owned shop through and through. Kids are welcome, regulars are family, and new faces are greeted like old friends.

Service as priority

Giving the best service to every client is the shop's stated priority. Comfortable, confident, relaxed: that's the promise.
