About

Meet Dr. Robert Horner

Dr. Robert Horner, DDS, practices general dentistry in Owings Mills, Maryland. A graduate of the University of Maryland School of Dentistry (1987), he has spent his career providing steady, patient-first dental care to the Baltimore County community.

His philosophy is simple: treat people the way he would want to be treated. That means honest diagnoses, gentle hands, and time to answer every question before treatment begins.
