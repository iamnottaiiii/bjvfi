Our Story

Built on the before & after

A Lynbrook remodeling crew with a simple promise: professional work, honest guidance, and a finished project you are proud to show off.

Who we are

A local crew, led by Budd

Before & After Remodeling is a general contracting team based in Lynbrook, NY, serving homeowners across Nassau County. The team is led by Budd, whose customers describe a crew that is professional, hardworking, and generous with guidance and suggestions during the project.

The name says it all. We take the dated, the worn, and the not-quite-right, and we turn it into the finished space you pictured. Renovations go easier and finish quicker when the crew shows up, communicates, and treats your home like their own.

How we work

Straightforward, start to finish

No runaround, no mystery line items. You get one team from the first walkthrough to the final walkthrough, and a crew lead who picks up the phone.

  • 1 Walkthrough and honest assessment of your space
  • 2 Clear written scope and schedule before work starts
  • 3 Clean, steady work with progress you can see
  • 4 Final walkthrough, done when you say it is done
