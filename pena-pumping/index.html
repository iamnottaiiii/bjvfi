Our story

Sun Valley concrete, poured right

Our roots

Pena Pumping is a concrete contracting company based on Cranford Avenue in Sun Valley, California. We serve homeowners, builders, and contractors across the San Fernando Valley and greater Los Angeles.

Our craft

Concrete pumping is a trade of its own. We focus on it full time: matching the pump to the pour, coordinating with suppliers, and placing mix cleanly so your forms, finishers, and schedule all hold.
