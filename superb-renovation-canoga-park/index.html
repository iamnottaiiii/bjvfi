Our story

Built in Canoga Park, for Canoga Park

Superb Renovation Canoga Park started with a simple observation: the West San Fernando Valley is full of well-built homes that deserve better than a rushed remodel. From our shop at 8759 Remmet Ave, we set out to be the crew neighbors recommend by name.

We keep the team small on purpose. The people who measure your kitchen are the people who build it. That means fewer handoffs, fewer surprises, and a finished project that matches the drawing you approved.

Kitchens, baths, flooring, paint, drywall, and full home renovations: if it makes a house feel like home, we do it, and we stand behind it.
