About

The Palms neighborhood shop

Palms Barbershop is a Westside staple with a simple formula that works: consistent, quality cuts and a crew that treats people well. With 150 reviews behind it, the shop has earned its place in the Palms neighborhood routine.

Regulars praise the reliability: the cut you ask for is the cut you get. Parking in the area can be tight and busy days bring waits, so calling ahead is wise.
