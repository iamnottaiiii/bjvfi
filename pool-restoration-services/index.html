How it works

Our Restoration Process

Four clear steps, no guesswork. Here is how a typical Pool Restoration Services project runs from first call to first swim.

01

Assess

We inspect your pool surface, structure, plumbing, and equipment, and talk through what is bothering you. Photos and notes come back to you with an honest read.

02

Plan

You get a clear written plan: scope of work, finish options for plaster or tile, timeline, and pricing. Ask anything. We adjust until it is right.

03

Restore

Our crew preps, repairs, resurfaces, and finishes your pool with care for your yard and neighbors. We keep you updated as the work moves along.

04

Enjoy

Final walkthrough, startup chemistry guidance, and your pool back in action. Swim season starts whenever you are ready.

Restoration Services

About us

Pool Restoration Services

A swimming pool contractor focused on one thing: making old pools feel new again.

Pool Restoration Services is a swimming pool contractor based in Cincinnati, Ohio. We specialize in restoring aging residential pools, bringing back worn surfaces, tired tile, and outdated equipment so families can enjoy their backyard again.

Restoration is a craft of its own. It takes a careful eye to judge what can be repaired and what should be replaced, and the patience to prep a surface right before the new finish goes on. That is the work we show up for every day.

If your pool is stained, rough underfoot, losing water, or just looking its age, call us. We will take a look and give you an honest plan.
