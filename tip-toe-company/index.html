Waldorf, Maryland Nail Salon

Beautiful nails, one guest at a time.

Tip & Toe Company is a boutique nail salon known for calm, unhurried appointments and meticulous work. Every visit is personal, every detail is finished by hand.

Why guests keep coming back

Guests describe the shop as clean, cute, and inviting, with a relaxed pace that larger salons rarely offer. It is the kind of place where the technician knows your name and remembers exactly how you like your nails.

  • One-on-one appointments, never rushed
  • Meticulous nail art, matched from any photo
  • Welcoming to first timers and guests of all ages
  • 5.0 rating across Google reviews

A salon built on personal attention

Tip & Toe Company began with a simple idea: nail care should feel personal, not production line. Appointments are one-on-one, so the focus stays on you from the moment you sit down to the final top coat.

Guests travel from across the Waldorf area for the signature pedicures, careful shaping, and nail art that guests say comes out better than the photo they bring in.

The Tip & Toe difference

Walk in and you will notice the pace right away. No crowded stations, no feeling of being one more ticket in a queue. Guests mention the calm setting, the friendly conversation, and a technician who takes the time to get every detail right.

From careful cuticle work to intricate nail art matched from a photo, the goal is simple: you leave with nails you cannot stop looking at, and a plan to come back.

What guests say most

Review after review mentions the same things: a clean and adorable shop, a great personality, and work that never disappoints. Guests bring their daughters, their friends, and their inspiration photos, and they keep returning for years.
