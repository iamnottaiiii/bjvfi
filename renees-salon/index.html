Our story

Upscale service with a personal touch

Renee's Salon is an upscale beauty salon in Syracuse with 25 years of experience behind the chair. The philosophy is simple: deliver the results you asked for, in color, multi-dimensional foiling, trend-setting haircuts, a high-style finish and waxing, and make the whole visit feel personal.

Guests come for the color expertise and stay for the atmosphere: an elegant, immaculately kept room where stylists take time to listen, explain your options and work with you toward the look you want.

Beyond hair, the salon offers manicures, pedicures and waxing, so you can leave feeling finished from head to toe.

Good to know

Appointments are required. Booking ahead guarantees your stylist's full attention, with no walk-in rush.

The salon is wheelchair accessible with on-site parking right outside the door.

We accept major credit cards and offer free wi-fi while you relax.
