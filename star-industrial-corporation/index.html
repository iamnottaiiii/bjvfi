Our story

An electrical contractor with industrial roots

Built for serious electrical work

Star Industrial Corporation is based at 842 Valley Crest St in La Cañada Flintridge, California. Our name says it plainly: we are set up for industrial-grade electrical work, and we bring that same standard to every residential and commercial job.

From panel upgrades and EV chargers to three-phase machinery hookups, we size every job correctly, pull the right permits, and wire it to last.

Safety first, always

Electricity does not forgive shortcuts. We test every circuit, torque every connection, and never leave a job energized until it has been checked twice. That discipline is why commercial and industrial clients trust us with their facilities.

Homeowners get the same treatment: clean work areas, clear explanations, and no upsells you do not need.
