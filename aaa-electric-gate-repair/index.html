Common questions

My gate will not open. What should I check first?
Check that the opener has power (breaker, outlet, or battery backup) and that nothing is blocking the safety sensors. If the motor hums but nothing moves, or the remote does nothing, call us at (818) 877-7751 and describe the symptom.
How much does a gate repair cost?
Simple fixes like sensor alignment or remote programming are on the lower end; opener motor or control board work costs more. We give a free, written quote before any work begins, so you always know the number first.
Do you install new electric gates?
Yes. We install sliding and swing gate operators, plus keypads, intercoms, and phone-entry systems, for both residential driveways and commercial properties.
My gate reverses for no reason. Why?
That is almost always the safety sensors: misaligned, dirty, or damaged photo eyes tell the opener something is in the way. It is one of our most common same-visit fixes.
Do you service commercial gates and HOAs?
Yes. We service business lots, apartment and HOA entrances, and commercial operators, including access control systems.
