01 Why coatings

Tear-offs are the last resort, not the first quote.

Most aging flat roofs do not need to come off. They need to be cleaned, repaired at the seams and penetrations, and sealed under one continuous waterproof membrane.

A fluid-applied coating goes on like thick paint and cures into a seamless, rubber-like skin. No seams to split, no tear-off dumpsters, and a fraction of the downtime of a full replacement.

Common questions

Coating questions, answered.

How long does a roof coating last?

Service life depends on the system, the thickness applied, sun exposure, and maintenance. Ask us what is realistic for your specific roof during the inspection, and keep up with the maintenance plan to get the most out of it.

Can a coating really replace a new roof?

Sometimes. If the deck is solid and the existing membrane is mostly sound, a restoration can add many years at a lower cost. If there is structural damage or saturated insulation, replacement is the honest answer, and we will say so.

Will a white coating lower my cooling bills?

Reflective coatings reduce roof surface temperature, which eases the load on air conditioning. Savings vary with insulation, building use, and climate, so treat it as a real but building-specific benefit.

How disruptive is the work?

Far less than a tear-off. There is no dumpster full of old roofing, and most buildings stay fully occupied. Coatings need dry weather and cure time, so we schedule around the forecast.
