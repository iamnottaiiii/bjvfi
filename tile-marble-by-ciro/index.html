Install, repair, and expert design, under one roof

Most tile jobs fail at the planning stage, not the grouting stage. Ciro plans the layout first: how the pattern meets the doorway, where the cuts land, how the light moves across the stone.

That is the expert design part of the work. Then comes the installation, done by hand, one tile at a time. And when existing tile needs help, cracked pieces, loose tiles, tired grout, there is the repair side, too.

Residential kitchens and baths, commercial lobbies and offices: if it is tile or marble, it is our trade.

The craft

25+ years of setting tile the slow way

Ciro learned tile and marble work the Italian way: as a craft, not a commodity. That means proper prep, honest materials, and layouts drawn out before the first tile is cut.

For more than 25 years, he has installed and repaired tile and marble across the Valley, in kitchens and baths, entryways and lobbies, for homeowners and businesses alike.

He works as a licensed California contractor (#443524), bonded and insured, and every job starts the same way: a free on-site estimate, with references available so you can ask around first.
