Our story

An authentic Dominican salon experience

That is how Blue Moon Salon describes itself, and it shows in every visit. On Bay Street in Staten Island, the salon has built its name on warmth: kind people, good music, free coffee, and stylists who take genuine pride in their work.

It is a family friendly space where clients of all ages feel at home, where top-notch products meet experienced hands, and where leaving with great hair is simply what happens here.
