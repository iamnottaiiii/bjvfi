Our story

Four decades of building in Newport

Jim Stegman Construction Co was founded in 1983 by Jim Stegman Jr in Newport, Kentucky. From the start, the focus was simple: deliver the highest quality craftsmanship in the industry, and treat every client like a future repeat customer.

That approach compounded. The company is now described as an award-winning construction and remodeling firm, and it says plainly that repeat business and customer referrals have always been a major source of its work. For a contractor, there is no better report card.

Today the company handles general remodeling, additions, custom homes, and decks and porches, with its Legacy Kitchens division dedicated to kitchen and bath projects. Electrical, plumbing, and HVAC are handled through long-trusted subcontractors, so clients get one accountable team from demolition day to final walkthrough.

  • 1983Founded. Jim Stegman Jr starts the company in Newport, KY.
  • 1990s-2000sReferral engine. Repeat clients and word of mouth become the company's main source of new work.
  • LegacyLegacy Kitchens. A dedicated kitchen and bath division launches under the Stegman name.
  • TodayStill building. Forty-plus years in, still based at 517 Monmouth St in Newport.
