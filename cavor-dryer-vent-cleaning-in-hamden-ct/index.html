The warning signs

Your dryer is asking
for help when

If any of these sound familiar, the vent is likely restricted. Each one means the dryer is working harder, running hotter, and costing more.

Damp after a full cycle

Clothes come out damp or need two cycles. Moist air cannot escape through a clogged line.

Dryer runs hot

The top of the dryer feels hot to the touch, or the laundry room turns into a sauna.

Burning smell

A scorched or musty smell during a cycle means lint is baking onto hot metal. Call right away.

Vent flap stays shut

The outside vent flap barely opens, or lint mats around it. Airflow is not making it out.

How we work

The full run,
inside to outside

We do not just vacuum behind the dryer and leave. Every visit starts with an airflow check at the exterior cap, then we clean the entire vent run with a rotary brush and a strong vacuum, from the dryer connection to the outside.

We also clear the lint trap housing, check the transition hose for crushing and tears, and finish with a second airflow reading so you can see the difference in plain numbers.
