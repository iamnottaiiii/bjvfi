Our Story

A NEIGHBORHOOD SHOP ON BAILEY AVE

Beardown Cuts is a barber shop on Bailey Avenue in Buffalo, New York, serving the neighborhood one chair at a time. The idea behind the shop is simple: great barbering does not need to be complicated. Listen to the client, take your time, and send everyone out the door looking their best.

The shop has earned a 4.8 star rating across 18 Google reviews, with clients praising the careful, consistent work. Whether it is a weekly maintenance cut or a full transformation, the approach stays the same: unhurried, detailed, and honest.
