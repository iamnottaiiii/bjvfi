About the chapter

Serving Valley landscape professionals

California Landscape Contra is the San Fernando Valley Chapter of the California Landscape Contractors Association (CLCA), the statewide trade association for licensed landscape contractors.

The chapter office is located at 20946 Devonshire St #200 in Chatsworth, California, and can be reached at (818) 772-7233 during business hours.

Through events, awards, publications, and advocacy, the chapter supports the businesses and people who design, build, and maintain the Valley's outdoor spaces.
