About Us

Your hometown source for bathtub refinishing since 1994

All Tub & Tile was established in 1994 as a low-cost alternative to high-cost traditional bath and kitchen remodeling. For three decades we have served the greater Cincinnati area, Northern Kentucky and Southeastern Indiana.

Our promise is simple: we cannot make everything in your bathroom look like new, but your tub, tile, sinks and countertops will come remarkably close, for a fraction of replacement cost and without the mess of demolition.

All work is done on site and is ready for your use the next day. White is the most common color choice, but we offer an unlimited palette of colors to meet your decorating needs.

Why refinishing?

Replacing a bathtub means plumbers, tile setters, drywall and paint crews, and weeks of disruption. Refinishing bonds a new finish directly to your existing fixture in a single visit. Same bathroom, new look, next-day use.
