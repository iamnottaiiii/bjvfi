Services

Heating and cooling, handled

Every service below is available by phone at (747) 209-2108. Call for hours and scheduling.

01

Air conditioning repair

Systems that do not cool, blow weak air, leak water, freeze up, or make grinding and rattling sounds. We diagnose the fault and repair it, from capacitors and contactors to refrigerant and blower issues.

02

Heating and furnace repair

Furnaces that will not ignite, short cycle, or push cold air, plus heat pump trouble. Fast fixes to keep your home warm through the cold snaps.

03

Maintenance and tune ups

Seasonal service that includes coil cleaning, filter checks, electrical testing, and airflow measurement. Regular tune ups cut breakdowns and lower energy bills.

04

New system installation

When a unit is beyond sensible repair, we size and install a replacement matched to your home, ductwork, and budget, and haul the old equipment away.

05

Thermostats and controls

Faulty thermostats, inaccurate readings, and upgrades to programmable or smart controls that give you real control over comfort and cost.

06

Indoor air quality

Filtration upgrades, duct inspection, and ventilation checks that reduce dust, odors, and stale air inside the home.
