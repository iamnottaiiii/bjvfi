Our story

The ledger never lies

JC Auto Repair, LLC is a working auto shop on Twist Lane in Springfield, Virginia, and it runs the way good shops have always run: the car goes on the lift, the problem gets found, the fix gets quoted before it gets done. The name customers ask for is Juan Carlos, an easygoing mechanic with a reputation for honest work at competitive rates.

There is no upsell script here. Reviewers describe the same experience again and again: the shop looks at what is actually wrong, fixes it promptly, and the bill matches the quote. Water pumps, check engine lights, brakes, tires, transmissions, the everyday failures that strand people, get handled the same way: found, explained, fixed.

Word of mouth does the advertising. Other vendors in the area have sent drivers here when they could not take the work themselves, and travelers passing through have had breakdowns sorted the same visit. Asian, European and American vehicles all roll through the bays, and new and used tires keep the lot busy too.

If you are looking for a mechanic you can trust with the family car, that is the whole pitch. Bring it in, get a straight answer, drive it home.
