Our services

Complete dental care for the whole family

Prevention first, treatment when you need it. Choose a category to see what the practice offers.

Preventive dentistry

Regular prevention is the simplest way to keep your smile healthy for life.

  • Routine exams - thorough checkups that catch small issues early
  • Professional cleanings - removal of plaque and tartar buildup
  • Dental fillings - durable repairs for cavities and minor damage
  • Family checkups - care tailored for kids, adults and seniors

Restorative dentistry

Repairing and rebuilding teeth so they look and feel like your own.

  • Tooth-colored fillings - natural-looking cavity repair
  • Deep cleaning - scaling and root planing for gum disease
  • Crowns and bridges - ask the office about restorative options
  • Personalized plans - treatment options explained in plain language

Surgical and gum care

In-office treatment for more involved dental needs.

  • Dental extractions - gentle removal of problem teeth
  • Bone grafts - supporting healthy jaw structure
  • Gum grafts - care for receding or damaged gum tissue
  • Follow-up care - clear aftercare guidance at every step

About the practice

Dr. Spiro Papadatos, DMD

Dr. Papadatos leads A Plus Family Dentistry, a family dental practice on Monmouth Street in Jersey City. His approach is simple: treat every patient like family, explain every option clearly, and keep the experience as comfortable as possible.

Patients describe him as gentle, meticulous and caring, with great bedside manner and a calm presence that puts even nervous patients at ease. The friendly office staff and prompt appointments keep visits easy from check-in to checkout.

The practice also serves patients at a second location, A Plus Family Dentistry Down the Shore in Neptune, NJ.
