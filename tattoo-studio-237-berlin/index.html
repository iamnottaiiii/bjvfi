About the studio

Tattoo Studio 237 is a tattoo and piercing shop serving Berlin and the surrounding South Jersey towns, with a sister shop in Westville. One family, two chairs, same standard.

The crew

Resident Tattoo Artists

A rotating crew of tattoo artists covering custom work, flash, blackwork, fine line and traditional American. Ask the shop who is in the chair this week and see their flash.

The crew

Professional Piercer

Clean, clinical piercing with proper technique and quality jewelry. The Studio 237 piercers are known across both shops for steady hands and honest advice.

The standard

Clean Shop, Always

Single-use needles, hospital-grade sterilization, fresh barriers on every station. The studio holds itself to the standard you would want for your own skin.

Good to know: the shop is 18+ with valid ID. Deposits are typically required to book custom appointments and go toward your final price. Call (856) 809-2650 for the current booking policy.
