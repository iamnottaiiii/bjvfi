What we do

Concrete work done right the first time

Concrete is permanent. The prep, the pour, and the finish all have to be right while the mix is still wet. That is why we plan every job around the site itself: grade, drainage, access, and schedule.

Services

How every project runs

Amador Development Co is a concrete contractor. Concrete installation, repair, and replacement work is quoted per project after a site evaluation, so you pay for the job you actually need.

01

Project consultation

Call (626) 334-2011 and describe the project: new install, repair, or replacement. We ask the right questions up front so nothing gets missed.

02

Site evaluation

We review the site conditions that drive the scope: grade, drainage, access for equipment, and existing concrete.

03

Written quote

You get the scope and the price in writing before any work is scheduled. No vague estimates, no mid-job surprises.

04

Scheduled work

The job gets a firm date. We arrive with forms, mix, and crew ready to pour, finish, and clean up.
