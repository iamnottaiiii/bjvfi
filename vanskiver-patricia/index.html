Hygiene care

Preventive dental hygiene care focused on clean teeth and healthy gums. Call the office to ask which services are currently offered.

Professional cleanings

A dental hygienist removes plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing leave behind, then polishes teeth for a smooth, clean feel. Call the office to ask about scheduling a cleaning visit.

Gum health checks

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile. Hygiene visits include a look at your gums for early signs of trouble, with guidance on what to do about it.

Better habits at home

The right brushing and flossing technique makes every cleaning last longer. Ask for personalized tips at your visit, tailored to your teeth and gums.
