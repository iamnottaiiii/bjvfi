Our story

A family shop
Creighton trusts

Simpson Tire Services Inc is family-owned and run. Customers know the family by name, and many have been coming back for decades.

The short version

Big tire chains sell volume. Simpson Tire sells trust. The family gives you an upfront price, gets you in fast, and does the job right the first time. That is why drivers keep coming back for 10, 20, even 30 years, and why they send their kids here too.

Ask around Creighton and you will hear the same advice: "Always go see Mike."
