Our Story

A Lynn cleaning team that sweats the details

Nova Cleaning inc is a cleaning service based at 2 Haines Terrace in Lynn, Massachusetts. We serve homes and small businesses across Lynn and nearby North Shore communities.

Our approach is simple: show up on time, follow a thorough checklist in every room, and treat your space like it is our own. No rushed jobs, no skipped corners.

  • Lynn based, serving the North Shore
  • Residential and commercial cleaning
  • Free estimates before every job
  • Supplies and equipment included

How a visit works

  • 1. Free estimate. Call (781) 632-2166 and describe your space.
  • 2. Scheduled visit. We confirm a day and time that suits you.
  • 3. Room by room clean. Our checklist covers kitchens, baths, bedrooms and common areas.
  • 4. Final walkthrough. We check the work before we leave.
