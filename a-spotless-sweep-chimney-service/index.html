Our story

It started on a beach in Jamaica

Back in 1992, on vacation in Jamaica, our founder met David Foot, owner of Footie's Chimney Sweep of Gloucester. David trained him in the trade and encouraged him to start his own business. He did, and he never looked back.

More than three decades later, A Spotless Sweep is still a small, two-person operation serving Lynn and the North Shore. There was never a big advertising budget. The business was built the old-fashioned way: one satisfied homeowner telling the next.

Along the way came real credentials to back up the craft: certification in masonry from the Wentworth Institute of Technology, and certification in chimney cleaning and repair from the Chimney Sweep Institute of America. Fully licensed and insured, background checked, and named a Thumbtack Top Pro.

What has not changed is the favorite part of the job: sharing expertise and helping homeowners understand their options, so every chimney we leave behind is a safe one.
