Our Story

A Burbank crew that decided gate work deserved its own specialists.

Who we are

One specialty, done well

Burbank Side & Front Gates Services focuses on a single trade: the gates around your home or business. Side gates, front gates, and the electric motors that drive them. Because it is all we do, we have seen every failure a gate can throw at you, and we know the fix that actually lasts.

Customers describe the experience the same way: a technician who arrives on time, explains the problem plainly, and repairs it without pushing extras. That is the whole business model.

How we work

Straightforward from the first call

You describe the problem, we come look at it, and we tell you what it needs and what it costs before any work starts. If the motor can be repaired, we repair it. If a hinge is the real culprit, we say so. Reviews from Burbank homeowners back it up: professional manner, fair price, no upsell.

What we stand by

Three promises

Good price, stated up front. Reviewers specifically note we do not try to upsell.

On-time work

We show up when we say we will and finish the job in a professional manner.

Done right

Fast and economical repairs that hold. If it is not right, we make it right.
