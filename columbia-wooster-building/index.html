The road home

How living here works Sample process

Every building has its own rhythm. Here is the typical path from first visit to settled neighbor. Details vary by unit and by the association's current rules, so call to confirm each step.

  1. 01

    See the building

    Drive Wooster Pike, walk the grounds, and get a feel for the street, the parking, and the neighbors you will share a hallway with.

  2. 02

    Meet the association

    Ask for the current declaration, bylaws, fee schedule, and any move-in requirements before you sign anything.

  3. 03

    Move in

    Coordinate elevator or stairwell time, parking assignments, and key or fob handoff with building management.

  4. 04

    Settle in

    Introduce yourself, learn the trash and recycling routine, and show up to the next association meeting. That is how buildings stay good.
