Our story

The shop on Crown Point Rd

Swift Auto Services is a neighborhood auto repair shop in West Deptford, built around one idea: fix it right, explain it plainly, charge fairly.

Straightforward car care

Nobody likes car trouble, and nobody likes the bill that comes with it. We started Swift Auto Services to be the shop we would want to take our own cars to: the kind of place where the estimate is clear before the work begins, and where nobody talks you into repairs you do not need.

We keep our bays open early on weekdays and through the weekend, because breakdowns do not check your calendar. Drop your car off in the morning, and we will call you with a diagnosis and a number you can count on.

How we work

1. You tell us the symptom. The noise, the light, the leak. We listen.

2. We diagnose and quote. A real inspection, then a straight answer on cost, before any work starts.

3. We fix it and road-test it. You pick up a car that drives like it should, with the old parts back in your trunk if you want them.
