What to expect

When you call,
this is what happens

A water damage emergency is stressful. Knowing the process takes the fear out of it.

You call

Describe what happened: burst pipe, appliance leak, sewage backup, storm water. The team prioritizes by urgency and gets a crew moving.

We assess

On arrival, the crew finds the water source, checks how far moisture has spread with meters, and explains the plan in plain language.

We extract and dry

Standing water comes out with pumps and vacuums. Then air movers and dehumidifiers run until everything reads dry, including the hidden spots.

We restore

Contaminated areas are sanitized, damaged materials are repaired or replaced, and the space is returned to a safe, livable condition.

Good to know

A few things worth knowing
about water damage

Fast action protects your home and your wallet.

Mold starts in 24 to 48 hours

Damp drywall and carpet can start growing mold within a day or two. Drying quickly is the cheapest mold prevention there is.

Moisture hides

Water wicks up drywall and under flooring. Professional meters find what your eyes and hands cannot.

Not all water is equal

Clean supply-line water, gray water from appliances, and black water from sewage each need different handling and sanitizing.

Document everything

Photos before cleanup, moisture readings during drying, and an itemized scope all help if you file an insurance claim.
