Our services

Chimney Inspections

Estimates are free. Inspection pricing quoted before the visit.
  • Full visual inspection of the chimney exterior, crown, flashing, and firebox
  • Camera scan of the flue to spot cracks, gaps, and blockages you cannot see from below
  • Written findings with photos, suitable for insurance records and real estate transactions
  • Creosote level check and burn-safety assessment
  • Clear next steps: sweep, repair, or all clear, ranked by urgency

Tip: the National Fire Protection Association recommends a chimney inspection at least once a year.

Sweeping & Cleaning

Sample starting price: contact us for current rates.
  • Complete creosote and soot removal from flue walls
  • Firebox, smoke chamber, and damper cleaning
  • Debris and animal nest removal
  • Drop cloths and HEPA-filtered vacuums to protect your home from dust
  • Post-cleaning inspection notes included with every sweep

Pricing is a sample range. Final pricing is confirmed in your free, itemized estimate.

Chimney Repairs

Repairs are quoted per job after inspection. Estimates are free.
  • Brick and mortar repair, repointing, and tuckpointing
  • Chimney crown repair and waterproofing
  • Flashing repair and replacement where the chimney meets the roof
  • Smoke chamber and firebox rebuild work
  • Water leak diagnosis: staining, odors, and damp fireplace smells traced to the source

Freeze-thaw cycles in Maryland winters are hard on masonry. Small cracks now become big repairs later.

Liners, Caps & Dampers

Parts plus installation, quoted per job. Estimates are free.
  • Stainless steel chimney liner installation and replacement
  • Chimney cap installation to block rain, animals, and debris
  • Top-sealing and throat damper repair or replacement
  • Spark arrestor and animal guard add-ons
  • Draft and smoke-draw troubleshooting

A damaged liner is a fire risk. If your liner is cracked or missing, get it checked before your next fire.
