Why it matters

Three signs your attic needs attention.

Rooms that never cool down, energy bills that climb every summer, and insulation that looks thin, dirty or matted are the usual tells. Old insulation also traps allergens and gives pests a place to nest.

Attic cleaning removes the old material and debris first, then new insulation goes in at the right depth for the space. For new construction, insulation is installed as the build goes up, so the envelope is sealed from day one.

"Clean first, then insulate. The order matters more than most people think."Standard attic-service practice
