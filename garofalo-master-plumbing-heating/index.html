About Garofalo

A licensed master plumbing company rooted in Queens.

Garofalo Master Plumbing & Heating is a plumbing and heating company based at 66-70 69th St in Flushing, Queens, serving homes and businesses across the borough. The company operates under NYC Licensed Master Plumber #1577, a license held in the Garofalo family name, and is a member of the Master Plumbers Council of the City of New York.

Customers describe the team as careful and quality-focused: technicians who explain the work, respect the home, and would rather do the job right than rush through it. One reviewer put it simply, saying they will keep Garofalo as their family's plumbers.

Whether it is a water heater that quit, a drain that will not clear, or a heating system that needs attention before winter, you get a licensed professional and a quote before any work starts.
