Our story

A Malden landscape designer with a simple belief.

Renald landscaping is a landscape design business based on Ashland Street in Malden, Massachusetts, serving homeowners across the city and nearby towns.

The belief is simple: a yard should be designed before it is planted. Too many landscapes are a collection of random shrubs. Ours start with a plan that considers light, soil, water, and the way your family actually spends time outside.

The result is an outdoor space that feels inevitable, like it was always meant to look that way, and that grows more beautiful with each season instead of needing constant rescue.
