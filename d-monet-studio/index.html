D-Monet Studio is a women's hair salon in the Congress Heights neighborhood of Southeast Washington, known for high quality work and an emphasis on hair health.

The studio is owned by Danielle M, a licensed hair care specialist with over 20 years of experience in the DC metropolitan area. Her work has been featured in hair magazines and has graced the covers of hair trade show magazines.

Clients describe the staff as knowledgeable, professional, talented, and friendly, and praise the attention to detail, the customer service, and the clean, professional, secure ambience of the studio.

Whether your hair is relaxed or natural, the focus is the same: healthy hair first, styled to fit the uniqueness of you and your lifestyle.

Our story

The art of hair,
fit to your life

D-Monet Studio was built on a simple idea: bring the art of hair styling to fit the uniqueness of each person and their lifestyle.

Quality hair care services, coupled with the ultimate customer service experience. Danielle M, owner

D-Monet Studio is a women's hair salon at 3130 Martin Luther King Jr Ave SE in Washington's Congress Heights neighborhood, offering relaxed hair services, natural hair care, weaves, hair extensions, non-surgical hair replacements, and braiding.

The studio is owned by Danielle M, a licensed hair care specialist with over 20 years of experience in the DC metropolitan area. Her passion is bringing the art of hair styling to fit the uniqueness of each person and their lifestyle, and she takes pride in offering quality hair care coupled with the ultimate customer service experience.

Her editorial work has been featured in various hair magazines and has graced the cover of hair trade show magazines.

Clients describe the studio's ambience as clean, professional, and secure, and the staff as friendly, talented, and attentive. Each client is treated with the utmost care, and customers rave about the attention to detail and the emphasis on hair health.

Every budget, every texture, every woman

D-Monet introduces women of all races and colors to the salon, offering a variety of services in a range of prices designed to fit your budget. Healthy hair first, beautiful styling always.
