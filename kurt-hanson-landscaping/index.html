Services

Landscaping services, end to end.

From weekly mowing to seasonal cleanups, we keep homes and small commercial properties in Worcester looking their best. Call for a quote tailored to your property.

Lawn mowing and maintenance

Scheduled mowing with crisp edging and trimming around beds, walkways, and fences. We can set up a weekly or biweekly route so your lawn never gets ahead of you.

  • Weekly and biweekly mowing routes
  • Edging and line trimming
  • Blowing walkways and driveways clean

Spring and fall cleanups

Seasonal cleanups clear out leaves, branches, and winter debris so your lawn and beds start each season fresh. A single visit can transform a tired-looking yard.

  • Leaf and debris removal
  • Bed re-edging and mulch refresh
  • Storm debris hauling

Garden beds, edging, and mulch

Defined beds with fresh mulch make the whole property look cared for. We shape beds, lay crisp edges, and spread mulch that keeps weeds down and moisture in.

  • Bed shaping and natural edging
  • Mulch installation and refresh
  • Weed control in beds

Hedge and shrub trimming

Overgrown hedges shrink a yard. Regular trimming keeps shrubs healthy, shapely, and off your siding, walkways, and windows.

  • Seasonal hedge trimming
  • Overgrowth cutback
  • Small tree pruning
