Our story

The cleanest corner of State Street

Eastrock Laundry is a locally run laundromat in Hamden, Connecticut, built on a simple idea: a laundromat should be a place you are happy to spend an hour.

Built different on purpose

Walk into Eastrock and the first thing you notice is that it does not look or smell like the laundromats you remember. New machines. Bright, air conditioned space. Floors that shine. Folding benches with actual room to fold.

The second thing you notice is the people. An attendant is there when the doors open, keeping everything spotless and helping anyone who needs it, in English or Spanish. Customers describe the owner as genuinely helpful, especially with non-English speaking neighbors.

Set at 1946 State St near Dunkin Donuts and the DMV, with ample parking out front, Eastrock has become the go-to for Hamden residents who want laundry day to be easy. Fair prices, honest machines, and a place that respects your time. That is the whole story, and customers say it shows.
