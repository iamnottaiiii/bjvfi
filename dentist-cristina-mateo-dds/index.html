Services

Complete care for every smile

General, restorative, and preventive dentistry for adults and families. Treatment descriptions below are typical for this kind of practice. Call (718) 454-2442 to confirm exactly what is offered and what your visit will include.

01

Exams and cleanings

Routine checkups, professional cleanings, and X-rays that catch small problems before they grow.

Most patients visit twice a year. Call to book your first exam.

02

Tooth colored fillings

Natural looking composite fillings that repair cavities and blend with your teeth.

Ask about same visit options when you call.

03

Crowns and bridges

Durable restorations that rebuild damaged teeth and replace missing ones, planned around your bite.

Treatment plans and costs are explained before work begins.

04

Extractions

Gentle removal of teeth that cannot be saved, with clear aftercare guidance for a smooth recovery.

Sedation and comfort options available, ask the office.

05

Dentures

Full and partial dentures fitted for comfort, function, and a natural look.

Multiple fittings ensure the right fit. Call for details.

06

Emergency dental care

Toothaches, chipped teeth, lost crowns, and other urgent problems seen as quickly as possible.

Call the office first so the team can prepare for your arrival.
