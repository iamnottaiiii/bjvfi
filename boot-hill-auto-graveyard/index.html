The Legend of Boot Hill

A junkyard with a name straight out of the old west, sitting on Twelve Mile Road in New Richmond, Ohio.

Boot Hill Auto Graveyard leans into its name: rows of retired iron resting under the Ohio sky, each one still holding parts with miles left in them. It is a working salvage yard, not a museum. Folks come to pull a fender, hunt down a tire, or sell off the old beast that finally gave up.

What the records say

Public business records list Boot Hill Auto Graveyard as a privately held junkyard and automotive wholesaler at 1217 Twelve Mile Rd, New Richmond, OH 45157. Public directories list daily hours of 9 AM to 6 PM and note that credit and debit cards are accepted. The yard is also listed on junk car buying directories as a place that purchases vehicles in rough condition.
