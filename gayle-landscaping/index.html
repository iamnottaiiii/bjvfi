Our Story

Gayle Landscaping is a Harrisburg landscaping company built on a simple idea: show up, do the work right, and treat every property like it matters.

Rooted in Harrisburg,
growing with our neighbors

From 2311 N Front St, Gayle Landscaping serves homes and properties across the Harrisburg area. We are the crew that notices the details: the edge line along the walk, the bed that needs fresh mulch, the branch that should come down before the next storm.

Landscaping is our trade and gardening is our craft. We design plantings for how central Pennsylvania actually grows, maintain them through the season, and keep properties safe and clear when winter arrives.

Most of our work comes the old fashioned way: a neighbor sees the yard, asks who does it, and calls. We would like to earn that from you too.
