Our story

Formerly Lotus Nails and Spa, Kyla Nail Spa was reborn around one idea: the health of the natural nail comes first. That focus earned recognition from the Viet Nails and Beauty Association for outstanding performance in cleanliness, sanitation, and creative designs. Technicians like Quang, Willow, and Kim take time with every set, walk clients to their cars in the rain, and remember your name. Bright, beautiful, and clean.
