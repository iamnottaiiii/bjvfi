A Meriden Tire Shop Built On Fair Deals

Ponce Turnpike Tire LLC is a Connecticut limited liability company registered on December 5, 2019, and its status with the state is active. The shop sits on North Broad Street in Meriden, serving drivers across the area with tires, alignments, and mufflers.

The business describes itself simply: alignments, used tires, new tires, and mufflers for the Meriden, CT area. Customers describe it the same way, adding the words that matter most: honest, helpful, polite, and fair on price.
