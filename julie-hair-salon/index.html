About Julie Hair Salon

Julie Hair Salon is a Brooklyn hair salon serving the Fort Hamilton neighborhood. Reviewers describe a stylist with a kind personality and real attention to detail, patient and fun staff, and results that keep clients returning for years.

The salon is known for reliable hair coloring and blowouts, affordable prices, and a warm, friendly atmosphere where individual needs come first.
