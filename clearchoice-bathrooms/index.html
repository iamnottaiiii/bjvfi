Remodeling

Four ways to rebuild your bathroom.

Every project starts with a consultation and a written scope. Pricing is project-specific, so items marked Sample are examples only. Call (818) 806-6560 for an exact quote.

The Refresh

A cosmetic reset that makes an dated bathroom feel new without moving a single pipe. Ideal when the layout works but the finishes do not.

  • Vanity, mirror, and lighting upgrades
  • Toilet and faucet replacement
  • Re-grout, re-caulk, and paint
  • New hardware and accessories

Timeline: typically a few days · Pricing: Sample, call for a quote

Tub to Shower Conversion

The most requested project: remove the old tub and build a walk-in shower with proper waterproofing, a low threshold, and tile you choose.

  • Tub removal and drain reconfiguration
  • Full waterproofing system behind the tile
  • Glass door or open walk-in design
  • Built-in niche and bench options

Timeline: typically one to two weeks · Pricing: Sample, call for a quote

Full Gut Remodel

Down to the studs and back up again. New layout, new plumbing runs, new electrical, custom tile, and fixtures selected around how you actually use the room.

  • Layout redesign and permit handling
  • New plumbing, electrical, and ventilation
  • Custom tile, stone, and shower systems
  • Vanity, lighting, and mirror design

Timeline: typically three to five weeks · Pricing: Sample, call for a quote

Accessible Bathrooms

Safer bathing without the institutional look. Curbless entries, grab bars that match your fixtures, and seating designed into the tile.

  • Curbless and low-threshold showers
  • Grab bars and handheld shower systems
  • Comfort-height toilets and lever hardware
  • Non-slip tile and improved lighting

Timeline: project dependent · Pricing: Sample, call for a quote

Our Story

ClearChoice Bathrooms keeps it simple: one room, done properly.

The name is the mission. When a homeowner in La Cañada Flintridge calls ClearChoice Bathrooms, the choices are clear: a written scope, honest materials, licensed trade work, and a crew that treats the rest of the house like it is not a construction site.

Foothill homes come with quirks: older plumbing, tight footprints, and bathrooms added in eras with different ideas about waterproofing. A specialist sees those quirks coming. We plan for them in the estimate instead of discovering them in week three.

We would rather talk you out of a bad idea than build it and bill you.
