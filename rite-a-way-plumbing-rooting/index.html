Our story

A Shadow Hills shop with a simple standard

Rite-A-Way Plumbing & Rooting works out of 9837 Sunland Blvd in Shadow Hills, a foothill neighborhood where older homes and small businesses sit side by side. That mix is the reason the shop lists antique plumbing repair alongside modern repiping: the houses here have history, and their pipes do too.

The business keeps the model straightforward. Answer the phone, show up, explain the work, do it right, and leave the place clean. Reviewers keep coming back to the same themes: fast scheduling, technicians who teach as they work, and repairs that hold up.
