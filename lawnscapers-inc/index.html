    About

    Rooted in Fairfax since 1989

    You will find us at the corner of Spring Street and Wooster Pike in Fairfax, where we have been caring for greater Cincinnati landscapes for 37 years.

    Lawnscapers, Inc. is a complete lawn and landscaping company specializing in commercial accounts throughout greater Cincinnati and residential homeowners in the central and eastern communities.

    We do all of our work in house with our own full time team, which grows from 9 to 20 people during peak season. That means the crew that designs your project is the crew that builds it, and the quality you deserve and expect is the quality you get.

    Our philosophy has not changed since day one: we want a relationship with you, not just a transaction. Fast, friendly, reliable service, honest answers to every question, and entry level pricing on top level service for new customers.
