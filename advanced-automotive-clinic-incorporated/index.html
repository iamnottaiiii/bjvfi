Engineers under the hood

Most shops start with a wrench. Ours started with engineering. Advanced Automotive Clinic was founded by engineers and technicians with more than 25 years of field experience, and that shows in how every repair is approached: like a diagnosis, not a guess.

Our story

Twenty years of straight answers

Advanced Automotive Clinic Incorporated is a Honda and Acura specialist auto repair shop on Crain Highway in Brandywine, founded by engineers and technicians and serving Brandywine, Waldorf, and Charles County for over 20 years.

The engineering habit

The shop describes itself simply: a new automobile repair facility founded by engineers and technicians with more than 25 years of experience in the field. That engineering habit shapes everything, from the first scan to the final road test.

Where some shops replace parts until the problem stops, the clinic diagnoses. The check engine light gets traced to its cause. The brake job gets measured, not eyeballed. The estimate gets explained in plain language before a single bolt turns.

"Doc has been working on my family's cars for many years. They do great work." A theme from customer reviews

The long game

Twenty-plus years in one spot on Crain Highway teaches a shop what matters: honesty, fair pricing, and work that holds up. Customers describe the team as professional and the pricing as on point, and they keep coming back, often bringing the whole family.

It is an appointment-only shop, which is part of the formula. Every car gets dedicated time instead of being squeezed between walk-ins. Call (301) 782-7778 to book yours, new customers are welcome.
