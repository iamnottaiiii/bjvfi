About the shop

Two Names, One Trade

Eagle & Zureick Masons is a small Cincinnati masonry contractor with a long record and a short phone number.

Eagle & Zureick Masons has operated out of Mt. Healthy, Ohio since 1980. That is 46 years of laying brick and block for Greater Cincinnati, through every kind of weather the Ohio Valley can throw at a wall.

Public business records list partner Daniel J. Eagle as the principal contact, and the company is on file with the BBB of Cincinnati for residential and commercial brick and block masonry.

Built Like a Keystone

A keystone is the wedge at the top of an arch. It looks small, but pull it out and the whole arch falls. That is the mason's philosophy: the small details, the plumb lines, the full joints, are what hold everything up.

Eagle and Zureick put their names on the arch. Then they build it to stay.
