The community

Hunter's Ridge, Burlington KY

A tree-lined boulevard, miles of sidewalks, 50 acres of planned green space and a community pool. Country setting, city convenience, just off Burlington Pike.

1

The setting

Hunter's Ridge is nestled in a beautiful country setting in Burlington, Kentucky, with a tree-lined boulevard as its centerpiece. It feels removed, but Burlington Pike puts shopping, dining and I-71/75 within easy reach.

2

Built for walking

Miles of sidewalks wind through the neighborhood, so evening walks, bike rides and stroller loops are part of daily life here, not an afterthought.

3

50 acres of green space

The community plan includes 50 acres of green space, plus a community pool for summer afternoons. New sections have opened recently, with more home sites on the way.

4

A builder with a local name

Arlinghaus Builders builds across Northern Kentucky. At Hunter's Ridge you work directly with the on-site sales team, who know every plan, lot and upgrade option by heart.

5

Good to know

Recent listings in Hunter's Ridge report HOA dues of about $21 per month. Confirm current dues, restrictions and the community pool schedule with the sales office before you buy.
