Our Story

From Modern Nails to EllE: the same trusted hands, a fresh new chapter

EllE Nail Spa grew out of Modern Nails, the longtime Hebron salon at 32 Main Street. When the name changed, the things that mattered stayed: the skilled team, the loyal guests, and the commitment to doing nails properly.

Today the salon is known as a clean, elegant spot in the heart of Hebron where the staff is friendly and welcoming, the chairs are comfortable, and the work speaks for itself. Guests bring photos of dream designs, book ahead for weddings and holidays, and come back every few weeks for gel that simply does not quit.

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, expect to be greeted warmly, seated comfortably, and sent out with nails you cannot stop looking at.
