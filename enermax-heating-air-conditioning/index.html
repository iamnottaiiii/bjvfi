The repair-first promise

Plenty of HVAC companies make their money on replacements. The review record here points the other way: diagnose honestly, repair when repair is right, replace only when the numbers favor it.

Fair pricing gets mentioned by name in customer feedback. That is not an accident; it is a business model.
