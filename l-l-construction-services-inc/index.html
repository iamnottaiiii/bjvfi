What we do

One call covers the whole project

Painting is our signature, but we handle the remodeling, repair, and finishing work around it, so you deal with one trusted crew, not five subcontractors.

Interior & Exterior Painting

Full repaints inside and out, plus elastomeric coatings built for California sun. We prep every surface thoroughly: wood, sheetrock, plaster, and metal.

Cabinet Refinishing

Kitchen cabinet refinishing that looks like new cabinets at a fraction of the cost, plus staining, finishing, and custom looks designed with you.

Remodeling & Additions

Bathroom and kitchen remodels, additions, home offices, doors, windows, and skylights, additions and remodels handled start to finish.

Drywall, Stucco & Texture

Drywall patching, finishing, and installation. Stucco and drywall repair. Acoustic removal and repair. Plaster and texture repair that blends invisibly.

Wood Repair & Preservation

Wood repair and replacement, pressure washing decks, and wood preservation, plus crown and chair rail molding and custom cabinetry and molding.

Homes, Condos & Commercial

Apartment buildings, commercial buildings, and condo painting alongside single family homes, serving Santa Clarita and surrounding Los Angeles.
