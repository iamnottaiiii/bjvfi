The Shop

Zang's Auto Body sits at 4165 Harrison Ave in Cincinnati's West Side, a working auto body shop with a simple philosophy: treat every car like it is your own, and every customer like a neighbor.

In the shop's own words, from its public directory profile: "Located on the Westside of Cincinnati Zang's cares just as much about the community as we do about the quality of work we produce."

That community spirit shows in the details: clear communication, careful craftsmanship, and cars returned looking their best.
