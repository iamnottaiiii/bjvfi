Services

Full service pool care.

Pool Tigers focuses on pool maintenance for residential and commercial customers: regular cleaning, repairs, and chemical treatments. Details below are typical examples. Call for specifics and pricing.

Weekly cleaning visits

Regular cleaning visits keep the pool swimmable and protect the finish and equipment. Typical example of what a visit covers:

  • Surface skimming and debris removal
  • Brushing walls, steps and tile line
  • Vacuuming the pool floor
  • Emptying skimmer and pump baskets
Ask About Scheduling

Chemical and water care

Balanced water protects swimmers, the pool surface and the equipment. Typical example of water care:

  • Testing chlorine, pH and alkalinity
  • Chemical dosing and balancing
  • Shock treatments when needed
  • Algae prevention and treatment
Ask About Water Care

Repairs

Equipment problems caught during visits, before they turn into bigger bills. Typical example of repair work:

  • Pump and filter troubleshooting
  • Cleaner and automation checks
  • Leak and plumbing referrals
  • Heater and light inspections
Report a Problem

Commercial pools

Service for shared and commercial pools that need consistent, documented care. Typical example:

  • Scheduled service for HOAs and facilities
  • Chemical logging and compliance basics
  • High use season scheduling
Discuss Commercial Service
