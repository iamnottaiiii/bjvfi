DWG WD-03 Work Procedure REV A

02How a job runs

A fixed procedure keeps surprises out of your invoice and your day.

01

Intake & symptom log

Tell us what you hear, feel, or see. We record the symptoms exactly as reported, no translation loss.

02

Inspect & measure

Diagnostics and physical inspection. We find the cause and price the repair before any work begins.

03

Approve the spec

You approve a clear, itemized scope. Nothing gets done that you did not sign off on.

04

Repair & verify

The work is performed and re-checked. The car leaves only when it meets the standard it came in for.

DWG WD-20 About / Facility Record REV A

20About the shop

Wölfsburg Den - Cincinnati is an independent auto repair shop in Cincinnati, Ohio. The name is a nod to German engineering culture. Precision, documentation, and honest work are the house standards.

Operating principle

A car is a machine with published tolerances. Our job is to return it to those tolerances, show you the measurements, and charge you only for what was actually required. That is the entire business model.

Independence notice

This is an independent shop. It is not a franchise, not a dealer, and not authorized or endorsed by any vehicle manufacturer. Any manufacturer names are used only to describe the vehicles we service.
