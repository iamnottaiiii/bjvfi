Our Story

Millay & Co is a general contractor based at 3619 O'Hara Road, Suite C, in Erlanger, Kentucky, serving homeowners and businesses across Northern Kentucky.

Building in Northern Kentucky since the early 2000s

Public records show Millay & Co has pulled more than 70 building permits since 2003, and Kentucky licensing records list active contractor and electrical licenses. That is two decades of showing up, pulling permits, and finishing jobs.

Today the company takes on custom homes, remodels, additions, and commercial and industrial builds, managing every trade under one roof so clients deal with one crew and one standard of quality.
