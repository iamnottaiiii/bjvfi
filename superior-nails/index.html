Our story

Superior Nails is a locally owned nail salon in the Manokeek Village Center, built on friendly service and careful work.

The salon

Owned by neighbors,
run like family

Superior Nails sits in the Manokeek Village Center in Accokeek, an easy stop for anyone in southern Prince George's County. Inside you will find a calm, organized salon with comfortable pedicure chairs and technicians who take pride in detailed work.

Clients describe the atmosphere as relaxing and the staff as friendly and welcoming. Many regulars have been coming for years, and they ask for their technician by name.

Whether you need a quick polish change or a full spa afternoon, you will be greeted warmly and looked after from start to finish.

D&H

Dao and Harley

Owners and operators. Clients call them the nicest people in the salon, and they are usually right there working alongside the team.

Attention to detail

Careful shaping, neat finishes and designs that match your reference. Speak up during your visit, the team wants to get it right.

Comfort first

Relaxing pedicure chairs, a calm room and a pace that never feels rushed. Your appointment is your time.

Honest and local

A neighborhood shop where the owners know the regulars. Ask about pricing before you start so there are no surprises.
