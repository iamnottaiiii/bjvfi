Get in touch

Start with an inspection

Most roof problems are cheaper to fix early. If you see a stain on the ceiling, granules in the gutter, or shingles that look curled or cracked, call and describe what you see. You will get a clear plan and a clear price before any work begins.

No pressure. No mystery line items. Just the work your roof actually needs.

How a project runs

Every job follows the same straightforward sequence, so you always know what happens next.

  • 1Call and describe the problem. Photos help if you have them.

Services

Roofing and contracting, done right

Jr Walker Roofing is a general contractor based at 2951 Santa Rosa Ave in Altadena, CA, serving homeowners and small commercial properties across the San Gabriel Valley.

01

Roof replacement

Complete tear off and replacement. Shingle, tile, and flat roof systems installed to manufacturer spec, with new underlayment and flashing where the old roof has failed. Haul away and site cleanup included in every job.
02

Roof repair

Leak tracing to the true source, not just the stain. Damaged shingles and tiles replaced, flashing resealed or rebuilt, vent boots and pipe collars fixed, and storm damage patched before it spreads.
03

Roof inspections

A full condition check of the roof surface, flashing, gutters, and attic. Useful before buying or selling a home, after a wind or rain event, or when a roof is simply getting old and you want the facts.
04

Gutters and drainage

Gutter repair and replacement, downspout routing, and fixes that keep water moving away from the foundation instead of into the walls.
05

Skylights and vents

Skylight installation and resealing, plus attic ventilation improvements that cut heat buildup and extend shingle life.
06

General contracting

As a general contractor, the crew also handles the work around a roofing project: fascia and soffit repair, siding touch ups, and interior fixes from water damage. One call, one crew, one finished job.

Our story

A local contractor with a roofer's eye

Jr Walker Roofing operates out of Altadena, California, at 2951 Santa Rosa Ave. The business grew out of hands on contracting work across the San Gabriel Valley: roofs first, then the full range of general contracting that homeowners kept asking for once the roof was done.

What guides the work

Three rules on every job

Find the real leak

Water travels. The stain on the ceiling is rarely right under the hole. Every repair starts by tracing the problem to its source so the fix lasts.

Write it down

Scope, materials, and price go in writing before work starts. If something changes mid job, you hear about it first, not on the invoice.

Leave it clean

Tear offs make a mess. The crew hauls debris, runs magnets for nails, and walks the property with you before calling the job done.
