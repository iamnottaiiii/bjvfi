Our Story

A neighborhood shop on Pacific Ave

Long Beach Barber and Beauty sits in the heart of the Wrigley district, serving the neighbors who live and work along Pacific Avenue. We keep it simple: skilled hands, honest prices and a chair that is always open to you.
