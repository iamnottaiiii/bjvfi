Why it matters

Lint buildup is a fire risk. Dusty ducts are an air quality problem.

Dryers work harder and vents run hotter when lint chokes the line. Air ducts collect dust, pet dander, and debris that ends up back in the rooms you live in. A professional cleaning addresses both.

  • ✓Clothes take longer than they should to dry
  • ✓The dryer or laundry room feels unusually hot
  • ✓A burning or musty smell near vents
  • ✓Visible lint around the exterior vent cap
  • ✓Dust returns quickly after you clean

Noticing any of these? A call is the fastest way to get answers.

Services

Dryer vent and air duct services in Peabody, MA

Four focused services. Call (339) 232-0953 to talk through what your home needs.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Lint, dust, and debris are pulled from the full vent run, from behind the dryer to the exterior cap. A clear vent helps the dryer run cooler, dry faster, and use less energy.

Air Duct Cleaning

Supply and return ductwork is cleared of accumulated dust and debris. Cleaner ducts support cleaner air in every room the system serves.

Dryer Vent Repair

Disconnected elbows, crushed sections, and damaged runs get fixed so exhaust actually leaves the house. Options are explained and approved by you first.

Camera Vent Inspection

A camera is run through the full length of the vent to locate lint blockages, damage, bird nests, and disconnections hidden inside walls or ceilings.

When to call

If your dryer is telling you something, listen.

Most vent problems show up as small annoyances before they become real hazards. Here is what to watch for.

  • ✓Loads take two cycles to dry fully
  • ✓The top of the dryer is hot to the touch
  • ✓Lint is visible around the outside vent
  • ✓A burning smell during or after a cycle
  • ✓It has been years since the last cleaning
