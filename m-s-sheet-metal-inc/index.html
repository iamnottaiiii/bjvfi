Our Story

38 years of sparks and seams

The shop

A Northridge metal shop since day one

M & S Sheet Metal Inc set up at 18247 Parthenia St in Northridge and never left. Yellow Pages lists the business at 38 years in operation, a run built on repeat contractors, property managers, and homeowners who keep coming back with the next project.

The craft

Measure twice, cut once

Sheet metal work is unforgiving: a sixteenth off at the brake becomes a gap at the install. The shop's reputation rests on fittings that line up, seams that stay sealed, and edges finished safe to handle.

The area

Built for the San Fernando Valley

From Northridge to North Hollywood to Pacoima, the shop's ductwork and flashing sit on homes and businesses across the Valley, spec'd for heat, wind, and the occasional hard rain.

Today

Still answering the phone

No call centers, no ticket queues. Call (818) 885-7865 during shop hours and talk through your project with the people who will build it.
