Our Story

A cleaner built on careful work.

Americana Dry Cleaning sits on Amity Road in Woodbridge, serving the neighbors, commuters, and families who pass through every day.

How We Work

Slow down the process, speed up nothing that matters

Good dry cleaning is a craft of small decisions. Which solvent for this fabric. How much heat this pleat can take. Whether that spot needs a second treatment before it ever sees the press. We make those decisions one garment at a time.

01

Inspected at intake

Every piece is looked over with you at the counter. Spots are noted, loose buttons flagged, and special instructions written down.

02

Cleaned for the fabric

Wool, silk, rayon, blends, each fabric gets the process it needs rather than a one size fits all cycle.

03

Finished by hand

Pressing is done by hand and every order is inspected before it is bagged, so what you pick up looks the way you hoped.

Our Promise

If it is not right, we make it right

Check your order at the counter before you leave. If a collar is not sitting right or a spot needs another pass, say so, and we will take care of it. That is how a neighborhood cleaner earns its regulars.
