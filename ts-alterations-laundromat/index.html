Meet Theresa

The needle behind T's

T's Alterations & Laundromat started with a simple idea: one shop where North Haven can get laundry done in a place that feels clean and calm, and get clothes fitted by someone who truly cares about the result.

Theresa, the owner and seamstress, takes her time with every fitting. Customers notice. They talk about how she double checks measurements, explains the options, and treats every garment, from work pants to wedding dresses, like it matters. Because to her, it does.

"Theresa is the best seamstress around. She is such a caring person." Paraphrased from customer reviews

Pair that care with bright coin op machines, fair prices, and a shop that stays spotless, and you get the kind of neighborhood place people recommend to their friends.
