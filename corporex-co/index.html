About

A developer-builder with a hands-on approach

Corporex Development and Construction Management applies an intensive hands-on approach to every aspect of developing, designing, and constructing projects. The result is the highest quality product delivered at below-market construction costs.

Range

From build-outs to landmarks

Our team tackles projects ranging from tenant improvement build-outs to complex ground-up new developments, including manufacturing facilities, Class A office towers, and luxury residential landmarks like The Ascent at Roebling's Bridge in Kentucky.

KY + CO

Active developments across Northern Kentucky, Greater Cincinnati, and the Denver region.

Footprint

Shaping the region

Our portfolio includes RiverCenter Towers in Covington, the Ovation mixed-use development in Newport, Olympic Corporate Center in Erlanger's CirclePort, and Fitz 500 in Aurora, Colorado.

Approach

Accountability in every phase

Strategic planning blended with precise execution: needs analysis, coordinated MEP systems, detailed budgeting, and certification and closeout handled by in-house leadership.
