Our story

Brothers who build

A family-run building company from Oceanside, NY. We put our name on every job because our name is the business.

The Strianese way

Strianese Brothers builder and general contractor grew out of a simple idea: treat every house like it is your own family's. As brothers, we work side by side on every project, which means the people who quote your job are the people who show up to do it.

Being based at 3535 Knight St in Oceanside keeps us close to our customers across Nassau County and the South Shore. When you call, you talk to us, not a call center and not a sales rep.

  • Family-run: the owners are on the jobsite
