The short version

Quiet consistency, year after year

You will not find a lot of fuss at Mae's Cleaners. What you will find is a neighborhood dry cleaner that customers have trusted with everything from weekly shirts to expensive and delicate pieces.

Public reviews going back years describe the same experience: clothes cleaned as promised, ready when promised, and pressed properly. Fast service, fair prices, and a friendly counter.

Part dry cleaner, part laundromat, Mae's is the kind of corner business a neighborhood leans on: washers and dryers for self-service loads, plus full dry cleaning and wash and fold for the loads you would rather hand off.
