A Sierra Madre landscaping crew, through and through

Gagnier Landscape is a landscaping company based in Sierra Madre, California. Contractor directories date the company to 1996 and describe an established local crew working across Sierra Madre and the surrounding foothill neighborhoods.

The work covers the full life of a yard: design and planting, stone paver installation, lawn dethatching and turf care, grading and drainage, pond building, and the ongoing maintenance that keeps it all looking intentional.

Public business records list a crew of about sixteen, and contractor directories list the company as licensed in California. The model is simple: one local team that designs it, builds it and keeps caring for it.
