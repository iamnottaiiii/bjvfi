Services

Design, build, and care for every corner of your yard

A full service design and build landscape company. Every service below is done by our own knowledgeable team, never handed off and hoped for the best.

Landscape Design

Site-specific plans that balance beauty, drainage, sunlight, and budget. We design for how your family actually uses the space, not just how it photographs.

Planting and Garden Beds

Layered plantings chosen for Cincinnati soil and seasons, with bed edging and mulch that keep everything tidy and healthy.

Paver Patios and Walkways

Precision-set pavers with proper base and drainage, so your patio stays level and beautiful for decades. A customer favorite.

Retaining Walls

Engineered walls that tame slopes, stop erosion, and turn unusable hillsides into terraced, plantable space.

Outdoor Living Spaces

Fire pits, seating walls, and gathering areas that extend your home into the backyard for three seasons of the year.

LED Landscape Lighting

Low-voltage, energy efficient lighting that highlights trees and architecture, extends evenings outdoors, and improves safety.

Lawn Mowing

Dependable weekly mowing with crisp edges, so the whole property looks cared for between bigger projects.

Seasonal Maintenance

Spring cleanups, bed refreshes, pruning, and fall prep from the same crew that knows your landscape by name.

Drainage Solutions

Grading, swales, and drains that move water where it belongs and protect your foundation and plantings.

Pricing is quoted per project after a walkthrough. No two yards are alike, so call for a free consultation and an honest estimate.

Our story

East side roots, twenty years deep

Davidson Landscape LLC has been providing landscape solutions to the east side of Cincinnati since 2004.

One company, from sketch to Saturday mowing

Most landscapers do one thing. We built Davidson Landscape to do the whole thing: design the space, install the hardscape and plantings, light it beautifully, and then keep it looking that way with dependable maintenance.

Owner Bobby Davidson is personally on every project, and the same knowledgeable team that builds your patio is the team you see for seasonal care. That continuity is why our work holds up, and why our customers recommend us.
