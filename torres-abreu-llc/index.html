Our story

Two names. One standard: leave it better than we found it.

Torres & Abreu LLC started with a simple observation: the hardest tradesperson to find is the one who will actually come fix the small stuff. Big remodelers pass on half-day jobs. We built the whole business around them.

Local

Rooted in Kissimmee

Our shop sits at 2732 Barclay Ln in Kissimmee, and most of our work is a short drive away. We know the neighborhoods, the HOAs, and the quirks of Florida homes.

Personal

The owners do the work

When you call Torres & Abreu, you get Torres and Abreu. No rotating crews, no handoffs, no wondering who is in your house.

Honest

Straight about scope

If a job is bigger than a handyman should take on, we say so and help you find the right specialist. We would rather lose a job than do one badly.
