Chapter one

Notes from the workbench

Carr's Boardroom is a woodworker in Cincinnati, Ohio. Every project starts here, with pencil marks, sawdust, and an honest conversation about what you want built.

There is something a good piece of woodwork can do that flat-pack furniture never will. It holds a room together. It outlasts trends, and it tells the story of the hands that shaped it.

That is the whole idea behind Carr's Boardroom: sturdy, careful woodwork for homes and businesses around Cincinnati, built one project at a time.

Call to confirm: every project starts with a phone call, not a shopping cart.

Have a repair, a custom build, or a finish carpentry job in mind? Pick up the phone and describe it. You will get a straight answer about whether it is a fit.

Chapter three

About the shop

A small independent woodworking shop in Cincinnati, Ohio, run the old fashioned way: one project, one client, one careful build at a time.

A note pinned to the wall

Good woodwork is slow work. Measure twice, cut once, sand until it feels right, and finish it like it will be in someone's home for decades. Because it will.

That is the whole philosophy. Nothing fancy, just honest craft.
