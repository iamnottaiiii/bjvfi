Our story

A Brooklyn contractor that picks up the phone.

Cobra Contracting LLC. is a locally owned construction company based in Bensonhurst, Brooklyn. We grew the business the old-fashioned way: one project at a time, on word of mouth from neighbors who were happy with the work.

How we got here

Then

Started on the tools

Cobra Contracting began as a small crew taking on renovations, siding, and repair jobs across Brooklyn. Eight years later, the formula has not changed: show up on time, do clean work, and stand behind it.

Now

7416 Bay Pkwy, Bensonhurst

Today we run full residential and light commercial projects from our Brooklyn base, with accessibility construction and design as one of our specialties. Our clients are homeowners, landlords, and small businesses across the borough.
