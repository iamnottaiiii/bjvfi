Life Around the Lake

The Lake at Mundy's Mill is a suburban community of single-family homes in Jonesboro, in Clayton County. The neighborhood grew up around its lake, and shared amenities give it a settled, established feel.

  • Clubhouse for gatherings and events
  • Community lake with scenic views
  • Sidewalks and street lights on every street
  • Level homesites, many on quiet cul-de-sacs

About Crown Communities

For almost two decades, Crown Communities has built its reputation one neighborhood at a time.

What began as a small, family-owned Atlanta homebuilder grew into the largest builder in metro Atlanta. In 2014, D.R. Horton acquired Crown Communities' homebuilding operations, and Crown continues to operate as a separate division of America's Builder.

Across its communities, the company emphasizes building homes the right way from the ground up: defining effective community infrastructure, preserving green space, and applying finishing touches that give new communities an established look and feel.
