Our story

A neighborhood shop with a family feel.

Genesis and Barber Shop sits on East 4th Street in Bethlehem, and it runs the way neighborhood shops should: everybody on the team takes pride in the work, clients are treated with respect, and kids are as welcome as grown-ups.

The shop covers the full menu in one place, which is why families keep it on speed dial. Dad can get a fade and a shape up while mom gets her brows done and the kids get fresh cuts for school. Reviewers mention the owner and stylists by name and say they would not trust their hair to anyone else.

Walk in, call ahead, or just stop by when you are on 4th Street. The coffee is not the draw, the chairs are.
