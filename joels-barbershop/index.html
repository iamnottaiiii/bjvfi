Our story

Cutting Main Street since 2014

Joels Barbershop opened its doors on Main Street in Worcester in 2014 and quickly became a Main South staple. The shop runs on walk ins, word of mouth, and barbers who take their time with every head in the chair.

It is a community shop first. Neighbors catch up over the buzz of clippers, kids get their first real haircut here, and the fade game stays sharp because the barbers keep learning.

Cash and credit cards accepted. Wheelchair accessible entrance and parking.
