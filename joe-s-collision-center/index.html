About

About Joe's Collision Center

Joe's Collision Center is an auto body shop on Jay Street in Newark specializing in collision repair. The team works with drivers through every step, from the first estimate to the final polish, restoring vehicles to pre-accident condition.

We are a auto body shop serving Newark and the surrounding Essex County communities. Whether it is routine maintenance or an unexpected breakdown, our goal is the same: get you back on the road safely, with work you can trust and a bill you understand.
