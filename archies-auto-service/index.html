A Family Shop, Not a Franchise

Archie's Auto Service describes itself as a family owned and operated automotive service center. The shop is led by an ASE certified technician with 35 years of experience and 29 years of GM training, according to the business's own public listing.

In June 2025 the shop celebrated 19 years serving Dayton, Kentucky and the surrounding areas, a milestone the business announced publicly with thanks to the community for its trust and loyalty.

Reviewers consistently describe the same experience: honest diagnoses, fair prices, repairs explained clearly, and a team that treats customers like family. Some customers report traveling from nearby areas specifically for the shop's service.
