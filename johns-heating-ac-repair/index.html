Know the signs

Your system is trying to tell you something

Catch these early and a tune-up usually does the trick. Ignore them and you are looking at a repair bill.

Weak or warm airflow

Vents blowing lukewarm air, or barely blowing at all, often point to airflow, refrigerant, or compressor issues.

New noises or smells

Grinding, banging, or musty and burning smells from the vents mean something inside is wearing out. Do not wait on these.

Bills climbing fast

A sudden jump in your energy bill with no change in usage usually means the system is working overtime to keep up.
