Our story

A sports-themed shop with a simple playbook

Major League Barber Shop sits at 16 E Broad St in Bethlehem, PA, serving the Lehigh Valley with classic barbering. The shop is sports-themed, with vending machines offering water and snacks while you wait.

Built for volume, tuned for detail

  • ✓
    Seven chairs, short waits. Public reviews highlight the large team and fast turnover, even on busy days.
  • ✓
    Men and boys welcome. Specialists in everyday haircuts, from simple trims to more involved men's styles.
  • ✓
    Razors and scissors. Basic cuts plus more complicated men's cuts, beard cutting, and shaving.
  • ✓
    Sports on, always. A sports-themed shop where the game is part of the experience.
  • ✓
    Open seven days. Including Sunday hours for weekend cleanups.
