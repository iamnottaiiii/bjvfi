Our story

Staten Island builders since 1984

A corporation built on long relationships and repeat clients.

Visit our office

CLJ Building Enterprises has served New York as a general building contractor and construction manager since 1984. For more than 40 years our approach has stayed the same: serve and care for clients as we would our friends and family.

That approach has built trusted relationships and a reputation that still stands strong today. We utilize our construction knowledge and strengths to help build our clients projects quickly and economically, which lets clients focus on their business while continuing to grow doing what they do best.

Quality is our first concern. It has been since day one.

The firm is led by president Anthony Carollo with Linda Sellitti as Executive Director of Operations. Our office sits at 19 Cameron Avenue on Staten Island, and our work spans commercial, educational, and institutional projects up to $15 million.

Recent public work includes serving as general contractor for the Integration Charter Schools campus, an 800 student K-12 school project at Corporate Commons in Staten Island, and construction management work at Corporate Commons, 1441 South Avenue.
