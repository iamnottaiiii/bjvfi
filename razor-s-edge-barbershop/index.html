The Shop

A barbershop in the old tradition

No appointments, no fuss. Just a chair, sharp tools, and a barber who takes the work seriously.

Razor’s Edge Barbershop sits at 27 E Main Street in the heart of Macungie. It is the kind of shop where regulars are known by name and first-timers are treated like regulars from the first visit.

The menu covers the essentials of the trade: clean haircuts, close fades, beard trims, and straight razor work. Walk-ins are welcome, so you can stop by whenever the moment strikes.

Families across the Lehigh Valley make the trip for honest pricing and consistent results. The shop keeps its hours steady through the week, with Saturday mornings set aside for the early risers.

About

A Main Street institution

Razor’s Edge Barbershop has served Macungie from its East Main Street storefront for years, building a steady base of regulars across the borough and the Lehigh Valley.

The shop keeps things simple: walk in, take a seat, and get a careful, consistent cut. There are no gimmicks here, just the fundamentals of the trade done well, week after week.

Whether it is a weekly cleanup, a first haircut for a young client, or a straight razor shave for a special occasion, the shop treats every visit with the same attention.

Located in downtown Macungie, the shop is an easy stop for anyone in the area. Come see why neighbors keep coming back.
