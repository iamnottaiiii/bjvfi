What is oral surgery

A surgeon for the mouth, jaws, and face

Oral and maxillofacial surgeons are the dental specialists trained to diagnose and surgically treat conditions of the teeth, jaws, and facial structures, from impacted wisdom teeth to complex extractions.

If your dentist has referred you for surgical care, Dr. Mygil's office can walk you through what to expect.

Extractions

Tooth removal, including wisdom teeth

Surgical extraction is the core of oral surgery: removing teeth that are impacted, damaged beyond repair, or crowded out, with planning for your bite and healing afterward. Ask the office about your specific case.

Illustration
Surgical planning

Diagnosis and treatment planning

Every surgical case starts with evaluation: imaging, a review of your health history, and a clear explanation of the recommended approach, the alternatives, and what recovery looks like.

Illustration
Aftercare

Recovery support

Healing well matters as much as the procedure itself. Expect detailed aftercare instructions, guidance on managing discomfort, and a clear path to reach the office with questions during recovery.

Good to know: oral surgery covers a defined specialty scope. Procedures and availability vary, so please call (410) 766-1008 to ask what is offered before your visit.
