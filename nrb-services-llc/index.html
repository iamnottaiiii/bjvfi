The company

Licensed. Bonded. On the road.

NRB Services LLC is a licensed and bonded freight shipping and trucking company running its freight hauling business from Paxton, MA. It operates as an authorized for-hire carrier under DOT number 3172491, with active status registered with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration.

The company is also authorized to transport hazardous materials under its FMCSA registration, a credential that puts it a step above many small fleets.

If you are comparing carriers, those credentials are public record. Ask for the DOT number, check it, then call and talk freight.
