Our Story

Four decades of raising iron in Maryland.

Since 1985

Tri State Steel Erectors Inc was incorporated in Maryland in 1985 and has been setting structural steel ever since. Based at 7535 WB&A Rd in Glen Burnie, our crews serve general contractors, developers, and public projects across the Baltimore-Washington region.

A specialty trade, run like one

We are listed as a structural steel specialty contractor. That focus is the point: steel erection demands its own safety culture, its own sequencing logic, and crews who have set iron together for years. Our project history spans commercial, healthcare, and institutional work, the sectors where schedule certainty matters most.

Safety first, always

Every lift is planned, every connection is verified, and every site runs under strict fall protection and rigging standards. The best compliment a steel erector can earn is a project that finishes with zero incidents and the crane gone on time.
