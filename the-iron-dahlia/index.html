About the studio

Iron on the outside, dahlia within.

The Iron Dahlia grew out of a simple idea: a tattoo studio that feels as considered as the art it puts on skin. The space on North Front Street blends industrial ironwork with a love of botanical illustration, and the artists carry that contrast into every piece, from soft floral linework to heavy blackwork.

The studio offers tattooing, body piercing and cosmetic tattoo services, with consultations that put design and placement first. Walk ins are welcome when the books allow, but booking ahead is the surest way to get the artist and time you want.

  • Custom design for every client, no flash only pressure.
  • Hospital grade sterilization and single use needles.
  • Honest guidance on placement, sizing and aging.
Resident artists

Hands you can trust.

The studio is home to artists working across floral, blackwork and fine line styles, alongside professional piercers. Scientific illustrator turned tattooer Katy Wiedemann has been noted for her precise blackwork linework created at The Iron Dahlia. Ask the studio about current resident artists and guest spots when you book.
