What we paint

Services, start to finish

Every job starts with prep and ends with a walkthrough. Here is everything the crew takes on, inside and out.

  1. 01
    Interior PaintingMost called for

    Walls, ceilings, trim, and interior doors. Furniture protected, edges cut crisp, and roller marks left nowhere.

  2. 02
    Exterior PaintingWhole house

    Full home exteriors, stucco, and siding. Customers report whole two-story homes finished in about a day and a half.

  3. 03
    Surface Prep & Repair

    Crack and hole patching, caulking, sanding, and priming. The unglamorous work that makes paint last.

  4. 04
    Doors, Trim & Details

    Front doors, garage doors, baseboards, window frames, downspouts, and gutters. The details that frame the house.

  5. 05
    Color Consultation

    Stuck between swatches? The crew helps pick colors that brighten the home, inside and out.

  6. 06
    Move-Ready Refresh

    Repainting before a move, patching nail holes and scuffs so the place shows its best.
