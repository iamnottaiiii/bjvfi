About

About Legacy Commercial Flooring

Legacy Commercial Flooring is a commercial flooring contractor based in Columbus, Ohio, with roots in the trade going back to 1900.

As a member of the StarNet Commercial Flooring Cooperative, the company installs and maintains carpet, ceramic, resilient, and hardwood flooring for commercial clients. Project scope is quoted directly by the company.
