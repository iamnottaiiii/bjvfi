The craft

Hardwood is a long decision. It deserves a careful installer.

A wood floor is one of the few home surfaces you touch every single day. Species, plank width, stain and finish all change how a room feels, and they cannot be swapped out on a whim. A contractor who specializes in hardwood helps you choose once and live with it happily for decades.

Custom, not catalog.

The word custom is in the name. Layouts, borders, inlays and mixed widths are the difference between a floor and a feature.

Sand, stain, seal.

Refinishing brings tired oak back to life: sanding out the scratches, re-staining to the tone you want, and sealing it for the next twenty years. Ask what a refinish could do for your existing floors before you replace them.

Services

What a hardwood specialist does.

Tap a tab to see what each service typically covers. This is a sample outline of standard hardwood flooring work; call to confirm the exact services offered.

New installationSample

Site-finished or prefinished hardwood laid over a prepared subfloor, with layout planning so plank joints and transitions land where they should.

  • Solid and engineered hardwood installation
  • Layout, transitions and stair nosing
  • Custom borders and mixed widths
  • Stain color sampling on your actual boards

Sanding and refinishingSample

A full sand takes the floor back to bare wood, then stain and finish bring it forward to the look you want now.

  • Dust-conscious sanding of worn floors
  • Custom stain colors and natural finishes
  • Screen-and-recoat maintenance refreshes
  • Sheen options from matte to satin

Repairs and detailsSample

Small problems in wood floors are fixable without replacing the room.

  • Board replacement for water or pet damage
  • Squeak and movement fixes
  • Vent and threshold detailing
  • Color matching to existing floors
