What an inspection covers

A thorough check, top to bottom

Chimney inspections look for creosote buildup, liner damage, cracked masonry and blockages. Call and ask what a visit includes for your fireplace or stove.

01

Flue and liner check

Cracked or missing flue tiles and damaged liners let heat and gases reach the framing around your chimney. An inspection spots trouble early.

02

Creosote and buildup

Creosote is highly flammable. Regular inspection tells you when buildup has reached a level that needs attention before burning season.

03

Masonry and crown

Loose bricks, failing mortar joints and a cracked crown let water in, and water is the fastest way to wreck a chimney in New England winters.

Why it matters

Most chimney fires start quietly

Homeowners usually cannot see inside their own flue. An annual inspection is the standard recommendation for anyone who burns wood or pellets, even if the fireplace looked fine last season.

  • Find blockages from nests, leaves or debris
  • Confirm the damper opens and seals correctly
  • Catch water damage before freeze cycles spread it
  • Get clear guidance on next steps, in plain language

About

Focused on one thing: chimney safety

Q & G Chimney Inspectors is a chimney inspection business based in Bedford, Massachusetts. The focus is straightforward: inspect chimneys carefully, explain what is found in plain language, and help homeowners burn safely.

Chimneys do more than carry smoke. They keep carbon monoxide and other combustion gases out of the home, protect surrounding framing from heat, and take a beating from New England freeze cycles. A dedicated inspection business exists because those jobs deserve a specialist's eye.
