Meet the Garage

A neighborhood shop in Sayler Park with a long memory and a short waiting list.

From the shop's public website

Over 25 years in Cincinnati

Jake's Auto Service has served the Cincinnati area for over 25 years, offering complete auto and truck repair at competitive prices. The shop's own site describes an honest, hardworking team that keeps vehicles running great.

The technicians are ASE-certified and work on top name brands, both foreign and domestic. Whether it is an oil change, Freon charging, brake work, or a full diagnostic, the pitch is simple: quality service at fair rates, done right the first time.

The shop sits at 90 Huey Ave in the Sayler Park neighborhood of Cincinnati, and reviewers regularly describe it as honest, reasonably priced, fast, and friendly.
