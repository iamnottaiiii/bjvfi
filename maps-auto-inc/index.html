Our story

A Schenectady shop,
licensed and accountable

MAPS AUTO INC is a New York State licensed vehicle repair facility on State Street in Schenectady, in business since 2015. State records list the shop as a DMV-registered safety service facility, which means our inspections and repairs meet the standards New York requires.

We built the shop on a simple idea: treat every car like it belongs to a neighbor, because around here it usually does. Most of our customers come from a few zip codes away and come back because the estimate matched the invoice.

When you drop off your car, you talk to the person who will actually work on it. We explain what is wrong in plain language, show you the worn part when possible, and only do the work you approve.
