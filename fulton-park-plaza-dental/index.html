Services

General dentistry for the whole family

Fulton Park Plaza Dental is listed as a general dentist and dental clinic. Choose a topic to see what to ask about when you call.

Exams and cleanings

Routine dental exams and professional cleanings are the foundation of general dentistry. Regular visits help catch decay and gum issues early, when treatment is simplest.

  • Ask about new patient exams
  • Ask about cleaning appointments
  • Ask about X ray services

Fillings and crowns

When a tooth is damaged by decay or injury, general dentists typically repair it with a filling or a crown. These restorations rebuild strength and shape.

  • Ask about tooth colored fillings
  • Ask about crown options
  • Ask about treatment timelines

Dentures and partials

Missing teeth can be replaced with full dentures or partial dentures, restoring the ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

  • Ask about full dentures
  • Ask about partial dentures
  • Ask about fittings and adjustments

Extractions

Sometimes a tooth cannot be saved and needs to be removed. General dentists perform routine extractions and advise on replacement options afterward.

  • Ask about simple extractions
  • Ask about aftercare
  • Ask about replacement options
