Meet Dr. Said M. Khalefa

Dr. Said M. Khalefa, DDS, is a Virginia-licensed dentist practicing general dentistry in Northern Virginia. His individual NPI record and the practice's organizational NPI record (1760678569) are active in the national provider registry, which dates the practice's registration to 2007.

The practice serves patients at two offices: the Alexandria office at 4600B Pinecrest Office Park Dr and a Springfield office at 6115 Backlick Rd. Public directories list general dentistry, endodontics, and pediatric dentistry among the practice's areas of care.

In Washington Consumers Checkbook's patient survey, Dr. Khalefa earned 3.5 out of 5 across 12 ratings, with patients describing him as very honest and willing to do what it takes to support his patients.
