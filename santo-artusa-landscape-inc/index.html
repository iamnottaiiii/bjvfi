Our Story

A Five Towns family business, rooted for generations.

Santo Artusa Landscape Inc is built around the Artusa family, with Santo and John named by customers as the knowledgeable pair behind the work. Public listings trace the business back to 1964, and at least one customer reports hiring them since 1967.

Honest, dependable, creative

That is how a Lawrence homeowner described the company in a public review, after Santo Artusa landscaped a backyard that drew more compliments than ever. It is a pattern in the reviews: fair prices, no pressure, and work finished on time or early.

The neighborhood standard

"Everyone around here uses him," wrote one reviewer. For a family business in Lawrence, that is the whole business plan: do the work well, clean up after, and let the block do the advertising.
