What We Do

From Concept Sketch to Living Garden

La Loma guides every project from the first idea to the finished landscape: conceptual design, drafting, and permit-ready plans, then construction by our own crews using natural techniques like recycled-concrete terraces and earth-bag walls.

Our work blends art and high design with practical garden ecology, replacing lawns, asphalt, and concrete with beautiful habitat for homes, schools, parks, and businesses.

Signature Work

Natural Pools, Green Roofs, Rain Gardens

We are known for living water: natural pools and habitat ponds that clean themselves biologically, rain gardens that catch stormwater, and green roofs and walls that cool buildings and feed pollinators.

Every project is rooted in over fifteen years of permaculture experience and a simple goal: landscapes that are beautiful, functional, and ecologically alive.

Our Story

Rooted in Pasadena Since 2007

La Loma Development Company began in 2007 from home and field offices in Pasadena and grew into one of Southern California's leading ecologically sustainable landscape contractors, with over a hundred completed projects across Los Angeles County.

Founder Marco Barrantes, a former Pasadena Parks Commissioner who studied ecology, landscape architecture, and city planning at UC Berkeley, built the company on permaculture experience and the belief that every person deserves access to nurturing habitat and food forests in the urban landscape.

La Loma's work has earned public recognition, including the Pasadena Beautiful Golden Arrow Award for a classic terraced vineyard project and the Green Hero Award from Environmental Charter Schools. The company has partnered on community landmarks such as Arlington Gardens, a three-acre award-winning community garden.
