Why daylight

A skylight changes how a room feels

Natural light, lower bills

Daylighting cuts daytime electric lighting and, with venting models, lets hot air escape in summer. Solar-powered Fresh Air skylights may qualify for a federal tax credit. Ask us about current incentives.

Fresh air on demand

Venting and solar-powered models open with a remote, flushing out cooking steam, bathroom humidity and stale air, even on roofs you cannot reach by hand.

Four decades under the same sun

Freelite Skylights has been a Northridge skylight contractor since the 1980s, a licensed, bonded and insured general contractor doing one thing exceptionally well.

Specialists, not generalists

Many roofers install a skylight now and then. We install and service them every day: the full VELUX line including Solar-Powered Fresh Air skylights and blinds, Sun Tunnel tubular skylights, plus dome skylights and glass skylights in fixed, manual, electric and solar-powered models with remote control, in standard and custom sizes.

The estimate experience

Reviewers consistently mention the same things: the crew arrives on time, explains every option in detail, and installs beautiful skylights in hours, not days. Fast service, reasonable prices, and knowledgeable people is how one customer summed it up, and we intend to keep it that way.

Licensed, bonded, insured

Freelite Skylights operates as a licensed, bonded and insured general contractor. Cutting into a roof is serious work, and we treat it that way: proper flashing, proper sealing, proper cleanup.

Repairs welcome

Not every skylight needs replacing. If yours leaks, fogs, or has yellowed with age, we will tell you honestly whether a re-seal or dome swap will fix it, and only recommend replacement when it is truly the better value.
