Our Story

Great at desserts. Famously bad at naming them.

The Name Says It All

BANDS stands for Bad At Naming Desserts Shoppe, and we wear it proudly. Somewhere along the way we realized our true talent was baking, not branding, so we stopped pretending and put the truth right on the sign.

What started as a neighborhood sweet shop in Lynwood grew one brownie at a time. Our chocolate brownies earned a devoted following, our cupcakes became the go-to for local celebrations, and our candy counter keeps the kid in everyone happy.

We are a small, friendly shop where the staff knows the regulars and the regulars know exactly which shelf holds their favorite. Whether you stop in for a single cookie or a cake for fifty, you will be greeted like a neighbor, because you are one.

Come taste what happens when all the creativity goes into the recipe box instead of the name tags.
