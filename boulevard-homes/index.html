About

A Cincinnati builder on Erie Avenue

Boulevard Homes is a general contractor based at 3414 Erie Ave in Cincinnati, Ohio. We build new homes and manage renovations and additions across the Cincinnati area.

As a general contractor, we do more than swing hammers. We plan the job, pull the trades together, keep the schedule honest, and stand behind the finished work. That is the whole job description, and we take it seriously.
