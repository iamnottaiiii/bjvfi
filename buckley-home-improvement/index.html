About

Meet Leon Buckley Jr.

Owner, operator and the guy who shows up to do the work.

The owner

One man, one truck,
one high standard

Buckley Home Improvement is owned and operated by Leon Buckley Jr. of Worcester, MA, a registered home improvement contractor with 10+ years of experience in building projects and remodeling homes.

Leon has worked with contractors who flip houses and remodel condo complexes, and he spent years as a department manager and buyer for Building Materials at the Home Depot. That background means a deep, practical knowledge of every part of a home improvement project, from lumber to the final coat of paint.

He works directly with the customer on every job, starting with the initial consultation and estimate, then design plans and the contract. Most projects are completed by Leon himself. Bigger projects get a trusted 3-man crew, and certified, registered subcontractors handle the trades that need them, like major plumbing and electrical.

He also runs a fully operational workshop with all the tools needed to design and build custom built-ins, bookshelves, cabinetry and furniture, in paint or stain grade.

  • Registered home improvement contractor, Worcester MA
  • 10+ years remodeling homes and building projects
  • Former Home Depot building materials dept. manager and buyer
  • Fully equipped workshop for custom fabrication
