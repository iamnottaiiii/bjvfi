Our story

A local crew with local pride

Florida Home Improvement Company is a Tampa-based construction company serving the Tampa Bay area from our shop on S 86th St.

Why we exist

Remodeling done the right way, not the fast way

Too many homeowners have a horror story about a contractor who vanished, cut corners or never picked up the phone. We started this company to be the opposite of that.

We are a local team, not a national franchise. When you call (813) 404-0404, you talk to people who will actually be on your job site. We show up on time, we communicate clearly, and we stand behind our work.

Florida homes face Florida challenges: heat, humidity, storms and strict building codes. We build for all of it, with materials and methods suited to this climate.

Our shop sits at 2433 S 86th St Ste J in Tampa, and our crews work across Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, Polk, Manatee and Sarasota counties.
