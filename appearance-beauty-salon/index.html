About

A small studio with a personal touch

Appearance is a unisex beauty salon on Victory Boulevard in Staten Island, run as an intimate one-chair style studio where every appointment gets full attention.

Stylist Suna is the name clients mention again and again. Reviewers describe never having to wait, getting exactly the cut or color they asked for, and returning for years because the experience feels personal.

The space is small and cozy by design. No crowded waiting area, no rushed chairs. Just focused, friendly service in a modern and inviting atmosphere.
