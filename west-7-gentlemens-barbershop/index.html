About Us

West 7 Gentlemen's Barbershop has served the Seven Mile corridor in Detroit for over 15 years. Known for consistent, quality cuts and a welcoming shop atmosphere, it is a neighborhood staple for gentlemen who like their grooming classic and their conversation good.

Walk-ins are welcome during business hours. Find us at 20412 Seven Mile W, Detroit, MI 48219, or call (313) 537-8326.
