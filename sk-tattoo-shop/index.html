First visit

How It Works.

Simple process, no pressure. Call or walk in, talk through the idea, approve the drawing, get the ink.

1 · Consult

Talk first

Bring references or a rough idea. You will talk placement, size, style, and price before anything is final.

2 · Draw

Approve the art

The design is drawn for your body. You see it, you approve it, and only then does the session get booked.

3 · Ink

Heal right

Sterile setup, steady work, and aftercare instructions so the piece heals clean.

You must be 18 or older. Bring a photo ID. Call (267) 962-5801 to start.
