Artist statement

A family shop with a craftsman's eye

Tucked off the beaten track on Sycamore Road, Specialty Paint & Auto Body is the kind of shop people drive out of their way to find.

Our story

Specialty Paint & Auto Body is a family run auto body repair shop at 4401 Sycamore Rd in Cincinnati, Ohio. The shop is licensed with the Ohio Board of Motor Vehicle Repair as a collision shop.

Drivers describe the crew as delightful to deal with, reliable, and excellent at the work, all for a fair price. That reputation was earned one repair at a time, with no shortcuts.

We treat every car like a piece going up on the gallery wall: prepped carefully, finished beautifully, and inspected before it leaves the shop.
