Our story

Flushing’s 46th Avenue bargain stop

Super Joy (99¢ & up) sits on 46th Avenue in Flushing, in the busy retail corridor near Utopia Parkway. It is the kind of store the neighborhood runs on: a discount shop where a forgotten birthday card, a pack of cleaning wipes, or a last-minute party banner costs pocket change instead of a trip across town.


The formula never changes. Keep the shelves full, keep the prices low, and keep the doors open every day from 9 in the morning to 9 at night. Regulars stop in on the way to and from the Q26 and Q27 buses on 46th Avenue, and new faces become regulars fast.


The store is also a licensed New York State lottery retailer, so your scratch-offs and draw tickets are on the same trip.
