Our Story

Rooted in Cincinnati for Generations

Stefani Landscaping is locally owned and offers a comprehensive line of lawn care and landscape services. We customize our work in order to meet your specific needs, a simple philosophy that has kept us serving Cincinnati-area properties since 1978.

Our team is led by Greg Stefani, an ISA Certified Arborist serving Hamilton County, bringing professional tree and plant knowledge to every job we take on.

  • In business since 1978, serving Cincinnati and Hamilton County
  • ISA Certified Arborist on staff
  • Free project estimates
  • Warranties offered on new plants
  • 5% senior discount
  • Emergency service offered
