Our story

A neighborhood practice with a careful touch

Landmark Dental Care is a general dentistry practice on South Whiting Street in Alexandria, Virginia, serving patients of all ages.

About

Dr. Homan Solemaninejad

Dr. Solemaninejad leads Landmark Dental Care as a general dentist, with patients noting his exceptional dental skill, thorough approach, and careful eye for dental aesthetics across general, restorative, and cosmetic treatment.

Approach
