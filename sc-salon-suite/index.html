Services

Service descriptions below are editorial overviews. Call (240) 841-1070 for current services and pricing.

Haircuts

A great cut is the foundation of everything. Whether you want a trim that keeps your length or a fresh new shape, your stylist will consult with you first, then cut, finish, and style so you leave knowing how to recreate the look at home.

Color

From root touch-ups to dimensional color, the suite offers coloring services tailored to your hair type and the tone you want. Because this is a boutique suite, your color appointment gets the full, focused attention it deserves.

Styling

Blowouts, event styling, and finishing services round out the menu. Planning something special? Call ahead and the team will set aside the time your style needs.
