The Coaching Season, Whistle Stop by Whistle Stop

How a coaching engagement typically moves down the field. SAMPLE process, call to confirm

Stop 1: Film Review

We look at where your shop stands today: car count, sales numbers, margins, and marketing. You cannot fix what you do not measure.

Stop 2: Draw the Game Plan

Pick your targets: more customers, higher sales, better profits, or all three. Then map the plays to get there.

Stop 3: Training Camp

Install the systems: marketing calendar, advising scripts, pricing math, and team accountability. Reps make it stick.

Stop 4: Game Day

Run the plays in real life with your coach on the sideline. Adjust at halftime when the numbers tell you to.

Stop 5: Victory Lap

Review the scoreboard: car count up, sales up, profits up, and you with more time away from the shop.
