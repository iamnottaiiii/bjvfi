Our story

Rooted in the Queen City

Local people, local knowledge

Queen City Buyer is a Cincinnati real estate consulting business based at 2344 Kemper Ln. We spend our days talking with homeowners across Hamilton County about one of the biggest decisions of their lives: what to do with a house.

Sometimes the answer is a traditional listing with an agent. Sometimes it is a direct sale that skips the repairs and the waiting. Our role is to help you see the tradeoffs clearly so you can choose with confidence.

We believe a good consultant earns trust by being useful even when the answer is "you do not need us." That is how we have built our name in Cincinnati, one honest conversation at a time.
