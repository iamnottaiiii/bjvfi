About

The shop on Paramount

Proper Barbershop keeps things straightforward: sharp tools, steady hands, and a cut that looks as good leaving as it did in the chair. The Downey shop has built its name on attention to detail, from crisp lineups to carefully blended fades.

Regulars mention the welcoming atmosphere and barbers who take their time instead of rushing the chair. Walk in for a classic cut or book ahead when the weekend queue fills up.

What the shop stands for

Detail first

Crisp edges, clean blends, and a finish that holds up for weeks.

An easygoing shop where first timers get the same care as regulars.
