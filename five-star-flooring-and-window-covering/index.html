About

A Burbank shop for floors and windows.

Five Star Flooring and Window Covering is located at 411 W Alameda Ave in Burbank, California. We specialize in two things that change how a home feels: what is under your feet and what is on your windows.

Wood floor installation is our core trade, from new hardwood to refinishing tired floors. Window coverings, blinds, shades, shutters, and drapery, round out the offering so each room gets a finished look.
