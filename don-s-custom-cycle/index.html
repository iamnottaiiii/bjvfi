About Don's

Don's Custom Cycle is a motorcycle parts store and repair shop at 8623 Harrison Pike in Cleves, Ohio, just west of Cincinnati. It is the kind of place riders describe as a westside favorite: a shop where the owner knows his bikes, knows torque specs, and has been in business as long as locals can remember.

The shop serves Harley-Davidson riders across the tri-state, from Shovelheads to the latest M8s, with parts on the counter and a wrench in the back. Reviewers consistently point to the same things: honest work, fair prices, and the most experience in the tri-state.
