Our story

A neighborhood tire shop, plain and simple

MG New and Used Tire Shop sits right on the White Horse Pike in Waterford Works, serving drivers across Camden County who need honest tire work without the dealership markup.

The shop describes itself in six words: mounted tires, balancing, brakes, suspension, low price, quality service. That is the whole business model. Stock both new and used tires so every budget has an option, do the work the same day, and price it fairly enough that customers send their neighbors.

Drivers mention quick flat repairs, lawn mower tires, and being back on the road the same visit. It is the kind of shop where the phone gets answered and the quote you hear is the price you pay.
