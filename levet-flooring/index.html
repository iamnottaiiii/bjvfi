Our story

Flooring, the easy way.

Artwork placeholder

We take flooring to the next level.

Levet Flooring started with a simple frustration: picking new floors meant driving to store after store, guessing how samples would look at home, and hoping the installer would show up on time.

So we flipped the model. Our team of floor designers and expert installers brings the showroom to your home, gives you a clear written estimate on the spot, and installs with care. The goal is the best customer experience in flooring, from the first sample to the day you excitedly take your shoes off on your new floors.

We are a bonded, licensed, and insured contractor based in Glendale, California, offering 0% APR finance plans to make great floors fit real budgets.
