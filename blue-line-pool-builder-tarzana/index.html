From dirt to dive-in

A pool project has a lot of moving parts. Here is the order they happen in, so you always know what comes next.

1 / consult

Design consult

We walk your yard, talk through shape, depth, and features, and sketch a plan that fits the lot.

2 / permits

Plans and permits

Engineered drawings and the permit paperwork handled in the right order. Ask about timelines when you call.

3 / build

Excavation and build

Dig, steel, plumbing, shotcrete, then tile, coping, plaster, and deck. Staged and inspected along the way.

4 / swim

Startup and handoff

Water chemistry balanced, equipment walked through with you, then your first swim.
