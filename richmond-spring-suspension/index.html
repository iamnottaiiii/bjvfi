How it works

Suspension and spring specialists since 1968

Richmond Spring & Suspension in Secaucus has been fixing what holds your vehicle up for over 55 years: leaf springs, coil springs, shocks, and alignments for trucks and cars.

Step 1: Call or drive in

Tell us the symptom: sagging, bouncing, pulling, or uneven tire wear.

Step 2: Inspect on the lift

We measure ride height and check every spring, bushing, and shock.

Step 3: Quote it straight

You get the real number for repair, rebuild, or replace.

Step 4: Fix it right

Springs re-arched or replaced, shocks and alignment dialed in.

Step 5: Road test

We drive it before you do, so it tracks straight when you leave.

Services

What we handle

Re-arching, replacement, and custom leaf packs for trucks and trailers.

Replacement coils for cars, SUVs, and light trucks, matched to factory spec.

Heavy-duty and standard shocks, struts, and full assemblies.

Two and four-wheel alignments after suspension work, done to spec.

Air bags, compressors, and leveling systems diagnosed and repaired.

The brackets, bushings, and hardware that hold it all together.

About

Meet the shop

Richmond Spring & Suspension is a specialty shop at 280 County Avenue in Secaucus, NJ, in business since 1968. While general repair shops send suspension work out, this is the shop they send it to.

Leaf springs, coil springs, air ride, and alignments: over five decades of doing one thing has made Richmond the name North Jersey trusts when a vehicle will not sit or ride right.
