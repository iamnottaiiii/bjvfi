About

Four generations of Cincinnati tile craft

From marble fireplaces in 1894 to arena-scale installations today, the Winston name has stayed attached to tile and stone work done right.

  • 1894

    Mr. T.H. Winston starts a marble fireplace fabrication and installation business in Cincinnati. The Tile Contractors' Association of America records 1894 as the company's founding year.

    Sources: TCAA 9300 Contractor, Summer 2019; Cincinnati Mosaic "About Us" at this address.

  • The middle years

    The business pivots with the trade: from fireplaces to operating a tile and marble showroom, then to residential tile installation across the Cincinnati area.

    Source: Cincinnati Mosaic "About Us" history of the Glenshade Ave operation.

  • Today

    A family-oriented company led by Nick Rusche, now specializing in commercial tile and stone installations with some of the region's most experienced installers and support staff.

    Sources: TCAA 9300 Contractor, Summer 2019; Cincinnati Mosaic "About Us".

  • 2019

    Featured in TCAA's national magazine for the University of Cincinnati Fifth Third Arena project: custom waterjet "C-Paw" mosaic murals in the locker rooms and hydrotherapy room, thickset mortar bed work, and an on-time finish on a tight schedule with multiple crews. General contractor: Skanska USA and Megen Construction (joint venture). Architect: Populous. LEED Silver.

    Source: TCAA 9300 Contractor, Summer 2019.

Featured project

UC Fifth Third Arena

T.H. Winston Company, Inc. served as tile contractor for the University of Cincinnati's Fifth Third Arena renovation. The highlight: custom waterjet "C-Paw" mosaic murals in the locker rooms and hydrotherapy room.
