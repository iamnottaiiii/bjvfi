Services

Site work, start to finish

From the first clearing pass to the final connection, Inishowen Contracting LLC keeps your project moving in the ground.

01
Ground prep

Site Clearing

Every project starts with a clean slate. We clear trees, brush, stumps, and debris so crews can work on a safe, open footprint.

A properly cleared site saves time later: no surprises underfoot when the excavation begins, and a work area the whole project can rely on.

Tree and brush removalStump clearingDebris haul-off
02
Excavation

Foundation Excavation

Digging the foundations is where the building truly begins. We excavate to spec, holding grade so footings and concrete go in on a solid, level base.

Foundations, additions, and outbuildings: if it sits on the ground, the ground has to be right first.

Full foundationsAdditionsPrecise grading
03
Utilities

Sewer & Water Connections

We install public and private water and sewer system connections, tying your project into municipal lines or on-site systems to town standards.

Licensed drainlayer work filed with the Town of Auburn. Connections done clean, tested, and signed off.

Sewer tie-insWater service linesDrainlaying
04
Drainage

Storm Water Management

Water goes where it wants unless you tell it otherwise. We build the grading, swales, and drainage work that keeps storm water away from structures and off the site.

Smart storm water work protects foundations, driveways, and landscaping for the life of the property.

Grading and swalesDrainage installsErosion control
