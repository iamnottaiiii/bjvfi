Process

How removal day goes

A clean, contained process from cover-up to cleanup.

1. Protect the room

Floors, walls, and fixtures covered. Dust contained to the work area.

2. Soften and scrape

Texture softened and scraped away without gouging the drywall.

3. Skim and smooth

Surface skim-coated and sanded to a flat, even finish.

4. Clean up

Debris bagged and hauled, room left broom-clean and paint-ready.
