What we do

Heating and cooling services, end to end

From a dead thermostat to a full system replacement, Refreshing HVAC Repair Harrisburg handles the work that keeps your home comfortable. Call for a full list of services and current availability.

01
Most called

Heating repair

No heat, weak heat, strange noises, short cycling: we diagnose furnaces, boilers and heat pumps and get your heat back on. If a part can be repaired instead of replaced, we will tell you.

02
Summer essential

Air conditioning repair

Warm air, frozen coils, refrigerant leaks, dead compressors. We service central air conditioning and ductless mini split systems so your home stays cool through the hottest weeks.

03
Prevent breakdowns

HVAC maintenance and tune ups

Seasonal checkups catch small problems before they become midnight emergencies: filters, coils, electrical connections, refrigerant levels and airflow, checked and cleaned.

04
Long term fix

System replacement and installation

When a system is past its prime, we help you size and install the right replacement for your home and budget, then install it clean and test everything before we leave.

05
Breathe easier

Indoor air quality

Better filtration, humidity control and ventilation improvements for homes dealing with dust, dryness, or stale air.
