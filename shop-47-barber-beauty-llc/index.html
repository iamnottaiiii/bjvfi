Our story

A neighborhood shop with two trades

SHOP 47 - Barber & Beauty LLC sits on York Road in Baltimore, built on a simple idea: one shop where the whole neighborhood can get sharp. The barber side handles classic cuts, fades and straight razor shaves. The beauty side covers wash, color and styling. Families come in together and leave together.

What clients mention most is the detail: clean fades, careful lineups, and barbers who take their time with kids and first timers. It is an easygoing shop with a steady chair-side manner and fair, straightforward pricing.
