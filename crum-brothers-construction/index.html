Services

What we build

These are the kinds of projects a general contractor like ours typically handles. Every job is quoted on its own scope, so call (717) 728-3393 and describe what you need.

Ground-up builds

Full construction of new residential or commercial structures, coordinated from foundation to finish.

Framing & structure

Structural framing, sheathing, and rough carpentry for new builds and extensions.

Site coordination

Scheduling and managing the trades on site so the project moves in the right order.

Whole-home remodeling

Updating and reconfiguring existing homes, room by room or floor by floor.

Kitchen & bath updates

Renovating kitchens and bathrooms with new layouts, fixtures, and finishes.

Interior renovations

Flooring, drywall, trim, doors, and interior finish work that refreshes a space.

Room additions

Adding bedrooms, family rooms, or living space that blends with the existing home.

Garages & outbuildings

Attached or detached garages, shops, and accessory structures.

Porches & decks

Covered porches, decks, and outdoor living spaces built to last.

Structural repairs

Fixing framing, rot, and storm or wear damage to keep the building sound.

Exterior improvements

Siding, roofing coordination, windows, and other exterior upgrades.

Maintenance projects

Smaller improvements and punch-list work around homes and commercial spaces.

Project lists above describe common general contracting work, not a fixed menu. Call for hours and to discuss your specific project.
