Our story

T & U Roofing has been a roofing contractor in the Mission Hills area since 1998. More than twenty-five years on, the work is still the same: keep water out, do it right, and give straight answers about what a roof actually needs.

The company serves both residential and commercial properties across Los Angeles County, handling everything from a single leak repair to full re-roofing, plus gutters, chimney work, and ongoing maintenance. Listings describe the crew as licensed, trained, and insured, with free estimates as standard.
