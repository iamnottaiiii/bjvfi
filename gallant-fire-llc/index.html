Who we are

Electrical plus fire protection, by design

Gallant Fire, LLC is based in West Boylston, Massachusetts. The company works as an electrical contractor and in fire protection, which means the same crew can wire your building and handle the alarm and detection side of the job.

That combination matters more than most people realize. Fire alarm systems are electrical systems. Panels, circuits and detectors all tie together, and when one contractor owns both sides, problems get diagnosed faster and fixed once.

We are licensed in Massachusetts for electrical work (license #9260-EL-A1) and we treat every job, residential or commercial, with the same care: clean work, clear communication, and systems you can trust.
