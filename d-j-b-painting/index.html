Our story

A local painter with a simple standard: leave it better than we found it.

D.J.B Painting serves Lindenwold and the surrounding Camden County towns from our shop on E Gibbsboro Rd. We are a working painting crew, not a call center, so the people who quote your job are the people who show up to do it.

  • Local and reachable. Based in Lindenwold, on the job across South Jersey.
  • Prep-first approach. Caulk, sand, prime, then paint. That order never changes.
  • Residential focus. Homes, rentals, and small offices are our bread and butter.
  • Free estimates. Written quotes with no obligation and no pressure.
