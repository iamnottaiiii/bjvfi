Our story

The salon Avenue U
keeps coming back to

Ten Perfect Nail Salon is a neighborhood nail salon in Gravesend, Brooklyn, known for warm service and careful work.

Since the neighborhood found us

Friends behind the chair

Ask around Avenue U and people mention the same names: Candy, Bobo, Fiona. Long-time regulars describe them as friendly and caring, techs who remember you and take their time.

The salon has built its name on detail. Reviewers praise how carefully nails are shaped, how well gel color lasts, and how often they are offered free nail art designs just because.

It is a comfortable, welcoming space where clients are encouraged to try new styles, and where fair prices keep neighbors coming back for years.
