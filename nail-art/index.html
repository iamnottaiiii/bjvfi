Nail Art has been caring for East Rochester's nails for years, one gentle appointment at a time.

From the front desk

Walk in as a guest,
leave as a regular

Tucked into the Country Club Plaza on Fairport Road, Nail Art is the kind of salon people find once and never leave. The owner is usually on site, the techs know their regulars by name, and the chairs are the calmest part of a busy week.

The salon's own words say it best: "We welcome our loyal customers and all new customers." Appointments are encouraged, walk-ins are welcome, and everyone gets the same gentle, careful service.
