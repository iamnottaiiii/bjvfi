Chapter 01 · Design

Designed for how you live outside

A good garden starts with listening. How much sun does the yard get? Where do you drink your morning coffee? What should bloom in April, and what should stay green through August?

Landscape design is the craft of answering those questions with plants, paths, and open space, so the finished garden feels inevitable, like it was always meant to be there.
