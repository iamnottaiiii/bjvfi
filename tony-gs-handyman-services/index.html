Our story
A Burbank handyman with a mechanic's eye

Tony G's Handyman Services is based at 1316 N Hollywood Way in Burbank, and has served the greater Burbank area for over two decades. Directory profiles list the company as established in the early 2000s, and customers describe a crew with longstanding relationships across the neighborhood.

Owner Tony Griffith is described in contractor profiles as a master mechanic and excellent carpenter, mechanically inclined since childhood, with a knack for figuring out how things work and fixing them when others give up.

The reputation the crew has earned is straightforward: reliable, hardworking, and trustworthy, with fair prices and punctual arrivals. Customers mention Tony, Brian, and Ernie by name, and many have called the company back for years of projects.

Doing what you say you will do, being reliable, and creating satisfied customers. That is the business, in Tony's own framing.
