Our story

Two words: we serve.

Serve Bros started with a simple idea: when a pipe bursts at midnight or a storm soaks a storefront, somebody has to show up and get to work. We built Serve Bros to be that crew for North Hollywood and the greater San Fernando Valley.

Our name is our promise. We serve homeowners dealing with their first flood, property managers juggling tenants and timelines, and businesses that cannot afford to stay closed. Water damage, mold, fire residue, and the deep cleaning that follows: one call, one crew, handled.

We are based at 11852 Elkwood St in North Hollywood, which means fast response times across the Valley and clear communication from the first assessment to the final walkthrough.
