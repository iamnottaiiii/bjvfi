Our story

A Pasadena family business, still run the way it started.

Since 1995

C R Windows Inc. was founded in 1995 and is owned by Charles J. Manning. For more than 30 years we have served Pasadena, Millersville, and the surrounding Anne Arundel County communities with windows, doors, siding, and roofing.

We are family owned. Our installation teams are trained in-house, and we pride ourselves on workmanship we can warranty, not on sales volume.
