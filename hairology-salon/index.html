Our story

A neighborhood salon with a natural touch

Hairology Salon sits on Genesee Street in the heart of Chittenango, a small-town salon with a big philosophy: hair should be healthy first and beautiful always.

Led by owner and stylist Janet, the salon is built around natural and organic products, health-conscious artistry, and the kind of one-on-one attention that keeps clients coming back. Communication comes before the color, consultation comes before the cut, and every guest leaves with hair that feels as good as it looks.

  • Natural and organic product focus
  • Color specialist expertise
  • Licensed New York State appearance enhancement business
  • Wheelchair-accessible with nearby parking

Why guests choose us

Reviewers describe a cozy, beautifully decorated space and stylists who listen. From first-time visitors to longtime regulars, the experience is personal from consultation to finish.
