About

A neighborhood staple

Nelsons Barber Shop has the feel of a classic neighborhood shop: steady barbers, honest prices, and cuts that regulars keep coming back for. The atmosphere is old school in the best way, with conversation as much a part of the visit as the haircut.

Reviewers praise the careful, unhurried approach to each cut. Weekend waits can run long when the shop is busy, so calling ahead is a smart move.
