Services at Silverman Jack DDS

Preventive Dentistry

Routine exams, professional cleanings, digital x-rays, gum disease care, and oral cancer examinations. Regular visits keep small problems from becoming big ones.

Restorative Dentistry

Tooth-colored fillings, crowns and caps, bridges, dentures, dental implants, and emergency dental care when you need it most.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, veneers, Invisalign clear aligners, and dental bonding. Ask about a smile consultation tailored to your goals.
