Our story

A North Hollywood crew that shows up

Uptown Construction Group is a construction company based at 12228 Wixom St in North Hollywood, California, serving homeowners and small businesses across the San Fernando Valley.

The business was built on a simple observation: most remodeling headaches come from poor communication, not poor craftsmanship. So the company keeps the process tight. One point of contact, a written scope before work begins, and updates as each phase moves forward.

From kitchen and bathroom remodels to room additions, flooring, and the everyday repair list, the crew takes on projects of every size with the same standard: build it right the first time, and leave the site clean.
