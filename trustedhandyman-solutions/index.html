Why we exist

A handyman you can actually reach

Everyone has the same story: a faucet that drips for months, a door that sticks every summer, a shelf that never got hung. Not because the fixes are hard, but because finding someone reliable felt harder than living with the problem.

We built TrustedHandyman Solutions to be the opposite of that story. One number, one clear price, one visit that knocks out the whole list. Based on Honolulu Ave in the heart of Montrose, we are your neighbors first and your handyman second.

We treat every home like it is our own: drop cloths down, shoes clean, work areas tidied before we leave. If something is not right, we come back and make it right. That is what the "Trusted" in our name has to mean, every single job.
