Welcome

A calm chair, a careful hand

L A Nails & Spa is known for more than nails. Clients praise the facials for softer, healthier-looking skin, and Lily's brow waxing draws regulars from across the area.

Walk-ins are welcome, credit cards are accepted, and there is private lot parking. Regulars mention Sonny for pedicures and Jess for acrylics.
