Property Development

We take residential and commercial projects from concept to completion: feasibility review, entitlements, design coordination, and delivery. Our focus is on communities that perform for decades, not just at opening.

Acquisition & Investment

We are continuously evaluating acquisition opportunities across New England. If you own land or an underperforming property, we can move quickly with a straightforward review process.

  • Land and income-property acquisitions
  • Off-market and broker submissions welcome

Construction Oversight

  • General contractor coordination
  • Schedule and budget tracking
  • Quality and safety compliance
  • Punch-list and turnover management

Property Management

  • Leasing and resident screening

About

A Worcester developer with a long view

Carabetta Enterprises is a real estate development company based in Worcester, Massachusetts. Our work centers on building and managing residential and commercial properties that serve their communities for the long term.

How we work

We stay close to every project. Development, construction oversight, and management sit under one roof, so decisions get made quickly and nothing gets lost between handoffs. The result is property that is built carefully and cared for consistently.
