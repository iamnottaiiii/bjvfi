About the shop

A small garage with a big reputation

M.A.C.K Automotive, LLC sits at 90 Saratoga Ave in Waterford, the kind of shop regulars describe as a needle in a haystack.

Ask around Waterford about where to take your car, and this address comes up. Under new ownership, the shop kept what mattered: a crew that diagnoses before it replaces, explains before it bills, and treats a tight budget like it is their own money.

Reviewers name the people, not just the place. Mechanics praised for honesty and a helpful attitude. A service writer who works directly with you on pricing. A team that fits in urgent jobs, like a muffler that could not wait, and stays transparent the whole way through.

The shop works on any car make and model, from daily drivers to work trucks. If a part is expensive through the usual channels, they will say so, and they have been known to help you source it cheaper and install it for you. Try finding that at a chain.
