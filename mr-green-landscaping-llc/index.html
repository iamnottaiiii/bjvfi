Services, by ecosystem layer

A healthy yard works like a little forest. We plan each job across three layers so trees, shrubs, and lawn all thrive together. Service list sourced from the business listings on Porch and HomeGuide.

Canopy

Layer one: trees

The top layer sets the shade and the structure. We plant, clear, and tidy the trees so the rest of the yard gets the light it needs.

Tree PlantingNew trees placed for shade and curb appeal
Shrub RemovalClearing overgrown or unwanted shrubs
Tree Stump RemovalStumps ground out and hauled away
Brush RemovalClearing brush, limbs, and debris

Understory

Layer two: beds and shrubs

The middle layer is where the color lives. We plant, mulch, and maintain beds so pollinators and passersby both stop to look.

Garden PlantingNew beds designed and planted
Garden MaintenanceRoutine care for established beds
MulchingFresh mulch to feed soil and stop weeds
Decorative LandscapingStone, edging, and accent features

Groundcover

The ground layer ties it all together. From fresh sod to weekly mowing, we keep the lawn thick, green, and healthy.

Sod InstallationInstant lawn, laid and watered in
Multi Service Lawn CareMowing, trimming, and edging plans
