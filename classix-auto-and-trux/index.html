About Classix Auto and Trux

A family salvage and restoration business on E Vorhees Street in Reading, Ohio.

Our story

Classix Auto and Trux started in September 1989 as Classix Auto Recyclers, Inc. Founder and owner Steve Ruben grew up in the trade, learning the auto salvage business as a child working alongside his father and uncle. That lifetime around wrecked, worn, and wonderful old cars became the foundation of the shop.

From those roots in auto recycling, the business grew into a trusted source for classic car and truck parts in the Cincinnati area. Restorers come to us for used components they cannot find anywhere else, and for hands on help like panel welding on vintage bodies. The name says it all: classix, the classic cars and trucks we love, and the people who keep them alive.
