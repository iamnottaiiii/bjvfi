About

03 / SHOP RECORD

Mueller's Auto Services is an independent, owner-operated auto repair shop at 2793 Robertson Ave in Cincinnati's Oakley neighborhood. Public business listings trace the shop's presence at this address back to at least 2010.

The shop is listed as an ACDelco service center, and reviewers describe it as a domestic-vehicle specialty shop known for fair pricing, on-time work, and an owner who diagnoses problems personally rather than handing them off.

Customer feedback spans decades, from locals who have used the shop for twenty years to road-trip travelers helped out on the spot. Hours and specific services vary across public listings, so confirm details by phone before visiting.
