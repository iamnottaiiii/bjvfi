Our Story

A stone shop on Keswick Street

Alvarez Marble works out of 14760 Keswick St in Van Nuys, in the middle of the Valley's stone and tile district. Being a marble contractor here means living close to the slab yards, which means more stone to choose from and faster turnarounds for our clients.

Stone is unforgiving material. A bad cut cannot be patched and a rushed install shows forever. That is why every Alvarez Marble project is measured twice, templated on site, and finished by hand. We would rather talk a client into the right stone than sell them the expensive one.

From a single bathroom vanity top to a full kitchen in Calacatta, our promise is the same: the vein lines up, the seams stay tight, and the finish is sealed to protect your investment for years.
