The Shop Story

A Northern Kentucky original with a second location across the river.

How It Started

  • Local directories list the shop as founded in 2016 on Alexandria Pike in Cold Spring.
  • Co-owners Adrian Echeveste and Jon Huddleston opened a second NorthSide Chop Shop location in New Richmond, Ohio, reported by The Clermont Sun in 2021.
  • The shop describes itself as a traditional barbershop: cuts, fades, beard and mustache trims, and hot towel straight-razor shaves.
  • The owners say the shop is a hangout spot too, and visitors are welcome to stop by anytime.

Good To Know

  • Walk-ins are welcomed daily, subject to chair availability.
  • Many barbers book appointments through theCut app.
  • Cash is preferred by the barbers; some accept CashApp.
  • Military discount offered, per local listings.
  • Wheelchair accessible entrance and parking lot.
  • First responder discounts have been offered in the past.
