What we do

Flooring services, tab by tab

Pick a material to see what Central PA Flooring installs and refinishes. Every project starts with an in-home look and a clear, written quote.

Hardwood flooring

Real wood floors installed right, with the grain patterns, stains and finishes that suit your home. We also sand and refinish tired hardwood back to life.

  • Solid and engineered hardwood installation
  • Oak, maple and other popular species
  • Stain color and finish guidance
  • Stair treads and transitions

Carpet

Soft underfoot for bedrooms, living rooms and basements. We help you pick a fiber and pad that can handle real life, then install it tight and clean.

  • Whole-room and whole-home installs
  • Stain-resistant and pet-friendly options
  • Padding selection and replacement
  • Stairs and landings

Laminate and luxury vinyl

The look of wood or tile with the toughness busy households need. Great for kitchens, mudrooms, basements and rentals.

  • Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and tile (LVT)
  • Waterproof and scratch-resistant lines
  • Laminate for high-traffic rooms
  • Underlayment and subfloor prep

Tile and stone

Ceramic, porcelain and stone for bathrooms, kitchens, entries and laundry rooms. Straight lines, tight grout, done once and done right.

  • Ceramic and porcelain floor tile
  • Bathrooms, kitchens and entries
  • Backsplashes to match your floors
  • Grout color and sealing advice

Sanding and refinishing

Love your wood floors but not their scratches? Refinishing costs far less than replacement and brings the original beauty back.

  • Sanding with dust control
  • New stain color or natural finish
  • Polyurethane and hardwax oil options
  • Spot repairs before the finish goes on
