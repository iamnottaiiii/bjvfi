Our services

Directory listings describe The Dental Corner Care Pc as a practice covering dentistry and optometry. Services below are general descriptions, not a confirmed fee schedule. Call (718) 523-0060 to confirm what the office currently offers.

01

General dentistry

Routine dental care starts with regular exams and cleanings. The office provides general dental services such as checkups, professional cleanings, fillings and preventive care for patients of all ages.

Consistent preventive visits are the simplest way to keep teeth and gums healthy. Ask the office about scheduling your next exam when you call.

02

Preventive and restorative care

Preventive care focuses on stopping problems before they start, while restorative care addresses issues like cavities when they arise. The practice serves patients in the 11432 area with everyday general dental treatment.

If something hurts or a filling feels off, do not wait. Call the office to describe your symptoms and ask about appointment availability.
