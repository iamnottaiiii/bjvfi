III

Our Story

California, Kentucky

Starlight Ridge Creations grew out of a simple love for working wood by hand. From our shop on Flagg Springs Pike in California, Kentucky, we build custom furniture, signs, and home pieces one commission at a time.

We believe a table should outlast the house it sits in, a sign should still be readable in fifty years, and a cutting board should get better every time you oil it. That means solid hardwood, honest joinery, and finishes applied by hand, not rushed off a line.

Whether it is a farmhouse table for Sunday dinners, a carved sign for your front porch, or a one-of-a-kind gift, we treat every commission like it is going in our own home. Call us and tell us what you are dreaming of. If it can be built from wood, we will build it well.
