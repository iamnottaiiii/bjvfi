How We Solve a Rodent Problem

01

Full Inspection

We find every entry point, nesting site, and run. Rodent work fails when the inspection is skipped. We never skip it.

02

Trap and Remove

Targeted trapping to physically remove the current population from your property.

03

Seal and Exclude

Every gap, vent, and crack sealed against re-entry. This is the step that makes the fix permanent.

04

Clean and Sanitize

Droppings, urine, and nesting material safely removed and the area decontaminated.

05

Monitor

Follow up checks to confirm the property stays rodent free, season after season.

Warning Signs

Sign 01

Droppings in kitchens, basements, and pantries

Sign 02

Scratching or scurrying in walls at night

Sign 03

Gnaw marks on wood, wires, and food packaging

Sign 04

A strong musky odor in enclosed spaces

Sign 05

Nesting material like shredded paper or insulation

Sign 06

Chewed electrical wiring, a fire risk
