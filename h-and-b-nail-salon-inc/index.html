Our story

About H & B

Small salon, big reputation

Business records show H & B Nail Salon Inc opened its doors in 2012, and more than a decade later it is still going strong on Avenue U in Sheepshead Bay.

Clients describe a clean, patient environment with a family oriented and welcoming atmosphere. Technicians are known for listening to exactly what you want, then adding their own creative spin, from cat eye designs to custom nail art.

The salon holds a New York State appearance enhancement business license, and reviewers regularly mention the owner, Helen, doing beautiful, careful work herself.
