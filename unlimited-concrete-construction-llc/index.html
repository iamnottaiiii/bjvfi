Our story

A local crew with a simple standard

Unlimited Concrete Construction LLC has served Taylor Mill and Northern Kentucky since 2018. Owner Adrian Hall focuses the company on residential flatwork: the driveways, patios, porches, sidewalks and steps that get used every single day.

The approach is straightforward. Show up when promised, prep the base properly, pour a clean slab, and leave the yard as tidy as it was found. Decorative and stamped work gets the same care, because a patio is where a family actually lives in summer.
