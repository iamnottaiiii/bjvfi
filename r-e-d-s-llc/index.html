Services

Consulting for every phase of the build

Pick a service to see what it covers. Every engagement is scoped around your project, your timeline and your budget.

Project planning

The blueprint for everything else. We define scope, sequence and success criteria before costs start moving.

  • ✓Scope definition. Clear deliverables and exclusions, agreed in writing.
  • ✓Milestone creation. A schedule with real checkpoints, not wishful dates.
  • ✓Process flow. Who does what, in what order, with what handoffs.
  • ✓Resource planning. Crews, equipment and materials lined up ahead of need.

On-site management

Experienced consultants on the ground, keeping trades coordinated and the schedule honest.

  • ✓Daily coordination. Subcontractors sequenced so nobody waits on anybody.
  • ✓Progress tracking. Milestone reporting you can actually read.
  • ✓Issue resolution. Problems solved on the slab, not in email threads.
  • ✓Paperwork reduction. We tame the change orders, logs and RFIs.

Budgeting and estimating

Numbers you can build on. We estimate with field experience, then guard the budget like it is our own money.

  • ✓Detailed estimates. Line-item costs for labor, materials and contingencies.
  • ✓Cost tracking. Spend vs. budget, visible at every milestone.
  • ✓Bid review. A second set of expert eyes on subcontractor pricing.
  • ✓Value options. Ways to cut cost without cutting quality.

Excavation and site work

The "E" in R.E.D.S. We advise on and coordinate the dirt work that every solid project starts with.

  • ✓Site evaluation. Grading, access and utility considerations up front.
  • ✓Excavation planning. Sequencing earthwork with the build schedule.
  • ✓Utility coordination. Locates and line crossings handled properly.
  • ✓Trade oversight. Excavation contractors held to spec and schedule.
Always call 811 before you dig. We build utility locating and safe excavation practice into every site plan.

Compliance consulting

Permits, codes and inspections without the runaround. We keep your project on the right side of every requirement.

  • ✓Permit guidance. What you need, where to file, and in what order.
  • ✓Code awareness. Kentucky and Campbell County requirements flagged early.
  • ✓Inspection readiness. Walkthroughs prepared so inspections pass the first time.
  • ✓Documentation. Records organized for owners, lenders and authorities.

Every project is different. Tell us about yours and we will scope the right level of support.

Our story

Built by people who have run the job site

Field-first consultants

Our people come from solid backgrounds in commercial and residential construction. You get experience earned year after year, job after job, not theory from a desk.

Local roots
