Our story

A neighborhood shop with a personal touch

Dry Cleaning Plaza, run by Connham L.L.C, is the kind of dry cleaner people talk about to their friends. Set on Dixwell Avenue in Hamden, next to Di Matteo's Pizza, the shop has built its name one garment at a time.

Regulars describe the gentleman who runs the shop as friendly, kind, and accommodating to whatever needs doing. It is the personal attention that keeps people loyal: suits tailored to look exactly as expected, laundry ready in a short time, and a counter experience that feels like visiting a friend.

Whether it is a full suit before an event, weekly shirts, or a simple hem, the goal is the same: quality work, fair pricing, and service that makes you want to come back.

Why it works

  • One owner who knows his regulars
  • Careful hand finishing on every piece
  • Fast, reliable turnarounds
  • Fair, no surprise pricing
