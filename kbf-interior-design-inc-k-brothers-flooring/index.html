Services

Flooring and design, all in one place

KBF is listed as a flooring store, a contractor and an interior designer. Select a service below to see what each one covers. Call (877) 523-2010 for details on your project.

Hardwood Flooring

Classic solid and engineered hardwood, supplied and installed by the KBF team. A strong choice for living rooms, bedrooms and hallways where you want warmth that lasts.

  • Material selection from flooring store inventory
  • Professional installation and finishing
  • Layout planning for grain direction and transitions
  • Repairs and refinishing guidance on existing wood floors
Pricing varies by wood species, board width and room size. Call for a project estimate.

Waterproof Flooring

Waterproof SPC and luxury vinyl style flooring that stands up to kitchens, basements, entryways and busy households with kids or pets.

  • Waterproof SPC plank options in wood looks
  • Moisture prone room planning and subfloor prep
  • Clean, tight installation with finished edges
  • Low maintenance care guidance after install
Pricing varies by product line and square footage. Call for a project estimate.

Carpet

Soft, quiet carpet for bedrooms, stairs and family rooms, selected with the same design eye as the rest of your project.

  • Carpet styles suited to bedrooms and living spaces
  • Color coordination with walls and furnishings
  • Professional installation with proper padding
  • Stair runners and custom fits
Pricing varies by fiber, pile and room size. Call for a project estimate.

Interior Design

The design side of KBF: help choosing flooring colors and finishes that work with your furniture, lighting and the way you actually live.

  • Room by room material and color consultation
  • Coordinating floors with paint, trim and furnishings
  • Space planning for remodels and refreshes
  • One team handling both the design and the install
Design consults are typically part of a flooring project. Ask when you call.

Our Story

The K Brothers behind the floors

KBF Interior Design Inc, known in the neighborhood as K Brothers Flooring, is a family style flooring business based on Mott Avenue in Inwood, New York. The idea is simple: sell good flooring, install it well and help people choose it with confidence.

Reviewers describe the experience in warm terms. One called it a quiet place with lovely people and a sweet atmosphere. Another simply said awesome. With a 4.7 average across third party reviews, the pattern is consistent: friendly people, a calm showroom and floors done right.

Because the business pairs contracting with interior design, customers get more than an installer. They get a second set of eyes on the whole room, so the floor they choose is the floor they will still love in ten years.
