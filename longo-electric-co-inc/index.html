Services

The work order.

Common electrical services for Stratford homes and job sites. Every visit starts with a clear diagnosis and a straight price before any work begins.

01Service calls

Electrical repairs

Outlets, switches, breakers, and fixtures that quit working. We find the fault and fix it, not just the symptom.

02Safety

Panel upgrades

Aging fuse boxes and overloaded panels swapped for modern breaker panels sized for today's loads.

03Backup power

Generator service and repair

Standby generator service for homes and small businesses, so the lights stay on when the grid goes down.
