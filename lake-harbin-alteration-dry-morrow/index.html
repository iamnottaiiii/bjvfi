Alterations that make clothes yours again

Off-the-rack rarely fits like it should. Bring in pants, dresses, jackets, and uniforms and leave with garments that feel made for you.

  • Hemming for pants, skirts, and dresses
  • Taking in and letting out
  • Zipper and seam repairs
  • Uniform and workwear adjustments

Dry cleaning for the pieces you love

Suits, dresses, coats, and delicates are cleaned gently and finished with a crisp press, so everything comes back ready to wear.
