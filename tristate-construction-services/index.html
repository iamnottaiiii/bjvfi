About Tristate
Construction Services

A remodeling contractor built on repeat customers

Tristate Construction Services is a Florence, Kentucky contractor providing exterior and interior remodeling services including roofing, siding, bathrooms, kitchens, basements, flooring, and more.

Our reputation is simple: we walk homeowners through each step of the process, sweat the details, and stand behind the work. That is why customers who hire us for one project call us back for the next, like the homeowner who trusted us with a kitchen remodel and then with a full roof replacement after wind damage.

From our office on Kentucky Drive in Florence, we serve homeowners across the Greater Cincinnati tristate area.
