Our story

A small local crew with one standard: clearly clean.

Clear Services LLC is a cleaning business based in Alexandria, Virginia. We started with a simple idea: most people do not need a big company with a big process. They need someone reliable who shows up, does the work carefully, and treats their home with respect.

That is how we work. Every visit gets the same attention whether it is a weekly upkeep clean or a full move out reset. We bring our own supplies, we follow a room by room checklist, and we do a final walkthrough before we leave.

If something is not right, tell us. We would rather come back and fix it than leave you wondering. Clear name, clear promise.
