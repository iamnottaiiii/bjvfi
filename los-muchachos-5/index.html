About us

Los Muchachos 5 is a Dominican barbershop on Myrtle Avenue in Brooklyn, New York. The shop has a team of barbers offering a variety of haircut styles and grooming services in a friendly, welcoming atmosphere. Appointments are recommended to avoid long wait times.
