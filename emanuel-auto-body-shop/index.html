About Emanuel

Emanuel Auto Body Shop at 58-10 Broadway in Woodside (Flushing), Queens is a customer-focused collision and paint shop. Drivers praise the team, including Roula and Niko, for fast turnarounds, fair pricing, and treating people like neighbors instead of claim numbers.

Services include collision repair, auto painting, bumper repair, and body and trim work. One customer brought in a busted bumper and had it fixed the same day; another credits the shop with saving their SUV after a serious accident, with paint work so clean it looked untouched.

We work with most insurance companies and also welcome out-of-pocket repairs. Call (718) 361-0002 for a free estimate.
