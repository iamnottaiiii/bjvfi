About

Paul's standards

Since 2007

A neighborhood shop
that treats cars like its own

Paul's Auto Body opened on 80th Street in Glendale in 2007, and it has stayed deliberately small: a tight crew led by Paul Trinca, doing body work, paint and detailing the careful way.

Drivers come in for a scraped bumper or a crumpled fender and leave talking about two things: the paint match, and the honesty. Estimates are explained plainly, the cheapest sensible fix is on the table, and the car comes back on the day it was promised.

With a 5.0 rating across dozens of reviews, the shop has become the place Glendale and Ridgewood drivers recommend by name when someone asks where to get body work done right.

Small crew

A handful of people who know your car by sight, not a ticket number.

Paint-first reputation

Color matching so close that repairs vanish into the factory finish.

Fair pricing

Explained estimates and honest options, mentioned in review after review.
