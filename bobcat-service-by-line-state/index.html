Services

Site Preparation

Getting a lot ready for construction: stripping topsoil, cutting and filling to design grades, and leaving a clean, buildable pad for the next phase of work.

  • Topsoil stripping and stockpiling
  • Cut and fill to planned grades
  • Building pads and lot leveling

Trenching

Straight, safe trenches for the lines that run under your project, cut to depth and backfilled compactly so nothing settles later.

  • Utility trenches for water, sewer and electric
  • Drainage and French drain trenches
  • Footing trenches for additions and outbuildings

Grading and Leveling

Rough and finish grading that shapes the land to drain correctly and look right doing it.

  • Rough grading after construction
  • Finish grading for lawns and landscaping
  • Swales and drainage correction

Land Clearing

Removing brush, stumps and debris so the real work can begin, with the site left workable.

  • Brush and small tree removal
  • Stump digging and removal
  • Debris cleanup and rough clearing

Septic and Utility Digging

Excavation for septic systems and underground utilities, coordinated to the specs your installer or county requires.

  • Septic tank and drain field excavation
  • Utility line digging
  • Repair access digging

Backfill and Hauling

Putting the ground back right: compacted backfill around foundations and trenches, and hauling away what you do not need.

  • Foundation and trench backfill
  • Soil and material hauling
  • Spread and compact services

Our Story

A Pasadena excavation outfit that treats every lot like it is the only one that matters.

Bobcat Service By Line State works out of 8343 Baltimore Annapolis Boulevard in Pasadena, Maryland, right in the middle of the Anne Arundel County neighborhoods it serves. The name says what we do: bobcat and excavation service, by the line, on state roads and local lots alike.

Excavation is invisible work when it is done well. Nobody admires a trench. But every foundation, driveway and drainage system in this county sits on dirt somebody moved. We take that seriously: straight lines, correct depths, grades that drain, and a site the next trade can walk onto without tripping.

We work with homeowners digging out for additions, septic repairs and yard drainage, and with builders and contractors who need a reliable machine on site. One crew, honest numbers, and communication that does not leave you guessing.
