From public listing. Solid and engineered hardwood installed in remodels and new construction: nail-down, glue-down, and floating methods depending on your subfloor.

Good installers measure moisture, acclimate the wood to your house, and plan the layout so the boards run the right direction. That is what decades of practice buys you.

Typical for the trade. Call to confirm. Sanding old finish down to bare wood, then staining and sealing with fresh coats. Usually the best value in flooring: a fraction of replacement cost for a floor that looks new.

Many longtime shops offer dust-containment sanding systems that keep most of the mess out of your living room. Ask about it when you call.

From public listing. Board replacement, squeak fixes, gap repair, cupping and buckling from moisture, and patching after water damage.

A repair-first shop will tell you when a floor can be saved, and when it honestly cannot. That is the advantage of a contractor that only works with wood.

Typical for the trade. Call to confirm. Stair treads, risers, and nosing in matching wood, plus thresholds and reducers where hardwood meets tile or carpet.

Stairs take the most abuse of any floor in the house. Refinishing them along with the floors keeps everything matching.

Typical for the trade. Call to confirm. Changing the color of existing floors: going darker, lighter, or matching new boards to old ones.

Color matching across rooms and years is part craft, part chemistry. Bring a photo when you call; the shop can tell you what is realistic.

Typical for the trade. Call to confirm. Offices, rentals, restaurants, and small commercial spaces with hardwood that needs to hold up under foot traffic.

Why hardwood-only matters

Specialists, not generalists

A contractor that only installs and refinishes hardwood sees every quirk the material has: moisture movement, sun bleaching, finish failures, subfloor problems. That focus shows up in the final floor.

If your floors are worn but solid, a refinishing-first mindset can save you thousands over replacement. It is worth asking the question.
