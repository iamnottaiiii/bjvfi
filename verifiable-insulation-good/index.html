What we do

One trade, done precisely

We focus on the building envelope: the insulation and air sealing that decide how hard your HVAC works and how even your rooms feel.

01 · Attics

Attic insulation, done to spec

Most heat gain and loss happens through the attic. We bring attics up to proper R-value with blown-in or batt insulation, installed evenly with no gaps or compression.

02 · Air sealing

Seal the leaks first

Insulation underperforms when air moves through it. We seal top plates, penetrations, and chases before insulating, so the R-value you pay for is the R-value you get.

Services · Insulation technology

The right material,
for the right cavity

Different parts of a house need different insulation. Expand each technology below to see what it does and where it fits.

Loose-fill cellulose or fiberglass blown to a uniform depth across the attic floor. It flows around pipes, wires, and framing, leaving far fewer gaps than hand-placed batts in irregular attics.

Best for: attic floors and topping up old, settled insulation

Pre-cut blankets fitted between studs, joists, and rafters. Cost-effective and predictable in open, accessible framing, and a solid choice for new walls and garage ceilings.

Best for: open wall cavities, floors, and accessible framing

Expanding foam that both insulates and air-seals in one pass, reaching the highest R-value per inch of common residential options. Ideal where space is tight or air sealing is the priority.

Best for: rim joists, knee walls, and hard-to-reach cavities

Reflective foil installed under the roof deck to bounce radiant heat back out before it ever reaches the attic. A strong complement to attic insulation in hot Southern California summers.

Best for: reducing summer heat gain in vented attics

Sealing the gaps insulation cannot fix: plumbing and electrical penetrations, top plates, recessed light housings, and attic hatches. Done before insulating, it protects the R-value of everything above it.

Best for: every attic, before any new insulation goes in

Old, contaminated, or pest-damaged insulation is vacuumed out, the attic is cleaned and sanitized, and fresh insulation goes in on a clean slate. Often paired with rodent-proofing entry points.

Best for: attics with old, damaged, or contaminated insulation
