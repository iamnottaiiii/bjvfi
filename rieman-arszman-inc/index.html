Services

Finish samples, honest and up close

These panels show common drywall finishes. Call to confirm which finishes are currently offered and to discuss your project.

Smooth / Level 5
Sample finish, call to confirm

A flat, uniform surface with no visible texture. The premium choice for rooms with strong natural light and bold paint colors, where every ripple would show.

Knockdown
Sample finish, call to confirm

Sprayed texture lightly flattened for a soft, mottled look. Forgiving of minor wall imperfections and popular in living areas and bedrooms.

Orange Peel
Sample finish, call to confirm

A fine, even stipple that resembles citrus skin. Subtle, durable, and the standard texture in much of new residential construction.
