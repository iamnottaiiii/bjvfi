What we do

One shop, every repair

Collision, bodywork, paint, and everyday mechanical care. Below are the kinds of jobs customers know us for. Sample list

Collision repair done right

From fender benders to major accidents, we bring damaged vehicles back to pre-accident condition. Our customers say their cars leave looking stunning, like new again.

We are a Tesla certified repair shop serving drivers across DC, Maryland, and Virginia.

  • Accident damage repair - structural and cosmetic
  • Frame and panel work - aligned back to spec
  • Free estimates - know the cost before you commit

Dents, dings, and bumpers

Small damage makes a big difference in how a car looks. We repair dents, replace and refinish bumpers, and handle the little jobs other shops rush through.

  • Dent and ding repair - careful metal finishing
  • Bumper repair and replacement - color matched
  • Trim and accessory installs - done cleanly, first try

Paint that looks factory fresh

Faded panels, a scuffed bumper cover, or a full refresh: we match factory colors and lay down a clean, even finish that holds up.

  • Bumper cover painting - a customer favorite
  • Fade and scratch correction - panels restored
  • Color matching - blended to factory spec

Our story

A family shop with a neighborhood reputation

Built on honest work

G & S Auto Svc & Collision has been serving Springfield and the surrounding Northern Virginia area since 2009. We are a family run business, and customers regularly mention the father and son team working side by side in the shop.

Our philosophy is simple and printed on everything we do: top quality auto repair work at a fair price. No pressure, no mystery charges, just clear explanations and craftsmanship we stand behind.

Drivers come to us after accidents, before inspections, and for the everyday care their cars need. Many tell us they will never take their vehicles anywhere else, and they send their friends and family too.
