What we do

Concrete Services

Schultz Construction handles the concrete work homes and small businesses rely on. Pick a service below to learn more, then call to talk through your project.

Concrete Driveways

A driveway takes daily abuse from cars, weather, and freeze-thaw cycles. A properly graded, reinforced, and cured concrete driveway handles all of it for decades. We remove old concrete, prep the base, pour to the right thickness, and finish it for drainage and durability. Call to discuss new installs or full tear-outs and replacements.

Concrete Patios

Extend your living space outside with a concrete patio that fits your yard and your budget. From simple square pours to curved edges and decorative finishes, a patio gives you a level, low-maintenance surface for furniture, grills, and gatherings. Call to walk through sizes, finishes, and options.

Sidewalks & Walkways

Cracked or uneven walkways are trip hazards and curb-appeal killers. We pour new sidewalks and walkways and replace failing ones, grading for proper drainage so water flows away from the slab and your foundation. Ask about a site visit to look at your current walk.

Repair & Replacement

Not every slab needs a full replacement. Some can be patched, sealed, or lifted back into place. When we look at your concrete, we will tell you honestly whether repair makes sense or replacement is the smarter money. No upselling, just a straight recommendation.
