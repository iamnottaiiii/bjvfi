Our story

The shop is a van

Montae mobile mechanic is a mobile auto repair service based in Brandywine, Maryland. No waiting room, no front desk, no shuttle home. Just a mechanic with a well-stocked van and a phone that gets answered.

Built around your schedule,
not a shop's hours

The idea is simple: most car repairs do not need a building. Brakes, batteries, starters, alternators, diagnostics, oil changes, all of it can be done cleanly and correctly in a driveway or parking spot.

Montae mobile mechanic was built on that idea for drivers in Brandywine and nearby Southern Maryland. You call with a problem, you get an upfront price, and the repair happens where your car already is.

Evening appointments run until 8 PM Monday through Friday and until 8 PM Saturday, because breakdowns do not check the clock. Sundays are for rest, and for catching up on the week's paperwork.

If your car will not come to the shop, the shop comes to your car.
