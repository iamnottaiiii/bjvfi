About us

Family owned.
Valley rooted.

SCCCI has been remodeling homes across the San Fernando Valley, Ventura County and Santa Clarita since 1986.

Nearly four decades of construction experience, one family name on the door.

President Larry Litberg leads a team that treats every home like their own. We specialize in long lasting, energy efficient products because that is what California homes need: comfort without constant upkeep.

Our service area covers the San Fernando Valley, Ventura County, Santa Clarita Valley and Burbank, from Chatsworth and Northridge to Thousand Oaks, Simi Valley and Santa Monica.
