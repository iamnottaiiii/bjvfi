Our story

Faccia Bella means "beautiful face" in Italian. On Lyell Avenue, it means a salon where everyone is welcome and every head of hair gets the attention it deserves.

A neighborhood salon,
done with heart

Faccia Bella is a family-friendly salon in the Lyell Avenue neighborhood of Rochester. The team specializes in the full range of hair services: precision cuts for men, women, and children, plus expert coloring including highlights, balayage, ombre, and corrective color.

What sets the salon apart is patience. Stylists are known for taking their time, asking questions as they work, and checking that the result matches what you pictured. They are trained on all hair types, from curly to straight, so everyone can sit in the chair with confidence.

Beyond cuts and color, the salon offers keratin smoothing, perms, blowouts, roller sets, deep conditioning, extensions, braiding, and locs. One visit, every need.
