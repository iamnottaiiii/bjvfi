Our Story

North Hills Comfort, Industrial Strength

Industrial Electrical and HVAC Services, Inc. is based at 15905 Chase St in North Hills, CA, serving homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley.

We take a different approach to climate control: integrating professional electrical work with HVAC service. That means safer installations, more energy-efficient systems, and work that meets California codes and standards, all from a single crew.

For commercial and industrial clients, downtime is costly. We handle rooftop units, ventilation systems, process cooling, and custom ductwork for facilities of all sizes, and we work discreetly to keep your operations running while we work.

Whether it is a broken AC on a summer afternoon or a new furnace before winter, our technicians deliver fast, reliable service with top-quality workmanship.
