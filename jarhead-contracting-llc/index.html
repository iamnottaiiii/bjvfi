What we paint

Residential and light commercial work across Harrisburg and the surrounding area. One crew, one standard.

01

Interior painting

Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, hallways, ceilings, and trim. Clean lines at every edge, floors and furniture protected while we work.

02

Exterior painting

Siding, brick, stucco, porches, and decks. Proper scraping, priming, and caulking first, so the finish holds up to Pennsylvania weather.

03

Prep and repair

Drywall patching, sanding, caulking, and stain blocking. The unglamorous part of the job, done thoroughly, because paint only looks as good as the surface under it.

04

Color help and touch-ups

Help picking colors and finishes for your light and your space, plus touch-ups and single-room refreshes when a full repaint is more than you need.

How a job runs

No surprises. Here is the sequence, start to finish.

1. Call

Tell us about the project. We will set a time to look at it in person.

2. Estimate

A written estimate with the scope, the paint, and the timeline.

3. Prep

Protection, scraping, sanding, patching, priming. The foundation of the finish.

4. Paint

Two coats where they are needed, clean-up daily, walkthrough when we are done.

About

A Harrisburg painting crew

Jarhead Contracting LLC is a Pennsylvania-registered painting contractor based at 2145 N 5th St, Harrisburg, PA.

EST

Local and accountable

Jarhead Contracting LLC is registered in Pennsylvania and works across Harrisburg and the surrounding communities. When you call, you reach the people doing the work, not a call center.

HOW

Prep first, always

Most paint failures start before the first coat goes on. We scrape, sand, patch, caulk, and prime properly, then paint. It takes longer up front and lasts years longer after.

WHO

Homeowners and property managers

Single rooms, whole houses, rental turnovers, and small commercial spaces. If it needs paint and it is in the Harrisburg area, it is worth a call.

WHY

Done right the first time

Straight cut lines, even coverage, and a clean site at the end of every day. The goal is simple: work you would recommend to a neighbor.
