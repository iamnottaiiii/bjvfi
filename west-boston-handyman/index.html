About

Your local fix-it crew

West Boston Handyman is a contractor and handyman service based in Westborough, Massachusetts, serving homeowners across the MetroWest area.

Who we are

Big jobs. Small jobs.
Done right.

We started West Boston Handyman because too many homeowners sit on a growing repair list, waiting for a contractor big enough to care about small jobs, or handy enough to do them well.

That is where we live: the leaky faucet and the full room repaint, the wobbly shelf and the new backsplash. One reliable crew, one clear estimate, one visit that actually finishes the list.

  • Residential repairs, installs and improvements
  • Based in Westborough, serving MetroWest Boston
  • On time, tidy and easy to reach by phone
Our promise

Respect for your home
and your time.

Trust takes years to build and one bad visit to lose. We protect ours on every job: shoe covers when it makes sense, drop cloths always, and a walkthrough before we call it done.

Not happy with something? Tell us on the spot and we make it right. That is the whole policy.
