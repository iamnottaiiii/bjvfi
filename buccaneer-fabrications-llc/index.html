How it works

Three steps to finished steel

1. Tell us the job

Call (813) 495-6849 with your measurements, a sketch, or a photo of what is broken. We will talk through options and give you a straight quote.

2. We build it

Cutting, fitting, welding, grinding, and finishing in the shop, or on your site with our mobile rig.

3. It fits, period

We check fit and finish before it leaves our hands. If it is not right, it does not ship.

Our story

A small shop with
a big standard

Buccaneer Fabrications LLC is a steel fabrication shop in Sun City Center, Florida, built on a simple idea: treat every job like it carries your name.

Measure twice, cut once

Steel is unforgiving, so we are careful. Dimensions get double-checked, welds get inspected, and nothing leaves the shop until it is right.

Ask Roy

Customers know Roy by name. He asks the questions other shops skip, because understanding the job is how you get it right the first time.

Local and reachable

We are right here on Sage Creek Dr in Sun City Center, serving Riverview, Apollo Beach, Ruskin, and greater Tampa Bay. Call and a person answers.
