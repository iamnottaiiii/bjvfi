The house story

A small lot with a big gavel

Cincy’s Finest Auto sits at 34 W State Rd in Cleves, Ohio, an independent car lot that does business the old fashioned way: face to face, handshake to handshake. The name on the sign promises the finest, and the regulars who keep coming back are the reason it sticks.

Public reviews paint the picture of a working lot run by Tre and a small crew. Customers describe him as genuine, sympathetic, and willing to go above and beyond when something goes wrong after a purchase, including stepping in fast for families who needed a replacement vehicle in a hurry.

The detailing side of the business has its own following. Reviewers talk about vehicles transformed inside and out, from a well worn 2010 Nissan Maxima brought back to showroom form to a 28 year old truck returned looking and smelling new. One reviewer even notes the shop’s friendly puppy, Max, greeting customers at the door.

Doors are open Monday through Saturday, 2:00 PM to 9:00 PM, closed Sundays. If you are passing through Cleves, stop in and see what is on the block this week.
