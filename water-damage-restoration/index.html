Services

What our crew does,
step by step

Pick a service to see exactly what is involved. Every job starts with an inspection and a clear plan, agreed with you before work begins.

Water Extraction

The first and most urgent step. Standing water keeps soaking deeper into floors, walls, and subfloors every hour it sits, so we remove it fast with commercial grade equipment.

  • High powered pumps and vacuums for standing water
  • Moisture mapping to find hidden pockets behind walls
  • Belongings moved to dry areas to prevent further damage
  • Source verification so the leak is stopped, not just mopped
Standing water right now?Call (626) 470-1857

Structural Drying

Once standing water is out, moisture remains trapped in drywall, wood, and concrete. Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers pull it out over several days while we monitor progress.

  • Commercial air movers and low grain refrigerant dehumidifiers
  • Daily moisture readings to track drying to the dry standard
  • Hardwood floor mat drying systems to save cupping wood
  • Containment to keep drying focused and your home livable
Damp walls or musty smells?Call (626) 470-1857

Mold Remediation

Where moisture lingers, mold follows. Our remediation work is thorough enough to pass inspection the first time, with containment that keeps spores from spreading.

  • Mold inspection and moisture source identification
  • Containment barriers and negative air pressure
  • Safe removal of affected materials
  • Antimicrobial treatment and prevention guidance
Worried about mold?Call (626) 470-1857

Sewage Cleanup

Sewage backups are a health hazard, not a DIY project. We handle contaminated water with full protective protocols, thorough sanitizing, and proper disposal.

  • Safe extraction of contaminated Category 3 water
  • Removal and disposal of unsalvageable materials
  • Deep sanitizing of affected surfaces
  • Odor removal and air treatment
Sewage backup emergency?Call (626) 470-1857

Fire and Smoke Damage

Fires leave two problems: the burn damage, and the water damage from putting the fire out. We handle both, plus the soot and smoke odor that settles into everything.

  • Water extraction from firefighting efforts
  • Soot and smoke residue cleaning
  • Odor neutralization throughout the property
  • Contents cleaning and restoration where possible
Dealing with fire aftermath?Call (626) 470-1857

Reconstruction

Once everything is dry and clean, we put your space back together: drywall, flooring, paint, and trim, finished to match what was there before.

  • Drywall replacement and finishing
  • Flooring repair and replacement
  • Interior painting and trim work
  • Final walkthrough before we call it done
Ready to rebuild?Call (626) 470-1857

Who we are

Neighbors helping
neighbors dry out

Our crew is based on Lincoln Ave in Altadena, and most of our work happens within a few miles of home. That matters when water is spreading: a local crew gets there faster and knows these houses, these streets, and these storms.

We started this work because we kept seeing the same story: a burst pipe or a storm leak turns into a much bigger disaster when nobody shows up fast enough. So we built a crew around showing up, communicating clearly, and staying particular about the details until the job is truly done.

From burst pipes inside walls to storm flooding to mold hiding behind drywall, we have seen just about every way water finds into a home, and we know how to get it back out.
