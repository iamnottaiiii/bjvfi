Our story

Four decades of keeping promises

The Fischer Group was founded in 1980 in Northern Kentucky. From the beginning, the operating philosophy has been straightforward: promise only what you can deliver, and deliver what you promise.

1

1980: The beginning

A family-founded builder opens its doors in Northern Kentucky with a focus on quality craftsmanship and honest dealing.

2

Growth across the region

Communities expand across Northern Kentucky, Cincinnati, Columbus, Indianapolis, Louisville, Dayton, and St. Louis, with later growth into Atlanta and Florida markets.

3

A national top-30 builder

Recognition as one of the nation's 30 largest builders reflects more than 30,000 homes delivered to American families.

4

Today

Headquartered at 3940 Olympic Blvd in Erlanger, KY, the team continues to develop communities and operate the Lifestyle Design Center for homebuyers.

Public review themes are mixed. Positive reviewers mention community variety and design choices, while critical reviews raise concerns about build timelines, communication during construction, and post-closing warranty service. We encourage every buyer to read recent reviews and ask direct questions before signing.

Design and community choice

Satisfied reviewers point to a wide selection of floor plans and the ability to personalize finishes at the design center.

Build timelines

Several critical reviews describe builds taking longer than expected, with frustration over shifting completion dates.

Communication during the build

A recurring theme in lower-rated reviews is poor communication from the builder while the home is under construction.

Post-closing service

Some reviewers report difficulty getting warranty items addressed after move-in, while others report smooth resolutions.
