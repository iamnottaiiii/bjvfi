Our story

Family owned. Toolbelt tested.

HandiWorx of Greater Cincinnati grew out of more than 35 years in and around the construction field. We specialize in the projects most larger contractors don't want: the small, fiddly, must-get-done jobs that make a house feel like home.

Professional, reliable, and detailed. That's the standard on every visit, whether it's a full bathroom tile replacement or a ten-minute door-knob patch.

We're based on Pamela Road in Anderson Township and serve the Greater Cincinnati area, with free estimates, affordable rates, and 24-hour emergency service when things can't wait.
