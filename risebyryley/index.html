Our story

Rise by Ryley:
a bakery with a sunrise in its name

Every bakery has a story. Ours starts early, with a hot oven and a simple promise: bake it like it matters, because it does.

From the beginning

Baked for neighbors,
named with love

RisebyRyley is a hometown bakery on 121st Place in Alsip, Illinois. The name is the mission: bread that rises with the sun, made by Ryley, for the people around her.

Small batches, honest ingredients, and custom cakes for the moments that matter. That is the whole business plan, and it is a good one.

This page is a preview. Ryley, when you are ready, tell us your real story, your first bake, your regulars, your why, and we will make this page sing.

Small batches

Nothing mass-produced. When the case is empty, the day's baking is done, and that is the point.

Real ingredients

Real butter, real vanilla, real care. You can taste the difference, and so can your guests.

Hometown heart

A neighborhood shop in Alsip, baking for the block, the school fundraiser, and the Sunday table.
