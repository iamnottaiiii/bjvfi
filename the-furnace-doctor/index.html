Heating help, three ways

Most calls fall into one of these. Pick yours and we will take it from there.

01 / REPAIR

It stopped working

No heat, strange noises, short cycling, or a system that will not stay lit. We diagnose first, then fix what is actually broken.

02 / TUNE-UP

It is working, barely

Rising bills or uneven rooms mean your furnace is working too hard. A full tune-up restores efficiency before winter peaks.

03 / REPLACE

It is time for a new one

When repairs cost more than they are worth, we size and install a new system for your home and your budget.

About The Furnace Doctor

A local heating specialist in Hebron, Kentucky, focused on one thing: keeping your heat on.

HEBRON BASED

Your neighbors, not a call center

We are located at 1050 Arbor Tech Dr in Hebron and serve homes across Boone County and Northern Kentucky. When you call, you talk to someone who knows the area and the equipment in it.
