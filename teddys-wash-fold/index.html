Our story

A fresh start on
East Ferry Street

Teddy's Wash & Fold opened under new ownership with one simple idea: a neighborhood laundromat that is genuinely clean, fairly priced, and run by people who care.

Fair Haven, New Haven

New name, new energy,
same neighborhood

Teddy's sits on East Ferry Street in the heart of Fair Haven, one of New Haven's most lived-in, hard-working neighborhoods. When new ownership took over, the mission was simple: keep the machines humming, keep the place spotless, and treat every customer like a neighbor.

It is working. Reviews keep mentioning the same things: how clean it is, how cozy it feels, how fair the prices are, and how the owner, Ryan, goes out of his way to help, especially if you are new to the area or new to doing laundry outside the home.

Whether you speak English or Spanish, you will be welcomed here. Laundry is universal, and so is good service.

  • Under new ownership, refreshed and re-energized
  • Bilingual help: English and Spanish spoken here
  • All supplies sold on site, nothing to remember to bring
