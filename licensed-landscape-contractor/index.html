About

A licensed contractor for Valley yards.

Licensed Landscape Contractor is a landscaping contractor based at 10055 Pali Ave in Tujunga, California, serving homeowners across Tujunga and the San Fernando Valley. The name says the priority: licensed work, done to a professional standard.

Tujunga yards live in a unique spot, foothill sun, canyon breezes, and real water limits. We plan landscapes around those realities: plants that belong here, irrigation that wastes nothing, and hardscaping that handles the heat.
