Our story

A small shop with a simple promise

GS AUTO REPAIR is an independent auto repair shop at 2660 Millersport Highway in Getzville, NY. No corporate script, no service-writer runaround. You talk to the people who work on your car.

The shop handles the work most drivers actually need: oil changes and fluid service, tires and brakes, batteries, diagnostics and general mechanical repair. The kind of maintenance that keeps a daily driver reliable through Buffalo winters and summer road trips alike.

Because it is a neighborhood shop, every customer matters. Jobs are quoted up front, old parts are shown on request, and if something can safely wait, we say so.

Getzville, Amherst, Williamsville and the surrounding towns keep us busy. If your car needs honest attention, come see what a small shop does differently.
