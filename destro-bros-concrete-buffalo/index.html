How we work

From cracked slab
to solid ground

A straight process, no surprises. You know what is happening, when, and what it costs before a single form is set.

01

Look + estimate

We come out, measure the job, and talk through options: repair vs. replace, finishes, and drainage. You get a clear written estimate.

02

Prep + pour

Old concrete out, base graded and compacted, forms set true. Then the pour: leveled, floated, and finished the right way for the job.

03

Finish + stand behind it

Clean edges, proper joints, and a full cure before we call it done. We leave the site clean and the work ready for Buffalo weather.

Who we are

Family name on the trucks

Destro & Bros Concrete Co Inc runs a working fleet of trucks out of 411 Ludington St, Buffalo, NY 14206, pouring concrete for homes and for the city itself.

The company has served as general contractor on City of Buffalo projects, the kind of municipal flatwork and utility concrete that has to meet spec and hold up under real traffic.

On the residential side, it is the everyday work Buffalo homeowners actually need: driveways that drain, sidewalks that stay level, and patios that survive freeze-thaw season after season.
