Chapter one · The July test

When the valley heats up, your system has one job.

Every summer, Arcadia afternoons climb into the high 90s and your air conditioner becomes the hardest working appliance in the house. That is when weak capacitors, clogged coils, and tired compressors show themselves, usually on the hottest Saturday of the year.

We keep homes comfortable with honest diagnostics and repairs that fix the actual problem. If a simple part will do, we say so. If your system is done, we will tell you that too, and lay out replacement options without pressure.

What a visit looks like

  • Full system check: airflow, refrigerant, electrical, and thermostat
  • Plain-language explanation before any work begins
  • Upfront quote for the repair, approved by you first
  • Work area left clean when we leave

Good to know

  • Change or check your filter every 1 to 3 months in cooling season
  • Keep two feet of clearance around your outdoor condenser
  • A tune-up each spring is cheaper than a compressor in August
  • Short cycling or ice on the lines means it is time to call

Who we are

Built for Arcadia summers.

Summer Heating and Air Conditioning is an HVAC contractor based in Arcadia, California, serving homes across the San Gabriel Valley. The name says it plainly: we keep you cool when the heat arrives and warm when the cold snaps follow.
