Laundry, done right in the North End.

Lan’s Laundry is the neighborhood’s trusted spot for self service washing, careful wash and fold, dry cleaning, and alterations, all at 256 North Street in Boston’s North End.

For years, neighbors on North Street have brought their laundry to Lan’s and gotten it back clean, folded, and on time. The machines are clean and dependable, the owner is known by name, and the service is personal in a way the big chains never manage.

Whether you do it yourself or drop it off, whether it is a weekly bag or a suit that needs dry cleaning, Lan’s treats your clothes like they matter. Because here, they do.

About

Three decades on North Street

Lan’s Laundry has served Boston’s North End for about 36 years, a small shop that earned its reputation one load at a time.

The shop

Personal service, professional results

Lan’s is the kind of place where the owner knows the regulars and the regulars know the machines. It sits at 256 North St, in one of Boston’s oldest and most walked neighborhoods, and it has been part of the street’s daily life for decades.

What sets it apart is not size or flash. It is consistency: clean machines, honest prices, careful folding, dry cleaning done right, and alterations with a tailor’s eye. Neighbors describe years of reliable service in exactly those words.
