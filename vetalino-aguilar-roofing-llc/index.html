Services

Pick a lane. We handle the rest.

Choose the tab that fits your situation. Every job starts with an inspection and a written estimate, and you decide nothing until you are ready.

Full roof replacement

When repairs stop making sense, we do a complete tear-off and install a new system: shingles, underlayment, flashing, and ventilation.

  • Architectural asphalt shingles
  • Ice and water shield in valleys and eaves
  • Ridge vent and intake ventilation
  • Full debris removal and magnetic nail sweep
Sample: full replacements often start near $7,500 for average homes. Your written estimate is free.

New roof installation

Building or adding on? We roof new construction and additions to match your existing home and local code.

  • Coordination with your builder or GC
  • Material and color selection help
  • Proper decking inspection before install

Gutters and flashing

Many "roof leaks" are really gutter or flashing problems. We repair, replace, and reseal the details that keep water out.

  • Seamless gutter repair and replacement
  • Chimney and wall flashing
  • Pipe boot and vent resealing
Sample: most gutter repairs fall between $150 and $600.

Roof inspections

Buying, selling, or just want peace of mind? Get photos, a condition report, and honest advice on repair versus replace.

  • Photo-documented reports
  • Insurance and real-estate friendly
  • Maintenance recommendations

Small commercial roofs

Shops, offices, and rental properties need roofs that protect the business below. We handle low-slope and small flat systems.

  • TPO and modified bitumen repair
  • Coating and resealing options
  • Scheduled maintenance plans

Preventive maintenance

Twice-a-year checkups catch ponding, seam wear, and clogged drains before they become interior damage.

  • Spring and fall inspections
  • Drain and scupper clearing
  • Written condition logs for your records

Leak repair

Water stains, drips, or damp attic insulation? We trace leaks to the source instead of guessing.

  • Same-week appointments when possible
  • Shingle, flashing, and valley repairs
  • Attic moisture checks
Sample: most leak repairs land between $250 and $900 depending on the cause.

Storm damage response

After wind or hail, we tarp exposed areas fast, document the damage, and help you understand the repair path.

  • Emergency tarping
  • Photo documentation
  • Insurance claim support

Our story

Our name goes on every roof.

Vetalino Aguilar Roofing LLC is a roofing contractor based in Covington, Kentucky, serving homeowners and small businesses across Northern Kentucky. We are a working crew, not a call center: the people who inspect your roof are the people who fix it.

We started this company on a simple idea. Most roofing stress comes from not knowing who to trust. So we show our work: photos before and after, written estimates with real numbers, and a cleanup you can verify with your own eyes.

Kentucky weather is rough on roofs, from summer storms to freeze-thaw winters. We build and repair with that in mind, using materials and methods suited to this climate, and we stand behind the workmanship.
