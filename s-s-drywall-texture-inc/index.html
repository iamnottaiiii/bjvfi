Drywall Installation

New construction, additions and remodels across St Cloud and Central Florida. We hang board flat and true, tape every seam and finish smooth so paint goes on clean.

  • New walls and full-room installations
  • Remodel and addition tie-ins
  • Moisture-resistant board for kitchens and baths
  • Fire-rated assemblies where required
  • Level 3 to Level 5 finish options
  • Dust control and daily cleanup

Texture & Finishes

Texture hides small imperfections and gives walls their character. We match existing patterns or apply a fresh finish through the whole room.

  • Knockdown texture
  • Orange peel texture
  • Smooth, level finish
  • Texture matching for patches
  • Whole-room retexture
  • Paint-ready surface prep

Drywall Repair

Doorknob holes, cracked seams, nail pops and water stains. We cut out the damage, rebuild the surface and blend the texture so the repair disappears.

  • Small hole and dent repair
  • Cracked seam and corner repair
  • Water damage cut-out and replacement
  • Nail pop correction
  • Texture blending to match
  • Ready for your painter in days, not weeks
Sample pricing · call for an exact quote

Ceilings & Popcorn Removal

Ceilings take a beating from leaks, settling and outdated textures. We scrape, skim and refinish ceilings to a clean, modern look.

  • Popcorn ceiling removal and refinishing
  • Ceiling water stain repair
  • Sagging drywall replacement
  • Smooth or light texture ceiling finish
  • Recessed can light patching
  • Garage and porch ceilings
Sample pricing · call for an exact quote

Plaster Repair

Older Florida homes with plaster need a patient hand. We repair cracks and failing plaster with compatible materials and a smooth finish.

  • Plaster crack and crazing repair
  • Delaminated plaster re-bonding
  • Lath and plaster patching
  • Skim coating for a smooth surface
  • Historic detail preservation
  • Primed and paint-ready on completion
Sample pricing · call for an exact quote

Insulation Support

Walls opened for drywall work are the best time to improve insulation. We coordinate insulation work with your drywall project for comfort and efficiency.

  • Wall insulation coordination during remodels
  • Soundproofing board options
  • Attic hatch and access panel finishing
  • Vapor barrier detailing
  • Energy-efficient wall assemblies
  • Post-work drywall restoration

Our story

S & S Drywall & Texture Inc was incorporated in 1990 by Steve K. Ruffner, and it has stayed a family operation ever since. Steve serves as president and Pamela G. Ruffner serves as vice president, and both names are on the filings, the trucks and the finished work.

Thirty-plus years in one county

From our shop at 3273 Majestic Oak Dr in St Cloud, we have watched Central Florida grow one subdivision at a time. We have hung drywall in new builds, repaired hurricane-season damage, and refinished more popcorn ceilings than we can count. The work changed, the name on the door never did.

We finish what we start

Hang, tape, texture, cleanup. You are not left chasing a painter or a patcher.

We stand behind the seams

If a seam cracks or a texture misses, we come back and make it right.
