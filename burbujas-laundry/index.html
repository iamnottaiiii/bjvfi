Our story

Burbujas: bubbles, and a neighborhood institution

Burbujas means bubbles in Spanish. For over two decades, this little shop on Maverick Street has been where East Boston gets its laundry done.

Since 2002

A small shop with loyal regulars.

Burbujas Laundry has been part of East Boston since 2002. It is a small, family-style operation: the kind of place where regulars have been bringing their laundry for years and know the team by name.

Reviewers describe quick service, clothes that come back smelling wonderful, and a convenient location close to home. It is not fancy, and that is the point: a dependable neighborhood laundromat that treats people well.

Whether you wash it yourself or drop it off, you will be in and out with fresh, clean laundry and one less thing on your list.
