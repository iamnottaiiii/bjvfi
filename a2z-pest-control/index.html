01General pest control+
Our core service: inspection, targeted treatment, and prevention for the everyday pests found in New York buildings.
  • Cockroaches, ants, spiders, and silverfish
  • Crickets, centipedes, and occasional invaders
  • One-time visits or recurring protection plans
02Rodent control+
Mice and rats are a fact of life in the five boroughs. We find how they get in, remove them, and seal them out.
  • Entry-point inspection and exclusion work
  • Trapping and removal
  • Follow-up monitoring visits
03Bed bug treatment+
Bed bugs need a thorough, room-by-room approach. We inspect mattresses, furniture, and baseboards, then treat with a plan matched to the infestation.
  • Detailed inspection of sleeping and living areas
  • Targeted treatment with follow-up visits
  • Prep guidance so the treatment works the first time
04Residential protection plans+
Scheduled visits through the year that catch problems early, with treatments adjusted by season.
  • Seasonal exterior and interior treatments
  • Monitoring between visits
  • Priority scheduling for plan members
05Commercial pest management+
Discreet, documented service for restaurants, shops, offices, and residential buildings where compliance and reputation matter.
  • Scheduled service routes
  • Service documentation for health inspections
  • Flexible hours to avoid disrupting your business
06Inspection and prevention+
Not sure what you are dealing with? Start with an inspection. We identify the pest, show you the evidence, and lay out options with honest pricing.
  • Full property walkthrough
  • Written findings and recommendations
  • No-obligation quote for any treatment
