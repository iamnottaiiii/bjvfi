Front Page

A Classic Barber Shop on Plaza Drive

Old Standards Barber Shop serves Hamilton, Ohio from 1407 Plaza Dr. Call (513) 868-2888 to book your chair.

Walk into Old Standards Barber Shop and you step into the old standards of the trade: the chair, the clippers, the straight razor, and a barber who takes his time. This Hamilton shop has built its name the slow way, one haircut at a time, at 1407 Plaza Drive.

Public listings rate the shop 4.8 out of 5 across dozens of reviews, with regulars praising fast, sharp cuts. Hours run Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday from 10 in the morning to 6 in the evening, and Saturday from 9 to 3. Wednesday and Sunday the shop rests. Hours per public listings

There is no official website for the shop, so this paper serves as an unofficial guide. Every unverified detail below is marked plainly. When in doubt, call the shop at (513) 868-2888 and ask.

The address puts the shop on Plaza Drive in Hamilton's 45013, easy to reach by car with parking at the plaza. See the Services page for the menu, the Reviews page for what customers say, and Contact to get in touch.
