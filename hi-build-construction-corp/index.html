Our Story

A Brooklyn Contractor Built on Word of Mouth

Hi-Build Construction Corp is a general contractor based at 238 Newkirk Ave in Brooklyn. For 27 years, we have served homeowners and property managers across New York City with exterior and interior construction: roofing, waterproofing, facade restoration, masonry, and full renovations.

We are listed as a home improvement contractor with New York City, an M.W.B.E. masonry contractor in the Blue Book, and carry an active permit history across the five boroughs, from $5,000 repairs to $500,000-plus commercial projects.

  • 27 years serving the five boroughs
  • Licensed NYC home improvement contractor
  • M.W.B.E.-listed masonry contractor
  • Local Law 10/11 facade specialists
  • Residential and commercial experience: apartments, schools, hospitals, retail, and restaurants
