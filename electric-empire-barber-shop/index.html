Our story

A Long Beach Original Since 2016

Electric Empire Barber Shop opened its doors on East 4th Street in 2016, founded by Drew with a simple idea: bring back the traditional barbershop, where the cut matters and the customer matters more.

In 2021, barber Alex took over the shop and kept that promise going. A barber since 2009, Alex built the shop around an authentic old-school experience: straight razor shaves, hot towels, and a chair where everyone is welcome.

Today the shop runs on skill and personality. The team takes pride in personalized cuts, reasonable prices, and a lively room where regulars are remembered by name and first-timers leave as regulars.
