How a job goes

Straightforward,
start to finish

No mystery, no upsell. Here is how a typical project with us runs:

  • 1. Walk the site. We look at the ground, the access, and what you want.
  • 2. Written quote. Scope, materials and price, on paper.
  • 3. We build. Footings first, level courses, clean joints, daily cleanup.
  • 4. Walkthrough. We do not leave until you are happy with every course.
