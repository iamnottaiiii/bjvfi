About

Oxon Hill's
friendly nail spot

Linh Nails is a neighborhood nail salon on Indian Head Highway, where regulars come for the nails and stay for the people.

A salon that feels like family

Clients describe the team as super friendly, funny, and personable. Technicians like Jennifer, and Sunny and his wife, are called out by name in reviews for getting nails exactly right.

The vibe is part of the experience: R&B from the 80s to now playing in the background, massage chairs for pedicures, and a team that can do your mani and pedi at the same time, so you are in and out feeling pampered.

Prices are very affordable, which is why so many clients say they will definitely be back, and why visitors coming home to the DMV make Linh Nails a must-stop.
