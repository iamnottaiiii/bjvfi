Our story

South Side Salon sits on Riva Trace Parkway in Annapolis, serving the surrounding South River community. It is the kind of salon built on relationships: stylists with loyal followings, clients who follow them across locations, and new guests who arrive because a friend would not stop talking about their haircut.

One guest wrote that she has followed her stylist through three locations over the years.

The salon keeps things personal rather than corporate. You book with a stylist, you get a consultation, and you leave with hair that feels like you. That simple formula is why the reviews read like letters from friends.

The team

Guests name favorite stylists in their reviews, with special praise for Mandi's loyal following and Susie's first visit transformations. Every stylist shares the same approach: listen first, cut second.
