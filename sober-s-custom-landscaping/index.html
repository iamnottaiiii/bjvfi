About

Horticulture training, hands-on ownership.

Sober's Custom Landscaping is a full-service landscape and hardscape contractor serving Fairview Township and the greater Harrisburg area. The company is led by owner Kenneth Sober, whose background includes a Bachelor of Science degree in ornamental horticulture from Delaware Valley College, as listed on the company's BuildZoom contractor profile.

That training shows in the details: plants selected for the site, not just the season; beds built with soil health in mind; stonework laid to stay put. It is the difference between a yard that looks good in June and one that looks good for years.
