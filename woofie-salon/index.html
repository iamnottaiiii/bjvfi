About Woofie Salon

A neighborhood salon for Henriettas dogs

Woofie Salon is a locally rooted pet grooming salon on E Henrietta Rd, built around one simple idea: grooming should feel good for the dog, not just look good to us.

Tucked into the E Henrietta Rd corridor, Woofie Salon serves the dogs of Henrietta and greater Rochester with patient, one on one grooming. Every appointment is about the individual dog in the chair: their coat, their comfort level, their quirks.

Puppies get gentle first grooms that build confidence. Seniors get unhurried handling and extra breaks. Anxious dogs get calm voices and steady hands. And every dog leaves clean, trimmed and happy, with tidy nails and fresh ears.

Booking is simple: one phone call, six days a week. No apps, no accounts, no hassle. Just call (585) 489-6640 and tell us about your dog.
