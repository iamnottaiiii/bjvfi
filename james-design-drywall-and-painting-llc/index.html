Our story

A finish carpenter’s eye for drywall

James Design Drywall and Painting LLC grew out of a simple frustration: too many walls that looked fine at noon and terrible at dusk. We built the company around the finishing steps other crews rush, the taping, sanding and priming that decide how a room really looks.

Based in Plainfield, NJ, we serve homeowners who want it done right the first time. We are fully licensed and insured, we keep the site clean, and we treat the estimate like a promise.
