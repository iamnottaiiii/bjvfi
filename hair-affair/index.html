Our story

Small shop, long roots.

Hair Affair opened on Upton's Main Street in 1996, and it has been a neighborhood fixture ever since. Owner Gloria Profetto built the salon on a simple idea: listen to people, cut with care, and charge a fair price.

Three decades later, that idea still fills the chairs. Clients come back year after year, and many bring their kids and grandkids too. The shop stays small on purpose, so every appointment feels personal and unhurried.

Whether it is your first visit or your five hundredth, you will be greeted by name and leave with hair you love.
