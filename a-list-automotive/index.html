Our story

La Plata’s shop since 2011.

A-List Automotive opened its doors in La Plata in 2011, and it has been earning drivers one honest repair at a time ever since.

It is a locally owned and operated shop on Crain Highway, the kind of place where the owner knows your name and the crew remembers your car. The philosophy is simple: do quality work, charge a fair price, and treat every customer like more than just a customer.

Over the years that approach has built a loyal base across Charles County. Families bring every vehicle they own. Travelers passing through on Route 301 stop in with brake trouble and leave as reviewers. The common thread in all of it is trust: clear explanations, straight answers, and work done right.

Whether it is a quick oil change or a repair that keeps you off the road, the team at A-List aims to make the experience as painless as the problem allows.
