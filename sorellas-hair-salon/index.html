Who we are

About

Sorellas hair salon sits at 3923 Amboy Road in Staten Island, New York. The name means sisters, and the salon is run by sisters who treat clients like family.

Services include haircuts and styling, highlights, hair coloring, conditioning treatments, and makeup services for special occasions.

Guests praise the personal attention, the honest advice, and results that photograph beautifully. Book ahead, the chairs fill up.
