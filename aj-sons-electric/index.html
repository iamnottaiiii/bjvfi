About us

A small shop with high standards

A.J. & Sons Electric is a small family owned and operated business that still takes pride in its work and in taking time with every customer to ensure a successful, quality job.

A.J. & Sons was previously Jeremy Ruhe Electric, and has spent years offering fast, reliable, and competitively priced electrical service to local homes and businesses.
