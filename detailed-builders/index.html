Our story

A builder named for
the details

Detailed Builders is a Winnetka based construction company serving homeowners across the San Fernando Valley.

Why the name

The difference between good and great is in the details

Anyone can frame a wall. The details, tight tile lines, flush trim, clean paint edges, doors that close right, are what separate a job you tolerate from a home you love.

That is the standard Detailed Builders holds on every project, from a single bathroom to a full addition. We treat the small stuff as the whole job, because to the homeowner living with it, it is.

Based on Ingomar St in Winnetka, we work across the Valley with one crew and one point of contact, so nothing gets lost between the estimate and the final walkthrough.
