About

A Pickerington remodeling team since 1988

Divine Co General Contractors is a full-service home improvement company based at 11117 Milnor Rd, Pickerington, OH 43147. For 38 years the team has focused on one goal: satisfying clients while keeping disruption to daily routines as small as possible.

The company is a member of NARI and NKBA and the Central Ohio BBB, and is known for handicap and elderly-accessible design and build renovations as well as kitchens, baths, additions, and basements.
