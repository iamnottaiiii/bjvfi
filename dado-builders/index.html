Why Dado Builders

One builder, start to finish

Most contractors hand your project down a chain of subs and hope the details survive. Dado Builders works the other way. The owner started as a finish carpenter, ran crews as a lead man for a general contractor, then earned his own general B license and has been building on his own for over 14 years.

“I am responsible and passionate builder and I have over 20 years of hands on general construction experience as a owner operator.”Owner description, MapQuest business listing

That background shows up where it matters: in the finish. Reviewers mention attention to detail, responsiveness, and a builder who sees every project through instead of rushing the final ten percent.

Residential or commercial, the approach is the same: walk the job, plan it properly, and stay on it until it is right. The company takes on projects from A to Z, which means one point of responsibility instead of a phone tree of subcontractors.

Our story

From finish carpenter to general contractor

The story of Dado Builders is the classic contractor's path, told in the owner's own words on the company's public listing: start as a finish carpenter, work as a lead man for a general contractor, earn your own general B license, and build on your own.

“I started as a finish carpenter, then I worked for a general contractor as a lead man, I got my own general B license and working for my self over 14 years on residential or commercial projects from A to Z.”Owner description, MapQuest business listing

Fourteen plus years self employed, and more than twenty years of hands on construction experience in total, all as an owner operator. That is the whole story, and it is told in the work: reviewers keep coming back to the same words, responsive, detailed, professional.

Why finish carpentry matters. Finish work is where a project is judged. A framer can be a sixteenth off and nobody notices; a trim carpenter cannot. Starting there builds an eye for the final ten percent that many contractors rush through.
