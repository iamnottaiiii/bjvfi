II.

Redevelopment and mixed use

JRA takes on complex existing places and repositions them. The firm was reported in escrow to acquire and redevelop Lido Marina Village, a four acre waterfront commercial district in Newport Beach, with plans to remake the aging retail and marine related buildings into a working harbor destination.

A developer that sees what a tired waterfront could become, and assembles the capital to build it.
  • Commercial district repositioning
  • Mixed use planning and design
  • Historic and adaptive reuse sensibility

About the firm

A Pasadena developer, building since 1991.

James Ratkovich and Associates was founded in Pasadena, California in 1991, with Jim Ratkovich serving as president and chief executive officer from the firm's inception.

Before forming JRA, Ratkovich worked with the Ratkovich Company, the Los Angeles development firm known for landmark rehabilitations across the city. That grounding in complex urban projects shaped JRA's approach: patient capital, careful entitlement work, and design that respects its setting.

Today the firm develops and invests in luxury residential and mixed use projects across California, Hawaii and Mexico, and its principal also serves as a managing director of the real estate equity and development platform at Salem Partners.

Three decades of practice across the West Coast and Hawaii.
