Our story

A studio built around
one room

LuxeBasin started with a simple observation: the bathroom is the hardest-working room in the house, and it deserves a specialist, not a generalist squeezing it between bigger jobs.

The idea

Focus is a feature

Most remodelers treat the bathroom as a side project. We flipped that: bathrooms are the entire business. Every crew member, every supplier relationship, every process we have exists to make one room exceptional.

That focus buys you things a generalist cannot promise: waterproofing details we have done a thousand times, tile setters who only set tile, and timelines measured in days because there is no other trade waiting on us.

We are based on Bell St in Pasadena and serve homeowners across the San Gabriel Valley who want a bathroom that feels less like a utility and more like a retreat.

What we believe

Three ideas guide every project we take on.

  • Design first: a beautiful bathroom starts on paper, not at the tile store.
  • Honest pricing: detailed written estimates, no mystery line items.
  • Respect for your home: daily cleanup, clear communication, protected floors.

Meet us at your bathroom.

The consultation is free and takes about an hour. We measure, you talk, and we leave you with an honest read on the possibilities.
