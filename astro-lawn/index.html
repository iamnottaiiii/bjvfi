Our story

Rooted in Crescent Springs

Astro-Lawn is a neighborhood lawn care and landscaping company based right on High Street in Crescent Springs, Kentucky. We are the crew your neighbors call when the yard needs more than a quick mow.

Our approach is simple: diagnose first, then treat. Every new customer gets a free lawn diagnosis, an honest read on what the yard needs, and a clear plan with pricing agreed before any work begins.

From weekly mowing to seasonal feeding, planting, mulch, and full yard cleanups, we handle the whole property so you can just enjoy it. Reviewers mention friendly service, free diagnoses, and lawns that visibly improve within days.

Astro-Lawn keeps a listed profile with the Better Business Bureau and serves homeowners across Crescent Springs and nearby Northern Kentucky communities.
