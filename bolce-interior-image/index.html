About the Studio

Design that begins with a feeling

Bolce Interior Image is an interior design studio in Cincinnati’s Oakley neighborhood. Our philosophy is simple and it is our tagline:

“Interior design that defines intangible ideas and moods into definable spaces.”

We believe a room should hold a feeling before it holds furniture. Some clients arrive with a clear vision; others arrive with only a sense that something could be better. Both are a perfect place to start.

The studio works by appointment from 3066 Madison Road, Monday through Friday, 9 to 5. Reach us anytime at (513) 651-4510.
