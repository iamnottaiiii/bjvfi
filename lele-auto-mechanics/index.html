Our story

Careful auto repair in the City of Orange

Shop art placeholder

Lele Auto Mechanics is the kind of shop where the person who diagnoses your car is the person who fixes it. No service-writer telephone game, no mystery line items, just a mechanic who looks you in the eye and tells you what your car needs.

The shop handles the full spread of general repair: brakes, oil service, suspension, electrical, and the check-engine lights that send other drivers into a panic. Every job starts with a real diagnosis, because guessing with your money is not a business model.

Drivers in the City of Orange keep the bays busy by word of mouth. If your car needs attention, call (973) 266-1082 or stop by 274 North Day Street and talk to the shop directly.
