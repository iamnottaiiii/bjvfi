04 · About

Nearly 20 years
of making the unusual

Imagivations is an entertainment construction company based at 11314 Sheldon St in Sun Valley, California.

We have been in this business for almost 20 years, building custom sets, props, design elements, gadgets, models and miniatures, and permanent installations for corporate clients, ad agencies, and retail and commercial customers.

Our client list includes names like Disney, Dior, Nissan, NASA and JPL. Big names trust us with unusual builds because we deliver: on time, on budget, and built to perform.

What sets us apart is simple. We pride ourselves on being able to make anything, particularly the unusual that other propmakers say cannot be done. Bring us the hard one.
