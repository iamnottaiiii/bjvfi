Our Story

A Neighborhood Tailor Shop With a Loyal Following

Husky Cleaners & Alteration sits on Washington Ave in North Haven, where it has become the shop locals recommend by name. Reviewers know the owners, John and Theresa, and praise their one of a kind attention to detail.

The reputation was built one fitting at a time: a formal dress hemmed for a big night, a gown transformed in under a month, leather vest patches sewn within a couple of hours. Quick turnarounds, honest work, and staff who treat urgent jobs like their own emergency.
