Services

Insulation services, explained

Common insulation project types, shown as samples only. Call (626) 654-1818 to confirm what Insulation Experts offers and to price your job.

Blown-in or batt insulation for attic floors and slopes, often the single most effective upgrade for cutting heat transfer through the roof. Coverage depth and air sealing around penetrations matter most here.Sample scope
Insulating wall cavities steadies room-to-room temperatures and reduces noise between rooms. Retrofit options exist for homes with uninsulated walls.Sample scope
Insulating under floors and in crawl spaces keeps ground-level rooms warmer in winter and helps block moisture-related comfort problems.Sample scope
Old, compressed, or contaminated insulation underperforms. Removal followed by fresh material restores the thermal barrier the home was designed to have.Sample scope
Sealing gaps around wiring, plumbing, ducts, and fixtures stops conditioned air from escaping. Air sealing paired with new insulation delivers the best results.Sample scope
