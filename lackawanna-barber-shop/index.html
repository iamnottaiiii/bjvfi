Our Story

A neighborhood institution

Lackawanna Barber Shop has earned its reputation the old fashioned way: one excellent cut at a time. With a 4.8 rating across 157 Google reviews, it stands as one of the best reviewed barbershops in the Morristown and Cedar Knolls area.

The shop pairs classic technique with genuine hospitality. Johnny, the barber reviewers rave about, has cut for celebrities and keeps clients laughing through the whole appointment, with an uncanny memory for exactly how each regular likes their hair. John brings patience and kindness to every chair, and keeps the shop clean and welcoming.

It is also one of the few shops open late: most days the chairs run until 8:15 PM, and the doors open seven days a week, including Sunday mornings.
