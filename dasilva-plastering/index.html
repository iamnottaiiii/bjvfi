Our services

Plaster services in West Boylston, MA

Every service below is core plaster work. Call (508) 723-4309 for a free estimate on any of them.

Interior Plastering

New plaster walls and ceilings for construction, additions and full remodels. Applied in proper coats and finished smooth.

Plaster Repair and Patching

Cracks from settling, holes from renovations, and plaster damaged by leaks. We cut out the bad, patch it, and blend it in.

Skim Coating

One smooth coat over old, textured or damaged walls. The fastest way to make a room look brand new and paint-ready.

Exterior Stucco

Traditional stucco application and patching for home exteriors, including crack repair and re-coating worn areas.

Ceiling Work

Sagging, stained or cracked ceilings repaired and re-plastered, including textured ceiling smoothing.
