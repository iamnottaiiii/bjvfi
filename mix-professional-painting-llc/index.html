Prep first. Paint second.
That’s the whole secret.

Anyone can roll paint on a wall. A professional finish comes from what happens before the can opens: careful masking, patching and sanding, quality primer, and clean, straight lines at every edge.

  • Full prep: patching, sanding, caulking, and masking
  • Premium paints and primers suited to each surface
  • Tidy crews that protect floors and furniture
  • Walkthrough at the end so every detail is right
