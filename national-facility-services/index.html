Who we are

Built for properties that cannot afford downtime

NATIONAL FACILITY SERVICES, INC. brings landscaping, snow removal, janitorial service, and building maintenance together under one commercial contractor. The model is simple: property managers and owners deal with one accountable partner instead of four separate vendors.
