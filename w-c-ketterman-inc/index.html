Mechanical Contracting, Set in Solid Ink

By the Gazette Staff · Cincinnati, Ohio
W C Ketterman Inc brings commercial mechanical contracting to the Queen City: the systems that heat, cool, ventilate, and move buildings that work.

In every working building in Cincinnati there is a story told in ductwork, piping, and airflow. W C Ketterman Inc is a mechanical contractor headquartered at 11313 Dallas Blvd, serving commercial and industrial clients across the region.

About the Firm

W C Ketterman Inc is a mechanical contractor based in Cincinnati, Ohio, operating from 11313 Dallas Blvd. The firm works in the commercial and industrial mechanical trades, the field responsible for the heating, cooling, ventilation, piping, and sheet metal systems inside working buildings.
