About Bill's

A Loveland Original

Bill's Kustom Kut Barber Shop is a traditional walk-in barbershop located at 641 Loveland Madeira Rd in Loveland, Ohio, in the Shoppers Haven Shopping Center. Public business listings show the shop was established in 1980 and has served the Loveland community for decades.

Reviewers on public sites describe a relaxed, friendly atmosphere, experienced barbers, and a shop that has become a hometown staple across generations of families.
