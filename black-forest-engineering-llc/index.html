Services

Civil engineering services

Select a service to see what it covers. These are standard civil engineering capabilities, call to discuss your specific project.

Site & Land Development

Turning raw land into buildable lots. Feasibility review, conceptual layout, grading and access planning for subdivisions and commercial sites.

  • Conceptual site layouts and yield studies
  • Grading, access and circulation planning
  • Coordination with surveyors and geotechnical teams

Civil Design

Detailed engineering for the horizontal infrastructure your project depends on: roads, utilities, drainage and earthwork, designed to local and PennDOT standards.

  • Roadway and parking lot design
  • Water, sewer and utility layout
  • Construction documents ready for bidding

Permitting Support

Land development approvals take patience and paperwork. We prepare submittals, respond to review comments and shepherd applications through township and agency review.

  • Land development plan submittals
  • Review-letter responses and resubmittals
  • NPDES and conservation district coordination

Stormwater & Infrastructure

Water is the quiet risk on every site. We design stormwater systems that meet Pennsylvania requirements and protect your investment through every storm.

  • Detention and infiltration basin design
  • Erosion and sediment control plans
  • Post-construction stormwater management

Municipal Consulting

Independent engineering support for townships and boroughs: plan reviews, ordinance guidance and technical backup at public meetings.

  • Subdivision and land development plan review
  • Ordinance and standards guidance
  • Meeting attendance and technical support
