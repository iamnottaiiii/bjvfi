Why erosion control matters

Uncontrolled runoff washes away topsoil, floods low spots, and carries sediment into waterways. The fix is part engineering, part gardening: shape the land, slow the water, and plant roots that hold it all together.

  • Site grading that sheds water correctly
  • Retaining walls built to last
  • Native plantings that stabilize soil
  • Drainage planned before the rain comes
