About

Pauls Service

Pauls Service has kept Carlstadt drivers rolling with honest diagnostics, fair prices and work backed by people who answer the phone. Rated about 4.5 stars across Google reviews, it is the kind of shop neighbors recommend to neighbors.

Pauls Service is an independent auto repair shop on Washington Avenue in Carlstadt. The team focuses on maintenance and repair for daily drivers of every make.

Bring the car in, get a straight answer, and approve the work before a wrench turns. Call for hours and same-week appointments.
