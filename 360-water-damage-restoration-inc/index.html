What we do

Services

Complete water damage restoration, from emergency extraction to final repairs. Tap a service to learn what is included, then call for help.

Emergency Water Extraction

Powerful pumps and vacuums remove standing water from floors, carpets, and furnishings fast. Getting water out quickly is the single most important step in limiting damage.

We handle burst pipes, appliance leaks, and flooding, then move straight into drying so moisture never gets a chance to settle in.

Need extraction now? Call (323) 784-9259

Structural Drying + Dehumidification

Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers pull residual moisture from walls, subfloors, and hard to reach spaces. Moisture meters verify the structure is truly dry, not just dry to the touch.

Thorough drying is what prevents warping, swelling, and mold growth weeks after the water is gone.

Questions about drying? Call (323) 784-9259

Mold Inspection + Remediation

Where water lingers, mold follows. We inspect for mold growth, contain affected areas, remove contaminated materials safely, and treat surfaces to prevent regrowth.

Protect your indoor air quality and your health with a proper remediation plan, not a surface wipe down.

Concerned about mold? Call (323) 784-9259

Sewage + Contaminated Water Cleanup

Category 3 water carries serious health risks. We extract contaminated water, remove affected materials, and sanitize thoroughly so your space is safe to occupy again.

This is not a DIY job. Protective equipment and proper disposal procedures are required by the nature of the work.

Sewage backup? Call (323) 784-9259

Repairs + Full Restoration

Once everything is dry and clean, we rebuild: drywall replacement, flooring, painting, and finish work that returns your property to its pre loss condition.

One team handles cleanup and reconstruction, so nothing gets lost between contractors.

Need repairs after a loss? Call (323) 784-9259

Insurance Claim Assistance

Good records from day one make claims move faster and disputes less likely.

How it works

The Restoration Process

Every job follows the same proven sequence, so nothing is skipped and your property is truly restored, not just dried on the surface.

1

Inspection + Damage Assessment

We find the water source, map every affected area, and check for hazards like electrical issues or structural weakness before work begins.

2

Water Extraction

Powerful pumps and vacuums remove standing water from floors, carpets, and furnishings to stop water from soaking deeper.

3

Drying + Dehumidification

Air movers and dehumidifiers eliminate residual moisture from walls, subfloors, and hidden spaces. Moisture readings confirm the job is done.

4

Cleaning + Sanitizing

Thorough sanitization removes contaminants and odors, especially important after sewage or floodwater intrusion.

5

Restoration + Repairs

Drywall, flooring, paint, and finishes are rebuilt to return your property to its pre loss condition.
