Our Story

A small crew with one big idea.

Paradise Home is a landscape design company based in Burbank, California. We believe a beautiful yard should not require a luxury budget, so we design every project around the price our customers want to spend.

Custom design should be the starting point, not the upsell.

From first sketch to final walkthrough, you work with people who care about the details: the right plant in the right spot, clean edges, solid walls, and water features that actually sound like water. That is how a yard becomes a paradise.
