About us

Avenue M, with
a little star power

Hollywood Nail is a Midwood nail salon with a simple philosophy: treat every guest like it is premiere night.

★

The Hollywood standard

At 1722 Avenue M you will find a full-service nail salon that takes the craft seriously. Guests single out the quality of the products and the care in the application, the kind of detail that keeps people coming back twice in a row and leaving happy both times.

The menu covers the modern classics: dip powder, acrylic and gel extensions, gel and classic manicures, spa and men's pedicures, and nail art for when plain polish will not do.

Come as you are

Walk-ins welcome, street parking nearby, and hours that work around real life: 10 AM to 7:30 PM every single day.
