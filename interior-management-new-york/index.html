How a typical project runs.

1. Consultation. We walk the space, review drawings if you have them, and discuss budget and timing.

2. Proposal and schedule. You receive a clear scope of work with line items, milestones, and a realistic timeline.

3. Construction. Our site team manages trades, deliveries, and inspections while keeping you updated.

4. Walkthrough and closeout. Punch-list completion, final cleaning, and documentation before handover.

About

A Manhattan contractor built around precision.

Interior Management is a general contracting and construction management firm based at 275 Madison Avenue in New York City.

The firm concentrates on interior work, the kind of project where details matter most: apartment and townhome renovations, lobby upgrades, and commercial interior buildouts across Manhattan and the surrounding boroughs.

As a general contractor, Interior Management takes full responsibility for the job: permits, trades, materials, schedule, and the finished result.

Our Approach

Meticulous execution

Interior work lives or dies on the details. We plan finishes, transitions, and tolerances before the first day on site.

Clear communication

You always know where your project stands: schedule updates, budget tracking, and one point of contact.

Respect for the building

We coordinate with building staff, follow board alteration agreements, and keep common areas clean and quiet.

Finished on time

Realistic scheduling up front, disciplined trade coordination during the job, and a clean punch-list closeout.
