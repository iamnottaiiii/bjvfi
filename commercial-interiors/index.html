commercial jobs run

Commercial Interiors is a North Hollywood drywall contractor focused on one thing: the interior package, done right and on schedule.

Who we are

A subcontractor GCs can set and forget

Commercial schedules are unforgiving. Framing runs late, drywall runs later, and suddenly the painter, the electrician, and the flooring crew are all standing around. We exist so that does not happen on your job. From our base at 5617 Whitnall Hwy in North Hollywood, we take on the interior scope, metal stud framing, drywall, finishing, and acoustical ceilings, and we run it like the schedule depends on it. Because it does.

We bid from plans, show up with the right crew, and hand off clean work to the next trade. Simple, professional, repeatable.
