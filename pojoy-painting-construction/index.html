About the crew

A Lynn crew with a simple reputation

Pojoy Painting & Construction is based at 64 Essex St in Lynn, Massachusetts, and works across the North Shore. The crew is led by Francisco Pojoy, and reviewers consistently describe the same team: hard working, dedicated, neat, and precise.

Customers say they felt completely comfortable with the crew in their homes, that changes were always discussed and agreed before being done, and that the team takes visible pride in the finished work. Painting, carpentry, drywall, tile, and porch construction all come from the same crew, so one call covers the whole project.

The business is fully insured and holds a 5.0 star average across 42 homeowner reviews.
