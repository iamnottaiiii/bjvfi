Our shop

Metal work on Woodley Ave

A working sheet metal shop in the heart of Van Nuys, serving contractors and neighbors across the Valley.

A shop built around the brake

Woodley Custom Metal is a sheet metal contracting shop at 6855 Woodley Ave in Van Nuys, California. Our work centers on the sheet metal brake, the shears, and the skilled hands that turn flat stock into parts that fit.

Most of our work comes from contractors and property managers who need metal that fits the first time: ductwork for a tenant improvement, flashing for a re roof, a custom pan for an odd sized opening. We measure carefully, fabricate in house, and stand behind the fit.

We are easy to reach and easy to work with. Bring us a drawing, a sample, or just a photo with a tape measure in the frame, and we will figure out the rest together.
