Restoration Services

Select a service to see what is included. Every job starts with an assessment and a clear explanation of the work ahead.

Water Extraction

The first step after any flood, leak, or burst pipe. Standing water is removed quickly to stop damage from spreading into walls, subfloors, and belongings.

  • Standing water removal from floors and rooms
  • Water source identification and shutoff guidance
  • Removal of saturated unsalvageable materials
  • Documentation of affected areas

Structural Drying

Surfaces can look dry while framing and subfloors stay soaked. Professional drying equipment pulls moisture out of the structure, and readings confirm it is truly dry before repairs begin.

  • Commercial air movers and dehumidifiers
  • Moisture mapping and daily readings
  • Wall cavity and subfloor drying
  • Dry verification before rebuild
Placeholder

Mold Remediation

Where water sat for more than a day or two, mold often follows. Affected areas are contained, contaminated materials are removed, and surfaces are treated to stop regrowth.

  • Containment of affected areas
  • Removal of mold-damaged materials
  • Antimicrobial treatment of surfaces
  • Moisture source correction guidance

Repairs and Rebuild

Once everything is verified dry, damaged drywall, flooring, baseboards, and paint are repaired or replaced. The goal is simple: the room looks like the water was never there.

  • Drywall removal, replacement, and finishing
  • Flooring repair and replacement
  • Baseboard, trim, and paint
  • Final walkthrough and cleanup
