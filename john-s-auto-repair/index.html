About John's Auto Repair

John's Auto Repair has been a Franklin Street fixture since 1984. It is a family owned shop where the same people who answer the phone are the people who work on your car.

Neighbors keep coming back because the estimates are honest and the work is done right the first time. From oil changes to engine overhauls, the team treats every car like their own.
