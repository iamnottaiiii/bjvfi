Why rodent-focused

General pest companies spray.
We solve the rodent problem.

Rats and mice are different from bugs. They chew, they nest, they come back through the same holes. That is why we specialize.

Built for Pittsburgh's older homes

Pittsburgh's housing stock, old foundations, stone basements, and attached rowhouses, is exactly where rodents thrive. We know where to look.

  • Full property inspection, ground to roofline, attic to basement
  • Entry points sealed with steel, copper mesh and concrete, not foam
  • Sanitizing of droppings and nesting areas after removal
  • Damage repair: chewed wires, insulation and gnawed wood

Residential

Homes, apartments and rentals. Quiet, clean, pet-conscious methods that respect your space.

Commercial

Restaurants, warehouses and offices. Discreet service with documentation for health inspections.

Why us

Rodents are all we do.
That is the point.

Precision Rodent Management focuses on one pest family, so every method, material and visit is built around how mice and rats actually behave.

No poison-first shortcuts

Poisoned rodents die inside walls, ducts and crawlspaces, where they are hard to find and worse than alive. We trap, remove and seal, so the problem ends instead of moving into your walls.

  • Trapping placed and monitored by a professional
  • Pet- and family-conscious methods
  • Exclusion first, so new rodents cannot move in

Licensed pros

Work performed to Pennsylvania pest control standards, with documentation commercial clients can hand to inspectors.

Follow-through

We come back until the activity stops. A single visit rarely ends a rodent problem, so we don't sell it that way.
