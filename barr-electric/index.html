Our story

One electrician, four decades, zero shortcuts.

Barr Electric was established in 1984 and has been run by owner Kent Barr from 4526 Cypress Dr in La Cañada Flintridge ever since. It is a true one-person shop: the person who answers the phone is the person who shows up at your door.

That matters in a foothill town full of older homes. Many La Cañada Flintridge houses were built decades before today's electrical demands, and Barr Electric has spent forty years learning exactly how they are wired. From cloth-insulated conductors to underpowered panels, there is not much this shop has not seen and fixed properly.

The approach is simple: diagnose honestly, quote fairly, and do work clean enough to be proud of behind the walls where nobody looks. Customers describe Barr Electric as honest with great work, which is exactly the reputation a neighborhood electrician should earn one job at a time.

If you want a big crew, Barr Electric is not it. If you want the owner himself handling your panel, your lighting, or your remodel wiring, call (818) 952-1424.
