Anatomy of a Resurrection

The typical path a vehicle takes through a full body and restoration job. Steps vary by vehicle, call to confirm the process for yours.

STEP I

Assessment & Estimate

The car is inspected panel by panel. You get a straight estimate and an honest read on what is worth fixing.

STEP II

Teardown & Metalwork

Damaged panels come off, metal gets straightened or replaced, and the body returns to true.

STEP III

Paint & Refinish

Primed, blocked, painted, and blended, until the repair disappears into the original finish.

STEP IV

Reassembly & Detail

Trim, glass, and hardware go back on. The car is detailed and road checked before you pick it up.
