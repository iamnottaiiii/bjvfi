Water heater repair

Most problems are fixable the same visit. Tom stocks common parts and tells you upfront if a repair is worth it or if your money is better spent on a new unit.

  • No hot water or running out too fast
  • Leaks from tank, fittings, or relief valve
  • Pilot light and ignition failures
  • Rumbling, popping, or banging noises
  • Rusty, discolored, or smelly hot water
  • Thermostat, element, and anode rod service
Schedule a repair

Tank replacement

When a tank is past its prime, a new high-efficiency unit pays for itself in reliability and lower energy use. Typical replacement in a single visit.

  • Gas and electric tank water heaters
  • Correct sizing for your household
  • Seismic strapping and code-compliant venting
  • Expansion tank where required
  • Old unit hauled away
  • Startup, testing, and walkthrough
Get a replacement quote

Tankless installation

Endless hot water and a smaller footprint. Tom evaluates your gas line, venting, and demand to make sure tankless is the right fit before recommending it.

  • Whole-home tankless sizing
  • Gas line and venting upgrades
  • Descaling service and maintenance plans
  • Recirculation options for instant hot water
Ask about tankless

Maintenance and tune-ups
