What the shop is known for

Hopple Used Tires is a no-frills used tire shop on Hopple Street in Cincinnati. Reviewers describe quick, efficient service from polite staff who know what they are doing, with tires often in good shape at prices hard to beat. It is one of the few used tire shops in the area open on Sundays, which customers in a bind appreciate. On the flip side, some reviewers report inconsistent tire quality or sizing, so it pays to verify your size and inspect before you buy.
