Subsurface Intelligence, Start to Finish

One crew can locate it, scan it, map it, pothole it and hand you the CAD file. Four reasons project owners across California call us before the first bucket hits dirt.

01 / LOCATE

Private Utility Locating

Electromagnetic induction and magnetometer locating trace buried electric, gas, water, telecom and unknown lines that public 811 marking does not cover. Every find is marked on the surface and documented.

Truck mounted CCTV video inspection of main sewers and storm drains, plus down hole monitoring well inspections, with recorded footage.

Our story

The Locators Behind the Marks

Pacific Coast Locators is a privately owned subsurface utility locating company founded in 2004 and headquartered in La Crescenta, California. What started as a small locating crew has grown into a statewide team serving contractors, municipalities, engineering firms, environmental consultants and the oil and gas industry.

Their mission is simple and stated plainly: protecting the client's facility by locating every line accurately and on time. Every technician carries Loss Prevention System training certification, API Worksafe certification and 40 hours of HAZWOPER training under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120, so they walk onto refineries, rail corridors and public works jobsites with the paperwork already done.

As a certified Small Business Enterprise with Metro small business certification, they partner on public infrastructure across California, backing up every mark with GIS and AutoCAD deliverables that engineers can build from.
