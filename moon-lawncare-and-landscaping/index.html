What we do

Landscaping services, start to finish.

From a single bed refresh to a full backyard build, one crew handles design, installation and the details. Every project is quoted up front with no surprises.

01

Landscape design

Planning · Plant palettes · Layouts

A plan built around your home, your light and how you use the yard. We pick plants that thrive in Northern Kentucky so the design looks better every year, not worse.

02

Landscape and plant installations

Beds · Trees · Shrubs · Mulch

New beds, fresh mulch, trees and shrubs planted right the first time. Clean edges, healthy soil and a finished look the day we leave.

03

Patios

Pavers · Stone · Outdoor living

Paver and stone patios sized for real life: grilling, chairs, and room to move. A solid base and tight joints keep it level for years.

04

Retaining walls

Block · Stone · Grade work

Walls that hold back the hill and look like they belong. Proper drainage behind every wall so it stays straight through freeze and thaw.

05

Waterfalls and water features

Ponds · Streams · Fountains

The sound of moving water changes a whole backyard. Natural-looking rock work, clean plumbing and low-maintenance setups.

06

Decks

Wood · Composite · Railings

Decks built for cookouts and quiet evenings alike, with sturdy framing and railings that meet code and look sharp.

07

Lawn care and mowing

Weekly · Seasonal · Cleanup

Routine mowing, edging and seasonal cleanups that keep the whole property crisp between projects.

Prices vary by project size and materials. Call for a free on-site estimate.

Our story

Ten years of moonlit standards in Union, KY.

Moon Lawncare and Landscaping, LLC is a Union, Kentucky company with 10 years in the trade, per Porch. We are dedicated to offering very high quality work with 100% customer satisfaction, and we pride ourselves in designing and creating your ideal outdoor living space.

From landscape and plant installations to patios, retaining walls, waterfalls, water features and decks, every job gets the same care: clear communication, a fair price and a yard we would be proud to put our name on.
