About us

A cut above, by name and by work

A Worcester general contractor built on straightforward dealing and solid carpentry, from roofs to windows, siding, decks, and additions.

Our story

Built the old way: measure twice, cut once

A Cut Above Carpentry started with a simple idea: do the work you say you will do, do it well, and treat people fairly. That is still the whole business plan.

We are a Massachusetts-registered home improvement contractor, and we carry an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau. But the credential we care about most is the one no plaque gives you: the customer who calls us back for the next project, and tells a neighbor to do the same.

How we work

Straightforward, start to finish

  • Free, written estimates. We walk the project with you and put the number on paper before work begins.
  • One crew, whole project. Roofing, windows, siding, decks, additions, coordinated under one roof.
  • Clean job sites. We protect your property and leave the site broom-clean every day.
  • Local accountability. Based in Worcester. When you call, you reach the people who did the work.
