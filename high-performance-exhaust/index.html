About High Performance Exhaust

High Performance Exhaust focuses on what many general shops rush through: exhaust systems and brakes. From muffler replacement and catalytic converters to full brake service, the work is done by people who specialize in these systems every day.

We are a brake shop serving Newark and the surrounding Essex County communities. Whether it is routine maintenance or an unexpected breakdown, our goal is the same: get you back on the road safely, with work you can trust and a bill you understand.
