Our story

Raised in the business. Trusted on the North Shore.

Farm Creek Landscaping was established in Essex, Massachusetts in 1987. Owner James Ramsey grew up in the landscaping trade, learning every part of the work from the ground up, from plantings and grading to fine stone construction.

That background is why clients describe him as a gem in his field: well versed in all aspects of landscaping, inventive when a project gets tricky, and honest about what a job should cost. He approaches hardscaping with an artist's eye and a builder's precision, so patios, walkways, and walls come out both structurally sound and visually striking.

Farm Creek is a small, owner operated company. When you call, you talk to James. When the crew shows up, James is on site. And when the work is done, it is built to be enjoyed for decades.
