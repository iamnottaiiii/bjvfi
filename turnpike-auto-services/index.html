Our story

The shop on the Turnpike

Turnpike Auto Services sits at 6304 Little River Turnpike, and for years local drivers have known the shop by its people. Customers mention the crew by name in review after review: Ben, Matt and Drew, described as masters of their craft and three good, honest men.

The philosophy has never changed. Explain the problem in plain language. Quote a fair price. Do the work right. And never, ever sell a repair a car does not need. One longtime customer wrote that the mechanics talked them out of repairs that would have made the shop more money. That is the whole business model, and it is why drivers keep coming back for a decade or more.

Whether you drive a daily commuter, a family SUV or a European import, you will get the same straight talk and careful work.

How we work

  • You describe the problem, we listen
  • We diagnose and show you what we found
  • You approve a clear, itemized quote
  • We repair, test and road check the car
  • We follow up to make sure it is still perfect
