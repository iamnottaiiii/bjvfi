What we do

One crew, the whole job

Good construction is mostly communication. One clear point of contact, one schedule everyone can see, and one crew that shows up when it says it will. That is how Construction Services Unlimited runs a job, from the first walkthrough to the final punch list.

We handle the full range of construction work: new builds, additions, whole-home and office renovations, remodeling of kitchens and baths, and the repairs that keep a property in good shape. If it involves framing, drywall, roofing, flooring, or finishing, it is in our wheelhouse.

We measure twice, cut once, and clean up like we were never there.

Who we are

Builders, not salesmen

We got into this trade to build things, not to sell things. That shows in how we bid, how we schedule, and how we treat your property while we are on it. Boots off at the door, drop cloths down, radios at a reasonable volume.

As a local company based right here in New Cumberland, our reputation travels the same streets we work on. Most of our jobs come from referrals and repeat customers, and we plan to keep it that way.

If we would not do it in our own house, we will not do it in yours.
