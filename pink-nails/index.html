Our Story

A neighborhood salon, built on friendly service and fair prices.

Pink Nails is the kind of place regulars describe as their neighborhood salon. Set right on Little River Turnpike in Alexandria, it is an easy stop on the way home, with a full menu of nail services and beauty extras.

Guests who love it talk about friendly technicians, fair pricing, and manicures that hold up through the week. It is a relaxed, unpretentious spot where you can walk in, get pampered, and get on with your day.

Whether it is a quick polish change, a full acrylic set for an event, or a spa pedicure after a long week, the team is happy to help. Come see why so many Alexandria neighbors make Pink Nails their go-to.
