What we build

Garage conversions,
start to finish

One specialist team for the whole job: design, permits, and construction. Typical scopes below, every project is quoted individually.

01Full Garage-to-ADU ConversionMost requested

Complete transformation of your garage into a permitted accessory dwelling unit: insulated walls and ceiling, new flooring, electrical, plumbing, kitchenette, and full bath.

02Studio & In-Law SuitesFamily living

Private, comfortable space for parents, adult kids, or guests, with its own entrance, bath, and kitchenette, designed to feel like home, not a garage.

03Rental-Ready UnitsIncome

Conversions built to rental standards with separate metering options, durable finishes, and layouts tenants love, so your square footage starts paying you back.

04Home Office & Flex SpaceWork from home

Quiet, climate-controlled workspace separate from the house: proper insulation, lighting, data wiring, and sound control for focus.

05Plans, Permits & EngineeringPaperwork

Floor plans, Title 24 energy compliance, structural details, and city submittals with inspection scheduling, the unglamorous part, done right.

06Kitchen & Bath BuildoutsFinishes

Compact kitchens and full baths fitted into garage footprints: plumbing rough-in, fixtures, tile, and ventilation that meet code.

About budgets: every garage is different, so every quote is custom. As a rough, sample-only reference, basic flex-space conversions in our area often start in the low five figures, while full permitted ADUs with kitchen and bath typically run well into six figures. Who we are

A Burbank team focused on conversions

Garage Conversion San Fernando Valley is a construction company based in Burbank, built around a single specialty: the garage-to-home transformation. Where general contractors juggle a dozen project types, we live in the details of ADU code, garage slab retrofits, and compact floor plans.

That focus shows up where it matters: in plans that pass plan check, in inspections that pass the first time, and in finished spaces that feel like they were always part of the home.

Our clients are Valley homeowners making room for family, for work, or for rental income, and our 5.0-star rating across 12 public reviews reflects the care we put into every build.
