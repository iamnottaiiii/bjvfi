How it works

Simple, careful, thorough

Every visit follows the same careful routine, so nothing gets missed.

01

Walkthrough

We look over every window with you and note anything special, like paint spots or hard-water stains.

02

Wash

Professional scrubbers and pure-water solution loosen dirt, pollen and grime from the glass.

03

Detail

Squeegee finish, then hand-detailed edges, plus cleaned screens, sills and tracks.

04

Inspect

Final walk-around in natural light. If a pane is not perfect, we redo it on the spot.
