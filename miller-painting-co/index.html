Our story

A South Jersey painter built on repeat customers.

One proprietor, one standard.

Miller Painting Co is a professional custom painter based at 1420 Prospect Ridge Blvd in Haddon Heights, NJ, serving Haddonfield, Cherry Hill, Maple Shade, Mount Laurel, and nearby South Jersey towns.

The business grew the old fashioned way: careful work, fair dealing, and customers who come back year after year, some for decades, and send their neighbors next. Reviewers describe the owner as personable and professional, the prep as thorough, and the cleanup as wonderful.

The specialty that sets the work apart is surface repair. Plaster that other painters paint over gets repaired. Sheetrock that looks tired gets refinished. The paint is the last step, not the only step.

  • Plaster and sheetrock repair specialty
