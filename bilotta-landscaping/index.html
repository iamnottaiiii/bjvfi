Our story

Twenty years of showing up in Newton.

Bilotta Landscaping opened its doors in 2006 and has been caring for lawns and landscapes around West Newton ever since.

We are a small, local landscaping company based on Whitlowe Road in West Newton. For two decades, our work has been built the old-fashioned way: word of mouth, repeat customers, and yards that speak for themselves.

A well-kept yard is not luck. It is a schedule, a sharp blade, and someone who cares.

Led by principal Frank Bilotta, the company keeps things simple. You call, we come out, we do the work we promised, and we come back when it is time. That is the whole business model, and after 20 years, it still works.
