Our story

A neighborhood nail spot on Avenue U

ABS Nails has served the Gravesend and Homecrest area from 2011 Avenue U for years, building a steady base of regulars who come back for careful work and honest prices.

A

What guests notice

Reviews point to the same things again and again: a hygienic feeling space, technicians who take their time with shaping and color choice, and pedicures thorough enough to be worth writing about. It is the kind of salon where the staff remembers your favorite shade.

B

The details

Walk-ins are welcome and credit cards are accepted. Appointments are recommended at peak times to avoid a wait. Hours change seasonally, so a quick call is the surest way to plan your visit.

C

Our promise

No rushed jobs and no surprises. If a shade is not right or a nail needs fixing, say so and we will make it right. That is how a neighborhood salon earns its regulars, one set of nails at a time.
