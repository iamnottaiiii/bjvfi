Our Story

A small salon with a simple promise.

Salon on Spring began with a simple idea: Fayetteville deserves a beauty salon that treats every client like a regular. Set on quiet Spring Street, the salon is an easy stop whether you are coming from downtown or the surrounding neighborhoods.

We listen first, then we cut.

Appointments here are never rushed. Your stylist takes the time to understand your hair, your routine, and the look you are after, then crafts a cut or color that works in real life, not just in the mirror.

Salon on Spring is a licensed beauty salon serving Fayetteville, New York. Come in for a cut, stay for the conversation.
