About Oaxaca Barber & Beauty Salon

Oaxaca Barber and Beauty Salon serves the Pico neighborhood with classic barbering and beauty services side by side. It is a practical one-stop shop for the whole household: fades and trims on one side, styling and color on the other.

Every visit follows the same simple rhythm: a real consultation about what you want, careful work in the chair, and a finish you can feel good about. No rushed chairs, no surprises.
