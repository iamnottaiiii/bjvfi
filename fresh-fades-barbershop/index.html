There is a single chair in this shop and a single barber behind it. That is the whole idea: you get the same hands, the same standard and the same clean shop every single visit, from the greeting at the door to the last brush-off at the neck.

Customers stick around here. Some have been coming back for years, and the shop has earned a 5.0 star rating on Google for it.
