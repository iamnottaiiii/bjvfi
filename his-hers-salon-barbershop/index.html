About the House

2310 Mack Road, Fairfield, Ohio

His & Hers Salon & Barbershop is located in Fairfield, Ohio, conveniently off I-275. One shop, two traditions: classic barbering on one side, full salon styling on the other.

Clients describe a cozy, family-friendly room where the owners are passionate about their craft and every guest is made to feel welcome. Reviewers praise the meticulous attention to detail, the warm waiting area for kids, and fair, reasonable prices.

With a 4.7 star rating across 120+ Google reviews, it has become a Fairfield favorite for cuts, color, shaves, and beard work. Call today to schedule an appointment and get a fresh cut or color.
