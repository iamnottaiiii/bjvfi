A flooring studio, not just an installer

No. 01

Choosing a floor is a design decision as much as a construction one. The material under your feet changes how light moves through a room, how sound carries, and how a space feels at the end of a long day. Top Flooring & Design San Gabriel treats that choice with the care it deserves, pairing material guidance with professional installation.

From the first conversation, the focus is on fit: your rooms, your light, your budget, your timeline. Whether you are refreshing a single room or re-flooring a whole home, you get straight advice on hardwood, laminate, luxury vinyl plank, tile, and carpet, and a crew that installs it cleanly.

The right floor does not shout. It makes everything else in the room look better.

The studio on Rosemead Boulevard

Our story

Top Flooring & Design San Gabriel sits at 7220 Rosemead Blvd #551, in the middle of one of the San Gabriel Valley's busiest corridors. The idea behind the business is simple: flooring deserves the same design attention as paint colors and furniture, because it shapes every room it touches.

As a flooring contractor, the team pairs that design eye with installation craft. Floors are measured carefully, subfloors are prepped properly, and transitions between rooms are finished cleanly, so the final result looks intentional from every angle.

Homeowners come in with samples, photos, and half-formed ideas, and leave with a plan: a material that suits their light, their lifestyle, and their budget, plus a clear sense of timing and cost before work begins.

Good design is not about the most expensive material. It is about the right material, installed well.
