  • ▸Full tear off down to the roof deck
  • ▸Deck repair where wood is damaged

  • ▸Material options walked through in plain language

Our Story

Roofers who treat your home like the job site it is not.

Peak Performance Roofing is a roofing contractor based in Burbank, California. We started with a simple frustration: too many roofing jobs begin with a high pressure sales visit and end with a yard full of nails.

So we do it differently. We climb the roof, we take photos, we show you exactly what we see. Then we fix what needs fixing and tell you honestly what can wait. That approach has made us the roofer Burbank neighbors call back, and the one they hand our number to.

Residential or commercial, shingle or flat, we hold every job to the same standard: done right, priced straight, and cleaned up at the end of the day.
