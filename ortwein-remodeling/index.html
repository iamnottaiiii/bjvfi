About

From 1977 to today

Ortwein Remodeling traces back to 1977, when it was founded as Ortwein Builders. The remodeling focus began in 1979, and the company became Ortwein Remodeling in 2001. That is more than 40 years of kitchens, bathrooms, room additions, decks, and patio enclosures in the Cincinnati area, plus design and budget consultation. The business is based at 7536 Bridgetown Rd #2015 in Cincinnati, Ohio. One directory lists a different address (6267 Springmyer Dr) with the same phone number; use the Bridgetown Road address above.
