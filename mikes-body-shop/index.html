Orange-Peel Paint Notes

Factory paint has a light orange-peel texture, and a good refinish matches it instead of laying glass-smooth clear over one panel. The booth log calls out texture, flake lay, and blend distance on every paint job so the repaired panel reads like the rest of the car. Ask the estimator to show you the peel card for your color before the gun ever fires.
