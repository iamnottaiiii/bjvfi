Our Story

SolutionsStar Inspections Co started with a simple observation: most roofing problems are small problems that went unnoticed. A cracked flashing, a clogged valley, a handful of lifted shingles. Left alone, each one becomes a leak, and a leak becomes drywall, paint and mold.

So we built the company around the inspection itself. Based in Panorama City, we serve homeowners across the San Fernando Valley who want straight answers about the roof over their heads. No upsell script, no commission-driven recommendations, just a careful look and an honest report.

When you book with us, you get photos of everything we find and a ranked list of what matters. Fix what needs fixing, watch what bears watching, and sleep easy about the rest.
