Our story

A little salon with a long history

Hairs Looking At You has been part of the Transit Rd corridor in Elma for more than three decades, one appointment at a time.

Photo placeholder 🏥

Swap in a photo of the storefront at 1761 Transit Rd here.

Some salons are built for volume. This one was built for people. For over 30 years, Hairs Looking At You has kept things personal: one guest in the chair, the stylist's full attention, and a finish you love.

The appointment-only approach is not about being exclusive. It is about respect for your time. You will never cool your heels in a crowded waiting room while someone squeezes you in between double bookings.

Guests come from Elma, East Aurora, West Seneca, and beyond, and many have been coming for years. When your hair looks this good, why go anywhere else?
