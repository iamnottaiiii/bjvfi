WHO WE ARE

General Contracting Partners, Ltd. is a full service general contracting firm based in Cincinnati, Ohio, specializing in interior commercial finish and construction projects. The firm's portfolio includes projects across Ohio, Kentucky and Indiana.

Backed by over 30 years of experience in the industry, the team coordinates among clients, architects, builders, subcontractors, property managers and building owners to deliver quality work product in a professional manner. The standard is simple: projects delivered on time and on budget.
