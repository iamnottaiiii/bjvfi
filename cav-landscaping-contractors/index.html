Our Story

Rooted in North Plainfield

CAV LANDSCAPING contractors is a local landscaping company based at 544 Mountain Ave in North Plainfield, NJ. We serve homeowners across the area with hands-on landscape design, installation, and year-round maintenance.

Our crew handles the full range of outdoor work: landscaping, lawn maintenance, mulch, trimming, pavers, patios, stone, tree service, new plantings, replacements, and fall and spring cleanups. One call covers the whole yard, and we treat every property like it is our own.

Why neighbors choose us

Reliability, communication, and pride in the finished product. We show up on time, explain the work clearly, and leave the site clean. That is how we earn repeat customers and referrals season after season.
