No. 01 · The brief

Why a roof fails, and what a good contractor does about it

Most roofs in Worcester do not fail all at once. They fail one shingle, one flashing, one ice dam at a time, usually in February, usually right over the room you use most. A roof is a system: shingles, underlayment, flashing, ventilation and gutters all working together. When one part gives out, the rest pick up the slack until they cannot.

That is why the first visit matters more than the last. A careful look at the roof, the attic and the gutters tells you whether you need a targeted repair or a full replacement, and an honest contractor will tell you which one it is. No pressure, no mystery line items, just the roof in front of you and what it needs.

A roof is the one part of your house you never look at until it is too late. Look at it now.

Services

Every service, explained in plain language

The full index of what Worcester Roofing Contractors does. If it involves the top of your house, start here.

01

Full roof replacement

When a roof is at the end of its life, patching stops making sense. We strip the old roof down to the deck, replace damaged decking, lay new underlayment, and install a complete new shingle system with fresh flashing at every penetration. You get one clean start and a roof built as a system, not a stack of fixes.

Most requested
02

Roof repair

Leaks, lifted or missing shingles, cracked boots around vents, flashing pulling away from the chimney. Most repairs are smaller than homeowners fear, and catching them early is the cheapest roofing work there is.

03

Storm and wind damage

New England throws ice, wind and falling limbs at roofs every year. We document the damage, make the roof watertight fast, then complete the permanent repair. If insurance is involved, we give you clear documentation of what we found.

04

Gutters and downspouts

Gutters are part of the roof system: they move water away from the foundation and keep it off the fascia. We install and repair gutters and downspouts so water goes where it should.

05

Flashing, skylights and ventilation

Chimney flashing, skylight curbs and seals, and attic ventilation. These are the details that decide whether a roof lasts its full life or starts leaking in year eight.

06

Roof inspections

Buying a house, selling one, or just uneasy after a rough winter. We walk the roof and the attic, photograph what we see, and give you a straight written summary.
