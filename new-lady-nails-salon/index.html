Our story

A friendly stop on 18th Avenue

What we are about

New Lady Nails Salon is a neighborhood nail salon at 5915 18th Ave in Brooklyn. The focus is simple: friendly service, careful work, and prices that make regular visits easy.

Clients mention the welcoming atmosphere and the patience of the technicians. Whether it is your first set of dip powder nails or your fiftieth pedicure, the goal is the same: you leave happy with how your nails look.

What to expect

Walk in or call ahead at (718) 234-4320. Pick your service and color, settle into the chair, and let the team handle the rest.

The salon is closed on Saturdays and open 9:30 AM to 7 PM every other day, so a Sunday visit fits right into the weekend.
