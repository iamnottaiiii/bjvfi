The practice, in brief

Straightforward dentistry from a dentist who treats every mouth like his own family.

There is a reason patients describe Dr. Bergida as a perfectionist. He built his practice on the belief that careful work lasts, whether that means a crown that fits exactly right, a filling that feels like nothing was ever there, or an honest opinion about what treatment you truly need.

Alongside general dentistry, the practice offers modern options including Invisalign and other clear aligners, mini dental implants, and oral appliance therapy for patients who snore or suffer from sleep apnea. About the dentist

Dr. Robert Bergida, DDS

Dr. Bergida grew up in Montreal, Canada and graduated from McGill University School of Dentistry. For more than thirty years he has been in private practice in New York, building a reputation for above standard care and honest, unhurried dentistry.

He takes pride in treating patients with care he would give his own family, and he has continued training throughout his career, earning certifications with the World Clinical Institute, Mini Dental Implants, Ortho Clear, Invisalign, Clear Correct, the Inman Aligner, and Fastbraces.

In addition to general dentistry, Dr. Bergida has incorporated oral appliance therapy into his practice to treat patients suffering from sleep apnea and snoring.
