About Five-Star Contracting llc.

Among the fastest growing remodeling contractors in Harrisburg

With a passion for delivering outstanding home building services designed to suit the specific needs of its clients, Five Star Contracting has been among the fastest growing, upcoming home remodeling contractors in Harrisburg, PA. We serve the Greater Harrisburg and surrounding areas.

Five Star Contracting is backed by over 20 years of experience in the various aspects of residential construction and remodeling. Dedicated to providing total customer satisfaction, our foundation is deeply rooted in our unflinching commitment to integrity, quality, and responsiveness to our customers' needs.

Being a family owned and operated business, Five Star Contracting treats each project as if it were our own. We take the time to listen to our clients' needs and discuss solutions that address their every need. We make sure we provide a more personal level of service that keeps the customer involved in all decision-making processes. We strive to keep the lines of communication open, because we have learned that they are essential to a successfully completed project and another satisfied client.

Next time you need expert builder services, remember the name that has your best interests at heart: Five Star Contracting.
