About Stratus HVAC Repair Harrisburg

A Harrisburg HVAC contractor, plain and simple

Stratus HVAC Repair Harrisburg is an HVAC contractor based at 225 Walnut St in Harrisburg, PA, focused on heating and cooling repair, system replacement, and maintenance for local homes.

Our approach is straightforward: show up, diagnose honestly, explain your options in plain language, and fix it right. Whether it is a furnace that will not fire on the coldest night of the year or an AC struggling through a heat wave, the goal is the same, getting your home comfortable again without surprises on the bill.
