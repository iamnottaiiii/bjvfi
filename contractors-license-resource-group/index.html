Our Story

Teaching contractors since 1975

Placeholder

From a Bellflower classroom to seven schools

Our service history began more than 35 years ago, when the first school opened in Bellflower in 1975 as Contractors License School. Over the years we have helped thousands of contractors pass their state exam and discover what the construction industry can offer.

Today the Contractors License Resource Group operates schools across Southern California, including Baldwin Park, Bellflower, Carson, Garden Grove, Glendale, Riverside, and Van Nuys.

Placeholder

Directors and consultants who hold your hand

School directors and license consultants guide you through the entire process: enrollment, scheduled classes and seminars, application and testing, passing, business planning, branding, marketing, and beyond.

That extra push matters. Students get encouragement through the hardest parts of the state exam, plus practical business advice for what comes after.
