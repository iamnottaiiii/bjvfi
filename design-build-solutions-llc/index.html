From the Archives: About Us

ARCHIVE / DBS / 001

One Team, From the First Thought to Occupancy

Design Build Solutions LLC is a Cincinnati, Ohio construction firm headquartered at 11590 Century Blvd, Cincinnati, OH 45246. The firm offers complete construction services under the design build model: instead of hiring a designer and a builder separately, clients work with a single team responsible for both the plans and the finished building.

Their stated approach is simple: listen to and understand the client's needs, because every project is unique, and design specific solutions for each one. The company describes itself not as a distributor of building systems but as a service business that provides solutions, from the very first thought and even after occupancy.

Project history referenced on the firm's public materials includes large corporate headquarters, office buildings, industrial and distribution facilities, and special purpose projects across the Cincinnati region, including work in Hamilton, Mason, West Chester, Sharonville, and Dent, Ohio.
