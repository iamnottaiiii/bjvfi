About MB3 Mobile Mechanic

My name is Berny, owner of MB3. I do automotive and motorcycle repairs and preventive maintenance, and I do it all mobile, at your location.

MB3 is based at 3508 Ripplegrove Dr in Cincinnati, OH, and the whole idea is simple: instead of you arranging a tow, a ride, and a day off, the shop comes to you. Your driveway, your workplace parking lot, or wherever the vehicle is sitting becomes the service bay.

From check engine diagnostics to brakes, batteries, belts, and routine maintenance, you deal directly with the person turning the wrenches. No service advisors, no upsell scripts, just the work your vehicle actually needs.

  • Fully mobile: we come to your home, office, or roadside location
  • Cars, trucks, and motorcycles serviced
  • You talk directly to the owner and technician
  • Serving Cincinnati, OH and nearby communities (call to confirm service area)
