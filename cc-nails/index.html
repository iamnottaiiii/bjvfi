Our story

A new studio with a simple promise

CC Nails recently opened its doors at 100 Marketplace Dr in Rochester, NY, licensed as a New York State appearance enhancement business in 2025.

Our promise is simple: unhurried appointments, honest pricing and nails you are excited to show off. Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, every client gets the same careful attention.

Stop by our Marketplace district studio and let us earn your trust, one manicure at a time.
