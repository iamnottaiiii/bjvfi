Company dossier

A-Tom Home Maintenance LLC

Since 2011, A-Tom has been the call Northern Kentucky homeowners make when something needs fixing. We specialize in residential and multi-family maintenance and repairs at affordable rates, from the squeaky hinge to the tired bathroom to the cracked front walk.

We are a small, family-run company based in the Alexandria and Claryville area of Campbell County. When you call A-Tom, you talk to people who live here, work here, and stake their name on every job.

Our reputation was built the slow way: one on-time arrival, one careful repair, one happy neighbor at a time. Landlords across the Cincinnati area now trust us with their rental properties instead of the big management companies, because we treat every unit like it is our own home.
