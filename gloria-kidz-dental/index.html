Dental services for kids

General pediatric dental services described below. Call to confirm which services are offered and whether they are right for your child.

Routine dental exams help catch small issues early and keep your child's smile on track. We recommend regular checkups so kids stay comfortable with dental visits from an early age.

Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing can miss, and give our hygienists a chance to teach great brushing habits.

When a cavity is found, we treat it promptly and gently, keeping your child comfortable and explaining what is happening in simple terms.

From teething to thumb-sucking to first braces conversations, we answer parents' questions and share practical tips for healthy teeth at home.

Service descriptions are general. Please call (410) 969-9090 to confirm services for your child.
