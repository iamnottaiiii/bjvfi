About the department

Serving the Town of New Whiteland.

The New Whiteland Street Department serves the Town of New Whiteland, Indiana, from 324 500 N. It keeps town streets safe and passable for residents and visitors.

Residents can report potholes, road obstructions, sign damage, leaf pickup needs, or animal remains through the Town of New Whiteland website, or call (317) 535-4770.
