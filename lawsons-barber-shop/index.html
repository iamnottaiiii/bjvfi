The Story

A Woodlawn Original

The House Band

Lawson's Barber Shop has been holding down 9870 Springfield Pike in Woodlawn, Cincinnati since 2000, led by owner Robert Lawson. It is a classic single-location neighborhood shop: buzzing clippers, straight razor work, and the kind of banter you only get from barbers who know their regulars by name.

Walk-ins and regulars alike come for the same thing: a sharp, honest cut at a fair price, no pretense, no rush.
