the menu

Tattoo styles and services

From first tiny tattoos to full custom sleeves, plus pro piercing. Every style below is drawn custom for your body, never pulled off a wall twice.

Fine Line

Delicate single needle work, florals, micro script, minimal symbols. Summer's linework gets name dropped in reviews for a reason.

Black and Grey

Smooth shading, soft gradients, portraits and realism with depth. Built to age well and heal clean.

Traditional

Bold lines, solid color, roses, daggers, eagles. Old school flash with new school cleanliness.

Lettering and Script

Names, dates, quotes in hand drawn lettering. Sized and flowed to your placement, not stamped on.

Neo Traditional

Illustrated, jewel toned, and decorative. Moths, snakes, and botanicals with serious detail.

Cover Ups

Free consult and an honest read on your old ink. Skilled reworks that turn regrets into keepers.

Body Piercing

Nostrils, septums, industrials, snake bites, navels, and ears. Sterile technique, quality jewelry, calm hands.

First Tattoos

Never been inked? Perfect. The crew walks you through placement, pain, and aftercare with zero attitude.

Touch Ups

Healed a little light? Bring it back and the shop will take care of it. Standing behind the work is the whole point.

About the craft: every tattoo starts with a consult and a custom drawing. Bring references, photos, or just the idea in your head. The artists refine with you until it is right, then book the session. Walk-ins welcome daily.
