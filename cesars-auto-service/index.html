The Founding Story

A Workshop Raised on Trust

On State Avenue in Cincinnati's Lower Price Hill neighborhood stands Cesar's Auto Service, an auto repair shop known to locals for straight dealing. Public reviews spanning several years tell a consistent story: one reviewer says they have been a customer for about eight years and calls Cesar one of the best mechanics in the city; another calls the work quick, accurate, and fairly priced.

The shop carries a 4.4 out of 5 star standing across public listings, and reviewers repeatedly use words like honest, reasonable, and reliable. Some note that jobs other shops kept overnight were finished in a single afternoon. That record, written by customers rather than the shop itself, is the closest thing to a founding charter: do the work well, charge fairly, and treat people like neighbors.
