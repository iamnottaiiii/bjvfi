About

The story of Tabe68 BARBER SHOP

Tabe68 BARBER SHOP is a neighborhood barbershop on Crenshaw Boulevard in Gardena. Clients come in for sharp, careful work and a straightforward, no-rush visit.

Located at 14426 Crenshaw Blvd Ste F, Gardena, CA 90249, the shop serves Gardena and the surrounding neighborhoods. Walk-ins and regulars are both welcome, and the team is happy to talk through the cut before the clippers come out.
