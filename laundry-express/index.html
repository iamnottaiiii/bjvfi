About the machine

The short story of this Mt Zion Road laundromat

A neighborhood workhorse

Laundry Express is a self service laundromat at 220 Mt Zion Rd in Florence, Kentucky, serving the neighborhoods along the Mt Zion corridor. It is the kind of place built for routine: steady machines, an attendant on duty, and posted hours that run 6 AM to 11 PM every day.

Reviewers describe it as a newer feeling, generally clean store with plenty of washer sizes to choose from, and staff who pitch in when machines act up.

We keep claims modest here. What is written above comes from public listings and public reviews. For anything else, call the store.

Reviews, straight up

An honest read of what the public says, good and bad

What reviewers tend to agree on

Public Google reviews for this location paint a mixed but useful picture. On the positive side, reviewers praise the variety of washer sizes, the generally clean and newer feeling interior, and attendants who help when machines misbehave. On the critical side, some reviewers report the store closing earlier than posted hours, card reader and wifi hiccups at the machine panels, and uneven customer service from newer staff.

Common praisePlenty of machine sizes at different price points, clean interior, helpful attendants, convenient Mt Zion Road location.
Common complaintsEarly closings versus posted hours, card payment panel issues, and inconsistent help from staff on some visits.
