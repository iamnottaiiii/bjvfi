Our story

Dependable work, generation after generation

Jerry Harmeyer Construction Co describes itself as a second generation company that specializes in quality and dependable home and business remodeling. It is the kind of firm where the family name is on the truck, and the reputation is earned one homeowner at a time.

The company is family owned and based at 6146 Wesselman Rd in Cincinnati, Ohio, serving the Greater Cincinnati area. Public business listings trace the company's history back to 1975, giving it more than five decades of roots in the local building trade.

Across Angi, Birdeye, and aggregate review sources, customers rate the company 5 out of 5 stars, describing excellent workmanship and a crew that is easy and honest to deal with. That track record is the foundation every new project builds on.
