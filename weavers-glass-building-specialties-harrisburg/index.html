01 Who we are

Fabrication and installation, under one roof

Weaver's Glass & Building Specialties, Inc. is a Harrisburg commercial contractor focused on architectural glass and aluminum. Directory listings describe the company as serving the Central Pennsylvania region for more than 20 years, with a 60,000 square foot warehousing facility and in-house CAD shop drawing development.

The core of the work is the building envelope: aluminum entrances and storefronts, curtain walls, skylights, glass and glazing, plus aluminum panels, fascia, soffits and column wraps in aluminum and stainless steel.

02 The company

Built around the building envelope

Weaver's Glass & Building Specialties, Inc. is based at 7631 Derry St in Harrisburg, PA, and works as a specialty contractor in glass and glazing. The Blue Book construction directory lists the company under doors (aluminum, bronze and stainless steel), glass and glazing contractors, and storefronts, with a listing that dates to 1987.

Directory profiles describe service across Eastern Pennsylvania, including Dauphin County and the Harrisburg area, for commercial, healthcare and institutional projects. Pennsylvania corporate records list the company as an active business corporation formed in 1986.

Operationally, the company runs a 60,000 square foot warehousing facility and keeps CAD shop drawing development in house, supporting both fabrication and installation crews.

One contractor for entrances, storefronts, curtain walls, skylights and the metalwork around them.
