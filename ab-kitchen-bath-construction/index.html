Good to know

Before you hire any contractor

Tip 01

Get it in writing

A written scope and price protects both sides. Never start a remodel on a handshake alone.

Tip 02

Verify the license

California contractors must be licensed by the CSLB for jobs over $500. Look up any license at cslb.ca.gov.

Tip 03

Check insurance

Ask for proof of general liability and workers comp coverage before work begins.

Tip 04

Compare apples to apples

Collect two or three detailed quotes so you can compare scope, materials, and timelines fairly.
