Our story

Tottenville's corner
for beautiful nails

Crystal's Nails is a local nail salon on Page Avenue, serving the south shore of Staten Island one set at a time.

Careful work, comfortable chairs

A great nail appointment is equal parts craft and calm. The techs at Crystal's Nails take their time with prep and shaping, because a beautiful set starts with healthy natural nails underneath. Then comes the fun part: color, finish and art, chosen with you, not for you.

From a quick polish change before an event to a full acrylic set with hand painted details, every client gets the same patience and attention.

Clean tools, every time

Sanitation is non negotiable. Fresh setups for every client, every visit.

Walk-ins welcome

No appointment needed. Stop by whenever a chair is free.
