A care calendar for every season

Valley weather asks different things of a landscape through the year. Here is how a typical Cacho maintenance plan moves with the seasons. Exact tasks and pricing are set at your free walkthrough.

Spring

Mar to May
  • Post-winter cleanup and debris removal
  • Fresh mulch and new color plantings
  • Sprinkler startup and coverage check
  • First deep mow and crisp bed edges

Summer

Jun to Aug
  • Weekly mowing at heat-safe heights
  • Irrigation tuning to beat the Valley heat
  • Hedge trimming and shape-ups
  • Weed control in beds and hardscape

Fall

Sep to Nov
  • Leaf and seed-pod cleanup
  • Overseeding thin lawn areas
  • Tree and shrub shaping before winter
  • Drainage and gutter-line clearing

Winter

Dec to Feb
  • Dormant pruning for roses and fruit trees
  • Storm-debris pickup after wind events
  • Irrigation winterization checks
  • Planning spring installs and upgrades

Our story

Rooted in the Valley since 1979

Cacho Landscape Maintenance Co has been caring for San Fernando Valley landscapes for more than four decades. Business directories list the company as established in 1979 and incorporated in California.

Directories name Eddie Cacho as president and estimate a crew of about 50 people, which is how the company keeps weekly routes, condo communities, and commercial properties all on schedule from its shop at 711 Truman St. in San Fernando.

The same directories describe Cacho's clients across homes, condos, and corporate properties, and the company has shown up on Los Angeles County proposers lists for public grounds maintenance work. Big enough for a county bid, local enough to know your street.

  • 1979

    The company is established in California, per business directory records, and begins serving Valley properties.

  • Today

    A crew of about 50 keeps homes, condo communities, and commercial grounds on schedule from 711 Truman St., San Fernando.

  • Public work
