Our story

Thirteen years of calm, skilled nail care

V Spa & Nail Studio has served Waldorf for over a decade, growing into one of the area most spacious nail salons at 2215 Crain Highway. The studio is known for its serene, beautifully decorated interior and a team that regulars return to for years.

Owners Jimmy and K V built the studio around consistency: skilled technicians, a clean and well kept space, steady attention to detail, and small touches like complimentary tea that make an appointment feel like a real break. Guests travel from an hour away for the atmosphere and the craft.

Appointments are recommended and walk ins are welcome. Free parking sits right outside, and the studio is wheelchair accessible throughout.
