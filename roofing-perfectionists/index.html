How we work

Three ways we keep you dry

Pick your lane. Every service starts with a look at your roof and an honest assessment.

Full roof installations

When a roof is past its prime, we tear off and rebuild it the right way: solid decking, quality underlayment, precise shingle or tile work and sealed flashing at every penetration.

  • ✓Shingle and tile roof replacements
  • ✓Top-grade materials with advanced techniques
  • ✓Clean tear-off and full job site cleanup

Leak repairs that hold

Stains on the ceiling or shingles in the yard mean water is getting in. We find the real entry point, fix it properly and check the surrounding area so the leak does not come back.

  • ✓Leak detection and targeted repairs
  • ✓Flashing, vent and valley fixes
  • ✓Storm and wind damage repair

Maintenance that extends roof life

A little upkeep goes a long way. Scheduled inspections, debris clearing and minor resealing keep your roof performing and protect your warranty.

  • ✓Seasonal inspections and tune-ups
  • ✓Gutter and debris clearing
  • ✓Minor resealing before the rainy season
