How booking works

Thiagotattousa is a working tattoo shop, not a walk-in factory. Here is the usual path from idea to ink.

1

Call the shop. Talk through your idea, size, and where on the body you want it.

2

Lock the design. Get your custom piece drawn up and approve it before needle meets skin.

3
