Our story

Nails R'Us is a small, locally-owned salon on West Main Street built around one thing: Clare's personal service. Clients who came to her as children still come back years later. The owner is praised for her sweet demeanor and strong work ethic, and for making every client feel like family. The salon offers manicures, pedicures, gel polish, nail art, eyebrow shaping, and lash treatments, with affordable prices and a quiet, welcoming atmosphere.
