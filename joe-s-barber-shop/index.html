About

A classic shop on
Providence Rd

Joe's Barber Shop is a neighborhood barbershop in Linwood, Massachusetts, the kind of place built on regulars, referrals, and a reputation for getting it right.

Set on Providence Road in Linwood, Joe's Barber Shop serves neighbors from Linwood and the surrounding towns with straightforward, skilled barbering. Reviewers describe it as one of the best places in the area to get a haircut, and the reasons come up again and again.

Quick scheduling means you are not waiting weeks for a chair. Fair prices mean a great cut stays affordable. And a friendly, comfortable environment means the visit is as enjoyable as the result.

Above all, the barbers listen. Bring a photo, describe the look, or just say "the usual", and the cut comes out the way you asked. That simple promise is the whole business model, and it is why customers say they will definitely be returning.
