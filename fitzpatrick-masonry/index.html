The services ledger

Every trade we practice

Pick a chapter. Each one covers what we do, how we do it, and what to expect when you call.

Brick & Block Work

New construction and structural masonry: additions, garages, foundations, and load-bearing walls, laid plumb and true.

  • New brick construction. Additions, porches, and outbuildings matched to your home's existing brick.
  • Concrete block foundations. Footings to frost line, waterproofed and backfilled right.
  • Brick mailboxes & pillars. The small jobs that make the street look finished.
  • Commercial masonry. Storefronts, veneer, and block work for local businesses.
Estimates are free. Every job is priced on site after we see the work. The ranges below are samples only, not quotes.

Chimney & Fireplace

From a cracked crown to a full rebuild, we keep chimneys drafting safely and looking like they belong on the house.

  • Chimney rebuilds. Partial and full rebuilds with proper flashing and new flue liners.
  • Crowns, caps & flashing. The top of the chimney is where most leaks start. We fix it there.
  • Fireplace facings. Brick and stone refacing that turns a dated fireplace into the room's centerpiece.
  • Waterproofing. Breathable sealers that keep water out without trapping it in.
Fall is chimney season. Call before the first cold snap and beat the rush. Estimates are free.

Stone & Veneer

Natural stone and manufactured veneer for walls, facades, and landscape features, set with an eye for the pattern.

  • Stone veneer siding. Full and partial facades in natural or manufactured stone.
  • Retaining walls. Block and stone walls engineered to hold grade and shed water.
  • Patios, steps & walkways. Stone and brick hardscaping that ties the yard together.
  • Outdoor fireplaces & kitchens. The backyard projects everyone gathers around.
Bring us a photo. A picture of your space and a rough measurement is enough to start the conversation.

Repair & Restoration

Old masonry deserves a mason who respects it. We repair and restore brick, block, and stone the way it was built.

  • Tuckpointing & repointing. Failed mortar joints removed and repointed with the right mix for the era of the building.
  • Spalling & cracked brick. Damaged units cut out and replaced with matching brick.
  • Foundation & wall cracks. Step cracks and bowing walls assessed honestly, repaired properly.
  • Lintel & sill replacement. Rusted lintels and crumbling sills swapped before they take the wall with them.
Worried about a crack? Send a photo when you call. We will tell you straight whether it needs a mason or just a watchful eye.

Since 1992

The short version: we never left the trade

Fitzpatrick Masonry started as a small family crew in the early nineties and stayed one. While bigger outfits chased volume, we chased the kind of work that still looks right thirty years later.

That is why our phone still rings for the same reasons it always has: a chimney that needs rebuilding before winter, a brick wall that has seen better decades, a new addition that has to match a 1960s ranch.

We are based on Ann Street in Villa Hills and work across Northern Kentucky, for homeowners who care about craft and for commercial clients who need it done right the first time.
