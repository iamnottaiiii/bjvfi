Our story

Four decades, one family, and a simple standard: a quality product at a competitive price.

Krallman Masonry Inc was incorporated in Kentucky in 1982 and has been laying brick, block, and stone across Northern Kentucky ever since. Kentucky Secretary of State records list the company as family-led, with Michael Krallman as president alongside Martin, Lee, and Kevin Krallman.

The company is a member of the Mason Contractors Association of America and the Northern Kentucky Chamber of Commerce, and carries a BuildZoom score of 90. Its commercial work includes block and brick construction on the Crescent Springs firehouse, and its residential crews handle everything from chimneys to patios.
