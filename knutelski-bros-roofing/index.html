Our story

Sixty years on Rahway roofs

Knutelski Bros Roofing has worked out of 615 Union Street in Rahway, New Jersey since 1965. Three generations of homeowners have called the same number, (732) 382-8286, and talked to the people who will actually climb the ladder.

The company runs a small, steady crew of about six. On most jobs under $10,000 there is no subcontracting and no down payment: the work gets done, the customer walks the job, and payment comes when the job is complete. Warranties back the work.

That old-school way of doing business shows up in the ratings: 4.9 out of 5 across 21 Angi reviews, with customers noting free estimates and a crew that stands behind the work.
