About

A neighborhood shop with a steady hand

Hannam Barber Shop serves La Palma from 4943 La Palma Ave, inside a Korean commercial plaza on one of the city’s main corridors. It is the kind of shop where the regulars know the chairs and the chairs know the regulars.

Reviewers mention careful scissor work and cuts that keep them coming back. First time visitors describe an easy walk in experience and a shop that earns repeat business the simple way: one good cut at a time.

Whether you need a weekly cleanup or a full restyle, the approach stays the same. Listen first, cut with care, and make sure you like what you see in the mirror before you leave.
