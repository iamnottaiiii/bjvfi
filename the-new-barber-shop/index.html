Our story

NOT SO NEW.
ALWAYS SHARP.

The name always gets a laugh from longtime customers. They remember when it really was the new barber shop in town. Years later, it is a Long Beach staple, and the name stuck.

A neighborhood shop

Opened on Santa Fe Avenue to give Long Beach a straightforward, honest barbershop: great cuts, fair prices, no pretension.

Regulars by name

Generations of clients have come through the door. Barbers like Denise and Ryan built loyal followings one haircut at a time, including plenty of father-and-son visits.

The craft, kept classic

Low fades blended to skin, straight-blade finishes, clean tapers and sharp line-ups. Old-school technique, modern style.

A peaceful chair

Reviewers call the atmosphere peaceful and professional, a place to sit back, get a great cut, and leave with renewed confidence.
