Our story

A neighborhood salon built on trust.

Crystal's Salon has served Baldwinsville from 9 E Genesee St with one simple promise: leave every guest feeling beautiful when they walk out the door.

Our stylists specialize in hair services for men, women and children, from everyday cuts to full color transformations. Guests know Crystal as a gifted colorist and stylist with a special way of bringing inner beauty to the surface, and Erika as a true artist with hair color who studies face shape, texture and hairline before every service.

Longtime clients tell us they feel like family here, and many have trusted us with their hair for over a decade.
