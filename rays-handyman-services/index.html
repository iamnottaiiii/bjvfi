Our story

About Ray's

Ray's Handyman Services is a Staten Island home services business built on repeat customers and word of mouth. The formula is simple: show up on time, treat the home like your own, and do the job properly the first time.

Reviewers describe a nice crew that is professional, reasonable, and very knowledgeable. Multi-day projects around the house, new kitchen installs, and bathroom renovations all earn the same verdict: very satisfied, would use again.

The business is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so urgent repairs do not have to wait for Monday morning. One call, one crew, one less thing on your list.
