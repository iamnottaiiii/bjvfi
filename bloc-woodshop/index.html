Our story

Woodwork with
a deeper purpose

At 605 Burns St in Cincinnati, BLOC Woodshop builds more than furniture. It builds futures.

A second chance, built daily

The shop's mission extends beyond craftsmanship. By offering meaningful employment, BLOC Woodshop, also known as Restoration Woodshop, empowers men and women to create beautiful pieces while rebuilding their futures. Each creation embodies both skillful artistry and a commitment to positive change.

Renowned makers

Known in Cincinnati for quality handcrafted furniture, custom cabinets and built-ins, shaped by hands that know the value of a fresh start.
