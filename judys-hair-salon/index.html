the short version

A salon that treats
you like a regular

Walk into Judy's and you join a long line of neighbors who have trusted these chairs for years. The stylists are known for doing genuinely great hair: sharp cuts, beautiful updos, color that flatters.

The experience guests mention most is the wash: a thorough, relaxing shampoo with a massage that turns a routine appointment into a small escape. It is the kind of detail that keeps people coming back for decades.

Prices stay honest, the vibe stays unpretentious, and the work speaks for itself. Bring a picture of the style you want and let the team take it from there.
