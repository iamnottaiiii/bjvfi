Our story

From one man with a truck to a full service contractor

The short version of four decades in the San Gabriel Valley.

1983

Sierra Gardening Service begins

Richard Watchler starts a one man operation providing residential and commercial landscape maintenance throughout the San Gabriel Valley.

Growth

Back to school, then bigger work

Richard returns to college to deepen his knowledge of every aspect of landscaping, and the company grows into design, installation, and irrigation.

Today

Sierra Landscape Co

A full service landscape, erosion control, irrigation and maintenance contractor based at 1124 S Santa Anita Ave in Arcadia, still serving the valley it started in.
