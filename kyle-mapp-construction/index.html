Services

Six specialties, one standard: it has to be right.

With 25 years of experience (per Angi), Kyle Mapp Construction focuses on tile, stone, and the remodels built around them. Every project is quoted individually. Call (678) 300-5806 for pricing.

01

Bathroom remodels

Full master and hall bath remodels with design help, so you know how each step of the remodel will go before work starts.

 02

Custom tile and stone

Custom tile and stonework for any room, from feature walls to full surrounds, in ceramic, porcelain, marble, and granite.

 03

Shower builds and shower pans

New showers and shower pan re-work done by tile specialists, with waterproofing handled correctly underneath the tile.

Tile and ceramic repair

Ceramic and porcelain tile repair: cracked tiles, failing grout, and problem spots fixed without tearing out what still works.

Flooring

Tile and stone flooring installation in granite, marble, and ceramic and porcelain, measured and laid for a flat, lasting finish.

Saunas, steam rooms, and wet areas

Saunas, showers, and steam rooms tiled and sealed for constant moisture, built to handle heat and humidity for years.
