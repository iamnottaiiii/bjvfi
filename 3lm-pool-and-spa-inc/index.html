What we do

Every part of pool and spa care

Pick a service area to see what is included. Every job starts with a free estimate, confirmed in writing before any work begins.

Weekly pool service

The set-it-and-forget-it plan. We stop by every week and keep your water balanced, clean, and safe.

  • Skimming, brushing, and vacuuming
  • Chemical testing and balancing
  • Filter checks and basket emptying
  • Equipment walk-through every visit
Good to know: weekly plans are priced per pool. Call (818) 389-5771 for a free estimate based on your pool size and condition.

Pool repairs

From mystery leaks to tired plaster, we find the problem and fix it right the first time.

  • Leak detection and repair
  • Plaster, tile, and coping repair
  • Plumbing and valve work
  • Green-to-clean water recovery
Good to know: repairs are quoted up front. No work starts until you approve the price.

Equipment install and upgrades

Modern equipment runs quieter, lasts longer, and can cut your energy bill.

  • Variable-speed pumps
  • Heaters and heat pumps
  • Salt systems and chlorinators
  • Filters, timers, and automation
Good to know: equipment pricing varies by model. We will recommend options at a few price points.

Remodeling and resurfacing

Give an aging pool a fresh start, from a simple replaster to a full new look.

  • Replastering and resurfacing
  • New tile and coping
  • Deck refinishing
  • Water features and lighting
Good to know: remodels are custom-quoted after an on-site visit. Call to schedule yours.

Spa and hot tub care

Spas need their own chemistry and their own attention. We handle both, alone or bundled with pool service.

  • Spa water balancing and sanitation
  • Jet and heater service
  • Cover and cabinet care
  • Drain, clean, and refill service
Good to know: spa service can be added to any weekly pool plan. Ask when you call.
