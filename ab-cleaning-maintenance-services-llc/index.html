About

A Revere crew for the whole to-do list.

Ab Cleaning & Maintenance Services LLC is a locally based cleaning and maintenance company at 54 Adams St in Revere, MA. The idea is simple: most properties do not just need cleaning, they need upkeep, and juggling two vendors for both is a waste of time.

So we do both. The crew that details your kitchen can also handle the small maintenance tasks around it, which means fewer appointments, fewer invoices, and a property that stays in better shape between visits.
