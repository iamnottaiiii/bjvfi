Recurring home cleaning

Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly visits that keep your whole home guest-ready without you lifting a finger.

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, appliance exteriors, microwave
  • Bathrooms: toilet, tub, shower, mirrors, fixtures
  • Bedrooms and living areas: dusting, vacuuming, mopping
  • Trash removal and liner replacement
  • Mirrors, glass, and high-touch surfaces
  • Bed linens changed on request
Sample pricing: most recurring plans fall between $120 and $220 per visit. Call for your exact quote.

Deep cleaning

A top-to-bottom reset for homes that need more than a maintenance clean. Perfect before holidays, after renovations, or once a season.

  • Everything in a recurring clean, plus detail work
  • Baseboards, window sills, and door frames
  • Inside oven, refrigerator, and cabinets
  • Grout scrubbing and tile detailing
  • Ceiling fans, vents, and light fixtures
  • Interior windows and blinds
Sample pricing: deep cleans typically range from $200 to $400 by home size. Call for your exact quote.

Move-in / move-out cleaning

Empty-home detail cleaning for renters protecting a deposit, landlords turning a unit, or buyers starting fresh.

  • Full deep-clean checklist, room by room
  • Inside all cabinets, drawers, and closets
  • Appliance interiors: oven, fridge, dishwasher
  • Wall spot-cleaning and baseboard detail
  • Laundry room and utility areas
  • Final walkthrough checklist provided
Sample pricing: move cleans often range from $250 to $500. Call for your exact quote.

One-time cleaning

Hosting guests, recovering from a busy month, or just want to try us out? A single thorough visit, no commitment.

  • Whole-home maintenance-level clean
  • Priority areas: tell us what matters most
  • Laundry and dishes add-on available
  • Eco-friendly products on request
Sample pricing: one-time visits typically start around $150. Call for your exact quote.
