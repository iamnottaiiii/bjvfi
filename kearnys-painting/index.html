Services

What a painter
should do well

Core painting services for homes in Haddonfield and nearby Camden County neighborhoods. Call to confirm details for your project.

Interior Painting

Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, hallways, ceilings and closets. Includes moving and covering furniture, patching small holes, sanding, priming where needed, and two finish coats for even coverage.

Exterior Painting

Siding, brick accents, shutters, front doors, porches and railings. Scraped, primed and finished with exterior-grade coatings built for New Jersey weather.

Cabinets & Trim

Kitchen cabinet refinishing, baseboards, crown molding, window casings and doors, sprayed or brushed for a smooth factory-like finish.

Drywall & Prep

Patching, skim coating, caulking and sanding. The prep work that makes paint look right and last.

Color Consulting

Stuck between swatches? We will help you narrow it down to colors that fit your light, your trim, and your home.
