What we do

Contracting, end to end

Pick a service to see what a typical project covers. Exact scope and pricing are confirmed on your call.

01Kitchen remodeling

The heart of the home, rebuilt around how you cook and gather. Typical projects cover layouts, cabinetry, counters, backsplash, lighting, and flooring, with plumbing and electrical coordinated by one crew.

  • Layout redesign and space planning
  • Cabinetry, counters, and backsplash
  • Lighting, flooring, and paint
  • Appliance and fixture coordination

02Bathroom remodeling

Guest baths to primary suites. Typical projects cover tile, vanities, fixtures, glass enclosures, and proper waterproofing, with dust control so the rest of the house stays livable.

  • Tile, tub, and shower rebuilds
  • Vanities, fixtures, and mirrors
  • Waterproofing done right
  • Ventilation and lighting upgrades

03Home additions

Need more room without moving? Additions and conversions add real square footage. We handle design coordination, permits, and the build as a single managed project.

  • Room additions and bump-outs
  • Garage and space conversions
  • Permit and inspection handling
  • Matching existing finishes

04Whole-home projects

For fixers and forever homes alike. Full-home updates cover the surfaces and systems that make a house feel new again, phased so you can stay in the home where possible.

  • Flooring, paint, and drywall
  • Doors, trim, and millwork
  • Lighting and fixture updates
  • Phased scheduling that fits your life

05General contracting

Already have plans, or need someone to run the whole show? We manage permits, inspections, scheduling, and licensed trades under one contract and one phone number.

  • Permits and inspections
  • Trade scheduling and supervision
  • Budget tracking and updates
  • Final walkthrough and punch list
