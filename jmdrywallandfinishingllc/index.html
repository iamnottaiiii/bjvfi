Why drywall finishing matters

Anyone can hang a sheet of drywall. The finish is what you see every day. Good taping and mud work is the difference between walls that look flat and walls that show every seam under the light.

  • Taped seams that disappear under paint
  • Sanded smooth and ready for primer
  • Straight corners and clean lines
  • Job site kept tidy as we work
