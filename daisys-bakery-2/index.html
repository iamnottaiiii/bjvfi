Our Story

A family bakery, blooming in Chicago Heights.

Panaderia Daisy's Inc. is a family-owned bakery established in 1998, specializing in Mexican sweet bread and baked goods. For over twenty-five years, the family has been up before dawn, mixing, shaping, and baking the pan dulce that neighborhoods grow up on.

Daisy's Bakery 2 brings that tradition to Chicago Heights at 209 W 15th St. It is the same family recipes, the same early-morning ovens, and the same warm welcome, now closer to home for the Southland.

Regulars come for the conchas and stay for the personal touch: custom birthday cakes, cake pops shaped for parties, and bulk bread orders delivered ready for a crowd.
