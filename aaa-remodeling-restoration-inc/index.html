Our story

A Van Nuys crew with deep roots

Remodeling and restoration, done by people who show up

AAA Remodeling & Restoration Inc is a construction company based at 7105 Gerald Ave in Van Nuys, California. The crew takes on the projects other contractors avoid: full kitchen and bath remodels, room additions, and the messy middle of water and fire damage restoration.

The approach is simple. One team plans the work, one team does the work, and one person answers the phone. Homeowners deal with LD and the crew directly, from the first walkthrough to the final punch list, which is why reviewers keep mentioning how responsive and straightforward the process feels.

How we work

Three rules on every job

Protect the home first

Dust barriers, floor protection, and daily cleanup, because most clients are living in the house while the work happens.
