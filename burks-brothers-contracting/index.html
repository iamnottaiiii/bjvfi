OUR STORY

A family trade, carried forward

The Burks name has been on Northern Kentucky job sites for decades. This is how the next generation picked up the tools.

Watching their father build

"Watching our father work showed us the passion that he had for the company that he built over time. That passion was something truly amazing to see in his eyes."

That is Danny Burks, describing what he learned from his father Tim Burks, owner of Tim Burks Builder, Inc. Tim is well known for building European-style homes throughout Northern Kentucky.

When Chip and Danny decided they shared that same passion for residential construction, starting their own company was, in Danny's words, "a no-brainer." Burks Brothers' Contracting was born in Fort Thomas, offering remodeling, additions, and new construction.

PHOTO SOON

Making their mark in Fort Thomas

The brothers are known in Fort Thomas for remodels, additions, and tearing down older properties to build new homes on the same lot. A recent job on Sterling Ave. is a prime example: the owner chose to demolish the former home and build new on the same lot.

It is the same hands-on philosophy their father practiced: stay close to every job, care about the client, and build something that lasts.
