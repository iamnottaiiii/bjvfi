About Broad Street Auto Center

Broad Street Auto Center has served Newark drivers from its Broad Street location for over 20 years. The shop provides general auto repair and maintenance with the steadiness that comes from decades in the business.

We are a auto repair shop serving Newark and the surrounding Essex County communities. Whether it is routine maintenance or an unexpected breakdown, our goal is the same: get you back on the road safely, with work you can trust and a bill you understand.
