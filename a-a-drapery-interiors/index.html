The atelier

Everything starts with the fabric

For more than four decades, our Glendale shop has helped neighbors choose cloth, color, and finish for the rooms they live in. This is how a project moves through the atelier.

Step 01

Choose in the shop

Browse bolts of drapery and upholstery fabric in person at 125 Carr Dr. Hold the cloth up to the light, feel the weight, and take swatches home.

Step 02

Measure and plan

Windows, rods, and hardware are measured so panels hang at the right length and fullness for each room.

Step 03

Made for your windows

Custom draperies are cut and sewn to your measurements, with linings, pleats, and hems finished to order.

Step 04

Finish the room

Complete the look with home decor furnishings, mirrors, blinds, and shades from the same shop.

Our story

Forty four years on Carr Drive

A A Drapery Interiors has served Glendale as a drapery, fabric, and home furnishings shop for 44 years, listed among the area's interior designers, decorators, and window treatment specialists.

Then

A neighborhood workroom

The shop grew up around custom drapery: measuring windows, cutting cloth, and sewing panels made for the homes around Glendale.

Now

Fabric, blinds, and decor

Today the store pairs its drapery workroom roots with a fabric store selection, blinds and shades, home decor furnishings, and mirrors.

"The kind of shop where you come in for a curtain rod and leave with a whole room planned."
