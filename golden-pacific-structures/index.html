About Golden Pacific Structures

A greenhouse manufacturer whose gothic arch designs earned a place in research trials and trade press across the country.

A specialist in cold structures

Golden Pacific Structures grew up as a manufacturer of cold structure greenhouses in Redlands, California. Its signature was the gothic arch: square steel tubing, tall interiors, and kit designs that growers could assemble themselves. The company's structures appear in university research, including a high tunnel organic vegetable trial in Tennessee and a solar array study at the University of Arizona's Controlled Environment Agriculture Center.

Part of greenhouse history

In January 2013, Rough Brothers Inc. of Cincinnati announced it would absorb the Golden Pacific product line, folding the GPS 5000 and 6000 series of hoop and gutter connect products into its commercial greenhouse division alongside the XS Smith line. Rough Brothers was later acquired by Gibraltar Industries in 2015 and rebranded as Prospiant in May 2021.
