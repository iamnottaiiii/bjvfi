Our story

Genesis Auto Repair is an independent auto repair shop on Fullerton Road in Springfield, Virginia. No corporate script, no upsell playbook. Just technicians who fix cars and talk straight about what your car needs.

We work on all makes and models of cars and trucks, use quality certified parts, and hold ourselves to one standard: the car should leave running better than it arrived, and you should understand exactly what was done and why.

Our customers keep us honest. Their reviews mention timely work, polite service, and prices that feel right. That reputation is the whole business, and we protect it on every single job.
