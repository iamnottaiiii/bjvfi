The shop

Built for comfort,
focused on craft

Visions Barbershop sits on Lakeside Blvd in Owings Mills, inside the Lakeside Village area. It is a modern shop: clean, comfortable, with leather seating and big screens to enjoy while you wait.

Reviews describe a crew of knowledgeable, professional barbers and a family atmosphere that regulars appreciate. Walk-ins are part of the shop's rhythm, and the team takes the time to do the cut right.

Cash is accepted, and it is always smart to call ahead about other payment options and today's hours.
