Services

Treatment begins with a physical examination, review of your medical history and lifestyle, and a clear diagnosis. Your chiropractor then builds a personalized plan around your condition. The adjustment techniques used in this office, as listed in professional directories, are noted below.

Technique

Diversified Adjustments

The most widely used hands-on chiropractic method, applying precise manual thrusts to restore alignment and motion in the spine and joints.

Technique

Cox Flexion-Distraction

A gentle, non-force technique using a specialized table, often used for disc-related conditions, sciatica, and low back pain.

Technique

Thompson Method

A drop-table technique that uses the table sections to assist the adjustment, allowing for lighter-force corrections.

Technique

Activator Methods

An instrument-assisted technique delivering controlled, low-force impulses, a comfortable option for patients who prefer gentler adjustments.

Evaluation

Spinal Examination and X-ray Review

A thorough first visit includes a physical exam to locate the root cause of pain, review of any imaging, and discussion of your history before any treatment begins.

Conditions

What Patients Come In For

Typical concerns addressed in a chiropractic office include back pain, neck pain, headaches and migraines, sciatica, whiplash and auto injuries, sports injuries, fibromyalgia, and repetitive strain. Call the office to discuss whether your condition is a good fit for care.
