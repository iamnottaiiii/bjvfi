Our story

A Dundalk crew with two specialties

East Painting Service and Dryer Vent Cleaning Services started with a simple observation: the same homeowners who needed a fresh coat of paint were also asking who could clean their dryer vents. So we built one crew that does both, and does both well.

On the painting side, we handle interior rooms, full exteriors, drywall repair and texture finishing. On the airflow side, we clean dryer vents and air ducts and inspect every vent run we touch. Customers tell us they notice the difference right away: rooms that look new, dryers that run faster, air that feels cleaner.

"They showed me how much lint and dust had built up inside the vents, then cleaned it all out. My dryer works better and the air feels cleaner."

We are based at 7008 5th Ave in Dundalk and work across the surrounding neighborhoods. Reviewers mention our punctuality, our fair prices and the way we explain every step before we start. That is the whole business model: show up on time, do thorough work, charge a fair price.
