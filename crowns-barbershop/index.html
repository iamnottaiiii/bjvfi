Our Story

A neighborhood shop built on craft

Crowns Barbershop brings old school barbering to Huntington Beach: skilled hands, honest prices, and a shop that feels like home.

The shop

Classic bones, good energy

Step inside and you will find wood paneling, art on the walls, a comfortable couch, and classic movies or old school music playing in the background. It is the kind of place where conversation comes easy and regulars are on a first name basis with the barbers.

The shop sits on Edinger Ave with easy parking out front and extra spots in the rear, and it welcomes walk-ins alongside booked appointments.

Placeholder image
Placeholder image
The barbers

A crew that takes pride in the chair

Reviewers name barbers like Joshua, Eren, and Lorenzo for their skill, patience, and conversation. Clients describe barbers who listen to exactly what they want, take their time, and never rush a cut, even close to closing time.

From first timers nervous about going short to regulars who drive in from across Orange County, the chairs at Crowns are open to everyone.

The promise

Leave feeling like royalty

The name says it all. Every client gets the full treatment: a careful consultation, a precise cut, and a finish with clean lines. Reviewers say they leave feeling amazing, and many will not trust their hair to anyone else.
