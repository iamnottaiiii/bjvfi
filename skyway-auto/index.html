About

A small shop with a big reputation

Skyway Auto keeps a small footprint on Pulaski Street and lets the work do the talking.

Skyway Auto sits at 132 Pulaski St in Newark's Ironbound, in the shadow of the landmark the neighborhood is known for: the Pulaski Skyway, the elevated highway that has carried traffic over this part of the city for generations. Public business listings name David Rei as the owner.

The shop is listed as a general automotive repair and maintenance business, with auto parts and accessories also in the mix. It keeps weekday hours, Monday through Friday, 9 to 5, and that is about the extent of its public footprint. There is no big website, no online booking, no published price list.

What it does have is a 5.0 rating across 4 Google reviews, and the reviews all hum the same tune: fair prices, fast help when something breaks, and work you can trust. One reviewer writes that Bidu, the mechanic, handles cars for her whole family and makes things happen quickly even in emergencies.

Another writes that she felt safe bringing her car in as a woman and would recommend the shop to anyone afraid of being ripped off by other mechanics. In a business where trust is everything, that is the review that matters most.
