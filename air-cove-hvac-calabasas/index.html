Services

Heating, cooling, and everything between

Typical HVAC services for Calabasas homes. Expand each item for details, then call (808) 219-8524 to confirm availability and get a quote.

When the AC blows warm air, short-cycles, or stops altogether, a technician diagnoses the cause and repairs it. Common fixes include:

  • Refrigerant leak detection and recharge
  • Capacitor, contactor, and fan motor replacement
  • Thermostat troubleshooting
  • Electrical and control board issues

Furnaces and heat pumps get inspected and repaired so your home stays warm on cold nights. Service commonly covers:

  • Furnace ignition and burner issues
  • Heat pump reversing valve and defrost problems
  • Airflow and ductwork checks
  • Carbon monoxide safety checks on gas systems

When a system is past its prime, a new unit is sized and installed to match your home. The process generally includes:

  • Load calculation for correct sizing
  • Equipment selection and efficiency options
  • Removal and responsible disposal of old equipment
  • Startup testing and walkthrough

Seasonal tune-ups keep efficiency up and breakdowns down. A typical visit includes:

  • Coil cleaning and filter check
  • Refrigerant and electrical inspection
  • Drain line clearing
  • Performance and safety testing

Cleaner air through better filtration and ventilation. Options often include:

  • High-efficiency media filters
  • UV lights and air purifiers
  • Humidity control
  • Duct cleaning assessments

No cooling on a hot day or no heat on a cold night counts as urgent. Call (808) 219-8524 and describe the problem. Availability for after-hours calls varies, so calling is the fastest way to check.
