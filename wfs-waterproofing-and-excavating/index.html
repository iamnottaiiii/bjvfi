How we protect your home

Built like a dam, layer by layer

Every waterproofing job is a system of protection layers. Here is how WFS stacks them to keep water out of your basement or crawl space.

01

Excavation

We open the trench along your foundation the safe way, protecting your yard and utilities while we work.

03

Services

The protection-layer process

WFS pairs waterproofing with its own excavation crew, so one team handles the dig, the seal, and the finishing work. Here is how a typical job flows.

1

Free inspection and estimate

We look at where water is getting in and quote a solution matched to your property. No obligation, no pressure.

2

Safe excavation

Our own crew opens the trench along the foundation, working safely and efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily routine.

3

Seal and drain

Exterior waterproofing seals the outside of the foundation wall. Interior drainage channels catch anything that still gets through and route it to the sump.

4

Grade, backfill, and verify

Downspouts are routed away from the house, concrete is removed or finished as needed, and the site is left clean with water moving where it should.
