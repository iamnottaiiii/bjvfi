Services

The full picture, in three chapters

Each service below is a starting point. Call to confirm scope and pricing for your specific project.

01

Home Repairs

The running list every house builds up: sticking doors, cracked drywall, tired caulk, loose fixtures. We work through it efficiently, in as few visits as possible.

  • Drywall patching, crack repair, and texture matching
  • Door, hinge, handle, and lock adjustments
  • Caulking, weatherstripping, and minor leak stops
  • Carpentry touch-ups: trim, baseboards, and shelving
  • Furniture assembly and secure wall mounting
02

Painting

Paint is the fastest way to change how a home feels. We prep properly, which is what makes the finish last: sanding, patching, priming, then crisp topcoats.

  • Interior rooms, ceilings, and accent walls
  • Exterior siding, stucco, trim, and doors
  • Cabinet and built-in refinishing
  • Drywall repair and texture before paint
  • Color guidance for a cohesive whole-home palette
03

Heating and Cooling Support

Comfort work that keeps systems running well: filter changes, thermostat upgrades, vent cleaning, and seasonal checkups that catch small issues early.

  • Thermostat installation and smart upgrades
  • Vent and register cleaning for better airflow
  • Filter replacement schedules and reminders
  • Seasonal system checkups and minor adjustments
  • Insulation and sealing advice to hold temperature

How pricing works

Describe the job by phone, or send photos for a faster read. You approve the price before work begins. Sample service visit: $95, credited toward work booked the same day. Something not listed above? Ask, most household projects fit.

Our Story

Why "collaborative" is in the name

Home projects go wrong when trades work in isolation: the painter arrives before the drywall is fixed, the thermostat gets upgraded while the vents stay clogged. We started Collaborative Home Improvements to plan the whole job together with the homeowner, so each step sets up the next.

Good work is a conversation, not a transaction.

Based on Manzanita Ave in Pasadena, we serve nearby neighborhoods with repairs, painting, and heating and cooling support. Expect plain-language explanations, upfront pricing, and a tidy home when we leave.

Our process
The Process

Walk, plan, then work

1. Walkthrough. We look at the whole space with you and note everything, not just the headline job.

2. Plan and price. You get a clear order of work and an upfront price. You approve it before anything starts.

3. Work and review. We protect your home, do the work, clean up, and walk through the result together.
