Our story

A local electrician,
not a call center.

Mj Electric is an electrical contractor based in Covington, Kentucky, at 1819 Greenup St. We serve homeowners across Covington and Northern Kentucky with residential electrical work: repairs, upgrades, lighting, and everything in between.

We keep it simple. You call, a real person answers, and we show up when we say we will. We explain what is wrong in plain language, give you a price before we start, and treat your home like it is our own while we work.

A lot of our work is in the older homes that make this area great: knob-and-tube upgrades, panel replacements, and bringing aging wiring up to modern safety standards without tearing the character out of the house.

How we work

  • You approve the price first. Written estimate, no mystery fees.
  • We protect your home. Drop cloths, shoe covers, tidy work areas.
  • We test everything. Every circuit, outlet, and switch verified before we leave.
  • We stand behind it. If something we touched is not right, we come back and make it right.
