Our story

Chatsworth's fence yard
since 2002

Outdoor Living started with a simple idea: sell a vinyl fence that actually survives a Southern California summer, and install it like it is your own yard.

Why we are different

Made here,
made thicker

Our vinyl is produced locally in Los Angeles, not shipped in from the East Coast. It is thicker than the panels at Home Depot and Lowes, and the formulation is tuned for the hot Southwest sun, so it does not chalk, warp or fade the way thin imports do.

We back it with a lifetime guarantee, and we sell the same material two ways: installed by our crew, or direct from the yard for do-it-yourself builders.

A team you will know

Customers know our crew by name. Hutch runs the installs, Anita keeps the office humming, and the whole team is known for being friendly, knowledgeable and respectful of your property.

Local through and through

Based at 21730 Nordhoff St in Chatsworth, with materials produced in Los Angeles. When you call, you talk to someone who knows the Valley, not a call center.

Word of mouth

With a 5.0 star rating across 48+ public reviews, most of our work comes from neighbors telling neighbors. That only happens when the fence is straight and the yard is clean.
