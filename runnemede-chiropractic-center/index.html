II

Diversified Technique

About the Practice

✦

Dr. Kelly D. Kennedy, DC

Chiropractor

Dr. Kennedy graduated from Sherman College of Straight Chiropractic in 1983 and has practiced for more than 42 years. He is licensed in New Jersey and his documented adjusting techniques include Diversified and Toggle Recoil. Patients in public reviews describe him as knowledgeable, patient, friendly, and attentive, taking time to hear what is going on before adjusting.
