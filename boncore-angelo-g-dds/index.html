Our Story

A neighborhood practice on School Street, built on trust and straightforward care.

Dr.

Angelo G. Boncore, DDS

Dr. Angelo Guy Boncore is a general practice dentist serving Marblehead, Massachusetts from his office at 37 School Street. He graduated from Tufts University and holds Massachusetts dental license number 10417.

His approach to dentistry is simple and steady: know your patients, explain every option honestly, and do careful work that holds up. As the primary dental care provider for patients of all ages, he takes responsibility for the diagnosis, treatment, management, and overall coordination of each patient's oral health needs.

New patients are welcome, and patients can call (781) 631-7950 to book an appointment or simply walk in.
