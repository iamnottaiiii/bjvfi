Our story

A Pacoima shop built for stone

CG Tile & Marble Inc. operates from a 16,000 square foot, state of the art facility at 13571 Desmond Street in Pacoima, CA, serving the greater Los Angeles and Santa Barbara area.

We are a full service tile contractor: tiling service, masonry, kitchen and bath work, and granite and marble supply, all handled by our own staff and crews.

Our work has appeared in published architecture, including stonework with Belzberg Architects on The Gores Group headquarters in Beverly Hills. The same standard applies to a single bathroom as to a headquarters lobby.

We are even available to you years down the line for the restoration and maintenance of your stonework.
