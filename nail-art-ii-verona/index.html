About

Nail Art II on Bloomfield Avenue in Verona is a highly rated nail salon known for friendly, welcoming service and technicians who are perfectionists about detail. Guests report manicures and pedicures that last four weeks or more without chipping, with great availability and quick service.

The business-verified service list on Yelp covers manicures, manicure and pedicure combos, nail fill-ins, polish changes, and polish removal, plus waxing. The salon is by appointment, accepts credit cards, and is wheelchair accessible.
