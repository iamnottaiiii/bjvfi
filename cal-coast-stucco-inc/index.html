Services

Six specialties, one shop

Services below are taken from the company's own business listing descriptions, plus its trade listing. For scope, pricing, and scheduling, call for a written estimate.

Metal Lath & Plaster

Full three coat plaster systems over metal lath for new walls, additions, and full re-stuccos. The foundation every finish depends on.

General Sheet Metal

Flashing, drip edges, and sheet metal details that keep water out from behind the plaster. Listed as a core line of business.

Manufactured Stone

Stone veneer accents for entryways, columns, and facades, installed to pair cleanly with stucco finishes.

Plaster Restoration

Crack repair, patching, and restoration of aging or damaged stucco, matched to the existing texture and profile.

Venetian Plaster

Polished, marble-like interior and exterior plaster finishes for a high end look on feature walls and facades.

Arcustone

Arcustone decorative finishes, one of the specialty product lines the company lists in its business profile.

Good to know: service descriptions above come from public business listings, not from a published price list. Pricing for stucco work depends on square footage, condition of the existing walls, and finish choice. Call (818) 767-0115 for a written estimate.
Our story

24 years of plaster
in Los Angeles

Cal Coast Stucco. Inc is a Sun Valley based stucco and plaster contractor serving Los Angeles County, listed in the trade Blue Book since 2017.

The shop

Built around the trade

Business listings describe the company as a metal lath and plaster specialist with sheet metal capability in house, a combination that matters because flashing and plaster have to work together to keep walls dry.

The operation runs out of 10932 Tuxford St in Sun Valley, with crews fanning out across Los Angeles County. Public employee reviews describe an early morning roll call, crews of five or six, and a friendly, hardworking shop culture.
