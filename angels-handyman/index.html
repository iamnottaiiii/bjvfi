Our Story

A Canoga Park handyman with drywall in his DNA.

The name

Angel’s handyman

Based at 7115 Milwood Ave in Canoga Park, Angel’s handyman is a local drywall and handyman service serving the San Fernando Valley. The workday runs 7 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week, because wall damage and punch lists do not keep office hours.

The approach

Walk the job first

Every job starts with a walkthrough: understand what the owner needs, note the details, and confirm the plan before tools come out. The goal is simple: repairs that disappear into the wall and a home that looks cared for.

The specialty

Drywall done smooth

Drywall is a trade of patience: hang it straight, tape it tight, feather the mud, and match the texture. That is the core of the business, and it shows in walls that need no second look.

The promise

One call, handled

No call centers and no runaround. Call (805) 409-6115, talk to the person doing the work, and get the job scheduled.
