About the Shop

Classics Barber Shop is a neighborhood barbershop at 649 Stevenson Rd in Erlanger, Kentucky, built around the old school way of doing things: honest cuts, hot towel shaves, and a chair that feels like home.

The shop serves Erlanger and the surrounding Northern Kentucky community with traditional barbering: classic haircuts, beard trims, buzz cuts, and straight razor finishes. Clients come for the cut and stay for the conversation.

Public listings show the shop's barbers earning strong reviews for professionalism and quality cuts, with many clients calling it the best haircut they have had.
