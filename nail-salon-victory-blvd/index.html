Our Story

An Old School Salon on Victory Blvd

Nail Salon at 73 Victory Blvd is a longtime neighborhood nail salon in Tompkinsville, Staten Island. Reviewers describe an older, no frills shop run by longtime owners, where workers are kind, guests are treated like family, and the work is great at cheap prices. Guests mention techs by name: Lisa for eyebrows that reviewers call some of the best they have had, and Carl for nail designs he takes his time perfecting. One reviewer says the owner once walked a customer to her car and buckled her in so she would not mess up her nails.
