Our Story

An Old Torrance
institution.

Top Barber has been part of the Old Torrance landscape for years, welcoming the neighborhood into its chairs at 1119 Sartori Ave. City of Torrance records welcomed the shop to the Downtown Redevelopment Project Area, and it has been a fixture of the block ever since.

The shop's recent remodel gave it a fresh face: clean stations, a comfortable waiting area, and the same crew regulars trust. Customers describe a welcoming atmosphere where the barbers are both skilled and personable.

The team is the heart of it. Barbers like Nathan, Kevin, and Tyler have earned loyal followings, praised for quality haircuts, hot towel treatments, and careful shaving work. Men, women, and kids all take a seat here.

Friendly, knowledgeable, and the cuts speak for themselves. I have been coming for years.
