Our story

A family bakery in the heart of downtown Rensselaer

Bienvenidos to La Trinidad

La Trinidad Bakery was welcomed to Rensselaer's downtown district with a Chamber of Commerce ribbon cutting, and it has been a local favorite ever since. The bakery is owned and operated by Rocio Martinez Cruz and Oscar Nunez, who bake Mexican style breads, desserts and birthday cakes fresh every day.

Walk in and you will smell the conchas before you see them. The case holds pan dulce, donuts and custom cakes, while the counter serves agua frescas, horchata, mangonadas, hand dipped ice cream and elotes. Reviewers call it a hidden gem with kind, generous staff and prices that keep the whole family coming back.

Whether you grew up with pan dulce or you are tasting your first concha, there is a warm welcome waiting at 209 W Harrison St.
