About

About RoyalStone Bath Co.

A bathroom remodeling company based on Huntington Drive in San Marino, serving the San Gabriel Valley with focused, craft driven bath work.

One trade, done well

We specialize in bathrooms rather than doing a little of everything. That focus means our crews know the details that make or break a bath remodel: proper waterproofing behind the tile, accurate slopes on shower floors, clean grout lines, and finishes that age gracefully.

Homeowners in San Marino, Pasadena, and the surrounding neighborhoods call us when they want a bathroom that looks timeless and is built to last. Every project starts with a conversation and a written estimate, so you always know what is happening and what it costs.
