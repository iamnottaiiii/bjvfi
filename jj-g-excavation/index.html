Our story

A local excavation crew for the Valley

J,J & G Excavation is an excavating contractor based in the San Fernando Valley, serving Pacoima, Sylmar, and the surrounding Los Angeles neighborhoods.

The work is the kind you cannot fake: moving earth to exact grade, cutting clean trenches, and setting up septic and leach drain fields that perform for decades. We approach every dig with the same discipline, from a single trench to a full site cut.

As a small local operation, you talk directly to the people doing the work. No runaround, no surprises, just groundwork you can build on.
