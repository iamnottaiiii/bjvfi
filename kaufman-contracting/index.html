Our Story

A Burbank contractor with a builder's eye and a neighbor's standards

Kaufman Contracting is a kitchen remodeling contractor based on South Glenoaks Boulevard in Burbank, California. We focus on one room and do it well: the kitchen, the hardest working room in the house.

Most of our work comes from Burbank and the surrounding San Fernando Valley neighborhoods, where mid century floor plans meet modern cooking. We have learned what those homes hide behind their walls, and we plan for it before demolition day.

Our approach is simple. We listen before we draw. We price what we draw. We protect your home while we build, keep the jobsite clean, and communicate in plain language every step of the way. When the last outlet cover goes on, we walk the kitchen with you, punch list in hand, until you are satisfied.

If you are comparing contractors, we encourage you to ask us anything: about our process, our timeline, our materials, or our past work. A good remodel starts with honest answers.
