Our story

A neighborhood garage, done right

Auriemma's Auto Repair LLC is an independent shop on Tague Road in Troy, NY, serving drivers from Troy, Schaghticoke, and the surrounding Capital Region.

Why independent matters

Big chains and dealerships answer to quotas. An independent shop answers to its neighbors. When the person who fixes your car also knows your name, the work gets done differently: no inflated estimates, no mystery charges, no repairs you did not ask for.

That is the shop we run. Bring your car in, tell us what it is doing, and we will tell you straight what it needs. If we cannot help, we will tell you that too and point you somewhere that can.
