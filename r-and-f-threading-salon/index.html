R & F Threading Salon is a Brooklyn beauty studio built on precision, warmth and trust.

the short version

A craft,
not a chore

Threading is one of the oldest hair-removal arts in the world, and R & F Threading Salon practices it like a craft: careful, exact and personal.

Clients describe the studio as their favorite place to go, a place where the team feels like family. Some have been coming for many years, and a few travel from as far as Staten Island just to have their brows done here.

That kind of loyalty does not happen by accident. It comes from consistent, excellent work, visit after visit, and from making every client feel welcome the moment they walk in.
