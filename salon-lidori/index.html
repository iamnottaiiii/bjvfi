Our story

A neighborhood salon
with high standards

Salon Lidori is a full-service beauty salon on Bradley Ave in Staten Island, where every appointment gets the same care we would give our own family.

Every great hair day starts with listening. At Salon Lidori, your appointment begins with a real conversation: what you love about your hair, what drives you crazy, and how much time you actually want to spend on it in the morning.

From there, our stylists build a plan around you. Cuts are shaped to your face and texture, not copied from a chart. Color is mixed for your skin tone and placed with intention. And if something will not work for your hair type or your lifestyle, we will tell you honestly before we begin.

We keep the atmosphere warm and unpretentious. Come as you are, bring a photo or just a feeling, and leave with hair that feels unmistakably yours.

We do not do rushed hair. We do your hair, the way it should be done.

The Salon Lidori team

Honest consultations

We will tell you what your hair can and cannot do, and map the path to get you where you want to be.

Healthy hair first

Color and chemical services are planned around the integrity of your hair, never at its expense.

Styling you can repeat

You leave knowing how to recreate your look at home, with product advice that fits your routine.
