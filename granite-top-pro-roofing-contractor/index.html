Our story

A Sylmar roofer with a simple standard

Granite Top Pro Roofing Contractor is a roofing company based on Eldridge Avenue in Sylmar, serving homes and small businesses across the San Fernando Valley.

Our name says what we aim for: work as solid as granite, done with a pro's discipline. That means honest inspections first. If your roof has five good years left, we will tell you that instead of selling you a new one.

When replacement is the right call, we install complete systems, not just shingles: tear off, underlayment, flashing, ventilation and cleanup, all in writing before work begins.

  • Free inspections with photos
  • Written quotes before any work
  • Licensed and insured work practices, ask us for details
