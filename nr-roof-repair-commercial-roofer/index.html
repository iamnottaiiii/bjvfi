Our Services

Roofing services tailored to commercial properties: repairs, installations, and maintenance that keep your building protected.

Targeted repairs for leaks, membrane damage, punctures, and flashing problems on commercial roof systems. We diagnose the source and repair it properly, not just patch the symptom.

New roof installations for commercial buildings. We walk you through material and system options so you can pick what fits your building, your budget, and your timeline.

When a roof is past repair, we replace it. Full tear-off and replacement service with attention to drainage, flashing, and the details that make a roof last.

Scheduled inspections and maintenance programs for property managers and building owners. Regular checkups extend roof life and help you plan instead of react.

  • Routine roof inspections
  • Drain and gutter clearing
  • Sealant and flashing checks
  • Written condition reports

Water shows up far from where it gets in. We trace leaks to their source with a careful, methodical inspection of the full roof assembly.

Storm damage and sudden leaks cannot wait. Call us and we will get out to assess the damage and stop the water as fast as the schedule allows.

Not sure what your roof needs? A quick call is the fastest way to find out.

About Us

A local roofing contractor focused on doing commercial roof work right.

Who We Are

NR Roof Repair & Commercial Roofer is a roofing contractor based at 777 Terrace Ave in Hasbrouck Heights, New Jersey. Our work centers on commercial properties: the warehouses, offices, and retail buildings whose roofs protect real businesses underneath.

We keep it straightforward. Show up when we say we will, explain what we find in plain language, and do the work the way it should be done.

What We Do

Our team specializes in three things: repairs, installations, and maintenance. That focus means every job gets the attention of people who work on roofs like yours every day. From a single leak to a full replacement, the goal is the same: a roof you do not have to think about.

When We Are Open
