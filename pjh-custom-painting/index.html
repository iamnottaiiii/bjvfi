The process

Straightforward from the first call

1. Free estimate

Call and describe the project. Paul comes out, looks at the work in person, and gives you a fair written price.

2. Prep comes first

Washing, scraping, patching, sanding, caulking, priming. The part you do not see is the part that lasts.

3. Clean painting

Crisp lines, even coverage, protected floors and furniture. We stay on the job until it is finished.

4. Final walkthrough

We walk the whole project with you and touch up anything that is not perfect before we call it done.

5. Repeat customers

Many of our customers have used us for years and call us back room by room. That is the goal every time.

6. Fair and insured

Reasonably priced, fully insured, registered with the State of New Jersey. No surprises, no shortcuts.

Services

Everything a painted
surface needs

From a single room to a whole building, and every bit of prep in between. Free estimates on all of it.

Residential interior painting

Rooms, hallways, ceilings, trim and doors. We help you pick colors and sheens that fit the light and the life of each room, then deliver a smooth, even finish.

Residential exterior painting

Full exterior repaints: siding, trim, doors, shutters and porches. Power washed, scraped, caulked and primed before the topcoats go on.

Commercial painting Interior + exterior

Offices, storefronts, rentals and multi unit properties. Evening and weekend scheduling available so your business keeps running.

Pressure washing

Houses, siding, decks, fences and driveways. The right first step before exterior paint, and a great refresh all on its own.

Drywall repair

Dents, holes, cracks, nail pops and bad seams. Patched, taped, sanded and primed so the repair vanishes under paint.

Popcorn ceiling removal

We remove dated popcorn texture, skim and sand the ceiling smooth, then paint it bright. Rooms feel taller and newer instantly.
