HOW WE WORK

No shortcuts.
No drama.

A paint job is only as good as the prep under it. That is the whole philosophy: mask it, patch it, prime it, then lay down color that stays.

We are a small crew, which means the person who quotes your job is on site doing the work. Fewer handoffs, fewer surprises, better edges.

  • Drop cloths, masking, and full site protection
  • Quality paints, matched to your color
  • Walkthrough at the end, touch-ups on the spot
