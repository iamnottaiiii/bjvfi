Nail Salon, Halethorpe MD

Calm, clean, and carefully done

DNA Nails on Sulphur Spring Road is a cozy neighborhood salon known for fair prices, friendly techs, and nails done right.

Under new ownership since 2019

Since May 2019, DNA Nails has built a loyal following with a calm atmosphere, soul music in the background, and technicians who take suggestions seriously. Appointments are preferred, and walk-ins are served with minimal wait.

What we do best

Manicures, pedicures, gel, acrylics, dip powder, and detailed nail art, plus eyebrow services. Simple pedicure options, honest prices, and a team that wants you to leave happy.
