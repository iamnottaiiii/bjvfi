About Net Quest Services, Inc.

A Cincinnati-area contractor with roots going back to 2005, built on self-performed work and long relationships with owners and general contractors.

Who We Are

Net Quest Services, Inc. is a specialty trades and full-service subcontractor serving all of Greater Cincinnati and adjacent regions. Since our founding in 2005, our goal has been to provide safe, cost-effective, innovative solutions to the commercial construction industry.

We work across heavy/civil and building projects, from highways and earthwork to finished carpentry and building finishes. Our crews are experienced in the demands of occupied facilities, including hospitals, medical centers, universities, and government buildings.
