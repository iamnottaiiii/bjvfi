Why it matters

A dirty or damaged chimney is not just ugly. It is unsafe.

Creosote buildup, cracked crowns, missing caps, and crumbling mortar let water in and heat out, and in the worst case they let smoke and carbon monoxide back into your home. That is why annual sweeps and honest inspections matter.

  • Sweeps and safety inspections that catch problems early
  • Same-day help for smoke backing up into the house
  • Full rebuilds and restoration, not just patch jobs

Chimney Repairs

Cracked crowns, spalling brick, damaged flashing, leaning stacks. We repair the failure points that let water destroy a chimney from the inside out.

Partial & Full Rebuilds

When repair is throwing good money after bad, we rebuild, from the roofline up or from the ground, matched to your home.

Caps, Crowns & Liners

Chimney caps keep rain and critters out. Sound crowns shed water. Sound liners keep flue gases where they belong. We install and replace all three.

Waterproofing

Our Story

A family trade, four decades deep

Doker Chimney & Masonry is the chimney and masonry arm of Doker Brick Contractor, Inc., a company that has been working brick in Erlanger since 1980.

Brick first. Chimneys naturally followed.

Owner Larry Doker built the company on masonry fundamentals: proper mortar, plumb walls, and work that outlives the warranty. Chimneys were the natural next step, because nobody understands a chimney stack like the masons who could build it from scratch.

That is still the difference today. When we rebuild a chimney or repoint a flue, we are not guessing at masonry, it is the trade we grew up in.

According to BBB records, the business dates to 1980, making it one of the longest-running masonry outfits in the Erlanger area. The Angi listing notes 45 years of experience and free estimates, and every one of its six reviews is five stars.
