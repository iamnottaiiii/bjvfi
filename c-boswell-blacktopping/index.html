How a job runs

No mystery, no upsell. This is the standard paving sequence for a typical job.

01

Walk the site

We look at the grade, drainage, and base, then talk through options and give you a free, plain-language estimate.

02

Prep + pave

Old surface is prepped or removed, base is graded and compacted, then fresh asphalt goes down and gets rolled smooth.

03

Cure + seal

After the mat cures, sealcoating (when requested) locks out water and sun so the surface holds its finish for years.
