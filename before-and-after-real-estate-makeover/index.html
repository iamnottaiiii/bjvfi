Our story

Every home has an after photo waiting

We started Before & After Real Estate Makeover on a simple idea: most homes do not need to be torn down, they need to be fixed up.

Why we exist

A makeover mindset for Florence homes

Walk through any neighborhood in Florence and you will see it: solid houses hiding behind tired paint, worn floors and a punch list that never got punched. That is where we come in.

We are a local remodeling and handyman crew, not a big-box contractor. We take the time to look at a home the way a buyer would, or the way you will five years from now, and we fix what matters most first.

For sellers, that means a home that photographs better, shows better and appraises with fewer headaches. For homeowners staying put, it means finally loving the place you live in again.
