Complete interior build outs

Turnkey construction from an empty shell to a finished, code compliant space. Compass Point acts as your single general contractor, scheduling every trade and keeping the jobsite clean and professional.

  • Partition framing and drywall finishing
  • Flooring, ceilings and paint systems
  • Permit coordination and code compliance
  • Single point of contact from kickoff to punch list

Retail, hospitality and medical

Our story

Pointed in the right direction since 1993.

Compass Point Contracting Inc has operated as a general contractor out of Pasadena, Maryland for over three decades, serving commercial clients across Maryland, Delaware, Virginia and the Washington DC metropolitan area.

1993

Founded in Maryland

Compass Point Contracting Inc begins work as a general contractor focused on commercial interiors, tenant construction and office build outs.

2000s

National retail rollouts

The company performs complete interior build outs for well known national chain stores and high end retail department stores, developing its specialty in fixture mounting and custom installations.

Today

Full service commercial GC

Medical facilities, hospitality spaces and retail clients rely on Compass Point for drywall, electrical, HVAC, data cabling, carpentry, cabinetry and landscaping, all under one contract.

How we work.

Professional conduct, professional appearance and clean working areas throughout the project. That is the standard Compass Point holds on every jobsite, whether it is a single retail bay or a full medical facility.

Where we work.

Maryland, Delaware, Virginia and the Washington DC metropolitan area. Industry standards, local codes and required permits are never overlooked, wherever the project sits.
