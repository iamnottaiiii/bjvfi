Our story Sample

Every cookie has a story. Ours starts the way all good bakery stories do: with a kitchen that always smelled like butter and vanilla, and a belief that a cookie should never be boring.

Red Thread Cookies grew out of that kitchen into a little Palos Park shop where the case is full by morning and the custom orders keep the piping bags busy. The name comes from the old idea of the red thread: an invisible cord that ties people to the ones they are meant to meet.

We tie every box with a red thread, because cookies are meant to connect people.

Birthdays, thank-yous, new neighbors, tough weeks. If there is a reason to show up for someone, there is a cookie for it, and we would love to bake it.

Why the red thread?

In folklore, a red thread ties together people who are destined to meet, no matter how tangled life gets. We liked the idea so much we made it our signature: every gift box leaves the shop tied with a real red thread.

It is our little promise that what is inside was made with care, by hand, for someone you care about.
