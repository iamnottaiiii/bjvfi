About

A neighborhood shop that says thanks

"Thank you for being part of new haircut barbershop."

New haircutbarbersho Inc sits at 1905 W Allen St in Allentown, and it runs on a simple idea: treat every person in the chair like a regular.

The shop is largely staffed with bilingual barbers who are skilled at their job, so walk-in customers can get a great haircut without a long wait, especially in the morning.

Cleanliness is a point of pride here. The whole team keeps the shop spotless, and free parking next door makes every visit easy.

Customers recommend the first chair for a full-service cut and the second chair on the right for design work. Come by and find your chair.
