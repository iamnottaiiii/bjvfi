Services

Every service below is work the Weber family has done for decades. If your project needs something adjacent, call and ask. If we cannot do it right, we will tell you.

01

Grading and Site Preparation

Rough grading to shape the land, finish grading to bring it to exact elevation. Proper grading controls drainage, supports foundations, and keeps water away from structures. We grade residential lots, commercial pads, and roadways, and we check our work against the plan before we leave.

Best forNew construction pads, lot leveling, drainage correction, driveway and roadway subgrade.
02

Excavation

Foundation digs, footings, utility trenches, and mass excavation. Tight urban lots and hillside sites are our home turf in Burbank and the surrounding hills. Our operators know how to dig to the line without disturbing what stays.

Best forFoundations, additions, retaining walls, utility trenches, swimming pool digs.
03

Demolition

Selective and full demolition of structures, concrete removal, and site clearing. We take buildings down in a controlled sequence, separate materials, and haul debris so the site is left clean and ready for the next phase.

Best forStructure removal, concrete and asphalt removal, interior strip-outs, site clearing.

Trucking and Hauling

Our own fleet moves dirt, base material, aggregate, and debris. Running our own trucks means our schedule does not depend on a third party, and your export and import quantities stay honest, load by load.

Our Story

From Germany to Burbank, one machine at a time.

In 1962, Joe Weber Sr. emigrated to Southern California from Germany with a dream: to build a heavy equipment grading and trucking business. He started with his hands, his work ethic, and a belief that a machine in the right hands could shape the land itself.

Over the following decades, Joe Weber Equipment and Excavating grew into one of the most widely trusted construction services businesses in the Southland. The company accumulated a near-perfected knowledge of heavy equipment grading work that spans every segment of the trade.

Only after learning all facets of the excavation services industry did Joe Sr. hand the boys the keys to the business. Company history

In the eighties and nineties, Joe introduced his sons Leo and Joe Weber Jr. to the family business and instilled in them a commitment to excellence. Nothing was handed over until both sons had learned every facet of the excavation services industry from the seat of a machine.
