Every service, in plain words

What we do, and the signs that tell you it is time to call.

1

Shingle roof replacement

When repair stops making sense, we strip the roof to the deck and install a fresh asphalt shingle system. Typical signs: widespread curling, missing shingles, or granules filling your gutters.

2

Roof repair

Leaks around chimneys and valleys, storm-lifted shingles, and isolated damage. Fixed early, a repair can buy your roof years.

3

Roof inspections

Buying a home, filing a claim, or just overdue for a look? We inspect, photograph, and tell you straight what your roof needs, and what it does not.

4

Roof sealant

Protective sealant applications that help an aging but sound roof shed water longer. A smart middle step between repairs and replacement.

Prices vary by roof size, pitch, and condition, so every job gets a written estimate after we see your roof. No phone-ballpark surprises.

Our story

A family trade,
since 1974

James W. Smith & Sons has been roofing homes in the Cincinnati tri-state for over fifty years, and it is still family-run today.

One crew. One name. Fifty-two years.

The business started in 1974 as a sole proprietorship in Newport, Kentucky, with owner Donald Smith at the helm. There was no marketing department, no call center, just a crew, a truck, and roofs that needed doing right.

In 2014 the company earned BBB accreditation and an A+ rating, a standing it has kept ever since. That kind of record does not come from advertising. It comes from showing up, doing the work, and leaving the yard immaculate.

Today the name on the truck still means what it always has: a family business that treats your roof like its reputation depends on it, because it does.
