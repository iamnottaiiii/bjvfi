About

Meet David O'neal

A West Virginia native who has made Oxon Hill his dental home for more than three decades.

Illustration

His story

David O'neal graduated from the West Virginia University School of Dentistry in 1990, where he received first rate training in all aspects of general dentistry. He then served for two years as a dental officer in the United States Navy.

In 1993 he joined an Oxon Hill practice as an associate dentist, and shortly after acquired the practice from the retiring Dr. Abraham Dekelbaum. He has cared for the community ever since.

He is licensed by the Dental Board of Maryland and is a member of the American Dental Association, the Maryland State Dental Association and the Southern Maryland Dental Society.
