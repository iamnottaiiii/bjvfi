Services

What we can do for your smile

The treatments below reflect common general dentistry services. Availability varies by office, so call (703) 256-1183 to confirm before your visit.

Preventive Dentistry

Regular preventive visits catch small problems before they become big ones.

  • Dental exams and checkups
  • Professional teeth cleanings
  • Digital X-rays
  • Fluoride treatments and sealants
  • Oral cancer screenings

Restorative Dentistry

Repair damage and rebuild function with durable, natural-looking solutions.

  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Dental crowns and bridges
  • Root canal therapy
  • Dental implants
  • Full and partial dentures
  • Tooth extractions

Cosmetic Dentistry

Look forward to your smile with treatments focused on appearance and confidence.

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Dental bonding
  • Smile makeover consultations

Urgent Dental Care

Dental pain cannot wait. Call the office right away if you are experiencing:

  • Severe or persistent toothache
  • Chipped, cracked, or knocked-out teeth
  • Lost fillings or crowns
  • Swelling or signs of infection
