About us

Family owned and operated

iNails & Spa is a family-owned and operated nail salon and spa at iNails & Spa, 160 Ritchie Hwy A-4B, Severna Park, MD 21146, in the heart of Severna Park.

Guests highlight the reasonable prices, the clean and welcoming space, and the resident nail artists known for detailed, creative designs.

Walk-ins are welcome and the team does its best to accommodate appointments.
