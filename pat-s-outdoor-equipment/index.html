Our Story

Fifty years of
keeping Burtonsville running

Pat's Outdoor Equipment opened in 1974 and has been family owned ever since. That's five decades of mowers fixed, tractors tuned and neighbors helped.

Since 1974

A local institution,
not a chain

When Pat's opened its doors on Sandy Spring Rd in 1974, Burtonsville looked a lot different. What hasn't changed: a family team that treats your equipment like their own and tells you straight what's wrong with it.

Customers describe the shop as a family environment: wonderful, polite, professional and honest mechanics who go above and beyond. One reviewer says the team will look at your equipment even close to closing time.

Today Pat's handles everything from push mowers to farm tractors, with a BBB A+ rating and a 4.9-star reputation built one repair at a time.
