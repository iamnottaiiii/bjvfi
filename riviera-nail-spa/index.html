Who we are

About

Riviera Nail and Spa is located at 3295 Amboy Road, Suite 6, in Staten Island, New York.

The salon offers manicures, pedicures, gel nails, acrylic and nail extensions, spa pedicures, and waxing services.

Guests highlight the soothing setting, the thorough pedicures, and staff who remember their preferences. Walk in or call ahead.
