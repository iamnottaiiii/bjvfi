Our story

About Snips & Tips

Snips & Tips is a neighborhood beauty salon on Bowleys Quarters Road in Middle River. Walk in for a clean cut, fresh color, or a style that fits your week, and walk out feeling put together.

Every appointment starts with listening. Tell us what you want, show us a photo if you have one, and we will shape the service around you, not the other way around.
