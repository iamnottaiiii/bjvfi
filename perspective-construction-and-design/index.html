Our Process

From first call to final walkthrough

A clear process keeps a construction project calm. Here is how working together typically unfolds.

Illustration

Four steps, no mysteries

1. The call. You describe the project, we ask questions, and we set up a time to see the space.

2. Design and estimate. Plans take shape, materials get chosen, and you receive a clear written estimate.

3. Construction. The crew builds to the plan, and you hear from us before anything changes.

4. Walkthrough. We walk the finished space together and nothing is done until you are satisfied.

Clear communication
