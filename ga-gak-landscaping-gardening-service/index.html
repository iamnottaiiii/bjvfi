Our story

A Granada Hills crew for everyday yards

Ga Gak Landscaping & Gardening Service is a local gardening crew based on Balboa Boulevard in Granada Hills. The work is straightforward: mow the lawn, edge it clean, keep the beds weeded, shape the hedges, and haul everything away when the job is done.

Many customers keep the crew on a standing weekly or biweekly route, so the yard never gets ahead of them. Others call for one-time cleanups when a property needs a reset before a season, a sale, or a family gathering. Either way, the quote comes first and the work starts only when the price is agreed.

If you live in Granada Hills or nearby, one call is all it takes to get on the schedule.
