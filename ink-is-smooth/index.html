The studio, in its own words

A neighborhood tattoo shop where the vibe is unhurried and the work is exact. Come as you are, leave with art.

Ink is Smooth sits on Buist Avenue in Southwest Philadelphia, a studio built around one idea: great tattoos should feel easy. The name is the promise. Lines laid down smooth, shading that melts, and a session that feels more like a lounge set than a procedure.

Every piece starts with a one on one consult. You bring the idea, the artists shape it for your body and your skin, and nothing gets inked until the design feels exactly right.

"The best tattoo is the one you forget you are getting, because the room, the artist and the art all feel right."

Walk ins are welcome when the books allow, and the aftercare guidance is thorough: what to expect day by day, what to avoid, and when to come back for a check.

The players

The studio keeps its artist roster close. Meet the crew in person and find the hand that fits your idea.

Every studio has a sound, and the sound here is collaboration. The artists at Ink is Smooth work across styles, from crisp fine line to bold color, and they will tell you honestly which hand in the room is the best match for your piece.

Public artist portfolios for this shop are still thin online, which is exactly why the consult matters: bring references, talk through the idea, and watch a sketch take shape before you commit.

"Ask who does what best. A good studio will point you to the right artist, even if it is not the one you asked for."

Piercing is handled with the same care: sterile setup, quality jewelry and aftercare you can actually follow.
