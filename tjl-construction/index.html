Our story

A father, a son,
and a shop full of sawdust

TJL Construction has been a family affair since 1999, built on woodworking first and general contracting second.

Since 1999

Woodworkers at heart,
contractors by trade

TJL Construction is owned and operated by father-son duo Tim LaGaly and Andrew LaGaly, based in Burbank, California.

What began with fine woodworking grew into a full-service, high-end general contracting firm. The shop mentality never left: measure twice, cut once, and treat every home like it is your own.

Today the firm specializes in custom cabinetry, kitchen and bathroom renovations, CNC routing and edge banding, and full-home remodels across the greater Los Angeles area.
