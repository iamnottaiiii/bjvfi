Our Story

A working stone shop in Burbank

Armnar Marble, Inc is a fabrication and installation shop on Vanowen Street in Burbank. The work is straightforward: take raw stone slabs and turn them into finished surfaces that fit real rooms, real cabinets and real lives.

Keeping fabrication and installation under one roof means nothing gets lost between the shop and the job site. Measurements, cuts and setting are handled by one team, so the finished piece matches the plan.

Whether it is a single vanity top or stone throughout a home, the goal is the same: clean cuts, honest setting and surfaces that will look right for decades.
