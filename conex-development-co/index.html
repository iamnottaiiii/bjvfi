Services

New Construction

Ground up commercial construction for developers, owners, and businesses. We coordinate design, engineering, permitting, and trades so the build stays on schedule and on budget.

  • Commercial ground up builds and additions
  • Structural, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing coordination
  • Design build delivery with a single point of responsibility
  • Schedule and budget reporting through completion

Tenant Improvement

Interior build outs and remodels that turn leased or owned space into working space. Our permit history includes office remodels, interior tenant improvements, and revised interior layouts.

  • Office remodels and interior revisions
  • Non structural wall additions and layout changes
  • Lighting, millwork, and interior finish upgrades
  • Phased work to keep businesses operating
Discuss a Tenant Improvement

Restaurant and Retail Build Outs

Food and retail spaces have their own rules. Permit records show our work on restaurant change of use projects, retail tenant improvements, and a catering shop conversion with a Type I hood.

  • Restaurant and retail change of use conversions
  • Kitchen and catering shop build outs
  • Retail tenant improvements with new finishes and lighting
  • Coordination with health and building departments
Discuss a Food or Retail Build

Permits and Planning

Commercial alterations in Los Angeles move through plan check, permits, and inspections. We handle the paperwork side so your project does not stall between trades.

  • Building alteration and repair permits
  • Change of use and address clearance filings
  • Supplemental permits and revised plans
  • Inspection scheduling and final sign off
