About

Kendall Key, broker and Eastside native at heart.

Kendall Key is the owner and broker of Realpro Eastside (CA DRE lic #01874944), a boutique brokerage established in 2009 on York Boulevard in Highland Park. A third generation real estate broker with 22+ years in the industry, she is an expert in every type of multi and single-family transaction and a master in rehabbed properties.

Her signature work is Real Estate Appreciation Consulting: helping buyers, sellers, and investors gain the maximum value out of a home or investment, immediate and long term. She pairs market knowhow with hands-on design, landscape design, and construction expertise to create the highest appreciation possible within the four corners of a property.

Kendall is an expert on ADU new construction building and existing structure conversion to ADU, and has played an intricate role in over 285 property transformations, new construction, and long term hold renovation ventures.

Illustration

Rooted in the neighborhood.

Realpro Eastside takes pride in being a complete, all-in real estate brokerage: the firm can sell land, build the home, design and landscape it, then stage and resell, with in-house construction, design, landscape, staging, and property management support for its clients.

Kendall is an animal activist who shares her home with rescue animals, cooks vegan food, and gardens. The firm supports the local community, including co-hosting fundraisers for the North Central Animal Shelter on Lacy Street alongside other Eastside brokerages.
