Our story

A Brooklyn General Contractor

ABCD Construction (ABCD Construction Corp.) is a general contractor based at 5702 3rd Ave in Brooklyn, NY 11220. The company is led by George Bouroudis and serves commercial clients and public agencies across New York City.

Our work centers on commercial renovations, facility upgrades, and public sector projects. ABCD Construction appears regularly on plan holder lists for MTA New York City Transit procurements, including station rehabilitation and component repair contracts, as well as DASNY facility projects. That bid environment demands precise estimating, disciplined scheduling, and complete documentation, and we bring the same discipline to every private commercial job.

Clients get a single accountable prime contractor: one estimate, one schedule, one point of contact from mobilization through punch list and closeout.

Hours: call for hours. The best way to reach us is by phone at (718) 439-3385.
