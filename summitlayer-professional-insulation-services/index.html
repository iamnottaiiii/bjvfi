01 · HOW IT WORKS

Comfort is built in layers

Good insulation works as a system. Here is the simple path from first call to finished job.

1

Talk about the symptoms

Hot rooms, cold rooms, high bills, drafts. Describe what you notice and we will point to the likely causes.

2

Look at the key areas

Attic, walls, crawlspace, and the air leaks between them. Each layer plays a role in how your home holds temperature.

3

Choose the right layers

Different areas call for different materials and approaches. We walk through the options in plain language.

4

Install and verify

After installation, we review what was done so you know each layer is in place and doing its job.
