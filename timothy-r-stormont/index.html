How engagements work

A clear path from idea to keys

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Walk the site, talk goals and budget, and decide together whether the project is a fit. Call (661) 619-0170 to start.

  2. 02

    Schematic design

    Sketches and plans that test the big moves: layout, massing, light, and how the building sits on its lot.

  3. 03

    Design development and documents

    Refined drawings and specifications detailed enough for accurate pricing and permit submittal.

  4. 04

    Construction support

    Submittal review, site visits, and answers when the builder has questions, so the design survives contact with reality.

About

Timothy R. Stormont, Architect, AIA

Timothy R. Stormont is a licensed architect and member of the American Institute of Architects (AIA). Public records associate his practice with the Bakersfield area of Kern County, where he appears on school-district approved vendor lists and has participated in AIA Golden Empire Chapter civic efforts.

His approach is practical and client-first: understand the site, respect the budget, and produce drawings that builders can actually build from. Whether the project is a family home or a commercial space, the goal is the same, a building that works as good as it looks.
