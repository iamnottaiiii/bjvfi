About us

A Jonesboro contractor you can actually reach.

Leeks Remodeling is a general contracting business based on Sleepy Hollow Lane in Jonesboro, Georgia. We take on kitchen and bathroom remodels, flooring, carpentry, painting, and home repairs across Clayton County and the surrounding south metro area.
