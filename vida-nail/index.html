Our Story

A neighborhood salon built on consistency and care.

Years of regulars

Vida Nail has served Sheepshead Bay from its Avenue U location for years, growing a loyal base of clients who keep coming back. Reviewers describe technicians who communicate clearly, work carefully, and make sure every nail is finished to a high standard before you leave the chair.

Clean, kind, and creative

The salon is known for a clean, comfortable space and an accommodating team. Clients praise the variety of designs on offer and the patience of the technicians, from first visits to family outings that have become a tradition.

Come as you are

Walk-ins are welcome, and the team is glad to fit you in. Whether it is a quick polish change or a full set of extensions for a special occasion, you will be greeted warmly at 2711 Avenue U.
