How we got here

A designer who answers

Dan founded First-in-class Landscape & Design on a simple idea: custom tailored, sustainable landscape designs that actually work, for real homes and real budgets.

That means no handoffs to a sales team and no surprises mid project. Dan is the person you meet, the person who draws the plan, and the person on site making sure it is done right.

Responsive, responsible with other people's money. Everything he said he would do, he did, and he went above and beyond.

Clients describe the experience the same way, job after job: innovative ideas, honest communication, and a finished landscape with remarkable curb appeal that stays within budget.
