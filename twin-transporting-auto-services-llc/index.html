Our story

One name,
two trades

Twin Transporting Auto Services LLC grew out of a simple observation on Accokeek Rd: car trouble is really two problems, and nobody likes solving them separately.

The Shop on Accokeek Rd

Our bay at 4901 Accokeek Rd in Brandywine is set up for the work drivers actually need: brakes, tires, oil, diagnostics, and the electrical puzzles that strand people at the worst times. We explain the problem in plain language, quote it straight, and do not start work until you say go.

The Truck Behind the Name

The "Transporting" half is not decoration. Our carrier moves the vehicles we service: breakdown pickups to the bay, non-runners bought at auction, cars that need to get across town. When one company handles the move and the fix, nothing gets lost between the tow driver and the mechanic.
