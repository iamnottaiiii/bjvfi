About us

Le's Nails has served the Timonium community from its York Road location for years. The salon is known for friendly service, a quiet and clean space, and technicians praised for gentle technique and attention to detail.

Why clients choose us

Walk-ins are welcome, pricing is competitive, and the team handles everything from simple polish changes to gel and pedicures. Clients mention the wide polish selection and careful work.
