A studio, not a
salon assembly line

Walk into Creative Edge Hair Studio on Forest Ave and the first thing you notice is the intent. Every appointment starts with a conversation about what you want your hair to say, and every cut is built around your bone structure, your texture and your routine.

We are color nerds and shape obsessives. We would rather spend the extra twenty minutes blending a foil than rush you out the door. That is the creative edge: the difference between hair that is done and hair that is designed.

"The difference between hair that is done and hair that is designed."

From natural, low-maintenance color to bold fashion shades, from crisp bobs to long layered movement, bring us a reference photo or just a feeling. We will translate it into hair.

Our story

Designed on
Forest Avenue

Creative Edge Hair Studio grew out of a simple frustration with rushed, one-size-fits-all salon visits. We wanted a studio where the work gets the time it deserves.

Every neighborhood has that one salon people whisper about, the one where the color always looks expensive and the cuts always grow out right. On Forest Ave in the 10310, we are building that reputation one head at a time.

Creative Edge started with a belief: great hair is designed, not just done. That means consultations that actually listen, formulations mixed for your exact hair, and cuts mapped to the way you live, not just the way you looked in the chair.

"Consultations that actually listen, formulations mixed for your exact hair."

We keep our skills sharp and our standards sharper. Whether you want quietly polished or unapologetically bold, you leave with hair that feels like you on your best day, and the know-how to keep it that way.

What we stand for

Honest consultations. No upsell pressure. Color and cuts that respect your hair's health first, because the boldest looks need the healthiest canvas.
