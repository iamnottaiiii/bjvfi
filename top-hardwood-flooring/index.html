Services

Hardwood expertise is the core. Remodeling services from the list below reflect work described in public customer reviews. Call to confirm exactly what your project needs.

New hardwood installation

New hardwood floors for single rooms or whole homes, installed with attention to layout, transitions, and finish.

  • Solid and engineered hardwood installation
  • Layout planning for pattern and board direction
  • Transitions, thresholds, and baseboards
  • Finish options matched to your home
Sample pricing note: flooring projects vary widely by material and square footage. Call (833) 546-3701 for an estimate on your project.

Sanding and refinishing

Bring tired floors back to life without replacing them. Sanding smooths years of wear so a fresh finish can go on strong.

  • Dust-aware sanding of worn hardwood
  • Stain color changes and refreshes
  • Protective finish coats for durability
  • Spot refinishing for high-traffic areas
Sample pricing note: refinishing cost depends on floor condition and area size. Call (833) 546-3701 for an estimate.

Floor repairs

Damaged boards, gaps, squeaks, and worn spots get fixed so your floor looks and feels right again.

  • Board replacement for damaged planks
  • Gap, squeak, and scratch repair
  • Water and wear damage assessment
  • Finish touch-ups and blending
Sample pricing note: repairs are quoted per job after a look at the damage. Call (833) 546-3701 to describe your issue.

Home remodeling

Customer reviews describe full remodeling projects handled by the same team: kitchens, bathrooms, painting, roofing coordination, and pre-sale home updates.

  • Kitchen and bathroom remodeling
  • Interior and exterior painting
  • Coordinated roofing and solar panel handling
  • Pre-sale updates, staging, and landscaping touch-ups
Sample pricing note: remodeling scopes vary too much for a flat price. Call (833) 546-3701 to talk through your project.
