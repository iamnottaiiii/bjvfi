Tour & production trucking

Moving a show is not standard freight. Cases stack a certain way, load in has a hard start time, and the next city is already on the clock. Our drivers work with production managers on timing, dock access, and overnight holds so gear arrives ready for the crew.

  • Stage, sound, lighting, and backline transport
  • Load in window planning with production contacts
  • Multi city routing for tour legs
  • Careful handling of cased and crated equipment

Good fit when

You have a venue date that cannot move, a production manager who needs the truck at a specific dock at a specific hour, or a multi stop run across the region.

Staging & event logistics

Corporate events, trade shows, and staging builds involve lots of pieces arriving from different places. We coordinate pickup sequences and on site timing so the build crew is never standing around waiting on a truck.

  • Trade show and expo freight transport
  • Corporate event and staging gear moves
  • Multi stop pickup routing
  • On site arrival coordination

Good fit when

You are producing an event with gear coming from multiple vendors, or staging a build that needs materials sequenced to the install plan.

General freight hauling

Beyond entertainment, we run standard commercial freight: palletized goods, crated equipment, and commercial commodities on regional lanes. The same discipline applies: confirmed windows, tracked progress, and a call if anything changes.

  • Palletized and crated commercial freight
  • Regional lanes across PA, OH, and WV
  • Confirmed pickup and delivery windows

Cross dock & short haul

About

Built for the nights the show must go on

Performer Staging Net Inc is based at 306 Pontiac Ave in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Our name comes from the work: moving what performers and productions need, where and when they need it. That work taught us something every shipper benefits from: deadlines are real, communication is everything, and the truck showing up on time is the whole job.

A

Deadline driven

Entertainment schedules do not flex. We plan every run backward from the hard time and build in margin for the road.

B

Direct contact

You deal with the dispatcher who booked the load. Status updates come from us before you have to ask.

C

Handled with care

Production gear is expensive and fragile. That care carries over to every pallet and crate we move.
