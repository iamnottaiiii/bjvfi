About

Clean the right way, not the fast way

Phoenix Property Development is a pressure washing service based in Mount Laurel Township, NJ, serving homes and businesses across the area.

South Jersey exteriors take a beating. Humid summers grow algae and mildew on siding, roofs and shaded concrete. Deicing salts, oil and leaf tannins stain driveways through the colder months. Left alone, that buildup does more than look tired: it makes surfaces slippery and shortens their life.

Our approach is simple. Match the method to the surface. Soft washing uses low pressure and cleaning solutions for siding, roofs and delicate materials, killing organic growth without damage. Pressure washing brings full power to concrete, pavers and other hard surfaces where it is safe and effective. Landscaping gets protected, and the rinse leaves nothing behind but a clean property.

Call (609) 923-4069 to talk through your project and get a free quote.

How a job goes

  • 1. Walkthrough. We look at every surface and pick the right method and pressure.
  • 2. Protection. Plants, outlets and delicate areas are covered before water flows.
  • 3. Treatment. Cleaning solutions loosen algae, mold, oil and grime first.
  • 4. Wash and rinse. Soft wash or pressure wash, then a full clean rinse.
  • 5. Final check. We walk it with you and make sure it is right.
