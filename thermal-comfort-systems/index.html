Our story

A family business since 1982

Thermal Comfort Systems Inc. was established in April 1982 by Phil and Pam Brown, bringing experience in the trade going back to the 1950s and 1960s. Based in Northridge in the San Fernando Valley, the company serves the greater Los Angeles area, including Santa Clarita, Simi Valley, the South Bay and Arcadia.

The team holds a California Warm Air Heating, Ventilating and Air Conditioning and Refrigeration license (440532), and prides itself on being honest, on time, courteous and sensitive to client needs, with good communication and quality workmanship.
