About Us

A Local Contractor, Plain and Simple

C2 Home Improvements LLP is a home improvement contractor based in Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania. The business focuses on the residential projects homeowners request most: bathroom remodels, flooring installation, and vinyl siding.

Being local means something practical. We are easy to reach at (717) 645-4246, we show up when we say we will, and we stand behind the work because our name is on it in the community we serve.
