Who we are

About us

CT Simple Junk And Hauling is a debris removal service based on New Haven Rd in Naugatuck, CT. Simple is in the name and in the process.

Our approach

Hauling without the hassle

Most people put off junk removal because it sounds like a project: renting a truck, finding help, figuring out where it all goes. We built this service to remove every one of those steps.

You make one call. Our crew shows up, does the lifting, loads the truck, and sorts everything for recycling, donation, or proper disposal. You get your space back the same day.

We serve Naugatuck and the surrounding Naugatuck Valley, from single item pickups to full property cleanouts. If it is non-hazardous and you want it gone, we will haul it.
