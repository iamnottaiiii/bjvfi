What the Studio Does

One of a kind designs drawn for your placement and skin. Black and grey, full color, geometric, dotwork, script, and realism are all on the table. Bring references and the artist builds the final design with you.
Delicate, precise linework that heals clean. Reviewers praise the fine line work here again and again. Ideal for first tattoos, minimalist designs, and script.

  • Implant grade jewelry options
