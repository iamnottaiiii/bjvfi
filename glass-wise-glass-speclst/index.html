Why glass matters

Small chip, big deal

Your windshield is part of your vehicle's structure. A small chip can spread into a long crack with one hot afternoon or one cold snap, turning a quick repair into a full replacement.

Clear visibility

Cracks in the driver's line of sight are a safety issue, not just cosmetic.
