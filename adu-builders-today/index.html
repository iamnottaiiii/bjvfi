Our story

Builders who only build ADUs

ADU Builders Today is a general contractor focused on one thing: accessory dwelling units done right.

California changed the rules to make ADUs easier to build, and homeowners responded. A backyard cottage can house aging parents, give adult kids a start, create rental income, or simply add a quiet place to work. But an ADU is still a full home in miniature, with the same design, engineering, and permit demands as any house.

That is why we specialize. Our team works on ADUs every day: detached cottages, attached additions, garage conversions, and junior units. We know the state ADU code, we know how La Cañada Flintridge and neighboring foothill cities review plans, and we know where projects stall so we can keep yours moving.

An ADU is a small project with big paperwork. We handle both.

From the first site visit to the final inspection, you work with one accountable team. No hand offs between a designer who disappears and a builder who never saw the plans. The people who design your ADU are the people who build it.
