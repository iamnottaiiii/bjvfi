Our story

Built on fences, kept by lawns

WK Fence & Lawn LLC is a local contractor based on Cliff Street in Cliffside Park, New Jersey. We started with a simple idea: most fence companies leave the yard a mess, and most lawn companies do not touch fences.

So we do both. The crew that sets your posts is the same crew that cuts the grass when the job is done, which means your property looks finished, not just fenced.

We serve Cliffside Park and nearby Bergen County neighborhoods, one property line at a time.
