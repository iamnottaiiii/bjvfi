Signs you need us

Roofs talk. Listen early.

A small repair now beats a full replacement later. If you see any of these, call before the next rain.

5 warning signs

  • 1
    Missing or curling shinglesExposed underlayment soaks up sun and rain.
  • 2
    Stains on ceilings or wallsWater is already getting in somewhere above.
  • 3
    Granules in the guttersShingles shedding their protective coating.
  • 4
    Sagging spotsA structural red flag, get eyes on it fast.
  • 5
    Roof older than 20 yearsMost asphalt roofs are near the end by then.
