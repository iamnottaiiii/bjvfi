What We Offer

Dental services for the whole family

General dentistry covering prevention, repair, and cosmetic care.

1Exams and Checkups

Thorough dental exams to catch small issues early, with digital X rays when needed.

2Professional Cleanings

Routine hygiene visits that remove plaque and tartar and keep gums healthy.

3Tooth Colored Fillings

Natural looking fillings that blend in with your teeth and stop decay.

4Crowns and Bridges

Strong, custom restorations that rebuild damaged teeth and replace missing ones.

5Root Canal Treatment

Gentle treatment for infected or painful teeth, designed to save the natural tooth.

6Tooth Extractions

Safe, comfortable removal of teeth that cannot be saved.

7Dentures and Partials

Removable replacements that restore chewing, speech, and confidence.

8Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening options for a brighter, more confident smile.

9Children's Dentistry

Patient, friendly first visits and ongoing care to build lifelong habits.
