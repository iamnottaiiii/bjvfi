neighbors recommend

Londono Construction Co grew the old-fashioned way: one job done right, one neighbor telling the next.

★

A reputation built on job sites

We are a general contractor based on Matchlock Drive in Orlando, working across the metro on the projects homeowners actually talk about: the deck they live on all summer, the fence that finally keeps the dog in, the patio that became the favorite room of the house.

Our reviews tell the story better than we can: customers describe crews that are professional and on time, an owner who answers questions and delivers on promises, and finished work, decks, patios, fences, that they are proud to show off and happy to refer to friends.

That is the whole business model. No pressure tactics, no vanishing subcontractors. Just clear quotes, steady communication, and work we would put our name on, because we do.
