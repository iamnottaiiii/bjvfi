What a batch plant does

A ready-mix plant is where concrete is born. Cement, aggregates, water and admixtures are weighed to exact proportions, mixed, and loaded into a mixer truck that keeps the drum turning all the way to your jobsite. The result is fresh, consistent concrete, every load the same as the last.

National Redi Mix operates its batch plant at 1851 E Huntington Dr in Duarte, in the San Gabriel Valley materials corridor near Irwindale. From here it serves contractors and homeowners across Duarte, Irwindale, Azusa, Monrovia, Arcadia and surrounding cities.

The plant is listed with the National Cement ready-mix network at nationalcement.com/nrmc.
