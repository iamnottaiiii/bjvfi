Our story

Remodelers who treat
your home like our own

Alpha Kitchen Bath Bathroom Remodeling Chatsworth is a Chatsworth-based remodeling company focused on the two rooms that matter most: kitchens and baths.

How we work

One team, the whole project

Remodels go wrong when the work is split across strangers. We keep your project under one roof: the same team that designs your layout handles the demolition, the rough work, and the final detail. You always know who is in your home and what happens next.

We specialize on purpose. Kitchens and bathrooms have the trickiest mix of plumbing, electrical, tile, and finish work in the house. Doing them every week means we spot problems early and finish clean.

No high-pressure sales, no mystery pricing. Just a written estimate, a clear schedule, and rooms you will enjoy for years.
