How a job runs

A process you can inspect at every step.

No mystery, no surprises. Every project follows the same four-stage sequence.

STAGE 01

Walk and quote

We look at the surfaces in person, talk color and scope, and put the price in writing before a brush is lifted.

STAGE 02

Prep and protect

Masking, drop cloths, patching, sanding, and priming. The part most painters rush is the part we spend the most time on.

STAGE 03

Paint

Full, even coats with sharp cut lines at every edge. Two coats minimum on color changes.

STAGE 04

Walkthrough and clean

We walk the finished work with you, touch up anything you spot, and leave the space clean.
