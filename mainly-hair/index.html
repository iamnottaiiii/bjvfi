Our Story

Rooted in East Aurora

Mainly Hair has been serving East Aurora from its home in Oakwood Square, offering the full range of salon services in a relaxed, welcoming shop. It is a working neighborhood salon: no pretense, just skilled hands and honest prices.

The stylists are the heart of it. Clients single out Celia for rescuing bad haircuts and delivering cuts that earn compliments, and Judy for friendly, attentive care. Regulars describe the whole team as nice people who do great work.

Whether you need a quick trim, a fresh color or a full restyle, you will be greeted like a neighbor, because here, you are one.
