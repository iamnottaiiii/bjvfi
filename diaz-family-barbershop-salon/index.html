Our story

A family name
over the door

Diaz Family Barbershop & Salon has been part of the Hamilton St neighborhood in Worcester since 2009, a family-run shop built one haircut at a time.

Since 2009

Family owned,
neighborhood loved.

The idea was simple: one shop where the whole family could get a great cut without the rush. Barbershop craft for the guys, salon care for everyone else, and a room that feels like home.

Over the years the chairs filled with regulars, kids who grew up in them, and neighbors who send their friends. The name over the door is a promise: you will be treated like family here.

01 Craft first

Clean fades, careful scissor work, and straight-razor finishes. The details are the difference, and they show in the mirror.

02 Everyone welcome

Dads, moms, kids, grandparents. The shop serves the whole block, and the whole block keeps coming back.

03 Part of Worcester

A Hamilton St fixture for well over a decade, and proud of it. This is a neighborhood shop in the best sense.
