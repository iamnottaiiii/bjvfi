Our story

A contractor built like
a general store

One shop for everything your home needs: that is the whole idea behind Inception Home and Remodeling.

Inception Home and Remodeling started with a simple frustration: homeowners juggling five contractors for one project. The plumber blames the tile guy, the tile guy waits on the electrician, and the homeowner becomes an unpaid project manager. We built this company to end that.

From our office at 5627 Sepulveda Blvd in Sherman Oaks, our crews handle kitchens, bathrooms, decks, fences, driveways, painting, electrical, and everything in between, across the greater Los Angeles area. When every trade answers to the same company, the schedule holds and the quote means something.

Deal directly with the source to save money. We do it all.

The Inception promise

That promise shapes how we quote: you talk to the people who will actually do the work. No sales office, no phone tree, no mystery line items. Just a straight scope, a straight schedule, and a crew that shows up.

What we believe

  • Direct beats delegated. You should talk to the crew, not a call center.
  • One schedule. Every trade coordinated in-house, nothing waits on anyone.
  • Straight quotes. Scoped trade by trade, priced honestly.
