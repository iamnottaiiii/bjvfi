Why masonry

Nothing outlasts a well laid wall

Brick and stone shrug off New England weather that destroys lesser materials. A chimney we rebuild today will still be standing when your grandkids argue over who gets the house. That is the whole pitch: build it once, build it right.

From cracked mortar joints to full chimney rebuilds, one call gets a mason on site to look, measure and tell you straight what the job needs.

The company

A local masonry contractor

Architectural Masonry Construction is listed as an active business entity operating out of 1680 Wachusett St in the Jefferson and Holden area of central Massachusetts.

As a masonry contractor, the work centers on the craft that holds buildings together: brick, concrete block, stone and the mortar between them. Chimneys, foundations, facades, steps and walls, the parts of a home that have to be right.

Masonry is slow, physical, exacting work, and it rewards the contractor who respects it. That is the standard every job is held to here.
