About us

A mechanical contractor built on accountability.

Mechanical H2O LLC is a mechanical contractor based in Camp Hill, Pennsylvania, led by owner Gregory Weigel. The company works across plumbing, heating, water heating, and kitchen and bath remodeling, so related work stays with one crew instead of getting split between subcontractors.
