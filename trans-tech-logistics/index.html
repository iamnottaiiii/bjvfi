ABOUT

Built on Grand Avenue. Built for hard work.

We are the kind of trucking company that still picks up the phone.

Trans Tech Logistics operates from Grand Avenue in Pittsburgh, in the middle of the region's industrial and energy corridors. That location is no accident: it puts our trucks close to the jobsites, terminals, and facilities that need reliable hauling every day.

Our work has included bulk water and liquid hauling for industrial and energy operations across Pennsylvania, where drivers follow strict site protocols and run around the clock. The same discipline carries into every flatbed load and every local haul: show up prepared, secure the load, deliver on time.

We keep the fleet right-sized and the operation lean. No layers, no excuses. When you call (412) 771-5767, you reach someone who can put a truck on your job.
