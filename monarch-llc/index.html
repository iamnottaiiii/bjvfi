About

Monarch LLC

Monarch LLC is a real estate development company based in Camp Hill, Pennsylvania. We focus on residential development in the greater Harrisburg market: finding the right land, securing approvals, and delivering projects that hold their value.

Our model is deliberately focused. A small, single-location team means every project gets principal-level attention, and every partner gets straight answers. We underwrite conservatively, plan thoroughly, and execute with the contractors and municipalities we know well.

  • Real estate developer serving the Harrisburg / Camp Hill region
  • Small team with direct principal access
  • B2B services for investors, landowners, and partners
  • Conservative underwriting and clear reporting
  • Office: 3909 Hartzdale Dr #901, Camp Hill, PA 17011
