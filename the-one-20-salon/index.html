About

The Story of One 20

Established 2013 · reopened on James St

A Fresh Start, Same Great Team

The One 20 Salon began in Armory Square in 2013. After being closed for a time, the staff chose to reconnect and reopen in the Imperial Gardens apartment complex on James St, ground floor, with easy free parking. Stylists from Fringe hair salons joined the team too.

What Makes It Different

Top stylists, educated internationally. A relaxed atmosphere. A central location. Clients describe the staff as friendly, fun, and professional, and say their hair has never been so healthy.
