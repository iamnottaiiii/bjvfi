Our Story

Arms, hands, and honest work

Why the name says it all

Some jobs are done by machines on autopilot. Carpet cleaning is not one of them. Every room we clean gets hands-on attention: we see the stains, we work the traffic lanes, and we do not leave until the floor looks right. The name is the promise. Arms and hands on every job.

A Lynn neighborhood business

Arm & Hand Cleaners is based at 53 Willow St in Lynn, Massachusetts. We serve homes across Lynn and nearby North Shore neighborhoods, bringing the same careful detail to a studio apartment and a full family home.

How we clean

We start with a walkthrough of your space, note the problem areas, then clean methodically room by room. Stains get targeted treatment instead of a one-size-fits-all pass. When we finish, we walk it with you.

Straightforward pricing

No upsells at the door and no mystery fees. We agree on the price before the work starts, and the price you agree to is the price you pay.
