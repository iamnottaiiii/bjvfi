The process

From standing to gone in five steps

Demolition is choreography. Here is how a McVey tear-down runs.

Free walk-through and estimate

You show us the structure; we price the tear-down and the haul-off. No surprises on the invoice.

Permits and disconnects

We handle the paperwork: permits, utility disconnects, and neighbor notifications where required.

Dust and debris control

Water control, fencing, and containment keep the site and the neighbors protected while work runs.

The tear-down

Excavators and experienced operators bring it down fast, section by section, in the planned order.

Haul-off and clean lot

Every load leaves the site. You get a cleared, graded lot ready for what comes next.
