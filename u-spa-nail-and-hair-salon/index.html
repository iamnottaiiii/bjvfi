Our story

Avenue U's one stop
beauty salon

U Spa Nail and Hair Salon Inc. has served the neighborhood from 1806 Avenue U since 2010, nails and hair under one roof.

Since 2010

Big salon, fair prices,
everything in one trip

U Spa opened its doors on Avenue U in 2010 and has been a neighborhood fixture ever since. The idea was simple: a spacious, comfortable salon where you could get your nails done and your hair cut without booking two places across town.

Inside you will find full nail stations offering everything from classic manicures and pedicures to gel, acrylics, extensions, and detailed nail art, plus a hair side for cuts and styling.

The team is known for being friendly and bilingual, speaking Chinese and English, and reviewers often mention the fair prices and the sheer convenience of handling nails and hair in a single visit.
