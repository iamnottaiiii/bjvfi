Our Story

One owner. One shop. Decades of locks and doors.

The owner is the business.

All-Safe Lock and Door Inc. has been owner operated since 1975, according to the company's own listing. That means when you call, you talk to John Abbadessa, the owner and operator, not a dispatcher and not a franchise script.

The company is a complete, professional locksmith and door services business based in Uniondale, serving all of Long Island from Queens to Riverhead, with multiple dispatch office locations for fast local response.

Locks and doors are a craft. After decades of fitting, repairing, and opening them across the island, there is very little the owner has not seen, and very little he cannot fix on the spot.
