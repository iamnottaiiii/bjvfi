What We Do

ROOFING SERVICES, CHAPTER BY CHAPTER

Every project starts with a look and an honest plan. Call (818) 527-9043 for a free conversation about your roof.

01

Roof Replacement

When repairs stop making sense, a full replacement resets the clock. We tear off worn material, check the decking, and install a clean new system built for Valley heat and wind.

  • Tear-off and disposal
  • Decking inspection
  • Underlayment and flashing
  • Shingle or tile finish
  • Final walkthrough
02

Roof Repairs

Leaks, lifted shingles, cracked tiles, damaged flashing. Most problems are smaller than they look if you catch them early, and we will tell you honestly when a repair is all you need.

  • Leak diagnosis
  • Shingle and tile repair
  • Flashing and vent work
  • Storm damage fixes
03

Roof Inspections

Buying a home, selling one, or just want to know where you stand? A straight inspection report with photos of what we find, so you can plan instead of panic.

  • Full roof walkthrough
  • Photo documentation
  • Written condition report
  • Maintenance priorities
04

Leak and Storm Response

Water where it should not be is an emergency. We respond fast, stop the active damage, and then fix the underlying problem for good.

  • Fast response scheduling
  • Temporary weatherproofing
  • Permanent repair plan
