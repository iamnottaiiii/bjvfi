The checklist

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, stovetop, microwave, appliance fronts, floors
  • Bathrooms: toilets, tubs and showers, mirrors, sinks, floors
  • Bedrooms: dusting, mirrors, bed making, floors and vacuuming
  • Living areas: dusting, baseboards, windowsills, floors and vacuuming
  • Extras on request: inside oven and fridge, laundry, interior windows
