The Rebrand Story

The name on the sign changed. The garage at 3387 Dixie Highway in Hamilton did not. This is the publicly documented story of the shop, told as a timeline.

The Early Days

Robert's Auto Sales and Service

Business records identify the shop as Roberts' Auto Sales and Service, Inc., operating at 3387 Dixie Highway, Hamilton, OH 45015 with phone (513) 868-2467. Under the Robert's name it built a loyal local following in the Hamilton and Fairfield area.

The Reputation Years

A Name Customers Trusted

Customer reviews consistently describe a knowledgeable crew, clear communication, and fair pricing. The shop became known for doing its own diagnostics, saving clients significant money compared to other mechanics, and for work ranging from simple fixes to complete overhauls on classic cars.

The Rename
