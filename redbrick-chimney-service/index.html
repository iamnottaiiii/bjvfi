The craft

A chimney is a system, not just a stack of bricks.

Every safe fire needs three things working together: a clear flue, a sound liner, and a draft that pulls smoke up and out. Creosote buildup, cracked liners, and failing mortar quietly break that chain, long before you smell smoke in the living room.

We sweep, inspect, and repair the whole system: from the firebox to the flue to the crown and cap. One visit, one honest report, one safe hearth.

Why sweeps matter

Creosote does not negotiate.

Every wood fire leaves creosote in the flue. Left alone, it hardens, narrows the passage, and becomes fuel for a chimney fire. Annual sweeping is the single most effective thing a fireplace owner can do, and it takes most of an afternoon at most.

Burning this winter? If it has been more than a year since your last sweep, now is the time.

Signs to call us

Fireplaces are polite about their problems. Catch these early and most fixes stay small.

Call sooner if you notice

  • !Smoke drifting back into the room
  • !White staining (efflorescence) on the brick
  • !Crumbling mortar or loose bricks
  • !A damper that will not open or close
  • !Animals or debris in the flue
  • !A rusted or missing chimney cap

How we work

Old school craft,
straightforward business.

Chimney work is one of the oldest trades in the book, and the good ones still work the same way: show up on time, protect the house, do the work properly, and tell the truth about what you found.

That is the whole RedBrick method. We brush every flue like it heats our own home. We photograph what we find. We explain what is urgent, what can wait a season, and what it will cost, in plain language, before you spend a dollar.

Hamden homes run the full range: colonial center chimneys, 1970s prefabs, inserts, and stoves. We service them all, and we will tell you honestly when a fireplace is not worth saving.

A chimney fire is the most expensive fire you never see coming. A sweep is the cheapest insurance you will ever buy.
