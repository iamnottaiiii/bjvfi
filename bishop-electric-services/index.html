Our story

Bishop Electric Services has been wiring Cincinnati homes since 1987. We are a small, local electrical contractor based on Eversole Road in Anderson Township, and most of our work is within a short drive of the East Side.

The reviews tell the rest of the story. Customers mention a small crew led by Matt, straight quotes, and electricians who show up when they say they will. That is not a marketing line. It is just how we have always worked: answer the phone, keep the appointment, do the job right, and charge a fair price.

We hold an active Ohio electrical contractor license and handle the work homeowners actually call about: breakers, panels, EV chargers, generators, and lighting. If a job is bigger than we can do well, we will tell you that too.
