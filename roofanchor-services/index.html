RoofAnchor Services is a roofing contractor based in Burbank, CA, built on a simple idea: tell the truth about roofs.

What we believe

Honest roofs,
honest work

Roofing has a trust problem, and we know it. Too many homeowners have paid for work they did not need, or needed work they never got told about.

So we built RoofAnchor Services around the opposite approach: inspect carefully, show our work with photos, explain the options in plain language, and let you decide. Repair when repair is right. Replace when replacement is smarter. Never pressure, never mystery pricing.

We are based at 524 N Brighton St in Burbank and serve homes and businesses across the surrounding area.
