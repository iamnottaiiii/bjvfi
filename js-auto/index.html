About J's Auto

A family shop with a loyal following

J's Auto is a family owned auto repair shop on Cincinnati Columbus Rd in Cincinnati, Ohio. The owner is known to customers as Mr. J, and reviewers consistently describe him as warm, respectful, and honest about cost and repair options.

Customers say routine service and small repairs are done at bargain prices, work is finished correctly the first time, and timelines are realistic. The shop has an excellent reputation among local Chinese drivers, and the owner speaks Cantonese, Mandarin, and English.
