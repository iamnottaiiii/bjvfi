Our Story

The bar with your name on the chair

Ludvig's Hair Bar is a neighborhood hair salon on East 17th Street in Brooklyn, led by licensed stylist Ludvig. The salon holds an active New York State appearance enhancement license, and its reputation was built the slow way: one careful appointment at a time.

✦

Thorough, never rushed

Clients consistently describe Ludvig as thorough, attentive, and professional. He cuts with care rather than rushing through the appointment, and clients say they frequently get compliments on the results.

✦

A team with an eye for detail

Reviewers also praise stylist Ina for her impressive technique and attention to detail, taking the time to understand exactly what each client is looking for.

Walk in, sit down, and leave with hair that turns heads. That has been the idea from the start.
