Rooted in Burbank.

Clear View Gardening is a local gardening service working out of Tufts Ave in Burbank, caring for yards across the neighborhood and beyond.

The Approach

One Gardener, Every Visit

You get the same person each time, someone who learns your yard: which corner dries out, which hedge grows fastest, where the sprinklers miss. That continuity is what makes a garden thrive instead of just survive.

The Knowledge

Built for This Climate

Burbank heat, Santa Ana winds, and water restrictions are facts of life here. Plant choices, pruning timing, and irrigation all get planned around what actually works in the Valley, not what looks good in a catalog.

The Promise

Tidy When We Leave

Every visit ends the same way: clippings bagged, walks blown clean, gates latched. Your yard should look better than when we arrived, and so should everything around it.
