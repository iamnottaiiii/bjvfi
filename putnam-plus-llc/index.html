What we do

One crew for the whole teardown

Demolition is a skilled trade, not just heavy machinery. Our crews plan the sequence, protect what stays, and clear what goes, so your lot is left ready for whatever comes next. We handle the permits coordination, the dust control, and the final sweep.

A clean lot, handed back on schedule, is the whole job.

Whether it is a single garage, an interior gut for a remodel, or a full structure coming down, call (470) 507-7344 and tell us what needs to come down. We will walk the site and give you a straight estimate.

Residential demolition

Old homes, additions, and outbuildings taken down with care for neighboring property and clear access planning.

  • Whole house teardowns · sequenced demolition of houses and duplexes

Commercial demolition

Strip outs and full teardowns for offices, retail, and industrial space, scheduled around your business.

  • Office and retail strip outs · back to shell for the next tenant
  • Full building demolition · warehouses and commercial structures
  • Selective demolition · walls, slabs, and systems removed to spec

Interior strip outs

Clean guts of kitchens, baths, and commercial interiors without touching what stays.

  • Kitchen and bath demos · cabinets, tile, fixtures, and flooring

  • Drywall and framing · partitions and ceilings taken down clean
