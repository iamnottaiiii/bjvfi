Why Roofs Fail in the Valley

Heat, sun, and the occasional hard rain do the damage. Knowing the pattern helps you catch trouble early.

Sun and Heat

Valley summers bake roofing materials year after year. Shingles curl, coatings crack, and sealants dry out long before their time.

Winter Rain

When the rain finally comes, it finds every weak spot the summer made. A roof that looked fine in August can leak by January.
