Our story

Built on service

The short version of who Pro South Construction Services, LLC is and how we got here.

Peachtree City, Georgia

A Georgia construction company

Pro South Construction Services, LLC is based at 250 Parkade Ct in Peachtree City, Georgia, serving commercial, industrial, and institutional clients across the region. We are builders first: the people who answer the phone are the people who understand the work.

Veteran-owned

Service-disabled veteran-owned

The company is a service-disabled veteran-owned small business, listed in the VA VetBiz database (per a 2011 U.S. Government Accountability Office bid protest decision). Military discipline shows up in how we run a job: clear plans, accountable crews, and finished work that holds up.

Federal experience

Federal facility project work

Through its Pro South-Emcon joint venture, the company pursued Department of Veterans Affairs contracts for air handler unit replacements at the VA Medical Center in Memphis, Tennessee (U.S. GAO, 2011). Federal work demands documentation, compliance, and precision, and that is how we build everything.
