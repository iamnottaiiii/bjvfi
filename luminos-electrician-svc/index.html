Electrical repairs and troubleshooting

Outlets that stopped working, breakers that keep tripping, lights that flicker, and partial outages. We diagnose the cause and fix it, then verify the circuit is sound before we leave.

Panel upgrades and replacements

Old fuse boxes and 60-amp panels cannot keep up with modern homes. We size and install replacement panels so your service handles today is load with room to grow.

Lighting, fans, and fixtures

Recessed cans, pendant lights, chandeliers, dimmer switches, and ceiling fans. We handle the wiring, mounting, and finish work so it looks like it was always there.

Smoke, CO, and safety devices

Hardwired smoke and carbon monoxide detectors with battery backup, placed where they actually protect you, plus GFCI and AFCI protection where code calls for it.

Small commercial work

Troubleshooting, lighting retrofits, dedicated circuits, and maintenance for offices, retail, and rental properties around Harrisburg.
