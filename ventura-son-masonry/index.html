Our story

A father, a son, and a trowel.

Ventura & Son Masonry started the way most good trades do: one mason teaching the next. The name on the truck is the family behind it, and the family behind it is on every job.

We are based at 8301 Dempsey Ave in North Hills, and most of our work is right here in the San Fernando Valley. Brick walls that straighten a yard. Chimneys that draw right. Patios that sit level year after year.

We bid plainly, show up when we say we will, and leave the site swept. That is the whole business plan.

Plumb and level, every course

No leaning walls, no wavy joints. If a course is off, it comes down and goes back up.

Written bids, no surprises

You see the scope and the price before we lift a trowel.

Clean jobsites

We sweep daily and haul debris when the job is done.

Family accountability

The name on the bid is the family answering the phone.
