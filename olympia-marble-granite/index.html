What we do

Fabrication and installation services.

Olympia Marble & Granite specializes in fabrication and installation of fine stones, quartz, and manufactured stone. Pick a category to see what the shop handles.

Natural stone countertops

Fabrication and installation of fine natural stone, cut to template and finished in the shop. Olympia works with customer-supplied slabs as well as stone from its own stock.

  • Granite countertops
  • Marble countertops and vanities
  • Quartzite slabs and remnants
  • Limestone and slate surfaces
  • Stone flooring and thresholds
  • Fireplace surrounds

Engineered and manufactured stone

Man-made stones for kitchens and baths where consistency and low maintenance matter. Olympia lists Caesarstone, Zodiaq, Silestone, and Pental among the lines it works with.

  • Caesarstone countertops
  • Zodiaq surfaces
  • Silestone countertops
  • Pental quartz surfaces
  • Quartz vanity tops
  • Quartz shower and tub surrounds

Shop and field services

From templating to the final polish, the family crew handles the full job in the shop and on site. Languages spoken include English, Greek, and Spanish.

  • Templating and design help
  • Fabrication from your slab or ours
  • Installation on site
  • Countertop cut, resize, and replacement
  • Remnant pieces for small projects
  • Repair follow-up after installation

Our story

Three decades of stone in Van Nuys.

Olympia Marble & Granite has been listed in the Blue Book of building and construction since 1993, serving Los Angeles and the surrounding counties from its Keswick Street shop.

The shop sits in the Van Nuys stone corridor on Keswick Street, among fabricators and slab yards, close to the 405 and 101 freeways. Yellow Pages lists the facility at more than 10,000 square feet and the business at 38 years in the trade.

Reviewers describe a family-operated company. The themes repeat: knowledgeable staff who answer new questions without rushing, quality workmanship in the shop and during installation, and a proactive response when something needs correcting.

"Costa and team are amazing. Highly recommend for all your stone needs. Very accommodating, responsive, and really nice."

Based on 5 Birdeye reviews, the pattern is consistent: people bring slabs from elsewhere or pick from Olympia stock, the crew templates and fabricates with care, and the install crew gets it right. One reviewer had a quartz countertop cut, resized, and replaced in a single day during a garden window installation, in 116-degree heat, and the fit was perfect.
