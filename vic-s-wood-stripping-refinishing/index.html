Our story

Vic built the shop around one idea: do it right.

Vic's Wood Stripping & Refinishing has served Northridge from its shop on Napa Street for years, earning an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau along the way. The business grew the old-fashioned way: one satisfied customer telling the next.

Walk in with a piece and Vic will tell you straight what it needs, what it will cost, and when it will be done. That honesty is why customers keep coming back for years and keep sending their friends.

The shop handles the full journey: stripping away decades of old finish, repairing what time broke, then staining and sealing the wood so it looks better than the day it was made.
