What we do

Custom precast, from mold to installation.

We are a Southern California company with three decades in the making. Everything is customized and upgraded on residential and commercial properties using precast stone, and we can make almost any of our products to a customer’s specifications. We do not limit products to a fixed color or size.

From production to installation, we handle the full arc: design, in-house molds, casting, and the finishing work on site. Builders and homeowners across Los Angeles County and the San Fernando Valley trust us when the details have to look right.

Why precast

The look of carved stone, without the carved-stone price.

Precast stone gives you crisp profiles and consistent color at a fraction of natural stone cost, and because we cast in house, custom shapes are routine, not special orders.

Our story

Thirty years of stone, one standard.

Paramount Precast Inc is a Sun Valley, California company with thirty years in the making. We work hand in hand with companies and individuals who want to improve the look of their properties, using precast stone customized for each project.

Our pride is in two things: the best quality of products, and customers who are happy with the work. We can make almost any of our products to a customer’s specifications, with no limits on color or size. The customer is our first priority. If they are not happy, we are not happy.

Owner Jeff works directly with customers on designs, including full custom fireplace projects, and the team carries each job from production through installation.
