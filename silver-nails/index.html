Dependable nail care, every visit

A straightforward salon that focuses on finishing the job well.

Silver Nails keeps it simple: clean shaping, careful cuticle work, and color that goes on even. Guests praise the dip powder sets and neat, natural-looking styles, plus French ombre and French toes for a clean, classic finish.

The team is known for fitting people in. Even later in the day, clients say they are not rushed, and walk-ins with irregular schedules report waits of around 15 minutes or less.

With 6 to 10 technicians on staff, there is usually a chair open. Call ahead for large parties or nail art appointments.
