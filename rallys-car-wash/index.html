How it works

Two ways to get clean

Pick the bay that fits your mood: take the wheel yourself or ride through hands-free.

Self-Service Bays

Coin and card operated bays with presoak, high-pressure soap, foam brush, and spot-free rinse. Great for a careful, hands-on wash.

Drive-through touchless wash. Pick your cycle and the equipment does the rest, with no brushes touching your paint.
