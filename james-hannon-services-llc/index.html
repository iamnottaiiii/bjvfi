James Hannon Services, LLC is a construction company based in Walton, Kentucky, working as a home builder across Boone County and Northern Kentucky. The company is built around a simple belief: a home is only as good as the care put into building it.

That means straight answers on cost, steady hands on the tools, and a finished home the builder is proud to put a name on. No corporate layers, no disappearing acts, just a local builder doing the work properly.
