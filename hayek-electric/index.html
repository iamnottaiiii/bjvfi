Circuit 01

Troubleshooting & repairs

Electrical problems rarely announce themselves all at once. They start small: a breaker that trips on laundry day, a switch plate that runs warm. We trace the fault to its source instead of patching symptoms.

  • Breakers that trip or will not reset
  • Dead or intermittent outlets and circuits
  • Flickering or dimming lights under load
  • Buzzing, crackling, or burning smells near devices

Circuit 02

Panels & breakers

Older panels were not built for modern loads. If your panel is maxed out, uses outdated breakers, or double-taps circuits, an upgrade is the safest money you can spend on a house.

  • Full panel upgrades and replacements
  • Breaker replacement and AFCI/GFCI protection
  • New dedicated circuits for appliances and workshops
  • Subpanels for garages, additions, and outbuildings
