Anatomy of a good pour

Every slab we set follows the same stack: compacted base, steel reinforcement, formed edges, finished surface.

How a pour goes

Four steps, same order, every job. No shortcuts in the base means no cracks in the surface.

01

Site prep and forms

Grade, excavation, and rock-solid forms set to exact line and fall for drainage.

02

Base and steel

Compacted gravel base with rebar or mesh where the load calls for it.

03

Pour and finish

Placed, screeded, and finished to your choice: broom, smooth, or decorative.

04

Cure and seal

Proper cure time and an optional sealer so the surface stands up to freeze and thaw.
