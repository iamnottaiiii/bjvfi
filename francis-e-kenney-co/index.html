Our story

Longevity is a reputation you repaint every day.

Francis E Kenney & Co was established in 1953 in Woburn, Massachusetts. For more than seventy years the company has served homeowners, businesses and institutions across New England, building a record that earned it a listing in the Blue Book, the construction industry's directory, in 1987.

Today the company is family run, with owner Neil Kenney at 16 Union St in Woburn. The work spans interior and exterior painting, commercial repainting, wallcoverings and specialty finishes, and maintenance programs that keep buildings looking their best year after year.

In the trades, companies do not last seven decades by accident. They last by preparing properly, finishing cleanly, and standing behind the work.
