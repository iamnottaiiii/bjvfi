Our story

A neighborhood shop, run like family

Rutsos Barber Shop sits on Union Avenue in Rutherford, and it has been there for regulars through a decade of haircuts. The shop is run by Ruth, a barber reviewers praise for taking her time, treating customers like family, and mastering the scissor cut that is getting harder to find.

Appointments help you plan your day around your cut, and walk-ins are welcome whenever there is a free chair. Kids get patient, friendly service, and yes, Lucy the shop dog will be happy to see you.

Fair prices, reliable scheduling, and a cut you will still like in three weeks. That is the whole business model.
