Our Story

A Fayetteville favorite for friendly, honest hair care.

Salon Salon has served the Fayetteville community from its East Genesee Street location for years. Guests keep returning for the same reasons reviewers mention again and again: a welcoming atmosphere, skilled stylists and service that feels personal rather than rushed.

Whether it is a routine trim, a color refresh or styling for a special day, the focus stays on what suits you: your hair type, your routine and the look you want to walk out with.
