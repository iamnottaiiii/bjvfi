Our Story

A Main Street dealership where the same people who sell you the car can fix it too.

One Lot, Two Businesses, One Team

Fast Lane Auto Sales & Services has been serving North Tonawanda from 250 Main St with a simple formula: sell solid used vehicles, then stand behind them with a real repair shop on the same lot. Customers buy here because they know exactly who will service their car.

Visit Sue, Stu, and the staff for sales or service. Our repeat customers are the best proof of how we work.

Service First, Always

Our repair facility runs on ASE certified mechanics and the latest diagnostic equipment. Engine and transmission work, brakes, electrical, air conditioning, 4WD systems, steering and suspension, NYS inspections, tires, and oil changes: major and minor repairs, all in house.

  • Inspected & Warranted VehiclesEvery used vehicle is NYS inspected and warranted before it hits the lot.
  • ASE Certified MechanicsFactory-level training and modern diagnostic equipment in our service bays.
  • Financing AvailableWe offer financing on our used vehicles to get you driving sooner.
