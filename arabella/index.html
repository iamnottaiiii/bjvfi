Our story

Where beauty meets precision

The Salon

Arabella Hair Studio describes itself with a short credo: where beauty meets precision. Luxury hair, personalized style. The team works closely with each client so the finished look feels unmistakably theirs.

Inside, the space is warm and inviting: elegant decor, soft lighting, plush seating and a relaxed, comfortable atmosphere where guests can unwind with a complimentary coffee or tea during their visit.

The salon is a local favorite in Skaneateles for haircuts, color, styling, waxing and spa treatments, including bridal hair and makeup for weddings and special events.
