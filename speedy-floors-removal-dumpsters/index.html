Our Story

The crew contractors call first

Speedy Floors Removal & Dumpsters started with a simple observation in Canoga Park: every remodel gets stuck at the same stage. The new floors are picked out, the installer is scheduled, and then the old floor refuses to come up on time.

We built a company that does one thing better than anyone else in the San Fernando Valley. We remove floors fast, we scrape them clean, and we haul everything away in our own dumpsters. No subcontractor shuffle, no debris sitting in your driveway for a week.

One crew, one call, one clean slab. That is the whole business model.

Today our trucks run across the Valley every day serving homeowners, flooring installers, general contractors, and property managers. Many of our contractor clients have worked with us for years and call us a dependable asset to their teams.
