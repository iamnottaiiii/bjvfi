How an S.O.S. call works

One call. Four moves.

Water damage is a race against the clock. Here is exactly what happens after you dial.

You call, we roll

Day or night, you talk to the crew, not a call center. We ask where the water is and head your way.

We find every drop

Moisture meters and a trained eye map how far the water traveled, inside walls, under floors, behind cabinets.

We extract and dry

Pumps pull the standing water, then air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure to verified safe levels.

We document it all

Photos, moisture logs, and a clear record of the work, ready for your insurance adjuster.

We restore the space

Cleaning, sanitizing, odor control, and repairs that bring the room back to how it was.

We verify it is dry

The job is not done until readings confirm safe moisture levels throughout. No guessing.

Services

Everything a flood touches, we handle

Emergency water restoration for homes and businesses across the Five Towns. Here is the full scope.

01

Emergency Water Extraction

The first priority is always the same: get the water out. We bring the pumps and the crew, and we start the moment we arrive.

  • Basement flood pump-outs
  • Burst pipe response
  • Appliance and water heater leaks
  • Contents protection
02

Structural Drying

Surfaces dry fast; structures do not. We place commercial dehumidifiers and air movers and keep them running until moisture meters say the job is done.

  • Moisture mapping
  • Commercial dehumidification
  • Hardwood and subfloor drying
  • Daily monitoring logs
03

Mold Remediation

Water that sits becomes mold. We contain the area, remove what cannot be saved, HEPA-filter the air, and treat what remains.

  • Containment setup
  • Affected material removal
  • HEPA air scrubbing
  • Antimicrobial treatment
04

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Contaminated water is a health hazard, not a DIY job. We remove it safely, disinfect thoroughly, and restore the area to sanitary condition.

  • Safe waste extraction
  • Full disinfection
  • Odor neutralization
  • Material disposal
05

Storm and Flood Recovery

Coastal storms push water where it does not belong. We handle the aftermath: extraction, drying, and protection against the next wave.

  • Flood water removal
  • Roof and window leak drying
  • Debris clearing
  • Temporary protection
06

Cleanup and Restoration

After the drying comes the return to normal: sanitizing, deodorizing, and repairing damaged finishes so the space feels like yours again.

  • Sanitizing and deodorizing
  • Drywall and flooring repair
  • Paint and finish work
  • Final walkthrough
