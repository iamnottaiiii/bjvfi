About Bish Transportation Inc

A Pennsylvania trucking company running out of Midway, in Washington County. No corporate gloss, just trucks, drivers, and freight.

Bish Transportation Inc operates from 158 Midway Candor Rd in Midway, PA 15060. Public FMCSA records list the company as an active for-hire carrier running intrastate, non-hazardous freight with 13 power units and 12 drivers.
