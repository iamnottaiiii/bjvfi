Our story

A Staten Island chair with a simple promise

What we do

Vidalys Beauty Salon is a hair extension and styling salon on Targee Street in Stapleton, Staten Island. Our focus is straightforward: healthy hair first, then the length, color, and style you came in dreaming about.

Extension work is a craft, and it starts with listening. We talk through your hair history, your routine, and your goal, then match the method to you. No one-size-fits-all wefts. No rushed chairs.

What to expect

  • Consultation first for every extension service
  • Shade matching so extensions blend invisibly
  • Honest advice on what your hair can carry
  • Aftercare guidance so your look lasts
