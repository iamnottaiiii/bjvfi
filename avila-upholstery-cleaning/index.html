Why professional cleaning

What sits under the surface

Upholstery looks clean long after it stops being clean. Dust mites, pollen, cooking oils, and ground-in soil settle below the surface, dulling color and wearing fibers from the inside. Regular vacuuming helps. It does not extract.

A professional deep clean lifts embedded soil, refreshes color, and can ease the musty smell older pieces develop. For allergy-sensitive homes, it makes the room feel noticeably lighter.

Services

Every piece, its own treatment

Upholstery is not one material. Microfiber, cotton blends, velvet, and leather each need their own approach. We identify the fabric first, then clean accordingly.

01

Sofas and sectionals

The centerpiece of the living room takes the most abuse: snacks, naps, pets, guests. Our deep clean works through cushions, arms, backs, and bases, extracting ground-in soil and refreshing the fabric from the weave up. Sectionals are quoted by section so you pay for what you have, nothing more.

Most sofas dry within a few hours and are ready for movie night the same day.
Sample price: from $99 for a standard three seat sofa
02

Armchairs and recliners

Favorite chairs collect body oils on armrests and headrests, the spots that darken first. We give high-contact areas extra attention, treat the whole chair evenly so there are no clean patches, and finish with a fabric rinse that leaves no residue.

Sample price: from $49 per chair
03

Dining chairs and ottomans

Dining chair seats catch everything the table misses. Ottomans double as footrests and extra seating. Both get a thorough but gentle clean that lifts food spots and everyday grime without soaking the padding underneath, so drying stays quick.

Sample price: from $15 per dining chair, from $29 per ottoman
04

Spot and odor treatment

One stubborn stain or a lingering smell does not always mean a full cleaning. Targeted spot treatment addresses coffee, wine, ink, and pet accidents, while odor treatment neutralizes smells instead of masking them. Honest note: some old or heat-set stains lighten but do not vanish, and we will tell you which before we start.

Sample price: from $29 per area
05

Mattresses and headboards

You spend a third of your life on it. Mattress cleaning removes dust mites, dead skin, and sweat buildup from the sleep surface and sides, and upholstered headboards get the same careful treatment as living room pieces. A cleaner mattress simply sleeps better.

Sample price: from $79 for a queen mattress
06

Fabric protector

After cleaning, an optional fabric protector adds an invisible barrier that makes future spills bead up instead of soaking in. It buys you time to blot before a stain sets, which is the difference between a scare and a permanent mark. Best applied right after a deep clean.

Sample price: from $25 per piece, added to any cleaning
