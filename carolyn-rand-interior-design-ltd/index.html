About the studio

Carolyn Rand Interior Design, Ltd is led by Carolyn R. Rand, ASID, NCIDQ certified, in Dublin, Ohio.

Her background includes a degree in art education, interior design study at the Columbus College of Art and Design, and more than thirty years of design experience.

The studio’s stated focus: space planning, lighting design, architectural detailing and furnishings that reflect each client’s personality.
