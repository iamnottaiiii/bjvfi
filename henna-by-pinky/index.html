About Pinky

The artist behind the mehndi

Pinky is a mehndi designer in Bellmawr, NJ, known for creating beautiful and creative henna designs for weddings, special events, and everyday beauty. Her passion for henna shows in every line: clients describe her work as highly detailed, and her freestyle designs as always perfect.

What keeps people coming back is the person as much as the art. Reviewers praise her kindness, respectfulness, and efficiency, and many describe their visit as a heavenly experience. Guests are often welcomed with delicious tea and samosas, which is why so many call themselves her forever customers.
