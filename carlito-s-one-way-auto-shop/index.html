Welcome to Carlito's One Way Auto Shop

Why drivers in Belleville choose us

Carlito's One Way Auto Shop on Belmont Avenue in Belleville, NJ is the kind of repair shop built on repeat customers. The idea is simple: diagnose it once, fix it right, and send you on your way with a car you can trust.

From brakes and oil changes to the deeper mechanical work, every job gets the same careful approach. Call for hours and bring the car by; you will get a straight answer and a fair price.

  • Fixed right the first time: Careful diagnosis up front means no repeat visits for the same problem.
  • Fair, clear pricing: Estimates explained in plain language before any work begins.
  • Full-service repairs: Brakes, maintenance, diagnostics, and major mechanical work.
