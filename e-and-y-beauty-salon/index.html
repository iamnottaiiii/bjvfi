About

16th Ave's everyday barber shop

E&Y Beauty Salon sits in the heart of Bensonhurst at 7209 16th Ave, serving the block with straightforward barbering. It is listed as a barber shop with beauty salon and hair salon services, which means the chair covers the whole household, not just one kind of client.

No gimmicks: honest cuts, fair prices, and hours that actually fit a working week. Open daily from 9:30 AM to 7:30 PM, so early risers and after-work regulars are both covered.

  • Bensonhurst location on 16th Ave
  • Barber shop plus beauty and hair salon services
