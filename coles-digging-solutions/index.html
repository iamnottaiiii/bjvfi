Our story

A local crew that digs in

Cole's Digging Solutions llc is an earth works company based in Covington, Kentucky. We started with one machine and a simple idea: do honest digging work for neighbors, and treat every property like it is our own.

Today we serve homeowners, builders, and landowners across Northern Kentucky with excavation, grading, land clearing, drainage, and site prep. We keep the operation lean so estimates stay fair and the work stays personal.

When you call, you talk to the people doing the work. When we leave, the site is clean and the grade is right. That is the whole business model.
