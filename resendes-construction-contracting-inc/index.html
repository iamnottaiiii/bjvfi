Our services

Built right, inside and out

Siding, tile, painting, and general home improvement for Hudson, MA area homeowners. Every job starts with a written estimate.

01Siding InstallationExterior

Full siding replacement that protects your home from wind, rain, and winter, and gives the whole house a clean, updated look. We help you choose a style and color that fits the home and the neighborhood.

02Siding RepairExterior

Storm damage, warped boards, loose panels, and problem spots around windows and trim. Targeted repairs that blend in and stop small issues from becoming big ones.

03Tile WorkInterior

Kitchen backsplashes, bathroom walls and floors, and entryways. Laid level, grouted clean, sealed right, the details you notice every day.

04Interior PaintingInterior

Rooms, ceilings, trim, and doors. Proper prep, sharp lines, and low-mess work so your home stays livable while we paint.

05Exterior PaintingExterior

Siding, trim, and porches prepped and painted to stand up to New England weather. Power washing, scraping, priming, and two-coat finishes.

06Home Improvement & RemodelingGeneral

Small renovations, repairs, and upgrades around the house. If it needs fixing, freshening, or finishing, call and ask. We will tell you straight whether it is in our wheelhouse.

About us

A Hudson contractor
you can reach

Resendes Construction Contracting Inc is based at 213 Main St in Hudson, Massachusetts, serving local homeowners with siding, tile, painting, and home improvement work.

Local, reachable, accountable

We are not a call center and we are not a franchise. When you hire Resendes Construction Contracting Inc, you get a local contracting company that picks up the phone, shows up when it says it will, and stands behind the work.

Our bread and butter is the building envelope and the finished surfaces: siding that keeps weather out, tile laid straight, paint that looks sharp for years. The kind of work you live with every day, so we do it like we will see it every day. Because around here, we probably will.

Straight talk

Written estimates, honest timelines, and plain answers when something is not in our scope. We would rather lose a job than oversell one.

Clean sites

Drop cloths, daily cleanup, and a final walkthrough with you. Your home is a home, not a construction zone.

One point of contact

The person who estimates your job is the person you can call during it. No phone tag across three offices.
