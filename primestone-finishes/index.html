Our story

A Pasadena studio with a craftsman streak

The name says it

PrimeStone Finishes started with a simple idea: a paint job should be built like stonework. Solid at the base, precise in the details, and meant to last. From our shop on South Lake Avenue in Pasadena, we take on the projects other painters rush: the cracked stucco, the sun-bleached siding, the kitchen cabinets that need a second life.

We are a small crew, and we like it that way. The person who walks your project with you is the person who stands behind the finished work.

CA

Rooted in Pasadena

We work across Pasadena and the San Gabriel Valley, painting everything from craftsman bungalows to modern stucco homes. Local jobs mean we are nearby if you ever need a touch-up down the road.

06

Open six to eight, every day

Our posted hours run 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily, so early birds and after-work planners can both reach us. Prefer to talk in person? Call ahead and we will set a time at the shop.

What we promise

Honest color advice, thorough prep, premium paint, clean job sites, and a final walkthrough on every project. If something is not right, we make it right. That is the whole business model.
