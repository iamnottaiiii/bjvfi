Our process

How a project runs

Remodeling is stressful when communication is vague. The process here is built around the things reviewers actually praised: honest talk, detailed plans, and staying ahead of schedule.

What reviewers keep saying

  • Dependable and honest throughout the project
  • Detail oriented, spots issues in plans early
  • Ahead of schedule, not scrambling at the end
  • Design advice on colors and finishes
STEP 1

Walkthrough and honest numbers

It starts with a call and an on-site look. You describe what you want; the contractor tells you what it will really take, including anything your plans might be missing. One reviewer noted plan issues were spotted before they became expensive problems.

STEP 2

Plans, permits, and selections

Scope, materials, and timeline in writing. For ADUs and larger jobs, that includes help preparing and submitting correction plans to the city. You also get extra time to choose finishes and materials, something reviewers called out as a real plus.

STEP 3

The build

A skilled, experienced crew does the work while keeping the jobsite orderly. Reviewers describe the team as easy to work with and focused, with projects staying ahead of schedule rather than behind it.

STEP 4

Final walkthrough

You walk the finished space together and build a punch list. The job is done when the details are right, from soft-close drawers to the last dab of paint.
