About Ez2tow & Repair

Ez2tow & Repair pairs towing with a working repair shop, so a breakdown does not have to mean two phone calls. The team tows your vehicle in and gets to work on the fix, keeping the whole process simple from pickup to keys back.

  • Towing Vehicle towing for breakdowns and accidents in and around Orange.
  • Roadside Assistance Help when you are stuck: jump starts, fuel delivery, and more.
  • Jump Starts Dead battery? A quick jump gets you moving again.
  • Tire Changes Flat tire swapped for your spare at the roadside.
  • Lockout Help Locked out of your car? Fast, damage-free entry.
  • General Repair Brakes, diagnostics, and maintenance once your car is in the shop.
