AWho we are

Landscaping and cleaning,
on purpose

DND Landscaping works out of 693 Western Ave in Lynn, Massachusetts, serving homeowners and property managers across the North Shore. We are listed as a landscaper, a gardener, and a cleaning service, because that is genuinely what the crew does.

The idea is simple: a yard job is not finished when the last branch falls. It is finished when the lawn is cut, the beds are edged, the hedges are shaped, and every last leaf and clipping is gone. That is the standard we hold ourselves to on every visit.

Call us for routine lawn care, a one-time cleanup, a garden refresh, or the kind of whole-property reset that makes neighbors slow down as they drive by.

We cut it, we plant it, we trim it, and then we clean it all up. When we leave, it looks like someone cares.
