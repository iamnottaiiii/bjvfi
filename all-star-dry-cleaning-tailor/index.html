About

A small shop with
forty-year customers

All Star Dry Cleaning & Tailor is a small, family owned shop on Massachusetts Ave in Arlington, the kind of place people drive past three other cleaners to reach.

Why neighbors stay

Care you can feel at pickup

Review themes come back to the same things: friendly, knowledgeable staff who go above and beyond, alterations that come back perfect, and prices that stay reasonable. One customer writes that after forty years, the alterations were always perfect, whether lengthening sweatpants, changing buttons, or adjusting a waistline.

It is the rare cleaner where the tailor is the draw. Quick, affordable repairs sit next to meticulous restoration work, like the wedding dress one reviewer says arrived in rough shape and left beautifully restored.

Good to know

Straight answers, fair prices

Not every request is a yes. One reviewer notes the shop declined a taper job they did not think would work, rather than taking the money anyway. That honesty is part of why people trust the counter.

Hours are built for working people: open at 7 AM on weekdays, Saturday mornings too, so drop off and pickup fit around the workday. Credit cards accepted.
