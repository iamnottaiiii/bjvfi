About Us

A Family Roofing Company Since 1994

Pro-Tek Exterior Services, Inc. is a family owned and operated commercial roofing company based in Cincinnati, Ohio. We have been in business since 1994, serving commercial property owners across the Cincinnati area and Hamilton County.

Our specialty is single-ply membrane roofing. We are proud to be a Duro-Last Elite Master Contractor, and Duro-Last is the product we have used successfully for many years. Our repeat customers are our testimony.
