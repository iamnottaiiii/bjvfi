About Louis Todd

An excavating contractor rooted in Pacoima, working job sites across the Valley and Los Angeles.

Dirt work is the foundation of every build

Louis Todd Corporation is listed as an excavation and site preparation contractor at 11048 Telfair Ave in Pacoima, California. The trade covers the unglamorous but essential work that every build stands on: clearing the land, cutting the grade, digging the trenches and footings, and hauling away what does not belong.

For homeowners, that can mean a level pad for an addition or a trench for a new line. For builders and developers, it means a full site delivered clean and ready for the next trade. Either way, the job starts with a phone call and a walk of the site.
