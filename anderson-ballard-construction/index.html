Services

Construction, end to end.

Every project follows the same five steps, from discovery to warranty, so you always know what happens next.

Discovery and Estimate

Walk the site, talk goals and budget, and get a clear, written estimate before anything is designed.

Design and Permitting

Plans are drawn and shepherded through city permitting, handled by people who know the local process.

Pre-Construction Planning

Schedules, trades, and materials are locked in up front, so the build phase runs on rails.

Build and Inspections

Licensed crews build to plan, with inspections passed and progress reported along the way.

Final Walkthrough and Warranty

You walk every room before sign-off, and the work is backed after the keys change hands.

Our Story

Close to a century in the making.

Look around Greater Los Angeles and Orange County and you will see Anderson Ballard Companies at work. Family owned and operated, the group has spent the better part of a hundred years building, developing, selling, and managing real estate across Southern California.

How We Work

People first, then property.

The firm prioritizes the needs of its employees, clients, investors, and tenants, and stays close to an ever-changing market so every project, from a four-unit renovation to a ground-up spec home, is built on current knowledge and long experience.

Headquartered at 1313 Foothill Blvd in La Cañada Flintridge, the team is in the office Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and on the job sites in between.
