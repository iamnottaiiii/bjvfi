Our story

A Fort Thomas shop, built on call-backs

Brothers Electric Co. works out of Fort Thomas, Kentucky, serving the homes and small businesses of Campbell County. The name says it plainly: this is a shop built like a family trade, where the electrician who quotes your job is the one who shows up to do it.

A lot of the housing stock around here, from the historic streets of Fort Thomas to the river towns of Newport and Bellevue, runs on wiring installed decades ago. That is the bread and butter: panels that need room to breathe, circuits that were never meant for modern loads, and lighting that makes an old house feel new again.

  • Show up when we say.Appointments are kept. If a job runs long, you hear about it before, not after.
  • Explain before we bill.You will understand what failed, what we are doing about it, and what it costs, in that order.
  • Leave it better.Labeled breakers, tested circuits, clean floors. The next electrician who opens your panel should smile.
