Our story

Three decades of dependable painting

Minogue Brothers Inc is a painting contractor based in Hanover, Maryland. Our mission is simple: provide both builders and homeowners with consistently high quality, dependable painting.

How we work

We combine skilled craftsmen and quality products with competitive pricing and total commitment to on time scheduling. That formula has kept builders, commercial property owners and homeowners coming back for more than thirty years.

Who we serve

Homeowners, national and regional builders with multiple subdivisions, custom homebuilders, commercial property owners and home improvement contractors.

Where we work

Baltimore County and Harford County, with a focus on Bel Air and Havre de Grace, plus Baltimore City, Howard County, Carroll County, Anne Arundel County, Cecil County, and New Freedom and York County, PA.
