Lab notes

Insulation questions, answered generally.

General industry information, not advice from this contractor. Sample info

What is R-value?
R-value measures resistance to heat flow. Higher numbers mean better insulating performance. Total R-value depends on material type and installed thickness.
Why does air sealing come before insulation?
Air leaks move heat around insulation, so even thick insulation underperforms if air bypasses it. Sealing gaps first lets the insulation do its rated job.
How do I know my attic needs more insulation?
Common signs include uneven room temperatures, ice dams in winter, high heating or cooling bills, and attic insulation that sits below the tops of the joists. A contractor can confirm with an inspection.

Why insulation matters General info

Heating and cooling a leaky envelope wastes energy every day of the year. Insulation slows heat flow, air sealing stops bypasses, and together they steady indoor temperatures. The specifics for any home depend on its construction, which is why a contractor visit matters.
