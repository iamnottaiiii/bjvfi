Our story

Clinton's corner
for honest car care

Safe Auto Repair grew around a simple idea: treat every car like it is carrying your family, because it usually is.

Mechanics first,
salespeople never

Walk into Safe Auto Repair on Old Branch Ave and you will find a working shop, not a showroom. Lifts up, hoods open, techs who would rather show you the worn part than sell you a package.

That approach is why the shop's own description leads with three things: experienced mechanics, fast service and good prices. It is also why drivers across Clinton and southern Prince George's County trust the shop with the cars their families ride in every day.

German, American or Japanese, daily driver or work truck, the standard is the same: diagnose honestly, repair properly, charge fairly.

Safe
is in the name for a reason

Every repair is about one thing: getting you and your family home safely, in a car you can trust.
