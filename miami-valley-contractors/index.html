  • Building pads cut to grade
  • Swales and drainage shaping
  • Final grade ready for seed or sod

Foundations & Footers

Footer and foundation excavation dug square, deep, and ready for the pour for homes, additions, garages, and commercial shells.
  • Footer digs to spec
  • Full foundation excavations
  • Basement and crawlspace prep

Site Work & Development

Complete site development packages. We work with owners and project managers to take a raw parcel to build-ready in one coordinated schedule.
  • Clearing to final grade, one contract
  • Coordination with other trades
  • Commercial and residential parcels

Pond excavation and shaping for farm ponds, decorative water features, and retention. Dug with proper depth and shelf for the long haul.

Dug In Since 1982

Miami Valley Contractors was formed in 1982 by Ken Worley and has grown steadily ever since, handling residential and commercial construction projects of any size across the Cincinnati region.

The pitch is simple: a related set of earthwork services under one roof creates a real scheduling advantage and a cost-effective alternative to juggling multiple subcontractors. One call covers the grading, the trench, the foundation dig, and the haul-off.

The company works directly with both project management teams and property owners, and can outline and implement a complete site development package from raw ground to build-ready.
