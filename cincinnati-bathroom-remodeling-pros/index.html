How it works

A simple, predictable process

Remodeling feels stressful when communication breaks down. We keep it simple: one point of contact, a written scope, and updates as the work moves.

Talk it through

Call or send a message with what you want changed. We ask questions, look at photos if you have them, and set a time to measure.

Measure and plan

We visit, measure the space, and talk through layout, materials, and the level of finish you want.

Written scope

You get a clear written scope and price before anything is scheduled. No surprises mid-project.

Build and walkthrough

We remodel, keep the site clean, and finish with a walkthrough so everything is right before we leave.

Good to know

Before you call

It helps to know your rough budget range, whether you want to keep the same layout or move fixtures, and any materials you already love. Photos of your current bathroom and a few inspiration pictures make the first conversation much more productive.

  • Same-layout updates cost less than moving plumbing
  • Pick fixtures and tile before demo day when possible
  • Ask about waterproofing behind tile, it matters most
  • Plan for a second bathroom during the work
