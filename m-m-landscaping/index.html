Our story

Rooted in Granada Hills for generations

M & M Landscaping has been part of the neighborhood landscape for decades, growing along with the Valley it serves.

How we grew

M & M Landscaping started as a local Granada Hills crew and grew the old-fashioned way: one well-kept yard at a time. Today the company handles everything from weekly lawn routes to complete landscape renovations.

The formula never changed. Show up on time, treat every garden like it is your own, and leave the customer smiling. Flowers, bushes, and trees are professionally handled to create landscapes that attract and inspire, and prompt, excellent customer service is the whole point.
