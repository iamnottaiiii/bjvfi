How we work

Straight answers about your roof

Roofing has a trust problem, and we know it. So here is our whole pitch: we come out, we look at the roof, we tell you what it needs and what it does not, and we give you a price in writing.

If the roof is fine, we will tell you that too. The best roofing job is the one you do not need yet.

What to ask any roofer

Including us. A good contractor welcomes these questions:

  • Are you licensed and insured in California?
  • Will the estimate be in writing, itemized?
  • Who is actually on my roof, your crew or a sub?
  • What warranty comes with the work?
