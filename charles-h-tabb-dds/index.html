Services

The notes below describe general dentistry in general terms. Call the office to ask which treatments are offered here.

  • 1

    Checkups and cleanings

    Routine exams and professional cleanings help prevent cavities and gum disease and catch problems early. Ask the office about scheduling.

  • 2

    Fillings

    Small areas of decay are commonly repaired with tooth-colored or amalgam fillings. Call to ask what restorative care this office provides.

  • 3

    Crowns and bridges

    Damaged or missing teeth are often restored with crowns or bridges that look and work like natural teeth. Ask the office about your options.

  • 4

    Gum health

    Bleeding gums and persistent bad breath deserve attention. General dentists evaluate gum health and recommend care. Call to ask.

  • 5

    Toothaches and emergencies

    Dental pain should not wait. If you have an urgent problem, call (410) 339-7077 right away to ask about prompt care.
