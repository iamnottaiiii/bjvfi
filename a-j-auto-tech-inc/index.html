About Us

A father and son shop in Newark

A & J Auto Tech Inc. is a family-run auto repair shop at 210 Ridge St in Newark, NJ. The shop is run by a father and son, and that shows in how the work gets done: personally, carefully, and with your budget in mind.

Customers describe the owner, Manny, as polite, trustworthy, and straight about what a car actually needs. One driver with a Lexus said she is picky about who works under her hood, and A & J became her go-to shop. Another said that in all her visits she never paid a price that felt too high or unfair.

The shop's own description is simple: we do computer diagnostic, all work guaranteed, it is a full service auto repair shop. No upsell games, no mystery charges, just the repair your car needs.

Open Monday through Friday 8 AM to 6 PM and Saturday 8 AM to 5 PM. Call (973) 991-3459 to book.
