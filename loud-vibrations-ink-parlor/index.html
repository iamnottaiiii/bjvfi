The shop

Not a quiet shop.
A loud one.

High energy, zero snobbery. Bring your wildest idea and leave with ink that hits different.

Loud vibrations ink parlor is Chester's spot for tattoos and piercings with personality. This is not a sterile, hushed studio. It is a place with the volume turned up, where artists take your idea seriously no matter how wild it sounds.

Custom work is the core of the shop: one of a kind designs drawn for your body, your story, your vibe. Piercings round out the menu, done with clean technique and straight up aftercare advice.

First timers welcome. The crew is known for being friendly and unpretentious, so ask every question you have.
