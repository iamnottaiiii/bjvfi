01The Machines

Built for real laundry day, from weekly loads to the comforters that never fit at home.

With 30 washers and 30 dryers on the floor, waits stay short even on busy weekends. The machines handle everything from daily clothes to king-size comforters, blankets, and sleeping bags, and the larger dryers finish bulky loads in one go.

Full-time attendants keep the machines and the space clean, and help when a cycle needs a hand. Machines take cards as well as coins, and free WiFi plus folding space make the whole routine easy.

Forgot the detergent? The convenience store up front has it, along with snacks and drinks for the wait.

02The Store

It is the small things regulars mention: a clean floor, change available, staff at the counter, and everything you need for laundry day under one roof on N Main St.

Our story

Two businesses,
one friendly stop

What we know about J Convenience Store & Laundromat comes from the business itself and the people who use it every week.

01The Facts

A family-owned stop on N Main St

Local directories describe J Convenience Store & Laundromat as a family-owned business, listed as established in 2009, sitting on Route 9 and 27 near Stop & Shop. The laundromat side runs 30 washers and 30 dryers, and the business notes full-time employees on hand to keep the place clean and friendly.

Up front, the convenience store rounds out the stop: laundry supplies, snacks and drinks, lottery and Keno, and an ATM. Directory listings also note credit cards accepted, free WiFi, bike parking, and private lot parking.

It is a simple formula that regulars appreciate: get the laundry done and the small errands handled in a single visit.

This place is run by a very kind couple. It was my first time using these machines and they were so helpful.
Paraphrased from a March 2026 public review
02The People

Kind owners, helpful staff

Review after review mentions the same thing: the people. First-timers get walked through the machines, regulars get greeted by name, and the owners have been known to wait past closing so a customer could finish a load.

The business describes its own goal simply: customer satisfaction, with staff ready to answer questions and lend a hand.

Come on a Sunday morning and you might find it quiet. Come on a Saturday afternoon and you will find neighbors folding side by side. Either way, the machines are clean, the store is stocked, and someone is around to help.
