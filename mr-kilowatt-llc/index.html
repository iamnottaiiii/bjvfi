Our story

Small shop. Serious about
safe power.

Why the name

Mr. Kilowatt is the old mascot of the electric age, a cheerful reminder that electricity powers modern life. We borrowed the name because it fits how we work: friendly on the phone, exacting on the job.

Mr. Kilowatt LLC is based at 7916 Ajay Dr #4100 in Sun Valley, CA, and serves homeowners and small businesses across the San Fernando Valley, from panel upgrades to middle of the night emergencies.

How we work

1. Diagnose. We test and trace before we quote, so you pay for the fix, not the guess.

2. Quote in writing. Firm price, clear scope, no surprises on the invoice.

3. Fix and verify. Every circuit is tested under load before we pack up.
