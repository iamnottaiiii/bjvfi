A small shop with old-school standards.

Theiss Woodworking & Remodel works out of a shop on Manor Lane in Fort Thomas, Kentucky, building custom woodwork and remodeling homes across Northern Kentucky.

We are woodworkers first. That means tight joints, honest materials, and finishes done by hand, whether the project is a single mantel or a full kitchen remodel.

Bring us a sketch, a photo, or just an idea. We will help you turn it into something solid.
