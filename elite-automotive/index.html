Our story

A reputation earned one repair at a time

Elite Automotive is an independent auto repair shop on Enterprise Drive in Maineville, Ohio, serving Warren County drivers with general automotive repair and light truck service.

What sets the shop apart is the way it treats people: clear communication from drop-off to pickup, pricing that regularly comes in below other estimates, and a crew that has been known to suggest ways to save money rather than upsell. Customers praise the team for recommending only necessary repairs and finishing work sooner than expected.

With a 4.9 star rating across more than 200 public reviews, Elite Automotive has earned a loyal following, including customers who say they have trusted the shop for over a decade.

  • Honest estimates with no upsells
  • Work completed on time, often early
  • Long term customers across Warren County
