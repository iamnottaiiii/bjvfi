Our story

A small Chatham shop with a craftsman's standard

Marchant Plumbing & Heating, LLC has served Chatham and the surrounding New Jersey towns since 2002. We are a small, hands-on team led by Chris, and we have built the business the old way: one careful job at a time.

Customers keep coming back, some five times and counting, because the work is done right and priced fairly. Reviewers call Chris a craftsman, and mention that the team is happy to explain what they are doing and even share pointers for the jobs a homeowner can handle themselves.

We offer free project estimates, stand behind our work with warranties, and accept credit cards for your convenience.
