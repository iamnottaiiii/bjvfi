Our Story

Built around one idea: one call fixes it.

Gemini Property Solutions grew out of a simple frustration: property owners bouncing between a mason, a gate company, an electrician, and a remodeler for one project. So the company built a general contracting practice that covers the full property, inside and out.

Today that means masonry and gates next to kitchens and bathrooms, and security systems next to stamped concrete. Residential or commercial, the promise is the same: the solution to all of your property needs.
