Our Story

Twenty years of
no-nonsense barbering

A family-run shop that treats every head like its reputation depends on it, because it does.

Since 2004

Old school is
a choice, not a limitation

A.T.'s Barber Shop has been cutting hair in Owings Mills since 2004. Owner Andy Tran and his wife run the chairs themselves, a small man-and-wife team that regulars know by name.

The shop is deliberately simple: a few chairs, a TV, good conversation, and barbers who would rather get your fade exactly right than rush you out the door. Prices stay low, the work stays sharp, and customers keep coming back, some for years, some driving in from Baltimore.

You'll find the shop at the end of a small strip mall on Owings Mills Blvd, in the same center as Courtney's Bagels and Vito's Pizza, across from Stevenson University.

The owner seems like a genuine guy. Just happy doing what he does best.
