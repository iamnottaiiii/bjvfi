About the Shop

A working diesel and auto repair shop in Fairfield, Ohio.

HD DIESEL & AUTO SERVICES is a truck repair shop at 3385 Port Union Rd in Fairfield, Ohio, describing itself as a provider of diesel and gasoline truck repair services.

Public reviews paint the picture of a shop that takes diagnostics seriously: technicians work through check engine codes step by step rather than jumping straight to parts replacement, and reviewers repeatedly call the work honest.

The shop handles everything from diesel pickups, including 6.0L Powerstroke engines and even a Japanese import Toyota Hiace diesel, to full size semi trucks with fuel leaks. Multiple reviewers highlight quick turnaround, with jobs like full glow plug replacement finished in a single day.
