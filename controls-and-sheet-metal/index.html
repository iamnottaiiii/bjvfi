Our Story

27 years of Cincinnati metalwork.

Controls & Sheet Metal has spent nearly three decades as a Cincinnati sheet metal contractor and metal fabricator. The shop fabricates ductwork and sheet metal, supplies furnace parts and heating and cooling equipment, and serves commercial and industrial customers from two Cincinnati locations.

Directory listings describe a working fab shop with a straightforward reputation: fair pricing, a laid-back crew, and jobs that get in and get done on time.

Note: some web directories attach the website controlsinc.com to this business. That site belongs to a different company (an engine-display maker in Medina, Ohio) and is not shown here.
