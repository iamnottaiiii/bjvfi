Our story

A second chair, a bigger promise

D, Joel Barbershop 2 is the next chapter of a barbering name Worcester already knows. The first shop built the reputation on Main Street, and this second location carries it forward with the same hands on approach: listen first, cut with care, and never rush the finish.
