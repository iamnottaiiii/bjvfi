Bethlehem's Broadway barbershop

Shepherd Boy Barbershop sits on Broadway in Bethlehem's South Side, serving the neighborhood with straightforward barbering: clean cuts, honest prices, and a shop you actually want to spend time in.

Regulars come for the consistency: the same careful lineup, the same attention to the fade, every single visit. New faces are welcome, and walk-ins are part of the culture.
