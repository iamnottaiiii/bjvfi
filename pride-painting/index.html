Interior Painting

Fresh color for the rooms you live in. Walls, ceilings, and trim, prepped right so the finish lasts.

  • Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens & baths
  • Ceilings and trim with crisp lines
  • Whole-home repaints and single rooms
  • Surface prep, patching, and priming
Good to know: every project starts with a conversation about scope and color. Call (410) 876-8322 to talk through your rooms.

Exterior Painting

Curb appeal that holds up. Exterior finishes chosen and applied for Maryland sun, rain, and winter.

  • House siding and full exterior repaints
  • Trim, shutters, doors & porches
  • Scraping, caulking, and weather-ready prep
  • Touch-ups and seasonal refreshes
Good to know: exterior work is weather dependent. Call (410) 876-8322 to plan around the season.

Residential & Commercial

Homes, rentals, and business properties. One crew, the same careful finish, whatever the size of the job.

  • Single-family homes and townhomes

  • Scheduled work to fit your calendar
