Services

One crew for the whole yard

Bonilla's Landscape handles routine maintenance and bigger projects across Granada Hills. Call (818) 831-3243 for a free look at your yard.

Lawn Care

Regular mowing, crisp edging, and blowing so your lawn looks fresh every visit. Weekly and biweekly routes available.

Garden Design and Planting

New beds, fresh plants, and layouts built for Valley heat, including drought smart choices that save water.

Yard Cleanups

Overgrown side yards, post-storm mess, or a full seasonal reset. We haul it out and leave it clean.
