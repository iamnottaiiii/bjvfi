Our Story

Hamden's Electrician Since 1984

CAG Electrical Co Inc opened its doors in Hamden, Connecticut in 1984 and has been serving the area ever since. What started as straightforward electrical contracting grew, job by job and referral by referral, into a full service shop covering wiring, panels, lighting, generators, carpentry, and painting.

The philosophy has never changed: do the work the American way, treat the customer like a neighbor, and stand behind every job. That is why 100% of our HomeAdvisor reviews are five stars, and why we carry an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau.
