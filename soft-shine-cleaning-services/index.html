Cleaning for every kind of mess

Pick the service that fits your life. Every visit is built around your checklist and your priorities.

Regular home cleaning

Weekly, biweekly or monthly visits that keep your whole home fresh without you lifting a finger.

  • Dusting all reachable surfaces, shelves and decor
  • Kitchen: counters, sink, appliance exteriors, trash out
  • Bathrooms: toilets, tubs, showers, mirrors, floors
  • Vacuuming and mopping all floors
  • Bedrooms: linens changed on request, surfaces tidied

Deep cleaning

The top-to-bottom reset for homes that need extra attention: seasonal cleans, first visits and post-event recovery.

  • Everything in regular cleaning, plus baseboards and trim
  • Inside cabinets, drawers and closets on request
  • Interior windows, sills and light fixtures
  • Grout, tile and hard-water detail work
  • Behind and under furniture and appliances

Office cleaning

Keep your workplace welcoming for staff and clients with dependable recurring office care.

  • Desks, common areas and reception spaces
  • Restrooms sanitized and restocked
  • Trash and recycling removed
  • Kitchenettes and break rooms wiped down
  • After-hours scheduling available

Move in / move out cleaning

Empty-home deep cleans that help renters get deposits back and buyers walk into a fresh start.

  • Full deep clean of every room, top to bottom
  • Inside oven, fridge and all cabinets
  • Detail work on fixtures, vents and windows
  • Flexible timing around your moving day
