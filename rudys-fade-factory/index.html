Our story

A Hertel Ave original

Some shops chase trends. This one perfected a craft and stuck with it.

Cutting since 2009

Rudys Fade Factory opened its doors on Hertel Avenue in 2009, and it has been doing one thing better than anyone since: the fade. High, mid, low or skin, every cut leaves the chair blended clean and lined up tight.

The formula never changed. Walk in, ask for Rudy, get in the chair. No appointments to juggle, no waiting room purgatory. Clients say it plainly in their reviews: quick cut, no waiting time, fantastic job, chill vibe.

It is a neighborhood shop in the truest sense, a New York State licensed barbershop rooted on one of Buffalo's best commercial strips. Come see why people keep coming back.
