About

A Kissimmee contractor, plain and simple

Meridian Construction is a general contractor based at 5251 Cane Island Loop in Kissimmee, FL, serving homeowners across Osceola County.

01

Local work, personal accountability

We are based in Kissimmee, not a call center in another state. When you call (407) 396-6490, you reach the people who will actually do the work. That keeps promises short and delivery honest.

Whether it is a single bathroom refresh or a multi-room remodel, the approach is the same: walk the space, write the scope, agree on the price, then get to work.

Built for Florida homes

Central Florida homes take a beating: humidity, hard water, storm seasons. We choose materials and methods that hold up here, and we will tell you straight when a cheaper option will cost you later.
