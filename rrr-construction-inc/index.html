OUR STORY / SHT S-102

A Pacoima builder,
built on the work itself

Rrr Construction Inc is an owner-operated construction company based on Welk Ave in Pacoima, serving homes and businesses across the San Fernando Valley since 2014.

SINCE 2014

Twelve years of showing up

RRR started as a small crew with a simple approach: plan carefully, build honestly, and treat every home like it matters. That approach has kept the company working in Pacoima and the surrounding Valley for over a decade.

We are deliberately small. Five people, one owner on site, and a reputation built one foundation at a time.

OWNER-OPERATED

The owner is on your job site

Big firms hand your project down a chain. At RRR the chain is short: the person who estimated your job is the person swinging the hammer. Questions get answered on the spot, and decisions get made the same day.

That is how small jobs stay on budget and big jobs stay on schedule.

LOCAL ROOTS

Pacoima is home, not just a service area

Our office sits on Welk Ave in the neighborhood we build in. We hire local, buy local, and drive past our finished projects every day. When your builder lives with the results, the results are better.
