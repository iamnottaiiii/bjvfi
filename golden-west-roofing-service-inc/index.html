Roofing services

Everything your roof needs, under one roof

Residential and commercial roofing services across the San Fernando Valley and Los Angeles County. Every job starts with an inspection and a written estimate.

Full Roof Replacement

When a roof is past repairing, we do a complete tear-off down to the deck, check the structure underneath, and install a full new roofing system with proper underlayment, flashing, and ventilation.

Roof Repair

Targeted repairs for leaks, storm damage, missing or lifted shingles, cracked tile, and failing flashing. We fix the problem, not just the symptom, and we clean up when we leave.

Roof Inspections

An honest, written assessment of your roof's condition. Useful before buying or selling a home, after storm season, or when you just want to know where you stand. If your roof is fine, we will tell you it is fine.

Tile Roofing

Clay and concrete tile installation, tile replacement, and underlayment renewal. Tile roofs last decades when they are installed right, and we install them right.

Asphalt Shingle Roofing

New shingle roofs and re-roofs for single and multi-story homes. A reliable, cost-effective option, installed to manufacturer specifications.

Wood Shingles and Shakes

Installation and repair of wood shingle and shake roofing for homes that call for a natural, traditional look.

Our story

Licensed, bonded, and accountable

Golden West Roofing Service, Inc. holds California Contractors State License Board license #933907, issued in 2009 and currently active. The company carries workers compensation insurance and a surety bond, so you are protected on every job.

Over the years, the company has built its reputation the old-fashioned way: showing up on time, bidding jobs fairly, communicating clearly, and leaving a clean jobsite. Customers describe the service as honest and affordable, and the owner, Bryan Chang, is known for picking up the phone and standing behind the work.

Whether it is a straightforward repair or a full replacement with multiple layers to tear off, the crew treats every home like their own.
