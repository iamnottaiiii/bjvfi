  • 1990

    Chad Coslett starts out with an ultrasonic mini-blind cleaning company, humble beginnings and a strong work ethic.

  • 2000s

Owner: Chad Coslett

The philosophy behind the clean.

"I started on my epic carpet cleaning ride some 23 years ago," Chad says of the journey. The guiding belief has stayed constant: treat customers right, keep learning, reinvest in better equipment, and help people get the most out of their carpet, rugs, upholstery and flooring through proper maintenance, odor removal and protection.

The goal on every visit is simple: exceed expectations and deliver a cleaning experience that adds real value to the home or organization.
