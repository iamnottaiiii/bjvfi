Our story

A neighborhood chair
with a velvet rope

Prestige Beauty Salon has earned its name the slow way: one careful haircut, one happy regular, one five-star review at a time on Park Lane in Cedarhurst.

Why the name fits

Prestige is a promise,
kept daily

Open every single day from 9 AM to 7 PM, the salon runs like a concierge desk: you call, you are greeted, you sit, and you leave looking sharper than you arrived.

Clients describe a pleasant, homey feel rather than a rushed assembly line. Stylists take the time to get it right, whether that is a first haircut for a nervous kid or brows and nails before a big night out.

The through-line in every review is value: careful, professional work at prices people call extremely reasonable. That is the whole business model, and it shows in the loyalty. Some clients have trusted this chair since childhood.
