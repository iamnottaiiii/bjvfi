Our story

A one-man shop with a contractor's license and a neighbor's manners

Winterman Electric Co., Inc. is a Kentucky corporation in good standing, based at 23 McAlpin Avenue in Erlanger. It is a small, owner-operated electrical contracting business, the kind where the person who answers the phone is the person who shows up with the tools.

Craig Winterman holds active electrical contractor licenses and has dozens of permitted projects on record across the area. The work spans residential and commercial: general repairs, lighting, panels, and full remodel wiring, including subcontract work on large basement remodels.

What customers mention most is not the paperwork, though. It is the way the work gets done: professional, accommodating, and timely, with practical suggestions that save money and an easygoing manner that makes having an electrician in the house a non-event.
