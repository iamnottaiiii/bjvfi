About Yulis Barbershop


A local shop for classic and modern cuts in Montebello. Yulis Barbershop focuses on the details: clean lines, even fades, and a chair you will not want to leave.


Every appointment starts with a consultation: what you like, what you do not, and how much upkeep you want. The result is a cut that looks sharp on day one and grows out clean.


Find us at 901 W Beverly Blvd, Montebello, CA 90640, or call (323) 725-3799 to book your chair.
