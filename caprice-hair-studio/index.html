Our story

A studio built on great hair
and good company

Caprice Hair Studio opened in 2019 in North Grafton, and has been growing by word of mouth ever since.

Where guests become regulars

The studio describes itself simply: a full service hair salon for both women's and men's hair. Guests describe it with more color: relaxed and fun, gorgeous and clean, with stylists who listen and remember what you like.

Many clients say they followed their stylist to Caprice, which says a lot. People do not switch salons lightly, and they do not stay unless the work is excellent.

Whether you are after a bold new color, your first short cut in years, or just a trim that is easy to style at home, the team takes a personalized approach to every appointment.
