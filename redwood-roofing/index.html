Why Redwood Roofing

Built for New York weather, from the deck up.

Heavy rain, high wind and freeze-thaw cycles punish a roof in ways you only notice when water shows up inside. We build and repair roofs with materials and methods chosen for the five boroughs: solid decking, proper flashing, ventilation that lets the attic breathe and shingles rated for the storms we actually get.

Every job starts with a full inspection and an honest assessment. If your roof has years left, we will tell you. If it needs work, we will show you exactly why, in plain language, before any work begins.

What we do

Roofing services for Jamaica and Queens

Residential and light commercial roofing, from full replacements to the small repair that saves a big bill later.

Roof Replacement

Full tear-off and replacement with new decking where needed, proper underlayment, flashing and ventilation, installed to stand up to New York weather.

Leak Repair

Leaks traced to the source, not just patched where the stain appears. We fix the entry point and check the surrounding area so the same leak does not come back.

Storm Damage Response

Rapid securing of wind, hail and fallen-branch damage, followed by a complete repair plan and clear documentation for your insurance claim.

Gutters and Flashing

Gutter repair and replacement, plus flashing around chimneys, vents and valleys, the details that keep water moving off the roof instead of into it.

Roof Inspections

Thorough inspections for buyers, sellers and homeowners who want to know where their roof stands, with a plain-language report and photos of what we find.

Skylights and Vents

Skylight installation and resealing, ridge and soffit vent work, and ventilation fixes that cut heat buildup and extend shingle life.

Our story

A roofing crew built on honest work.

Redwood Roofing is a roofing contractor based on 148th Avenue in Jamaica, Queens. We started with a simple idea that has kept us busy ever since: show up when you say you will, explain the work in plain language, and treat every roof like it is sheltering your own family.

We focus on residential and light commercial roofing across Jamaica and the surrounding Queens neighborhoods. No high-pressure sales visits, no mystery line items. Just an inspection, a clear plan and a crew that cleans up like we were never there, except for the new roof.
