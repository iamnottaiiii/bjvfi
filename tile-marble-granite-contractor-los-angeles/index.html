Our story

Why us

The details are the job

Tile work is unforgiving. A crooked first row shows forever, cheap grout stains in a year, and a skipped waterproofing step floods the room below. We started this business to do the unglamorous prep work most people never see, so the beautiful part lasts.

Homeowners, landlords and property managers across the Valley call us when the job has to be right the first time: rental turnovers, pre sale refreshes, and the kitchen you have been saving for.

  • Licensed trade work with proper prep and waterproofing
  • Daily cleanup, so you can live in the house during the job
  • Straight timelines, in writing, before we start

The process

From first call to final walkthrough

01

Call and describe the job

Photos help. We will ask about materials, square footage and timing, then schedule a free in person estimate.

02

Free on site estimate

We measure, check the substrate, talk materials, and give you a written price and timeline. No pressure, no obligation.

03

Prep done right

Demo, waterproofing, leveling and layout lines first. The part you never see is the part that makes it last.

04

Install and finish

Tile set, grouted, sealed and polished. We clean up daily and walk the finished job with you before we call it done.
