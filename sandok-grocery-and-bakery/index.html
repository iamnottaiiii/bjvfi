Kain tayo! Your Filipino corner on Cicero Avenue

Groceries from home, fresh-baked Filipino favorites, hot dishes, and the friendliest welcome in Crestwood. One stop, all the flavors you miss.

Sandok Grocery and Bakery is the neighborhood spot where Crestwood comes for a taste of the Philippines, and stays for the company. In the morning the case fills with soft pan de sal and buttery ensaymada, the shelves stay stocked with the ingredients for lutong bahay, and the hot counter serves dishes that taste like somebody's lola made them.

Guests describe Sandok as warm and accommodating, the kind of place that helps make an 80th birthday party a success and sends you home happy. It is a grocery, a bakery, a quick meal, and a gathering place, all run by people who treat you like family.

Hosting a celebration? Guests rave about the plenty of food and the karaoke corner that keeps the night going. Birthdays, gatherings, or just a Tuesday craving, there is always a warm welcome here. Salamat for stopping by. Kain tayo!

Our story

A little of home in Crestwood

Sandok, the ladle, is the heart of every Filipino kitchen. This shop is ours.

Sandok Grocery and Bakery began with a simple idea: give Crestwood's Filipino community, and curious neighbors, one place for the flavors of home. Fresh-baked pan de sal in the morning, shelves stocked with the ingredients for lutong bahay, and hot dishes that taste like somebody's lola made them.

But ask the regulars and they will tell you the food is only half of it. The owners are known for being friendly and accommodating, going out of their way for celebrations, and making every guest feel like family. Birthdays, gatherings, or just a Tuesday craving, there is always a warm welcome here.

Salamat for stopping by. Kain tayo!
