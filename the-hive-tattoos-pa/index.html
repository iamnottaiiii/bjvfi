First tattoo? Start here.

Getting inked for the first time raises good questions. The short version: call, ask anything, and never rush the decision.

Ask

What should it cost?

Shops price by size, detail, and time. A consultation gets you a real number, not a guess.

Ask

How do I prepare?

Rest, hydrate, eat before your session, and bring reference images of styles you like.

Ask

How do I heal it?

Follow the aftercare instructions your artist gives you. Proper healing keeps the piece sharp.
