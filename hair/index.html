A Main Street chair with a loyal following

About

Meet Lisa Paul, the chair behind HAIR

HAIR is owned and run by Lisa Paul, a barber and stylist serving Coopersburg from her shop at 109 S Main St. It is a one-chair kind of place: when you book with Lisa, you get Lisa, and her full attention, from consultation to final check in the mirror.

Her regulars describe the same things again and again. She listens to what you actually want. She sweats the details, a razor-cut neckline, a cleaned-up brow, a finish that holds. And she keeps hours that work for working people, including Sunday mornings.

Sunday morning hours are convenient for me, and very hard to find.Based on Google reviews

Whether it is a young client's first cut, a sharp fade, or a color refresh, the approach is the same: honest work at a fair price, in a shop that feels like your neighborhood.
