Our story

A Harlem chair that remembers you

New Harlem Unisex sits on Adam Clayton Powell Jr Blvd, in the heart of Harlem, and it runs the way neighborhood shops should: everyone who walks in gets treated like a regular.

Reviewers talk about the barbers the way people talk about family. One client says they were remembered from a visit five years earlier. Another brought in a son with long curly hair and left with exactly the cut he asked for. A first-time visitor with curly hair called it one of the best haircuts he has ever had, and he was in and out fast.

Unisex means unisex here. Men, women, and kids all take a chair, and barbers are skilled with every hair type and texture. The shop has a reputation for warmth, good conversation, and good vibes from the moment you sit down.

Whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth, you will be heard, you will be remembered, and you will walk out sharp.
