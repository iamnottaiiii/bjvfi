Why ceilings matter

The ceiling is half the room, and all of the noise

A good acoustic ceiling does quiet work: it soaks up echo, hides the mechanicals, and makes an office or a basement feel finished instead of makeshift.

We have been installing and servicing them since 1974. That is five decades of grids, tiles, and drywall, and the judgment that only comes with doing it that long.

The long view

50+ years of the same trade

Bill Hayden started in the acoustical ceiling trade in 1974, and never left it. Five decades later, the shop is still in Winnetka, still owner operated, and still doing the same honest work: straight walls, quiet ceilings, clean finishes.

A small crew means the person who quotes the job does the job. It also means the kind of judgment you only get from doing one thing for a very long time: knowing which tile will stay flat, which texture will match, and when a repair beats a replacement.
