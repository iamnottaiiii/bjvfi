Process and FAQs

What replastering day looks like

A replaster is a construction project in miniature: loud for a day or two, then suddenly beautiful. Here is the full sequence and honest answers.

01

Drain and inspect

Water is pumped out and every square foot of the old surface is checked for hollow spots, cracks and delamination. What is failing gets marked for removal.

02

Chip-out and prep

Loose plaster comes off, the shell is cleaned, and plumbing fittings are protected. This is the dusty, noisy day. It is also the most important one.

03

Tile, coping and features

New waterline tile, coping, steps or benches go in while the pool is empty, so everything bonds into one finished system.

04

Bond coat and plaster

A bonding agent is applied, then fresh plaster is shot or hand-troweled on and finished smooth. Timing matters here, so crews work fast.

05

Fill immediately

The hose goes in the same day. New plaster must stay wet, so filling starts right away and runs until the pool is full.

06

Start-up care

The first week decides a lot: daily brushing, careful chemistry and no heater use until the surface has cured. Ask for written start-up instructions.
