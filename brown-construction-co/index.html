Who we are

Our Story

Brown Construction Co is a Cincinnati, Ohio construction company based at 3254 Parkhill Drive. Public business listings describe the company as a home builder and an altering and remodeling contractor, the kind of small, hands-on outfit that takes on additions, remodels, and new construction across the area.

Business records describe a small crew, which means the person you talk to about your project is close to the person swinging the hammer. For homeowners, that usually means straighter answers, fewer handoffs, and a build that gets personal attention from start to finish.

If you are comparing builders, the honest next step is a phone call. Ask about recent projects, timelines, and how estimates work, and judge the fit for yourself.
