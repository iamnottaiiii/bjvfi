Why it matters

Lint is quiet. The risk is not.

Dryers push hot, moist air through a vent to the outside of the house. When lint coats that path, drying takes longer, energy bills climb, and heat can build up where it should not. A clear vent lets the dryer breathe the way it was built to.

  • ✓Shorter dry times. Clothes dry in fewer cycles when air moves freely.
  • ✓Less strain on the dryer. The machine works less hard per load.
  • ✓A safer laundry room. Clearing lint removes fuel from a hot path.
