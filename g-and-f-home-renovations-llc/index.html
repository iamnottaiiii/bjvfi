Our story

A local crew with one guiding belief

G&F Home Renovations LLC started in New Richmond, Ohio in 2015, according to Better Business Bureau records, and is owned by Gary White. The company describes itself plainly: reasonable rates, quick work that is quality, and craftsmanship the crew stands behind.

"We think that if you are able to provide this type of service for customers, they will return to you again and again."
