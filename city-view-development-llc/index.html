About

A developer rooted in Glendale

City View Development, LLC is a real estate development company based in Glendale, California.

Development is a long game: finding the right site, shaping a plan the neighborhood can stand behind, and seeing it through to the final walkthrough. City View Development works in that full arc.

Based in the 91214 area, the company is positioned in one of the San Gabriel Valley's most active corridors. Whether the project is a new build, a repositioning, or an early-stage idea, the first step is a straightforward conversation.
