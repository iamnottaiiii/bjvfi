Our story

A St. Petersburg general contractor built around one idea: remodeling should be managed, not chaotic.

Who we are

Oasis Remodeling Company is a general contracting firm based at 433 Central Ave in downtown St. Petersburg, Florida. We take on kitchens, bathrooms, additions, and full home renovations across St. Petersburg and Pinellas County.

As a general contractor, we plan the job, pull it together, and manage every trade on site, so you deal with one team from the first call to the final walkthrough.

How we work

Clear written estimates. You know the price and the plan before we start.

One point of contact. You always know who to call and what is happening next.

Clean, scheduled jobsites. Work happens on a schedule you can plan around.

Signed-off finishes. We walk every room with you before the job is done.
