Supply catalog

What the counter stocks

Roofing materials for repair crews and re-roof contractors across the San Fernando Valley. Stock moves with the season, so call ahead for large pulls.

Shingles

Architectural and three tab asphalt shingles in common Valley colors.

Tile Roofing

Concrete and clay tile profiles plus ridge and hip pieces.

Metal Roofing

Standing seam and corrugated panels, trim, and clips.

Underlayment

Synthetic underlayment, felt, and ice and water barrier.

Flashing and Vents

Drip edge, step flashing, pipe boots, and attic vents.

Fasteners and Nails

Coil roofing nails, staples, and screws in contractor packs.

Sealants and Coatings

Roof cement, caulk, and elastomeric coatings for flat roofs.

Gutters

Seamless gutter coil, downspouts, and hangers.

Tools

Nailers, shingle cutters, tear off tools, and safety gear basics.
