About us

Glendale builders,
neighborhood mindset

Ace Home Remodeling is a construction company based at 210 S Kenwood St in Glendale, CA, serving homeowners who want the job done right the first time.

The short version

We are builders, not salespeople. Most of our work comes from people who live within a few miles of our shop, and that keeps us honest: we will see you at the grocery store after the job is done.

That means showing up when we say we will, keeping the site clean enough that you can live around it, and standing behind the work with a final walkthrough on every project.

  • Local crew, local accountability
  • Written scopes, no surprise change orders
  • Respect for your home while we work in it
