Our story

A Bay Street shop
that treats clothes
like they matter

Gentle Dry Cleaners and Tailor has served the Rosebank and East Shore neighborhoods of Staten Island from its Bay Street storefront, building a reputation one pressed suit at a time.

01

Gentle is the method

The name is the promise. Delicate fabrics get delicate treatment, and every piece is inspected before it goes back on the rack. Care is not a slogan here, it is the workflow.

02

A real tailor on site

Alterations happen in the shop, not shipped across town. That means faster turnarounds, direct fittings, and a tailor who has seen your garment before the first stitch.

03

Neighbors first

Long-time regulars have trusted the shop with suits and tailoring for years. The goal is simple: the kind of shop where the counter knows your name and your wardrobe.
