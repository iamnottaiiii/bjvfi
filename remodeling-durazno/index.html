Our Story

Remodeling Durazno is a locally owned and operated company serving Cincinnati, Ohio, including Colerain Township and the surrounding west side. Our dedicated team brings years of hands on experience to every project, offering innovative ideas and suggestions tailored to what each home needs.

We believe great remodeling is personal. Our services are customized to your individual needs, and we pay close attention to the details of each project we take on. Above all, we value honesty and integrity in every part of our business, from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.
