Our story

Nearly two decades
on Hertel Ave

Good Looks Hair and Fashions has been a North Buffalo fixture since 2006, a shop built on craft, culture, and community.

Since 2006

From neighborhood
roots to a Buffalo name

Good Looks opened its doors on Hertel Ave in 2006 and never really closed the conversation since. Co-owner Thomas Barwell started cutting hair as a kid in the neighborhood, free cuts for friends that became five-dollar cuts, then ten, then a career.

After training at a prominent Ken-Ton salon and running a Supercuts shop up to manager, he brought everything he learned back to North Buffalo and built the shop his way: skilled, welcoming, and open to every culture that walks through the door.

Today the shop is a small crew of barbers with loyal followings of their own. Reviewers call out barbers like Nick and Cecil by name, and regulars of ten-plus years keep the chairs full.
