About

Dr. Marlene Massaro

~
Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Marlene Mary Massaro, D.C., is a sole practitioner who loves chiropractic and says there is nothing else she would rather be doing. She began practicing in Philadelphia in 1982 and has served Berlin, New Jersey since 1988.

Her philosophy is simple and deeply held: there is a brain body connection that is lost when the brain cannot communicate with the parts of the body, and chiropractic care works to restore it. Her practice revolves around educating people about how that care can maximize the life expression in the body.

  • Sole practitioner: every visit is with Dr. Massaro herself
  • Cares for babies, children, athletes, and seniors
  • Education first: understand your body, own your health
  • Serving Berlin and the surrounding South Jersey community
