Our story

About A1 Truck Repair & Parts

A1 Truck Repair and Parts keeps commercial trucks rolling from Rizzolo Road in South Kearny. Engine diagnostics, steering and suspension, axles, and drivelines are all handled in house.

The shop also services semi trailers and stocks parts, so fleets and owner operators can get repairs and the parts behind them in one stop.
