Who we are

No fuss,
just a fresh cut

Tucked into Suite 113 at 8628-A Liberty Road, HAIR IT IS is the kind of barbershop every neighborhood needs: straightforward, welcoming, and focused on the craft.

The philosophy is simple. Listen to what the client wants, work with care, and send them out the door looking and feeling better than when they walked in. Whether it is a clean fade for the weekend, a sharp shape-up before an event, or a first haircut for a young gentleman, the standard stays the same.

For the Randallstown community, that means a reliable chair close to home, without the hassle of a long drive or a big-salon price tag.
