Our story

A body shop that
treats cars like its own

On Terminal Rd in Lorton, SK has built its name the slow way: one honest repair at a time.

The shop's own description says it best: every vehicle is treated like it is their own. Whether you have been in an accident or just need cosmetic repairs, the technicians give honest assessments, quality repairs, and clear communication at every step.

That is not just a tagline. It shows up in the reviews: progress photos texted during the work, fair prices that undercut the big shops, and a willingness to open on a Sunday so a customer could pick up before work on Monday.

The proprietor, known to customers as "J", is described as confident, personable, and someone who knows his stuff. Drivers come in stressed about a wreck and leave recommending the shop to everyone they know.
