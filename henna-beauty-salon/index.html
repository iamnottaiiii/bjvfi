About the salon

A Hackensack staple for brows and beauty

Henna Beauty salon at 445 Essex St brings together threading, henna, hair services, waxing, facials and makeup in one friendly neighborhood spot. Guests come back again and again for the eyebrow threading in particular: fast, steady, and consistently polished.

The team is known for being kind, patient and accommodating, and many clients have trusted the salon for years, with some families visiting across generations.
