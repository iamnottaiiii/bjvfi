About us

A hometown crew for Cincinnati disasters

Hometown Restoration of Cincinnati is a damage restoration service based in Newtown, Ohio, serving the Greater Cincinnati area around the clock.

Speed, professionalism, and customer-obsessed concern

That is how this company describes its own priorities, and it matches what customers say in public reviews: fast arrival, friendly crews, efficient work, and fair pricing.

Water damage, fire damage, and mold do not follow a schedule, so neither do we. Our phones are answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, because the first hours after a loss decide how bad the damage gets.

From our base at 6809 Main St in Newtown, we respond to homes and businesses across the Cincinnati metro, bringing extraction equipment, drying gear, and a crew that treats your property like their own neighborhood. Because it is.
