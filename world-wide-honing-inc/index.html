About

Twenty plus years behind the hone

World Wide Honing Inc. was established in 2001 in Pacoima, California, specializing in an array of honing and deep hole gun drilling services.

Our story

Skill, dedication, and an honest day's work

With well over 20 years of experience, skill, and honest dedication to this industry, we built World Wide Honing around one idea: have the resources necessary to meet our customers' wants and needs, so the job is done right and efficiently.

That means the right machines, the right tooling, and people who care about a tenth as much as you do. From automotive to nuclear, space, aircraft, medical, and military work, our customers trust us with bores that cannot be wrong.
