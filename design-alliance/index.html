How we work

From first sketch to final walkthrough

Every project follows the same considered path: a conversation about how you use your space, a design concept built around that reality, and careful follow-through to installation day.

  • 01Consultation. We talk through your goals, timeline, and budget, on site or in studio.

Our Studio

A Mechanicsburg studio with long roots

Design Alliance was founded to do one thing well: build close working relationships with clients and reach design goals together. More than thirty years later, that is still the brief on every project, whether it is a single room or a full commercial interior.

Our licensed designers create personalized environments tailored to individual styles and needs, and we stay with a project from concept to construction. The result is work that is timeless, functional, and honest about budget.

High-quality, timeless design that incorporates function and respects the way you live.
