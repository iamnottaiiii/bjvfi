Ivp autorepair is an independent auto repair shop in Springfield, VA. No corporate script, no upsell playbook, just cars and the people who drive them.

What we stand for

We let the reviews talk

Every one of our Birdeye reviews is five stars. The themes repeat: great service, unbeatable prices, and oil changes that are quick and convenient.

That is the whole business model, really. Do the job right, charge a fair number, and make it easy for the customer. A shop earns a reputation one car at a time, and we are earning ours on Fullerton Rd.

We are a small, independent shop, which means the person who looks at your car is the person who answers the phone. If you want a second opinion, a quick oil change, or a repair explained in plain language, that is what we do.
