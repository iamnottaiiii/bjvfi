Our story

Three decades of showing up and doing it right.

Godby Construction has served Northern Kentucky for more than 30 years from our shop at 8480 Decoursey Pike in Covington.

We are owner operated, which means no subcontractors and no handoffs. The crew that inspects your roof or chimney is the crew that does the work, and we treat older homes with the care their craftsmanship deserves.

Box gutters, metal work, and historical restoration became our calling card because so few contractors still do them well. Neighbors keep recommending us for the same reasons: we come out fast, we check the whole system instead of patching one spot, and we charge a fair price.

Estimates are always free. Call and tell us what is going on with your home, and we will tell you honestly what it needs.
