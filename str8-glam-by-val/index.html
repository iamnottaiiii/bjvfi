Our story

Built on great hair
and genuine care

Str8 Glam By Val is a neighborhood salon in Dyker Heights where the atmosphere is relaxed, the music is good, and the stylists treat you like family.

How it feels

Less appointment,
more hangout

Walk in and the first thing you notice is the vibe. It feels like a best friend's living room, warm, fun and unpretentious, with music that makes the time fly. The second thing you notice is that someone is actually listening to what you want.

That combination is the whole point. Val built the salon around skilled, licensed stylists who take the craft seriously and the experience lightly. Color is done with real expertise, especially blondes and corrections, and cuts are shaped to the person in the chair, not a template.

What clients say

A spa day
with better hair

Guests compare visits to a spa day, thanks in part to the head massage that comes with every shampoo. Reviewers talk about feeling completely comfortable, leaving confident and refreshed, and finally finding a stylist they trust with their vision.

Many clients have followed Val for years, through blonde phases, red phases and everything in between. New faces become regulars fast, because the work speaks and the welcome is real.
