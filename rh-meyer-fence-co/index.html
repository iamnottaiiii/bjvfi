About the Shop

A White Oak family business since 1968

Public records trace R.H. Meyer Fence Co. to 5728 Cheviot Road in Cincinnati, Ohio, where it has operated for decades as a family owned and operated fence contractor. The Better Business Bureau profile lists the business start year as 1968 and names Ronald Meyer as owner. Directory descriptions say the company offers residential, commercial, and industrial fencing at affordable prices and gives free estimates before starting a job, working with each customer on the style and feel they want.

The shop sits in the White Oak area of Cincinnati, serving the west side of the city and surrounding neighborhoods. It is listed in fence contractor, fence supply, and building material categories, with chain link, privacy, wood, and vinyl work among its listed specialties.
