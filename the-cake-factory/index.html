Our story

A working bakery, not a showroom

The Cake Factory is a wholesale bakery based in Crestwood, Illinois, focused on one job: producing cakes that hold up in real retail cases and at real celebrations.

The bakery runs like a factory in the best sense: consistent recipes, steady output, and cakes that taste as good on Friday as they did on Monday. Wholesale customers, from restaurants to local shops, count on that consistency for their own cases and menus.

Alongside wholesale production, the bakery takes custom occasion cakes, giving local families the same quality that goes out to stores every day. Reviewers have praised the banana pudding style Banana Burst and described the cakes as moist and decadent.

The details of the founding story are best told by the people behind the counter, and this page is holding a place for it. Owners are welcome to share their history and we will put it here in their own words.
