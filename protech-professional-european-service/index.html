Craftsmanship manifesto

A European car is not transportation. It is an engineered instrument, and it deserves a workshop that treats it like one.

Protech Professional European Service is an independent auto repair shop at 867 Ohio Pike in Cincinnati, Ohio, focused on European vehicles. Our approach is simple: measure first, guess never.

We believe a repair order should read like a technical report. What was tested, what was found, what was fixed, and to what specification. That discipline is what keeps a precision machine precise.

We also believe honesty is a specification. If your car does not need the work, we will tell you. If it needs it later, we will tell you when. Trust is built one accurate diagnosis at a time.

  • Principle 01Diagnostics before parts. We test, then we replace.
  • Principle 02Manufacturer specifications, not shortcuts.
  • Principle 03Plain language estimates. No jargon walls, no surprises.
  • Principle 04European focus. We know these platforms and their patterns.
  • Principle 05Your car returned clean and road tested.
