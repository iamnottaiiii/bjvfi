Our story

The mechanic Clinton keeps coming back to

Boone's Automotive is Billy Boone's shop at 5700 Spring St # D in Clinton, Maryland. It is the kind of place where the customers tell the story best.

One family has trusted Billy since the 90s: the father first, then his daughter, then her daughter. Another customer has done business with Billy Boone since 1998 and says she would not take her vehicle anywhere else. A third calls him one of, if not the best mechanics in Prince George's county.

The pattern in every review is the same: honest work, fair prices, and a mechanic who treats customers like neighbors. Engines installed in two days. A no start diagnosed as a bad belt and fixed the same morning. That is how a shop keeps families for three generations.
