About Us

Cincinnati's Home Repair Heroes

ProMaster Home Repair & Handyman has served the greater Cincinnati area since 2007, leading what the company calls a restoration of integrity, service, and excellence in the home repair industry.

Every craftsman is a ProMaster employee, never a subcontractor, and each one passes a strict criminal background check plus a rigorous skill and tool assessment before ever stepping into your home.

Appointments are set for an exact time convenient for you, not a four-hour window. ProMaster is a service-disabled veteran owned small business, fully insured, and a member of the Greater Cincinnati and Clermont County Chambers of Commerce.

  • 2017 BBB Torch Award for Marketplace Ethics, winner
  • BBB A+ rating, accredited business
  • Named a national "Big 50" industry leader by Remodeling Magazine, 2012
  • Expertise.com top handyman company and top deck contractor, 2022
  • Cincy Magazine Tristate Success Award, 2010
