About WoodLux

Craft is in the name

WoodLux is an interior construction contractor based in North Hollywood, focused on the rooms people live in every day.

Most contractors will tell you about square footage. We would rather talk about the things you will touch every day: the way a cabinet door closes, the line where tile meets wall, the finish on a handrail.

WoodLux takes on kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, and full interior renovations across the San Fernando Valley. We coordinate the trades, keep the job site clean, and communicate plainly about schedule and cost, because a renovation should feel exciting, not stressful.

Whether it is a single bathroom or a whole-home interior, we bring the same standard: careful prep, honest pricing, and finish work we are proud to sign.
