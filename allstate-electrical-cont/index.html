Our story

A Pasadena electrician, wired for the neighborhood.

Straightforward work, honest pricing

Allstate Electrical Cont is an electrical contractor based on East Colorado Boulevard in Pasadena, California. We serve homes and businesses across the Pasadena area with the kind of electrical work every building depends on: safe power, working lights, and panels that can handle the load.

Our approach is simple. Listen to what is wrong, diagnose it properly, explain the fix in plain language, and give you the price before we start. No upsells, no mystery line items, no leaving a mess behind.

Whether it is a single outlet that stopped working, a panel that needs more capacity, or lighting that makes a room feel right, we treat every job like it is our own building.
