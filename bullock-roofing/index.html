About Bullock Roofing

A Cincinnati roofing contractor serving the Greater Cincinnati area since 2007.

Local to Cincinnati

Bullock Roofing is based at 10265 Spartan Dr, Suite C, Cincinnati, OH 45215, and serves the Greater Cincinnati area including Hamilton County communities such as Addyston, Cleves, Harrison, North Bend, and Terrace Park.

What They Do

Roofing is the core trade: new roofs, tear offs, repairs, skylights, and flat and rubber roofs. The company also handles the parts of the exterior that meet the roof: gutters and gutter guards, chimney repairs and rebuilds, plus window and door replacements and siding.
