Bad concrete is expensive twice.

Driveways

Your driveway takes more abuse than any other concrete on the property. We build for it.

  • Full tear out and replacement of failed driveways
  • New pours with compacted base and steel reinforcement
  • Thickened edges where vehicles turn and park
  • Drainage slope set away from the garage and house
  • Broom finish for grip, with clean tooled joints
CALL FOR A DRIVEWAY ESTIMATE

Patios and walks

The concrete you live on every day. Poured flat, finished clean, and detailed to match the house.

  • Backyard patios in broom, exposed aggregate or stamped finishes
  • Front walkways and side paths with crisp edges
  • Control joints cut to manage cracking
  • Grades that move water away from the foundation
  • Options for borders and decorative saw cuts
CALL FOR A PATIO ESTIMATE

Repairs

Not every cracked slab needs replacing. We will tell you straight which ones do.

  • Crack assessment: cosmetic, structural, or replacement territory
  • Settled sections evaluated for lift versus replace
  • Spalling and surface damage repair
  • Trip hazard grinding on walkways
  • Honest call when replacement is the cheaper long term fix
CALL ABOUT A REPAIR
