Services index

Four chapters of insulation work

Services below are listed on our Porch contractor profile. Every job starts with a look at what is there now, then a plain-spoken recommendation.

Ch. 1Attic Insulation InstallationMost requested

New insulation for unfinished or under-insulated attics. We air-seal the gaps first, then build the blanket to the depth your home needs. A common fix for rooms that never match the thermostat.

Ch. 2Basement Insulation InstallationYear-round

Basement walls and rim areas are a major source of drafts and damp, chilly air. Insulating them makes the whole house feel warmer and helps finished basements stay comfortable.

Ch. 3Crawl Space Insulation InstallationUnderfoot comfort

Cold floors often trace back to an uninsulated crawl space. We insulate the floor above or the crawl walls, depending on how your home is built and vented.

Ch. 4Crawl Space EncapsulationMoisture control

Sealing a crawl space against ground moisture and outside air protects the structure, improves indoor air, and makes any insulation down there actually last.

Not sure which chapter is yours? Call and describe what you are feeling: hot upstairs, cold floors, high bills. We will tell you honestly whether insulation is the fix, and which part of the house to look at first.

Our story

One trade, done carefully, since 1999

Amity Partners Inc was established in 1999 and incorporated in Ohio, and has worked as an insulation contractor in the Bromley, Kentucky area ever since. We are a single-location company: the people who assess your home are the people who stand behind the work.

Company president Jeff Mcmahon leads a crew of about fifteen insulation specialists. We keep the trade narrow on purpose. Attics, basements, and crawl spaces are where comfort is won or lost, and that is where we spend our days.

Our approach has not changed in two and a half decades: look first, seal the leaks, insulate to the right depth, and leave the house cleaner than we found it.
