Why prep matters

Great tile is 80 percent what you never see.

Anyone can set a pretty tile. The difference between tile that lasts and tile that cracks is the substrate: level floors, true walls, proper waterproofing, and the right setting materials for the job.

  • Surface prep, leveling and layout planned before a single tile is cut
  • Waterproofing in showers and wet areas done to trade standard
  • Clean grout lines, sealed grout, and a tidy job site at day's end

Marble and natural stone

Stone deserves a specialist's touch.

Marble, travertine and natural stone move, stain and chip differently than ceramic. Proper sealing, matched veining and careful cuts keep stone installations looking intentional for years.

  • Stone layout planned around veining and color variation
  • Sealing and finishing guidance for long term care
  • Repairs and regrouting for tired stone floors and showers
