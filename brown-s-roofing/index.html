Signs you need us

Don't wait on a roof problem

If you spot any of these, call for an inspection before the next storm makes it worse.

Missing or curled shingles

Bare spots and lifted edges let water straight into the decking.

Stains on ceilings

Brown rings or drips inside mean water is already getting through.

Granules in gutters

Heavy granule loss means your shingles are wearing out.

Sagging areas

Dips in the roofline can signal structural trouble underneath.

Roof is 20+ years old

Most shingle roofs are near end of life by year twenty.

Higher energy bills

Poor roof ventilation and insulation drive heating and cooling costs up.

FAQ

Roofing questions, straight answers

How do I know if I need repair or replacement?

Age and extent of damage decide it. A newer roof with isolated damage usually gets a repair. Widespread wear, multiple leaks, or a roof past 20 years usually means replacement is the better value. Our free inspection gives you an honest read.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential replacements finish in one to three days, depending on size, pitch, and weather. You get a clear schedule before work starts.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. After storm damage we document everything with photos and written findings you can hand to your adjuster.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. Inspections and written estimates cost nothing and come with no obligation.
