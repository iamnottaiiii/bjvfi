The pour plan

How Every Job Runs

The same five steps on every project, so you always know what happens next.

1

Call and Site Look

We talk through the project and look at the site, measuring and noting access, grade and drainage.

2

Excavation and Base

Dig to depth, compact the subgrade and set a proper stone base. The part nobody sees is the part that matters most.

3

Formwork and Steel

Forms set straight and level, rebar placed for strength, everything checked before a single yard is ordered.

4

Pour and Finish

Placed, screeded and finished to spec, from broom finishes to stamped patterns.

5

Cure, Seal and Walkthrough

Proper curing time, sealing where specified, and a final walkthrough before we call it done.
