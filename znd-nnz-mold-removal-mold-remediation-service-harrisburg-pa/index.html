Why it matters

Mold is more than an eyesore

Mold can damage drywall, framing, and belongings, and it can aggravate allergies and breathing problems. The longer it sits, the further it spreads behind walls and under floors.

  • 1Musty odors, dark spots, or warped surfaces are common warning signs.
  • 2Bathrooms, basements, and crawl spaces are the usual trouble spots.
  • 3Fast action keeps a small cleanup from becoming a major rebuild.

Our process

What to expect, step by step

No surprises. You will know what we found, what we plan to do, and what it costs before any work begins.

01

Call and describe the problem

Tell us what you are seeing, smelling, or worried about. We will ask a few questions and schedule an on-site assessment.

02

On-site inspection

We examine the affected areas, take moisture readings, and trace the water source behind the growth.

03

Written plan and quote

You receive a clear scope of work with pricing before we lift a tool. Approve it and we schedule the job.

04

Containment and setup

We seal off the work area with barriers and set up HEPA air filtration so spores stay contained.

05

Removal and cleaning

Contaminated materials are removed and bagged. Remaining surfaces are HEPA vacuumed and treated.

06

Dry-out and moisture fix

The area is dried thoroughly and we address the moisture source so mold has nothing to feed on.

07

Final walkthrough

We inspect the finished work with you, recheck moisture levels, and hand over a completion summary.

08

Prevention guidance

You get practical steps for humidity control and early warning signs, so the problem stays solved.
