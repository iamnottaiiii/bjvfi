Our story

Southbridge's neighborhood chair

Raymon's Barber Shop sits on Main Street in Southbridge, serving men and boys from across the area with traditional barbering done right.

The shop's reputation was built the old way: one good haircut at a time. Regulars bring their sons, neighbors catch up in the waiting chairs, and the barbers are known for their patience with kids and their artistry with fades.

Affordable prices, a friendly room, and cuts that hold their shape. That is the whole formula.
