About

A Philadelphia painting business

Oscar Painting is based in South Philadelphia at 2101 Federal St, serving homes across the city with interior and exterior painting, cabinet work, staircase refinishing, and wall and ceiling painting.

The way we work

Prep first, paint second

Anyone can roll paint on a wall. The difference is everything that happens before the first coat.

A

Cover and mask

Floors, furniture and fixtures protected before a can is opened.

B

Repair and sand

Holes filled, rough spots sanded, surfaces made ready to hold paint.

C

Cut in clean

Straight lines where walls meet trim, ceilings and corners.

D

Leave it tidy

Workspace cleaned up at the end of the job, not left for you.
