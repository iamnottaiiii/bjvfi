Our story

The Below-the-Surface Specialists

American Construction Services describes itself as an all-in-one contractor for the jobs that protect a home from the ground up.

A practical kind of contractor

Based at 10602 Guilford Rd in Jessup, MD, the company focuses on work many contractors shy away from: mold testing and remediation, basement waterproofing, crawl space encapsulation, and the total renovations that follow once a home is dry and healthy.

The through-line is simple. Find the moisture, fix the moisture, remove the mold, then rebuild the space right. That sequence keeps homeowners from paying twice: once for a cosmetic fix, and again for the real one.
