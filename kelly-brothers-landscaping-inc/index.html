Our story

A Belmont landscaping crew since 1998

Kelly Brothers Landscaping Inc is based at 48 Bright Road in Belmont, Massachusetts. Directory records date the business to 1998, and list Stephen P. Kelly as its president.

For more than two decades, the company has served Belmont homeowners with everyday yard care and bigger outdoor projects alike. Customers mention large stone patios, complete landscape upgrades, and play areas, and describe Mr. Kelly as a sincere, hands-on owner who has run the business for a very long time.

It is a small, local operation: one team, one phone number, and a direct line to the owner on every job.
