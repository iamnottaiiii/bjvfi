About us

Harrisburg's restoration team

Local and responsive

Susquehanna Valley Restoration is based at 3915 Union Deposit Rd in Harrisburg, PA. Being local means faster arrival times when minutes matter, and a team that understands the homes, weather, and water risks of the Susquehanna Valley.

When disaster hits, you do not need a call center three states away. You need neighbors with pumps, dryers, and a plan. That is what we are.

What guides our work

  • Respond fast. Water damage gets worse by the hour, so we move quickly.
  • Be thorough. Hidden moisture causes mold. We find it and dry it.
  • Communicate. You always know what we are doing and what comes next.
  • Finish strong. We restore to pre-damage condition, not just good enough.
