Our story

A renovation supply home in North Hollywood

What public sources say about the business.

Home Renovation Supplies, known locally as Super Design Center, describes itself as an authorized Southern California dealer for home renovation supplies, a one-stop shop for flooring, cabinets and full remodels.

Its public posts show complete home transformations handled by the team, including floors, kitchen cabinets, wall panels, bathrooms, windows and interior doors. The business has announced a main store in Gardena at 809 W Gardena Blvd, and its North Hollywood location continues to serve the Valley at 7215 Whitsett Ave.

Customers in public reviews praise the helpful staff and reasonable prices, and say installations finish faster than expected.
