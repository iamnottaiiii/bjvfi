About

Two crafts,
one standard

MagicPro Painting and WallPapering is a Delran, New Jersey contractor built around a simple idea: walls look best when paint and paper are planned together.

The approach

Prep like it matters,
because it does

Whether the finish is paint or paper, the work starts the same way: patching, sanding and priming until the surface is right. That is what makes paint last and what makes wallpaper seams disappear.

Based in Delran and serving surrounding Burlington County communities, MagicPro takes on whole rooms and whole homes: paint the walls, paper the accent, refinish the trim, and hand it all back clean.
