THE STUDIO

Erin G. Torrano, designer

EGT Interiors is the interior design practice of Erin G. Torrano, based in Fort Thomas, Kentucky. Her work blends respect for a home's bones with fresh, livable style, creating rooms that feel collected over time rather than decorated overnight.

Good design is personal. It should feel like the best version of your life, not someone else's.

Clients meet Erin at her showroom inside The Studios of 33 North, where each room demonstrates a distinct style and the possibilities of thoughtful design.

The Studios of 33 North

In 2014, a group of Northern Kentucky design and building professionals transformed a former residence at 33 N. Fort Thomas Ave. into a shared showroom and studio home. Stained glass painstakingly restored stands alongside modern light fixtures and finishes, and every room shows a distinct design point of view.

EGT Interiors works alongside fellow 33 North businesses, including architecture, kitchen and bath design, custom building, and home technology specialists, giving clients one address for a whole-home vision.
