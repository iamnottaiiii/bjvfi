Our Story

A Palos Hills institution, one loaf at a time.

Palestine Bakery has been baking on Harlem Avenue in Palos Hills since 1962. The shop's own words say it best: "Offering the freshest and best tasting pita bread since 1962."

Generations of families have stocked their kitchens with the bakery's pita, shrak, and taboon bread, picked up maamoul cookies for the holidays, and stopped in for a hot falafel sandwich. It is the kind of place where the bread is still warm when it reaches your car.

Whether you grew up with these flavors or are tasting them for the first time, the oven is on and the door is open.
