A FIXTURE ON RIVER RD

Business directories list Hamant's Auto Body at 422 River Rd, Bromley, KY 41017 as established in 1968 and incorporated in Kentucky, with an estimated staff of about four people. Directory descriptions also reference 1967 as the start. Either way, this is a shop with decades behind it, not a fly by night operation.

Sources: Dun and Bradstreet business directory, Manta business listing. Owner contact name Larry Hamant appears on a public auto repair directory listing.

WHAT RIDERS PRAISE

Public comments praise the motorcycle side of the shop: fair prices, timely clutch work, hard to find parts sourced when other shops struck out, and paint jobs riders were happy with. Several comments describe it as the go to spot for bike service in the area.

WHAT TO ASK ABOUT

Some public reviews describe serious frustration with long waits on insurance funded front end repairs, with one account describing months without the car back. If you are booking body work, ask up front for a realistic timeline, get it in writing, and agree on how updates will be communicated.
