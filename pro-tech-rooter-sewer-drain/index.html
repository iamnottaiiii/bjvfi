What we fix

Tap a category to see what Pro-Tech handles. Every job starts with finding the blockage, not guessing.

Drain cleaning

Kitchen sinks, bathroom drains, laundry lines, and floor drains. We clear grease, hair, soap, and buildup so water moves freely again.

  • Kitchen and bathroom drain unclogging
  • Main line blockage removal
  • Recurring clog diagnosis

Sewer line service

Tree roots, breaks, bellies, and backups in the sewer lateral. We locate the failure and recommend the right repair, from spot fixes to full replacement.

  • Sewer line repair and replacement
  • Root intrusion removal
  • Sewer backup cleanup response
  • New sewer line installation

Camera inspection and hydro jetting

See exactly what is wrong inside the pipe before any digging. High pressure water jetting scours lines clean where snakes leave residue behind.

  • Video camera pipe inspection
  • Hydro jetting for heavy buildup
  • Pre purchase sewer scope checks
  • Line locating and mapping
