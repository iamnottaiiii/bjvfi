Our Story

One mechanic.
One reputation.

Wilcox Auto Services is a small, independent repair shop at 714 Mill St in Tarentum, run as a one-man operation. There is no service writer upselling you and no corporate script. You talk to the mechanic, he diagnoses the car, he fixes it.

That is how the shop earned its name around town: by word of mouth. Cousins send cousins. Out-of-state visitors plan brake jobs around a trip home. Reviewers describe a mechanic who is responsive, precise, affordable, and who phones the next day to check that a repair settled in right. Three other shops could not get a truck started; Wilcox had it fixed in three days.
