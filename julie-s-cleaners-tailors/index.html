Who we are

One shop, two crafts, done right

Julie's Cleaners and Tailors sits in the Princeton Place Shopping Center at 3747 Church Road, and for more than three decades it has been Mount Laurel's answer to both sides of garment care: careful dry cleaning and true master tailoring.

Owner Julie built the shop's reputation stitch by stitch. Customers describe her as a master tailor with a gift for fit, someone who can take a simple garment and make it feel made for you. Quick turnarounds, fair prices, and a genuinely friendly counter keep first-time visitors coming back as regulars.
