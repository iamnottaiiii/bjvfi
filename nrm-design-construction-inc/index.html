Our story

Nearly two decades of design and construction

NRM Design & Construction INC has served Valley Stream and the surrounding area since 2008.

Since 2008

Built on repeat customers

NRM Design & Construction INC was incorporated in New York in 2008 and is based at 27 Forest Rd in Valley Stream. The team brings 18 years of industry experience to every project.

The company holds a New York home improvement contractor license and is listed as a licensed general contractor, handling both commercial and residential painting and roofing work across Long Island and Queens.
