Our story

The last of the
old-time shops

A locally owned and run repair shop on Lorton Rd, the kind of place customers describe as honest, hardworking, and knowledgeable.

Nothing fancy. Just honest.

One longtime customer put it best: Laurel Hill is the last of the old-time, locally owned repair shops in the Lorton area. It is not fancy. It is honest, and it does the work well.

Families have been bringing their cars here for over a decade, and then their kids' cars too. That kind of loyalty is not bought. It is earned one fair repair at a time, year after year.

The team is known for explaining repairs in plain language, suggesting cost-effective solutions, and fitting customers in quickly for the small stuff: a state inspection, a light bulb, a pending flat tire.
