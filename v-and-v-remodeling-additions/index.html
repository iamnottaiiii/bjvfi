How a project runs

1. Call and walkthrough. You describe the room, we come measure it. No obligation talk, just tape measures and honest notes.

2. Plan on paper. Layout, materials, and scope get written down before anything is torn out.

3. Build in sequence. Demo, rough-in, finish work, then a final walkthrough where the punch list gets closed out.
