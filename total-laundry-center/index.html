Our story

A neighborhood laundry for 27 years

Total Laundry Center has served Baltimore's Westland Blvd corridor for nearly three decades. What keeps regulars coming back is simple: a clean store, working machines, fair prices, and staff who actually help.

Reviewers call it very clean with great customer service, and note the friendly staff and some of the best prices around. The center offers more than machines: wash and fold drop off, dry cleaning, and laundry supplies on the shelf.

Whether you are a weekly regular, a busy parent outsourcing the mountain, or a traveler with a suitcase of laundry, you are welcome here.
