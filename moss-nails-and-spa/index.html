Our Story

A neighborhood salon on Avenue J

Moss Nails & spa serves the Brooklyn community from 1117 Avenue J with manicures, pedicures, acrylics, gel, spa treatments, massage and waxing.

The salon

Clean, cozy and professional

Visitors describe a clean, sterile environment with a cozy atmosphere and an organized, tidy space throughout. The team focuses on quality, hygiene and professional attention so every guest leaves looking spectacular.

Walk-ins are welcome and reviewers report minimal wait times. Services are described as quality work at fair, moderate prices.

That sentiment comes up again and again in reviews: careful work, a courteous ambiance, and nails that hold up. Longtime clients return for gel manicures, pedicures and wax services.
