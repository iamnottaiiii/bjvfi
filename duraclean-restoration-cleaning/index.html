Our story

A local crew, trained like a national one.

Duraclean Restoration & Cleaning serves Canoga Park and the surrounding San Fernando Valley from our shop at 8040 Remmet Ave. We pair the responsiveness of a neighborhood crew with professional training standards: Duraclean technicians are trained and tested through Duraclean International and the IICRC, the inspection, cleaning, and restoration certification body.

A

No commission, no pressure

Our technicians do not work on commission, so recommendations are about what your home needs, never about a sales quota.

B

Trained and tested

Technicians are trained and tested by both the IICRC and Duraclean International, a standard that has backed Duraclean cleaning and restoration work for decades.

C

One call does it all

Water extraction, drying, mold prevention, and the final carpet clean come from one accountable team.
