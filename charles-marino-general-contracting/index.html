About

An East Rockaway builder

Charles Marino General Contracting works out of 5 Cooke St W in East Rockaway, a contractor shaped by the rebuilding years after Superstorm Sandy.

The story

Built in the rebuilding years

East Rockaway and the surrounding South Shore towns were hit hard by Superstorm Sandy, and the years after were defined by one question: how do we put these houses back, stronger?

Charles Marino General Contracting was part of that answer. Public permit records from 2017 show the company elevating and repairing a Sandy damaged one family dwelling right on Cooke St W, plus related storm repairs, with combined valuations near $238,000.

That history says a lot about the work: structural, permitted, inspected, and done in the neighborhood the company calls home. Today the company takes on residential additions, renovations and remodeling with the same seriousness.

What customers get

  1. Structural competence. The company is known for elevation and addition work, not just cosmetic updates.
  2. Local accountability. Based in East Rockaway, working in the same towns it lives in.
  3. Permitted work. The public record shows properly permitted projects, which protects your home's value.
  4. Direct contact. Call (516) 897-0857 and talk to the contractor, not a call center.
