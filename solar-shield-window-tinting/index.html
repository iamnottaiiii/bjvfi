Why tint

Four ways film upgrades your drive

01

Heat rejection

Quality window film rejects a large share of solar heat, so your cabin stays cooler and your AC works less.

02

Glare reduction

Cut harsh sun glare off the windshield and side glass for a calmer, safer view of the road.

03

UV protection

Most films block up to 99% of harmful UV rays, protecting skin and slowing interior fading and cracking.

04

Privacy

Darker shades keep valuables and passengers out of sight while giving your vehicle a clean, finished look.
